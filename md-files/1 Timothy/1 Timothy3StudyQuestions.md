# 1 Timothy 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Timothy 3:1

**Q1. What kind of saying is this?**  
**Answer:** This is a true saying.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:1

**Q2. What does a man desire if he desires the office of a bishop?**  
**Answer:** He desireth a good work.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:1

**Q3. What office does a man desire if he desires a good work?**  
**Answer:** The office of a bishop.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:1

---

### 1 Timothy 3:2

**Q1. What must a bishop be?**  
**Answer:** Blameless.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:2

**Q2. How many wives should the husband of one wife have?**  
**Answer:** One.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:2

**Q3. What should a bishop be regarding his behavior and home?**  
**Answer:** Vigilant, sober, of good behaviour, given to hospitality, apt to teach;  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:2

---

### 1 Timothy 3:3

**Q1. What should a bishop not be given to?**  
**Answer:** Wine.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:3

**Q2. How should a bishop be regarding fighting?**  
**Answer:** No striker.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:3

**Q3. What should a bishop not be greedy of?**  
**Answer:** Filthy lucre.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:3

---

### 1 Timothy 3:4

**Q1. Who must a bishop rule well?**  
**Answer:** His own house.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:4

**Q2. Who should have his children in subjection?**  
**Answer:** One that ruleth well his own house.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:4

**Q3. With what must his children be in subjection?**  
**Answer:** All gravity.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:4

---

### 1 Timothy 3:5

**Q1. What does a man not know how to do if he knows not how to rule his own house?**  
**Answer:** Take care of the church of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:5

**Q2. Whose house must a man know how to rule?**  
**Answer:** His own house.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:5

**Q3. Whose church is he unable to take care of if he cannot rule his own house?**  
**Answer:** The church of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:5

---

### 1 Timothy 3:6

**Q1. Who must a bishop not be?**  
**Answer:** A novice.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:6

**Q2. What might happen to a novice if he is lifted up with pride?**  
**Answer:** He fall into the condemnation of the devil.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:6

**Q3. What does being lifted up with pride lead to for a novice?**  
**Answer:** The condemnation of the devil.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:6

---

### 1 Timothy 3:7

**Q1. Who must a bishop also have a good report of?**  
**Answer:** Them that are without.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:7

**Q2. What might a bishop fall into if he does not have a good report?**  
**Answer:** Reproach and the snare of the devil.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:7

**Q3. Whose snare might a bishop fall into?**  
**Answer:** The devil.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:7

---

### 1 Timothy 3:8

**Q1. What must the deacons likewise be?**  
**Answer:** Grave.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:8

**Q2. What must deacons not be double in?**  
**Answer:** Double-tongued.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:8

**Q3. What should deacons not be greedy of?**  
**Answer:** Filthy lucre.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:8

---

### 1 Timothy 3:9

**Q1. What must deacons hold?**  
**Answer:** The mystery of the faith.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:9

**Q2. In what must they hold the mystery of the faith?**  
**Answer:** A pure conscience.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:9

**Q3. What kind of conscience should they have?**  
**Answer:** Pure.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:9

---

### 1 Timothy 3:10

**Q1. Who must first be proved?**  
**Answer:** These also.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:10

**Q2. When should they use the office of a deacon?**  
**Answer:** Being found blameless.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:10

**Q3. What should happen to them before they use the office of a deacon?**  
**Answer:** Let them be proved first.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:10

---

### 1 Timothy 3:11

**Q1. What must their wives likewise be?**  
**Answer:** Grave.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:11

**Q2. What must the wives not be?**  
**Answer:** Slanderers.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:11

**Q3. In how many things must they be sober and faithful?**  
**Answer:** Faithful in all things.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:11

---

### 1 Timothy 3:12

**Q1. What must the deacons be the husbands of?**  
**Answer:** One wife.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:12

**Q2. Who must the deacons rule well?**  
**Answer:** Their children and their own houses.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:12

**Q3. How must they rule their children and their own houses?**  
**Answer:** Well.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:12

---

### 1 Timothy 3:13

**Q1. What do they that have used the office of a deacon well purchase to themselves?**  
**Answer:** A good degree.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:13

**Q2. What else do they purchase besides a good degree?**  
**Answer:** Great boldness in the faith which is in Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:13

**Q3. In what faith is the great boldness?**  
**Answer:** The faith which is in Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:13

---

### 1 Timothy 3:14

**Q1. What things did Paul write to Timothy?**  
**Answer:** These things.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:14

**Q2. What did Paul hope to do shortly?**  
**Answer:** Come unto thee.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:14

**Q3. How soon did Paul hope to come unto Timothy?**  
**Answer:** Shortly.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:14

---

### 1 Timothy 3:15

**Q1. What might Paul tarry long?**  
**Answer:** If I tarry long.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:15

**Q2. What shouldest thou know how thou oughtest to behave thyself in?**  
**Answer:** The house of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:15

**Q3. What is the house of God defined as?**  
**Answer:** The church of the living God, the pillar and ground of the truth.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:15

---

### 1 Timothy 3:16

**Q1. Without controversy, what is great?**  
**Answer:** The mystery of godliness.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:16

**Q2. Who was manifest in the flesh?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:16

**Q3. How was God received?**  
**Answer:** Up into glory.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 3:16
