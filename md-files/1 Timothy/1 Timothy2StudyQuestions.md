# 1 Timothy 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Timothy 2:1

**Q1. What did Paul exhort to be made first of all?**  
**Answer:** Supplications, prayers, intercessions, and giving of thanks.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:1

**Q2. For whom should these supplications, prayers, intercessions, and giving of thanks be made?**  
**Answer:** For all men.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:1

---

### 1 Timothy 2:2

**Q3. For whom specifically should prayers be made besides all men?**  
**Answer:** For kings, and for all that are in authority.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:2

**Q4. For what purpose should we pray for kings and all in authority?**  
**Answer:** That we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and honesty.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:2

**Q5. In what manner are we to lead our lives under kings and those in authority?**  
**Answer:** In all godliness and honesty.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:2

### 1 Timothy 2:3

**Q6. What is described as good and acceptable?**  
**Answer:** This.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:3

**Q7. Before whom is this good and acceptable?**  
**Answer:** In the sight of God our Saviour.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:3

**Q8. How is God described in relation to our salvation in this verse?**  
**Answer:** God our Saviour.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:3

### 1 Timothy 2:4

**Q9. Who will have all men to be saved?**  
**Answer:** Who will have all men to be saved.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:4

**Q10. What else does God desire all men to come unto besides being saved?**  
**Answer:** And to come unto the knowledge of the truth.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:4

**Q11. What is the extent of men God desires to be saved and come to the knowledge of the truth?**

**Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:4

**Q14. Who is the one mediator between God and men?**

**Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:5

**Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:7

### 1 Timothy 2:8

**Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:8

**Q22. What should men lift up when they pray?**  
**Answer:** Holy hands.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:8

**Q23. What should be absent when men lift up holy hands?**

**Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:8

### 1 Timothy 2:9

**Q24. How should women adorn themselves?**  
**Answer:** In modest apparel, with shamefacedness and sobriety.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:9

**Q25. What should women not adorn themselves with regarding their hair?**  
**Answer:** Not with broidered hair.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:9

**Q26. What other items are listed that women should not use to adorn themselves?**  
**Answer:** Gold, or pearls, or costly array.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:9
