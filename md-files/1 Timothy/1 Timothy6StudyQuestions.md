# 1 Timothy 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Timothy 6:1

**Q1. Under what should servants count their own masters worthy of all honour?**  
**Answer:** The yoke.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:1

**Q2. What should not be blasphemed because of servants counting their masters worthy of honour?**  
**Answer:** The name of God and his doctrine.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:1

**Q3. Who are the masters that servants should count worthy of all honour?**  
**Answer:** Their own masters under the yoke.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:1

---

### 1 Timothy 6:2

**Q1. What should believing masters not despise because they are brethren?**  
**Answer:** Their servants.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:2

**Q2. Rather than despising them, how should they do service, seeing they are faithful and beloved?**  
**Answer:** Rather because they are faithful and beloved, partakers of the benefit.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:2

**Q3. What are these things that a man should teach and exhort?**  
**Answer:** These things teach and exhort.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:2

---

### 1 Timothy 6:3

**Q1. If any man teach otherwise, what words does he not consent to?**  
**Answer:** Wholesome words, even the words of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:3

**Q2. What doctrine is according to godliness?**  
**Answer:** The doctrine which is according to godliness.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:3

**Q3. What is a person who teaches otherwise and does not consent to wholesome words characterized as?**  
**Answer:** He is proud, knowing nothing.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:3

---

### 1 Timothy 6:4

**Q1. What is a man who is proud, knowing nothing, sick about?**  
**Answer:** Questions and strifes of words.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:4

**Q2. What are the results that come from questions and strifes of words?**  
**Answer:** Envy, strife, railings, evil surmisings.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:4

**Q3. Of what sort of men do perverse disputings come?**  
**Answer:** Men of corrupt minds, and destitute of the truth.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:4

---

### 1 Timothy 6:5

**Q1. What do men of corrupt minds suppose gain is?**  
**Answer:** Godliness.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:5

**Q2. From what kind of men should you withdraw yourself?**  
**Answer:** From such withdraw thyself.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:5

**Q3. What are perverse disputings of men of corrupt minds described as?**  
**Answer:** Perverse disputings of men of corrupt minds, and destitute of the truth.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:5

---

### 1 Timothy 6:6

**Q1. What is great gain when coupled with contentment?**  
**Answer:** Godliness.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:6

**Q2. What makes godliness with contentment great gain?**  
**Answer:** Contentment.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:6

**Q3. How is godliness with contentment described in terms of value?**  
**Answer:** Great gain.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:6

---

### 1 Timothy 6:7

**Q1. What did we bring into this world?**  
**Answer:** Nothing.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:7

**Q2. What is certain that we can carry out of this world?**  
**Answer:** It is certain we can carry nothing out.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:7

**Q3. What statement is made regarding bringing things into and carrying things out of the world?**  
**Answer:** For we brought nothing into this world, and it is certain we can carry nothing out.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:7

---

### 1 Timothy 6:8

**Q1. Having what two things should we be therewith content?**  
**Answer:** Food and raiment.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:8

**Q2. When we have food and raiment, what should we be with them?**  
**Answer:** Content.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:8

**Q3. Whose food and raiment are referenced in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Having food and raiment let us be therewith content.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:8

---

### 1 Timothy 6:9

**Q1. What do they that will be rich fall into?**  
**Answer:** Temptation and a snare.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:9

**Q2. Into what kind of foolish and hurtful lusts do they fall?**  
**Answer:** Many foolish and hurtful lusts.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:9

**Q3. Into what do these foolish and hurtful lusts drown men?**  
**Answer:** Destruction and perdition.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:9

---

### 1 Timothy 6:10

**Q1. What is the root of all evil?**  
**Answer:** The love of money.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:10

**Q2. What have some coveted after while erring from the faith?**  
**Answer:** The love of money (or money).  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:10

**Q3. What have those who coveted after money pierced themselves through with?**  
**Answer:** Many sorrows.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:10

---

### 1 Timothy 6:11

**Q1. O man of God, what should you flee?**  
**Answer:** These things.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:11

**Q2. What things should you follow after in addition to righteousness and godliness?**  
**Answer:** Faith, love, patience, meekness.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:11

**Q3. Who is addressed as fleeing these things and following after righteousness?**  
**Answer:** Thou, O man of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:11

---

### 1 Timothy 6:12

**Q1. What should you fight?**  
**Answer:** The good fight of faith.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:12

**Q2. What should you lay hold on?**  
**Answer:** Eternal life.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:12

**Q3. Whereunto were you called, where you have professed a good profession?**  
**Answer:** Whereunto thou art also called, and hast professed a good profession before many witnesses.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:12

---

### 1 Timothy 6:13

**Q1. Before whom do I give thee charge?**  
**Answer:** God, who quickeneth all things, and before Christ Jesus, who before Pontius Pilate witnessed a good confession.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:13

**Q2. What does God quicken?**  
**Answer:** All things.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:13

**Q3. Before whom did Christ Jesus witness a good confession?**  
**Answer:** Pontius Pilate.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:13

---

### 1 Timothy 6:14

**Q1. How should you keep this commandment?**  
**Answer:** Without spot, unrebukeable.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:14

**Q2. Until what event should you keep this commandment?**  
**Answer:** Until the appearing of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:14

**Q3. What kind of commandment is to be kept without spot and unrebukeable?**  
**Answer:** This commandment.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:14

---

### 1 Timothy 6:15

**Q1. Who shall show the appearing of our Lord Jesus Christ in His times?**  
**Answer:** Which in his times he shall shew.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:15

**Q2. How is God described regarding His blessedness and power?**  
**Answer:** The blessed and only Potentate, the King of kings, and Lord of lords.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:15

**Q3. Who is the Potentate who shall show this appearing?**  
**Answer:** The blessed and only Potentate.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:15

---

### 1 Timothy 6:16

**Q1. Who alone has immortality?**  
**Answer:** Who only hath immortality.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:16

**Q2. In what kind of light does He dwell?**  
**Answer:** Dwelling in the light which no man can approach unto.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:16

**Q3. What has no man seen or can see?**  
**Answer:** Whom no man hath seen, nor can see.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:16

---

### 1 Timothy 6:17

**Q1. Whom should you charge that are rich in this world?**  
**Answer:** Them that are rich in this world.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:17

**Q2. What should the rich not be, nor trust in?**  
**Answer:** Not highminded, nor trust in uncertain riches.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:17

**Q3. In whom should the rich trust for all things to enjoy?**  
**Answer:** In the living God, who giveth us richly all things to enjoy.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:17

---

### 1 Timothy 6:18

**Q1. What should the rich do that they be rich in good works?**  
**Answer:** That they do good.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:18

**Q2. How should the rich be with regard to distribution?**  
**Answer:** Ready to distribute, willing to communicate.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:18

**Q3. What kind of works should they be rich in?**  
**Answer:** Good works.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:18

---

### 1 Timothy 6:19

**Q1. What are the rich laying up in store for themselves?**  
**Answer:** A good foundation against the time to come.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:19

**Q2. What may they lay hold on by laying up this good foundation?**  
**Answer:** Lay hold on eternal life.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:19

**Q3. What kind of foundation are they laying up for the time to come?**  
**Answer:** A good foundation.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:19

---

### 1 Timothy 6:20

**Q1. O Timothy, what should you keep?**  
**Answer:** That which is committed to thy trust.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:20

**Q2. What should you avoid regarding profane and vain babblings?**  
**Answer:** Avoiding profane and vain babblings.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:20

**Q3. What are opposed to what is committed to thy trust and are called oppositions?**  
**Answer:** Oppositions of science falsely so called.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:20

---

### 1 Timothy 6:21

**Q1. What have some professed concerning the faith?**  
**Answer:** Which some professing have erred concerning the faith.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:21

**Q2. What is said at the very end of the chapter to the recipient(s)?**  
**Answer:** Grace be with thee. Amen.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:21

**Q3. Who is addressed in the closing salutation of the chapter?**  
**Answer:** Thee.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 6:21
