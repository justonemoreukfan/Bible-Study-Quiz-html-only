# 1 Timothy 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Timothy 1:1
**Q1. Of whom was Paul an apostle?**  
**Answer:** Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:1

**Q2. By whose commandment was Paul made an apostle?**  
**Answer:** By the commandment of God our Saviour, and Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:1

**Q3. Who is referred to as our hope?**  
**Answer:** Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:1

---

### 1 Timothy 1:2
**Q4. How did Paul refer to Timothy in his salutation?**  
**Answer:** My own son in the faith.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:2

**Q5. What three blessings did Paul extend to Timothy?**  
**Answer:** Grace, mercy, and peace.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:2

**Q6. From whom do grace, mercy, and peace come?**  
**Answer:** From God our Father and Jesus Christ our Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:2

---

### 1 Timothy 1:3
**Q7. Where did Paul beseech Timothy to abide still?**  
**Answer:** At Ephesus.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:3

**Q8. Where was Paul going when he urged Timothy to remain at Ephesus?**  
**Answer:** Into Macedonia.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:3

**Q9. What was Timothy instructed to charge some men regarding their teaching?**  
**Answer:** That they teach no other doctrine.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:3

---

### 1 Timothy 1:4
**Q10. What two things were they told not to give heed to?**  
**Answer:** Fables and endless genealogies.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:4

**Q11. What do fables and endless genealogies minister?**  
**Answer:** Questions.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:4

**Q12. What do fables and endless genealogies fail to promote, which is in faith?**  
**Answer:** Godly edifying.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:4

---

### 1 Timothy 1:5
**Q13. What is the end of the commandment?**  
**Answer:** Charity.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:5

**Q14. Out of what kind of heart and conscience does this charity come?**  
**Answer:** Out of a pure heart, and of a good conscience.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:5

**Q15. What kind of faith produces this charity?**  
**Answer:** Faith unfeigned.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:5

---

### 1 Timothy 1:6
**Q16. What did some do concerning a pure heart, good conscience, and unfeigned faith?**  
**Answer:** They swerved.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:6

**Q17. Unto what did those who swerved turn aside?**  
**Answer:** Unto vain jangling.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:6

**Q18. Who turned aside unto vain jangling?**  
**Answer:** Some having swerved from the end of the commandment.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:6

---

### 1 Timothy 1:7
**Q19. What were those who turned aside desiring to be?**  
**Answer:** Teachers of the law.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:7

**Q20. What did these aspiring teachers fail to understand?**  
**Answer:** Neither what they say, nor whereof they affirm.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:7

**Q21. Did those desiring to be teachers of the law understand the things they affirmed?**  
**Answer:** No, they understood neither what they said nor whereof they affirmed.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:7

---

### 1 Timothy 1:8
**Q22. What do we know about the law?**  
**Answer:** That the law is good.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:8

**Q23. Under what condition is the law good?**  
**Answer:** If a man use it lawfully.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:8

**Q24. How must a man use the law?**  
**Answer:** Lawfully.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:8

---

### 1 Timothy 1:9
**Q25. For whom is the law not made?**  
**Answer:** For a righteous man.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:9

**Q26. For what initial pairs of people is the law made?**  
**Answer:** For the lawless and disobedient, for the ungodly and for sinners, for unholy and profane.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:9

**Q27. What specific types of killers are mentioned in verse 9 as those for whom the law is made?**  
**Answer:** Murderers of fathers and murderers of mothers, and manslayers.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:9

---

### 1 Timothy 1:10
**Q28. What persons guilty of sexual sins and kidnapping are named in verse 10?**  
**Answer:** Whoremongers, them that defile themselves with mankind, and menstealers.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:10

**Q29. What two kinds of deceitful persons are listed in verse 10?**  
**Answer:** Liars and perjured persons.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:10

**Q30. Besides the named offenders, against what else is the law directed?**  
**Answer:** Any other thing that is contrary to sound doctrine.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:10

### 1 Timothy 1:11
**Q31. According to what was the law or sound doctrine established?**  
**Answer:** According to the glorious gospel of the blessed God.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:11  

**Q32. Whose glorious gospel is spoken of in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The glorious gospel of the blessed God.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:11  

**Q33. What had been committed to Paul's trust?**  
**Answer:** The glorious gospel of the blessed God.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:11  

---

### 1 Timothy 1:12
**Q34. Whom does Paul thank for enabling him?**  
**Answer:** Christ Jesus our Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:12  

**Q35. Why did Christ Jesus enable Paul and put him into the ministry?**  
**Answer:** For that he counted him faithful.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:12  

**Q36. Into what did Christ Jesus put Paul?**  
**Answer:** Into the ministry.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:12  

---

### 1 Timothy 1:13
**Q37. What was Paul before he obtained mercy?**  
**Answer:** A blasphemer, and a persecutor, and injurious.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:13  

**Q38. What did Paul obtain despite his past behavior?**  
**Answer:** Mercy.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:13  

**Q39. Why did Paul obtain mercy?**  
**Answer:** Because he did it ignorantly in unbelief.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:13  

---

### 1 Timothy 1:14
**Q40. How is the grace of our Lord described in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Exceeding abundant.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:14  

**Q41. With what was the grace of our Lord exceeding abundant?**  
**Answer:** With faith and love which is in Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:14  

**Q42. In whom is the faith and love mentioned found?**  
**Answer:** In Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:14  

---

### 1 Timothy 1:15
**Q43. What is described as a faithful saying and worthy of all acceptation?**  
**Answer:** That Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:15  

**Q44. For what purpose did Christ Jesus come into the world?**  
**Answer:** To save sinners.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:15  

**Q45. How did Paul describe his own standing among sinners?**  
**Answer:** "Of whom I am chief."  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:15  

---

### 1 Timothy 1:16
**Q46. For what cause did Paul obtain mercy?**  
**Answer:** That in him first Jesus Christ might shew forth all longsuffering.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:16  

**Q47. What did Jesus Christ show forth first in Paul?**  
**Answer:** All longsuffering.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:16  

**Q48. Unto whom was Paul's receiving of mercy intended to be a pattern?**  
**Answer:** To them which should hereafter believe on him to life everlasting.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:16  

---

### 1 Timothy 1:17
**Q49. How is the King described in this doxology?**  
**Answer:** Eternal, immortal, invisible, the only wise God.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:17  

**Q50. What is ascribed unto the only wise God for ever and ever?**  
**Answer:** Honour and glory.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:17  

**Q51. What word concludes this verse of praise?**  
**Answer:** Amen.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:17  

---

### 1 Timothy 1:18
**Q52. Unto whom did Paul commit this charge?**  
**Answer:** Son Timothy.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:18  

**Q53. According to what did Paul commit this charge unto Timothy?**  
**Answer:** According to the prophecies which went before on him.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:18  

**Q54. What was Timothy expected to do by those prophecies?**  
**Answer:** War a good warfare.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:18  

---

### 1 Timothy 1:19
**Q55. What two things was Timothy charged to hold?**  
**Answer:** Faith, and a good conscience.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:19  

**Q56. What had some people put away?**  
**Answer:** A good conscience.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:19  

**Q57. What happened to some concerning faith after putting away a good conscience?**  
**Answer:** They made shipwreck.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:19  

---

### 1 Timothy 1:20
**Q58. Which two men are specifically named as having made shipwreck concerning faith?**  
**Answer:** Hymenaeus and Alexander.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:20  

**Q59. Unto whom did Paul deliver Hymenaeus and Alexander?**  
**Answer:** Unto Satan.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:20  

**Q60. What was the purpose of delivering them unto Satan?**  
**Answer:** That they may learn not to blaspheme.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 1:20
