# 1 Timothy 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Timothy 5:1

**Q1. Who should Timothy not rebuke?**  
**Answer:** An elder.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:1

**Q2. How should Timothy entreat an elder?**  
**Answer:** As a father.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:1

**Q3. How should Timothy entreat the younger men?**  
**Answer:** As brethren.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:1

---

### 1 Timothy 5:2

**Q4. How should Timothy entreat the elder women?**  
**Answer:** As mothers.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:2

**Q5. How should Timothy entreat the younger women?**  
**Answer:** As sisters.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:2

**Q6. With what should Timothy entreat the younger women?**  
**Answer:** All purity.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:2

---

### 1 Timothy 5:3

**Q7. Who should be honoured?**  
**Answer:** Widows that are widows indeed.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:3

---

### 1 Timothy 5:4

**Q8. What should any widow have first?**  
**Answer:** Children or nephews.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:4

**Q9. What should children or nephews learn first?**  
**Answer:** To show piety at home, and to requite their parents.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:4

**Q10. Why is showing piety at home and requiting parents good and acceptable?**  
**Answer:** For that is good and acceptable before God.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:4

---

### 1 Timothy 5:5

**Q11. What is a widow indeed, and desolate, described as doing?**  
**Answer:** Trusteth in God.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:5

**Q12. What does a widow indeed continue in day and night?**  
**Answer:** Supplications and prayers.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:5

---

### 1 Timothy 5:6

**Q13. What is said of a widow that liveth in pleasure?**  
**Answer:** She is dead while she liveth.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:6

---

### 1 Timothy 5:7

**Q14. What things should Timothy give in charge?**  
**Answer:** These things.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:7

**Q15. Why should Timothy give these things in charge?**  
**Answer:** That they may be blameless.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:7

---

### 1 Timothy 5:8

**Q16. Who does a man not provide for if he provides not for his own?**  
**Answer:** His own, and specially for those of his own house.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:8

**Q17. What has a man denied if he provides not for his own?**  
**Answer:** The faith.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:8

**Q18. What is a man who provides not for his own worse than?**  
**Answer:** An infidel.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:8

---

### 1 Timothy 5:9

**Q19. What age must a widow not be under to be taken into the number?**  
**Answer:** Threescore years old.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:9

**Q20. Whose wife must a widow taken into the number have been?**  
**Answer:** The wife of one man.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:9

---

### 1 Timothy 5:10

**Q21. What must a widow be well reported of for?**  
**Answer:** Good works.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:10

**Q22. What are some of the good works mentioned for a widow?**  
**Answer:** If she have brought up children, if she have lodged strangers, if she have washed the saints' feet, if she have relieved the afflicted, if she have diligently followed every good work.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:10

---

### 1 Timothy 5:11

**Q23. What kind of widows should be refused?**  
**Answer:** The younger widows.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:11

**Q24. When do younger widows wax wanton against Christ?**  
**Answer:** When they have begun to wax wanton against Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:11

**Q25. What do they desire to do when they wax wanton?**  
**Answer:** To marry.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:11

---

### 1 Timothy 5:12

**Q26. What do younger widows have because they have cast off their first faith?**  
**Answer:** Damnation.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:12

---

### 1 Timothy 5:13

**Q27. What do younger widows learn to be besides idle?**  
**Answer:** Wanderers about from house to house.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:13

**Q28. What are they besides idle and wanderers?**  
**Answer:** Tattlers also and busybodies.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:13

**Q29. What do they speak that they ought not?**  
**Answer:** Things which they ought not.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:13

---

### 1 Timothy 5:14

**Q30. What do I will the younger women to do?**  
**Answer:** Marry, bear children, guide the house.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:14

**Q31. What occasion should be given to the adversary?**  
**Answer:** None.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:14

**Q32. Why should no occasion be given to the adversary?**  
**Answer:** To speak reproachfully.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:14

---

### 1 Timothy 5:15

**Q33. Who are some already turned aside?**  
**Answer:** Some are already turned aside after Satan.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:15

---

### 1 Timothy 5:16

**Q34. Who has believing men or women?**  
**Answer:** Any man or woman that believeth.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:16

**Q35. Who should be relieved by believers?**  
**Answer:** Widows.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:16

**Q36. Why should the church not be charged with widows?**  
**Answer:** That it may relieve them that are widows indeed.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:16

---

### 1 Timothy 5:17

**Q37. What kind of elders are counted worthy of double honour?**  
**Answer:** The elders that rule well.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:17

**Q38. Who, besides those that rule well, are especially worthy of double honour?**  
**Answer:** They who labour in the word and doctrine.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:17

---

### 1 Timothy 5:18

**Q39. What scripture is quoted about the ox?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not muzzle the ox that treadeth out the corn.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:18

**Q40. What is the labourer worthy of?**  
**Answer:** His reward.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:18

---

### 1 Timothy 5:19

**Q41. Against an elder, what should not be received?**  
**Answer:** An accusation.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:19

**Q42. How many or two witnesses are required for an accusation against an elder?**  
**Answer:** Before two or three witnesses.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:19

---

### 1 Timothy 5:20

**Q43. Who should be rebuked before all?**  
**Answer:** Them that sin.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:20

**Q44. What should others do when sinners are rebuked before all?**  
**Answer:** That others also may fear.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:20

---

### 1 Timothy 5:21

**Q45. Before whom do I charge thee?**  
**Answer:** Before God, and the Lord Jesus Christ, and the elect angels.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:21

**Q46. What should Timothy observe without preferring one before another?**  
**Answer:** These things.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:21

**Q47. How should Timothy do these things regarding partiality?**  
**Answer:** Without preferring one before another, doing nothing by partiality.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:21

---

### 1 Timothy 5:22

**Q48. What should Timothy not do suddenly?**  
**Answer:** Lay hands on no man.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:22

**Q49. Whose sins should Timothy not be partaker of?**  
**Answer:** Neither be partaker of other men's sins.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:22

**Q50. How should Timothy keep himself?**  
**Answer:** Keep thyself pure.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:22

---

### 1 Timothy 5:23

**Q51. What should Timothy drink no longer?**  
**Answer:** Water.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:23

**Q52. What should Timothy use for his stomach's sake?**  
**Answer:** A little wine.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:23

**Q53. What else should be considered for the use of a little wine?**  
**Answer:** Thine often infirmities.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:23

---

### 1 Timothy 5:24

**Q54. What are open beforehand?**  
**Answer:** Some men's sins.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:24

**Q55. Where do some men's sins go beforehand?**  
**Answer:** To judgment.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:24

**Q56. Where do some men's sins follow?**  
**Answer:** And some men they follow after.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:24

---

### 1 Timothy 5:25

**Q57. What are manifest beforehand?**  
**Answer:** Likewise also the good works of some.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:25

**Q58. What is true of good works that are otherwise?**  
**Answer:** And they that are otherwise cannot be hid.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 5:25
