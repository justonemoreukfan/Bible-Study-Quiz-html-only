# 1 Timothy 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Timothy 4:1

**Q1. What does the Spirit speak expressly about?**  
**Answer:** That in the latter times some shall depart from the faith.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:1

**Q2. What will some give heed to in the latter times?**  
**Answer:** Seducing spirits, and doctrines of devils.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:1

---

### 1 Timothy 4:2

**Q3. How will those who speak lies in hypocrisy be described regarding their conscience?**  
**Answer:** Having their conscience seared with a hot iron.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:2

---

### 1 Timothy 4:3

**Q4. What do the false teachers forbid?**  
**Answer:** To marry.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:3

**Q5. What do they command regarding meats?**  
**Answer:** To abstain from meats.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:3

**Q6. Who hath created meats to be received with thanksgiving?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:3

---

### 1 Timothy 4:4

**Q7. What is every creature of God?**  
**Answer:** Good.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:4

**Q8. What is to be refused if it is received with thanksgiving?**  
**Answer:** Nothing.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:4

---

### 1 Timothy 4:5

**Q9. How is every creature of God sanctified?**  
**Answer:** By the word of God and prayer.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:5

---

### 1 Timothy 4:6

**Q10. If thou put the brethren in remembrance of these things, what will you be?**  
**Answer:** A good minister of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:6

**Q11. What has Timothy been nourished up in?**  
**Answer:** The words of faith and of good doctrine.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:6

**Q12. Whose words has Timothy attained unto?**  
**Answer:** Whereunto thou hast attained.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:6

---

### 1 Timothy 4:7

**Q13. What kind of fables should be refused?**  
**Answer:** Profane and old wives' fables.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:7

**Q14. What should Timothy exercise himself unto?**  
**Answer:** Unto godliness.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:7

---

### 1 Timothy 4:8

**Q15. What profiteth little?**  
**Answer:** Bodily exercise.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:8

**Q16. What is profitable unto all things?**  
**Answer:** Godliness.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:8

**Q17. What does godliness have promise of?**  
**Answer:** The life that now is, and of that which is to come.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:8

---

### 1 Timothy 4:9

**Q18. What is this saying?**  
**Answer:** A faithful saying and worthy of all acceptation.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:9

---

### 1 Timothy 4:10

**Q19. For what do we both labour and suffer reproach?**  
**Answer:** Because we trust in the living God.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:10

**Q20. Of whom is God the Saviour?**  
**Answer:** Specially of those that believe.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:10

---

### 1 Timothy 4:11

**Q21. What things should Timothy command and teach?**  
**Answer:** These things.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:11

---

### 1 Timothy 4:12

**Q22. What should no man despise?**  
**Answer:** Thy youth.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:12

**Q23. In what areas should Timothy be an example of the believers?**  
**Answer:** In word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:12

---

### 1 Timothy 4:13

**Q24. Until I come, to what should Timothy give attendance?**  
**Answer:** To reading, to exhortation, to doctrine.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:13

---

### 1 Timothy 4:14

**Q25. What should Timothy not neglect?**  
**Answer:** The gift that is in thee.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:14

**Q26. How was the gift given to Timothy?**  
**Answer:** By prophecy, with the laying on of the hands of the presbytery.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:14

---

### 1 Timothy 4:15

**Q27. What should Timothy meditate upon?**  
**Answer:** These things.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:15

**Q28. How should Timothy give himself unto them?**  
**Answer:** Wholly.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:15

**Q29. What may appear to all?**  
**Answer:** Thy profiting.  
**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:15

---

**Verse:** 1 Timothy 4:16

**Q31. If Timothy continues in these things, what will he save?**
