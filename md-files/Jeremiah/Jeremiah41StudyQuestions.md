# Jeremiah 41 Study Questions (KJV)

### Jeremiah 41:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 41:1?**  
**Answer:** Now it came to pass in the seventh month, that Ishmael the son of Nethaniah the son of Elishama, of the seed royal, and the princes of the king, even ten men with him, came unto Gedaliah the son of Ahikam to Mizpah.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 41:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 41:1?**  
**Answer:** and there they did eat bread together in Mizpah.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 41:1

**Q3. According to Jeremiah 41:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Now it came to pass in the seventh month, that Ishmael the son of Nethaniah the son of Elishama, of the seed royal, and the princes of the king, even ten men with him, came unto Gedaliah the son of Ahikam to Mizpah; and there they did eat bread together in Mizpah.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 41:1
