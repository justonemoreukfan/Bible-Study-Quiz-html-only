### Jeremiah 22:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 22:1?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 22:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 22:1?**  
**Answer:** Go down to the house of the king of Judah, and speak there this word.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 22:1

**Q3. According to Jeremiah 22:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD; Go down to the house of the king of Judah, and speak there this word,.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 22:1
