### Jeremiah 17:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 17:1?**  
**Answer:** The sin of Judah is written with a pen of iron, and with the point of a diamond.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 17:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 17:1?**  
**Answer:** it is graven upon the table of their heart, and upon the horns of your altars.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 17:1

**Verse:** Jeremiah 17:1
