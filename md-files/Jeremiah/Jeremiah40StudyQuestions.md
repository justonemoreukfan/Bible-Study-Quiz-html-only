# Jeremiah 40 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jeremiah 40:1
**Q1. According to Jeremiah 40:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 40:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Jeremiah 40:1?**  
**Answer:** after that Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard had let him go from Ramah.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 40:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Jeremiah 40:1?**  
**Answer:** when he had taken him being bound in chains among all that were carried away captive of Jerusalem and Judah, which were carried away captive unto Babylon.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 40:1

---

### Jeremiah 40:2
**Q4. According to Jeremiah 40:2, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And the captain of the guard took Jeremiah.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 40:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Jeremiah 40:2?**  
**Answer:** and said unto him.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 40:2

**Q6. What further truth is given in Jeremiah 40:2?**

### Jeremiah 40:3
**Q7. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 40:3?**  
**Answer:** Now the LORD hath brought it, and done according as he hath said.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 40:3

**Q8. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 40:3?**  
**Answer:** because ye have sinned against the LORD, and have not obeyed his voice, therefore this thing is come upon you.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 40:3

**Q9. According to Jeremiah 40:3, what is the full truth recorded?**

**Verse:** Jeremiah 40:3

### Jeremiah 40:4
**Q10. What is stated in the opening of Jeremiah 40:4?**  
**Answer:** And now, behold, I loose thee this day from the chains which were upon thine hand. If it seem good unto thee to come with me into Babylon, come.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 40:4

**Q11. What is declared next in Jeremiah 40:4?**  
**Answer:** and I will look well unto thee.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 40:4

**Q12. What further truth is recorded in Jeremiah 40:4?**  
**Answer:** but if it seem ill unto thee to come with me into Babylon, forbear; behold, all the land is before thee; whither it seemeth good and convenient for thee to go, thither go.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 40:4

### Jeremiah 40:5
**Q13. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 40:5?**  
**Answer:** Now while he was not yet gone back, he said, Go back also to Gedaliah the son of Ahikam the son of Shaphan, whom the king of Babylon hath made governor over the cities of Judah, and dwell with him among the people.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 40:5

**Q14. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 40:5?**  
**Answer:** or go wheresoever it seemeth convenient unto thee to go. So the captain of the guard gave him victuals and a reward, and let him go.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 40:5

**Q15. According to Jeremiah 40:5, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Now while he was not yet gone back, he said, Go back also to Gedaliah the son of Ahikam the son of Shaphan, whom the king of Babylon hath made governor over the cities of Judah, and dwell with him among the people: or go wheresoever it seemeth convenient unto thee to go. So the captain of the guard gave him victuals and a reward, and let him go.
