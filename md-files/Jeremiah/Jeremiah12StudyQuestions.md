# Jeremiah 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jeremiah 12:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Jeremiah 12:1?**  
**Answer:** Righteous art thou, O LORD, when I plead with thee.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 12:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Jeremiah 12:1?**  
**Answer:** yet let me talk with thee of thy judgments.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 12:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Jeremiah 12:1?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore doth the way of the wicked prosper? wherefore are all they happy that deal very treacherously?  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 12:1

---

### Jeremiah 12:4
**Q10. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 12:4?**  
**Answer:** How long shall the land mourn, and the herbs of every field wither, for the wickedness of them that dwell therein? the beasts are consumed, and the birds.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 12:4

**Q11. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 12:4?**  
**Answer:** because they said, He shall not see our last end.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 12:4

**Q12. According to Jeremiah 12:4, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** How long shall the land mourn, and the herbs of every field wither, for the wickedness of them that dwell therein? the beasts are consumed, and the birds; because they said, He shall not see our last end.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 12:4

### Jeremiah 12:5
**Q13. According to Jeremiah 12:5, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** If thou hast run with the footmen.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 12:5

**Q14. What is declared next in Jeremiah 12:5?**  
**Answer:** and they have wearied thee.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 12:5

**Q15. What further truth is given in Jeremiah 12:5?**  
**Answer:** then how canst thou contend with horses? and if in the land of peace, wherein thou trustedst, they wearied thee, then how wilt thou do in the swelling of Jordan?  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 12:5
