# Jeremiah 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jeremiah 1:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Jeremiah 1:1 state?**  
**Answer:** The words of Jeremiah the son of Hilkiah.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 1:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Jeremiah 1:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** of the priests that were in Anathoth in the land of Benjamin.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 1:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Jeremiah 1:1?**  
**Answer:** The words of Jeremiah the son of Hilkiah, of the priests that were in Anathoth in the land of Benjamin:.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 1:1
