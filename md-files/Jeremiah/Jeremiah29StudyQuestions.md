### Jeremiah 29:1
**Q1. According to Jeremiah 29:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Now these are the words of the letter that Jeremiah the prophet sent from Jerusalem unto the residue of the elders which were carried away captives.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 29:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Jeremiah 29:1?**  
**Answer:** and to the priests.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 29:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Jeremiah 29:1?**  
**Answer:** and to the prophets, and to all the people whom Nebuchadnezzar had carried away captive from Jerusalem to Babylon.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 29:1
