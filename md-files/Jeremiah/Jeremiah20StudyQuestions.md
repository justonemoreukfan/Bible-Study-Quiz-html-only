# Jeremiah 20 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jeremiah 20:1
**Q1. According to Jeremiah 20:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Now Pashur the son of Immer the priest.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 20:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Jeremiah 20:1?**  
**Answer:** who was also chief governor in the house of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 20:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Jeremiah 20:1?**  
**Answer:** heard that Jeremiah prophesied these things.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 20:1

### Jeremiah 20:4
**Q10. What is stated in the opening of Jeremiah 20:4?**

**Verse:** Jeremiah 20:4

**Q11. What is declared next in Jeremiah 20:4?**

**Verse:** Jeremiah 20:4

**Verse:** Jeremiah 20:4

**Q18. According to Jeremiah 20:6, what is the full truth recorded?**
