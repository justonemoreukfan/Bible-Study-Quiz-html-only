### Jeremiah 4:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 4:1?**  
**Answer:** If thou wilt return, O Israel, saith the LORD, return unto me.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 4:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 4:1?**  
**Answer:** and if thou wilt put away thine abominations out of my sight, then shalt thou not remove.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 4:1

**Q3. According to Jeremiah 4:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** If thou wilt return, O Israel, saith the LORD, return unto me: and if thou wilt put away thine abominations out of my sight, then shalt thou not remove.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 4:1
