# Jeremiah 45 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jeremiah 45:1
**Q1. According to Jeremiah 45:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** The word that Jeremiah the prophet spake unto Baruch the son of Neriah.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 45:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Jeremiah 45:1?**  
**Answer:** when he had written these words in a book at the mouth of Jeremiah.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 45:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Jeremiah 45:1?**  
**Answer:** in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 45:1

---

### Jeremiah 45:2
**Q4. According to Jeremiah 45:2, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 45:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Jeremiah 45:2?**  
**Answer:** the God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 45:2

**Q6. What further truth is given in Jeremiah 45:2?**  
**Answer:** unto thee, O Baruch.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 45:2

---

### Jeremiah 45:3
**Q7. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 45:3?**  
**Answer:** Thou didst say, Woe is me now! for the LORD hath added grief to my sorrow.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 45:3

**Q8. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 45:3?**  
**Answer:** I fainted in my sighing, and I find no rest.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 45:3

**Q9. According to Jeremiah 45:3, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Thou didst say, Woe is me now! for the LORD hath added grief to my sorrow; I fainted in my sighing, and I find no rest.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 45:3

---

### Jeremiah 45:4
**Q10. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 45:4?**  
**Answer:** Thus shalt thou say unto him, The LORD saith thus.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 45:4

**Q11. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 45:4?**  
**Answer:** Behold, that which I have built will I break down, and that which I have planted I will pluck up, even this whole land.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 45:4

**Q12. According to Jeremiah 45:4, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Thus shalt thou say unto him, The LORD saith thus; Behold, that which I have built will I break down, and that which I have planted I will pluck up, even this whole land.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 45:4

---

### Jeremiah 45:5
**Q13. What is stated in the opening of Jeremiah 45:5?**  
**Answer:** And seekest thou great things for thyself? seek them not.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 45:5

**Q14. What is declared next in Jeremiah 45:5?**  
**Answer:** for, behold, I will bring evil upon all flesh, saith the LORD.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 45:5

**Q15. What further truth is recorded in Jeremiah 45:5?**  
**Answer:** but thy life will I give unto thee for a prey in all places whither thou goest.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 45:5
