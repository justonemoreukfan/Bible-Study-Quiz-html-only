# Jeremiah 42 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jeremiah 42:1
**Q1. According to Jeremiah 42:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Then all the captains of the forces.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 42:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Jeremiah 42:1?**  
**Answer:** and Johanan the son of Kareah.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 42:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Jeremiah 42:1?**  
**Answer:** and Jezaniah the son of Hoshaiah, and all the people from the least even unto the greatest, came near.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 42:1

### Jeremiah 42:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 42:2?**  
**Answer:** And said unto Jeremiah the prophet, Let, we beseech thee, our supplication be accepted before thee, and pray for us unto the LORD thy God, even for all this remnant.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 42:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 42:2?**  
**Answer:** (for we are left but a few of many, as thine eyes do behold us).  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 42:2

**Q6. According to Jeremiah 42:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And said unto Jeremiah the prophet, Let, we beseech thee, our supplication be accepted before thee, and pray for us unto the LORD thy God, even for all this remnant; (for we are left but a few of many, as thine eyes do behold us:).  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 42:2
