### Jeremiah 3:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 3:1?**  
**Answer:** They say, If a man put away his wife, and she go from him, and become another man’s, shall he return unto her again? shall not that land be greatly polluted? but thou hast played the harlot with many lovers.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 3:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 3:1?**  
**Answer:** yet return again to me, saith the LORD.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 3:1

**Q3. According to Jeremiah 3:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** They say, If a man put away his wife, and she go from him, and become another man’s, shall he return unto her again? shall not that land be greatly polluted? but thou hast played the harlot with many lovers; yet return again to me, saith the LORD.
