# Jeremiah 15 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jeremiah 15:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 15:1?**  
**Answer:** Then said the LORD unto me, Though Moses and Samuel stood before me, yet my mind could not be toward this people.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 15:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 15:1?**  
**Answer:** cast them out of my sight, and let them go forth.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 15:1

**Q3. According to Jeremiah 15:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Then said the LORD unto me, Though Moses and Samuel stood before me, yet my mind could not be toward this people: cast them out of my sight, and let them go forth.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 15:1
