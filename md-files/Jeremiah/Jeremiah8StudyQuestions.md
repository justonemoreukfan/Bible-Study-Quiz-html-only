# Jeremiah 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jeremiah 8:1
**Q1. According to Jeremiah 8:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** At that time.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 8:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Jeremiah 8:1?**  
**Answer:** saith the LORD.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 8:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Jeremiah 8:1?**  
**Answer:** they shall bring out the bones of the kings of Judah, and the bones of his princes, and the bones of the priests, and the bones of the prophets, and the bones of the inhabitants of Jerusalem, out of their graves.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 8:1
