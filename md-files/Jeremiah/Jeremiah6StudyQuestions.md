### Jeremiah 6:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 6:1?**  
**Answer:** O ye children of Benjamin, gather yourselves to flee out of the midst of Jerusalem, and blow the trumpet in Tekoa, and set up a sign of fire in Bethhaccerem.

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 6:1?**

**Verse:** Jeremiah 6:1
