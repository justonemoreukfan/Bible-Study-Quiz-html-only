### Jeremiah 5:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 5:1?**  
**Answer:** Run ye to and fro through the streets of Jerusalem, and see now, and know, and seek in the broad places thereof, if ye can find a man, if there be any that executeth judgment, that seeketh the truth.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 5:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 5:1?**  
**Answer:** and I will pardon it.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 5:1

**Q3. According to Jeremiah 5:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Run ye to and fro through the streets of Jerusalem, and see now, and know, and seek in the broad places thereof, if ye can find a man, if there be any that executeth judgment, that seeketh the truth; and I will pardon it.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 5:1
