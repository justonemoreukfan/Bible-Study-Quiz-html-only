# Jeremiah 21 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jeremiah 21:1
**Q1. According to Jeremiah 21:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** The word which came unto Jeremiah from the LORD.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 21:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Jeremiah 21:1?**  
**Answer:** when king Zedekiah sent unto him Pashur the son of Melchiah.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 21:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Jeremiah 21:1?**  
**Answer:** and Zephaniah the son of Maaseiah the priest.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 21:1

---

### Jeremiah 21:2
**Q4. What is stated in the opening of Jeremiah 21:2?**  
**Answer:** Enquire, I pray thee, of the LORD for us.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 21:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Jeremiah 21:2?**  
**Answer:** for Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon maketh war against us.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 21:2

**Q6. What further truth is recorded in Jeremiah 21:2?**  
**Answer:** if so be that the LORD will deal with us according to all his wondrous works, that he may go up from us.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 21:2

### Jeremiah 21:4
**Q10. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 21:4?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 21:4

**Q11. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 21:4?**  
**Answer:** Behold, I will turn back the weapons of war that are in your hands, wherewith ye fight against the king of Babylon, and against the Chaldeans, which besiege you without the walls, and I will assemble them into the midst of this city.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 21:4

**Q12. According to Jeremiah 21:4, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD God of Israel; Behold, I will turn back the weapons of war that are in your hands, wherewith ye fight against the king of Babylon, and against the Chaldeans, which besiege you without the walls, and I will assemble them into the midst of this city.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 21:4

### Jeremiah 21:7
**Q19. What is stated in the opening of Jeremiah 21:7?**  
**Answer:** And afterward, saith the LORD, I will deliver Zedekiah king of Judah, and his servants, and the people, and such as are left in this city from the pestilence, from the sword, and from the famine, into the hand of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, and into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of those that seek their life.
