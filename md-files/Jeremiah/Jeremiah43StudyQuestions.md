# Jeremiah 43 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jeremiah 43:1
**Q1. According to Jeremiah 43:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And it came to pass.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 43:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Jeremiah 43:1?**  
**Answer:** that when Jeremiah had made an end of speaking unto all the people all the words of the LORD their God.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 43:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Jeremiah 43:1?**  
**Answer:** for which the LORD their God had sent him to them, even all these words.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 43:1

---

### Jeremiah 43:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 43:2?**  
**Answer:** Then spake Azariah the son of Hoshaiah, and Johanan the son of Kareah, and all the proud men, saying unto Jeremiah, Thou speakest falsely.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 43:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 43:2?**  
**Answer:** the LORD our God hath not sent thee to say, Go not into Egypt to sojourn there.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 43:2

**Q6. According to Jeremiah 43:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Then spake Azariah the son of Hoshaiah, and Johanan the son of Kareah, and all the proud men, saying unto Jeremiah, Thou speakest falsely: the LORD our God hath not sent thee to say, Go not into Egypt to sojourn there:.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 43:2
