# Jeremiah 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jeremiah 9:1
**Q1. According to Jeremiah 9:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Oh that my head were waters.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 9:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Jeremiah 9:1?**  
**Answer:** and mine eyes a fountain of tears.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 9:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Jeremiah 9:1?**  
**Answer:** that I might weep day and night for the slain of the daughter of my people!  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 9:1

---

### Jeremiah 9:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 9:2?**  
**Answer:** Oh that I had in the wilderness a lodging place of wayfaring men.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 9:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 9:2?**

**Verse:** Jeremiah 9:2

**Verse:** Jeremiah 9:2
