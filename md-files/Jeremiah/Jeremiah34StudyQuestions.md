# Jeremiah 34 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jeremiah 34:1
**Q1. According to Jeremiah 34:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** The word which came unto Jeremiah from the LORD.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 34:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Jeremiah 34:1?**  
**Answer:** when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 34:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Jeremiah 34:1?**  
**Answer:** and all his army, and all the kingdoms of the earth of his dominion, and all the people, fought against Jerusalem, and against all the cities thereof.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 34:1
