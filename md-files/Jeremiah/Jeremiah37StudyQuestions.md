### Jeremiah 37:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Jeremiah 37:1 state?**  
**Answer:** And king Zedekiah the son of Josiah reigned instead of Coniah the son of Jehoiakim.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 37:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Jeremiah 37:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** whom Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon made king in the land of Judah.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 37:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Jeremiah 37:1?**  
**Answer:** And king Zedekiah the son of Josiah reigned instead of Coniah the son of Jehoiakim, whom Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon made king in the land of Judah.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 37:1
