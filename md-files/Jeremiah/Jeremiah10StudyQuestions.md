# Jeremiah 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jeremiah 10:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Jeremiah 10:1 state?**  
**Answer:** Hear ye the word which the LORD speaketh unto you.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 10:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Jeremiah 10:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** O house of Israel.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 10:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Jeremiah 10:1?**  
**Answer:** Hear ye the word which the LORD speaketh unto you, O house of Israel:.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 10:1

---

### Jeremiah 10:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 10:2?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD, Learn not the way of the heathen, and be not dismayed at the signs of heaven.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 10:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 10:2?**  
**Answer:** for the heathen are dismayed at them.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 10:2

**Q6. According to Jeremiah 10:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD, Learn not the way of the heathen, and be not dismayed at the signs of heaven; for the heathen are dismayed at them.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 10:2
