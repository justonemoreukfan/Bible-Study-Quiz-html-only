# Jeremiah 47 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jeremiah 47:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Jeremiah 47:1 state?**  
**Answer:** The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet against the Philistines.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Jeremiah 47:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** before that Pharaoh smote Gaza.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Jeremiah 47:1?**  
**Answer:** The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet against the Philistines, before that Pharaoh smote Gaza.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:1

---

### Jeremiah 47:2
**Q4. What is stated in the opening of Jeremiah 47:2?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Jeremiah 47:2?**  
**Answer:** Behold, waters rise up out of the north, and shall be an overflowing flood, and shall overflow the land, and all that is therein.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:2

**Q6. What further truth is recorded in Jeremiah 47:2?**  
**Answer:** the city, and them that dwell therein; then the men shall cry, and all the inhabitants of the land shall howl.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:2

---

### Jeremiah 47:3
**Q7. According to Jeremiah 47:3, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** At the noise of the stamping of the hoofs of his strong horses.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:3

**Q8. What is declared next in Jeremiah 47:3?**  
**Answer:** at the rushing of his chariots.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:3

**Q9. What further truth is given in Jeremiah 47:3?**  
**Answer:** and at the rumbling of his wheels, the fathers shall not look back to their children for feebleness of hands.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:3

---

### Jeremiah 47:4
**Q10. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 47:4?**  
**Answer:** Because of the day that cometh to spoil all the Philistines, and to cut off from Tyrus and Zidon every helper that remaineth.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:4

**Q11. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 47:4?**  
**Answer:** for the LORD will spoil the Philistines, the remnant of the country of Caphtor.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:4

**Q12. According to Jeremiah 47:4, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Because of the day that cometh to spoil all the Philistines, and to cut off from Tyrus and Zidon every helper that remaineth: for the LORD will spoil the Philistines, the remnant of the country of Caphtor.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:4

---

### Jeremiah 47:5
**Q13. What is stated in the opening of Jeremiah 47:5?**  
**Answer:** Baldness is come upon Gaza.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:5

**Q14. What is declared next in Jeremiah 47:5?**  
**Answer:** Ashkelon is cut off with the remnant of their valley.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:5

**Q15. What further truth is recorded in Jeremiah 47:5?**  
**Answer:** how long wilt thou cut thyself?  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:5

---

### Jeremiah 47:6
**Q16. According to Jeremiah 47:6, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** O thou sword of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:6

**Q17. What is declared next in Jeremiah 47:6?**  
**Answer:** how long will it be ere thou be quiet? put up thyself into thy scabbard.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:6

**Q18. What further truth is given in Jeremiah 47:6?**  
**Answer:** and be still.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:6

---

### Jeremiah 47:7
**Q19. According to Jeremiah 47:7, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** How can it be quiet.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:7

**Q20. What is declared next in Jeremiah 47:7?**  
**Answer:** seeing the LORD hath given it a charge against Ashkelon.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:7

**Q21. What further truth is given in Jeremiah 47:7?**  
**Answer:** and against the sea shore? there hath he appointed it.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 47:7
