# Jeremiah 24 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jeremiah 24:1
**Q1. According to Jeremiah 24:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** The LORD shewed me.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 24:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Jeremiah 24:1?**  
**Answer:** two baskets of figs were set before the temple of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 24:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Jeremiah 24:1?**  
**Answer:** after that Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon had carried away captive Jeconiah the son of Jehoiakim king of Judah, and the princes of Judah, with the carpenters and smiths, from Jerusalem, and had brought them to Babylon.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 24:1

---

### Jeremiah 24:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 24:2?**  
**Answer:** One basket had very good figs, even like the figs that are first ripe.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 24:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 24:2?**  
**Answer:** and the other basket had very naughty figs, which could not be eaten, they were so bad.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 24:2

**Q6. According to Jeremiah 24:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** One basket had very good figs, even like the figs that are first ripe: and the other basket had very naughty figs, which could not be eaten, they were so bad.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 24:2

### Jeremiah 24:5
**Q13. What is stated in the first clause of Jeremiah 24:5?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD, the God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 24:5

**Q14. What additional detail is declared in Jeremiah 24:5?**  
**Answer:** Like these good figs, so will I acknowledge them that are carried away captive of Judah, whom I have sent out of this place into the land of the Chaldeans for their good.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 24:5

**Q15. According to Jeremiah 24:5, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD, the God of Israel; Like these good figs, so will I acknowledge them that are carried away captive of Judah, whom I have sent out of this place into the land of the Chaldeans for their good.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 24:5
