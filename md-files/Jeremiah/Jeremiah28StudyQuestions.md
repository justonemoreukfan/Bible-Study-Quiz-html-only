# Jeremiah 28 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jeremiah 28:1
**Q1. According to Jeremiah 28:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And it came to pass the same year.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 28:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Jeremiah 28:1?**  
**Answer:** in the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 28:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Jeremiah 28:1?**  
**Answer:** in the fourth year, and in the fifth month, that Hananiah the son of Azur the prophet, which was of Gibeon, spake unto me in the house of the LORD, in the presence of the priests and of all the people.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 28:1
