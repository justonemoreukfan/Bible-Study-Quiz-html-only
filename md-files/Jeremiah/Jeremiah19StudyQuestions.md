# Jeremiah 19 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jeremiah 19:1
**Q1. According to Jeremiah 19:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 19:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Jeremiah 19:1?**  
**Answer:** Go and get a potter’s earthen bottle.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 19:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Jeremiah 19:1?**  
**Answer:** and take of the ancients of the people, and of the ancients of the priests.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 19:1
