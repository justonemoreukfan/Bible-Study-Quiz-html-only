### Jeremiah 25:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Jeremiah 25:1 state?**  
**Answer:** The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the people of Judah in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 25:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Jeremiah 25:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** that was the first year of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon.  
**Verse:** Jeremiah 25:1

**Verse:** Jeremiah 25:1
