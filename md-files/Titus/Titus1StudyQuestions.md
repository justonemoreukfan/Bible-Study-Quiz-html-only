# Titus 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Titus 1:1

**Q1. Whose servant and apostle is Paul, according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Paul, a servant of God, and an apostle of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:1

**Q2. According to this verse, what is the faith of God's elect based upon?**  
**Answer:** The acknowledging of the truth which is after godliness.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:1

**Q3. In hope of what kind of life are the elect, which God that cannot lie promised before the world began?**  
**Answer:** Eternal life.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:1

---

### Titus 1:2

**Q1. When was eternal life promised by God?**  
**Answer:** Before the world began.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:2

**Q2. What characteristic of God is mentioned regarding His promise of eternal life?**  
**Answer:** God that cannot lie.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:2

**Q3. In what is the hope of eternal life grounded, according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** In hope of eternal life.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:2

---

### Titus 1:3

**Q1. In due times, what has God manifested?**  
**Answer:** His word.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:3

**Q2. Through what medium was God's word manifested?**  
**Answer:** Through preaching.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:3

**Q3. By whose commandment was the preaching committed unto Paul?**  
**Answer:** God our Saviour.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:3

---

### Titus 1:4

**Q1. To whom is the Epistle addressed as Paul's own son?**  
**Answer:** Titus.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:4

**Q2. What kind of faith do they share?**  
**Answer:** The common faith.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:4

**Q3. What blessing is wished from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ our Saviour?**  
**Answer:** Grace, mercy, and peace.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:4

---

### Titus 1:5

**Q1. For this cause was Titus left in what place?**  
**Answer:** Crete.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:5

**Q2. What was Titus left in Crete to set in order?**  
**Answer:** The things that are wanting.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:5

**Q3. What did Paul command Titus to ordain in every city?**  
**Answer:** Elders.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:5

---

### Titus 1:6

**Q1. What must an elder be, regarding his marital status and behavior?**  
**Answer:** Blameless, the husband of one wife.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:6

**Q2. What kind of children must an elder have?**  
**Answer:** Faithful children.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:6

**Q3. What must not be the accusation against the children of an elder?**  
**Answer:** Not accused of riot or unruly.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:6

---

### Titus 1:7

**Q1. What is a bishop required to be, as the steward of God?**  
**Answer:** Blameless.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:7

**Q2. What negative character traits must a bishop not possess?**  
**Answer:** Not selfwilled, not soon angry, not given to wine, no striker, not given to filthy lucre.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:7

**Q3. Whose steward is the bishop described as being?**  
**Answer:** God's steward.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:7

---

### Titus 1:8

**Q1. What must a bishop be a lover of?**  
**Answer:** A lover of hospitality, a lover of good men.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:8

**Q2. How should a bishop conduct himself in mind and behavior?**  
**Answer:** Sober, just, holy, temperate.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:8

**Q3. Which of these virtues from this verse describes self-control?**  
**Answer:** Temperate.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:8

---

### Titus 1:9

**Q1. Holding fast to what kind of word must the elder be?**  
**Answer:** Faithful word as he hath been taught.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:9

**Q2. For what two purposes is the elder to hold fast the faithful word?**  
**Answer:** That he may be able by sound doctrine both to exhort and to convince the gainsayers.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:9

**Q3. What kind of doctrine is used to exhort and convince?**  
**Answer:** Sound doctrine.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:9

---

### Titus 1:10

**Q1. What kind of people are described as being unruly and vain talkers?**  
**Answer:** Many unruly and vain talkers and deceivers.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:10

**Q2. Who are specifically mentioned as being among the deceivers?**  
**Answer:** They of the circumcision.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:10

**Q3. What must be done to these unruly talkers and deceivers?**  
**Answer:** Whose mouths must be stopped.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:10

---

### Titus 1:11

**Q1. What kind of houses are subverted by these deceivers?**  
**Answer:** Whole houses.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:11

**Q2. Why do these deceivers subvert whole houses?**  
**Answer:** Teaching things which they ought not.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:11

**Q3. For what reason do they teach things which they ought not?**  
**Answer:** For filthy lucre's sake.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:11

---

### Titus 1:12

**Q1. Who said, "The Cretians are alway liars, evil beasts, slow bellies"?**  
**Answer:** One of themselves, even a prophet of their own.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:12

**Q2. What does the prophet say about Cretians regarding lying?**  
**Answer:** The Cretians are alway liars.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:12

**Q3. What other two descriptions are given of Cretians in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Evil beasts, slow bellies.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:12

---

### Titus 1:13

**Q1. Is this witness true or false?**  
**Answer:** This witness is true.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:13

**Q2. How does Paul command Titus to rebuke them?**  
**Answer:** Sharply.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:13

**Q3. For what purpose are they to be rebuked sharply?**  
**Answer:** That they may be sound in the faith.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:13

---

### Titus 1:14

**Q1. What kind of fables should they not give heed to?**  
**Answer:** Jewish fables.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:14

**Q2. Whose commandments are to be turned from?**  
**Answer:** Commandments of men.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:14

**Q3. What kind of men turn from the truth?**  
**Answer:** That turn from the truth.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:14

---

### Titus 1:15

**Q1. To what kind of people are all things pure?**  
**Answer:** Unto the pure.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:15

**Q2. To what kind of people is nothing pure?**  
**Answer:** Unto them that are defiled and unbelieving.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:15

**Q3. What two parts of the defiled and unbelieving are defiled?**  
**Answer:** Even their mind and conscience.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:15

---

### Titus 1:16

**Q1. Whom do they profess that they know?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:16

**Q2. By what action do they deny God?**  
**Answer:** In works.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:16

**Q3. How are they described at the end of this verse regarding every good work?**  
**Answer:** Abominable, and disobedient, and unto every good work reprobate.  
**Verse:** Titus 1:16
