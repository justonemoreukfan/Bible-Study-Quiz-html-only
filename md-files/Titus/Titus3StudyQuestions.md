# Titus 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Titus 3:1

**Q1. What are believers to be subject to?**  
**Answer:** Principalities and powers.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:1

**Q2. What should believers be ready to do?**  
**Answer:** Every good work.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:1

**Q3. Whom should believers obey?**  
**Answer:** Magistrates.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:1

---

### Titus 3:2

**Q4. Whom should they speak evil of?**  
**Answer:** No man.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:2

**Q5. What should they not be?**  
**Answer:** Brawlers.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:2

**Q6. What manner of disposition should they show toward all men?**  
**Answer:** Gentle.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:2

---

### Titus 3:3

**Q7. What were they sometimes themselves?**  
**Answer:** Foolish, disobedient, deceived, serving divers lusts and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hateful, and hating one another.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:3

**Q8. What were they serving?**  
**Answer:** Divers lusts and pleasures.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:3

**Q9. In what were they living?**  
**Answer:** Malice and envy.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:3

---

### Titus 3:4

**Q10. What appeared after the kindness and love of God our Saviour toward man?**  
**Answer:** The love of God our Saviour toward man.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:4

**Q11. Whose kindness and love appeared?**  
**Answer:** The kindness and love of God our Saviour.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:4

**Q12. Toward whom was this kindness and love shown?**  
**Answer:** Man.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:4

---

### Titus 3:5

**Q13. By what works of righteousness did God save us?**  
**Answer:** Not by works of righteousness which we have done.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:5

**Q14. According to what did He save us?**  
**Answer:** According to his mercy.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:5

**Q15. By what means did He save us?**  
**Answer:** By the washing of regeneration, and renewing of the Holy Ghost.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:5

---

### Titus 3:6

**Q16. How did He shed the Holy Ghost on us?**  
**Answer:** Abundantly.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:6

**Q17. Through whom was the Holy Ghost shed on us?**  
**Answer:** Through Jesus Christ our Saviour.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:6

**Q18. What was shed on us abundantly?**  
**Answer:** The Holy Ghost.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:6

---

### Titus 3:7

**Q19. Being justified by whose grace should we be made heirs?**  
**Answer:** His grace.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:7

**Q20. What hope should we be made heirs according to?**  
**Answer:** The hope of eternal life.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:7

**Q21. How are we made heirs according to the hope of eternal life?**  
**Answer:** Being justified by his grace.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:7

---

### Titus 3:8

**Q22. What is a faithful saying?**  
**Answer:** This is a faithful saying.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:8

**Q23. What should they which have believed in God be careful to maintain?**  
**Answer:** Good works.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:8

**Q24. What are these things unto men?**  
**Answer:** Good and profitable.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:8

---

### Titus 3:9

**Q25. What should one avoid besides foolish questions?**  
**Answer:** Genealogies, and contentions, and strivings about the law.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:9

**Q26. Why should one avoid foolish questions, genealogies, contentions, and strivings about the law?**  
**Answer:** For they are unprofitable and vain.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:9

**Q27. What kind of questions should be avoided?**  
**Answer:** Foolish questions.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:9

---

### Titus 3:10

**Q28. What kind of man should be rejected after the first and second admonition?**  
**Answer:** A man that is an heretick.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:10

**Q29. After how many admonitions should a heretic be rejected?**  
**Answer:** After the first and second admonition.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:10

---

### Titus 3:11

**Q30. What is such a man subverted by?**  
**Answer:** That such that is turned is subverted.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:11

**Q31. What is he conscious of?**  
**Answer:** Being condemned of himself.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:11

**Q32. What state is such a man in regarding sin?**  
**Answer:** He sinneth.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:11

---

### Titus 3:12

**Q33. Whom shall Paul send unto Titus?**  
**Answer:** Artemas or Tychicus.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:12

**Q34. Where did Paul tell Titus to be diligent to come unto him?**  
**Answer:** To Nicopolis.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:12

**Q35. What had Paul determined to do in Nicopolis?**  
**Answer:** To winter there.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:12

---

### Titus 3:13

**Q36. Whom did Paul tell Titus to bring on their journey diligently?**  
**Answer:** Zenas the lawyer and Apollos.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:13

**Q37. What should be lacking unto Zenas and Apollos?**  
**Answer:** Nothing.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:13

---

### Titus 3:14

**Q38. What should ours also learn to do?**  
**Answer:** To maintain good works for necessary uses.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:14

**Q39. For what should they maintain good works?**  
**Answer:** For necessary uses.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:14

**Q40. What should they not be?**  
**Answer:** Unfruitful.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:14

---

### Titus 3:15

**Q41. Who saluted Titus?**  
**Answer:** All that are with me.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:15

**Q42. Whom should Titus greet?**  
**Answer:** Them that love us in the faith.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:15

**Q43. What is the final wish stated for them all?**  
**Answer:** Grace be with you all. Amen.  
**Verse:** Titus 3:15
