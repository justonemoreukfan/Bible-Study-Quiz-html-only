# Titus 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Titus 2:1

**Q1. What must the speaker speak?**  
**Answer:** The things which become sound doctrine.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:1

**Q2. Who is instructed to speak the things which become sound doctrine?**  
**Answer:** Thou.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:1

**Q3. What kind of doctrine do these things become?**  
**Answer:** Sound doctrine.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:1

---

### Titus 2:2

**Q1. What condition should the aged men be in regarding their temper?**  
**Answer:** Sober.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:2

**Q2. How should the aged men behave in regard to gravity?**  
**Answer:** Grave.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:2

**Q3. In what three things should the aged men be sound?**  
**Answer:** In faith, in charity, in patience.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:2

---

### Titus 2:3

**Q1. How should the aged women behave in their demeanor?**  
**Answer:** As becometh holiness.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:3

**Q2. What are the aged women not to be?**  
**Answer:** False accusers, not given to much wine.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:3

**Q3. What kind of things should the aged women teach?**  
**Answer:** Teachers of good things.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:3

---

### Titus 2:4

**Q1. Who are the young women to be taught to be sober?**  
**Answer:** The young women.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:4

**Q2. Whom are the young women to love?**  
**Answer:** Their husbands, to love their children.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:4

**Q3. What should the young women love besides their husbands?**  
**Answer:** Their children.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:4

---

### Titus 2:5

**Q1. What specific personal traits should the young women be taught to possess?**  
**Answer:** Discreet, chaste.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:5

**Q2. Where should the young women be keepers?**  
**Answer:** At home.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:5

**Q3. How should the young women behave toward their own husbands?**  
**Answer:** Obedient to their own husbands.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:5

---

### Titus 2:6

**Q1. Who is exhorted to be sober minded?**  
**Answer:** Young men.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:6

**Q2. What specific state of mind should the young men be exhorted to have?**  
**Answer:** Sober minded.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:6

**Q3. What is the action to be taken regarding the young men?**  
**Answer:** Exhort them to be sober minded.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:6

---

### Titus 2:7

**Q1. In all things, what should the person show himself to be?**  
**Answer:** A pattern of good works.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:7

**Q2. What attribute should be shown in doctrine?**  
**Answer:** Uncorruptness.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:7

**Q3. What other two qualities should accompany doctrine?**  
**Answer:** Gravity, sincerity.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:7

---

### Titus 2:8

**Q1. What kind of speech should be used?**  
**Answer:** Sound speech.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:8

**Q2. What characteristic must the sound speech have?**  
**Answer:** That cannot be condemned.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:8

**Q3. Why should the opponent be ashamed?**  
**Answer:** Having no evil thing to say of you.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:8

---

### Titus 2:9

**Q1. Who are exhorted to be obedient unto their own masters?**  
**Answer:** Servants.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:9

**Q2. In what manner should servants please their masters in all things?**  
**Answer:** To please them well.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:9

**Q3. What must servants not do against their masters?**  
**Answer:** Not answering again.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:9

---

### Titus 2:10

**Q1. What must servants not do with goods?**  
**Answer:** Not purloining.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:10

**Q2. What kind of fidelity should servants show?**  
**Answer:** All good fidelity.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:10

**Q3. What doctrine should they adorn in all things?**  
**Answer:** The doctrine of God our Saviour.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:10

---

### Titus 2:11

**Q1. What has appeared to all men?**  
**Answer:** The grace of God that bringeth salvation.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:11

**Q2. To whom has the grace of God appeared?**  
**Answer:** To all men.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:11

**Q3. What does the grace of God bring?**  
**Answer:** Salvation.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:11

---

### Titus 2:12

**Q1. What does the grace of God teach us to deny?**  
**Answer:** Ungodliness and worldly lusts.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:12

**Q2. How should we live in this present world?**  
**Answer:** Soberly, righteously, and godly.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:12

**Q3. In what time period should we live soberly, righteously, and godly?**  
**Answer:** In this present world.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:12

---

### Titus 2:13

**Q1. What are we looking for?**  
**Answer:** That blessed hope.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:13

**Q2. Whose glorious appearing are we looking for?**  
**Answer:** The great God and our Saviour Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:13

**Q3. How is the appearing described?**  
**Answer:** The glorious appearing.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:13

---

### Titus 2:14

**Q1. Who gave himself for us?**  
**Answer:** Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:14

**Q2. From what did He redeem us?**  
**Answer:** From all iniquity.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:14

**Q3. What kind of people did He purify unto himself?**  
**Answer:** A peculiar people, zealous of good works.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:14

---

### Titus 2:15

**Q1. What actions should be taken regarding these things?**  
**Answer:** Speak, and exhort, and rebuke.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:15

**Q2. With what level of authority should these things be done?**  
**Answer:** With all authority.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:15

**Q3. Who should not be allowed to despise the speaker?**  
**Answer:** No man.  
**Verse:** Titus 2:15
