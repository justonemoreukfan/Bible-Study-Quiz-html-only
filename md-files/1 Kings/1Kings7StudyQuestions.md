# 1 Kings 7 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Kings 7:1
**Q1. How long did Solomon build his own house?**
**Answer:** Thirteen years.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:1

**Q2. Did Solomon finish building his own house?**
**Answer:** Yes, he finished all his house.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:1

**Q3. How many years did it take Solomon to build his own house?**
**Answer:** Thirteen years.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:1

### 1 Kings 7:2
**Q1. What was the name of the house Solomon built?**
**Answer:** The house of the forest of Lebanon.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:2

**Q2. What were the dimensions of the house of the forest of Lebanon?**
**Answer:** The length thereof was an hundred cubits, and the breadth thereof fifty cubits, and the height thereof thirty cubits.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:2

**Q3. Upon what was the house of the forest of Lebanon built?**
**Answer:** Upon four rows of cedar pillars, with cedar beams upon the pillars.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:2

### 1 Kings 7:3
**Q1. What was the house of the forest of Lebanon covered with above?**
**Answer:** Cedar above upon the beams.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:3

**Q2. How many pillars did the beams lay on?**
**Answer:** Forty five pillars.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:3

**Q3. How were the pillars arranged?**
**Answer:** Fifteen in a row.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:3

### 1 Kings 7:4
**Q1. How many rows of windows were there?**
**Answer:** Three rows.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:4

**Q2. How was the light described in the windows?**
**Answer:** Light was against light in three ranks.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:4

**Q3. In how many ranks was the light arranged?**
**Answer:** Three ranks.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:4

### 1 Kings 7:5
**Q1. What shape were all the doors and posts?**
**Answer:** Square.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:5

**Q2. What shape were the windows?**
**Answer:** Square.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:5

**Q3. How was the light arranged in the doors and posts?**
**Answer:** Light was against light in three ranks.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:5

### 1 Kings 7:6
**Q1. What did Solomon make that had pillars?**
**Answer:** A porch of pillars.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:6

**Q2. What were the dimensions of the porch of pillars?**
**Answer:** The length thereof was fifty cubits, and the breadth thereof thirty cubits.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:6

**Q3. What was before the porch?**
**Answer:** The other pillars and the thick beam were before them.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:6

### 1 Kings 7:7
**Q1. What kind of porch did Solomon make for the throne?**
**Answer:** The porch of judgment.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:7

**Q2. For what purpose was this porch made?**
**Answer:** Where he might judge.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:7

**Q3. With what was the porch of judgment covered?**
**Answer:** Covered with cedar from one side of the floor to the other.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:7

### 1 Kings 7:8
**Q1. What did the house where Solomon dwelt have within the porch?**
**Answer:** Another court.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:8

**Q2. Who did Solomon make a house for that was like unto this porch?**
**Answer:** Pharaoh's daughter, whom he had taken to wife.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:8

**Q3. Was the house for Pharaoh's daughter like unto the porch?**
**Answer:** Yes.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:8

### 1 Kings 7:9
**Q1. What kind of stones were used for these buildings?**
**Answer:** Costly stones.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:9

**Q2. How were the stones prepared?**
**Answer:** According to the measures of hewed stones, sawed with saws.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:9

**Q3. Where were these stones used?**
**Answer:** Within and without, even from the foundation unto the coping, and so on the outside toward the great court.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:9

### 1 Kings 7:10
**Q1. What was the foundation made of?**
**Answer:** Costly stones.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:10

**Q2. What sizes of stones were used in the foundation?**
**Answer:** Great stones, stones of ten cubits, and stones of eight cubits.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:10

**Q3. What is the smallest size of stone mentioned in the foundation?**
**Answer:** Stones of eight cubits.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:10

### 1 Kings 7:11
**Q1. What was used above the foundation?**
**Answer:** Costly stones, after the measures of hewed stones, and cedars.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:11

**Q2. What were the measures of the stones used above?**
**Answer:** Hewed stones.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:11

**Q3. What material besides stones was used above the foundation?**
**Answer:** Cedars.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:11

### 1 Kings 7:12
**Q1. How was the great court round about built?**
**Answer:** With three rows of hewed stones, and a row of cedar beams.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:12

**Q2. For which two areas were these rows used?**
**Answer:** For the inner court of the house of the Lord, and for the porch of the house.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:12

**Q3. What were the rows consisting of?**
**Answer:** Three rows of hewed stones, and a row of cedar beams.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:12

### 1 Kings 7:13
**Q1. Whom did king Solomon send for?**
**Answer:** Hiram out of Tyre.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:13

**Q2. Where was Hiram from?**
**Answer:** Tyre.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:13

**Q3. Did Solomon fetch Hiram?**
**Answer:** Yes.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:13

### 1 Kings 7:14
**Q1. What was Hiram's heritage?**
**Answer:** He was a widow's son of the tribe of Naphtali.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:14

**Q2. What was Hiram's father's profession?**
**Answer:** A man of Tyre, a worker in brass.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:14

**Q3. What qualities was Hiram filled with?**
**Answer:** Wisdom, and understanding, and cunning to work all works in brass.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:14

### 1 Kings 7:15
**Q1. How many pillars of brass did Hiram cast?**
**Answer:** Two pillars of brass.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:15

**Q2. How high was each pillar?**
**Answer:** Eighteen cubits high apiece.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:15

**Q3. How wide was each pillar?**
**Answer:** A line of twelve cubits did compass either of them about.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:15

### 1 Kings 7:16
**Q1. What did Hiram make to set upon the tops of the pillars?**
**Answer:** Two chapiters of molten brass.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:16

**Q2. How high was the one chapiter?**
**Answer:** Five cubits.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:16

**Q3. How high was the other chapiter?**
**Answer:** Five cubits.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:16

### 1 Kings 7:17
**Q1. What were on the chapiters which were upon the top of the pillars?**
**Answer:** Nets of checker work, and wreaths of chain work.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:17

**Q2. How many nets of checker work were on the one chapiter?**
**Answer:** Seven.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:17

**Q3. How many wreaths of chain work were on the other chapiter?**
**Answer:** Seven.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:17

### 1 Kings 7:18

**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:18

**Q2. How many rows were round about upon the one network?**
**Answer:** Two rows.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 7:18

**Q3. What was done for the other chapiter?**
**Answer:** So did he for the other chapiter.
