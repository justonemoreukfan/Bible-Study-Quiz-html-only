# 1 Kings 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Kings 12:1
**Q1. Where did Rehoboam go?**
**Answer:** To Shechem.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:1

**Q2. Why did Rehoboam go to Shechem?**
**Answer:** For all Israel were come to Shechem to make him king.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:1

**Q3. Who was made king at Shechem?**
**Answer:** Rehoboam.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:1

### 1 Kings 12:2
**Q1. Who heard that Rehoboam was going to be made king?**
**Answer:** Jeroboam the son of Nebat.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:2

**Q2. Where was Jeroboam when he heard this?**
**Answer:** In Egypt.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:2

**Q3. Why had Jeroboam been in Egypt?**
**Answer:** For he was fled from the presence of king Solomon.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:2

### 1 Kings 12:3
**Q1. Who sent and called Jeroboam?**
**Answer:** They sent and called him.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:3

**Q2. Who came to Shechem and spake unto Rehoboam?**
**Answer:** Jeroboam and all the congregation of Israel.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:3

**Q3. What did Jeroboam and the congregation do?**
**Answer:** They came, and spake unto Rehoboam.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:3

### 1 Kings 12:4
**Q1. What did the people say about David's yoke?**
**Answer:** Thy father made our yoke grievous.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:4

**Q2. What did the people ask Rehoboam to do with the yoke?**
**Answer:** Now therefore make thou the grievous service of thy father, and his heavy yoke which he put upon us, lighter.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:4

**Q3. What would the people do if the yoke was made lighter?**
**Answer:** We will serve thee.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:4

### 1 Kings 12:5
**Q1. What did Rehoboam say to the people?**
**Answer:** Depart yet for three days, then come again to me.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:5

**Q2. How long did Rehoboam ask the people to wait?**
**Answer:** Three days.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:5

**Q3. Did the people depart?**
**Answer:** Yes, the people departed.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:5

### 1 Kings 12:6
**Q1. Whom did Rehoboam consult first?**
**Answer:** The old men, that stood before Solomon his father while he yet lived.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:6

**Q2. What did Rehoboam ask the old men?**
**Answer:** How do ye advise that I may answer this people?
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:6

**Q3. Who stood before Solomon?**
**Answer:** The old men.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:6

### 1 Kings 12:7
**Q1. What was the advice of the old men?**
**Answer:** If thou wilt be a servant unto this people this day, and wilt serve them, and answer them, and speak good words to them, then they will be thy servants for ever.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:7

**Q2. What should Rehoboam do to keep the people as servants?**
**Answer:** Be a servant unto this people, serve them, answer them, and speak good words to them.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:7

**Q3. What would be the result if Rehoboam followed the old men's advice?**
**Answer:** Then they will be thy servants for ever.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:7

### 1 Kings 12:8
**Q1. What did Rehoboam do with the counsel of the old men?**
**Answer:** He forsook the counsel of the old men.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:8

**Q2. Whom did Rehoboam consult instead?**
**Answer:** The young men that were grown up with him, and which stood before him.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:8

**Q3. Who were the young men?**
**Answer:** The young men that were grown up with him, and which stood before him.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:8

### 1 Kings 12:9
**Q1. What did Rehoboam ask the young men?**
**Answer:** What counsel give ye that we may answer this people, who have spoken to me, saying, Make the yoke which thy father did put upon us lighter?
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:9

**Q2. What had the people asked Rehoboam to do?**
**Answer:** Make the yoke which thy father did put upon us lighter.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:9

**Q3. Who was the yoke referred to in the request?**
**Answer:** The yoke which Solomon (his father) did put upon them.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:9

### 1 Kings 12:10
**Q1. What did the young men advise Rehoboam to say?**
**Answer:** Thus shalt thou speak unto this people that spake unto thee, saying, My little finger shall be thicker than my father's loins.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:10

**Q2. What comparison did the young men suggest for Rehoboam's "little finger"?**
**Answer:** My little finger shall be thicker than my father's loins.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:10

**Q3. What was the tone of the young men's advice?**
**Answer:** Harsh and proud.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:10

### 1 Kings 12:11
**Q1. What did the young men suggest Rehoboam say about the yoke?**
**Answer:** Now whereas my father did lade you with a heavy yoke, I will add to your yoke.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:11

**Q2. What did the young men suggest Rehoboam say about the punishment?**
**Answer:** My father hath chastised you with whips, but I will chastise you with scorpions.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:11

**Q3. What was the difference between the punishments suggested by the young men?**
**Answer:** Whips versus scorpions.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 12:11

### 1 Kings 12:14

### 1 Kings 12:15
**Q1. Why did the king not hearken to the people?**
