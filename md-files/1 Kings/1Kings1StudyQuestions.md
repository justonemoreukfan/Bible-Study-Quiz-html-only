### 1 Kings 1:1
**Q1. What was the physical condition of king David?**
**Answer:** King David was old and stricken in years.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 1:1

**Q2. What did they do for king David because he was old?**
**Answer:** They covered him with clothes.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 1:1

**Q3. Did the clothes help king David get heat?**
**Answer:** No, he gat no heat.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 1:1
