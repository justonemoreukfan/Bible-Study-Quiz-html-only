# 1 Kings 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Kings 3:1
**Q1. What did Solomon do with Pharaoh king of Egypt?**
**Answer:** Solomon made affinity with Pharaoh king of Egypt.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:1

**Q2. Whom did Solomon take as a wife?**
**Answer:** Pharaoh's daughter.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:1

**Q3. Where did Solomon bring Pharaoh's daughter until he finished building his house and the house of the Lord?**
**Answer:** Into the city of David.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:1

### 1 Kings 3:2
**Q1. Where did the people sacrifice?**
**Answer:** In high places.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:2

**Q2. Why did the people sacrifice in high places?**
**Answer:** Because there was no house built unto the name of the Lord, until those days.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:2

**Q3. What was the reason for sacrificing in high places?**
**Answer:** Because there was no house built unto the name of the Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:2

### 1 Kings 3:3
**Q1. Whom did Solomon love?**
**Answer:** Solomon loved the Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:3

**Q2. Whose statutes did Solomon walk in?**
**Answer:** The statutes of David his father.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:3

**Q3. What did Solomon still do despite loving the Lord?**
**Answer:** He sacrificed and burnt incense in high places.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:3

### 1 Kings 3:4
**Q1. Where did the king go to sacrifice?**
**Answer:** To Gibeon.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:4

**Q2. Why did he go to Gibeon?**
**Answer:** For that was the great high place.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:4

**Q3. How many burnt offerings did Solomon offer upon that altar?**
**Answer:** A thousand burnt offerings.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:4

### 1 Kings 3:5
**Q1. Where did the Lord appear to Solomon?**
**Answer:** In Gibeon.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:5

**Q2. How did the Lord appear to Solomon?**
**Answer:** In a dream by night.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:5

**Q3. What did God say to Solomon in the dream?**
**Answer:** Ask what I shall give thee.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:5

### 1 Kings 3:6
**Q1. How had the Lord shewed mercy to David, Solomon's father?**
**Answer:** Great mercy, according as he walked before thee in truth, and in righteousness, and in uprightness of heart with thee.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:6

**Q2. What great kindness did the Lord keep for David?**
**Answer:** That thou hast given him a son to sit on his throne, as it is this day.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:6

**Q3. Who walked before the Lord in truth, righteousness, and uprightness of heart?**
**Answer:** David, Solomon's father.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:6

### 1 Kings 3:7
**Q1. What did the Lord do for Solomon instead of David?**
**Answer:** Thou hast made thy servant king instead of David my father.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:7

**Q2. How did Solomon describe himself in this verse?**
**Answer:** I am but a little child.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:7

**Q3. What did Solomon say he did not know how to do?**
**Answer:** I know not how to go out or come in.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:7

### 1 Kings 3:8
**Q1. Where was Solomon's servant in the midst of?**
**Answer:** In the midst of thy people which thou hast chosen.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:8

**Q2. How is the people that the Lord chose described?**
**Answer:** A great people, that cannot be numbered nor counted for multitude.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:8

**Q3. Whom did the Lord choose?**
**Answer:** Thy people.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:8

### 1 Kings 3:9
**Q1. What did Solomon ask for his servant?**
**Answer:** Give therefore thy servant an understanding heart to judge thy people.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:9

**Q2. Why did Solomon want an understanding heart?**
**Answer:** That I may discern between good and bad.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:9

**Q3. Why did Solomon believe he needed this heart?**
**Answer:** For who is able to judge this thy so great a people?
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:9

### 1 Kings 3:10
**Q1. How did the Lord feel about Solomon's request?**
**Answer:** And the speech pleased the Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:10

**Q2. What did Solomon ask for that pleased the Lord?**
**Answer:** This thing [an understanding heart].
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:10

**Q3. Whose speech pleased the Lord?**
**Answer:** Solomon's.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:10

### 1 Kings 3:11
**Q1. Why did God say He was giving Solomon what he asked for?**
**Answer:** Because thou hast asked this thing, and hast not asked for thyself long life; neither hast asked riches for thyself, nor hast asked the life of thine enemies.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:11

**Q2. What did Solomon NOT ask for?**
**Answer:** Long life, riches for himself, nor the life of his enemies.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:11

**Q3. What did Solomon ask for instead of the things mentioned above?**
**Answer:** Understanding to discern judgment.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:11

### 1 Kings 3:12
**Q1. What did God do according to Solomon's words?**
**Answer:** I have given thee a wise and an understanding heart.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:12

**Q2. How did Solomon's heart compare to others before him?**
**Answer:** So that there was none like thee before thee.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:12

**Q3. Would anyone arise like Solomon after him?**
**Answer:** Neither after thee shall any arise like unto thee.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:12

### 1 Kings 3:13
**Q1. What did God give Solomon that he had NOT asked for?**
**Answer:** Both riches, and honour.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:13

**Q2. How would Solomon compare to other kings?**
**Answer:** So that there shall not be any among the kings like unto thee all thy days.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:13

**Q3. For how long would there be no king like Solomon?**
**Answer:** All thy days.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:13

### 1 Kings 3:14
**Q1. Under what condition would God lengthen Solomon's days?**
**Answer:** If thou wilt walk in my ways, to keep my statutes and my commandments.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:14

**Q2. Whose example was Solomon to follow in walking?**
**Answer:** As thy father David did walk.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:14

**Q3. What would be the reward for keeping the statutes and commandments?**
**Answer:** Then I will lengthen thy days.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:14

### 1 Kings 3:15
**Q1. What happened when Solomon awoke?**
**Answer:** Behold, it was a dream.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:15

**Q2. Where did Solomon go after he awoke?**
**Answer:** He came to Jerusalem.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:15

**Q3. What did Solomon do before the ark of the covenant of the Lord?**
**Answer:** Offered up burnt offerings, and offered peace offerings, and made a feast to all his servants.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:15

### 1 Kings 3:16
**Q1. Who came to the king?**
**Answer:** Two women, that were harlots.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:16

**Q2. What was the profession of the two women?**
**Answer:** Harlots.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:16

**Q3. Where did the women stand?**
**Answer:** Before him [the king].
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:16

### 1 Kings 3:17
**Q1. Where did the first woman and the other woman dwell?**
**Answer:** In one house.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:17

**Q2. What happened to the first woman in the house?**
**Answer:** I was delivered of a child with her in the house.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:17

**Q3. Who was delivered of a child in the house?**
**Answer:** The first woman.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:17

### 1 Kings 3:18
**Q1. When was the second woman delivered of a child?**
**Answer:** The third day after that I [the first woman] was delivered.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:18

**Q2. Who was with the women in the house?**
**Answer:** We were together; there was no stranger with us in the house, save we two in the house.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:18

**Q3. How many people were in the house at the time?**
**Answer:** Save we two in the house.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:18

### 1 Kings 3:19
**Q1. What happened to the second woman's child?**
**Answer:** This woman's child died in the night.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:19

**Q2. Why did the child die?**
**Answer:** Because she overlaid it.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:19

**Q3. When did the child die?**
**Answer:** In the night.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:19

### 1 Kings 3:20
**Q1. When did the woman arise to take the son?**
**Answer:** At midnight.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:20

**Q2. From where did she take the son?**
**Answer:** From beside me, while thine handmaid slept.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:20

**Q3. What did she do with the dead child?**
**Answer:** Laid her dead child in my bosom.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:20

### 1 Kings 3:21
**Q1. When did the woman rise to give her child suck?**
**Answer:** In the morning.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:21

**Q2. What did she find when she rose to give the child suck?**
**Answer:** Behold, it was dead.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:21

**Q3. What did she realize after considering it in the morning?**
**Answer:** Behold, it was not my son, which I did bear.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:21

### 1 Kings 3:22
**Q1. What did the second woman claim about the children?**
**Answer:** Nay; but the living is my son, and the dead is thy son.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:22

**Q2. What did the first woman claim in response?**
**Answer:** No; but the dead is thy son, and the living is my son.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:22

**Q3. Before whom did they speak?**
**Answer:** Before the king.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:22

### 1 Kings 3:23
**Q1. What did the king summarize about the first woman's claim?**
**Answer:** The one saith, This is my son that liveth, and thy son is the dead.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:23

**Q2. What did the king summarize about the second woman's claim?**
**Answer:** And the other saith, Nay; but thy son is the dead, and my son is the living.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:23

**Q3. Who was the king summarizing?**
**Answer:** The two women.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:23

### 1 Kings 3:24
**Q1. What did the king ask for?**
**Answer:** Bring me a sword.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:24

**Q2. What was brought before the king?**
**Answer:** A sword.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:24

**Q3. Who brought the sword?**
**Answer:** They [the servants] brought a sword before the king.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:24

### 1 Kings 3:25
**Q1. What did the king command to be done with the living child?**
**Answer:** Divide the living child in two.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:25

**Q2. How was the divided child to be distributed?**
**Answer:** Give half to the one, and half to the other.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:25

**Q3. Who was to be divided?**
**Answer:** The living child.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:25

### 1 Kings 3:26
**Q1. What did the woman whose the living child was say to the king?**
**Answer:** O my lord, give her the living child, and in no wise slay it.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:26

**Q2. Why did she speak to the king?**
**Answer:** For her bowels yearned upon her son.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:26

**Q3. What did the other woman say?**
**Answer:** Let it be neither mine nor thine, but divide it.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:26

### 1 Kings 3:27
**Q1. What was the king's final answer regarding the child?**
**Answer:** Give her the living child, and in no wise slay it.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:27

**Q2. Who did the king identify as the mother?**
**Answer:** She [the woman who asked for the child to be given to the other] is the mother thereof.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:27

**Q3. Did the king allow the child to be slain?**
**Answer:** No, he said "in no wise slay it".
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:27

### 1 Kings 3:28
**Q1. Who heard of the judgment the king had judged?**
**Answer:** All Israel.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:28

**Q2. How did the people react to the king after the judgment?**
**Answer:** They feared the king.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:28

**Q3. What did the people see in the king?**
**Answer:** They saw that the wisdom of God was in him, to do judgment.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 3:28
