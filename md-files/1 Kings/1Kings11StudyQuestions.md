# 1 Kings 11 Study Questions (KJV)

### 1 Kings 11:1
**Q1. Who did king Solomon love?**
**Answer:** Many strange women.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 11:1

**Q2. Besides the daughter of Pharaoh, which other women did Solomon love?**
**Answer:** Women of the Moabites, Ammonites, Edomites, Zidonians, and Hittites.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 11:1

**Q3. Which nations' women did Solomon love?**
**Answer:** Moabites, Ammonites, Edomites, Zidonians, and Hittites.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 11:1

### 1 Kings 11:2
**Q1. What had the Lord said to the children of Israel regarding these nations?**
**Answer:** Ye shall not go in to them, neither shall they come in unto you.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 11:2

**Q2. Why did the Lord forbid the children of Israel from these nations?**
**Answer:** For surely they will turn away your heart after their gods.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 11:2

**Q3. What did Solomon do despite the Lord's warning?**
**Answer:** Solomon clave unto these in love.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 11:2

### 1 Kings 11:3

**Verse:** 1 Kings 11:3

**Q3. What was the effect of these wives on Solomon?**
**Answer:** His wives turned away his heart.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 11:3
