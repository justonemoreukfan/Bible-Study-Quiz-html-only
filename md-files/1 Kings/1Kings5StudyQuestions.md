# 1 Kings 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Kings 5:1
**Q1. Who sent his servants unto Solomon?**
**Answer:** Hiram king of Tyre.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:1

**Q2. Why did Hiram send his servants?**
**Answer:** For he had heard that they had anointed him king in the room of his father.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:1

**Q3. What was Hiram's relationship with David?**
**Answer:** Hiram was ever a lover of David.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:1

### 1 Kings 5:2
**Q1. To whom did Solomon send a message?**
**Answer:** To Hiram.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:2

**Q2. Who was the recipient of Solomon's message?**
**Answer:** Hiram.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:2

**Q3. Did Solomon send a message to Hiram?**
**Answer:** Yes.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:2

### 1 Kings 5:3
**Q1. Why could David not build a house unto the name of the Lord his God?**
**Answer:** For the wars which were about him on every side.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:3

**Q2. What did David's father not build?**
**Answer:** An house unto the name of the Lord his God.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:3

**Q3. When could David's father not build the house?**
**Answer:** Until the Lord put them under the soles of his feet.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:3

### 1 Kings 5:4
**Q1. What has the Lord God given Solomon on every side?**
**Answer:** Rest.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:4

**Q2. What is there no longer, according to Solomon?**
**Answer:** Neither adversary nor evil occurrent.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:4

**Q3. What did the Lord God give Solomon on every side?**
**Answer:** Rest.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:4

### 1 Kings 5:5
**Q1. What does Solomon purpose to build?**
**Answer:** An house unto the name of the Lord my God.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:5

**Q2. To whom did the Lord speak regarding building the house?**
**Answer:** To David my father.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:5

**Q3. What did the Lord say about the son who would build the house?**
**Answer:** Thy son, whom I will set upon thy throne in thy room, he shall build an house unto my name.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:5

### 1 Kings 5:6
**Q1. What did Solomon ask Hiram to command?**
**Answer:** That they hew me cedar trees out of Lebanon.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:6

**Q2. Who would be with Hiram's servants?**
**Answer:** My [Solomon's] servants shall be with thy servants.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:6

**Q3. Why did Solomon say there was no one among them who could skill to hew timber like the Sidonians?**
**Answer:** For thou knowest that there is not among us any that can skill to hew timber like unto the Sidonians.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:6

### 1 Kings 5:7
**Q1. How did Hiram react when he heard the words of Solomon?**
**Answer:** He rejoiced greatly.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:7

**Q2. What did Hiram say after he rejoiced?**
**Answer:** Blessed be the Lord this day, which hath given unto David a wise son over this great people.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:7

**Q3. Whom did Hiram say the Lord had given to David?**
**Answer:** A wise son over this great people.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:7

### 1 Kings 5:8
**Q1. What did Hiram say about the things Solomon sent him for?**
**Answer:** I have considered the things which thou sentest to me for.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:8

**Q2. What did Hiram agree to do concerning timber?**
**Answer:** I will do all thy desire concerning timber of cedar, and concerning timber of fir.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:8

**Q3. Which two types of timber did Hiram agree to provide?**
**Answer:** Timber of cedar, and timber of fir.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:8

### 1 Kings 5:9
**Q1. Where would Hiram's servants bring the timber from?**
**Answer:** Down from Lebanon unto the sea.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:9

**Q2. How would Hiram convey the timber?**
**Answer:** By sea in floats unto the place that thou shalt appoint me.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:9

**Q3. What did Hiram desire in return for his servants' work?**
**Answer:** And thou shalt accomplish my desire, in giving food for my household.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:9

### 1 Kings 5:10
**Q1. What did Hiram give Solomon according to all his desire?**
**Answer:** Cedar trees and fir trees.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:10

**Q2. How did Hiram provide the cedar and fir trees?**
**Answer:** According to all his desire.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:10

**Q3. What trees did Hiram give Solomon?**
**Answer:** Cedar trees and fir trees.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:10

### 1 Kings 5:11
**Q1. How much wheat did Solomon give Hiram for food for his household?**
**Answer:** Twenty thousand measures of wheat.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:11

**Q2. How much pure oil did Solomon give Hiram?**
**Answer:** Twenty measures of pure oil.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:11

**Q3. How often did Solomon give these provisions to Hiram?**
**Answer:** Year by year.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:11

### 1 Kings 5:12
**Q1. What did the Lord give Solomon, as He promised?**
**Answer:** Wisdom.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:12

**Q2. What was the relationship between Hiram and Solomon?**
**Answer:** There was peace between Hiram and Solomon.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:12

**Q3. What did Hiram and Solomon do together?**
**Answer:** They two made a league together.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:12

### 1 Kings 5:13
**Q1. What did king Solomon raise out of all Israel?**
**Answer:** A levy.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:13

**Q2. How many men were in the levy?**
**Answer:** Thirty thousand men.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:13

**Q3. Out of whom did Solomon raise the levy?**
**Answer:** All Israel.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:13

### 1 Kings 5:14
**Q1. Where did Solomon send the levy?**
**Answer:** To Lebanon.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:14

**Q2. How many men were sent to Lebanon each month?**
**Answer:** Ten thousand a month by courses.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:14

**Q3. What was the rotation for the men in Lebanon?**
**Answer:** A month they were in Lebanon, and two months at home.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:14

### 1 Kings 5:15
**Q1. How many people bore burdens?**
**Answer:** Threescore and ten thousand.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:15

**Q2. How many hewers were in the mountains?**
**Answer:** Fourscore thousand hewers.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:15

**Q3. Who were the hewers in the mountains?**
**Answer:** Fourscore thousand hewers.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:15

### 1 Kings 5:16
**Q1. Who were the chief of Solomon's officers over the work?**
**Answer:** Three thousand and three hundred.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:16

**Q2. What was the role of the chief of Solomon's officers?**
**Answer:** Which ruled over the people that wrought in the work.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:16

**Q3. How many chief officers were there?**
**Answer:** Three thousand and three hundred.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:16

### 1 Kings 5:17
**Q1. What did the king command to be brought?**
**Answer:** Great stones, costly stones, and hewed stones.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:17

**Q2. For what purpose were the stones brought?**
**Answer:** To lay the foundation of the house.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:17

**Q3. What types of stones were brought?**
**Answer:** Great stones, costly stones, and hewed stones.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:17

### 1 Kings 5:18
**Q1. Who did the hewing of the stones?**
**Answer:** Solomon's builders and Hiram's builders and the stonesquarers.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:18

**Q2. What did the builders and stonesquarers prepare?**
**Answer:** Timber and stones.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:18

**Q3. What was the goal of preparing the timber and stones?**
**Answer:** To build the house.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 5:18
