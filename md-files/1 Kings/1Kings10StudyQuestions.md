# 1 Kings 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Kings 10:1
**Q1. Who heard of the fame of Solomon concerning the name of the Lord?**
**Answer:** The queen of Sheba.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:1

**Q2. Why did the queen of Sheba come to Solomon?**
**Answer:** To prove him with hard questions.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:1

**Q3. What had the queen of Sheba heard about Solomon?**
**Answer:** The fame of Solomon concerning the name of the Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:1

### 1 Kings 10:2
**Q1. With what did the queen of Sheba come to Jerusalem?**
**Answer:** With a very great train, with camels that bare spices, and very much gold, and precious stones.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:2

**Q2. What did the queen of Sheba do when she came to Solomon?**
**Answer:** She communed with him of all that was in her heart.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:2

**Q3. What did the camels of the queen of Sheba carry?**
**Answer:** Spices, and very much gold, and precious stones.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:2

### 1 Kings 10:3
**Q1. What did Solomon do regarding the queen of Sheba's questions?**
**Answer:** Solomon told her all her questions.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:3

**Q2. Was there anything hidden from the king that he did not tell her?**
**Answer:** No, there was not any thing hid from the king, which he told her not.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:3

**Q3. Who answered the queen of Sheba's questions?**
**Answer:** Solomon.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:3

### 1 Kings 10:4
**Q1. What two things did the queen of Sheba see that impressed her?**
**Answer:** All Solomon's wisdom, and the house that he had built.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:4

**Q2. Did the queen of Sheba see Solomon's wisdom?**
**Answer:** Yes.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:4

**Q3. What did the queen of Sheba see besides Solomon's wisdom?**
**Answer:** The house that he had built.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:4

### 1 Kings 10:5
**Q1. What specific things did the queen of Sheba observe regarding Solomon's table and servants?**
**Answer:** The meat of his table, and the sitting of his servants, and the attendance of his ministers, and their apparel, and his cupbearers.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:5

**Q2. What did the queen of Sheba observe about Solomon's ascent?**
**Answer:** His ascent by which he went up unto the house of the Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:5

**Q3. What was the queen of Sheba's reaction after seeing these things?**
**Answer:** There was no more spirit in her.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:5

### 1 Kings 10:6
**Q1. What did the queen of Sheba say to the king about the reports she had heard?**
**Answer:** It was a true report that I heard in mine own land of thy acts and of thy wisdom.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:6

**Q2. Where had the queen of Sheba heard the reports about Solomon?**
**Answer:** In her own land.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:6

**Q3. What reports were confirmed to be true?**
**Answer:** The reports of Solomon's acts and of his wisdom.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:6

### 1 Kings 10:7
**Q1. Why did the queen of Sheba not believe the words at first?**
**Answer:** Until she came, and her eyes had seen it.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:7

**Q2. What did the queen of Sheba conclude about the reports she had heard?**
**Answer:** Behold, the half was not told me.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:7

**Q3. How did Solomon's wisdom and prosperity compare to the fame she heard?**
**Answer:** Thy wisdom and prosperity exceedeth the fame which I heard.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:7

### 1 Kings 10:8
**Q1. Whom did the queen of Sheba call happy?**
**Answer:** Thy men, and these thy servants.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:8

**Q2. Where did the servants stand continually?**
**Answer:** Before the king.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:8

**Q3. Why were the servants happy?**
**Answer:** Because they hear thy wisdom.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:8

**Q1. Whom did the queen of Sheba bless?**

**Answer:** Because the Lord loved Israel for ever.
**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:9

**Q3. For what purpose did the Lord make Solomon king?**

**Verse:** 1 Kings 10:9
