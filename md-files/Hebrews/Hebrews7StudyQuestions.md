# Hebrews 7 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hebrews 7:1

**Q1. Who was this Melchisedec, king of Salem?**  
**Answer:** The priest of the most high God.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:1

**Q2. Who did Melchisedec meet returning from the slaughter of the kings?**  
**Answer:** Abraham.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:1

**Q3. What did Melchisedec do to Abraham?**  
**Answer:** Blessed him.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:1

---

### Hebrews 7:2

**Q1. What did Abraham give to Melchisedec?**  
**Answer:** A tenth part of all.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:2

**Q2. What is the first interpretation of the name King of righteousness?**  
**Answer:** King of righteousness.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:2

**Q3. After that, what is Melchisedec also called being king of Salem?**  
**Answer:** King of peace.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:2

---

### Hebrews 7:3

**Q1. What is said of Melchisedec regarding his father and mother?**  
**Answer:** Without father, without mother, without descent.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:3

**Q2. What is said of Melchisedec regarding time?**  
**Answer:** Having neither beginning of days, nor end of life.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:3

**Q3. Whom is Melchisedec made like unto?**  
**Answer:** The Son of God.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:3

---

### Hebrews 7:4

**Q1. Who should be considered to see how great this man was?**  
**Answer:** How great this man was.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:4

**Q2. Who gave the tenth of the spoils to Melchisedec?**  
**Answer:** The patriarch Abraham.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:4

**Q3. What did Abraham give unto him?**  
**Answer:** The tenth of the spoils.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:4

---

### Hebrews 7:5

**Q1. Who of the sons of Levi receive the office of the priesthood?**  
**Answer:** They that receive the office of the priesthood.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:5

**Q2. According to the law, who are they commanded to take tithes of?**  
**Answer:** The people.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:5

**Q3. From whom are the people descended, who are also their brethren?**  
**Answer:** Out of the loins of Abraham.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:5

---

### Hebrews 7:6

**Q1. Whose descent is not counted from them?**  
**Answer:** He whose descent is not counted from them.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:6

**Q2. What did this man receive from Abraham?**  
**Answer:** Received tithes of Abraham.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:6

**Q3. To whom did this man grant a blessing?**  
**Answer:** Him that had the promises.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:6

---

### Hebrews 7:7

**Q1. What is the less blessed of?**  
**Answer:** The better.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:7

**Q2. Beyond all contradiction, who blesses whom?**  
**Answer:** The less is blessed of the better.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:7

**Q3. What is this fact described as?**  
**Answer:** Beyond all contradiction.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:7

---

### Hebrews 7:8

**Q1. Who here receive tithes?**  
**Answer:** Men that die.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:8

**Q2. Who receives tithes there?**  
**Answer:** He of whom it is witnessed that he liveth.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:8

**Q3. What is witnessed of him who receives tithes there?**  
**Answer:** That he liveth.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:8

---

### Hebrews 7:9

**Q1. Who may as it were be said to pay tithes?**  
**Answer:** Levi also.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:9

**Q2. Who received tithes in this context?**  
**Answer:** Abraham.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:9

**Q3. By whom was Levi said to pay tithes?**  
**Answer:** By Abraham.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:9

---

### Hebrews 7:10

**Q1. Where was Levi when Melchisedec met Abraham?**  
**Answer:** In the loins of his father.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:10

**Q2. Whose father was Abraham in this verse?**  
**Answer:** His father.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:10

**Q3. Who met his father Abraham?**  
**Answer:** Melchisedec.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:10

---

### Hebrews 7:11

**Q1. What perfection was by the Levitical priesthood?**  
**Answer:** Perfection.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:11

**Q2. Under what priesthood did the people receive the law?**  
**Answer:** The Levitical priesthood.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:11

**Q3. What other kind of priest was necessary to rise after the order of Melchisedec?**  
**Answer:** Another priest.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:11

---

### Hebrews 7:12

**Q1. When the priesthood is changed, what else is made?**  
**Answer:** Of necessity a change also of the law.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:12

**Q2. What is changed concerning the priesthood?**  
**Answer:** The priesthood.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:12

**Q3. Is the change of the law of necessity?**  
**Answer:** Yes.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:12

---

### Hebrews 7:13

**Q1. Of what tribe are these things spoken?**  
**Answer:** Another tribe.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:13

**Q2. What did no man of this tribe give attendance to?**  
**Answer:** The altar.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:13

**Q3. Who gave no attendance to the altar?**  
**Answer:** No man.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:13

---

### Hebrews 7:14

**Q1. Out of what tribe is it evident that our Lord sprang?**  
**Answer:** Judah.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:14

**Q2. Concerning what tribe did Moses speak nothing touching priesthood?**  
**Answer:** Judah.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:14

**Q3. Who spoke nothing concerning priesthood touching that tribe?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:14

---

### Hebrews 7:15

**Q1. What is yet far more evident?**  
**Answer:** It is yet far more evident.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:15

**Q2. After whose similitude there ariseth another priest?**  
**Answer:** The similitude of Melchisedec.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:15

**Q3. What ariseth after the similitude of Melchisedec?**  
**Answer:** Another priest.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:15

---

### Hebrews 7:16

**Q1. After what kind of law was he made a priest?**  
**Answer:** Not after the law of a carnal commandment.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:16

**Q2. After what kind of power was he made a priest?**  
**Answer:** After the power of an endless life.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:16

**Q3. What kind of commandment is mentioned regarding the law?**  
**Answer:** Carnal commandment.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:16

---

### Hebrews 7:17

**Q1. What is testified concerning him?**  
**Answer:** Thou art a priest for ever.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:17

**Q2. According to what order is he a priest for ever?**  
**Answer:** After the order of Melchisedec.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:17

**Q3. For how long is he a priest?**  
**Answer:** For ever.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:17

---

### Hebrews 7:18

**Q1. What is verily there an annulling of?**  
**Answer:** The commandment going before.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:18

**Q2. Why is there an annulling of the commandment going before?**  
**Answer:** For the weakness and unprofitableness thereof.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:18

**Q3. What qualities of the commandment caused its annulling?**  
**Answer:** Weakness and unprofitableness.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:18

---

### Hebrews 7:19

**Q1. What did the law not make perfect?**  
**Answer:** Nothing.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:19

**Q2. What is the bringing in of a better hope?**  
**Answer:** The bringing in of a better hope did.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:19

**Q3. By what do we draw nigh unto God?**  
**Answer:** By the better hope.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:19

---

**Verse:** Hebrews 7:24

**Answer:** To the uttermost.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:25

**Q2. Whom does he save to the uttermost?**  
**Answer:** Them that come unto God by him.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:25

**Verse:** Hebrews 7:25

### Hebrews 7:26

**Q1. What kind of high priest became us?**  
**Answer:** Such an high priest became us.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:26

**Q2. Name two characteristics describing him in this verse.**  
**Answer:** Holy, harmless, undefiled, separate from sinners, and made higher than the heavens.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:26

**Answer:** The heavens.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:26

---

### Hebrews 7:27

**Q1. Who needs not daily to offer up sacrifice?**  
**Answer:** This high priest.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:27

**Q2. What did he do first for his own sins, and then for the people's?**  
**Answer:** Offered up sacrifice.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:27

**Q3. How often did he do this when he offered up himself?**  
**Answer:** Once.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:27

**Answer:** The word of the oath.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 7:28
