# Hebrews 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hebrews 9:1

**Q1. What did the first covenant have pertaining to it?**  
**Answer:** Ordinances of divine service, and a worldly sanctuary.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:1

**Q2. What kind of sanctuary did the first covenant have?**  
**Answer:** A worldly sanctuary.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:1

**Q3. What kind of ordinances did the first covenant have?**  
**Answer:** Ordinances of divine service.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:1

---

### Hebrews 9:2

**Q1. What was prepared in the first part of the tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** The candlestick, and the table, and the shewbread.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:2

**Q2. What is the first part of the tabernacle called?**  
**Answer:** The sanctuary.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:2

**Q3. What three things are mentioned as being in the first part?**  
**Answer:** The candlestick, the table, and the shewbread.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:2

---

### Hebrews 9:3

**Q1. What was behind the second veil?**  
**Answer:** The tabernacle which is called the Holiest of all.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:3

**Q2. What came after the second veil?**  
**Answer:** The tabernacle which is called the Holiest of all.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:3

**Q3. What was the name of the tabernacle behind the second veil?**  
**Answer:** The Holiest of all.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:3

---

### Hebrews 9:4

**Q1. What golden item was in the Holiest of all?**  
**Answer:** The golden censer.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:4

**Q2. What was the ark overlaid round about with?**  
**Answer:** Gold.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:4

**Q3. What three things were inside or associated with the ark of the covenant?**  
**Answer:** The golden pot that had manna, Aaron's rod that budded, and the tables of the covenant.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:4

---

### Hebrews 9:5

**Q1. What was over the ark?**  
**Answer:** The cherubims of glory shadowing the mercyseat.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:5

**Q2. What did the cherubims shadow?**  
**Answer:** The mercyseat.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:5

**Q3. Can these things be spoken of now in particular?**  
**Answer:** Of which we cannot now speak particularly.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:5

---

### Hebrews 9:6

**Q1. Who went always into the first tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** The priests.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:6

**Q2. What did the priests accomplish when they went always into the first tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** The service of God.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:6

**Q3. How often did the priests go into the first tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** Always.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:6

---

### Hebrews 9:7

**Q1. Who went into the second tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** The high priest alone.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:7

**Q2. How often did the high priest go into the second tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** Once every year.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:7

**Q3. What did the high priest not go in without?**  
**Answer:** Blood, which he offered for himself, and for the errors of the people.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:7

---

### Hebrews 9:8

**Q1. What was the Holy Ghost signifying by this?**  
**Answer:** That the way into the holiest of all was not yet made manifest.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:8

**Q2. While what was yet standing was the way not yet made manifest?**  
**Answer:** While as the first tabernacle was yet standing.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:8

**Q3. Whose way was not yet made manifest?**  
**Answer:** The way into the holiest of all.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:8

---

### Hebrews 9:9

**Q1. What was a figure for the time then present?**  
**Answer:** The tabernacle.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:9

**Q2. What were offered in the tabernacle that could not make him that did the service perfect?**  
**Answer:** Gifts and sacrifices.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:9

**Q3. Regarding what aspect could the gifts and sacrifices not make the service perfect?**  
**Answer:** As pertaining to the conscience.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:9

---

### Hebrews 9:10

**Q1. What were the services only in selain to?**  
**Answer:** Meats and drinks, and divers washings, and carnal ordinances.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:10

**Q2. Until what time were these carnal ordinances imposed?**  
**Answer:** Until the time of reformation.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:10

**Q3. What kind of ordinances were they?**  
**Answer:** Carnal ordinances.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:10

---

### Hebrews 9:11

**Q1. Who is come as high priest of good things to come?**  
**Answer:** Christ.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:11

**Q2. By what kind of tabernacle did Christ come?**  
**Answer:** A greater and more perfect tabernacle.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:11

**Q3. How was this greater and more perfect tabernacle described in relation to hands?**  
**Answer:** Not made with hands, that is to say, not of this building.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:11

---

### Hebrews 9:12

**Q1. Whose blood did Christ enter in by?**  
**Answer:** Neither by the blood of goats and calves, but by his own blood.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:12

**Q2. How often did Christ enter in into the holy place?**  
**Answer:** Once.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:12

**Q3. What did Christ obtain for us by entering in once?**  
**Answer:** Eternal redemption for us.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:12

---

### Hebrews 9:13

**Q1. Whose blood is mentioned along with the ashes of an heifer?**  
**Answer:** The blood of bulls and of goats.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:13

**Q2. What was sprinkled on the unclean?**  
**Answer:** The ashes of an heifer.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:13

**Q3. What did the sprinkling of the ashes sanctify to?**  
**Answer:** To the purifying of the flesh.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:13

---

### Hebrews 9:14

**Q1. How did Christ offer himself to God?**  
**Answer:** Through the eternal Spirit.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:14

**Q2. Without what was Christ offered?**  
**Answer:** Without spot.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:14

**Q3. What will the blood of Christ purge your conscience from?**  
**Answer:** From dead works to serve the living God.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:14

---

### Hebrews 9:15

**Q1. What is Christ the mediator of?**  
**Answer:** The new testament.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:15

**Q2. By means of what, for the redemption of the transgressions, was death incurred?**  
**Answer:** By means of death, for the redemption of the transgressions that were under the first testament.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:15

**Q3. What might they which are called receive?**  
**Answer:** The promise of eternal inheritance.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:15

---

### Hebrews 9:16

**Q1. What must necessarily be where a testament is?**  
**Answer:** The death of the testator.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:16

**Q2. What is required regarding a testament and a testator?**  
**Answer:** The death of the testator.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:16

**Q3. When must the death of the testator occur?**  
**Answer:** Where a testament is.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:16

---

### Hebrews 9:17

**Q1. When is a testament of force?**  
**Answer:** After men are dead.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:17

**Q2. When does a testament have no strength at all?**  
**Answer:** While the testator liveth.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:17

**Q3. Whose life affects whether a testament has strength?**  
**Answer:** The testator.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:17

---

### Hebrews 9:18

**Q1. Was the first testament dedicated without blood?**  
**Answer:** Neither the first testament was dedicated without blood.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:18

**Q2. What was not dedicated without blood?**  
**Answer:** The first testament.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:18

**Q3. What was required for the dedication of the first testament?**  
**Answer:** Blood.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:18

---

### Hebrews 9:19

**Q1. Who had spoken every precept to all the people according to the law?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:19

**Q2. What did Moses take along with water, scarlet wool, and hyssop?**  
**Answer:** The blood of calves and of goats.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:19

**Q3. What two things did Moses sprinkle upon?**  
**Answer:** Both the book, and all the people.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:19

---

### Hebrews 9:20

**Q1. Whose blood is this the blood of?**  
**Answer:** The testament which God hath enjoined unto you.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:20

**Q2. Who enjoined the testament?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:20

**Q3. To whom was the testament enjoined?**  
**Answer:** Unto you.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:20

---

### Hebrews 9:21

**Q1. What did Moses sprinkle likewise with blood?**  
**Answer:** The tabernacle, and all the vessels of the ministry.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:21

**Q2. What vessels were sprinkled with blood?**  
**Answer:** All the vessels of the ministry.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:21

**Q3. What else besides the vessels of the ministry was sprinkled with blood?**  
**Answer:** The tabernacle.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:21

---

### Hebrews 9:22

**Q1. Almost all things are purged with what by the law?**  
**Answer:** With blood.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:22

**Q2. What is not shed without the shedding of blood?**  
**Answer:** Remission.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:22

**Q3. What is required for remission?**  
**Answer:** The shedding of blood.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:22

---

### Hebrews 9:23

**Q1. What was it necessary that the patterns of things in the heavens should be purified with?**  
**Answer:** With these.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:23

**Q2. With what better things were the heavenly things themselves purified?**  
**Answer:** With better sacrifices than these.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:23

**Q3. What were purified with better sacrifices?**  
**Answer:** The heavenly things themselves.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:23

---

### Hebrews 9:24

**Q1. Where did Christ not enter?**  
**Answer:** Into the holy places made with hands, which are the figures of the true.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:24

**Q2. Where did Christ enter into?**  
**Answer:** Into heaven itself.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:24

**Q3. For whose appearance did Christ enter into heaven itself?**  
**Answer:** Now to appear in the presence of God for us.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:24

---

### Hebrews 9:25

**Q1. How often did the high priest enter into the holy place?**  
**Answer:** Every year.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:25

**Q2. Whose blood did the high priest enter with?**  
**Answer:** With blood of others.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:25

**Q3. Did Christ need to offer himself often?**  
**Answer:** Nor yet that he should offer himself often.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:25

---

### Hebrews 9:26

**Q1. What would Christ have had to do often since the foundation of the world if he suffered often?**  
**Answer:** Must he often have suffered since the foundation of the world.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:26

**Q2. When has Christ appeared once to put away sin?**  
**Answer:** In the end of the world.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:26

**Q3. How did Christ put away sin?**  
**Answer:** By the sacrifice of himself.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:26

---

### Hebrews 9:27

**Q1. What is appointed unto men once to do?**  
**Answer:** To die.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:27

**Q2. What comes after death?**  
**Answer:** The judgment.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:27

**Q3. How many times is it appointed unto men to die?**  
**Answer:** Once.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:27

---

### Hebrews 9:28

**Q1. How many times was Christ offered?**  
**Answer:** Once.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:28

**Q2. Whose sins were borne by the offering of Christ?**  
**Answer:** The sins of many.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:28

**Q3. How shall Christ appear the second time without sin?**  
**Answer:** Unto salvation unto them that look for him.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 9:28
