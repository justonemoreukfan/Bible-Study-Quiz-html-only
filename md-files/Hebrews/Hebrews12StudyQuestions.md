### Hebrews 12:1
**Q1. According to Hebrews 12:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore seeing we also are compassed about with so great a cloud of witnesses.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 12:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Hebrews 12:1?**  
**Answer:** let us lay aside every weight.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 12:1

**Q3. What conclusion or detail is given in Hebrews 12:1?**  
**Answer:** and the sin which doth so easily beset us, and let us run with patience the race that is set before us.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 12:1
