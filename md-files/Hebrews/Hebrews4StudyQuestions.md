# Hebrews 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hebrews 4:1

**Q1. What should we fear being left behind, seeing a promise being left us?**  
**Answer:** Lest any of you should seem to come short of it.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:1

**Q2. What is left us of entering into his rest?**  
**Answer:** A promise being left us.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:1

**Q3. Who might seem to come short of it?**  
**Answer:** Any of you.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:1

---

### Hebrews 4:2

**Q1. To whom was the gospel preached as well as unto us?**  
**Answer:** Unto them.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:2

**Q2. What did not profit the word preached to them?**  
**Answer:** The word preached.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:2

**Q3. Why did the word preached not profit them?**  
**Answer:** Not being mixed with faith in them that heard it.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:2

---

### Hebrews 4:3

**Q1. Who do enter into rest?**  
**Answer:** We which have believed.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:3

**Q2. As what did God say, "If they shall enter into my rest"?**  
**Answer:** As I have sworn in my wrath.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:3

**Q3. When were the works finished?**  
**Answer:** From the foundation of the world.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:3

---

### Hebrews 4:4

**Q1. Where did God speak of the seventh day on this wise?**  
**Answer:** In a certain place.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:4

**Q2. What did God do on the seventh day?**  
**Answer:** Did rest all his works.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:4

**Q3. From what did God rest on the seventh day?**  
**Answer:** From all his works.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:4

---

### Hebrews 4:5

**Q1. What did God say again in this place?**  
**Answer:** If they shall enter into my rest.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:5

**Q2. Whose rest is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** My rest.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:5

**Q3. What event or statement precedes this repetition?**  
**Answer:** And in this place again.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:5

---

### Hebrews 4:6

**Q1. Seeing therefore it remains that some must enter therein, who are they that entered not?**  
**Answer:** They to whom it was first preached.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:6

**Q2. Why did they to whom it was first preached enter not in?**  
**Answer:** Because of unbelief.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:6

**Q3. What remains concerning some entering therein?**  
**Answer:** That some must enter therein.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:6

---

### Hebrews 4:7

**Q1. Again, what does God limit?**  
**Answer:** A certain day.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:7

**Q2. In whom does God limit a certain day, saying today?**  
**Answer:** In David.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:7

**Q3. After how long a time is "today" said?**  
**Answer:** A long time.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:7

---

### Hebrews 4:8

**Q1. Who would not then have spoken of another day if he had given them rest?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:8

**Q2. What would Jesus not have done if he had given them rest?**  
**Answer:** Then would he not have spoken of another day.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:8

**Q3. Whose rest is being discussed in relation to Joshua?**  
**Answer:** If Jesus had given them rest.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:8

---

### Hebrews 4:9

**Q1. What remains therefore for the people of God?**  
**Answer:** A rest.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:9

**Q2. For whom does a rest remain?**  
**Answer:** The people of God.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:9

**Q3. What is the Greek term or description used for this rest in the verse?**  
**Answer:** A rest (Sabbatismos).  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:9

---

### Hebrews 4:10

**Q1. Who has entered into his rest?**  
**Answer:** He that is entered into his rest.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:10

**Q2. Whose works does he also cease from?**  
**Answer:** His own works.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:10

**Q3. How does he cease from his own works, as God did from?**  
**Answer:** As God did from his.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:10

---

### Hebrews 4:11

**Q1. What should we labor therefore to enter into?**  
**Answer:** That rest.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:11

**Q2. Lest any man fall after what example?**  
**Answer:** The same example of unbelief.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:11

**Q3. What should no man fall after?**  
**Answer:** After the same example of unbelief.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:11

---

### Hebrews 4:12

**Q1. How is the word of God described?**  
**Answer:** Quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:12

**Q2. What does the word of God pierce even to?**  
**Answer:** The dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:12

**Q3. What is the word of God a discerner of?**  
**Answer:** The thoughts and intents of the heart.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:12

---

### Hebrews 4:13

**Q1. What is not manifest in his sight?**  
**Answer:** Neither is there any creature that is not manifest in his sight.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:13

**Q2. How are all things in the sight of him?**  
**Answer:** Naked and opened.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:13

**Q3. With whom do we have to do?**  
**Answer:** Unto whom we have to do.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:13

---

### Hebrews 4:14

**Q1. What kind of high priest have we that is passed into the heavens?**  
**Answer:** A great high priest.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:14

**Q2. Who is the great high priest that is passed into the heavens?**  
**Answer:** Jesus the Son of God.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:14

**Q3. What should we hold fast?**  
**Answer:** Our profession.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:14

---

### Hebrews 4:15

**Q1. What do we not have which cannot be touched with the feeling of our infirmities?**  
**Answer:** An high priest which cannot be touched with the feeling of our infirmities.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:15

**Q2. In how many points was he tempted like as we are?**  
**Answer:** All points.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:15

**Q3. What condition was he in regarding sin while being tempted?**  
**Answer:** Yet without sin.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:15

---

### Hebrews 4:16

**Q1. How should we come boldly?**  
**Answer:** Unto the throne of grace.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:16

**Q2. Why may we come boldly unto the throne of grace?**  
**Answer:** That we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:16

**Q3. What specific type of throne is mentioned?**  
**Answer:** The throne of grace.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 4:16
