# Hebrews 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hebrews 3:1

**Q1. Who are the holy brethren addressed in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Partakers of the heavenly calling.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:1

**Q2. Who are the brethren exhorted to consider?**  
**Answer:** The Apostle and High Priest of our profession, Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:1

**Q3. What kind of calling do these brethren share?**  
**Answer:** The heavenly calling.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:1

---

### Hebrews 3:2

**Q1. To whom was Jesus faithful?**  
**Answer:** To him that appointed him.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:2

**Q2. Who else was faithful in all his house?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:2

**Q3. In what was Moses faithful?**  
**Answer:** In all his house.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:2

---

### Hebrews 3:3

**Q1. Of what is this man counted worthy?**  
**Answer:** More glory than Moses.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:3

**Q2. Who hath more honour than the house?**  
**Answer:** He who hath builded the house.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:3

**Q3. What hath more honour than the house?**  
**Answer:** He who hath builded it.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:3

---

### Hebrews 3:4

**Q1. What is built by every man?**  
**Answer:** Every house.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:4

**Q2. Who built all things?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:4

**Q3. Who is the builder of all things?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:4

---

### Hebrews 3:5

**Q1. How was Moses faithful in all his house?**  
**Answer:** As a servant.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:5

**Q2. For what purpose was Moses faithful as a servant?**  
**Answer:** For a testimony of those things which were to be spoken after.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:5

**Q3. Whose house was Moses faithful in?**  
**Answer:** His house.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:5

---

### Hebrews 3:6

**Q1. As what is Christ faithful over his own house?**  
**Answer:** As a son.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:6

**Q2. Whose house are we?**  
**Answer:** If we hold fast the confidence and the rejoicing of the hope firm unto the end.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:6

**Q3. What must we hold fast firm unto the end?**  
**Answer:** The confidence and the rejoicing of the hope.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:6

---

### Hebrews 3:7

**Q1. Who saith, To day if ye will hear his voice?**  
**Answer:** The Holy Ghost.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:7

**Q2. When should one hear his voice according to the Holy Ghost?**  
**Answer:** To day.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:7

**Q3. What should one do if they hear his voice today?**  
**Answer:** Hear it.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:7

---

### Hebrews 3:8

**Q1. What are hearts not to be as during the provocation?**  
**Answer:** Hardened.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:8

**Q2. When did the provocation take place?**  
**Answer:** In the day of temptation in the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:8

**Q3. Where did the temptation take place?**  
**Answer:** In the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:8

---

### Hebrews 3:9

**Q1. Who tempted the speaker in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** Your fathers.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:9

**Q2. What did the fathers do to the speaker in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** Tempted me, proved me.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:9

**Q3. How long did they see the speaker's works?**  
**Answer:** Forty years.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:9

---

### Hebrews 3:10

**Q1. With whom was the speaker grieved?**  
**Answer:** That generation.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:10

**Q2. What did the speaker say about their heart?**  
**Answer:** They do alway err in their heart.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:10

**Q3. What did they not know concerning the speaker?**  
**Answer:** They have not known my ways.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:10

---

### Hebrews 3:11

**Q1. What did the speaker swear in his wrath?**  
**Answer:** They shall not enter into my rest.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:11

**Q2. In what state did the speaker swear this?**  
**Answer:** In my wrath.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:11

**Q3. Whose rest were they barred from entering?**  
**Answer:** My rest.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:11

---

### Hebrews 3:12

**Q1. What must brethren take heed of?**  
**Answer:** Lest there be in any of you an evil heart of unbelief.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:12

**Q2. What kind of heart should be avoided?**  
**Answer:** An evil heart of unbelief.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:12

**Q3. From whom does the evil heart of unbelief cause one to depart?**  
**Answer:** The living God.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:12

---

### Hebrews 3:13

**Q1. How often should believers exhort one another?**  
**Answer:** Daily.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:13

**Q2. While what period should believers exhort one another?**  
**Answer:** While it is called To day.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:13

**Q3. What is the effect of the deceitfulness of sin?**  
**Answer:** Hardened through the deceitfulness of sin.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:13

---

### Hebrews 3:14

**Q1. Of whom are we made partakers?**  
**Answer:** Of Christ.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:14

**Q2. Under what condition are we made partakers of Christ?**  
**Answer:** If we hold the beginning of our confidence steadfast unto the end.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:14

**Q3. What must be held steadfast unto the end?**  
**Answer:** The beginning of our confidence.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:14

---

### Hebrews 3:15

**Q1. What saying is repeated in this verse?**  
**Answer:** To day if ye will hear his voice, harden not your hearts, as in the provocation.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:15

**Q2. When are hearts not to be hardened according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** To day if ye will hear his voice.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:15

**Q3. As what event should hearts not be hardened?**  
**Answer:** As in the provocation.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:15

---

### Hebrews 3:16

**Q1. Who heard and provoked?**  
**Answer:** Some, when they had heard.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:16

**Q2. Who provoked the speaker after hearing?**  
**Answer:** All that came out of Egypt by Moses.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:16

**Q3. By whom did they come out of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** By Moses.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:16

---

### Hebrews 3:17

**Q1. With whom was God grieved forty years?**  
**Answer:** With them that had sinned.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:17

**Q2. How long was God grieved with them?**  
**Answer:** Forty years.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:17

**Q3. Where did their carcasses fall?**  
**Answer:** In the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:17

---

### Hebrews 3:18

**Q1. To whom sware he that they should not enter into his rest?**  
**Answer:** To them that believed not.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:18

**Q2. What did he swear they should not enter?**  
**Answer:** Into his rest.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:18

**Q3. Who were the ones excluded from his rest?**  
**Answer:** Them that believed not.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:18

---

### Hebrews 3:19

**Q1. Why could they not enter in?**  
**Answer:** Because of unbelief.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:19

**Q2. What was the cause of their inability to enter?**  
**Answer:** Unbelief.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:19

**Q3. What were they unable to do because of unbelief?**  
**Answer:** Enter in.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 3:19
