# Hebrews 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hebrews 6:1

**Q1. What should we leave behind to go on unto perfection?**  
**Answer:** The principles of the doctrine of Christ.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:1

**Q2. Where should we go on unto?**  
**Answer:** Perfection.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:1

**Q3. What kind of foundation should we not lay again?**  
**Answer:** Of repentance from dead works, and of faith toward God.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:1

---

### Hebrews 6:2

**Q1. What doctrine is mentioned alongside the doctrine of baptisms?**  
**Answer:** The doctrine of baptisms, of laying on of hands, and of resurrection of the dead, and of eternal judgment.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:2

**Q2. Whose hands are laid on in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The doctrine of laying on of hands.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:2

**Q3. What kind of judgment is mentioned at the end of the verse?**  
**Answer:** Eternal judgment.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:2

---

### Hebrews 6:3

**Q1. What will we do if God permit?**  
**Answer:** This will we do.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:3

**Q2. Who must permit for this to happen?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:3

**Q3. What does "This will we do" depend upon?**  
**Answer:** If God permit.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:3

---

### Hebrews 6:4

**Q1. What has happened to those who were once enlightened?**  
**Answer:** It is impossible for them who were once enlightened.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:4

**Q2. What have they tasted of?**  
**Answer:** The heavenly gift.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:4

**Q3. What were they made partakers of?**  
**Answer:** The Holy Ghost.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:4

---

### Hebrews 6:5

**Q1. What have they tasted of in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The good word of God.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:5

**Q2. What powers have they tasted of?**  
**Answer:** The powers of the world to come.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:5

**Q3. Whose word have they tasted?**  
**Answer:** The good word of God.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:5

---

### Hebrews 6:6

**Q1. If they shall fall away, what is it to do to them?**  
**Answer:** To renew them again unto repentance.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:6

**Q2. What do they crucify to themselves?**  
**Answer:** The Son of God afresh.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:6

**Q3. What do they put the Son of God to?**  
**Answer:** An open shame.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:6

---

### Hebrews 6:7

**Q1. What does the earth drink in?**  
**Answer:** The rain that cometh oft upon it.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:7

**Q2. For whom does the earth bring forth herbs?**  
**Answer:** For them by whom it is dressed.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:7

**Q3. What does the earth receive from God?**  
**Answer:** Blessing.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:7

---

### Hebrews 6:8

**Q1. What does the earth bear if it brings forth thorns and briers?**  
**Answer:** Thorns and briers.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:8

**Q2. How is this earth regarded?**  
**Answer:** Rejected.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:8

**Q3. What is the end of such earth?**  
**Answer:** To be burned.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:8

---

### Hebrews 6:9

**Q1. How are the beloved spoken to regarding better things?**  
**Answer:** But, beloved, we are persuaded better things of you.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:9

**Q2. What kind of things do the writers accompany?**  
**Answer:** Things that accompany salvation.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:9

**Q3. How are they spoken of in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Though we thus speak.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:9

---

### Hebrews 6:10

**Q1. What is God not unrighteous to forget?**  
**Answer:** Your work and labour of love.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:10

**Q2. Whose name have they shown love toward?**  
**Answer:** His name.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:10

**Q3. Who have they ministered to and do minister?**  
**Answer:** The saints.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:10

---

### Hebrews 6:11

**Q1. What do we desire every one of you to show?**  
**Answer:** The same diligence.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:11

**Q2. To what end should this diligence be shown?**  
**Answer:** Unto the full assurance of hope unto the end.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:11

---

### Hebrews 6:12

**Q1. What should you not be?**  
**Answer:** Slothful.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:12

**Q2. Who should you be followers of?**  
**Answer:** Them who through faith and patience inherit the promises.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:12

**Q3. Through what do people inherit the promises?**  
**Answer:** Faith and patience.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:12

---

### Hebrews 6:13

**Q1. When God made promise to Abraham, who could He swear by?**  
**Answer:** Himself.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:13

**Q2. Because He could swear by no greater, who did He swear by?**  
**Answer:** Himself.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:13

**Q3. To whom did God make promise?**  
**Answer:** Abraham.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:13

---

### Hebrews 6:14

**Q1. What did God say He would do in blessing?**  
**Answer:** Blessing I will bless thee.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:14

**Q2. What did God say He would do in multiplying?**  
**Answer:** Multiplying I will multiply thee.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:14

**Q3. Whose name is associated with this promise?**  
**Answer:** Abraham (thee).  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:14

---

### Hebrews 6:15

**Q1. After what action did Abraham patiently endure?**  
**Answer:** After he had patiently endured.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:15

**Q2. What did Abraham obtain?**  
**Answer:** The promise.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:15

**Q3. How did Abraham endure before obtaining the promise?**  
**Answer:** Patiently.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:15

---

### Hebrews 6:16

**Q1. Who do men swear by?**  
**Answer:** By the greater.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:16

**Q2. What is an oath for confirmation to men?**  
**Answer:** An end of all strife.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:16

**Q3. What does an oath provide among men?**  
**Answer:** Confirmation.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:16

---

### Hebrews 6:17

**Q1. What did God desire more abundantly to show unto the heirs of promise?**  
**Answer:** The immutability of his counsel.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:17

**Q2. How did God confirm His counsel?**  
**Answer:** By an oath.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:17

**Q3. Who are the recipients of this shown counsel?**  
**Answer:** The heirs of promise.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:17

---

### Hebrews 6:18

**Q1. By how many immutable things is it impossible for God to lie?**  
**Answer:** Two immutable things.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:18

**Q2. What might we have, who have fled for refuge?**  
**Answer:** A strong consolation.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:18

**Q3. What have we been given to lay hold upon?**  
**Answer:** The hope set before us.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:18

---

### Hebrews 6:19

**Q1. What kind of hope do we have as an anchor?**  
**Answer:** An anchor of the soul.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:19

**Q2. How is the anchor described regarding its safety and steadfastness?**  
**Answer:** Both sure and stedfast.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:19

**Q3. Where does the anchor enter into?**  
**Answer:** Within the veil.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:19

---

### Hebrews 6:20

**Q1. Who entered for us within the veil?**  
**Answer:** The forerunner.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:20

**Q2. Who is the forerunner spoken of?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:20

**Q3. After whose order was he made an high priest for ever?**  
**Answer:** After the order of Melchisedec.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 6:20
