# Hebrews 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hebrews 2:1

**Q1. What ought we to give to the things which we have heard?**  
**Answer:** Earnest heed.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:1

**Q2. Why should we give earnest heed to the things which we have heard?**  
**Answer:** Lest at any time we should let them slip.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:1

### Hebrews 2:2

**Q3. What was spoken by angels?**  
**Answer:** The word.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:2

**Q4. What was steadfast concerning the word spoken by angels?**  
**Answer:** It was steadfast.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:2

**Q5. What did every transgression and disobedience receive?**  
**Answer:** A just recompence of reward.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:2

### Hebrews 2:3

**Q6. What will happen if we neglect so great salvation?**  
**Answer:** We shall not escape.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:3

**Q7. At the beginning, by whom was this great salvation spoken?**  
**Answer:** By the Lord.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:3

**Q8. By whom was this salvation confirmed unto us?**  
**Answer:** By them that heard him.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:3

### Hebrews 2:4

**Q9. With what did God bear witness?**  
**Answer:** Both with signs and wonders, and with divers miracles.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:4

**Q10. Besides signs, wonders, and divers miracles, with what else did God bear witness?**  
**Answer:** Gifts of the Holy Ghost.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:4

**Q11. According to whose will were these gifts distributed?**  
**Answer:** According to his own will.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:4

### Hebrews 2:5

**Q12. Unto whom hath God not put in subjection the world to come?**  
**Answer:** Unto the angels.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:5

**Q13. Concerning what world is this passage speaking?**  
**Answer:** The world to come.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:5

**Q14. Where whereof do we speak?**  
**Answer:** Whereof we speak.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:5

### Hebrews 2:6

**Q15. Who testified in a certain place?**  
**Answer:** One.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:6

**Q16. What is man that thou art mindful of him?**  
**Answer:** Man.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:6

**Q17. Who is visited besides man?**  
**Answer:** The son of man.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:7 *(Wait, verse 6 asks about the son of man).*  
**Answer:** The son of man.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:6

### Hebrews 2:7

**Q18. For how long was man made a little lower than the angels?**  
**Answer:** A little lower than the angels.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:7

**Q19. With what did thou crown him?**  
**Answer:** Glory and honour.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:7

**Q20. Over what did thou set him?**  
**Answer:** Over the works of thy hands.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:7

### Hebrews 2:8

**Q21. What didst thou put in subjection under his feet?**  
**Answer:** All things.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:8

**Q22. What was left that is not put in subjection under him?**  
**Answer:** Nothing that is not put under him.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:8

**Q23. What do we not yet see?**  
**Answer:** All things put under him.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:8

### Hebrews 2:9

**Q24. Who was made a little lower than the angels?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:9

**Q25. For what suffering of death was Jesus crowned with glory and honour?**  
**Answer:** The suffering of death.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:9

**Q26. By the grace of God, for whom should Jesus taste death?**  
**Answer:** For every man.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:9

### Hebrews 2:10

**Q27. For whom are all things, and by whom are all things?**  
**Answer:** Him.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:10

**Q28. In bringing many sons unto glory, whom did God make perfect through sufferings?**  
**Answer:** The captain of their salvation.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:10

**Q29. What was the captain of their salvation made perfect through?**  
**Answer:** Sufferings.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:10

### Hebrews 2:11

**Q30. Who are all of one?**  
**Answer:** Both he that sanctifieth and they who are sanctified.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:11

**Q31. For what cause is he not ashamed to call them what?**  
**Answer:** Brethren.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:11

**Q32. Who sanctifieth and who are sanctified?**  
**Answer:** He that sanctifieth and they who are sanctified.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:11

### Hebrews 2:12

**Q33. To whom will I declare thy name?**  
**Answer:** My brethren.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:12

**Q34. In the midst of whom will I sing praise unto thee?**  
**Answer:** The church.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:12

**Q35. Whose name will I declare?**  
**Answer:** Thy name.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:12

### Hebrews 2:13

**Q36. In whom will I put my trust?**  
**Answer:** In him.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:13

**Q37. Behold, I and the children which whom did God give me?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:13

**Q38. What does he say he will do in him?**  
**Answer:** Put my trust.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:13

### Hebrews 2:14

**Q39. What did the children partake of?**  
**Answer:** Flesh and blood.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:14

**Q40. What did he himself likewise take part of?**  
**Answer:** The same.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:14

**Q41. Through death, whom might he destroy?**  
**Answer:** Him that had the power of death.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:14

### Hebrews 2:15

**Q42. Whom might he deliver?**  
**Answer:** Them who through fear of death were all their lifetime subject to bondage.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:15

**Q43. Through what were they subject to bondage?**  
**Answer:** Fear of death.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:15

**Q44. How long were they subject to bondage?**  
**Answer:** All their lifetime.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:15

### Hebrews 2:16

**Q45. Verily, on what did he not take hold of?**  
**Answer:** The nature of angels.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:16

**Q46. On whose seed did he take hold?**  
**Answer:** The seed of Abraham.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:16

**Q47. Whose nature did he not take on?**  
**Answer:** The nature of angels.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:16

### Hebrews 2:17

**Q48. In all things, what behooved him to be made like unto?**  
**Answer:** His brethren.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:17

**Q49. For what purpose was he to be made like unto his brethren?**  
**Answer:** That he might be a merciful and faithful high priest.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:17

**Q50. In what things should he be a merciful and faithful high priest?**  
**Answer:** Things pertaining to God.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:17

### Hebrews 2:18

**Q51. In that he himself hath suffered being tempted, what is he able to do?**  
**Answer:** Succour them that are tempted.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:18

**Q52. Who himself suffered being tempted?**  
**Answer:** He himself.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:18

**Q53. Whom is he able to succour?**  
**Answer:** Them that are tempted.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 2:18
