# Hebrews 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hebrews 1:1

**Q1. In time past, how did God speak unto the fathers by the prophets?**  
**Answer:** At sundry times and in divers manners.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:1

**Q2. To whom did God speak in time past by the prophets?**  
**Answer:** Unto the fathers.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:1

**Q3. Who spoke unto the fathers by the prophets in time past?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:1

---

### Hebrews 1:2

**Q4. Hath in these last days spoken unto us by whom?**  
**Answer:** His Son.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:2

**Q5. Whom hath God appointed heir of all things?**  
**Answer:** His Son.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:2

**Q6. By whom also did God make the worlds?**  
**Answer:** By whom also he made the worlds.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:2

---

### Hebrews 1:3

**Q7. Who being the brightness of his glory, and the express image of his person?**  
**Answer:** His Son (the Son).  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:3

**Q8. What does he uphold all things by?**  
**Answer:** The word of his power.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:3

**Q9. When he had by himself purged our sins, where did he sit down?**  
**Answer:** On the right hand of the Majesty on high.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:3

---

### Hebrews 1:4

**Q10. Being made so much better than whom?**  
**Answer:** The angels.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:4

**Q11. As he hath by inheritance obtained a more excellent name than whom?**  
**Answer:** Than they.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:4

**Q12. What has he obtained by inheritance?**  
**Answer:** A more excellent name.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:4

---

### Hebrews 1:5

**Q13. Unto which of the angels said he at any time, Thou art my Son?**  
**Answer:** None.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:5

**Q14. When did he say, This day have I begotten thee?**  
**Answer:** At any time (not said to angels).  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:5

**Q15. And again, what will I be to him?**  
**Answer:** A Father.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:5

---

### Hebrews 1:6

**Q16. And again, when he bringeth in the firstbegotten into the world, what does he say?**  
**Answer:** And let all the angels of God worship him.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:6

**Q17. Who are commanded to worship him?**  
**Answer:** All the angels of God.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:6

**Q18. Who is brought into the world in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The firstbegotten.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:6

---

### Hebrews 1:7

**Q19. And of the angels what saith he?**  
**Answer:** Who maketh his angels spirits, and his ministers a flame of fire.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:7

**Q20. What does he make his angels?**  
**Answer:** Spirits.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:7

**Q21. What does he make his ministers?**  
**Answer:** A flame of fire.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:7

---

### Hebrews 1:8

**Q22. But unto the Son what saith he, O God?**  
**Answer:** Thy throne is for ever and ever.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:8

**Q23. What is the scepter of thy kingdom?**  
**Answer:** A sceptre of righteousness.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:8

---

### Hebrews 1:9

**Q24. Thou hast loved righteousness, and hated what?**  
**Answer:** Iniquity.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:9

**Q25. Therefore God, even thy God, hath anointed thee with what?**  
**Answer:** The oil of gladness.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:9

**Q26. Above whom has he anointed thee with the oil of gladness?**  
**Answer:** Above thy fellows.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:9

---

### Hebrews 1:10

**Q27. And, Thou, Lord, in the beginning hast laid the foundation of what?**  
**Answer:** The earth.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:10

**Q28. The heavens are the works of whose hands?**  
**Answer:** Thy hands.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:10

**Q29. When did the Lord lay the foundation of the earth?**  
**Answer:** In the beginning.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:10

---

### Hebrews 1:11

**Q30. They shall perish; but who remaineth?**  
**Answer:** Thou.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:11

**Q31. They all shall wax old as doth what?**  
**Answer:** A garment.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:11

---

### Hebrews 1:12

**Q32. And as a vesture shalt thou fold them up, and they shall be what?**  
**Answer:** Changed.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:12

**Q33. But thou art the same, and what shall not fail?**  
**Answer:** Thy years.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:12

---

### Hebrews 1:13

**Q34. But to which of the angels said he at any time?**  
**Answer:** Sit on my right hand.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:13

**Q35. Until when did he say, Sit on my right hand?**  
**Answer:** Until I make thine enemies thy footstool.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:13

**Q36. Whose enemies will be made his footstool?**  
**Answer:** Thine enemies.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:13

---

### Hebrews 1:14

**Q37. Are they not all ministering spirits sent forth to minister for whom?**  
**Answer:** Them who shall be heirs of salvation.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:14

**Q38. What kind of spirits are they?**  
**Answer:** Ministering spirits.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:14

**Q39. For whom are they sent forth to minister?**  
**Answer:** Them who shall be heirs of salvation.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 1:14
