# Hebrews 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hebrews 8:1

**Q1. What is the sum of the things which are spoken?**  
**Answer:** We have such an high priest, who is set on the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens;  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:1

**Q2. Where is this high priest set?**  
**Answer:** On the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens;  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:1

**Q3. Whose throne is the high priest set on the right hand of?**  
**Answer:** The Majesty in the heavens;  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:1

---

### Hebrews 8:2

**Q1. Of what is the minister a minister?**  
**Answer:** Of the sanctuary, and of the true tabernacle,  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:2

**Q2. Who pitched the true tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** The Lord, and not man.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:2

**Q3. What is the true tabernacle also called?**  
**Answer:** The sanctuary;  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:2

---

### Hebrews 8:3

**Q1. What is every high priest ordained to offer?**  
**Answer:** Gifts and sacrifices:  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:3

**Q2. What is it of necessity that this man have?**  
**Answer:** Somewhat also to offer.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:3

**Q3. Who ordains every high priest to offer gifts and sacrifices?**  
**Answer:** [Implied by context/Not explicitly named in this verse, but every high priest is ordained]  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:3

---

### Hebrews 8:4

**Q1. Where would he not be a priest if he were on earth?**  
**Answer:** Seeing that there are priests that offer gifts according to the law:  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:4

**Q2. What do the priests on earth offer according to the law?**  
**Answer:** Gifts according to the law:  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:4

**Q3. What would he not be if he were on earth?**  
**Answer:** A priest,  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:4

---

### Hebrews 8:5

**Q1. Whom do these priests serve unto?**  
**Answer:** The example and shadow of heavenly things,  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:5

**Q2. Who was Moses admonished of when he was about to make the tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** God,  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:5

**Q3. What was Moses commanded to make all things according to?**  
**Answer:** The pattern shewed to thee in the mount.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:5

---

### Hebrews 8:6

**Q1. What ministry has he obtained that is more excellent?**  
**Answer:** A more excellent ministry,  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:6

**Q2. By how much is he the mediator of a better covenant?**  
**Answer:** By how much also he is the mediator of a better covenant,  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:6

**Q3. What are the better covenant's promises founded upon?**  
**Answer:** Better promises.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:6

---

### Hebrews 8:7

**Q1. What would not have been sought for if that first covenant had been faultless?**  
**Answer:** Then should no place have been sought for the second.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:7

**Q2. What was wrong with that first covenant?**  
**Answer:** It had a fault (it was "not faultless").  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:7

**Q3. What kind of covenant was the first one?**  
**Answer:** The first covenant.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:7

---

### Hebrews 8:8

**Q1. What did he find fault with them for?**  
**Answer:** For finding fault with them,  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:8

**Q2. What days will come, according to the Lord?**  
**Answer:** The days come, saith the Lord,  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:8

**Q3. With whom will the Lord make a new covenant?**  
**Answer:** With the house of Israel and with the house of Judah:  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:8

---

### Hebrews 8:9

**Q1. Not according to what covenant was this?**  
**Answer:** Not according to the covenant that I made with their fathers in the day when I took them by the hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt;  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:9

**Q2. What did they not continue in?**  
**Answer:** My covenant,  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:9

**Q3. How did the Lord regard them because they continued not in His covenant?**  
**Answer:** And I regarded them not, saith the Lord.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:9

---

### Hebrews 8:10

**Q1. What is this covenant that I will make with the house of Israel after those days?**  
**Answer:** I will put my laws into their mind, and write them in their hearts:  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:10

**Q2. Whose God will I be?**  
**Answer:** And I will be to them a God,  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:10

**Q3. Whose people shall they be to me?**  
**Answer:** And they shall be to me a people:  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:10

---

### Hebrews 8:11

**Q1. What shall they not teach every man?**  
**Answer:** His neighbour, and every man his brother, saying, Know the Lord:  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:11

**Q2. Who shall know the Lord?**  
**Answer:** All shall know me, from the least to the greatest.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:11

**Q3. Who is excluded from knowing the Lord in this group?**  
**Answer:** None ("all shall know me, from the least to the greatest").  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:11

---

### Hebrews 8:12

**Q1. What will I be to their unrighteousness?**  
**Answer:** Merciful to their unrighteousness,  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:12

**Q2. What will I remember no more?**  
**Answer:** Their sins and their iniquities will I remember no more.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:12

**Q3. Whose unrighteousness, sins, and iniquities will be addressed?**  
**Answer:** Theirs (the house of Israel).  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:12

---

### Hebrews 8:13

**Q1. In that he saith, A new covenant, what has he made the first?**  
**Answer:** Old.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:13

**Q2. What is that which decayeth and waxeth old ready to do?**  
**Answer:** Ready to vanish away.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:13

**Q3. What happens to that which is made old?**  
**Answer:** It decayeth and waxeth old.  
**Verse:** Hebrews 8:13
