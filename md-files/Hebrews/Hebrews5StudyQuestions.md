# Hebrews 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hebrews 5:1

**Q1. From among whom is every high priest taken?**  
**Answer:** Men  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:1

**Q2. For whom is every high priest ordained in things pertaining to God?**  
**Answer:** Men  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:1

**Q3. What does every high priest offer for sins?**  
**Answer:** Gifts and sacrifices  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:1

---

### Hebrews 5:2

**Q1. Who can the high priest have compassion on?**  
**Answer:** The ignorant, and on them that are out of the way  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:2

**Q2. What is the high priest himself compassed with?**  
**Answer:** Infirmity  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:2

---

### Hebrews 5:3

**Q1. By reason of infirmity, what ought the high priest to do, as for the people, so also for himself?**  
**Answer:** To offer for sins  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:3

---

### Hebrews 5:4

**Q1. What honor does no man take unto himself?**  
**Answer:** This honour  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:4

**Q2. Who is the only one that takes this honor unto himself?**  
**Answer:** He that is called of God  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:4

**Q3. Who was called of God, even as Aaron was?**  
**Answer:** He  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:4

---

### Hebrews 5:5

**Q1. Who glorified not himself to be made an high priest?**  
**Answer:** Christ  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:5

**Q2. Who said unto him, Thou art my Son, to day have I begotten thee?**  
**Answer:** He that said unto him  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:5

---

### Hebrews 5:6

**Q1. Where does he say also in another place, Thou art a priest for ever?**  
**Answer:** In another place  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:6

**Q2. After whose order is he a priest for ever?**  
**Answer:** After the order of Melchisedec  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:6

---

### Hebrews 5:7

**Q1. In what days did he offer up prayers and supplications with strong crying and tears?**  
**Answer:** In the days of his flesh  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:7

**Q2. To whom did he offer up prayers and supplications?**  
**Answer:** Unto him that was able to save him from death  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:7

**Q3. In what was he heard?**  
**Answer:** In that he feared  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:7

---

### Hebrews 5:8

**Q1. Though he were a Son, what did he learn by the things which he suffered?**  
**Answer:** Obedience  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:8

---

### Hebrews 5:9

**Q1. And being what, became he the author of eternal salvation unto all them that obey him?**  
**Answer:** Made perfect  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:9

**Q2. Unto whom did he become the author of eternal salvation?**  
**Answer:** Unto all them that obey him  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:9

---

### Hebrews 5:10

**Q1. By whom was he called an high priest after the order of Melchisedec?**  
**Answer:** Of God  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:10

---

### Hebrews 5:11

**Q1. Of whom have we many things to say?**  
**Answer:** Of him  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:11

**Q2. What kind of things are they to utter?**  
**Answer:** Hard to be uttered  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:11

**Q3. Why are the things hard to be uttered to them?**  
**Answer:** Seeing ye are dull of hearing  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:11

---

### Hebrews 5:12

**Q1. For when for the time ye ought to be what, what do ye have need of again?**  
**Answer:** Teachers  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:12

**Q2. What do ye have need that one teach you again?**  
**Answer:** Which be the first principles of the oracles of God  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:12

**Q3. What are such become as have need of milk, and not of strong meat?**  
**Answer:** Such as have need of milk  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:12

---

### Hebrews 5:13

**Q1. For every one that useth milk is what?**  
**Answer:** Unskilful in the word of righteousness  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:13

**Q2. What is every one that useth milk?**  
**Answer:** A babe  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:13

---

### Hebrews 5:14

**Q1. To whom belongeth strong meat?**  
**Answer:** To them that are of full age  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:14

**Q2. By reason of use, what have those of full age exercised?**  
**Answer:** Their senses  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:14

**Q3. What have their senses been exercised to discern?**  
**Answer:** Both good and evil  
**Verse:** Hebrews 5:14
