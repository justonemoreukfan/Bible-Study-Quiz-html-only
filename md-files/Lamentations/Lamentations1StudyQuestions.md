# Lamentations 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Lamentations 1:1
**Q1. According to Lamentations 1:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** How doth the city sit solitary.  
**Verse:** Lamentations 1:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Lamentations 1:1?**  
**Answer:** that was full of people! how is she become as a widow! she that was great among the nations.  
**Verse:** Lamentations 1:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Lamentations 1:1?**  
**Answer:** and princess among the provinces, how is she become tributary!  
**Verse:** Lamentations 1:1
