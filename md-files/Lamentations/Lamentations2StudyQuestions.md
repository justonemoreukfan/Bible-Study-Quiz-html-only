# Lamentations 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Lamentations 2:1
**Q1. According to Lamentations 2:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** How hath the LORD covered the daughter of Zion with a cloud in his anger.  
**Verse:** Lamentations 2:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Lamentations 2:1?**  
**Answer:** and cast down from heaven unto the earth the beauty of Israel.  
**Verse:** Lamentations 2:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Lamentations 2:1?**  
**Answer:** and remembered not his footstool in the day of his anger!  
**Verse:** Lamentations 2:1

---

### Lamentations 2:2
**Q4. What is stated in the opening of Lamentations 2:2?**  
**Answer:** The LORD hath swallowed up all the habitations of Jacob, and hath not pitied.  
**Verse:** Lamentations 2:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Lamentations 2:2?**  
**Answer:** he hath thrown down in his wrath the strong holds of the daughter of Judah.  
**Verse:** Lamentations 2:2

**Q6. What further truth is recorded in Lamentations 2:2?**  
**Answer:** he hath brought them down to the ground; he hath polluted the kingdom and the princes thereof.  
**Verse:** Lamentations 2:2
