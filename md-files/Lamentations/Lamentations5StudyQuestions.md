### Lamentations 5:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Lamentations 5:1?**  
**Answer:** Remember, O LORD, what is come upon us.  
**Verse:** Lamentations 5:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Lamentations 5:1?**  
**Answer:** consider, and behold our reproach.  
**Verse:** Lamentations 5:1

**Q3. According to Lamentations 5:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Remember, O LORD, what is come upon us: consider, and behold our reproach.  
**Verse:** Lamentations 5:1
