### Habakkuk 3:2
**Q4. What is stated in the opening of Habakkuk 3:2?**  
**Answer:** O LORD, I have heard thy speech, and was afraid.  
**Verse:** Habakkuk 3:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Habakkuk 3:2?**

**Verse:** Habakkuk 3:2
