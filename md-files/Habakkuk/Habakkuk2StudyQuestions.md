# Habakkuk 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Habakkuk 2:1
**Q1. According to Habakkuk 2:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** I will stand upon my watch.  
**Verse:** Habakkuk 2:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Habakkuk 2:1?**  
**Answer:** and set me upon the tower.  
**Verse:** Habakkuk 2:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Habakkuk 2:1?**  
**Answer:** and will watch to see what he will say unto me, and what I shall answer when I am reproved.  
**Verse:** Habakkuk 2:1

---
