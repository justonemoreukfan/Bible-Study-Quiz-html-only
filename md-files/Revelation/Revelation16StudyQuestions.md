# Revelation 16 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 16:1

**Q1. What did the great voice out of the temple say to the seven angels?**  
**Answer:** Go your ways, and pour out the vials of the wrath of God upon the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:1

**Q2. Where did the great voice come from?**  
**Answer:** Out of the temple.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:1

**Q3. How many angels were told to pour out the vials of the wrath of God?**  
**Answer:** Seven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:1

---

### Revelation 16:2

**Q1. Who went and poured out his vial upon the earth?**  
**Answer:** The first.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:2

**Q2. What fell upon the men which had the mark of the beast, and upon them which worshipped his image?**  
**Answer:** A noisome and grievous sore.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:2

**Q3. Upon whom did the noisome and grievous sore fall?**  
**Answer:** The men which had the mark of the beast, and upon them which worshipped his image.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:2

---

### Revelation 16:3

**Q1. Where did the second angel pour out his vial?**  
**Answer:** Upon the sea.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:3

**Q2. What did the sea become like?**  
**Answer:** The blood of a dead man.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:3

**Q3. What happened to every living soul in the sea?**  
**Answer:** They died in the sea.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:3

---

### Revelation 16:4

**Q1. Where did the third angel pour out his vial?**  
**Answer:** Upon the rivers and fountains of waters.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:4

**Q2. What did the rivers and fountains of waters become?**  
**Answer:** Blood.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:4

**Q3. Who poured his vial upon the rivers and fountains of waters?**  
**Answer:** The third angel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:4

---

### Revelation 16:5

**Q1. Who did the angel of the waters hear?**  
**Answer:** The angel of the waters.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:5

**Q2. How is the Lord God described regarding His judgment?**  
**Answer:** Thou art righteous, O Lord, which art, and wast, and shalt be, because thou hast judged thus.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:5

**Q3. What past, present, and future state is attributed to the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Which art, and wast, and shalt be.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:5

---

### Revelation 16:6

**Q1. What did they shed the blood of?**  
**Answer:** Saints and prophets.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:6

**Q2. What did God give them to drink?**  
**Answer:** Blood.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:6

**Q3. Why did God give them blood to drink according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** For they are worthy.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:6

**Answer:** Even so, Lord God Almighty, true and righteous are thy judgments.

**Q2. Where did the voice come from?**  
**Answer:** Out of the altar.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:7

**Verse:** Revelation 16:7

### Revelation 16:8

**Q1. Where did the fourth angel pour out his vial?**  
**Answer:** Upon the sun.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:8

**Q2. What power was given unto the sun?**  
**Answer:** To scorch men with fire.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:8

**Q3. Who poured his vial upon the sun?**  
**Answer:** The fourth angel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:8

---

### Revelation 16:9

**Q1. How were men scorched by the great heat?**  
**Answer:** With great heat.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:9

**Q2. What did men blaspheme?**  
**Answer:** The name of God, which hath power over these plagues.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:9

**Q3. What did men fail to do despite the plagues?**  
**Answer:** Repented not to give him glory.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:9

---

### Revelation 16:10

**Q1. Where did the fifth angel pour out his vial?**  
**Answer:** Upon the seat of the beast.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:10

**Q2. What happened to the kingdom of the beast?**  
**Answer:** It was full of darkness.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:10

**Q3. What did men do because of the pain?**  
**Answer:** Gnawed their tongues for pain.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:10

---

### Revelation 16:11

**Q1. Whom did men blaspheme because of their pains and their sores?**  
**Answer:** The God of heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:11

**Q2. What caused the men to blaspheme the God of heaven?**  
**Answer:** Their pains and their sores.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:11

**Q3. What did the men fail to do regarding their deeds?**  
**Answer:** Repented not of their deeds.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:11

---

### Revelation 16:12

**Q1. Where did the sixth angel pour out his vial?**  
**Answer:** Upon the great river Euphrates.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:12

**Q2. What happened to the water of the river Euphrates?**  
**Answer:** The water thereof was dried up.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:12

**Q3. Why was the water of the river dried up?**  
**Answer:** That the way of the kings of the east might be prepared.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:12

---

### Revelation 16:13

**Q1. What came out of the mouth of the dragon, the beast, and the false prophet?**  
**Answer:** Three unclean spirits like frogs.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:13

**Q2. What do the three unclean spirits look like?**  
**Answer:** Frogs.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:13

**Q3. Name the three sources from whose mouths the unclean spirits came.**  
**Answer:** The dragon, the beast, and the false prophet.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:13

---

### Revelation 16:14

**Q1. Whose spirits were the spirits of devils?**  
**Answer:** The spirits of devils, working miracles.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:14

**Q2. Where did the spirits go unto?**  
**Answer:** The kings of the earth and of the whole world.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:14

**Q3. For what purpose were the spirits going to gather the kings?**  
**Answer:** To the battle of that great day of God Almighty.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:14

---

### Revelation 16:15

**Q1. How does the Lord describe His coming?**  
**Answer:** As a thief.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:15

**Q2. Who is pronounced blessed?**  
**Answer:** He that watcheth, and keepeth his garments.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:15

**Q3. Why should a person keep his garments?**  
**Answer:** Lest he walk naked, and they see his shame.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:15

**Verse:** Revelation 16:16

**Q3. Who was gathered into the place called Armageddon?**

**Answer:** A great hail out of heaven, every stone about the weight of a talent.

**Answer:** About the weight of a talent.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:21

**Answer:** Blasphemed God because of the plague of the hail.  
**Verse:** Revelation 16:21
