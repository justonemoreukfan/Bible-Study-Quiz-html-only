# Revelation 18 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 18:1

**Q1. Who came down from heaven?**  
**Answer:** Another angel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:1

**Q2. What power did this angel have?**  
**Answer:** Great power.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:1

**Q3. What happened to the earth as a result?**  
**Answer:** The earth was lightened with his glory.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:1

---

### Revelation 18:2

**Q1. How did the angel cry mightily?**  
**Answer:** With a strong voice.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:2

**Q2. What city did he say is fallen?**  
**Answer:** Babylon the great.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:2

**Q3. What has Babylon become the habitation of?**  
**Answer:** Devils, the hold of every foul spirit, and a cage of every unclean and hateful bird.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:2

---

### Revelation 18:3

**Q1. What have all nations drunk of?**  
**Answer:** The wine of the wrath of her fornication.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:3

**Q2. Who have committed fornication with her?**  
**Answer:** The kings of the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:3

**Q3. Who waxed rich through the abundance of her delicacies?**  
**Answer:** The merchants of the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:3

---

### Revelation 18:4

**Q1. Whose voice was heard from heaven?**  
**Answer:** Another voice.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:4

**Q2. What are the people called to do concerning her?**  
**Answer:** Come out of her, my people.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:4

**Q3. Why should they come out of her?**  
**Answer:** That ye be not partakers of her sins, and that ye receive not of her plagues.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:4

---

### Revelation 18:5

**Q1. How have her sins reached?**  
**Answer:** Unto heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:5

**Q2. What has God remembered concerning her?**  
**Answer:** Her iniquities.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:5

---

### Revelation 18:6

**Q1. What should be done unto her as she rewarded?**  
**Answer:** Reward her even as she rewarded you.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:6

**Q2. What should be doubled unto her?**  
**Answer:** Double unto her double according to her works.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:6

**Q3. What should be filled to her in the cup which she hath filled?**  
**Answer:** Double.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:6

---

### Revelation 18:7

**Q1. How much has she glorified herself, and lived deliciously?**  
**Answer:** So much torment and sorrow give her.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:7

**Q2. What does she say in her heart?**  
**Answer:** I sit a queen, and am no widow, and shall see no sorrow.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:7

---

### Revelation 18:8

**Q1. When shall her plagues come?**  
**Answer:** In one day.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:8

**Q2. What plagues shall come upon her?**  
**Answer:** Death, and mourning, and famine.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:8

**Q3. What shall she be utterly burned with?**  
**Answer:** Fire.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:8

---

### Revelation 18:9

**Q1. Who shall bewail her, and lament for her?**  
**Answer:** The kings of the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:9

**Q2. What did these kings commit with her?**  
**Answer:** Fornication and lived deliciously.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:9

**Q3. What shall they see when they bewail her?**  
**Answer:** The smoke of her burning.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:9

---

### Revelation 18:10

**Q1. Where shall the kings stand?**  
**Answer:** Afar off for the fear of her torment.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:10

**Q2. What shall they say in lamentation?**  
**Answer:** Alas, alas, that great city Babylon, that mighty city!  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:10

**Q3. In how much time is her judgment come?**  
**Answer:** In one hour.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:10

---

### Revelation 18:11

**Q1. Who shall weep and mourn over her?**  
**Answer:** The merchants of the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:11

**Q2. Why do the merchants weep and mourn?**  
**Answer:** For no man buyeth their merchandise any more.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:11

---

### Revelation 18:12

**Q1. Name three types of merchandise mentioned that no man buys anymore?**  
**Answer:** Gold, silver, and precious stones.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:12

**Q2. What kind of fabrics are listed among the merchandise?**  
**Answer:** Pearls, fine linen, purple, silk, and scarlet.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:12

**Q3. What kinds of wood and materials are listed?**  
**Answer:** All thyine wood, and all manner vessels of ivory, and all manner vessels of most precious wood, and of brass, and iron, and marble.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:12

---

### Revelation 18:13

**Q1. What spices and fragrances are listed?**  
**Answer:** Cinnamon, and odours, and ointments, and frankincense.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:13

**Q2. What foods and drinks are mentioned?**  
**Answer:** Wine, and oil, and fine flour, and wheat.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:13

**Q3. What animals and human lives are listed at the end of the merchandise?**  
**Answer:** Beasts, and sheep, and horses, and chariots, and slaves, and souls of men.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:13

---

### Revelation 18:14

**Q1. What has departed from her?**  
**Answer:** The fruits that thy soul lusted after.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:14

**Q2. What else has departed that were dainty and goodly?**  
**Answer:** All things which were dainty and goodly.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:14

**Q3. Shall men find these things anymore at all?**  
**Answer:** No more at all.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:14

---

### Revelation 18:15

**Q1. Who are the merchants of these things?**  
**Answer:** The merchants of these things, which were made rich by her.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:15

**Q2. Where shall these merchants stand?**  
**Answer:** Afar off.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:15

**Q3. Why shall they stand afar off?**  
**Answer:** For the fear of her torment, weeping and wailing.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:15

---

### Revelation 18:16

**Q1. What does the second part of verse 16 say happened to the great city?**  
**Answer:** For in one hour so great riches is come to nought.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:16

**Q2. How is the city clothed?**  
**Answer:** In fine linen, and purple, and scarlet.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:16

**Q3. With what is the city decked?**  
**Answer:** Gold, and precious stones, and pearls.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:16

---

### Revelation 18:17

**Q1. What happened to every shipmaster?**  
**Answer:** Every shipmaster, and all the company in ships, and sailors, and as many as trade by sea, stood afar off.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:17

**Q2. In how much time is so great riches come to nought?**  
**Answer:** In one hour.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:17

**Q3. Who stood afar off?**  
**Answer:** Every shipmaster, and all the company in ships, and sailors, and as many as trade by sea.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:17

---

### Revelation 18:18

**Q1. What did they cry when they saw the smoke of her burning?**  
**Answer:** Saying, What city is like unto this great city!  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:18

**Q2. Whose burning did they see?**  
**Answer:** Her burning.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:18

**Q3. What did the smoke cause them to cry out about?**  
**Answer:** What city is like unto this great city!  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:18

---

### Revelation 18:19

**Q1. What did they cast upon their heads?**  
**Answer:** Dust.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:19

**Q2. How did they cry, weeping and wailing?**  
**Answer:** Alas, alas, that great city.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:19

**Q3. Who was made rich by her costliness?**  
**Answer:** All that had ships in the sea.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:19

---

### Revelation 18:20

**Q1. Who is told to rejoice over her?**  
**Answer:** Thou heaven, and ye holy apostles and prophets.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:20

**Q2. Why should heaven and the holy apostles and prophets rejoice?**  
**Answer:** For God hath avenged you on her.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:20

**Q3. Who has God avenged them on?**  
**Answer:** Her.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:20

---

### Revelation 18:21

**Q1. What kind of stone did a mighty angel take up?**  
**Answer:** A stone like a great millstone.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:21

**Q2. Where did the angel cast the stone?**  
**Answer:** Into the sea.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:21

**Q3. With what violence shall that great city Babylon be thrown down?**  
**Answer:** With violence.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:21

---

### Revelation 18:22

**Q1. What sounds shall be heard no more at all in her?**  
**Answer:** The voice of harpers, and musicians, and of pipers, and trumpeters.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:22

**Q2. What craftsman of any craft shall be found no more at all in her?**  
**Answer:** No craftsman, of whatsoever craft he be.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:22

**Q3. What sound of a millstone shall be heard no more at all in her?**  
**Answer:** The sound of a millstone.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:22

---

### Revelation 18:23

**Q1. What light shall shine no more at all in her?**  
**Answer:** The light of a candle.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:23

**Q2. Whose voice shall be heard no more at all in her?**  
**Answer:** The voice of the bridegroom and of the bride.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:23

**Q3. Who were the great men of the earth?**  
**Answer:** Thy merchants.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:23

---

### Revelation 18:24

**Q1. What was found in her?**  
**Answer:** The blood of prophets, and of saints.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:24

**Q2. Whose blood besides prophets and saints was found in her?**  
**Answer:** Of all that were slain upon the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 18:24
