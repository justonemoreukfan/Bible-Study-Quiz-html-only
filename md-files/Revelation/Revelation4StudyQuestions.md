# Revelation 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 4:1

**Q1. What happened after this according to the verse?**  
**Answer:** I looked, and, behold, a door was opened in heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:1

**Q2. What was the first voice which was heard like?**  
**Answer:** As it were of a trumpet talking with me.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:1

**Q3. What did the voice say to the speaker?**  
**Answer:** Come up hither, and I will shew thee things which must be hereafter.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:1

---

### Revelation 4:2

**Q4. Where was the speaker immediately?**  
**Answer:** In the spirit.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:2

**Q5. What was set in heaven?**  
**Answer:** A throne.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:2

**Q6. Who sat on the throne?**  
**Answer:** One sat on the throne.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:2

---

### Revelation 4:3

**Q7. What was he that sat like in appearance?**  
**Answer:** To look upon like a jasper and a sardine stone.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:3

**Q8. What was round about the throne?**  
**Answer:** A rainbow.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:3

**Q9. What was the rainbow like in sight?**  
**Answer:** Unto an emerald.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:3

---

### Revelation 4:4

**Q10. What was round about the throne?**  
**Answer:** Four and twenty seats.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:4

**Q11. What did the speaker see upon the seats?**  
**Answer:** Four and twenty elders sitting.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:4

**Q12. How were the elders clothed and crowned?**  
**Answer:** Clothed in white raiment; and they had on their heads crowns of gold.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:4

---

### Revelation 4:5

**Q13. What proceeded out of the throne?**  
**Answer:** Lightnings and thunderings and voices.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:5

**Q14. What were burning before the throne?**  
**Answer:** Seven lamps of fire.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:5

**Q15. What do the seven lamps of fire represent?**  
**Answer:** The seven Spirits of God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:5

---

### Revelation 4:6

**Q16. What was before the throne like unto crystal?**  
**Answer:** A sea of glass.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:6

**Q17. What was in the midst of the throne, and round about the throne?**  
**Answer:** Four beasts full of eyes before and behind.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:6

---

### Revelation 4:7

**Q18. What was the first beast like?**  
**Answer:** A lion.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:7

**Q19. What was the second beast like?**  
**Answer:** A calf.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:7

**Q20. What was the third beast like?**  
**Answer:** A beast had a face as a man.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:7

---

### Revelation 4:8

**Q21. What was the fourth beast like?**  
**Answer:** A flying eagle.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:8

**Q22. How many wings did each of the four beasts have?**  
**Answer:** Six wings about him.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:8

**Q23. What did the beasts rest not day and night saying?**  
**Answer:** Holy, holy, holy, Lord God Almighty, which was, and is, and is to come.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:8

---

### Revelation 4:9

**Q24. What do the beasts give when those beasts give glory and honour and thanks?**  
**Answer:** Glory and honour and thanks to him that sat on the throne.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:9

**Q25. To whom are the glory, honour, and thanks given by the beasts?**  
**Answer:** To him that sat on the throne.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:9

**Q26. For how long does he sit on the throne?**  
**Answer:** Who liveth for ever and ever.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:9

---

### Revelation 4:10

**Q27. What do the four and twenty elders fall down before?**  
**Answer:** Him that sat on the throne.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:10

**Q28. Whom do the four and twenty elders worship?**  
**Answer:** Him that liveth for ever and ever.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:10

**Q29. What do the elders cast before the throne?**  
**Answer:** Their crowns.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:10

---

### Revelation 4:11

**Q30. What do the elders say the Lord is worthy to receive?**  
**Answer:** Glory and honour and power.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:11

**Q31. Why has the Lord created all things?**  
**Answer:** For thy pleasure.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:11

**Q32. What condition are all things in regarding their creation?**  
**Answer:** They are and were created.  
**Verse:** Revelation 4:11
