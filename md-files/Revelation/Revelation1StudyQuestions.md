# Revelation 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 1:1

**Q1. What is the origin of this revelation according to the verse?**  
**Answer:** The Revelation of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:1

**Q2. To whom did God give this revelation to show unto His servants?**  
**Answer:** Unto his servants.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:1

**Q3. How did He send and signify it by His angel unto His servant John?**  
**Answer:** By sending and signifying it by his angel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:1

---

### Revelation 1:2

**Q1. What did John bear record of in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The word of God, and the testimony of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:2

**Q2. What else did John bear record of besides the word of God and testimony of Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Of all things that he saw.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:2

---

### Revelation 1:3

**Q1. Who is blessed according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** He that readeth, and they that hear the words of this prophecy.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:3

**Q2. What are those who hear required to do with the things written therein?**  
**Answer:** Keep those things which are written therein.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:3

**Q3. What reason is given for keeping these things?**  
**Answer:** For the time is at hand.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:3

---

### Revelation 1:4

**Q1. To whom does John write this epistle?**  
**Answer:** The seven churches which are in Asia.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:4

**Q2. Who is mentioned as the source of grace and peace?**  
**Answer:** Him which is, and which was, and which is to come.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:4

**Q3. What else is mentioned as being before His throne besides Him?**  
**Answer:** The seven Spirits which are before his throne.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:4

---

### Revelation 1:5

**Q1. How is Jesus Christ described in relation to being a faithful witness?**  
**Answer:** And from Jesus Christ, who is the faithful witness.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:5

**Q2. What titles are given to Jesus regarding the dead and kings?**  
**Answer:** The first begotten of the dead, and the prince of the kings of the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:5

**Q3. What has Jesus loved us and done for us with His blood?**  
**Answer:** Unto him that loved us, and washed us from our sins in his own blood.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:5

---

### Revelation 1:6

**Q1. What has Jesus made us unto God and His Father?**  
**Answer:** Kings and priests unto God and his Father.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:6

**Q2. To whom belong glory and dominion?**  
**Answer:** To him.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:6

**Q3. For how long are glory and dominion ascribed to Him?**  
**Answer:** For ever and ever.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:6

---

### Revelation 1:7

**Q1. With what is He coming?**  
**Answer:** With clouds.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:7

**Q2. Who shall see Him come?**  
**Answer:** Every eye shall see him, and they also which pierced him.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:7

**Q3. How shall all kindreds of the earth react because of Him?**  
**Answer:** All kindreds of the earth shall wail because of him.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:7

---

### Revelation 1:8

**Q1. What does the Lord God call Himself regarding the alphabet?**  
**Answer:** Alpha and Omega.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:8

**Q2. What else does the Lord God call Himself concerning the beginning and ending?**  
**Answer:** The beginning and the ending.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:8

**Q3. How is the Almighty described regarding His existence?**  
**Answer:** Which is, and which was, and which is to come, the Almighty.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:8

---

### Revelation 1:9

**Q1. Who is speaking and sharing in tribulation and kingdom?**  
**Answer:** I John, who also am your brother, and companion in tribulation, and in the kingdom and patience of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:9

**Q2. Where was John when he was in the isle called Patmos?**  
**Answer:** In the isle that is called Patmos.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:9

**Q3. For what two reasons was John in the isle of Patmos?**  
**Answer:** For the word of God, and for the testimony of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:9

---

### Revelation 1:10

**Q1. On what day was John in the Spirit?**  
**Answer:** On the Lord's day.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:10

**Q2. What did John hear behind him?**  
**Answer:** A great voice.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:10

**Q3. To what instrument was the voice behind him compared?**  
**Answer:** As of a trumpet.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:10

---

### Revelation 1:11

**Q1. What did the voice say to John?**  
**Answer:** I am Alpha and Omega, the first and the last.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:11

**Q2. What was John told to do with what he saw?**  
**Answer:** What thou seest, write in a book.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:11

**Q3. To which seven churches was John told to send it?**  
**Answer:** Unto the seven churches which are in Asia; unto Ephesus, and unto Smyrna, and unto Pergamos, and unto Thyatira, and unto Sardis, and unto Philadelphia, and unto Laodicea.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:11

---

### Revelation 1:12

**Q1. What did John turn to see when he heard the voice?**  
**Answer:** To see the voice that spake with him.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:12

**Q2. What did John see when he turned?**  
**Answer:** Seven golden candlesticks.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:12

---

### Revelation 1:13

**Q1. Who was in the midst of the seven candlesticks?**  
**Answer:** One like unto the Son of man.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:13

**Q2. How was the Son of man clothed?**  
**Answer:** Clothed with a garment down to the foot.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:13

**Q3. What was about His paps?**  
**Answer:** Girt about the paps with a golden girdle.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:13

---

### Revelation 1:14

**Q1. To what were His head and His hairs compared?**  
**Answer:** Like wool as white as snow.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:14

**Q2. To what were His eyes compared?**  
**Answer:** As a flame of fire.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:14

---

### Revelation 1:15

**Q1. To what were His feet likened?**  
**Answer:** Like unto fine brass, as if they burned in a furnace.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:15

**Q2. To what was His voice compared?**  
**Answer:** As the sound of many waters.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:15

---

### Revelation 1:16

**Q1. What did He have in His right hand?**  
**Answer:** Seven stars.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:16

**Q2. What went out of His mouth?**  
**Answer:** A sharp twoedged sword.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:16

**Q3. To what was His countenance compared?**  
**Answer:** As the sun shineth in his strength.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:16

---

### Revelation 1:17

**Q1. What did John do when he saw Him?**  
**Answer:** I fell at his feet as dead.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:17

**Q2. What did He lay upon John with His right hand?**  
**Answer:** His right hand upon me.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:17

**Q3. What did He say to John regarding fear?**  
**Answer:** Fear not.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:17

---

### Revelation 1:18

**Q1. How did He identify Himself regarding death and life?**  
**Answer:** I am he that liveth, and was dead.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:18

**Q2. What does He claim to be for evermore?**  
**Answer:** Alive for evermore.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:18

**Q3. What keys does He hold?**  
**Answer:** The keys of hell and of death.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:18

---

### Revelation 1:19

**Q1. What three things is John told to write?**  
**Answer:** The things which thou hast seen, and the things which are, and the things which shall be hereafter.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:19

---

### Revelation 1:20

**Q1. What is the mystery of the seven stars?**  
**Answer:** The seven stars are the angels of the seven churches.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:20

**Q2. What are the seven candlesticks which John saw?**  
**Answer:** The seven candlesticks which thou sawest are the seven churches.  
**Verse:** Revelation 1:20
