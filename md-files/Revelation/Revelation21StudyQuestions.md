# Revelation 21 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 21:1

**Q1. What did John see according to Revelation 21:1?**  
**Answer:** A new heaven and a new earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:1

**Q2. What happened to the first heaven and the first earth?**  
**Answer:** They were passed away.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:1

**Q3. What was no more in this vision?**  
**Answer:** There was no more sea.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:1

---

### Revelation 21:2

**Q4. What did John see coming down from God out of heaven?**  
**Answer:** The holy city, new Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:2

**Q5. How was the holy city prepared?**  
**Answer:** As a bride adorned for her husband.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:2

**Q6. From where did the holy city come down?**  
**Answer:** From God out of heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:2

---

### Revelation 21:3

**Q7. What great voice did John hear out of heaven?**  
**Answer:** Saying, Behold, the tabernacle of God is with men.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:3

**Q8. Who will dwell with men according to the voice?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:3

**Q9. Whose people shall they be, and what shall God Himself be to them?**  
**Answer:** They shall be his people, and God himself shall be with them, and be their God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:3

---

### Revelation 21:4

**Q10. What will God wipe away from their eyes?**  
**Answer:** All tears.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:4

**Q11. What state or condition shall be no more?**  
**Answer:** There shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:4

**Q12. What happened to the former things?**  
**Answer:** They are passed away.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:4

---

### Revelation 21:5

**Q13. What did he that sat upon the throne say?**  
**Answer:** Behold, I make all things new.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:5

**Q14. What did he command John to write?**  
**Answer:** For these words are true and faithful.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:5

**Q15. How are the words described?**  
**Answer:** True and faithful.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:5

---

### Revelation 21:6

**Q16. What did he say unto John concerning the completion of things?**  
**Answer:** It is done.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:6

**Q17. What are the two titles he applies to himself in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:6

**Q18. What will he give unto him that is athirst freely?**  
**Answer:** Of the fountain of the water of life.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:6

---

### Revelation 21:7

**Q19. What shall he that overcometh inherit?**  
**Answer:** All things.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:7

**Q20. What relationship will God have to the overcomer?**  
**Answer:** I will be his God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:7

**Q21. What relationship will the overcomer have to Him?**  
**Answer:** He shall be my son.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:7

---

### Revelation 21:8

**Q22. Who are listed first among those who shall have their part in the lake which burneth with fire and brimstone?**  
**Answer:** The fearful, and unbelieving.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:8

**Q23. Which groups are listed after the abominable and murderers?**  
**Answer:** Whoremongers, and sorcerers, and idolaters, and all liars.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:8

**Q24. What is the second death?**  
**Answer:** The lake which burneth with fire and brimstone.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:8

---

### Revelation 21:9

**Q25. Who came unto John talking with him?**  
**Answer:** One of the seven angels which had the seven vials full of the seven last plagues.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:9

**Q26. What did the angel talk with John about?**  
**Answer:** Saying, Come hither, I will shew thee the bride, the Lamb's wife.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:9

**Q27. How is the bride described in relation to the Lamb?**  
**Answer:** The Lamb's wife.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:9

---

### Revelation 21:10

**Q28. How did the angel carry John away in the spirit?**  
**Answer:** To a great and high mountain.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:10

**Q29. What did the angel show John descending out of heaven from God?**  
**Answer:** That great city, the holy Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:10

**Q30. From where did the holy Jerusalem descend?**  
**Answer:** Out of heaven from God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:10

---

### Revelation 21:11

**Q31. Having what did the city descend?**  
**Answer:** The glory of God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:11

**Q32. What was the light of the city like?**  
**Answer:** Like unto a stone most precious, even like a jasper stone, clear as crystal.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:11

**Q33. What kind of stone was it clear as?**  
**Answer:** Crystal.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:11

---

### Revelation 21:12

**Q34. What kind of wall did the city have?**  
**Answer:** A great and high wall.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:12

**Q35. How many gates did the wall have?**  
**Answer:** Twelve gates.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:12

**Q36. Who was at the twelve gates, and what was written on them?**  
**Answer:** Twelve angels, and names written thereon, which are the names of the twelve tribes of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:12

---

### Revelation 21:13

**Q37. How many gates were on the east?**  
**Answer:** Three gates.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:13

**Q38. How many gates were on the north and south?**  
**Answer:** On the north three gates; on the south three gates.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:13

**Q39. How many gates were on the west?**  
**Answer:** Three gates.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:13

---

### Revelation 21:14

**Q40. What did the wall of the city have?**  
**Answer:** Twelve foundations.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:14

**Q41. What was in the twelve foundations?**  
**Answer:** The names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:14

**Q42. Whose apostles were they?**  
**Answer:** The Lamb.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:14

---

### Revelation 21:15

**Q43. What did he that talked with me have?**  
**Answer:** A golden reed to measure the city, and the gates thereof, and the wall thereof.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:15

**Q44. What was the golden reed used for?**  
**Answer:** To measure the city, and the gates thereof, and the wall thereof.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:15

**Q45. What material was the reed made of?**  
**Answer:** Gold.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:15

---

### Revelation 21:16

**Q46. What shape was the city?**  
**Answer:** Lieth foursquare.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:16

**Q47. How large was the length and the breadth of the city?**  
**Answer:** As large as the breadth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:16

**Q48. What were the length and the breadth and the height of the city measured with the reed?**  
**Answer:** Twelve thousand furlongs.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:16

---

### Revelation 21:17

**Q49. What was the measurement of the wall?**  
**Answer:** An hundred and forty and four cubits.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:17

**Q50. According to whose measure was the wall measured?**  
**Answer:** The measure of a man, that is, of the angel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:17

**Q51. What kind of measure was used?**  
**Answer:** The measure of a man.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:17

---

### Revelation 21:18

**Q52. Of what material was the building of the wall of it?**  
**Answer:** Jasper.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:18

**Q53. Of what material was the city?**  
**Answer:** Pure gold.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:18

**Q54. What kind of glass was the city like unto?**  
**Answer:** Clear glass.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:18

---

### Revelation 21:19

**Q55. With what were the foundations of the wall garnished?**  
**Answer:** All manner of precious stones.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:19

**Q56. What was the first foundation?**  
**Answer:** Jasper.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:19

**Q57. What were the second and third foundations?**  
**Answer:** Sapphire; the third, a chalcedony; the fourth, an emerald.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:19

---

### Revelation 21:20

**Q58. What were the fifth and sixth foundations?**  
**Answer:** Sardonyx; the sixth, sardius.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:20

**Q59. What were the seventh, eighth, and ninth foundations?**  
**Answer:** Chrysolyte; the eighth, beryl; the ninth, a topaz.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:20

**Q60. What were the tenth, eleventh, and twelfth foundations?**  
**Answer:** A chrysoprasus; the eleventh, a jacinth; the twelfth, an amethyst.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:20

---

### Revelation 21:21

**Q61. What were the twelve gates?**  
**Answer:** Twelve pearls.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:21

**Q62. What was every several gate made of?**  
**Answer:** Of one pearl.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:21

**Q63. What was the street of the city?**  
**Answer:** Pure gold, as it were transparent glass.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:21

---

### Revelation 21:22

**Q64. What did John see in the city regarding a temple?**  
**Answer:** No temple therein.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:22

**Q65. Why was there no temple in the city?**  
**Answer:** For the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are the temple of it.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:22

**Q66. Who are the temple of the city?**  
**Answer:** The Lord God Almighty and the Lamb.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:22

---

### Revelation 21:23

**Q67. What did the city have no need of regarding the sun or moon?**  
**Answer:** Neither of the sun, nor of the moon, to shine in it.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:23

**Q68. Why did the city not need the sun or moon to shine in it?**  
**Answer:** For the glory of God did lighten it.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:23

**Q69. What is the light of the city?**  
**Answer:** The Lamb.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:23

---

### Revelation 21:24

**Q70. Who shall walk in the light of the city?**  
**Answer:** The nations of them which are saved.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:24

**Q71. What shall the kings of the earth bring into it?**  
**Answer:** Their glory and honour into it.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:24

**Q72. Into what place shall the kings bring their glory and honour?**  
**Answer:** Into it (the city).  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:24

---

### Revelation 21:25

**Q73. When shall the gates of the city not be shut at all?**  
**Answer:** By day.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:25

**Q74. What shall there be no more of in that place?**  
**Answer:** There shall be no night there.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:25

**Q75. Because there is no night there, what does that mean for the gates?**  
**Answer:** They shall not be shut at all by day.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:25

---

### Revelation 21:26

**Q76. What shall men bring into the city?**  
**Answer:** The glory and honour of the nations.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:26

**Q77. Whose glory and honour shall be brought into it?**  
**Answer:** The nations.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:26

**Q78. Where shall the glory and honour of the nations be brought?**  
**Answer:** Into it.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:26

---

### Revelation 21:27

**Q79. What shall in no wise enter into the city?**  
**Answer:** Anything that defileth, neither whatsoever worketh abomination, or maketh a lie.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:27

**Q80. Who are the only ones allowed to enter?**  
**Answer:** They which are written in the Lamb's book of life.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:27

**Q81. In whose book must their names be written to enter?**  
**Answer:** The Lamb's book of life.  
**Verse:** Revelation 21:27
