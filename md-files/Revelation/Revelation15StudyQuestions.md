# Revelation 15 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 15:1

**Q1. What did the author see in heaven as another sign?**  
**Answer:** Seven angels having the seven last plagues.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:1

**Q2. What was filled up in these seven last plagues?**  
**Answer:** The wrath of God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:1

**Q3. How many last plagues were there?**  
**Answer:** Seven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:1

---

### Revelation 15:2

**Q1. What was seen as it were a sea of glass?**  
**Answer:** Mingled with fire.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:2

**Q2. Who stood on the sea of glass?**  
**Answer:** Them that had gotten the victory over the beast, and over his image, and over his mark, and over the number of his name.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:2

**Q3. What did those who got the victory have in their hands?**  
**Answer:** The harps of God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:2

---

### Revelation 15:3

**Q1. Whose song did they sing?**  
**Answer:** The song of Moses the servant of God, and the song of the Lamb.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:3

**Q2. What did they call the works of the Lord God Almighty?**  
**Answer:** Great and marvellous.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:3

**Q3. What did they call the ways of the King of saints?**  
**Answer:** Just and true.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:3

---

### Revelation 15:4

**Q1. Who shall not fear the Lord, and glorify His name?**  
**Answer:** Who shall not fear thee, O Lord, and glorify thy name?  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:4

**Q2. Who alone is holy?**  
**Answer:** For thou only art holy.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:4

**Q3. What shall come and worship before the Lord?**  
**Answer:** All nations.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:4

---

### Revelation 15:5

**Q1. After those things, what was looked upon?**  
**Answer:** And after that I looked.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:5

**Q2. What was opened in heaven?**  
**Answer:** The temple of the tabernacle of the testimony.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:5

**Q3. What was the temple called?**  
**Answer:** The temple of the tabernacle of the testimony in heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:5

---

### Revelation 15:6

**Q1. Who came out of the temple?**  
**Answer:** The seven angels having the seven plagues.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:6

**Q2. How were the seven angels clothed?**  
**Answer:** In pure and white linen.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:6

**Q3. What was about their girts?**  
**Answer:** Golden girdles.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:6

---

### Revelation 15:7

**Q1. Who gave the seven angels seven golden vials?**  
**Answer:** One of the four beasts.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:7

**Q2. What were the seven golden vials full of?**  
**Answer:** The wrath of God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:7

**Q3. For how long does God live?**  
**Answer:** Who liveth for ever and ever.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:7

---

### Revelation 15:8

**Q1. What was the temple filled with?**  
**Answer:** Smoke from the glory of God, and from his power.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:8

**Q2. From what was the temple filled with smoke?**  
**Answer:** From the glory of God, and from his power.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:8

**Q3. Who was able to enter into the temple?**  
**Answer:** No man was able to enter into the temple, till the seven plagues of the seven angels were fulfilled.  
**Verse:** Revelation 15:8
