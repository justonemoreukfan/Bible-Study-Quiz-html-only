# Revelation 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 9:1

**Q1.** Who sounded the fifth angel?  
**Answer:** The fifth angel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:1

**Q2.** What did the star see fall from heaven unto the earth?  
**Answer:** A star fall from heaven unto the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:1

**Q3.** What was given unto the star?  
**Answer:** The key of the bottomless pit.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:1

---

### Revelation 9:2

**Q1.** What did the star open?  
**Answer:** The bottomless pit.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:2

**Q2.** What arose out of the pit?  
**Answer:** A smoke out of the pit.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:2

**Q3.** What was the smoke like?  
**Answer:** The smoke of a great furnace.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:2

---

### Revelation 9:3

**Q1.** What came out of the smoke?  
**Answer:** Locusts upon the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:3

**Q2.** What was given unto the locusts?  
**Answer:** Power.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:3

**Q3.** What power was like the power of the locusts?  
**Answer:** The power of the scorpions of the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:3

---

### Revelation 9:4

**Q1.** What were the locusts commanded not to hurt?  
**Answer:** The grass of the earth, neither any green thing, neither any tree.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:4

**Q2.** Who were the locusts commanded to hurt instead?  
**Answer:** Only those men which have not the seal of God in their foreheads.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:4

**Q3.** Where must the seal of God be for men not to be hurt?  
**Answer:** In their foreheads.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:4

---

### Revelation 9:5

**Q1.** What were the locusts not permitted to do to men?  
**Answer:** Kill them.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:5

**Q2.** How long should men be tormented?  
**Answer:** Five months.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:5

**Q3.** What was the torment like?  
**Answer:** The torment of a scorpion, when he striketh a man.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:5

---

### Revelation 9:6

**Q1.** What shall men seek in those days?  
**Answer:** Death.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:6

**Q2. What shall men not find?**  
**Answer:** Death.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:6

**Q3.** What shall men desire?  
**Answer:** To die.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:6

---

### Revelation 9:7

**Q1.** What were the shapes of the locusts like?  
**Answer:** Horses prepared unto battle.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:7

**Q2.** What was on their heads like crowns?  
**Answer:** Crowns like gold.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:7

**Q3.** What were their faces like?  
**Answer:** The faces of men.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:7

---

### Revelation 9:8

**Q1.** What hair did they have?  
**Answer:** The hair of women.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:8

**Q2.** Whose teeth were like their teeth?  
**Answer:** The teeth of lions.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:8

**Q3.** What animals were their teeth compared to?  
**Answer:** Lions.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:8

---

### Revelation 9:9

**Q1.** What breastplates did they have?  
**Answer:** Breastplates of iron.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:9

**Q2.** What was the sound of their wings like?  
**Answer:** The sound of chariots of many horses running to battle.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:9

**Q3.** Whose running to battle did their wings sound like?  
**Answer:** Many horses running to battle.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:9

---

### Revelation 9:10

**Q1.** What did they have like unto scorpions?  
**Answer:** Tails.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:10

**Q2.** What was in their tails?  
**Answer:** Stings.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:10

**Q3.** What was their power to do?  
**Answer:** Hurt men five months.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:10

---

### Revelation 9:11

**Q1.** Who had a king over them?  
**Answer:** The locusts.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:11

**Q2.** Who was the angel of the bottomless pit?  
**Answer:** The king over them.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:11

**Q3.** What is his name in the Hebrew tongue?  
**Answer:** Abaddon.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:11

---

### Revelation 9:12

**Q1.** What is past?  
**Answer:** One woe.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:12

**Q2.** How many woes come hereafter?  
**Answer:** Two woes more.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:12

**Q3.** When do the two woes come?  
**Answer:** Hereafter.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:12

---

### Revelation 9:13

**Q1.** Who sounded the sixth angel?  
**Answer:** The sixth angel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:13

**Q2.** What did I hear from the four horns?  
**Answer:** A voice.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:13

**Q3.** Where were the four horns located?  
**Answer:** Of the golden altar which is before God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:13

---

### Revelation 9:14

**Q1.** What did the voice say to the sixth angel?  
**Answer:** Loose the four angels which are bound in the great river Euphrates.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:14

**Q2.** Which angel was told to loose the four angels?  
**Answer:** The sixth angel which had the trumpet.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:14

**Q3.** Where were the four angels bound?  
**Answer:** In the great river Euphrates.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:14

---

### Revelation 9:15

**Q1.** Who were the four angels?  
**Answer:** Prepared for an hour, and a day, and a month, and a year.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:15

**Q2.** What were the four angels prepared to do?  
**Answer:** Slay the third part of men.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:15

**Q3.** For what specific time periods were they prepared?  
**Answer:** An hour, and a day, and a month, and a year.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:15

---

### Revelation 9:16

**Q1.** What was the number of the army of the horsemen?  
**Answer:** Two hundred thousand thousand.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:16

**Q2.** What did the speaker hear?  
**Answer:** The number of them.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:16

**Q3.** What group of people made up this army?  
**Answer:** Horsemen.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:16

---

### Revelation 9:17

**Q1.** How did I see the horses in the vision?  
**Answer:** In the vision.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:17

**Q2.** What were the breastplates of them that sat on them?  
**Answer:** Firekind, and of jacinth, and brimstone.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:17

**Q3.** What did the heads of the horses resemble?  
**Answer:** The heads of lions.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:17

---

### Revelation 9:18

**Q1.** What three things issued out of their mouths?  
**Answer:** Fire, and smoke, and brimstone.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:18

**Q2.** What fraction of men was killed by these three things?  
**Answer:** The third part of men.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:18

**Q3.** Out of what body part did the fire, smoke, and brimstone issue?  
**Answer:** Their mouths.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:18

---

### Revelation 9:19

**Q1.** Where was the power of the horses?  
**Answer:** In their mouth, and in their tails.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:19

**Q2.** What were their tails like?  
**Answer:** Serpents.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:19

**Q3.** What did their tails have to hurt with?  
**Answer:** Heads.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:19

---

### Revelation 9:20

**Q1.** What did the rest of the men which were not killed by these plagues not repent of?  
**Answer:** The works of their hands.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:20

**Q2.** What did they not worship?  
**Answer:** Devils, and idols of gold, and silver, and brass, and stone, and of wood.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:20

**Q3.** What can idols neither see nor do?  
**Answer:** Neither can see, nor hear, nor walk.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:20

---

### Revelation 9:21

**Q1.** What did they not repent of regarding their murders?  
**Answer:** Their murders.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:21

**Q2.** What did they not repent of regarding their sorceries?  
**Answer:** Their sorceries.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:21

**Q3.** What did they not repent of regarding their fornication and thefts?  
**Answer:** Their fornication, nor of their thefts.  
**Verse:** Revelation 9:21
