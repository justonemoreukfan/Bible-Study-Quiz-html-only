# Revelation 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 6:1

**Q1. What did the Lamb open when the first of the seals was opened?**  
**Answer:** One of the seals.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:1

**Q2. What did the author hear as it were the noise of thunder?**  
**Answer:** One of the four beasts.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:1

**Q3. What did the beast say with a voice like thunder?**  
**Answer:** Come and see.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:1

---

### Revelation 6:2

**Q1. What did the author behold after being told to come and see?**  
**Answer:** A white horse.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:2

**Q2. What was held by him that sat on the white horse?**  
**Answer:** A bow.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:2

**Q3. What was given unto him that went forth conquering, and to conquer?**  
**Answer:** A crown.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:2

---

### Revelation 6:3

**Q1. What did the Lamb open when the second seal was opened?**  
**Answer:** The second seal.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:3

**Q2. Who did the author hear say, "Come and see"?**  
**Answer:** The second beast.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:3

**Q3. What was the second beast doing when it spoke?**  
**Answer:** Saying, Come and see.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:3

---

### Revelation 6:4

**Q1. What went out that was another horse?**  
**Answer:** A red horse.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:4

**Q2. What was given to him that sat thereon regarding peace?**  
**Answer:** Power to take peace from the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:4

**Q3. What was given unto him that sat on the red horse?**  
**Answer:** A great sword.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:4

---

### Revelation 6:5

**Q1. What did the Lamb open when the third seal was opened?**  
**Answer:** The third seal.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:5

**Q2. Who did the author hear say, "Come and see"?**  
**Answer:** The third beast.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:5

**Q3. What kind of horse did the author behold when the third seal was opened?**  
**Answer:** A black horse.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:5

---

### Revelation 6:6

**Q1. What was heard in the midst of the four beasts?**  
**Answer:** A voice.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:6

**Q2. How much corn was specified for a penny?**  
**Answer:** A measure of wheat for a penny, and three measures of barley for a penny.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:6

**Q3. What substances were commanded not to be hurt?**  
**Answer:** The oil and the wine.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:6

---

### Revelation 6:7

**Q1. What did the Lamb open when the fourth seal was opened?**  
**Answer:** The fourth seal.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:7

**Q2. Whose voice did the author hear when the fourth seal was opened?**  
**Answer:** The voice of the fourth beast.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:7

**Q3. What did the voice of the fourth beast say?**  
**Answer:** Come and see.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:7

---

### Revelation 6:8

**Q1. What color was the horse that the author looked and beheld?**  
**Answer:** A pale horse.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:8

**Q2. What was the name of him that sat on the pale horse?**  
**Answer:** Death.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:8

**Q3. What followed with him that sat on the pale horse?**  
**Answer:** Hell.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:8

---

### Revelation 6:9

**Q1. What did the Lamb open when the fifth seal was opened?**  
**Answer:** The fifth seal.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:9

**Q2. What did the author see under the altar?**  
**Answer:** The souls of them that were slain for the word of God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:9

**Q3. For what reason were the souls slain under the altar?**  
**Answer:** For the testimony which they held.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:9

---

### Revelation 6:10

**Q1. With what kind of voice did they cry?**  
**Answer:** With a loud voice.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:10

**Q2. How did they address the Lord?**  
**Answer:** O Lord, holy and true.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:10

**Q3. What did they ask the Lord not to do regarding their blood?**  
**Answer:** Dost thou not judge and avenge our blood on them that dwell on the earth?  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:10

---

### Revelation 6:11

**Q1. What was given unto every one of them?**  
**Answer:** White robes.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:11

**Q2. What were they told to do for a little season?**  
**Answer:** Rest yet for a little season.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:11

**Q3. Until when were they told to rest?**  
**Answer:** Until their fellowservants also and their brethren, that should be killed as they were, should be fulfilled.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:11

---

### Revelation 6:12

**Q1. What did the Lamb open when he had opened the sixth seal?**  
**Answer:** Behold, there was a great earthquake.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:12

**Q2. What happened to the sun when the sixth seal was opened?**  
**Answer:** The sun became black as sackcloth of hair.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:12

**Q3. What did the moon become like?**  
**Answer:** As blood.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:12

---

### Revelation 6:13

**Q1. Where did the stars of heaven fall unto?**  
**Answer:** Unto the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:13

**Q2. What tree is used to describe the falling of the untimely figs?**  
**Answer:** A fig tree.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:13

**Q3. By what wind are the untimely figs shaken?**  
**Answer:** A mighty wind.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:13

---

### Revelation 6:14

**Q1. How did the heaven depart as a scroll?**  
**Answer:** As a scroll when it is rolled together.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:14

**Q2. What happened to every mountain?**  
**Answer:** Were moved out of their places.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:14

**Q3. What happened to every island?**  
**Answer:** Were moved out of their places.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:14

---

### Revelation 6:15

**Q1. Who hid themselves in the dens and in the rocks of the mountains?**  
**Answer:** The kings of the earth, and the great men, and the rich men, and the chief captains, and the mighty men, and every bondman, and every free man.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:15

**Q2. Where did the kings and great men hide themselves?**  
**Answer:** In the dens and in the rocks of the mountains.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:15

**Q3. Who, besides the kings and great men, hid themselves in the dens and rocks?**  
**Answer:** Every bondman, and every free man.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:15

---

### Revelation 6:16

**Q1. What did they say to the mountains and rocks?**  
**Answer:** Fall on us.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:16

**Q2. Whose face did they want to hide from?**  
**Answer:** From the face of him that sitteth on the throne.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:16

**Q3. Whose wrath did they want to hide from?**  
**Answer:** From the wrath of the Lamb.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:16

---

### Revelation 6:17

**Q1. What has come regarding the great day of his wrath?**  
**Answer:** The great day of his wrath is come.  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:17

**Q2. Who is able to stand in the great day of his wrath?**  
**Answer:** Who shall be able to stand?  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:17

**Q3. Whose wrath is referenced in the final verse of the chapter?**  
**Answer:** His wrath (the wrath of the Lamb).  
**Verse:** Revelation 6:17
