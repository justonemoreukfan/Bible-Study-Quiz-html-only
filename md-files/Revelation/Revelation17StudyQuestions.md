# Revelation 17 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 17:1

**Q1. Who talked with the person addressed in this verse?**  
**Answer:** One of the seven angels which had the seven vials.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:1

**Q2. What did the angel say he would show unto the reader?**  
**Answer:** The judgment of the great whore that sitteth upon many waters.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:1

**Q3. Where is the great whore described as sitting?**  
**Answer:** Upon many waters.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:1

---

### Revelation 17:2

**Q1. Who has committed fornication with the great whore?**  
**Answer:** The kings of the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:2

**Q2. Who has been made drunk with the wine of her fornication?**  
**Answer:** The inhabitants of the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:2

**Q3. What kind of wine is mentioned in connection with her fornication?**  
**Answer:** The wine of her fornication.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:2

---

### Revelation 17:3

**Q1. Where did the angel carry the person away in the spirit?**  
**Answer:** Into the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:3

**Q2. What did the woman sit upon?**  
**Answer:** A scarlet coloured beast.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:3

**Q3. What was the beast full of?**  
**Answer:** Names of blasphemy.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:3

---

### Revelation 17:4

**Q1. How was the woman arrayed?**  
**Answer:** In purple and scarlet colour.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:4

**Q2. With what was the woman decked?**  
**Answer:** Gold and precious stones and pearls.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:4

**Q3. What was in her hand?**  
**Answer:** A golden cup full of abominations and filthiness of her fornication.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:4

---

### Revelation 17:5

**Q1. What was written upon her forehead?**  
**Answer:** A name written, MYSTERY, BABYLON THE GREAT, THE MOTHER OF HARLOTS AND ABOMINATIONS OF THE EARTH.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:5

**Q2. What is the mother of harlots and abominations called in her name?**  
**Answer:** Babylon the great.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:5

**Q3. Where was the name written?**  
**Answer:** Upon her forehead.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:5

---

### Revelation 17:6

**Q1. With what was the woman drunken?**  
**Answer:** With the blood of the saints, and with the blood of the martyrs of Jesus.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:6

**Q2. How did the person react when he saw the woman?**  
**Answer:** With great admiration.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:6

**Q3. Whose martyrs were the ones whose blood she was drunken with?**  
**Answer:** The martyrs of Jesus.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:6

---

### Revelation 17:7

**Q1. What did the angel ask why he did?**  
**Answer:** Marvel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:7

**Q2. What would the angel tell the person?**  
**Answer:** The mystery of the woman, and of the beast that carrieth her, which hath the seven heads and ten horns.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:7

**Q3. How many heads and horns did the beast have?**  
**Answer:** Seven heads and ten horns.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:7

---

### Revelation 17:8

**Q1. What was the beast that the person saw?**  
**Answer:** Was, and is not; and shall ascend out of the bottomless pit, and go into perdition.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:8

**Q2. Who shall wonder when they behold the beast?**  
**Answer:** They that dwell on the earth shall wonder, whose names were not written in the book of life from the foundation of the world.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:8

**Q3. What state of the beast causes them to wonder when they see it was, is not, and yet is?**  
**Answer:** When they behold the beast that was, and is not, and yet is.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:8

---

### Revelation 17:9

**Q1. What is needed to understand this here?**  
**Answer:** The mind which hath wisdom.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:9

**Q2. What do the seven heads represent?**  
**Answer:** Seven mountains, on which the woman sitteth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:9

**Q3. Where does the woman sit in relation to the seven mountains?**  
**Answer:** On them.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:9

---

### Revelation 17:10

**Q1. How many kings are mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Seven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:10

**Q2. What happened to five of the kings?**  
**Answer:** Are fallen.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:10

**Q3. What happens when the other king comes?**  
**Answer:** He must continue a short space.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:10

---

### Revelation 17:11

**Q1. What is the beast that was, and is not?**  
**Answer:** He is the eighth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:11

**Q2. From whom is the eighth king?**  
**Answer:** Is of the seven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:11

**Q3. Where is the beast going?**  
**Answer:** Into perdition.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:11

---

### Revelation 17:12

**Q1. What do the ten horns represent?**  
**Answer:** Ten kings, which have received no kingdom as yet.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:12

**Q2. For how long do they receive power as kings with the beast?**  
**Answer:** One hour.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:12

**Q3. When do they receive power as kings?**  
**Answer:** As kings one hour with the beast.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:12

---

### Revelation 17:13

**Q1. What do these ten kings have in common?**  
**Answer:** One mind.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:13

**Q2. What do they give unto the beast?**  
**Answer:** Their power and strength.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:13

**Q3. What do they give their power and strength unto?**  
**Answer:** The beast.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:13

---

### Revelation 17:14

**Q1. Whom shall these kings make war with?**  
**Answer:** The Lamb.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:14

**Q2. Who shall overcome them?**  
**Answer:** The Lamb.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:14

**Q3. What titles are given to the Lamb in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Lord of lords, and King of kings.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:14

### Revelation 17:15

**Q1. What did the angel say the waters where the whore sitteth represent?**

**Verse:** Revelation 17:15

**Q2. Where does the whore sit?**  
**Answer:** Upon many waters.

### Revelation 17:16

**Q1. What shall the ten horns upon the beast do to the whore?**  
**Answer:** Shall hate the whore, and shall make her desolate and naked.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:16

**Q2. What shall they eat?**  
**Answer:** Her flesh.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:16

**Q3. What shall happen to the whore by fire?**  
**Answer:** Burn her with fire.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:16

---

### Revelation 17:17

**Q1. Who put it in their hearts to fulfill his will?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:17

**Q2. What kind of mind did they agree to?**  
**Answer:** To agree, and give their kingdom unto the beast.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:17

**Q3. Until when shall they give their kingdom unto the beast?**  
**Answer:** Until the words of God shall be fulfilled.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:17

### Revelation 17:18

**Q1. What does the woman represent?**  
**Answer:** That great city.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:18

**Q2. Over whom does the great city reign?**  
**Answer:** The kings of the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:18

**Q3. Who is the woman described as in this verse?**  
**Answer:** That great city, which reigneth over the kings of the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 17:18
