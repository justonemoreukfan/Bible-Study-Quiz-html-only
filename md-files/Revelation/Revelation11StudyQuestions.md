# Revelation 11 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 11:1

**Q1. What was given to the speaker like unto a rod?**  
**Answer:** A reed.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:1

**Q2. Who stood and said, Rise, and measure the temple of God, and the altar?**  
**Answer:** The angel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:1

**Q3. Who was to be measured by the one standing with the reed?**  
**Answer:** Them that worship therein.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:1

---

### Revelation 11:2

**Q4. What part of the temple did the speaker say to leave out, and measure it not?**  
**Answer:** The court which is without the temple.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:2

**Q5. To whom has the court which is without the temple been given?**  
**Answer:** The Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:2

**Q6. How long shall the holy city be trodden under foot by the Gentiles?**  
**Answer:** Forty and two months.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:2

---

### Revelation 11:3

**Q7. Whom will God give power unto during this time?**  
**Answer:** My two witnesses.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:3

**Q8. How long shall the two witnesses prophesy?**  
**Answer:** A thousand two hundred and threescore days.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:3

**Q9. How shall the two witnesses be clothed while prophesying?**  
**Answer:** Clothed in sackcloth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:3

---

### Revelation 11:4

**Q10. What are the two witnesses represented as, standing before the God of the earth?**  
**Answer:** The two olive trees, and the two candlesticks.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:4

**Q11. Before whom do the two olive trees and two candlesticks stand?**  
**Answer:** The God of the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:4

**Q12. How many olive trees and candlesticks are mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Two.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:4

---

### Revelation 11:5

**Q13. What will happen to anyone who desires to hurt the two witnesses?**  
**Answer:** Fire proceedeth out of their mouth, and devoureth their enemies.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:5

**Q14. What happens if any man will hurt the two witnesses?**  
**Answer:** He must in this manner be killed.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:5

**Q15. What comes out of the mouth of the witnesses to devour their enemies?**  
**Answer:** Fire.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:5

---

### Revelation 11:6

**Q16. What power do these witnesses have over heaven during the days of their prophecy?**  
**Answer:** Power to shut heaven, that it rain not.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:6

**Q17. What power do they have over waters?**  
**Answer:** Power over waters to turn them to blood.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:6

**Q18. How often do they have power to smite the earth with plagues?**  
**Answer:** As often as they will.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:6

---

### Revelation 11:7

**Q19. What will the beast that ascendeth out of the bottomless pit do when they shall have finished their testimony?**  
**Answer:** Make war against them, and shall overcome them, and kill them.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:7

**Q20. From where does the beast ascend that makes war against them?**  
**Answer:** Out of the bottomless pit.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:7

**Q21. What will the beast do to the two witnesses after making war against them?**  
**Answer:** Overcome them, and kill them.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:7

---

### Revelation 11:8

**Q22. Where shall their dead bodies lie?**  
**Answer:** In the street of the great city.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:8

**Q23. By what spiritual names is the great city spiritually called?**  
**Answer:** Sodom and Egypt.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:8

**Q24. Whose Lord was crucified in this great city?**  
**Answer:** Our Lord.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:8

---

### Revelation 11:9

**Q25. Who shall see their dead bodies out of the people and kindreds and tongues and nations?**  
**Answer:** Some of people and kindreds and tongues and nations.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:9

**Q26. How long shall their dead bodies be seen by people?**  
**Answer:** Three days and an half.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:9

**Q27. What will they not suffer to be put in graves?**  
**Answer:** Their dead bodies.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:9

---

### Revelation 11:10

**Q28. Who shall dwell upon the earth and rejoice over them and make merry?**  
**Answer:** They that dwell upon the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:10

**Q29. What will those dwelling upon the earth send to one another because of the prophets' death?**  
**Answer:** Gifts.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:10

**Q30. Why did they rejoice and send gifts to one another?**  
**Answer:** Because these two prophets tormented them that dwelt on the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:10

---

### Revelation 11:11

**Q31. After how many days did the Spirit of life from God enter into them?**  
**Answer:** After three days and an half.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:11

**Q32. How did they stand upon their feet after the Spirit of life entered them?**  
**Answer:** Upon their feet.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:11

**Q33. What fell upon them which saw the witnesses stand up?**  
**Answer:** Great fear.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:11

---

### Revelation 11:12

**Q34. What great voice did they hear from heaven saying unto them?**  
**Answer:** Come up hither.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:12

**Q35. Where did the two witnesses ascend up to in a cloud?**  
**Answer:** To heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:12

**Q36. Who beheld them ascending up to heaven?**  
**Answer:** Their enemies.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:12

---

### Revelation 11:13

**Q37. What took place in the same hour as the ascent?**  
**Answer:** A great earthquake.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:13

**Q38. What portion of the city fell in the earthquake?**  
**Answer:** The tenth part of the city.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:13

**Q39. How many men were slain in the earthquake, and who were affrighted and gave glory to the God of heaven?**  
**Answer:** Seven thousand men of names; and the remnant were affrighted, and gave glory to the God of heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:13

---

### Revelation 11:14

**Q40. What woe is past according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** The second woe.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:14

**Q41. What comes quickly after the second woe?**  
**Answer:** The third woe.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:14

**Q42. Which woe is described as coming quickly?**  
**Answer:** The third woe.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:14

---

### Revelation 11:15

**Q43. Who sounded the seventh angel?**  
**Answer:** The seventh angel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:15

**Q44. What great voices were heard in heaven after the seventh angel sounded?**  
**Answer:** The kingdoms of this world are become the kingdoms of our Lord, and of his Christ.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:15

**Q45. For how long shall He reign?**  
**Answer:** For ever and ever.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:15

---

### Revelation 11:16

**Q46. Who sat before God on their seats?**  
**Answer:** The four and twenty elders.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:16

**Q47. What did the four and twenty elders do when they sat before God?**  
**Answer:** Fell upon their faces, and worshipped God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:16

**Q48. How many elders sat before God on their seats?**  
**Answer:** Four and twenty.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:16

---

### Revelation 11:17

**Q49. Whom did the elders give thanks to, calling Him Almighty?**  
**Answer:** O Lord God Almighty.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:17

**Q50. What two time descriptions are used for God who is and was to come?**  
**Answer:** Which art, and wast, and art to come.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:17

**Q51. What has God taken to Himself and reigned?**  
**Answer:** Thy great power.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:17

---

### Revelation 11:18

**Q52. What were the nations angry with, according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Thy wrath is come.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:18

**Q53. What time has come for the dead, that they should be judged?**  
**Answer:** The time of the dead, that they should be judged.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:18

**Q54. To whom should God give reward among His servants and saints?**  
**Answer:** Unto thy servants the prophets, and to the saints, and them that fear thy name, small and great.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:18

---

### Revelation 11:19

**Q55. What was opened in heaven?**  
**Answer:** The temple of God was opened in heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:19

**Q56. What was seen in His temple?**  
**Answer:** The ark of his testament.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:19

**Q57. What accompanied the lightnings, voices, thunderings, earthquake, and great hail?**  
**Answer:** Lightnings, and voices, and thunderings, and an earthquake, and great hail.  
**Verse:** Revelation 11:19
