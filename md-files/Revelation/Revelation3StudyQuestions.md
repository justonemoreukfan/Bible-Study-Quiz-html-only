# Revelation 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 3:1

**Q1. What is the angel of the church in Sardis said to have?**  
**Answer:** The seven Spirits of God, and the seven stars.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:1

**Q2. What does He know regarding their works?**  
**Answer:** That thou hast a name that thou livest, and art dead.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:1

**Q3. What is the state of their works before God?**  
**Answer:** Not found perfect before God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:1

---

### Revelation 3:2

**Q4. What should the church in Sardis do?**  
**Answer:** Be watchful.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:2

**Q5. What should they strengthen concerning the things that remain?**  
**Answer:** The things which remain, that are ready to die.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:2

**Q6. What has He not found regarding their works?**  
**Answer:** Thy works perfect before God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:2

---

### Revelation 3:3

**Q7. What should they remember and hold fast?**  
**Answer:** How thou hast received and heard.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:3

**Q8. What action should they take after remembering?**  
**Answer:** Hold fast, and repent.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:3

**Q9. What will happen if they do not watch?**  
**Answer:** I will come on thee as a thief, and thou shalt not know what hour I will come upon thee.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:3

---

### Revelation 3:4

**Q10. Who does He mention that have not defiled their garments in Sardis?**  
**Answer:** A few names even in Sardis.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:4

**Q11. With whom shall these few walk?**  
**Answer:** With me.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:4

**Q12. In what attire shall they walk with Him?**  
**Answer:** In white: for they are worthy.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:4

---

### Revelation 3:5

**Q13. How shall he that overcometh be clothed?**  
**Answer:** In white raiment.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:5

**Q14. What will He not blot out of the book of life?**  
**Answer:** His name.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:5

**Q15. Before whom will He confess his name?**  
**Answer:** Before my Father, and before his angels.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:5

---

### Revelation 3:6

**Q16. Who has an ear?**  
**Answer:** He that hath an ear.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:6

**Q17. What should he hear?**  
**Answer:** Let him hear.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:6

**Q18. To whom is this said?**  
**Answer:** What the Spirit saith unto the churches.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:6

---

### Revelation 3:7

**Q19. To the angel of what church is this written?**  
**Answer:** The church in Philadelphia.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:7

**Q20. How is He described who is holy and true?**  
**Answer:** He that hath the key of David.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:7

**Q21. What does He do regarding doors?**  
**Answer:** He that openeth, and no man shutteth; and shutteth, and no man openeth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:7

---

### Revelation 3:8

**Q22. What does He know about them?**  
**Answer:** Thy works.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:8

**Q23. What has He set before them?**  
**Answer:** An open door.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:8

**Q24. What can no man do to this open door?**  
**Answer:** Shut it.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:8

---

### Revelation 3:9

**Q25. Who does He say belong to the synagogue of Satan?**  
**Answer:** Them which say they are Jews, and are not, but do lie.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:9

**Q26. What will He make them do before their feet?**  
**Answer:** To come and worship.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:9

**Q27. What will they know regarding His feeling toward Philadelphia?**  
**Answer:** That I have loved thee.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:9

---

### Revelation 3:10

**Q28. What word of His patience has been kept?**  
**Answer:** The word of my patience.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:10

**Q29. From what will He keep them?**  
**Answer:** The hour of temptation.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:10

**Q30. Upon whom shall the hour of temptation come?**  
**Answer:** Upon all the world, to try them that dwell upon the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:10

---

### Revelation 3:11

**Q31. How quickly is He coming?**  
**Answer:** Behold, I come quickly.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:11

**Q32. What should they hold fast?**  
**Answer:** That which thou hast.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:11

**Q33. What should no man take?**  
**Answer:** Thy crown.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:11

---

### Revelation 3:12

**Q34. Where will He make an overcomer a pillar?**  
**Answer:** In the temple of my God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:12

**Q35. What will go no more out?**  
**Answer:** He shall go no more out.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:12

**Q36. What names will be written upon him?**  
**Answer:** The name of my God, and the name of the city of my God, which is new Jerusalem, and my new name.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:12

---

### Revelation 3:13

**Q37. Who has an ear in this verse?**  
**Answer:** He that hath an ear.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:13

**Q38. What should he do?**  
**Answer:** Let him hear.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:13

**Q39. What is he listening to?**  
**Answer:** What the Spirit saith unto the churches.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:13

---

### Revelation 3:14

**Q40. To the angel of what church is this written?**  
**Answer:** The church of the Laodiceans.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:14

**Q41. Who speaks these things as the Amen?**  
**Answer:** The faithful and true witness.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:14

**Q42. What is He called regarding the creation of God?**  
**Answer:** The beginning of the creation of God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:14

---

### Revelation 3:15

**Q43. What does He know about their works?**  
**Answer:** That thou art neither cold nor hot.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:15

**Q44. What does He wish they were?**  
**Answer:** Cold or hot.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:15

---

### Revelation 3:16

**Q45. How are they described because they are lukewarm?**  
**Answer:** Neither cold nor hot.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:16

---

### Revelation 3:16 (Continued)

**Q46. What will He do because they are lukewarm?**  
**Answer:** I will spew thee out of my mouth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:16

---

### Revelation 3:17

**Q47. What do they say about themselves?**  
**Answer:** I am rich, and increased with goods, and have need of nothing.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:17

---

### Revelation 3:17 (Continued)

**Q48. What do they not know about their actual condition?**  
**Answer:** That thou art wretched, and miserable, and poor, and blind, and naked.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:17

---

### Revelation 3:18

**Q49. What does He counsel them to buy of Him?**  
**Answer:** Gold tried in the fire.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:18

---

### Revelation 3:18 (Continued)

**Q50. What else does He counsel them to buy for clothing?**  
**Answer:** White raiment.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:18

---

### Revelation 3:18 (Continued)

**Q51. What should they anoint their eyes with?**  
**Answer:** Eye salve.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:18

---

### Revelation 3:19

**Q52. What does He do to as many as He loves?**  
**Answer:** I rebuke and chasten.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:19

---

### Revelation 3:19 (Continued)

**Q53. Therefore, what should they be and do?**  
**Answer:** Be zealous therefore, and repent.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:19

---

### Revelation 3:20

**Q54. Where does He stand and knock?**  
**Answer:** At the door.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:20

---

### Revelation 3:20 (Continued)

**Q55. What will He do if any man hears His voice and opens the door?**  
**Answer:** I will come in to him, and will sup with him, and he with me.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:20

---

### Revelation 3:21

**Q56. Where will He grant an overcomer to sit?**  
**Answer:** With me in my throne.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:21

---

### Revelation 3:21 (Continued)

**Q57. How did He Himself overcome and sit down?**  
**Answer:** Even as I also overcame, and am set down with my Father in his throne.  
**Verse:** Revelation 3:21
