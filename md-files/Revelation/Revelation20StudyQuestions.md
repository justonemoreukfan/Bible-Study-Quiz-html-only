# Revelation 20 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 20:1

**Q1. Who came down from heaven?**  
**Answer:** An angel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:1

**Q2. What did the angel have in his hand?**  
**Answer:** The key of the bottomless pit and a great chain.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:1

**Q3. Where did the key and the great chain lead to?**  
**Answer:** The bottomless pit.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:1

---

### Revelation 20:2

**Q1. Who was laid hold of by the angel?**  
**Answer:** The dragon, that old serpent, which is the Devil, and Satan.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:2

**Q2. For how long was he bound?**  
**Answer:** A thousand years.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:2

**Q3. What was done to the dragon?**  
**Answer:** He was bound.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:2

---

### Revelation 20:3

**Q1. Where was he cast?**  
**Answer:** Into the bottomless pit.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:3

**Q2. What was set upon him to keep him from deceiving the nations any more?**  
**Answer:** A shut and sealed door (shut him up, and set a seal upon him).  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:3

**Q3. After what period of time must he be loosed a little season?**  
**Answer:** A thousand years.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:3

---

### Revelation 20:4

**Q1. Who sat upon the thrones?**  
**Answer:** They that sat upon them, and judgment was given unto them.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:4

**Q2. Whose souls were beheaded for the witness of Jesus and for the word of God?**  
**Answer:** The souls of them that were beheaded for the witness of Jesus, and for the word of God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:4

**Q3. With whom did they live and reign for a thousand years?**  
**Answer:** Christ.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:4

---

### Revelation 20:5

**Q1. Who lived not again until the thousand years were finished?**  
**Answer:** The rest of the dead.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:5

**Q2. What is this called?**  
**Answer:** The first resurrection.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:5

**Q3. When were the rest of the dead to live again?**  
**Answer:** Until the thousand years were finished.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:5

---

### Revelation 20:6

**Q1. Who is blessed and holy?**  
**Answer:** He that hath part in the first resurrection.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:6

**Q2. What power does the second death not have over such?**  
**Answer:** Power.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:6

**Q3. As what shall they be priests of God and of Christ?**  
**Answer:** Priests of God and of Christ, and shall reign with him a thousand years.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:6

---

### Revelation 20:7

**Q1. When the thousand years are expired, what shall happen to Satan?**  
**Answer:** He shall be loosed out of his prison.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:7

---

### Revelation 20:8

**Q1. What will Satan go out to do to the nations which are in the four quarters of the earth?**  
**Answer:** Deceive them.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:8

---

### Revelation 20:9

**Q1. What did they go up on the breadth of the earth and compass about?**  
**Answer:** The camp of the saints and the beloved city.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:9

**Q2. What came down from God out of heaven and devoured them?**  
**Answer:** Fire.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:9

---

### Revelation 20:10

**Q1. Where was the devil that deceived them cast?**  
**Answer:** Into the lake of fire and brimstone.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:10

**Q2. Who else was already in the lake of fire and brimstone?**  
**Answer:** The beast and the false prophet.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:10

**Q3. How long shall they be tormented day and night?**  
**Answer:** For ever and ever.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:10

---

### Revelation 20:11

**Q1. What did I see?**  
**Answer:** A great white throne, and him that sat on it.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:11

**Q2. From whose face fled the earth and the heaven?**  
**Answer:** From his face.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:11

**Q3. Was there found any place for them?**  
**Answer:** No place was found for them.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:11

---

### Revelation 20:12

**Q1. Who stood before God?**  
**Answer:** The dead, small and great.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:12

**Q2. What books were opened?**  
**Answer:** Books, and another book was opened, which is the book of life.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:12

**Q3. According to what were the dead judged?**  
**Answer:** According to their works written in the books.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:12

---

### Revelation 20:13

**Q1. Who delivered up the dead which were in them?**  
**Answer:** The sea, and death and hell.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:13

**Q2. How were they judged?**  
**Answer:** Every man according to their works.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:13

---

### Revelation 20:14

**Q1. Where were death and hell cast?**  
**Answer:** Into the lake of fire.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:14

**Q2. What is the lake of fire called?**  
**Answer:** The second death.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:14

---

### Revelation 20:15

**Q1. If anyone was not found written in what book, where were they cast?**  
**Answer:** The book of life; into the lake of fire.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:15

**Q2. What book must a person's name be found written in to avoid the lake of fire?**  
**Answer:** The book of life.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:15

**Q3. Where were those whose names were not in the book of life cast?**  
**Answer:** Into the lake of fire.  
**Verse:** Revelation 20:15
