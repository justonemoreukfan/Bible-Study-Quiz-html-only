# Revelation 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 8:1

**Q1. What did the Lamb open when the seventh seal was opened?**  
**Answer:** The seventh seal.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:1

**Q2. What happened in heaven when the seventh seal was opened?**  
**Answer:** There was silence.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:1

**Q3. For about how long was there silence in heaven?**  
**Answer:** The space of half an hour.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:1

---

### Revelation 8:2

**Q1. Who did the writer see standing before God?**  
**Answer:** The seven angels.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:2

**Q2. What was given to the seven angels?**  
**Answer:** Seven trumpets.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:2

---

### Revelation 8:3

**Q1. Who stood at the altar having a golden censer?**  
**Answer:** Another angel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:3

**Q2. What was given unto the angel to offer much incense?**  
**Answer:** Many prayers.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:3

**Q3. Upon what was the angel to offer the prayers with much incense?**  
**Answer:** Upon the golden altar which was before the throne.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:3

---

### Revelation 8:4

**Q1. What ascended up before God out of the angel's hand?**  
**Answer:** The smoke of the incense.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:4

**Q2. Along with the smoke of the incense, what else ascended up before God?**  
**Answer:** The prayers of the saints.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:4

---

### Revelation 8:5

**Q1. What did the angel take and fill with fire of the altar?**  
**Answer:** The censer.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:5

**Q2. Where did the angel cast the fire from the censer?**  
**Answer:** Into the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:5

**Q3. What accompanied the casting of the fire into the earth?**  
**Answer:** Voices, and thunderings, and lightnings, and an earthquake.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:5

---

### Revelation 8:6

**Q1. How many angels had the seven trumpets?**  
**Answer:** The seven angels.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:6

**Q2. What did the seven angels prepare themselves to do?**  
**Answer:** To sound.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:6

---

### Revelation 8:7

**Q1. Which angel sounded first?**  
**Answer:** The first angel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:7

**Q2. What followed the sounding of the first angel?**  
**Answer:** Hail and fire mingled with blood.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:7

**Q3. What was burned up as this mixture was cast upon the earth?**  
**Answer:** The third part of trees was burnt up, and all green grass was burnt up.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:7

---

### Revelation 8:8

**Q1. Which angel sounded second?**  
**Answer:** The second angel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:8

**Q2. What was cast into the sea when the second angel sounded?**  
**Answer:** As it were a great mountain burning with fire.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:8

**Q3. What did the third part of the sea become?**  
**Answer:** Blood.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:8

---

### Revelation 8:9

**Q1. What happened to the creatures in the sea that had life?**  
**Answer:** The third part died.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:9

**Q2. What happened to the ships in the sea?**  
**Answer:** The third part of the ships were destroyed.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:9

---

### Revelation 8:10

**Q1. Which angel sounded third?**  
**Answer:** The third angel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:10

**Q2. What fell from heaven when the third angel sounded?**  
**Answer:** A great star burning as it were a lamp.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:10

**Q3. Upon what did the great star fall?**  
**Answer:** Upon the third part of the rivers, and upon the fountains of waters.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:10

---

### Revelation 8:11

**Q1. What was the name of the star?**  
**Answer:** Wormwood.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:11

**Q2. What did the third part of the waters become?**  
**Answer:** Wormwood.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:11

**Q3. What happened to many men because the waters were made bitter?**  
**Answer:** They died.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:11

---

### Revelation 8:12

**Q1. Which angel sounded fourth?**  
**Answer:** The fourth angel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:12

**Q2. What was smitten when the fourth angel sounded?**  
**Answer:** The third part of the sun, and the third part of the moon, and the third part of the stars.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:12

**Q3. What was darkened by this smiting?**  
**Answer:** The third part of them, and the day shone not for a third part of it, and the night likewise.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:12

---

### Revelation 8:13

**Q1. What did the writer behold and hear flying through the midst of heaven?**  
**Answer:** An angel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:13

**Q2. With what voice did the angel cry?**  
**Answer:** A loud voice.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:13

**Q3. What three words did the angel say concerning the inhabitants of the earth by reason of the other voices of the trumpet?**  
**Answer:** Woe, woe, woe.  
**Verse:** Revelation 8:13
