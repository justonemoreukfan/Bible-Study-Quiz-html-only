# Revelation 14 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 14:1

**Q1. What did the Lamb stand on?**  
**Answer:** The mount Sion.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:1

**Q2. Who stood with the Lamb on the mount Sion?**  
**Answer:** An hundred forty and four thousand.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:1

**Q3. What was written in their foreheads?**  
**Answer:** His Father's name.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:1

---

### Revelation 14:2

**Q1. What did the voice from heaven sound like?**  
**Answer:** As the voice of many waters, and as the voice of a great thunder.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:2

**Q2. What did the voice I heard sound like besides many waters and thunder?**  
**Answer:** As the voice of harpers harping with their harps.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:2

**Q3. Where did the voice come from?**  
**Answer:** From heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:2

---

### Revelation 14:3

**Q1. What did they sing before the throne?**  
**Answer:** As it were a new song.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:3

**Q2. Who could learn that song besides the hundred and forty and four thousand?**  
**Answer:** No man.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:3

**Q3. From where were the hundred and forty and four thousand redeemed?**  
**Answer:** From the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:3

---

### Revelation 14:4

**Q1. With whom were these not defiled?**  
**Answer:** With women.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:4

**Q2. What are these persons described as?**  
**Answer:** Virgins.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:4

**Q3. Whithersoever the Lamb goeth, what do they do?**  
**Answer:** They follow the Lamb.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:4

---

### Revelation 14:5

**Q1. What was not found in their mouth?**  
**Answer:** Guile.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:5

**Q2. What condition are they in before the throne of God?**  
**Answer:** Without fault.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:5

**Q3. What was not found?**  
**Answer:** Guile.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:5

---

### Revelation 14:6

**Q1. Where did I see another angel fly?**  
**Answer:** In the midst of heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:6

**Q2. What did the angel have to preach?**  
**Answer:** The everlasting gospel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:6

**Q3. To whom was the everlasting gospel to be preached?**  
**Answer:** Unto them that dwell on the earth, and to every nation, and kindred, and tongue, and people.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:6

---

### Revelation 14:7

**Q1. What did the angel say with a loud voice?**  
**Answer:** Fear God, and give glory to him.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:7

**Q2. Whose hour of judgment is come?**  
**Answer:** The hour of his judgment.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:7

**Q3. What should men worship according to the angel?**  
**Answer:** Him that made heaven, and earth, and the sea, and the fountains of waters.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:7

---

### Revelation 14:8

**Q1. What did the second angel follow saying?**  
**Answer:** Babylon is fallen, is fallen, that great city.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:8

**Q2. What did Babylon make all nations drink?**  
**Answer:** The wine of the wrath of her fornication.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:8

**Q3. What did the angel call Babylon?**  
**Answer:** That great city.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:8

---

### Revelation 14:9

**Q1. Who followed the third angel?**  
**Answer:** Saying with a loud voice.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:9

**Q2. What happens to any man who worships the beast and his image?**  
**Answer:** The same shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:9

**Q3. Where might the mark be received?**  
**Answer:** In his forehead, or in his hand.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:9

---

### Revelation 14:10

**Q1. What shall the same drink?**  
**Answer:** Of the wine of the wrath of God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:10

**Q2. How is the wine poured out?**  
**Answer:** Poured out without mixture into the cup of his indignation.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:10

**Q3. With what shall he be tormented?**  
**Answer:** With fire and brimstone.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:10

---

### Revelation 14:11

**Q1. What ascends up for ever and ever?**  
**Answer:** The smoke of their torment.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:11

**Q2. Who have no rest day nor night?**  
**Answer:** They who worship the beast and his image, and whosoever receiveth the mark of his name.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:11

**Q3. What is not had day nor night?**  
**Answer:** Rest.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:11

---

### Revelation 14:12

**Q1. What is the patience of the saints here?**  
**Answer:** Here is the patience of the saints.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:12

**Q2. What do those who keep the commandments of God also keep?**  
**Answer:** The faith of Jesus.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:12

**Q3. What do the saints keep?**  
**Answer:** The commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:12

---

### Revelation 14:13

**Q1. What did I hear from heaven saying unto me?**  
**Answer:** Write, Blessed are the dead which die in the Lord from henceforth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:13

**Q2. What does the Spirit say the dead may do?**  
**Answer:** Rest from their labours.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:13

**Q3. What follows with them?**  
**Answer:** Their works.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:13

---

### Revelation 14:14

**Q1. What did I look and behold upon the white cloud?**  
**Answer:** One sat like unto the Son of man.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:14

**Q2. What was on the head of the One sitting on the cloud?**  
**Answer:** A golden crown.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:14

**Q3. What was in his hand?**  
**Answer:** A sharp sickle.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:14

---

### Revelation 14:15

**Q1. Where did another angel come out of?**  
**Answer:** The temple.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:15

**Q2. How did the angel cry to him that sat on the cloud?**  
**Answer:** With a loud voice.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:15

**Q3. Why was the angel telling him to thrust in his sickle and reap?**  
**Answer:** For the time is come for thee to reap; for the harvest of the earth is ripe.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:15

---

### Revelation 14:16

**Q1. Who sat on the cloud?**  
**Answer:** He that sat on the cloud.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:16

**Q2. What did he thrust on the earth?**  
**Answer:** His sickle.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:16

**Q3. What happened to the earth after he thrust in his sickle?**  
**Answer:** The earth was reaped.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:16

---

### Revelation 14:17

**Q1. Where did another angel come out of?**  
**Answer:** The temple which is in heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:17

**Q2. What did the other angel also have?**  
**Answer:** A sharp sickle.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:17

**Q3. Where is the temple located from which this angel came?**  
**Answer:** In heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:17

---

### Revelation 14:18

**Q1. Where did another angel come out from?**  
**Answer:** The altar.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:18

**Q2. What power did the angel have over?**  
**Answer:** Fire.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:18

**Q3. What did he cry with a loud cry to him that had the sharp sickle?**  
**Answer:** Thrust in thy sharp sickle, and gather the clusters of the vine of the earth; for her grapes are fully ripe.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:18

---

### Revelation 14:19

**Q1. What did the angel thrust into the earth?**  
**Answer:** His sickle.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:19

**Q2. What did the angel gather?**  
**Answer:** The vine of the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:19

**Q3. Into what did he cast the vine of the earth?**  
**Answer:** Into the great winepress of the wrath of God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:19

---

### Revelation 14:20

**Q1. Where was the winepress trodden?**  
**Answer:** Without the city.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:20

**Q2. What came out of the winepress?**  
**Answer:** Blood.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:20

**Q3. How high did the blood reach?**  
**Answer:** Unto the horse bridles.  
**Verse:** Revelation 14:20
