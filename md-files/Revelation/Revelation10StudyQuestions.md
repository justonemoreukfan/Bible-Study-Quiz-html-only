# Revelation 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 10:1

**Q1. What else did the angel come down from heaven clothed with besides a cloud?**  
**Answer:** A rainbow.  
**Verse:** Revelation 10:1

**Q2. What was upon the head of this mighty angel?**  
**Answer:** His head was as the sun.  
**Verse:** Revelation 10:1

**Q3. How were the feet of the angel described?**  
**Answer:** As pillars of fire.  
**Verse:** Revelation 10:1

---

### Revelation 10:2

**Q1. What did the angel have in his hand?**  
**Answer:** A little book open.  
**Verse:** Revelation 10:2

**Q2. Where did the angel set his right foot?**  
**Answer:** Upon the sea.  
**Verse:** Revelation 10:2

**Q3. Where did the angel set his left foot?**  
**Answer:** On the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 10:2

---

### Revelation 10:3

**Q1. How did the angel cry?**  
**Answer:** With a loud voice, as when a lion roareth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 10:3

**Q2. What happened when the angel cried?**  
**Answer:** Seven thunders uttered their voices.  
**Verse:** Revelation 10:3

---

### Revelation 10:4

**Q1. What did John start to do when the seven thunders had uttered their voices?**  
**Answer:** Write.  
**Verse:** Revelation 10:4

**Q2. What did John hear from heaven telling him not to write?**  
**Answer:** A voice saying, Seal up those things which the seven thunders uttered, and write them not.  
**Verse:** Revelation 10:4

---

### Revelation 10:5

**Q1. Where was the angel standing when he lifted up his hand to heaven?**  
**Answer:** Upon the sea and upon the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 10:5

---

### Revelation 10:6

**Q1. By whom did the angel swear?**  
**Answer:** By him that liveth for ever and ever.  
**Verse:** Revelation 10:6

---

### Revelation 10:7

**Q1. What should be finished in the days of the voice of the seventh angel?**  
**Answer:** The mystery of God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 10:7

---

### Revelation 10:8

**Q1. Whose voice did John hear again from heaven?**  
**Answer:** The voice which he heard from heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 10:8

---

### Revelation 10:9

**Q1. What did John ask the angel to give him?**  
**Answer:** The little book.  
**Verse:** Revelation 10:9

---

### Revelation 10:10

**Q1. What did John do with the little book out of the angel's hand?**  
**Answer:** He ate it up.  
**Verse:** Revelation 10:10

---

### Revelation 10:11

**Q1. What did they say unto John after he ate the book?**  
**Answer:** Thou must prophesy again before many peoples, and nations, and tongues, and kings.  
**Verse:** Revelation 10:11
