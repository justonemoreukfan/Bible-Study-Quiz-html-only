# Revelation 19 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 19:1

**Q1. What was heard in heaven?**  
**Answer:** A great voice of much people.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:1

**Q2. What did the great voice in heaven say?**  
**Answer:** Alleluia.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:1

**Q3. Whose are salvation, glory, and honour, according to this voice?**  
**Answer:** The Lord our God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:1

---

### Revelation 19:2

**Q1. What are true and righteous?**  
**Answer:** His judgments.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:2

**Q2. What did He judge?**  
**Answer:** The great whore.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:2

**Q3. What did the great whore do to the earth?**  
**Answer:** Corrupted the earth with her fornication.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:2

---

### Revelation 19:3

**Q1. What did they say the second time?**  
**Answer:** Alleluia.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:3

**Q2. What rose up for ever and ever?**  
**Answer:** Her smoke.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:3

---

### Revelation 19:4

**Q1. Who fell down and worshipped God?**  
**Answer:** The four and twenty elders and the four beasts.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:4

**Q2. Where did the elders and beasts sit?**  
**Answer:** On the throne.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:4

**Q3. What did they say when they fell down and worshipped?**  
**Answer:** Amen; Alleluia.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:4

---

### Revelation 19:5

**Q1. What came out of the throne?**  
**Answer:** A voice.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:5

**Q2. Who were told to praise our God?**  
**Answer:** All his servants, and ye that fear him, both small and great.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:5

---

### Revelation 19:6

**Q1. What did the voice sound like?**  
**Answer:** The voice of a great multitude, and as the voice of many waters, and as the voice of mighty thunderings.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:6

**Q2. What word was repeated again in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Alleluia.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:6

**Q3. Who reigneth?**  
**Answer:** The Lord God omnipotent.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:6

---

### Revelation 19:7

**Q1. What should we do and be?**  
**Answer:** Be glad and rejoice, and give honour to him.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:7

**Q2. Whose marriage is come?**  
**Answer:** The marriage of the Lamb.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:7

**Q3. What has the Lamb's wife made herself?**  
**Answer:** Ready.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:7

---

### Revelation 19:8

**Q1. What was granted to her that she should be arrayed in?**  
**Answer:** Fine linen, clean and white.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:8

**Q2. What is the fine linen?**  
**Answer:** The righteousness of saints.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:8

---

### Revelation 19:9

**Q1. What was he saith unto me to write?**  
**Answer:** Blessed are they which are called unto the marriage supper of the Lamb.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:9

**Q2. What did he say unto me?**  
**Answer:** These are the true sayings of God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:9

---

### Revelation 19:10

**Q1. At whose feet did I fall to worship him?**  
**Answer:** His feet.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:10

**Q2. What did he say unto me regarding his identity?**  
**Answer:** See thou do it not: I am thy fellowservant, and of thy brethren that have the testimony of Jesus.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:10

**Q3. What is the testimony of Jesus?**  
**Answer:** The spirit of prophecy.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:10

---

### Revelation 19:11

**Q1. What was opened?**  
**Answer:** Heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:11

**Q2. What was the name of him that sat upon the white horse?**  
**Answer:** Faithful and True.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:11

**Q3. In what manner does he judge and make war?**  
**Answer:** In righteousness.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:11

---

### Revelation 19:12

**Q1. What were his eyes like?**  
**Answer:** As a flame of fire.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:12

**Q2. What was on his head?**  
**Answer:** Many crowns.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:12

**Q3. What did he have written that no man knew, save he himself?**  
**Answer:** A name.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:12

---

### Revelation 19:13

**Q1. With what was his vesture dipped?**  
**Answer:** In blood.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:13

**Q2. What is his name called?**  
**Answer:** The Word of God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:13

---

### Revelation 19:14

**Q1. What armies followed him?**  
**Answer:** The armies which were in heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:14

**Q2. Upon what did the armies in heaven ride?**  
**Answer:** White horses.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:14

**Q3. With what kind of linen were the armies clothed?**  
**Answer:** Fine linen, white and clean.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:14

---

### Revelation 19:15

**Q1. What went out of his mouth?**  
**Answer:** A sharp sword.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:15

**Q2. What will he smite the nations with?**  
**Answer:** The sharp sword that went out of his mouth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:15

**Q3. With what rod shall he rule them?**  
**Answer:** With a rod of iron.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:15

---

### Revelation 19:16

**Q1. What was on his vesture and on his thigh?**  
**Answer:** A name written.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:16

**Q2. What title was written on his vesture and thigh?**  
**Answer:** KING OF KINGS, AND LORD OF LORDS.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:16

---

### Revelation 19:17

**Q1. Where did an angel stand?**  
**Answer:** In the sun.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:17

**Q2. To whom did the angel cry with a loud voice?**  
**Answer:** To all the fowls that fly in the midst of heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:17

**Q3. What were the fowls told to gather themselves together unto?**  
**Answer:** The supper of the great God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:17

---

### Revelation 19:18

**Q1. Whose flesh were the fowls told to eat?**  
**Answer:** The flesh of kings, and the flesh of captains, and the flesh of mighty men, and the flesh of horses, and of them that sit on them.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:18

**Q2. Whose flesh was mentioned as being eaten, including all men?**  
**Answer:** Both free and bond, both small and great.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:18

---

### Revelation 19:19

**Q1. Who was gathered together?**  
**Answer:** The beast, and the kings of the earth, and their armies.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:19

**Q2. Against whom were they gathered to make war?**  
**Answer:** Against him that sat on the horse, and against his army.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:19

---

### Revelation 19:20

**Q1. Who was taken along with the beast?**  
**Answer:** The false prophet that wrought miracles before him.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:20

**Q2. What did the false prophet deceive with his miracles?**  
**Answer:** Them that had received the mark of the beast, and them that worshipped his image.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:20

**Q3. Where were both the beast and the false prophet cast alive?**  
**Answer:** Into a lake of fire burning with brimstone.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:20

---

### Revelation 19:21

**Q1. How were the remnant slain?**  
**Answer:** With the sword of him that sat upon the horse, which sword proceeded out of his mouth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:21

**Q2. With whose flesh were all the fowls filled?**  
**Answer:** With their flesh.  
**Verse:** Revelation 19:21
