# Revelation 22 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 22:1

**Q1. What did the angel shew unto John?**  
**Answer:** A pure river of water of life, clear as crystal.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:1

**Q2. Where did the river proceed from?**  
**Answer:** Out of the throne of God and of the Lamb.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:1

**Q3. How is the water of the river described regarding its clarity?**  
**Answer:** Clear as crystal.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:1

---

### Revelation 22:2

**Q1. Where was the tree of life located in relation to the street and the river?**  
**Answer:** In the midst of the street of it, and on either side of the river.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:2

**Q2. How often did the tree of life bear fruit?**  
**Answer:** Every month.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:2

**Q3. What were the leaves of the tree used for?**  
**Answer:** For the healing of the nations.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:2

---

### Revelation 22:3

**Q1. What will there be no more of?**  
**Answer:** Any more curse.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:3

**Q2. What will be in the city?**  
**Answer:** The throne of God and of the Lamb.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:3

**Q3. Who shall serve Him?**  
**Answer:** His servants.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:3

---

### Revelation 22:4

**Q1. What shall the servants see?**  
**Answer:** His face.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:4

**Q2. What shall be in their foreheads?**  
**Answer:** His name.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:4

**Q3. Whose name shall be in their foreheads?**  
**Answer:** His name.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:4

---

### Revelation 22:5

**Q1. What will there be no more of in that place?**  
**Answer:** Night.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:5

**Q2. What kind of light will they have no need of?**  
**Answer:** Candle, neither light of the sun.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:5

**Q3. Who giveth them light?**  
**Answer:** The Lord God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:5

---

### Revelation 22:6

**Q1. How are the sayings described?**  
**Answer:** Faithful and true.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:6

**Q2. Who sent his angel?**  
**Answer:** The Lord God of the holy prophets.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:6

**Q3. What did the Lord God send his angel to shew unto his servants?**  
**Answer:** The things which must shortly be done.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:6

---

### Revelation 22:7

**Q1. Who says, "Behold, I come quickly"?**  
**Answer:** Behold, I come quickly.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:7

**Q2. Who is blessed?**  
**Answer:** He that keepeth the sayings of the prophecy of this book.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:7

**Q3. What book are the sayings from?**  
**Answer:** The prophecy of this book.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:7

---

### Revelation 22:8

**Q1. Who saw and heard these things?**  
**Answer:** I John.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:8

**Q2. What did John do when he had heard and seen?**  
**Answer:** I fell down to worship before the feet of the angel which shewed me these things.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:8

**Q3. Before whose feet did John fall down to worship?**  
**Answer:** The feet of the angel which shewed me these things.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:8

---

### Revelation 22:9

**Q1. What did the angel say to John regarding doing that?**  
**Answer:** See thou do it not.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:9

**Q2. Whose fellowservant is the angel?**  
**Answer:** Thy fellowservant, and of thy brethren the prophets.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:9

**Q3. Whom should John worship?**  
**Answer:** Worship God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:9

---

### Revelation 22:10

**Q1. What instruction was given regarding the sayings of the prophecy?**  
**Answer:** Seal not the sayings of the prophecy of this book.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:10

**Q2. Why should the sayings not be sealed?**  
**Answer:** For the time is at hand.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:10

**Q3. What book contains these sayings?**  
**Answer:** This book.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:10

**Answer:** Be holy still.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:11

**Q3. What should he that is righteous do?**

### Revelation 22:12

**Q1. Who states, "And, behold, I come quickly"?**  
**Answer:** Behold, I come quickly.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:12

**Q2. What is with Him?**  
**Answer:** My reward.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:12

**Q3. To whom will He give according to his work?**

### Revelation 22:13

**Answer:** I am Alpha and Omega.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:13

**Q2. Who is the beginning and the end?**  
**Answer:** The beginning and the end.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:13

**Q3. Who is the first and the last?**  
**Answer:** The first and the last.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:13

---

### Revelation 22:14

**Q1. Who are blessed?**  
**Answer:** They that do his commandments.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:14

**Q2. What right will they have?**  
**Answer:** To the tree of life.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:14

**Q3. How may they enter in?**  
**Answer:** Through the gates into the city.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:14

---

### Revelation 22:15

**Q1. What is outside?**  
**Answer:** Without are dogs, and sorcerers, and whoremongers, and murderers, and idolaters.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:15

**Q2. Who else is without besides dogs, sorcerers, whoremongers, murderers, and idolaters?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever loveth and maketh a lie.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:15

**Q3. What kind of persons are included along with whoremongers and murderers?**  
**Answer:** Sorcerers and idolaters.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:15

---

### Revelation 22:16

**Q1. Who sent mine angel to testify these things unto you in the churches?**  
**Answer:** I Jesus.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:16

**Q2. Whose root and offspring is He?**  
**Answer:** The root and the offspring of David.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:16

**Q3. What else is He called in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The bright and morning star.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:16

---

### Revelation 22:17

**Q1. Who say, "Come"?**  
**Answer:** And the Spirit and the bride say, Come.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:17

**Q2. What should he that heareth say?**  
**Answer:** Come.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:17

**Q3. Who should take the water of life freely?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever will.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:17

---

### Revelation 22:18

**Q1. Unto whom does the speaker testify?**  
**Answer:** Every man that heareth the words of the prophecy of this book.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:18

**Q2. What will God add unto him if any man shall add unto these things?**  
**Answer:** The plagues that are written in this book.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:18

**Q3. What book contains these things?**  
**Answer:** This book.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:18

---

### Revelation 22:19

**Q1. What will God take away if any man shall take away from the words of the book of this prophecy?**  
**Answer:** His part out of the book of life.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:19

**Q2. Out of what place will his part be taken away?**  
**Answer:** Out of the holy city.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:19

**Q3. From what else will his part be taken away?**  
**Answer:** From the things which are written in this book.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:19

---

### Revelation 22:20

**Q1. Who testifieth these things?**  
**Answer:** He which testifieth these things.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:20

**Q2. What does He say?**  
**Answer:** Surely I come quickly.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:20

**Q3. What was John's response?**  
**Answer:** Even so, come, Lord Jesus.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:20

---

### Revelation 22:21

**Q1. Whose grace is mentioned?**  
**Answer:** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:21

**Q2. With whom should the grace be?**  
**Answer:** With you all.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:21

**Q3. What is the final word of the verse and chapter?**  
**Answer:** Amen.  
**Verse:** Revelation 22:21
