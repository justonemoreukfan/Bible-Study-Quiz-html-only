# Revelation 7 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 7:1

**Q1. What did John see standing on the four corners of the earth?**  
**Answer:** Four angels.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:1

**Q2. What were the four angels holding back?**  
**Answer:** The four winds of the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:1

**Q3. What was forbidden to blow upon the earth, the sea, or any tree?**  
**Answer:** Any wind.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:1

---

### Revelation 7:2

**Q1. From where did John see another angel ascend?**  
**Answer:** From the east.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:2

**Q2. What did the angel have in his hand?**  
**Answer:** The seal of the living God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:2

**Q3. To whom did the angel cry with a loud voice?**  
**Answer:** To the four angels, to whom it was given to hurt the earth and the sea.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:2

---

### Revelation 7:3

**Q1. What did the angel tell the others not to hurt?**  
**Answer:** The earth, neither the sea, nor the trees.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:3

**Q2. Until what event were they told not to hurt the earth, sea, or trees?**  
**Answer:** Till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:3

**Q3. Where were the servants of God to be sealed?**  
**Answer:** In their foreheads.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:3

---

### Revelation 7:4

**Answer:** An hundred and forty and four thousand.

### Revelation 7:9

**Q1. After this, what did John behold?**  
**Answer:** A great multitude, which no man could number.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:9

**Q2. From where were the people gathered?**  
**Answer:** Of all nations, and kindreds, and people, and tongues.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:9

**Q3. Where did they stand, and what were they wearing?**  
**Answer:** Stood before the throne, and before the Lamb, clothed with white robes, and palms in their hands.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:9

---

### Revelation 7:10

**Q1. How did the multitude cry?**  
**Answer:** With a loud voice.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:10

**Q2. To whom did they ascribe salvation?**  
**Answer:** To our God which sitteth upon the throne, and unto the Lamb.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:10

**Q3. Whose throne is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The throne of our God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:10

---

### Revelation 7:11

**Q1. Who stood round about the throne, and about the elders and the four beasts?**  
**Answer:** All the angels.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:11

**Q2. How did the angels fall before the throne?**  
**Answer:** On their faces.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:11

**Q3. Whom did the angels worship?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:11

---

### Revelation 7:12

**Q1. What word begins the angels' praise to God?**  
**Answer:** Amen.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:12

**Q2. Name three attributes ascribed to God by the angels in this verse.**  
**Answer:** Blessing, and glory, and wisdom, and thanksgiving, and honour, and power, and might. (Any three)  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:12

**Q3. For how long did the angels say this power and might belongs to God?**  
**Answer:** For ever and ever.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:12

---

### Revelation 7:13

**Q1. Who answered and spoke unto John?**  
**Answer:** One of the elders.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:13

**Q2. What question did the elder ask John?**  
**Answer:** What are these which are arrayed in white robes? and whence came they?  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:13

**Q3. What were the people in question arrayed in?**  
**Answer:** White robes.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:13

---

### Revelation 7:14

**Q1. What did John call the elder in his response?**  
**Answer:** Sir.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:14

**Q2. What did the elder say these people came out of?**  
**Answer:** Great tribulation.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:14

**Q3. What did they wash and make white their robes in?**  
**Answer:** The blood of the Lamb.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:14

### Revelation 7:15

**Q1. Where are they before the throne of God?**  
**Answer:** Before the throne of God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:15

**Q2. What do they serve Him day and night in?**  
**Answer:** His temple.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:15

**Q3. Who shall dwell among them?**  
**Answer:** He that sitteth on the throne.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:15

---

### Revelation 7:16

**Q1. What will they do no more?**  
**Answer:** Hunger no more, neither thirst any more.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:16

**Q2. What will not light on them?**  
**Answer:** The sun, nor any heat.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:16

**Q3. Why will the sun not light on them?**  
**Answer:** For the Lamb which is in the midst of the throne shall feed them (implied context of protection/feeding).  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:16

---

### Revelation 7:17

**Q1. Who shall feed them?**  
**Answer:** The Lamb which is in the midst of the throne.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:17

**Q2. To what will the Lamb lead them?**  
**Answer:** Unto living fountains of waters.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:17

**Q3. What will God wipe away from their eyes?**  
**Answer:** All tears.  
**Verse:** Revelation 7:17
