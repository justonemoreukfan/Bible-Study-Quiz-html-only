# Revelation 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 5:1

**Q1. What did he see in the right hand of him that sat on the throne?**  
**Answer:** A book.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:1

**Q2. How was the book written?**  
**Answer:** Written within and on the backside.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:1

**Q3. How was the book sealed?**  
**Answer:** Sealed with seven seals.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:1

---

### Revelation 5:2

**Q1. What kind of angel was seen?**  
**Answer:** A strong angel.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:2

**Q2. What did the strong angel proclaim with?**  
**Answer:** A loud voice.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:2

**Q3. What question did the angel ask?**  
**Answer:** Who is worthy to open the book, and to loose the seals thereof?  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:2

---

### Revelation 5:3

**Q1. Where was no man able to open the book?**  
**Answer:** In heaven, nor in earth, neither under the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:3

**Q2. Who specifically was unable to open the book?**  
**Answer:** No man.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:3

**Q3. What else was no man able to do besides opening the book?**  
**Answer:** Neither to look thereon.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:3

---

### Revelation 5:4

**Q1. How much did the speaker weep?**  
**Answer:** Much.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:4

**Q2. Why did he weep much?**  
**Answer:** Because no man was found worthy to open and to read the book, neither to look thereon.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:4

**Q3. Who was not found worthy to open and read the book?**  
**Answer:** No man.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:4

---

### Revelation 5:5

**Q1. What did one of the elders say to him?**  
**Answer:** Weep not.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:5

**Q2. Who has prevailed to open the book and to loose the seven seals thereof?**  
**Answer:** The Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:5

**Q3. What did the Lion of the tribe of Judah prevail to do?**  
**Answer:** To open the book, and to loose the seven seals thereof.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:5

---

### Revelation 5:6

**Q1. What did he behold in the midst of the throne and of the four beasts, and in the midst of the elders?**  
**Answer:** A Lamb.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:6

**Q2. How did the Lamb appear, as though it had been what?**  
**Answer:** Slain.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:6

**Q3. How many horns and how many eyes did the Lamb have?**  
**Answer:** Having seven horns and seven eyes.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:6

---

### Revelation 5:7

**Q1. Who came and took the book?**  
**Answer:** The Lamb.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:7

**Q2. From whose right hand did the Lamb take the book?**  
**Answer:** From the right hand of him that sat upon the throne.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:7

**Q3. What action did the Lamb perform with the book?**  
**Answer:** He came and took it.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:7

---

### Revelation 5:8

**Q1. Who fell down before the Lamb when he had taken the book?**  
**Answer:** The four beasts and four and twenty elders.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:8

**Q2. What did every one of them have?**  
**Answer:** Harps.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:8

**Q3. What were the golden vials full of?**  
**Answer:** Odours, which are the prayers of saints.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:8

---

### Revelation 5:9

**Q1. What did they sing?**  
**Answer:** A new song.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:9

**Q2. Why is the Lamb worthy to take the book and to open the seals?**  
**Answer:** For thou wast slain, and hast redeemed us to God by thy blood.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:9

**Q3. Out of what kindreds and tongues and people and nations were they redeemed?**  
**Answer:** Out of every kindred, and tongue, and people, and nation.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:9

---

### Revelation 5:10

**Q1. Unto what have we been made unto our God?**  
**Answer:** Kings and priests.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:10

**Q2. What shall we do on the earth?**  
**Answer:** We shall reign on the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:10

**Q3. Who made us unto our God kings and priests?**  
**Answer:** Thou hast made us (referring to the Lamb/God).  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:10

---

### Revelation 5:11

**Q1. Whose voice did I behold and hear?**  
**Answer:** The voice of many angels.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:11

**Q2. Where were the angels positioned in relation to the throne, beasts, and elders?**  
**Answer:** Round about the throne and the beasts and the elders.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:11

**Q3. How many angels were there?**  
**Answer:** Ten thousand times ten thousand, and thousands of thousands.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:11

---

### Revelation 5:12

**Q1. What did the angels say with a loud voice?**  
**Answer:** Worthy is the Lamb that was slain to receive power, and riches, and wisdom, and strength, and honour, and glory, and blessing.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:12

**Q2. What was the Lamb worthy to receive?**  
**Answer:** Power, and riches, and wisdom, and strength, and honour, and glory, and blessing.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:12

**Q3. What kind of condition was the Lamb in when declared worthy?**  
**Answer:** Slain.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:12

---

### Revelation 5:13

**Q1. Where were every creature heard saying blessing, and honour, and glory, and power unto him?**  
**Answer:** In heaven, and on the earth, and under the earth, and such as are in the sea, and all that are in them.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:13

**Q2. To whom did every creature ascribe blessing, and honour, and glory, and power?**  
**Answer:** Unto him that sitteth upon the throne, and unto the Lamb.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:13

**Q3. For how long did they ascribe blessing and honour and glory and power?**  
**Answer:** For ever and ever.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:13

---

### Revelation 5:14

**Q1. What did the four beasts say in response?**  
**Answer:** Amen.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:14

**Q2. Who fell down and worshipped?**  
**Answer:** The four and twenty elders.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:14

**Q3. For how long did the elders worship him?**  
**Answer:** Him that liveth for ever and ever.  
**Verse:** Revelation 5:14
