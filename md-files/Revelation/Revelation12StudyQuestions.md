# Revelation 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 12:1

**Q1. What appeared in heaven?**  
**Answer:** A great wonder.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:1

**Q2. What was the woman clothed with?**  
**Answer:** The sun.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:1

**Q3. What was under the woman's feet?**  
**Answer:** The moon.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:1

---

### Revelation 12:2

**Q1. What was the woman with child doing?**  
**Answer:** Crying.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:2

**Q2. What was the woman travailing in?**  
**Answer:** Birth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:2

**Q3. What was the woman pained to do?**  
**Answer:** To be delivered.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:2

---

### Revelation 12:3

**Q1. What else appeared in heaven?**  
**Answer:** Another wonder.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:3

**Q2. What color was the great red dragon?**  
**Answer:** Red.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:3

**Q3. How many heads and horns did the dragon have?**  
**Answer:** Seven heads and ten horns, and seven crowns upon his heads.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:3

---

### Revelation 12:4

**Q1. What did the dragon's tail draw?**  
**Answer:** The third part of the stars of heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:4

**Q2. Where did the dragon cast the stars?**  
**Answer:** To the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:4

**Q3. What did the dragon stand before to devour her child?**  
**Answer:** The woman which was ready to be delivered.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:4

---

### Revelation 12:5

**Q1. What did the woman bring forth?**  
**Answer:** A man child.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:5

**Q2. With what rod is the child to rule all nations?**  
**Answer:** A rod of iron.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:5

**Q3. Where was her child caught up unto?**  
**Answer:** Unto God, and to his throne.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:5

---

### Revelation 12:6

**Q1. Where did the woman flee?**  
**Answer:** Into the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:6

**Q2. Who prepared the place in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:6

**Q3. How many days should they feed her there?**  
**Answer:** A thousand two hundred and threescore days.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:6

---

### Revelation 12:7

**Q1. What happened in heaven?**  
**Answer:** There was war.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:7

**Q2. Who fought against the dragon?**  
**Answer:** Michael and his angels.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:7

**Q3. Who fought against Michael and his angels?**  
**Answer:** The dragon and his angels.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:7

---

### Revelation 12:8

**Q1. What happened to the dragon and his angels in the fight?**  
**Answer:** They prevailed not.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:8

**Q2. What was no longer found for them in heaven?**  
**Answer:** Neither was their place found any more in heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:8

**Q3. Who are "they" that prevailed not?**  
**Answer:** The dragon and his angels.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:8

---

### Revelation 12:9

**Q1. What was cast out, that great dragon?**  
**Answer:** That old serpent.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:9

**Q2. What are two names given to the dragon in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The Devil, and Satan.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:9

**Q3. What does he do to the whole world?**  
**Answer:** Deceiveth the whole world.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:9

---

### Revelation 12:10

**Q1. What did John hear in heaven?**  
**Answer:** A loud voice.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:10

**Q2. Who is accused before our God day and night?**  
**Answer:** The accuser of our brethren.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:10

**Q3. By what means did they overcome him?**  
**Answer:** By the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:10

---

### Revelation 12:11

**Q1. What did they love not unto?**  
**Answer:** Their lives.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:11

**Q2. Unto what did they love not their lives?**  
**Answer:** Unto the death.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:11

**Q3. Who overcame the dragon?**  
**Answer:** They (the brethren).  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:11

---

### Revelation 12:12

**Q1. Who is commanded to rejoice?**  
**Answer:** Ye heavens, and ye that dwell in them.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:12

**Q2. To whom is woe pronounced?**  
**Answer:** To the inhabiters of the earth and of the sea!  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:12

**Q3. Why does the devil have great wrath?**  
**Answer:** Because he knoweth that he hath but a short time.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:12

---

### Revelation 12:13

**Q1. What did the dragon see when he was cast unto the earth?**  
**Answer:** That he was cast unto the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:13

**Q2. Whom did the dragon persecute?**  
**Answer:** The woman which brought forth the man child.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:13

**Q3. What did the dragon do to the woman?**  
**Answer:** Persecuted her.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:13

---

### Revelation 12:14

**Q1. What were given to the woman?**  
**Answer:** Two wings of a great eagle.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:14

**Q2. Where might the woman fly?**  
**Answer:** Into her place in the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:14

**Q3. For how long is she nourished there from the face of the serpent?**  
**Answer:** For a time, and times, and half a time.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:14

---

### Revelation 12:15

**Q1. What did the serpent cast out of his mouth after the woman?**  
**Answer:** Water as a flood.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:15

**Q2. Why did the serpent cast water as a flood?**  
**Answer:** That he might cause her to be carried away of the flood.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:15

**Q3. From whose mouth did the flood come?**  
**Answer:** The serpent's mouth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:15

---

### Revelation 12:16

**Q1. What did the earth do to help the woman?**  
**Answer:** The earth helped the woman.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:16

**Q2. What did the earth open?**  
**Answer:** Her mouth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:16

**Q3. What did the earth swallow up?**  
**Answer:** The flood which the dragon cast out of his mouth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:16

---

### Revelation 12:17

**Q1. What was the dragon wroth with?**  
**Answer:** The woman.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:17

**Q2. To make war with whom did the dragon go?**  
**Answer:** The remnant of her seed.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:17

**Q3. What two things do the remnant of her seed keep and have?**  
**Answer:** The commandments of God, and the testimony of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Revelation 12:17
