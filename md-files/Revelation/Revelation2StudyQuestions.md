# Revelation 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 2:1

**Q1. Unto the angel of what church write?**  
**Answer:** The church of Ephesus.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:1

**Q2. Who holds the seven stars in his right hand?**  
**Answer:** He that holdeth the seven stars in his right hand.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:1

**Q3. In the midst of what does he walk?**  
**Answer:** The midst of the seven golden candlesticks.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:1

---

### Revelation 2:2

**Q1. What does he know besides thy works?**  
**Answer:** Thy labour, and thy patience.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:2

**Q2. What can they not bear?**  
**Answer:** Them which are evil.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:2

**Q3. Whom did they try, and find them liars?**  
**Answer:** Them which say they are apostles, and are not.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:2

---

### Revelation 2:3

**Q1. What has been borne and what has been had?**  
**Answer:** Hast borne, and hast patience.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:3

**Q2. For whose name's sake hast thou laboured?**  
**Answer:** For my name's sake.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:3

**Q3. What have they not done regarding labor?**  
**Answer:** Hast not fainted.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:3

---

### Revelation 2:4

**Q1. Against whom do I have somewhat?**  
**Answer:** Against thee.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:4

**Q2. What have you left?**  
**Answer:** Thy first love.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:4

---

### Revelation 2:5

**Q1. From where shouldest thou remember?**  
**Answer:** From whence thou art fallen.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:5

**Q2. What shouldest thou do after remembering?**  
**Answer:** Repent, and do the first works.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:5

**Q3. What will happen if thou dost not repent?**  
**Answer:** I will come unto thee quickly, and will remove thy candlestick out of his place.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:5

---

### Revelation 2:6

**Q1. What doest thou have that is also hated?**  
**Answer:** That thou hatest the deeds of the Nicolaitans.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:6

**Q2. Whose deeds do I also hate?**  
**Answer:** The deeds of the Nicolaitans.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:6

**Q3. What is the relationship between the speaker and the hearer regarding the deeds of the Nicolaitans?**  
**Answer:** Which I also hate.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:6

### Revelation 2:7

**Q1. Who should hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches?**  
**Answer:** He that hath an ear.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:7

**Q2. To whom will I give to eat of the tree of life?**  
**Answer:** To him that overcometh.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:7

**Q3. Where is the tree of life located?**  
**Answer:** In the midst of the paradise of God.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:7

**Answer:** The church in Smyrna.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:8

**Q2. Who saith these things, being the first and the last?**  
**Answer:** The first and the last.  
**Verse:** Revelation 2:8

### Revelation 2:9

**Verse:** Revelation 2:9
