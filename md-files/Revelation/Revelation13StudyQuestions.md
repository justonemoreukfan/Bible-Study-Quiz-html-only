# Revelation 13 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Revelation 13:1

**Q1. Upon what did the beast stand?**  
**Answer:** Upon the sand of the sea.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:1

**Q2. How many heads and horns did the beast have?**  
**Answer:** Seven heads and ten horns.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:1

**Q3. What was upon his horns and upon his heads?**  
**Answer:** Upon his horns ten crowns, and upon his heads the name of blasphemy.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:1

---

### Revelation 13:2

**Q1. What was the beast like?**  
**Answer:** Like unto a leopard.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:2

**Q2. Whose feet and mouth were like the beast's?**  
**Answer:** His feet were as the feet of a bear, and his mouth as the mouth of a lion.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:2

**Q3. What did the dragon give unto the beast?**  
**Answer:** His power, and his seat, and great authority.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:2

---

### Revelation 13:3

**Q1. What was seen as one of the heads of the beast?**  
**Answer:** As it were wounded to death.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:3

**Q2. What happened to his deadly wound?**  
**Answer:** His deadly wound was healed.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:3

**Q3. How did all the world react to the beast?**  
**Answer:** All the world wondered after the beast.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:3

---

### Revelation 13:4

**Q1. Whom did the world worship because he gave power unto the beast?**  
**Answer:** The dragon.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:4

**Q2. Whom did they also worship, saying, Who is like unto the beast?**  
**Answer:** The beast.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:4

**Q3. What question did they ask concerning the beast's ability to fight?**  
**Answer:** Who is able to make war with him?  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:4

---

### Revelation 13:5

**Q1. What was given unto the beast?**  
**Answer:** A mouth speaking great things and blasphemies.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:5

**Q2. What else was given unto him?**  
**Answer:** Power to continue.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:5

**Q3. How long was the power given unto him to continue?**  
**Answer:** Forty and two months.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:5

---

### Revelation 13:6

**Q1. What did the beast open in blasphemy against God?**  
**Answer:** His mouth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:6

**Q2. What did he blaspheme regarding God's name?**  
**Answer:** His name.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:6

**Q3. What else did he blaspheme, besides God's tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** And his tabernacle, and them that dwell in heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:6

---

### Revelation 13:7

**Q1. What was it given unto the beast to make with the saints?**  
**Answer:** War.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:7

**Q2. What did the beast have power to do to the saints?**  
**Answer:** To overcome them.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:7

**Q3. Over what groups was power given him?**  
**Answer:** Over all kindreds, and tongues, and nations.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:7

---

### Revelation 13:8

**Q1. Who shall worship the beast?**  
**Answer:** All that dwell upon the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:8

**Q2. Whose names are not written in the book of life?**  
**Answer:** Every one whose names are not written in the book of life of the Lamb.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:8

**Q3. How is the Lamb described in relation to the foundation of the world?**  
**Answer:** Slain from the foundation of the world.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:8

---

### Revelation 13:9

**Q1. What instruction is given to anyone who has an ear?**  
**Answer:** Let him hear.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:9

---

### Revelation 13:10

**Q1. What happens to him that leadeth into captivity?**  
**Answer:** He shall go into captivity.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:10

**Q2. What happens to him that killeth with the sword?**  
**Answer:** He must be killed with the sword.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:10

**Q3. What do the patience and the faith of the saints involve?**  
**Answer:** Here is the patience and the faith of the saints.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:10

---

### Revelation 13:11

**Q1. Where did the second beast come up from?**  
**Answer:** Out of the earth.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:11

**Q2. How many horns did he have like a lamb?**  
**Answer:** Two horns.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:11

**Q3. How did the second beast speak?**  
**Answer:** As a dragon.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:11

---

### Revelation 13:12

**Q1. Whose power did the second beast exercise before him?**  
**Answer:** All the power of the first beast.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:12

**Q2. What did the second beast cause the earth and them which dwell therein to do?**  
**Answer:** To worship the first beast.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:12

**Q3. What had happened to the first beast's deadly wound?**  
**Answer:** Whose deadly wound was healed.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:12

---

### Revelation 13:13

**Q1. What great wonders did the second beast do?**  
**Answer:** He doeth great wonders.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:13

**Q2. What did he make fire do from heaven?**  
**Answer:** Come down from heaven.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:13

**Q3. In the sight of whom did he make the fire come down?**  
**Answer:** In the sight of men.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:13

---

### Revelation 13:14

**Q1. What did he deceive them that dwell on the earth by?**  
**Answer:** By the means of those miracles which he had power to do in the sight of the beast.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:14

**Q2. What did he say to them that dwell on the earth?**  
**Answer:** That they should make an image to the beast.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:14

**Q3. What had happened to the beast by a sword?**  
**Answer:** Which had the wound by a sword, and did live.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:14

---

### Revelation 13:15

**Q1. What was he given power to give unto the image of the beast?**  
**Answer:** Life.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:15

**Q2. What should the image of the beast both speak and do?**  
**Answer:** Cause that as many as would not worship the image of the beast should be killed.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:15

**Q3. What would happen to as many as would not worship the image?**  
**Answer:** Should be killed.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:15

---

### Revelation 13:16

**Q1. Whom did he cause to receive a mark?**  
**Answer:** All, both small and great, rich and poor, free and bond.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:16

**Q2. Where did they receive the mark?**  
**Answer:** In their right hand, or in their foreheads.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:16

---

### Revelation 13:17

**Q1. Who might buy or sell?**  
**Answer:** That no man might buy or sell.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:17

**Q2. What must a man have in order to buy or sell?**  
**Answer:** Save he that had the mark.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:17

**Q3. What else did the mark consist of besides the mark?**  
**Answer:** Or the name of the beast, or the number of his name.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:17

---

### Revelation 13:18

**Q1. What is required to calculate the number of the beast?**  
**Answer:** Wisdom.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:18

**Q2. Whose number is it?**  
**Answer:** The number of a man.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:18

**Q3. What is his number?**  
**Answer:** Six hundred threesix.  
**Verse:** Revelation 13:18
