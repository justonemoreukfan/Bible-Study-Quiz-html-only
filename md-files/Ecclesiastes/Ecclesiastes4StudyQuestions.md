# Ecclesiastes 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ecclesiastes 4:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Ecclesiastes 4:1?**  
**Answer:** So I returned, and considered all the oppressions that are done under the sun.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 4:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Ecclesiastes 4:1?**  
**Answer:** and behold the tears of such as were oppressed, and they had no comforter.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 4:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Ecclesiastes 4:1?**  
**Answer:** and on the side of their oppressors there was power; but they had no comforter.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 4:1
