# Ecclesiastes 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ecclesiastes 5:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Ecclesiastes 5:1?**  
**Answer:** Keep thy foot when thou goest to the house of God, and be more ready to hear, than to give the sacrifice of fools.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 5:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Ecclesiastes 5:1?**  
**Answer:** for they consider not that they do evil.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 5:1

**Q3. According to Ecclesiastes 5:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Keep thy foot when thou goest to the house of God, and be more ready to hear, than to give the sacrifice of fools: for they consider not that they do evil.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 5:1
