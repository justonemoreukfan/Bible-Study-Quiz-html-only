# Ecclesiastes 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ecclesiastes 9:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Ecclesiastes 9:1?**  
**Answer:** For all this I considered in my heart even to declare all this, that the righteous, and the wise, and their works, are in the hand of God.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 9:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Ecclesiastes 9:1?**  
**Answer:** no man knoweth either love or hatred by all that is before them.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 9:1

**Q3. According to Ecclesiastes 9:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** For all this I considered in my heart even to declare all this, that the righteous, and the wise, and their works, are in the hand of God: no man knoweth either love or hatred by all that is before them.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 9:1

### Ecclesiastes 9:2
**Q4. What is stated in the opening of Ecclesiastes 9:2?**  
**Answer:** All things come alike to all.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 9:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Ecclesiastes 9:2?**  
**Answer:** there is one event to the righteous, and to the wicked.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 9:2

**Q6. What further truth is recorded in Ecclesiastes 9:2?**  
**Answer:** to the good and to the clean, and to the unclean; to him that sacrificeth, and to him that sacrificeth not; as is the good, so is the sinner; and he that sweareth, as he that feareth an oath.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 9:2
