# Ecclesiastes 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ecclesiastes 3:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Ecclesiastes 3:1 state?**  
**Answer:** To every thing there is a season.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 3:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Ecclesiastes 3:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** and a time to every purpose under the heaven.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 3:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Ecclesiastes 3:1?**  
**Answer:** To every thing there is a season, and a time to every purpose under the heaven:.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 3:1
