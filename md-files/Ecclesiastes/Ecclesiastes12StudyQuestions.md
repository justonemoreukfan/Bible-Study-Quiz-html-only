# Ecclesiastes 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ecclesiastes 12:1
**Q1. According to Ecclesiastes 12:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Remember now thy Creator in the days of thy youth.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 12:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Ecclesiastes 12:1?**  
**Answer:** while the evil days come not.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 12:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Ecclesiastes 12:1?**  
**Answer:** nor the years draw nigh, when thou shalt say, I have no pleasure in them.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 12:1

---

### Ecclesiastes 12:2
**Q4. According to Ecclesiastes 12:2, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** While the sun.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 12:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Ecclesiastes 12:2?**  
**Answer:** or the light.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 12:2

**Q6. What further truth is given in Ecclesiastes 12:2?**  
**Answer:** or the moon, or the stars, be not darkened, nor the clouds return after the rain.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 12:2

### Ecclesiastes 12:3
**Q7. According to Ecclesiastes 12:3, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** In the day when the keepers of the house shall tremble.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 12:3

**Q8. What is declared next in Ecclesiastes 12:3?**  
**Answer:** and the strong men shall bow themselves.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 12:3

**Q9. What further truth is given in Ecclesiastes 12:3?**  
**Answer:** and the grinders cease because they are few, and those that look out of the windows be darkened.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 12:3

### Ecclesiastes 12:4
**Q10. According to Ecclesiastes 12:4, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And the doors shall be shut in the streets.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 12:4

**Q11. What is declared next in Ecclesiastes 12:4?**

**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 12:4

### Ecclesiastes 12:5
**Q13. What is stated in the first clause of Ecclesiastes 12:5?**  
**Answer:** Also when they shall be afraid of that which is high, and fears shall be in the way, and the almond tree shall flourish, and the grasshopper shall be a burden, and desire shall fail.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 12:5

**Q14. What additional detail is declared in Ecclesiastes 12:5?**  
**Answer:** because man goeth to his long home, and the mourners go about the streets.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 12:5

**Q15. According to Ecclesiastes 12:5, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Also when they shall be afraid of that which is high, and fears shall be in the way, and the almond tree shall flourish, and the grasshopper shall be a burden, and desire shall fail: because man goeth to his long home, and the mourners go about the streets:.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 12:5
