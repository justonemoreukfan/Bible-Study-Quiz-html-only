# Ecclesiastes 11 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ecclesiastes 11:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Ecclesiastes 11:1?**  
**Answer:** Cast thy bread upon the waters.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 11:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Ecclesiastes 11:1?**  
**Answer:** for thou shalt find it after many days.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 11:1

**Q3. According to Ecclesiastes 11:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Cast thy bread upon the waters: for thou shalt find it after many days.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 11:1

### Ecclesiastes 11:8
**Q22. What is stated in the opening of Ecclesiastes 11:8?**  
**Answer:** But if a man live many years, and rejoice in them all.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 11:8

**Q23. What is declared next in Ecclesiastes 11:8?**  
**Answer:** yet let him remember the days of darkness.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 11:8

**Q24. What further truth is recorded in Ecclesiastes 11:8?**  
**Answer:** for they shall be many. All that cometh is vanity.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 11:8

### Ecclesiastes 11:9
**Q25. What is stated in the opening of Ecclesiastes 11:9?**  
**Answer:** Rejoice, O young man, in thy youth.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 11:9

**Q26. What is declared next in Ecclesiastes 11:9?**  
**Answer:** and let thy heart cheer thee in the days of thy youth, and walk in the ways of thine heart, and in the sight of thine eyes.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 11:9

**Q27. What further truth is recorded in Ecclesiastes 11:9?**

**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 11:9

**Q30. According to Ecclesiastes 11:10, what is the full truth recorded?**
