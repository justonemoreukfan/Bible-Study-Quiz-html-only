# Ecclesiastes 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ecclesiastes 8:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Ecclesiastes 8:1 state?**  
**Answer:** Who is as the wise man? and who knoweth the interpretation of a thing? a man’s wisdom maketh his face to shine.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 8:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Ecclesiastes 8:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** and the boldness of his face shall be changed.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 8:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Ecclesiastes 8:1?**  
**Answer:** Who is as the wise man? and who knoweth the interpretation of a thing? a man’s wisdom maketh his face to shine, and the boldness of his face shall be changed.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 8:1

**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 8:17

**Q50. What is declared next in Ecclesiastes 8:17?**  
**Answer:** because though a man labour to seek it out, yet he shall not find it.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 8:17

**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 8:17
