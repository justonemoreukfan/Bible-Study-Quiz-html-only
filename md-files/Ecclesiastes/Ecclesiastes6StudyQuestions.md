# Ecclesiastes 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ecclesiastes 6:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Ecclesiastes 6:1 state?**  
**Answer:** There is an evil which I have seen under the sun.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 6:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Ecclesiastes 6:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** and it is common among men.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 6:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Ecclesiastes 6:1?**  
**Answer:** There is an evil which I have seen under the sun, and it is common among men:.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 6:1

### Ecclesiastes 6:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Ecclesiastes 6:2?**  
**Answer:** A man to whom God hath given riches, wealth, and honour, so that he wanteth nothing for his soul of all that he desireth, yet God giveth him not power to eat thereof, but a stranger eateth it.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 6:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Ecclesiastes 6:2?**  
**Answer:** this is vanity, and it is an evil disease.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 6:2

**Q6. According to Ecclesiastes 6:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** A man to whom God hath given riches, wealth, and honour, so that he wanteth nothing for his soul of all that he desireth, yet God giveth him not power to eat thereof, but a stranger eateth it: this is vanity, and it is an evil disease.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 6:2

### Ecclesiastes 6:3

**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 6:3

**Q8. What additional detail is declared in Ecclesiastes 6:3?**  
**Answer:** I say, that an untimely birth is better than he.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 6:3

**Q9. According to Ecclesiastes 6:3, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** If a man beget an hundred children, and live many years, so that the days of his years be many, and his soul be not filled with good, and also that he have no burial; I say, that an untimely birth is better than he.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 6:3
