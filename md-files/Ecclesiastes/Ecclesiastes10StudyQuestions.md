# Ecclesiastes 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ecclesiastes 10:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Ecclesiastes 10:1?**  
**Answer:** Dead flies cause the ointment of the apothecary to send forth a stinking savour.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 10:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Ecclesiastes 10:1?**  
**Answer:** so doth a little folly him that is in reputation for wisdom and honour.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 10:1

**Q3. According to Ecclesiastes 10:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Dead flies cause the ointment of the apothecary to send forth a stinking savour: so doth a little folly him that is in reputation for wisdom and honour.  
**Verse:** Ecclesiastes 10:1
