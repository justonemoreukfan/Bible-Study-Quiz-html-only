# Jude 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jude 1:1

**Q1. Whose servant is Jude?**  
**Answer:** The servant of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:1

**Q2. Whose brother is Jude?**  
**Answer:** The brother of James.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:1

**Q3. To whom is the epistle sanctified?**  
**Answer:** Them that are sanctified by God the Father, and preserved in Jesus Christ, and called.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:1

---

### Jude 1:2

**Q4. What is desired to be multiplied unto the readers?**  
**Answer:** Mercy, and peace, and love.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:2

---

### Jude 1:3

**Q5. When the writer gave all diligence to write, what was it concerning?**  
**Answer:** Of the common salvation.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:3

---

### Jude 1:4

**Q6. What have certain men crept in unawares to turn into lasciviousness?**  
**Answer:** The grace of our God.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:4

---

### Jude 1:5

**Q7. What did the Lord, after that he saved the people out of the land of Egypt, afterward do?**  
**Answer:** Destroyed them that believed not.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:5

---

### Jude 1:6

**Q8. What did the angels not keep?**  
**Answer:** Their first estate.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:6

---

### Jude 1:7

**Q9. Even as what cities, going after strange flesh, are set forth for an example?**  
**Answer:** Sodom and Gomorrha.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:7

---

### Jude 1:8

**Q10. Likewise also what do these filthy dreamers do?**  
**Answer:** Defile the flesh, despise dominion, and speak evil of dignities.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:8

---

### Jude 1:9

**Q11. Who, contending with the devil, disputed about the body of Moses?**  
**Answer:** Michael the archangel.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:9

---

### Jude 1:10

**Q12. What do these men speak evil of?**  
**Answer:** Those things which they know not.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:10

---

### Jude 1:11

**Q13. Woe unto them! for they have gone in what way?**  
**Answer:** In the way of Cain.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:11

---

### Jude 1:12

**Q14. These are spots in what kind of feasts?**  
**Answer:** Your feasts of charity.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:12

---

### Jude 1:13

**Q15. What do these raging waves of the sea foam out?**  
**Answer:** Their own shame.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:13

---

### Jude 1:14

**Q16. Who prophesied of these, the seventh from Adam?**  
**Answer:** Enoch.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:14

---

### Jude 1:15

**Q17. To execute judgment upon whom does the Lord come with ten thousands of his saints?**  
**Answer:** Upon all.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:15

---

### Jude 1:16

**Q18. What are these murmurers and complainers, walking after?**  
**Answer:** Their own lusts.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:16

---

### Jude 1:17

**Q19. But remember ye the words which were spoken before of whom?**  
**Answer:** Of the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:17

---

### Jude 1:18

**Q20. What did they tell you that there should be in the last time?**  
**Answer:** Mockers, walking after their own ungodly lusts.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:18

---

### Jude 1:19

**Q21. These be they who separate themselves, being what?**  
**Answer:** Sensual.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:19

---

### Jude 1:20

**Q22. But ye, beloved, building up yourselves on what?**  
**Answer:** Your most holy faith.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:20

---

### Jude 1:21

**Q23. Keep yourselves in what?**  
**Answer:** The love of God.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:21

---

### Jude 1:22

**Q24. And of some have compassion, making what?**  
**Answer:** A difference.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:22

---

### Jude 1:23

**Q25. And others save with what, pulling them out of the fire?**  
**Answer:** Fear.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:23

---

### Jude 1:24

**Q26. Now unto him that is able to keep you from what?**  
**Answer:** Falling.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:24

---

### Jude 1:25

**Q27. To the only wise God our Saviour, be glory and majesty, dominion and power, both when?**  
**Answer:** Now and ever.  
**Verse:** Jude 1:25
