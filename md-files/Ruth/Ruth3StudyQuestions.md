# Ruth 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ruth 3:1

**Q1. Who said unto Ruth her daughter in law, Naomi her mother in law?**  
**Answer:** Naomi her mother in law.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:1

**Q2. What did Naomi ask regarding Ruth?**  
**Answer:** My daughter, shall I not seek rest for thee, that it may be well with thee?  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:1

**Q3. For whom was Naomi seeking rest?**  
**Answer:** For Ruth her daughter in law.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:1

---

### Ruth 3:2

**Q1. Who is Boaz connected to, according to Naomi?**  
**Answer:** With whose maidens thou wast.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:2

**Q2. What is Boaz expected to do this night?**  
**Answer:** Winnow barley tonight in the threshingfloor.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:2

**Q3. Whose kinsman is Boaz?**  
**Answer:** Our kinsman.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:2

---

### Ruth 3:3

**Q1. What did Naomi tell Ruth to wash and anoint herself with?**  
**Answer:** Wash thyself therefore, and anoint thee.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:3

**Q2. What should Ruth put upon her after washing and anointing?**  
**Answer:** Put thy raiment upon thee.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:3

**Q3. Where should Ruth go down after putting on her raiment?**  
**Answer:** To the floor.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:3

---

### Ruth 3:4

**Q1. When should Ruth mark the place where Boaz lieth?**  
**Answer:** When he lieth down.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:4

**Q2. What should Ruth uncover after he lies down?**  
**Answer:** His feet.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:4

**Q3. What will Boaz do after she uncovers his feet and lies down?**  
**Answer:** He will tell thee what thou shalt do.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:4

---

### Ruth 3:5

**Q1. What did Ruth say she would do in response to Naomi?**  
**Answer:** All that thou sayest unto me I will do.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:5

**Q2. To whom did Ruth give her answer?**  
**Answer:** Unto her.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:5

**Q3. Whose instructions did Ruth agree to follow completely?**  
**Answer:** Naomi's.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:5

---

### Ruth 3:6

**Q1. Where did Ruth go down?**  
**Answer:** Unto the floor.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:6

**Q2. Whose instructions did Ruth do according to?**  
**Answer:** According to all that her mother in law bade her.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:6

**Q3. Who bade Ruth what to do?**  
**Answer:** Her mother in law.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:6

---

### Ruth 3:7

**Q1. What was the state of Boaz's heart when he had eaten and drunk?**  
**Answer:** His heart was merry.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:7

**Q2. Where did Boaz go to lie down?**  
**Answer:** At the end of the heap of corn.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:7

**Q3. How did Ruth come to Boaz?**  
**Answer:** Softly, and uncovered his feet, and laid her down.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:7

---

### Ruth 3:8

**Q1. What happened at midnight?**  
**Answer:** The man was afraid.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:8

**Q2. What did the man do at midnight?**  
**Answer:** Turned himself.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:8

**Q3. What did the man discover lying at his feet?**  
**Answer:** A woman lay at his feet.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:8

---

### Ruth 3:9

**Q1. What question did Boaz ask the woman lying at his feet?**  
**Answer:** Who art thou?  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:9

**Q2. What was the woman's answer concerning her identity?**  
**Answer:** I am Ruth thine handmaid.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:9

**Q3. What did Ruth ask Boaz to spread over her?**  
**Answer:** Spread therefore thy skirt over thine handmaid.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:9

---

### Ruth 3:10

**Q1. How did Boaz bless Ruth?**  
**Answer:** Blessed be thou of the LORD, my daughter.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:10

**Q2. In what matter did Ruth show more kindness at the latter end than at the beginning?**  
**Answer:** Inasmuch as thou followedst not young men, whether poor or rich.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:10

**Q3. Whom did Ruth choose not to follow?**  
**Answer:** Young men, whether poor or rich.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:10

---

### Ruth 3:11

**Q1. What did Boaz tell Ruth not to fear?**  
**Answer:** Fear not.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:11

**Q2. What did Boaz promise to do for Ruth regarding all she required?**  
**Answer:** I will do to thee all that thou requirest.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:11

**Q3. What did all the city of his people know about Ruth?**  
**Answer:** That thou art a virtuous woman.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:11

---

### Ruth 3:12

**Q1. What did Boaz acknowledge regarding his relationship to Ruth?**  
**Answer:** And now it is true that I am thy near kinsman.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:12

**Q2. What is noted about another kinsman?**  
**Answer:** Howbeit there is a kinsman nearer than I.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:12

**Q3. How does Boaz describe the other kinsman compared to himself?**  
**Answer:** Nearer than I.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:12

---

### Ruth 3:13

**Q1. Where did Boaz tell Ruth to tarry that night?**  
**Answer:** Tarry this night.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:13

**Q2. What would happen in the morning if the kinsman performed the part of a kinsman?**  
**Answer:** Well, let him do the kinsman's part.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:13

**Q3. What did Boaz swear by the LORD he would do if the kinsman would not do the part of a kinsman?**  
**Answer:** Then will I do the part of a kinsman to thee, as the LORD liveth.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:13

---

### Ruth 3:14

**Q1. Where did Ruth lie until the morning?**  
**Answer:** At his feet until the morning.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:14

**Q2. When did Ruth rise up?**  
**Answer:** Before one could know another.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:14

**Q3. What did Boaz say regarding anyone knowing that a woman came into the floor?**  
**Answer:** Let it not be known that a woman came into the floor.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:14

---

### Ruth 3:15

**Q1. What did Boaz ask Ruth to bring?**  
**Answer:** The vail that thou hast upon thee, and hold it.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:15

**Q2. How much barley did Boaz measure and lay on Ruth?**  
**Answer:** Six measures of barley.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:15

**Q3. Where did Ruth go after Boaz laid the barley on her?**  
**Answer:** Into the city.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:15

---

### Ruth 3:16

**Q1. To whom did Ruth come after going into the city?**  
**Answer:** To her mother in law.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:16

**Q2. What question did her mother in law ask Ruth?**  
**Answer:** Who art thou, my daughter?  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:16

**Q3. What did Ruth tell her mother in law?**  
**Answer:** All that the man had done to her.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:16

---

### Ruth 3:17

**Q1. How much barley did Ruth say Boaz gave her?**  
**Answer:** These six measures of barley.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:17

**Q2. What reason did Ruth give for Boaz giving her the barley?**  
**Answer:** For he said to me, Go not empty unto thy mother in law.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:17

**Q3. Who was supposed not to be gone to empty according to Boaz?**  
**Answer:** Thy mother in law.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:17

---

### Ruth 3:18

**Q1. What did Naomi tell Ruth to do until she knew how the matter would fall?**  
**Answer:** Sit still, my daughter.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:18

**Q2. What would the man not do until he had finished the thing that day?**  
**Answer:** He will not rest.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:18

**Q3. When would the man finish the thing?**  
**Answer:** This day.  
**Verse:** Ruth 3:18
