# Ruth 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ruth 1:1

**Q1. Who judged in the days when the judges ruled?**  
**Answer:** The judges judged.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:1

**Q2. What happened in the land according to verse 1?**  
**Answer:** There was a famine in the land.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:1

**Q3. From where did a certain man of Bethlehemjudah go to sojourn in the country of Moab?**  
**Answer:** Bethlehemjudah.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:1

---

### Ruth 1:2

**Q4. What was the name of the man who went to sojourn in Moab?**  
**Answer:** Elimelech.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:2

**Q5. What were the names of Elimelech's two sons?**  
**Answer:** Mahlon and Chilion.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:2

**Q6. Of what city and tribe were Elimelech, Naomi, and their sons?**  
**Answer:** Ephrathites of Bethlehemjudah.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:2

---

### Ruth 1:3

**Q7. Who died after they came into the country of Moab?**  
**Answer:** Elimelech Naomi's husband.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:3

**Q8. Who was left after Elimelech died?**  
**Answer:** Naomi and her two sons.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:3

**Q9. Whose husband was Elimelech?**  
**Answer:** Naomi's.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:3

---

### Ruth 1:4

**Q10. Whom did the sons take for their wives in Moab?**  
**Answer:** Women of Moab.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:4

**Q11. What was the name of the one wife?**  
**Answer:** Orpah.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:4

**Q12. What was the name of the other wife?**  
**Answer:** Ruth.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:4

---

### Ruth 1:5

**Q13. Who died also after they had dwelt there about ten years?**  
**Answer:** Mahlon and Chilion both.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:5

**Q14. Who was left behind after her two sons and her husband died?**  
**Answer:** The woman.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:5

**Q15. Of whom was the woman bereft after the deaths of her sons and husband?**  
**Answer:** Her two sons and her husband.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:5

---

### Ruth 1:6

**Q16. Why did Naomi arise with her daughters in law?**  
**Answer:** That she might return from the country of Moab.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:6

**Q17. What had Naomi heard in the country of Moab?**  
**Answer:** How that the Lord had visited his people in giving them bread.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:6

**Q18. From where did Naomi and her daughters in law return?**  
**Answer:** The country of Moab.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:6

---

### Ruth 1:7

**Q19. Where did Naomi go out from the place where she was?**  
**Answer:** Out of the place where she was.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:7

**Q20. Who went with Naomi when she went out of her place?**  
**Answer:** Her two daughters in law with her.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:7

**Q21. Toward what land did they go in the way to return?**  
**Answer:** The land of Judah.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:7

---

### Ruth 1:8

**Q22. To whom did Naomi say, Go, return each to her mother's house?**  
**Answer:** Her two daughters in law.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:8

**Q23. What did Naomi wish the Lord would deal kindly with them as?**  
**Answer:** They have dealt with the dead, and with her.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:8

**Q24. With whom did Naomi say the Lord should deal kindly?**  
**Answer:** Her two daughters in law.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:8

---

### Ruth 1:9

**Q25. What did Naomi pray the Lord would grant them?**  
**Answer:** That ye may find rest, each in the house of her husband.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:9

**Q26. What did Naomi do before she kissed them?**  
**Answer:** She kissed them.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:9

**Q27. What did the daughters in law do and lift up?**  
**Answer:** They lifted up their voice, and wept.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:9

---

### Ruth 1:10

**Q28. What did the daughters in law say unto Naomi?**  
**Answer:** Surely we will return with thee unto thy people.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:10

**Q29. To whom did they say they would return?**  
**Answer:** Unto thy people.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:10

**Q30. Whose people did they want to return to with Naomi?**  
**Answer:** Naomi's people.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:10

---

### Ruth 1:11

**Q31. What did Naomi call her daughters in law in verse 11?**  
**Answer:** My daughters.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:11

**Q32. What question did Naomi ask her daughters in law about sons?**  
**Answer:** Are there yet any more sons in my womb, that they may be your husbands?  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:11

**Q33. What did Naomi ask if she could have?**  
**Answer:** Husbands.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:11

---

### Ruth 1:12

**Q34. How did Naomi describe her age regarding husbands?**  
**Answer:** I am too old to have an husband.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:12

**Q35. What did Naomi say she should do if she should say, I have hope?**  
**Answer:** If I should have an husband also to night, and should also bear sons.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:12

**Q36. What did Naomi ask if they would tarry for?**  
**Answer:** For them till they were grown.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:12

---

### Ruth 1:13

**Q37. What did Naomi ask if they would stay for from having husbands?**  
**Answer:** Would ye stay for them from having husbands?  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:13

**Q38. Why did Naomi say it was much more bitter for her than for them?**  
**Answer:** For the hand of the Lord is gone out against me.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:13

**Q39. Whose daughters did Naomi address in verse 13?**  
**Answer:** My daughters.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:13

---

### Ruth 1:14

**Q40. What did they do again after Naomi spoke?**  
**Answer:** They lifted up their voice, and wept again.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:14

**Q41. Who kissed her mother in law?**  
**Answer:** Orpah.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:14

**Q42. Who clave unto Naomi?**  
**Answer:** Ruth.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:14

---

### Ruth 1:15

**Q43. Who did Naomi say had gone back unto her people, and unto her gods?**  
**Answer:** Thy sister in law.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:15

**Q44. What did Naomi tell Ruth to do following her sister in law?**  
**Answer:** Return thou after thy sister in law.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:15

**Q45. Unto what did Naomi say Orpah had gone back?**  
**Answer:** Unto her people, and unto her gods.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:15

---

### Ruth 1:16

**Q46. What did Ruth tell Naomi not to entreat her to do?**  
**Answer:** To leave thee, or to return from following after thee.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:16

**Q47. Where did Ruth say she would go?**  
**Answer:** Whither thou goest, I will go.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:16

**Q48. Whose people and God did Ruth claim as her own?**  
**Answer:** Thy people shall be my people, and thy God my God.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:16

---

### Ruth 1:17

**Q49. Where did Ruth say she would die and be buried?**  
**Answer:** Where thou diest, will I die, and there will I be buried.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:17

**Q50. What did Ruth invoke the Lord to do to her if anything but death part them?**  
**Answer:** The Lord do so to me, and more also, if ought but death part thee and me.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:17

**Q51. What alone did Ruth say should part her and Naomi?**  
**Answer:** Death.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:17

---

### Ruth 1:18

**Q52. What did Naomi see that Ruth was steadfastly minded to do with her?**  
**Answer:** That she was stedfastly minded to go with her.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:18

**Q53. What did Naomi do when she saw Ruth's steadfast mind?**  
**Answer:** She left speaking unto her.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:18

**Q54. To whom did Naomi leave speaking?**  
**Answer:** Ruth.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:18

---

### Ruth 1:19

**Q55. Until they came to where did they two go?**  
**Answer:** Until they came to Bethlehem.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:19

**Q56. What happened when they were come to Bethlehem?**  
**Answer:** All the city was moved about them.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:19

**Q57. What did the women of the city say when they saw them?**  
**Answer:** Is this Naomi?  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:19

---

### Ruth 1:20

**Q58. What did Naomi tell the women not to call her?**  
**Answer:** Call me Naomi.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:20

**Q59. What name did Naomi tell them to call her instead?**  
**Answer:** Call me Mara.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:20

**Q60. Why did Naomi say to call her Mara?**  
**Answer:** For the Almighty hath dealt very bitterly with me.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:20

---

### Ruth 1:21

**Q61. How did Naomi describe her departure and return?**  
**Answer:** I went out full, and the Lord hath brought me home again empty.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:21

**Q62. Why did Naomi ask why they should call her Naomi?**  
**Answer:** Seeing the Lord hath testified against me, and the Almighty hath afflicted me.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:21

**Q63. Who did Naomi say had testified against her and afflicted her?**  
**Answer:** The Lord and the Almighty.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:21

---

### Ruth 1:22

**Q64. Who returned out of the country of Moab with Naomi?**  
**Answer:** Naomi, and Ruth the Moabitess, her daughter in law.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:22

**Q65. From where did Ruth and Naomi return?**  
**Answer:** Out of the country of Moab.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:22

**Q66. At what time of the year did they return to Bethlehem?**  
**Answer:** In the beginning of barley harvest.  
**Verse:** Ruth 1:22
