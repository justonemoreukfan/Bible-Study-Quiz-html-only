# Ruth 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ruth 4:1

**Q1. Where did Boaz go up?**  
**Answer:** To the gate.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:1

**Q2. Who did Boaz see when he sat down there?**  
**Answer:** The kinsman of whom Boaz spake.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:1

**Q3. What did Boaz say to the kinsman when he passed by?**  
**Answer:** Ho, such a one! turn aside, sit down here.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:1

---

### Ruth 4:2

**Q1. How many men of the elders of the city did Boaz take?**  
**Answer:** Ten men.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:2

**Q2. What did Boaz tell the ten men to do?**  
**Answer:** Sit ye down here.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:2

**Q3. Where did the men sit down after Boaz spoke to them?**  
**Answer:** They sat down.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:2

---

### Ruth 4:3

**Q1. Who did Boaz speak unto concerning the parcel of land?**  
**Answer:** The kinsman.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:3

**Q2. Whose parcel of land was it that Naomi was selling?**  
**Answer:** Which was our brother Elimelech's.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:3

---

### Ruth 4:4

**Q1. What did Boaz think to do in the ear of the inhabitants and elders?**  
**Answer:** Advertise thee.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:4

**Q2. Who did Boaz say should redeem the land if the kinsman would not redeem it?**  
**Answer:** Boaz.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:4

**Q3. What was the kinsman's response regarding redemption?**  
**Answer:** I will redeem it.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:4

---

### Ruth 4:5

**Q1. From whose hand must the kinsman buy the field on the day he buys it?**  
**Answer:** From the hand of Naomi.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:5

**Q2. Whom else must the kinsman buy the field from to raise up the name of the dead?**  
**Answer:** Ruth the Moabitess, the wife of the dead.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:5

**Q3. What was the purpose of buying the field from Ruth?**  
**Answer:** To raise up the name of the dead upon his inheritance.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:5

---

### Ruth 4:6

**Q1. What did the kinsman say he could not do for himself?**  
**Answer:** Redeem it.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:6

**Q2. Why did the kinsman say he could not redeem it?**  
**Answer:** Lest I mar mine own inheritance.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:6

**Q3. What did the kinsman tell Boaz to redeem for himself?**  
**Answer:** My right.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:6

---

### Ruth 4:7

**Q1. What was the manner in former time in Israel concerning redeeming and changing?**  
**Answer:** To confirm all things.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:7

**Q2. What did a man pluck off to give to his neighbour?**  
**Answer:** His shoe.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:7

**Q3. What was this custom in Israel called?**  
**Answer:** A testimony in Israel.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:7

---

### Ruth 4:8

**Q1. What did the kinsman say to Boaz regarding the purchase?**  
**Answer:** Buy it for thee.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:8

**Q2. What did the kinsman do after speaking to Boaz?**  
**Answer:** He drew off his shoe.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:8

---

### Ruth 4:9

**Q1. To whom did Boaz speak after the transaction?**  
**Answer:** Unto the elders, and unto all the people.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:9

**Q2. What did Boaz call the elders and people to be this day?**  
**Answer:** Witnesses.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:9

**Q3. Whose all things did Boaz buy from the hand of Naomi?**  
**Answer:** All that was Elimelech's, and all that was Chilion's and Mahlon's.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:9

---

### Ruth 4:10

**Q1. Whom did Boaz purchase to be his wife?**  
**Answer:** Ruth the Moabitess, the wife of Mahlon.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:10

**Q2. Why did Boaz purchase Ruth to be his wife?**  
**Answer:** To raise up the name of the dead upon his inheritance.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:10

**Q3. From where should the name of the dead not be cut off?**  
**Answer:** From among his brethren, and from the gate of his place.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:10

---

### Ruth 4:11

**Q1. Who said, "We are witnesses"?**  
**Answer:** All the people that were in the gate, and the elders.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:11

**Q2. To whom did the people and elders pray the Lord would make the woman like?**  
**Answer:** Rachel and like Leah.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:11

**Q3. What did Rachel and Leah do regarding the house of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Did build the house of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:11

---

### Ruth 4:12

**Q1. Whose house did the elders say Boaz's house should be like?**  
**Answer:** The house of Pharez.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:12

**Q2. Who bare Pharez unto Judah?**  
**Answer:** Tamar.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:12

**Q3. What kind of seed did the people pray the Lord would give Boaz of this young woman?**  
**Answer:** Seed.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:12

---

### Ruth 4:13

**Q1. Who took Ruth to be his wife?**  
**Answer:** Boaz.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:13

**Q2. When Boaz went in unto Ruth, what did the Lord give her?**  
**Answer:** Conception.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:13

**Q3. What did Ruth bare after conception?**  
**Answer:** A son.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:13

---

### Ruth 4:14

**Q1. What did the women say unto Naomi after the child was born?**  
**Answer:** Blessed be the Lord, which hath not left thee this day without a kinsman.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:14

**Q2. What did the women say the child's name should be famous in?**  
**Answer:** Israel.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:14

---

### Ruth 4:15

**Q1. What did the women say the child would be unto Naomi?**  
**Answer:** A restorer of her life, and a nourisher of thine old age.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:15

**Q2. Who was the child born unto that loved Naomi?**  
**Answer:** Thy daughter in law.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:15

**Q3. How did the women describe Ruth's value to Naomi compared to sons?**  
**Answer:** Which is better to thee than seven sons.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:15

---

### Ruth 4:16

**Q1. What did Naomi do with the child?**  
**Answer:** Took the child.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:16

**Q2. Where did Naomi lay the child?**  
**Answer:** In her bosom.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:16

**Q3. What became of Naomi regarding the child?**  
**Answer:** Became nurse unto it.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:16

---

### Ruth 4:17

**Q1. What did the women her neighbours give the child?**  
**Answer:** A name.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:17

**Q2. What name did the women give the child?**  
**Answer:** Obed.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:17

**Q3. Whose father was Obed?**  
**Answer:** The father of Jesse, the father of David.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:17

---

### Ruth 4:18

**Q1. Whose generations are these?**  
**Answer:** Pharez.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:18

**Q2. Who begat Hezron?**  
**Answer:** Pharez.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:18

---

### Ruth 4:19

**Q1. Who begat Ram?**  
**Answer:** Hezron.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:19

**Q2. Who begat Aminadab?**  
**Answer:** Ram.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:19

---

### Ruth 4:20

**Q1. Who begat Nahshon?**  
**Answer:** Aminadab.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:20

**Q2. Who begat Salmon?**  
**Answer:** Nahshon.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:20

---

### Ruth 4:21

**Q1. Who begat Booz?**  
**Answer:** Salmon.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:21

**Q2. Who begat Obed?**  
**Answer:** Booz.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:21

---

### Ruth 4:22

**Q1. Who begat Jesse?**  
**Answer:** Obed.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:22

**Q2. Who begat David?**  
**Answer:** Jesse.  
**Verse:** Ruth 4:22
