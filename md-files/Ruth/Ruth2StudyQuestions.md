# Ruth 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ruth 2:1

**Q1. What was the name of Naomi's kinsman?**  
**Answer:** Boaz.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:1

**Q2. Whose family was Boaz of?**  
**Answer:** The family of Elimelech.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:1

**Q3. What kind of man was Boaz described as?**  
**Answer:** A mighty man of wealth.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:1

---

### Ruth 2:2

**Q1. Who spoke to Naomi in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Ruth the Moabitess.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:2

**Q2. Where did Ruth ask to go?**  
**Answer:** To the field.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:2

**Q3. What did Ruth want to do in the field?**  
**Answer:** Glean ears of corn after him in whose sight I shall find grace.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:2

---

### Ruth 2:3

**Q1. Where did Ruth go and glean?**  
**Answer:** In the field.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:3

**Q2. After whom did Ruth glean in the field?**  
**Answer:** The reapers.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:3

**Q3. Whose part of the field did her hap light on?**  
**Answer:** The part belonging unto Boaz.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:3

---

### Ruth 2:4

**Q1. Who came from Bethlehem in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Boaz.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:4

**Q2. What did Boaz say unto the reapers?**  
**Answer:** The LORD be with you.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:4

**Q3. What did the reapers answer Boaz?**  
**Answer:** The LORD bless thee.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:4

---

### Ruth 2:5

**Q1. Whom did Boaz speak to regarding the damsel?**  
**Answer:** His servant that was set over the reapers.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:5

**Q2. What did Boaz ask his servant about the damsel?**  
**Answer:** Whose damsel is this?  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:5

**Q3. What was the servant's relation or knowledge of the damsel in the verse?**  
**Answer:** It was the servant that was set over the reapers.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:5

---

### Ruth 2:6

**Q1. Who answered Boaz?**  
**Answer:** The servant that was set over the reapers.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:6

**Q2. What damsel did the servant say she was?**  
**Answer:** The Moabitish damsel that came back with Naomi out of the country of Moab.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:6

**Q3. What did the servant say about where she came from?**  
**Answer:** Out of the country of Moab.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:6

---

### Ruth 2:7

**Q1. What did Ruth say she prayed to do?**  
**Answer:** I pray you, let me glean and gather after the reapers among the sheaves.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:7

**Q2. How long did Ruth continue from the morning until now?**  
**Answer:** She came, and hath continued even from the morning until now.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:7

**Q3. Where did Ruth take a little rest?**  
**Answer:** In the house.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:7

---

### Ruth 2:8

**Q1. Who spoke to Ruth in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Boaz.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:8

**Q2. Where did Boaz tell Ruth not to go?**  
**Answer:** To glean in another field.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:8

**Q3. Where did Boaz tell Ruth to abide?**  
**Answer:** Fast by my maidens.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:8

---

### Ruth 2:9

**Q1. What did Boaz tell Ruth to keep her eyes on?**  
**Answer:** The field that they do reap.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:9

**Q2. Whom did Boaz say he had charged not to touch her?**  
**Answer:** The young men.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:9

**Q3. Where was Ruth told to go when she was athirst?**  
**Answer:** Unto the vessels, and drink of that which the young men have drawn.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:9

---

### Ruth 2:10

**Q1. What did Ruth do when Boaz spoke to her?**  
**Answer:** She fell on her face, and bowed herself to the ground.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:10

**Q2. What did Ruth ask Boaz about why she found grace in his eyes?**  
**Answer:** Why have I found grace in thine eyes, that thou shouldest take knowledge of me, seeing I am a stranger?  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:10

**Q3. What did Ruth call herself in relation to Boaz?**  
**Answer:** A stranger.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:10

---

### Ruth 2:11

**Q1. Who answered and said unto Ruth in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Boaz.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:11

**Q2. What had been fully shewed unto Boaz?**  
**Answer:** All that thou hast done unto thy mother in law since the death of thine husband.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:11

**Q3. What did Boaz say Ruth had left behind?**  
**Answer:** Thy father and thy mother, and the land of thy nativity.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:11

---

### Ruth 2:12

**Q1. What did Boaz say the LORD should do for Ruth?**  
**Answer:** Recompense thy work.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:12

**Q2. Whose wings did Boaz say Ruth was come to trust under?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:12

**Q3. What kind of full reward did Boaz mention?**  
**Answer:** A full reward be given thee of the LORD God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:12

---

### Ruth 2:13

**Q1. What did Ruth say she had found in Boaz's sight?**  
**Answer:** Favour.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:13

**Q2. How did Ruth say Boaz had spoken?**  
**Answer:** Comfortably unto thine handmaid.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:13

**Q3. What did Ruth say she was not, even though she was comforted?**  
**Answer:** Though I be not like unto one of thine handmaidens.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:13

---

### Ruth 2:14

**Q1. When mealtime came, what did Boaz say to Ruth?**  
**Answer:** Come thou hither, and eat of the bread, and dip thy morsel in the vinegar.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:14

**Q2. Where did Ruth sit beside the reapers?**  
**Answer:** Beside the reapers.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:14

**Q3. What did Boaz reach unto Ruth?**  
**Answer:** Parched corn.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:14

---

### Ruth 2:15

**Q1. When Ruth was risen up to glean, what did Boaz command his young men to do?**  
**Answer:** Glean even among the sheaves, and reproach her not.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:15

**Q2. Did Boaz command the young men to reproach Ruth?**  
**Answer:** No.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:15

**Q3. Where did Boaz tell the young men she could glean?**  
**Answer:** Even among the sheaves.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:15

---

### Ruth 2:16

**Q1. What did Boaz tell the young men to let fall purposely for her?**  
**Answer:** Handfuls of corn.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:16

**Q2. What did Boaz tell the young men to leave for her?**  
**Answer:** That she may glean them.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:16

**Q3. What did Boaz instruct the young men not to do to her concerning the dropped corn?**  
**Answer:** Rebuke her not.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:16

---

### Ruth 2:17

**Q1. Where did Ruth glean until?**  
**Answer:** Until even.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:17

**Q2. What did Ruth do with what she had gleaned after beating it out?**  
**Answer:** It was about an ephah of barley.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:17

**Q3. Approximately how much barley did Ruth glean?**  
**Answer:** An ephah of barley.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:17

---

### Ruth 2:18

**Q1. Where did Ruth take up the gleaned barley and go?**  
**Answer:** Into the city.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:18

**Q2. To whom did Ruth show what she had brought forth?**  
**Answer:** Her mother in law.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:18

**Q3. What did Ruth take out and give to Naomi besides what she brought home?**  
**Answer:** That she had reserved after she was sufficed.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:18

---

### Ruth 2:19

**Q1. What did Naomi ask Ruth where she had gleaned?**  
**Answer:** Where hast thou gleaned today? and where wroughtest thou?  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:19

**Q2. What did Naomi say about the man that did take knowledge of Ruth?**  
**Answer:** Blessed be he that did take knowledge of thee.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:19

**Q3. What did Ruth tell her mother in law about the man she worked with?**  
**Answer:** The man's name with whom I wrought today is Boaz.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:19

---

### Ruth 2:20

**Q1. What did Naomi say about Boaz regarding the LORD's mercy?**  
**Answer:** Blessed be he of the LORD, who hath not left off his kindness to the living and to the dead.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:20

**Q2. What did Naomi say Boaz was to them?**  
**Answer:** Near of kin unto us.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:20

**Q3. What category of kinsman did Naomi call Boaz?**  
**Answer:** One of our next kinsmen.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:20

---

### Ruth 2:21

**Q1. Who spoke to Naomi in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Ruth the Moabitess.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:21

**Q2. What did Boaz say to Ruth according to her report?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt keep fast by my young men, until they have ended all my harvest.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:21

**Q3. Until when did Boaz tell Ruth to keep fast by his young men?**  
**Answer:** Until they have ended all my harvest.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:21

---

### Ruth 2:22

**Q1. Who spoke to Ruth in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Naomi.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:22

**Q2. To whom did Naomi call Ruth her daughter?**  
**Answer:** Ruth.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:22

**Q3. Why did Naomi say it was good for Ruth to go out with Boaz's maidens?**  
**Answer:** That they meet thee not in any other field.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:22

---

### Ruth 2:23

**Q1. Whose maidens did Ruth keep fast by to glean?**  
**Answer:** Boaz's maidens.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:23

**Q2. Until the end of what harvests did Ruth glean?**  
**Answer:** Barley harvest and of wheat harvest.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:23

**Q3. With whom did Ruth dwell after the harvests?**  
**Answer:** Her mother in law.  
**Verse:** Ruth 2:23
