### John 17:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of John 17:1?**  
**Answer:** These words spake Jesus, and lifted up his eyes to heaven, and said, Father, the hour is come.  
**Verse:** John 17:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in John 17:1?**  
**Answer:** glorify thy Son, that thy Son also may glorify thee.  
**Verse:** John 17:1

**Q3. According to John 17:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** These words spake Jesus, and lifted up his eyes to heaven, and said, Father, the hour is come; glorify thy Son, that thy Son also may glorify thee:.  
**Verse:** John 17:1
