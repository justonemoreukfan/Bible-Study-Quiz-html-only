# John 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### John 9:1
**Q1. What does the first part of John 9:1 state?**  
**Answer:** And as Jesus passed by.  
**Verse:** John 9:1

**Q2. What does the second part of John 9:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** he saw a man which was blind from his birth.  
**Verse:** John 9:1

**Q3. What is recorded in John 9:1?**  
**Answer:** And as Jesus passed by, he saw a man which was blind from his birth.  
**Verse:** John 9:1

---
