# John 21 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### John 21:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of John 21:1?**  
**Answer:** After these things Jesus shewed himself again to the disciples at the sea of Tiberias.  
**Verse:** John 21:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in John 21:1?**  
**Answer:** and on this wise shewed he himself.  
**Verse:** John 21:1

**Q3. According to John 21:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** After these things Jesus shewed himself again to the disciples at the sea of Tiberias; and on this wise shewed he himself.  
**Verse:** John 21:1
