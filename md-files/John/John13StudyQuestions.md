### John 13:1
**Q1. According to John 13:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Now before the feast of the passover.  
**Verse:** John 13:1

**Q2. What is declared next in John 13:1?**  
**Answer:** when Jesus knew that his hour was come that he should depart out of this world unto the Father.  
**Verse:** John 13:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in John 13:1?**  
**Answer:** having loved his own which were in the world, he loved them unto the end.  
**Verse:** John 13:1
