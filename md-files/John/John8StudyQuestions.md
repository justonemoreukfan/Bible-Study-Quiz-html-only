**Answer:** Then said the Jews unto him, Now we know that thou hast a devil. Abraham is dead, and the prophets.  
**Verse:** John 8:52

**Q155. What additional detail is declared in John 8:52?**  
**Answer:** and thou sayest, If a man keep my saying, he shall never taste of death.  
**Verse:** John 8:52
