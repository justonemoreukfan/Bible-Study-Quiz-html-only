### John 15:1
**Q1. What does the first part of John 15:1 state?**  
**Answer:** I am the true vine.  
**Verse:** John 15:1

**Q2. What does the second part of John 15:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** and my Father is the husbandman.  
**Verse:** John 15:1

**Q3. What is recorded in John 15:1?**  
**Answer:** I am the true vine, and my Father is the husbandman.  
**Verse:** John 15:1
