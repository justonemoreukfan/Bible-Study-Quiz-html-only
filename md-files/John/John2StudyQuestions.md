### John 2:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of John 2:1?**  
**Answer:** And the third day there was a marriage in Cana of Galilee.  
**Verse:** John 2:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in John 2:1?**  
**Answer:** and the mother of Jesus was there.  
**Verse:** John 2:1

**Q3. According to John 2:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And the third day there was a marriage in Cana of Galilee; and the mother of Jesus was there:.
