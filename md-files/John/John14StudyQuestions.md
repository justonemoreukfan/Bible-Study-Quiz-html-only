### John 14:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of John 14:1?**  
**Answer:** Let not your heart be troubled.  
**Verse:** John 14:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in John 14:1?**  
**Answer:** ye believe in God, believe also in me.  
**Verse:** John 14:1

**Q3. According to John 14:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Let not your heart be troubled: ye believe in God, believe also in me.  
**Verse:** John 14:1
