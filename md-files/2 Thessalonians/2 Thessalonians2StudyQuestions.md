# 2 Thessalonians 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Thessalonians 2:1

**Q1. What are we beseeched by concerning the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** By the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, and by our gathering together unto him.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:1

**Q2. What should we not be soon shaken in?**  
**Answer:** In mind.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:1

**Q3. What should we not be troubled by?**  
**Answer:** Neither by spirit, nor by word, nor by letter as from us.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:1

---

### 2 Thessalonians 2:2

**Q4. What should not be done soon?**  
**Answer:** That ye be not soon shaken in mind, or be troubled.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:2

**Q5. How might someone falsely claim the day of Christ is at hand?**  
**Answer:** Neither by spirit, nor by word, nor by letter as from us.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:2

**Q6. What day is falsely said to be at hand in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The day of Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:2

---

### 2 Thessalonians 2:3

**Q7. What should no man deceive you by?**  
**Answer:** By any means.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:3

**Q8. What must happen first before that day comes?**  
**Answer:** Except there come a falling away first.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:3

**Q9. Who must be revealed first?**  
**Answer:** That man of sin be revealed, the son of perdition.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:3

---

### 2 Thessalonians 2:4

**Q10. What does the man of sin oppose and exalt himself above?**  
**Answer:** Above all that is called God, or that is worshipped.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:4

**Q11. Where does he as God sit?**  
**Answer:** In the temple of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:4

**Q12. What does he shew himself to be?**  
**Answer:** That he is God.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:4

---

### 2 Thessalonians 2:5

**Q13. What did Paul do while he was yet with them?**  
**Answer:** Remember ye not, that, when I was yet with you, I told you these things?  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:5

**Q14. What did Paul tell them when he was with them?**  
**Answer:** These things.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:5

**Q15. Who is speaking in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Paul (implied by "when I was yet with you").  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:5

---

### 2 Thessalonians 2:6

**Q16. What do they know now?**  
**Answer:** And now ye know what withholdeth.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:6

**Q17. Why is the withholding thing known?**  
**Answer:** That he might be revealed in his time.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:6

**Q18. When is he to be revealed?**  
**Answer:** In his time.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:6

---

### 2 Thessalonians 2:7

**Q19. What mystery is already working?**  
**Answer:** The mystery of iniquity.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:7

**Q20. Who will continue to withhold until he be taken out of the way?**  
**Answer:** He who now letteth.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:7

**Q21. What is already at work?**  
**Answer:** The mystery of iniquity.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:7

---

### 2 Thessalonians 2:8

**Q22. Who shall then be revealed?**  
**Answer:** That Wicked.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:8

**Q23. What will the Lord consume him with?**  
**Answer:** With the spirit of his mouth.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:8

**Q24. How will the Lord destroy him?**  
**Answer:** With the brightness of his coming.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:8

---

### 2 Thessalonians 2:9

**Q25. Whose coming is after the working of Satan?**  
**Answer:** Even him, whose coming is after the working of Satan.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:9

**Q26. With what power and signs will he come?**  
**Answer:** With all power and signs.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:9

**Q27. What kind of wonders will accompany his coming?**  
**Answer:** Lying wonders.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:9

---

### 2 Thessalonians 2:10

**Q28. What kind of deceivableness of unrighteousness will be used?**  
**Answer:** With all deceivableness of unrighteousness.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:10

**Q29. Who are the ones being deceived?**  
**Answer:** In them that perish.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:10

**Q30. Why do they perish according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Because they received not the love of the truth, that they might be saved.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:10

---

### 2 Thessalonians 2:11

**Q31. What will God send unto them?**  
**Answer:** Strong delusion.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:11

**Q32. Why will God send strong delusion?**  
**Answer:** That they should believe a lie.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:11

**Q33. What will they believe because of this delusion?**  
**Answer:** A lie.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:11

---

### 2 Thessalonians 2:12

**Q34. Who will be damned?**  
**Answer:** That they all might be damned.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:12

**Q35. What did they not believe?**  
**Answer:** Who believed not the truth.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:12

---

### 2 Thessalonians 2:13

**Q36. Who are we bound to give thanks always to God for?**  
**Answer:** Brethren beloved of the Lord.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:13

**Q37. From the beginning, what has God chosen you to?**  
**Answer:** Salvation.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:13

**Q38. Through what means has God chosen you to salvation?**  
**Answer:** Through sanctification of the Spirit and belief of the truth.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:13

---

### 2 Thessalonians 2:14

**Q39. Whereunto did He call you?**  
**Answer:** By our gospel.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:14

**Q40. For what purpose were you called by the gospel?**  
**Answer:** To the obtaining of the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:14

**Q41. Whose glory are you called to obtain?**  
**Answer:** The glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:14

---

### 2 Thessalonians 2:15

**Q42. Therefore, what should the brethren do?**  
**Answer:** Stand fast.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:15

**Q43. What should they hold fast?**  
**Answer:** The traditions which ye have been taught.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:15

**Q44. How should the traditions have been taught?**  
**Answer:** Whether by word, or our epistle.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:15

---

### 2 Thessalonians 2:16

**Q45. Who loved us and hath given us everlasting consolation?**  
**Answer:** Now our Lord Jesus Christ himself, and God, even our Father.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:16

**Q46. What has God, even our Father, given us through grace?**  
**Answer:** Everlasting consolation.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:16

**Q47. What kind of hope has He given us?**  
**Answer:** Good hope through grace.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:16

---

### 2 Thessalonians 2:17

**Q48. What should the Lord comfort?**  
**Answer:** Comfort your hearts.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:17

**Q49. What should the Lord stablish you in?**  
**Answer:** In every good word and work.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:17

**Q50. Who is asked to comfort and stablish your hearts?**  
**Answer:** Our Lord Jesus Christ himself, and God, even our Father.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 2:17
