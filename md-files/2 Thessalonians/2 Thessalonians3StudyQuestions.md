# 2 Thessalonians 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Thessalonians 3:1

**Q1. What did Paul ask the Thessalonians to pray for regarding them?**  
**Answer:** That the word of the Lord may have free course, and be glorified.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:1

**Q2. How did Paul say the word of the Lord should act among them?**  
**Answer:** Even as it is with you.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:1

**Q3. Who was addressed in this prayer request alongside Paul?**  
**Answer:** And us.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:1

---

### 2 Thessalonians 3:2

**Q1. What did Paul ask to be delivered from?**  
**Answer:** From unreasonable and wicked men.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:2

**Q2. What reason is given regarding men in this verse?**  
**Answer:** For all men have not faith.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:2

**Q3. Whose deliverance was Paul requesting in this verse?**  
**Answer:** That we may be delivered.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:2

---

### 2 Thessalonians 3:3

**Q1. What is the Lord described as being in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Faithful.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:3

**Q2. What will the Lord establish and keep you from?**  
**Answer:** From evil.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:3

**Q3. What action will the Lord perform toward you according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Shall stablish you.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:3

---

### 2 Thessalonians 3:4

**Q1. What did the apostles have confidence in regarding the Lord concerning the Thessalonians?**  
**Answer:** That ye both do and will do the things which we command you.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:4

**Q2. Who expressed confidence in the believers in this verse?**  
**Answer:** We have confidence in the Lord.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:4

**Q3. What actions were the believers expected to take?**  
**Answer:** Both do and will do the things which we command you.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:4

---

### 2 Thessalonians 3:5

**Q1. Where did the Lord direct the hearts of the people?**  
**Answer:** Into the love of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:5

**Q2. Whose patience was the Lord to direct their hearts into?**  
**Answer:** Into the patient waiting for Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:5

**Q3. What action did they pray the Lord would direct?**  
**Answer:** Direct your hearts.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:5

---

### 2 Thessalonians 3:6

**Q1. In whose name did they command the brethren to withdraw themselves?**  
**Answer:** In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:6

**Q2. From whom should the brethren withdraw themselves?**  
**Answer:** From every brother that walketh disorderly.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:6

**Q3. What specific teaching did the disorderly brother fail to follow?**  
**Answer:** After the tradition which he received of us.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:6

---

### 2 Thessalonians 3:7

**Q1. What did the brethren know they ought to do regarding the apostles?**  
**Answer:** How ye ought to follow us.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:7

**Q2. How did the apostles behave when they were among the Thessalonians?**  
**Answer:** For we behaved not ourselves disorderly among you.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:7

**Q3. What is said about the behavior of the apostles while they were present?**  
**Answer:** We behaved not ourselves disorderly among you.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:7

---

### 2 Thessalonians 3:8

**Q1. Whose bread did they not eat for nought?**  
**Answer:** Any man's bread.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:8

**Q2. How did they work to avoid being chargeable to any?**  
**Answer:** With labour and travail night and day.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:8

**Q3. What schedule of labor did they keep?**  
**Answer:** Labour and travail night and day.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:8

---

### 2 Thessalonians 3:9

**Q1. Why did they not use power, even though they had it?**  
**Answer:** To make ourselves an ensample unto you to follow us.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:9

**Q2. What did the apostles lack regarding power?**  
**Answer:** Not because we have not power.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:9

**Q3. What was the purpose of being an ensample?**  
**Answer:** To follow us.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:9

---

### 2 Thessalonians 3:10

**Q1. What rule was given regarding work and eating?**  
**Answer:** That if any would not work, neither should he eat.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:10

**Q2. What was commanded even when they were with the Thessalonians?**  
**Answer:** This we commanded you.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:10

**Q3. What is the condition for a person not to eat?**  
**Answer:** If any would not work.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:10

---

### 2 Thessalonians 3:11

**Q1. What did they hear that some were doing?**  
**Answer:** That there are some which walk among you disorderly.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:11

**Q2. What kind of work were these disorderly people failing to do?**  
**Answer:** Working not at all.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:11

**Q3. Instead of working, what were these people doing?**  
**Answer:** But are busybodies.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:11

---

### 2 Thessalonians 3:12

**Q1. What did they command and exhort by our Lord Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** That with quietness they work.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:12

**Q2. What kind of bread were the people commanded to eat?**  
**Answer:** And eat their own bread.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:12

**Q3. Through whom were these things commanded and exhorted?**  
**Answer:** By our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:12

---

### 2 Thessalonians 3:13

**Q1. What should the brethren not be weary in?**  
**Answer:** In well doing.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:13

**Q2. Who is addressed as not being weary in well doing?**  
**Answer:** But ye, brethren.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:13

**Q3. What instruction is given to the brethren in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Be not weary in well doing.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:13

---

### 2 Thessalonians 3:14

**Q1. What should be done to a person who does not obey the epistle's word?**  
**Answer:** Note that man, and have no company with him.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:14

**Q2. What is the purpose of having no company with that man?**  
**Answer:** That he may be ashamed.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:14

**Q3. By what means was this word communicated?**  
**Answer:** By this epistle.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:14

---

### 2 Thessalonians 3:15

**Q1. How should the person not be counted?**  
**Answer:** Yet count him not as an enemy.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:15

**Q2. How should the person be admonished?**  
**Answer:** But admonish him as a brother.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:15

**Q3. What action should be taken toward him as a brother?**  
**Answer:** Admonish him as a brother.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:15

---

### 2 Thessalonians 3:16

**Q1. Who is identified as the Lord of peace?**  
**Answer:** Now the Lord of peace himself.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:16

**Q2. What did the writer wish the Lord to give the believers?**  
**Answer:** Give you peace always by all means.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:16

**Q3. Who did the writer say the Lord should be with?**  
**Answer:** The Lord be with you all.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:16

---

### 2 Thessalonians 3:17

**Q1. Whose salutation was written with Paul's own hand?**  
**Answer:** The salutation of Paul with mine own hand.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:17

**Q2. What is said to be the token in every epistle?**  
**Answer:** Which is the token in every epistle.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:17

**Q3. How did Paul write this salutation?**  
**Answer:** Thus I write.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:17

---

### 2 Thessalonians 3:18

**Q1. Whose grace was wished to be with them all?**  
**Answer:** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:18

**Q2. Who were included in this final blessing?**  
**Answer:** Be with you all.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:18

**Q3. What single word concludes the final blessing?**  
**Answer:** Amen.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 3:18
