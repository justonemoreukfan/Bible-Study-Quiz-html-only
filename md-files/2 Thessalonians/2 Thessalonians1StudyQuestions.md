# 2 Thessalonians 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Thessalonians 1:1

**Q1. Who wrote this epistle along with Paul and Silvanus?**  
**Answer:** Timotheus.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:1

**Q2. Unto whose church in God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ is this written?**  
**Answer:** The church of the Thessalonians.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:1

**Q3. Who are mentioned together with Paul as senders of this epistle?**  
**Answer:** Silvanus and Timotheus.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:1

---

### 2 Thessalonians 1:2

**Q1. What two things are wished from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** Grace unto you, and peace.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:2

**Q2. From whom are grace and peace specifically stated to come?**  
**Answer:** God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:2

**Q3. What title is given to Jesus Christ in this verse regarding the source of grace and peace?**  
**Answer:** The Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:2

---

### 2 Thessalonians 1:3

**Q1. What are the writers bound to thank God always for?**  
**Answer:** You, brethren.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:3

**Q2. Why is it meet that the writers give thanks always for the brethren?**  
**Answer:** Because that your faith groweth exceedingly.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:3

**Q3. What else abounds in every one of them toward each other besides faith?**  
**Answer:** The charity of every one of you all toward each other.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:3

---

### 2 Thessalonians 1:4

**Q1. What do the writers themselves glory in regarding the churches of God?**  
**Answer:** You in the churches of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:4

**Q2. What did the brethren display in all their persecutions and tribulations?**  
**Answer:** Patience and faith.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:4

**Q3. Where did the brethren endure persecutions and tribulations?**  
**Answer:** In all your persecutions and tribulations that ye endure.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:4

---

### 2 Thessalonians 1:5

**Q1. What is the manifest token of the righteous judgment of God?**  
**Answer:** That ye may be counted worthy of the kingdom of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:5

**Q2. What are the brethren suffering for in this verse?**  
**Answer:** For which ye also suffer.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:5

**Q3. The manifest token of God's righteous judgment is for what kingdom?**  
**Answer:** The kingdom of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:5

---

### 2 Thessalonians 1:6

**Q1. What is a righteous thing with God to recompense?**  
**Answer:** Tribulation to them that trouble you.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:6

**Q2. To whom does God deem it a righteous thing to recompense tribulation?**  
**Answer:** Them that trouble you.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:6

**Q3. Whose righteous act is it to recompense tribulation to troublers?**  
**Answer:** God's.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:6

---

### 2 Thessalonians 1:7

**Q1. What is to be given to you who are troubled, along with the writers?**  
**Answer:** Rest.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:7

**Q2. When shall the troubled be given rest with the writers?**  
**Answer:** When the Lord Jesus shall be revealed from heaven.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:7

**Q3. With whom shall the Lord Jesus be revealed from heaven?**  
**Answer:** His mighty angels.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:7

---

### 2 Thessalonians 1:8

**Q1. In what manner shall the Lord Jesus be revealed?**  
**Answer:** In flaming fire.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:8

**Q2. What will the Lord Jesus take vengeance on?**  
**Answer:** Them that know not God.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:8

**Q3. What other group will the Lord Jesus take vengeance on besides those who know not God?**  
**Answer:** Them that obey not the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:8

---

### 2 Thessalonians 1:9

**Q1. What punishment shall those people be punished with?**  
**Answer:** Everlasting destruction.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:9

**Q2. From whose presence shall this everlasting destruction come?**  
**Answer:** From the presence of the Lord.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:9

**Q3. What else shall this destruction come from besides the presence of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** From the glory of his power.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:9

---

### 2 Thessalonians 1:10

**Q1. When He shall come to be glorified in whom, on that day?**  
**Answer:** In his saints.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:10

**Q2. Who shall be admired in that day among all them that believe?**  
**Answer:** Him.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:10

**Q3. Why shall He be admired among all them that believe in that day?**  
**Answer:** Because our testimony among you was believed.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:10

---

### 2 Thessalonians 1:11

**Q1. What do the writers pray always for the brethren regarding their calling?**  
**Answer:** That our God would count you worthy of this calling.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:11

**Q2. What kind of pleasure of goodness does God fulfill?**  
**Answer:** All the good pleasure of his goodness.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:11

**Q3. What work of faith is fulfilled with power?**  
**Answer:** The work of faith.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:11

---

### 2 Thessalonians 1:12

**Q1. What may be glorified in you?**  
**Answer:** The name of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:12

**Q2. In whom may ye also be glorified?**  
**Answer:** In him.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:12

**Q3. According to what is the name of our Lord Jesus Christ glorified in you?**  
**Answer:** According to the grace of our God and the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Thessalonians 1:12
