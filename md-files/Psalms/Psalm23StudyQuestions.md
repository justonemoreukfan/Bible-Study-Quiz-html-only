# Psalm 23 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Psalm 23:1
**Q1. Who is the psalmist's shepherd?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Psalm 23:1

**Q2. What is the psalmist's condition because the LORD is his shepherd?**  
**Answer:** I shall not want.  
**Verse:** Psalm 23:1

**Q3. Who wrote this psalm?**  
**Answer:** David (A Psalm of David).  
**Verse:** Psalm 23:1

---

### Psalm 23:2
**Q4. Where does the shepherd make him to lie down?**  
**Answer:** In green pastures.  
**Verse:** Psalm 23:2

**Q5. Beside what waters does the shepherd lead him?**  
**Answer:** Beside the still waters.  
**Verse:** Psalm 23:2

**Q6. What does lying in green pastures represent?**  
**Answer:** Rest, nourishment, and spiritual peace.  
**Verse:** Psalm 23:2

---

### Psalm 23:3
**Q7. What does the shepherd restore?**  
**Answer:** My soul.  
**Verse:** Psalm 23:3

**Q8. In what paths does the shepherd lead him?**  
**Answer:** In the paths of righteousness.  
**Verse:** Psalm 23:3

**Q9. For whose name's sake does He lead him?**  
**Answer:** For his name's sake.  
**Verse:** Psalm 23:3

---

### Psalm 23:4
**Q10. Through what valley does the psalmist walk?**  
**Answer:** The valley of the shadow of death.  
**Verse:** Psalm 23:4

**Q11. Why will the psalmist fear no evil?**  
**Answer:** For thou art with me.  
**Verse:** Psalm 23:4

**Q12. What two things comfort the psalmist?**  
**Answer:** Thy rod and thy staff.  
**Verse:** Psalm 23:4

---

### Psalm 23:5
**Q13. What does the Lord prepare before the psalmist?**  
**Answer:** A table.  
**Verse:** Psalm 23:5

**Q14. In whose presence is the table prepared?**  
**Answer:** In the presence of mine enemies.  
**Verse:** Psalm 23:5

**Q15. With what does the Lord anoint his head, and what happens to his cup?**  
**Answer:** Thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over.  
**Verse:** Psalm 23:5

---

### Psalm 23:6
**Q16. What two virtues shall follow the psalmist?**  
**Answer:** Goodness and mercy.  
**Verse:** Psalm 23:6

**Q17. How long shall goodness and mercy follow him?**  
**Answer:** All the days of my life.  
**Verse:** Psalm 23:6

**Q18. Where will the psalmist dwell, and for how long?**  
**Answer:** In the house of the LORD for ever.  
**Verse:** Psalm 23:6
