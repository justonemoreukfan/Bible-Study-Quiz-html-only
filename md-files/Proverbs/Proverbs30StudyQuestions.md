### Proverbs 30:1

**Q1. Whose words are recorded in Proverbs 30:1?**  
**Answer:** The words of Agur.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 30:1

**Q2. Whose son was Agur according to Proverbs 30:1?**  
**Answer:** The son of Jakeh.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 30:1

**Q3. Unto which two individuals did the man speak in Proverbs 30:1?**  
**Answer:** Ithiel and Ucal.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 30:1
