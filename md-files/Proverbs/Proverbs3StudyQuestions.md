# Proverbs 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Proverbs 3:1

**Q1. What should a son not forget?**  
**Answer:** My law.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 3:1

**Q2. What should a son keep?**  
**Answer:** My commandments.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 3:1

**Q3. Where should the commandments be kept?**  
**Answer:** In thine heart.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 3:1

---

**Q4. What shall add length of days?**

**Verse:** Proverbs 3:2

**Q6. What shall be added besides length of days?**
