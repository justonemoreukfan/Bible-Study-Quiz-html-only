# Proverbs 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Proverbs 10:1
**Q1. Whose son maketh a glad father?**  
**Answer:** A wise son.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 10:1

**Q2. Who is the heaviness of his mother?**  
**Answer:** A foolish son.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 10:1

**Q3. What kind of son causes heaviness?**  
**Answer:** A foolish son.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 10:1
