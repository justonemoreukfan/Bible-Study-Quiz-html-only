# Proverbs 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Proverbs 9:1
**Q1. What has wisdom builded?**  
**Answer:** Her house.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:1

**Q2. How many pillars has wisdom hewn out?**  
**Answer:** Seven.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:1

**Q3. What has wisdom hewn out?**  
**Answer:** Her seven pillars.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:1

---

### Proverbs 9:2
**Q4. What has wisdom killed?**  
**Answer:** Her beasts.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:2

**Q5. What has wisdom mingled?**  
**Answer:** Her wine.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:2

**Q6. What has wisdom furnished?**  
**Answer:** Her table.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:2

### Proverbs 9:3
**Q7. Whom has wisdom sent forth?**  
**Answer:** Her maidens.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:3

**Q8. Where has wisdom cried?**  
**Answer:** Upon the highest places of the city.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:3

**Q9. Who cried upon the highest places?**  
**Answer:** Wisdom.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:3

### Proverbs 9:4
**Q10. Unto whom does wisdom say, Turn in hither?**  
**Answer:** Whoso is simple.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:4

**Q11. Whom does wisdom call?**  
**Answer:** Him that wanteth understanding.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:4

**Q12. Who is invited to turn in?**  
**Answer:** Whoso is simple.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:4

### Proverbs 9:5

**Answer:** Of my bread.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:5

**Q14. What should he drink?**  
**Answer:** Of the wine which I have mingled.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:5

**Q15. Whose wine is mentioned?**  
**Answer:** My wine.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:5

### Proverbs 9:6

**Answer:** The foolish.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:6

**Q17. What should he live?**  
**Answer:** Go in the way of understanding.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:6

**Q18. In what way should he go?**  
**Answer:** The way of understanding.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:6

### Proverbs 9:7
**Q19. What happens to him that reproveth a scorner?**  
**Answer:** He getteth to himself shame.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:7

**Q20. What happens to him that rebuketh a wicked man?**  
**Answer:** He getteth himself a blot.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:7

**Q21. What does the reprover get to himself?**  
**Answer:** Shame.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:7

---

### Proverbs 9:8
**Q22. Whom should a person not reprove?**  
**Answer:** A scorner.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:8

**Q23. What will the scorner hate?**  
**Answer:** Thee.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 9:8

**Q24. Whom should a person rebuke?**

**Verse:** Proverbs 9:8
