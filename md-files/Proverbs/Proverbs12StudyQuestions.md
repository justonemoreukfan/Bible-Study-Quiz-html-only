# Proverbs 12 Study Questions (KJV)

### Proverbs 12:1
**Q1. Who loveth knowledge?**  
**Answer:** He that loveth instruction.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 12:1

**Q2. Who is brutish?**  
**Answer:** He that hateth reproof.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 12:1

**Q3. What does the person who loves instruction love?**  
**Answer:** Knowledge.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 12:1
