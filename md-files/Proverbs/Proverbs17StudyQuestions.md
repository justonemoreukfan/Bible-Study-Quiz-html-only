# Proverbs 17 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Proverbs 17:1
**Q1. What is better than a house full of sacrifices with strife?**  
**Answer:** A dry morsel.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 17:1

**Q2. What kind of house is troublesome?**  
**Answer:** A house full of sacrifices with strife.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 17:1

**Q3. What should accompany a dry morsel?**  
**Answer:** Quietness.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 17:1
