# Proverbs 22 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Proverbs 22:1

**Q1. What is rather to be chosen than great riches?**  
**Answer:** A good name.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 22:1

**Q2. What is rather to be chosen than silver and gold?**  
**Answer:** Loving favour.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 22:1

**Q3. What two things are compared in Proverbs 22:1?**  
**Answer:** A good name and great riches.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 22:1

---

### Proverbs 22:2

**Q4. Who meet together according to Proverbs 22:2?**  
**Answer:** The rich and poor.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 22:2

**Q5. Who is the maker of them all?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 22:2

**Q6. Of whom is the LORD the maker?**  
**Answer:** The rich and poor.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 22:2

### Proverbs 22:3

**Q7. What does a prudent man foresee?**  
**Answer:** The evil.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 22:3

**Q8. What does a prudent man do when he foresees evil?**  
**Answer:** He hideth himself.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 22:3

**Q9. What happens to the simple when they pass on?**  
**Answer:** They are punished.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 22:3

### Proverbs 22:4

**Q10. By what two things are riches, honour, and life gained?**  
**Answer:** By humility and the fear of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 22:4

**Q11. What is one result of humility and the fear of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Riches.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 22:4

**Q12. What three things are named in Proverbs 22:4?**  
**Answer:** Riches, honour, and life.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 22:4

**Verse:** Proverbs 22:5
