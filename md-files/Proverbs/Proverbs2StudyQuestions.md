# Proverbs 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Proverbs 2:1

**Q1. What should a son receive according to Proverbs 2:1?**  
**Answer:** My words.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 2:1

**Q2. What should a son hide with him?**  
**Answer:** My commandments.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 2:1

**Q3. Whose commandments are mentioned in Proverbs 2:1?**  
**Answer:** My commandments.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 2:1
