# Proverbs 13 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Proverbs 13:1
**Q1. Who heareth his father's instruction?**  
**Answer:** A wise son.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 13:1

**Q2. Who refuseth reproof?**  
**Answer:** A scorner.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 13:1

**Q3. What does a scorner not hear?**  
**Answer:** Rebuke.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 13:1
