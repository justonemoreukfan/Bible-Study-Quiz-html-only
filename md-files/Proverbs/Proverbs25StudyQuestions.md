# Proverbs 25 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Proverbs 25:1

**Q1. Whose proverbs are these also according to Proverbs 25:1?**  
**Answer:** Solomon.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 25:1

**Q2. Who copied out these proverbs of Solomon?**  
**Answer:** The men of Hezekiah king of Judah.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 25:1

**Q3. Of what nation was Hezekiah king?**  
**Answer:** Judah.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 25:1

---

### Proverbs 25:2

**Q4. What is the glory of God according to Proverbs 25:2?**  
**Answer:** To conceal a thing.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 25:2

**Q5. What is the honour of kings?**  
**Answer:** To search out a matter.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 25:2

**Q6. Whose honour is it to search out a matter?**

**Verse:** Proverbs 25:2
