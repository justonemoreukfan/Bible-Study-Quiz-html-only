# Proverbs 28 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Proverbs 28:1

**Q1. Who flee when no man pursueth?**  
**Answer:** The wicked.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 28:1

**Q2. When do the wicked flee?**  
**Answer:** When no man pursueth.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 28:1

**Q3. As what are the righteous bold?**  
**Answer:** As a lion.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 28:1

---

### Proverbs 28:2

**Q4. For what are many the princes of a land?**  
**Answer:** For the transgression of a land.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 28:2

**Q5. By whom shall the state thereof be prolonged?**  
**Answer:** By a man of understanding and knowledge.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 28:2

**Q6. What kind of man prolongs the state of a land?**  
**Answer:** A man of understanding and knowledge.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 28:2

**Q7. What kind of man is described as oppressing the poor?**  
**Answer:** A poor man.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 28:3

**Q8. Whom does the poor man oppress in Proverbs 28:3?**  
**Answer:** The poor.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 28:3

**Q9. Like what is a poor man that oppresseth the poor?**

**Answer:** Praise the wicked.

**Answer:** In his uprightness.
