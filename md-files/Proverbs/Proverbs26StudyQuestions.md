# Proverbs 26 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Proverbs 26:1

**Q1. What is compared to snow in summer and rain in harvest?**  
**Answer:** Honour that is not seemly for a fool.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 26:1

**Q2. When is snow mentioned as occurring in Proverbs 26:1?**  
**Answer:** In summer.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 26:1

**Q3. For whom is honour not seemly?**  
**Answer:** A fool.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 26:1

---

**Q4. What two birds are mentioned in Proverbs 26:2?**

**Verse:** Proverbs 26:2

### Proverbs 26:4

**Q10. How should you not answer a fool?**  
**Answer:** According to his folly.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 26:4

**Q11. Why should you not answer a fool according to his folly?**  
**Answer:** Lest thou also be like unto him.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 26:4

**Q12. Whom should you not answer according to his folly?**  
**Answer:** A fool.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 26:4

**Answer:** Wise in his own conceit.

**Verse:** Proverbs 26:5
