# Proverbs 21 Study Questions (KJV)

### Proverbs 21:1

**Q1. Where is the king's heart according to Proverbs 21:1?**  
**Answer:** In the hand of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 21:1

**Q2. What is the king's heart compared to in Proverbs 21:1?**  
**Answer:** The rivers of water.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 21:1

**Q3. What does the LORD do with the king's heart?**  
**Answer:** He turneth it whithersoever He will.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 21:1
