# Proverbs 8 Study Questions (KJV)

### Proverbs 8:1
**Q1. What cries according to Proverbs 8:1?**  
**Answer:** Wisdom.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 8:1

**Q2. What does understanding put forth?**  
**Answer:** Her voice.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 8:1

**Q3. Who puts forth her voice?**  
**Answer:** Understanding.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 8:1
