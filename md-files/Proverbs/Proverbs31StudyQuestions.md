### Proverbs 31:1

**Q1. Whose words are recorded in Proverbs 31:1?**  
**Answer:** King Lemuel.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 31:1

**Q2. What did King Lemuel's mother do for him?**  
**Answer:** She taught him.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 31:1

**Q3. What is the collection of words in Proverbs 31:1 called?**  
**Answer:** The prophecy.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 31:1
