# Proverbs 27 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Proverbs 27:1

**Q1. Of what shouldest thou not boast thyself?**  
**Answer:** Of to morrow.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 27:1

**Q2. Why should one not boast of tomorrow?**  
**Answer:** For thou knowest not what a day may bring forth.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 27:1

**Q3. What is it that thou knowest not concerning tomorrow?**  
**Answer:** What a day may bring forth.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 27:1
