# Proverbs 11 Study Questions (KJV)

### Proverbs 11:1
**Q1. What is an abomination to the LORD?**  
**Answer:** A false balance.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 11:1

**Q2. What is his delight?**  
**Answer:** A just weight.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 11:1

**Q3. What kind of weight is mentioned?**  
**Answer:** A just weight.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 11:1
