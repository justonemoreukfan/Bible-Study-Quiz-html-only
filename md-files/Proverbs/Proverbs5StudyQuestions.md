# Proverbs 5 Study Questions (KJV)

### Proverbs 5:1

**Q1. To what should the son attend?**  
**Answer:** My wisdom.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 5:1

**Q2. To what should he bow his ear?**  
**Answer:** To my understanding.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 5:1

**Q3. Whose understanding is mentioned?**  
**Answer:** My understanding.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 5:1
