# Proverbs 29 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Proverbs 29:1

**Q1. What shall suddenly happen to him that being often reproved hardeneth his neck?**  
**Answer:** He shall suddenly be destroyed.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 29:1

**Q2. What does a person do to his neck when he is often reproved?**  
**Answer:** Hardeneth his neck.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 29:1

**Q3. What kind of remedy will there be when he is destroyed?**  
**Answer:** And that without remedy.  
**Verse:** Proverbs 29:1

**Q4. When do the people rejoice according to Proverbs 29:2?**
