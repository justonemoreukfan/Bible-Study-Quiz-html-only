# James 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### James 4:1
**Q1. What does the first part of James 4:1 state?**  
**Answer:** From whence come wars and fightings among you? come they not hence.  
**Verse:** James 4:1

**Q2. What does the second part of James 4:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** even of your lusts that war in your members?  
**Verse:** James 4:1

**Q3. What is recorded in James 4:1?**  
**Answer:** From whence come wars and fightings among you? come they not hence, even of your lusts that war in your members?  
**Verse:** James 4:1

---

### James 4:2
**Q4. What is stated in the opening of James 4:2?**  
**Answer:** Ye lust, and have not.  
**Verse:** James 4:2

**Q5. What is declared next in James 4:2?**  
**Answer:** ye kill, and desire to have, and cannot obtain.  
**Verse:** James 4:2

**Q6. What further truth is recorded in James 4:2?**  
**Answer:** ye fight and war, yet ye have not, because ye ask not.  
**Verse:** James 4:2

### James 4:11
**Q31. What is stated in the first clause of James 4:11?**  
**Answer:** Speak not evil one of another, brethren. He that speaketh evil of his brother, and judgeth his brother, speaketh evil of the law, and judgeth the law.  
**Verse:** James 4:11

**Q32. What additional detail is declared in James 4:11?**  
**Answer:** but if thou judge the law, thou art not a doer of the law, but a judge.  
**Verse:** James 4:11

**Q33. According to James 4:11, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Speak not evil one of another, brethren. He that speaketh evil of his brother, and judgeth his brother, speaketh evil of the law, and judgeth the law: but if thou judge the law, thou art not a doer of the law, but a judge.  
**Verse:** James 4:11
