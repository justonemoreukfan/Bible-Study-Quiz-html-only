# James 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### James 5:1
**Q1. According to James 5:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Go to now.  
**Verse:** James 5:1

**Q2. What is declared next in James 5:1?**  
**Answer:** ye rich men.  
**Verse:** James 5:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in James 5:1?**  
**Answer:** weep and howl for your miseries that shall come upon you.  
**Verse:** James 5:1
