# James 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### James 3:1
**Q1. According to James 3:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** My brethren.  
**Verse:** James 3:1

**Q2. What is declared next in James 3:1?**  
**Answer:** be not many masters.  
**Verse:** James 3:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in James 3:1?**  
**Answer:** knowing that we shall receive the greater condemnation.  
**Verse:** James 3:1

---

### James 3:2
**Q4. According to James 3:2, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** For in many things we offend all. If any man offend not in word.  
**Verse:** James 3:2

**Q5. What is declared next in James 3:2?**  
**Answer:** the same is a perfect man.  
**Verse:** James 3:2

**Q6. What further truth is given in James 3:2?**

**Verse:** James 3:2

### James 3:3
**Q7. What is stated in the first clause of James 3:3?**  
**Answer:** Behold, we put bits in the horses’ mouths, that they may obey us.  
**Verse:** James 3:3

**Q8. What additional detail is declared in James 3:3?**  
**Answer:** and we turn about their whole body.  
**Verse:** James 3:3

**Q9. According to James 3:3, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Behold, we put bits in the horses’ mouths, that they may obey us; and we turn about their whole body.  
**Verse:** James 3:3
