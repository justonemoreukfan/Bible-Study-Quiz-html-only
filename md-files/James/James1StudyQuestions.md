### James 1:1
**Q1. What does the first part of James 1:1 state?**  
**Answer:** a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** James 1:1

**Q2. What does the second part of James 1:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** to the twelve tribes which are scattered abroad.  
**Verse:** James 1:1

**Q3. What is recorded in James 1:1?**  
**Answer:** James, a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ, to the twelve tribes which are scattered abroad, greeting.  
**Verse:** James 1:1
