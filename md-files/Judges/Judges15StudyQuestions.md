# Judges 15 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Judges 15:1

**Q1. Within what time after the time of wheat harvest did it come to pass that Samson visited his wife?**  
**Answer:** Within the time of wheat harvest.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:1

**Q2. What did Samson bring with him when he visited his wife?**  
**Answer:** A kid.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:1

**Q3. Whose into what place did Samson say he would go to see his wife?**  
**Answer:** Into her chamber.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:1

---

### Judges 15:2

**Q2. Who would not suffer Samson to go in?**  
**Answer:** Her father.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:2

**Q5. What did her father think Samson had done to her?**  
**Answer:** Utterly hated her.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:2

**Q6. Whom did the father suggest Samson take instead of his wife?**  
**Answer:** Her younger sister.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:2

---

### Judges 15:3

**Q7. Regarding whom did Samson say they would be blameless?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:3

**Q8. What did Samson say he would do to the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** Do them a displeasure.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:3

**Q9. From this time forward, what did Samson state about his action toward the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** More blameless than though I do them a displeasure.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:3

---

### Judges 15:4

**Q10. And whom did Samson go and catch?**  
**Answer:** Three hundred foxes.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:4

**Q11. What did Samson take after catching the foxes?**  
**Answer:** Firebrands.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:4

**Q12. How did Samson place the firebrands in relation to the foxes?**  
**Answer:** Turned tail to tail, and put a firebrand in the midst between two tails.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:4

---

### Judges 15:5

**Q13. And when he had set the brands on fire, where did he let the foxes go?**  
**Answer:** Into the standing corn of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:5

**Q14. What grew in the fields that was burned up besides the standing corn?**  
**Answer:** The shocks, and also the vineyard, and olives.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:5

---

### Judges 15:6

**Q15. Who asked, "Who hath done this?"**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:6

**Q16. What answer was given regarding who did this?**  
**Answer:** Samson, the son in law of the Timnite, because he had taken his wife, and given her to his companion.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:6

**Q17. What did the Philistines do when they found out who did it?**  
**Answer:** Came up, and burnt her and her father with fire.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:6

---

### Judges 15:7

**Q18. What did Samson tell the Philistines concerning their deed?**  
**Answer:** Though ye have done this, yet will I be avenged of you, and after that I will cease.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:7

**Q19. Did Samson say he would stop before or after being avenged of them?**  
**Answer:** After that I will cease.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:7

**Q20. Whose actions prompted Samson's vow of vengeance in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:7

---

### Judges 15:8

**Q21. How did Samson smite the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** Hip and thigh.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:8

**Q22. With what kind of slaughter did Samson smite them?**  
**Answer:** A great slaughter.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:8

**Q23. After the slaughter, where did Samson go and dwell?**  
**Answer:** In the top of the rock Etam.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:8

---

### Judges 15:9

**Q24. Where did the Philistines go up and pitch?**  
**Answer:** In Judah.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:9

**Q25. Where did the Philistines spread themselves?**  
**Answer:** Abroad in Lehi.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:9

**Q26. Against whom did the Philistines spread themselves abroad?**  
**Answer:** Judah.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:9

---

### Judges 15:10

**Q27. Who asked, "Why are ye come up against us?"**  
**Answer:** The men of Judah.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:10

**Q28. What did the Philistines answer to the men of Judah?**  
**Answer:** To bind Samson are we come up, to do to him as he hath done to us.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:10

**Q29. What reason did the Philistines give for coming up to bind Samson?**  
**Answer:** To do to him as he hath done to us.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:10

---

### Judges 15:11

**Q30. How many men of Judah went to the top of the rock Etam?**  
**Answer:** Three thousand men.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:11

**Q31. What question did the men of Judah ask Samson regarding the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** Knowest thou not that the Philistines are rulers over us?  
**Verse:** Judges 15:11

**Q32. What did Samson say the Philistines had done unto him?**  
**Answer:** As they did unto me, so have I done unto them.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:11

---

### Judges 15:12

**Q33. For what purpose did the men of Judah say they were come down?**  
**Answer:** To bind thee, that we may deliver thee into the hand of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:12

**Q34. What did Samson ask the men of Judah to swear unto him?**  
**Answer:** That ye will not fall upon me yourselves.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:12

**Q35. With what did the men of Judah speak to Samson?**  
**Answer:** Saying, No; but we will bind thee fast, and deliver thee into their hand: but surely we will not kill thee.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:12

---

### Judges 15:13

**Q36. With what did the men of Judah bind Samson?**  
**Answer:** With two new cords.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:13

**Q37. From where did they bring Samson up?**  
**Answer:** From the rock.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:13

**Q38. Who shouted against Samson when he was brought to them?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:13

---

### Judges 15:14

**Q39. Who came unto Samson when he came unto Lehi?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:14

**Q40. How did the cords upon his arms become when the Spirit of the LORD came mightily upon him?**  
**Answer:** Became as flax that was burnt with fire.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:14

**Q41. What happened to his bands?**  
**Answer:** Loosed from off his hands.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:14

---

### Judges 15:15

**Q42. What did Samson find a new jawbone of?**  
**Answer:** An ass.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:15

**Q43. What did Samson put forth his hand and take?**  
**Answer:** The new jawbone of an ass.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:15

**Q44. How many men did Samson slay with the jawbone?**  
**Answer:** A thousand men.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:15

---

### Judges 15:16

**Q45. With what weapon did Samson say he had slain an heap and heaps?**  
**Answer:** With the jawbone of an ass.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:16

**Q46. How many times did Samson mention the jawbone of an ass in his saying?**  
**Answer:** Twice.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:16

**Q47. What did Samson say he had slain with the jawbone?**  
**Answer:** A thousand men.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:16

---

### Judges 15:17

**Q48. What did Samson do when he had made an end of speaking?**  
**Answer:** Cast away the jawbone out of his hand.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:17

**Q49. What name was given to that place?**  
**Answer:** Ramathlehi.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:17

**Q50. Who cast away the jawbone out of his hand?**  
**Answer:** Samson.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:17

---

### Judges 15:18

**Q51. To whom did Samson sore athirst call?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:18

**Q52. What did Samson say the LORD had given by the hand of His servant?**  
**Answer:** This great deliverance.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:18

**Q53. Of what did Samson express a fear he would die after the great deliverance?**  
**Answer:** Die for thirst, and fall into the hand of the uncircumcised.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:18

---

### Judges 15:19

**Q54. What did God do to a hollow place that was in the jaw?**  
**Answer:** Cave a hollow place that was in the jaw.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:19

**Q55. What came thereout when Samson drank?**  
**Answer:** Water.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:19

**Q56. What happened to Samson's spirit and when he drank?**  
**Answer:** His spirit came again, and he revived.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:19

---

### Judges 15:20

**Q57. Over Israel, how long did Samson judge in the days of the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** Twenty years.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:20

**Q58. In whose days did Samson judge Israel?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:20

**Q59. Who judged Israel for twenty years?**  
**Answer:** Samson.  
**Verse:** Judges 15:20
