# Judges 18 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Judges 18:1

**Q1. What was lacking in those days in Israel?**  
**Answer:** There was no king in Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:1

**Q2. Which tribe was seeking an inheritance to dwell in at that time?**  
**Answer:** The tribe of the Danites.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:1

**Q3. What had not fallen unto them among the tribes of Israel until that day?**  
**Answer:** All their inheritance among the tribes of Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:1

---

### Judges 18:2

**Q2. Who did the children of Dan send from their coasts to spy out the land?**  
**Answer:** Five men of their coasts, men of valour.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:2

**Q3. From which specific families were the men who were sent?**  
**Answer:** From Zorah and from Eshtaol.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:2

**Q4. What did the children of Dan instruct these men to do?**  
**Answer:** Go, search the land.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:2

---

### Judges 18:2 (continued) / 3

**Q5. Where did the spies lodge when they were by the house of Micah?**  
**Answer:** They turned in thither.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:3

**Q6. Whose voice did the spies recognize while they were near Micah's house?**  
**Answer:** The voice of the young man the Levite.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:3

**Q7. What did the spies do when they turned in and recognized the young man?**  
**Answer:** They turned in thither, and said unto him, Who brought thee hither? and what makest thou in this place? and what hast thou here?  
**Verse:** Judges 18:3

---

### Judges 18:4

**Q8. How did the Levite respond to the men of Dan regarding his situation?**  
**Answer:** Thus and thus dealeth Micah with me, and hath hired me, and I am his priest.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:4

---

### Judges 18:5

**Q9. What did the men of Dan ask the Levite to do for them?**  
**Answer:** Ask counsel, we pray thee, of God, that we may know whether our way which we go shall be prosperous.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:5

---

### Judges 18:6

**Q10. What was the priest's answer to the men of Dan?**  
**Answer:** Go in peace: before the LORD is your way wherein ye go.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:6

---

### Judges 18:7

**Q11. To which city did the five men depart and go?**  
**Answer:** Laish.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:7

**Q12. How did the people in Laish dwell, and what were they like?**  
**Answer:** Careless, after the manner of the Zidonians, quiet and secure; and there was no magistrate in the land, that might put them to shame in any thing.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:7

**Q13. How were the people of Laish situated in relation to the Zidonians?**  
**Answer:** They were far from the Zidonians, and had no business with any man.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:7

---

### Judges 18:8

**Q14. To whom did the five spies return?**  
**Answer:** Unto their brethren to Zorah and Eshtaol.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:8

---

### Judges 18:8 (continued) / 9

**Q15. What did their brethren ask the spies when they returned?**  
**Answer:** What say ye?  
**Verse:** Judges 18:8

---

### Judges 18:9

**Q16. What did the spies invite their brethren to do regarding Laish?**  
**Answer:** Arise, that we may go up against them.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:9

**Q17. What did the spies see when they entered the land of Laish?**  
**Answer:** We have seen the land, and, behold, it is very good.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:9

**Q18. What command did the spies give their brethren after seeing the land?**  
**Answer:** And are ye still? be not slothful to go, and to enter to possess the land.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:9

---

### Judges 18:10

**Q19. How did the spies describe the people and the place they would come to?**  
**Answer:** When ye go, ye shall come unto a people secure, and to a large land.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:10

**Q20. What reason did God give into their hands according to the spies?**  
**Answer:** For God hath given it into your hands.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:10

**Q21. How did the spies describe the resources of the place?**  
**Answer:** A place where there is no want of any thing that is in the earth.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:10

---

### Judges 18:11

**Q22. How many men departed from there out of the family of the Danites?**  
**Answer:** Six hundred men appointed with weapons of war.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:11

**Q23. From which specific locations did these six hundred men depart?**  
**Answer:** Out of Zorah and out of Eshtaol.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:11

---

### Judges 18:12

**Q24. Where did the Danites go up and pitch their camp?**  
**Answer:** In Kirjathjearim, in Judah.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:12

**Q25. What name was given to that place behind Kirjathjearim unto this day?**  
**Answer:** Mahaneedan.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:12

---

### Judges 18:13

**Q26. Through which mountainous region did they pass?**  
**Answer:** Mount Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:13

**Q27. Whose house did they come unto while passing through Mount Ephraim?**  
**Answer:** The house of Micah.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:13

---

### Judges 18:14

**Q28. What did the five men who went to spy out the country of Laish say to their brethren?**  
**Answer:** Do ye know that there are in these houses an ephod, and teraphim, and a graven image, and a molten image?  
**Verse:** Judges 18:14

**Q29. What instruction did the five men give to their brethren based on this knowledge?**  
**Answer:** Now therefore consider what ye have to do.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:14

---

### Judges 18:15

**Q30. Where did the six hundred men turn aside?**  
**Answer:** Thither, even to the house of the young man the Levite.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:15

---

### Judges 18:15 (continued) / 16

**Q31. Whose house did they enter?**  
**Answer:** Into the house of Micah.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:15

**Q32. What did the six hundred men of the children of Dan do at Micah's house?**  
**Answer:** They saluted him.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:15

---

### Judges 18:16

**Q33. How many men of the children of Dan stood by the entering of the gate?**  
**Answer:** The six hundred men appointed with their weapons of war.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:16

**Q34. From which city were these armed men?**  
**Answer:** Of the children of Dan.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:16

---

### Judges 18:17

**Q35. Who went up into the house of Micah?**  
**Answer:** The five men that went to spy out the land.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:17

**Q36. What specific items did the five spies take from the house?**  
**Answer:** The graven image, and the ephod, and the teraphim, and the molten image.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:17

**Q37. Where did the priest stand while the five men took the images?**  
**Answer:** Standing in the entering of the gate with the six hundred men that were appointed with weapons of war.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:17

---

### Judges 18:18

**Q38. What did the five men take when they went into Micah's house?**  
**Answer:** The carved image, the ephod, and the teraphim, and the molten image.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:18

**Q39. What did the priest say to the five men who entered the house?**  
**Answer:** What do ye?  
**Verse:** Judges 18:18

---

### Judges 18:19

**Q40. What did the men say to the priest to silence him?**  
**Answer:** Hold thy peace, lay thine hand upon thy mouth.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:19

**Q41. What alternatives did they offer the priest?**  
**Answer:** Go with us, and be to us a father and a priest.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:19

**Q42. What question did they ask to convince him to be a priest for a tribe rather than just one man?**  
**Answer:** Is it better for thee to be a priest unto the house of one man, or that thou be a priest unto a tribe and a family in Israel?  
**Verse:** Judges 18:19

---

### Judges 18:20

**Q43. How did the priest's heart react to the offer?**  
**Answer:** His heart was glad.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:20

**Q44. What did the priest take with him?**  
**Answer:** The ephod, and the teraphim, and the graven image.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:20

**Q45. Where did the priest go after taking the items?**  
**Answer:** He went in the midst of the people.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:20

---

### Judges 18:21

**Q46. Who did the Danites turn and depart with?**  
**Answer:** The little ones and the cattle and the carriage.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:21

**Q47. What was put before them as they departed?**  
**Answer:** The baggage.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:21

---

### Judges 18:22

**Q48. When they were a good way from Micah's house, who was gathered together?**  
**Answer:** The men that were in the houses near to Micah's house.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:22

**Q49. What did these men do after gathering together?**  
**Answer:** They overtook the children of Dan.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:22

---

### Judges 18:23

**Q50. What did the men do as they cried unto the children of Dan?**  
**Answer:** They turned their faces.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:23

**Q51. What question did Micah ask the children of Dan when he overtook them?**  
**Answer:** What aileth thee, that thou comest with such a company?  
**Verse:** Judges 18:23

---

### Judges 18:24

**Q52. How did Micah explain that they had taken away his things?**  
**Answer:** Ye have taken away my gods which I made, and the priest, and ye are gone away.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:24

**Q53. What question did Micah ask regarding what he had left?**  
**Answer:** And what have I more?  
**Verse:** Judges 18:24

**Q54. What was Micah's response when asked "What aileth thee?" by the Danites?**  
**Answer:** And what is this that ye say unto me, What aileth thee?  
**Verse:** Judges 18:24

---

### Judges 18:25

**Q55. What did the children of Dan instruct Micah not to do?**  
**Answer:** Let not thy voice be heard among us.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:25

**Q56. What did the Danites warn might happen if angry fellows ran upon them?**  
**Answer:** Lest angry fellows run upon thee, and thou lose thy life, with the lives of thy household.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:25

---

### Judges 18:26

**Q57. In which direction did the children of Dan go after speaking to Micah?**  
**Answer:** Their way.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:26

**Q58. What did Micah realize when he saw that they were too strong for him?**  
**Answer:** He saw that they were too strong for him.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:26

**Q59. Where did Micah do after realizing they were too strong?**  
**Answer:** He turned and went back unto his house.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:26

---

### Judges 18:27

**Q60. What did the children of Dan take with them that Micah had made?**  
**Answer:** The things which Micah had made, and the priest which he had.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:27

**Q61. Unto whom did the Danites come?**  
**Answer:** Unto a people that were quiet and secure.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:27

---

### Judges 18:27 (continued) / 28

**Q62. What city did the Danites come to?**  
**Answer:** To Laish.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:27

**Q63. What did the Danites do to the people of Laish with the edge of the sword?**  
**Answer:** They smote them.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:27

**Q64. What did the Danites do to the city of Laish with fire?**  
**Answer:** They burned the city with fire.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:27

---

### Judges 18:28

**Q65. Why was there no deliverer for the people of Laish?**  
**Answer:** Because it was far from Zidon, and they had no business with any man.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:28

**Q66. In whose valley was the city of Laish located?**  
**Answer:** The valley that is by Bethrehob.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:28

**Q67. What did the Danites do to the city after burning it?**  
**Answer:** They built a city, and dwelt therein.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:28

---

### Judges 18:29

**Q68. What name did the children of Dan give to the city?**  
**Answer:** Dan.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:29

---

### Judges 18:29 (continued) / 30

**Q69. After whom was the city named Dan?**  
**Answer:** After the name of Dan their father, who was born unto Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:29

**Q70. What was the former name of the city?**  
**Answer:** Laish.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:29

---

### Judges 18:30

**Q71. What did the children of Dan set up for themselves?**  
**Answer:** The graven image.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:30

**Q72. Who was the son of Gershom, the son of Manasseh, that became priest to the tribe of Dan?**  
**Answer:** Jonathan.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:30

**Q73. Until what time were Jonathan and his sons priests to the tribe of Dan?**  
**Answer:** Until the day of the captivity of the land.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:30

---

### Judges 18:31

**Q74. What did the children of Dan set up for themselves throughout all the time?**  
**Answer:** Micah's graven image, which he made.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:31

**Q75. How long did Micah's graven image remain set up among them?**  
**Answer:** All the time that the house of God was in Shiloh.  
**Verse:** Judges 18:31
