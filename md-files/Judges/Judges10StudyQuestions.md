# Judges 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Judges 10:1

**Q1. Who arose to defend Israel after Abimelech?**  
**Answer:** Tola the son of Puah, the son of Dodo, a man of Issachar.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:1

**Q2. Where did Tola dwell?**  
**Answer:** In Shamir in mount Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:1

**Q3. What did Tola do for Israel?**  
**Answer:** He arose to defend Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:1

---

### Judges 10:2

**Q1. How long did Tola judge Israel?**  
**Answer:** Twenty and three years.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:2

**Q2. What happened to Tola after he judged Israel?**  
**Answer:** He died.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:2

**Q3. Where was Tola buried?**  
**Answer:** In Shamir.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:2

---

### Judges 10:3

**Q1. Who arose after Tola?**  
**Answer:** Jair, a Gileadite.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:3

**Q2. How long did Jair judge Israel?**  
**Answer:** Twenty and two years.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:3

**Q3. What kind of Israelite was Jair?**  
**Answer:** A Gileadite.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:3

---

### Judges 10:4

**Q1. How many sons did Jair have?**  
**Answer:** Thirty sons.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:4

**Q2. What did Jair's sons ride upon?**  
**Answer:** Thirty ass colts.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:4

**Q3. What cities did Jair's sons have?**  
**Answer:** Thirty cities.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:4

---

### Judges 10:5

**Q1. What happened to Jair?**  
**Answer:** He died.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:5

**Q2. Where was Jair buried?**  
**Answer:** In Camon.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:5

**Q3. What land were Jair's cities in?**  
**Answer:** The land of Gilead.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:5

---

### Judges 10:6

**Q1. What did the children of Israel do again in the sight of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Evil.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:6

**Q2. Which Baalim did the children of Israel serve?**  
**Answer:** Baalim, and Ashtaroth, and the gods of Syria, and the gods of Zidon, and the gods of Moab, and the gods of the children of Ammon, and the gods of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:6

**Q3. Whom did the children of Israel forsake?**  
**Answer:** The Lord, and served not him.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:6

---

### Judges 10:7

**Q1. Against whom was the anger of the Lord hot?**  
**Answer:** Against Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:7

**Q2. Into whose hands did the Lord deliver Israel?**  
**Answer:** Into the hands of the Philistines, and into the hands of the children of Ammon.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:7

**Q3. What did the Lord do to Israel in His anger?**  
**Answer:** He sold them into the hands of the Philistines and the children of Ammon.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:7

---

### Judges 10:8

**Q1. Whom did the children of Ammon vex and oppress that year?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:8

**Q2. For how long did the children of Ammon vex and oppress the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Eighteen years.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:8

**Q3. Where were all the children of Israel that were on the other side Jordan located?**  
**Answer:** In the land of the Amorites, which is in Gilead.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:8

---

### Judges 10:9

**Q1. Who passed over Jordan to fight against whom?**  
**Answer:** The children of Ammon passed over Jordan to fight also against Judah, and against Benjamin, and against the house of Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:9

**Q2. What was the condition of Israel during this time?**  
**Answer:** Israel was sore distressed.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:9

**Q3. Which specific tribes did the children of Ammon fight against?**  
**Answer:** Judah, Benjamin, and the house of Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:9

---

### Judges 10:10

**Q1. To whom did the children of Israel cry?**  
**Answer:** Unto the Lord.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:10

**Q2. What confession did the children of Israel make to the Lord?**  
**Answer:** We have sinned against thee, both because we have forsaken our God, and also served Baalim.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:10

**Q3. Whose god did they forsake?**  
**Answer:** Our God.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:10

---

### Judges 10:11

**Q1. Who asked a question of the children of Israel in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:11

**Q2. From whom did the Lord deliver Israel?**  
**Answer:** The Egyptians, and from the Amorites, from the children of Ammon, and from the Philistines.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:11

**Q3. Did the Lord deliver Israel from the Egyptians?**  
**Answer:** Yes.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:11

---

### Judges 10:12

**Q1. Who also oppressed Israel according to the Lord?**  
**Answer:** The Zidonians, and the Amalekites, and the Maonites.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:12

**Q2. What did the Zidonians, Amalekites, and Maonites do to Israel?**  
**Answer:** They oppressed you.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:12

**Q3. What happened when Israel cried to the Lord from these oppressors?**  
**Answer:** He delivered you out of their hand.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:12

---

### Judges 10:13

**Q1. Whom did Israel forsake?**  
**Answer:** Me (the Lord).  
**Verse:** Judges 10:13

**Q2. Whom did Israel serve instead?**  
**Answer:** Other gods.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:13

**Q3. What did the Lord say He would do no more?**  
**Answer:** Deliver you no more.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:13

---

### Judges 10:14

**Q1. Where did the Lord tell Israel to go?**  
**Answer:** Go and cry unto the gods which ye have chosen.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:14

**Q2. What should the chosen gods do for Israel?**  
**Answer:** Let them deliver you in the time of your tribulation.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:14

**Q3. When did the Lord say the gods should deliver them?**  
**Answer:** In the time of your tribulation.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:14

---

### Judges 10:15

**Q1. What did the children of Israel say unto the Lord?**  
**Answer:** We have sinned: do thou unto us whatsoever seemeth good unto thee; deliver us only, we pray thee, this day.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:15

**Q2. What request did Israel make regarding deliverance?**  
**Answer:** Deliver us only, we pray thee, this day.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:15

**Q3. What did Israel acknowledge about themselves?**  
**Answer:** We have sinned.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:15

---

### Judges 10:16

**Q1. What did the children of Israel put away from among them?**  
**Answer:** The strange gods.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:16

**Q2. Whom did the children of Israel serve?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:16

**Q3. What was the Lord's reaction to the misery of Israel?**  
**Answer:** His soul was grieved for the misery of Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:16

---

### Judges 10:17

**Q1. Who were gathered together?**  
**Answer:** The children of Ammon.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:17

**Q2. Where did the children of Ammon encamp?**  
**Answer:** In Gilead.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:17

**Q3. Where did the children of Israel assemble themselves?**  
**Answer:** In Mizpeh.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:17

---

### Judges 10:18

**Q1. What did the people and princes of Gilead say to one another?**  
**Answer:** What man is he that will begin to fight against the children of Ammon?  
**Verse:** Judges 10:18

**Q2. What would the man who begins the fight become?**  
**Answer:** Head over all the inhabitants of Gilead.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:18

**Q3. Who asked the question about who would begin the fight?**  
**Answer:** The people and princes of Gilead.  
**Verse:** Judges 10:18
