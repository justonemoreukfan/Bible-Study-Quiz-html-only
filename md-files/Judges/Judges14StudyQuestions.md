# Judges 14 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Judges 14:1

**Q1. Where did Samson go down?**  
**Answer:** Timnath  
**Verse:** Judges 14:1

**Q2. Whom did Samson see in Timnath?**  
**Answer:** A woman of the daughters of the Philistines  
**Verse:** Judges 14:1

**Q3. Of what nation were the daughters from whom Samson saw a woman?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines  
**Verse:** Judges 14:1

---

### Judges 14:2

**Q1. What did Samson do after coming up from Timnath?**  
**Answer:** He told his father and his mother  
**Verse:** Judges 14:2

**Q2. Whom did Samson say he had seen in Timnath?**  
**Answer:** A woman of the daughters of the Philistines  
**Verse:** Judges 14:2

**Q3. What command did Samson give his parents regarding the woman?**  
**Answer:** Get her for me to wife  
**Verse:** Judges 14:2

---

### Judges 14:3

**Q1. What question did his father and mother ask concerning the daughters of the uncircumcised Philistines?**  
**Answer:** Is there never a woman among the daughters of thy brethren, or among all my people, that thou goest to take a wife of the uncircumcised Philistines?  
**Verse:** Judges 14:3

**Q2. What was Samson's response to his father regarding the woman?**  
**Answer:** Get her for me; for she pleaseth me well  
**Verse:** Judges 14:3

**Q3. Why did Samson want this specific woman?**  
**Answer:** For she pleaseth me well  
**Verse:** Judges 14:3

---

### Judges 14:4

**Q1. Who did not know that this thing was of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** His father and his mother  
**Verse:** Judges 14:4

**Q2. Of whom was the thing that Samson sought?**  
**Answer:** Of the LORD  
**Verse:** Judges 14:4

**Q3. What occasion did the LORD seek against the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** An occasion against the Philistines  
**Verse:** Judges 14:4

---

### Judges 14:5

**Q1. Who went down with Samson to Timnath?**  
**Answer:** His father and his mother  
**Verse:** Judges 14:5

**Q2. To where did Samson and his parents come?**  
**Answer:** To the vineyards of Timnath  
**Verse:** Judges 14:5

**Q3. What met Samson in the vineyards of Timnath?**  
**Answer:** A young lion roared against him  
**Verse:** Judges 14:5

---

### Judges 14:6

**Q1. What came upon Samson?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit of the LORD  
**Verse:** Judges 14:6

**Q2. What did Samson do to the lion with no instrument in his hand?**  
**Answer:** He rent him as he would have rent a kid  
**Verse:** Judges 14:6

**Q3. Did Samson tell his father and mother what he had done?**  
**Answer:** No  
**Verse:** Judges 14:6

---

### Judges 14:7

**Q1. Where did Samson go down?**  
**Answer:** To Timnath  
**Verse:** Judges 14:7

**Q2. To whom did Samson talk?**  
**Answer:** The woman  
**Verse:** Judges 14:7

**Q3. How did the woman appear to Samson?**  
**Answer:** She pleased Samson well  
**Verse:** Judges 14:7

---

### Judges 14:8

**Q1. After a time, what did Samson do when he returned?**  
**Answer:** He turned aside to see the carcase of the lion  
**Verse:** Judges 14:8

**Q2. What was in the carcase of the lion?**  
**Answer:** A swarm of bees and honey  
**Verse:** Judges 14:8

**Q3. What else was found in the carcase besides bees?**  
**Answer:** Honey  
**Verse:** Judges 14:8

---

### Judges 14:9

**Q1. How did Samson take the honey into his hands?**  
**Answer:** He took thereof in his hands  
**Verse:** Judges 14:9

**Q2. What did Samson do as he went on eating?**  
**Answer:** He came to his father and mother, and he gave them, and they did eat  
**Verse:** Judges 14:9

**Q3. What did Samson not tell his parents about the honey?**  
**Answer:** That he had taken the honey out of the carcase of the lion  
**Verse:** Judges 14:9

---

### Judges 14:10

**Q1. Who went down unto the woman?**  
**Answer:** His father  
**Verse:** Judges 14:10

**Q2. What did Samson make there?**  
**Answer:** A feast  
**Verse:** Judges 14:10

**Q3. Why did Samson make a feast there?**  
**Answer:** For so used the young men to do  
**Verse:** Judges 14:10

---

### Judges 14:11

**Q1. Whom did they see when they saw him?**  
**Answer:** They brought thirty companions to be with him  
**Verse:** Judges 14:11

**Q2. How many companions were brought?**  
**Answer:** Thirty  
**Verse:** Judges 14:11

**Q3. What were the companions to be with him?**  
**Answer:** Thirty companions  
**Verse:** Judges 14:11

---

### Judges 14:12

**Q1. What did Samson say unto them?**  
**Answer:** I will now put forth a riddle unto you  
**Verse:** Judges 14:12

**Q2. What must the companions do within the seven days of the feast?**  
**Answer:** Find it out and declare it  
**Verse:** Judges 14:12

**Q3. What will Samson give them if they can declare the riddle?**  
**Answer:** Thirty sheets and thirty change of garments  
**Verse:** Judges 14:12

---

### Judges 14:13

**Q1. What did Samson say they should give him if they cannot declare the riddle?**  
**Answer:** Thirty sheets and thirty change of garments  
**Verse:** Judges 14:13

**Q2. What was the companions' reply to Samson's riddle proposal?**  
**Answer:** Put forth thy riddle, that we may hear it  
**Verse:** Judges 14:13

**Q3. Did the companions ask to hear the riddle?**  
**Answer:** Yes  
**Verse:** Judges 14:13

### Judges 14:14

**Q1. What came out of the eater?**  
**Answer:** Meat  
**Verse:** Judges 14:14

**Q2. What came out of the strong?**  
**Answer:** Sweetness  
**Verse:** Judges 14:14

**Q3. How many days could they not expound the riddle?**  
**Answer:** Three days  
**Verse:** Judges 14:14

---

### Judges 14:15

**Q1. On what day did they say unto Samson's wife?**  
**Answer:** The seventh day  
**Verse:** Judges 14:15

**Q2. What did they tell Samson's wife to entice her husband to do?**  
**Answer:** Declare unto us the riddle  
**Verse:** Judges 14:15

**Q3. With what did they threaten Samson's wife and her father's house if she did not entice him?**  
**Answer:** Burn thee and thy father's house with fire  
**Verse:** Judges 14:15

---

### Judges 14:16

**Q1. What did Samson's wife do upon him?**  
**Answer:** She wept  
**Verse:** Judges 14:16

**Q2. Of what did she accuse Samson regarding his feelings?**  
**Answer:** Thou hatest me, and lovest me not  
**Verse:** Judges 14:16

**Q3. What did she complain Samson had not done for her people?**  
**Answer:** Thou hast put forth a riddle unto the children of my people, and hast not told it me  
**Verse:** Judges 14:16

---

### Judges 14:17

**Q1. How many days did she weep before him?**  
**Answer:** The seven days, while their feast lasted  
**Verse:** Judges 14:17

**Q2. Why did Samson finally tell her the riddle on the seventh day?**  
**Answer:** Because she lay sore upon him  
**Verse:** Judges 14:17

**Q3. To whom did she tell the riddle?**  
**Answer:** The children of her people  
**Verse:** Judges 14:17

---

### Judges 14:18

**Q1. What did the men of the city say unto him on the seventh day before the sun went down?**  
**Answer:** What is sweeter than honey? and what is stronger than a lion?  
**Verse:** Judges 14:18

**Q2. What was Samson's reply regarding what they had used?**  
**Answer:** If ye had not plowed with my heifer, ye had not found out my riddle  
**Verse:** Judges 14:18

**Q3. What did Samson accuse them of doing?**  
**Answer:** Plowing with his heifer  
**Verse:** Judges 14:18

---

### Judges 14:19

**Q1. What came upon Samson after the men answered?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit of the LORD  
**Verse:** Judges 14:19

**Q2. Where did Samson go down, and how many men did he slay?**  
**Answer:** To Ashkelon, and slew thirty men of them  
**Verse:** Judges 14:19

**Q3. To whom did Samson give the change of garments?**  
**Answer:** Unto them that expounded the riddle  
**Verse:** Judges 14:19

---

### Judges 14:20

**Q1. How was Samson's wrath kindled?**  
**Answer:** His wrath was kindled  
**Verse:** Judges 14:20

**Q2. Where did Samson go after his wrath was kindled?**  
**Answer:** Up to his father's house  
**Verse:** Judges 14:20

**Q3. Who was given to Samson's companion whom he had used as his friend?**  
**Answer:** Samson's wife  
**Verse:** Judges 14:20
