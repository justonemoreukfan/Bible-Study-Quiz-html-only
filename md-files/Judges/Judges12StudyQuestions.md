# Judges 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Judges 12:1

**Q1. Who gathered themselves together and went northward?**  
**Answer:** The men of Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:1

**Q2. What did the men of Ephraim say to Jephthah concerning his crossing over to fight against the children of Ammon?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore passedst thou over to fight against the children of Ammon, and didst not call us to go with thee?  
**Verse:** Judges 12:1

**Q3. What did the men of Ephraim threaten to do to Jephthah’s house with fire?**  
**Answer:** Burn thine house upon thee with fire.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:1

---

### Judges 12:2

**Q1. What did Jephthah and his people have with the children of Ammon?**  
**Answer:** A great strife.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:2

**Q2. Whom did Jephthah and his people call when they delivered them out of the hands of the children of Ammon?**  
**Answer:** Ye called me not.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:2

---

### Judges 12:3

**Q1. Whose life did Jephthah put in his hands when he passed over against the children of Ammon?**  
**Answer:** My life.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:3

**Q2. Who delivered the children of Ammon into Jephthah's hand?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:3

**Q3. What question did Jephthah ask the men of Ephraim for coming up against him that day?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore then are ye come up unto me this day, to fight against me?  
**Verse:** Judges 12:3

---

### Judges 12:4

**Q1. Who did Jephthah gather together?**  
**Answer:** All the men of Gilead.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:4

**Q2. Whom did Jephthah fight with after gathering the men of Gilead?**  
**Answer:** Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:4

**Q3. What did the men of Gilead smite Ephraim because they said, "Ye Gileadites are fugitives of Ephraim among the Ephraimites, and among the Manassites"?**  
**Answer:** Because they said, Ye Gileadites are fugitives of Ephraim among the Ephraimites, and among the Manassites.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:4

---

### Judges 12:5

**Q1. What did the Gileadites take before Ephraim?**  
**Answer:** The passages of Jordan.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:5

**Q2. Who said, "Let me go over"?**  
**Answer:** Those escapes of Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:5

**Q3. What did the men of Gilead ask the person who desired to cross over?**  
**Answer:** Art thou an Ephraimite?  
**Verse:** Judges 12:5

---

### Judges 12:6

**Q1. What word were the people told to say?**  
**Answer:** Shibboleth.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:6

**Q2. How did the Ephraimite pronounce the word if he could not frame to pronounce it right?**  
**Answer:** Sibboleth.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:6

**Q3. How many men of the Ephraimites fell at that time?**  
**Answer:** Forty and two thousand.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:6

---

### Judges 12:7

**Q1. How long did Jephthah judge Israel?**  
**Answer:** Six years.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:7

**Q2. Who died after judging Israel for six years?**  
**Answer:** Jephthah the Gileadite.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:7

**Q3. Where was Jephthah buried?**  
**Answer:** In the cities of Gilead.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:7

---

### Judges 12:8

**Q1. Who judged Israel after Jephthah?**  
**Answer:** Ibzan of Bethlehem.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:8

---

### Judges 12:9

**Q1. How many sons did Ibzan have?**  
**Answer:** Thirty sons.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:9

**Q2. How many daughters did Ibzan have whom he sent abroad?**  
**Answer:** Thirty daughters.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:9

**Q3. How many daughters did Ibzan take in from abroad for his sons?**  
**Answer:** Thirty daughters.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:9

---

### Judges 12:10

**Q1. How long did Ibzan judge Israel?**  
**Answer:** Seven years.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:10

**Q2. Who died and was buried at Bethlehem?**  
**Answer:** Ibzan.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:10

---

### Judges 12:11

**Q1. Who judged Israel after Ibzan?**  
**Answer:** Elon, a Zebulonite.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:11

**Q2. How long did Elon judge Israel?**  
**Answer:** Ten years.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:11

---

### Judges 12:12

**Q1. Who died and was buried in Aijalon?**  
**Answer:** Elon the Zebulonite.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:12

**Q2. In what country was Aijalon, where Elon was buried?**  
**Answer:** In the country of Zebulun.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:12

---

### Judges 12:13

**Q1. Who judged Israel after Elon the Zebulonite?**  
**Answer:** Abdon the son of Hillel, a Pirathonite.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:13

---

### Judges 12:14

**Q1. How many sons did Abdon have?**  
**Answer:** Forty sons.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:14

**Q2. How many nephews did Abdon have?**  
**Answer:** Thirty nephews.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:14

**Q3. How many asses did Abdon's sons and nephews ride on?**  
**Answer:** Threescore and ten asses.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:14

---

### Judges 12:15

**Q1. How long did Abdon the son of Hillel judge Israel?**  
**Answer:** Eight years.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:15

**Q2. Where did Abdon the Pirathonite die and get buried?**  
**Answer:** In Pirathon in the land of Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:15

**Q3. In whose mount was the place where Abdon was buried?**  
**Answer:** In the mount of the Amalekites.  
**Verse:** Judges 12:15
