# Judges 20 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Judges 20:1

**Q1. Who went out and gathered together from Dan even to Beersheba, with the land of Gilead?**  
**Answer:** All the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 20:1

**Q2. Where was the congregation gathered as one man unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Mizpeh.  
**Verse:** Judges 20:1

**Q3. Whose land was included along with Dan and Beersheba in this gathering?**  
**Answer:** The land of Gilead.  
**Verse:** Judges 20:1

---

### Judges 20:2

**Q4. Who presented themselves in the assembly of the people of God?**  
**Answer:** The chief of all the people, even of all the tribes of Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 20:2

**Q5. How many footmen that drew sword were in this assembly?**  
**Answer:** Four hundred thousand.  
**Verse:** Judges 20:2
