# Judges 13 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Judges 13:1

**Q1. What did the children of Israel do again in the sight of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** They did evil again in the sight of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:1

**Q2. Into whose hand did the LORD deliver the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Into the hand of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:1

**Q3. For how long did the LORD deliver the children of Israel into the hand of the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** Forty years.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:1

---

### Judges 13:2

**Q4. What was the name of the man from Zorah?**  
**Answer:** Manoah.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:2

**Q5. Of what family was the man named Manoah?**  
**Answer:** Of the family of the Danites.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:2

**Q6. What was the condition of Manoah's wife?**  
**Answer:** She was barren, and bare not.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:2

---

### Judges 13:3

**Q7. Who appeared unto the woman?**  
**Answer:** The angel of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:3

**Q8. What did the angel say to the woman regarding her children?**  
**Answer:** Thou art barren, and bearest not: but thou shalt conceive, and bear a son.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:3

---

### Judges 13:4

**Q9. What did the angel instruct the woman not to drink?**  
**Answer:** Wine nor strong drink.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:4

**Q10. What was the woman instructed not to eat?**  
**Answer:** Any unclean thing.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:4

---

### Judges 13:5

**Q11. What shall come upon the woman's head while she bears a son?**  
**Answer:** No razor shall come on his head.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:5

**Q12. What shall the child be unto God from the womb?**  
**Answer:** A Nazarite unto God.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:5

**Q13. What is the child begin to do for Israel?**  
**Answer:** He shall begin to deliver Israel out of the hand of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:5

---

### Judges 13:6

**Q14. To whom did the woman come and tell her experience?**  
**Answer:** Her husband.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:6

**Q15. Who did the woman say came unto her?**  
**Answer:** A man of God.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:6

**Q16. What did the woman say the countenance of the man of God was like?**  
**Answer:** Like the countenance of an angel of God, very terrible.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:6

---

### Judges 13:7

**Q17. What did the man of God say the woman would do?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt conceive and bear a son.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:7

**Q18. What did the man of God tell the woman not to drink?**  
**Answer:** Neither wine nor strong drink.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:7

**Q19. Until what time shall the child be a Nazarite to God?**  
**Answer:** To the day of his death.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:7

---

### Judges 13:8

**Q20. Unto whom did Manoah intreat the LORD?**  
**Answer:** O my Lord.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:8

**Q21. What did Manoah pray that the man of God which came would do?**  
**Answer:** Come again unto us.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:8

**Q22. What did Manoah want the man of God to teach them?**  
**Answer:** What we shall do unto the child that shall be born.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:8

---

### Judges 13:9

**Q23. Did God hearken to the voice of Manoah?**  
**Answer:** God hearkened to the voice of Manoah.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:9

**Q24. Who came again unto the woman?**  
**Answer:** The angel of God.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:9

**Q25. Where was the woman sitting when the angel of God came again?**  
**Answer:** In the field.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:9

---

### Judges 13:10

**Q26. What did the woman make haste to do when the angel appeared?**  
**Answer:** Ran, and shewed her husband.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:10

**Q27. What did the woman say to her husband about the man?**  
**Answer:** Behold, the man hath appeared unto me, that came the other day.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:10

---

### Judges 13:11

**Q28. What did Manoah do upon hearing his wife?**  
**Answer:** Arose, and went after his wife.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:11

**Q29. What question did Manoah ask the man?**  
**Answer:** Art thou the man that spakest unto the woman?  
**Verse:** Judges 13:11

**Q30. What was the man's answer to Manoah's question?**  
**Answer:** I am.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:11

---

### Judges 13:12

**Q31. What did Manoah say would come to pass when his words happened?**  
**Answer:** How shall we order the child, and how shall we do unto him?  
**Verse:** Judges 13:12

---

### Judges 13:13

**Q32. Who spoke unto Manoah?**  
**Answer:** The angel of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:13

**Q33. Of all that I said unto the woman what must she beware of?**  
**Answer:** Let her beware.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:13

---

### Judges 13:14

**Q34. What must the woman not eat from the vine?**  
**Answer:** Any thing that cometh of the vine.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:14

**Q35. What must the woman not drink?**  
**Answer:** Neither let her drink wine or strong drink.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:14

**Q36. What must the woman not eat concerning unclean things?**  
**Answer:** Nor eat any unclean thing.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:14

---

### Judges 13:15

**Q37. What did Manoah say unto the angel of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** I pray thee, let us detain thee, until we shall have made ready a kid for thee.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:15

---

### Judges 13:16

**Q38. What did the angel of the LORD say he would not do even if Manoah detained him?**  
**Answer:** I will not eat of thy bread.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:16

**Q39. What did the angel tell Manoah to offer if he would offer a burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** Offer it unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:16

**Q40. What did Manoah not know about the angel of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** That he was an angel of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:16

---

### Judges 13:17

**Q41. What did Manoah ask the angel of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** What is thy name, that when thy sayings come to pass we may do thee honour?  
**Verse:** Judges 13:17

---

### Judges 13:18

**Q42. What did the angel of the LORD ask Manoah?**  
**Answer:** Why askest thou thus after my name?  
**Verse:** Judges 13:18

**Q43. How did the angel describe his name?**  
**Answer:** Seeing it is secret.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:18

---

### Judges 13:19

**Q44. What did Manoah take along with a meat offering?**  
**Answer:** A kid.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:19

**Q45. Upon what did Manoah offer the kid and the meat offering?**  
**Answer:** Upon a rock.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:19

**Q46. Who did wondrously while Manoah and his wife looked on?**  
**Answer:** The angel of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:19

---

### Judges 13:20

**Q47. Where did the flame go up from off the altar?**  
**Answer:** Toward heaven.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:20

**Q48. Who ascended up in the flame of the altar?**  
**Answer:** The angel of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:20

**Q49. What did Manoah and his wife do when they saw this?**  
**Answer:** Fell on their faces to the ground.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:20

---

### Judges 13:21

**Q50. The angel of the LORD did not appear to whom any more?**  
**Answer:** To Manoah and to his wife.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:21

**Q51. What did Manoah then know?**  
**Answer:** That he was an angel of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:21

---

### Judges 13:22

**Q52. What did Manoah say unto his wife regarding their lives?**  
**Answer:** We shall surely die, because we have seen God.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:22

---

### Judges 13:23

**Q53. What did Manoah's wife say the LORD would not have done if He meant to kill them?**  
**Answer:** Pleased to kill us.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:23

**Q54. What did Manoah's wife say the LORD would not have showed them at this time?**  
**Answer:** All these things.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:23

**Q55. What did Manoah's wife say the LORD would not have told them?**  
**Answer:** Such things as these.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:23

---

### Judges 13:24

**Q56. What did the woman bear?**  
**Answer:** A son.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:24

**Q57. What name did she call her son?**  
**Answer:** Samson.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:24

**Q58. How did the child grow?**  
**Answer:** The child grew.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:24

**Q59. Who blessed the child?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:24

---

### Judges 13:25

**Q60. What spirit began to move Samson at times?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:25

**Q61. In what camp was Samson moved at times?**  
**Answer:** In the camp of Dan.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:25

**Q62. Between what two places is the camp of Dan located?**  
**Answer:** Between Zorah and Eshtaol.  
**Verse:** Judges 13:25
