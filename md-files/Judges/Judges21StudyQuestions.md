# Judges 21 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Judges 21:1

**Q1. Where had the men of Israel sworn an oath?**  
**Answer:** In Mizpeh.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:1

**Q2. What did the men of Israel say regarding their daughters?**  
**Answer:** There shall not any of us give his daughter unto Benjamin to wife.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:1

**Q3. Who made this vow at Mizpeh concerning the tribe of Benjamin?**  
**Answer:** The men of Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:1

---

### Judges 21:2

**Q1. To what place did the people come after making the oath?**  
**Answer:** The house of God.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:2

**Q2. Until when did the people abide there before God?**  
**Answer:** Even until even.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:2

**Q3. What did the people do and how did they express their grief?**  
**Answer:** Lifted up their voices, and wept sore.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:2

---

### Judges 21:3

**Q1. What title did the people use to address the Lord God of Israel?**  
**Answer:** O LORD God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:3

**Q2. What did the people ask regarding something that happened in Israel?**  
**Answer:** Why is this come to pass in Israel?  
**Verse:** Judges 21:3

**Q3. What would be lacking in Israel today because of this event?**  
**Answer:** That there should be to day one tribe lacking in Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:3

---

### Judges 21:4

**Q1. When did the people rise early the next day?**  
**Answer:** On the morrow.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:4

**Q2. What structure did the people build there?**  
**Answer:** An altar.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:4

**Q3. What offerings did the people bring to the altar?**  
**Answer:** Burnt offerings and peace offerings.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:4

---

### Judges 21:5

**Q1. Who did the children of Israel ask about among all the tribes?**  
**Answer:** Who is there among all the tribes of Israel that came not up with the congregation unto the LORD?  
**Verse:** Judges 21:5

**Q2. What great oath had they made concerning anyone who failed to come to the Lord?**  
**Answer:** For they had made a great oath concerning him that came not up to the LORD to Mizpeh.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:5

**Q3. What fate was declared for the person who did not come to the Lord at Mizpeh?**  
**Answer:** Saying, He shall surely be put to death.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:5

---

### Judges 21:6

**Q1. How did the children of Israel feel concerning their brother Benjamin?**  
**Answer:** Repented them for Benjamin their brother.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:6

**Q2. What did the children of Israel say had been cut off?**  
**Answer:** There is one tribe cut off from Israel this day.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:6

**Q3. Who was cut off from Israel this day according to the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Benjamin.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:6

---

### Judges 21:7

**Q1. What question did the children of Israel ask about wives for the survivors?**  
**Answer:** How shall we do for wives for them that remain?  
**Verse:** Judges 21:7

**Q2. Who did they realize were missing from the equation?**  
**Answer:** Them that remain.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:7

**Q3. By what means had they sworn not to give their daughters to them?**  
**Answer:** We have sworn by the LORD that we will not give them of our daughters to wives.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:7

---

### Judges 21:8

**Q1. What question did they ask concerning the tribes of Israel?**  
**Answer:** What one is there of the tribes of Israel that came not up to Mizpeh to the LORD?  
**Verse:** Judges 21:8

**Q2. What did they discover about Jabeshgilead?**  
**Answer:** Behold, there came none to the camp from Jabeshgilead to the assembly.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:8

**Q3. Where was Jabeshgilead in relation to the assembly?**  
**Answer:** None came to the camp from Jabeshgilead to the assembly.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:8

---

### Judges 21:9

**Q1. When the people were numbered, who was found to be absent?**  
**Answer:** Behold, there were none of the inhabitants of Jabeshgilead there.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:9

**Q2. What group was numbered by the assembly?**  
**Answer:** The people.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:9

**Q3. Who was missing from the camp among the inhabitants?**  
**Answer:** The inhabitants of Jabeshgilead.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:9

---

### Judges 21:10

**Q1. How many men of valour did the congregation send to Jabeshgilead?**  
**Answer:** Twelve thousand men of valour.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:10

**Q2. What command did they give to the sent men?**  
**Answer:** Go and smite the inhabitants of Jabeshgilead with the edge of the sword, with the women and the children.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:10

**Q3. Who were to be smitten along with the inhabitants?**  
**Answer:** With the women and the children.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:10

---

### Judges 21:11

**Q1. What specific instruction did they give regarding the males?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall utterly destroy every male.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:11

**Q2. Who else were they commanded to utterly destroy besides the males?**  
**Answer:** And every woman that hath lain by man.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:11

**Q3. How were they instructed to identify the women to be destroyed?**  
**Answer:** Every woman that hath lain by man by sleeping with him.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:11

---

### Judges 21:12

**Q1. Whom did they find among the inhabitants of Jabeshgilead?**  
**Answer:** Four hundred young virgins.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:12

**Q2. What characteristic described these young women?**  
**Answer:** That had known no man by lying with any male.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:12

**Q3. To where did they bring these young virgins?**  
**Answer:** Unto the camp to Shiloh, which is in the land of Canaan.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:12

---

### Judges 21:13

**Q1. Who sent some to speak to the children of Benjamin?**  
**Answer:** The whole congregation.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:13

**Q2. Where were the children of Benjamin when the congregation sent to them?**  
**Answer:** In the rock Rimmon.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:13

**Q3. What action did the congregation take toward the children of Benjamin?**  
**Answer:** And to call peaceably unto them.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:13

---

### Judges 21:14

**Q1. When did Benjamin come again?**  
**Answer:** At that time.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:14

**Q2. Whom did they give to Benjamin as wives?**  
**Answer:** The women which they had saved alive of the women of Jabeshgilead.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:14

**Q3. What was noted about the women given to Benjamin?**  
**Answer:** And yet so they sufficed them not.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:14

---

### Judges 21:15

**Q1. How did the people feel about Benjamin?**  
**Answer:** The people repented them because that the LORD had made a breach in the tribes of Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:15

**Q2. Who did the people feel had made a breach in the tribes of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:15

**Q3. What event involving Benjamin caused this feeling?**  
**Answer:** A breach in the tribes of Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:15

---

### Judges 21:16

**Q1. Who spoke to the congregation?**  
**Answer:** The elders of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:16

**Q2. What question did the elders ask about wives?**  
**Answer:** How shall we do for wives for them that remain, seeing the women are destroyed out of Benjamin?  
**Verse:** Judges 21:16

**Q3. What had happened to the women out of Benjamin?**  
**Answer:** The women are destroyed out of Benjamin.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:16

---

### Judges 21:17

**Q1. What did the elders say must be for them that have escaped of Benjamin?**  
**Answer:** There must be an inheritance for them that be escaped of Benjamin.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:17

**Q2. What must not be blotted out according to the elders?**  
**Answer:** That a tribe be not destroyed out of Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:17

**Q3. Who were the survivors referred to in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Them that be escaped of Benjamin.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:17

---

### Judges 21:18

**Q1. What reason did the elders give for not giving their daughters to Benjamin?**  
**Answer:** We may not give them wives of our daughters.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:18

**Q2. What did the children of Israel swear?**  
**Answer:** For the children of Israel have sworn, saying, Cursed be he that giveth a wife to Benjamin.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:18

**Q3. What was pronounced upon anyone who gave a wife to Benjamin?**  
**Answer:** Cursed be he that giveth a wife to Benjamin.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:18

---

### Judges 21:19

**Q1. What feast of the LORD was held yearly?**  
**Answer:** A feast of the LORD in Shiloh yearly.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:19

**Q2. Where was Shiloh located geographically in relation to Bethel and Lebonah?**  
**Answer:** On the north side of Bethel, on the east side of the highway that goeth up from Bethel to Shechem, and on the south of Lebonah.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:19

**Q3. What was north of Shiloh according to the directions given?**  
**Answer:** Bethel.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:19

---

### Judges 21:20

**Q1. What did the elders command the children of Benjamin to do?**  
**Answer:** Go and lie in wait in the vineyards.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:20

**Q2. Where were the children of Benjamin told to hide?**  
**Answer:** In the vineyards.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:20

**Q3. Who gave this instruction to the children of Benjamin?**  
**Answer:** The elders.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:20

---

### Judges 21:21

**Q1. What should the children of Benjamin watch for?**  
**Answer:** And see, and, behold, if the daughters of Shiloh come out to dance in dances.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:21

**Q2. What were the daughters of Shiloh doing?**  
**Answer:** Come out to dance in dances.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:21

**Q3. What were the men of Benjamin told to do when the daughters came out?**  
**Answer:** Then come ye out of the vineyards, and catch you every man his wife of the daughters of Shiloh, and go to the land of Benjamin.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:21

---

### Judges 21:22

**Q1. What did the elders say they would tell the fathers or brethren when they came to complain?**  
**Answer:** Be favourable unto them for our sakes.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:22

**Q2. What reason did the elders give for reserving each man his wife in the war?**  
**Answer:** Because we reserved not to each man his wife in the war.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:22

**Q3. What did the elders note about the people of Shiloh giving wives to Benjamin?**  
**Answer:** For ye did not give them unto them at this time, that ye should be guilty.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:22

---

### Judges 21:23

**Q1. What did the children of Benjamin do according to the instructions?**  
**Answer:** And did so, and took them wives, according to their number, of them that danced, whom they caught.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:23

**Q2. How did they obtain these wives?**  
**Answer:** Whom they caught.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:23

**Q3. Where did the Benjamites go after taking the wives?**  
**Answer:** And went and returned unto their inheritance, and repaired the cities, and dwelt in them.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:23

---

### Judges 21:24

**Q1. When did the children of Israel depart from thence?**  
**Answer:** At that time.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:24

**Q2. To what destinations did the people depart?**  
**Answer:** Every man to his tribe and to his family.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:24

**Q3. Where did every man go from that place?**  
**Answer:** And they went out from thence every man to his tribe and to his family, and they went out from thence every man to his inheritance.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:24

---

### Judges 21:25

**Q1. What condition regarding kings is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** In those days there was no king in Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:25

**Q2. What did every man do in those days?**  
**Answer:** Every man did that which was right in his own eyes.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:25

**Q3. Whose eyes were used as the standard for what every man did?**  
**Answer:** His own eyes.  
**Verse:** Judges 21:25
