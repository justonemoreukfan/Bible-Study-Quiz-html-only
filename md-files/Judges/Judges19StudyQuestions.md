# Judges 19 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Judges 19:1

**Q1. Who had no king in Israel during these days?**  
**Answer:** There was no king in Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:1

**Q2. Where was a certain Levite a stranger?**  
**Answer:** On the side of mount Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:1

**Q3. From where did the Levite take to him a concubine?**  
**Answer:** Out of Bethlehemjudah.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:1

---

### Judges 19:2

**Q1. What did the concubine do against her husband?**  
**Answer:** Played the whore against him.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:2

**Q2. Where did the concubine go away from him?**  
**Answer:** Away from him unto her father's house to Bethlehemjudah.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:2

**Q3. How long was the concubine there?**  
**Answer:** Four whole months.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:2

---

### Judges 19:3

**Q1. Who arose and went after the concubine?**  
**Answer:** Her husband.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:3

**Q2. With whom did the husband speak friendly to bring her again?**  
**Answer:** With her.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:3

**Q3. Who brought the husband into his house?**  
**Answer:** The damsel's father.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:3

---

### Judges 19:4

**Q1. Who retained the husband?**  
**Answer:** His father in law, the damsel's father.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:4

**Q2. How many days did the husband abide with him?**  
**Answer:** Three days.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:4

**Q3. What did they do during those days?**  
**Answer:** Did eat and drink, and lodged there.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:4

---

### Judges 19:5

**Q1. On which day did they arise early in the morning to depart?**  
**Answer:** On the fourth day.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:5

**Q2. What did the damsel's father say unto his son in law?**  
**Answer:** Comfort thine heart with a morsel of bread, and afterward go your way.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:5

**Q3. What did the two do after sitting down?**  
**Answer:** Did eat and drink both of them together.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:5

---

### Judges 19:6

**Q1. What did the father-in-law desire the man to do?**  
**Answer:** Tarry, I pray thee, and let thine heart be merry.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:6

**Q2. Who sat down together according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** The man and his father in law.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:6

---

### Judges 19:7

**Q1. When the man rose up to depart, what did his father in law do?**  
**Answer:** Urged him.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:7

**Q2. Where did the man return and lodge after being urged?**  
**Answer:** Lodged there yet again.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:7

---

### Judges 19:8

**Q1. On which day did the man arise early in the morning to depart?**  
**Answer:** On the fifth day.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:8

**Q2. What did the damsel's father say to the man?**  
**Answer:** Comfort thine heart, I pray thee.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:8

**Q3. Until what time did they delay?**  
**Answer:** Until afternoon.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:8

---

### Judges 19:9

**Q1. Who rose up to depart, he, and his concubine, and his servant?**  
**Answer:** The man.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:9

**Q2. What did his father in law, the damsel's father, say about the day?**  
**Answer:** Behold, now the day draweth toward evening.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:9

**Q3. What did the father in law suggest they do instead of leaving?**  
**Answer:** Lodge here, that thine heart may be merry; and to morrow get you early on your way.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:9

---

### Judges 19:10

**Q1. Who would not tarry that night?**  
**Answer:** The man.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:10

**Q2. Over against what place did he rise up and depart?**  
**Answer:** Over against Jebus, which is Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:10

**Q3. What did he have with him, besides his concubine?**  
**Answer:** His ass saddled, his concubine also was with him.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:10

---

### Judges 19:11

**Q1. Where were they when the day was far spent?**  
**Answer:** By Jebus.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:11

**Q2. What did the servant say unto his master concerning Jebus?**  
**Answer:** Come, I pray thee, and let us turn in into this city of the Jebusites, and lodge in it.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:11

---

### Judges 19:12

**Q1. What kind of city did the master say it was?**  
**Answer:** A city of a stranger.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:12

**Q2. Whose children were not in the city where the master refused to turn aside?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:12

**Q3. To where did the master say he would pass over?**  
**Answer:** To Gibeah.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:12

---

### Judges 19:13

**Q1. What did the master say to his servant regarding two places?**  
**Answer:** Come, and let us draw near to one of these places to lodge all night, in Gibeah, or in Ramah.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:13

**Q2. How many places were mentioned as options to draw near to?**  
**Answer:** Two places (Gibeah, or in Ramah).  
**Verse:** Judges 19:13

---

### Judges 19:14

**Q1. Who passed on and went their way?**  
**Answer:** They.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:14

**Q2. Which city went down upon them as they passed?**  
**Answer:** Gibeah.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:14

**Q3. In which tribe's territory did Gibeah belong?**  
**Answer:** Which belongeth to Benjamin.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:14

---

### Judges 19:15

**Q1. Whither did they turn aside to go in and lodge in Gibeah?**  
**Answer:** To go in and to lodge in Gibeah.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:15

**Q2. Where did the man sit down when he went into the city?**  
**Answer:** In a street of the city.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:15

**Q3. What was the result regarding taking them into a house to lodge?**  
**Answer:** There was no man that took them into his house to lodging.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:15

---

### Judges 19:16

**Q1. Who came out of his work from the field at even?**  
**Answer:** An old man.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:16

**Q2. From where was the old man by descent?**  
**Answer:** From mount Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:16

**Q3. Where did the old man sojourn?**  
**Answer:** In Gibeah.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:16

---

### Judges 19:17

**Q1. When the old man had lifted up his eyes, what did he see?**  
**Answer:** A wayfaring man in the street of the city.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:17

**Q2. What question did the old man ask the wayfaring man?**  
**Answer:** Whither goest thou? and whence comest thou?  
**Verse:** Judges 19:17

---

### Judges 19:18

**Q1. From where were they passing over?**  
**Answer:** From Bethlehemjudah toward the side of mount Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:18

**Q2. Where had the traveler been?**  
**Answer:** To Bethlehemjudah.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:18

**Q3. To whose house was no man going?**  
**Answer:** The house of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:18

---

### Judges 19:19

**Q1. What did the traveler say they had for their asses?**  
**Answer:** Both straw and provender for our asses.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:19

**Q2. What did the traveler say they lacked for himself, his concubine, and the servant?**  
**Answer:** There is no bread nor wine for me, nor for thy handmaid, nor for the young man which is with thy servants.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:19

**Q3. What did the traveler state about their needs?**  
**Answer:** There is no want of any thing.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:19

---

### Judges 19:20

**Q1. What did the old man tell the traveler regarding peace?**  
**Answer:** Peace be with thee.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:20

**Q2. What did the old man say about all the traveler's wants?**  
**Answer:** Howsoever, let all thy wants lie upon me.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:20

**Q3. Where did the old man command the traveler not to lodge?**  
**Answer:** Only lodge not in the street.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:20

---

### Judges 19:21

**Q1. Into whose house did the old man bring the traveler?**  
**Answer:** Into his house.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:21

**Q2. What did the old man give to the asses?**  
**Answer:** Provender.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:21

**Q3. What did the travelers wash and do after coming in?**  
**Answer:** Washed their feet, and did eat and drink.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:21

---

### Judges 19:22

**Q1. What kind of men beset the house round about?**  
**Answer:** Certain sons of Belial.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:22

**Q2. What city were these men from?**  
**Answer:** The men of the city.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:22

**Q3. What did these men say to the master of the house, the old man?**  
**Answer:** Bring forth the man that came into thine house, that we may know him.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:22

---

### Judges 19:23

**Q1. Who went out unto the men of the city?**  
**Answer:** The man, the master of the house.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:23

**Q2. What did the master of the house call the men?**  
**Answer:** My brethren.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:23

**Q3. What did the master ask the men not to do?**  
**Answer:** I pray you, do not so wickedly.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:23

---

### Judges 19:24

**Q1. Whom did the master offer to bring out unto them instead?**  
**Answer:** My daughter a maiden, and his concubine.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:24

**Q2. What did the master tell the wicked men to do with them?**  
**Answer:** Humble ye them, and do with them what seemeth good unto you.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:24

**Q3. What did the master ask the men not to do to the man?**  
**Answer:** Unto this man do not so vile a thing.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:24

---

### Judges 19:25

**Q1. Would the men hearken to the master?**  
**Answer:** The men would not hearken to him.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:25

**Q2. What did the man take and bring forth unto them?**  
**Answer:** His concubine.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:25

**Q3. What did the men do unto her all night until the morning?**  
**Answer:** Knew her, and abused her all the night until the morning.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:25

---

### Judges 19:26

**Q1. When did the woman come?**  
**Answer:** In the dawning of the day.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:26

**Q2. Where did the woman fall down?**  
**Answer:** At the door of the man's house where her lord was.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:26

**Q3. Until what time did she lie there?**  
**Answer:** Till it was light.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:26

---

### Judges 19:27

**Q1. Who rose up in the morning?**  
**Answer:** Her lord.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:27

**Q2. What did her lord do to the doors of the house?**  
**Answer:** Opened the doors of the house.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:27

**Q3. Where was the woman found when he went out?**  
**Answer:** Fallen down at the door of the house, and her hands were upon the threshold.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:27

---

### Judges 19:28

**Q1. What did the man say to the woman?**  
**Answer:** Up, and let us be going.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:28

**Q2. What was the woman's response?**  
**Answer:** None answered.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:28

**Q3. Where did the man take her?**  
**Answer:** Upon an ass, and the man rose up, and gat him unto his place.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:28

---

### Judges 19:29

**Q1. What did the man take when he came into his house?**  
**Answer:** A knife.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:29

**Q2. Whom did the man lay hold on and divide into pieces?**  
**Answer:** His concubine.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:29

**Q3. Into how many pieces did he divide her?**  
**Answer:** Into twelve pieces.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:29

---

### Judges 19:30

**Q1. What did all that saw it say?**  
**Answer:** There was no such deed done nor seen from the day that the children of Israel came up out of the land of Egypt unto this day.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:30

**Q2. What did the people consider regarding this matter?**  
**Answer:** Consider of it, take advice, and speak your minds.  
**Verse:** Judges 19:30
