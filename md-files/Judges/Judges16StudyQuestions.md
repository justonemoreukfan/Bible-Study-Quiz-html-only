# Judges 16 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Judges 16:1

**Q1. Where did Samson go in Judges 16:1?**  
**Answer:** Gaza.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:1

**Q2. Whom did Samson see when he went to Gaza?**  
**Answer:** An harlot.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:1

**Q3. What did Samson do after seeing the harlot in Gaza?**  
**Answer:** Went in unto her.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:1

---

### Judges 16:2

**Q1. Who was told that Samson was come thither?**  
**Answer:** The Gazites.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:2

**Q2. What did the Gazites compass round, and lay wait for him?**  
**Answer:** The gate of the city.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:2

**Q3. During what time did the Gazites lay wait all night in the gate of the city?**  
**Answer:** All night.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:2

---

### Judges 16:3

**Q1. How long did Samson lie till?**  
**Answer:** Midnight.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:3

**Q2. What parts of the gate did Samson take hold of?**  
**Answer:** The doors of the gate of the city, and the two posts.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:3

**Q3. Where did Samson carry the gate doors, posts, and bar?**  
**Answer:** To the top of an hill that is before Hebron.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:3

---

### Judges 16:4

**Q2. What was the name of the woman Samson loved?**  
**Answer:** Delilah.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:4

**Q3. In what valley did Samson love Delilah?**  
**Answer:** The valley of Sorek.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:4

### Judges 16:5

**Q1. Who came up unto Delilah?**  
**Answer:** The lords of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:5

**Q2. What did the lords of the Philistines ask Delilah to entice Samson to see?**  
**Answer:** Where his great strength lieth, and by what means we may prevail against him.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:5

**Q3. How much money did every lord promise to give Delilah?**  
**Answer:** Eleven hundred pieces of silver.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:5

### Judges 16:6

**Answer:** Wherewith thou mightest be bound to afflict thee.

**Q3. Who asked Samson where his great strength lieth?**  
**Answer:** Delilah.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:6

---

### Judges 16:7

**Q1. What did Samson say he would be as another man if bound with?**  
**Answer:** Seven green withs that were not dried.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:7

**Q2. What condition must the seven green withs be in?**  
**Answer:** Not dried.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:7

**Q3. If bound with these, what would Samson become?**  
**Answer:** Weak, and as another man.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:7

---

### Judges 16:8

**Q1. Who brought up seven green withs to Delilah?**  
**Answer:** The lords of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:8

**Q2. What did Delilah do with the seven green withs brought to her?**  
**Answer:** Bound him with them.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:8

**Q3. How many green withs were brought to Delilah?**  
**Answer:** Seven.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:8

### Judges 16:9

**Q1. Where were men abiding with her?**  
**Answer:** In the chamber.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:9

**Answer:** The Philistines be upon thee, Samson.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:9

**Answer:** As a thread of tow is broken when it toucheth the fire.  
**Verse:** Judges 16:9
