# Judges 17 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Judges 17:1

**Q1. Who was of mount Ephraim?**  
**Answer:** A man whose name was Micah.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:1

**Q2. Whose name was Micah?**  
**Answer:** A man of mount Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:1

**Q3. Where was the man from who was named Micah?**  
**Answer:** Mount Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:1

---

### Judges 17:2

**Q1. What did Micah say to his mother regarding the eleven hundred shekels of silver?**  
**Answer:** The eleven hundred shekels of silver that were taken from thee, about which thou cursedst, and spakest of also in mine ears, behold, the silver is with me; I took it.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:2

**Q2. What did his mother say in response to Micah?**  
**Answer:** Blessed be thou of the LORD, my son.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:2

**Q3. How much silver did Micah return to his mother?**  
**Answer:** Eleven hundred shekels of silver.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:2

---

### Judges 17:3

**Q1. What did Micah restore to his mother?**  
**Answer:** The eleven hundred shekels of silver to his mother.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:3

**Q2. What did his mother say she had wholly dedicated the silver for?**  
**Answer:** From her hand unto the LORD for her son, to make a graven image and a molten image.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:3

**Q3. What did his mother say she would do with the silver now?**  
**Answer:** Restore it again unto thee.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:3

---

### Judges 17:4

**Q1. How much money did Micah restore to his mother?**  
**Answer:** Two hundred shekels of silver.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:4

**Q2. To whom did his mother give the two hundred shekels of silver?**  
**Answer:** The founder.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:4

**Q3. What did the founder make with the silver?**  
**Answer:** A graven image and a molten image.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:4

---

### Judges 17:5

**Q1. What did the man Micah have?**  
**Answer:** An house of gods.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:5

**Q2. What did Micah make in addition to his house of gods?**  
**Answer:** An ephod, and teraphim.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:5

**Q3. Whom did Micah consecrate to be his priest?**  
**Answer:** One of his sons.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:5

---

### Judges 17:6

**Q1. What was the condition of Israel in those days?**  
**Answer:** There was no king in Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:6

**Q2. What did every man do in those days?**  
**Answer:** That which was right in his own eyes.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:6

**Q3. When was there no king in Israel?**  
**Answer:** In those days.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:6

---

### Judges 17:7

**Q1. Where was there a young man?**  
**Answer:** Out of Bethlehemjudah of the family of Judah.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:7

**Q2. Who was the young man out of Bethlehemjudah?**  
**Answer:** A Levite.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:7

**Q3. Where did the young man sojourn?**  
**Answer:** There.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:7

---

### Judges 17:8

**Q1. From where did the man depart?**  
**Answer:** Out of the city of Bethlehemjudah to sojourn where he could find a place.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:8

**Q2. To whose house did the man come as he journeyed?**  
**Answer:** To mount Ephraim, to the house of Micah.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:8

**Q3. As he journeyed, what did the man do?**  
**Answer:** He came to mount Ephraim, to the house of Micah, as he journeyed.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:8

---

### Judges 17:9

**Q1. What did Micah ask him?**  
**Answer:** Whence comest thou?  
**Verse:** Judges 17:9

**Q2. What was the Levite's response to Micah's question about where he came from?**  
**Answer:** I am a Levite of Bethlehemjudah, and I go to sojourn where I may find a place.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:9

**Q3. Of what place and family was the Levite?**  
**Answer:** Bethlehemjudah.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:9

---

### Judges 17:10

**Q1. What did Micah say to the Levite?**  
**Answer:** Dwell with me, and be unto me a father and a priest.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:10

**Q2. What did Micah promise to give him by the year?**  
**Answer:** Ten shekels of silver, and a suit of apparel, and thy victuals.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:10

**Q3. What did the Levite do?**  
**Answer:** He went in.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:10

---

### Judges 17:11

**Q1. What was the Levite content to do?**  
**Answer:** To dwell with the man.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:11

**Q2. How was the young man regarded by Micah?**  
**Answer:** As one of his sons.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:11

**Q3. Whose son was the young man regarded as?**  
**Answer:** Micah's.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:11

---

### Judges 17:12

**Q1. What did Micah do to the Levite?**  
**Answer:** Consecrated the Levite.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:12

**Q2. Who became Micah's priest?**  
**Answer:** The young man.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:12

**Q3. Where did the young man dwell?**  
**Answer:** In the house of Micah.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:12

---

### Judges 17:13

**Q1. What did Micah say he knew?**  
**Answer:** That the LORD will do me good.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:13

**Q2. Why did Micah say the LORD would do him good?**  
**Answer:** Seeing I have a Levite to my priest.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:13

**Q3. Who had become Micah's priest to make him feel the LORD would do him good?**  
**Answer:** A Levite.  
**Verse:** Judges 17:13
