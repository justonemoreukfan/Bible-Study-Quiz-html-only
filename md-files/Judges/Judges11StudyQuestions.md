# Judges 11 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Judges 11:1

**Q1. What was the name of the mighty man of valour in Judges 11:1?**  
**Answer:** Jephthah.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:1

**Q2. Who was Jephthah's father?**  
**Answer:** Gilead.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:1

**Q3. Who was Jephthah's mother?**  
**Answer:** An harlot.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:1

---

### Judges 11:2

**Q4. Who else bore sons unto Gilead besides Jephthah's mother?**  
**Answer:** Gilead's wife.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:2

**Q5. What happened to Gilead's wife's sons when they grew up?**  
**Answer:** They thrust out Jephthah.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:2

**Q6. What reason did Gilead's wife's sons give for casting Jephthah out?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not inherit in our father's house; for thou art the son of a strange woman.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:2

---

### Judges 11:3

**Q7. Where did Jephthah flee from his brethren?**  
**Answer:** The land of Tob.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:3

**Q8. Who gathered themselves to Jephthah in the land of Tob?**  
**Answer:** Vain men.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:3

**Q9. What did the vain men do with Jephthah?**  
**Answer:** They went out with him.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:3

---

### Judges 11:4

**Q10. What happened in process of time regarding the children of Ammon?**  
**Answer:** They made war against Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:4

---

### Judges 11:5

**Q11. What did the elders of Gilead do when the children of Ammon made war against Israel?**  
**Answer:** They went to fetch Jephthah out of the land of Tob.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:5

---

### Judges 11:6

**Q12. What did the elders say unto Jephthah?**  
**Answer:** Come, and be our captain, that we may fight with the children of Ammon.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:6

---

### Judges 11:7

**Q13. How did Jephthah address the elders of Gilead?**  
**Answer:** Did not ye hate me, and expel me out of my father's house?  
**Verse:** Judges 11:7

**Q14. What question did Jephthah ask the elders about their current situation?**  
**Answer:** And why are ye come unto me now when ye are in distress?  
**Verse:** Judges 11:7

---

### Judges 11:8

**Q15. What did the elders of Gilead say to Jephthah concerning their turning again to him?**  
**Answer:** Therefore we turn again to thee now.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:8

**Q16. What did the elders want Jephthah to do with them?**  
**Answer:** Go with us, and fight against the children of Ammon.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:8

**Q17. What position did the elders promise Jephthah over the inhabitants of Gilead?**  
**Answer:** Be our head over all the inhabitants of Gilead.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:8

---

### Judges 11:9

**Q18. What condition did Jephthah state for returning home with the elders?**  
**Answer:** If ye bring me home again to fight against the children of Ammon, and the LORD deliver them before me.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:9

**Q19. What title did Jephthah ask if they would truly give him?**  
**Answer:** Shall I be your head?  
**Verse:** Judges 11:9

---

### Judges 11:10

**Q20. Who did the elders of Gilead say was witness between them?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:10

**Q21. What did the elders vow to do regarding Jephthah's words?**  
**Answer:** Be it so according to thy words.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:10

---

### Judges 11:11

**Q22. Who went with the elders of Gilead?**  
**Answer:** Jephthah.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:11

**Q23. What did the people do with Jephthah at Mizpeh?**  
**Answer:** Made him head and captain over them.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:11

**Q24. Where did Jephthah utter all his words before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** In Mizpeh.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:11

---

### Judges 11:12

**Q25. Whom did Jephthah send messengers unto?**  
**Answer:** The king of the children of Ammon.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:12

**Q26. What question did Jephthah ask the king through his messengers?**  
**Answer:** What hast thou to do with me, that thou art come against me to fight in my land?  
**Verse:** Judges 11:12

---

### Judges 11:13

**Q27. What answer did the king of the children of Ammon give to Jephthah's messengers?**  
**Answer:** Because Israel took away my land, when they came up out of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:13

**Q28. Which specific bodies of water did the king say Israel's land acquisition reached?**  
**Answer:** From Arnon even unto Jabbok, and unto Jordan.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:13

**Q29. What demand did the king make regarding the land?**  
**Answer:** Now therefore restore those lands again peaceably.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:13

---

### Judges 11:14

**Q30. What did Jephthah do again regarding the king of the children of Ammon?**  
**Answer:** Sent messengers.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:14

---

### Judges 11:15

**Q31. What did the messengers say Israel did not take away?**  
**Answer:** The land of Moab, nor the land of the children of Ammon.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:15

---

### Judges 11:16

**Q32. Where did Israel walk after coming up from Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Through the wilderness unto the Red sea.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:16

**Q33. Where did Israel come after walking through the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** Kadesh.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:16

---

### Judges 11:17

**Q34. Who did Israel send messengers unto asking to pass through his land?**  
**Answer:** The king of Edom.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:17

**Q35. Did the king of Edom hearken to Israel's request?**  
**Answer:** No.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:17

**Q36. Whom else did Israel send messengers unto, who also would not consent?**  
**Answer:** The king of Moab.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:17

---

### Judges 11:18

**Q37. Through which places did Israel go along as they journeyed through the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** Compassed the land of Edom, and the land of Moab.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:18

**Q38. On which side of the land of Moab did Israel come?**  
**Answer:** The east side of the land of Moab.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:18

**Q39. Where did Israel pitch their tents on the other side of Arnon?**  
**Answer:** In the border of Moab.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:18

---

### Judges 11:19

**Q40. Who did Israel send messengers unto regarding Sihon king of the Amorites?**  
**Answer:** Sihon king of the Amorites, the king of Heshbon.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:19

**Q41. What did Israel say to Sihon?**  
**Answer:** Let us pass, we pray thee, through thy land into my place.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:19

---

### Judges 11:20

**Q42. Did Sihon trust Israel to pass through his coast?**  
**Answer:** No.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:20

**Q43. What did Sihon gather together?**  
**Answer:** All his people.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:20

**Q44. Where did Sihon pitch?**  
**Answer:** In Jahaz.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:20

---

### Judges 11:21

**Q45. Who delivered Sihon and all his people into the hand of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:21

**Q46. What did Israel do to Sihon and his people?**  
**Answer:** They smote them.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:21

**Q47. What did Israel possess throughout all the land of the Amorites?**  
**Answer:** All the land of the Amorites, the inhabitants of that country.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:21

---

### Judges 11:22

**Q48. What borders did Israel possess from Arnon even unto Jabbok?**  
**Answer:** All the coasts of the Amorites.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:22

**Q49. From which wilderness unto which river did Israel possess the coasts?**  
**Answer:** From the wilderness even unto Jordan.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:22

---

### Judges 11:23

**Q50. Who dispossessed the Amorites from before his people Israel?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:23

**Q51. What question did Jephthah ask the king of Ammon about possessing that land?**  
**Answer:** And shouldest thou possess it?  
**Verse:** Judges 11:23

---

### Judges 11:24

**Q52. What do you possess that Chemosh your god giveth you to possess?**  
**Answer:** Wilt not thou possess that which Chemosh thy god giveth thee to possess?  
**Verse:** Judges 11:24

**Q53. Who did Jephthah say the LORD their God drove out from before them?**  
**Answer:** Whomsoever the LORD our God shall drive out from before us, them will we possess.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:24

---

### Judges 11:25

**Q54. Who was Balak the son of Zippor compared to, according to Jephthah?**  
**Answer:** King of Moab.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:25

**Q55. Did Balak strive against Israel or fight against them?**  
**Answer:** He did not strive against Israel, nor fight against them.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:25

---

### Judges 11:26

**Q56. How long did Israel dwell in Heshbon and her towns, and in Aroer and her towns, and in all the cities by the coasts of Arnon?**  
**Answer:** Three hundred years.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:26

**Q57. What question did Jephthah ask about why they did not recover those cities within that time?**  
**Answer:** Why did ye not recover them within that time?  
**Verse:** Judges 11:26

---

### Judges 11:27

**Q58. Who has not sinned against the king of Ammon?**  
**Answer:** Jephthah (I have not sinned against thee).  
**Verse:** Judges 11:27

**Q59. Who doest thou wrong against, according to Jephthah?**  
**Answer:** Thou doest me wrong to war against me.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:27

**Q60. Who did Jephthah call as judge between the children of Israel and the children of Ammon?**  
**Answer:** The LORD the Judge.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:27

---

### Judges 11:28

**Q61. Did the king of the children of Ammon hearken unto the words of Jephthah?**  
**Answer:** He hearkened not.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:28

**Q62. Who sent the words to the king of the children of Ammon?**  
**Answer:** Jephthah.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:28

---

### Judges 11:29

**Q63. Whose Spirit came upon Jephthah?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:29

---

### Judges 11:30

**Q64. What did Jephthah vow unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** If thou shalt without fail deliver the children of Ammon into mine hands.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:30

---

### Judges 11:31

**Q65. What did Jephthah promise would surely come forth to meet him from the doors of his house?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever cometh forth of the doors of my house to meet me.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:31

**Q66. To whom would that which came forth belong?**  
**Answer:** The LORD'S.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:31

**Q67. What did Jephthah vow to offer it up for?**  
**Answer:** A burnt offering.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:31

---

### Judges 11:32

**Q68. Over to whom did Jephthah pass over unto the children of Ammon?**  
**Answer:** To fight against them.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:32

**Q69. Who delivered the children of Ammon into Jephthah's hands?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:32

---

### Judges 11:33

**Q70. From where unto Minnith did Jephthah smite them?**  
**Answer:** From Aroer.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:33

**Q71. How many cities did he smite unto the plain of the vineyards?**  
**Answer:** Even twenty cities.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:33

**Q72. Who were subdued before the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The children of Ammon.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:33

---

### Judges 11:34

**Q73. To where did Jephthah come to his house at Mizpeh?**  
**Answer:** His house.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:34

**Q74. Who came out to meet Jephthah with timbrels and with dances?**  
**Answer:** His daughter.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:34

**Q75. What relationship was she to him besides being his daughter?**  
**Answer:** She was his only child; beside her he had neither son nor daughter.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:34

---

### Judges 11:35

**Q76. What did Jephthah do when he saw his daughter?**  
**Answer:** He rent his clothes.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:35

**Q77. What did Jephthah say had brought him very low?**  
**Answer:** Alas, my daughter! thou hast brought me very low, and thou art one of them that trouble me.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:35

**Q78. What did Jephthah say he had opened unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** I have opened my mouth unto the LORD, and I cannot go back.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:35

---

### Judges 11:36

**Q79. What did his daughter say unto him regarding his mouth opened unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** My father, if thou hast opened thy mouth unto the LORD, do to me according to that which hath proceeded out of thy mouth.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:36

**Q80. What reason did she give for him doing to her according to his vow?**  
**Answer:** Forasmuch as the LORD hath taken vengeance for thee of thine enemies, even of the children of Ammon.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:36

---

### Judges 11:37

**Q81. What did the daughter ask her father to let her do for two months?**  
**Answer:** Let this thing be done for me: let me alone two months.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:37

**Q82. Where did she want to go up and down?**  
**Answer:** Up and down upon the mountains.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:37

**Q83. What did she want to bewail?**  
**Answer:** My virginity, I and my fellows.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:37

---

### Judges 11:38

**Q84. What did her father say?**  
**Answer:** Go.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:38

**Q85. For how long did he send her away?**  
**Answer:** Two months.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:38

**Q86. Who went with her to bewail her virginity upon the mountains?**  
**Answer:** She and her companions.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:38

---

### Judges 11:39

**Q87. What happened at the end of two months?**  
**Answer:** She returned unto her father.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:39

**Q88. What did her father do with her according to his vow which he had vowed?**  
**Answer:** Who did with her according to his vow which he had vowed.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:39

**Q89. What did she know no man of?**  
**Answer:** She knew no man.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:39

---

### Judges 11:40

**Q90. What custom became a custom in Israel?**  
**Answer:** That the daughters of Israel went yearly.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:40

**Q91. For how many days did the daughters of Israel go yearly to lament the daughter of Jephthah the Gileadite?**  
**Answer:** Four days in a year.  
**Verse:** Judges 11:40
