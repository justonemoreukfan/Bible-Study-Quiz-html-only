# 2 Peter 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Peter 2:1

**Q1. Who was among the people in time past, according to the verse?**  
**Answer:** False prophets.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:1

**Q2. What will there be among the people, comparable to the false prophets of the past?**  
**Answer:** False teachers.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:1

**Q3. What kind of heresies will these false teachers privily bring in?**  
**Answer:** Damnable heresies.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:1

---

### 2 Peter 2:2

**Q1. What will many follow concerning these false teachers?**  
**Answer:** Their pernicious ways.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:2

**Q2. What will be spoken of by reason of these false teachers?**  
**Answer:** The way of truth.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:2

**Q3. How will the way of truth be spoken of?**  
**Answer:** Evil spoken of.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:2

---

### 2 Peter 2:3

**Q1. Through what will these false teachers make merchandise of you?**  
**Answer:** Covetousness.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:3

**Q2. What has been prepared of a long time for them?**  
**Answer:** Their judgment.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:3

**Q3. What does not slumber concerning them?**  
**Answer:** Their damnation.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:3

---

### 2 Peter 2:4

**Q1. Whom did God not spare when they sinned?**  
**Answer:** The angels.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:4

**Q2. Where did God cast the sinning angels?**  
**Answer:** Down to hell.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:4

**Q3. Into what kind of chains were they delivered to be reserved unto judgment?**  
**Answer:** Chains of darkness.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:4

---

### 2 Peter 2:5

**Q1. Whose old world did God not spare?**  
**Answer:** The old world.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:5

**Q2. Who was saved when the flood was brought upon the world of the ungodly?**  
**Answer:** Noah the eighth person.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:5

**Q3. What occupation or role is Noah described as having?**  
**Answer:** A preacher of righteousness.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:5

---

### 2 Peter 2:6

**Q1. What cities did God turn into ashes?**  
**Answer:** The cities of Sodom and Gomorrha.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:6

**Q2. With what kind of overthrow were these cities condemned?**  
**Answer:** With an overthrow.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:6

**Q3. For whom did God make these cities an example?**  
**Answer:** Unto those that after should live ungodly.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:6

---

### 2 Peter 2:7

**Q1. Who was delivered by God?**  
**Answer:** Just Lot.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:7

**Q2. What kind of conversation or behavior of the wicked vexed Lot?**  
**Answer:** The filthy conversation of the wicked.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:7

---

### 2 Peter 2:8

**Q1. What did that righteous man dwelling among them see and hear?**  
**Answer:** Their unlawful deeds.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:8

**Q2. How was his righteous soul affected from day to day?**  
**Answer:** Vexed.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:8

---

### 2 Peter 2:9

**Q1. Who knows how to deliver the godly out of temptations?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:9

**Q2. Whom does the Lord know how to reserve unto the day of judgment?**  
**Answer:** The unjust.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:9

**Q3. To be punished, how are the unjust reserved?**  
**Answer:** Unto the day of judgment.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:9

---

### 2 Peter 2:10

**Q1. After what do chiefly those walk who walk in the flesh?**  
**Answer:** After the flesh.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:10

**Q2. What kind of lusts do they walk in?**  
**Answer:** In the lust of uncleanness.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:10

**Q3. What do they not fear to speak evil of?**  
**Answer:** Dignities.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:10

---

### 2 Peter 2:11

**Q1. Who are greater in power and might than the false teachers?**  
**Answer:** Angels.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:11

**Q2. What do angels not bring against them before the Lord?**  
**Answer:** A railing accusation.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:11

---

### 2 Peter 2:12

**Q1. Like what kind of natural brute beasts are these men made to be taken and destroyed?**  
**Answer:** As natural brute beasts.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:12

**Q2. What do these men speak evil of?**  
**Answer:** The things that they understand not.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:12

**Q3. In what shall they utterly perish?**  
**Answer:** In their own corruption.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:12

---

### 2 Peter 2:13

**Q1. What shall they receive as the reward of unrighteousness?**  
**Answer:** The reward of unrighteousness.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:13

**Q2. What do they count pleasure?**  
**Answer:** To riot in the day time.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:13

**Q3. With what are they filled while they feast with you?**  
**Answer:** With their own deceivings.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:13

---

### 2 Peter 2:14

**Q1. With what are the eyes of these people full?**  
**Answer:** Full of adultery.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:14

**Q2. What can they not cease from?**  
**Answer:** From sin.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:14

**Q3. What kind of souls do they beguile?**  
**Answer:** Unstable souls.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:14

---

### 2 Peter 2:15

**Q1. What path have they forsaken?**  
**Answer:** The right way.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:15

**Q2. Whose way have they gone astray and followed?**  
**Answer:** The way of Balaam the son of Bosor.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:15

**Q3. What did Balaam love?**  
**Answer:** The wages of unrighteousness.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:15

---

### 2 Peter 2:16

**Q1. What was Balaam rebuked for?**  
**Answer:** For his iniquity.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:16

**Q2. What kind of beast spoke with man's voice?**  
**Answer:** The dumb ass.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:16

**Q3. Whose madness did the dumb beast forbid?**  
**Answer:** The madness of the prophet.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:16

---

### 2 Peter 2:17

**Q1. What are these people described as being without?**  
**Answer:** Wells without water.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:17

**Q2. What kind of mists are they compared to?**  
**Answer:** Clouds that are carried with a tempest.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:17

**Q3. What is reserved for them?**  
**Answer:** The mist of darkness for ever.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:17

---

### 2 Peter 2:18

**Q1. What kind of great swelling words do they speak?**  
**Answer:** Great swelling words of vanity.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:18

**Q2. Through the lusts of the flesh, what do they allure?**  
**Answer:** Through much wantonness.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:18

**Q3. Whom do they allure through the lusts of the flesh?**  
**Answer:** Those that were clean escaped from them who live in error.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:18

---

### 2 Peter 2:19

**Q1. What do they promise these people while they themselves are servants of corruption?**  
**Answer:** Liberty.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:19

**Q2. By whom is a person brought into bondage?**  
**Answer:** By whom a man is overcome.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:19

---

### 2 Peter 2:20

**Q1. Through what have they escaped the pollutions of the world?**  
**Answer:** Through the knowledge of the Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:20

**Q2. If they are again entangled therein and overcome, what is the latter end compared to?**  
**Answer:** Worse than the beginning.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:20

---

### 2 Peter 2:21

**Q1. What would have been better for them not to have known?**  
**Answer:** The way of righteousness.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:21

---

### 2 Peter 2:22

**Q1. According to the true proverb, to what has the dog returned?**  
**Answer:** To his own vomit again.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:22

**Q2. According to the true proverb, to what has the sow returned?**  
**Answer:** To her wallowing in the mire.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 2:22
