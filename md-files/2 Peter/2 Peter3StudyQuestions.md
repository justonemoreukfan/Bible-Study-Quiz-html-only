# 2 Peter 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Peter 3:1

**Q1. What is this second epistle that the writer is now writing unto them?**  
**Answer:** This second epistle I write unto you.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:1

**Q2. What did the writer seek to stir up by way of remembrance in both their first and second epistles?**  
**Answer:** Your pure minds by way of remembrance.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:1

**Q3. How many epistles had the writer written unto them prior to this verse?**  
**Answer:** This second epistle.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:1

---

### 2 Peter 3:2

**Q1. What should they be mindful of according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** That ye may be mindful of the words which were spoken before by the holy prophets.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:2

**Q2. Whose commandment should they be mindful of?**  
**Answer:** And of the commandment of us the apostles of the Lord and Saviour.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:2

**Q3. Who spoke the words that were spoken before?**  
**Answer:** The holy prophets.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:2

---

### 2 Peter 3:3

**Q1. What should you know first?**  
**Answer:** That there shall come in the last days scoffers.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:3

**Q2. How will these scoffers walk?**  
**Answer:** Walking after their own lusts.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:3

**Q3. When will these scoffers come?**  
**Answer:** In the last days.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:3

---

### 2 Peter 3:4

**Q1. What question do the scoffers ask about His coming?**  
**Answer:** Where is the promise of his coming?  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:4

**Q2. What state do they claim continues since the fathers fell asleep?**  
**Answer:** All things continue as they were from the beginning of the creation.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:4

**Q3. Since when do they claim all things continue as they were?**  
**Answer:** From the beginning of the creation.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:4

---

### 2 Peter 3:5

**Q1. What do they willingly are ignorant of regarding the word of God?**  
**Answer:** For this they willingly are ignorant of, that by the word of God the heavens were of old.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:5

**Q2. What else was of old by the word of God besides the heavens?**  
**Answer:** And the earth standing out of the water and in the water.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:5

**Q3. How was the earth standing in relation to water?**  
**Answer:** Standing out of the water and in the water.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:5

---

### 2 Peter 3:6

**Q1. What by water perished at that time?**  
**Answer:** Whereby the world that then was, being overflowed with water, perished.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:6

**Q2. What happened to the world that then was?**  
**Answer:** Being overflowed with water, perished.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:6

**Q3. By what means was the world overflowed?**  
**Answer:** By water.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:6

---

### 2 Peter 3:7

**Q1. By what word are the present heavens and earth kept in store?**  
**Answer:** But the heavens and the earth, which are now, by the same word are kept in store.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:7

**Q2. Unto what are the present heavens and earth reserved?**  
**Answer:** Reserved unto fire against the day of judgment and perdition of ungodly men.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:7

**Q3. Against what day are they reserved?**  
**Answer:** Against the day of judgment and perdition of ungodly men.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:7

---

### 2 Peter 3:8

**Q1. What should be not ignorant of, beloved?**  
**Answer:** That one day is with the Lord as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:8

**Q2. How long is one day with the Lord compared to?**  
**Answer:** As a thousand years.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:8

**Q3. How long is a thousand years compared to with the Lord?**  
**Answer:** As one day.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:8

---

### 2 Peter 3:9

**Q1. What is the Lord not concerning His promise?**  
**Answer:** The Lord is not slack concerning his promise.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:9

**Q2. How do some men count the Lord's promise?**  
**Answer:** As some men count slackness.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:9

**Q3. What does the Lord not want to perish?**  
**Answer:** But is longsuffering to us-ward, not willing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:9

---

### 2 Peter 3:10

**Q1. How will the day of the Lord come?**  
**Answer:** But the day of the Lord will come as a thief in the night.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:10

**Q2. What will happen to the heavens in that day?**  
**Answer:** In the which the heavens shall pass away with a great noise.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:10

**Q3. What will happen to the earth and the works therein?**  
**Answer:** The earth also and the works that are therein shall be burned up.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:10

---

### 2 Peter 3:11

**Q1. What manner of persons ought ye to be in all holy conversation and godliness since all these things shall be dissolved?**  
**Answer:** What manner of persons ought ye to be in all holy conversation and godliness.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:11

**Q2. What will happen to all these things?**  
**Answer:** Seeing then that all these things shall be dissolved.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:11

**Q3. What kind of conversation and godliness are mentioned?**  
**Answer:** All holy conversation and godliness.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:11

---

### 2 Peter 3:12

**Q1. What should they be looking for and hasting unto?**  
**Answer:** Looking for and hasting unto the coming of the day of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:12

**Q2. What will happen to the heavens by fire?**  
**Answer:** Wherein the heavens being on fire shall be dissolved.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:12

**Q3. What will happen to the elements?**  
**Answer:** And the elements shall melt with fervent heat.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:12

---

### 2 Peter 3:13

**Q1. What do we look for according to His promise?**  
**Answer:** Nevertheless we, according to his promise, look for new heavens and a new earth.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:13

**Q2. What dwells in the new heavens and new earth?**  
**Answer:** Wherein dwelleth righteousness.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:13

**Q3. Whose promise do we look for these things according to?**  
**Answer:** His promise.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:13

---

### 2 Peter 3:14

**Q1. What should ye be diligent to be found of him in?**  
**Answer:** That ye may be found of him in peace.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:14

**Q2. In what condition should they be found by him?**  
**Answer:** Without spot, and blameless.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:14

**Q3. What should ye be seeing that ye look for such things?**  
**Answer:** Be diligent that ye may be found of him in peace, without spot, and blameless.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:14

---

### 2 Peter 3:15

**Q1. What should we account the longsuffering of our Lord to be?**  
**Answer:** And account that the longsuffering of our Lord is salvation.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:15

**Q2. According to what wisdom did our beloved brother Paul write unto you?**  
**Answer:** Even as our beloved brother Paul also according to the wisdom given unto him hath written unto you.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:15

**Q3. Who is referred to as our beloved brother?**  
**Answer:** Paul.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:15

---

### 2 Peter 3:16

**Q1. In which of his epistles does Paul speak of these things?**  
**Answer:** As also in all his epistles, speaking in them of these things.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:16

**Q2. What are some things in Paul's epistles described as?**  
**Answer:** In which are some things hard to be understood.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:16

**Q3. Who wrests the unlearned and unstable things?**  
**Answer:** Which they that are unlearned and unstable wrest, as they do also the other scriptures, unto their own destruction.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:16

---

### 2 Peter 3:17

**Q1. What should ye do knowing these things before?**  
**Answer:** Ye therefore, beloved, seeing ye know these things before, beware.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:17

**Q2. What should ye beware of lest ye also fall?**  
**Answer:** Lest ye also, being led away with the error of the wicked, fall from your own stedfastness.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:17

**Q3. From what might you fall if you are led away?**  
**Answer:** Fall from your own stedfastness.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:17

---

### 2 Peter 3:18

**Q1. In what should ye grow according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** But grow in grace, and in the knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:18

**Q2. Whose grace and knowledge should we grow in?**  
**Answer:** Of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:18

**Q3. To whom be glory both now and for ever?**  
**Answer:** To him be glory both now and for ever.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 3:18
