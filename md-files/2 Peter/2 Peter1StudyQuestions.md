# 2 Peter 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Peter 1:1

**Q1. Whose servant and apostle is Simon Peter?**  
**Answer:** Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:1

**Q2. To whom has Simon Peter obtained like precious faith?**  
**Answer:** Them that have obtained like precious faith with us through the righteousness of God and our Saviour Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:1

**Q3. Whose righteousness is mentioned alongside our Saviour Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:1

---

### 2 Peter 1:2

**Q4. What is asked to be multiplied unto the readers?**  
**Answer:** Grace and peace.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:2

**Q5. Through what is grace and peace to be multiplied?**  
**Answer:** Through the knowledge of God, and of Jesus our Lord.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:2

**Q6. Who is mentioned along with God regarding this knowledge?**  
**Answer:** Jesus our Lord.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:2

---

### 2 Peter 1:3

**Q7. What has his divine power given unto us?**  
**Answer:** All things that pertain unto life and godliness.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:3

**Q8. Through what means are these things given unto us?**  
**Answer:** Through the knowledge of him that hath called us to glory and virtue.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:3

**Q9. To what has he called us?**  
**Answer:** Glory and virtue.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:3

---

### 2 Peter 1:4

**Q10. What are given unto us by exceeding great and precious promises?**  
**Answer:** Exceeding great and precious promises (or: that by these ye might be partakers of the divine nature).  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:4

**Q11. What might we be partakers of through these promises?**  
**Answer:** The divine nature.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:4

**Q12. What have we escaped that is in the world through lust?**  
**Answer:** Corruption.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:4

---

### 2 Peter 1:5

**Q13. What should the reader give beside this, giving all diligence?**  
**Answer:** Add to your faith virtue.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:5

**Q14. What is to be added to virtue?**  
**Answer:** Knowledge.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:5

**Q15. What should accompany this effort alongside diligence?**  
**Answer:** Faith.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:5

---

### 2 Peter 1:6

**Q16. What is to be added to knowledge?**  
**Answer:** Temperance.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:6

**Q17. What is to be added to temperance?**  
**Answer:** Patience.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:6

**Q18. What is to be added to patience?**  
**Answer:** Godliness.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:6

---

### 2 Peter 1:7

**Q19. What is to be added to godliness?**  
**Answer:** Brotherly kindness.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:7

**Q20. What is to be added to brotherly kindness?**  
**Answer:** Charity.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:7

**Q21. Whose kindness is specifically mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Brotherly kindness.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:7

---

### 2 Peter 1:8

**Q22. What qualities, if these things be in you and abound, make you that ye shall neither be barren nor unfruitful?**  
**Answer:** These things (faith, virtue, knowledge, temperance, patience, godliness, brotherly kindness, charity).  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:8

**Q23. In what knowledge do these things prevent you from being barren or unfruitful?**  
**Answer:** The knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:8

**Q24. What two negative conditions will you avoid if these things are in you and abound?**  
**Answer:** Barren nor unfruitful.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:8

---

### 2 Peter 1:9

**Q25. Who is described as blind and cannot see afar off?**  
**Answer:** He that lacketh these things.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:9

**Q26. What has he forgotten?**  
**Answer:** That he was purged from his old sins.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:9

**Q27. From what has he been purged?**  
**Answer:** His old sins.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:9

---

### 2 Peter 1:10

**Q28. What should the brethren give diligence to make?**  
**Answer:** Your calling and election sure.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:10

**Q29. What will happen if you do these things?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall never fall.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:10

**Q30. Whose calling and election should be made sure?**  
**Answer:** Your calling and election.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:10

---

### 2 Peter 1:11

**Q31. What shall be ministered unto you abundantly?**  
**Answer:** An entrance.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:11

**Q32. Into what place is this entrance ministered?**  
**Answer:** Into the everlasting kingdom of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:11

**Q33. Whose everlasting kingdom is it?**  
**Answer:** Our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:11

---

### 2 Peter 1:12

**Q34. What does Peter not be negligent to do?**  
**Answer:** To put you always in remembrance of these things.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:12

**Q35. Do the readers already know these things, and are they established in what?**  
**Answer:** The present truth.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:12

**Q36. How often does Peter want to put them in remembrance?**  
**Answer:** Always.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:12

---

### 2 Peter 1:13

**Q37. What does Peter think it right to do as long as he is in this tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** To stir you up by putting you in remembrance.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:13

**Q38. What does Peter call his body or current state of existence?**  
**Answer:** This tabernacle.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:13

---

### 2 Peter 1:14

**Q39. What does Peter know he must shortly put off?**  
**Answer:** His tabernacle.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:14

**Q40. Who has showed Peter this?**  
**Answer:** Even as our Lord Jesus Christ hath shewed me.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:14

**Q41. Who showed Peter that he must put off his tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** Our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:14

---

### 2 Peter 1:15

**Q42. What will Peter endeavour that the readers may be able to do after his decease?**  
**Answer:** Have these things always in remembrance.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:15

**Q43. When does Peter want them to have these things in remembrance?**  
**Answer:** Always (after his decease).  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:15

**Q44. After what event does he want them to remember these things?**  
**Answer:** His decease.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:15

---

### 2 Peter 1:16

**Q45. What did they not follow when they made known the power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** Cunningly devised fables.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:16

**Q46. What were they eyewitnesses of?**  
**Answer:** His majesty.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:16

**Q47. Whose power and coming did they make known?**  
**Answer:** Our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:16

---

### 2 Peter 1:17

**Q48. From whom did Jesus Christ receive honour and glory?**  
**Answer:** From God the Father.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:17

**Q49. What voice came to him from the excellent glory?**  
**Answer:** This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:17

**Q50. Who spoke the words from the excellent glory?**  
**Answer:** God the Father (implied by "such a voice to him from the excellent glory").  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:17

---

### 2 Peter 1:18

**Q51. Where did this voice come from when they heard it?**  
**Answer:** From heaven.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:18

**Q52. Where were they with him when they heard the voice?**  
**Answer:** In the holy mount.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:18

**Q53. Who heard this voice from heaven?**  
**Answer:** We (which came from heaven when we were with him in the holy mount).  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:18

---

### 2 Peter 1:19

**Q54. What do we have also more sure?**  
**Answer:** A word of prophecy.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:19

**Q55. To what should ye do well that ye take heed, as unto a light that shineth in a dark place?**  
**Answer:** The word of prophecy.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:19

**Q56. What star arises in your hearts?**  
**Answer:** The day star.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:19

---

### 2 Peter 1:20

**Q57. What must you know first regarding any prophecy of the scripture?**  
**Answer:** That no prophecy of the scripture is of any private interpretation.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:20

**Q58. Of what is no prophecy of the scripture?**  
**Answer:** Any private interpretation.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:20

**Q59. What kind of prophecy is not of any private interpretation?**  
**Answer:** Any prophecy of the scripture.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:20

---

### 2 Peter 1:21

**Q60. By whose will was prophecy brought in old time?**  
**Answer:** Not in old time by the will of man.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:21

**Q61. Who spake as they were moved by the Holy Ghost?**  
**Answer:** Holy men of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:21

**Q62. By what power were holy men of God moved to speak?**  
**Answer:** The Holy Ghost.  
**Verse:** 2 Peter 1:21
