# 1 Corinthians 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Corinthians 6:1
**Q1. Where does Paul ask if any of the believers dare to go to law against another?**
**Answer:** Before the unjust.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:1

**Q2. Who does Paul suggest should instead be the judges in such matters?**
**Answer:** The saints.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:1

**Q3. What is the nature of the dispute mentioned in this verse?**
**Answer:** Having a matter against another.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:1

### 1 Corinthians 6:2
**Q1. What do the saints shall judge?**
**Answer:** The world.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:2

**Q2. What rhetorical question does Paul ask regarding the saints' ability to judge small matters?**
**Answer:** "and if the world shall be judged by you, are ye unworthy to judge the smallest matters?"
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:2

**Q3. What is the point Paul is making about the saints' capacity for judgment?**
**Answer:** If they can judge the world, they are certainly capable of judging smaller matters.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:2

### 1 Corinthians 6:3
**Q1. What do the saints know that they shall judge?**
**Answer:** Angels.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:3

**Q2. What does Paul say the saints shall judge besides angels?**
**Answer:** Things that pertain to this life.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:3

**Q3. How does Paul emphasize the capacity of the saints' judgment?**
**Answer:** By stating they will judge angels, and therefore much more the things of this life.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:3

### 1 Corinthians 6:4
**Q1. What should be set to judge if the believers have judgments of things pertaining to this life?**
**Answer:** Those who are least esteemed in the church.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:4

**Q2. Who is suggested as the judges for matters pertaining to this life?**
**Answer:** Those who are least esteemed in the church.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:4

**Q3. What is the context of these judgments?**
**Answer:** Things pertaining to this life.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:4

### 1 Corinthians 6:5
**Q1. To what does Paul say he speaks?**
**Answer:** To your shame.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:5

**Q2. What does Paul ask if there is any wise man among them?**
**Answer:** "Is it so, that there is not a wise man among you?"
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:5

**Q3. What would a wise man be able to do in this context?**
**Answer:** He would be able to judge between his brethren.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:5

### 1 Corinthians 6:6
**Q1. What is happening between the brothers in this verse?**
**Answer:** Brother goeth to law with brother.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:6

**Q2. Before whom is this legal action taking place?**
**Answer:** Before the unbelievers.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:6

**Q3. Who are the opposing parties in the legal action?**
**Answer:** Brother and brother.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:6

### 1 Corinthians 6:7
**Q1. What does Paul say is "utterly a fault" among the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** Because they go to law one with another.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:7

**Q2. What two things does Paul suggest they should rather do?**
**Answer:** Take wrong, or suffer themselves to be defrauded.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:7

**Q3. Why is going to law one with another considered a fault?**
**Answer:** Because it is a failure in the community of believers.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:7

### 1 Corinthians 6:8
**Q1. What two things are the Corinthians doing to their brethren?**
**Answer:** They do wrong, and defraud.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:8

**Q2. Who are they doing wrong to and defrauding?**
**Answer:** Their brethren.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:8

**Q3. What is the general accusation against the believers in this verse?**
**Answer:** They are doing wrong and defrauding their own brethren.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:8

### 1 Corinthians 6:9
**Q1. Who shall NOT inherit the kingdom of God?**
**Answer:** The unrighteous.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:9

**Q2. What warning does Paul give?**
**Answer:** "Be not deceived".
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:9

**Q3. Which five groups of people are listed in this verse as not inheriting the kingdom?**
**Answer:** Fornicators, idolaters, adulterers, effeminate, and abusers of themselves with mankind.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:9

### 1 Corinthians 6:10
**Q1. Which five other groups of people are listed as not inheriting the kingdom of God?**
**Answer:** Thieves, covetous, drunkards, revilers, and extortioners.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:10

**Q2. What is the common fate of all these groups listed in verses 9 and 10?**
**Answer:** They shall not inherit the kingdom of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:10

**Q3. Who are the ones excluded from the kingdom of God?**
**Answer:** The unrighteous.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:10

### 1 Corinthians 6:11
**Q1. What were some of the Corinthians previously?**
**Answer:** "And such were some of you".
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:11

**Q2. What three things have happened to them since then?**
**Answer:** They are washed, sanctified, and justified.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:11

**Q3. In whose name and by whose Spirit were they washed, sanctified, and justified?**
**Answer:** In the name of the Lord Jesus, and by the Spirit of our God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:11

### 1 Corinthians 6:12
**Q1. What is the first statement about what is "lawful" for Paul?**
**Answer:** "All things are lawful unto me".
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:12

**Q2. What does Paul say about the expediency of all these things?**
**Answer:** "but all things are not expedient".
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:12

**Q3. What does Paul say he will NOT be brought under the power of?**
**Answer:** The power of any.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:12

### 1 Corinthians 6:13
**Q1. What does Paul say about "meats for the belly"?**
**Answer:** Meats for the belly, and the belly for meats.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:13

**Q2. What will God do to both the belly and the meats?**
**Answer:** God shall destroy both it and them.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:13

**Q3. For what purpose is the body NOT intended?**
**Answer:** For fornication.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:13

### 1 Corinthians 6:14
**Q1. Who has God raised up?**
**Answer:** The Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:14

**Q2. What will God also do for us?**
**Answer:** He will also raise up us.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:14

**Q3. By what means will God raise us up?**
**Answer:** By his own power.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:14

### 1 Corinthians 6:15
**Q1. What are the believers' bodies described as?**
**Answer:** The members of Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:15

**Q2. What does Paul ask if he should take the members of Christ and make them what?**
**Answer:** The members of an harlot.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:15

**Q3. What is Paul's response to the idea of making Christ's members into harlot's members?**
**Answer:** "God forbid."
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:15

### 1 Corinthians 6:16
**Q1. What happens to one who is joined to an harlot?**
**Answer:** He is one body.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:16

**Q2. What does the scripture say about two people joined as one?**
**Answer:** "for two, saith he, shall be one flesh."
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:16

**Q3. What is the result of joining with an harlot?**
**Answer:** Being one body.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:16

### 1 Corinthians 6:17
**Q1. Who is joined unto the Lord?**
**Answer:** He that is joined unto the Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:17

**Q2. What is the result of being joined unto the Lord?**
**Answer:** He is one spirit.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:17

**Q3. What is the difference between joining an harlot and joining the Lord?**
**Answer:** Joining an harlot makes one body (flesh), while joining the Lord makes one spirit.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:17

### 1 Corinthians 6:18
**Q1. What is the command given in this verse?**
**Answer:** "Flee fornication."
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:18

**Q2. Where is most sin committed according to this verse?**
**Answer:** Without the body.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:18

**Q3. What is unique about the sin of fornication?**
**Answer:** He that committeth fornication sinneth against his own body.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:18

### 1 Corinthians 6:19
**Q1. What is the body described as in this verse?**
**Answer:** The temple of the Holy Ghost which is in you.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:19

**Q2. Where does the Holy Ghost come from?**
**Answer:** From God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:19

**Q3. Do the believers own their own bodies?**
**Answer:** No, "ye are not your own".
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:19

### 1 Corinthians 6:20
**Q1. Why are the believers not their own?**
**Answer:** For they are bought with a price.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:20

**Q2. What is the command given regarding the body and spirit?**
**Answer:** "therefore glorify God in your body, and in your spirit".
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:20

**Q3. To whom do the body and spirit belong?**
**Answer:** They are God's.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 6:20
