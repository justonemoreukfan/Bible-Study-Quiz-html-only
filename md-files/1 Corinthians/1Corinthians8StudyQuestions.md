# 1 Corinthians 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Corinthians 8:1
**Q1. What does Paul say about the relationship between knowledge and charity?**
**Answer:** Knowledge puffeth up, but charity edifieth.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:1

**Q2. What does knowledge do?**
**Answer:** It puffeth up.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:1

**Q3. What does charity do?**
**Answer:** It edifieth.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:1

### 1 Corinthians 8:2
**Q1. What does Paul say about anyone who thinks they know something?**
**Answer:** He knoweth nothing yet as he ought to know.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:2

**Q2. If a man thinks he knoweth any thing, what is the truth about his knowledge?**
**Answer:** He knows nothing yet as he ought to know.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:2

**Q3. What is the contrast between thinking one knows and knowing as one ought?**
**Answer:** Thinking one knows often means they do not know as they ought to.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:2

### 1 Corinthians 8:3
**Q1. Who is known of God?**
**Answer:** Any man that love God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:3

**Q2. What is the requirement to be known of God?**
**Answer:** To love God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:3

**Q3. What is the result of loving God?**
**Answer:** The person is known of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:3

### 1 Corinthians 8:4
**Q1. What is Paul's knowledge regarding idols in the world?**
**Answer:** An idol is nothing in the world.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:4

**Q2. How many Gods are there according to this verse?**
**Answer:** There is none other God but one.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:4

**Q3. What is the general conclusion about idols?**
**Answer:** An idol is nothing in the world.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:4

### 1 Corinthians 8:5
**Q1. What does Paul say about those who are called gods?**
**Answer:** Whether in heaven or in earth, there be gods many, and lords many.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:5

**Q2. Where are these "gods" and "lords" found?**
**Answer:** In heaven or in earth.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:5

**Q3. What is the status of these many gods and lords?**
**Answer:** They are called gods.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:5

### 1 Corinthians 8:6
**Q1. Who is the one God?**
**Answer:** The Father.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:6

**Q2. What is the relationship between the Father and all things?**
**Answer:** He is the Father, of whom are all things, and we in him.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:6

**Q3. Who is the one Lord?**
**Answer:** Jesus Christ, by whom are all things, and we by him.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:6

### 1 Corinthians 8:7
**Q1. Does every man have the knowledge that an idol is nothing?**
**Answer:** No, not in every man.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:7

**Q2. What happens to those with a weak conscience regarding idols?**
**Answer:** Their conscience being weak is defiled.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:7

**Q3. What do some people eat as a thing offered unto an idol?**
**Answer:** Meat offered unto an idol.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:7

### 1 Corinthians 8:8
**Q1. Does meat commend us to God?**
**Answer:** No, meat commendeth us not to God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:8

**Q2. Are we better if we eat meat offered to idols?**
**Answer:** No, if we eat, we are not the better.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:8

**Q3. Are we worse if we do not eat?**
**Answer:** No, if we eat not, we are not the worse.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:8

### 1 Corinthians 8:9
**Q1. What should the believers "take heed" of?**
**Answer:** Lest by any means this liberty of yours become a stumblingblock to them that are weak.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:9

**Q2. For whom could the liberty of the knowledgeable become a stumblingblock?**
**Answer:** To them that are weak.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:9

**Q3. What is the potential danger of exercising one's liberty?**
**Answer:** It could become a stumblingblock to those who are weak.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:9

### 1 Corinthians 8:10
**Q1. What happens if a man with knowledge sits at meat in the idol's temple?**
**Answer:** The conscience of him which is weak be emboldened to eat those things which are offered to idols.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:10

**Q2. Who is the person with knowledge in this verse?**
**Answer:** The one sitting at meat in the idol's temple.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:10

**Q3. What is the effect on the weak person's conscience?**
**Answer:** It is emboldened to eat those things offered to idols.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:10

### 1 Corinthians 8:11
**Q1. Through what does the weak brother perish?**
**Answer:** Through thy knowledge.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:11

**Q2. For whom did Christ die?**
**Answer:** For the weak brother.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:11

**Q3. What is the result of using knowledge without consideration for the weak?**
**Answer:** The weak brother perishes.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:11

### 1 Corinthians 8:12
**Q1. When do the believers sin against Christ?**
**Answer:** When they sin so against the brethren, and wound their weak conscience.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:12

**Q2. Who is wounded when the brethren's weak conscience is attacked?**
**Answer:** The brethren's weak conscience.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:12

**Q3. Against whom is the sin committed when wounding a brother's conscience?**
**Answer:** Against Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:12

### 1 Corinthians 8:13
**Q1. What will Paul do if meat makes his brother to offend?**
**Answer:** He will eat no flesh while the world standeth.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:13

**Q2. Why will Paul eat no flesh?**
**Answer:** Lest he make his brother to offend.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:13

**Q3. For how long will Paul abstain from eating flesh?**
**Answer:** While the world standeth.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 8:13
