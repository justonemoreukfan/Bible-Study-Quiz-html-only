# 1 Corinthians 13 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Corinthians 13:1
**Q1. What happens if one speaks with the tongues of men and of angels but has not charity?**
**Answer:** I am become as sounding brass, or a tinkling cymbal.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:1

**Q2. What is the condition for not becoming sounding brass or a tinkling cymbal?**
**Answer:** Having charity.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:1

**Q3. What two types of tongues are mentioned in this verse?**
**Answer:** Tongues of men and of angels.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:1

### 1 Corinthians 13:2
**Q1. What gifts are mentioned in this verse?**
**Answer:** Gift of prophecy, understanding all mysteries, all knowledge, and all faith.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:2

**Q2. What can all faith do?**
**Answer:** I could remove mountains.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:2

**Q3. What is the result of having these gifts but not having charity?**
**Answer:** I am nothing.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:2

### 1 Corinthians 13:3
**Q1. What action is mentioned as giving all goods to the poor?**
**Answer:** Bestow all my goods to feed the poor.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:3

**Q2. What extreme action is mentioned besides feeding the poor?**
**Answer:** Give my body to be burned.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:3

**Q3. What is the result of these actions without charity?**
**Answer:** It profiteth me nothing.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:3

### 1 Corinthians 13:4
**Q1. What are three characteristics of charity mentioned in this verse?**
**Answer:** Charity suffereth long, and is kind; charity envieth not; charity vaunteth not itself.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:4

**Q2. What does charity NOT do?**
**Answer:** Charity envieth not; charity vaunteth not itself.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:4

**Q3. How does charity behave towards others?**
**Answer:** It suffereth long and is kind.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:4

### 1 Corinthians 13:5
**Q1. How does charity behave?**
**Answer:** Doth not behave itself unseemly.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:5

**Q2. What does charity NOT seek?**
**Answer:** Seeketh not her own.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:5

**Q3. What are two other characteristics of charity in this verse?**
**Answer:** Is not easily provoked, thinketh no evil.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:5

### 1 Corinthians 13:6
**Q1. In what does charity NOT rejoice?**
**Answer:** Rejoiceth not in iniquity.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:6

**Q2. In what does charity rejoice?**
**Answer:** Rejoiceth in the truth.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:6

**Q3. What is the focus of charity's joy?**
**Answer:** The truth.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:6

### 1 Corinthians 13:7
**Q1. List four things that charity does.**
**Answer:** Beareth all things, believeth all things, hopeth all things, endureth all things.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:7

**Q2. What does charity bear?**
**Answer:** All things.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:7

**Q3. What does charity endure?**
**Answer:** All things.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:7

### 1 Corinthians 13:8
**Q1. What is said about charity's endurance?**
**Answer:** Charity never faileth.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:8

**Q2. What happens to prophecies?**
**Answer:** They shall fail.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:8

**Q3. What happens to tongues, knowledge, and prophecies?**
**Answer:** Tongues shall cease, knowledge shall vanish away, and prophecies shall fail.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:8

### 1 Corinthians 13:9
**Q1. How much do we know?**
**Answer:** We know in part.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:9

**Q2. How much do we prophesy?**
**Answer:** We prophesy in part.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:9

**Q3. What is the state of our current knowledge and prophecy?**
**Answer:** They are in part.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:9

### 1 Corinthians 13:10
**Q1. What happens when that which is perfect is come?**
**Answer:** Then that which is in part shall be done away.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:10

**Q2. What will be done away?**
**Answer:** That which is in part.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:10

**Q3. What is the trigger for this change?**
**Answer:** The coming of that which is perfect.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:10

### 1 Corinthians 13:11
**Q1. How did the speaker speak, understand, and think when he was a child?**
**Answer:** I spake as a child, I understood as a child, I thought as a child.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:11

**Q2. What happened when he became a man?**
**Answer:** I put away childish things.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:11

**Q3. What is the difference between childhood and manhood in this verse?**
**Answer:** Putting away childish things.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:11

### 1 Corinthians 13:12
**Q1. How do we see now?**
**Answer:** Through a glass, darkly.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:12

**Q2. How will we see then?**
**Answer:** Face to face.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:12

**Q3. How will we know then compared to now?**
**Answer:** Now I know in part; but then shall I know even as also I am known.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:12

### 1 Corinthians 13:13
**Q1. What three things abide?**
**Answer:** Faith, hope, charity.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:13

**Q2. Which of these three is the greatest?**
**Answer:** Charity.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:13

**Q3. What are the three things that abide?**
**Answer:** Faith, hope, and charity.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 13:13
