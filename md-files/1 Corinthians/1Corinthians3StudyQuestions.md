# 1 Corinthians 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Corinthians 3:1
**Q1. To whom could Paul not speak as unto spiritual?**
**Answer:** To the brethren.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:1

**Q2. How did Paul describe the spiritual state of the brethren in this verse?**
**Answer:** As carnal, even as unto babes in Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:1

**Q3. Instead of spiritual, how did Paul have to speak to them?**
**Answer:** As unto carnal.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:1

### 1 Corinthians 3:2
**Q1. With what did Paul feed the brethren?**
**Answer:** With milk.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:2

**Q2. What did Paul say he had not fed them with?**
**Answer:** Meat.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:2

**Q3. Why did Paul feed them with milk and not meat?**
**Answer:** For hitherto they were not able to bear it, neither yet now are they able.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:2

### 1 Corinthians 3:3
**Q1. Why does Paul say the brethren are yet carnal?**
**Answer:** Because there is among them envying, and strife, and divisions.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:3

**Q2. What three things indicate that the brethren are carnal?**
**Answer:** Envying, strife, and divisions.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:3

**Q3. How does Paul describe the way the carnal brethren walk?**
**Answer:** They walk as men.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:3

### 1 Corinthians 3:4
**Q1. What claim does one person make in this verse?**
**Answer:** "I am of Paul".
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:4

**Q2. What claim does another person make?**
**Answer:** "I am of Apollos".
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:4

**Q3. According to Paul, what does this behavior indicate about the believers?**
**Answer:** That they are carnal.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:4

### 1 Corinthians 3:5
**Q1. Who is Paul and who is Apollos according to this verse?**
**Answer:** Ministers by whom the brethren believed.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:5

**Q2. How did the brethren come to believe?**
**Answer:** Through ministers like Paul and Apollos.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:5

**Q3. Who gave to every man the ability to believe?**
**Answer:** The Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:5

### 1 Corinthians 3:6
**Q1. What did Paul do in the process of the believers' growth?**
**Answer:** He planted.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:6

**Q2. What did Apollos do?**
**Answer:** He watered.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:6

**Q3. Who gave the increase?**
**Answer:** God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:6

### 1 Corinthians 3:7
**Q1. What does Paul say about the one who planteth?**
**Answer:** He is any thing (meaning he is not the primary cause).
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:7

**Q2. What does Paul say about the one who watereth?**
**Answer:** He is any thing.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:7

**Q3. Who is the only one that truly giveth the increase?**
**Answer:** God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:7

### 1 Corinthians 3:8
**Q1. What is the relationship between the one who planteth and the one who watereth?**
**Answer:** They are one.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:8

**Q2. What shall every man receive?**
**Answer:** His own reward.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:8

**Q3. On what basis shall each man receive his reward?**
**Answer:** According to his own labour.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:8

### 1 Corinthians 3:9
**Q1. With whom are the ministers labourers?**
**Answer:** Together with God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:9

**Q2. What is the believers' relationship to God described as in the first instance?**
**Answer:** They are God's husbandry.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:9

**Q3. What is the believers' relationship to God described as in the second instance?**
**Answer:** They are God's building.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:9

### 1 Corinthians 3:10
**Q1. How does Paul describe himself in terms of building?**
**Answer:** As a wise masterbuilder.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:10

**Q2. What did Paul do as a wise masterbuilder?**
**Answer:** He laid the foundation.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:10

**Q3. What warning does Paul give to those who build upon the foundation?**
**Answer:** Let every man take heed how he buildeth thereupon.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:10

### 1 Corinthians 3:11
**Q1. Can any man lay a foundation other than the one already laid?**
**Answer:** No.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:11

**Q2. Who is the foundation that has been laid?**
**Answer:** Jesus Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:11

**Q3. What is the only foundation that can be laid?**
**Answer:** Jesus Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:11

### 1 Corinthians 3:12
**Q1. What are the six materials mentioned that a man might use to build on the foundation?**
**Answer:** Gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, and stubble.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:12

**Q2. Which materials are considered valuable?**
**Answer:** Gold, silver, and precious stones.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:12

**Q3. Which materials are considered less valuable?**
**Answer:** Wood, hay, and stubble.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:12

### 1 Corinthians 3:13
**Q1. When shall every man's work be made manifest?**
**Answer:** For the day shall declare it.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:13

**Q2. By what means shall the work be revealed?**
**Answer:** By fire.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:13

**Q3. What is the purpose of the fire?**
**Answer:** To try every man's work of what sort it is.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:13

### 1 Corinthians 3:14
**Q1. What happens if a man's work abides?**
**Answer:** He shall receive a reward.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:14

**Q2. On what condition does a man receive a reward?**
**Answer:** If his work abide which he hath built upon the foundation.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:14

**Q3. What is the result of work that abides?**
**Answer:** The worker shall receive a reward.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:14

### 1 Corinthians 3:15
**Q1. What happens if a man's work is burned?**
**Answer:** He shall suffer loss.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:15

**Q2. Despite the loss of his work, what happens to the man himself?**
**Answer:** He himself shall be saved.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:15

**Q3. In what manner is he saved?**
**Answer:** Yet so as by fire.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:15

### 1 Corinthians 3:16
**Q1. What are the believers described as in this verse?**
**Answer:** The temple of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:16

**Q2. Who dwelleth in the believers?**
**Answer:** The Spirit of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:16

**Q3. What is the relationship between the Spirit of God and the believers?**
**Answer:** The Spirit of God dwelleth in them.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:16

### 1 Corinthians 3:17
**Q1. What will God do to any man who defiles the temple of God?**
**Answer:** Him shall God destroy.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:17

**Q2. Why is defiling the temple of God so serious?**
**Answer:** For the temple of God is holy.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:17

**Q3. Who is the holy temple of God?**
**Answer:** The believers (which temple ye are).
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:17

### 1 Corinthians 3:18
**Q1. What does Paul warn men not to do?**
**Answer:** Deceive themselves.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:18

**Q2. If a man seems wise in this world, what should he do?**
**Answer:** Let him become a fool.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:18

**Q3. Why should a man become a fool?**
**Answer:** That he may be wise.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:18

### 1 Corinthians 3:19
**Q1. How does the wisdom of this world appear to God?**
**Answer:** It is foolishness with God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:19

**Q2. What is written regarding how God deals with the wise?**
**Answer:** He taketh the wise in their own craftiness.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:19

**Q3. What is the result of the wise's craftiness?**
**Answer:** God taketh them in it.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:19

### 1 Corinthians 3:20
**Q1. What does the Lord know about the thoughts of the wise?**
**Answer:** That they are vain.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:20

**Q2. How does the Lord view the thoughts of the wise?**
**Answer:** As vain.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:20

**Q3. Who is the subject of the Lord's knowledge in this verse?**
**Answer:** The wise.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:20

### 1 Corinthians 3:21
**Q1. In whom should no man glory?**
**Answer:** In men.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:21

**Q2. What does Paul say belongs to the believers?**
**Answer:** All things are yours.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:21

**Q3. What is the command regarding glorying?**
**Answer:** Let no man glory in men.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:21

### 1 Corinthians 3:22
**Q1. Which specific people are mentioned as belonging to the believers?**
**Answer:** Paul, Apollos, and Cephas.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:22

**Q2. What other things are mentioned as belonging to the believers?**
**Answer:** The world, life, death, things present, and things to come.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:22

**Q3. What is the general statement about these things?**
**Answer:** All are yours.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:22

### 1 Corinthians 3:23
**Q1. To whom do the believers belong?**
**Answer:** They are Christ's.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:23

**Q2. To whom does Christ belong?**
**Answer:** Christ is God's.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:23

**Q3. What is the chain of belonging described in this verse?**
**Answer:** The believers belong to Christ, and Christ belongs to God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 3:23
