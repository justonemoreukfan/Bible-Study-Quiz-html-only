# 1 Corinthians 16 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Corinthians 16:1
**Q1. To whom is Paul speaking regarding the collection for the saints?**
**Answer:** Now concerning the gathering into the saints.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 16:1

**Q2. What instruction does Paul give regarding the collection?**
**Answer:** As I have commanded the churches of Galatia.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 16:1

**Q3. What is the subject of this instruction?**
**Answer:** The gathering into the saints.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 16:1

### 1 Corinthians 16:2
**Q1. When should each one lay by in store for the collection?**
**Answer:** Upon the first day of the week.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 16:2

**Q2. How should the amount be set aside?**
**Answer:** Let each one of you according to his own prosperity lay by him in store.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 16:2

**Q3. What is the purpose of laying by in store?**
**Answer:** That when I come, I may gather no offerings already.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 16:2

### 1 Corinthians 16:3
**Q1. What will Paul do once he arrives?**
**Answer:** Whomsoever ye shall approve, I will send them with the letters.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 16:3

**Q2. Who will Paul send with the letters?**
**Answer:** Whomsoever the brethren approve.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 16:3

**Q3. What will accompany the approved persons?**
**Answer:** Letters.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 16:3

### 1 Corinthians 16:4
**Q1. Under what condition will Paul accompany the approved persons?**
**Answer:** If it be your will, and if I also be able.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 16:4

**Q2. What is the intended destination for Paul and the approved persons?**
**Answer:** Not only will I send, but I myself also will come.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 16:4

**Q3. What does "if I also be able" imply?**
**Answer:** That his coming depends on his ability.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 16:4
