# 1 Corinthians 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Corinthians 1:1
**Q1. Who was called to be an apostle of Jesus Christ through the will of God?**
**Answer:** Paul.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:1

**Q2. Who is mentioned as "our brother" alongside Paul?**
**Answer:** Sosthenes.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:1

**Q3. Through whose will was Paul called to be an apostle?**
**Answer:** The will of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:1

### 1 Corinthians 1:2
**Q1. To which church is this letter addressed?**
**Answer:** The church of God which is at Corinth.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:2

**Q2. How are those in the church at Corinth described in relation to Christ Jesus?**
**Answer:** They are sanctified in Christ Jesus and called to be saints.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:2

**Q3. Who else is included in the greeting besides those at Corinth?**
**Answer:** All that in every place call upon the name of Jesus Christ our Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:2

### 1 Corinthians 1:3
**Q1. What two things does Paul wish for the recipients?**
**Answer:** Grace and peace.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:3

**Q2. From whom does the grace and peace come first?**
**Answer:** From God our Father.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:3

**Q3. Who is the second source of grace and peace mentioned?**
**Answer:** From the Lord Jesus Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:3

### 1 Corinthians 1:4
**Q1. For whom does Paul always thank God?**
**Answer:** On your behalf (the Corinthians).
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:4

**Q2. What is Paul thanking God for?**
**Answer:** For the grace of God which is given them by Jesus Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:4

**Q3. How often does Paul thank God on their behalf?**
**Answer:** Always.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:4

### 1 Corinthians 1:5
**Q1. In what ways have the Corinthians been enriched by Him?**
**Answer:** In all utterance and in all knowledge.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:5

**Q2. By whom were they enriched?**
**Answer:** By him (Jesus Christ).
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:5

**Q3. What is the result of the grace given to them as mentioned in this verse?**
**Answer:** That in every thing they are enriched.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:5

### 1 Corinthians 1:6
**Q1. What was confirmed in the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** The testimony of Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:6

**Q2. What is the status of the testimony of Christ in the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** It was confirmed in them.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:6

**Q3. To what does this confirmation relate?**
**Answer:** To the testimony of Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:6

### 1 Corinthians 1:7
**Q1. In what do the Corinthians not come behind?**
**Answer:** In no gift.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:7

**Q2. What are the Corinthians waiting for?**
**Answer:** The coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:7

**Q3. What is the current state of the Corinthians regarding spiritual gifts?**
**Answer:** They come behind in no gift.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:7

### 1 Corinthians 1:8
**Q1. Who shall confirm the Corinthians unto the end?**
**Answer:** He (Lord Jesus Christ).
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:8

**Q2. What is the purpose of being confirmed unto the end?**
**Answer:** That they may be blameless.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:8

**Q3. On what day are they to be blameless?**
**Answer:** In the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:8

### 1 Corinthians 1:9
**Q1. What characteristic of God is stated in this verse?**
**Answer:** God is faithful.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:9

**Q2. By whom were the Corinthians called?**
**Answer:** By God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:9

**Q3. To what were they called?**
**Answer:** Unto the fellowship of his Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:9

### 1 Corinthians 1:10
**Q1. In whose name does Paul beseech the brethren?**
**Answer:** By the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:10

**Q2. What does Paul want them all to do regarding their speech?**
**Answer:** That they all speak the same thing.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:10

**Q3. What does Paul want to avoid among the brethren?**
**Answer:** Divisions.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:10

### 1 Corinthians 1:11
**Q1. Who declared to Paul that there are contentions among the brethren?**
**Answer:** Them which are of the house of Chloe.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:11

**Q2. What did the house of Chloe report was happening among the brethren?**
**Answer:** That there are contentions among them.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:11

**Q3. To whom was this report declared?**
**Answer:** To Paul.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:11

### 1 Corinthians 1:12
**Q1. Which group of people does some say they are of?**
**Answer:** Paul.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:12

**Q2. Besides Paul, who else are some claiming to be of?**
**Answer:** Apollos, Cephas, and Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:12

**Q3. What is the general nature of the statements being made by the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** Every one of them saith they are of a specific leader (Paul, Apollos, Cephas, or Christ).
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:12

### 1 Corinthians 1:13
**Q1. What rhetorical question does Paul ask about Christ?**
**Answer:** Is Christ divided?
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:13

**Q2. Was Paul crucified for the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** No (asked as a rhetorical question).
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:13

**Q3. In whose name does Paul ask if they were baptized?**
**Answer:** In the name of Paul.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:13

### 1 Corinthians 1:14
**Q1. For what does Paul thank God in this verse?**
**Answer:** That he baptized none of them, except Crispus and Gaius.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:14

**Q2. Which two specific individuals did Paul baptize?**
**Answer:** Crispus and Gaius.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:14

**Q3. What is Paul's general statement about baptizing the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** He baptized none of them (with two exceptions).
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:14

### 1 Corinthians 1:15
**Q1. Why was Paul thankful that he didn't baptize many of them?**
**Answer:** Lest any should say that he had baptized in his own name.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:15

**Q2. What did Paul want to prevent people from saying?**
**Answer:** That he had baptized in his own name.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:15

**Q3. What was the motive behind Paul's limited baptisms?**
**Answer:** To avoid the claim that he baptized in his own name.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:15

### 1 Corinthians 1:16
**Q1. Whose household did Paul baptize?**
**Answer:** The household of Stephanas.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:16

**Q2. Does Paul know for sure if he baptized anyone else?**
**Answer:** No, he says "I know not whether I baptized any other."
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:16

**Q3. Who was baptized along with Crispus and Gaius?**
**Answer:** The household of Stephanas.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:16

### 1 Corinthians 1:17
**Q1. For what purpose did Christ NOT send Paul?**
**Answer:** To baptize.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:17

**Q2. For what purpose DID Christ send Paul?**
**Answer:** To preach the gospel.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:17

**Q3. Why did Paul avoid preaching with "wisdom of words"?**
**Answer:** Lest the cross of Christ should be made of none effect.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:17

### 1 Corinthians 1:18
**Q1. To whom is the preaching of the cross considered "foolishness"?**
**Answer:** To them that perish.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:18

**Q2. To whom is the preaching of the cross the "power of God"?**
**Answer:** Unto us which are saved.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:18

**Q3. What two opposing views of the preaching of the cross are mentioned?**
**Answer:** Foolishness (to those perishing) and the power of God (to those saved).
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:18

### 1 Corinthians 1:19
**Q1. What does the scripture say about the wisdom of the wise?**
**Answer:** "I will destroy the wisdom of the wise".
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:19

**Q2. What will happen to the understanding of the prudent?**
**Answer:** God will bring it to nothing.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:19

**Q3. What is the general theme of this verse regarding human wisdom?**
**Answer:** God will destroy the wisdom of the wise and the understanding of the prudent.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:19

### 1 Corinthians 1:20
**Q1. Who are the three types of people Paul asks about in this verse?**
**Answer:** The wise, the scribe, and the disputer of this world.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:20

**Q2. What has God done to the wisdom of this world?**
**Answer:** He has made it foolish.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:20

**Q3. What does Paul ask about the "disputer of this world"?**
**Answer:** "Where is the disputer of this world?"
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:20

### 1 Corinthians 1:21
**Q1. How did the world fail to know God using its own wisdom?**
**Answer:** After that in the wisdom of God the world by wisdom knew not God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:21

**Q2. What did it please God to use to save those that believe?**
**Answer:** The foolishness of preaching.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:21

**Q3. Who are the ones that God saves through the foolishness of preaching?**
**Answer:** Them that believe.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:21

### 1 Corinthians 1:22
**Q1. What do the Jews require?**
**Answer:** A sign.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:22

**Q2. What do the Greeks seek after?**
**Answer:** Wisdom.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:22

**Q3. Which two groups are mentioned as seeking different things?**
**Answer:** The Jews and the Greeks.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:22

### 1 Corinthians 1:23
**Q1. What do the apostles preach?**
**Answer:** Christ crucified.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:23

**Q2. To the Jews, what is Christ crucified?**
**Answer:** A stumblingblock.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:23

**Q3. To the Greeks, what is Christ crucified?**
**Answer:** Foolishness.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:23

### 1 Corinthians 1:24
**Q1. Who are "them which are called"?**
**Answer:** Both Jews and Greeks.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:24

**Q2. To those who are called, what is Christ?**
**Answer:** The power of God, and the wisdom of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:24

**Q3. What two attributes of God are Christ identified with for the called?**
**Answer:** The power of God and the wisdom of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:24

### 1 Corinthians 1:25
**Q1. How does the "foolishness of God" compare to men?**
**Answer:** It is wiser than men.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:25

**Q2. How does the "weakness of God" compare to men?**
**Answer:** It is stronger than men.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:25

**Q3. What comparison is made between God's perceived weakness/foolishness and man's strength/wisdom?**
**Answer:** God's foolishness is wiser than men, and His weakness is stronger than men.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:25

### 1 Corinthians 1:26
**Q1. Who are the "brethren" Paul is addressing?**
**Answer:** The Corinthians.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:26

**Q2. According to the flesh, were many wise men called?**
**Answer:** No, not many.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:26

**Q3. Besides wise men, what other two types of people are mentioned as "not many" being called?**
**Answer:** Mighty men and noble men.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:26

### 1 Corinthians 1:27
**Q1. What has God chosen to confound the wise?**
**Answer:** The foolish things of the world.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:27

**Q2. What has God chosen to confound the things which are mighty?**
**Answer:** The weak things of the world.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:27

**Q3. Why did God choose the foolish and weak things of the world?**
**Answer:** To confound the wise and the mighty.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:27

### 1 Corinthians 1:28
**Q1. What other things of the world has God chosen?**
**Answer:** Base things, things which are despised, and things which are not.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:28

**Q2. What did God choose the base and despised things to do?**
**Answer:** To bring to nought things that are.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:28

**Q3. What does "things which are not" refer to in the context of God's choice?**
**Answer:** Things that God chose to bring to nought things that are.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:28

### 1 Corinthians 1:29
**Q1. Why did God choose the foolish, weak, base, and despised things?**
**Answer:** That no flesh should glory in his presence.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:29

**Q2. Who is the "his" in "in his presence"?**
**Answer:** God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:29

**Q3. What is the intended result regarding human glory in God's presence?**
**Answer:** That no flesh should glory.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:29

### 1 Corinthians 1:30
**Q1. In whom are the believers "of him"?**
**Answer:** In Christ Jesus.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:30

**Q2. What has God made Christ to be for us?**
**Answer:** Wisdom, righteousness, sanctification, and redemption.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:30

**Q3. How many specific things is Christ made to be for us according to this verse?**
**Answer:** Four (wisdom, righteousness, sanctification, and redemption).
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:30

### 1 Corinthians 1:31
**Q1. According to the scripture, who should the one that glorieth glory in?**
**Answer:** In the Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:31

**Q2. What does the verse say about he that glorieth?**
**Answer:** "He that glorieth, let him glory in the Lord."
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:31

**Q3. What is the source of the statement "He that glorieth, let him glory in the Lord"?**
**Answer:** It is written (scripture).
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 1:31
