# 1 Corinthians 11 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Corinthians 11:1
**Q1. Who does Paul ask the believers to be followers of?**
**Answer:** Be ye followers of me.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:1

**Q2. Who is Paul a follower of?**
**Answer:** Even as I also am of Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:1

**Q3. What is the relationship between Paul and Christ mentioned here?**
**Answer:** Paul is a follower of Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:1

### 1 Corinthians 11:2
**Q1. Why does Paul praise the brethren?**
**Answer:** That ye remember me in all things, and keep the ordinances, as I delivered them to you.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:2

**Q2. What have the brethren kept as delivered by Paul?**
**Answer:** The ordinances.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:2

**Q3. In what way do the brethren remember Paul?**
**Answer:** In all things.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:2

### 1 Corinthians 11:3
**Q1. Who is the head of every man?**
**Answer:** Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:3

**Q2. Who is the head of the woman?**
**Answer:** The man.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:3

**Q3. Who is the head of Christ?**
**Answer:** God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:3

### 1 Corinthians 11:4
**Q1. What is a man doing when he dishonoureth his head?**
**Answer:** Praying or prophesying, having his head covered.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:4

**Q2. Under what condition does a man dishonour his head?**
**Answer:** When he has his head covered while praying or prophesying.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:4

**Q3. What actions are mentioned in this verse?**
**Answer:** Praying or prophesying.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:4

### 1 Corinthians 11:5
**Q1. What does a woman do to dishonour her head?**
**Answer:** Prayeth or prophesieth with her head uncovered.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:5

**Q2. What is it "all one as if" she were when she is uncovered?**
**Answer:** As if she were shaven.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:5

**Q3. When does a woman dishonour her head?**
**Answer:** When she prays or prophesies with her head uncovered.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:5

### 1 Corinthians 11:6
**Q1. What should happen to a woman if she is not covered?**
**Answer:** Let her also be shorn.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:6

**Q2. What is considered a shame for a woman?**
**Answer:** To be shorn or shaven.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:6

**Q3. What is the instruction if it is a shame for a woman to be shorn or shaven?**
**Answer:** Let her be covered.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:6

### 1 Corinthians 11:7
**Q1. Why ought a man not to cover his head?**
**Answer:** Forasmuch as he is the image and glory of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:7

**Q2. What is the man described as in relation to God?**
**Answer:** The image and glory of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:7

**Q3. What is the woman described as in this verse?**
**Answer:** The woman is the glory of the man.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:7

### 1 Corinthians 11:8
**Q1. Is the man of the woman?**
**Answer:** No, the man is not of the woman.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:8

**Q2. Who is the woman of?**
**Answer:** The woman of the man.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:8

**Q3. What is the stated origin of the woman in this verse?**
**Answer:** The woman is of the man.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:8

### 1 Corinthians 11:9
**Q1. Was the man created for the woman?**
**Answer:** No, neither was the man created for the woman.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:9

**Q2. For whom was the woman created?**
**Answer:** The woman for the man.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:9

**Q3. What is the purpose of the woman's creation according to this verse?**
**Answer:** She was created for the man.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:9

### 1 Corinthians 11:10
**Q1. What ought the woman to have on her head?**
**Answer:** Power.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:10

**Q2. Why ought the woman to have power on her head?**
**Answer:** Because of the angels.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:10

**Q3. Who is the reason for the woman having power on her head?**
**Answer:** The angels.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:10

### 1 Corinthians 11:11
**Q1. Is the man without the woman in the Lord?**
**Answer:** No, neither is the man without the woman.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:11

**Q2. Is the woman without the man in the Lord?**
**Answer:** No, neither the woman without the man.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:11

**Q3. In whom is this mutual dependence stated?**
**Answer:** In the Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:11

### 1 Corinthians 11:12
**Q1. If the woman is of the man, what is true about the man?**
**Answer:** Even so is the man also by the woman.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:12

**Q2. From whom do all things come?**
**Answer:** All things of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:12

**Q3. What is the source of all things mentioned in this verse?**
**Answer:** God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:12

### 1 Corinthians 11:13
**Q1. What question does Paul ask the believers to judge in themselves?**
**Answer:** Is it comely that a woman pray unto God uncovered?
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:13

**Q2. What action is being questioned in terms of comeliness?**
**Answer:** A woman praying unto God uncovered.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:13

**Q3. To whom is the woman praying in this question?**
**Answer:** Unto God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:13

### 1 Corinthians 11:14
**Q1. What does nature itself teach about a man with long hair?**
**Answer:** That it is a shame unto him.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:14

**Q2. What is the result of a man having long hair according to nature?**
**Answer:** It is a shame unto him.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:14

**Q3. What is the source of this teaching about long hair on men?**
**Answer:** Nature itself.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:14

### 1 Corinthians 11:15
**Q1. What is long hair to a woman?**
**Answer:** It is a glory to her.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:15

**Q2. Why is long hair a glory to a woman?**
**Answer:** For her hair is given her for a covering.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:15

**Q3. What was hair given to the woman for?**
**Answer:** For a covering.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:15

### 1 Corinthians 11:16
**Q1. What should happen if any man seem to be contentious?**
**Answer:** He should know that "we have no such custom, neither the churches of God."
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:16

**Q2. Who does not have this custom of being contentious about this matter?**
**Answer:** Paul ("we") and the churches of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:16

**Q3. What is the response to a contentious man regarding this custom?**
**Answer:** That they (Paul and the churches of God) have no such custom.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:16

### 1 Corinthians 11:17
**Q1. In what way does Paul NOT praise the believers for coming together?**
**Answer:** That ye come together not for the better, but for the worse.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:17

**Q2. How are the believers coming together?**
**Answer:** Not for the better, but for the worse.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:17

**Q3. What is Paul's sentiment about their current way of coming together?**
**Answer:** He does not praise them for it.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:17

### 1 Corinthians 11:18
**Q1. What does Paul hear is happening when they come together in the church?**
**Answer:** That there be divisions among you.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:18

**Q2. Where are the divisions occurring?**
**Answer:** In the church.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:18

**Q3. Does Paul believe entirely that there are divisions?**

**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:18

**Q2. What is the result of there being heresies?**
**Answer:** Those who are approved are made manifest.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:19

**Q3. Who is made manifest through the presence of heresies?**
**Answer:** They which are approved.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:19

**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:21

**Q3. How is the supper being taken?**
**Answer:** Every one takes his own supper before others.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:21

**Q2. What are they doing to the church of God?**
**Answer:** They despise the church of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:22

**Q3. Who are they shaming in this verse?**

**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:22

**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:23

### 1 Corinthians 11:24
**Q1. What did the Lord do before breaking the bread?**
**Answer:** He had given thanks.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:24

**Q2. What did the Lord say the bread was?**

**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:24

**Answer:** In remembrance of me.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:24

**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:25

**Q2. Under what condition is one guilty of the body and blood of the Lord?**

**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 11:27
