# 1 Corinthians 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Corinthians 10:1
**Q1. What does Paul want the brethren to not be ignorant of?**
**Answer:** How that all our fathers were under the cloud, and all passed through the sea.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 10:1

**Q2. Who were "our fathers" in this verse?**
**Answer:** The Israelites (implied by context).
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 10:1

**Q3. What two events are mentioned as something the fathers experienced?**
**Answer:** Being under the cloud and passing through the sea.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 10:1

### 1 Corinthians 10:2
**Q1. To whom were all the fathers baptized?**
**Answer:** Unto Moses.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 10:2

**Q2. In what two places were they baptized?**
**Answer:** In the cloud and in the sea.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 10:2

**Q3. Who is the leader mentioned as the one they were baptized unto?**
**Answer:** Moses.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 10:2

### 1 Corinthians 10:3
**Q1. What did all the fathers eat?**
**Answer:** The same spiritual meat.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 10:3

**Q2. What is the nature of the meat they ate?**
**Answer:** Spiritual meat.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 10:3

**Q3. Did all the fathers eat the same meat?**
**Answer:** Yes.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 10:3

### 1 Corinthians 10:4

**Q2. From what source did they drink?**

**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 10:4

**Q3. Who was the spiritual Rock?**

**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 10:4

### 1 Corinthians 10:5

**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 10:5

**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 10:5

### 1 Corinthians 10:6
**Q1. What purpose did these things serve as for the believers?**
**Answer:** They were our examples.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 10:6

**Q2. What should the believers not lust after?**
**Answer:** Evil things.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 10:6

**Q3. Why should the believers avoid lusting after evil things?**
**Answer:** Because the fathers also lusted, and it served as an example of what not to do.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 10:6

### 1 Corinthians 10:7
**Q1. What is the warning given in this verse?**
**Answer:** "Neither be ye idolaters".
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 10:7

**Q2. What evidence is provided as an example of idolatry?**

**Q3. What should the believers avoid becoming?**
**Answer:** Idolaters.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 10:7

### 1 Corinthians 10:13
