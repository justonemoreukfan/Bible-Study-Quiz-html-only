# 1 Corinthians 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Corinthians 9:1
**Q1. What are the three questions Paul asks about his status?**
**Answer:** "Am I am not an apostle? am I not free? have I not seen Jesus Christ our Lord?"
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:1

**Q2. Who are described as Paul's "work in the Lord"?**
**Answer:** The Corinthians.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:1

**Q3. Who did Paul see that validates his apostleship?**
**Answer:** Jesus Christ our Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:1

### 1 Corinthians 9:2
**Q1. To whom does Paul say he is doubtless an apostle?**
**Answer:** To the Corinthians.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:2

**Q2. What is the "seal" of Paul's apostleship?**
**Answer:** The Corinthians themselves in the Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:2

**Q3. What does the status of the Corinthians prove about Paul?**
**Answer:** That he is an apostle to them.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:2

### 1 Corinthians 9:3
**Q1. To whom does Paul give his answer?**
**Answer:** To them that do examine him.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:3

**Q2. What is Paul doing in this verse?**
**Answer:** Providing an answer to those who examine him.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:3

**Q3. Who are the "them" that examine Paul?**
**Answer:** Those who question or test his authority.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:3

### 1 Corinthians 9:4
**Q1. What power do apostles have regarding food and drink?**
**Answer:** The power to eat and to drink.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:4

**Q2. What does Paul ask about the power to eat and drink?**
**Answer:** "Have we not power to eat and to drink?"
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:4

**Q3. What is the general point of this question?**
**Answer:** To establish the rights of an apostle.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:4

### 1 Corinthians 9:5
**Q1. What power do apostles have regarding a "sister, a wife"?**
**Answer:** The power to lead her about.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:5

**Q2. Who else has the power to lead about a sister, a wife?**
**Answer:** Other apostles, the brethren of the Lord, and Cephas.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:5

**Q3. What is the nature of the power mentioned in this verse?**
**Answer:** The right to have a spouse and their support.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:5

### 1 Corinthians 9:6
**Q1. Who are the two people Paul mentions having the power to forbear working?**
**Answer:** Paul and Barnabas.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:6

**Q2. What does it mean to "forbear working"?**
**Answer:** To abstain from secular labor for the sake of the ministry.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:6

**Q3. What power is being discussed in this verse?**
**Answer:** The power to forbear working.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:6

### 1 Corinthians 9:7
**Q1. Who goeth a warfare at his own charges?**
**Answer:** No one (implied by the rhetorical question).
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:7

**Q2. What happens to the one who planteth a vineyard?**
**Answer:** He eateth of the fruit thereof.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:7

**Q3. What happens to the one who feedeth a flock?**
**Answer:** He eateth of the milk of the flock.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:7

### 1 Corinthians 9:8
**Q1. Does Paul say these things only as a man?**
**Answer:** No, he asks "or saith not the law the same also?"
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:8

**Q2. What is the source of the principles Paul is mentioning?**
**Answer:** Both human reasoning and the law.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:8

**Q3. What is Paul's aim in mentioning the law?**
**Answer:** To show that these rights are divinely sanctioned.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:8

### 1 Corinthians 9:9
**Q1. What is written in the law of Moses regarding the ox?**
**Answer:** "Thou shalt not muzzle the mouth of the ox that treadeth out the corn."
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:9

**Q2. What rhetorical question does Paul ask about God and oxen?**
**Answer:** "Doth God take care for oxen?"
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:9

**Q3. What animal is mentioned in the law of Moses in this verse?**
**Answer:** An ox.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:9

### 1 Corinthians 9:10
**Q1. For whose sakes did God write the law about the ox?**
**Answer:** For our sakes.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:10

**Q2. What should the one who ploweth do?**
**Answer:** Plow in hope.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:10

**Q3. What should the one who thresheth be a partaker of?**
**Answer:** Partaker of his hope.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:10

### 1 Corinthians 9:11
**Q1. What have the apostles sown unto the believers?**
**Answer:** Spiritual things.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:11

**Q2. What do the apostles ask to reap in return?**
**Answer:** Carnal things.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:11

**Q3. Is it a "great thing" to reap carnal things after sowing spiritual things?**
**Answer:** Paul asks "is it a great thing", implying it is a reasonable expectation.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:11

### 1 Corinthians 9:12
**Q1. Have others been partakers of the power over the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** Yes.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:12

**Q2. Did Paul use this power?**
**Answer:** No, "Nevertheless we have not used this power".
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:12

**Q3. Why did Paul suffer all things instead of using this power?**
**Answer:** Lest he should hinder the gospel of Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:12

### 1 Corinthians 9:13
**Q1. How do those who minister about holy things live?**
**Answer:** They live of the things of the temple.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:13

**Q2. Who are partakers with the altar?**
**Answer:** They which wait at the altar.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:13

**Q3. What is the source of support for those who minister about holy things?**
**Answer:** The things of the temple.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:13

### 1 Corinthians 9:14
**Q1. What has the Lord ordained for those who preach the gospel?**
**Answer:** That they should live of the gospel.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:14

**Q2. Who ordained that gospel preachers should live of the gospel?**
**Answer:** The Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:14

**Q3. How should those who preach the gospel be supported?**
**Answer:** By living of the gospel.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:14

### 1 Corinthians 9:15
**Q1. Did Paul use the rights mentioned in the previous verses?**
**Answer:** No, "But I have used none of these things".
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:15

**Q2. Why did Paul not write these things to have them done unto him?**
**Answer:** For it were better for him to die, than that any man should make his glorying void.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:15

**Q3. What would happen if Paul's glorying was made void?**
**Answer:** It would be better for him to die.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:15

### 1 Corinthians 9:16
**Q1. Does Paul have anything to glory of for preaching the gospel?**
**Answer:** No, "I have nothing to glory of".
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:16

**Q2. What is laid upon Paul regarding his preaching?**
**Answer:** Necessity.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:16

**Q3. What does Paul say would happen if he did not preach the gospel?**
**Answer:** "woe is unto me, if I preach not the gospel!"
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:16

### 1 Corinthians 9:17
**Q1. What is the reward if Paul preaches the gospel willingly?**
**Answer:** He has a reward.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:17

**Q2. What happens if he preaches against his will?**
**Answer:** A dispensation of the gospel is committed unto him.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:17

**Q3. What two ways of preaching are mentioned in this verse?**
**Answer:** Willingly and against his will.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:17

### 1 Corinthians 9:18
**Q1. What is Paul's reward?**
**Answer:** That when he preacheth the gospel, he may make the gospel of Christ without charge.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:18

**Q2. Why does Paul make the gospel without charge?**
**Answer:** That he abuse not his power in the gospel.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:18

**Q3. What does Paul avoid by making the gospel without charge?**
**Answer:** Abusing his power in the gospel.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:18

### 1 Corinthians 9:19
**Q1. Is Paul free from all men?**
**Answer:** Yes, "though I be free from all men".
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:19

**Q2. What has Paul made himself to all men?**
**Answer:** A servant.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:19

**Q3. Why did Paul make himself a servant to all?**
**Answer:** That he might gain the more.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:19

### 1 Corinthians 9:20
**Q1. How did Paul become in relation to the Jews?**
**Answer:** As a Jew.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:20

**Q2. Why did Paul become as a Jew?**
**Answer:** That he might gain the Jews.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:20

**Q3. How did Paul become in relation to those under the law?**
**Answer:** As under the law.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:20

### 1 Corinthians 9:21
**Q1. How did Paul become in relation to those without law?**
**Answer:** As without law.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:21

**Q2. Was Paul actually without law to God?**
**Answer:** No, he was "not without law to God, but under the law to Christ".
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:21

**Q3. Why did Paul become as those without law?**
**Answer:** That he might gain them that are without law.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:21

### 1 Corinthians 9:22
**Q1. How did Paul become in relation to the weak?**
**Answer:** As weak.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:22

**Q2. Why did Paul become as weak?**
**Answer:** That he might gain the weak.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:22

**Q3. What is Paul's general approach to different types of people?**
**Answer:** He is made all things to all men, that he might by all means save some.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:22

### 1 Corinthians 9:23
**Q1. For whose sake does Paul do all these things?**
**Answer:** For the gospel's sake.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:23

**Q2. What is the desired result of doing these things for the gospel?**
**Answer:** That he might be partaker thereof with the Corinthians.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:23

**Q3. What does Paul hope to be a partaker of?**
**Answer:** The gospel.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:23

### 1 Corinthians 9:24
**Q1. What analogy does Paul use regarding a race?**
**Answer:** That they which run in a race run all, but one receiveth the prize.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:24

**Q2. What is the instruction to those running the race?**
**Answer:** "So run, that ye may obtain."
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:24

**Q3. How many people receive the prize in the race analogy?**
**Answer:** One.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:24

### 1 Corinthians 9:25
**Q1. What is the characteristic of every man that striveth for the mastery?**
**Answer:** He is temperate in all things.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:25

**Q2. What kind of crown do those in the world strive for?**
**Answer:** A corruptible crown.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:25

**Q3. What kind of crown do the believers strive for?**
**Answer:** An incorruptible crown.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:25

### 1 Corinthians 9:26
**Q1. How does Paul describe his running?**
**Answer:** Not as uncertainly.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:26

**Q2. How does Paul describe his fighting?**
**Answer:** Not as one that beateth the air.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:26

**Q3. What does Paul avoid in his running and fighting?**
**Answer:** Uncertainty and beating the air.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:26

### 1 Corinthians 9:27
**Q1. What does Paul do to his body?**
**Answer:** He keepeth it under and bringeth it into subjection.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:27

**Q2. Why does Paul bring his body into subjection?**
**Answer:** Lest that by any means, when he had preached to others, he himself should be a castaway.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:27

**Q3. What is the risk Paul wants to avoid?**
**Answer:** Being a castaway.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 9:27
