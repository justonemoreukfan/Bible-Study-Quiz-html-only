# 1 Corinthians 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Corinthians 2:1
**Q1. How did Paul describe his speech and wisdom when he came to the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** He came not with excellency of speech or of wisdom.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:1

**Q2. What was Paul declaring to them?**
**Answer:** The testimony of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:1

**Q3. To whom was Paul speaking?**
**Answer:** To the brethren.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:1

### 1 Corinthians 2:2
**Q1. What did Paul determine not to know among the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** Any thing save Jesus Christ, and him crucified.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:2

**Q2. What was the primary focus of Paul's knowledge when he was with them?**
**Answer:** Jesus Christ, and him crucified.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:2

**Q3. What was Paul's determination regarding his knowledge among them?**
**Answer:** Not to know any thing save Jesus Christ, and him crucified.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:2

### 1 Corinthians 2:3
**Q1. In what state was Paul when he was with the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** In weakness, and in fear, and in much trembling.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:3

**Q2. What three emotions or states are mentioned as being present with Paul?**
**Answer:** Weakness, fear, and much trembling.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:3

**Q3. Who was with them in this state of weakness and fear?**
**Answer:** Paul.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:3

### 1 Corinthians 2:4
**Q1. What was not used in Paul's speech and preaching?**
**Answer:** Enticing words of man's wisdom.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:4

**Q2. In what was Paul's speech and preaching characterized?**
**Answer:** In demonstration of the Spirit and of power.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:4

**Q3. What was the contrast provided between man's wisdom and Paul's preaching?**
**Answer:** Enticing words of man's wisdom versus demonstration of the Spirit and of power.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:4

### 1 Corinthians 2:5
**Q1. Why did Paul preach in a demonstration of the Spirit and power?**
**Answer:** That their faith should not stand in the wisdom of men, but in the power of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:5

**Q2. Where should the faith of the believers not stand?**
**Answer:** In the wisdom of men.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:5

**Q3. Where should the faith of the believers stand?**
**Answer:** In the power of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:5

### 1 Corinthians 2:6
**Q1. To whom does Paul say we speak wisdom?**
**Answer:** Among them that are perfect.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:6

**Q2. What kind of wisdom is NOT being spoken of?**
**Answer:** The wisdom of this world, nor of the princes of this world.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:6

**Q3. What happens to the wisdom of this world and its princes?**
**Answer:** They come to nought.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:6

### 1 Corinthians 2:7
**Q1. What kind of wisdom do they speak?**
**Answer:** The wisdom of God in a mystery.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:7

**Q2. How is the wisdom of God described in this verse?**
**Answer:** As a mystery, even the hidden wisdom.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:7

**Q3. When did God ordain this hidden wisdom?**
**Answer:** Before the world unto our glory.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:7

### 1 Corinthians 2:8
**Q1. Who did not know the hidden wisdom of God?**
**Answer:** None of the princes of this world.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:8

**Q2. What would the princes of this world have NOT done if they had known the hidden wisdom?**
**Answer:** They would not have crucified the Lord of glory.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:8

**Q3. Who is referred to as the "Lord of glory"?**
**Answer:** Jesus Christ (implied by context).
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:8

### 1 Corinthians 2:9
**Q1. According to the scripture, what has the eye not seen?**
**Answer:** The things which God hath prepared for them that love him.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:9

**Q2. According to the scripture, what has the ear not heard?**
**Answer:** The things which God hath prepared for them that love him.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:9

**Q3. What has not entered into the heart of man?**
**Answer:** The things which God hath prepared for them that love him.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:9

### 1 Corinthians 2:10
**Q1. How has God revealed the prepared things to us?**
**Answer:** By his Spirit.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:10

**Q2. What does the Spirit search?**
**Answer:** All things, yea, the deep things of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:10

**Q3. What is the role of the Spirit in revealing God's deep things?**
**Answer:** The Spirit searcheth all things and reveals them unto us.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:10

### 1 Corinthians 2:11
**Q1. Who knows the things of a man?**
**Answer:** The spirit of man which is in him.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:11

**Q2. Who knows the things of God?**
**Answer:** The Spirit of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:11

**Q3. What comparison is made between the spirit of man and the Spirit of God?**
**Answer:** Just as the spirit of man knows his own things, the Spirit of God knows the things of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:11

### 1 Corinthians 2:12
**Q1. What spirit did we not receive?**
**Answer:** The spirit of the world.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:12

**Q2. What spirit did we receive?**
**Answer:** The spirit which is of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:12

**Q3. Why did we receive the spirit which is of God?**
**Answer:** That we might know the things that are freely given to us of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:12

### 1 Corinthians 2:13
**Q1. In what words are these things spoken?**
**Answer:** Not in the words which man's wisdom teacheth.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:13

**Q2. Who teaches the words used to speak these things?**
**Answer:** The Holy Ghost.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:13

**Q3. How are spiritual things compared?**
**Answer:** Comparing spiritual things with spiritual.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:13

### 1 Corinthians 2:14
**Q1. What does the natural man receive?**
**Answer:** He receiveth not the things of the Spirit of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:14

**Q2. How are the things of the Spirit of God viewed by the natural man?**
**Answer:** They are foolishness unto him.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:14

**Q3. Why can the natural man not know the things of the Spirit of God?**
**Answer:** Because they are spiritually discerned.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:14

### 1 Corinthians 2:15
**Q1. Who is able to judge all things?**
**Answer:** He that is spiritual.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:15

**Q2. Is the spiritual man judged by any man?**
**Answer:** No, he himself is judged of no man.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:15

**Q3. What is the capacity of the spiritual man regarding judgment?**
**Answer:** He judgeth all things.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:15

### 1 Corinthians 2:16
**Q1. Who has known the mind of the Lord?**
**Answer:** No one (implied by rhetorical question).
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:16

**Q2. What can no man do for the Lord?**
**Answer:** Instruct him.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:16

**Q3. What do the believers have?**
**Answer:** We have the mind of Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 2:16
