# 1 Corinthians 14 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Corinthians 14:1
**Q1. What should the believers follow after?**
**Answer:** Follow after charity.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:1

**Q2. What should they desire?**
**Answer:** Desire spiritual gifts.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:1

**Q3. What specifically should they rather desire?**
**Answer:** Rather that ye may prophesy.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:1

### 1 Corinthians 14:2
**Q1. To whom does he that speaketh in an unknown tongue speak?**
**Answer:** Not unto men, but unto God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:2

**Q2. Why does he not speak to men?**
**Answer:** For no man understandeth him.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:2

**Q3. What does he speak in the spirit?**
**Answer:** He speaketh mysteries.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:2

### 1 Corinthians 14:3
**Q1. To whom does he that prophesieth speak?**
**Answer:** Speaketh unto men.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:3

**Q2. For what purposes does he prophesy?**
**Answer:** To edification, and exhortation, and comfort.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:3

**Q3. What are the three benefits of prophesying to men?**
**Answer:** Edification, exhortation, and comfort.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:3

### 1 Corinthians 14:4
**Q1. Who does he that speaketh in an unknown tongue edify?**
**Answer:** Edifieth himself.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:4

**Q2. Who does he that prophesieth edify?**
**Answer:** Edifieth the church.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:4

**Q3. What is the difference in edification between tongues and prophesying?**
**Answer:** Tongues edify the self, prophesying edifies the church.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:4

### 1 Corinthians 14:5
**Q1. What would Paul wish for all the believers?**
**Answer:** I would that ye all spake with tongues.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:5

**Q2. What would he rather that they did?**
**Answer:** But rather that ye prophesied.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:5

**Q3. Why is he that prophesieth greater than he that speaketh with tongues?**
**Answer:** Except he interpret, that the church may receive edifying.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:5

### 1 Corinthians 14:6
**Q1. What would Paul profit the believers if he came speaking with tongues?**
**Answer:** Nothing, except he shall speak to you either by revelation, or by knowledge, or by prophesying, or by doctrine.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:6

**Q2. By what four means could Paul profit the believers?**
**Answer:** By revelation, knowledge, prophesying, or doctrine.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:6

**Q3. What is the condition for Paul to profit the believers in this verse?**
**Answer:** That he speaks by revelation, knowledge, prophesying, or doctrine.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:6

### 1 Corinthians 14:7
**Q1. What do things without life give?**
**Answer:** Sound.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:7

**Q2. What examples of things without life are given?**
**Answer:** Pipe or harp.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:7

**Q3. What must they give in order for it to be known what is piped or harped?**
**Answer:** A distinction in the sounds.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:7

### 1 Corinthians 14:8
**Q1. What happens if the trumpet give an uncertain sound?**
**Answer:** Who shall prepare himself to the battle?
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:8

**Q2. What is the result of an uncertain trumpet sound?**
**Answer:** No one will prepare for the battle.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:8

**Q3. What instrument is mentioned in this verse?**
**Answer:** The trumpet.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:8

### 1 Corinthians 14:9
**Q1. What must a person utter by the tongue for it to be known what is spoken?**
**Answer:** Words easy to be understood.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:9

**Q2. What happens if words are not easy to be understood?**
**Answer:** Ye shall speak into the air.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:9

**Q3. What is the condition for spoken words to be known?**
**Answer:** They must be easy to be understood.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:9

### 1 Corinthians 14:10
**Q1. How many kinds of voices are there in the world?**
**Answer:** So many kinds of voices.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:10

**Q2. Are any of these voices without signification?**
**Answer:** None of them is without signification.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:10

**Q3. What is the nature of voices in the world?**
**Answer:** There are many kinds, and all have signification.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:10

### 1 Corinthians 14:11
**Q1. What happens if the speaker does not know the meaning of the voice?**
**Answer:** I shall be unto him that speaketh a barbarian, and he that speaketh shall be a barbarian unto me.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:11

**Q2. Who becomes a barbarian to whom?**
**Answer:** The listener becomes a barbarian to the speaker, and the speaker becomes a barbarian to the listener.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:11

**Q3. What is the result of not knowing the meaning of the voice?**
**Answer:** Both parties are as barbarians to each other.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:11

### 1 Corinthians 14:12
**Q1. What should those who are zealous of spiritual gifts seek?**
**Answer:** Seek that ye may excel to the edifying of the church.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:12

**Q2. What is the goal for those zealous of spiritual gifts?**
**Answer:** To excel in the edifying of the church.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:12

**Q3. Who are those that should seek to excel in edifying the church?**
**Answer:** Those who are zealous of spiritual gifts.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:12

### 1 Corinthians 14:13
**Q1. What should he that speaketh in an unknown tongue pray for?**

**Q2. What is the request for the person speaking in tongues?**
**Answer:** That he may interpret.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:13

**Q3. Why should he pray for interpretation?**

**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:13

### 1 Corinthians 14:14
**Q1. When praying in an unknown tongue, what prays?**

**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:14

**Q2. What is the state of the understanding when praying in tongues?**

**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:14

**Q3. What is the result of praying in an unknown tongue?**

**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 14:14

**Q3. What is Paul's approach to praying and singing?**
