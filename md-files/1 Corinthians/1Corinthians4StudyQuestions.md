# 1 Corinthians 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Corinthians 4:1
**Q1. How should men account of Paul and his associates?**
**Answer:** As of the ministers of Christ, and stewards of the mysteries of God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:1

**Q2. What specific role do they have in relation to the mysteries of God?**
**Answer:** They are stewards.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:1

**Q3. Of whom are they ministers?**
**Answer:** Of Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:1

### 1 Corinthians 4:2
**Q1. What is required in stewards?**
**Answer:** That a man be found faithful.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:2

**Q2. What quality is essential for a steward?**
**Answer:** Faithfulness.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:2

**Q3. What must a steward be found to be?**
**Answer:** Faithful.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:2

### 1 Corinthians 4:3
**Q1. What does Paul consider a "very small thing"?**
**Answer:** That he should be judged of the Corinthians, or of man's judgment.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:3

**Q2. Does Paul judge his own self?**
**Answer:** No, he says "yea, I judge not mine own self."
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:3

**Q3. How does Paul view the judgment of man?**
**Answer:** As a very small thing.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:3

### 1 Corinthians 4:4
**Q1. What does Paul say he knows by himself?**
**Answer:** Nothing.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:4

**Q2. Does knowing nothing by himself justify Paul?**
**Answer:** No, he says "yet am I not hereby justified".
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:4

**Q3. Who is the one that judgeth Paul?**
**Answer:** The Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:4

### 1 Corinthians 4:5
**Q1. What is Paul's instruction regarding judging?**
**Answer:** Judge nothing before the time.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:5

**Q2. When should judgment happen?**
**Answer:** Until the Lord come.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:5

**Q3. What will the Lord do when He comes?**
**Answer:** He will bring to light the hidden things of darkness and make manifest the counsels of the hearts.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:5

### 1 Corinthians 4:6
**Q1. To whom did Paul transfer these things in a figure?**
**Answer:** To himself and to Apollos.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:6

**Q2. Why did Paul do this?**
**Answer:** For the Corinthians' sakes, that they might learn in them not to think of men above that which is written.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:6

**Q3. What did Paul want to prevent among the believers?**
**Answer:** That no one of them be puffed up for one against another.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:6

### 1 Corinthians 4:7
**Q1. What question does Paul ask about what makes one person differ from another?**
**Answer:** "For who maketh thee to differ from another?"
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:7

**Q2. What does Paul ask about the things a person possesses?**
**Answer:** "and what hast thou that thou didst not receive?"
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:7

**Q3. Why does Paul question the act of glorying?**
**Answer:** Because if everything was received, there is no reason to glory as if it had not been received.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:7

### 1 Corinthians 4:8
**Q1. How do the Corinthians perceive themselves in this verse?**
**Answer:** They are full, rich, and have reigned as kings without the apostles.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:8

**Q2. What did Paul wish for them?**
**Answer:** That they did reign.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:8

**Q3. What would be the result if the Corinthians reigned?**
**Answer:** That the apostles also might reign with them.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:8

### 1 Corinthians 4:9
**Q1. Where does Paul think God has set the apostles?**
**Answer:** Last, as it were appointed to death.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:9

**Q2. To whom are the apostles made a spectacle?**
**Answer:** Unto the world, and to angels, and to men.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:9

**Q3. How does Paul describe the apostles' position?**
**Answer:** Set last, appointed to death, and as a spectacle.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:9

### 1 Corinthians 4:10
**Q1. For whose sake are the apostles "fools"?**
**Answer:** For Christ's sake.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:10

**Q2. What contrast does Paul make between the apostles and the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** Apostles are fools, Corinthians are wise in Christ; apostles are weak, Corinthians are strong; apostles are despised, Corinthians are honourable.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:10

**Q3. How are the apostles described in terms of strength?**
**Answer:** They are weak.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:10

### 1 Corinthians 4:11
**Q1. Up until what time did the apostles experience hunger and thirst?**
**Answer:** Even unto this present hour.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:11

**Q2. What four physical hardships are mentioned in this verse?**
**Answer:** Hunger, thirst, nakedness, and being buffeted.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:11

**Q3. What is stated about the apostles' dwellingplace?**
**Answer:** They have no certain dwellingplace.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:11

### 1 Corinthians 4:12
**Q1. How do the apostles obtain their living?**
**Answer:** They labour, working with their own hands.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:12

**Q2. How do the apostles respond when they are reviled?**
**Answer:** They bless.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:12

**Q3. How do the apostles respond when they are persecuted?**
**Answer:** They suffer it.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:12

### 1 Corinthians 4:13
**Q1. What do the apostles do when they are defamed?**
**Answer:** They intreat.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:13

**Q2. How are the apostles described in relation to the world?**
**Answer:** As the filth of the world.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:13

**Q3. What are the apostles described as being unto this day?**
**Answer:** The offscouring of all things.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:13

### 1 Corinthians 4:14
**Q1. Why is Paul writing these things?**
**Answer:** Not to shame them, but to warn them.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:14

**Q2. How does Paul refer to the Corinthians in this verse?**
**Answer:** As his beloved sons.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:14

**Q3. What is Paul's intent in warning them?**
**Answer:** To warn them as a father would his beloved sons.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:14

### 1 Corinthians 4:15
**Q1. How many instructers in Christ might the Corinthians have?**
**Answer:** Ten thousand.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:15

**Q2. What do they not have many of?**
**Answer:** Fathers.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:15

**Q3. How did Paul beget the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** In Christ Jesus through the gospel.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:15

### 1 Corinthians 4:16
**Q1. What does Paul beseech the Corinthians to do?**
**Answer:** To be followers of him.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:16

**Q2. Who does Paul want the Corinthians to follow?**
**Answer:** Paul himself.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:16

**Q3. What is Paul's request to the believers?**
**Answer:** That they be followers of him.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:16

### 1 Corinthians 4:17
**Q1. Who did Paul send to the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** Timotheus.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:17

**Q2. How does Paul describe Timotheus?**
**Answer:** His beloved son, and faithful in the Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:17

**Q3. What is Timotheus's purpose in visiting them?**
**Answer:** To bring them into remembrance of Paul's ways which be in Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:17

### 1 Corinthians 4:18
**Q1. Why are some people "puffed up" in this verse?**
**Answer:** As though Paul would not come to them.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:18

**Q2. What is the state of some of the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** They are puffed up.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:18

**Q3. What was the assumption of those who were puffed up?**
**Answer:** That Paul would not come to them.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:18

### 1 Corinthians 4:19
**Q1. When does Paul intend to come to the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** Shortly, if the Lord will.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:19

**Q2. What will Paul seek to know when he arrives?**
**Answer:** Not the speech of them which are puffed up, but the power.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:19

**Q3. What does Paul contrast the "speech of them which are puffed up" with?**
**Answer:** The power.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:19

### 1 Corinthians 4:20
**Q1. In what is the kingdom of God NOT found?**
**Answer:** In word.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:20

**Q2. In what IS the kingdom of God found?**
**Answer:** In power.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:20

**Q3. What is the nature of the kingdom of God according to this verse?**
**Answer:** It is not in word, but in power.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:20

### 1 Corinthians 4:21
**Q1. What does Paul ask the Corinthians if they want him to come with?**
**Answer:** A rod, or in love, and in the spirit of meekness.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:21

**Q2. What are the two contrasting ways Paul could come?**
**Answer:** With a rod, or in love and the spirit of meekness.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:21

**Q3. What question is Paul posing to the believers?**
**Answer:** "What will ye?" regarding how he should come to them.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 4:21
