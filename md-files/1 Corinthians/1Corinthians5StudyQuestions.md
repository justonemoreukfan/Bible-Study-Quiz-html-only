# 1 Corinthians 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Corinthians 5:1
**Q1. What is reported commonly among the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** That there is fornication among them.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:1

**Q2. How is the fornication described in terms of comparison to the Gentiles?**
**Answer:** Such fornication as is not so much as named among the Gentiles.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:1

**Q3. What specific act of fornication is mentioned?**
**Answer:** That one should have his father's wife.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:1

### 1 Corinthians 5:2
**Q1. How have the Corinthians reacted to the report of fornication?**
**Answer:** They are puffed up.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:2

**Q2. What should the Corinthians have done instead of being puffed up?**
**Answer:** They should have mourned.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:2

**Q3. Why should they have mourned?**
**Answer:** That he that hath done this deed might be taken away from among them.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:2

### 1 Corinthians 5:3
**Q1. In what state was Paul when he judged the matter?**
**Answer:** Absent in body, but present in spirit.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:3

**Q2. How did Paul judge the person who did this deed?**
**Answer:** As though he were present.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:3

**Q3. Who is the judge in this situation?**
**Answer:** Paul.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:3

### 1 Corinthians 5:4
**Q1. In whose name will the judgment be carried out when the believers are gathered?**
**Answer:** In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:4

**Q2. When should the believers be gathered together?**
**Answer:** When they are gathered together.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:4

**Q3. What is combined with Paul's spirit in this action?**
**Answer:** The power of our Lord Jesus Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:4

### 1 Corinthians 5:5
**Q1. What is the purpose of delivering such a person to Satan?**
**Answer:** For the destruction of the flesh.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:5

**Q2. What is the intended result for the person's spirit?**
**Answer:** That the spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord Jesus.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:5

**Q3. To whom is the person to be delivered?**
**Answer:** Unto Satan.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:5

### 1 Corinthians 5:6
**Q1. What does Paul say about the Corinthians' glorying?**
**Answer:** Your glorying is not good.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:6

**Q2. What analogy does Paul use regarding leaven?**
**Answer:** "Know ye not that a little leaven leaveneth the whole lump?"
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:6

**Q3. What is the warning about "a little leaven"?**
**Answer:** That it leavens the whole lump.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:6

### 1 Corinthians 5:7
**Q1. What should the Corinthians purge out?**
**Answer:** The old leaven.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:7

**Q2. Why should they purge out the old leaven?**
**Answer:** That they may be a new lump, as they are unleavened.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:7

**Q3. Who is described as our passover and was sacrificed for us?**
**Answer:** Christ.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:7

### 1 Corinthians 5:8
**Q1. How should the believers keep the feast?**
**Answer:** Not with old leaven, neither with the leaven of malice and wickedness.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:8

**Q2. With what kind of bread should they keep the feast?**
**Answer:** With the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:8

**Q3. What two types of "leaven" are mentioned as things to avoid?**
**Answer:** The leaven of malice and wickedness.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:8

### 1 Corinthians 5:9
**Q1. What did Paul write in an epistle regarding fornicators?**
**Answer:** Not to company with fornicators.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:9

**Q2. In what form was this instruction given?**
**Answer:** In an epistle.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:9

**Q3. What is the instruction regarding fornicators?**
**Answer:** Not to company with them.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:9

### 1 Corinthians 5:10
**Q1. Does Paul mean they should avoid all fornicators in the world?**
**Answer:** No, not altogether with the fornicators of this world.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:10

**Q2. Besides fornicators of the world, whom else should they not avoid completely?**
**Answer:** Covetous, extortioners, or idolaters.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:10

**Q3. What would happen if they avoided all fornicators, covetous, extortioners, and idolaters of the world?**
**Answer:** They must needs go out of the world.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:10

### 1 Corinthians 5:11
**Q1. Under what condition should a "brother" not be kept company with?**
**Answer:** If any man that is called a brother be a fornicator, or covetous, or an idolator, or a railer, or a drunkard, or an extortioner.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:11

**Q2. What is the instruction regarding eating with such a person?**
**Answer:** With such an one no not to eat.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:11

**Q3. Which six types of people are listed as "brothers" not to keep company with?**
**Answer:** Fornicators, covetous, idolators, railers, drunkards, and extortioners.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:11

### 1 Corinthians 5:12
**Q1. What does Paul ask about judging those who are "without"?**
**Answer:** "For what have I to do to judge them also that are without?"
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:12

**Q2. Who are the ones the Corinthians should be judging?**
**Answer:** Them that are within.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:12

**Q3. What is Paul's point regarding judging those outside the church?**
**Answer:** That he has nothing to do with judging those who are without.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:12

### 1 Corinthians 5:13
**Q1. Who judgeth them that are without?**
**Answer:** God.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:13

**Q2. What is the instruction given to the Corinthians regarding the "wicked person"?**
**Answer:** Put away from among yourselves that wicked person.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:13

**Q3. What action should be taken regarding the wicked person?**
**Answer:** Put them away from among themselves.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 5:13
