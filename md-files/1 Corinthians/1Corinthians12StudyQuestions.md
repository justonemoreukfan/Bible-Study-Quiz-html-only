# 1 Corinthians 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Corinthians 12:1
**Q1. What does Paul not want the brethren to be ignorant of?**
**Answer:** Concerning spiritual gifts.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 12:1

**Q2. Who is Paul addressing in this verse?**
**Answer:** Brethren.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 12:1

**Q3. What is the topic of this section?**
**Answer:** Spiritual gifts.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 12:1

### 1 Corinthians 12:2
**Q1. Who were the believers before?**
**Answer:** Gentiles.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 12:2

**Q2. Where were the Gentiles carried away to?**
**Answer:** Unto these dumb idols.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 12:2

**Q3. How were they led to these idols?**
**Answer:** Even as ye were led.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 12:2

### 1 Corinthians 12:3
**Q1. What is said about a man speaking by the Spirit of God?**
**Answer:** No man speaking by the Spirit of God calleth Jesus accursed.

**Q2. How can a man say that Jesus is the Lord?**
**Answer:** But by the Holy Ghost.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 12:3

**Q3. What is the requirement for declaring Jesus is the Lord?**
**Answer:** It must be by the Holy Ghost.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 12:3

### 1 Corinthians 12:10
**Q1. List three gifts mentioned in this verse.**
**Answer:** The working of miracles, prophecy, and discerning of spirits.

**Answer:** Divers kinds of tongues, and the interpretation of tongues.

**Q3. How many total gifts are listed in this verse?**
**Answer:** Six.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 12:10

**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 12:12

### 1 Corinthians 12:17
**Q1. What would happen if the whole body were an eye?**
**Answer:** Where were the hearing?
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 12:17

**Answer:** Where were the smelling?
