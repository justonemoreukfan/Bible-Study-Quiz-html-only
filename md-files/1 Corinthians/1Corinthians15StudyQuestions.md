# 1 Corinthians 15 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Corinthians 15:1
**Q1. What does Paul declare unto the brethren?**
**Answer:** The gospel which I preached unto you, which also ye have received, and wherein ye stand.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:1

**Q2. What has the brethren received?**
**Answer:** The gospel.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:1

**Q3. In what does the brethren stand?**
**Answer:** In the gospel.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:1

### 1 Corinthians 15:2
**Q1. By what means are the believers saved?**
**Answer:** By the gospel.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:2

**Q2. What is the condition for being saved by the gospel?**
**Answer:** If ye keep in memory what I preached unto you, unless ye have believed in vain.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:2

**Q3. What is the risk of believing in vain?**
**Answer:** Not being saved.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:2

### 1 Corinthians 15:3
**Q1. What did Paul deliver first of all?**
**Answer:** That which I also received.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:3

**Q2. What is the core message delivered?**
**Answer:** That Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:3

**Q3. According to what was it known that Christ died for our sins?**
**Answer:** According to the scriptures.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:3

### 1 Corinthians 15:4
**Q1. What happened to Christ after he died?**
**Answer:** He was buried.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:4

**Q2. When did Christ rise again?**
**Answer:** The third day.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:4

**Q3. According to what did Christ rise again on the third day?**
**Answer:** According to the scriptures.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:4

### 1 Corinthians 15:5

**Answer:** Cephas.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:5

**Answer:** The twelve.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:5

**Q3. Who are the two groups mentioned as seeing Christ?**
**Answer:** Cephas and the twelve.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:5

**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:6

**Q3. Who are the two groups mentioned as seeing Christ in this verse?**

### 1 Corinthians 15:12
**Q1. What is the contradiction mentioned in this verse?**
**Answer:** If Christ be preached that he rose from the dead, how say some among you that there is no resurrection of the dead?
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:12

**Q2. What do some among the believers say?**
**Answer:** That there is no resurrection of the dead.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:12

**Q3. What is the core message of the preaching?**
**Answer:** That Christ rose from the dead.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:12

### 1 Corinthians 15:13
**Q1. What is the implication if there be no resurrection of the dead?**
**Answer:** Then is Christ not risen.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:13

**Q2. If Christ is not risen, what happens to the resurrection of the dead?**
**Answer:** It does not happen.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:13

**Q3. What is the link between Christ's resurrection and the resurrection of the dead?**
**Answer:** If there is no resurrection of the dead, then Christ is not risen.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 15:13

**Q2. What happens to the faith of the believers?**

**Q3. What is the result of Christ not rising?**
