### 1 Corinthians 7:1
**Q1. What does Paul say is "good" for a man regarding women?**
**Answer:** It is good for a man not to touch a woman.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 7:1

**Q2. What is the context of this statement?**
**Answer:** Concerning the things whereof they wrote to him.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 7:1

**Q3. What is the general advice given in this verse?**
**Answer:** That it is good for a man not to touch a woman.
**Verse:** 1 Corinthians 7:1
