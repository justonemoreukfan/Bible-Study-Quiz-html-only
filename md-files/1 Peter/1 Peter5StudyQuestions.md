# 1 Peter 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Peter 5:1

**Q1. Who is exhorting the elders among them?**  
**Answer:** The elders which are among you, who am also an elder, and a witness of the sufferings of Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:1

**Q2. What is the writer a partaker of, besides being a witness of the sufferings of Christ?**  
**Answer:** And also a partaker of the glory that shall be revealed.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:1

**Q3. To whom is the writer giving this exhortation?**  
**Answer:** The elders which are among you.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:1

---

### 1 Peter 5:2

**Q1. What are the elders instructed to feed?**  
**Answer:** The flock of God which is among you.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:2

**Q2. How are the elders to take the oversight of the flock?**  
**Answer:** Not by constraint, but willingly.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:2

**Q3. How should the elders feed the flock regarding filthy lucre?**  
**Answer:** Not for filthy lucre, but of a ready mind.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:2

---

### 1 Peter 5:3

**Q1. How should elders act regarding God's heritage?**  
**Answer:** Neither as being lords over God's heritage.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:3

**Q2. What should the elders be instead of being lords over God's heritage?**  
**Answer:** But being ensamples to the flock.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:3

**Q3. Whose heritage is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** God's heritage.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:3

---

### 1 Peter 5:4

**Q1. Who shall appear when the chief Shepherd shall appear?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall receive a crown of glory.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:4

**Q2. What kind of crown will they receive?**  
**Answer:** A crown of glory.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:4

**Q3. What title is given to the Shepherd in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The chief Shepherd.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:4

---

### 1 Peter 5:5

**Q1. Who is instructed to submit themselves unto the elder?**  
**Answer:** Likewise, ye younger.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:5

**Q2. What should all of you be clothed with toward one another?**  
**Answer:** Be clothed with humility.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:5

**Q3. Who does God resist, and to whom does He give grace?**  
**Answer:** God resisteth the proud, and giveth grace to the humble.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:5

---

### 1 Peter 5:6

**Q1. Under what are the readers commanded to humble themselves?**  
**Answer:** Under the mighty hand of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:6

**Q2. When will God exalt the humbled?**  
**Answer:** That he may exalt you in due time.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:6

**Q3. Whose mighty hand is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The mighty hand of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:6

---

### 1 Peter 5:7

**Q1. What should be cast upon the Lord?**  
**Answer:** All your care.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:7

**Q2. Why should they cast all their care upon Him?**  
**Answer:** For he careth for you.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:7

**Q3. How much of your care should be cast upon Him?**  
**Answer:** All your care.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:7

---

### 1 Peter 5:8

**Q1. How are the readers instructed to be regarding their adversary?**  
**Answer:** Be sober, be vigilant.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:8

**Q2. Who is described as walking about like a roaring lion?**  
**Answer:** Your adversary the devil.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:8

**Q3. What does the devil do as he walks about?**  
**Answer:** Seeketh whom he may devour.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:8

---

### 1 Peter 5:9

**Q1. How should the readers resist the devil?**  
**Answer:** Stedfast in the faith.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:9

**Q2. What is being accomplished among your brethren that are in the world?**  
**Answer:** The same afflictions are accomplished in your brethren.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:9

**Q3. Where are your brethren experiencing these afflictions?**  
**Answer:** In the world.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:9

---

### 1 Peter 5:10

**Q1. What kind of God is described as having called us unto His eternal glory?**  
**Answer:** The God of all grace.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:10

**Q2. What did God call us unto?**  
**Answer:** Unto his eternal glory by Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:10

**Q3. After you have suffered a while, what will the God of all grace do?**  
**Answer:** Make you perfect, stablish, strengthen, settle you.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:10

---

### 1 Peter 5:11

**Q1. To whom do glory and dominion belong?**  
**Answer:** To him.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:11

**Q2. How long are glory and dominion ascribed to Him?**  
**Answer:** For ever and ever.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:11

**Q3. What word concludes the praise in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Amen.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:11

---

### 1 Peter 5:12

**Q1. By whom has the writer written briefly?**  
**Answer:** By Silvanus, a faithful brother unto you.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:12

**Q2. What is the purpose of this exhortation?**  
**Answer:** Exhorting, and testifying that this is the true grace of God wherein ye stand.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:12

**Q3. Where are they instructed to stand?**  
**Answer:** Wherein ye stand [the true grace of God].  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:12

---

### 1 Peter 5:13

**Q1. Who is elected together with you, residing in Babylon?**  
**Answer:** The church that is at Babylon.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:13

**Q2. Who is mentioned as being greeted alongside the church at Babylon?**  
**Answer:** And so doth Marcus my son.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:13

**Q3. Where is the church located that salutes you?**  
**Answer:** At Babylon.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:13

---

### 1 Peter 5:14

**Q1. With what kind of kiss should they greet one another?**  
**Answer:** With a kiss of charity.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:14

**Q2. What is wished upon all that are in Christ Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Peace be with you all that are in Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:14

**Q3. What is the final word of the chapter?**  
**Answer:** Amen.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 5:14
