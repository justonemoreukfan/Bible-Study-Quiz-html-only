# 1 Peter 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Peter 1:1

**Q1. Who wrote this epistle?**  
**Answer:** Peter.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:1

**Q2. What is Peter described as in this verse?**  
**Answer:** An apostle of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:1

**Q3. To whom are the strangers scattered throughout Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia addressed?**  
**Answer:** The strangers scattered throughout Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:1

---

### 1 Peter 1:2

**Q1. According to whose foreknowledge are the recipients chosen?**  
**Answer:** God the Father.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:2

**Q2. Through what is the sanctification of the Spirit manifested?**  
**Answer:** Unto obedience and sprinkling of the blood of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:2

**Q3. What two things does Peter wish to be multiplied unto them?**  
**Answer:** Grace and peace.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:2

---

### 1 Peter 1:3

**Q1. Who is blessed in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:3

**Q2. According to his abundant mercy, what has God begotten us again unto?**  
**Answer:** A lively hope.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:3

**Q3. By what event is the lively hope begotten again?**  
**Answer:** By the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:3

---

### 1 Peter 1:4

**Q1. To what kind of inheritance are we begotten?**  
**Answer:** An inheritance incorruptible, and undefiled, and that fadeth not away.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:4

**Q2. Where is this inheritance reserved?**  
**Answer:** In heaven.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:4

**Q3. For whom is this inheritance reserved?**  
**Answer:** For you.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:4

---

### 1 Peter 1:5

**Q1. By what are we kept?**  
**Answer:** By the power of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:5

**Q2. Through what is this keeping accomplished?**  
**Answer:** Through faith.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:5

**Q3. Unto what are we kept ready to be revealed?**  
**Answer:** Unto salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:5

---

### 1 Peter 1:6

**Q1. In what should you greatly rejoice?**  
**Answer:** In this.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:6

**Q2. For how long might you need to be in heaviness?**  
**Answer:** For a season.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:6

**Q3. What causes you to be in heaviness?**  
**Answer:** Manifold temptations.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:6

---

### 1 Peter 1:7

**Q1. What is the trial of your faith being compared to?**  
**Answer:** Gold that perisheth.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:7

**Q2. Even though gold is tried with fire, how is the trial of your faith described as being tried?**  
**Answer:** Though it be tried with fire.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:7

**Q3. Unto what might the trial of your faith be found unto?**  
**Answer:** Unto praise and honour and glory at the appearing of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:7

---

### 1 Peter 1:8

**Q1. Whom have ye not seen?**  
**Answer:** Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:8

**Q2. Though you see him not, what do you do?**  
**Answer:** Ye love him.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:8

**Q3. With what kind of joy do you believe and rejoice?**  
**Answer:** With joy unspeakable and full of glory.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:8

---

### 1 Peter 1:9

**Q1. What are you receiving as the end of your faith?**  
**Answer:** The end of your faith, even the salvation of your souls.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:9

**Q2. What is the salvation of your souls identified as?**  
**Answer:** The end of your faith.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:9

**Q3. Whose souls are receiving this salvation?**  
**Answer:** Your souls.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:9

---

### 1 Peter 1:10

**Q1. Who inquired and searched diligently regarding this salvation?**  
**Answer:** The prophets.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:10

**Q2. What did the prophets prophesy about?**  
**Answer:** Of the grace that should come unto you.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:10

**Q3. Whose grace did they prophesy of?**  
**Answer:** The grace that should come unto you.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:10

---

### 1 Peter 1:11

**Q1. What did the Spirit of Christ which was in them signify?**  
**Answer:** The sufferings of Christ, and the glory that should follow.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:11

**Q2. When did the Spirit testify of these things?**  
**Answer:** When it testified beforehand.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:11

**Q3. Whose sufferings and glory did the Spirit signify?**  
**Answer:** The sufferings of Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:11

---

### 1 Peter 1:12

**Q1. Unto whom was it revealed that they did not minister the things unto themselves?**  
**Answer:** Unto them.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:12

**Q2. Unto whom are these things now reported?**  
**Answer:** Unto you.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:12

**Q3. By what means were these things reported unto you?**  
**Answer:** By them that have preached the gospel unto you with the Holy Ghost sent down from heaven.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:12

---

### 1 Peter 1:13

**Q1. What should you do to the loins of your mind?**  
**Answer:** Gird up the loins of your mind.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:13

**Q2. How should you act in regards to sobriety?**  
**Answer:** Be sober.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:13

**Q3. To the end, upon what should you hope to the end?**  
**Answer:** For the grace that is to be brought unto you at the revelation of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:13

---

### 1 Peter 1:14

**Q1. As what kind of children should you fashion yourselves?**  
**Answer:** As obedient children.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:14

**Q2. What should you not fashion yourselves according to?**  
**Answer:** According to the former lusts in your ignorance.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:14

**Q3. When did the former lusts take place?**  
**Answer:** In your ignorance.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:14

---

### 1 Peter 1:15

**Q1. As he which hath called you is holy, what should you be?**  
**Answer:** Holy.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:15

**Q2. In what manner should you be holy?**  
**Answer:** In all manner of conversation.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:15

**Q3. Who is the one that hath called you?**  
**Answer:** He which hath called you (holy).  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:15

---

### 1 Peter 1:16

**Q1. What is written in the scripture quoted in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Be ye holy; for I am holy.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:16

**Q2. Who states "I am holy"?**  
**Answer:** God (implied by the commandment "for I am holy").  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:16

**Q3. What must you be because God is holy?**  
**Answer:** Holy.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:16

---

### 1 Peter 1:17

**Q1. Whom do you call on as Father?**  
**Answer:** Him who without respect of persons judgeth according to every man's work.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:17

**Q2. How does the Father judge according to every man's work?**  
**Answer:** Without respect of persons.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:17

**Q3. During what time should you pass the time of your sojourning here in fear?**  
**Answer:** The time of your sojourning here.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:17

---

### 1 Peter 1:18

**Q1. From what were you redeemed?**  
**Answer:** From your vain conversation received by tradition from your fathers.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:18

**Q2. How were you not redeemed?**  
**Answer:** With corruptible things, as silver and gold.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:18

**Q3. From whom or what source was the vain conversation received?**  
**Answer:** By tradition from your fathers.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:18

---

### 1 Peter 1:19

**Q1. With what precious blood were you redeemed?**  
**Answer:** With the precious blood of Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:19

**Q2. Christ is compared to what kind of lamb?**  
**Answer:** As of a lamb without blemish and without spot.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:19

**Q3. What characteristics does the lamb have?**  
**Answer:** Without blemish and without spot.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:19

---

### 1 Peter 1:20

**Q1. When was Christ foreordained?**  
**Answer:** Before the foundation of the world.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:20

**Q2. When was Christ manifest?**  
**Answer:** In these last times.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:20

**Q3. For whose sake was Christ manifest in these last times?**  
**Answer:** For you.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:20

---

### 1 Peter 1:21

**Q1. Through whom do you believe in God?**  
**Answer:** By him.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:21

**Q2. Who raised Christ up from the dead?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:21

**Q3. What did God give to Christ after raising him up?**  
**Answer:** Glory.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:21

---

### 1 Peter 1:22

**Q1. How have you purified your souls?**  
**Answer:** In obeying the truth through the Spirit.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:22

**Q2. Unto what end have you purified your souls in obeying the truth?**  
**Answer:** Unto unfeigned love of the brethren.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:22

**Q3. How are you commanded to love one another?**  
**Answer:** See that ye love one another with a pure heart fervently.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:22

---

### 1 Peter 1:23

**Q1. How have you been born again?**  
**Answer:** Not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:23

**Q2. By what is the incorruptible seed described?**  
**Answer:** By the word of God, which liveth and abideth for ever.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:23

**Q3. How long does the word of God live and abide?**  
**Answer:** For ever.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:23

---

### 1 Peter 1:24

**Q1. What is all flesh compared to?**  
**Answer:** As grass.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:24

**Q2. What is the glory of man compared to?**  
**Answer:** As the flower of grass.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:24

**Q3. What happens to the flower of grass?**  
**Answer:** The grass withereth, and the flower thereof falleth away.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:24

---

### 1 Peter 1:25

**Q1. What endureth for ever?**  
**Answer:** The word of the Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:25

**Q2. What is this word that endureth for ever?**  
**Answer:** This is the word which by the gospel is preached unto you.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:25

**Q3. How was the word preached unto you?**  
**Answer:** By the gospel.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 1:25
