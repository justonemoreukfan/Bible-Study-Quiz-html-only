# 1 Peter 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Peter 3:1

**Q1. Who are the wives instructed to be in subjection to?**  
**Answer:** Their own husbands.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:1

**Q2. What may happen to some husbands if they obey not the word?**  
**Answer:** They also may be won without the word.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:1

**Q3. By what means may the husbands be won without the word?**  
**Answer:** By the conversation of the wives.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:1

---

### 1 Peter 3:2

**Q1. What kind of conversation do the wives behold?**  
**Answer:** Your chaste conversation.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:2

**Q2. What is coupled with their chaste conversation?**  
**Answer:** Fear.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:2

**Q3. What do the husbands behold in the wives?**  
**Answer:** Your chaste conversation coupled with fear.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:2

---

### 1 Peter 3:3

**Q1. Whose adorning should not be that outward adorning?**  
**Answer:** Whose adorning let it not be that outward adorning.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:3

**Q2. What kind of hair styling is mentioned as part of outward adorning?**  
**Answer:** Plaiting the hair.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:3

**Q3. What kind of jewelry or clothing is mentioned as part of outward adorning?**  
**Answer:** Wearing of gold, or of putting on of apparel.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:3

---

### 1 Peter 3:4

**Q1. Where should the hidden man of the heart be?**  
**Answer:** In that which is not corruptible.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:4

**Q2. What kind of spirit is described as being of great price?**  
**Answer:** A meek and quiet spirit.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:4

**Q3. In the sight of whom is a meek and quiet spirit of great price?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:4

---

### 1 Peter 3:5

**Q1. Who after this manner in the old time adorned themselves?**  
**Answer:** The holy women also, who trusted in God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:5

**Q2. What did these holy women adorn themselves with?**  
**Answer:** Themselves.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:5

**Q3. Who were these holy women in subjection unto?**  
**Answer:** Their own husbands.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:5

---

### 1 Peter 3:6

**Q1. Who obeyed Abraham?**  
**Answer:** Sara.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:6

**Q2. What did Sara call Abraham?**  
**Answer:** Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:6

**Q3. Whose daughters are ye, as long as ye do well and are not afraid with any amazement?**  
**Answer:** Whose daughters ye are.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:6

---

### 1 Peter 3:7

**Q1. How should husbands dwell with their wives?**  
**Answer:** According to knowledge.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:7

**Q2. As what kind of vessel should the wife be given honor?**  
**Answer:** As unto the weaker vessel.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:7

**Q3. What are the husband and wife being heirs together of?**  
**Answer:** The grace of life.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:7

---

### 1 Peter 3:8

**Q1. What should they all be of?**  
**Answer:** One mind.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:8

**Q2. How should they be toward one another regarding compassion?**  
**Answer:** Having compassion one of another.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:8

**Q3. What kind of spirit should they be of?**  
**Answer:** Be pitiful, be courteous.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:8

---

### 1 Peter 3:9

**Q1. What should they not render for evil?**  
**Answer:** Evil for evil.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:9

**Q2. What should they not render for railing?**  
**Answer:** Railing for railing.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:9

**Q3. What should they contrariwise do?**  
**Answer:** Bless.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:9

---

### 1 Peter 3:10

**Q1. What must a man love if he will love life?**  
**Answer:** Life.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:10

**Q2. What must he see if he will love life and see good days?**  
**Answer:** Good days.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:10

**Q3. What must his tongue and lips refrain from?**  
**Answer:** Refrain his tongue from evil, and his lips that they speak no guile.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:10

---

### 1 Peter 3:11

**Q1. What should a man eschew?**  
**Answer:** Evil.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:11

**Q2. What should a man do besides eschewing evil?**  
**Answer:** Do good.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:11

**Q3. What should he seek and pursue?**  
**Answer:** Seek peace, and ensue it.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:11

---

### 1 Peter 3:12

**Q1. Whose eyes are over the righteous?**  
**Answer:** The eyes of the Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:12

**Q2. Whose ears are open unto the prayers of the righteous?**  
**Answer:** His ears.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:12

**Q3. Whose face is against them that do evil?**  
**Answer:** The face of the Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:12

---

### 1 Peter 3:13

**Q1. Who is going to harm you if ye be followers of that which is good?**  
**Answer:** And who is he that will harm you.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:13

**Q2. What must you be followers of?**  
**Answer:** That which is good.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:13

**Q3. What does being followers of that which is good prevent?**  
**Answer:** Harm.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:13

---

### 1 Peter 3:14

**Q1. What will happen if ye suffer for righteousness' sake?**  
**Answer:** Happy are ye.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:14

**Q2. What should you not be afraid of?**  
**Answer:** Their terror.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:14

**Q3. What should you not be?**  
**Answer:** Troubled.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:14

---

### 1 Peter 3:15

**Q1. What should you sanctify in your hearts?**  
**Answer:** The Lord God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:15

**Q2. What should you be ready always to give?**  
**Answer:** An answer to every man that asketh you a reason of the hope that is in you.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:15

**Q3. With what attitude should you give the answer?**  
**Answer:** With meekness and fear.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:15

---

### 1 Peter 3:16

**Q1. What kind of conscience should you have?**  
**Answer:** A good conscience.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:16

**Q2. What happens whereas they speak evil of you?**  
**Answer:** They may be ashamed.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:16

**Q3. What kind of conversation in Christ are they falsely accused of?**  
**Answer:** Your good conversation in Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:16

---

### 1 Peter 3:17

**Q1. What is better if the will of God be so?**  
**Answer:** That ye suffer for well doing.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:17

**Q2. What kind of doing is contrasted with suffering for evil doing?**  
**Answer:** Well doing.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:17

**Q3. Whose will is mentioned regarding suffering?**  
**Answer:** The will of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:17

---

### 1 Peter 3:18

**Q1. Who also hath once suffered for sins?**  
**Answer:** Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:18

**Q2. For whose sins did the just suffer for the unjust?**  
**Answer:** The unjust.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:18

**Q3. To what place might Christ bring us?**  
**Answer:** To God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:18

---

### 1 Peter 3:19

**Q1. By what did Christ go and preach unto the spirits in prison?**  
**Answer:** By which also he went and preached.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:19

**Q2. To whom did Christ preach?**  
**Answer:** The spirits in prison.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:19

**Q3. By what means or Spirit did He go?**  
**Answer:** By the Spirit (referencing "quickened by the Spirit" from verse 18).  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:19

---

### 1 Peter 3:20

**Q1. When were the spirits disobedient?**  
**Answer:** When once the longsuffering of God waited.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:20

**Q2. In the days of whom did the longsuffering of God wait?**  
**Answer:** Noah.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:20

**Q3. How many souls were saved by water?**  
**Answer:** Few, that is, eight souls.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:20

---

### 1 Peter 3:21

**Q1. What does the like figure unto baptism also now save us?**  
**Answer:** Baptism.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:21

**Q2. What is baptism not the putting away of?**  
**Answer:** The filth of the flesh.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:21

**Q3. What is baptism instead the answer of?**  
**Answer:** The answer of a good conscience toward God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:21

---

### 1 Peter 3:22

**Q1. Where is Jesus Christ gone into?**  
**Answer:** Into heaven.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:22

**Q2. Where is Jesus Christ sitting in relation to God?**  
**Answer:** On the right hand of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:22

**Q3. What has been made subject unto Him?**  
**Answer:** Angels and authorities and powers.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 3:22
