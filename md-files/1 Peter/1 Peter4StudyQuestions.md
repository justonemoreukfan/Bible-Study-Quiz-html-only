# 1 Peter 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Peter 4:1

**Q1. Forasmuch then as Christ hath suffered for us in the flesh, what should we do?**  
**Answer:** Arm yourselves likewise with the same mind.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:1

**Q2. According to this verse, what has he that hath suffered in the flesh done?**  
**Answer:** Ceased from sin.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:1

**Q3. What did Christ do for us in the flesh?**  
**Answer:** Suffered for us in the flesh.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:1

---

### 1 Peter 4:2

**Q1. For how much longer should a man live the rest of his time in the flesh?**  
**Answer:** Not to the lusts of men, but to the will of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:2

**Q2. Whose lusts should a man no longer live the rest of his time in the flesh to?**  
**Answer:** The lusts of men.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:2

**Q3. To whose will should a man live the rest of his time in the flesh?**  
**Answer:** The will of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:2

---

### 1 Peter 4:3

**Q1. How much time past is sufficient for us to have wrought the will of the Gentiles?**  
**Answer:** The time past of our life.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:3

**Q2. When walking in lasciviousness, lusts, excess of wine, revellings, banquetings, and abominable idolatries, what was that the will of?**  
**Answer:** The Gentiles.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:3

**Q3. Name one of the things the Gentiles walked in according to this verse.**  
**Answer:** Lasciviousness (or lusts, excess of wine, revellings, banquetings, abominable idolatries).  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:3

---

### 1 Peter 4:4

**Q1. What do they think it strange that ye run not with them to?**  
**Answer:** The same excess of riot.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:4

**Q2. What do they do to you regarding the excess of riot?**  
**Answer:** Speak evil of you.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:4

**Q3. What do they think of you not running with them to the same excess of riot?**  
**Answer:** Strange.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:4

---

### 1 Peter 4:5

**Q1. To whom shall they give account?**  
**Answer:** To him that is ready to judge the quick and the dead.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:5

**Q2. Who is he ready to judge?**  
**Answer:** The quick and the dead.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:5

**Q3. What must they give to him?**  
**Answer:** Account.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:5

---

### 1 Peter 4:6

**Q1. For this cause was what preached also to them that are dead?**  
**Answer:** The gospel.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:6

**Q2. Why was the gospel preached also to them that are dead?**  
**Answer:** That they might be judged according to men in the flesh, but live according to God in the spirit.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:6

**Q3. How might they live according to God in the spirit?**  
**Answer:** That they might live according to God in the spirit.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:6

---

### 1 Peter 4:7

**Q1. What is at hand?**  
**Answer:** The end of all things.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:7

**Q2. Therefore, how should you be?**  
**Answer:** Be ye therefore sober.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:7

**Q3. Along with being sober, what else should you be unto prayer?**  
**Answer:** Watchful unto prayer.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:7

---

### 1 Peter 4:8

**Q1. Above all things, what should you have among yourselves?**  
**Answer:** Fervent charity.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:8

**Q2. How should the charity be among yourselves?**  
**Answer:** Fervent.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:8

**Q3. What shall charity cover?**  
**Answer:** The multitude of sins.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:8

---

### 1 Peter 4:9

**Q1. How should you use hospitality one to another?**  
**Answer:** Use hospitality one to another without grudging.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:9

**Q2. To whom should you use hospitality?**  
**Answer:** One to another.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:9

**Q3. Without what should you use hospitality?**  
**Answer:** Without grudging.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:9

---

### 1 Peter 4:10

**Q1. As every man hath received what, let him minister the same one to another?**  
**Answer:** The gift.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:10

**Q2. How should they minister the gift one to another?**  
**Answer:** As good stewards of the manifold grace of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:10

**Q3. Of what are they good stewards?**  
**Answer:** The manifold grace of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:10

---

### 1 Peter 4:11

**Q1. If any man speak, how should he speak?**  
**Answer:** As the oracles of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:11

**Q2. If any man minister, how should he do it?**  
**Answer:** As of the ability which God giveth.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:11

**Q3. In all things, who may be glorified through Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:11

---

### 1 Peter 4:12

**Q1. Beloved, what should you not think strange concerning?**  
**Answer:** The fiery trial which is to try you.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:12

**Q2. What kind of trial is it that is to try you?**  
**Answer:** The fiery trial.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:12

**Q3. As though what happened unto you?**  
**Answer:** As though some strange thing happened unto you.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:12

---

### 1 Peter 4:13

**Q1. In what should you rejoice, inasmuch as ye are partakers of Christ's sufferings?**  
**Answer:** Rejoice.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:13

**Q2. When his glory shall be revealed, what may ye be with exceeding joy?**  
**Answer:** Glad also with exceeding joy.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:13

**Q3. Of whose sufferings are ye partakers?**  
**Answer:** Christ's sufferings.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:13

---

### 1 Peter 4:14

**Q1. If ye be reproached for the name of Christ, what are ye?**  
**Answer:** Happy.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:14

**Q2. Why are you happy if you are reproached for the name of Christ?**  
**Answer:** For the spirit of glory and of God resteth upon you.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:14

**Q3. What spirit resteth upon you on their part?**  
**Answer:** The spirit of glory and of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:14

---

### 1 Peter 4:15

**Q1. Let none of you suffer as what?**  
**Answer:** A murderer, or as a thief, or as an evildoer, or as a busybody in other men's matters.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:15

**Q2. Should any of you suffer as a murderer?**  
**Answer:** No (Let none of you suffer as a murderer).  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:15

**Q3. Should any of you suffer as a busybody in what?**  
**Answer:** Other men's matters.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:15

---

### 1 Peter 4:16

**Q1. If any man suffer as a Christian, what should he not do?**  
**Answer:** Let him not be ashamed.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:16

**Q2. But what should he do in this behalf?**  
**Answer:** Let him glorify God on this behalf.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:16

**Q3. If any man suffer as what, should he not be ashamed?**  
**Answer:** A Christian.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:16

---

### 1 Peter 4:17

**Q1. For the time is come that judgment must begin where?**  
**Answer:** At the house of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:17

**Q2. If judgment must first begin at us, what shall be the end of them that obey not?**  
**Answer:** What shall be the end of them that obey not the gospel of God?  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:17

**Q3. What do they not obey?**  
**Answer:** The gospel of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:17

---

### 1 Peter 4:18

**Q1. If the righteous scarcely be saved, where shall appear?**  
**Answer:** The ungodly and the sinner.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:18

**Q2. How shall the righteous be saved?**  
**Answer:** Scarcely.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:18

**Q3. Who are mentioned alongside the sinner in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The ungodly.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:18

---

### 1 Peter 4:19

**Q1. Wherefore let them that suffer according to the will of God commit the keeping of what to him?**  
**Answer:** Their souls.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:19

**Q2. To whom should they commit the keeping of their souls?**  
**Answer:** To him, as unto a faithful Creator.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:19

**Q3. As what should they commit the keeping of their souls to him in well doing?**  
**Answer:** As unto a faithful Creator.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 4:19
