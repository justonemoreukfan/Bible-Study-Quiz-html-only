# 1 Peter 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Peter 2:1

**Q1. What are believers commanded to lay aside according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** All malice, and all guile, and hypocrisies, and envies, and all evil speakings.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:1

**Q2. What kind of malice and evil speakings should be laid aside?**  
**Answer:** All malice and all evil speakings.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:1

**Q3. Along with malice and guile, what else does the verse say to lay aside?**  
**Answer:** Hypocrisies and envies.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:1

---

### 1 Peter 2:2

**Q1. As newborn babes, what should believers desire?**  
**Answer:** The sincere milk of the word.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:2

**Q2. What is the milk of the word described as?**  
**Answer:** Sincere.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:2

**Q3. For what purpose should they desire the sincere milk of the word?**  
**Answer:** That ye may grow thereby.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:2

---

### 1 Peter 2:3

**Q1. If believers have done what, should they desire the milk of the word?**  
**Answer:** If so be ye have tasted that the Lord is gracious.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:3

**Q2. What have believers tasted about the Lord?**  
**Answer:** That the Lord is gracious.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:3

**Q3. What conditional phrase introduces this verse?**  
**Answer:** If so be.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:3

---

### 1 Peter 2:4

**Q1. To whom do believers come as unto a living stone?**  
**Answer:** To whom coming.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:4

**Q2. How is the stone described in relation to life?**  
**Answer:** Living stone.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:4

**Q3. How is the stone regarded by men versus how it is chosen of God?**  
**Answer:** Disallowed indeed of men, but chosen of God, and precious.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:4

---

### 1 Peter 2:5

**Q1. What are believers also, as lively stones?**  
**Answer:** Built up a spiritual house.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:5

**Q2. What kind of priesthood are they made to be?**  
**Answer:** An holy priesthood.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:5

**Q3. What kind of sacrifices are believers ordained to offer up?**  
**Answer:** Spiritual sacrifices acceptable to God by Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:5

---

### 1 Peter 2:6

**Q1. What is also contained in the scripture?**  
**Answer:** Behold, I lay in Sion a chief corner stone, elect, precious.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:6

**Q2. Where is the chief corner stone laid?**  
**Answer:** In Sion.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:6

**Q3. What will happen to he that believeth on him?**  
**Answer:** Shall not be confounded.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:6

---

### 1 Peter 2:7

**Q1. Unto you therefore which believe, what is the stone?**  
**Answer:** Precious.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:7

**Q2. But unto them which be what, is the stone disallowed?**  
**Answer:** Disobedient.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:7

**Q3. What has the same stone become?**  
**Answer:** The head of the corner.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:7

---

### 1 Peter 2:8

**Q1. What is the stone described as in this verse?**  
**Answer:** A stone of stumbling, and a rock of offence.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:8

**Q2. To whom are they a stone of stumbling and rock of offence?**  
**Answer:** To them which stumble at the word, being disobedient.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:8

**Q3. Whereunto also were they appointed?**  
**Answer:** Whereunto also they were appointed.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:8

---

### 1 Peter 2:9

**Q1. What kind of generation are believers called?**  
**Answer:** A chosen generation.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:9

**Q2. What kind of priesthood and nation are they?**  
**Answer:** A royal priesthood, an holy nation.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:9

**Q3. Out of what did God call believers into His marvellous light?**  
**Answer:** Out of darkness.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:9

---

### 1 Peter 2:10

**Q1. What were believers in time past?**  
**Answer:** Not a people.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:10

**Q2. What are believers now?**  
**Answer:** The people of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:10

**Q3. What mercy had believers not obtained, but now have obtained?**  
**Answer:** Had not obtained mercy, but now have obtained mercy.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:10

---

### 1 Peter 2:11

**Q1. How does the writer address the readers in this verse?**  
**Answer:** As strangers and pilgrims.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:11

**Q2. What are believers beseeched to abstain from?**  
**Answer:** Fleshly lusts.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:11

**Q3. Against what do these fleshly lusts war?**  
**Answer:** Against the soul.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:11

---

### 1 Peter 2:12

**Q1. How should believers have their conversation among the Gentiles?**  
**Answer:** Honest.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:12

**Q2. What do the Gentiles speak against believers as?**  
**Answer:** Evildoers.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:12

**Q3. What may the Gentiles do by seeing the believers' good works?**  
**Answer:** Glorify God in the day of visitation.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:12

---

### 1 Peter 2:13

**Q1. To what ordinance should believers submit themselves?**  
**Answer:** To every ordinance of man.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:13

**Q2. For whose sake should believers submit to every ordinance of man?**  
**Answer:** For the Lord's sake.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:13

**Q3. To whom should believers submit as supreme?**  
**Answer:** To the king.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:13

---

### 1 Peter 2:14

**Q1. Unto whom else should believers submit?**  
**Answer:** Unto governors.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:14

**Q2. By whom are these governors sent?**  
**Answer:** By him.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:14

**Q3. For what two purposes are governors sent?**  
**Answer:** For the punishment of evildoers, and for the praise of them that do well.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:14

---

### 1 Peter 2:15

**Q1. What is the will of God regarding well doing?**  
**Answer:** That with well doing ye may put to silence the ignorance of foolish men.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:15

**Q2. Whose ignorance may be put to silence?**  
**Answer:** The ignorance of foolish men.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:15

**Q3. By what means is this ignorance to be put to silence?**  
**Answer:** With well doing.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:15

---

### 1 Peter 2:16

**Q1. How should believers act as free men?**  
**Answer:** As free.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:16

**Q2. What should believers not use their liberty for?**  
**Answer:** For a cloke of maliciousness.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:16

**Q3. As what should believers live?**  
**Answer:** As the servants of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:16

---

### 1 Peter 2:17

**Q1. What should be done to all men?**  
**Answer:** Honour all men.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:17

**Q2. What should be done to the brotherhood and to God?**  
**Answer:** Love the brotherhood. Fear God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:17

**Q3. Whom should believers honour after fearing God?**  
**Answer:** Honour the king.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:17

---

### 1 Peter 2:18

**Q1. Who are servants told to be subject to?**  
**Answer:** To your masters.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:18

**Q2. With what kind of fear should servants be subject to their masters?**  
**Answer:** With all fear.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:18

**Q3. To which masters should servants be subject, besides the good and gentle?**  
**Answer:** Also to the froward.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:18

---

### 1 Peter 2:19

**Q1. What is thankworthy if a man endures grief?**  
**Answer:** For this is thankworthy, if a man for conscience toward God endure grief, suffering wrongfully.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:19

**Q2. Toward whom must the conscience be directed when enduring grief?**  
**Answer:** Toward God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:19

**Q3. How is the suffering described in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Suffering wrongfully.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:19

---

### 1 Peter 2:20

**Q1. What glory is it if ye be buffeted for your faults?**  
**Answer:** What glory is it, if, when ye be buffeted for your faults, ye shall take it patiently?  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:20

**Q2. What is acceptable with God if you do well and suffer for it?**  
**Answer:** This is acceptable with God.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:20

**Q3. What do you take patiently when it is acceptable with God?**  
**Answer:** If, when ye do well, and suffer for it, ye take it patiently.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:20

---

### 1 Peter 2:21

**Q1. For what purpose were believers even called?**  
**Answer:** Hereunto were ye called.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:21

**Q2. Who suffered for us, leaving us an example?**  
**Answer:** Christ also suffered for us.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:21

**Q3. What should believers follow according to His example?**  
**Answer:** That ye should follow his steps.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:21

### 1 Peter 2:22

**Q1. What did Christ not do regarding sin?**  
**Answer:** Who did no sin.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:22

**Q2. What was not found in His mouth?**  
**Answer:** Neither was guile found in his mouth.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:22

**Q3. Whose mouth is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** His mouth.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:22

---

### 1 Peter 2:23

**Q1. When He was reviled, what did He not do?**  
**Answer:** Reviled not again.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:23

**Q2. When He suffered, what did He not do?**  
**Answer:** Threatened not.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:23

**Q3. To whom did He commit Himself?**  
**Answer:** To him that judgeth righteously.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:23

### 1 Peter 2:24

**Q1. Who his own self bore our sins in His own body?**  
**Answer:** Who his own self bare our sins in his own body.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:24

**Q2. Where did He bear our sins?**  
**Answer:** On the tree.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:24

**Q3. By whose stripes were ye healed?**  
**Answer:** By whose stripes ye were healed.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:24

### 1 Peter 2:25

**Q1. What were believers like before returning to the Shepherd?**  
**Answer:** As sheep going astray.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:25

**Q2. To whom are believers now returned?**  
**Answer:** Unto the Shepherd and Bishop of your souls.  
**Verse:** 1 Peter 2:25

**Answer:** Bishop of your souls.
