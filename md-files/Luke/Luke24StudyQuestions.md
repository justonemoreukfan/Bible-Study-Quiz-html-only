# Luke 24 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Luke 24:1

**Q1. Upon what day of the week did the women come unto the sepulchre?**  
**Answer:** Upon the first day of the week.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:1

**Q2. At what time of the day did they come unto the sepulchre?**  
**Answer:** Very early in the morning.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:1

**Q3. What did the women bring with them when they came?**  
**Answer:** The spices which they had prepared.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:1

---

### Luke 24:2

**Q1. What did they find rolled away from the sepulchre?**  
**Answer:** The stone.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:2

---

### Luke 24:3

**Q1. When they entered in, what did they not find?**  
**Answer:** The body of the Lord Jesus.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:3

---

### Luke 24:4

**Q1. As they were much perplexed thereabout, who stood by them?**  
**Answer:** Two men.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:4

---

### Luke 24:5

**Q1. What did the women do as the men stood by in shining garments?**  
**Answer:** They were afraid, and bowed down their faces to the earth.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:5

---

### Luke 24:6

**Q1. Where did the men say He is, declaring He is risen?**  
**Answer:** He is not here, but is risen.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:6

---

### Luke 24:7

**Q1. Where did the Son of man need to be delivered into the hands of sinful men?**  
**Answer:** Into the hands of sinful men.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:7

---

### Luke 24:8

**Q1. What did the women remember upon hearing these things?**  
**Answer:** His words.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:8

---

### Luke 24:9

**Q1. From where did the women return to tell all these things?**  
**Answer:** From the sepulchre.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:9

---

### Luke 24:10

**Q1. Who were the women that told these things unto the apostles?**  
**Answer:** Mary Magdalene, and Joanna, and Mary the mother of James, and other women that were with them.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:10

---

### Luke 24:11

**Q1. How did their words seem unto the apostles?**  
**Answer:** As idle tales.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:11

---

### Luke 24:12

**Q1. Who arose and ran unto the sepulchre?**  
**Answer:** Peter.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:12

---

### Luke 24:13

**Q1. To what village were two of them going that same day?**  
**Answer:** Emmaus.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:13

---

### Luke 24:14

**Q1. What were they talking about as they walked?**  
**Answer:** All these things which had happened.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:14

---

### Luke 24:15

**Q1. While they communed and reasoned, what did Jesus Himself do?**  
**Answer:** Drew near, and went with them.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:15

---

### Luke 24:16

**Q1. What happened to their eyes that they should not know Him?**  
**Answer:** Their eyes were holden.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:16

---

### Luke 24:17

**Q1. What did Jesus ask them as they walked and were sad?**  
**Answer:** What manner of communications are these that ye have one to another, as ye walk, and are sad?  
**Verse:** Luke 24:17

---

### Luke 24:18

**Q1. What was the name of the one answering who asked if Jesus was a stranger in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Cleopas.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:18

---

### Luke 24:19

**Q1. What did Jesus ask them regarding the things concerning Jesus of Nazareth?**  
**Answer:** What things?  
**Verse:** Luke 24:19

---

### Luke 24:20

**Q1. Who delivered Him to be condemned to death?**  
**Answer:** The chief priests and our rulers.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:20

---

### Luke 24:21

**Q1. What had they trusted that He should have redeemed?**  
**Answer:** Israel.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:21

---

### Luke 24:22

**Q1. Who made them astonished, having been early at the sepulchre?**  
**Answer:** Certain women of our company.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:22

---

### Luke 24:23

**Q1. When they found not His body, what did they come saying they had seen?**  
**Answer:** A vision of angels, which said that he was alive.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:23

---

### Luke 24:24

**Q1. Who went to the sepulchre and found it even so as the women had said?**  
**Answer:** Certain of them which were with us.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:24

---

### Luke 24:25

**Q1. What did Jesus call them for being slow of heart to believe all that the prophets have spoken?**  
**Answer:** O fools, and slow of heart to believe all that the prophets have spoken.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:25

---

### Luke 24:26

**Q1. What did Christ need to suffer according to verse 26?**  
**Answer:** These things.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:26

---

### Luke 24:27

**Q1. Beginning at Moses and all the prophets, what did Jesus expound unto them?**  
**Answer:** In all the scriptures the things concerning himself.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:27

---

### Luke 24:28

**Q1. As they drew nigh unto the village, what did Jesus make as though He would have done?**  
**Answer:** He would have gone further.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:28

---

### Luke 24:29

**Q1. What did they constrain Him to do?**  
**Answer:** Abide with us.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:29

---

### Luke 24:30

**Q1. What did Jesus do when He sat at meat with them?**  
**Answer:** Took bread, and blessed it, and brake, and gave to them.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:30

---

### Luke 24:31

**Q1. What happened to their eyes when the bread was given?**  
**Answer:** Their eyes were opened, and they knew him.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:31

---

### Luke 24:32

**Q1. What did they say to one another regarding their hearts burning within them?**  
**Answer:** Did not our heart burn within us, while he talked with us by the way, and while he opened to us the scriptures?  
**Verse:** Luke 24:32

---

### Luke 24:33

**Q1. What did they do the very same hour, returning to Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Rose up, and returned to Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:33

---

### Luke 24:34

**Q1. What did the eleven and those with them say?**  
**Answer:** The Lord is risen indeed, and hath appeared to Simon.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:34

---

### Luke 24:35

**Q1. What did the two tell about in the way, and how He was known of them?**  
**Answer:** The things that were done in the way, and how he was known of them in breaking of bread.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:35

---

### Luke 24:36

**Q1. As they thus spoke, who stood in the midst of them?**  
**Answer:** Jesus himself.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:36

---

### Luke 24:37

**Q1. As they were terrified and affrighted, what did they suppose they had seen?**  
**Answer:** A spirit.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:37

---

### Luke 24:38

**Q1. What did Jesus ask why they were and why thoughts arose in their hearts?**  
**Answer:** Why are ye troubled? and why do thoughts arise in your hearts?  
**Verse:** Luke 24:38

---

### Luke 24:39

**Q1. What did Jesus tell them to handle and see?**  
**Answer:** My hands and my feet.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:39

---

### Luke 24:40

**Q1. When He had thus spoken, what did He show them?**  
**Answer:** His hands and his feet.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:40

---

### Luke 24:41

**Q1. While they yet believed not for joy, and wondered, what did Jesus ask them?**  
**Answer:** Have ye here any meat?  
**Verse:** Luke 24:41

---

### Luke 24:42

**Q1. What did they give Him?**  
**Answer:** A piece of a broiled fish, and of an honeycomb.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:42

---

### Luke 24:43

**Q1. What did He take and eat before them?**  
**Answer:** It.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:43

---

### Luke 24:44

**Q1. What did Jesus say must be fulfilled, which were written in the law of Moses, and in the prophets, and in the psalms concerning Him?**  
**Answer:** All things.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:44

---

### Luke 24:45

**Q1. What did He open that they might understand the scriptures?**  
**Answer:** Their understanding.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:45

---

### Luke 24:46

**Q1. According to what was written, what was necessary for Christ to do?**  
**Answer:** To suffer, and to rise from the dead the third day.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:46

---

### Luke 24:47

**Q1. What should be preached in His name among all nations?**  
**Answer:** Repentance and remission of sins.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:47

---

### Luke 24:48

**Q1. Of what things are these people witnesses?**  
**Answer:** These things.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:48

---

### Luke 24:49

**Q1. What did Jesus say He sends upon them, promising to tarry in the city of Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The promise of my Father.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:49

---

### Luke 24:50

**Q1. Whither did He lead them out as far as?**  
**Answer:** To Bethany.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:50

---

### Luke 24:51

**Q1. While He blessed them, what happened to Him?**  
**Answer:** He was parted from them, and carried up into heaven.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:51

---

### Luke 24:52

**Q1. What did they do after worshipping Him?**  
**Answer:** Returned to Jerusalem with great joy.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:52

---

### Luke 24:53

**Q1. Where were they continually, praising and blessing God?**  
**Answer:** In the temple.  
**Verse:** Luke 24:53
