### Luke 23:1

**Q1. Who arose and led Jesus unto Pilate?**  
**Answer:** The whole multitude of them.  
**Verse:** Luke 23:1

**Q2. Where did the multitude lead Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Unto Pilate.  
**Verse:** Luke 23:1

**Q3. What did the whole multitude do after arising?**  
**Answer:** Led him unto Pilate.  
**Verse:** Luke 23:1
