### Luke 16:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Luke 16:1?**  
**Answer:** And he said also unto his disciples, There was a certain rich man, which had a steward.  
**Verse:** Luke 16:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Luke 16:1?**  
**Answer:** and the same was accused unto him that he had wasted his goods.
