# Luke 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Luke 3:1
**Q1. According to Luke 3:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Now in the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar.  
**Verse:** Luke 3:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Luke 3:1?**  
**Answer:** Pontius Pilate being governor of Judaea.  
**Verse:** Luke 3:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Luke 3:1?**  
**Answer:** and Herod being tetrarch of Galilee, and his brother Philip tetrarch of Ituraea and of the region of Trachonitis, and Lysanias the tetrarch of Abilene.  
**Verse:** Luke 3:1
