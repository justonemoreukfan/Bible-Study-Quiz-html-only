# Malachi 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Malachi 3:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Malachi 3:1?**  
**Answer:** Behold, I will send my messenger, and he shall prepare the way before me.  
**Verse:** Malachi 3:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Malachi 3:1?**  
**Answer:** and the LORD, whom ye seek, shall suddenly come to his temple, even the messenger of the covenant, whom ye delight in.  
**Verse:** Malachi 3:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Malachi 3:1?**  
**Answer:** behold, he shall come, saith the LORD of hosts.  
**Verse:** Malachi 3:1
