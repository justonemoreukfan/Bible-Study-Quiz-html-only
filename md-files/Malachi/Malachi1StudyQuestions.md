### Malachi 1:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Malachi 1:2?**  
**Answer:** I have loved you, saith the LORD. Yet ye say, Wherein hast thou loved us? Was not Esau Jacob’s brother? saith the LORD.  
**Verse:** Malachi 1:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Malachi 1:2?**  
**Answer:** yet I loved Jacob.  
**Verse:** Malachi 1:2

**Q6. According to Malachi 1:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** I have loved you, saith the LORD. Yet ye say, Wherein hast thou loved us? Was not Esau Jacob’s brother? saith the LORD: yet I loved Jacob,.  
**Verse:** Malachi 1:2

### Malachi 1:6
**Q16. What is stated in the first clause of Malachi 1:6?**  
**Answer:** A son honoureth his father, and a servant his master.  
**Verse:** Malachi 1:6

**Q17. What additional detail is declared in Malachi 1:6?**  
**Answer:** if then I be a father, where is mine honour? and if I be a master, where is my fear? saith the LORD of hosts unto you, O priests, that despise my name. And ye say, Wherein have we despised thy name?  
**Verse:** Malachi 1:6

**Q18. According to Malachi 1:6, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** A son honoureth his father, and a servant his master: if then I be a father, where is mine honour? and if I be a master, where is my fear? saith the LORD of hosts unto you, O priests, that despise my name. And ye say, Wherein have we despised thy name?  
**Verse:** Malachi 1:6
