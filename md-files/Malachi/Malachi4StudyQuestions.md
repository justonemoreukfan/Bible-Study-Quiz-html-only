# Malachi 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Malachi 4:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Malachi 4:1?**  
**Answer:** For, behold, the day cometh, that shall burn as an oven.  
**Verse:** Malachi 4:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Malachi 4:1?**  
**Answer:** and all the proud, yea, and all that do wickedly, shall be stubble.  
**Verse:** Malachi 4:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Malachi 4:1?**  
**Answer:** and the day that cometh shall burn them up, saith the LORD of hosts, that it shall leave them neither root nor branch.  
**Verse:** Malachi 4:1

---

### Malachi 4:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Malachi 4:2?**  
**Answer:** But unto you that fear my name shall the Sun of righteousness arise with healing in his wings.  
**Verse:** Malachi 4:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Malachi 4:2?**  
**Answer:** and ye shall go forth, and grow up as calves of the stall.  
**Verse:** Malachi 4:2

**Q6. According to Malachi 4:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** But unto you that fear my name shall the Sun of righteousness arise with healing in his wings; and ye shall go forth, and grow up as calves of the stall.  
**Verse:** Malachi 4:2

---

### Malachi 4:3
**Q7. What is stated in the first clause of Malachi 4:3?**

**Verse:** Malachi 4:3

**Q8. What additional detail is declared in Malachi 4:3?**  
**Answer:** for they shall be ashes under the soles of your feet in the day that I shall do this, saith the LORD of hosts.  
**Verse:** Malachi 4:3

**Q9. According to Malachi 4:3, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And ye shall tread down the wicked; for they shall be ashes under the soles of your feet in the day that I shall do this, saith the LORD of hosts.  
**Verse:** Malachi 4:3
