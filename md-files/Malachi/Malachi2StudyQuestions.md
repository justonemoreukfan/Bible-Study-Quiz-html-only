# Malachi 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Malachi 2:1
**Q1. According to Malachi 2:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And now.  
**Verse:** Malachi 2:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Malachi 2:1?**  
**Answer:** O ye priests.  
**Verse:** Malachi 2:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Malachi 2:1?**  
**Answer:** this commandment is for you.  
**Verse:** Malachi 2:1

### Malachi 2:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Malachi 2:2?**  
**Answer:** If ye will not hear, and if ye will not lay it to heart, to give glory unto my name, saith the LORD of hosts, I will even send a curse upon you, and I will curse your blessings.  
**Verse:** Malachi 2:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Malachi 2:2?**  
**Answer:** yea, I have cursed them already, because ye do not lay it to heart.  
**Verse:** Malachi 2:2

**Q6. According to Malachi 2:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** If ye will not hear, and if ye will not lay it to heart, to give glory unto my name, saith the LORD of hosts, I will even send a curse upon you, and I will curse your blessings: yea, I have cursed them already, because ye do not lay it to heart.  
**Verse:** Malachi 2:2

**Q51. According to Malachi 2:17, what is the full truth recorded?**
