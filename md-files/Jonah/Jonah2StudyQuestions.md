# Jonah 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jonah 2:1
**Q1. What is declared in Jonah 2:1?**  
**Answer:** Then Jonah prayed unto the LORD his God out of the fish’s belly,.  
**Verse:** Jonah 2:1

**Q2. According to Jonah 2:1, what truth is stated?**  
**Answer:** Then Jonah prayed unto the LORD his God out of the fish’s belly,.  
**Verse:** Jonah 2:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Jonah 2:1?**  
**Answer:** Then Jonah prayed unto the LORD his God out of the fish’s belly,.  
**Verse:** Jonah 2:1

---

### Jonah 2:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Jonah 2:2?**  
**Answer:** And said, I cried by reason of mine affliction unto the LORD, and he heard me.  
**Verse:** Jonah 2:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Jonah 2:2?**  
**Answer:** out of the belly of hell cried I, and thou heardest my voice.  
**Verse:** Jonah 2:2

**Q6. According to Jonah 2:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And said, I cried by reason of mine affliction unto the LORD, and he heard me; out of the belly of hell cried I, and thou heardest my voice.  
**Verse:** Jonah 2:2

### Jonah 2:3

**Verse:** Jonah 2:10

**Answer:** And the LORD spake unto the fish, and it vomited out Jonah upon the dry land.
