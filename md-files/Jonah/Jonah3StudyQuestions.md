### Jonah 3:7
**Q19. What is stated in the first clause of Jonah 3:7?**  
**Answer:** And he caused it to be proclaimed and published through Nineveh by the decree of the king and his nobles, saying, Let neither man nor beast, herd nor flock, taste any thing.  
**Verse:** Jonah 3:7

**Q20. What additional detail is declared in Jonah 3:7?**  
**Answer:** let them not feed, nor drink water.  
**Verse:** Jonah 3:7

**Q21. According to Jonah 3:7, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And he caused it to be proclaimed and published through Nineveh by the decree of the king and his nobles, saying, Let neither man nor beast, herd nor flock, taste any thing: let them not feed, nor drink water:.  
**Verse:** Jonah 3:7
