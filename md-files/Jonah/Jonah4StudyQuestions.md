# Jonah 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Jonah 4:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Jonah 4:1 state?**  
**Answer:** But it displeased Jonah exceedingly.  
**Verse:** Jonah 4:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Jonah 4:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** and he was very angry.  
**Verse:** Jonah 4:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Jonah 4:1?**  
**Answer:** But it displeased Jonah exceedingly, and he was very angry.  
**Verse:** Jonah 4:1

---

### Jonah 4:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Jonah 4:2?**  
**Answer:** And he prayed unto the LORD, and said, I pray thee, O LORD, was not this my saying, when I was yet in my country? Therefore I fled before unto Tarshish.  
**Verse:** Jonah 4:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Jonah 4:2?**  
**Answer:** for I knew that thou art a gracious God, and merciful, slow to anger, and of great kindness, and repentest thee of the evil.  
**Verse:** Jonah 4:2

**Q6. According to Jonah 4:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And he prayed unto the LORD, and said, I pray thee, O LORD, was not this my saying, when I was yet in my country? Therefore I fled before unto Tarshish: for I knew that thou art a gracious God, and merciful, slow to anger, and of great kindness, and repentest thee of the evil.  
**Verse:** Jonah 4:2

### Jonah 4:5
**Q13. According to Jonah 4:5, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** So Jonah went out of the city.  
**Verse:** Jonah 4:5

**Q14. What is declared next in Jonah 4:5?**  
**Answer:** and sat on the east side of the city.  
**Verse:** Jonah 4:5

**Q15. What further truth is given in Jonah 4:5?**  
**Answer:** and there made him a booth, and sat under it in the shadow, till he might see what would become of the city.  
**Verse:** Jonah 4:5

### Jonah 4:6
**Q16. According to Jonah 4:6, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And the LORD God prepared a gourd.  
**Verse:** Jonah 4:6

**Q17. What is declared next in Jonah 4:6?**  
**Answer:** and made it to come up over Jonah.  
**Verse:** Jonah 4:6

**Q18. What further truth is given in Jonah 4:6?**  
**Answer:** that it might be a shadow over his head, to deliver him from his grief. So Jonah was exceeding glad of the gourd.  
**Verse:** Jonah 4:6

### Jonah 4:7
**Q19. What does the first part of Jonah 4:7 state?**  
**Answer:** But God prepared a worm when the morning rose the next day.  
**Verse:** Jonah 4:7

**Q20. What does the second part of Jonah 4:7 declare?**  
**Answer:** and it smote the gourd that it withered.  
**Verse:** Jonah 4:7

**Q21. What is recorded in Jonah 4:7?**  
**Answer:** But God prepared a worm when the morning rose the next day, and it smote the gourd that it withered.  
**Verse:** Jonah 4:7

---

### Jonah 4:8
**Q22. What is stated in the first clause of Jonah 4:8?**  
**Answer:** And it came to pass, when the sun did arise, that God prepared a vehement east wind.  
**Verse:** Jonah 4:8

**Q23. What additional detail is declared in Jonah 4:8?**  
**Answer:** and the sun beat upon the head of Jonah, that he fainted, and wished in himself to die, and said, It is better for me to die than to live.  
**Verse:** Jonah 4:8

**Q24. According to Jonah 4:8, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And it came to pass, when the sun did arise, that God prepared a vehement east wind; and the sun beat upon the head of Jonah, that he fainted, and wished in himself to die, and said, It is better for me to die than to live.  
**Verse:** Jonah 4:8

**Q32. What additional detail is declared in Jonah 4:11?**

**Q33. According to Jonah 4:11, what is the full truth recorded?**
