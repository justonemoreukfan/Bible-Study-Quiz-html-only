# Philippians 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Philippians 2:1

**Q1. What is mentioned first if there be any consolation in Christ?**  
**Answer:** If there be any consolation in Christ.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:1

**Q2. What is mentioned after any consolation in Christ?**  
**Answer:** If any comfort of love.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:1

**Q3. What fellowship is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** If any fellowship of the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:1

---

### Philippians 2:2

**Q1. What does Paul ask the Philippians to fulfill?**  
**Answer:** Fulfil ye my joy.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:2

**Q2. What should they be regarding their mindset?**  
**Answer:** That ye be likeminded.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:2

**Q3. What should they be of?**  
**Answer:** Of one accord, of one mind.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:2

---

### Philippians 2:3

**Q1. What should be avoided in doing?**  
**Answer:** Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:3

**Q2. How should one regard others in lowliness of mind?**  
**Answer:** In lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:3

**Q3. Whose state of mind should be characterized by lowliness?**  
**Answer:** Each.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:3

---

### Philippians 2:4

**Q1. What should every man not look upon?**  
**Answer:** Look not every man on his own things.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:4

**Q2. Whose things should every man also look upon?**  
**Answer:** Every man also on the things of others.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:4

**Q3. Who is commanded to look on these things?**  
**Answer:** Every man.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:4

---

### Philippians 2:5

**Q1. What kind of mind should be in you?**  
**Answer:** Let this mind be in you.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:5

**Q2. Whose mind is this which was also in Christ Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:5

**Q3. In whom should this mind be?**  
**Answer:** In you.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:5

---

### Philippians 2:6

**Q1. In what form did He exist?**  
**Answer:** Who, being in the form of God.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:6

**Q2. What did He not think robbery?**  
**Answer:** Thought it not robbery.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:6

**Q3. What was He equal with?**  
**Answer:** To be equal with God.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:6

---

### Philippians 2:7

**Q1. Of what did He make Himself of no reputation?**  
**Answer:** Made himself of no reputation.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:7

**Q2. What form did He take upon Him?**  
**Answer:** Took upon him the form of a servant.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:7

**Q3. In what likeness was He made?**  
**Answer:** Was made in the likeness of men.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:7

---

### Philippians 2:8

**Q1. In what fashion was He found?**  
**Answer:** And being found in fashion as a man.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:8

**Q2. What did He humble Himself unto?**  
**Answer:** He humbled himself.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:8

**Q3. Unto what kind of death did He become obedient?**  
**Answer:** Obedient unto death, even the death of the cross.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:8

---

### Philippians 2:9

**Q1. What did God also do to Him?**  
**Answer:** God also hath highly exalted him.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:9

**Q2. What did God give Him?**  
**Answer:** Given him a name.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:9

**Q3. How does this name compare to every other name?**  
**Answer:** Which is above every name.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:9

---

### Philippians 2:10

**Q1. At what name should every knee bow?**  
**Answer:** That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:10

**Q2. Where are those whose knees should bow of things in heaven?**  
**Answer:** Of things in heaven.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:10

**Q3. What other two realms are mentioned where knees should bow?**  
**Answer:** Of things in earth, and things under the earth.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:10

---

### Philippians 2:11

**Q1. What should every tongue confess?**  
**Answer:** That every tongue should confess.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:11

**Q2. What is the confession about Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** That Jesus Christ is Lord.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:11

**Q3. To whose glory is this confession made?**  
**Answer:** To the glory of God the Father.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:11

---

### Philippians 2:12

**Q1. How have they always obeyed?**  
**Answer:** As ye have always obeyed.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:12

**Q2. Whose presence is mentioned regarding this obedience?**  
**Answer:** Not in my presence only.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:12

**Q3. How should they work out their own salvation?**  
**Answer:** Work out your own salvation with fear and trembling.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:12

---

### Philippians 2:13

**Q1. Who is it that worketh in you?**  
**Answer:** For it is God which worketh in you.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:13

**Q2. What does God work in you to do?**  
**Answer:** To will.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:13

**Q3. What else does God work in you to do of his good pleasure?**  
**Answer:** To do of his good pleasure.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:13

---

### Philippians 2:14

**Q1. What should be done without murmurings?**  
**Answer:** Do all things without murmurings.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:14

**Q2. What else should be avoided alongside murmurings?**  
**Answer:** And disputings.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:14

**Q3. How many things should be done without these?**  
**Answer:** All things.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:14

---

### Philippians 2:15

**Q1. How should they be blameless and harmless?**  
**Answer:** That ye may be blameless and harmless.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:15

**Q2. Whose children should they be without rebuke?**  
**Answer:** The sons of God, without rebuke.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:15

**Q3. In the midst of what kind of nation should they shine?**  
**Answer:** In the midst of a crooked and perverse nation.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:15

---

### Philippians 2:16

**Q1. What should they hold forth?**  
**Answer:** Holding forth the word of life.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:16

**Q2. For what day could Paul rejoice that he had not run in vain?**  
**Answer:** That I may rejoice in the day of Christ.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:16

**Q3. What did Paul say he had not done in vain regarding his labor?**  
**Answer:** That I have not run in vain, neither laboured in vain.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:16

---

### Philippians 2:17

**Q1. Upon what sacrifice and service is Paul offered?**  
**Answer:** If I be offered upon the sacrifice and service of your faith.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:17

**Q2. How does Paul feel about this offering?**  
**Answer:** I joy.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:17

**Q3. With whom does Paul joy?**  
**Answer:** And rejoice with you all.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:17

---

### Philippians 2:18

**Q1. What should the Philippians also do?**  
**Answer:** For the same cause also do ye joy.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:18

**Q2. With whom should they joy?**  
**Answer:** And rejoice with me.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:18

**Q3. What is the reason they should joy?**  
**Answer:** For the same cause.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:18

---

### Philippians 2:19

**Q1. In whom does the Lord Jesus trust to send Timothy shortly?**  
**Answer:** But I trust in the Lord Jesus to send Timotheus shortly unto you.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:19

**Q2. Why does Paul want to send Timothy?**  
**Answer:** That I also may be of good comfort.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:19

**Q3. What does Paul desire to know when he sends Timothy?**  
**Answer:** When I know your state.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:19

---

**Answer:** For I have no man likeminded.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:20

**Q2. How will Timothy care for their state?**  
**Answer:** Who will naturally care for your state.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:20

**Q3. What does Paul say about having another man like him?**  
**Answer:** I have no man likeminded.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:20

**Q1. What do all men seek?**  
**Answer:** For all seek their own.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:21

**Q2. Whose things do they not seek?**  
**Answer:** Not the things which are Jesus Christ's.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:21

**Q3. How many men seek their own?**

**Verse:** Philippians 2:21

### Philippians 2:22

**Q1. What do the Philippians know about Timothy?**  
**Answer:** But ye know the proof of him.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:22

**Q2. As what kind of child has Timothy served with Paul?**  
**Answer:** That, as a son with the father.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:22

**Q3. In what work did Timothy serve with Paul?**  
**Answer:** He hath served with me in the gospel.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:22

### Philippians 2:23

**Q1. Whom does Paul hope to send presently?**  
**Answer:** Him therefore I hope to send presently.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:23

**Q2. When does Paul hope to send him?**  
**Answer:** So soon as I shall see how it will go with me.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:23

**Q3. Whose situation determines the timing?**  
**Answer:** Mine.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:23

### Philippians 2:24

**Q1. In whom does Paul trust regarding his own coming?**  
**Answer:** But I trust in the Lord.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:24

**Q2. What does Paul trust that he also shall do shortly?**  
**Answer:** That I also myself shall come shortly.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:24

**Q3. Who will come shortly?**  
**Answer:** I also myself.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:24

---

### Philippians 2:25

**Q1. Who did Paul suppose it necessary to send to you?**  
**Answer:** Yet I supposed it necessary to send to you Epaphroditus.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:25

**Q2. What three terms does Paul use to describe Epaphroditus?**  
**Answer:** My brother, and companion in labour, and fellowsoldier.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:25

**Q3. Who sent Epaphroditus to minister to Paul's wants?**  
**Answer:** Your messenger, and he that ministered to my wants.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:25

---

### Philippians 2:26

**Q1. For whom did Epaphroditus long?**  
**Answer:** For he longed after you all.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:26

**Q2. How did Epaphroditus feel because they had heard he had been sick?**  
**Answer:** And was full of heaviness.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:26

**Q3. What did the Philippians hear about Epaphroditus?**  
**Answer:** That he had been sick.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:26

---

### Philippians 2:27

**Q1. How sick was Epaphroditus?**  
**Answer:** For indeed he was sick nigh unto death.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:27

**Q2. Who had mercy on Epaphroditus?**  
**Answer:** But God had mercy on him.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:27

**Q3. Upon whom else did God have mercy besides Epaphroditus?**  
**Answer:** Not on him only, but on me also.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:27

---

**Q1. How much more carefully did Paul send Epaphroditus?**  
**Answer:** I sent him therefore the more carefully.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:28

**Q2. What would the Philippians do when they saw him again?**  
**Answer:** That, when ye see him again, ye may rejoice.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:28

**Q3. How would seeing Epaphroditus again affect Paul's sorrow?**  
**Answer:** And that I may be the less sorrowful.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:28

---

### Philippians 2:29

**Answer:** Receive him therefore in the Lord with all gladness.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:29

**Q2. With what kind of gladness should they receive him?**  
**Answer:** With all gladness.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:29

**Q3. How should such men be held?**  
**Answer:** And hold such in reputation.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:29

---

### Philippians 2:30

**Q1. For the work of whom was he nigh unto death?**  
**Answer:** Because for the work of Christ he was nigh unto death.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:30

**Q2. What did Epaphroditus regard not?**  
**Answer:** Not regarding his life.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:30

**Q3. What was lacking on their part that Epaphroditus supplied?**  
**Answer:** To supply your lack of service toward me.  
**Verse:** Philippians 2:30
