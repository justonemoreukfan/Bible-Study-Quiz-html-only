# Philippians 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Philippians 1:1

**Q1. Who are the servants mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Paul and Timotheus.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:1

**Q2. Whose servants are Paul and Timotheus?**  
**Answer:** The servants of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:1

**Q3. To whom is the epistle sent?**  
**Answer:** To all the saints in Christ Jesus which are at Philippi, with the bishops and deacons.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:1

---

### Philippians 1:2

**Q4. What is wished upon the recipients from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** Grace be unto you, and peace.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:2

**Q5. From whom does grace and peace come?**  
**Answer:** From God our Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:2

**Q6. Whose grace and peace is referenced regarding the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** Grace be unto you, and peace, from God our Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:2

---

**Q15. In what do they have fellowship?**

**Verse:** Philippians 1:5

### Philippians 1:6

**Q16. Of what is the writer confident?**  
**Answer:** Being confident of this very thing.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:6

**Q17. Who hath begun a good work in you?**  
**Answer:** He which hath begun a good work in you.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:6

**Q18. Until when will He perform the good work?**  
**Answer:** Until the day of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:6

---

### Philippians 1:7

**Q19. Why is it meet for the writer to think this of them all?**  
**Answer:** Because I have you in my heart.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:7

**Q20. What do they share with the writer in his bonds?**  
**Answer:** Inasmuch as both in my bonds, and in the defence and confirmation of the gospel, ye all are partakers of my grace.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:7

**Q21. Of what are they all partakers together with the writer?**  
**Answer:** Of my grace.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:7

---

### Philippians 1:8

**Q22. Who is the record for the writer's feelings?**  
**Answer:** For God is my record.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:8

**Q23. How greatly does the writer long after them all?**  
**Answer:** How greatly I long after you all.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:8

**Q24. In whose affection does the writer long after them?**  
**Answer:** In the bowels of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:8

---

### Philippians 1:9

**Q25. What does the writer pray that their love may abound yet more and more?**  
**Answer:** That your love may abound yet more and more.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:9

**Q26. In what two things should their love abound?**  
**Answer:** In knowledge and in all judgment.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:9

---

### Philippians 1:10

**Q27. What does the writer pray they may approve?**  
**Answer:** That ye may approve things that are excellent.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:10

**Q28. How are they to be until the day of Christ?**  
**Answer:** That ye may be sincere and without offence till the day of Christ.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:10

**Q29. Until what specific time are they to remain sincere and without offence?**  
**Answer:** Till the day of Christ.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:10

---

### Philippians 1:11

**Q30. With what are they to be filled?**  
**Answer:** Being filled with the fruits of righteousness.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:11

**Q31. By whom are the fruits of righteousness produced?**  
**Answer:** By Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:11

**Q32. What is the ultimate purpose of being filled with the fruits of righteousness?**  
**Answer:** Unto the glory and praise of God.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:11

---

### Philippians 1:12

**Q33. What does the writer want the brethren to understand?**  
**Answer:** But I would ye should understand, brethren.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:12

**Q34. What have the things concerning the writer actually done?**  
**Answer:** That the things which happened unto me have fallen out rather unto the furtherance of the gospel.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:12

**Q35. To what have the writer's circumstances fallen out?**  
**Answer:** Unto the furtherance of the gospel.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:12

**Answer:** So that my bonds in Christ are manifest.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:13

**Q37. To whom are his bonds in Christ manifest?**  
**Answer:** In all the palace, and in all other places.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:13

**Q38. In whom are his bonds held or recognized?**  
**Answer:** In Christ.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:13

---

### Philippians 1:14

**Q39. Who has grown more confident by the writer's bonds?**  
**Answer:** And many of the brethren in the Lord.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:14

**Q40. What did the brethren become through the writer's bonds?**  
**Answer:** Waxing confident by my bonds.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:14

**Q41. How are the brethren described as speaking the word?**  
**Answer:** Are much more bold to speak the word without fear.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:14

---

### Philippians 1:15

**Q42. How do some preach Christ, according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Some indeed preach Christ even of envy and strife.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:15

**Q43. How do others preach Christ?**  
**Answer:** And some also of good will.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:15

**Q44. What specific motivations are listed for the first group preaching Christ?**  
**Answer:** Envy and strife.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:15

---

### Philippians 1:16

**Q45. Why do the one preach Christ of contention?**  
**Answer:** The one preach Christ of contention, not sincerely.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:16

**Q46. How do they intend to add to the writer's bonds?**  
**Answer:** Supposing to add affliction to my bonds.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:16

**Q47. How are they described as preaching rather than sincerely?**  
**Answer:** Not sincerely, supposing to add affliction to my bonds.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:16

---

**Q48. Why do the other preach Christ of love?**

**Verse:** Philippians 1:17

**Q49. What does the writer know regarding his situation?**  
**Answer:** Knowing that I am set for the defence of the gospel.

**Q50. For what is the writer set?**  
**Answer:** For the defence of the gospel.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:17

### Philippians 1:18

**Q51. In what way is Christ preached?**  
**Answer:** What then? notwithstanding, every way, whether in pretence, or in truth, Christ is preached.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:18

**Q52. How does the writer feel about Christ being preached?**  
**Answer:** And I therein do rejoice, yea, and will rejoice.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:18

**Q53. What will the writer continue to do regarding this?**  
**Answer:** Yea, and will rejoice.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:18

**Q54. What does the writer know this shall turn to?**  
**Answer:** For I know that this shall turn to my salvation.

**Q55. Through what means does he know this will turn to his salvation?**  
**Answer:** Through your prayer.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:19

**Answer:** And the supply of the Spirit of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:19

---

### Philippians 1:20

**Q57. According to his earnest expectation and hope, what will happen?**  
**Answer:** That in nothing I shall be ashamed.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:20

**Q58. How will Christ be magnified in his body?**  
**Answer:** But that with all boldness, as always, so now also Christ shall be magnified in my body.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:20

**Q59. By what two methods might Christ be magnified in his body?**  
**Answer:** Whether it be by life, or by death.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:20

### Philippians 1:21

**Q60. To the writer, what is life?**  
**Answer:** For to me to live is Christ.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:21

**Q61. To the writer, what is death?**  
**Answer:** And to die is gain.  
**Verse:** Philippians 1:21

**Q62. Whose life is referenced as the writer's living?**

**Verse:** Philippians 1:21
