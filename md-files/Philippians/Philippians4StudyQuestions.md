# Philippians 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Philippians 4:1

**Q1. How does Paul address his brethren in this verse?**  
**Answer:** My brethren dearly beloved and longed for, my joy and crown.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:1

**Q2. What are the brethren told to do in the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Stand fast in the Lord.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:1

**Q3. How does Paul specifically refer to his dearly beloved?**  
**Answer:** My dearly beloved.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:1

---

### Philippians 4:2

**Q1. Who is besought by Paul in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Euodias, and beseech Syntyche.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:2

**Q2. What are these two persons exhorted to be of?**  
**Answer:** Of the same mind.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:2

**Q3. Where are they told to be of the same mind?**  
**Answer:** In the Lord.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:2

---

### Philippians 4:3

**Q1. How is the true yokefellow addressed in this verse?**  
**Answer:** True yokefellow.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:3

**Q2. Who labored with Paul in the gospel?**  
**Answer:** Those women which laboured with me in the gospel.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:3

**Q3. Whose names are written in the book of life?**  
**Answer:** Clement also, and with other my fellowlabourers, whose names are in the book of life.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:3

---

### Philippians 4:4

**Q1. Where are the recipients told to rejoice?**  
**Answer:** In the Lord.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:4

**Q2. How often are they told to rejoice?**  
**Answer:** Alway.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:4

**Q3. What does Paul say again regarding rejoicing?**  
**Answer:** And again I say, Rejoice.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:4

---

### Philippians 4:5

**Q1. What is to be known unto all men?**  
**Answer:** Your moderation.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:5

**Q2. What is said about the Lord?**  
**Answer:** The Lord is at hand.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:5

**Q3. Whose moderation is to be known unto all men?**  
**Answer:** Your moderation.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:5

---

### Philippians 4:6

**Q1. What are the brethren told to be about?**  
**Answer:** Be careful for nothing.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:6

**Q2. In what manner should requests be made known unto God?**  
**Answer:** By prayer and supplication with thanksgiving.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:6

**Q3. What should be made known unto God in every thing?**  
**Answer:** Your requests.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:6

---

### Philippians 4:7

**Q1. What passes all understanding?**  
**Answer:** The peace of God.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:7

**Q2. What will the peace of God keep?**  
**Answer:** Your hearts and minds.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:7

**Q3. Through whom will the peace of God keep hearts and minds?**  
**Answer:** Through Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:7

---

### Philippians 4:8

**Q1. What kind of things are mentioned first in the list?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever things are true.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:8

**Q2. What kind of report is to be thought on?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever things are of good report.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:8

**Q3. What are the brethren told to do regarding these things?**  
**Answer:** Think on these things.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:8

---

### Philippians 4:9

**Q1. What things are the brethren told to do?**  
**Answer:** Those things, which ye have both learned, and received, and heard, and seen in me.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:9

**Q2. Who shall be with them if they do these things?**  
**Answer:** The God of peace.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:9

**Q3. What did the brethren do with the things learned and received?**  
**Answer:** Learned, and received, and heard, and seen in me, do.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:9

---

### Philippians 4:10

**Q1. In whom did the brethren's care of Paul flourish again?**  
**Answer:** In the Lord.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:10

**Q2. What were the brethren careful of, though lacked opportunity?**  
**Answer:** Wherein ye were also careful, but ye lacked opportunity.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:10

**Q3. How greatly did Paul rejoice?**  
**Answer:** I rejoiced in the Lord greatly.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:10

---

### Philippians 4:11

**Q1. Why does Paul speak in respect of want?**  
**Answer:** Not that I speak in respect of want.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:11

**Q2. What has Paul learned in whatsoever state he is?**  
**Answer:** To be content.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:11

**Q3. What kind of state has Paul learned to be content in?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever state I am.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:11

---

### Philippians 4:12

**Q1. What does Paul know how to be?**  
**Answer:** Both to be abased, and I know how to abound.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:12

**Q2. What has Paul been instructed in everywhere and in all things?**  
**Answer:** Both to be full and to be hungry, both to abound and to suffer need.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:12

**Q3. What do both to be full and to be hungry refer to?**  
**Answer:** Everywhere and in all things.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:12

---

### Philippians 4:13

**Q1. What can Paul do through Christ?**  
**Answer:** All things.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:13

**Q2. Through whom does Paul strengthen himself to do all things?**  
**Answer:** Through Christ.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:13

**Q3. What does Christ do for Paul?**  
**Answer:** Which strengtheneth me.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:13

---

### Philippians 4:14

**Q1. What did the brethren well to do?**  
**Answer:** Ye have well done.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:14

**Q2. In what did the brethren communicate with Paul?**  
**Answer:** That ye did communicate with my affliction.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:14

**Q3. Whose affliction did they communicate with?**  
**Answer:** My affliction.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:14

---

### Philippians 4:15

**Q1. Who knows that in the beginning of the gospel Philippians gave and received?**  
**Answer:** Now ye Philippians know also.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:15

**Q2. When did the church communicate with Paul as concerning giving and receiving?**  
**Answer:** In the beginning of the gospel.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:15

**Q3. Who was the only church that communicated with Paul in this manner?**  
**Answer:** No church communicated with me as concerning giving and receiving, but ye only.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:15

---

### Philippians 4:16

**Q1. Where did the brethren send once and again unto Paul's necessity?**  
**Answer:** Even in Thessalonica.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:16

**Q2. How often did they send unto Paul's necessity in Thessalonica?**  
**Answer:** Once and again.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:16

**Q3. Whose necessity did they send unto?**  
**Answer:** My necessity.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:16

---

### Philippians 4:17

**Q1. What does Paul not desire?**  
**Answer:** Not because I desire a gift.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:17

**Q2. What does Paul desire regarding the brethren?**  
**Answer:** But I desire fruit that may abound to your account.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:17

**Q3. To whose account may fruit abound?**  
**Answer:** To your account.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:17

---

### Philippians 4:18

**Q1. What does Paul have and abound in?**  
**Answer:** I have all, and abound.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:18

**Q2. From whom was the thing sent by Epaphroditus received?**  
**Answer:** The things which were sent from you.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:18

**Q3. How is the odour of the sacrifice described?**  
**Answer:** An odour of a sweet smell, a sacrifice acceptable, wellpleasing to God.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:18

---

### Philippians 4:19

**Q1. What will God supply according to His riches?**  
**Answer:** Shall supply all your need.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:19

**Q2. According to what does God supply all need?**  
**Answer:** According to his riches in glory.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:19

**Q3. By whom does God supply all your need?**  
**Answer:** By Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:19

---

### Philippians 4:20

**Q1. Who is given glory for ever and ever?**  
**Answer:** Now unto God and our Father be glory.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:20

**Q2. For how long is glory ascribed to God and our Father?**  
**Answer:** For ever and ever.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:20

**Q3. What word concludes the verse?**  
**Answer:** Amen.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:20

---

### Philippians 4:21

**Q1. Who is to be saluted?**  
**Answer:** Every saint in Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:21

**Q2. Who salutes the brethren with Paul?**  
**Answer:** The brethren which are with me.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:21

**Q3. In whom are the saints located?**  
**Answer:** In Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:21

---

### Philippians 4:22

**Q1. Who salutes the brethren alongside all the saints?**  
**Answer:** All the saints salute you.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:22

**Q2. Specially, who else salutes them?**  
**Answer:** Chiefly they that are of Caesar's household.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:22

**Q3. Whose household are some of the saints from?**  
**Answer:** Caesar's household.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:22

---

### Philippians 4:23

**Q1. Whose grace is invoked with you all?**  
**Answer:** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:23

**Q2. With whom is the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ desired to be?**  
**Answer:** With you all.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:23

**Q3. What word concludes the chapter?**  
**Answer:** Amen.  
**Verse:** Philippians 4:23
