# Philippians 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Philippians 3:1

**Q1. What did the apostle tell the brethren to do in the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Rejoice in the Lord.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:1

**Q2. What did Paul say was not grievous to him?**  
**Answer:** To write the same things to them.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:1

**Q3. For whom did Paul write the same things?**  
**Answer:** For your safety.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:1

---

### Philippians 3:2

**Q4. What should the brethren beware of?**  
**Answer:** Dogs, evil workers, and the concision.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:2

---

### Philippians 3:3

**Q5. Who are the circumcision?**  
**Answer:** We which worship God in the spirit, and rejoice in Christ Jesus, and have no confidence in the flesh.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:3

---

### Philippians 3:4

**Q6. Who might have whereof he might trust in the flesh?**  
**Answer:** Though I might also have confidence in the flesh.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:4

**Q7. What should anyone do if he thinks he has whereof he might trust in the flesh?**  
**Answer:** If any other man thinketh that he hath whereof he might trust in the flesh, I more.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:4

---

### Philippians 3:5

**Q8. On what day was Paul circumcised?**  
**Answer:** The eighth day.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:5

**Q9. Of what stock was Paul?**  
**Answer:** Of the stock of Israel.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:5

**Q10. Of what tribe was Paul?**  
**Answer:** Of the tribe of Benjamin.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:5

**Q11. What was Paul concerning the law?**  
**Answer:** A Hebrew of the Hebrews; as touching the law, a Pharisee.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:5

---

### Philippians 3:6

**Q12. What did Paul do concerning zeal?**  
**Answer:** Concerning zeal, persecuting the church.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:6

**Q13. How was Paul touching the righteousness which is in the law?**  
**Answer:** Blameless.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:6

---

### Philippians 3:7

**Q14. What things were gain to Paul?**  
**Answer:** But what things were gain to me.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:7

**Q15. How did Paul count those things that were gain to him?**  
**Answer:** Those I counted loss for Christ.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:7

---

### Philippians 3:8

**Q16. What did Paul count all things to be?**  
**Answer:** Loss.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:8

**Q17. Why did Paul count all things but loss?**  
**Answer:** For the excellency of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:8

**Q18. For whom did Paul suffer the loss of all things, and count them but dung?**  
**Answer:** That I may win Christ.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:8

---

### Philippians 3:9

**Q19. Where did Paul desire to be found?**  
**Answer:** In him.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:9

**Q20. What kind of righteousness did Paul not have?**  
**Answer:** Not having mine own righteousness, which is of the law.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:9

**Q21. Through what is the righteousness which is of God obtained?**  
**Answer:** Through the faith of Christ, the righteousness which is of God by faith.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:9

---

### Philippians 3:10

**Q22. What did Paul want to know?**  
**Answer:** That I may know him.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:10

**Q23. What else did Paul want to know besides him?**  
**Answer:** And the power of his resurrection, and the fellowship of his sufferings.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:10

**Q24. To what was Paul to be made conformable?**  
**Answer:** Being made conformable unto his death.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:10

---

### Philippians 3:11

**Q25. By what means did Paul hope to attain unto?**  
**Answer:** If by any means I might attain unto the resurrection of the dead.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:11

---

### Philippians 3:12

**Q26. What did Paul say he had not already attained?**  
**Answer:** Not as though I had already attained, either were already perfect.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:12

**Q27. What did Paul say he follows after?**  
**Answer:** But I follow after.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:12

**Q28. Why did Paul follow after?**  
**Answer:** If that I may apprehend that for which also I am apprehended of Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:12

---

### Philippians 3:13

**Q29. What did Paul count not himself to have apprehended?**  
**Answer:** Brethren, I count not myself to have apprehended.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:13

**Q30. What was one thing Paul did regarding things behind?**  
**Answer:** Forgetting those things which are behind.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:13

**Q31. What was Paul reaching forth unto?**  
**Answer:** And reaching forth unto those things which are before.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:13

---

### Philippians 3:14

**Q32. Where did Paul press toward?**  
**Answer:** I press toward the mark.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:14

**Q33. For what did Paul press toward the mark?**  
**Answer:** For the prize of the high calling of God in Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:14

---

### Philippians 3:15

**Q34. Who should be thus minded?**  
**Answer:** Let us therefore, as many as be perfect, be thus minded.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:15

**Q35. What would God do if anything was otherwise minded?**  
**Answer:** And if in any thing ye be otherwise minded, God shall reveal even this unto you.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:15

---

### Philippians 3:16

**Q37. To what extent had they already attained?**  
**Answer:** Nevertheless, whereto we have already attained.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:16

**Q38. How should they walk according to what they had attained?**  
**Answer:** Let us walk by the same rule, let us mind the same thing.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:16

---

### Philippians 3:17

**Q39. Who should followers together of Paul be?**  
**Answer:** Brethren, be followers together of me.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:17

**Q40. Whose example should they mark?**  
**Answer:** And mark them which walk so as ye have us for an ensample.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:17

---

### Philippians 3:18

**Q41. Whom did Paul often speak of weeping?**  
**Answer:** (For many walk, of whom I have told you often, and now tell you even weeping.)  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:18

**Q42. What are those people called by Paul?**  
**Answer:** That they are the enemies of the cross of Christ.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:18

---

### Philippians 3:19

**Q43. What is the end of those enemies?**  
**Answer:** Whose end is destruction.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:19

**Q44. What is their god?**  
**Answer:** Whose God is their belly.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:19

**Q45. What do they mind?**  
**Answer:** And whose glory is in their shame, who mind earthly things.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:19

---

### Philippians 3:20

**Q46. Where is our conversation?**  
**Answer:** For our conversation is in heaven.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:20

**Q47. From where do we look for the Saviour?**  
**Answer:** From whence also we look for the Saviour, the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:20

---

### Philippians 3:21

**Q48. What will Christ change?**  
**Answer:** Who shall change our vile body.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:21

**Q49. Like what body will our vile body be fashioned?**  
**Answer:** That it may be fashioned like unto his glorious body.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:21

**Q50. By what power does Christ subdue all things unto himself?**  
**Answer:** According to the working whereby he is able even to subdue all things unto himself.  
**Verse:** Philippians 3:21
