# Hosea 14 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hosea 14:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Hosea 14:1?**  
**Answer:** O Israel, return unto the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Hosea 14:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Hosea 14:1?**  
**Answer:** for thou hast fallen by thine iniquity.  
**Verse:** Hosea 14:1

**Q3. According to Hosea 14:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** O Israel, return unto the LORD thy God; for thou hast fallen by thine iniquity.  
**Verse:** Hosea 14:1

---

### Hosea 14:2
**Q4. What is stated in the opening of Hosea 14:2?**  
**Answer:** Take with you words, and turn to the LORD.  
**Verse:** Hosea 14:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Hosea 14:2?**  
**Answer:** say unto him, Take away all iniquity, and receive us graciously.  
**Verse:** Hosea 14:2

**Q6. What further truth is recorded in Hosea 14:2?**  
**Answer:** so will we render the calves of our lips.  
**Verse:** Hosea 14:2

### Hosea 14:3
**Q7. What is stated in the opening of Hosea 14:3?**  
**Answer:** Asshur shall not save us.  
**Verse:** Hosea 14:3

**Q8. What is declared next in Hosea 14:3?**

**Verse:** Hosea 14:3

**Q9. What further truth is recorded in Hosea 14:3?**
