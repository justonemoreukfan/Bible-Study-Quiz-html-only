# Hosea 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hosea 3:1

**Q1. Who said unto the prophet to go yet, love a woman?**  
**Answer:** Then said the LORD unto me.  
**Verse:** Hosea 3:1

**Q2. What was the woman beloved of her friend, yet doing?**  
**Answer:** Yet an adulteress.  
**Verse:** Hosea 3:1

**Q3. According to the Lord, how should the children of Israel be loved, according to the love of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** According to the love of the LORD toward the children of Israel, who look to other gods, and love flagons of wine.  
**Verse:** Hosea 3:1

---

### Hosea 3:2

**Q1. For how much silver was the woman bought to me?**  
**Answer:** Fifteen pieces of silver.  
**Verse:** Hosea 3:2

**Q2. What measure of barley was bought along with the silver?**  
**Answer:** An homer of barley, and an half homer of barley.  
**Verse:** Hosea 3:2

**Q3. Whose property was the homer and half homer of barley?**  
**Answer:** And for an homer of barley, and an half homer of barley.  
**Verse:** Hosea 3:2

---

### Hosea 3:3

**Q1. What did the prophet say unto her regarding her dwelling?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt abide for me many days.  
**Verse:** Hosea 3:3

**Q2. What must the woman not play?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not play the harlot.  
**Verse:** Hosea 3:3

**Q3. Whose presence must the woman not be for?**  
**Answer:** And thou shalt not be for another man.  
**Verse:** Hosea 3:3

---

### Hosea 3:4

**Q1. For how long shall the children of Israel abide without a king, and without a prince?**  
**Answer:** For the children of Israel shall abide many days without a king, and without a prince.  
**Verse:** Hosea 3:4

**Q2. What else shall the children of Israel abide without, besides a sacrifice and an image?**  
**Answer:** And without a sacrifice, and without an image.  
**Verse:** Hosea 3:4

**Q3. What shall the children of Israel be without, besides an ephod and without teraphim?**  
**Answer:** And without an ephod, and without teraphim.  
**Verse:** Hosea 3:4

---

### Hosea 3:5

**Q1. Afterward, who shall the children of Israel return and seek?**  
**Answer:** Afterward shall the children of Israel return, and seek the LORD their God.  
**Verse:** Hosea 3:5

**Q2. Whose king shall the children of Israel seek?**  
**Answer:** And David their king.  
**Verse:** Hosea 3:5

**Q3. How shall the children of Israel fear the LORD and his goodness in the latter days?**  
**Answer:** And shall fear the LORD and his goodness in the latter days.  
**Verse:** Hosea 3:5
