# Hosea 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hosea 2:1

**Q1. What are the children of Israel to be called by their brethren?**  
**Answer:** Ammi; and to your sisters, Ruhamah.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:1

**Q2. Whose children are addressed in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Ye your brethren.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:1

**Q3. To whom are the sisters addressed in relation to Ruhamah?**  
**Answer:** To your sisters.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:1

---

### Hosea 2:2

**Q1. What are the people told to plead with?**  
**Answer:** Plead with your mother, plead.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:2

**Q2. What is the mother's status regarding being the prophet's wife?**  
**Answer:** For she is not my wife, neither am I her husband.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:2

**Q3. What is the mother told to put away and remove?**  
**Answer:** Let her therefore put away her whoredoms out of her sight, and her adulteries from between her breasts;  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:2

---

### Hosea 2:3

**Q1. What might happen if the mother is not stripped?**  
**Answer:** Lest I strip her naked, and set her as in the day that she was born.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:3

**Q2. What will the mother be made like as in the day she was born?**  
**Answer:** And make her as a wilderness, and set her like a dry land, and slay her with thirst.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:3

**Q3. With what will the mother be slain?**  
**Answer:** And slay her with thirst.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:3

---

### Hosea 2:4

**Q1. Upon whom will the speaker not have mercy?**  
**Answer:** And I will not have mercy upon her children.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:4

**Q2. Why will the speaker not have mercy upon her children?**  
**Answer:** For they be the children of whoredoms.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:4

---

### Hosea 2:5

**Q1. What has the mother done?**  
**Answer:** For their mother hath played the harlot.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:5

**Q2. What did she that conceived them do?**  
**Answer:** She that conceived them hath done shamefully.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:5

**Q3. What did the mother say she would go after?**  
**Answer:** I will go after my lovers, that give me my bread and my water, my wool and my flax, mine oil and my drink.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:5

---

### Hosea 2:6

**Q1. What will the speaker do up the mother's way?**  
**Answer:** Therefore, behold, I will hedge up thy way with thorns.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:6

**Q2. What will the speaker make against the mother?**  
**Answer:** And make a wall.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:6

**Q3. What will happen to the mother's paths?**  
**Answer:** That she shall not find her paths.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:6

---

### Hosea 2:7

**Q1. What will the mother do after her lovers?**  
**Answer:** And she shall follow after her lovers, but she shall not overtake them.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:7

**Q2. What will the mother seek after failing to overtake them?**  
**Answer:** And she shall seek them, but shall not find them.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:7

**Q3. What will the mother then say she will do?**  
**Answer:** Then shall she say, I will go and return to my first husband; for then was it better with me than now.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:7

---

### Hosea 2:8

**Q1. What did the mother not know?**  
**Answer:** For she did not know that I gave her corn, and wine, and oil.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:8

**Q2. What did the speaker multiply unto her?**  
**Answer:** And multiplied her silver and gold.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:8

**Q3. What did the mother prepare or make with the silver and gold?**  
**Answer:** Which they prepared for Baal.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:8

---

### Hosea 2:9

**Q1. What will the speaker take away in the time thereof?**  
**Answer:** Therefore will I return, and take away my corn in the time thereof, and my wine in the season thereof.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:9

**Q2. What will the speaker recover?**  
**Answer:** And will recover my wool and my flax given to cover her nakedness.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:9

**Q3. What was the wool and flax given to cover?**  
**Answer:** To cover her nakedness.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:9

---

### Hosea 2:10

**Q1. What will the speaker now discover?**  
**Answer:** And now will I discover her lewdness in the sight of her lovers.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:10

**Q2. In whose sight will her lewdness be discovered?**  
**Answer:** In the sight of her lovers.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:10

**Q3. Who shall deliver her out of the speaker's hand?**  
**Answer:** And none shall deliver her out of mine hand.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:10

---

### Hosea 2:11

**Q1. What will the speaker cause to cease?**  
**Answer:** I will also cause all her mirth to cease.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:11

**Q2. Which of her special days are listed to cease?**  
**Answer:** Her feast days, her new moons, and her sabbaths, and all her solemn feasts.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:11

---

### Hosea 2:12

**Q1. What will the speaker destroy?**  
**Answer:** And I will destroy her vines and her fig trees.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:12

**Q2. What did the mother say these vines and fig trees were?**  
**Answer:** Whereof she hath said, These are my rewards that my lovers have given me.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:12

**Q3. What will the speaker make the vines and fig trees become?**  
**Answer:** And I will make them a forest, and the beasts of the field shall eat them.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:12

---

### Hosea 2:13

**Q1. What days will the speaker visit upon her?**  
**Answer:** And I will visit upon her the days of Baalim.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:13

**Q2. To what did she burn incense?**  
**Answer:** Wherein she burned incense to them.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:13

**Q3. With what did she deck herself, and what did she forget?**  
**Answer:** And she decked herself with her earrings and her jewels, and she went after her lovers, and forgat me, saith the LORD.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:13

---

### Hosea 2:14

**Q1. What will the speaker do to allure her?**  
**Answer:** Therefore, behold, I will allure her, and bring her into the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:14

**Q2. Where will the speaker bring her?**  
**Answer:** And bring her into the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:14

---

### Hosea 2:15

**Q1. What will the speaker give her from thence?**  
**Answer:** And I will give her her vineyards from thence.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:15

**Q2. What valley will be given as a door of hope?**  
**Answer:** And the valley of Achor for a door of hope.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:15

**Q3. As what days shall she sing?**  
**Answer:** And she shall sing there, as in the days of her youth, and as in the day when she came up out of the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:15

---

### Hosea 2:16

**Q1. What shall the person call the speaker at that day?**  
**Answer:** That thou shalt call me Ishi; and shalt call me no more Baali.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:16

**Q2. What name shall she call the speaker no more?**  
**Answer:** And shalt call me no more Baali.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:16

---

### Hosea 2:17

**Q1. What names of the Baalim will the speaker take away?**  
**Answer:** For I will take away the names of Baalim out of her mouth.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:17

**Q2. What will no more be remembered by their name?**  
**Answer:** And they shall no more be remembered by their name.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:17

---

### Hosea 2:18

**Q1. What will the speaker make for them in that day?**  
**Answer:** And in that day will I make a covenant for them with the beasts of the field, and with the fowls of heaven, and with the creeping things of the ground.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:18

**Q2. What will the speaker break out of the earth?**  
**Answer:** And I will break the bow and the sword and the battle out of the earth.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:18

**Q3. How will the people be made to lie down safely?**  
**Answer:** And will make them to lie down safely.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:18

---

### Hosea 2:19

**Q1. Who will the speaker betroth unto himself forever?**  
**Answer:** And I will betroth thee unto me for ever.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:19

**Q2. In what ways will the speaker betroth her?**  
**Answer:** Yea, I will betroth thee unto me in righteousness, and in judgment, and in lovingkindness, and in mercies.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:19

---

### Hosea 2:20

**Q1. In what will the speaker betroth her?**  
**Answer:** I will even betroth thee unto me in faithfulness.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:20

**Q2. What shall she know?**  
**Answer:** And thou shalt know the LORD.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:20

---

### Hosea 2:21

**Q1. What will the speaker hear in that day?**  
**Answer:** And it shall come to pass in that day, I will hear, saith the LORD.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:21

**Q2. Who will the speaker hear?**  
**Answer:** I will hear the heavens.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:21

**Q3. What shall the heavens hear?**  
**Answer:** And they shall hear the earth.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:21

---

### Hosea 2:22

**Q1. What shall the earth hear?**  
**Answer:** And the earth shall hear the corn, and the wine, and the oil.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:22

**Q2. What shall the corn, wine, and oil hear?**  
**Answer:** And they shall hear Jezreel.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:22

---

### Hosea 2:23

**Q1. Where will the speaker sow her unto himself?**  
**Answer:** And I will sow her unto me in the earth.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:23

**Q2. Upon whom will the speaker have mercy?**  
**Answer:** And I will have mercy upon her that had not obtained mercy.  
**Verse:** Hosea 2:23
