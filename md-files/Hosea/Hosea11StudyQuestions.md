# Hosea 11 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hosea 11:1
**Q1. According to Hosea 11:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** When Israel was a child.  
**Verse:** Hosea 11:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Hosea 11:1?**  
**Answer:** then I loved him.  
**Verse:** Hosea 11:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Hosea 11:1?**  
**Answer:** and called my son out of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Hosea 11:1

---

### Hosea 11:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Hosea 11:2?**  
**Answer:** As they called them, so they went from them.  
**Verse:** Hosea 11:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Hosea 11:2?**  
**Answer:** they sacrificed unto Baalim, and burned incense to graven images.  
**Verse:** Hosea 11:2

**Q6. According to Hosea 11:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** As they called them, so they went from them: they sacrificed unto Baalim, and burned incense to graven images.  
**Verse:** Hosea 11:2
