# Hosea 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hosea 8:1
**Q1. According to Hosea 8:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Set the trumpet to thy mouth. He shall come as an eagle against the house of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Hosea 8:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Hosea 8:1?**  
**Answer:** because they have transgressed my covenant.  
**Verse:** Hosea 8:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Hosea 8:1?**  
**Answer:** and trespassed against my law.  
**Verse:** Hosea 8:1

---
