# Hosea 7 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hosea 7:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Hosea 7:1?**  
**Answer:** When I would have healed Israel, then the iniquity of Ephraim was discovered, and the wickedness of Samaria.  
**Verse:** Hosea 7:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Hosea 7:1?**  
**Answer:** for they commit falsehood.  
**Verse:** Hosea 7:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Hosea 7:1?**  
**Answer:** and the thief cometh in, and the troop of robbers spoileth without.  
**Verse:** Hosea 7:1
