# Hosea 13 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hosea 13:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Hosea 13:1?**  
**Answer:** When Ephraim spake trembling, he exalted himself in Israel.  
**Verse:** Hosea 13:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Hosea 13:1?**  
**Answer:** but when he offended in Baal, he died.  
**Verse:** Hosea 13:1

**Q3. According to Hosea 13:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** When Ephraim spake trembling, he exalted himself in Israel; but when he offended in Baal, he died.  
**Verse:** Hosea 13:1
