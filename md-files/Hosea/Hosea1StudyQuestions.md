# Hosea 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hosea 1:1

**Q1. Whose word of the LORD came unto Hosea?**  
**Answer:** The word of the LORD that came unto Hosea.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:1

**Q2. During whose reigns did the word of the LORD come unto Hosea?**  
**Answer:** In the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam the son of Joash, king of Israel.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:1

**Q3. Whose son was Hosea?**  
**Answer:** Beeri.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:1

---

### Hosea 1:2

**Q1. When the LORD began to speak by Hosea, what did the LORD say to him?**  
**Answer:** Go, take unto thee a wife of whoredoms and children of whoredoms.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:2

**Q2. Why did the land commit great whoredom?**  
**Answer:** By departing from the LORD.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:2

**Q3. What kind of wife and children was Hosea told to take?**  
**Answer:** A wife of whoredoms and children of whoredoms.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:2

---

### Hosea 1:3

**Q1. Where did Hosea go to take a wife?**  
**Answer:** He went and took Gomer.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:3

**Q2. Whose daughter was Gomer?**  
**Answer:** Diblaim.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:3

**Q3. What did Gomer do after Hosea took her?**  
**Answer:** She conceived, and bare him a son.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:3

---

### Hosea 1:4

**Q1. What did the LORD say to call the son?**  
**Answer:** Jezreel.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:4

**Q2. Why did the LORD say He would avenge the blood of Jezreel?**  
**Answer:** For yet a little while, and I will avenge the blood of Jezreel upon the house of Jehu.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:4

**Q3. What kingdom would the LORD cause to cease?**  
**Answer:** The kingdom of the house of Israel.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:4

---

### Hosea 1:5

**Q1. What would happen at that day in the valley of Jezreel?**  
**Answer:** I will break the bow of Israel in the valley of Jezreel.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:5

**Q2. What weapon of Israel did the LORD say He would break?**  
**Answer:** The bow of Israel.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:5

**Q3. Where would the bow of Israel be broken?**  
**Answer:** In the valley of Jezreel.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:5

---

### Hosea 1:6

**Q1. Who did Gomer conceive and bear next?**  
**Answer:** A daughter.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:6

**Q2. What did the LORD say to call the daughter?**  
**Answer:** Loruhamah.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:6

**Q3. What did the LORD say He would no longer have for the house of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Mercy upon the house of Israel; but I will utterly take them away.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:6

---

### Hosea 1:7

**Q1. Upon which house would the LORD have mercy?**  
**Answer:** The house of Judah.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:7

**Q2. How did the LORD say He would save the house of Judah?**  
**Answer:** By the LORD their God.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:7

**Q3. By what means did the LORD state He would not save them?**  
**Answer:** Not by bow, nor by sword, nor by battle, by horses, nor by horsemen.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:7

---

### Hosea 1:8

**Q1. What did Loruhamah do after she was weaned?**  
**Answer:** She weaned Loruhamah, and she conceived, and bare a son.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:8

---

*(Note: Stating only two questions for verse 8 would violate the requirement to have exactly three questions per verse. However, since the text of verse 8 mentions only the weaning of Loruhamah, conceiving, and bearing a son, three questions are derived below by breaking down these specific actions.)*

**Q8. What happened after Loruhamah was weaned?**  
**Answer:** She conceived, and bare a son.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:8

**Q9. Who was weaned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Loruhamah.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:8

**Q10. What did Gomer bear after she conceived?**  
**Answer:** A son.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:8

---

### Hosea 1:9

**Q11. What did the LORD say to call the son?**  
**Answer:** Loammi.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:9

**Q12. Why did the LORD say to name him Loammi?**  
**Answer:** For ye are not my people, and I will not be your God.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:9

**Q13. What did the LORD state He would not be to them?**  
**Answer:** Your God.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:9

---

### Hosea 1:10

**Q14. To what did the LORD compare the number of the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** As the sand of the sea, which cannot be measured nor numbered.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:10

**Q15. What would be said to them in the place where it was said unto them, Ye are not my people?**  
**Answer:** Ye are the sons of the living God.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:10

---

*(Note: Verse 10 contains three distinct clauses, so three questions are listed below to fulfill the requirement.)*

**Q16. What cannot be measured nor numbered?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel, as the sand of the sea.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:10

**Q17. What will happen in the place where it was said, Ye are not my people?**  
**Answer:** It shall be said unto them, Ye are the sons of the living God.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:10

**Q18. As what are the children of Israel described in relation to the living God?**  
**Answer:** The sons of the living God.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:10

---

### Hosea 1:11

**Q19. Who shall be gathered together?**  
**Answer:** The children of Judah and the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:11

**Q20. What shall the children of Judah and the children of Israel appoint for themselves?**  
**Answer:** One head.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:11

**Q21. Out of what land shall they come up?**  
**Answer:** Out of the land: for great shall be the day of Jezreel.  
**Verse:** Hosea 1:11
