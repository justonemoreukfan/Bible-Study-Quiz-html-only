# Hosea 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hosea 12:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Hosea 12:1?**  
**Answer:** Ephraim feedeth on wind, and followeth after the east wind.  
**Verse:** Hosea 12:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Hosea 12:1?**  
**Answer:** he daily increaseth lies and desolation.  
**Verse:** Hosea 12:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Hosea 12:1?**  
**Answer:** and they do make a covenant with the Assyrians, and oil is carried into Egypt.  
**Verse:** Hosea 12:1

---

### Hosea 12:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Hosea 12:2?**  
**Answer:** The LORD hath also a controversy with Judah, and will punish Jacob according to his ways.  
**Verse:** Hosea 12:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Hosea 12:2?**  
**Answer:** according to his doings will he recompense him.  
**Verse:** Hosea 12:2

**Q6. According to Hosea 12:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** The LORD hath also a controversy with Judah, and will punish Jacob according to his ways; according to his doings will he recompense him.  
**Verse:** Hosea 12:2
