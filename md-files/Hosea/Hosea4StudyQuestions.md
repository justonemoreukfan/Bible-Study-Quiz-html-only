# Hosea 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hosea 4:1

**Q1. Who does the LORD have a controversy with?**  
**Answer:** The inhabitants of the land.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:1

**Q2. What is not in the land?**  
**Answer:** Truth, nor mercy, nor knowledge of God.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:1

**Q3. Who are the inhabitants of the land compared to in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:1

---

### Hosea 4:2

**Q1. What acts are specifically mentioned by which the land mourns?**  
**Answer:** Swearing, and lying, and killing, and stealing, and committing adultery.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:2

**Q2. What happens to blood touchers in this verse?**  
**Answer:** They break out, and blood toucheth blood.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:2

**Q3. What general state fills the land according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** By swearing, and lying, and killing, and stealing, and committing adultery, they break out.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:2

---

### Hosea 4:3

**Q1. What shall the land do because of these things?**  
**Answer:** Shall mourn.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:3

**Q2. Who shall languish along with everyone that dwelleth therein?**  
**Answer:** Every one that dwelleth therein, with the beasts of the field, and with the fowls of heaven.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:3

**Q3. What else shall take away the fishes of the sea?**  
**Answer:** The fishes of the sea also shall be taken away.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:3

---

### Hosea 4:4

**Q1. What qualification is given regarding who should strive or reprove?**  
**Answer:** Let no man strive, nor reprove another.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:4

**Q2. Who is likened to the people in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Thy people are as they that strive with the priest.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:4

**Q3. To whom are the people compared in their striving?**  
**Answer:** As they that strive with the priest.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:4

---

### Hosea 4:5

**Q1. When shall the prophet and the mother fall?**  
**Answer:** Therefore shalt thou fall in the day, and the prophet also shall fall with thee in the night.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:5

**Q2. With whom shall the prophet fall in the night?**  
**Answer:** With thee (the mother).  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:5

**Q3. What will the LORD destroy in this verse?**  
**Answer:** I will destroy thy mother.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:5

---

### Hosea 4:6

**Q1. What are the people destroyed for?**  
**Answer:** Lack of knowledge.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:6

**Q2. What has the priest rejected?**  
**Answer:** Knowledge.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:6

**Q3. What will the LORD forget regarding the priest's children?**  
**Answer:** I will also forget thy children.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:6

---

### Hosea 4:7

**Q1. What did the increased priests do against the LORD?**  
**Answer:** They sinned against me.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:7

**Q2. What will the LORD change their glory into?**  
**Answer:** Shame.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:7

**Q3. Did they increase in number?**  
**Answer:** As they were increased, so they sinned against me.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:7

---

### Hosea 4:8

**Q1. What do the people eat up?**  
**Answer:** The sin of my people.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:8

**Q2. What do they set their heart on?**  
**Answer:** Their iniquity.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:8

**Q3. Whose sin do they eat up?**  
**Answer:** The sin of my people.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:8

---

### Hosea 4:9

**Q1. Who shall be like the people?**  
**Answer:** Like people, like priest.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:9

**Q2. What will the LORD punish them for?**  
**Answer:** Their ways.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:9

**Q3. What will the LORD reward them for?**  
**Answer:** Their doings.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:9

---

### Hosea 4:10

**Q1. What will the people do and not have?**  
**Answer:** They shall eat, and not have enough.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:10

**Q2. What will the people do and not increase?**  
**Answer:** They shall commit whoredom, and shall not increase.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:10

**Q3. Why have they left off to do what?**  
**Answer:** Because they have left off to take heed to the LORD.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:10

---

### Hosea 4:11

**Q1. What two things take away the heart?**  
**Answer:** Whoredom and wine and new wine.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:11

**Q2. What does wine take away?**  
**Answer:** The heart.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:11

**Q3. What does new wine take away?**  
**Answer:** The heart.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:11

---

### Hosea 4:12

**Q1. What do the people consult regarding their stock?**  
**Answer:** Their wood.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:12

**Q2. What does their staff declare unto them?**  
**Answer:** Their staff declareth unto them.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:12

**Q3. What has the spirit of whoredoms caused them to do?**  
**Answer:** Caused them to err, and they have gone a whoring from under their God.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:12

---

### Hosea 4:13

**Q1. Upon what do the people sacrifice?**  
**Answer:** Upon the tops of the mountains.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:13

**Q2. Upon what do they burn incense?**  
**Answer:** Upon the hills.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:13

**Q3. Under what trees do they burn incense?**  
**Answer:** Oaks and poplars and elms.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:13

---

### Hosea 4:14

**Q1. Who shall not be punished for their whoredom and adultery?**  
**Answer:** Your daughters shall not commit whoredom, nor your spouses commit adultery.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:14

**Q2. Who themselves are separated with who?**  
**Answer:** The men themselves are separated with whores, and they sacrifice with harlots.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:14

**Q3. What happens to the people that do not understand?**  
**Answer:** They shall fall.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:14

---

### Hosea 4:15

**Q1. Though Israel plays the harlot, who should not offend?**  
**Answer:** Judah.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:15

**Q2. Where should the people not go?**  
**Answer:** Unto Gilgal.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:15

**Q3. Where should the people not go up?**  
**Answer:** Neither go ye up to Beth-aven.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:15

---

### Hosea 4:16

**Q1. How is Israel sliding back?**  
**Answer:** As a backsliding heifer.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:16

**Q2. How will the LORD now feed them?**  
**Answer:** As a lamb in a large place.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:16

**Q3. What animal is Israel compared to as a backslider?**  
**Answer:** A backsliding heifer.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:16

---

### Hosea 4:17

**Q1. Who is joined to idols?**  
**Answer:** Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:17

**Q2. What instruction is given regarding Ephraim?**  
**Answer:** Let him alone.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:17

**Q3. Who is Ephraim joined to?**  
**Answer:** Idols.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:17

---

### Hosea 4:18

**Q1. What is their drink sour?**  
**Answer:** Their drink is sour.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:18

**Q2. What have they committed continually?**  
**Answer:** They have committed whoredom continually.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:18

**Q3. Who have loved shame exceedingly?**  
**Answer:** Her rulers.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:18

---

### Hosea 4:19

**Q1. What has bound her up in her wings?**  
**Answer:** The wind.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:19

**Q2. What shall they be ashamed of because of their sacrifices?**  
**Answer:** They shall be ashamed because of their sacrifices.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:19

**Q3. What element binds her up?**  
**Answer:** The wind.  
**Verse:** Hosea 4:19
