# Hosea 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hosea 5:1

**Q1. Who is told to hear this?**  
**Answer:** O ye priests.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:1

**Q2. To whom should the house of Israel give ear?**  
**Answer:** The house of Israel.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:1

**Q3. Whose house is commanded to give ear?**  
**Answer:** The house of the king.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:1

---

### Hosea 5:2

**Q1. What are the revolters deep in?**  
**Answer:** Slaughter.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:2

**Q2. Who have the revolters been deep in slaughter to?**  
**Answer:** Though I have been a rebuker of them all.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:2

**Q3. What has the speaker been to them all?**  
**Answer:** A rebuker.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:2

---

### Hosea 5:3

**Q1. Who is known to the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:3

**Q2. Is Israel hid from the Lord?**  
**Answer:** No.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:3

**Q3. What has Ephraim done, showing he is defiled?**  
**Answer:** Ephraim committeth whoredom, and Israel is defiled.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:3

---

### Hosea 5:4

**Q1. What will their doings not allow them to do?**  
**Answer:** They will not frame their doings to turn unto their God.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:4

**Q2. What is in the midst of them?**  
**Answer:** The spirit of whoredoms.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:4

**Q3. Whom have they not known?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:4

---

### Hosea 5:5

**Q1. What testifieth to his face?**  
**Answer:** The pride of Israel.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:5

**Q2. Who shall fall in their iniquity?**  
**Answer:** Israel and Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:5

**Q3. Who shall also fall with them?**  
**Answer:** Judah.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:5

---

### Hosea 5:6

**Q1. With what shall they go to seek the Lord?**  
**Answer:** With their flocks and with their herds.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:6

**Q2. Will they find Him when they go with flocks and herds?**  
**Answer:** They shall not find him.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:6

**Q3. What has He done from them?**  
**Answer:** He hath withdrawn himself from them.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:6

---

### Hosea 5:7

**Q1. What have they dealt against the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Treacherously.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:7

**Q2. What have they begotten?**  
**Answer:** Strange children.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:7

**Q3. What shall devour them with their portions?**  
**Answer:** A month.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:7

---

### Hosea 5:8

**Q1. Where should the ram be blown?**  
**Answer:** In Gibeah.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:8

**Q2. Where should the trumpet be sounded?**  
**Answer:** In Ramah.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:8

**Q3. What cry should be raised after Benjamin, indicating danger?**  
**Answer:** O Benjamin, after thee.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:8

---

### Hosea 5:9

**Q1. What shall Ephraim become in the day of rebuke?**  
**Answer:** Desolate.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:9

**Q2. What have I made known among the tribes of Israel?**  
**Answer:** That which shall surely be.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:9

**Q3. Among whom have I made known that which shall surely be?**  
**Answer:** The tribes of Israel.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:9

---

### Hosea 5:10

**Q1. What were the princes of Judah like?**  
**Answer:** Them that remove the bound.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:10

**Q2. Upon whom will God pour out His wrath like water?**  
**Answer:** Them.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:10

**Q3. Like what will God pour out His wrath upon them?**  
**Answer:** Water.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:10

---

### Hosea 5:11

**Q1. How is Ephraim oppressed?**  
**Answer:** Oppressed and broken in judgment.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:11

**Q2. Why was Ephraim willing to walk?**  
**Answer:** Because he willingly walked after the commandment.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:11

**Q3. What did Ephraim walk after willingly?**  
**Answer:** The commandment.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:11

---

### Hosea 5:12

**Q1. What will I be unto Ephraim?**  
**Answer:** As a moth.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:12

**Q2. What will I be to the house of Judah?**  
**Answer:** As rottenness.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:12

**Q3. To whom will I be as rottenness?**  
**Answer:** The house of Judah.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:12

---

### Hosea 5:13

**Q1. When Ephraim saw his sickness and Judah saw his wound, where did Ephraim go?**  
**Answer:** To the Assyrian.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:13

**Q2. To whom did Judah send?**  
**Answer:** King Jareb.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:13

**Q3. Could King Jareb or Assyria heal them or cure them?**  
**Answer:** Yet could he not heal you, nor cure you of your wound.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:13

---

### Hosea 5:14

**Q1. What will I be unto Ephraim?**  
**Answer:** As a lion.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:14

**Q2. What will I be to the house of Judah?**  
**Answer:** As a young lion.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:14

**Q3. What will I do, take away, and none shall rescue?**  
**Answer:** I, even I, will tear and go away; I will take away, and none shall rescue him.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:14

---

### Hosea 5:15

**Q1. Where will I go and return?**  
**Answer:** I will go and return to my place.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:15

**Q2. Until what point will they acknowledge their offense?**  
**Answer:** Till they acknowledge their offense.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:15

**Q3. What will they do in their affliction?**  
**Answer:** Seek me early.  
**Verse:** Hosea 5:15
