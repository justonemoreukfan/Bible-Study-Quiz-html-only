# Hosea 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hosea 9:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Hosea 9:1?**  
**Answer:** Rejoice not, O Israel, for joy, as other people.  
**Verse:** Hosea 9:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Hosea 9:1?**  
**Answer:** for thou hast gone a whoring from thy God, thou hast loved a reward upon every cornfloor.  
**Verse:** Hosea 9:1

**Q3. According to Hosea 9:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Rejoice not, O Israel, for joy, as other people: for thou hast gone a whoring from thy God, thou hast loved a reward upon every cornfloor.  
**Verse:** Hosea 9:1
