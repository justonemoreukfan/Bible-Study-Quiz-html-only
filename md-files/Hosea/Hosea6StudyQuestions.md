# Hosea 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hosea 6:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Hosea 6:1?**  
**Answer:** Come, and let us return unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Hosea 6:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Hosea 6:1?**  
**Answer:** for he hath torn, and he will heal us.  
**Verse:** Hosea 6:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Hosea 6:1?**  
**Answer:** he hath smitten, and he will bind us up.  
**Verse:** Hosea 6:1

---

### Hosea 6:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Hosea 6:2?**  
**Answer:** After two days will he revive us.  
**Verse:** Hosea 6:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Hosea 6:2?**  
**Answer:** in the third day he will raise us up, and we shall live in his sight.  
**Verse:** Hosea 6:2

**Q6. According to Hosea 6:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** After two days will he revive us: in the third day he will raise us up, and we shall live in his sight.  
**Verse:** Hosea 6:2

---

**Verse:** Hosea 6:3

### Hosea 6:4
**Q10. According to Hosea 6:4, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** O Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Hosea 6:4

**Q11. What is declared next in Hosea 6:4?**  
**Answer:** what shall I do unto thee? O Judah.  
**Verse:** Hosea 6:4

**Q12. What further truth is given in Hosea 6:4?**  
**Answer:** what shall I do unto thee? for your goodness is as a morning cloud, and as the early dew it goeth away.  
**Verse:** Hosea 6:4
