# Hosea 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Hosea 10:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Hosea 10:1?**  
**Answer:** Israel is an empty vine, he bringeth forth fruit unto himself.  
**Verse:** Hosea 10:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Hosea 10:1?**  
**Answer:** according to the multitude of his fruit he hath increased the altars.  
**Verse:** Hosea 10:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Hosea 10:1?**  
**Answer:** according to the goodness of his land they have made goodly images.  
**Verse:** Hosea 10:1

---

### Hosea 10:2
**Q4. What is stated in the opening of Hosea 10:2?**  
**Answer:** Their heart is divided.  
**Verse:** Hosea 10:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Hosea 10:2?**  
**Answer:** now shall they be found faulty.  
**Verse:** Hosea 10:2

**Q6. What further truth is recorded in Hosea 10:2?**  
**Answer:** he shall break down their altars, he shall spoil their images.  
**Verse:** Hosea 10:2

### Hosea 10:3
**Q7. What is stated in the first clause of Hosea 10:3?**  
**Answer:** For now they shall say, We have no king, because we feared not the LORD.  
**Verse:** Hosea 10:3

**Q8. What additional detail is declared in Hosea 10:3?**  
**Answer:** what then should a king do to us?  
**Verse:** Hosea 10:3

**Q9. According to Hosea 10:3, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** For now they shall say, We have no king, because we feared not the LORD; what then should a king do to us?  
**Verse:** Hosea 10:3
