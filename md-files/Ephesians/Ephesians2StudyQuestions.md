# Ephesians 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ephesians 2:1

**Q1. What has been quickened who were dead in trespasses and sins?**  
**Answer:** You.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:1

**Q2. In what state were they previously concerning trespasses and sins?**  
**Answer:** Dead.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:1

**Q3. What specific things were they dead in?**  
**Answer:** Trespasses and sins.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:1

---

### Ephesians 2:2

**Q4. According to what did they time past walk?**  
**Answer:** According to the course of this world.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:2

**Q5. According to the prince of the power of whom did they walk?**  
**Answer:** The prince of the power of the air.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:2

**Q6. In whom does the spirit now work?**  
**Answer:** The children of disobedience.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:2

---

### Ephesians 2:3

**Q7. Among whom also we all had our conversation in times past?**  
**Answer:** All we also had our conversation in times past.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:3

**Q8. In the lusts of what did we fulfill?**  
**Answer:** The lusts of our flesh.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:3

**Q9. By nature, what were we by the wrath?**  
**Answer:** Children of wrath, even as others.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:3

---

### Ephesians 2:4

**Q10. Who is rich in mercy?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:4

**Q11. For what great reason did God love us?**  
**Answer:** For his great love wherewith he loved us.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:4

**Q12. What is God described as being rich in?**  
**Answer:** Mercy.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:4

---

### Ephesians 2:5

**Q13. When we were dead in sins, what did God do to us together with Christ?**  
**Answer:** Quickened us together with Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:5

**Q14. By what are ye saved?**  
**Answer:** Grace.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:5

**Q15. When were we quickened together with Christ?**  
**Answer:** Even when we were dead in sins.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:5

---

### Ephesians 2:6

**Q16. What did God raise us up together with?**  
**Answer:** Him.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:6

**Q17. Where did God make us sit together?**  
**Answer:** In heavenly places.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:6

**Q18. In whom are we made to sit together in heavenly places?**  
**Answer:** In Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:6

---

### Ephesians 2:7

**Q19. That in the ages to come He might show what?**  
**Answer:** The exceeding riches of his grace.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:7

**Q20. Through what does He show the exceeding riches of His grace?**  
**Answer:** His kindness toward us.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:7

**Q21. Toward whom is His kindness shown through Christ Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Toward us.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:7

---

### Ephesians 2:8

**Q22. By what are ye saved?**  
**Answer:** Grace.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:8

**Q23. Through what is salvation by grace?**  
**Answer:** Faith.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:8

**Q24. Of whom is salvation not of yourselves?**  
**Answer:** It is the gift of God.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:8

---

### Ephesians 2:9

**Q25. Not of what is salvation?**  
**Answer:** Works.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:9

**Q26. Why is salvation not of works?**  
**Answer:** Lest any man should boast.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:9

**Q27. Whose boast is prevented by salvation not being of works?**  
**Answer:** Any man.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:9

---

### Ephesians 2:10

**Q28. Whose workmanship are we?**  
**Answer:** His workmanship.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:10

**Q29. Created in whom unto good works?**  
**Answer:** In Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:10

**Q30. What has God before ordained that we should walk in them?**  
**Answer:** Good works.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:10

---

### Ephesians 2:11

**Q31. Wherefore remember that ye being in time past what?**  
**Answer:** Gentiles in the flesh.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:11

**Q32. By what name are they called who are called Uncision?**  
**Answer:** Uncision.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:11

**Q33. By what is the Circumcision in the flesh made?**  
**Answer:** By hands.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:11

---

### Ephesians 2:12

**Q34. At what time were ye without Christ?**  
**Answer:** That time.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:12

**Q35. From what commonwealth were they aliens?**  
**Answer:** The commonwealth of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:12

**Q36. Having what in the world and without God?**  
**Answer:** No hope.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:12

---

### Ephesians 2:13

**Q37. Who sometimes were far off?**  
**Answer:** Ye.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:13

**Q38. Where are ye made nigh?**  
**Answer:** In Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:13

**Q39. By what are ye made nigh?**  
**Answer:** By the blood of Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:13

---

### Ephesians 2:14

**Q40. Who is our peace?**  
**Answer:** For he is our peace.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:14

**Q41. What did He make both?**  
**Answer:** One.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:14

**Q42. What did He break down between us?**  
**Answer:** The middle wall of partition between us.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:14

---

### Ephesians 2:15

**Q43. What did He abolish in His flesh?**  
**Answer:** The enmity.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:15

**Q44. What law of commandments contained in ordinances did He abolish?**  
**Answer:** The law of commandments contained in ordinances.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:15

**Q45. For to make in Himself of twain one what?**  
**Answer:** New man.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:15

---

### Ephesians 2:16

**Q46. That He might reconcile both unto God in one what?**  
**Answer:** Body.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:16

**Q47. By what did He slay the enmity?**  
**Answer:** By the cross.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:16

**Q48. What was slain thereby?**  
**Answer:** The enmity.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:16

---

### Ephesians 2:17

**Q49. And came and preached what to you?**  
**Answer:** Peace.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:17

**Q50. To whom did He preach peace that were afar off?**  
**Answer:** You which were afar off.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:17

**Q51. To whom did He preach peace that were nigh?**  
**Answer:** To them that were nigh.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:17

---

### Ephesians 2:18

**Q52. Through whom do we both have access?**  
**Answer:** Through him.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:18

**Q53. Unto whom do we have access?**  
**Answer:** Unto the Father.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:18

**Q54. By what spirit do we have access?**  
**Answer:** By one Spirit.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:18

---

### Ephesians 2:19

**Q55. Therefore ye are no more what?**  
**Answer:** Strangers and foreigners.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:19

**Q56. But fellow citizens with whom?**  
**Answer:** The saints.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:19

**Q57. Of the household of whom are ye?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:19

---

### Ephesians 2:20

**Q58. Upon what foundation are ye built?**  
**Answer:** The foundation of the apostles and prophets.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:20

**Q59. Who is the chief corner stone?**  
**Answer:** Jesus Christ himself.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:20

---

### Ephesians 2:21

**Q60. In whom all the building fitly framed together grows unto what?**  
**Answer:** An holy temple.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:21

**Q61. In whom does the building grow unto an holy temple?**  
**Answer:** In the Lord.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:21

---

### Ephesians 2:22

**Q62. In whom are ye also builded together for what?**  
**Answer:** An habitation of God.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:22

**Q63. Through what is the habitation of God built?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 2:22
