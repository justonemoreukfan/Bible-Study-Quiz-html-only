# Ephesians 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ephesians 6:1

**Q1. Who are children commanded to obey in the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Their parents.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:1

**Q2. Whose obedience should children exhibit toward their parents?**  
**Answer:** Their parents.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:1

**Q3. What makes this obedience right according to the verse?**  
**Answer:** For it is right.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:1

---

### Ephesians 6:2

**Q1. What specific commandment are children told to honor?**  
**Answer:** Honour thy father and mother.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:2

**Q2. What is noted as the first commandment?**  
**Answer:** Which is the first commandment.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:2

**Q3. What is attached to this commandment?**  
**Answer:** With promise.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:2

---

### Ephesians 6:3

**Q1. What may be well with the obedient child?**  
**Answer:** That it may be well with thee.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:3

**Q2. How long may the child live on the earth?**  
**Answer:** Thou mayest live long on the earth.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:3

**Q3. Whose father and mother are referenced in this promise?**  
**Answer:** Thy father and mother.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:3

---

### Ephesians 6:4

**Q1. Who is addressed regarding how they provoke their children?**  
**Answer:** Ye fathers.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:4

**Q2. What should fathers not do to their children?**  
**Answer:** Provoke not your children to wrath.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:4

**Q3. How should fathers bring up their children?**  
**Answer:** In the nurture and admonition of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:4

---

### Ephesians 6:5

**Q1. Who are servants told to obey?**  
**Answer:** Your masters according to the flesh.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:5

**Q2. With what attitude should servants obey their masters?**  
**Answer:** With fear and trembling.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:5

**Q3. In what manner should servants obey concerning their heart?**  
**Answer:** In singleness of your heart, as unto Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:5

---

### Ephesians 6:6

**Q1. What kind of service should be avoided regarding eye-service?**  
**Answer:** Not with eyeservice, as menpleasers.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:6

**Q2. As what should servants act in doing the will of God?**  
**Answer:** As the servants of Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:6

**Q3. From where should servants do the will of God?**  
**Answer:** From the heart.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:6

---

### Ephesians 6:7

**Q1. With what disposition should service be done?**  
**Answer:** With good will.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:7

**Q2. Whom should the service be done to, rather than to men?**  
**Answer:** As to the Lord.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:7

**Q3. What does the verse say about service done as to the Lord?**  
**Answer:** And not to men.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:7

---

**Answer:** The same shall he receive of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:8

**Q2. Who receives this reward regardless of their social status?**  
**Answer:** Whether he be bond or free.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:8

**Q3. What kind of thing done by a man is rewarded?**  
**Answer:** Any good thing the same shall he receive.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:8

**Answer:** Do the same things unto them.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:9

**Q2. What should masters forbear concerning their servants?**  
**Answer:** Forbearing threatening.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:9

**Q3. Who is in heaven for both master and servant?**  
**Answer:** Your Master also is in heaven.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:9

### Ephesians 6:10

**Q1. Whom should brethren be strong in?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:10

**Q2. What else should be drawn upon alongside the Lord?**  
**Answer:** In the power of his might.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:10

**Q3. What command is given regarding brethren and strength?**  
**Answer:** Be strong in the Lord.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:10

### Ephesians 6:11

**Q1. What should a person put on?**  
**Answer:** The whole armour of God.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:11

**Q2. For what purpose is the armour to be put on?**  
**Answer:** That ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:11

**Q3. Whose wiles must one stand against?**  
**Answer:** The devil.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:11

---

### Ephesians 6:12

**Q1. Against whom do we not wrestle?**  
**Answer:** Against flesh and blood.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:12

**Q2. Against what types of entities do we wrestle?**  
**Answer:** Against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:12

**Q3. Where is the spiritual wickedness located?**  
**Answer:** In high places.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 6:12

---
