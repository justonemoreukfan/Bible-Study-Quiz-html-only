# Ephesians 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ephesians 4:1

**Q1. Who is the prisoner in the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Therefore I, the prisoner of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:1

**Q2. What are the readers besought to walk in a manner worthy of?**  
**Answer:** That ye walk worthy of the vocation wherewith ye are called.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:1

**Q3. What kind of calling have the readers received?**  
**Answer:** The vocation wherewith ye are called.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:1

---

### Ephesians 4:2

**Q2. With what attitudes should the readers walk with all men?**  
**Answer:** With all lowliness and meekness.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:2

**Q3. How should the readers treat one another with long-suffering?**  
**Answer:** Forbearing one another in love.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:2

---

### Ephesians 4:3

**Q4. What are the readers endeavoring to keep?**  
**Answer:** The unity of the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:3

**Q5. In what bond is the unity of the Spirit to be kept?**  
**Answer:** In the bond of peace.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:3

**Q6. What is the bond mentioned in verse 3?**  
**Answer:** The bond of peace.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:3

**Answer:** Unto every one of us.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:7

**Q17. According to what is grace given?**  
**Answer:** According to the measure of the gift of Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:7

**Answer:** Grace.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:7

### Ephesians 4:8

**Q19. Wherefore does the scripture say He ascended up on high?**  
**Answer:** When he ascended up on high.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:8

**Q20. What did He lead when He ascended up on high?**  
**Answer:** Led captivity captive.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:8

**Q21. What did He give unto men?**  
**Answer:** Gave gifts unto men.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:8

---

### Ephesians 4:9

**Q22. What does "now that he ascended" imply about what He first did?**  
**Answer:** That he also descended first into the lower parts of the earth.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:9

**Q23. Into what parts of the earth did He descend first?**  
**Answer:** The lower parts of the earth.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:9

**Q24. What expression is used about His ascent before mentioning His descent?**  
**Answer:** Now that he ascended, what is it but that he also descended first.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:9

---

### Ephesians 4:10

**Q25. Who is the one that descended?**  
**Answer:** He that descended.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:10

**Q26. What did the one who descended also do?**  
**Answer:** Is the same also that ascended up far above all heavens.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:10

**Q27. Why did He ascend up far above all heavens?**  
**Answer:** That he might fill all things.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:10

---

### Ephesians 4:11

**Q28. Who gave some to be apostles?**  
**Answer:** And he gave some, apostles.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:11

**Q29. What office did He give some to be besides apostles, prophets, and evangelists?**  
**Answer:** And some, pastors and teachers.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:11

**Q30. Which specific offices are listed in verse 11?**  
**Answer:** Apostles, and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:11

---

### Ephesians 4:12

**Q31. For what purpose are the gifts given regarding the perfecting of the saints?**  
**Answer:** For the perfecting of the saints.

**Q32. For what work is the ministry intended?**  
**Answer:** For the work of the ministry.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:12

**Q33. For the edifying of what body are these given?**  
**Answer:** For the edifying of the body of Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:12

### Ephesians 4:13

**Q34. Until we all come in what unity?**  
**Answer:** In the unity of the faith.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:13

**Q35. Of the knowledge of whose Son must we come in the unity?**  
**Answer:** Of the Son of God.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:13

**Verse:** Ephesians 4:13

### Ephesians 4:14

**Q37. What should we henceforth no longer be?**  
**Answer:** That we henceforth be no more children.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:14

**Q38. How are children tossed to and fro?**  
**Answer:** Tossed to and fro, and carried about with every wind of doctrine.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:14

**Q39. By what means do men lie in wait to deceive?**  
**Answer:** By the sleight of men, and cunning craftiness, whereby they lie in wait to deceive.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:14

---

### Ephesians 4:15

**Q40. What should we do speaking the truth?**  
**Answer:** But speaking the truth in love.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:15

**Q41. In what manner are we to speak the truth?**  
**Answer:** In love.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:15

**Q42. Into what are we to grow up in all things?**  
**Answer:** May grow up into him in all things, which is the head, even Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:15

---

### Ephesians 4:16

**Q43. From whom does the whole body fitly joined together and compacted take effect?**  
**Answer:** From whom the whole body fitly joined together and compacted.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:16

**Q44. By what is every joint supplied?**  
**Answer:** By that which every joint supplieth.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:16

**Q45. What does the working in the measure of every part cause?**  
**Answer:** Maketh increase of the body unto the edifying of itself in love.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:16

### Ephesians 4:17

**Q46. In what authority does Paul say this in the Lord?**  
**Answer:** This I say therefore, and testify in the Lord.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:17

**Answer:** That ye henceforth walk not as other Gentiles walk.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:17

**Q48. In the vanity of what do the other Gentiles walk?**  
**Answer:** In the vanity of their mind.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:17

**Q49. Where is the understanding of the Gentiles mentioned in verse 18?**

**Q50. From what are the Gentiles alienated?**  
**Answer:** Being alienated from the life of God.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 4:18
