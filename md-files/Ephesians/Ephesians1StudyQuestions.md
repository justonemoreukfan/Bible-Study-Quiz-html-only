# Ephesians 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ephesians 1:1

**Q1. Who wrote the epistle according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:1

**Q2. To whom is the epistle addressed?**  
**Answer:** To the saints which are at Ephesus, and to the faithful in Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:1

**Q3. By whose will did Paul become an apostle of Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** By the will of God.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:1

---

### Ephesians 1:2

**Q4. What is wished upon the recipients in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Grace be to you, and peace.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:2

**Q5. From whom are grace and peace stated to come?**  
**Answer:** From God our Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:2

**Q6. What title is given to Jesus Christ in this greeting?**  
**Answer:** Lord.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:2

---

### Ephesians 1:3

**Q7. Who is blessed according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** The God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:3

**Q8. How has God blessed us?**  
**Answer:** With all spiritual blessings.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:3

**Q9. Where are these spiritual blessings located?**  
**Answer:** In heavenly places in Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:3

---

### Ephesians 1:4

**Q10. According to this verse, when did God choose us in Him?**  
**Answer:** Before the foundation of the world.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:4

**Q11. What is our condition before Him according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** That we should be holy and without blame before him in love.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:4

**Q12. In whom were we chosen?**  
**Answer:** In him.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:4

---

### Ephesians 1:5

**Q13. Having predestinated us unto what did He destine us?**  
**Answer:** Unto the adoption of children by Jesus Christ to himself.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:5

**Q14. According to what did He predestinate us?**  
**Answer:** According to the good pleasure of his will.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:5

**Q15. Through whom does the adoption of children come?**  
**Answer:** By Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:5

---

### Ephesians 1:6

**Q16. To the praise of the glory of what is this to the adoption?**  
**Answer:** To the praise of the glory of his grace.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:6

**Q17. Wherein has He made us accepted?**  
**Answer:** In the beloved.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:6

**Q18. What kind of grace is mentioned in connection with His glory?**  
**Answer:** His grace.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:6

---

### Ephesians 1:7

**Q19. What do we have through His blood in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Redemption.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:7

**Q20. What is the specific result of this redemption regarding our sins?**  
**Answer:** The forgiveness of sins.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:7

**Q21. According to what are we granted this forgiveness of sins?**  
**Answer:** According to the riches of his grace.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:7

---

### Ephesians 1:8

**Q22. Toward whom has He abounded in all wisdom and prudence?**  
**Answer:** Toward us.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:8

**Q23. In what did He abound toward us?**  
**Answer:** In all wisdom and prudence.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:8

**Q24. What general category is used to describe the abundance mentioned here?**  
**Answer:** Wherein he hath abounded toward us.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:8

---

### Ephesians 1:9

**Q25. What has He made known unto us according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** The mystery of his will.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:9

**Q26. According to what did He make known this mystery?**  
**Answer:** According to his good pleasure.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:9

**Q27. Where did He purpose this mystery?**  
**Answer:** In himself.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:9

---

### Ephesians 1:10

**Q28. For what dispensation is this purpose intended?**  
**Answer:** In the dispensation of the fulness of times.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:10

**Q29. What does He purpose to gather together in one?**  
**Answer:** All things in Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:10

**Q30. Where are the things that He purposes to gather together in Christ?**  
**Answer:** Both which are in heaven, and which are on earth; even in him.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:10

---

### Ephesians 1:11

**Q31. In whom also have we obtained an inheritance?**  
**Answer:** In whom also we have obtained an inheritance.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:11

**Q32. How are we predestinated according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Being predestinated according to the purpose of him who worketh all things after the counsel of his own will.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:11

**Q33. Whose counsel is it that works all things?**  
**Answer:** The counsel of his own will.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:11

---

### Ephesians 1:12

**Q34. Who should be to the praise of His glory?**  
**Answer:** That we should be to the praise of his glory.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:12

**Q35. Who is singled out as having first trusted in Christ?**  
**Answer:** Who first trusted in Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:12

**Q36. Whose glory are we to be the praise of?**  
**Answer:** His glory.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:12

---

### Ephesians 1:13

**Q37. In whom did you also trust after hearing a specific message?**  
**Answer:** In whom ye also trusted, after that ye heard the word of truth.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:13

**Q38. What is the word of truth described as in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The gospel of your salvation.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:13

**Q39. With what were you sealed after that ye believed?**  
**Answer:** With that holy Spirit of promise.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:13

---

### Ephesians 1:14

**Q40. What is the Holy Spirit described as in relation to our inheritance?**  
**Answer:** The earnest of our inheritance.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:14

**Q41. Until what event is the earnest of our inheritance given?**  
**Answer:** Until the redemption of the purchased possession.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:14

**Q42. What ultimate praise is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Unto the praise of his glory.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:14

---

### Ephesians 1:15

**Q43. What did Paul hear of in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Your faith in the Lord Jesus.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:15

**Q44. Toward whom is the love mentioned in this verse directed?**  
**Answer:** Unto all the saints.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:15

**Q45. After hearing what did Paul make this mention?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore I also, after I heard of your faith in the Lord Jesus, and love unto all the saints.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:15

---

### Ephesians 1:16

**Q46. What does Paul never cease to do regarding the recipients?**  
**Answer:** Cease not to give thanks for you.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:16

**Q47. When does Paul make mention of them?**  
**Answer:** Making mention of you in my prayers.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:16

**Q48. Who is doing the giving of thanks and making mention?**  
**Answer:** Paul (I).  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:16

---

### Ephesians 1:17

**Q49. Whose God does Paul pray the God of our Lord Jesus Christ to be?**  
**Answer:** The God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:17

**Q50. What does Paul pray that God may give unto you?**  
**Answer:** The spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:17

**Q51. What title is given to God in relation to glory in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The Father of glory.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:17

---

### Ephesians 1:18

**Q52. What part of your understanding does Paul pray be enlightened?**  
**Answer:** The eyes of your understanding being enlightened.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:18

**Q53. What is one thing you may know regarding His calling?**  
**Answer:** What is the hope of his calling.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:18

**Q54. What else may you know regarding the saints?**  
**Answer:** What the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:18

---

### Ephesians 1:19

**Q55. What is said about the exceeding greatness of His power?**  
**Answer:** What is the exceeding greatness of his power to us-ward who believe.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:19

**Q56. According to what working is this power measured?**  
**Answer:** According to the working of his mighty power.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:19

**Q57. Toward whom is this exceeding greatness of power directed?**  
**Answer:** To us-ward who believe.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:19

---

### Ephesians 1:20

**Q58. In whom did He work this power?**  
**Answer:** When he wrought in Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:20

**Q59. From where did He raise Christ?**  
**Answer:** When he raised him from the dead.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:20

**Q60. Where did He set Christ after raising Him?**  
**Answer:** And set him at his own right hand in the heavenly places.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:20

---

### Ephesians 1:21

**Q61. Above what is Christ set in heavenly places?**  
**Answer:** Far above all principality, and power, and might, and dominion.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:21

**Q62. What is named besides every name in this world?**  
**Answer:** Every name that is named, not only in this world, but also in that which is to come.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:21

**Q63. What worlds are mentioned in connection with every name that is named?**  
**Answer:** This world, and also that which is to come.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:21

---

### Ephesians 1:22

**Q64. What has God put under Christ's feet?**  
**Answer:** And hath put all things under his feet.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:22

**Q65. To what position was He given over all things?**  
**Answer:** And gave him to be the head over all things.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:22

**Q66. To whom is He given as head over all things?**  
**Answer:** To the church.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:22

---

### Ephesians 1:23

**Q67. What is the church called in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Which is his body.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:23

**Q68. How is the church further described in relation to Him?**  
**Answer:** The fulness of him that filleth all in all.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:23

**Q69. Who is described as filling all in all?**  
**Answer:** Him that filleth all in all.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 1:23
