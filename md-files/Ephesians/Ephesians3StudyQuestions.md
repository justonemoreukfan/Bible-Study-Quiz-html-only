# Ephesians 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ephesians 3:1

**Q1. Who is the prisoner mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Paul the prisoner of Jesus Christ for you Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:1

**Q2. For whom is Paul the prisoner of Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** For you Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:1

**Q3. What is Paul's relationship to Jesus Christ in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The prisoner of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:1

---

### Ephesians 3:2

**Q2. What have ye heard of?**  
**Answer:** The dispensation of the grace of God.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:2

**Q2. To whom was the dispensation of the grace of God given regarding you?**  
**Answer:** Given me to you-ward.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:2

**Q3. What kind of dispensation is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The dispensation of the grace of God.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:2

---

### Ephesians 3:3

**Q7. How was the mystery made known unto Paul?**  
**Answer:** By revelation.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:3

**Q8. What was made known unto Paul by revelation?**  
**Answer:** The mystery.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:3

**Q9. As to what did Paul write afore in few words?**  
**Answer:** As I wrote afore in few words.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:3

---

### Ephesians 3:4

**Q10. What may ye understand when ye read?**  
**Answer:** My knowledge in the mystery of Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:4

**Q11. When may ye understand my knowledge?**  
**Answer:** When ye read.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:4

**Q12. Whose knowledge in the mystery of Christ is mentioned?**  
**Answer:** My knowledge (Paul's).  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:4

---

### Ephesians 3:5

**Q13. In what other ages was the mystery not made known?**  
**Answer:** In other ages.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:5

**Q14. Unto whom was the mystery not made known as it is now revealed?**  
**Answer:** Unto the sons of men.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:5

**Q15. By whom is the mystery now revealed unto his holy apostles and prophets?**  
**Answer:** By the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:5

---

### Ephesians 3:6

**Q16. That the Gentiles should be what?**  
**Answer:** Fellowheirs.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:6

**Q17. Of the same body, and partakers of what?**  
**Answer:** His promise in Christ by the gospel.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:6

**Q18. Through what medium are they partakers of his promise in Christ?**  
**Answer:** By the gospel.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:6

---

### Ephesians 3:7

**Q19. Whereof Paul was made what?**  
**Answer:** A minister.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:7

**Q20. According to what was Paul made a minister?**  
**Answer:** According to the gift of the grace of God.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:7

**Q21. How was the gift of the grace of God given unto him?**  
**Answer:** By the effectual working of his power.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:7

---

### Ephesians 3:8

**Q22. Unto who was this grace given, who is less than the least of all saints?**  
**Answer:** Unto me.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:8

**Q23. What should Paul preach among the Gentiles?**  
**Answer:** The unsearchable riches of Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:8

**Q24. Where was Paul to preach the unsearchable riches of Christ?**  
**Answer:** Among the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:8

---

### Ephesians 3:9

**Q25. And to make all men see what?**  
**Answer:** What is the fellowship of the mystery.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:9

**Q26. From what time has the mystery been hid in God?**  
**Answer:** From the beginning of the world.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:9

**Q27. Who created all things?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:9

---

### Ephesians 3:10

**Q28. To the intent that now unto the principalities and powers in heavenly places might be known by whom what?**  
**Answer:** The manifold wisdom of God.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:10

**Q29. By whom might the manifold wisdom of God be known?**  
**Answer:** By the church.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:10

**Q30. Where are the principalities and powers located?**  
**Answer:** In heavenly places.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:10

---

### Ephesians 3:11

**Q31. According to what eternal purpose was this done?**  
**Answer:** According to the eternal purpose.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:11

**Q32. In whom was the eternal purpose purposed?**  
**Answer:** In Christ Jesus our Lord.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:11

**Q33. What kind of purpose is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Eternal purpose.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:11

---

### Ephesians 3:12

**Q34. In whom have we boldness and access?**  
**Answer:** In whom we have boldness and access.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:12

**Q35. How do we have access?**  
**Answer:** With confidence.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:12

**Q36. By what means do we have access with confidence?**  
**Answer:** By the faith of him.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:12

---

### Ephesians 3:13

**Q37. What does Paul desire that ye faint not at?**  
**Answer:** At my tribulations for you.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:13

**Q38. For whom are Paul's tribulations?**  
**Answer:** For you.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:13

**Q39. What are Paul's tribulations considered to be for you?**  
**Answer:** Your glory.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:13

---

### Ephesians 3:14

**Q40. For this cause what does Paul do?**  
**Answer:** I bow my knees unto the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:14

**Q41. Unto whom does Paul bow his knees?**  
**Answer:** Unto the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:14

**Q42. What physical action does Paul take in this verse?**  
**Answer:** I bow my knees.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:14

---

### Ephesians 3:15

**Q43. Of whom is the whole family named?**  
**Answer:** Of whom the whole family in heaven and earth is named.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:15

**Q44. Where are the parts of the whole family located?**  
**Answer:** In heaven and earth.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:15

**Q45. What is the whole family in heaven and earth?**  
**Answer:** Named.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:15

---

### Ephesians 3:16

**Q46. According to what would he grant you to be strengthened?**  
**Answer:** According to the riches of his glory.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:16

**Q47. With what would he grant you to be strengthened?**  
**Answer:** With might.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:16

**Q48. Where would you be strengthened with might by his Spirit?**  
**Answer:** In the inner man.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:16

---

### Ephesians 3:17

**Q49. That Christ may dwell where by faith?**  
**Answer:** In your hearts.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:17

**Q50. That ye, being rooted and grounded in what, may be able to comprehend?**  
**Answer:** In love.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:17

**Q51. What two states describe being established in love?**  
**Answer:** Rooted and grounded.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:17

---

### Ephesians 3:18

**Q52. May be able to comprehend with all saints what?**  
**Answer:** What is the breadth, and length, and depth, and height.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:18

**Q53. With whom may ye be able to comprehend these dimensions?**  
**Answer:** With all saints.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:18

**Q54. What is the breadth, length, depth, and height of?**  
**Answer:** The love of Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:18

---

### Ephesians 3:19

**Q55. And to know what, which passeth knowledge?**  
**Answer:** The love of Christ.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:19

**Q56. That ye might be filled with what?**  
**Answer:** With all the fulness of God.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:19

**Q57. What does the love of Christ pass?**  
**Answer:** Knowledge.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:19

---

### Ephesians 3:20

**Q58. Unto whom is glory given in the church?**  
**Answer:** Unto him that is able to do exceeding abundantly above all that we ask or think.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:20

**Q59. According to what power that worketh in us?**  
**Answer:** According to the power that worketh in us.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:20

**Q60. What is God able to do above all that we ask or think?**  
**Answer:** Exceeding abundantly.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:20

---

### Ephesians 3:21

**Q61. Unto whom be glory in the church?**  
**Answer:** Unto him.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:21

**Q62. Where is glory to be given unto him throughout all ages?**  
**Answer:** In the church by Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:21

**Q63. Throughout what time span is glory to be given?**  
**Answer:** World without end. Amen.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 3:21
