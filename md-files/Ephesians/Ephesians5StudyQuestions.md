# Ephesians 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ephesians 5:1

**Q1. Who are we to be followers of?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:1

**Q2. Whose example should we follow as dear children?**  
**Answer:** As dear children.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:1

**Q3. What kind of children are we exhorted to be in relation to God?**  
**Answer:** Dear children.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:1

---

### Ephesians 5:2

**Q1. What example should we walk in?**  
**Answer:** As Christ also hath loved us.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:2

**Q2. What did Christ give for us?**  
**Answer:** Himself.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:2

**Q3. How is Christ's offering described in relation to God?**  
**Answer:** An offering and a sacrifice to God for a sweetsmelling savour.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:2

---

### Ephesians 5:3

**Q1. What things should not even be once named among the saints?**  
**Answer:** Fornication, and all uncleanness, or covetousness.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:3

**Q2. How should these things not be named among the saints, as becometh what?**  
**Answer:** Saints.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:3

**Q3. What specific type of uncleanness or greed is grouped together in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Covetousness.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:3

---

### Ephesians 5:4

**Q1. What kind of speech should not be named among believers?**  
**Answer:** Neither filthiness, nor foolish talking, nor jesting.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:4

**Q2. What are foolish talking and jesting described as?**  
**Answer:** Which are not convenient.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:4

**Q3. Instead of filthiness and foolish talking, what should be given?**  
**Answer:** Giving of thanks.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:4

---

### Ephesians 5:5

**Q1. What types of persons are stated to have no inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and of God?**  
**Answer:** No whoremonger, nor unclean person, nor covetous man.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:5

**Q2. What is a covetous man equated to in this verse?**  
**Answer:** An idolater.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:5

**Q3. Whose kingdom is it specified that these persons have no inheritance in?**  
**Answer:** The kingdom of Christ and of God.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:5

---

### Ephesians 5:6

**Q1. What should no man deceive you with?**  
**Answer:** With vain words.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:6

**Q2. Because of these things, what comes upon the children of disobedience?**  
**Answer:** The wrath of God.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:6

**Q3. Upon whom does the wrath of God come because of these things?**  
**Answer:** The children of disobedience.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:6

---

### Ephesians 5:7

**Q1. What are believers told not to be with the children of disobedience?**  
**Answer:** Partakers with them.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:7

**Q2. Whom should believers not be partakers with?**  
**Answer:** Them.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:7

**Q3. Based on the previous verse, who are "them" that believers should not be partakers with?**  
**Answer:** The children of disobedience.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:7

---

### Ephesians 5:8

**Q1. What were believers sometimes in the past?**  
**Answer:** Darkness.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:8

**Q2. What are believers now in the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Light.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:8

**Q3. How are believers instructed to walk in relation to their current state?**  
**Answer:** Walk as children of light.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:8

---

### Ephesians 5:9

**Q1. What is the fruit of the Spirit in all of?**  
**Answer:** All goodness and righteousness and truth.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:9

**Q2. What specific virtues make up the fruit of the Spirit mentioned here?**  
**Answer:** Goodness, righteousness, and truth.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:9

**Q3. What is goodness, righteousness, and truth called?**  
**Answer:** The fruit of the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:9

---

### Ephesians 5:10

**Q1. What are believers to prove concerning the Lord?**  
**Answer:** What is acceptable unto the Lord.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:10

**Q2. Whose approval is being sought when proving what is acceptable?**  
**Answer:** Unto the Lord.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:10

**Q3. What action are believers to take regarding what is acceptable to the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Proving what is acceptable.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:10

**Answer:** Reprove them.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:11

**Answer:** Unfruitful.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:11

---

### Ephesians 5:12

**Verse:** Ephesians 5:12

**Q2. Where are these shameful things done?**  
**Answer:** In secret.  
**Verse:** Ephesians 5:12
