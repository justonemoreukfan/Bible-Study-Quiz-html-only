# Colossians 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Colossians 1:1

**Q1. Who wrote this epistle according to the verse?**  
**Answer:** Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:1

**Q2. Who is joined with Paul in the opening of the epistle?**  
**Answer:** Timotheus our brother.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:1

**Q3. To whom is the epistle addressed?**  
**Answer:** To the saints and faithful brethren in Christ which are at Colosse.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:1

---

### Colossians 1:2

**Q1. What salutation is extended to the saints and faithful brethren at Colosse?**  
**Answer:** Grace be unto you, and peace, from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:2

---

### Colossians 1:3

**Q1. To whom do Paul and his companion give thanks?**  
**Answer:** To God and the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:3

---

### Colossians 1:4

**Q1. What had Paul and his companion heard of?**  
**Answer:** Your faith in Christ Jesus, and of the love which ye have to all the saints.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:4

---

### Colossians 1:5

**Q1. For what is the hope laid up for the saints in heaven?**  
**Answer:** For the hope which is laid up for you in heaven.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:5

---

### Colossians 1:6

**Q1. Where is the word of the truth of the gospel coming unto the saints?**  
**Answer:** As it is in all the world.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:6

---

### Colossians 1:7

**Q1. Who taught the saints concerning the grace of God in truth?**  
**Answer:** Epaphras our dear fellowservant.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:7

---

### Colossians 1:8

**Q1. What did Epaphras declare unto Paul and his companions?**  
**Answer:** Your love in the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:8

---

### Colossians 1:9

**Q1. What did Paul and his companions not cease to pray for the saints?**  
**Answer:** To pray for you, and to desire that ye might be filled with the knowledge of his will in all wisdom and spiritual understanding.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:9

---

### Colossians 1:10

**Q1. How are the saints instructed to walk worthy of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Unto all pleasing, being fruitful in every good work, and increasing in the knowledge of God.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:10

---

### Colossians 1:11

**Q1. With what power are the saints strengthened according to His glorious power?**  
**Answer:** Strengthened with all might, according to his glorious power.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:11

---

### Colossians 1:12

**Q1. Unto whom are the saints giving thanks?**  
**Answer:** Unto the Father, which hath made us meet to be partakers of the inheritance of the saints in light.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:12

---

### Colossians 1:13

**Q1. From what power has God delivered us?**  
**Answer:** From the power of darkness.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:13

---

### Colossians 1:14

**Q1. What do we have through His blood?**  
**Answer:** Redemption through his blood, even the forgiveness of sins.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:14

---

### Colossians 1:15

**Q1. Who is the image of the invisible God?**  
**Answer:** Who is the image of the invisible God.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:15

---

### Colossians 1:16

**Q1. What was created by Him?**  
**Answer:** All things, that are in heaven, and that are in earth, visible and invisible.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:16

---

### Colossians 1:17

**Q1. What is the relation of Christ to all things?**  
**Answer:** And he is before all things, and by him all things consist.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:17

---

### Colossians 1:18

**Q1. Of what body is He the head?**  
**Answer:** And he is the head of the body, the church.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:18

---

### Colossians 1:19

**Q1. What pleased the Father to dwell in Him?**  
**Answer:** That in him should all fulness dwell.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:19

---

### Colossians 1:20

**Q1. By what did He make peace?**  
**Answer:** Through the blood of his cross.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:20

---

### Colossians 1:21

**Q1. What were the saints sometime in time past?**  
**Answer:** Enemies in your mind by wicked works.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:21

---

### Colossians 1:22

**Q1. In what body has He reconciled the saints?**  
**Answer:** In the body of his flesh through death.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:22

---

### Colossians 1:23

**Q1. How must the saints continue in the faith?**  
**Answer:** Grounded and settled, and be not moved away from the hope of the gospel.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:23

---

### Colossians 1:24

**Q1. In what does Paul now rejoice?**  
**Answer:** In my sufferings for you.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:24

---

### Colossians 1:25

**Q1. Of what church is Paul made a minister?**  
**Answer:** Whereof I am made a minister, according to the dispensation of God which is given to me for you.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:25

---

### Colossians 1:26

**Q1. What is the mystery spoken of in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Even the mystery which hath been hid from ages and from generations.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:26

---

### Colossians 1:27

**Q1. To whom would God make known the riches of the glory of this mystery?**  
**Answer:** To whom God would make known what is the riches of the glory of this mystery among the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:27

---

### Colossians 1:28

**Q1. Whom do Paul and his companions preach?**  
**Answer:** Whom we preach, warning every man, and teaching every man in all wisdom.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:28

---

### Colossians 1:29

**Q1. How does Paul labour according to His working?**  
**Answer:** Whereunto I also labour, striving according to his working, which worketh in me mightily.  
**Verse:** Colossians 1:29
