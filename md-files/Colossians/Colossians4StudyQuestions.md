# Colossians 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Colossians 4:1

**Q1. What are masters instructed to give unto their servants?**  
**Answer:** That which is just and equal.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:1

**Q2. What should masters know that they have?**  
**Answer:** A Master in heaven.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:1

**Q3. Who are masters commanded to give just and equal things unto?**  
**Answer:** Their servants.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:1

---

### Colossians 4:2

**Q4. What should believers continue in?**  
**Answer:** Prayer.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:2

**Q5. How are believers to watch in the same?**  
**Answer:** With thanksgiving.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:2

**Q6. What attitude should accompany watching in prayer?**  
**Answer:** Thanksgiving.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:2

---

### Colossians 4:3

**Q7. For whom should believers pray alongside themselves?**  
**Answer:** Us also.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:3

**Q8. What would God open unto them?**  
**Answer:** A door of utterance.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:3

**Q9. What mystery are they to speak?**  
**Answer:** The mystery of Christ.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:3

---

### Colossians 4:4

**Q10. What is the speaker's goal regarding the mystery of Christ?**  
**Answer:** That I may make it manifest.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:4

**Q11. How does the speaker ought to speak?**  
**Answer:** As I ought to speak.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:4

**Q12. What subject is to be made manifest as he ought to speak?**  
**Answer:** The mystery of Christ (for which I am also in bonds).  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:4

---

### Colossians 4:5

**Q13. How should believers walk toward those who are without?**  
**Answer:** In wisdom.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:5

**Q14. What should believers redeem regarding their time?**  
**Answer:** The time.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:5

**Q15. Toward whom should believers walk in wisdom?**  
**Answer:** Them that are without.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:5

---

### Colossians 4:6

**Q16. How should your speech always be?**  
**Answer:** With grace.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:6

**Q17. With what seasoning should speech be flavored?**  
**Answer:** Seasoned with salt.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:6

**Q18. What should you know how to answer?**  
**Answer:** How ye ought to answer every man.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:6

---

### Colossians 4:7

**Q19. Who will declare all my state unto you?**  
**Answer:** Tychicus.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:7

**Q20. What is Tychicus described as in the Lord?**  
**Answer:** A beloved brother.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:7

**Q21. What offices does Tychicus hold besides a beloved brother?**  
**Answer:** A faithful minister and fellowservant.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:7

---

### Colossians 4:8

**Q22. For what specific purpose have I sent Tychicus unto you?**  
**Answer:** That he might know your estate, and comfort your hearts.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:8

**Q23. Unto whom was Tychicus sent for this purpose?**  
**Answer:** Unto you.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:8

**Q24. Whose estate and heart comfort is the focus of sending Tychicus?**  
**Answer:** Your estate and your hearts.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:8

---

### Colossians 4:9

**Q25. Who is sent along with Tychicus?**  
**Answer:** Onesimus.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:9

**Q26. How is Onesimus described?**  
**Answer:** A faithful and beloved brother.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:9

**Q27. Where is Onesimus from?**  
**Answer:** Who is one of you.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:9

---

### Colossians 4:10

**Q28. Who salutes you along with others?**  
**Answer:** Aristarchus my fellowprisoner.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:10

**Q29. Whose sister's son sends salutations?**  
**Answer:** Marcus, sister's son to Barnabas.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:10

**Q30. What instructions were received concerning Marcus?**  
**Answer:** Concerning whom ye received commandments: if he come unto you, receive him.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:10

---

### Colossians 4:11

**Q31. What is Jesus, who is called Justus, identified as?**  
**Answer:** Which is of the circumcision.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:11

**Q32. Who are the only ones of the circumcision that are fellowworkers unto the kingdom of God?**  
**Answer:** These only.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:11

**Q33. What effect did these fellowworkers have on the speaker?**  
**Answer:** Which have been a comfort unto me.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:11

---

### Colossians 4:12

**Q34. Who salutes you, described as one of you?**  
**Answer:** Epaphras.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:12

**Q35. Whose servant is Epaphras?**  
**Answer:** A servant of Christ.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:12

**Q36. How does Epaphras always labor for you in prayers?**  
**Answer:** Fervently praying for you.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:12

---

### Colossians 4:13

**Q37. What does the speaker bear record of concerning Epaphras?**  
**Answer:** That he hath a great zeal for you.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:13

**Q38. In what two places besides Laodicea are those for whom he has zeal?**  
**Answer:** And them in Hierapolis.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:13

**Q39. Who are the people in Laodicea and Hierapolis that Epaphras has zeal for?**  
**Answer:** And them that are in Laodicea.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:13

---

### Colossians 4:14

**Q40. Who is the beloved physician that salutes you?**  
**Answer:** Luke.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:14

**Q41. Who is mentioned alongside Luke in the salutations?**  
**Answer:** And Demas.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:14

**Q42. Who is mentioned as a beloved person along with Luke?**  
**Answer:** Luke, the beloved physician.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:14

---

### Colossians 4:15

**Q43. To whom should you send salutations?**  
**Answer:** The brethren which are in Laodicea.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:15

**Q44. Who is mentioned specifically alongside the brethren in Laodicea?**  
**Answer:** And Nymphas.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:15

**Q45. Where is the church that is in Nymphas' house located?**  
**Answer:** And the church which is in his house.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:15

---

### Colossians 4:16

**Q46. When this epistle is read among you, what should be done with it?**  
**Answer:** Cause that it be read also in the church of the Laodiceans.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:16

**Q47. What other epistle should you read in like manner?**  
**Answer:** And that ye likewise read the epistle from Laodicea.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:16

**Q48. Where should the epistle from Laodicea be read?**  
**Answer:** Among you.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:16

---

### Colossians 4:17

**Q49. What instruction is given to Archippus?**  
**Answer:** Take heed to the ministry which thou hast received in the Lord.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:17

**Q50. In whom has Archippus received his ministry?**  
**Answer:** In the Lord.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:17

**Q51. What must Archippus do with his ministry?**  
**Answer:** That thou fulfil it.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:17

---

### Colossians 4:18

**Q52. By whose hand is the salutation written?**  
**Answer:** The salutation by the hand of me Paul.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:18

**Q53. What reminder is given at the end of the epistle?**  
**Answer:** Remember my bonds.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:18

**Q54. What is the final wish expressed in the verse?**  
**Answer:** Grace be with you. Amen.  
**Verse:** Colossians 4:18
