# Colossians 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Colossians 2:1

**Q1. What did the apostle have for the believers?**  
**Answer:** Great conflict.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:1

**Q2. For whom did he have this great conflict?**  
**Answer:** For them at Laodicea, and for as many as have not seen his face in the flesh.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:1

**Q3. Whose face in the flesh had they not seen?**  
**Answer:** His face in the flesh.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:1

---

### Colossians 2:2

**Q1. What might be comforted?**  
**Answer:** Their hearts.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:2

**Q2. How were their hearts to be knit together?**  
**Answer:** In love.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:2

**Q3. Unto what riches were they to attain?**  
**Answer:** The full assurance of understanding.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:2

---

### Colossians 2:3

**Q1. What are hid in him?**  
**Answer:** All the treasures of wisdom and knowledge.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:3

**Q2. In whom are these treasures hid?**  
**Answer:** In him.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:3

**Q3. What kind of treasures of wisdom and knowledge are in him?**  
**Answer:** All the treasures.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:3

---

### Colossians 2:4

**Q1. Who should beguile them?**  
**Answer:** Any man.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:4

**Q2. With what should no man beguile them?**  
**Answer:** Enticing words.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:4

**Q3. Why should no man beguile them with enticing words?**  
**Answer:** Lest any man should beguile you with enticing words.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:4

---

### Colossians 2:5

**Q1. Though the writer is absent in what, yet is he with them in the spirit?**  
**Answer:** In the flesh.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:5

**Q2. What did he joy and behold?**  
**Answer:** Your order, and the stedfastness of your faith in Christ.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:5

**Q3. Where was he with them?**  
**Answer:** In the spirit.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:5

---

### Colossians 2:6

**Q1. As you have therefore received whom, so walk ye in him?**  
**Answer:** Christ Jesus the Lord.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:6

**Q2. How are they to walk in him?**  
**Answer:** As ye have therefore received Christ Jesus the Lord.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:6

**Q3. Who are they to walk in?**  
**Answer:** Him.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:6

---

### Colossians 2:7

**Q1. How were they to be rooted?**  
**Answer:** Rooted and built up in him.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:7

**Q2. In what were they to be stablished?**  
**Answer:** The faith.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:7

**Q3. With what were they to abound in it?**  
**Answer:** Thanksgiving.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:7

---

### Colossians 2:8

**Q1. Who should spoil them?**  
**Answer:** Any man.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:8

**Q2. Through what should no man spoil them?**  
**Answer:** Philosophy and vain deceit.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:8

**Q3. After what is the tradition of men?**  
**Answer:** After the rudiments of the world, and not after Christ.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:8

---

### Colossians 2:9

**Q1. What dwells in him bodily?**  
**Answer:** All the fulness of the Godhead.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:9

**Q2. In whom does all the fulness of the Godhead dwell?**  
**Answer:** In him.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:9

**Q3. How does the fulness of the Godhead dwell in him?**  
**Answer:** Bodily.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:9

---

### Colossians 2:10

**Q1. In whom are ye complete?**  
**Answer:** In him.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:10

**Q2. Who is the head of all principality and power?**  
**Answer:** Which is the head of all principality and power.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:10

**Q3. What are they in him?**  
**Answer:** Complete.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:10

---

### Colossians 2:11

**Q1. In whom also ye are circumcised?**  
**Answer:** In him.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:11

**Q2. What kind of circumcision was it?**  
**Answer:** With the circumcision made without hands.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:11

**Q3. Whose circumcision is it?**  
**Answer:** The circumcision of Christ.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:11

---

### Colossians 2:12

**Q1. With whom were ye buried?**  
**Answer:** With him in baptism.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:12

**Q2. Wherein also ye are risen with whom?**  
**Answer:** Him.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:12

**Q3. Through what operation are ye risen?**  
**Answer:** Through the faith of the operation of God.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:12

---

### Colossians 2:13

**Q1. When you were dead in your sins, what did he do with you?**  
**Answer:** Quickened together with him.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:13

**Q2. What had he forgiven you?**  
**Answer:** All trespasses.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:13

**Q3. What was the uncircumcision of?**  
**Answer:** Your flesh.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:13

---

### Colossians 2:14

**Q1. What was blotted out?**  
**Answer:** The handwriting of ordinances that was against us.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:14

**Q2. Where did he take it?**  
**Answer:** Nailing it to his cross.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:14

**Q3. What was contrary to us?**  
**Answer:** The handwriting of ordinances.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:14

---

### Colossians 2:15

**Q1. What did he spoil?**  
**Answer:** Principalities and powers.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:15

**Q2. What did he make of them openly?**  
**Answer:** A shew.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:15

**Q3. Over whom did he triumph in it?**  
**Answer:** Over them.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:15

---

### Colossians 2:16

**Q1. Who should judge you in meat or in drink?**  
**Answer:** No man.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:16

**Q2. In respect of what feast should no man judge you?**  
**Answer:** An holyday, or of the new moon, or of the sabbath days.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:16

**Q3. What should no man do regarding these things?**  
**Answer:** Judge you.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:16

---

### Colossians 2:17

**Q1. What are these things?**  
**Answer:** A shadow of things to come.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:17

**Q2. Whose body is of Christ?**  
**Answer:** But the body is of Christ.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:17

**Q3. What are things to come compared to?**  
**Answer:** A shadow.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:17

---

### Colossians 2:18

**Q1. Who should beguile you of your reward?**  
**Answer:** No man.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:18

**Q2. In what did he intrude?**  
**Answer:** In those things which he hath not seen.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:18

**Q3. By what was he puffed up?**  
**Answer:** His fleshly mind.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:18

---

### Colossians 2:19

**Q1. From whom not holding the Head?**  
**Answer:** And not holding the Head.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:19

**Q2. What does all the body do?**  
**Answer:** Increaseth with the increase of God.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:19

**Q3. How is the body nourished and knit together?**  
**Answer:** By joints and bands.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:19

---

### Colossians 2:20

**Q1. With whom were ye dead?**  
**Answer:** With Christ.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:20

**Q2. From what were ye dead with Christ?**  
**Answer:** The rudiments of the world.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:20

**Q3. As though living in the world, what are ye subject to?**  
**Answer:** Ordinances.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:20

---

### Colossians 2:21

**Q1. What should you not touch?**  
**Answer:** Touch not.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:21

**Q2. What should you not taste?**  
**Answer:** Taste not.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:21

**Q3. What should you not handle?**  
**Answer:** Handle not.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:21

---

### Colossians 2:22

**Q1. What are all to perish?**  
**Answer:** Which things are all to perish with the using.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:22

**Q2. After whose commandments and doctrines are these things?**  
**Answer:** After the commandments and doctrines of men.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:22

**Q3. With what do these things perish?**  
**Answer:** With the using.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:22

---

### Colossians 2:23

**Q1. Which things have indeed what?**  
**Answer:** A shew of wisdom.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:23

**Q2. In what do these things have a shew of wisdom?**  
**Answer:** Will worship, and humility, and neglecting of the body.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:23

**Q3. To what are these things not in any honour?**  
**Answer:** To the satisfying of the flesh.  
**Verse:** Colossians 2:23
