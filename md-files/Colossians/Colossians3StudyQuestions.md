# Colossians 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Colossians 3:1

**Q1. If ye then be risen with Christ, what should men seek?**  
**Answer:** Those things which are above.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:1

**Q2. Where does Christ sit?**  
**Answer:** On the right hand of God.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:1

**Q3. What conditional state concerning Christ precedes seeking the things above?**  
**Answer:** If ye then be risen with Christ.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:1

---

### Colossians 3:2

**Q4. What kind of things should men set their affection on?**  
**Answer:** Things above.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:2

**Q5. What kind of things should men not set their affection on?**  
**Answer:** Things on the earth.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:2

**Q6. What action are men commanded to take regarding things above?**  
**Answer:** Set your affection on them.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:2

---

### Colossians 3:3

**Q7. What happened to the reader in relation to life?**  
**Answer:** Ye are dead.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:3

**Q8. With whom is the life hid?**  
**Answer:** With Christ in God.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:3

**Q9. What is hid with Christ in God?**  
**Answer:** Your life.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:3

---

### Colossians 3:4

**Q10. Who is our life?**  
**Answer:** Christ.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:4

**Q11. What action will Christ take regarding our life?**  
**Answer:** Shall appear.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:4

**Q12. How will the saints appear with Christ?**  
**Answer:** In glory.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:4

---

### Colossians 3:5

**Q13. What should men mortify upon the earth?**  
**Answer:** Your members.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:5

**Q14. Name the first three sins listed in this verse to be mortified.**  
**Answer:** Fornication, uncleanness, inordinate affection.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:5

**Q15. What evil desire and sin are listed at the end of the verse?**  
**Answer:** Evil concupiscence, and covetousness, which is idolatry.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:5

---

### Colossians 3:6

**Q16. For which things does the wrath of God come?**  
**Answer:** For which things' sake the wrath of God cometh.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:6

**Q17. Upon whom does the wrath of God come?**  
**Answer:** On the children of disobedience.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:6

**Q18. Whose wrath comes on the children of disobedience?**  
**Answer:** The wrath of God.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:6

---

### Colossians 3:7

**Q19. In what did the readers walk some time?**  
**Answer:** In the same.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:7

**Q20. When did the readers walk in those things?**  
**Answer:** In the which ye walked sometime.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:7

**Q21. What did the readers do when they lived in those things?**  
**Answer:** Ye lived in them.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:7

---

### Colossians 3:8

**Q22. What should men now put off?**  
**Answer:** All these.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:8

**Q23. Name the first three attitudes to put off in this verse.**  
**Answer:** Anger, wrath, malice.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:8

**Q24. Name the remaining two communication sins to put off.**  
**Answer:** Blasphemy, filthy communication out of your mouth.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:8

---

### Colossians 3:9

**Q25. What should men not do to one another?**  
**Answer:** Lie not one to another.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:9

**Q26. What has been put off?**  
**Answer:** The old man.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:9

**Q27. With what has the old man been put off?**  
**Answer:** With his deeds.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:9

---

### Colossians 3:10

**Q28. What has been put on?**  
**Answer:** The new man.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:10

**Q29. In what state is the new man renewed?**  
**Answer:** Renewed in knowledge.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:10

**Q30. After whose image is the new man renewed?**  
**Answer:** After the image of him that created him.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:10

---

### Colossians 3:11

**Q31. Where is there neither Greek nor Jew?**  
**Answer:** Where there is neither Greek nor Jew.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:11

**Q32. What physical status is listed alongside circumcision and uncircumcision?**  
**Answer:** Barbarian, Scythian, bond nor free.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:11

**Q33. Who is all, and in all?**  
**Answer:** Christ.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:11

---

### Colossians 3:12

**Q34. As what kind of people should men put on bowels of mercies?**  
**Answer:** As the elect of God, holy and beloved.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:12

**Q35. Name the first two personal traits to put on in this verse.**  
**Answer:** Bowels of mercies, kindness.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:12

**Q36. Name the remaining three traits to put on.**  
**Answer:** Humbleness of mind, meekness, longsuffering.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:12

---

### Colossians 3:13

**Q37. What should men do with one another?**  
**Answer:** Forbearing one another.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:13

**Q38. What should men do if any man has a quarrel against any?**  
**Answer:** Forgiving one another.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:13

**Q39. As who forgave you, so also should ye do?**  
**Answer:** Even as Christ forgave you.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:13

---

### Colossians 3:14

**Q40. Above all these things, what should men put on?**  
**Answer:** Charity.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:14

**Q41. What is charity described as?**  
**Answer:** The bond of perfectness.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:14

**Q42. Where does charity fit in relation to all these things?**  
**Answer:** Above all these things.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:14

---

### Colossians 3:15

**Q43. What should rule in your hearts?**  
**Answer:** The peace of God.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:15

**Q44. To what are men called in one body?**  
**Answer:** To which also ye are called in one body.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:15

**Q45. What attitude should men be regarding peace?**  
**Answer:** And be ye thankful.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:15

---

### Colossians 3:16

**Q46. What should dwell in you richly?**  
**Answer:** The word of Christ.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:16

**Q47. How should men teach and admonish one another?**  
**Answer:** In all wisdom.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:16

**Q48. How should men sing with grace in their hearts to the Lord?**  
**Answer:** In psalms and hymns and spiritual songs.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:16

---

### Colossians 3:17

**Q49. Whatsoever ye do in word or deed, how should it be done?**  
**Answer:** Do all in the name of the Lord Jesus.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:17

**Q50. Through whom should men give thanks to God and the Father?**  
**Answer:** Giving thanks to God and the Father by him.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:17

**Q51. What should be done in the name of the Lord Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever ye do in word or deed.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:17

---

### Colossians 3:18

**Q52. Who are wives commanded to submit themselves unto?**  
**Answer:** Unto your own husbands.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:18

**Q53. Where is this submission fit?**  
**Answer:** As it is fit in the Lord.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:18

**Q54. Who is addressed in this verse regarding submission?**  
**Answer:** Wives.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:18

---

### Colossians 3:19

**Q55. Who is addressed in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Husbands.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:19

**Q56. What are husbands commanded to do to their wives?**  
**Answer:** Love your wives.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:19

**Q57. What should husbands not be against their wives?**  
**Answer:** And be not bitter against them.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:19

---

### Colossians 3:20

**Q58. Who is addressed in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Children.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:20

**Q59. What are children commanded to do?**  
**Answer:** Obey your parents in all things.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:20

**Q60. Why is this obedience commanded?**  
**Answer:** For this is well pleasing unto the Lord.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:20

---

### Colossians 3:21

**Q61. Who is addressed in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Fathers.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:21

**Q62. What should fathers not do to their children?**  
**Answer:** Provoke not your children to anger.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:21

**Q63. What negative condition might occur if children are provoked?**  
**Answer:** Lest they be discouraged.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:21

---

### Colossians 3:22

**Q64. Who is addressed concerning obedience in earthly matters?**  
**Answer:** Servants.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:22

**Q65. Whom should servants obey in all things?**  
**Answer:** Your masters according to the flesh.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:22

**Q66. With what kind of service should servants not serve?**  
**Answer:** Not with eyeservice, as menpleasers.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:22

---

### Colossians 3:23

**Q67. Whatsoever ye do, how should it be done?**  
**Answer:** Do it heartily.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:23

**Q68. As unto whom should the work be done?**  
**Answer:** As to the Lord.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:23

**Q69. As unto whom should the work not be done?**  
**Answer:** And not unto men.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:23

---

### Colossians 3:24

**Q70. What shall the servant receive of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** The reward of the inheritance.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:24

**Q71. Whom do servants serve?**  
**Answer:** For ye serve the Lord Christ.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:24

**Q72. What are servants serving in relation to the Lord?**  
**Answer:** The Lord Christ.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:24

---

### Colossians 3:25

**Q73. What will happen to him that does wrong?**  
**Answer:** He that doeth wrong shall receive for the wrong which he hath done.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:25

**Q74. Who receives for the wrong that was done?**  
**Answer:** He that doeth wrong.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:25

**Q75. What is there none of with God?**  
**Answer:** And there is no respect of persons.  
**Verse:** Colossians 3:25
