# 1 Thessalonians 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:1

**Q1. What did the brethren have no need of according to Paul?**  
**Answer:** That I write unto you.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:1

**Q2. Of what did Paul not need to write to the brethren?**  
**Answer:** Of the times and the seasons.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:1

**Q3. Who did Paul state had no need of him writing about times and seasons?**  
**Answer:** The brethren.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:1

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:2

**Q1. What do the brethren yourselves know perfectly?**  
**Answer:** That the day of the Lord so cometh as a thief in the night.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:2

**Q2. How does the day of the Lord come?**  
**Answer:** As a thief in the night.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:2

**Q3. Who knows perfectly that the day of the Lord cometh as a thief in the night?**  
**Answer:** Yourselves.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:2

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:3

**Q1. What shall they say when they shall say, Peace and safety?**  
**Answer:** Then sudden destruction cometh upon them.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:3

**Q2. Upon whom does sudden destruction come?**  
**Answer:** Upon them.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:3

**Q3. As what does travail come upon a woman with child?**  
**Answer:** As birth pains / as travail upon a woman with child (they shall not escape).  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:3

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:4

**Q1. Who are not in darkness?**  
**Answer:** Brethren.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:4

**Q2. What should that day not overtake as a thief?**  
**Answer:** You.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:4

**Q3. Why should that day not overtake you as a thief?**  
**Answer:** Because ye are not in darkness.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:4

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:5

**Q1. What are ye all?**  
**Answer:** The children of light, and the children of the day.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:5

**Q2. What are we not?**  
**Answer:** Of the night, nor of darkness.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:5

**Q3. Whose children are ye?**  
**Answer:** The children of light, and the children of the day.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:5

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:6

**Q1. Therefore what should we not do as others?**  
**Answer:** Sleep.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:6

**Q2. What should we do instead of sleeping?**  
**Answer:** Watch and be sober.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:6

**Q3. Who should watch and be sober?**  
**Answer:** Let us (we).  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:6

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:7

**Q1. When do they that sleep, sleep?**  
**Answer:** In the night.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:7

**Q2. When are they that be drunken, drunken?**  
**Answer:** In the night.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:7

**Q3. What do they that sleep, do in the night?**  
**Answer:** Sleep.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:7

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:8

**Q1. Who are of the day?**  
**Answer:** Let us.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:8

**Q2. What should we be, being of the day?**  
**Answer:** Sober.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:8

**Q3. What should we put on?**  
**Answer:** The breastplate of faith and love; and for an helmet, the hope of salvation.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:8

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:9

**Q1. For what have we not been appointed by God?**  
**Answer:** Unto wrath.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:9

**Q2. Unto what have we been appointed by God?**  
**Answer:** To obtain salvation.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:9

**Q3. Through whom do we obtain salvation?**  
**Answer:** By our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:9

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:10

**Q1. Who died for us?**  
**Answer:** Our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:10

**Q2. Why did Christ die for us?**  
**Answer:** That, whether we wake or sleep, we should live together with him.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:10

**Q3. Together with whom should we live whether we wake or sleep?**  
**Answer:** With him (Jesus Christ).  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:10

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:11

**Q1. What should ye do yourselves together?**  
**Answer:** Comfort yourselves together, and edify one another.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:11

**Q2. What should ye do to one another?**  
**Answer:** Edify one another.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:11

**Q3. Even as what do ye also do?**  
**Answer:** Even as also ye do.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:11

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:12

**Q1. What do we beseech you to do, brethren?**  
**Answer:** To know them which labour among you.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:12

**Q2. Who are over you in the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Them which labour among you, and are over you in the Lord, and admonish you.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:12

**Q3. What do those who are over you in the Lord do?**  
**Answer:** Admonish you.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:12

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:13

**Q1. How should you esteem them very highly in love?**  
**Answer:** For their work's sake.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:13

**Q2. Whom should ye esteem very highly in love?**  
**Answer:** Them.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:13

**Q3. What should ye be among yourselves?**  
**Answer:** At peace among yourselves.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:13

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:14

**Q1. Who should we exhort, brethren?**  
**Answer:** Warn them that are unruly.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:14

**Q2. Whom should we comfort?**  
**Answer:** The feebleminded.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:14

**Q3. Toward whom should we be patient?**  
**Answer:** Toward all men.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:14

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:15

**Q1. What should none render unto any man?**  
**Answer:** Evil for evil.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:15

**Q2. What should ye ever follow?**  
**Answer:** That which is good.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:15

**Q3. Unto whom should ye ever follow that which is good?**  
**Answer:** Both among yourselves, and to all men.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:15

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:16

**Q1. What should you do always?**  
**Answer:** Rejoice evermore.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:16

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:17

**Q1. What should you do without ceasing?**  
**Answer:** Pray without ceasing.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:17

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:18

**Q1. In what should you give thanks?**  
**Answer:** In everything.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:18

**Q2. What is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you?**  
**Answer:** For this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:18

**Q3. What should you do in everything?**  
**Answer:** Give thanks.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:18

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:19

**Q1. What should you not quench?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:19

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:20

**Q1. What should you not despise?**  
**Answer:** Despise not prophesyings.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:20

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:21

**Q1. What should you do to all things?**  
**Answer:** Prove all things.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:21

**Q2. What should you do after proving all things?**  
**Answer:** Hold fast that which is good.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:21

**Q3. What kind of things should you hold fast?**  
**Answer:** That which is good.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:21

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:22

**Q1. From what should you abstain?**  
**Answer:** From all appearance of evil.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:22

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:23

**Q1. Who Himself should sanctify you wholly?**  
**Answer:** The very God of peace.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:23

**Q2. What parts of you are prayed to be preserved blameless unto the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** Your whole spirit and soul and body.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:23

**Q3. Until when should your whole spirit and soul and body be preserved blameless?**  
**Answer:** Unto the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:23

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:24

**Q1. Who is faithful?**  
**Answer:** He that calleth you.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:24

**Q2. What will He also do?**  
**Answer:** He will also do it.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:24

**Q3. Who does "He that calleth you" refer to in context?**  
**Answer:** The one who calls you (God).  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:24

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:25

**Q1. For whom should the brethren pray?**  
**Answer:** Us.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:25

**Q2. Who is asked to pray for us?**  
**Answer:** Brethren.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:25

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:26

**Q1. How should all the brethren be greeted?**  
**Answer:** With an holy kiss.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:26

**Q2. Who should be greeted with an holy kiss?**  
**Answer:** All the brethren.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:26

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:27

**Q1. By whom is the Lord charged that this epistle be read?**  
**Answer:** I charge you by the Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:27

**Q2. To whom is the epistle to be read?**  
**Answer:** All the holy brethren.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:27

**Q3. What is charged to be read unto all the holy brethren?**  
**Answer:** This epistle.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:27

---

### 1 Thessalonians 5:28

**Q1. What should be with you?**  
**Answer:** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:28

**Q2. What is said at the very end of the verse?**  
**Answer:** Amen.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 5:28
