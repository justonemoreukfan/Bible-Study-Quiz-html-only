# 1 Thessalonians 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Thessalonians 4:1

**Q1. Furthermore then what did the apostles beseech and exhort the brethren by the Lord Jesus?**  
**Answer:** That as ye have received of us how ye ought to walk and to please God, so ye would abound more and more.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:1

**Q2. By whom did the apostles beseech and exhort the brethren?**  
**Answer:** By the Lord Jesus.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:1

**Q3. How were they to walk and to please God in relation to how they received of the apostles?**  
**Answer:** So ye would abound more and more.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:1

---

### 1 Thessalonians 4:2

**Q4. For what do ye know?**  
**Answer:** What commandments we gave you by the Lord Jesus.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:2

**Q5. By whom were the commandments given?**  
**Answer:** By the Lord Jesus.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:2

**Q6. What do ye know concerning the commandments?**  
**Answer:** What commandments we gave you by the Lord Jesus.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:2

---

### 1 Thessalonians 4:3

**Q7. What is the will of God?**  
**Answer:** Even your sanctification.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:3

**Q8. From what should ye abstain according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** From fornication.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:3

**Q9. Whose will is your sanctification?**  
**Answer:** God's will.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:3

---

### 1 Thessalonians 4:4

**Q10. That every one of you should know how to do what with his vessel?**  
**Answer:** Possess his vessel in sanctification and honour.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:4

**Q11. In what two things should every one of you know how to possess his vessel?**  
**Answer:** In sanctification and honour.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:4

**Q12. What should every one of you know how to possess?**  
**Answer:** His vessel.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:4

---

### 1 Thessalonians 4:5

**Q13. In what manner should they not possess their vessel?**  
**Answer:** In the lust of concupiscence.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:5

**Q14. Who are the people that know not God?**  
**Answer:** The Gentiles.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:5

**Q15. What do the Gentiles do that is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Know not God.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:5

---

### 1 Thessalonians 4:6

**Q16. Who should not go beyond and defraud his brother in any matter?**  
**Answer:** That no man go beyond and defraud his brother in any matter.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:6

**Q17. Who is the avenger of all such?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:6

**Q18. As what has the Lord also forewarned and testified?**  
**Answer:** As we also have forewarned you and testified.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:6

---

### 1 Thessalonians 4:7

**Q19. Unto what has God not called us?**  
**Answer:** Unto uncleanness.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:7

**Q20. Unto what has God called us?**  
**Answer:** Unto holiness.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:7

**Q21. Who has called us unto holiness rather than uncleanness?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:7

---

### 1 Thessalonians 4:8

**Q22. Therefore what does he that despiseth?**  
**Answer:** Despiseth not man, but God.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:8

**Q23. Who has also given unto us his Holy Spirit?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:8

**Q24. Unto whom has God given his Holy Spirit?**  
**Answer:** Unto us.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:8

---

### 1 Thessalonians 4:9

**Q25. As touching what do ye not need that I write unto you?**  
**Answer:** As touching brotherly love.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:9

**Q26. Who taught you to love one another?**  
**Answer:** Ye yourselves are taught of God to love one another.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:9

**Q27. Whom are ye taught of God to love?**  
**Answer:** One another.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:9

---

### 1 Thessalonians 4:10

**Q28. Towards whom do ye do this?**  
**Answer:** Toward all the brethren which are in all Macedonia.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:10

**Q29. What do we beseech you to do, brethren?**  
**Answer:** That ye increase more and more.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:10

**Q30. Where are the brethren located who are mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** In all Macedonia.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:10

---

### 1 Thessalonians 4:11

**Q31. What do we beseech you to study?**  
**Answer:** To be quiet.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:11

**Q32. Whose own business are ye commanded to do?**  
**Answer:** Your own business.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:11

**Q33. With whose hands are ye commanded to work?**  
**Answer:** With your own hands.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:11

---

### 1 Thessalonians 4:12

**Q34. Toward whom may ye walk honestly?**  
**Answer:** Toward them that are without.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:12

**Q35. What may ye have of any?**  
**Answer:** Lack of nothing.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:12

**Q36. How should ye walk toward them that are without?**  
**Answer:** Honestly.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:12

---

### 1 Thessalonians 4:13

**Q37. What would the brethren not have you to be concerning them which are asleep?**  
**Answer:** Ignorant.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:13

**Q38. Why should ye not sorrow, even as others which have no hope?**  
**Answer:** That ye sorrow not, even as others which have no hope.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:13

**Q39. Who are described as having no hope?**  
**Answer:** Others (them which are asleep contextually/others).  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:13

---

### 1 Thessalonians 4:14

**Q40. What do we believe if we believe that Jesus died and rose again?**  
**Answer:** Even so them also which sleep in Jesus will God bring with him.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:14

**Q41. What happened to Jesus according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Died and rose again.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:14

**Q42. Whom will God bring with him?**  
**Answer:** Them also which sleep in Jesus.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:14

---

### 1 Thessalonians 4:15

**Q43. By what do we say this unto you?**  
**Answer:** By the word of the Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:15

**Q44. Who shall not prevent them which are asleep?**  
**Answer:** We which are alive and remain unto the coming of the Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:15

**Q45. Until what event shall we which are alive and remain not prevent them which are asleep?**  
**Answer:** Unto the coming of the Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:15

---

### 1 Thessalonians 4:16

**Q46. With what shall the Lord himself descend from heaven?**  
**Answer:** With a shout, with the voice of the archangel, and with the trump of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:16

**Q47. Who shall rise first?**  
**Answer:** The dead in Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:16

**Q48. From where shall the Lord himself descend?**  
**Answer:** From heaven.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:16

---

### 1 Thessalonians 4:17

**Q49. Together with whom shall we be caught up?**  
**Answer:** Together with them in the clouds, to meet the Lord in the air.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:17

**Q50. Where shall we ever be?**  
**Answer:** With the Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:17

**Q51. What will happen to us after we are caught up?**  
**Answer:** And so shall we ever be with the Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:17

---

### 1 Thessalonians 4:18

**Q52. What are the brethren told to do with these words?**  
**Answer:** Comfort one another.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:18

**Q53. With what should they comfort one another?**  
**Answer:** These words.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:18

**Q54. Who is instructed to comfort one another with these words?**  
**Answer:** One another (brethren).  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 4:18
