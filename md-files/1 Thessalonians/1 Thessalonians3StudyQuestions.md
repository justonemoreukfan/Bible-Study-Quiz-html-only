# 1 Thessalonians 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Thessalonians 3:1

**Q1. When Paul could no longer forbear, where did he think it good to be left alone?**  
**Answer:** At Athens.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:1

**Q2. What did Paul and his companions decide they could no longer do?**  
**Answer:** Forbear.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:1

**Q3. Where were Paul and his companions when they thought it good to be left at Athens alone?**  
**Answer:** They were in a place where they could no longer forbear.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:1

---

### 1 Thessalonians 3:2

**Q1. Who did Paul send to the Thessalonians?**  
**Answer:** Timotheus.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:2

**Q2. How is Timotheus described in relation to God?**  
**Answer:** Our brother, and minister of God, and our fellowlabourer in the gospel of Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:2

**Q3. For what purpose was Timotheus sent?**  
**Answer:** To establish you, and to comfort you concerning your faith.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:2

---

### 1 Thessalonians 3:3

**Q1. What should no man be moved by?**  
**Answer:** These afflictions.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:3

**Q2. What do the Thessalonians themselves know about their situation?**  
**Answer:** That we are appointed thereunto.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:3

**Q3. What purpose do the afflictions serve according to the verse?**  
**Answer:** That no man should be moved by these afflictions.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:3

---

### 1 Thessalonians 3:4

**Q1. What did Paul and his companions tell the Thessalonians before it happened?**  
**Answer:** That we should suffer tribulation.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:4

**Q2. Where did Paul say they should suffer tribulation?**  
**Answer:** Even as it came to pass, and ye know.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:4

**Q3. When did Paul and his companions tell them these things?**  
**Answer:** When we were with you.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:4

---

### 1 Thessalonians 3:5

**Q1. When Paul could no longer forbear, what did he send to know?**  
**Answer:** Your faith.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:5

**Q2. What was Paul afraid the tempter might have done?**  
**Answer:** Tempted you, and our labour be in vain.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:5

**Q3. What reason did Paul give for sending to know their faith?**  
**Answer:** For this cause, when I could no longer forbear.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:5

---

### 1 Thessalonians 3:6

**Q1. When Timotheus came from the Thessalonians unto Paul, what did he bring?**  
**Answer:** Good tidings of your faith and charity.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:6

**Q2. What did Timotheus report about how the Thessalonians always remembered Paul?**  
**Answer:** That ye have good remembrance of us always, desiring greatly to see us, as we also to see you.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:6

**Q3. Who came from the Thessalonians unto Paul with good tidings?**  
**Answer:** Timotheus.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:6

---

### 1 Thessalonians 3:7

**Q1. What was Paul comforted over in all his affliction and distress?**  
**Answer:** Your faith.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:7

**Q2. By what was Paul comforted concerning the brethren?**  
**Answer:** By your faith.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:7

**Q3. In what did Paul experience affliction and distress?**  
**Answer:** In all our affliction and distress.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:7

---

### 1 Thessalonians 3:8

**Q1. What makes Paul and his companions live?**  
**Answer:** If ye stand fast in the Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:8

**Q2. Under what condition do Paul and his companions now live?**  
**Answer:** Now we live, if ye stand fast in the Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:8

**Q3. Where must the Thessalonians stand fast for Paul to live?**  
**Answer:** In the Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:8

---

### 1 Thessalonians 3:9

**Q1. What thanks can Paul render to God?**  
**Answer:** All the thanks wherewith we joy for your sakes before our God.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:9

**Q2. Before whom does Paul joy for the sakes of the Thessalonians?**  
**Answer:** Before our God.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:9

**Q3. What is the reason Paul asks what thanks can he render to God?**  
**Answer:** For all the joy wherewith we joy for your sakes.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:9

---

### 1 Thessalonians 3:10

**Q1. How earnestly did Paul pray night and day?**  
**Answer:** Night and day praying exceedingly.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:10

**Q2. What did Paul desire to see?**  
**Answer:** Your face.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:10

**Q3. What did Paul want to perfect?**  
**Answer:** That which is lacking in your faith.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:10

---

### 1 Thessalonians 3:11

**Q1. Who does Paul pray may direct their way unto the Thessalonians?**  
**Answer:** God himself and our Father, and our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:11

**Q2. What does Paul ask God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ to direct?**  
**Answer:** Our way unto you.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:11

**Q3. Who is associated with God the Father in directing the way?**  
**Answer:** Our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:11

---

### 1 Thessalonians 3:12

**Q1. What does the Lord make the Thessalonians to increase and abound in?**  
**Answer:** In love one toward another, and toward all men.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:12

**Q2. Toward whom should the love increase and abound?**  
**Answer:** One toward another, and toward all men.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:12

**Q3. Whose example of love is given in the verse to do as Paul does?**  
**Answer:** Even as we do toward you.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:12

---

### 1 Thessalonians 3:13

**Q1. What may the Lord establish in order to be unblameable in holiness?**  
**Answer:** Your hearts.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:13

**Q2. Before whom are the hearts to be established unblameable in holiness?**  
**Answer:** Before God, even our Father.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:13

**Q3. At the coming of which person will the hearts be established unblameable in holiness?**  
**Answer:** At the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ with all his saints.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 3:13
