# 1 Thessalonians 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:1

**Q1. What do the brethren themselves know concerning the apostles' entrance unto the Thessalonians?**  
**Answer:** That it was not in vain.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:1

**Q2. Whose entrance unto the Thessalonians is spoken of as not in vain?**  
**Answer:** The entrance of the brethren (the apostles).  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:1

**Q3. What do the brethren know about the apostles' entrance?**  
**Answer:** That it was not in vain.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:1

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:2

**Q1. What had happened before the apostles came to Thessalonica?**  
**Answer:** They had suffered before, and were shamefully entreated, as ye know, at Philippi.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:2

**Q2. In what manner were they bold in their God?**  
**Answer:** To speak unto you the gospel of God with much contention.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:2

**Q3. What did the apostles speak unto the Thessalonians with much contention?**  
**Answer:** The gospel of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:2

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:3

**Q1. Of what was the apostles' exhortation not of?**  
**Answer:** Of deceit, nor of uncleanness, nor in guile.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:3

**Q2. What did the exhortation not come from?**  
**Answer:** Deceit, nor uncleanness, nor guile.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:3

**Q3. Is the exhortation mentioned as coming from guile?**  
**Answer:** No, it was not in guile.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:3

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:4

**Q1. How were the apostles allowed of God to be put in trust with the gospel?**  
**Answer:** As we were allowed of God to be put in trust with the gospel.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:4

**Q2. Even so do the apostles speak, not as pleasing whom?**  
**Answer:** Men.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:4

**Q3. Who tries the hearts according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** God, which trieth our hearts.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:4

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:5

**Q1. What did the apostles never use at any time?**  
**Answer:** Flattering words, as ye know.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:5

**Q2. What was not used as a cloak for covetousness?**  
**Answer:** A cloke of covetousness.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:5

**Q3. Who is witness to the apostles not using a cloak of covetousness?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:5

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:6

**Q1. From whom did the apostles not seek glory?**  
**Answer:** From men.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:6

**Q2. Whose glory did the apostles not seek?**  
**Answer:** Neither of you, nor yet of others.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:6

**Q3. When the apostles might have been burdensome, what were they?**  
**Answer:** As the apostles of Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:6

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:7

**Q1. How did the apostles behave among the Thessalonians?**  
**Answer:** Even as a nurse cherisheth her children.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:7

**Q2. What did a nurse do to her children?**  
**Answer:** Cherisheth them.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:7

**Q3. Among whom were they gentle, even as a nurse cherisheth her children?**  
**Answer:** Among you.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:7

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:8

**Q1. What did the apostles affectionately desire of the Thessalonians?**  
**Answer:** To have imparted unto you, not the gospel of God only, but also our own souls.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:8

**Q2. Why were the apostles willing to impart their own souls?**  
**Answer:** Because ye were dear unto us.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:8

**Q3. What else besides their own souls were they willing to impart?**  
**Answer:** Not the gospel of God only.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:8

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:9

**Q1. What did the brethren remember about the apostles?**  
**Answer:** Our labour and travail.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:9

**Q2. When did the apostles labour and travail?**  
**Answer:** Labouring night and day.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:9

**Q3. Why did they labour night and day?**  
**Answer:** Because we would not be chargeable unto any of you.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:9

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:10

**Q1. Who are witnesses of how holily and justly and unblameably the apostles behaved?**  
**Answer:** Ye are witnesses, and God also.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:10

**Q2. Among whom did the apostles behave holily, justly, and unblameably?**  
**Answer:** Among you that believe.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:10

**Q3. What three terms describe how the apostles behaved among believers?**  
**Answer:** Holily and justly and unblameably.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:10

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:11

**Q1. How did the apostles deal with every one of the Thessalonians?**  
**Answer:** As a father doth his children.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:11

**Q2. What did the apostles do as a father does to his children?**  
**Answer:** Exhorted and comforted and charged every one of you.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:11

**Q3. Whose children are compared to the way the apostles dealt with the Thessalonians?**  
**Answer:** A father's children.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:11

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:12

**Q1. What were the Thessalonians called unto?**  
**Answer:** Unto his kingdom and glory.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:12

**Q2. Who called the Thessalonians unto his kingdom and glory?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:12

**Q3. How were the Thessalonians instructed to walk?**  
**Answer:** That ye would walk worthy of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:12

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:13

**Q1. For what do the apostles thank God without ceasing?**  
**Answer:** Because, when ye received the word of God which ye heard of us, ye received it not as the word of men.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:13

**Q2. How did the Thessalonians receive the word of God?**  
**Answer:** As it is in truth, the word of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:13

**Q3. In whom does the word of God effectually work?**  
**Answer:** In you that believe.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:13

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:14

**Q1. What churches of God in Judea did the Thessalonians become followers of?**  
**Answer:** The churches of God which in Judea are in Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:14

**Q2. What did the Thessalonians suffer like things of?**  
**Answer:** Of their own countrymen.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:14

**Q3. From whom did the churches in Judea suffer the same things?**  
**Answer:** Of the Jews.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:14

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:15

**Q1. Who killed the Lord Jesus and their own prophets?**  
**Answer:** The Jews.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:15

**Q2. Who have persecuted the apostles?**  
**Answer:** Have persecuted us.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:15

**Q3. What do they not please?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:15

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:16

**Q1. What are the Jews forbidding the apostles to do?**  
**Answer:** To speak to the Gentiles that they might be saved.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:16

**Q2. To whom are the Jews forbidding the apostles to speak for salvation?**  
**Answer:** The Gentiles.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:16

**Q3. Upon whom is wrath come to the uttermost?**  
**Answer:** Upon them (to fill up their sins alway).  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:16

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:17

**Q1. For how long were the apostles taken from the Thessalonians?**  
**Answer:** For a short time.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:17

**Q2. In what was the apostles' absence from the Thessalonians?**  
**Answer:** In presence, not in heart.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:17

**Q3. With what desire did the apostles endeavor to see the Thessalonians' face?**  
**Answer:** With great desire.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:17

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:18

**Q1. Who would have come unto the Thessalonians, even I Paul, once and again?**  
**Answer:** We would have come unto you.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:18

**Q2. Who hindered the apostles from coming?**  
**Answer:** Satan.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:18

**Q3. How often did Paul specifically desire to come?**  
**Answer:** Once and again.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:18

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:19

**Q1. What is the apostles' hope, joy, or crown of rejoicing?**  
**Answer:** Are not even ye in the presence of our Lord Jesus Christ at his coming?  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:19

**Q2. In whose presence will they be the crown of rejoicing?**  
**Answer:** In the presence of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:19

**Q3. At whose coming will this take place?**  
**Answer:** At his coming.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:19

---

### 1 Thessalonians 2:20

**Q1. Who are the apostles' glory and joy?**  
**Answer:** Ye are our glory and joy.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:20

**Q2. What relation are the Thessalonians to the apostles in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Our glory and joy.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:20

**Q3. Whose glory and joy are the Thessalonians?**  
**Answer:** Ours (the apostles').  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 2:20
