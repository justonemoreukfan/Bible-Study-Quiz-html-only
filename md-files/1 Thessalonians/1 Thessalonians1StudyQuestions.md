# 1 Thessalonians 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Thessalonians 1:1

**Q1. Who wrote this epistle?**  
**Answer:** Paul, and Silvanus, and Timotheus.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:1

**Q2. Unto what church is this epistle written?**  
**Answer:** Unto the church of the Thessalonians which is in God the Father and in the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:1

**Q3. What is desired unto them from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** Grace be unto you, and peace, from God our Father, and the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:1

---

### 1 Thessalonians 1:2

**Q4. Whom do the writers give thanks to always for you all?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:2

**Q5. When do the writers make mention of you?**  
**Answer:** In their prayers.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:2

**Q6. Whose name do they make mention of you in?**  
**Answer:** Always.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:2

---

### 1 Thessalonians 1:3

**Q7. What work of yours do the writers remember without ceasing?**  
**Answer:** Your work of faith.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:3

**Q8. What labor do they remember without ceasing?**  
**Answer:** Labour of love.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:3

**Q9. What patience do they remember without ceasing?**  
**Answer:** Patience of hope in our Lord Jesus Christ, in the sight of God and our Father.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:3

---

### 1 Thessalonians 1:4

**Q10. What do the writers know concerning their brethren?**  
**Answer:** Their election of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:4

**Q11. How are the brethren described by the writers?**  
**Answer:** Beloved.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:4

**Q12. Whose election do the writers know?**  
**Answer:** Their election of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:4

---

### 1 Thessalonians 1:5

**Q13. How did the gospel come unto you not in word only?**  
**Answer:** In power, and in the Holy Ghost, and in much assurance.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:5

**Q14. What kind of men were the writers among you?**  
**Answer:** Such as ye know what manner of men we were among you for your sake.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:5

**Q15. Did the gospel come in word only?**  
**Answer:** Not in word only, but also in power.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:5

---

### 1 Thessalonians 1:6

**Q16. Whose followers did ye become?**  
**Answer:** Of us, and of the Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:6

**Q17. With what did ye receive the word?**  
**Answer:** Much affliction, with joy of the Holy Ghost.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:6

**Q18. What kind of affliction accompanied their reception of the word?**  
**Answer:** Much affliction.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:6

---

### 1 Thessalonians 1:7

**Q19. To whom did ye become examples?**  
**Answer:** All that believe in Macedonia and Achaia.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:7

**Q20. Where are the believers located who received you as examples?**  
**Answer:** In Macedonia and Achaia.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:7

**Q21. What did the Thessalonians become to all that believe?**  
**Answer:** Ensamples.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:7

---

### 1 Thessalonians 1:8

**Q22. From whom sounded out the word of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** You.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:8

**Q23. Where was your faith to God-ward spread abroad?**  
**Answer:** In every place.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:8

**Q24. What did not need to be spoken by the writers?**  
**Answer:** Anything.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:8

---

### 1 Thessalonians 1:9

**Q25. What do they themselves show of us?**  
**Answer:** What manner of entering in we had unto you.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:9

**Q26. From what did ye turn to God?**  
**Answer:** From idols.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:9

**Q27. Whom did ye turn to serve?**  
**Answer:** The living and true God.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:9

---

### 1 Thessalonians 1:10

**Q28. For whom are ye waiting from heaven?**  
**Answer:** His Son.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:10

**Q29. Whom did God raise from the dead?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:10

**Q30. From what does Jesus deliver us?**  
**Answer:** From the wrath to come.  
**Verse:** 1 Thessalonians 1:10
