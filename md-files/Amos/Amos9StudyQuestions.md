# Amos 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Amos 9:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Amos 9:1?**  
**Answer:** I saw the LORD standing upon the altar.  
**Verse:** Amos 9:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Amos 9:1?**  
**Answer:** and he said, Smite the lintel of the door, that the posts may shake.  
**Verse:** Amos 9:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Amos 9:1?**  
**Answer:** and cut them in the head, all of them; and I will slay the last of them with the sword; he that fleeth of them shall not flee away, and he that escapeth of them shall not be delivered.  
**Verse:** Amos 9:1

---

### Amos 9:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Amos 9:2?**  
**Answer:** Though they dig into hell, thence shall mine hand take them.  
**Verse:** Amos 9:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Amos 9:2?**  
**Answer:** though they climb up to heaven, thence will I bring them down.  
**Verse:** Amos 9:2

**Q6. According to Amos 9:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Though they dig into hell, thence shall mine hand take them; though they climb up to heaven, thence will I bring them down:.  
**Verse:** Amos 9:2

---

### Amos 9:3
**Q7. What is stated in the first clause of Amos 9:3?**  
**Answer:** And though they hide themselves in the top of Carmel, I will search and take them out thence.  
**Verse:** Amos 9:3

**Q8. What additional detail is declared in Amos 9:3?**  
**Answer:** and though they be hid from my sight in the bottom of the sea, thence will I command the serpent, and he shall bite them.  
**Verse:** Amos 9:3

**Q9. According to Amos 9:3, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And though they hide themselves in the top of Carmel, I will search and take them out thence; and though they be hid from my sight in the bottom of the sea, thence will I command the serpent, and he shall bite them:.  
**Verse:** Amos 9:3

### Amos 9:5
**Q13. What is stated in the opening of Amos 9:5?**  
**Answer:** And the Lord GOD of hosts is he that toucheth the land, and it shall melt, and all that dwell therein shall mourn.  
**Verse:** Amos 9:5

**Q14. What is declared next in Amos 9:5?**  
**Answer:** and it shall rise up wholly like a flood.  
**Verse:** Amos 9:5

**Q15. What further truth is recorded in Amos 9:5?**  
**Answer:** and shall be drowned, as by the flood of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Amos 9:5

### Amos 9:6
**Q16. What is stated in the opening of Amos 9:6?**

**Verse:** Amos 9:6

**Q17. What is declared next in Amos 9:6?**

**Verse:** Amos 9:6

### Amos 9:7
**Q19. According to Amos 9:7, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Are ye not as children of the Ethiopians unto me.  
**Verse:** Amos 9:7

**Q20. What is declared next in Amos 9:7?**  
**Answer:** O children of Israel? saith the LORD. Have not I brought up Israel out of the land of Egypt? and the Philistines from Caphtor.  
**Verse:** Amos 9:7

**Q21. What further truth is given in Amos 9:7?**  
**Answer:** and the Syrians from Kir?  
**Verse:** Amos 9:7

### Amos 9:8
**Q22. What is stated in the first clause of Amos 9:8?**  
**Answer:** Behold, the eyes of the Lord GOD are upon the sinful kingdom, and I will destroy it from off the face of the earth.  
**Verse:** Amos 9:8

**Q23. What additional detail is declared in Amos 9:8?**  
**Answer:** saving that I will not utterly destroy the house of Jacob, saith the LORD.  
**Verse:** Amos 9:8

**Q24. According to Amos 9:8, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Behold, the eyes of the Lord GOD are upon the sinful kingdom, and I will destroy it from off the face of the earth; saving that I will not utterly destroy the house of Jacob, saith the LORD.  
**Verse:** Amos 9:8
