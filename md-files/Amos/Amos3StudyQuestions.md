# Amos 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Amos 3:1
**Q1. According to Amos 3:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Hear this word that the LORD hath spoken against you.  
**Verse:** Amos 3:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Amos 3:1?**  
**Answer:** O children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Amos 3:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Amos 3:1?**  
**Answer:** against the whole family which I brought up from the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Amos 3:1

---

### Amos 3:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Amos 3:2?**  
**Answer:** You only have I known of all the families of the earth.  
**Verse:** Amos 3:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Amos 3:2?**  
**Answer:** therefore I will punish you for all your iniquities.  
**Verse:** Amos 3:2

**Q6. According to Amos 3:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** You only have I known of all the families of the earth: therefore I will punish you for all your iniquities.  
**Verse:** Amos 3:2
