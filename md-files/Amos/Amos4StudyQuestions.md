# Amos 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Amos 4:1
**Q1. According to Amos 4:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Hear this word.  
**Verse:** Amos 4:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Amos 4:1?**  
**Answer:** ye kine of Bashan.  
**Verse:** Amos 4:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Amos 4:1?**  
**Answer:** that are in the mountain of Samaria, which oppress the poor, which crush the needy, which say to their masters, and let us drink.  
**Verse:** Amos 4:1

---

### Amos 4:2
**Q4. According to Amos 4:2, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** The Lord GOD hath sworn by his holiness.  
**Verse:** Amos 4:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Amos 4:2?**  
**Answer:** the days shall come upon you.  
**Verse:** Amos 4:2

**Q6. What further truth is given in Amos 4:2?**  
**Answer:** that he will take you away with hooks, and your posterity with fishhooks.  
**Verse:** Amos 4:2
