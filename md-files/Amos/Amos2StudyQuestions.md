# Amos 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Amos 2:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Amos 2:1?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD.  
**Verse:** Amos 2:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Amos 2:1?**  
**Answer:** For three transgressions of Moab, and for four, I will not turn away the punishment thereof.  
**Verse:** Amos 2:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Amos 2:1?**  
**Answer:** because he burned the bones of the king of Edom into lime.  
**Verse:** Amos 2:1

---

### Amos 2:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Amos 2:2?**  
**Answer:** But I will send a fire upon Moab, and it shall devour the palaces of Kirioth.  
**Verse:** Amos 2:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Amos 2:2?**  
**Answer:** and Moab shall die with tumult, with shouting, and with the sound of the trumpet.  
**Verse:** Amos 2:2

**Q6. According to Amos 2:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** But I will send a fire upon Moab, and it shall devour the palaces of Kirioth: and Moab shall die with tumult, with shouting, and with the sound of the trumpet:.  
**Verse:** Amos 2:2
