# Amos 7 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Amos 7:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Amos 7:1?**  
**Answer:** Thus hath the Lord GOD shewed unto me.  
**Verse:** Amos 7:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Amos 7:1?**  
**Answer:** and, behold, he formed grasshoppers in the beginning of the shooting up of the latter growth.  
**Verse:** Amos 7:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Amos 7:1?**  
**Answer:** and, lo, it was the latter growth after the king’s mowings.  
**Verse:** Amos 7:1

### Amos 7:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Amos 7:2?**  
**Answer:** And it came to pass, that when they had made an end of eating the grass of the land, then I said, O Lord GOD, forgive, I beseech thee.  
**Verse:** Amos 7:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Amos 7:2?**  
**Answer:** by whom shall Jacob arise? for he is small.

**Verse:** Amos 7:2
