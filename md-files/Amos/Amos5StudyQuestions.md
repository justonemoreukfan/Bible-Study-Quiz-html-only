# Amos 5 Study Questions (KJV)

### Amos 5:1
**Q1. According to Amos 5:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Hear ye this word which I take up against you.  
**Verse:** Amos 5:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Amos 5:1?**  
**Answer:** even a lamentation.  
**Verse:** Amos 5:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Amos 5:1?**  
**Answer:** O house of Israel.  
**Verse:** Amos 5:1
