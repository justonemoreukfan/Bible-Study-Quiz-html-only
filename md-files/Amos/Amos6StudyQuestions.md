# Amos 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Amos 6:1
**Q1. According to Amos 6:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Woe to them that are at ease in Zion.  
**Verse:** Amos 6:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Amos 6:1?**  
**Answer:** and trust in the mountain of Samaria.  
**Verse:** Amos 6:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Amos 6:1?**  
**Answer:** which are named chief of the nations, to whom the house of Israel came!  
**Verse:** Amos 6:1

---

### Amos 6:2
**Q4. What is stated in the opening of Amos 6:2?**  
**Answer:** Pass ye unto Calneh, and see.  
**Verse:** Amos 6:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Amos 6:2?**  
**Answer:** and from thence go ye to Hamath the great.  
**Verse:** Amos 6:2

**Q6. What further truth is recorded in Amos 6:2?**  
**Answer:** then go down to Gath of the Philistines; be they better than these kingdoms? or their border greater than your border?  
**Verse:** Amos 6:2

### Amos 6:6
**Q16. What is stated in the first clause of Amos 6:6?**  
**Answer:** That drink wine in bowls, and anoint themselves with the chief ointments.  
**Verse:** Amos 6:6

**Q17. What additional detail is declared in Amos 6:6?**

**Q18. According to Amos 6:6, what is the full truth recorded?**

**Verse:** Amos 6:6
