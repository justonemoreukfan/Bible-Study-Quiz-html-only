# Amos 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Amos 1:1
**Q1. According to Amos 1:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** The words of Amos.  
**Verse:** Amos 1:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Amos 1:1?**  
**Answer:** who was among the herdmen of Tekoa.  
**Verse:** Amos 1:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Amos 1:1?**  
**Answer:** which he saw concerning Israel in the days of Uzziah king of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam the son of Joash king of Israel, two years before the earthquake.  
**Verse:** Amos 1:1

### Amos 1:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Amos 1:2?**
