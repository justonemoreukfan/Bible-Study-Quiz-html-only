# Amos 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Amos 8:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Amos 8:1?**  
**Answer:** Thus hath the Lord GOD shewed unto me.  
**Verse:** Amos 8:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Amos 8:1?**  
**Answer:** and behold a basket of summer fruit.  
**Verse:** Amos 8:1

**Q3. According to Amos 8:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Thus hath the Lord GOD shewed unto me: and behold a basket of summer fruit.  
**Verse:** Amos 8:1

---

### Amos 8:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Amos 8:2?**  
**Answer:** And he said, Amos, what seest thou? And I said, A basket of summer fruit. Then said the LORD unto me, The end is come upon my people of Israel.  
**Verse:** Amos 8:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Amos 8:2?**  
**Answer:** I will not again pass by them any more.  
**Verse:** Amos 8:2

**Q6. According to Amos 8:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And he said, Amos, what seest thou? And I said, A basket of summer fruit. Then said the LORD unto me, The end is come upon my people of Israel; I will not again pass by them any more.  
**Verse:** Amos 8:2
