# 2 Kings 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Kings 3:1

**Q1. Who began to reign over Israel in Samaria in the eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah?**  
**Answer:** Jehoram the son of Ahab.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:1

**Q2. For how many years did Jehoram reign over Israel?**  
**Answer:** Twelve years.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:1

**Q3. In what year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah did Jehoram begin to reign?**  
**Answer:** The eighteenth year.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:1

---

### 2 Kings 3:2

**Q4. In whose sight did Jehoram do evil?**  
**Answer:** In the sight of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:2

**Q5. Whose parents did Jehoram not like, though not to the same degree?**  
**Answer:** Not like his father, and like his mother.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:2

**Q6. What object did Jehoram put away that his father had made?**  
**Answer:** The image of Baal.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:2

---

### 2 Kings 3:3

**Q7. To whose sins did Jehoram cleave?**  
**Answer:** The sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:3

**Q8. What did Jeroboam make Israel do that Jehoram continued in?**  
**Answer:** Sin.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:3

**Q9. Did Jehoram depart from the sins of Jeroboam?**  
**Answer:** No, he departed not therefrom.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:3

---

### 2 Kings 3:4

**Q10. Who was Mesha?**  
**Answer:** King of Moab.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:4

**Q11. What was Mesha a sheepmaster?**  
**Answer:** A sheepmaster.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:4

**Q12. How many lambs and rams with the wool did Mesha render unto the king of Israel?**  
**Answer:** An hundred thousand lambs, and an hundred thousand rams.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:4

---

### 2 Kings 3:5

**Q13. What happened when Ahab was dead?**  
**Answer:** The king of Moab rebelled against the king of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:5

**Q14. Against whom did the king of Moab rebel?**  
**Answer:** The king of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:5

**Q15. Whose death preceded the rebellion of the king of Moab?**  
**Answer:** Ahab.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:5

---

### 2 Kings 3:6

**Q16. From where did king Joram go out at that time?**  
**Answer:** Samaria.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:6

**Q17. What did king Joram do at that time concerning Israel?**  
**Answer:** Numbered all Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:6

**Q18. From Samaria, what did king Joram do?**  
**Answer:** Went out and numbered all Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:6

---

### 2 Kings 3:7

**Q19. To whom did Joram send, saying, "The king of Moab hath rebelled against me"?**  
**Answer:** Jehoshaphat the king of Judah.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:7

**Q20. What question did Joram ask Jehoshaphat?**  
**Answer:** "Wilt thou go with me against Moab to battle?"  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:7

**Q21. What was Jehoshaphat's response to Joram's request?**  
**Answer:** "I will go up: I am as thou art, my people as thy people, my horses as thy horses."  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:7

---

### 2 Kings 3:8

**Q22. What question did Joram ask regarding the way they should go up?**  
**Answer:** "Which way shall we go up?"  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:8

**Q23. What was Joram's suggested route?**  
**Answer:** "The way through the wilderness of Edom."  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:8

**Q24. Who answered, "The way through the wilderness of Edom"?**  
**Answer:** The king of Israel (Joram).  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:8

---

### 2 Kings 3:9

**Q25. Who went with the king of Israel and the king of Judah?**  
**Answer:** The king of Edom.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:9

**Q26. For how many days' journey did they fetch a compass?**  
**Answer:** Seven days' journey.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:9

**Q27. What ran out for the host and the cattle that followed them?**  
**Answer:** Water.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:9

---

### 2 Kings 3:10

**Q28. What did the king of Israel say the LORD had called them together for?**  
**Answer:** To deliver them into the hand of Moab.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:10

**Q29. Which three kings were gathered together according to verse 10?**  
**Answer:** The king of Israel, the king of Judah, and the king of Edom.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:10

**Q30. Into whose hand did the king of Israel say they would be delivered?**  
**Answer:** The hand of Moab.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:10

---

### 2 Kings 3:11

**Q31. Who asked, "Is there not here a prophet of the LORD"?**  
**Answer:** Jehoshaphat.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:11

**Q32. Through whom might they enquire of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** A prophet of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:11

**Q33. Who answered and said, "Here is Elisha the son of Shaphat, which poured water on the hands of Elijah"?**  
**Answer:** One of the king of Israel's servants.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:11

---

### 2 Kings 3:12

**Q34. Who said, "The word of the LORD is with him"?**  
**Answer:** Jehoshaphat.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:12

**Q35. Who went down to Elisha?**  
**Answer:** The king of Israel and Jehoshaphat and the king of Edom.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:12

**Q36. Which king specifically stated, "The word of the LORD is with him"?**  
**Answer:** Jehoshaphat.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:12

---

### 2 Kings 3:13

**Q37. What did Elisha say to the king of Israel?**  
**Answer:** "What have I to do with thee?"  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:13

**Q38. To whose prophets did Elisha tell the king of Israel to go?**  
**Answer:** The prophets of thy father, and to the prophets of thy mother.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:13

**Q39. What did the king of Israel reply to Elisha regarding the LORD gathering them together?**  
**Answer:** "Nay: for the LORD hath called these three kings together, to deliver them into the hand of Moab."  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:13

---

### 2 Kings 3:14

**Q40. By whom did Elisha swear?**  
**Answer:** The LORD of hosts.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:14

**Q41. Whose presence did Elisha acknowledge?**  
**Answer:** Jehoshaphat the king of Judah.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:14

**Q42. What did Elisha say he would not do for the king of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Not regard him, nor see him.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:14

---

### 2 Kings 3:15

**Q43. What did Elisha ask for?**  
**Answer:** A minstrel.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:15

**Q44. What happened when the minstrel played?**  
**Answer:** The hand of the LORD came upon him.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:15

**Q45. Whose hand came upon Elisha?**  
**Answer:** The hand of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:15

---

### 2 Kings 3:16

**Q46. What did Elisha say the LORD said?**  
**Answer:** "Make this valley full of ditches."  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:16

**Q47. Where were the ditches to be made?**  
**Answer:** In this valley.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:16

**Q48. Who instructed to make the valley full of ditches?**  
**Answer:** The LORD (spoken through Elisha).  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:16

---

### 2 Kings 3:17

**Q49. What did the LORD say they would not see?**  
**Answer:** Wind, neither shall ye see rain.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:17

**Q50. What would that valley be filled with?**  
**Answer:** Water.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:17

**Q51. Who would drink from the filled valley?**  
**Answer:** Ye, and your cattle, and your beasts.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:17

---

### 2 Kings 3:18

**Q52. How did Elisha describe this thing in the sight of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** A light thing.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:18

**Q53. Into whose hand would the LORD deliver the Moabites?**  
**Answer:** Your hand.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:18

**Q54. What would the LORD deliver into their hand besides the Moabites?**  
**Answer:** Nothing else mentioned in this verse, just the Moabites.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:18

---

### 2 Kings 3:19

**Q55. What kind of cities were they to smite?**  
**Answer:** Every fenced city, and every choice city.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:19

**Q56. What were they to do to every good tree?**  
**Answer:** Fell every good tree.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:19

**Q57. What were they to do to wells of water and pieces of land?**  
**Answer:** Stop all wells of water, and mar every good piece of land with stones.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:19

---

### 2 Kings 3:20

**Q58. When did the meat offering happen?**  
**Answer:** In the morning.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:20

**Q59. When the meat offering was offered, what came by the way of Edom?**  
**Answer:** Water.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:20

**Q60. What filled the country when the water came?**  
**Answer:** Water.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:20

### 2 Kings 3:21

**Q61. What did all the Moabites hear when the kings came up to fight against them?**  
**Answer:** That the kings were come up to fight against them.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:21

**Q62. Who were gathered together against Moab?**  
**Answer:** All that were able to put on armour, and upward.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:21

**Q63. Where did they stand?**  
**Answer:** In the border.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:21

**Q64. When they rose up early in the morning, what was shining upon the water?**  
**Answer:** The sun.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:22

**Q65. What did the Moabites see the water as across from them?**  
**Answer:** Red as blood.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:22

**Q66. What color did the water appear to the Moabites?**  
**Answer:** Red as blood.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:22

---

### 2 Kings 3:23

**Q67. What did the Moabites say the blood was?**  
**Answer:** "This is blood."  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:23

**Q68. What did the kings supposedly do to one another according to the Moabites?**  
**Answer:** Smitten one another.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:23

**Q69. What did the Moabites say to each other, "Now therefore..."?**  
**Answer:** "...Moab, to the spoil."  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:23

---

### 2 Kings 3:24

**Q70. When the Moabites came to the camp of Israel, what did the Israelites do?**  
**Answer:** Rose up and smote the Moabites.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:24

**Q71. What did the Moabites do before the Israelites?**  
**Answer:** Fled before them.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:24

**Q72. What did they go forward in, smiting the Moabites?**  
**Answer:** Even in smiting the Moabites.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:24

---

### 2 Kings 3:25

**Q73. What did they beat down?**  
**Answer:** The cities.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:25

**Q74. On every good piece of land, what did every man cast?**  
**Answer:** His stone, and filled it.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:25

**Q75. What was left in Kirharaseth?**  
**Answer:** The stones thereof.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:25

---

### 2 Kings 3:26

**Q76. When the king of Moab saw that the battle was too sore for him, who did he take with him?**  
**Answer:** Seven hundred men that drew swords.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:26

**Q77. What did the king of Moab try to break through unto?**  
**Answer:** The king of Edom.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:26

**Q78. Did they manage to break through?**  
**Answer:** They could not.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:26

---

### 2 Kings 3:27

**Q79. Whose eldest son did the king of Moab take?**  
**Answer:** His eldest son that should have reigned in his stead.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:27

**Q80. What did the king of Moab offer his eldest son for?**  
**Answer:** A burnt offering upon the wall.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:27

**Q81. What came upon Israel after this event?**  
**Answer:** Great indignation.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 3:27
