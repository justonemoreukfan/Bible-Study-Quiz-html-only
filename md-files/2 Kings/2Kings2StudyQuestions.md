# 2 Kings 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Kings 2:1

**Q1. When the LORD would take up Elijah into heaven by a whirlwind, where were Elijah and Elisha going?**  
**Answer:** From Gilgal.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:1

**Q2. Who was about to be taken up into heaven by a whirlwind?**  
**Answer:** Elijah.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:1

**Q3. By what means would the LORD take up Elijah into heaven?**  
**Answer:** By a whirlwind.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:1

---

### 2 Kings 2:2

**Q1. What did Elijah say unto Elisha at Bethel?**  
**Answer:** Tarry here, I pray thee; for the LORD hath sent me to Bethel.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:2

**Q2. What did Elisha swear as the LORD liveth, and as his soul liveth?**  
**Answer:** I will not leave thee.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:2

**Q3. Where did Elijah and Elisha go down together after leaving Bethel?**  
**Answer:** To Jericho.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:2

---

### 2 Kings 2:3

**Q1. Who came forth to Elisha at Jericho?**  
**Answer:** The sons of the prophets that were at Jericho.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:3

**Q2. What question did the sons of the prophets ask Elisha?**  
**Answer:** Knowest thou that the LORD will take away thy master from thy head to day?  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:3

**Q3. What was Elisha's response to the sons of the prophets?**  
**Answer:** Yea, I know it; hold ye your peace.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:3

---

### 2 Kings 2:4

**Q1. What did Elijah say unto Elisha at Jericho?**  
**Answer:** Elisha, tarry here, I pray thee; for the LORD hath sent me to Jericho.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:4

**Q2. What did Elisha reply concerning leaving Elijah?**  
**Answer:** As the LORD liveth, and as thy soul liveth, I will not leave thee.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:4

**Q3. Where did they come to?**  
**Answer:** To Jordan.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:4

---

### 2 Kings 2:5

**Q1. Who came to Elisha at Jordan?**  
**Answer:** The sons of the prophets that were at Jericho.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:5

**Q2. What did the sons of the prophets ask Elisha at Jordan?**  
**Answer:** Knowest thou that the LORD will take away thy master from thy head to day?  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:5

**Q3. What did Elisha answer them?**  
**Answer:** Yea, I know it; hold ye your peace.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:5

---

### 2 Kings 2:6

**Q1. What did Elijah say to Elisha at Jordan?**  
**Answer:** Tarry here, I pray thee; for the LORD hath sent me to Jordan.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:6

**Q2. What was Elisha's response?**  
**Answer:** As the LORD liveth, and as thy soul liveth, I will not leave thee.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:6

**Q3. Who went on together?**  
**Answer:** They two went on.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:6

---

### 2 Kings 2:7

**Q1. How many men of the sons of the prophets went, and stood to view afar off?**  
**Answer:** Fifty men.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:7

**Q2. Where did Elijah and Elisha stand by?**  
**Answer:** By Jordan.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:7

**Q3. Where did the fifty men stand?**  
**Answer:** Afar off.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:7

---

### 2 Kings 2:8

**Q1. What did Elijah take when he stood by Jordan?**  
**Answer:** His mantle.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:8

**Q2. What did Elijah do with his mantle?**  
**Answer:** Wrapped it together, and smote the waters.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:8

**Q3. How did Elijah and Elisha go over Jordan?**  
**Answer:** They went over on dry ground.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:8

---

### 2 Kings 2:9

**Q1. What did Elijah say to Elisha after they were gone over?**  
**Answer:** Ask what I shall do for thee, before I be taken away from thee.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:9

**Q2. What did Elisha ask for?**  
**Answer:** I pray thee, let a double portion of thy spirit be upon me.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:9

**Q3. When did Elisha want this done?**  
**Answer:** Before Elijah was taken away from him.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:9

---

### 2 Kings 2:10

**Q1. What did Elijah say Elisha had asked?**  
**Answer:** A hard thing.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:10

**Q2. Under what condition would Elisha's request be granted to him?**  
**Answer:** If thou see me when I am taken from thee.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:10

**Q3. What would happen if Elisha did not see Elijah when he was taken?**  
**Answer:** It shall be so unto thee; but if not, it shall not be so.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:10

---

### 2 Kings 2:11

**Q1. As they still went on and talked, what appeared between them?**  
**Answer:** There appeared a chariot of fire, and horses of fire.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:11

**Q2. Who were the chariot and horses separating?**  
**Answer:** Both of them (Elijah and Elisha).  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:11

**Q3. How did Elijah go up into heaven?**  
**Answer:** By a whirlwind into heaven.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:11

---

### 2 Kings 2:12

**Q1. What did Elisha see and cry out?**  
**Answer:** My father, my father, the chariot of Israel, and the horsemen thereof!  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:12

**Q2. When Elisha saw Elijah no more, what did he do to his own clothes?**  
**Answer:** Took hold of his own clothes, and rent them in two pieces.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:12

**Q3. Into how many pieces did Elisha rend his clothes?**  
**Answer:** In two pieces.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:12

---

### 2 Kings 2:13

**Q1. What mantle did Elisha take up?**  
**Answer:** The mantle of Elijah that fell from him.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:13

**Q2. Where did Elisha go back and stand?**  
**Answer:** By the bank of Jordan.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:13

**Q3. What did Elisha do after going back?**  
**Answer:** Stood by the bank of Jordan.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:13

---

### 2 Kings 2:14

**Q1. What did Elisha take when he smote the waters?**  
**Answer:** The mantle of Elijah that fell from him.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:14

**Q2. What question did Elisha ask as he smote the waters?**  
**Answer:** Where is the LORD God of Elijah?  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:14

**Q3. What happened when Elisha had smitten the waters?**  
**Answer:** They parted hither and thither: and Elisha went over.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:14

---

### 2 Kings 2:15

**Q1. When the sons of the prophets which were to view at Jericho saw Elisha, what did they say?**  
**Answer:** The spirit of Elijah doth rest on Elisha.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:15

**Q2. Who came to meet Elisha?**  
**Answer:** The sons of the prophets which were to view at Jericho.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:15

**Q3. What did the sons of the prophets do when they met Elisha?**  
**Answer:** Bowed themselves to the ground before him.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:15

---

### 2 Kings 2:16

**Q1. What did the sons of the prophets say there were with them?**  
**Answer:** Fifty strong men.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:16

**Q2. What did they ask Elisha to let their lords go and seek?**  
**Answer:** Thy master.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:16

**Q3. By what means did they suggest the Spirit of the LORD might have cast Elijah?**  
**Answer:** Upon some mountain, or into some valley.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:16

---

### 2 Kings 2:17

**Q1. How long did Elisha urge them not to send until he was ashamed?**  
**Answer:** Till he was ashamed.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:17

**Q2. What did Elisha finally tell them?**  
**Answer:** Send.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:17

**Q3. How many men did they send, and how long did they seek without finding him?**  
**Answer:** Fifty men; and sought three days, but found him not.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:17

---

### 2 Kings 2:18

**Q1. Where did the men return while Elisha tarried?**  
**Answer:** To him (to Jericho).  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:18

**Q2. What did Elisha say to them upon their return?**  
**Answer:** Did I not say unto you, Go not?  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:18

**Q3. Where was Elisha staying when they returned?**  
**Answer:** At Jericho.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:18

---

### 2 Kings 2:19

**Q1. Who said unto Elisha, Behold, the situation of this city is pleasant?**  
**Answer:** The men of the city.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:19

**Q2. What did the men of the city say was naught?**  
**Answer:** The water.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:19

**Q3. What else did the men of the city say about the water and the land?**  
**Answer:** The ground barren.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:19

---

### 2 Kings 2:20

**Q1. What did Elisha tell them to bring him?**  
**Answer:** A new cruse.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:20

**Q2. What did Elisha tell them to put in the new cruse?**  
**Answer:** Salt.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:20

**Q3. Where did they bring the cruse to Elisha?**  
**Answer:** Unto him.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:20

---

### 2 Kings 2:21

**Q1. Where did Elisha go forth to?**  
**Answer:** Unto the spring of the waters.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:21

**Q2. What did Elisha cast into the spring of the waters?**  
**Answer:** The salt in there.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:21

**Q3. What did Elisha say the LORD had said?**  
**Answer:** I have healed these waters; there shall not be from thence any more death or barren land.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:21

---

### 2 Kings 2:22

**Q1. What happened to the waters according to the saying of Elisha?**  
**Answer:** Were healed.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:22

**Q2. Until when were the waters healed?**  
**Answer:** Unto this day.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:22

**Q3. Whose saying were the waters healed according to?**  
**Answer:** According to the saying of Elisha which he spake.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:22

---

### 2 Kings 2:23

**Q1. Where did Elisha go up from thence?**  
**Answer:** Unto Bethel.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:23

**Q2. Who came forth out of the city and mocked Elisha?**  
**Answer:** Little children.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:23

**Q3. What did the little children say to Elisha?**  
**Answer:** Go up, thou bald head; go up, thou bald head.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:23

---

### 2 Kings 2:24

**Q1. When Elisha turned back and looked on them, what did he do?**  
**Answer:** Cursed them in the name of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:24

**Q2. What came forth out of the wood?**  
**Answer:** Two she bears.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:24

**Q3. How many children did the bears tare?**  
**Answer:** Forty and two children.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:24

---

### 2 Kings 2:25

**Q1. Where did Elisha go from Bethel?**  
**Answer:** To mount Carmel.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:25

**Q2. Where did Elisha return from mount Carmel?**  
**Answer:** To Samaria.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:25

**Q3. Who went to mount Carmel and returned to Samaria?**  
**Answer:** Elisha.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 2:25
