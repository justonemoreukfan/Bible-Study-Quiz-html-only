# 2 Kings 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Kings 4:1

**Q1. Whose wife cried unto Elisha?**  
**Answer:** The wife of one of the sons of the prophets.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 4:1

**Q2. What did the creditor come to take unto him?**  
**Answer:** The two sons to be bondmen.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 4:1

**Q3. What did the woman say her servant did?**  
**Answer:** Did fear the Lord.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 4:1

---

### 2 Kings 4:2

**Q1. What did Elisha ask the woman to tell him she had in the house?**  
**Answer:** What she had in the house.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 4:2

**Q2. What did the woman say her handmaid had not in the house?**  
**Answer:** Any thing in the house.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 4:2

**Q3. What was the one thing the woman had in the house?**  
**Answer:** A pot of oil.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 4:2
