# 2 Kings 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Kings 1:1

**Q1. Who rebelled against Israel after the death of Ahab?**  
**Answer:** Moab.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:1

**Q2. Whose death occurred prior to Moab's rebellion against Israel?**  
**Answer:** Ahab.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:1

**Q3. Against whom did Moab rebel?**  
**Answer:** Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:1

---

### 2 Kings 1:2

**Q1. Through what did Ahaziah fall down in Samaria?**  
**Answer:** A lattice in his upper chamber.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:2

**Q2. In what city did Ahaziah fall through the lattice?**  
**Answer:** Samaria.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:2

**Q3. Whom did Ahaziah send messengers to, and what did he tell them to ask?**  
**Answer:** He sent messengers, and said unto them, Go, enquire of Baalzebub the god of Ekron whether I shall recover of this disease.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:2

---

### 2 Kings 1:3

**Q1. Whom did the angel of the Lord speak to?**  
**Answer:** Elijah the Tishbite.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:3

**Q2. What question did the angel of the Lord tell Elijah to ask the messengers of Samaria?**  
**Answer:** Is it not because there is not a God in Israel, that ye go to enquire of Baalzebub the god of Ekron?  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:3

**Q3. Whose messengers were told this message by the angel of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** The messengers of the king of Samaria.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:3

---

### 2 Kings 1:4

**Q1. What did the Lord say Ahaziah shall not come down from?**  
**Answer:** That bed on which thou art gone up.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:4

**Q2. What did the Lord say would happen to Ahaziah?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt surely die.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:4

**Q3. Who departed after this message was given?**  
**Answer:** Elijah.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:4

---

### 2 Kings 1:5

**Q1. Who returned unto Ahaziah?**  
**Answer:** The messengers.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:5

**Q2. What question did Ahaziah ask the messengers when they turned back?**  
**Answer:** Why are ye now turned back?  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:5

**Q3. To whom did the messengers return?**  
**Answer:** Unto him (Ahaziah).  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:5

---

### 2 Kings 1:6

**Q1. Who told the messengers, "Go, turn again unto the king that sent you"?**  
**Answer:** A man.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:6

**Q2. What did the man ask the king, through the messengers, regarding other gods?**  
**Answer:** Is it not because there is not a God in Israel, that thou sendest to enquire of Baalzebub the god of Ekron?  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:6

**Q3. What did the man repeat regarding the bed Ahaziah went up on?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not come down from that bed on which thou art gone up, but shalt surely die.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:6

---

### 2 Kings 1:7

**Q1. What did Ahaziah ask the messengers?**  
**Answer:** What manner of man was he which came up to meet you, and told you these words?  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:7

**Q2. What did the king want to know about the man who met them?**  
**Answer:** What manner of man was he.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:7

**Q3. Whom did the king ask this question of?**  
**Answer:** The messengers.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:7

---

### 2 Kings 1:8

**Q1. How did the messengers describe the man's clothing?**  
**Answer:** He was an hairy man, and girt with a girdle of leather about his loins.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:8

**Q2. Who did the king say the man was, based on their description?**  
**Answer:** It is Elijah the Tishbite.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:8

**Q3. With what was the man girt about his loins?**  
**Answer:** A girdle of leather.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:8

---

### 2 Kings 1:9

**Q1. Whom did the king send unto Elijah?**  
**Answer:** A captain of fifty with his fifty.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:9

**Q2. Where was Elijah sitting when the captain and his fifty went up to him?**  
**Answer:** On the top of an hill.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:9

**Q3. What did the captain say to Elijah?**  
**Answer:** Thou man of God, the king hath said, Come down.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:9

---

### 2 Kings 1:10

**Q1. How did Elijah answer the captain of fifty?**  
**Answer:** If I be a man of God, then let fire come down from heaven, and consume thee and thy fifty.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:10

**Q2. What came down from heaven and consumed the captain and his fifty?**  
**Answer:** Fire.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:10

**Q3. Who was consumed along with the captain of fifty?**  
**Answer:** His fifty.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:10

---

### 2 Kings 1:11

**Q1. Whom did the king send again to Elijah?**  
**Answer:** Another captain of fifty with his fifty.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:11

**Q2. What did the second captain answer and say to Elijah?**  
**Answer:** O man of God, thus hath the king said, Come down quickly.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:11

**Q3. What specific word did the second captain add that the first did not use regarding coming down?**  
**Answer:** Quickly.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:11

---

### 2 Kings 1:12

**Q1. How did Elijah answer the second captain?**  
**Answer:** If I be a man of God, let fire come down from heaven, and consume thee and thy fifty.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:12

**Q2. What came down from heaven the second time?**  
**Answer:** Fire of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:12

**Q3. Whom did the fire of God consume?**  
**Answer:** Him and his fifty.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:12

---

### 2 Kings 1:13

**Q1. Whom did the king send yet a third time?**  
**Answer:** A captain of a third fifty with his fifty.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:13

**Q2. What did the third captain of fifty do when he went up?**  
**Answer:** Came and fell on his knees before Elijah, and besought him.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:13

**Q3. What did the third captain call Elijah and ask him to let his life be worth?**  
**Answer:** O man of God, I pray thee, let my life, and the life of these fifty thy servants, be precious in thy sight.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:13

---

### 2 Kings 1:14

**Q1. What came down from heaven and consumed the two previous captains and their fifties?**  
**Answer:** Behold, there came fire down from heaven, and burnt up the two captains of the former fifties with their fifties.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:14

**Q2. What did the third captain ask to be precious in Elijah's sight?**  
**Answer:** Therefore let my life now be precious in thy sight.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:14

**Q3. Whose lives did the third captain ask to be precious along with his own?**  
**Answer:** The life of these fifty thy servants.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:14

---

### 2 Kings 1:15

**Q1. Who spoke unto Elijah?**  
**Answer:** The angel of the Lord.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:15

**Q2. What did the angel of the Lord tell Elijah to do?**  
**Answer:** Go down with him.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:15

**Q3. What instruction did the angel give Elijah regarding fear?**  
**Answer:** Be not afraid of him.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:15

---

### 2 Kings 1:16

**Q1. What did Elijah say the king sent messengers to do?**  
**Answer:** To enquire of Baalzebub the god of Ekron.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:16

**Q2. What reason did Elijah give the king for not coming down from his bed?**  
**Answer:** Is it not because there is no God in Israel to enquire of his word?  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:16

**Q3. What did Elijah tell the king would happen because of this?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not come down off that bed on which thou art gone up, but shalt surely die.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:16

---

### 2 Kings 1:17

**Q1. What happened to Ahaziah according to the word of the Lord which Elijah had spoken?**  
**Answer:** So he died.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:17

**Q2. Who reigned in Ahaziah's stead?**  
**Answer:** Jehoram.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:17

**Q3. In what year of Jehoram the son of Jehoshaphat king of Judah did Jehoram begin to reign?**  
**Answer:** The second year of Jehoram the son of Jehoshaphat king of Judah.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:17

### 2 Kings 1:18

**Q1. What else is said to be written regarding the acts of Ahaziah?**  
**Answer:** Are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:18

**Q2. Whose acts are referenced as being written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Ahaziah.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:18

**Q3. In which book are the rest of the acts of Ahaziah which he did written?**  
**Answer:** The book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 1:18
