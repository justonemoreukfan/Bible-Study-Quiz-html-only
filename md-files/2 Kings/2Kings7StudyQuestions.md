# 2 Kings 7 Study Questions (KJV)

### 2 Kings 7:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of 2 Kings 7:1?**  
**Answer:** Then Elisha said, Hear ye the word of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 7:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in 2 Kings 7:1?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD, To morrow about this time shall a measure of fine flour be sold for a shekel, and two measures of barley for a shekel, in the gate of Samaria.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 7:1

**Q3. According to 2 Kings 7:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Then Elisha said, Hear ye the word of the LORD; Thus saith the LORD, To morrow about this time shall a measure of fine flour be sold for a shekel, and two measures of barley for a shekel, in the gate of Samaria.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 7:1
