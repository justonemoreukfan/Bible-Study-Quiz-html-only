### 2 Kings 24:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of 2 Kings 24:1?**  
**Answer:** In his days Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up, and Jehoiakim became his servant three years.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 24:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in 2 Kings 24:1?**  
**Answer:** then he turned and rebelled against him.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 24:1

**Q3. According to 2 Kings 24:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** In his days Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up, and Jehoiakim became his servant three years: then he turned and rebelled against him.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 24:1
