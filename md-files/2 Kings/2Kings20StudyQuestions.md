### 2 Kings 20:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of 2 Kings 20:1?**  
**Answer:** In those days was Hezekiah sick unto death. And the prophet Isaiah the son of Amoz came to him, and said unto him, Thus saith the LORD, Set thine house in order.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 20:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in 2 Kings 20:1?**  
**Answer:** for thou shalt die, and not live.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 20:1

**Q3. According to 2 Kings 20:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** In those days was Hezekiah sick unto death. And the prophet Isaiah the son of Amoz came to him, and said unto him, Thus saith the LORD, Set thine house in order; for thou shalt die, and not live.  
**Verse:** 2 Kings 20:1
