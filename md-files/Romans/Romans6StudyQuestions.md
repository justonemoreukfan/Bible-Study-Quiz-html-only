# Romans 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Romans 6:1

**Q1. What question is asked regarding continuing in sin?**  
**Answer:** What shall we say then? Shall we continue in sin, that grace may abound?  
**Verse:** Romans 6:1

**Q2. What is the purpose mentioned in relation to sin and grace in this verse?**  
**Answer:** That grace may abound.  
**Verse:** Romans 6:1

**Q3. Whose action of continuing in sin is being questioned?**  
**Answer:** "we"  
**Verse:** Romans 6:1

---

### Romans 6:2

**Q1. How is the suggestion to continue in sin answered?**  
**Answer:** God forbid.  
**Verse:** Romans 6:2

**Q2. What state have "we" regarding sin died to?**  
**Answer:** How shall we, that are dead to sin...  
**Verse:** Romans 6:2

**Q3. What is asked about living any longer after dying to sin?**  
**Answer:** ...live any longer therein?  
**Verse:** Romans 6:2

---

### Romans 6:3

**Q1. What ignorance is addressed regarding those baptized into Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** Know ye not, that so many of us as were baptized into Jesus Christ...  
**Verse:** Romans 6:3

**Q2. What specific event were these individuals baptized into?**  
**Answer:** ...into his death?  
**Verse:** Romans 6:3

**Q3. Who were they baptized into according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Jesus Christ  
**Verse:** Romans 6:3

---

### Romans 6:4

**Q1. By what means were "we" buried with him?**  
**Answer:** Therefore we are buried with him by baptism into death...  
**Verse:** Romans 6:4

**Q2. Who was Christ raised up from the dead by?**  
**Answer:** ...by the glory of the Father...  
**Verse:** Romans 6:4

**Q3. In what manner should "we" also walk?**  
**Answer:** ...even so we also should walk in newness of life.  
**Verse:** Romans 6:4

---

### Romans 6:5

**Q1. What likeness are "we" planted together in?**  
**Answer:** For if we have been planted together in the likeness of his death...  
**Verse:** Romans 6:5

**Q2. What other likeness shall "we" be in?**  
**Answer:** ...we shall be also in the likeness of his resurrection:  
**Verse:** Romans 6:5

**Q3. What kind of connection to his death is mentioned first in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The likeness of his death  
**Verse:** Romans 6:5

---

### Romans 6:6

**Q1. What is known regarding "our old man"?**  
**Answer:** Knowing this, that our old man is crucified with him...  
**Verse:** Romans 6:6

**Q2. What was the purpose of the body of sin being destroyed?**  
**Answer:** ...that the body of sin might be destroyed...  
**Verse:** Romans 6:6

**Q3. What should "we" not serve henceforth?**  
**Answer:** ...that henceforth we should not serve sin.  
**Verse:** Romans 6:6

---

### Romans 6:7

**Q1. What has happened to the person that is dead?**  
**Answer:** For he that is dead is freed from sin.  
**Verse:** Romans 6:7

**Q2. From what is a dead person freed?**  
**Answer:** Sin  
**Verse:** Romans 6:7

**Q3. Who is freed from sin according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** He that is dead  
**Verse:** Romans 6:7

---

### Romans 6:8

**Q1. If "we" be dead with Christ, what do "we" believe?**  
**Answer:** Now if we be dead with Christ, we believe that we shall also live with him:  
**Verse:** Romans 6:8

**Q2. With whom do "we" believe "we" shall also live?**  
**Answer:** ...with him  
**Verse:** Romans 6:8

**Q3. What previous condition with Christ is stated in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Dead with Christ  
**Verse:** Romans 6:8

---

### Romans 6:9

**Q1. Knowing that Christ being raised from the dead dies no more, what rules over him no more?**  
**Answer:** ...death hath no more dominion over him.  
**Verse:** Romans 6:9

**Q2. From where has Christ been raised?**  
**Answer:** ...from the dead...  
**Verse:** Romans 6:9

**Q3. How often does Christ die now that he is raised?**  
**Answer:** ...dieth no more...  
**Verse:** Romans 6:9

---

### Romans 6:10

**Q1. In that Christ died, how did he die regarding sin?**  
**Answer:** For in that he died, he died unto sin once...  
**Verse:** Romans 6:10

**Q2. How often did Christ die unto sin?**  
**Answer:** Once  
**Verse:** Romans 6:10

**Q3. In that Christ liveth, unto whom does he live?**  
**Answer:** ...but in that he liveth, he liveth unto God.  
**Verse:** Romans 6:10

---

### Romans 6:11

**Q1. How should "ye" reckon yourselves regarding sin?**  
**Answer:** Likewise reckon ye also yourselves to be dead indeed unto sin...  
**Verse:** Romans 6:11

**Q2. How should "ye" reckon yourselves regarding God?**  
**Answer:** ...but alive unto God through Jesus Christ our Lord.  
**Verse:** Romans 6:11

**Q3. Through whom are "ye" alive unto God?**  
**Answer:** ...through Jesus Christ our Lord.  
**Verse:** Romans 6:11

---

### Romans 6:12

**Q1. What must not reign in your mortal body?**  
**Answer:** Let not sin therefore reign in your mortal body...  
**Verse:** Romans 6:12

**Q2. What should you obey regarding the mortal body?**  
**Answer:** ...that ye should obey it in the lusts thereof.  
**Verse:** Romans 6:12

**Q3. What kind of body is mentioned where sin must not reign?**  
**Answer:** Your mortal body  
**Verse:** Romans 6:12

---

### Romans 6:13

**Q1. What should "ye" not yield your members as unto sin?**  
**Answer:** Neither yield ye your members as instruments of unrighteousness unto sin...  
**Verse:** Romans 6:13

**Q2. As whom should "ye" yield yourselves unto God?**  
**Answer:** ...but yield yourselves unto God, as those that are alive from the dead...  
**Verse:** Romans 6:13

**Q3. As what should your members be yielded unto God?**  
**Answer:** ...and your members as instruments of righteousness unto God.  
**Verse:** Romans 6:13

---

### Romans 6:14

**Q1. What shall not have dominion over you?**  
**Answer:** For sin shall not have dominion over you...  
**Verse:** Romans 6:14

**Q2. Why shall sin not have dominion over you?**  
**Answer:** ...for ye are not under the law, but under grace.  
**Verse:** Romans 6:14

**Q3. What are you under instead of the law?**  
**Answer:** Grace  
**Verse:** Romans 6:14

### Romans 6:15

**Q1. What question is asked because we are not under the law, but under grace?**

**Verse:** Romans 6:15

**Q2. How is the question about sinning under grace answered?**  
**Answer:** God forbid.  
**Verse:** Romans 6:15

**Q3. What condition are "we" under in this verse's question?**  
**Answer:** ...not under the law, but under grace.  
**Verse:** Romans 6:15

### Romans 6:16

**Q1. Know ye not that to whom ye yield yourselves servants to obey, whose servants ye are whom ye obey?**  
**Answer:** Know ye not, that to whom ye yield yourselves servants to obey, his servants ye are to whom ye obey...  
**Verse:** Romans 6:16

**Q2. What does obedience lead unto?**  
**Answer:** ...whether of sin unto death...  
**Verse:** Romans 6:16

**Q3. What does obedience to righteousness lead unto?**  
**Answer:** ...or of obedience unto righteousness?  
**Verse:** Romans 6:16

---

### Romans 6:17

**Verse:** Romans 6:17

**Verse:** Romans 6:17

**Verse:** Romans 6:19
