# Romans 11 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Romans 11:1

**Q1. Who does Paul ask God has not cast away?**  
**Answer:** His people.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:1

**Q2. What does Paul say he himself is?**  
**Answer:** An Israelite.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:1

**Q3. Of whose seed is Paul?**  
**Answer:** Of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:1

---

### Romans 11:2

**Q1. Who has not cast away his people?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:2

**Q2. What did God do with his people whom he foreknew?**  
**Answer:** He hath not cast them away.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:2

**Q3. What does Paul ask regarding what the scripture saith of Elias?**  
**Answer:** How he maketh intercession to God against Israel.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:2

---

### Romans 11:3

**Q1. Who did they kill according to Elias's complaint?**  
**Answer:** Thy prophets.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:3

**Q2. What did they dig down according to the verse?**  
**Answer:** Thine altars.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:3

**Q3. Who is left, and what are they seeking?**  
**Answer:** I am left alone, and they seek my life.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:3

---

### Romans 11:4

**Q1. What was the answer of God unto him?**  
**Answer:** I have reserved to myself seven thousand men.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:4

**Q2. What have the seven thousand men not bowed the knee to?**  
**Answer:** The image of Baal.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:4

**Q3. Whose image is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Baal.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:4

---

### Romans 11:5

**Q1. What is there at this present time also?**  
**Answer:** A remnant.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:5

**Q2. According to what election is the remnant?**  
**Answer:** According to the election of grace.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:5

**Q3. What kind of election is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The election of grace.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:5

---

### Romans 11:6

**Q1. If it is by grace, what is it no more?**  
**Answer:** No more of works.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:6

**Q2. What would grace no more be if it were of works?**  
**Answer:** No more grace.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:6

**Q3. What would work no more be if it were of grace?**  
**Answer:** Otherwise work is no more work.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:6

---

### Romans 11:7

**Q1. What has Israel not obtained?**  
**Answer:** That which he seeketh for.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:7

**Q2. Who has obtained what Israel sought for?**  
**Answer:** The election.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:7

**Q3. What happened to the rest?**  
**Answer:** They were blinded.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:7

---

### Romans 11:8

**Q1. According as it is written, what has God given them?**  
**Answer:** The spirit of slumber.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:8

**Q2. What kind of eyes have they been given so that they should not see?**  
**Answer:** Eyes that they should not see.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:8

**Q3. What kind of ears have they been given so that they should not hear?**  
**Answer:** Ears that they should not hear.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:8

---

### Romans 11:9

**Q1. Whose words are cited in David saying, "Let their table be made..."?**  
**Answer:** David.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:9

**Q2. What should be made a snare and a trap?**  
**Answer:** Their table.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:9

**Q3. What else should be made for them besides a snare and a trap?**  
**Answer:** A stumblingblock, and a recompence unto them.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:9

---

### Romans 11:10

**Q1. What should happen to their eyes so that they may not see?**  
**Answer:** Let their eyes be darkened.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:10

**Q2. How far should their eyes be darkened?**  
**Answer:** That they may not see.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:10

**Q3. What should be done to their back continually?**  
**Answer:** Bow down their back alway.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:10

---

### Romans 11:11

**Q1. What question does Paul ask about their stumbling?**  
**Answer:** Have they stumbled that they should fall?  
**Verse:** Romans 11:11

**Q2. How does Paul answer his own question about whether they should fall?**  
**Answer:** God forbid.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:11

**Q3. Through whose fall is salvation come unto the Gentiles?**  
**Answer:** Through their fall.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:11

---

### Romans 11:12

**Q1. If the fall of them is what to the world?**  
**Answer:** The riches of the world.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:12

**Q2. What is the diminishing of them to the Gentiles?**  
**Answer:** The riches of the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:12

**Q3. How much more shall their fulness be?**  
**Answer:** Much more their fulness.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:12

---

### Romans 11:13

**Q1. To whom does Paul speak in this verse?**  
**Answer:** You Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:13

**Q2. Inasmuch as Paul is the apostle of whom, does he magnify his office?**  
**Answer:** The Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:13

**Q3. What does Paul magnify?**  
**Answer:** Mine office.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:13

---

### Romans 11:14

**Q1. By any means, to provoke to emulation whom does Paul hope?**  
**Answer:** Them which are my flesh.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:14

**Q2. What might Paul save by provoking them to emulation?**  
**Answer:** Some of them.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:14

**Q3. Who does Paul refer to as "my flesh"?**  
**Answer:** His kinsmen according to the flesh (implied by context / Jews).  
**Verse:** Romans 11:14

---

### Romans 11:15

**Q1. If the casting away of them is the reconciling of what, what is the receiving of them?**  
**Answer:** The reconciling of the world.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:15

**Q2. What is the receiving of them, if the casting away is the reconciling of the world?**  
**Answer:** Life from the dead.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:15

**Q3. What is spoken of as being cast away?**  
**Answer:** Them.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:15

---

### Romans 11:16

**Q1. If the firstfruit be holy, what is the lump?**  
**Answer:** The lump is also holy.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:16

**Q2. If the root be holy, what are the branches?**  
**Answer:** The branches are also holy.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:16

**Q3. What is described as holy in relation to the branches?**  
**Answer:** The root.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:16

---

### Romans 11:17

**Q1. If some of the branches be what, what were you?**  
**Answer:** Broken off.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:17

**Q2. What kind of tree were the Gentiles grafted in among?**  
**Answer:** A wild olive tree.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:17

**Q3. What part of the olive tree were the Gentiles made partakers of?**  
**Answer:** The root and fatness of the olive tree.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:17

---

### Romans 11:18

**Q1. Against whom should you not boast?**  
**Answer:** Against the branches.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:18

**Q2. If you boast, what supports whom?**  
**Answer:** Thou bearest not the root, but the root thee.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:18

**Q3. What bears the person being addressed?**  
**Answer:** The root.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:18

---

### Romans 11:19

**Q1. What will you say about the branches?**  
**Answer:** The branches were broken off.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:19

**Q2. For what purpose were the branches broken off according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** That I might be grafted in.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:19

**Q3. Who might be grafted in because the branches were broken off?**  
**Answer:** I (the speaker/Gentile).  
**Verse:** Romans 11:19

---

### Romans 11:20

**Q1. Because of what were they broken off?**  
**Answer:** Because of unbelief.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:20

**Q2. By what do you stand?**  
**Answer:** By faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:20

**Q3. What attitude should you not hold, but instead fear?**  
**Answer:** Be not highminded, but fear.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:20

---

### Romans 11:21

**Verse:** Romans 11:21

**Q2. What might God not do to thee either?**  
**Answer:** Spare not thee.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:21

**Q3. Whose natural branches are mentioned?**  
**Answer:** The natural branches.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:21

---

### Romans 11:22

**Q1. Behold therefore the goodness and what else of God?**  
**Answer:** Severity.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:22

**Q2. Upon them which fell, what is it?**  
**Answer:** Severity.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:22

**Q3. Toward thee, what is it if thou continue in his goodness?**  
**Answer:** Goodness.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:22

### Romans 11:23

**Q1. And they also, if they abide not still in what, shall be grafted in?**

**Verse:** Romans 11:23

**Q2. Who is able to graft them in again?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:23

**Q3. What is God able to do to them again?**

### Romans 11:24

**Q1. Out of what olive tree were you cut?**  
**Answer:** The olive tree which is wild by nature.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:24

**Q2. Where were you grafted contrary to nature?**  
**Answer:** Into a good olive tree.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:24

**Q3. Whose natural branches shall be grafted into their own olive tree?**  
**Answer:** These, which be the natural branches.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:24

**Verse:** Romans 11:25

**Answer:** Wise in your own conceits.  
**Verse:** Romans 11:25
