# Romans 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Romans 4:1

**Q1. What shall we say that Abraham, our father as pertaining to the flesh, has found?**  
**Answer:** What Abraham, our father as pertaining to the flesh, has found.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:1

**Q2. Who is referred to as "our father as pertaining to the flesh"?**  
**Answer:** Abraham.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:1

**Q3. To whom does the text attribute the fatherhood as pertaining to the flesh regarding Abraham?**  
**Answer:** "our father"  
**Verse:** Romans 4:1

---

### Romans 4:2

**Q1. If Abraham were justified by works, what would he have?**  
**Answer:** Whereof to glory.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:2

**Q2. Before whom would Abraham have whereof to glory if he were justified by works?**  
**Answer:** Before God.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:2

**Q3. But before whom does Abraham not have whereof to glory?**  
**Answer:** Not before God.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:2

---

### Romans 4:3

**Q1. What scripture says that Abraham believed God?**  
**Answer:** What saith the scripture?  
**Verse:** Romans 4:3

**Q2. How did Abraham regard God according to the scripture?**  
**Answer:** Abraham believed God.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:3

**Q3. What was that belief counted unto him for?**  
**Answer:** Righteousness.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:3

---

### Romans 4:4

**Q1. To him that works, is the reward reckoned of grace or of debt?**  
**Answer:** Not of grace, but of debt.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:4

**Q2. What is not reckoned of grace, but of debt, to him?**  
**Answer:** The reward.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:4

**Q3. What does the person do to make the reward reckoned of debt?**  
**Answer:** Works.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:4

---

### Romans 4:5

**Q1. To him that works not, but believes on whom, is his faith counted for righteousness?**  
**Answer:** On him that justifieth the ungodly.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:5

**Q2. What is counted for righteousness to him that works not?**  
**Answer:** His faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:5

**Q3. Who does the person believe on in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Him that justifieth the ungodly.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:5

---

### Romans 4:6

**Q1. Whose blessedness does David also describe?**  
**Answer:** The blessedness of the man.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:6

**Q2. To whom does God impute righteousness apart from works?**  
**Answer:** Unto whom God imputeth righteousness without works.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:6

**Q3. Who describes this blessedness?**  
**Answer:** David.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:6

---

### Romans 4:7

**Q1. Whose iniquities are forgiven?**  
**Answer:** They whose iniquities are forgiven.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:7

**Q2. What is covered regarding those whose iniquities are forgiven?**  
**Answer:** Their sins.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:7

**Q3. Whose state is described as having sins covered?**  
**Answer:** They whose sins are covered.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:7

---

### Romans 4:8

**Q1. To whom will the Lord not impute sin?**  
**Answer:** The man to whom the Lord will not impute sin.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:8

**Q2. What will the Lord not impute to this man?**  
**Answer:** Sin.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:8

**Q3. Who is the one who will not impute sin to this man?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:8

---

### Romans 4:9

**Q1. Cometh this blessedness then upon the circumcision only, or upon what else?**  
**Answer:** Or upon the uncircumcision also.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:9

**Q2. What do we say that faith was reckoned to Abraham for?**  
**Answer:** For righteousness.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:9

**Q3. Whose faith was reckoned for righteousness?**  
**Answer:** Abraham's.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:9

---

### Romans 4:10

**Q1. How was Abraham's faith then reckoned?**  
**Answer:** When he was in circumcision, or in uncircumcision.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:10

**Q2. When was it not reckoned?**  
**Answer:** In circumcision.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:10

**Q3. When was it reckoned?**  
**Answer:** In uncircumcision.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:10

---

### Romans 4:11

**Q1. What sign did Abraham receive?**  
**Answer:** The sign of circumcision.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:11

**Q2. Of what was circumcision a seal?**  
**Answer:** The righteousness of the faith which he had yet being uncircumcised.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:11

**Q3. For what purpose was circumcision given to him, that he might be what?**  
**Answer:** The father of all them that believe, though they be not circumcised.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:11

---

### Romans 4:12

**Q1. Whose father is he also to those who are not of the circumcision only?**  
**Answer:** The father of circumcision.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:12

**Q2. Who else does he walk in the steps of?**  
**Answer:** The steps of that faith of our father Abraham.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:12

**Q3. When did Abraham have that faith?**  
**Answer:** Being yet uncircumcised.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:12

---

### Romans 4:13

**Q1. To whom and to his seed was the promise that he should be the heir of the world?**  
**Answer:** To Abraham.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:13

**Q2. Through what was this promise given?**  
**Answer:** Through the righteousness of faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:13

**Q3. What was Abraham and his seed promised to be?**  
**Answer:** The heir of the world.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:13

---

### Romans 4:14

**Q1. If they which are of the law be heirs, what is made void?**  
**Answer:** Faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:14

**Q2. If they which are of the law be heirs, what is made of no effect?**  
**Answer:** The promise.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:14

**Q3. Who would have to be the heirs for faith to be made void?**  
**Answer:** They which are of the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:14

---

### Romans 4:15

**Q1. What does the law work?**  
**Answer:** Wrath.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:15

**Q2. Where there is no law, what is there not?**  
**Answer:** Transgression.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:15

**Q3. What operates where the law is present?**  
**Answer:** Wrath.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:15

---

### Romans 4:16

**Q1. Therefore why is it of faith?**  
**Answer:** That it might be by grace.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:16

**Q2. To whom is the promise sure?**  
**Answer:** To all the seed.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:16

**Q3. Whose seed is referred to in relation to the law?**  
**Answer:** Not to that only which is of the law, but to that also which is of the faith of Abraham.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:16

---

### Romans 4:17

**Q1. As it is written, what has God made Abraham?**  
**Answer:** A father of many nations.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:17

**Q2. Before whom did Abraham believe?**  
**Answer:** Before him whom he believed, even God.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:17

**Q3. What two things does God do in relation to the dead and things that are not?**  
**Answer:** Quickeneth the dead, and calleth those things which be not as though they were.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:17

---

### Romans 4:18

**Q1. Against what did Abraham believe in hope?**  
**Answer:** Against hope.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:18

**Q2. Why did he believe in hope against hope?**  
**Answer:** That he might become the father of many nations.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:18

**Q3. Whose word was spoken concerning that which was spoken, "So shall thy seed be"?**  
**Answer:** According to that which was spoken.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:18

---

### Romans 4:19

**Q1. Being not weak in faith, what did Abraham not consider?**  
**Answer:** His own body now dead.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:19

**Q2. How old was Abraham when he considered his body now dead?**  
**Answer:** About an hundred years old.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:19

**Q3. Whose deadness did he also not consider besides his own body?**  
**Answer:** The deadness of Sarah's womb.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:19

---

### Romans 4:20

**Q1. What did Abraham not stagger at through unbelief?**  
**Answer:** At the promise of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:20

**Q2. How was he strengthened regarding the promise of God?**  
**Answer:** Strong in faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:20

**Q3. To whom did he give glory?**  
**Answer:** To God.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:20

---

### Romans 4:21

**Q1. What was Abraham fully persuaded of?**  
**Answer:** That what he had promised, he was able also to perform.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:21

**Q2. Who was able to perform what He had promised?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:21

**Q3. How persuaded was Abraham?**  
**Answer:** Fully persuaded.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:21

---

### Romans 4:22

**Q1. And therefore what was imputed to him?**  
**Answer:** It was imputed to him for righteousness.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:22

**Q2. What was imputed for righteousness?**  
**Answer:** It (his faith/belief).  
**Verse:** Romans 4:22

**Q3. To whom was it imputed for righteousness?**  
**Answer:** To him (Abraham).  
**Verse:** Romans 4:22

---

### Romans 4:23

**Q1. Now was it written for his sake alone that it was imputed to him?**  
**Answer:** Not for his sake alone.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:23

**Q2. What was not written for his sake alone?**  
**Answer:** It was not written for his sake alone, that it was imputed to him.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:23

**Q3. To whom was it imputed?**  
**Answer:** To him.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:23

---

### Romans 4:24

**Q1. For whom also was righteousness to be imputed?**  
**Answer:** For us also, to whom it shall be imputed.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:24

**Q2. Who must we believe on to have it imputed?**  
**Answer:** On him that raised up Jesus our Lord from the dead.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:24

**Q3. Who was raised up from the dead according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Jesus our Lord.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:24

---

### Romans 4:25

**Q1. For what was Jesus delivered?**  
**Answer:** For our offences.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:25

**Q2. For what was Jesus raised again?**  
**Answer:** For our justification.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:25

**Q3. What happened to Jesus for our offences?**  
**Answer:** He was delivered.  
**Verse:** Romans 4:25
