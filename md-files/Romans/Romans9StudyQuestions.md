# Romans 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Romans 9:1

**Q1. In whom do I say the truth, and lie not?**  
**Answer:** In Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:1

**Q2. What does my conscience also bear witness of?**  
**Answer:** In the Holy Ghost.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:1

**Q3. What does my conscience bear witness with?**  
**Answer:** The Holy Ghost.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:1

---

### Romans 9:2

**Q4. What do I have in my heart?**  
**Answer:** Great heaviness and continual sorrow.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:2

---

### Romans 9:3

**Q5. For whom could I wish that myself were accursed?**  
**Answer:** My brethren, my kinsmen according to the flesh.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:3

**Q6. From whom could I wish to be separated for my brethren?**  
**Answer:** From Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:3

---

### Romans 9:4

**Q7. Who are Israelites?**  
**Answer:** Who are Israelites; to whom pertaineth the adoption, and the glory, and the covenants, and the giving of the law, and the service of God, and the promises.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:4

---

### Romans 9:5

**Q8. Whose are the fathers?**  
**Answer:** Whose are the fathers.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:5

**Q9. Of whom as concerning the flesh came Christ?**  
**Answer:** Of whom as concerning the flesh Christ came.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:5

**Q10. How is God described who is over all?**  
**Answer:** Who is over all, God blessed for ever. Amen.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:5

---

### Romans 9:6

**Q11. What has not taken none effect?**  
**Answer:** The word of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:6

**Q12. Are all they Israel which are of Israel?**  
**Answer:** For they are not all Israel, which are of Israel.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:6

---

### Romans 9:7

**Q13. Are they all children because they are the seed of Abraham?**  
**Answer:** Neither, because they are the seed of Abraham, are they all children.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:7

**Q14. In whom shall thy seed be called?**  
**Answer:** In Isaac.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:7

---

### Romans 9:8

**Q15. Who are not the children of God?**  
**Answer:** They which are the children of the flesh.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:8

**Q16. Who are counted for the seed?**  
**Answer:** The children of the promise.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:8

---

### Romans 9:9

**Q17. What was the word of the promise?**  
**Answer:** At this time will I come, and Sarah shall have a son.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:9

---

### Romans 9:10

**Q18. By whom was Rebecca having conceiving, even by our father?**  
**Answer:** By one.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:10

---

### Romans 9:11

**Q19. When the children were not yet born, what had they not done?**  
**Answer:** Neither having done any good or evil.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:11

**Q20. What might stand according to election?**  
**Answer:** The purpose of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:11

**Q21. Of what did the purpose of God according to election stand, not of?**  
**Answer:** Not of works, but of him that calleth.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:11

---

### Romans 9:12

**Q22. What was it said unto her?**  
**Answer:** The elder shall serve the younger.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:12

---

### Romans 9:13

**Q23. Who was written of, "Jacob have I loved"?**  
**Answer:** As it is written.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:13

**Q24. Who was hated in the text?**  
**Answer:** Esau.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:13

---

### Romans 9:14

**Q25. What shall we say then?**  
**Answer:** Is there unrighteousness with God?  
**Verse:** Romans 9:14

**Q26. What is God's answer to "Is there unrighteousness with God?"**  
**Answer:** God forbid.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:14

---

### Romans 9:15

**Q27. To whom does He say, "I will have mercy on whom I will have mercy"?**  
**Answer:** To Moses.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:15

**Q28. On whom will He have compassion?**  
**Answer:** On whom I will have compassion.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:15

---

### Romans 9:16

**Q29. Of whom is it not of, neither of him that runneth?**  
**Answer:** Of him that willeth.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:16

**Q30. Of whom is it of, that sheweth mercy?**  
**Answer:** Of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:16

---

### Romans 9:17

**Q31. Unto whom does the scripture say this?**  
**Answer:** Unto Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:17

**Q32. For this same purpose have I raised thee up, that I might shew what in thee?**  
**Answer:** My power.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:17

**Q33. That my name might be declared where?**  
**Answer:** Throughout all the earth.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:17

---

### Romans 9:18

**Q34. On whom will He have mercy?**  
**Answer:** He will have mercy on whom he will have mercy.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:18

**Q35. Whom will He harden?**  
**Answer:** Whom he will he hardeneth.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:18

---

### Romans 9:19

**Q36. What will thou say then unto me?**  
**Answer:** Why doth he yet find fault?  
**Verse:** Romans 9:19

**Q37. Who hath resisted his will?**  
**Answer:** For who hath resisted his will?  
**Verse:** Romans 9:19

---

### Romans 9:20

**Q38. Who art thou that repliest against whom?**  
**Answer:** Against God.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:20

**Q39. What shall the thing formed say to him that formed it?**  
**Answer:** Why hast thou made me thus?  
**Verse:** Romans 9:20

---

### Romans 9:21

**Q40. Hath not the potter power over what?**  
**Answer:** Over the clay.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:21

**Q41. Of the same lump, what may the potter make?**  
**Answer:** One vessel unto honour, and another unto dishonour.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:21

---

### Romans 9:22

**Q42. What willing to do, God endured with much longsuffering?**  
**Answer:** What if God, willing to shew his wrath, and to make his power known.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:22

**Q43. What kind of vessels were the vessels of wrath?**  
**Answer:** Vessels of wrath fitted to destruction.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:22

---

### Romans 9:23

**Q44. What might He make known upon the vessels of mercy?**  
**Answer:** The riches of his glory.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:23

**Q45. Which He had afore prepared unto what?**  
**Answer:** Unto glory.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:23

---

### Romans 9:24

**Q46. Whom hath he called, not of the Jews only, but also of whom?**  
**Answer:** Of the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:24

**Q47. Even us, whom hath he called?**  
**Answer:** Even us, whom he hath called.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:24

---

### Romans 9:25

**Q48. As he saith also in whom?**  
**Answer:** In Osee.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:25

**Q49. What will I call them which were not my people?**  
**Answer:** My people.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:25

**Q50. What will I call her which was not beloved?**  
**Answer:** Beloved.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:25

---

### Romans 9:26

**Q51. Where shall it come to pass, in the place?**  
**Answer:** In the place where it was said unto them.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:26

**Q52. What was said unto them, Ye are not what?**  
**Answer:** Ye are not my people.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:26

**Q53. There shall they be called what?**  
**Answer:** The children of the living God.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:26

---

### Romans 9:27

**Q54. Esias also crieth concerning whom?**  
**Answer:** Concerning Israel.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:27

**Q55. Though the number of the children of Israel be as what?**  
**Answer:** As the sand of the sea.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:27

**Q56. What shall be saved?**  
**Answer:** A remnant.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:27

---

### Romans 9:28

**Q57. For he will finish the work, and cut it short in what?**  
**Answer:** In righteousness.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:28

**Q58. What will the Lord make upon the earth?**  
**Answer:** A short work.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:28

---

### Romans 9:29

**Q59. As Esaias said before, except whom had left us a seed?**  
**Answer:** The Lord of Sabaoth.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:29

**Q60. Unto what would we have been made like?**  
**Answer:** Sodoma.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:29

**Q61. Unto what would we have been resembled?**  
**Answer:** Gomorrha.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:29

---

### Romans 9:30

**Q62. What shall we say then?**  
**Answer:** That the Gentiles, which followed not after righteousness, have attained to righteousness.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:30

**Q63. What kind of righteousness is it that the Gentiles have attained to?**  
**Answer:** Even the righteousness which is of faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:30

---

### Romans 9:31

**Q64. But Israel, which followed after the law of righteousness, attained to what?**  
**Answer:** Attained not to the law of righteousness.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:31

---

### Romans 9:32

**Q65. Wherefore? Because they sought it not by what?**  
**Answer:** By faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:32

**Q66. But how did they seek it, as it were by what?**  
**Answer:** By the works of the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:32

**Q67. For what did they stumble?**  
**Answer:** For they stumbled at that stumblingstone.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:32

---

### Romans 9:33

**Q68. As it is written, Behold, what do I lay in Sion?**  
**Answer:** A stumblingstone and rock of offence.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:33

**Q69. Who shall not be ashamed?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever believeth on him.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:33

**Q70. What is laid in Sion?**  
**Answer:** A stumblingstone and rock of offence.  
**Verse:** Romans 9:33
