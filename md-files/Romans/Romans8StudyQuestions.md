# Romans 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Romans 8:1

**Q1. What is there now for them which are in Christ Jesus?**  
**Answer:** There is therefore now no condemnation.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:1

**Q2. How do those who have no condemnation walk?**  
**Answer:** Who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:1

**Q3. Who are those not walking after the flesh in Christ Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Them which are in Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:1

---

### Romans 8:2

**Q4. What has made me free from the law of sin and death?**  
**Answer:** The law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:2

**Q5. What law has the Spirit of life made me free from?**  
**Answer:** The law of sin and death.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:2

**Q6. In whom is the law of the Spirit of life found?**  
**Answer:** In Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:2

---

### Romans 8:3

**Q7. What was the law unable to do because it was weak through the flesh?**  
**Answer:** It could not do that the law could not do.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:3

**Q8. Who did God send in the likeness of sinful flesh?**  
**Answer:** His own Son.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:3

**Q9. What did God send His Son for, concerning sin?**  
**Answer:** For sin.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:3

---

### Romans 8:4

**Q10. That what might be fulfilled in us?**  
**Answer:** The righteousness of the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:4

**Q11. How do we walk, according to Romans 8:4?**  
**Answer:** Not after the flesh, but after the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:4

**Q12. What is fulfilled in us who walk after the Spirit?**  
**Answer:** The righteousness of the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:4

---

### Romans 8:5

**Q13. What do they that are after the flesh do?**  
**Answer:** Do mind the things of the flesh.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:5

**Q14. What do they that are after the Spirit do?**  
**Answer:** Mind the things of the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:5

**Q15. Whose things do those after the flesh mind?**  
**Answer:** The things of the flesh.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:5

---

### Romans 8:6

**Q16. To be carnally minded is what?**  
**Answer:** Death.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:6

**Q17. To be spiritually minded is what?**  
**Answer:** Life and peace.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:6

**Q18. What is the state of being carnally minded?**  
**Answer:** Death.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:6

---

### Romans 8:7

**Q19. Because the carnal mind is what against God?**  
**Answer:** Enmity against God.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:7

**Q20. What is the carnal mind not subject to?**  
**Answer:** The law of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:7

**Q21. Can the carnal mind be subject to the law of God?**  
**Answer:** Neither indeed can be.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:7

---

### Romans 8:8

**Q22. So then they that are in the flesh cannot do what?**  
**Answer:** Please God.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:8

**Q23. Who cannot please God?**  
**Answer:** They that are in the flesh.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:8

**Q24. Where are they who cannot please God?**  
**Answer:** In the flesh.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:8

---

### Romans 8:9

**Q25. Where are ye not, if the Spirit of God dwell in you?**  
**Answer:** Not in the flesh, but in the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:9

**Q26. What if any man have not?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit of Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:9

**Q27. If any man have not the Spirit of Christ, what is he?**  
**Answer:** None of his.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:9

---

### Romans 8:10

**Q28. And if Christ be in you, what is the body?**  
**Answer:** Dead because of sin.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:10

**Q29. What is the Spirit because of righteousness?**  
**Answer:** Life.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:10

**Q30. Why is the body dead because of sin?**  
**Answer:** Because Christ is in you.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:10

---

### Romans 8:11

**Q31. Whose Spirit dwells in you, according to Romans 8:11?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit of him that raised up Jesus from the dead.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:11

**Q32. Who shall also quicken your mortal bodies?**  
**Answer:** He that raised up Christ from the dead.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:11

**Q33. By what does he quicken your mortal bodies?**  
**Answer:** By his Spirit that dwelleth in you.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:11

---

### Romans 8:12

**Q34. Therefore, brethren, we are debtors to what?**  
**Answer:** Not to the flesh.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:12

**Q35. To live after what are we not debtors?**  
**Answer:** To live after the flesh.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:12

**Q36. Who are addressed as debtors not to the flesh?**  
**Answer:** Brethren.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:12

---

### Romans 8:13

**Q37. What shall happen if ye live after the flesh?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall die.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:13

**Q38. Through what do ye mortify the deeds of the body if ye live?**  
**Answer:** Through the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:13

**Q39. What shall happen if ye through the Spirit do mortify the deeds of the body?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall live.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:13

---

### Romans 8:14

**Q40. For as many as are led by whom?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:14

**Q41. Those led by the Spirit of God are what?**  
**Answer:** The sons of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:14

**Q42. Whose sons are they who are led by the Spirit of God?**  
**Answer:** The sons of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:14

---

### Romans 8:15

**Q43. What spirit have ye not received again?**  
**Answer:** The spirit of bondage again to fear.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:15

**Q44. What spirit have ye received?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit of adoption.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:15

**Q45. Whereby do we cry, Abba, Father?**  
**Answer:** Whereby we cry, Abba, Father.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:15

---

### Romans 8:16

**Q46. What bears witness with our spirit?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit itself.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:16

**Q47. With what does the Spirit bear witness?**  
**Answer:** With our spirit.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:16

**Q48. What are we, as the Spirit bears witness with our spirit?**  
**Answer:** The children of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:16

---

### Romans 8:17

**Q49. If children, then what are we?**  
**Answer:** Heirs.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:17

**Q50. Whose heirs are we, and joint-heirs with whom?**  
**Answer:** Heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:17

**Q51. What must we do if so be that we suffer with him, that we may be also glorified together?**  
**Answer:** If so be that we suffer with him.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:17

---

### Romans 8:18

**Q52. How does the apostle reckon the sufferings of this present time?**  
**Answer:** That they are not worthy to be compared.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:18

**Q53. With what are the sufferings not worthy to be compared?**  
**Answer:** With the glory which shall be revealed in us.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:18

**Q54. Where shall the glory be revealed?**  
**Answer:** In us.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:18

---

### Romans 8:19

**Q55. What does the earnest expectation of the creature wait for?**  
**Answer:** The manifestation of the sons of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 8:19
