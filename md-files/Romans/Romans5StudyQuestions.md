# Romans 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Romans 5:1

**Q1. How are we justified?**  
**Answer:** By faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:1

**Q2. What do we have with God being justified by faith?**  
**Answer:** Peace.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:1

**Q3. Through whom do we have peace with God?**  
**Answer:** Our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:1

---

### Romans 5:2

**Q4. Into what do we have access by faith?**  
**Answer:** Into this grace wherein we stand.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:2

**Q5. In what do we rejoice?**  
**Answer:** In hope of the glory of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:2

**Q6. By whom do we have access by faith into this grace?**  
**Answer:** By whom (our Lord Jesus Christ).  
**Verse:** Romans 5:2

---

### Romans 5:3

**Q7. In what else do we glory?**  
**Answer:** In tribulations also.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:3

**Q8. What does tribulation work?**  
**Answer:** Patience.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:3

**Q9. What do we know that tribulation worketh?**  
**Answer:** Patience.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:3

---

### Romans 5:4

**Q10. What does patience work?**  
**Answer:** Experience.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:4

**Q11. What does experience work?**  
**Answer:** Hope.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:4

**Q12. What follows patience?**  
**Answer:** Experience.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:4

---

### Romans 5:5

**Q13. What maketh not ashamed?**  
**Answer:** Hope.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:5

**Q14. What is shed abroad in our hearts?**  
**Answer:** The love of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:5

**Q15. By what is the love of God shed abroad in our hearts?**  
**Answer:** By the Holy Ghost which is given unto us.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:5

---

### Romans 5:6

**Q16. What was our condition when Christ died for the ungodly?**  
**Answer:** Without strength.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:6

**Q17. When did Christ die for the ungodly?**  
**Answer:** In due time.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:6

**Q18. For whom did Christ die?**  
**Answer:** For the ungodly.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:6

---

### Romans 5:7

**Q19. For whom will scarcely one die?**  
**Answer:** For a righteous man.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:7

**Q20. For whom would some peradventure even dare to die?**  
**Answer:** For a good man.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:7

**Q21. What might some dare to do for a good man?**  
**Answer:** Die.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:7

---

### Romans 5:8

**Q22. What does God commend toward us?**  
**Answer:** His love.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:8

**Q23. What were we when Christ died for us?**  
**Answer:** Sinners.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:8

**Q24. Who died for us while we were yet sinners?**  
**Answer:** Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:8

---

### Romans 5:9

**Q25. By what are we now justified?**  
**Answer:** By his blood.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:9

**Q26. From what shall we be saved through him?**  
**Answer:** From wrath.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:9

**Q27. Through whom shall we be saved from wrath?**  
**Answer:** Through him.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:9

---

### Romans 5:10

**Q28. When were we reconciled to God by the death of his Son?**  
**Answer:** When we were enemies.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:10

**Q29. By what were we reconciled to God?**  
**Answer:** By the death of his Son.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:10

**Q30. By what shall we be saved, being reconciled?**  
**Answer:** By his life.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:10

---

### Romans 5:11

**Q31. In whom do we joy through our Lord Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** In God.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:11

**Q32. Through whom do we joy in God?**  
**Answer:** Through our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:11

**Q33. What have we now received by our Lord Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** The atonement.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:11

---

### Romans 5:12

**Q34. By whom did sin enter into the world?**  
**Answer:** By one man.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:12

**Q35. What entered into the world by sin?**  
**Answer:** Death.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:12

**Q36. Upon whom did death pass?**  
**Answer:** Upon all men.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:12

---

### Romans 5:13

**Q37. Until when was sin in the world?**  
**Answer:** Until the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:13

**Q38. When is sin not imputed?**  
**Answer:** When there is no law.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:13

**Q39. What is not imputed when there is no law?**  
**Answer:** Sin.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:13

---

### Romans 5:14

**Q40. From whom to whom did death reign?**  
**Answer:** From Adam to Moses.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:14

**Q41. After whose similitude had some not sinned?**  
**Answer:** Adam's transgression.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:14

**Q42. Who is the figure of him that was to come?**  
**Answer:** Adam.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:14

---

### Romans 5:15

**Q43. What is not as the offence?**  
**Answer:** The free gift.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:15

**Q44. What happened to many through the offence of one?**  
**Answer:** Many be dead.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:15

**Q45. What hath abounded unto many by one man, Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** The grace of God, and the gift by grace.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:15

---

### Romans 5:16

**Q46. The judgment was by one to what?**  
**Answer:** Condemnation.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:16

**Q47. Unto what is the free gift of many offences?**  
**Answer:** Unto justification.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:16

**Q48. By how many was the judgment to condemnation?**  
**Answer:** By one.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:16

---

### Romans 5:17

**Q49. By what did death reign by one?**  
**Answer:** By one man's offence.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:17

**Q50. What do they receive who shall reign in life?**  
**Answer:** Abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:17

**Q51. By whom shall they reign in life?**  
**Answer:** By one, Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:17

---

### Romans 5:18

**Q52. By what did judgment come upon all men to condemnation?**  
**Answer:** By the offence of one.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:18

**Q53. By what did the free gift come upon all men unto justification of life?**  
**Answer:** By the righteousness of one.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:18

**Q54. Unto what did the free gift come upon all men?**  
**Answer:** Unto justification of life.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:18

---

### Romans 5:19

**Q55. What were many made by one man's disobedience?**  
**Answer:** Sinners.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:19

**Q56. By what shall many be made righteous?**  
**Answer:** By the obedience of one.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:19

**Q57. What shall many be made by the obedience of one?**  
**Answer:** Righteous.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:19

---

### Romans 5:20

**Q58. Why did the law enter?**  
**Answer:** That the offence might abound.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:20

**Q59. What did much more abound where sin abounded?**  
**Answer:** Grace.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:20

**Q60. Where did grace much more abound?**  
**Answer:** Where sin abounded.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:20

---

### Romans 5:21

**Q61. Unto what hath sin reigned?**  
**Answer:** Unto death.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:21

**Q62. Through what might grace reign unto eternal life?**  
**Answer:** Through righteousness.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:21

**Q63. By whom does grace reign unto eternal life?**  
**Answer:** By Jesus Christ our Lord.  
**Verse:** Romans 5:21
