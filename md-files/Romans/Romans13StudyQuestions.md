# Romans 13 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Romans 13:1

**Q1. Every soul should be subject unto what powers?**  
**Answer:** The higher powers.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:1

**Q2. There is no power but of what source?**  
**Answer:** Of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:1

**Q3. The powers that be are ordained by whom?**  
**Answer:** By God.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:1

---

### Romans 13:2

**Q4. Whosoever resisteth the power resisteth what?**  
**Answer:** The ordinance of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:2

**Q5. They that resist shall receive to themselves what?**  
**Answer:** Damnation.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:2

**Q6. Who do they resist when they resist the power?**  
**Answer:** The ordinance of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:2

---

### Romans 13:3

**Q7. Rulers are not a terror to what kind of works?**  
**Answer:** Good works.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:3

**Q8. Rulers are a terror to what kind of works?**  
**Answer:** To the evil.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:3

**Q9. What can you do to not be afraid of the power?**  
**Answer:** Do that which is good.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:3

---

### Romans 13:4

**Q10. The minister of God is to thee for what?**  
**Answer:** For good.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:4

**Q11. What does the ruler bear not in vain?**  
**Answer:** The sword.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:4

**Q12. The ruler is a revenger to execute wrath upon whom?**  
**Answer:** Him that doeth evil.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:4

---

### Romans 13:5

**Q13. Ye must needs be subject not only for wrath, but also for what?**  
**Answer:** For conscience sake.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:5

**Q14. Why must ye needs be subject besides conscience sake?**  
**Answer:** For wrath.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:5

**Q15. Whose sake should you be subject for?**  
**Answer:** Conscience sake.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:5

---

### Romans 13:6

**Q16. For this cause pay ye what also?**  
**Answer:** Tribute.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:6

**Q17. Rulers are God's what, attending continually upon this very thing?**  
**Answer:** Ministers.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:6

**Q18. What are the rulers attending continually upon?**  
**Answer:** This very thing.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:6

---

### Romans 13:7

**Q19. Render to all what is due to them, such as what?**  
**Answer:** Tribute to whom tribute is due.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:7

**Q20. What is to be rendered to whom custom is due?**  
**Answer:** Custom.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:7

**Q21. What is to be rendered to whom fear and honor are due?**  
**Answer:** Fear and honor.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:7

---

### Romans 13:8

**Q22. Owe no man anything, but what?**  
**Answer:** To love one another.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:8

**Q23. He that loveth another hath fulfilled what?**  
**Answer:** The law.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:8

**Q24. What is the one thing you should not owe any man?**  
**Answer:** Anything (except to love one another).  
**Verse:** Romans 13:8

---

### Romans 13:9

**Q25. Which commandments are briefly comprehended in this saying?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not commit adultery, Thou shalt not kill, Thou shalt not steal, Thou shalt not bear false witness, Thou shalt not covet.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:9

**Q26. What saying briefly comprehends those commandments?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:9

**Q27. How are you to love your neighbour?**  
**Answer:** As thyself.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:9

---

### Romans 13:10

**Q28. Love worketh no ill to whom?**  
**Answer:** His neighbour.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:10

**Q29. Therefore love is the fulfilling of what?**  
**Answer:** The law.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:10

**Q30. What does love not work to his neighbour?**  
**Answer:** Ill.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:10

---

### Romans 13:11

**Q31. Knowing the time, that now it is high time to do what?**  
**Answer:** Awake out of sleep.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:11

**Q32. Now is our salvation nearer than when we did what?**  
**Answer:** Believed.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:11

**Q33. What is now nearer than when we believed?**  
**Answer:** Our salvation.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:11

---

### Romans 13:12

**Q34. The night is far spent, and what is at hand?**  
**Answer:** The day.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:12

**Q35. Let us therefore cast off the works of what?**  
**Answer:** Darkness.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:12

**Q36. Let us put on the armor of what?**  
**Answer:** Light.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:12

---

### Romans 13:13

**Q37. Let us walk honestly, as in what?**  
**Answer:** The day.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:13

**Q38. What should we not walk in, according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Rioting and drunkenness, chambering and wantonness, strife and envying.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:13

**Q39. What kind of actions should be avoided in chambers and actions?**  
**Answer:** Chambering and wantonness.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:13

---

### Romans 13:14

**Q40. Put ye on whom?**  
**Answer:** The Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:14

**Q41. Make not provision for what?**  
**Answer:** The flesh.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:14

**Q42. What should you not fulfill?**  
**Answer:** The lusts thereof.  
**Verse:** Romans 13:14
