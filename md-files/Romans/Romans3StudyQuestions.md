# Romans 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Romans 3:1

**Q1. What advantage then hath the Jew?**  
**Answer:** Much every way.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:1

**Q2. What is chiefly the advantage of the Jew?**  
**Answer:** That unto them were committed the oracles of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:1

**Q3. To whom were the oracles of God committed?**  
**Answer:** Unto them (the Jews).  
**Verse:** Romans 3:1

---

### Romans 3:2

**Q4. What is true if some did not believe?**  
**Answer:** Their unbelief shall not make the faith of God without effect.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:2

**Q5. Whose faith shall not be made without effect?**  
**Answer:** The faith of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:2

**Q6. What shall not make the faith of God without effect?**  
**Answer:** Their unbelief.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:2

---

### Romans 3:3

**Q7. What if some did not believe?**  
**Answer:** Shall their unbelief make the faith of God without effect?  
**Verse:** Romans 3:3

**Q8. What question is asked regarding some who did not believe?**  
**Answer:** Shall their unbelief make the faith of God without effect?  
**Verse:** Romans 3:3

**Q9. What subject is addressed concerning some who did not believe?**  
**Answer:** Their unbelief.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:3

---

### Romans 3:4

**Q10. What is God?**  
**Answer:** True.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:4

**Q11. What is every man?**  
**Answer:** A liar.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:4

**Q12. That thou mightest be justified in what?**  
**Answer:** In thy sayings.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:4

---

### Romans 3:5

**Q13. What commendeth the righteousness of God?**  
**Answer:** Our unrighteousness.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:5

**Q14. What question is asked about God who taketh vengeance?**  
**Answer:** Is God unrighteous who taketh vengeance?  
**Verse:** Romans 3:5

**Q15. After what manner do I speak?**  
**Answer:** As a man.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:5

---

### Romans 3:6

**Q16. God forbid: for then how shall God judge?**  
**Answer:** The world.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:6

**Q17. What will God judge if it were otherwise?**  
**Answer:** The world.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:6

**Q18. What phrase precedes the question of God judging the world?**  
**Answer:** God forbid.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:6

---

### Romans 3:7

**Q19. What has more abounded through my lie?**  
**Answer:** The truth of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:7

**Q20. Unto whose glory has the truth of God more abounded?**  
**Answer:** Unto his glory.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:7

**Q21. Why am I also yet judged as what?**  
**Answer:** As a sinner.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:7

---

### Romans 3:8

**Q22. And not rather, (as we be slanderously reported, and as some affirm that we say,) what?**  
**Answer:** Let us do evil, that good may come.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:8

**Q23. Whose damnation is just?**  
**Answer:** Whose damnation is just (referring to those whose report is slanderous).  
**Verse:** Romans 3:8

**Q24. What are some reported and affirmed to say?**  
**Answer:** Let us do evil, that good may come.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:8

---

### Romans 3:9

**Q25. What then?**  
**Answer:** Are we better than they?  
**Verse:** Romans 3:9

**Q26. Are they (the Jews) better than the Gentiles?**  
**Answer:** No, in no wise.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:9

**Q27. What has been proved before regarding both Jews and Gentiles?**  
**Answer:** That they are all under sin.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:9

---

### Romans 3:10

**Q28. As it is written, who is righteous?**  
**Answer:** There is none righteous, no, not one.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:10

**Q29. How many righteous are there according to the scripture?**  
**Answer:** None, no, not one.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:10

**Q30. Where is the statement "There is none righteous" found?**  
**Answer:** As it is written.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:10

---

### Romans 3:11

**Q31. Who is described as understanding?**  
**Answer:** There is none that understandeth.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:11

**Q32. Who is described as seeking after God?**  
**Answer:** There is none that seeketh after God.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:11

**Q33. What is said of anyone understanding or seeking God?**  
**Answer:** There is none.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:11

---

### Romans 3:12

**Q34. Where have they all gone?**  
**Answer:** They are all gone out of the way.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:12

**Q35. What have they become together?**  
**Answer:** They are together become unprofitable.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:12

**Q36. How many doeth good?**  
**Answer:** There is none that doeth good, no, not one.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:12

---

### Romans 3:13

**Q37. What is their throat like?**  
**Answer:** An open sepulchre.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:13

**Q38. What have they used with their tongues?**  
**Answer:** With their tongues they have used deceit.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:13

**Q39. What is under their lips?**  
**Answer:** The poison of asps.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:13

---

### Romans 3:14

**Q40. What is whose mouth full of?**  
**Answer:** Cursing and bitterness.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:14

**Q41. Whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness?**  
**Answer:** Their mouth.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:14

**Q42. What two things fill their mouth?**  
**Answer:** Cursing and bitterness.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:14

---

### Romans 3:15

**Q43. What are their feet swift to do?**  
**Answer:** To shed blood.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:15

**Q44. Whose feet are swift to shed blood?**  
**Answer:** Their feet.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:15

**Q45. What quality do their feet possess regarding blood?**  
**Answer:** Swift.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:15

---

### Romans 3:16

**Q46. What is in their ways?**  
**Answer:** Destruction and misery.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:16

**Q47. Where are destruction and misery found?**  
**Answer:** In their ways.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:16

**Q48. Whose ways contain destruction and misery?**  
**Answer:** Theirs.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:16

---

### Romans 3:17

**Q49. What have they not known?**  
**Answer:** The way of peace.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:17

**Q50. Whose path or way has not been known by them?**  
**Answer:** The way of peace.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:17

**Q51. What did they fail to know?**  
**Answer:** The way of peace.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:17

---

### Romans 3:18

**Q52. What is not before their eyes?**  
**Answer:** There is no fear of God before their eyes.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:18

**Q53. Where is there no fear of God?**  
**Answer:** Before their eyes.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:18

**Q54. What is absent before their eyes?**  
**Answer:** The fear of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:18

---

### Romans 3:19

**Q55. What things soever the law saith, it saith to whom?**  
**Answer:** To them who are under the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:19

**Q56. Why does the law speak to those under it, that every mouth may be stopped?**  
**Answer:** That every mouth may be stopped.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:19

**Q57. What may become guilty before God?**  
**Answer:** All the world.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:19

---

### Romans 3:20

**Q58. By the deeds of the law shall what be justified?**  
**Answer:** No flesh.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:20

**Q59. What comes by the law?**  
**Answer:** The knowledge of sin.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:20

**Q60. In whose sight shall no flesh be justified by the deeds of the law?**  
**Answer:** In his sight.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:20

---

### Romans 3:21

**Q61. What is manifested apart from the law?**  
**Answer:** The righteousness of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:21

**Q62. What bears witness to the righteousness of God manifested without the law?**  
**Answer:** The law and the prophets.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:21

**Q63. What has been manifested without the law?**  
**Answer:** The righteousness of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:21

---

### Romans 3:22

**Q64. Even the righteousness of God which is by what?**  
**Answer:** By faith of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:22

**Q65. Unto whom and upon whom is the righteousness of God?**  
**Answer:** Unto all and upon all them that believe.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:22

**Q66. Is there any difference mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** For there is no difference.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:22

---

### Romans 3:23

**Q67. Who have sinned?**  
**Answer:** All.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:23

**Q68. What have all fallen short of?**  
**Answer:** The glory of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:23

**Q69. What have all done according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** All have sinned.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:23

---

### Romans 3:24

**Q70. How are they justified freely?**  
**Answer:** By his grace.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:24

**Q71. Through what is this free justification?**  
**Answer:** Through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:24

**Q72. In whom is the redemption found?**  
**Answer:** In Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:24

---

### Romans 3:25

**Q73. Whom hath God set forth to be a propitiation?**  
**Answer:** Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:25

**Q74. Through what is the propitiation in his blood?**  
**Answer:** Through faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:25

**Q75. To declare whose righteousness for the remission of sins?**  
**Answer:** His righteousness.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:25

---

### Romans 3:26

**Q76. To declare, I say, at this time what?**  
**Answer:** His righteousness.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:26

**Q77. That God might be what, and the justifier of him which believeth in Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Just.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:26

**Q78. Of whom is God the justifier?**  
**Answer:** Him which believeth in Jesus.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:26

---

### Romans 3:27

**Q79. Where is boasting then?**  
**Answer:** It is excluded.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:27

**Q80. By what law is boasting excluded?**  
**Answer:** By the law of works? Nay: but by the law of faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:27

**Q81. What kind of law excludes boasting?**  
**Answer:** The law of faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:27

---

### Romans 3:28

**Q82. Therefore we conclude that a man is justified by what?**  
**Answer:** By faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:28

**Q83. Without what is a man justified by faith?**  
**Answer:** Without the deeds of the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:28

**Q84. What do we conclude about a man's justification?**  
**Answer:** That a man is justified by faith without the deeds of the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:28

---

### Romans 3:29

**Q85. Is God the God of whom only?**  
**Answer:** The Jews only.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:29

**Q86. Is God not also the God of whom?**  
**Answer:** Of the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:29

**Q87. What question is asked regarding God and the Jews and Gentiles?**  
**Answer:** Is he not also of the Gentiles?  
**Verse:** Romans 3:29

---

### Romans 3:30

**Q88. Seeing it is one God, which shall justify what?**  
**Answer:** The circumcision by faith, and uncircumcision through faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:30

**Q89. How shall the circumcision be justified?**  
**Answer:** By faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:30

**Q90. How shall the uncircumcision be justified?**  
**Answer:** Through faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:30

---

### Romans 3:31

**Q91. Do we then make void what through faith?**  
**Answer:** The law.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:31

**Q92. God forbid: yea, what do we do to the law?**  
**Answer:** We establish the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:31

**Q93. Through what do we establish the law?**  
**Answer:** Through faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 3:31
