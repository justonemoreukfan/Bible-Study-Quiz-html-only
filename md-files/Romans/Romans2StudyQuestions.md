# Romans 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Romans 2:1

**Q1. Who is inexcusable, O man?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever thou art that judgest.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:1

**Q2. For wherein does a man condemn himself when he judges another?**  
**Answer:** Wherein thou judgest another, thou condemnest thyself.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:1

**Q3. What do thou that judgest do, which the same things are practiced by?**  
**Answer:** Thou that judgest doest the same things.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:1

---

### Romans 2:2

**Q1. According to what is the judgment of God against them which commit such things?**  
**Answer:** According to truth.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:2

---

### Romans 2:3

**Q1. What is asked of the man that judges them which do such things and does the same?**  
**Answer:** Thinkest thou this, O man, that judgest them which do such things, and doest the same?  
**Verse:** Romans 2:3

**Q2. What will the man that judgest them and doest the same escape?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt escape the judgment of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:3

---

### Romans 2:4

**Q1. What doest thou despise regarding the riches of His goodness and forbearance and longsuffering?**  
**Answer:** Or despisest thou the riches of his goodness and forbearance and longsuffering.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:4

**Q2. What is not knowing concerning the goodness of God?**  
**Answer:** Not knowing that the goodness of God leadeth thee to repentance.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:4

---

### Romans 2:5

**Q1. After what does the hard and impenitent heart treasure up unto itself?**  
**Answer:** After thy hardness and impenitent heart treasurest up unto thyself.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:5

**Q2. Against what day is wrath treasured up by the hard and impenitent heart?**  
**Answer:** Against the day of wrath.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:5

**Q3. What is revealed in the day of wrath?**  
**Answer:** The revelation of the righteous judgment of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:5

---

### Romans 2:6

**Q1. Who will render to every man according to his deeds?**  
**Answer:** Who will render to every man according to his deeds.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:6

---

### Romans 2:7

**Q1. To whom will eternal life be rendered by patient continuance in well doing?**  
**Answer:** To them who by patient continuance in well doing seek for glory and honour and immortality.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:7

**Q2. What are those who patiently continue in well doing seeking for?**  
**Answer:** Glory and honour and immortality.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:7

**Q3. What will be rendered to them who seek for glory and honour and immortality?**  
**Answer:** Eternal life.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:7

---

### Romans 2:8

**Q1. Who are unto indignation and wrath?**  
**Answer:** Unto them that are contentious.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:8

**Q2. What do those who are contentious obey instead of the truth?**  
**Answer:** Unrighteousness.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:8

**Q3. What is obeyed by those who are contentious and obey unrighteousness?**  
**Answer:** Indignation and wrath.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:8

---

### Romans 2:9

**Q1. Upon what soul of man is tribulation and anguish?**  
**Answer:** Upon every soul of man that doeth evil.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:9

**Q2. Who is first among those upon whom tribulation and anguish come for doing evil?**  
**Answer:** Of the Jew first, and also of the Gentile.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:9

---

### Romans 2:10

**Q1. What is upon every man that worketh good?**  
**Answer:** Glory, honour, and peace.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:10

**Q2. Who is first among those upon whom glory, honour, and peace come for working good?**  
**Answer:** To the Jew first, and also to the Gentile.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:10

---

### Romans 2:11

**Q1. What is not with God regarding any person?**  
**Answer:** There is no respect of persons with God.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:11

---

### Romans 2:12

**Q1. How do those who have sinned without law also perish?**  
**Answer:** Without law.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:12

**Q2. How shall those who have sinned in the law be judged?**  
**Answer:** By the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:12

---

### Romans 2:13

**Q1. Who are not justified before God?**  
**Answer:** The hearers of the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:13

**Q2. Who shall be justified?**  
**Answer:** The doers of the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:13

---

### Romans 2:14

**Q1. Who do by nature the things contained in the law?**  
**Answer:** The Gentiles, which have not the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:14

**Q2. What are these Gentiles, having not the law, unto themselves?**  
**Answer:** A law unto themselves.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:14

---

### Romans 2:15

**Q1. What is written in the hearts of the Gentiles who have not the law?**  
**Answer:** The work of the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:15

**Q2. What bearing witness along with the work of the law written in their hearts?**  
**Answer:** Their conscience also bearing witness.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:15

**Q3. What do their thoughts do regarding one another?**  
**Answer:** Accusing or else excusing one another.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:15

---

### Romans 2:16

**Q1. In what day shall God judge the secrets of men?**  
**Answer:** In the day when God shall judge the secrets of men.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:16

**Q2. By whom will God judge the secrets of men?**  
**Answer:** By Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:16

**Q3. According to what gospel will God judge the secrets of men?**  
**Answer:** According to my gospel.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:16

---

### Romans 2:17

**Q1. What is he called who is named a Jew and rests in the law?**  
**Answer:** Behold, thou art called a Jew.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:17

**Q2. In what does the one called a Jew make his boast?**  
**Answer:** In God.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:17

---

### Romans 2:18

**Q1. What does the Jew know regarding His will?**  
**Answer:** And knowest his will.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:18

**Q2. What kind of things are approved by the Jew who is instructed out of the law?**  
**Answer:** The things that are more excellent.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:18

---

### Romans 2:19

**Q1. Of what does the Jew confidently believe he is a guide?**  
**Answer:** A guide of the blind.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:19

**Q2. What does the Jew believe he is to them which are in darkness?**  
**Answer:** A light of them which are in darkness.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:19

---

### Romans 2:20

**Q1. What kind of instructor of the foolish is the Jew?**  
**Answer:** An instructor of the foolish.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:20

**Q2. Of what is the Jew a teacher?**  
**Answer:** A teacher of babes.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:20

**Q3. What form of knowledge and of the truth hast thou in the law?**  
**Answer:** The form of knowledge and of the truth.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:20

---

### Romans 2:21

**Q1. What does the person who teaches another fail to teach regarding himself?**  
**Answer:** Thou which teachest another, teachest thou not thyself?  
**Verse:** Romans 2:21

**Q2. What does the person who preaches that a man should not steal do himself?**  
**Answer:** Thou that preachest a man should not steal, dost thou steal?  
**Verse:** Romans 2:21

---

### Romans 2:22

**Q1. What does the person who says a man should not commit adultery do himself?**  
**Answer:** Thou that sayest a man should not commit adultery, dost thou commit adultery?  
**Verse:** Romans 2:22

**Q2. What does the person who abhors idols do regarding temples?**  
**Answer:** Thou that abhorrest idols, dost thou commit sacrilege?  
**Verse:** Romans 2:22

---

### Romans 2:23

**Q1. In what do you make your boast through breaking the law?**  
**Answer:** Thou that makest thy boast of the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:23

**Q2. What do you dishonor God by doing?**  
**Answer:** Through breaking the law dishonourest thou God.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:23

---

### Romans 2:24

**Q1. What is blasphemed among the Gentiles through you?**  
**Answer:** The name of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:24

**Q2. Among whom is the name of God blasphemed through you?**  
**Answer:** Among the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:24

---

### Romans 2:25

**Q1. What does circumcision profit if thou be a breaker of the law?**  
**Answer:** Circumcision verily profiteth, if thou keep the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:25

**Q2. What does your circumcision become if you are a breaker of the law?**  
**Answer:** Thy circumcision is made uncircumcision.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:25

---

### Romans 2:26

**Q1. If the uncircumcision keeps the righteousness of the law, what shall it be counted for?**  
**Answer:** Shall not his uncircumcision be counted for circumcision?  
**Verse:** Romans 2:26

---

### Romans 2:27

**Q1. Who shall judge thee if uncircumcision by nature fulfills the law?**  
**Answer:** And shall not uncircumcision which is by nature, if it fulfil the law, judge thee.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:27

**Q2. What does the transgressor of the law have despite the letter and circumcision?**  
**Answer:** Who by the letter and circumcision dost transgress the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:27

---

### Romans 2:28

**Q1. Who is not a Jew outwardly?**  
**Answer:** For he is not a Jew, which is one outwardly.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:28

**Q2. What is not circumcision which is outward in the flesh?**  
**Answer:** Neither is that circumcision, which is outward in the flesh.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:28

---

### Romans 2:29

**Q1. Who is a Jew inwardly?**  
**Answer:** But he is a Jew, which is one inwardly.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:29

**Q2. What kind of circumcision is that of the heart?**  
**Answer:** And circumcision is that of the heart.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:29

**Q3. By what is the circumcision of the heart performed, not in the letter?**  
**Answer:** In the spirit, and not in the letter.  
**Verse:** Romans 2:29
