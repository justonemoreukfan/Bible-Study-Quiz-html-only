# Romans 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Romans 10:1

**Q1. What is Paul's heart's desire and prayer to God for Israel?**  
**Answer:** That they might be saved.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:1

**Q2. Whose heart's desire and prayer to God is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Paul's.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:1

**Q3. For whom does Paul pray that they might be saved?**  
**Answer:** For Israel.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:1

---

### Romans 10:2

**Q1. What does Paul bear them record concerning Israel?**  
**Answer:** That they have a zeal of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:2

**Q2. How is Israel's zeal described in relation to God?**  
**Answer:** Not according to knowledge.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:2

**Q3. Who bears record that Israel has a zeal of God?**  
**Answer:** Paul.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:2

---

### Romans 10:3

**Q1. What have they being ignorant of?**  
**Answer:** God's righteousness.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:3

**Q2. What have they gone about to establish?**  
**Answer:** Their own righteousness.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:3

**Q3. What have they not submitted themselves unto?**  
**Answer:** The righteousness of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:3

---

### Romans 10:4

**Q1. What is Christ to the law?**  
**Answer:** The end of the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:4

**Q2. To whom is Christ the end of the law for righteousness?**  
**Answer:** For righteousness to every one that believeth.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:4

**Q3. What is the result of Christ being the end of the law for every one that believeth?**  
**Answer:** Righteousness.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:4

---

### Romans 10:5

**Q1. Moses describeth the righteousness of which law?**  
**Answer:** The righteousness which is of the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:5

**Q2. What shall the man do that doeth those things?**  
**Answer:** Live by them.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:5

**Q3. Who describeth the righteousness which is of the law?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:5

---

### Romans 10:6

**Q1. How does the righteousness which is of faith speak on this wise?**  
**Answer:** Say not in thine heart, Who shall ascend into heaven?  
**Verse:** Romans 10:6

**Q2. What is the stated meaning of ascending into heaven according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** (That is, to bring Christ down from above).  
**Verse:** Romans 10:6

**Q3. What kind of righteousness speaks in this manner?**  
**Answer:** The righteousness which is of faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:6

---

### Romans 10:7

**Q1. What question does faith ask instead of ascending to heaven?**  
**Answer:** Who shall descend into the deep?  
**Verse:** Romans 10:7

**Q2. What is the stated meaning of descending into the deep according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** (That is, to bring up Christ again from the dead).  
**Verse:** Romans 10:7

**Q3. Where does the question ask to descend?**  
**Answer:** Into the deep.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:7

---

### Romans 10:8

**Q1. What does the word saith?**  
**Answer:** The word is nigh thee, even in thy mouth, and in thy heart.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:8

**Q2. Whose word is spoken of as being nigh thee?**  
**Answer:** The word of faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:8

**Q3. Where are the two places the word is said to be?**  
**Answer:** In thy mouth, and in thy heart.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:8

---

### Romans 10:9

**Q1. What must thou confess with thy mouth?**  
**Answer:** The Lord Jesus.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:9

**Q2. What must thou believe in thine heart concerning God?**  
**Answer:** That God hath raised him from the dead.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:9

**Q3. What will happen to the person who does these things?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt be saved.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:9

---

### Romans 10:10

**Q1. With what does man believe unto righteousness?**  
**Answer:** With the heart.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:10

**Q2. With what does man make confession unto salvation?**  
**Answer:** With the mouth.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:10

**Q3. Unto what does believing with the heart lead?**  
**Answer:** Unto righteousness.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:10

---

### Romans 10:11

**Q1. What does the scripture saith concerning whosoever believeth on him?**  
**Answer:** Shall not be ashamed.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:11

**Q2. On whom must a person believe to avoid being ashamed?**  
**Answer:** Him.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:11

**Q3. Who does the scripture say shall not be ashamed?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever believeth on him.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:11

---

### Romans 10:12

**Q1. Is there difference between the Jew and the Greek according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** There is no difference.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:12

**Q2. Who is Lord over all?**  
**Answer:** The same Lord.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:12

**Q3. How is the same Lord described toward all that call upon him?**  
**Answer:** Rich unto all that call upon him.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:12

---

### Romans 10:13

**Q1. Whose name must be called upon to be saved?**  
**Answer:** The name of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:13

**Q2. What will happen to whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** He shall be saved.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:13

**Q3. Who shall be saved according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:13

---

### Romans 10:14

**Q1. How shall they call on him in whom they have not believed?**  
**Answer:** How shall they call on him in whom they have not believed?  
**Verse:** Romans 10:14

**Q2. How shall they believe in him of whom they have not heard?**  
**Answer:** How shall they believe in him of whom they have not heard?  
**Verse:** Romans 10:14

**Q3. How shall they hear without what?**  
**Answer:** Without a preacher.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:14

---

### Romans 10:15

**Q1. How shall they preach except they be sent?**  
**Answer:** Except they be sent.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:15

**Q2. What is written regarding the feet of them that preach the gospel of peace?**  
**Answer:** How beautiful are the feet of them that preach the gospel of peace, and bring glad tidings of good things!  
**Verse:** Romans 10:15

**Q3. What kind of tidings do they bring?**  
**Answer:** Glad tidings of good things.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:15

---

### Romans 10:16

**Q1. Have all obeyed the gospel?**  
**Answer:** They have not all obeyed the gospel.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:16

**Q2. Who said, Lord, who hath believed our report?**  
**Answer:** Esaias.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:16

**Q3. Whose report did Esaias ask who hath believed?**  
**Answer:** Our report.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:16

---

### Romans 10:17

**Q1. How then cometh faith?**  
**Answer:** By hearing.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:17

**Q2. By what is hearing achieved?**  
**Answer:** By the word of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:17

**Q3. What comes by hearing?**  
**Answer:** Faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:17

---

### Romans 10:18

**Q1. What did they not hear?**  
**Answer:** Yes verily, their sound went into all the earth, and their words unto the ends of the world.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:18

**Q2. Where did their sound go?**  
**Answer:** Into all the earth.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:18

**Q3. Where did their words go?**  
**Answer:** Unto the ends of the world.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:18

---

### Romans 10:19

**Q1. Did Israel know?**  
**Answer:** But I say, Did not Israel know?  
**Verse:** Romans 10:19

**Q2. Who first saith, I will provoke you to jealousy by them that are no people?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:19

**Q3. By whom will God provoke Israel to anger?**  
**Answer:** By a foolish nation.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:19

---

### Romans 10:20

**Q1. Who is very bold and saith, I was found of them that sought me not?**  
**Answer:** Esaias.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:20

**Q2. To whom was he made manifest?**  
**Answer:** Unto them that asked not after me.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:20

**Q3. What happened to them that sought him not?**  
**Answer:** I was found of them.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:20

---

### Romans 10:21

**Q1. To whom does he say, All day long I have stretched forth my hands?**  
**Answer:** To a disobedient and gainsaying people.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:21

**Q2. How long did he stretch forth his hands?**  
**Answer:** All day long.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:21

**Q3. What did he stretch forth?**  
**Answer:** My hands.  
**Verse:** Romans 10:21
