# Romans 16 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Romans 16:1

**Q1. Who is commended unto the church at Cenchrea?**  
**Answer:** Phebe our sister.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:1

**Q2. What office does Phebe hold in the church which is at Cenchrea?**  
**Answer:** A servant.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:1

**Q3. What is Cenchrea in relation to Phebe?**  
**Answer:** A church.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:1

---

### Romans 16:2

**Q1. How does Paul instruct the brethren to receive Phebe?**  
**Answer:** In the Lord, as becometh saints.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:2

**Q2. In what business should the brethren assist Phebe?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever business she hath need of you.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:2

**Q3. What has Phebe been to many, and to Paul himself?**  
**Answer:** A succourer of many, and of myself also.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:2

---

### Romans 16:3

**Q1. Who are the first individuals greeted by name?**  
**Answer:** Priscilla and Aquila.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:3

**Q2. What are Priscilla and Aquila called in Christ Jesus?**  
**Answer:** My helpers.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:3

**Q3. In what way have Priscilla and Aquila helped Paul?**  
**Answer:** My helpers in Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:3

---

### Romans 16:4

**Q1. What did Priscilla and Aquila lay down for Paul's life?**  
**Answer:** Their own necks.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:4

**Q2. Who, besides Paul, gives thanks unto Priscilla and Aquila?**  
**Answer:** All the churches of the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:4

**Q3. Whose lives did Priscilla and Aquila risk besides Paul's?**  
**Answer:** Unto whom not only I give thanks, but also all the churches of the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:4

---

### Romans 16:5

**Q1. What is the name of the church that is in Priscilla and Aquila's house?**  
**Answer:** The church that is in their house.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:5

**Q2. Who is greeted as Paul's wellbeloved, who is the firstfruits of Achaia unto Christ?**  
**Answer:** Epenetus.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:5

**Q3. Of what region is Epenetus the firstfruits unto Christ?**  
**Answer:** Achaia.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:5

---

### Romans 16:6

**Q1. Who is greeted for bestowing much labor on Paul?**  
**Answer:** Mary.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:6

**Q2. What did Mary bestow on Paul?**  
**Answer:** Much labour.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:6

**Q3. Who bestowed much labour on us?**  
**Answer:** Mary.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:6

---

### Romans 16:7

**Q1. Who are named as Paul's kinsmen and fellowprisoners?**  
**Answer:** Andronicus and Junia.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:7

**Q2. Among whom are Andronicus and Junia of note?**  
**Answer:** The apostles.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:7

**Q3. Who was in Christ before Paul?**  
**Answer:** Who also were in Christ before me.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:7

**Q2. Where is Amplias beloved by Paul?**

**Verse:** Romans 16:8

**Q3. Whose beloved in the Lord is Amplias?**

**Verse:** Romans 16:9

**Verse:** Romans 16:13

### Romans 16:14

**Q1. Name the first four men greeted in verse 14.**  
**Answer:** Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermas, Patrobas.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:14

**Q2. Name the fifth man greeted in verse 14.**  
**Answer:** Hermes.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:14

**Q3. Who is with the brethren mentioned in verse 14?**  
**Answer:** The brethren which are with them.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:14

---

### Romans 16:15

**Q1. Name the man and woman greeted together at the beginning of verse 15.**  
**Answer:** Philologus, and Julia.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:15

**Q2. Name the other three individuals greeted in verse 15.**  
**Answer:** Nereus, and his sister, and Olympas.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:15

**Q3. Who are with all the saints mentioned in verse 15?**  
**Answer:** All the saints which are with them.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:15

---

### Romans 16:16

**Q1. With what kind of kiss are the believers instructed to salute one another?**  
**Answer:** An holy kiss.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:16

**Q2. Whose churches salute the Roman believers?**  
**Answer:** The churches of Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:16

**Q3. How should the brethren salute one another?**  
**Answer:** Salute one another with an holy kiss.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:16

---

### Romans 16:17

**Q1. What does Paul beseech the brethren to mark?**  
**Answer:** Them which cause divisions and offences.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:17

**Q2. Contrary to what doctrine are the divisions and offences taught?**  
**Answer:** The doctrine which ye have learned.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:17

**Q3. What instruction is given regarding those who cause divisions?**  
**Answer:** Avoid them.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:17

---

### Romans 16:18

**Q1. Whom do such persons not serve?**  
**Answer:** Our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:18

**Q2. What do such persons serve?**  
**Answer:** Their own belly.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:18

**Q3. By what means do they deceive the hearts of the simple?**  
**Answer:** By good words and fair speeches.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:18

---

### Romans 16:19

**Q1. Whose obedience has come abroad unto all men?**  
**Answer:** Your obedience.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:19

**Q2. How does Paul want the brethren to be regarding that which is good?**  
**Answer:** Simple.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:19

**Q3. How does Paul want the brethren to be concerning evil?**  
**Answer:** Wise.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:19

---

### Romans 16:24

**Answer:** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:24

**Answer:** With you all.

### Romans 16:25

**Q1. Who is of power to stablish the believers?**  
**Answer:** Now to him that is of power to stablish you.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:25

**Q2. According to what is the establishment of the believers granted?**  
**Answer:** According to my gospel, and the preaching of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:25

**Q3. According to the revelation of what kind of mystery was the preaching?**  
**Answer:** The mystery, which was kept secret since the world began.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:25

---

### Romans 16:26

**Q1. By what kind of scriptures is the mystery now made manifest?**  
**Answer:** By the scriptures of the prophets.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:26

**Q2. To all nations, according to whose commandment is the mystery made known?**  
**Answer:** According to the commandment of the everlasting God.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:26

**Q3. For what purpose is the mystery made known to all nations?**  
**Answer:** For the obedience of faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:26

---

**Answer:** To God only wise.  
**Verse:** Romans 16:27

**Q2. Through whom is glory ascribed to God only wise?**  
**Answer:** Through Jesus Christ.
