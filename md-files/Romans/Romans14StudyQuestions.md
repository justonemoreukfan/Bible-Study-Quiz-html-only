# Romans 14 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Romans 14:1

**Q1. Who is to be received?**  
**Answer:** Him that is weak in the faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:1

**Q2. What should not be done to him that is weak in the faith regarding his thoughts?**  
**Answer:** Not to doubtful disputations.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:1

**Q3. Who is the person described as being weak?**  
**Answer:** Him that is weak in the faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:1

---

### Romans 14:2

**Q4. What does one believe that he may eat?**  
**Answer:** That he may eat all things.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:2

**Q5. What does another, who is weak, eat?**  
**Answer:** Herbs.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:2

**Q6. What does the person who eats herbs do?**  
**Answer:** Eateth herbs.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:2

---

### Romans 14:3

**Q7. What should he that eateth not do to him that eateth?**  
**Answer:** Not despise him that eateth.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:3

**Q8. What should he that eateth do to him that eateth not?**  
**Answer:** Not judge him that eateth not.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:3

**Q9. Who has received him?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:3

---

### Romans 14:4

**Q10. Who art thou that judgest another man's what?**  
**Answer:** Servant.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:4

**Q11. To whom does the servant stand or fall?**  
**Answer:** To his own master.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:4

**Q12. What shall the servant do, for God is able to make him stand?**  
**Answer:** He shall be holden up.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:4

---

### Romans 14:5

**Q13. What does one man esteem above another?**  
**Answer:** One day above another.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:5

**Q14. How does another man esteem every day?**  
**Answer:** Every day alike.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:5

**Q15. What should every man be fully persuaded in?**  
**Answer:** In his own mind.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:5

---

### Romans 14:6

**Q16. Unto whom does he that regardeth the day regard it?**  
**Answer:** Unto the Lord.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:6

**Q17. Unto whom does he not regard the day?**  
**Answer:** He doth not regard the day, unto the Lord.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:6

**Q18. Who gives God thanks for what he eats?**  
**Answer:** He that eateth, eateth to the Lord, for he giveth God thanks.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:6

---

### Romans 14:7

**Q19. For what does no man live to himself?**  
**Answer:** None of us liveth to himself.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:7

**Q20. For what does no man die to himself?**  
**Answer:** No man dieth to himself.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:7

**Q21. To whom do we live and die?**  
**Answer:** To himself (the Lord).  
**Verse:** Romans 14:7

---

### Romans 14:8

**Q22. Unto whom do we live if we live?**  
**Answer:** Unto the Lord.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:8

**Q23. Unto whom do we die if we die?**  
**Answer:** Unto the Lord.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:8

**Q24. Whose are we whether we live therefore, or die?**  
**Answer:** The Lord's.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:8

---

### Romans 14:9

**Q25. For to this end Christ both did what?**  
**Answer:** Died, and rose, and revived.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:9

**Q26. Over whom might Christ be Lord?**  
**Answer:** Both the dead and living.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:9

**Q27. What did Christ do to be Lord of both the dead and living?**  
**Answer:** Died, and rose, and revived.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:9

---

### Romans 14:10

**Q28. What should you not do to your brother?**  
**Answer:** Judge thy brother.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:10

**Q29. What should you not do to your brother in regard to setting at nought?**  
**Answer:** Set at nought thy brother.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:10

**Q30. Before whose judgment seat shall we all stand?**  
**Answer:** The judgment seat of Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:10

---

### Romans 14:11

**Q31. By whom does the Lord swear?**  
**Answer:** By myself.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:11

**Q32. What shall every knee do to the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Bow to me.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:11

**Q33. What shall every tongue do to God?**  
**Answer:** Confess to God.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:11

---

### Romans 14:12

**Q34. What shall every one of us give?**  
**Answer:** Account of himself.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:12

**Q35. To whom shall every one of us give account?**  
**Answer:** To God.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:12

**Q36. Who shall give account of himself to God?**  
**Answer:** Every one of us.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:12

---

### Romans 14:13

**Q37. What should we not judge any more?**  
**Answer:** One another.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:13

**Q38. What should you judge rather concerning your brother?**  
**Answer:** That no man put a stumblingblock or an occasion to fall in his brother's way.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:13

**Q39. In whose way should a stumblingblock not be put?**  
**Answer:** His brother's way.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:13

---

### Romans 14:14

**Q40. What is the speaker persuaded of by the Lord Jesus?**  
**Answer:** That there is nothing unclean of itself.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:14

**Q41. To whom is anything unclean?**  
**Answer:** To him that esteemeth any thing to be unclean.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:14

**Q42. Who is persuaded that nothing is unclean of itself?**  
**Answer:** I know, and am persuaded by the Lord Jesus.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:14

---

### Romans 14:15

**Q43. With what is your brother grieved because of your meat?**  
**Answer:** With thy meat.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:15

**Q44. How are you walking if your brother is grieved with your meat?**  
**Answer:** Not charitably.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:15

**Q45. Whom should you not destroy with your meat?**  
**Answer:** Him, for whom Christ died.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:15

---

### Romans 14:16

**Q46. What should not be spoken of as evil?**  
**Answer:** Your good.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:16

**Q47. Whose good is it that should not be evil spoken of?**  
**Answer:** Your good.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:16

**Q48. What should happen to your good?**  
**Answer:** Let not your good be evil spoken of.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:16

---

### Romans 14:17

**Q49. What is the kingdom of God not?**  
**Answer:** Meat and drink.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:17

**Q50. What is the kingdom of God in terms of righteousness?**  
**Answer:** Righteousness.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:17

**Q51. What two other things make up the kingdom of God besides righteousness?**  
**Answer:** Peace, and joy in the Holy Ghost.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:17

---

### Romans 14:18

**Q52. Whom does he that serveth Christ in these things serve?**  
**Answer:** Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:18

**Q53. To whom is he that serveth Christ acceptable?**  
**Answer:** To God.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:18

**Q54. By whom is he that serveth Christ approved?**  
**Answer:** Approved of men.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:18

---

### Romans 14:19

**Q55. What things should we follow after?**  
**Answer:** The things which make for peace.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:19

**Q56. What else should we follow after besides peace?**  
**Answer:** Things wherewith one may edify another.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:19

**Q57. What are we to do with one another?**  
**Answer:** Edify another.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:19

---

### Romans 14:20

**Q58. What should you not destroy for meat?**  
**Answer:** The work of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:20

**Q59. How are all things described?**  
**Answer:** Pure.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:20

**Q60. To whom is it evil for a man to eat with offence?**  
**Answer:** To that man who eateth with offence.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:20

---

### Romans 14:21

**Q61. What is it good not to do regarding meat and wine?**  
**Answer:** Not to eat flesh, nor to drink wine.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:21

**Q62. What should your brother not do whereby he stumbles, is offended, or made weak?**  
**Answer:** Anything wherewith thy brother stumbleth, or is offended, or is made weak.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:21

**Q63. What three things might happen to your brother?**  
**Answer:** Stumbleth, or is offended, or is made weak.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:21

---

### Romans 14:22

**Q64. What should you have to yourself?**  
**Answer:** Hast thou faith? have it to thyself before God.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:22

**Q65. Who is happy?**  
**Answer:** He that condemneth not himself in that thing which he alloweth.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:22

**Q66. In what should a man not condemn himself?**  
**Answer:** In that thing which he alloweth.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:22

---

### Romans 14:23

**Q67. Who is damned if he eat?**  
**Answer:** He that doubteth.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:23

**Q68. Why is he damned if he eat?**  
**Answer:** Because he eateth not of faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:23

**Q69. What is whatsoever is not of faith?**  
**Answer:** Sin.  
**Verse:** Romans 14:23
