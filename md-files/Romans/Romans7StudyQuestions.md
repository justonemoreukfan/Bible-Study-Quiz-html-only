# Romans 7 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Romans 7:1

**Q1. To whom is the apostle Paul speaking in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Them that know the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:1

**Q2. What does Paul say about the law regarding a man?**  
**Answer:** That the law hath dominion over a man as long as he liveth.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:1

**Q3. How long does the law have dominion over a man?**  
**Answer:** As long as he liveth.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:1

---

### Romans 7:2

**Q1. What is a woman which hath an husband bound by to her husband so long as he liveth?**  
**Answer:** By the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:2

**Q2. What happens if the husband be dead?**  
**Answer:** She is loosed from the law of her husband.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:2

**Q3. By what is the woman loosed from the law of her husband?**  
**Answer:** By the death of her husband.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:2

---

### Romans 7:3

**Q1. What is the woman called if she be married to another man while her husband liveth?**  
**Answer:** An adulteress.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:3

**Q2. What happens if her husband be dead?**  
**Answer:** She is free from that law.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:3

**Q3. What is she no longer, even though she be married to another man, if her husband is dead?**  
**Answer:** No adulteress.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:3

---

### Romans 7:4

**Q1. To what were the brethren also made dead?**  
**Answer:** To the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:4

**Q2. Through what medium were they made dead to the law?**  
**Answer:** By the body of Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:4

**Q3. To whom should they be married after being made dead to the law?**  
**Answer:** To another, even to him who is raised from the dead.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:4

---

### Romans 7:5

**Q1. What was in us when we were in the flesh?**  
**Answer:** The motions of sins.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:5

**Q2. By what were the motions of sins worked in our members?**  
**Answer:** By the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:5

**Q3. What did the motions of sins, which were by the law, bring forth?**  
**Answer:** Fruit unto death.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:5

---

### Romans 7:6

**Q1. From what are we now delivered?**  
**Answer:** From the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:6

**Q2. What happened to that wherein we were held?**  
**Answer:** That being dead wherein we were held.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:6

**Q3. In what newness should we serve?**  
**Answer:** In newness of spirit, and not in the oldness of the letter.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:6

---

### Romans 7:7

**Q1. What question does Paul ask concerning the law?**  
**Answer:** Is the law sin?  
**Verse:** Romans 7:7

**Q2. What does Paul say he had not known except by the law?**  
**Answer:** Sin.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:7

**Q3. What did Paul not know unless the law had said, "Thou shalt not covet"?**  
**Answer:** Lust.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:7

---

### Romans 7:8

**Q1. What took occasion by the commandment?**  
**Answer:** Sin.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:8

**Q2. What did sin work in Paul by the commandment?**  
**Answer:** All manner of concupiscence.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:8

**Q3. What was dead without the law?**  
**Answer:** Sin.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:8

---

### Romans 7:9

**Q1. What was Paul alive without once?**  
**Answer:** Without the law.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:9

**Q2. What happened when the commandment came?**  
**Answer:** Sin revived.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:9

**Q3. What happened to Paul when the commandment came?**  
**Answer:** He died.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:9

---

### Romans 7:10

**Q1. What was ordained to life for Paul?**  
**Answer:** The commandment.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:10

**Q2. What was found to be unto Paul instead of life?**  
**Answer:** The same commandment, which was ordained to life.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:10

**Q3. Unto what was the commandment found to be for Paul?**  
**Answer:** Unto death.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:10

---

### Romans 7:11

**Q1. What took occasion by the commandment?**  
**Answer:** Sin.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:11

**Q2. What did sin do to Paul by the commandment?**  
**Answer:** Deceived him.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:11

**Q3. What did sin do by the commandment after deceiving him?**  
**Answer:** Slew him.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:11

---

### Romans 7:12

**Q1. What is the law described as being?**  
**Answer:** Holy.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:12

**Q2. What is the commandment described as being?**  
**Answer:** Holy, and just, and good.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:12

**Q3. Who said that the law is holy, and the commandment holy, and just, and good?**  
**Answer:** The apostle Paul.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:12

---

### Romans 7:13

**Q1. What question does Paul ask about that which is good?**  
**Answer:** Was then that which is good made death unto me?  
**Verse:** Romans 7:13

**Q2. What answer does Paul give to the question about that which is good being made death?**  
**Answer:** God forbid.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:13

**Q3. By what did sin appear sin, working death in Paul by that which is good?**  
**Answer:** By sin.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:13

---

### Romans 7:14

**Q1. What does Paul know the law is?**  
**Answer:** Spiritual.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:14

**Q2. What does Paul say that he is?**  
**Answer:** Carnal.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:14

**Q3. To what is Paul sold?**  
**Answer:** Under sin.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:14

---

### Romans 7:15

**Q1. What does Paul say he allows not?**  
**Answer:** That which he doeth.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:15

**Q2. What does Paul do instead of what he would?**  
**Answer:** That what he hates, that do he.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:15

**Q3. What does Paul do regarding what he would?**  
**Answer:** He does not that.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:15

---

### Romans 7:16

**Q1. What does Paul do if he then does that which he would not?**  
**Answer:** He consents unto the law that it is good.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:16

**Q2. Unto what does Paul consent when he does that which he would not?**  
**Answer:** That the law is good.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:16

**Q3. What is the law declared to be when Paul consents to it?**  
**Answer:** Good.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:16

---

### Romans 7:17

**Q1. Who is no longer doing it, according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Paul himself, but sin that dwelleth in him.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:17

**Q2. What is said to dwell in Paul?**  
**Answer:** Sin.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:17

---

### Romans 7:18

**Q1. What does Paul know dwells in him (that is, in his flesh)?**  
**Answer:** No good thing.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:18

**Q2. Where does Paul know no good thing dwells?**  
**Answer:** In his flesh.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:18

**Q3. What is present with Paul, even though he finds how to perform it he does not?**  
**Answer:** The will.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:18

---

### Romans 7:19

**Q1. What good does Paul not do?**  
**Answer:** The good that he would.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:19

---

### Romans 7:20

**Q1. What does Paul do if he does that he would not?**  
**Answer:** It is no more he that does it, but sin that dwelleth in him.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:20

**Q2. What dwells in Paul when he does that which he would not?**  
**Answer:** Sin.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:20

**Q3. Who is doing the action when Paul does what he would not?**  
**Answer:** Sin that dwelleth in him.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:20

---

### Romans 7:21

**Q1. What law does Paul find present?**  
**Answer:** That, when he would do good, evil is present with him.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:21

**Q2. When is evil present with Paul according to this law?**  
**Answer:** When he would do good.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:21

**Q3. What is present with Paul when he would do good?**  
**Answer:** Evil.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:21

---

### Romans 7:22

**Q1. In what does Paul delight?**  
**Answer:** In the law of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:22

**Q2. According to what part of man does Paul delight in the law of God?**  
**Answer:** After the inward man.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:22

**Q3. Whose law does the inward man delight in?**  
**Answer:** The law of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:22

---

### Romans 7:23

**Q1. What does Paul see in his members?**  
**Answer:** Another law.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:23

**Q2. Against what does the law in his members war?**  
**Answer:** Against the law of his mind.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:23

**Q3. By what law is Paul brought into captivity?**  
**Answer:** The law of sin which is in his members.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:23

---

### Romans 7:24

**Q1. What does Paul call himself in this verse?**  
**Answer:** A wretched man.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:24

**Q2. What question does Paul ask concerning his body of death?**  
**Answer:** Who shall deliver me from the body of this death?  
**Verse:** Romans 7:24

**Q3. What kind of body does Paul want deliverance from?**  
**Answer:** The body of this death.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:24

---

### Romans 7:25

**Q1. Who does Paul thank for deliverance?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Romans 7:25
