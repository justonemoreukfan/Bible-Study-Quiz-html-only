# Romans 15 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Romans 15:1

**Q1. Who ought to bear the infirmities of the weak according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** We that are strong.  
**Verse:** Romans 15:1

**Q2. Whose infirmities are we instructed to bear instead of pleasing ourselves?**  
**Answer:** The weak.  
**Verse:** Romans 15:1

**Q3. What should we avoid doing in regard to ourselves?**  
**Answer:** Please ourselves.  
**Verse:** Romans 15:1

---

### Romans 15:2

**Q1. Who should every one of us please according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** His neighbour.  
**Verse:** Romans 15:2

**Q2. What is the purpose of pleasing our neighbour regarding his good?**  
**Answer:** For his good.  
**Verse:** Romans 15:2

**Q3. What is the ultimate goal concerning our neighbour's edification?**  
**Answer:** To edification.  
**Verse:** Romans 15:2

---

### Romans 15:3

**Q1. Who did not please himself, as an example provided?**  
**Answer:** Even Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 15:3

**Q2. Whose reproaches fell upon Christ, according to the written scripture?**  
**Answer:** The reproaches of them that reproached thee.  
**Verse:** Romans 15:3

**Q3. What fell upon Christ from those who reproached God?**  
**Answer:** Reproaches.  
**Verse:** Romans 15:3

---

**Verse:** Romans 15:4

**Verse:** Romans 15:4

**Q3. For what purpose were things written aforetime concerning our learning?**  
**Answer:** For our learning.  
**Verse:** Romans 15:4
