# Romans 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Romans 1:1

**Q1. Who wrote the epistle according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Paul.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:1

**Q2. What position or office does the writer claim in relation to Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** A servant of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:1

**Q3. To what specific work was the writer separated?**  
**Answer:** Called to be an apostle, separated unto the gospel of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:1

---

### Romans 1:2

**Q1. By whom had God promised before the gospel?**  
**Answer:** By his prophets.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:2

**Q2. Where were these promises regarding the gospel recorded?**  
**Answer:** In the holy scriptures.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:2

**Q3. What is the subject of these previous promises made by the prophets?**  
**Answer:** His gospel.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:2

---

### Romans 1:3

**Q1. Concerning whose Son is the gospel spoken of in this verse?**  
**Answer:** His Son Jesus Christ our Lord.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:3

**Q2. From whose seed was He made according to the flesh?**  
**Answer:** Of the seed of David.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:3

**Q3. According to what standard was He made of the seed of David?**  
**Answer:** According to the flesh.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:3

---

### Romans 1:4

**Q1. How was Jesus Christ declared to be the Son of God?**  
**Answer:** With power.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:4

**Q2. According to what spirit was He declared to be the Son of God with power?**  
**Answer:** According to the spirit of holiness.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:4

**Q3. By what specific event was this declaration made manifest?**  
**Answer:** By the resurrection from the dead.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:4

---

### Romans 1:5

**Q1. By whom did the writer receive grace and apostleship?**  
**Answer:** By whom [Jesus Christ our Lord].  
**Verse:** Romans 1:5

**Q2. For what purpose was this grace and apostleship received among all nations?**  
**Answer:** For obedience to the faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:5

**Q3. For whose name's sake was this obedience to the faith to be promoted among all nations?**  
**Answer:** For his name.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:5

---

### Romans 1:6

**Q1. Among whom are the readers also included?**  
**Answer:** Among whom are ye also.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:6

**Q2. Whose are the readers called to be?**  
**Answer:** The called of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:6

**Q3. What is the status of the readers mentioned in relation to Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** Called of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:6

---

### Romans 1:7

**Q1. To whom in Rome is the epistle addressed?**  
**Answer:** To all that be in Rome, beloved of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:7

**Q2. What are the recipients in Rome called to be?**  
**Answer:** Called to be saints.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:7

**Q3. What two things are wished for the recipients from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** Grace to you and peace.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:7

---

### Romans 1:8

**Q1. Whom does the writer thank first through Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** My God.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:8

**Q2. For whom does the writer thank his God?**  
**Answer:** For you all.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:8

**Q3. What is spoken of throughout the whole world concerning the recipients?**  
**Answer:** That your faith is spoken of throughout the whole world.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:8

---

### Romans 1:9

**Q1. Who is the writer's witness whom he serves?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:9

**Q2. With what part of the writer does he serve God?**  
**Answer:** With my spirit.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:9

**Q3. In what specific activity regarding the gospel of His Son does the writer continually remember them?**  
**Answer:** In the gospel of his Son.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:9

---

### Romans 1:10

**Q1. What does the writer make in his prayers?**  
**Answer:** Making request.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:10

**Q2. By whose will does the writer hope to come unto them?**  
**Answer:** By the will of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:10

**Q3. What does the writer hope might be prosperous?**  
**Answer:** A prosperous journey.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:10

---

### Romans 1:11

**Q1. What does the writer express a great desire to do?**  
**Answer:** For I long to see you.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:11

**Q2. What kind of gift does the writer wish to impart unto them?**  
**Answer:** Some spiritual gift.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:11

**Q3. For what purpose does the writer want to impart this spiritual gift?**  
**Answer:** To the end ye may be established.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:11

---

### Romans 1:12

**Q1. With whom does the writer desire to be comforted together?**  
**Answer:** With you.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:12

**Q2. By whose faith does the writer expect this mutual comfort?**  
**Answer:** By the mutual faith both of you and me.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:12

**Q3. Whose faith is included in the mutual comfort?**  
**Answer:** Both of you and me.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:12

---

### Romans 1:13

**Q1. What does the writer not want them to be ignorant of?**  
**Answer:** Brethren, that I would not have you ignorant.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:13

**Q2. How often was the writer purposed to come unto them?**  
**Answer:** Oftentimes.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:13

**Q3. What was the writer's desire regarding fruit among them, just as among other Gentiles?**  
**Answer:** That I might have some fruit among you also, even as among other Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:13

---

### Romans 1:14

**Q1. To whom is the writer a debtor?**  
**Answer:** Both to the Greeks, and to the barbarians.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:14

**Q2. To whom else besides Greeks and barbarians is the writer a debtor?**  
**Answer:** Both to the wise, and to the unwise.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:14

**Q3. What is the writer's stated obligation to these various groups?**  
**Answer:** I am debtor.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:14

---

### Romans 1:15

**Q1. What is the writer ready to do as much as in him is?**  
**Answer:** I am ready to preach the gospel.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:15

**Q2. To whom is the writer ready to preach the gospel?**  
**Answer:** To you that are at Rome also.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:15

**Q3. How much is the writer ready to preach the gospel to those at Rome?**  
**Answer:** As much as in me is.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:15

---

### Romans 1:16

**Q1. Of what is the writer not ashamed?**  
**Answer:** For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:16

**Q2. What is the gospel of Christ to everyone that believeth?**  
**Answer:** It is the power of God unto salvation.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:16

**Q3. To who specifically is the gospel the power of God unto salvation first?**  
**Answer:** To the Jew first, and also to the Greek.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:16

---

### Romans 1:17

**Q1. What is revealed in the gospel?**  
**Answer:** The righteousness of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:17

**Q2. From what faith to what faith is the righteousness of God revealed?**  
**Answer:** From faith to faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:17

**Q3. What shall the just do according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** The just shall live by faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:17

---

### Romans 1:18

**Q1. What is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of men?**  
**Answer:** The wrath of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:18

**Q2. Against what human behaviors is the wrath of God revealed from heaven?**  
**Answer:** Against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of men.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:18

**Q3. What do men do to the truth in their unrighteousness?**  
**Answer:** Who hold the truth in unrighteousness.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:18

---

### Romans 1:19

**Q1. What may be known of God?**  
**Answer:** That which may be known of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:19

**Q2. Where is that which may be known of God manifested in them?**  
**Answer:** Manifest in them.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:19

**Q3. Who has shown this manifest knowledge unto them?**  
**Answer:** For God hath shewed it unto them.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:19

---

### Romans 1:20

**Q1. What invisible things of Him are clearly seen since the creation of the world?**  
**Answer:** His eternal power and Godhead.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:20

**Q2. By what means are His invisible things understood?**  
**Answer:** By the things that are made.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:20

**Q3. What is the excuse status of the people mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** So that they are without excuse.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:20

---

### Romans 1:21

**Q1. When they knew God, how did they fail to glorify Him?**  
**Answer:** They glorified him not as God, neither were thankful.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:21

**Q2. In what did they become vain?**  
**Answer:** In their imaginations.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:21

**Q3. What happened to their foolish heart?**  
**Answer:** Their foolish heart was darkened.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:21

---

### Romans 1:22

**Q1. What did they profess themselves to be?**  
**Answer:** Professing themselves to be wise.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:22

**Q2. What did they actually become while making this profession?**  
**Answer:** They became fools.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:22

**Q3. What was the result of their profession of wisdom?**  
**Answer:** They became fools.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:22

---

### Romans 1:23

**Q1. Whose glory did they change into an image?**  
**Answer:** The glory of the uncorruptible God.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:23

**Q2. What kind of likeness did they make the image into?**  
**Answer:** Into an image made like to corruptible man, and to birds, and fourfooted beasts, and creeping things.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:23

**Q3. What attribute of God is contrasted with corruptible man and animals in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Uncorruptible God.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:23

---

### Romans 1:24

**Q1. To what did God also give them up?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore God also gave them up to uncleanness.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:24

**Q2. Through the lusts of whose hearts were they given up to uncleanness?**  
**Answer:** Through the lusts of their own hearts.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:24

**Q3. What did they dishonor between themselves with their uncleanness?**  
**Answer:** To dishonor their own bodies between themselves.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:24

---

### Romans 1:25

**Q1. What did they change into a lie?**  
**Answer:** The truth of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:25

**Q2. What did they worship and serve more than the Creator?**  
**Answer:** The creature.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:25

**Q3. How is the Creator described at the end of this verse?**  
**Answer:** Who is blessed for ever. Amen.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:25

**Answer:** Their natural use into that which is against nature.

**Answer:** Vile affections.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:26

---

**Q2. How did the men burn in their lust toward one another?**  
**Answer:** Burned in their lust one toward another.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:27

**Q3. What did men working with men receive in themselves?**  
**Answer:** Receiving in themselves that recompence of their error which was meet.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:27

**Answer:** To retain God in their knowledge.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:28

**Answer:** To a reprobate mind.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:28

**Q3. What do those with this mind do?**  
**Answer:** To do those things which are not convenient.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:28

### Romans 1:29

**Q1. Being filled with what unrighteousness are they mentioned as having?**  
**Answer:** All unrighteousness, fornication, wickedness, covetousness, maliciousness.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:29

**Q2. Full of what qualities are listed next in the verse?**  
**Answer:** Full of envy, murder, debate, deceit, malignity.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:29

**Q3. What character traits are mentioned at the very end of verse 29?**  
**Answer:** Whisperers.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:29

---

### Romans 1:30

**Q1. What category of speakers are mentioned first in verse 30?**  
**Answer:** Backbiters.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:30

**Q2. How are these people described regarding God?**  
**Answer:** Haters of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:30

**Q3. What additional negative traits conclude verse 30?**  
**Answer:** Despiteful, proud, boasters, inventors of evil things, disobedient to parents.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:30

---

### Romans 1:31

**Q1. What are they described as being without regarding understanding?**  
**Answer:** Without understanding.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:31

**Q2. What kind of covenants are they described as having?**  
**Answer:** Covenantbreakers.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:31

**Q3. What final two traits end verse 31?**  
**Answer:** Without natural affection, implacable, unmerciful.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:31

---

**Q1. What do they know of the judgment of God?**  
**Answer:** The judgment of God, that they which commit such things are worthy of death.  
**Verse:** Romans 1:32

**Q2. What do they do besides committing these things themselves?**  
**Answer:** Not only do the same, but have pleasure in them that do them.

**Answer:** In them that do them.
