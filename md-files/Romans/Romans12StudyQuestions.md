# Romans 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Romans 12:1

**Q1. By what does the writer beseech the brethren?**  
**Answer:** By the mercies of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:1

**Q2. How are the brethren asked to present their bodies?**  
**Answer:** A living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:1

**Q3. What is this presentation of their bodies called?**  
**Answer:** Your reasonable service.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:1

---

### Romans 12:2

**Q4. What are believers told not to be conformed to?**  
**Answer:** This world.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:2

**Q5. By what are they to be transformed?**  
**Answer:** By the renewing of your mind.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:2

**Q6. What may believers prove through this renewing?**  
**Answer:** What is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:2

---

### Romans 12:3

**Q7. Through what does the writer speak to every man among them?**  
**Answer:** Through the grace given unto me.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:3

**Q8. How is a man instructed not to think of himself?**  
**Answer:** More highly than he ought to think.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:3

**Q9. According to what standard should a man think soberly?**  
**Answer:** According as God hath dealt to every man the measure of faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:3

---

### Romans 12:4

**Q10. What do we have many of in one body?**  
**Answer:** Many members.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:4

**Q11. Do all members have the same office?**  
**Answer:** No ("all members have not the same office").  
**Verse:** Romans 12:4

**Q12. Where are the many members located?**  
**Answer:** In one body.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:4

---

### Romans 12:5

**Q13. What are we, being many, in Christ?**  
**Answer:** One body.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:5

**Q14. In whom are the many one body?**  
**Answer:** In Christ.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:5

**Q15. What is every one to one another?**  
**Answer:** Members one of another.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:5

---

### Romans 12:6

**Q16. According to what do our gifts differ?**  
**Answer:** According to the grace that is given to us.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:6

**Q17. What gift is specifically named first in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Prophecy.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:6

**Q18. According to what proportion should prophecy be exercised?**  
**Answer:** According to the proportion of faith.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:6

---

### Romans 12:7

**Q19. On what should one wait if having the gift of ministry?**  
**Answer:** On our ministering.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:7

**Q20. On what should he that teacheth wait?**  
**Answer:** On teaching.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:7

**Q21. What two gifts are addressed in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Ministry and teaching.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:7

---

### Romans 12:8

**Q22. On what should he that exhorteth wait?**  
**Answer:** On exhortation.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:8

**Q23. How should he that giveth do it?**  
**Answer:** With simplicity.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:8

**Q24. How should he that sheweth mercy do it?**  
**Answer:** With cheerfulness.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:8

---

### Romans 12:9

**Q25. How must love be?**  
**Answer:** Without dissimulation.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:9

**Q26. What are believers told to abhor?**  
**Answer:** That which is evil.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:9

**Q27. What are believers told to cleave to?**  
**Answer:** That which is good.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:9

---

### Romans 12:10

**Q28. How are believers to be affectioned one to another?**  
**Answer:** Kindly affectioned... with brotherly love.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:10

**Q29. What kind of love should believers have toward one another?**  
**Answer:** Brotherly love.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:10

**Q30. In what are they told to prefer one another?**  
**Answer:** In honour.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:10

---

### Romans 12:11

**Q31. In what are believers told not to be slothful?**  
**Answer:** In business.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:11

**Q32. How should believers be in spirit?**  
**Answer:** Fervent in spirit.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:11

**Q33. Whom are believers instructed to serve?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:11

---

### Romans 12:12

**Q34. In what should believers rejoice?**  
**Answer:** In hope.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:12

**Q35. How should believers be in tribulation?**  
**Answer:** Patient.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:12

**Q36. What should believers continue instant in?**  
**Answer:** In prayer.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:12

---

### Romans 12:13

**Q37. To what should believers be distributing?**  
**Answer:** To the necessity of saints.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:13

**Q38. To whose necessity should believers distribute?**  
**Answer:** Of saints.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:13

**Q39. What are believers instructed to be given to?**  
**Answer:** Hospitality.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:13

---

### Romans 12:14

**Q40. Whom are believers told to bless?**  
**Answer:** Them which persecute you.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:14

**Q41. What should believers do alongside blessing?**  
**Answer:** Curse not.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:14

**Q42. What action are believers forbidden to take toward persecutors?**  
**Answer:** Curse.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:14

---

### Romans 12:15

**Q43. With whom should believers rejoice?**  
**Answer:** With them that do rejoice.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:15

**Q44. With whom should believers weep?**  
**Answer:** With them that weep.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:15

**Q45. What two emotional responses are commanded toward others in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Rejoice and weep.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:15

---

### Romans 12:16

**Q46. What mind are believers to have toward one another?**  
**Answer:** The same mind.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:16

**Q47. To whom should believers condescend instead of minding high things?**  
**Answer:** To men of low estate.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:16

**Q48. In what should believers not be wise?**  
**Answer:** In your own conceits.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:16

---

### Romans 12:17

**Q49. What should not be recompensed to any man for evil?**  
**Answer:** Evil.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:17

**Q50. To how many men should evil be recompensed for evil?**  
**Answer:** To no man.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:17

**Q51. In the sight of whom should things honest be provided?**  
**Answer:** In the sight of all men.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:17

---

### Romans 12:18

**Q52. Under what condition are believers to live peaceably?**  
**Answer:** If it be possible, as much as lieth in you.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:18

**Q53. How should believers live with all men?**  
**Answer:** Peaceably.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:18

**Q54. With whom should believers live peaceably?**  
**Answer:** With all men.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:18

---

### Romans 12:19

**Q55. What are the dearly beloved told not to do?**  
**Answer:** Avenge not yourselves.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:19

**Q56. Unto what should believers give place?**  
**Answer:** Unto wrath.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:19

**Q57. To whom does vengeance belong according to what is written?**  
**Answer:** The Lord ("Vengeance is mine; I will repay, saith the Lord").  
**Verse:** Romans 12:19

---

### Romans 12:20

**Q58. What should you do if your enemy hungers?**  
**Answer:** Feed him.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:20

**Q59. What should you do if your enemy thirsts?**  
**Answer:** Give him drink.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:20

**Q60. What will you heap on your enemy's head by feeding and giving him drink?**  
**Answer:** Coals of fire.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:20

---

### Romans 12:21

**Q61. What should a believer not be overcome of?**  
**Answer:** Of evil.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:21

**Q62. With what is a believer instructed to overcome evil?**  
**Answer:** With good.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:21

**Q63. What are believers commanded to overcome with good?**  
**Answer:** Evil.  
**Verse:** Romans 12:21
