# 2 Timothy 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Timothy 2:1

**Q1. What is my son exhorted to be strong in?**  
**Answer:** The grace that is in Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:1

**Q2. Who is exhorted to be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus?**  
**Answer:** My son.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:1

**Q3. What should be done in the grace that is in Christ Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Be strong.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:1

---

### 2 Timothy 2:2

**Q1. What has been heard among many witnesses?**  
**Answer:** The things that thou hast heard of me.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:2

**Q2. To whom should these things be committed?**  
**Answer:** Faithful men.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:2

**Q3. Who shall be able to teach others also?**  
**Answer:** Faithful men who are able to teach others also.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:2

---

### 2 Timothy 2:3

**Q1. What should thou endure?**  
**Answer:** Hardness.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:3

**Q2. As what should thou endure hardness?**  
**Answer:** As a good soldier of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:3

**Q3. Whose good soldier is the one enduring hardness?**  
**Answer:** Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:3

---

### 2 Timothy 2:4

**Q1. What does no man that warred entangle himself with?**  
**Answer:** The affairs of this life.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:4

**Q2. Why does no man that war entangle himself with the affairs of this life?**  
**Answer:** That he may please him who hath chosen him to be a soldier.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:4

**Q3. Who has chosen the soldier?**  
**Answer:** Him who hath chosen him to be a soldier.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:4

---

### 2 Timothy 2:5

**Q1. If a man also strive for masteries, what is he not crowned except he do?**  
**Answer:** Strive lawfully.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:5

**Q2. When is a man not crowned?**  
**Answer:** Except he strive lawfully.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:5

**Q3. What does a man strive for in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Masteries.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:5

---

### 2 Timothy 2:6

**Q1. Who must be first partaker of the fruits?**  
**Answer:** The husbandman that laboureth.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:6

**Q2. What must the husbandman do before being partaker of the fruits?**  
**Answer:** Laboureth.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:6

**Q3. Of what must the labouring husbandman be the first partaker?**  
**Answer:** The fruits.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:6

---

### 2 Timothy 2:7

**Q1. What should thou consider?**  
**Answer:** What I say.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:7

**Q2. Who will give thee understanding in all things?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:7

**Q3. In how many things will the Lord give thee understanding?**  
**Answer:** All things.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:7

---

### 2 Timothy 2:8

**Q1. What should be remembered that Jesus Christ of the seed of David was raised from?**  
**Answer:** The dead.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:8

**Q2. Of whose seed was Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** David.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:8

**Q3. According to what is this gospel?**  
**Answer:** My gospel.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:8

---

### 2 Timothy 2:9

**Q1. Wherein do I suffer trouble?**  
**Answer:** As an evildoer.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:9

**Q2. Unto what, as an evildoer, do I suffer trouble?**  
**Answer:** Bonds.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:9

**Q3. What is not bound?**  
**Answer:** The word of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:9

---

### 2 Timothy 2:10

**Q1. Therefore what do I endure for the elects' sakes?**  
**Answer:** All things.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:10

**Q2. Why do I endure all things for the elects' sakes?**  
**Answer:** That they may also obtain the salvation which is in Christ Jesus with eternal glory.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:10

**Q3. What is in Christ Jesus with eternal glory?**  
**Answer:** The salvation.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:10

---

### 2 Timothy 2:11

**Q1. What kind of saying is this?**  
**Answer:** It is a faithful saying.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:11

**Q2. If we be dead with him, what shall we also do?**  
**Answer:** We shall also live with him.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:11

**Q3. With whom shall we live if we be dead with him?**  
**Answer:** Him.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:11

---

### 2 Timothy 2:12

**Q1. If we suffer, what shall we also do?**  
**Answer:** We shall also reign with him.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:12

**Q2. If we deny him, what will he do to us?**  
**Answer:** He also will deny us.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:12

**Q3. What will happen if we reign with him?**  
**Answer:** We suffer (implied from "If we suffer, we shall also reign with him").  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:12

---

### 2 Timothy 2:13

**Q1. If we believe not, how does he remain?**  
**Answer:** Faithful.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:13

**Q2. What can he not do?**  
**Answer:** Deny himself.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:13

**Q3. Even if we believe not, what attribute does he keep?**  
**Answer:** He abideth faithful.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:13

---

### 2 Timothy 2:14

**Q1. What things should be put them in remembrance of?**  
**Answer:** These things.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:14

**Q2. Charging them before the Lord that they strive not about what?**  
**Answer:** Words to no profit.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:14

**Q3. To whom is the striving of words to no profit?**  
**Answer:** To the subverting of the hearers.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:14

---

### 2 Timothy 2:15

**Q1. What should thou study to show thyself?**  
**Answer:** Approved unto God.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:15

**Q2. What kind of workman should not be ashamed?**  
**Answer:** A workman that needeth not to be ashamed.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:15

**Q3. How should one handle the word of truth?**  
**Answer:** Rightly dividing the word of truth.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:15

---

### 2 Timothy 2:16

**Q1. What kind of babblings should be shunned?**  
**Answer:** Profane and vain babblings.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:16

**Q2. To what will profane and vain babblings increase?**  
**Answer:** Unto more ungodliness.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:16

**Q3. What should be done to profane and vain babblings?**  
**Answer:** Shun them.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:16

---

### 2 Timothy 2:17

**Q1. Whose word will eat as doth a canker?**  
**Answer:** Their word.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:17

**Q2. Who are named as examples of those whose word eats as a canker?**  
**Answer:** Hymenaeus and Philetus.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:17

**Q3. What disease is their word compared to?**  
**Answer:** A canker.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:17

---

### 2 Timothy 2:18

**Q1. Concerning what have Hymenaeus and Philetus erred?**  
**Answer:** The truth.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:18

**Q2. What do Hymenaeus and Philetus say has already passed?**  
**Answer:** That the resurrection is past already.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:18

**Q3. Whose faith do they overthrow?**  
**Answer:** The faith of some.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:18

---

### 2 Timothy 2:19

**Q1. What foundation of God standeth?**  
**Answer:** Sure.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:19

**Q2. What seal does this foundation have?**  
**Answer:** The Lord knoweth them that are his.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:19

**Q3. What should every one that nameth the name of Christ depart from?**  
**Answer:** Iniquity.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:19

---

### 2 Timothy 2:20

**Q1. In a great house, what kind of vessels are there not only?**  
**Answer:** Vessels of gold and of silver.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:20

**Q2. Besides gold and silver, what other materials are vessels made of in a great house?**  
**Answer:** Wood and of earth.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:20

**Q3. What are some vessels unto, and some unto?**  
**Answer:** Some to honour, and some to dishonour.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:20

---

### 2 Timothy 2:21

**Q1. If a man purge himself from what, shall he be a vessel unto honour?**  
**Answer:** These.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:21

**Q2. How will a purged vessel be sanctified?**  
**Answer:** Meet for the master's use.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:21

**Q3. For what is the purged vessel prepared?**  
**Answer:** Unto every good work.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:21

---

### 2 Timothy 2:22

**Q1. What kind of lusts should be fled from?**  
**Answer:** Youthful lusts.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:22

**Q2. What things should be followed after?**  
**Answer:** Righteousness, faith, charity, peace.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:22

**Q3. With whom should righteousness, faith, charity, and peace be followed?**  
**Answer:** With them that call on the Lord out of a pure heart.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:22

---

### 2 Timothy 2:23

**Q1. What kind of questions should be avoided?**  
**Answer:** Foolish and unlearned questions.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:23

**Q2. What do foolish and unlearned questions gender?**  
**Answer:** Strifes.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:23

**Q3. What should be done to foolish and unlearned questions?**  
**Answer:** Avoid them.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:23

---

### 2 Timothy 2:24

**Q1. Who must not strive?**  
**Answer:** The servant of the Lord.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:24

**Q2. How must the servant of the Lord be unto all men?**  
**Answer:** Gentle.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:24

**Q3. What else must the servant of the Lord be, besides gentle?**  
**Answer:** Apt to teach, patient.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:24

---

### 2 Timothy 2:25

**Q1. In meekness, whom should the servant instruct?**  
**Answer:** Those that oppose themselves.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:25

**Q2. What is peradventure that God will give them?**  
**Answer:** Repentance to the acknowledging of the truth.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:25

**Q3. To the acknowledging of what will repentance be given?**  
**Answer:** The truth.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:25

---

### 2 Timothy 2:26

**Q1. From what snare may they recover themselves?**  
**Answer:** The snare of the devil.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:26

**Q2. By whom are they taken?**  
**Answer:** Him.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:26

**Q3. For what purpose are they taken by him?**  
**Answer:** At his will.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 2:26
