### 2 Timothy 1:1

**Q1. Who is Paul an apostle of?**  
**Answer:** Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 1:1

**Q2. By whose will is Paul an apostle?**  
**Answer:** By the will of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 1:1

**Q3. What promise is the life in Christ Jesus according to?**  
**Answer:** The promise of life which is in Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 1:1

**Q17. Where is the gift of God?**

**Q22. What should Timothy not be ashamed of?**
