# 2 Timothy 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Timothy 4:1

**Q1. Before God and the Lord Jesus Christ, who shall judge the quick and the dead at his appearing and his kingdom, what does Paul charge Timothy?**  
**Answer:** To charge them.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:1

**Q2. Who is described as the one who shall judge the quick and the dead at his appearing and his kingdom?**  
**Answer:** The Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:1

**Q3. When will the Lord Jesus Christ judge the quick and the dead according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** At his appearing and his kingdom.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:1

---

### 2 Timothy 4:2

**Q1. What is the first instruction given to Timothy concerning the word?**  
**Answer:** Preach the word.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:2

**Q2. When should Timothy be instant regarding preaching the word?**  
**Answer:** In season, out of season.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:2

**Q3. With what should Timothy reprove, rebuke, and exhort?**  
**Answer:** With all longsuffering and doctrine.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:2

---

### 2 Timothy 4:3

**Q1. What will time bring regarding sound doctrine?**  
**Answer:** They will not endure sound doctrine.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:3

**Q2. According to their own lusts, what will they heap to themselves?**  
**Answer:** Teachers.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:3

**Q3. What will the teachers they heap to themselves have regarding their ears?**  
**Answer:** Having itching ears.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:3

---

### 2 Timothy 4:4

**Q1. From what will they turn away their ears?**  
**Answer:** From the truth.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:4

**Q2. To what will they be turned aside?**  
**Answer:** Unto fables.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:4

**Q3. What action do they take concerning their ears and the truth?**  
**Answer:** Shall turn away their ears from the truth.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:4

---

### 2 Timothy 4:5

**Q1. What is Timothy told to watch in all things?**  
**Answer:** Thou in all things.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:5

**Q2. What should Timothy endure?**  
**Answer:** Afflictions.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:5

**Q3. What work should Timothy do and what should he fully do?**  
**Answer:** Do the work of an evangelist, make full proof of thy ministry.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:5

---

### 2 Timothy 4:6

**Q1. How is Paul's departure described in relation to a sacrifice?**  
**Answer:** I am now ready to be offered.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:6

**Q2. What time is at hand?**  
**Answer:** The time of my departure.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:6

**Q3. What is the status of Paul being offered?**  
**Answer:** I am now ready.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:6

---

### 2 Timothy 4:7

**Q1. What kind of fight has Paul fought?**  
**Answer:** A good fight.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:7

**Q2. What has Paul finished?**  
**Answer:** His course.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:7

**Q3. What has Paul kept?**  
**Answer:** The faith.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:7

---

### 2 Timothy 4:8

**Q1. What is laid up for Paul henceforth?**  
**Answer:** A crown of righteousness.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:8

**Q2. Who will give the crown of righteousness at that day?**  
**Answer:** The Lord, the righteous judge.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:8

**Q3. To whom else will the crown be given besides Paul?**  
**Answer:** All them also that love his appearing.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:8

---

### 2 Timothy 4:9

**Q1. What does Paul ask Timothy to do shortly?**  
**Answer:** Do thy diligence to come shortly unto me.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:9

**Q2. Whom does Paul address in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Timothy.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:9

**Q3. What action does Paul request of Timothy?**  
**Answer:** Come shortly unto me.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:9

---

### 2 Timothy 4:10

**Q1. Who has forsaken Paul having loved this present world?**  
**Answer:** Demas.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:10

**Q2. Where did Demas depart unto?**  
**Answer:** Thessalonica.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:10

**Q3. Where did Crescens and Titus go?**  
**Answer:** Crescens to Galatia, Titus unto Dalmatia.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:10

---

### 2 Timothy 4:11

**Q1. Who alone is with Paul?**  
**Answer:** Luke.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:11

**Q2. Whom should Timothy take and bring with him?**  
**Answer:** Mark.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:11

**Q3. Why does Paul want Mark brought with him?**  
**Answer:** For he is profitable to me for the ministry.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:11

---

### 2 Timothy 4:12

**Q1. Whom has Paul sent to Ephesus?**  
**Answer:** Tychicus.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:12

**Q2. Where was Tychicus sent?**  
**Answer:** To Ephesus.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:12

**Q3. Who sent Tychicus to Ephesus?**  
**Answer:** Paul.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:12

---

### 2 Timothy 4:13

**Q1. What did Paul leave at Troas with Carpus?**  
**Answer:** The cloke.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:13

**Q2. When Timothy comes, what should he bring with him regarding books?**  
**Answer:** The books.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:13

**Q3. What specific type of books does Paul mention besides the books?**  
**Answer:** But especially the parchments.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 4:13
