# 2 Timothy 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Timothy 3:1

**Q1. What should men know concerning the last days?**  
**Answer:** That in the last days perilous times shall come.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:1

**Q2. When shall perilous times come according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** In the last days.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:1

**Q3. What kind of times shall come in the last days?**  
**Answer:** Perilous times.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:1

---

### 2 Timothy 3:2

**Q1. What will men be lovers of?**  
**Answer:** Their own selves.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:2

**Q2. What will men be covetous of?**  
**Answer:** Covetous.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:2

**Q3. How are men described regarding their parents and thankfulness?**  
**Answer:** Boasters, proud, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:2

---

### 2 Timothy 3:3

**Q1. What are men said to be without?**  
**Answer:** Without natural affection.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:3

**Q2. What are men's attitudes toward trucebreakers and false accusers?**  
**Answer:** Trucebreakers, false accusers.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:3

**Q3. How are men described regarding temperance, goodness, and traitors?**  
**Answer:** Incontinent, fierce, despisers of those that are good, traitors.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:3

---

### 2 Timothy 3:4

**Q1. What are men described as being heady?**  
**Answer:** Heady.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:4

**Q2. What will men be lovers of rather than lovers of God?**  
**Answer:** Lovers of pleasures more than lovers of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:4

**Q3. How else are men described in this verse regarding pride?**  
**Answer:** Highminded.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:4

---

### 2 Timothy 3:5

**Q1. What do men have a form of?**  
**Answer:** Godliness.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:5

**Q2. What do men deny regarding godliness?**  
**Answer:** The power thereof.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:5

**Q3. What instruction is given concerning such people?**  
**Answer:** From such turn away.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:5

---

### 2 Timothy 3:6

**Q1. What do some of these people creep into?**  
**Answer:** Houses.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:6

**Q2. Who do they lead captive?**  
**Answer:** Silly women laden with sins.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:6

**Q3. What kind of lusts are these women led away by?**  
**Answer:** Divers lusts.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:6

---

### 2 Timothy 3:7

**Q1. What are these women always doing?**  
**Answer:** Learning.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:7

**Q2. What are they never able to come to?**  
**Answer:** The knowledge of the truth.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:7

**Q3. Are they ever able to come to the knowledge of the truth?**  
**Answer:** Never.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:7

---

### 2 Timothy 3:8

**Q1. Who resisted Moses in the same manner?**  
**Answer:** Jannes and Jambres.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:8

**Q2. How are the minds of these men described?**  
**Answer:** Corrupt.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:8

**Q3. How are these men described regarding the faith?**  
**Answer:** Reprobate concerning the faith.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:8

---

### 2 Timothy 3:9

**Q1. How far shall these men proceed?**  
**Answer:** No further.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:9

**Q2. What shall be manifest unto all men?**  
**Answer:** Their folly.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:9

**Q3. To whom shall their folly be manifest?**  
**Answer:** Unto all men.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:9

---

### 2 Timothy 3:10

**Q1. What has Timothy fully known of Paul's?**  
**Answer:** Doctrine, manner of life, purpose, faith, longsuffering, charity, patience.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:10

**Q2. Whose doctrine and manner of life had Timothy fully known?**  
**Answer:** Paul's.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:10

**Q3. What character traits of Paul did Timothy fully know?**  
**Answer:** Faith, longsuffering, charity, patience.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:10

---

### 2 Timothy 3:11

**Q1. What did Paul endure in Antioch, Iconium, and Lystra?**  
**Answer:** Persecutions, afflictions.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:11

**Q2. What did Paul come out of at Antioch, Iconium, and Lystra?**  
**Answer:** What persecutions I endured.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:11

**Q3. Who delivered Paul out of all these persecutions?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:11

---

### 2 Timothy 3:12

**Q1. Who shall suffer persecution?**  
**Answer:** All that will live godly in Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:12

**Q2. In whom must one live godly to suffer persecution?**  
**Answer:** In Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:12

**Q3. What will happen to all that will live godly in Christ Jesus?**  
**Answer:** They shall suffer persecution.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:12

---

### 2 Timothy 3:13

**Q1. What will evil men and seducers wax?**  
**Answer:** Worse and worse.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:13

**Q2. What are evil men and seducers described as doing to others?**  
**Answer:** Deceiving.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:13

**Q3. What are evil men and seducers described as doing to themselves?**  
**Answer:** Being deceived.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:13

---

### 2 Timothy 3:14

**Q1. What must Timothy continue in?**  
**Answer:** The things which he hath learned and has been assured of.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:14

**Q2. From whom has Timothy learned these things?**  
**Answer:** Of whom thou hast learned them.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:14

**Q3. What does the verse say Timothy has been?**  
**Answer:** Assured of.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:14

---

### 2 Timothy 3:15

**Q1. What has Timothy known from a child?**  
**Answer:** The holy scriptures.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:15

**Q2. What are the holy scriptures able to make Timothy wise unto?**  
**Answer:** Salvation.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:15

**Q3. Through what is the scripture able to make one wise unto salvation?**  
**Answer:** Through faith which is in Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:15

---

### 2 Timothy 3:16

**Q1. What is given by inspiration of God?**  
**Answer:** All scripture.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:16

**Q2. What is all scripture profitable for?**  
**Answer:** Doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:16

**Q3. How is all scripture given?**  
**Answer:** By inspiration of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:16

---

### 2 Timothy 3:17

**Q1. What may the man of God be?**  
**Answer:** Perfect.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:17

**Q2. To what state is the man of God thoroughly furnished?**  
**Answer:** Unto all good works.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:17

**Q3. What is the man of God through the scriptures?**  
**Answer:** Thoroughly furnished unto all good works.  
**Verse:** 2 Timothy 3:17
