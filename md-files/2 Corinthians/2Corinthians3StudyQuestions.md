# 2 Corinthians 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Corinthians 3:1
**Q1. Does Paul ask if they begin again to commend themselves?**
**Answer:** Do we begin again to commend ourselves?
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:1

**Q2. What does Paul ask about "some others"?**
**Answer:** Or need we, as some others, epistles of commendation to you, or letters of commendation from you?
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:1

**Q3. What are the two types of commendation mentioned?**
**Answer:** Epistles of commendation to them, or letters of commendation from the believers.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:1

### 2 Corinthians 3:2
**Q1. What are the believers to the apostles?**
**Answer:** Ye are our epistle.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:2

**Q2. Where is this epistle written?**
**Answer:** Written in our hearts.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:2

**Q3. Who knows and reads this epistle?**
**Answer:** Known and read of all men.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:2

### 2 Corinthians 3:3
**Q1. Who is the epistle of Christ?**
**Answer:** Ye [the believers].
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:3

**Q2. Who ministered the epistle of Christ?**
**Answer:** Ministered by us.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:3

**Q3. With what is this epistle written, and where is it written?**
**Answer:** Written not with ink, but with the Spirit of the living God; not in tables of stone, but in fleshy tables of the heart.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:3

### 2 Corinthians 3:4
**Q1. Through whom does the apostles have trust to God-ward?**
**Answer:** Through Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:4

**Q2. To whom is this trust directed?**
**Answer:** To God-ward.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:4

**Q3. What is the nature of their relationship to God in this verse?**
**Answer:** Such trust have we through Christ to God-ward.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:4

### 2 Corinthians 3:5
**Q1. Do the apostles think they are sufficient of themselves?**
**Answer:** Not that we are sufficient of ourselves to think any thing as of ourselves.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:5

**Q2. Where does their sufficiency come from?**
**Answer:** Our sufficiency is of God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:5

**Q3. What is the point of this verse?**
**Answer:** That their sufficiency is not from themselves, but from God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:5

### 2 Corinthians 3:6
**Q1. Who made the apostles able ministers of the new testament?**
**Answer:** God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:6

**Q2. What is the nature of the new testament ministry?**
**Answer:** Not of the letter, but of the spirit.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:6

**Q3. What is the difference between the letter and the spirit?**
**Answer:** For the letter killeth, but the spirit giveth life.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:6

### 2 Corinthians 3:7
**Q1. What was the ministration of death written and engraven in?**
**Answer:** In stones.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:7

**Q2. Why could the children of Israel not stedfastly behold the face of Moses?**
**Answer:** For the glory of his countenance.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:7

**Q3. What happened to this glory?**
**Answer:** Which glory was to be done away.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:7

### 2 Corinthians 3:8
**Q1. What does Paul ask about the ministration of the spirit?**
**Answer:** How shall not the ministration of the spirit be rather glorious?
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:8

**Q2. To what is the ministration of the spirit being compared?**
**Answer:** To the ministration of death.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:8

**Q3. What is the expectation for the ministration of the spirit?**
**Answer:** That it be rather glorious.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:8

### 2 Corinthians 3:9
**Q1. If the ministration of condemnation be glory, what is the result?**
**Answer:** Much more doth the ministration of righteousness exceed in glory.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:9

**Q2. What is compared to the ministration of condemnation?**
**Answer:** The ministration of righteousness.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:9

**Q3. Which ministration exceeds in glory?**
**Answer:** The ministration of righteousness.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:9

### 2 Corinthians 3:10
**Q1. What is said about the thing that was made glorious?**
**Answer:** For even that which was made glorious had no glory in this respect.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:10

**Q2. Why did it have no glory in this respect?**
**Answer:** By reason of the glory that excelleth.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:10

**Q3. What is the nature of the glory that excelleth?**
**Answer:** It is a glory that surpasses the former.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:10

### 2 Corinthians 3:11
**Q1. What is the contrast between that which is done away and that which remaineth?**
**Answer:** For if that which is done away was glorious, much more that which remaineth is glorious.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:11

**Q2. Which is more glorious?**
**Answer:** That which remaineth.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:11

**Q3. What is the state of the first glory?**
**Answer:** It is done away.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:11

### 2 Corinthians 3:12
**Q1. What do the apostles use because they have such hope?**
**Answer:** Great plainness of speech.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:12

**Q2. Why do they use great plainness of speech?**
**Answer:** Seeing then that we have such hope.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:12

**Q3. What is the manner of their speech?**
**Answer:** Great plainness of speech.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:12

### 2 Corinthians 3:13
**Q1. How did Moses act regarding the end of that which is abolished?**
**Answer:** Which put a veil over his face.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:13

**Q2. Why did Moses put a veil over his face?**
**Answer:** That the children of Israel could not stedfastly look to the end of that which is abolished.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:13

**Q3. What did Moses do to his face?**
**Answer:** Put a veil over his face.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:13

### 2 Corinthians 3:14
**Q1. What happened to the minds of the children of Israel?**
**Answer:** But their minds were blinded.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:14

**Q2. What remains untaken away in the reading of the old testament?**
**Answer:** The same vail.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:14

**Q3. In whom is this vail done away?**
**Answer:** In Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:14

### 2 Corinthians 3:15
**Q1. What is the state of the vail when Moses is read?**
**Answer:** But even unto this day, when Moses is read, the vail is upon their heart.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:15

**Q2. Where is the vail located when Moses is read?**
**Answer:** Upon their heart.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:15

**Q3. Who is being read in this verse?**
**Answer:** Moses.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:15

### 2 Corinthians 3:16
**Q1. When shall the vail be taken away?**
**Answer:** Nevertheless when it shall turn to the Lord.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:16

**Q2. What will happen when they turn to the Lord?**
**Answer:** The vail shall be taken away.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:16

**Q3. What is the condition for the vail to be taken away?**
**Answer:** Turning to the Lord.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:16

### 2 Corinthians 3:17
**Q1. Who is that Spirit?**
**Answer:** The Lord is that Spirit.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:17

**Q2. Where is there liberty?**
**Answer:** Where the Spirit of the Lord is.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:17

**Q3. What is the result of the Spirit of the Lord's presence?**
**Answer:** There is liberty.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:17

### 2 Corinthians 3:18
**Q1. How do the believers behold the glory of the Lord?**
**Answer:** With open face beholding as in a glass.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:18

**Q2. What is the result of beholding the glory of the Lord?**
**Answer:** Are changed into the same image from glory to glory.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:18

**Q3. By whom are they changed?**
**Answer:** Even as by the Spirit of the Lord.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 3:18
