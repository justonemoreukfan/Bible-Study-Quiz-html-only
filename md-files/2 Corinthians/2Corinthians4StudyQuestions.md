# 2 Corinthians 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Corinthians 4:1
**Q1. Why do the apostles not faint?**
**Answer:** Seeing we have this ministry, as we have received mercy.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:1

**Q2. What did they receive that prevents them from fainting?**
**Answer:** Mercy.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:1

**Q3. What is the reason they do not faint?**
**Answer:** Because they have this ministry and received mercy.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:1

### 2 Corinthians 4:2
**Q1. What have the apostles renounced?**
**Answer:** The hidden things of dishonesty.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:2

**Q2. How do they NOT walk?**
**Answer:** Not walking in craftiness.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:2

**Q3. How do they NOT handle the word of God?**
**Answer:** Nor handling the word of God deceitfully.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:2

### 2 Corinthians 4:3
**Q1. To whom is the gospel hid if it is hid?**
**Answer:** To them that are lost.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:3

**Q2. What happens to the gospel for those who are lost?**
**Answer:** It is hid.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:3

**Q3. Who are the ones to whom the gospel is hid?**
**Answer:** Them that are lost.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:3

### 2 Corinthians 4:4
**Q1. Who has blinded the minds of those who believe not?**
**Answer:** The god of this world.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:4

**Q2. Why has he blinded their minds?**
**Answer:** Lest the light of the glorious gospel of Christ, who is the image of God, should shine unto them.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:4

**Q3. Who is the image of God?**
**Answer:** Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:4

### 2 Corinthians 4:5
**Q1. Who do the apostles preach?**
**Answer:** Not ourselves, but Christ Jesus the Lord.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:5

**Q2. What are the apostles to the believers for Jesus' sake?**
**Answer:** Ourselves your servants.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:5

**Q3. For whose sake are they servants?**
**Answer:** For Jesus' sake.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:5

### 2 Corinthians 4:6
**Q1. What did God command to shine out of darkness?**
**Answer:** The light.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:6

**Q2. Where has God shined the light?**
**Answer:** In our hearts.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:6

**Q3. What is the purpose of this light in the heart?**
**Answer:** To give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:6

### 2 Corinthians 4:7
**Q1. Where do the apostles have this treasure?**
**Answer:** In earthen vessels.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:7

**Q2. Why is the treasure in earthen vessels?**
**Answer:** That the excellency of the power may be of God, and not of us.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:7

**Q3. What is the treasure's excellency a result of?**
**Answer:** Of God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:7

### 2 Corinthians 4:8
**Q1. In what way are the apostles troubled?**
**Answer:** Troubled on every side.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:8

**Q2. Despite being troubled, what are they NOT?**
**Answer:** Yet not distressed.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:8

**Q3. What is the result of being perplexed?**
**Answer:** But not in despair.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:8

### 2 Corinthians 4:9
**Q1. What is the status of the apostles in terms of persecution?**
**Answer:** Persecuted, but not forsaken.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:9

**Q2. What is the status of the apostles in terms of being cast down?**
**Answer:** Cast down, but not destroyed.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:9

**Q3. Are the apostles forsaken or destroyed?**
**Answer:** Not forsaken and not destroyed.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:9

### 2 Corinthians 4:10
**Q1. What do the apostles bear about in the body?**
**Answer:** The dying of the Lord Jesus.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:10

**Q2. Why do they bear the dying of the Lord Jesus?**
**Answer:** That the life also of Jesus might be made manifest in our body.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:10

**Q3. In what is the life of Jesus made manifest?**
**Answer:** In our body.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:10

### 2 Corinthians 4:11
**Q1. For whose sake are those who live delivered unto death?**
**Answer:** For Jesus' sake.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:11

**Q2. What is the purpose of being delivered unto death?**
**Answer:** That the life also of Jesus might be made manifest in our mortal flesh.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:11

**Q3. In what is the life of Jesus made manifest in this verse?**
**Answer:** In our mortal flesh.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:11

### 2 Corinthians 4:12
**Q1. How does death work in the apostles?**
**Answer:** Death worketh in us.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:12

**Q2. How does life work in the believers?**
**Answer:** But life in you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:12

**Q3. What is the contrast between the apostles and the believers in this verse?**
**Answer:** Death worketh in us, but life in you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:12

### 2 Corinthians 4:13
**Q1. What spirit do the apostles have?**
**Answer:** The same spirit of faith.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:13

**Q2. What is written about faith and speaking?**
**Answer:** I believed, and therefore have I spoken.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:13

**Q3. What do the apostles do because they believe?**
**Answer:** We also believe, and therefore speak.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:13

### 2 Corinthians 4:14
**Q1. What is the knowledge regarding the one who raised the Lord Jesus?**
**Answer:** That he which raised up the Lord Jesus shall raise up us also.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:14

**Q2. Through whom will the apostles be raised?**
**Answer:** By Jesus.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:14

**Q3. What will happen after they are raised?**
**Answer:** Shall present us with you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:14

### 2 Corinthians 4:15
**Q1. For whose sakes are all things?**
**Answer:** For your sakes.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:15

**Q2. How should the abundant grace redound to the glory of God?**
**Answer:** Through the thanksgiving of many.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:15

**Q3. To what should the abundant grace redound?**
**Answer:** To the glory of God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:15

### 2 Corinthians 4:16
**Q1. For what cause do the apostles not faint?**
**Answer:** For which cause we faint not.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:16

**Q2. What happens to the outward man?**
**Answer:** Though our outward man perish.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:16

**Q3. What happens to the inward man?**
**Answer:** Yet the inward man is renewed day by day.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:16

### 2 Corinthians 4:17
**Q1. How is the current affliction described?**
**Answer:** Light affliction.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:17

**Q2. How long is this light affliction?**
**Answer:** But for a moment.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:17

**Q3. What does this light affliction work for?**
**Answer:** A far more exceeding and eternal weight of glory.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:17

### 2 Corinthians 4:18
**Q1. At what things do the apostles NOT look?**
**Answer:** Not at the things which are seen.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:18

**Q2. At what things do they look?**
**Answer:** At the things which are not seen.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:18

**Q3. What is the difference between the things which are seen and those which are not seen?**
**Answer:** For the things which are seen are temporal; but the things which are not seen are eternal.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 4:18
