# 2 Corinthians 11 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Corinthians 11:1
**Q1. What does Paul wish the believers could bear with him in?**
**Answer:** A little in my folly.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:1

**Q2. Does Paul ask them to bear with him?**
**Answer:** Yes, and indeed bear with me.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:1

**Q3. What is the nature of what Paul is asking them to bear with?**
**Answer:** A little in my folly.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:1

### 2 Corinthians 11:2
**Q1. What kind of jealousy does Paul have over the believers?**
**Answer:** Godly jealousy.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:2

**Q2. To whom has Paul espoused the believers?**
**Answer:** To one husband.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:2

**Q3. How does Paul wish to present the believers to Christ?**
**Answer:** As a chaste virgin to Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:2

### 2 Corinthians 11:3
**Q1. What does Paul fear for the believers?**
**Answer:** Lest by any means, as the serpent beguiled Eve through his subtilty, so your minds should be corrupted from the simplicity that is in Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:3

**Q2. How did the serpent beguile Eve?**
**Answer:** Through his subtilty.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:3

**Q3. From what simplicity should the believers' minds not be corrupted?**
**Answer:** The simplicity that is in Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:3

### 2 Corinthians 11:4
**Q1. What would the believers bear with if someone preached "another Jesus"?**
**Answer:** Ye might well bear with him.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:4

**Q2. What else might they receive or accept that they would bear with?**
**Answer:** Another spirit, which ye have not received, or another gospel, which ye have not accepted.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:4

**Q3. What are the three things mentioned that the believers might bear with?**
**Answer:** Another Jesus, another spirit, and another gospel.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:4

### 2 Corinthians 11:5
**Q1. What does Paul suppose about himself compared to the "very chiefest apostles"?**
**Answer:** I was not a whit behind the very chiefest apostles.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:5

**Q2. Who are the "very chiefest apostles"?**
**Answer:** The very chiefest apostles.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:5

**Q3. Is Paul behind the chiefest apostles in any way?**
**Answer:** No, he was not a whit behind.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:5

### 2 Corinthians 11:6
**Q1. What is said about Paul's speech?**
**Answer:** Though I be rude in speech.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:6

**Q2. Is Paul lacking in knowledge?**
**Answer:** Yet not in knowledge.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:6

**Q3. How have the apostles been made manifest among the believers?**
**Answer:** Throughly made manifest among you in all things.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:6

### 2 Corinthians 11:7
**Q1. What does Paul ask if he committed an offence in doing what?**
**Answer:** In abasing myself that ye might be exalted.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:7

**Q2. Why did Paul abase himself?**
**Answer:** That ye might be exalted.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:7

**Q3. How did Paul preach the gospel of God to the believers?**
**Answer:** Freely.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:7

### 2 Corinthians 11:8
**Q1. What did Paul do regarding other churches?**
**Answer:** I robbed other churches.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:8

**Q2. What did he take from other churches?**
**Answer:** Taking wages of them.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:8

**Q3. Why did he take wages from other churches?**
**Answer:** To do you service.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:8

### 2 Corinthians 11:9
**Q1. When Paul was present with the believers, what was his condition?**
**Answer:** And wanted.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:9

**Q2. Was Paul chargeable to any man?**
**Answer:** I was chargeable to no man.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:9

**Q3. Who supplied what was lacking to Paul?**
**Answer:** The brethren which came from Macedonia supplied.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:9

### 2 Corinthians 11:10
**Q1. What is in Paul?**
**Answer:** The truth of Christ is in me.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:10

**Q2. What will no man stop Paul from doing in the regions of Achaia?**
**Answer:** No man shall stop me of this boasting in the regions of Achaia.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:10

**Q3. Where is the boasting taking place?**
**Answer:** In the regions of Achaia.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:10

### 2 Corinthians 11:11
**Q1. Why does Paul boast?**
**Answer:** Because I love you not? God knoweth.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:11

**Q2. Who knows whether Paul loves the believers?**
**Answer:** God knoweth.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:11

**Q3. What is the question Paul asks about his love?**
**Answer:** Wherefore? because I love you not?
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:11

### 2 Corinthians 11:12
**Q1. What does Paul do to "cut off occasion" from those who desire occasion?**
**Answer:** But what I do, that I will do, that I may cut off occasion from them which desire occasion.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:12

**Q2. What do those who desire occasion hope for?**
**Answer:** That wherein they glory, they may be found even as we.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:12

**Q3. What is the goal of Paul's actions?**
**Answer:** To cut off occasion from them which desire occasion.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:12

### 2 Corinthians 11:13
**Q1. Who are described as "false apostles"?**
**Answer:** For such are false apostles, deceitful workers.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:13

**Q2. What do these false apostles do?**
**Answer:** Transforming themselves into the apostles of Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:13

**Q3. What are these false apostles called?**
**Answer:** False apostles, deceitful workers.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:13

### 2 Corinthians 11:14
**Q1. Who is transformed into an angel of light?**
**Answer:** Satan himself.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:14

**Q2. Into what is Satan transformed?**
**Answer:** Into an angel of light.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:14

**Q3. Why is it "no marvel" that some ministers are transformed?**
**Answer:** Because Satan himself is transformed into an angel of light.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:14

### 2 Corinthians 11:15
**Q1. Who are the ministers of righteousness?**
**Answer:** His ministers [Satan's ministers].
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:15

**Q2. How are these ministers transformed?**
**Answer:** Transformed as the ministers of righteousness.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:15

**Q3. What will be the end of these ministers?**
**Answer:** Whose end shall be according to their works.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:15

### 2 Corinthians 11:16
**Q1. What does Paul say about being a fool?**
**Answer:** I say again, let no man think me a fool.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:16

**Q2. If he is a fool, how should the believers receive him?**
**Answer:** If otherwise, yet as a fool receive me.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:1 la 16
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:16

**Q3. What does he wish to do as a fool?**
**Answer:** That I may boast myself a little.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:16

### 2 Corinthians 11:17
**Q1. How does Paul speak in this verse?**
**Answer:** I speak it not after the Lord, but as it were foolishly, in this confidence of boasting.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:17

**Q2. Does he speak after the Lord?**
**Answer:** No, I speak it not after the Lord.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:17

**Q3. In what confidence does he speak?**
**Answer:** In this confidence of boasting.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:17

### 2 Corinthians 11:18
**Q1. Why does Paul glory?**
**Answer:** Seeing that many glory after the flesh, I will glory also.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:18

**Q2. Who else glories after the flesh?**
**Answer:** Many.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:18

**Q3. In what way does he glory?**
**Answer:** After the flesh.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:18

### 2 Corinthians 11:19
**Q1. What do the believers "suffer gladly"?**
**Answer:** For ye suffer fools gladly, seeing ye yourselves are wise.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:19

**Q2. Why do they suffer fools gladly?**
**Answer:** Seeing ye yourselves are wise.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:19

**Q3. Who are the ones suffering fools?**
**Answer:** The believers.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:19

### 2 Corinthians 11:20
**Q1. List three things that a man might do to the believers?**
**Answer:** If a man bring you into bondage, if a man devour you, if a man take of you, if a man exalt himself, if a man smite you on the face.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:20

**Q2. If a man smites them on the face, do they suffer it?**
**Answer:** Yes.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:20

**Q3. What is the reaction of the believers to these actions?**
**Answer:** They suffer it.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:20

### 2 Corinthians 11:21
**Q1. What does Paul speak concerning?**
**Answer:** Concerning reproach.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:21

**Q2. How does Paul describe himself in this verse?**
**Answer:** As though we had been weak.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:21

**Q3. In what way is Paul bold?**
**Answer:** Whereinsoever any is bold, (I speak foolishly,) I am bold also.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:21

### 2 Corinthians 11:22
**Q1. Are they Hebrews?**
**Answer:** Are they Hebrews? so am I.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:22

**Q2. Are they Israelites?**
**Answer:** Are they Israelites? so am I.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:22

**Q3. Are they the seed of Abraham?**
**Answer:** Are they the seed of Abraham? so am I.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:22

### 2 Corinthians 11:23
**Q1. Are they ministers of Christ?**
**Answer:**Answer: Are they ministers of Christ? (I speak as a fool) I am more.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:23

**Q2. In what ways is Paul "more" than them?**
**Answer:** In labours more abundant, in stripes above measure, in prisons more frequent, in deaths oft.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:23

**Q3. List two of the ways Paul is "more" than them.**
**Answer:** Labours more abundant and stripes above measure.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:23

### 2 Corinthians 11:24
**Q1. How many times did Paul receive forty stripes save one from the Jews?**
**Answer:** Five times.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:24

**Q2. From whom did he receive these stripes?**
**Answer:** Of the Jews.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:24

**Q3. How many stripes did he receive each time?**
**Answer:** Forty stripes save one.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:24

### 2 Corinthians 11:25
**Q1. How many times was Paul beaten with rods?**
**Answer:** Thrice.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:25

**Q2. How many times was he stoned?**
**Answer:** Once.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:25

**Q3. How many times did he suffer shipwreck?**
**Answer:** Thrice.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:25

### 2 Corinthians 11:26
**Q1. List three perils Paul faced in his journeyings.**
**Answer:** Perils of waters, in perils of robbers, in perils by mine own countrymen, in perils by the heathen, in perils in the city, in perils in the wilderness, in perils in the sea, in perils among false brethren.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:26

**Q2. Where did he face perils in the city?**
**Answer:** In perils in the city.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:26

**Q3. Where did he face perils in the sea?**
**Answer:** In perils in the sea.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:26

### 2 Corinthians 11:27
**Q1. What was Paul's condition in his weariness and painfulness?**
**Answer:** In weariness and painfulness, in watchings often, in hunger and thirst, in fastings often, in cold and nakedness.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:27

**Q2. In what was he often in watchings?**
**Answer:** In watchings often.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:27

**Q3. What did he experience in terms of food and drink?**
**Answer:** In hunger and thirst.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:27

### 2 Corinthians 11:28
**Q1. What comes upon Paul daily besides those things that are without?**
**Answer:** The care of all the churches.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:28

**Q2. What are "those things that are without"?**
**Answer:** The hardships and perils mentioned in previous verses.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:28

**Q3. What is the nature of the daily care?**
**Answer:** The care of all the churches.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 11:28

**Q3. What is the question Paul asks?**
**Answer:** Who is weak, and I am not weak?

**Answer:** Of the things which concern mine infirmities.

**Answer:** The God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.
