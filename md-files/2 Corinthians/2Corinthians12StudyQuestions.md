# 2 Corinthians 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Corinthians 12:1
**Q1. Does Paul think it is expedient for him to glory?**
**Answer:** It is not expedient for me doubtless to glory.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:1

**Q2. What will he come to?**
**Answer:** I will come to visions and revelations of the Lord.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:1

**Q3. What is the nature of the things he will come to?**
**Answer:** Visions and revelations of the Lord.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:1

### 2 Corinthians 12:2
**Q1. Who did Paul know in Christ above fourteen years ago?**
**Answer:** A man in Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:2

**Q2. What is unsure about this man?**
**Answer:** Whether in the body, I cannot tell; or whether out of the body, I cannot tell: God knoweth.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:2

**Q3. Where was this man caught up to?**
**Answer:** To the third heaven.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:2

### 2 Corinthians 12:3
**Q1. What does Paul say about the man he knew?**
**Answer:** And I knew such a man.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:3

**Q2. What is unsure about this man's state?**
**Answer:** Whether in the body, or out of the body, I cannot tell: God knoweth.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:3

**Q3. Who knows if the man was in the body or out of the body?**
**Answer:** God knoweth.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:3

### 2 Corinthians 12:4
**Q1. Where was the man caught up to?**
**Answer:** Into paradise.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:4

**Q2. What did he hear in paradise?**
**Answer:** Unspeakable words.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:4

**Q3. Why is it not lawful for a man to utter these words?**
**Answer:** Which it is not lawful for a man to utter.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:4

### 2 Corinthians 12:5
**Q1. Of whom will Paul glory?**
**Answer:** Of such an one.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:5

**Q2. Of what will Paul NOT glory?**
**Answer:** Yet of myself I will not glory.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:5

**Q3. In what will Paul glory instead?**
**Answer:** But in mine infirmities.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:5

### 2 Corinthians 12:6
**Q1. If Paul desires to glory, why would he not be a fool?**
**Answer:** For I will say the truth.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:6

**Q2. Why does Paul forbear?**
**Answer:** Lest any man should think of me above that which he seeth me to be, or that he heareth of me.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:6

**Q3. What is the risk of glorying in himself?**
**Answer:** That any man should think of me above that which he seeth me to be, or that he heareth of me.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:6

### 2 Corinthians 12:7
**Q1. Why was a thorn in the flesh given to Paul?**
**Answer:** Lest I should be exalted above measure through the abundance of the revelations.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:7

**Q2. What was the thorn in the flesh?**
**Answer:** The messenger of Satan to buffet me.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:7

**Q3. What was the purpose of the thorn in the flesh?**
**Answer:** Lest I should be exalted above measure.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:7

### 2 Corinthians 12:8
**Q1. How many times did Paul beseech the Lord that the thorn depart from him?**
**Answer:** Thrice.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:8

**Q2. What did Paul beseech the Lord?**
**Answer:** That it might depart from me.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:8

**Q3. What was the thing he besought the Lord about?**
**Answer:** The thorn in the flesh.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:8

### 2 Corinthians 12:9
**Q1. What did the Lord say to Paul regarding his grace?**
**Answer:** My grace is sufficient for thee.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:9

**Q2. In what is the Lord's strength made perfect?**
**Answer:** For my strength is made perfect in weakness.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:9

**Q3. Why does Paul gladly glory in his infirmities?**
**Answer:** That the power of Christ may rest upon me.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:9

### 2 Corinthians 12:10
**Q1. In what things does Paul take pleasure?**
**Answer:** In infirmities, in reproaches, in necessities, in persecutions, in distresses.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:10

**Q2. For whose sake does he take pleasure in these things?**
**Answer:** For Christ's sake.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:10

**Q3. When is Paul strong?**
**Answer:** For when I am weak, then am I strong.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:10

### 2 Corinthians 12:11
**Q1. How does Paul describe himself in terms of glorying?**
**Answer:** I am become a fool in glorying.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:11

**Q2. Why has he become a fool in glorying?**
**Answer:** Ye have compelled me.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:11

**Q3. What does Paul say about himself compared to the chiefest apostles?**
**Answer:** In nothing am I behind the very chiefest apostles, though I be nothing.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:11

### 2 Corinthians 12:12
**Q1. What were the signs of an apostle wrought among the believers?**
**Answer:** In all patience, in signs, and wonders, and mighty deeds.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:12

**Q2. How were the signs wrought?**
**Answer:** In all patience.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:12

**Q3. List three of the signs of an apostle.**
**Answer:** Signs, wonders, and mighty deeds.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:12

### 2 Corinthians 12:13
**Q1. In what way were the Corinthians inferior to other churches?**
**Answer:** Except it be that I myself was not burdensome to you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:13

**Q2. Was Paul burdensome to them?**
**Answer:** No, I myself was not burdensome to you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:13

**Q3. What does Paul ask the believers to forgive?**
**Answer:** Forgive me this wrong.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:13

### 2 Corinthians 12:14
**Q1. How many times is Paul ready to come to the believers?**
**Answer:** The third time.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:14

**Q2. Will he be burdensome to them?**
**Answer:** I will not be burdensome to you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:14

**Q3. What does Paul seek from the believers?**
**Answer:** I seek not yours but you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:14

### 2 Corinthians 12:15
**Q1. What is Paul willing to do for the believers?**
**Answer:** I will very gladly spend and be spent for you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:15

**Q2. What is the contrast in the love shared?**
**Answer:** Though the more abundantly I love you, the less I be loved.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:15

**Q3. How much does Paul love the believers?**
**Answer:** More abundantly.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:15

### 2 Corinthians 12:16

**Q2. Was Paul burdensome to them?**
**Answer:** I did not burden you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:16

**Q3. What quality did Paul use to catch them with guile?**
**Answer:** Being crafty.

### 2 Corinthians 12:20
**Q1. What does Paul fear when he comes?**
**Answer:** Lest, when I come, I shall not find you such as I would.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:20

**Q2. What does Paul fear he will be found as?**
**Answer:** That I shall be found unto you such as ye would not.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:20

**Q3. List three things Paul fears will be present?**
**Answer:** Debates, envyings, wraths, strifes, backbitings, whisperings, swellings, tumults.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:20

### 2 Corinthians 12:21
**Q1. What does Paul fear his God will do among the believers?**
**Answer:** My God will humble me among you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:2 la 21
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:21

**Q3. Who are the ones Paul will bewail?**
**Answer:** Many which have sinned already.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:21

**Q3. What sins have they not repented of?**
**Answer:** The uncleanness and fornication and lasciviousness which they have committed.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 12:21
