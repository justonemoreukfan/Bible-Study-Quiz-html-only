# 2 Corinthians 13 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Corinthians 13:1
**Q1. How many times is this Paul coming to the believers?**
**Answer:** This is the third time I am coming to you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:1

**Q2. In whose mouth shall every word be established?**
**Answer:** In the mouth of two or three witnesses.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:1

**Q3. What happens in the mouth of two or three witnesses?**
**Answer:** Every word shall be established.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:1

### 2 Corinthians 13:2
**Q1. When did Paul tell the believers before?**
**Answer:** I told you before, and foretell you, as if I were present, the second time.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:2

**Q2. To whom is Paul writing now while absent?**
**Answer:** To them which heretofore have sinned, and to all other.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:2

**Q3. What will Paul do if he comes again?**
**Answer:** I will not spare.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:2

### 2 Corinthians 13:3
**Q1. What do the believers seek as a proof of Christ speaking in Paul?**
**Answer:** A proof of Christ speaking in me.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:3

**Q2. How is the speaking of Christ in Paul described?**
**Answer:** Which to you-ward is not weak, but is mighty in you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:3

**Q3. Is the speaking of Christ in Paul weak toward the believers?**
**Answer:** No, it is not weak, but is mighty in you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:3

### 2 Corinthians 13:4
**Q1. How was Christ crucified?**
**Answer:** Through weakness.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:4

**Q2. By whose power does Christ live?**
**Answer:** He liveth by the power of God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:4

**Q3. How will the apostles live with Christ?**
**Answer:** We shall live with him by the power of God toward you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:4

### 2 Corinthians 13:5
**Q1. What does Paul tell the believers to do?**
**Answer:** Examine yourselves, whether ye be in the faith.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:5

**Q2. What should they prove?**
**Answer:** Prove your own selves.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:5

**Q3. Who should be in the believers if they are not reprobates?**
**Answer:** Jesus Christ is in you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:5

### 2 Corinthians 13:6
**Q1. What does Paul trust regarding himself and others?**
**Answer:** I trust that ye shall know that we are not reprobates.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:6

**Q2. Does Paul believe the believers will know he is not a reprobate?**
**Answer:** Yes, I trust that ye shall know.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:6

**Q3. What is the word used for those who are not as they should be?**
**Answer:** Reprobates.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:6

### 2 Corinthians 13:7
**Q1. What does Paul pray to God that the believers do not do?**
**Answer:** Now I pray to God that ye do no evil.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:7

**Q2. Why does Paul pray that they do no evil?**
**Answer:** Not that we should appear approved, but that ye should do that which is honest.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:7

**Q3. What should the believers do instead of evil?**
**Answer:** Do that which is honest.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:7

### 2 Corinthians 13:8
**Q1. What can the apostles do nothing against?**
**Answer:** The truth.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:8

**Q2. For what can they do things?**
**Answer:** But for the truth.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:8

**Q3. What is the relationship between the apostles and the truth?**
**Answer:** We can do nothing against the truth, but for the truth.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:8

### 2 Corinthians 13:9
**Q1. When are the apostles glad?**
**Answer:** When we are weak, and ye are strong.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:9

**Q2. What else do the apostles wish for the believers?**
**Answer:** Even your perfection.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:9

**Q3. What is the condition for the apostles' gladness?**
**Answer:** When we are weak, and ye are strong.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:9

### 2 Corinthians 13:10
**Q1. Why does Paul write these things while absent?**
**Answer:** Lest being present I should use sharpness.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:10

**Q2. According to what power does Paul use sharpness?**
**Answer:** According to the power which the Lord hath given me.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:10

**Q3. For what purpose is the sharpness used?**
**Answer:** To edification, and not to destruction.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:10

### 2 Corinthians 13:11
**Q1. What are the final farewells and instructions to the brethren?**
**Answer:** Be perfect, be of good comfort, be of one mind, live in peace.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:11

**Q2. Who will be with the believers if they follow these instructions?**
**Answer:** The God of love and peace shall be with you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:11

**Q3. List three of the instructions in this verse.**
**Answer:** Be perfect, be of good comfort, and be of one mind.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:11

### 2 Corinthians 13:12
**Q1. How should the believers greet one another?**
**Answer:** With an holy kiss.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:12

**Q2. What is the greeting mentioned?**
**Answer:** An holy kiss.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:12

**Q3. To whom is the greeting directed?**
**Answer:** One another.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:12

### 2 Corinthians 13:13
**Q1. Who salutes the believers?**
**Answer:** All the saints.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:13

**Q2. Who are the ones saluting?**
**Answer:** All the saints.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:13

**Q3. What is the act being performed?**
**Answer:** Salute you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:13

### 2 Corinthians 13:14
**Q1. What are the three blessings mentioned in the final verse?**
**Answer:** The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the communion of the Holy Ghost.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:14

**Q2. Who is associated with grace?**
**Answer:** The Lord Jesus Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:14

**Q3. Who is associated with love and communion?**
**Answer:** The love of God, and the communion of the Holy Ghost.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 13:14
