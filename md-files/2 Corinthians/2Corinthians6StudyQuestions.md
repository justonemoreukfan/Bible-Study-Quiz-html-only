# 2 Corinthians 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Corinthians 6:1
**Q1. As workers together with him, what do the apostles beseech the believers?**
**Answer:** That ye receive not the grace of God in vain.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:1

**Q2. With whom are the apostles workers together?**
**Answer:** With him [God].
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:1

**Q3. What should not be received in vain?**
**Answer:** The grace of God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:1

### 2 Corinthians 6:2
**Q1. When did the Lord say he heard the speaker?**
**Answer:** In a time accepted.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:2

**Q2. In what day has the Lord succoured the speaker?**
**Answer:** In the day of salvation.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:2

**Q3. What is the present state of the time and day?**
**Answer:** Behold, now is the accepted time; behold, now is the day of salvation.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:2

### 2 Corinthians 6:3
**Q1. In what should the apostles give no offence?**
**Answer:** Giving no offence in any thing.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:3

**Q2. Why should they give no offence?**
**Answer:** That the ministry be not blamed.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:3

**Q3. What is the risk if they give offence?**
**Answer:** That the ministry be blamed.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:3

### 2 Corinthians 6:4
**Q1. In what things do the apostles approve themselves as ministers of God?**
**Answer:** In much patience, in afflictions, in necessities, in distresses.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:4

**Q2. What is the goal of approving themselves in these things?**
**Answer:** To be approved as the ministers of God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:4

**Q3. List two of the conditions mentioned in this verse.**
**Answer:** Afflictions and necessities.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:4

### 2 Corinthians 6:5
**Q1. List the types of hardship mentioned in this verse.**
**Answer:** In stripes, in imprisonments, in tumults, in labours, in watchings, in fastings.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:5

**Q2. What is the status of the apostles regarding imprisonments?**
**Answer:** In imprisonments.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:5

**Q3. What is the status of the apostles regarding fastings?**
**Answer:** In fastings.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:5

### 2 Corinthians 6:6
**Q1. By what means do the apostles approve themselves?**
**Answer:** By pureness, by knowledge, by long suffering, by kindness, by the Holy Ghost, by love unfeigned.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:6

**Q2. What kind of love is mentioned?**
**Answer:** Love unfeigned.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:6

**Q3. By whose power do they approve themselves?**
**Answer:** By the Holy Ghost.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:6

### 2 Corinthians 6:7
**Q1. By what means do the apostles approve themselves in this verse?**
**Answer:** By the word of truth, by the power of God, by the armour of righteousness on the right hand and on the left.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:7

**Q2. What is the armour of righteousness located?**
**Answer:** On the right hand and on the left.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:7

**Q3. By whose power are they approved?**
**Answer:** By the power of God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:7

### 2 Corinthians 6:8
**Q1. By what combination of things are they approved?**
**Answer:** By honour and dishonour, by evil report and good report.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:8

**Q2. How are they described despite being deceivers?**
**Answer:** As deceivers, and yet true.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:8

**Q3. What reports are mentioned?**
**Answer:** Evil report and good report.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:8

### 2 Corinthians 6:9
**Q1. How are the apostles described in terms of being known?**
**Answer:** As unknown, and yet well known.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:9

**Q2. What is said about their life?**
**Answer:** As dying, and, behold, we live.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:9

**Q3. What is said about being chastened?**
**Answer:** As chastened, and not killed.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:9

### 2 Corinthians 6:10
**Q1. How are the apostles described in terms of sorrow and rejoicing?**
**Answer:** As sorrowful, yet alway rejoicing.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:10

**Q2. How are they described in terms of poverty and richness?**
**Answer:** As poor, yet making many rich.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:10

**Q3. How are they described in terms of possession?**
**Answer:** As having nothing, and yet possessing all things.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:10

### 2 Corinthians 6:11
**Q1. To whom is Paul speaking in this verse?**
**Answer:** O ye Corinthians.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:11

**Q2. What is the state of Paul's mouth toward them?**
**Answer:** Our mouth is open unto you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:11

**Q3. What is the state of Paul's heart toward them?**
**Answer:** Our heart is enlarged.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:11

### 2 Corinthians 6:12
**Q1. Where are the believers straitened?**
**Answer:** Ye are straitened in your own bowels.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:12

**Q2. Are the believers straitened in the apostles?**
**Answer:** Ye are not straitened in us.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:12

**Q3. What does "straitened in your own bowels" mean?**
**Answer:** That they are limited by their own feelings or heart.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:12

### 2 Corinthians 6:13
**Q1. To whom is Paul speaking as to his children?**
**Answer:** To the Corinthians.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:13

**Q2. What does Paul ask the believers to do in return?**
**Answer:** Be ye also enlarged.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:13

**Q3. Why does he ask them to be enlarged?**
**Answer:** For a recompence in the same.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:13

### 2 Corinthians 6:14
**Q1. What does Paul warn against regarding unbelievers?**
**Answer:** Be ye not unequally yoked together with unbelievers.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:14

**Q2. What fellowship does righteousness have with unrighteousness?**
**Answer:** What fellowship hath righteousness with unrighteousness?
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:14

**Q3. What communion does light have with darkness?**
**Answer:** What communion hath light with darkness?
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:14

### 2 Corinthians 6:15
**Q1. What concord does Christ have with Belial?**
**Answer:** What concord hath Christ with Belial?
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:15

**Q2. What part does he that believeth have with an infidel?**
**Answer:** What part hath he that believeth with an infidel?
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:15

**Q3. Who is compared to Christ in this verse?**
**Answer:** Belial.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:15

### 2 Corinthians 6:16
**Q1. What agreement does the temple of God have with idols?**
**Answer:** What agreement hath the temple of God with idols?
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:16

**Q2. Who are the temple of the living God?**
**Answer:** Ye [the believers].
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:16

**Q3. What has God said he will do for the temple of the living God?**
**Answer:** I will dwell in them, and walk in them; and I will be their God, and they shall be my people.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:16

### 2 Corinthians 6:17
**Q1. What does the Lord command the believers to do regarding others?**
**Answer:** Come out from among them, and be ye separate.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:17

**Q2. What should they NOT touch?**
**Answer:** Touch not the unclean thing.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:17

**Q3. What is the promise if they separate?**
**Answer:** I will receive you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:17

### 2 Corinthians 6:18
**Q1. What will the Lord be to the believers?**
**Answer:** A Father.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:18

**Q2. What will the believers be to the Lord?**
**Answer:** My sons and daughters.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:18

**Q3. Who is the one speaking in this verse?**
**Answer:** The Lord Almighty.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 6:18
