# 2 Corinthians 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Corinthians 5:1
**Q1. What is the "earthly house of this tabernacle"?**
**Answer:** Our earthly house of this tabernacle.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:1

**Q2. What happens if the earthly house is dissolved?**
**Answer:** We have a building of God, an house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:1

**Q3. Where is the building of God located?**
**Answer:** Eternal in the heavens.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:1

### 2 Corinthians 5:2
**Q1. In what do the apostles groan?**
**Answer:** In this we groan.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:2

**Q2. What is the earnest desire of the apostles?**
**Answer:** Earnestly desiring to be clothed upon with our house which is from heaven.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:2

**Q3. From where is the desired house?**
**Answer:** From heaven.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:2

### 2 Corinthians 5:3
**Q1. What is the condition mentioned if they are clothed?**
**Answer:** If so be that being clothed we shall not be found naked.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:3

**Q2. What is the concern about being clothed?**
**Answer:** That we shall not be found naked.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:3

**Q3. What is the relationship between being clothed and being found naked?**
**Answer:** That being clothed we shall not be found naked.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:3

### 2 Corinthians 5:4
**Q1. Why do those in this tabernacle groan?**
**Answer:** Being burdened.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:4

**Q2. What is NOT their desire?**
**Answer:** Not for that we would be unclothed.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:4

**Q3. What is their desire, and why?**
**Answer:** But clothed upon, that mortality might be swallowed up of life.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:4

### 2 Corinthians 5:5
**Q1. Who has wrought them for the selfsame thing?**
**Answer:** God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:5

**Q2. What has God given to them?**
**Answer:** The earnest of the Spirit.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:5

**Q3. What is the earnest of the Spirit?**
**Answer:** The earnest of the Spirit.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:5

### 2 Corinthians 5:6
**Q1. Why are the apostles always confident?**
**Answer:** Knowing that, whilst we are at home in the body, we are absent from the Lord.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:6

**Q2. What is the state of those who are at home in the body?**
**Answer:** We are absent from the Lord.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:6

**Q3. What is the result of this knowledge?**
**Answer:** Therefore we are always confident.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:6

### 2 Corinthians 5:7
**Q1. How do the apostles walk?**
**Answer:** For we walk by faith, not by sight.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:7

**Q2. By what means do they walk?**
**Answer:** By faith.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:7

**Q3. What do they NOT walk by?**
**Answer:** Not by sight.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:7

### 2 Corinthians 5:8
**Q1. What are the apostles confident and willing about?**
**Answer:** Willing rather to be absent from the body, and to be present with the Lord.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:8

**Q2. Where do they want to be present?**
**Answer:** With the Lord.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:8

**Q3. What must happen for them to be present with the Lord?**
**Answer:** To be absent from the body.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:8

### 2 Corinthians 5:9
**Q1. Why do the apostles labour?**
**Answer:** That, whether present or absent, we may be accepted of him.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:9

**Q2. What is the goal of their labour?**
**Answer:** That we may be accepted of him.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:9

**Q3. Does it matter if they are present or absent?**
**Answer:** No, whether present or absent.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:9

### 2 Corinthians 5:10
**Q1. Before whose judgment seat must all appear?**
**Answer:** The judgment seat of Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:10

**Q2. What will every one receive?**
**Answer:** The things done in his body.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:10

**Q3. On what basis will they receive these things?**
**Answer:** According to that he hath done, whether it be good or bad.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:10

### 2 Corinthians 5:11
**Q1. Because of what do the apostles persuade men?**
**Answer:** Knowing therefore the terror of the Lord.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:11

**Q2. To whom are they made manifest?**
**Answer:** Made manifest unto God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:11

**Q3. Where else does Paul trust they are made manifest?**
**Answer:** In your consciences.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:11

### 2 Corinthians 5:12
**Q1. Why does Paul not commend himself again?**
**Answer:** But give you occasion to glory on our behalf.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:12

**Q2. What will the believers have to answer?**
**Answer:** That ye may have somewhat to answer them which glory in appearance, and not in heart.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:12

**Q3. Who are those whom the believers will answer?**
**Answer:** Them which glory in appearance, and not in heart.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:12

### 2 Corinthians 5:13
**Q1. To whom is it if the apostles are beside themselves?**
**Answer:** It is to God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:13

**Q2. For whose cause are they sober?**
**Answer:** It is for your cause.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:13

**Q3. What are the two states mentioned in this verse?**
**Answer:** Beside themselves and sober.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:13

### 2 Corinthians 5:14
**Q1. What constraineth the apostles?**
**Answer:** The love of Christ constraineth us.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:14

**Q2. What is the judgment regarding one who died for all?**
**Answer:** If one died for all, then were all dead.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:14

**Q3. Who is the one who died for all?**
**Answer:** Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:14

### 2 Corinthians 5:15
**Q1. Why did Christ die for all?**
**Answer:** That they which live should not henceforth live unto themselves, but unto him which died for them, and rose again.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:15

**Q2. To whom should the living not henceforth live?**
**Answer:** Unto themselves.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:15

**Q3. To whom should the living live?**
**Answer:** Unto him which died for them, and rose again.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:15

### 2 Corinthians 5:16
**Q1. How do the apostles henceforth know no man?**
**Answer:** Henceforth know we no man after the flesh.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:16

**Q2. How had they known Christ before?**
**Answer:** Yea, though we have known Christ after the flesh.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:16

**Q3. How do they know Christ now?**
**Answer:** Yet now henceforth know we him no more [after the flesh].
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:16

### 2 Corinthians 5:17
**Q1. What happens if any man be in Christ?**
**Answer:** He is a new creature.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:17

**Q2. What has passed away?**
**Answer:** Old things are passed away.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:17

**Q3. What is the state of all things now?**
**Answer:** Behold, all things are become new.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:17

### 2 Corinthians 5:18
**Q1. From whom are all things?**
**Answer:** All things are of God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:18

**Q2. How has God reconciled us to himself?**
**Answer:** By Jesus Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:18

**Q3. What ministry has God given to the apostles?**
**Answer:** The ministry of reconciliation.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:18

### 2 Corinthians 5:19
**Q1. What was God doing in Christ?**
**Answer:** Reconciling the world unto himself.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:19

**Q2. What did God NOT impute to the world?**
**Answer:** Not imputing their trespasses unto them.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:19

**Q3. What has God committed to the apostles?**
**Answer:** The word of reconciliation.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:19

### 2 Corinthians 5:20
**Q1. What are the apostles now?**
**Answer:** Ambassadors for Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:20

**Q2. How is God beseeching the world?**
**Answer:** As though God did beseech you by us.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:20

**Q3. What is the prayer of the apostles to the believers?**
**Answer:** We pray you in Christ's stead, be ye reconciled to God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:20

### 2 Corinthians 5:21
**Q1. For what purpose was He made to be sin for us?**
**Answer:** That we might be made the righteousness of God in him.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:21

**Q2. Who is the one who knew no sin?**
**Answer:** He [Christ].
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:21

**Q3. What was He made to be for us?**
**Answer:** He hath made him to be sin for us.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:21
