# 2 Corinthians 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Corinthians 9:1
**Q1. What is it superfluous for Paul to write to the believers regarding?**
**Answer:** The ministering to the saints.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:1

**Q2. To whom are the believers ministering?**
**Answer:** To the saints.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:1

**Q3. What is the act of ministering called in this verse?**
**Answer:** The ministering to the saints.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:1

### 2 Corinthians 9:2
**Q1. What does Paul boast of the believers to those of Macedonia?**
**Answer:** The forwardness of your mind.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:2

**Q2. How long has Achaia been ready?**
**Answer:** A year ago.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:2

**Q3. What has the zeal of the believers provoked?**
**Answer:** Your zeal hath provoked very many.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:2

### 2 Corinthians 9:3
**Q1. Why did Paul send the brethren?**
**Answer:** Lest our boasting of you should be in vain in this behalf.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:3

**Q2. What does Paul want the believers to be?**
**Answer:** That ye may be ready.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:3

**Q3. In what behalf was the boasting made?**
**Answer:** In this behalf [the ministering to the saints].
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:3

### 2 Corinthians 9:4
**Q1. What does Paul fear if the Macedonians come with him?**
**Answer:** And find you unprepared.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:4

**Q2. Who should be ashamed if the believers are unprepared?**
**Answer:** We (that we say not, ye) should be ashamed.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:4

**Q3. In what kind of boasting was this shame potential?**
**Answer:** In this same confident boasting.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:4

### 2 Corinthians 9:5
**Q1. Why did Paul think it necessary to exhort the brethren?**
**Answer:** That they would go before unto you, and make up beforehand your bounty.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:5

**Q2. How should the bounty be ready?**
**Answer:** As a matter of bounty, and not as of covetousness.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:5

**Q3. Did the believers have notice of the bounty before?**
**Answer:** Whereof ye had notice before.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:5

### 2 Corinthians 9:6
**Q1. What will the one who soweth sparingly reap?**
**Answer:** Shall reap also sparingly.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:6

**Q2. What will the one who soweth bountifully reap?**
**Answer:** Shall reap also bountifully.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:6

**Q3. What is the principle of reaping and sowing mentioned here?**
**Answer:** He which soweth sparingly shall reap also sparingly; and he which soweth bountifully shall reap also bountifully.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:6

### 2 Corinthians 9:7
**Q1. How should a man give?**
**Answer:** Every man according as he purposeth in his heart, so let him give.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:7

**Q2. How should he NOT give?**
**Answer:** Not grudgingly, or of necessity.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:7

**Q3. Whom does God love?**
**Answer:** God loveth a cheerful giver.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:7

### 2 Corinthians 9:8
**Q1. What is God able to make abound toward the believers?**
**Answer:** All grace.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:8

**Q2. What is the result of having all sufficiency in all things?**
**Answer:** That ye, always having all sufficiency in all things, may abound to every good work.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:8

**Q3. What is the goal of having all sufficiency?**
**Answer:** To abound to every good work.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:8

### 2 Corinthians 9:9
**Q1. What is written regarding those who give to the poor?**
**Answer:** He hath dispersed abroad; he hath given to the poor: his righteousness remaineth for ever.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:9

**Q2. What does the one who gives to the poor do?**
**Answer:** He hath dispersed abroad; he hath given to the poor.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:9

**Q3. What remains for ever?**
**Answer:** His righteousness remaineth for ever.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:9

### 2 Corinthians 9:10
**Q1. Who ministereth seed to the sower?**
**Answer:** He [God].
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:10

**Q2. What else does He minister for the believers' food?**
**Answer:** Minister bread for your food.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:10

**Q3. What does He do with the seed sown and the fruits of righteousness?**
**Answer:** Multiply your seed sown, and increase the fruits of your righteousness.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:10

### 2 Corinthians 9:11
**Q1. To what does the enrichment in every thing lead?**
**Answer:** To all bountifulness.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:11

**Q2. What does this bountifulness cause through the apostles?**
**Answer:** Causeth through us thanksgiving to God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:11

**Q3. Who is the one being thanked in this verse?**
**Answer:** God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:11

### 2 Corinthians 9:12
**Q1. What does the administration of this service supply?**
**Answer:** Supplieth the want of the saints.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:12

**Q2. What else is the administration of this service abundant in?**
**Answer:** Abundant also by many thanksgivings unto God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:12

**Q3. To whom are the thanksgivings directed?**
**Answer:** Unto God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:12

### 2 Corinthians 9:13
**Q1. Why do people glorify God for the believers' subjection?**
**Answer:** For your professed subjection unto the gospel of Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:13

**Q2. For what else do they glorify God?**
**Answer:** For your liberal distribution unto them, and unto all men.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:13

**Q3. To whom was the liberal distribution made?**
**Answer:** Unto them, and unto all men.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:13

### 2 Corinthians 9:14
**Q1. What are the people doing for the believers?**
**Answer:** By their prayer for you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:14

**Q2. What are they longing for in the believers?**
**Answer:** Long after you for the exceeding grace of God in you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:14

**Q3. What is the exceeding grace of God in the believers?**
**Answer:** The exceeding grace of God in you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:14

### 2 Corinthians 9:15
**Q1. What is the gift from God?**
**Answer:** His unspeakable gift.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:15

**Q2. To whom is thanks given?**
**Answer:** Thanks be unto God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:15

**Q3. What is the nature of the gift?**
**Answer:** Unspeakable.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 9:15
