# 2 Corinthians 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Corinthians 10:1
**Q1. By what does Paul beseech the believers?**
**Answer:** By the meekness and gentleness of Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:1

**Q2. How is Paul described when he is present?**
**Answer:** In presence am base among you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:1

**Q3. How is Paul described when he is absent?**
**Answer:** Being absent am bold toward you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:1

### 2 Corinthians 10:2
**Q1. What does Paul beseech the believers?**
**Answer:** That I may not be bold when I am present with that confidence, wherewith I think to be bold against some.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:2

**Q2. Who are those against whom Paul thinks he might be bold?**
**Answer:** Some, which think of us as if we walked according to the flesh.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:2

**Q3. How do some think the apostles walk?**
**Answer:** As if we walked according to the flesh.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:2

### 2 Corinthians 10:3
**Q1. How do the apostles walk?**
**Answer:** Though we walk in the flesh, we do not war after the flesh.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:3

**Q2. Do they war after the flesh?**
**Answer:** No, we do not war after the flesh.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:3

**Q3. In what do they walk?**
**Answer:** In the flesh.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:3

### 2 Corinthians 10:4
**Q1. What are the weapons of our warfare?**
**Answer:** Not carnal.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:4

**Q2. Through whom are the weapons of our warfare mighty?**
**Answer:** Mighty through God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:4

**Q3. What is the purpose of these weapons?**
**Answer:** To the pulling down of strong holds.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:4

### 2 Corinthians 10:5
**Q1. What are the weapons casting down?**
**Answer:** Casting down imaginations, and every high thing that exalteth itself against the knowledge of God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:5

**Q2. What is being brought into captivity?**
**Answer:** Bringing into captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:5

**Q3. To whose obedience are the thoughts brought?**
**Answer:** To the obedience of Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:5

### 2 Corinthians 10:6
**Q1. What does Paul have in a readiness to revenge?**
**Answer:** All disobedience.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:6

**Q2. When will he revenge all disobedience?**
**Answer:** When your obedience is fulfilled.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:6

**Q3. What is the condition for the revenge of disobedience?**
**Answer:** When your obedience is fulfilled.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:6

### 2 Corinthians 10:7
**Q1. How should the believers look on things?**
**Answer:** Do ye look on things after the outward appearance?
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:7

**Q2. If a man trust to himself that he is Christ's, what should he do?**
**Answer:** Let him of himself think this again.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:7

**Q3. What should he think regarding himself and others?**
**Answer:** That, as he is Christ's, even so are we Christ's.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:7

### 2 Corinthians 10:8
**Q1. For what would Paul boast?**
**Answer:** Of our authority, which the Lord hath given us for edification, and not for your destruction.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:8

**Q2. Who gave the apostles their authority?**
**Answer:** The Lord.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:8

**Q3. What is the purpose of the authority?**
**Answer:** For edification, and not for your destruction.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:8

### 2 Corinthians 10:9
**Q1. Why would Paul not be ashamed to boast of his authority?**
**Answer:** That I may not seem as if I would terrify you by letters.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:9

**Q2. What is the risk if he does not boast?**
**Answer:** That he may seem as if he would terrify you by letters.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:9

**Q3. How does Paul avoid terrifying the believers by letters?**
**Answer:** By boasting of the authority the Lord gave for edification.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:9

### 2 Corinthians 10:10
**Q1. What do some say about Paul's letters?**
**Answer:** For his letters, say they, are weighty and powerful.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:10

**Q2. What do some say about Paul's bodily presence?**
**Answer:** His bodily presence is weak.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:10

**Q3. What do some say about Paul's speech?**
**Answer:** His speech contemptible.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:10

### 2 Corinthians 10:11
**Q1. What should such an one think?**
**Answer:** That, such as we are in word by letters when we are absent, such will we be also in deed when we are present.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:11

**Q2. How are the apostles described in word by letters when absent?**
**Answer:** Such as we are in word by letters when we are absent.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:11

**Q3. How will they be in deed when present?**
**Answer:** Such will we be also in deed when we are present.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:11

### 2 Corinthians 10:12
**Q1. Why does Paul dare not make himself of the number?**
**Answer:** For we dare not make ourselves of the number, or compare ourselves with some that commend themselves.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:12

**Q2. How do those who commend themselves measure themselves?**
**Answer:** They measuring themselves by themselves, and comparing themselves among themselves.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:12

**Q3. Are those who measure themselves by themselves wise?**
**Answer:** No, they are not wise.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:12

### 2 Corinthians 10:13
**Q1. What does Paul avoid boasting of?**
**Answer:** Boast of things without our measure.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:13

**Q2. What is the basis for Paul's boasting?**
**Answer:** According to the measure of the rule which God hath distributed to us.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:13

**Q3. What is the range of this measure?**
**Answer:** A measure to reach even unto you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:13

### 2 Corinthians 10:14
**Q1. Why does Paul say he does not stretch himself beyond his measure?**
**Answer:** For we stretch not ourselves beyond our measure, as though we reached not unto you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:14

**Q2. How far have they come in preaching the gospel of Christ?**
**Answer:** We are come as far as to you also in preaching the gospel of Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:14

**Q3. Does Paul think they reached unto the believers?**
**Answer:** Yes, for they have come as far as to you also.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:14

### 2 Corinthians 10:15
**Q1. What does Paul avoid boasting of in this verse?**
**Answer:** Not boasting of things without our measure, that is, of other men's labours.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:15

**Q2. What is the hope of the apostles?**
**Answer:** When your faith is increased, that we shall be enlarged by you according to our rule abundantly.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:15

**Q3. What is the condition for the apostles to be enlarged by the believers?**
**Answer:** When your faith is increased.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 10:1 la 15
