# 2 Corinthians 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Corinthians 1:1
**Q1. Who is the apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God?**
**Answer:** Paul.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:1

**Q2. Who is mentioned as "our brother" alongside Paul?**
**Answer:** Timothy.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:1

**Q3. To whom is this letter addressed?**
**Answer:** Unto the church of God which is at Corinth, with all the saints which are in all Achaia.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:1

### 2 Corinthians 1:2
**Q1. What two things does Paul wish for the recipients?**
**Answer:** Grace and peace.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:2

**Q2. From whom does the grace and peace come?**
**Answer:** From God our Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:2

**Q3. Who is mentioned as the source of peace?**
**Answer:** God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:2

### 2 Corinthians 1:3
**Q1. Who is blessed in this verse?**
**Answer:** God, even the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:3

**Q2. What title is given to the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ regarding mercies?**
**Answer:** The Father of mercies.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:3

**Q3. What is God described as in relation to comfort?**
**Answer:** The God of all comfort.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:3

### 2 Corinthians 1:4
**Q1. In what does God comfort us?**
**Answer:** In all our tribulation.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:4

**Q2. Why does God comfort us in our tribulation?**
**Answer:** That we may be able to comfort them which are in any trouble.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:4

**Q3. By what means are we able to comfort others?**
**Answer:** By the comfort wherewith we ourselves are comforted of God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:4

### 2 Corinthians 1:5
**Q1. What abounds in us?**
**Answer:** The sufferings of Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:5

**Q2. What else abounds by Christ?**
**Answer:** Our consolation.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:5

**Q3. How does the consolation relate to the sufferings of Christ?**
**Answer:** As the sufferings of Christ abound in us, so our consolation also aboundeth by Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:5

### 2 Corinthians 1:6
**Q1. For whose consolation and salvation is it when the apostles are afflicted?**
**Answer:** For your [the Corinthians'] consolation and salvation.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:6

**Q2. How is this consolation and salvation effectual?**
**Answer:** In the enduring of the same sufferings which we also suffer.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:6

**Q3. What is the result if the apostles are comforted?**
**Answer:** It is for your consolation and salvation.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:6

### 2 Corinthians 1:7
**Q1. How is the hope of the Corinthians described?**
**Answer:** Stedfast.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:7

**Q2. Of what are the Corinthians partakers?**
**Answer:** Of the sufferings.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:7

**Q3. What shall the Corinthians also be partakers of?**
**Answer:** Of the consolation.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:7

### 2 Corinthians 1:8
**Q1. Where did the trouble that came to the apostles occur?**
**Answer:** In Asia.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:8

**Q2. How were they pressed in this trouble?**
**Answer:** Out of measure, above strength.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:8

**Q3. To what extent did they despair?**
**Answer:** Insomuch that we despaired even of life.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:8

### 2 Corinthians 1:9
**Q1. What did the apostles have in themselves?**
**Answer:** The sentence of death.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:9

**Q2. Why did they have the sentence of death in themselves?**
**Answer:** That we should not trust in ourselves, but in God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:9

**Q3. What is God described as doing in this verse?**
**Answer:** God which raiseth the dead.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:9

### 2 Corinthians 1:10
**Q1. From what did God deliver the apostles?**
**Answer:** From so great a death.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:10

**Q2. What is God doing currently in relation to their deliverance?**
**Answer:** Doth deliver.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:10

**Q3. In whom do they trust for future deliverance?**
**Answer:** In whom we trust that he will yet deliver us.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:10

### 2 Corinthians 1:11
**Q1. How are the believers helping the apostles?**
**Answer:** Helping together by prayer for us.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:11

**Q2. What was bestowed upon the apostles by the means of many persons?**
**Answer:** A gift.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:11

**Q3. What is the goal of the believers' prayers?**
**Answer:** That thanks may be given by many on our behalf.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:11

### 2 Corinthians 1:12
**Q1. What is the source of Paul's rejoicing?**
**Answer:** The testimony of our conscience.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:12

**Q2. In what manner have they had their conversation in the world?**
**Answer:** In simplicity and godly sincerity.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:12

**Q3. By what means have they had their conversation, rather than fleshly wisdom?**
**Answer:** By the grace of God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:12

### 2 Corinthians 1:13
**Q1. What is Paul's claim about the things he writes to the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** We write none other things unto you, that what ye read or acknowledge.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:13

**Q2. What does Paul trust the believers will do?**
**Answer:** I trust ye shall acknowledge even to the end.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:13

**Q3. What are the believers encouraged to acknowledge?**
**Answer:** What they read.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:13

### 2 Corinthians 1:14
**Q1. In what part have the believers acknowledged the apostles?**
**Answer:** That we are your rejoicing.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:14

**Q2. How are the Corinthians described in relation to the apostles?**
**Answer:** Ye also are our's.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:14

**Q3. When will this mutual rejoicing occur?**
**Answer:** In the day of the Lord Jesus.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:14

### 2 Corinthians 1:15
**Q1. What was Paul's confidence regarding visiting the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** I was minded to come unto you before.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:15

**Q2. Why did Paul want to come to them?**
**Answer:** That ye might have a second benefit.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:15

**Q3. How many benefits did Paul intend for them to have?**
**Answer:** A second benefit.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:15

### 2 Corinthians 1:16
**Q1. Where did Paul intend to pass by into after visiting Corinth?**
**Answer:** Macedonia.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:16

**Q2. From where did he intend to come back to Corinth?**
**Answer:** Out of Macedonia.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:16

**Q3. Where did he intend to be brought on his way toward?**
**Answer:** Judaea.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:16

### 2 Corinthians 1:17
**Q1. What question does Paul ask about his intentions?**
**Answer:** When I therefore was thus minded, did I use lightness?
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:17

**Q2. According to what does Paul ask if he purposed?**
**Answer:** Do I purpose according to the flesh.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:17

**Q3. What would be the result if he purposed according to the flesh?**
**Answer:** That with me there should be yea yea, and nay nay.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:17

### 2 Corinthians 1:18
**Q1. In whom does Paul base the truth of his word?**
**Answer:** As God is true.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:18

**Q2. What does Paul say about his word toward the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** Our word toward you was not yea and nay.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:18

**Q3. What was the nature of his word toward them?**
**Answer:** Not yea and nay.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:18

### 2 Corinthians 1:19
**Q1. Who is the Son of God mentioned in this verse?**
**Answer:** Jesus Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:19

**Q2. Who preached Jesus Christ among the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** By us, even by me and Silvanus and Timotheus.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:19

**Q3. What was the nature of the preaching about Jesus Christ?**
**Answer:** Was not yea and nay, but in him was yea.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:19

### 2 Corinthians 1:20
**Q1. What is the status of all the promises of God in Christ?**
**Answer:** In him are yea.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:20

**Q2. Besides being "yea", what else are the promises of God in Christ?**
**Answer:** In him Amen.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:20

**Q3. For whose glory are these promises?**
**Answer:** Unto the glory of God by us.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:20

### 2 Corinthians 1:21
**Q1. Who is the one who stablisheth the apostles with the believers in Christ?**
**Answer:** God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:21

**Q2. What else has God done for the apostles?**
**Answer:** Hath anointed us.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:21

**Q3. In whom does God stablisheth them?**
**Answer:** In Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:21

### 2 Corinthians 1:22
**Q1. What has God done to the apostles besides anointing them?**
**Answer:** Who hath also sealed us.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:22

**Q2. What has God given in their hearts?**
**Answer:** The earnest of the Spirit.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:22

**Q3. Where did God give the earnest of the Spirit?**
**Answer:** In our hearts.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:22

### 2 Corinthians 1:23
**Q1. Whom does Paul call as a record upon his soul?**
**Answer:** I call God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:23

**Q2. Why did Paul not come as yet unto Corinth?**
**Answer:** To spare you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:23

**Q3. What is the reason given for Paul's delay in visiting Corinth?**
**Answer:** To spare them.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:23

### 2 Corinthians 1:24
**Q1. Does Paul claim to have dominion over the believers' faith?**
**Answer:** Not for that we have dominion over your faith.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:24

**Q2. What are the apostles instead of having dominion?**
**Answer:** But are helpers of your joy.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:24

**Q3. By what means do the believers stand?**
**Answer:** For by faith ye stand.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 1:24
