# 2 Corinthians 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Corinthians 8:1
**Q1. What was bestowed on the churches of Macedonia?**
**Answer:** The grace of God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:1

**Q2. To whom is Paul making the believers aware of this?**
**Answer:** To the brethren.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:1

**Q3. What did Paul do regarding the grace of God in Macedonia?**
**Answer:** We do you to wit of the grace of God bestowed on the churches of Macedonia.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:1

### 2 Corinthians 8:2
**Q1. In what were the Macedonian churches in a great trial?**
**Answer:** In a great trial of affliction.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:2

**Q2. What abounded in their great trial of affliction?**
**Answer:** The abundance of their joy and their deep poverty.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:2

**Q3. To what did their joy and poverty abound?**
**Answer:** Unto the riches of their liberality.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:2

### 2 Corinthians 8:3
**Q1. To what extent were the Macedonian churches willing to give?**
**Answer:** For to their power, yea, and beyond their power.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:3

**Q2. How were they willing?**
**Answer:** Willing of themselves.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:3

**Q3. What does Paul bear record of?**
**Answer:** That they were willing of themselves, both to their power and beyond their power.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:3

### 2 Corinthians 8:4
**Q1. How did the Macedonian churches pray the apostles?**
**Answer:** With much intreaty.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:4

**Q2. What did they pray the apostles to receive?**
**Answer:** The gift.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:4

**Q3. What fellowship did they ask the apostles to take upon themselves?**
**Answer:** The fellowship of the ministering to the saints.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:4

### 2 Corinthians 8:5
**Q1. Did the Macedonian churches give as the apostles hoped?**
**Answer:** Not as we hoped.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:5

**Q2. Who did they first give their own selves to?**
**Answer:** The Lord.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:5

**Q3. To whom did they give themselves by the will of God?**
**Answer:** Unto us.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:5

### 2 Corinthians 8:6
**Q1. Who did the apostles desire to come and finish the grace in the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** Titus.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:6

**Q2. What did Titus begin in the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** He had begun the same grace.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:6

**Q3. What does Paul want Titus to finish?**
**Answer:** That as he had begun, so he would also finish in you the same grace also.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:6

### 2 Corinthians 8:7
**Q1. In what things do the believers abound?**
**Answer:** In faith, and utterance, and knowledge, and in all diligence, and in your love to us.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:7

**Q2. What should the believers see that they abound in?**
**Answer:** In this grace also.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:7

**Q3. List three of the things the believers abound in.**
**Answer:** Faith, utterance, and knowledge.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:7

### 2 Corinthians 8:8
**Q1. By what does Paul speak in this verse?**
**Answer:** Not by commandment.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:8

**Q2. What is the occasion for Paul speaking?**
**Answer:** Occasion of the forwardness of others.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:8

**Q3. What does Paul wish to prove?**
**Answer:** The sincerity of your love.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:8

### 2 Corinthians 8:9
**Q1. What is the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ?**
**Answer:** That, though he was rich, yet for your sakes he became poor.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:9

**Q2. Why did Jesus Christ become poor?**
**Answer:** That ye through his poverty might be rich.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:9

**Q3. What was the state of Jesus Christ before he became poor?**
**Answer:** He was rich.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:9

### 2 Corinthians 8:10
**Q1. What does Paul give in this verse?**
**Answer:** My advice.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:10

**Q2. Why is this advice expedient for the believers?**
**Answer:** For this is expedient for you, who have begun before.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:10

**Q3. What does Paul advice the believers to do?**
**Answer:** Not only to do, but also to be forward a year ago.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:10

### 2 Corinthians 8:11
**Q1. What should the believers perform?**
**Answer:** The doing of it.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:11

**Q2. What was there a readiness to will?**
**Answer:** A readiness to will.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:11

**Q3. How should the performance be based on what they have?**
**Answer:** That as there was a readiness to will, so there may be a performance also out of that which ye have.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:11

### 2 Corinthians 8:12
**Q1. If there is a willing mind, how is it accepted?**
**Answer:** According to that a man hath.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:12

**Q2. What is NOT the basis for acceptance of a willing mind?**
**Answer:** Not according to that he hath not.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:12

**Q3. What must be present for the gift to be accepted according to what a man has?**
**Answer:** A willing mind.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:12

### 2 Corinthians 8:13
**Q1. What does Paul mean by "other men be eased"?**
**Answer:** That other men be eased.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:13

**Q2. What does Paul NOT want for the believers?**
**Answer:** And ye burdened.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:13

**Q3. What is the goal of the giving?**
**Answer:** To avoid having the believers burdened while others are eased.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:13

### 2 Corinthians 8:14
**Q1. How should the abundance of the believers be used?**
**Answer:** Your abundance may be a supply for their want.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:14

**Q2. What should the abundance of others be used for?**
**Answer:** That their abundance also may be a supply for your want.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:14

**Q3. What is the ultimate goal of this arrangement?**
**Answer:** That there may be equality.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:14

### 2 Corinthians 8:15
**Q1. What is written regarding gathering?**
**Answer:** He that had gathered much had nothing over; and he that had gathered little had no lack.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:15

**Q2. What happened to the one who gathered much?**
**Answer:** Had nothing over.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:15

**Q3. What happened to the one who gathered little?**
**Answer:** Had no lack.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:15

### 2 Corinthians 8:16
**Q1. To whom does Paul give thanks?**
**Answer:** To God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:16

**Q2. What did God put into the heart of Titus?**
**Answer:** The same earnest care for you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:16

**Q3. Who is the one who had the earnest care for the believers?**
**Answer:** Titus.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:16

### 2 Corinthians 8:17
**Q1. Did Titus accept the exhortation?**
**Answer:** Indeed he accepted the exhortation.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:17

**Q2. How did Titus go to the believers?**
**Answer:** Being more forward, of his own accord he went unto you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:17

**Q3. Why did Titus go to the believers?**
**Answer:** Of his own accord.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:17

### 2 Corinthians 8:18
**Q1. Who was sent with Titus?**
**Answer:** The brother.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:18

**Q2. What is said about this brother's praise?**
**Answer:** Whose praise is in the gospel throughout all the churches.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:18

**Q3. Where is the brother's praise known?**
**Answer:** Throughout all the churches.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:18

### 2 Corinthians 8:19
**Q1. Why was the brother chosen by the churches?**
**Answer:** To travel with us with this grace, which is administered by us to the glory of the same Lord, and declaration of your ready mind.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:19

**Q2. What is being administered by the apostles?**
**Answer:** This grace.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:19

**Q3. To whose glory is this grace administered?**
**Answer:** To the glory of the same Lord.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:19

### 2 Corinthians 8:20
**Q1. Why does Paul avoid certain things?**
**Answer:** That no man should blame us in this abundance which is administered by us.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:20

**Q2. What is being administered?**
**Answer:** This abundance.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:20

**Q3. Who might blame the apostles?**
**Answer:** No man.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:20

### 2 Corinthians 8:21
**Q1. What should be provided for in the sight of the Lord?**
**Answer:** Honest things.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:21

**Q2. In whose sight should honest things be provided for?**
**Answer:** Not only in the sight of the Lord, but also in the sight of men.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:21

**Q3. What is the goal of providing honest things?**
**Answer:** To provide for honest things.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:21

### 2 Corinthians 8:22
**Q1. Who was sent with Titus and the other brother?**
**Answer:** Our brother.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:22

**Q2. How has this brother been proved?**
**Answer:** Oftentimes proved diligent in many things.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:22

**Q3. On what confidence does Paul have in this brother?**
**Answer:** Upon the great confidence which I have in you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:22

**Answer:** He is my partner and fellowhelper concerning you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:23

**Q2. Who are the other brethren?**
**Answer:** The messengers of the churches, and the glory of Christ.

**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:23

**Q1. What should the believers shew to the messengers?**
**Answer:** The proof of your love.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:24

**Q2. Before whom should they shew the proof of their love?**
**Answer:** Before the churches.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 8:24

**Q3. What else should they shew?**
