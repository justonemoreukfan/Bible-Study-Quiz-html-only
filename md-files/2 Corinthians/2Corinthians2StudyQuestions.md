# 2 Corinthians 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Corinthians 2:1
**Q1. What did Paul determine with himself regarding his return?**
**Answer:** That I would not come again to you in heaviness.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:1

**Q2. In what state did Paul NOT want to return to the Corinthians?**
**Answer:** In heaviness.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:1

**Q3. What was Paul's determination?**
**Answer:** Not to come again in heaviness.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:1

### 2 Corinthians 2:2
**Q1. What is the concern if Paul makes the believers sorry?**
**Answer:** Who is he then that maketh me glad, but the same which is made sorry by me?
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:2

**Q2. Who would be the one to make Paul glad if he makes the believers sorry?**
**Answer:** The same which is made sorry by him.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:2

**Q3. What emotion does Paul want to avoid causing?**
**Answer:** Making the believers sorry.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:2

### 2 Corinthians 2:3
**Q1. Why did Paul write the same thing to the believers?**
**Answer:** Lest, when I came, I should have sorrow from them of whom I ought to rejoice.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:3

**Q2. From whom did Paul want to avoid having sorrow?**
**Answer:** From them of whom he ought to rejoice.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:3

**Q3. What confidence did Paul have in all the believers?**
**Answer:** That my joy is the joy of you all.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:3

### 2 Corinthians 2:4
**Q1. Under what conditions did Paul write to the believers?**
**Answer:** Out of much affliction and anguish of heart.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:4

**Q2. With what did Paul write to them?**
**Answer:** With many tears.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:4

**Q3. What was the purpose of his letter, and what was it NOT?**
**Answer:** Not that ye should be grieved, but that ye might know the love which I have more abundantly unto you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:4

### 2 Corinthians 2:5
**Q1. What is Paul's view of the grief caused by someone?**
**Answer:** If any have caused grief, he hath not grieved me, but in part.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:5

**Q2. Why does Paul say the grief was only in part?**
**Answer:** That I may not overcharge you all.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:5

**Q3. What does Paul want to avoid doing to all the believers?**
**Answer:** Overcharging them all.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:5

### 2 Corinthians 2:6
**Q1. What is sufficient for the man who caused grief?**
**Answer:** This punishment, which was inflicted of many.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:6

**Q2. Who inflicted the punishment on the man?**
**Answer:** Many.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:6

**Q3. What was the nature of the punishment?**
**Answer:** Punishment which was inflicted of many.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:6

### 2 Corinthians 2:7
**Q1. What should the believers do instead of continuing the punishment?**
**Answer:** Ye ought rather to forgive him, and comfort him.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:7

**Q2. Why should they forgive and comfort him?**
**Answer:** Lest perhaps such a one should be swallowed up with overmuch sorrow.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:7

**Q3. What is the risk if they do not forgive him?**
**Answer:** That he should be swallowed up with overmuch sorrow.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:7

### 2 Corinthians 2:8
**Q1. What does Paul beseech the believers to do?**
**Answer:** That ye would confirm your love toward him.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:8

**Q2. Toward whom should the believers confirm their love?**
**Answer:** Toward him [the man who caused grief].
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:8

**Q3. What action is requested regarding the man?**
**Answer:** Confirm your love toward him.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:8

### 2 Corinthians 2:9
**Q1. For what end did Paul write the letter?**
**Answer:** That I might know the proof of you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:9

**Q2. What proof was Paul looking for?**
**Answer:** Whether ye be obedient in all things.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:9

**Q3. What does "the proof of you" mean in this context?**
**Answer:** Whether the believers are obedient in all things.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:9

### 2 Corinthians 2:10
**Q1. To whom does Paul forgive as the believers forgive?**
**Answer:** To whom ye forgive any thing, I forgive also.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:10

**Q2. For whose sake did Paul forgive?**
**Answer:** For your sakes.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:10

**Q3. In whose person did Paul forgive?**
**Answer:** In the person of Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:10

### 2 Corinthians 2:11
**Q1. Why should they forgive?**
**Answer:** Lest Satan should get an advantage of us.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:11

**Q2. What is the risk if they do not forgive?**
**Answer:** That Satan should get an advantage of us.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:11

**Q3. What does Paul say about our knowledge of Satan's devices?**
**Answer:** For we are not ignorant of his devices.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:11

### 2 Corinthians 2:12
**Q1. Where did Paul go to preach Christ's gospel?**
**Answer:** To Troas.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:12

**Q2. What happened when he arrived at Troas?**
**Answer:** A door was opened unto me of the Lord.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:12

**Q3. Who opened the door for Paul at Troas?**
**Answer:** The Lord.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:12

### 2 Corinthians 2:13
**Q1. Why did Paul have no rest in his spirit at Troas?**
**Answer:** Because I found not Titus my brother.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:13

**Q2. Who was missing that caused Paul unrest?**
**Answer:** Titus.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:13

**Q3. Where did Paul go after taking his leave of those in Troas?**
**Answer:** Into Macedonia.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:13

### 2 Corinthians 2:14
**Q1. To whom does Paul give thanks?**
**Answer:** Thanks be unto God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:14

**Q2. What does God always cause us to do in Christ?**
**Answer:** Always causeth us to triumph in Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:14

**Q3. What does God make manifest by us in every place?**
**Answer:** The savour of his knowledge.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:14

### 2 Corinthians 2:15
**Q1. To whom are the believers a sweet savour of Christ?**
**Answer:** In them that are saved, and in them that perish.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:15

**Q2. What are we unto God?**
**Answer:** A sweet savour of Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:15

**Q3. Who is the recipient of this sweet savour?**
**Answer:** God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:15

### 2 Corinthians 2:16
**Q1. To whom are the believers the savour of death?**
**Answer:** To the one.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:16

**Q2. To whom are they the savour of life?**
**Answer:** To the other.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:16

**Q3. What question does Paul ask at the end of the verse?**
**Answer:** And who is sufficient for these things?
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:16

### 2 Corinthians 2:17
**Q1. How are the believers different from "many" who corrupt the word of God?**
**Answer:** For we are not as many, which corrupt the word of God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:17

**Q2. In what manner do they speak?**
**Answer:** As of sincerity, but as of God, in the sight of God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:17

**Q3. In whom do they speak?**
**Answer:** In Christ.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 2:17
