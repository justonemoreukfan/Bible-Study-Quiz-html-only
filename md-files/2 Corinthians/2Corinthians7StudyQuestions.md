# 2 Corinthians 7 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Corinthians 7:1
**Q1. What should the dearly beloved cleanse themselves from?**
**Answer:** All filthiness of the flesh and spirit.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:1

**Q2. How should they perfect holiness?**
**Answer:** In the fear of God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:1

**Q3. What encourages them to do this?**
**Answer:** Having these promises.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:1

### 2 Corinthians 7:2
**Q1. To whom does Paul say "Receive us"?**
**Answer:** To the Corinthians.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:2

**Q2. Whom does Paul say they have not wronged?**
**Answer:** No man.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:2

**Q3. Whom does Paul say they have not defrauded?**
**Answer:** No man.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:2

### 2 Corinthians 7:3
**Q1. For what purpose does Paul say he does not speak?**
**Answer:** To condemn you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:3

**Q2. What has Paul said before regarding the believers?**
**Answer:** That ye are in our hearts to die and live with you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:3

**Q3. What is the heart's desire mentioned in this verse?**
**Answer:** To die and live with you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:3

### 2 Corinthians 7:4
**Q1. What is great regarding Paul's speech toward the believers?**
**Answer:** Great is my boldness of speech toward you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:4

**Q2. What is great regarding Paul's glorying of them?**
**Answer:** Great is my glorying of you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:4

**Q3. How does Paul feel in all their tribulation?**
**Answer:** I am filled with comfort, I am exceeding joyful.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:4

### 2 Corinthians 7:5
**Q1. Where had the apostles come when their flesh had no rest?**
**Answer:** Into Macedonia.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:5

**Q2. How were they troubled?**
**Answer:** Troubled on every side.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:5

**Q3. What was happening without and within?**
**Answer:** Without were fightings, within were fears.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:5

### 2 Corinthians 7:6
**Q1. Who comforteth those that are cast down?**
**Answer:** God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:6

**Q2. How did God comfort the apostles?**
**Answer:** By the coming of Titus.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:6

**Q3. Who is the one who came to comfort them?**
**Answer:** Titus.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:6

### 2 Corinthians 7:7
**Q1. Was the comfort from Titus' coming only?**
**Answer:** Not by his coming only.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:7

**Q2. How was Titus comforted in the believers?**
**Answer:** By the consolation wherewith he was comforted in you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:7

**Q3. What did Titus tell the apostles that made Paul rejoice more?**
**Answer:** Your earnest desire, your mourning, your fervent mind toward me.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:7

### 2 Corinthians 7:8
**Q1. With what did Paul make the believers sorry?**
**Answer:** With a letter.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:8

**Q2. Does Paul repent of making them sorry?**
**Answer:** I do not repent, though I did repent.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:8

**Q3. For how long did the epistle make them sorry?**
**Answer:** But for a season.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:8

### 2 Corinthians 7:9
**Q1. Why does Paul rejoice?**
**Answer:** Not that ye were made sorry, but that ye sorrowed to repentance.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:9

**Q2. In what manner were they made sorry?**
**Answer:** After a godly manner.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:9

**Q3. What was the result of being made sorry after a godly manner?**
**Answer:** That ye might receive damage by us in nothing.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:9

### 2 Corinthians 7:10
**Q1. What does godly sorrow work?**
**Answer:** Repentance to salvation not to be repented of.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:10

**Q2. What does the sorrow of the world work?**
**Answer:** The sorrow of the world worketh death.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:10

**Q3. What is the difference between godly sorrow and worldly sorrow?**
**Answer:** Godly sorrow works repentance to salvation, while worldly sorrow works death.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:10

### 2 Corinthians 7:11
**Q1. What did the believers' godly sorrow work in them?**
**Answer:** What carefulness it wrought in you, yea, what clearing of yourselves, yea, what indignation, yea, what fear, yea, what vehement desire, yea, what zeal, yea, what revenge!
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:11

**Q2. In what matter have they approved themselves to be clear?**
**Answer:** In all things ye have approved yourselves to be clear in this matter.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:11

**Q3. List three things their sorrow wrought in them.**
**Answer:** Carefulness, clearing of yourselves, and indignation.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:11

### 2 Corinthians 7:12
**Q1. For whose cause did Paul NOT write the letter?**
**Answer:** Not for his cause that had done the wrong, nor for his cause that suffered wrong.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:12

**Q2. Why did Paul write the letter?**
**Answer:** But that our care for you in the sight of God might appear unto you.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:12

**Q3. In whose sight was the apostles' care?**
**Answer:** In the sight of God.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:12

### 2 Corinthians 7:13
**Q1. How were the apostles comforted?**
**Answer:** Therefore we were comforted in your comfort.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:13

**Q2. Why were they exceedingly more joyed?**
**Answer:** For the joy of Titus.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:13

**Q3. Why was Titus' spirit refreshed?**
**Answer:** Because his spirit was refreshed by you all.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:13

### 2 Corinthians 7:14
**Q1. Is Paul ashamed of boasting about the believers to Titus?**
**Answer:** I am not ashamed.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:14

**Q2. How did the apostles speak all things to the believers?**
**Answer:** In truth.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:14

**Q3. How was the boasting made before Titus found?**
**Answer:** Found a truth.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:14

### 2 Corinthians 7:15
**Q1. How is Titus' inward affection toward the believers?**
**Answer:** More abundant.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:15

**Q2. What does Titus remember about the believers?**
**Answer:** The obedience of you all.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:15

**Q3. How did the believers receive Titus?**
**Answer:** With fear and trembling.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:15

### 2 Corinthians 7:16
**Q1. Why does Paul rejoice?**
**Answer:** Because I have confidence in you in all things.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:16

**Q2. In what does Paul have confidence?**
**Answer:** In the believers.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:16

**Q3. In how many things does Paul have confidence in them?**
**Answer:** In all things.
**Verse:** 2 Corinthians 7:16
