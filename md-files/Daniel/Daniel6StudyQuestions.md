# Daniel 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Daniel 6:1

**Q1. It pleased Darius to set over the kingdom how many princes?**  
**Answer:** An hundred and twenty.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:1

**Q2. Over the whole kingdom, Darius set an hundred and twenty what?**  
**Answer:** Princes.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:1

**Q3. Who pleased to set an hundred and twenty princes over the kingdom?**  
**Answer:** Darius.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:1

---

### Daniel 6:2

**Q1. Over the princes, how many presidents were set?**  
**Answer:** Three.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:2

**Q2. Of whom was Daniel the first?**  
**Answer:** The presidents.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:2

**Q3. Why did the princes give accounts unto the presidents?**  
**Answer:** That the king should have no damages.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:2

---

### Daniel 6:3

**Q1. Then this Daniel was preferred above whom?**  
**Answer:** The presidents and princes.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:3

**Q2. What was in Daniel that caused him to be preferred above the presidents and princes?**  
**Answer:** An excellent spirit.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:3

**Q3. What did the king think to do concerning Daniel?**  
**Answer:** To set him over the whole realm.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:3

---

### Daniel 6:4

**Q1. Who sought to find occasion against Daniel concerning the kingdom?**  
**Answer:** The presidents and princes.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:4

**Q2. What could they find against Daniel regarding his kingdom affairs?**  
**Answer:** None occasion nor fault.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:4

**Q3. Why could they find no fault in Daniel?**  
**Answer:** Forasmuch as he was faithful, neither was there any error or fault found in him.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:4

---

### Daniel 6:5

**Q1. Then said these men, We shall not find any what against this Daniel?**  
**Answer:** Occasion.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:5

**Q2. Unless they find occasion against Daniel concerning what, they would find none?**  
**Answer:** Concerning the law of his God.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:5

**Q3. Against what concerning Daniel did these men expect to find occasion?**  
**Answer:** The law of his God.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:5

---

### Daniel 6:6

**Q1. Then these presidents and princes assembled together to whom?**  
**Answer:** The king.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:6

**Q2. What did the presidents and princes say unto the king?**  
**Answer:** King Darius, live for ever.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:6

**Q3. How did the presidents and princes assemble together?**  
**Answer:** Tumultuously.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:6

---

### Daniel 6:7

**Q1. Who have consulted together to establish a royal statute?**  
**Answer:** All the presidents of the kingdom, the governors, and the princes, the counsellors, and the captains.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:7

**Q2. For how long should whosoever ask a petition of any god or man, save of the king, be cast into the den of lions?**  
**Answer:** Thirty days.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:7

**Q3. Of whom only might a petition be asked for thirty days according to the decree?**  
**Answer:** Of the king.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:7

---

### Daniel 6:8

**Q1. Now, O king, establish what?**  
**Answer:** The decree.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:8

**Q2. What must be signed so that it be not changed?**  
**Answer:** The writing.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:8

**Q3. According to what law of the Medes and Persians was the writing to be established?**  
**Answer:** Which altereth not.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:8

---

### Daniel 6:9

**Q1. Wherefore king Darius signed what?**  
**Answer:** The writing and the decree.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:9

**Q2. Who signed the writing and the decree?**  
**Answer:** King Darius.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:9

**Q3. What did Darius sign?**  
**Answer:** The writing and the decree.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:9

---

### Daniel 6:10

**Q1. When Daniel knew that the writing was signed, where did he go?**  
**Answer:** Into his house.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:10

**Q2. In his chamber toward what place were his windows open?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:10

**Q3. How many times a day did Daniel kneel upon his knees, and pray, and give thanks before his God?**  
**Answer:** Three times a day.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:10

---

### Daniel 6:11

**Q1. Then these men assembled what?**  
**Answer:** Tumultuously.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:11

**Q2. What did these men find Daniel doing?**  
**Answer:** Praying and making supplication before his God.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:11

**Q3. Who assembled tumultuously and found Daniel praying?**  
**Answer:** These men.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:11

---

### Daniel 6:12

**Q1. Then they came near, and spake before the king concerning what?**  
**Answer:** The king's decree.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:12

**Q2. What did the king answer and say concerning the thing?**  
**Answer:** The thing is true, according to the law of the Medes and Persians, which altereth not.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:12

**Q3. Within how many days did the man ask a petition, according to their question to the king?**  
**Answer:** Thirty days.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:12

---

### Daniel 6:13

**Q1. Who answered and said before the king, That Daniel, which is of the children of the captivity of Judah?**  
**Answer:** They.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:13

**Q2. Daniel regardeth not the king, nor what else?**  
**Answer:** The decree that thou hast signed.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:13

**Q3. How many times a day did they say Daniel maketh his petition?**  
**Answer:** Three times a day.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:13

---

### Daniel 6:14

**Q1. When the king heard these words, how was he affected?**  
**Answer:** Was sore displeased with himself.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:14

**Q2. Whom did the king set his heart on to deliver?**  
**Answer:** Daniel.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:14

**Q3. Until when did the king labour to deliver Daniel?**  
**Answer:** Unto the going down of the sun.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:14

---

### Daniel 6:15

**Q1. Then these men assembled unto whom?**  
**Answer:** The king.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:15

**Q2. What did these men say the law of the Medes and Persians is, that no decree or statute which the king establisheth may be?**  
**Answer:** Changed.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:15

**Q3. Who assembled unto the king?**  
**Answer:** These men.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:15

---

### Daniel 6:16

**Q1. Then the king commanded, and they brought whom?**  
**Answer:** Daniel.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:16

**Q2. Where was Daniel cast?**  
**Answer:** Into the den of lions.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:16

**Q3. What did the king spake and say unto Daniel?**  
**Answer:** Thy God whom thou servest continually, he will deliver thee.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:16

---

### Daniel 6:17

**Q1. What was brought and laid upon the mouth of the den?**  
**Answer:** A stone.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:17

**Q2. With what did the king seal the stone?**  
**Answer:** With his own signet, and with the signet of his lords.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:17

**Q3. Why was the stone sealed concerning Daniel?**  
**Answer:** That the purpose might not be changed concerning Daniel.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:17

---

### Daniel 6:18

**Q1. To where went the king?**  
**Answer:** His palace.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:18

**Q2. What instruments of music were not brought before the king?**  
**Answer:** Instruments of musick.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:18

**Q3. What happened to the king's sleep?**  
**Answer:** His sleep went from him.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:18

---

### Daniel 6:19

**Q1. When arose the king?**  
**Answer:** Very early in the morning.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:19

**Q2. In where went the king in haste?**  
**Answer:** Unto the den of lions.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:19

**Q3. When the king went unto the den of lions, how did he go?**  
**Answer:** In haste.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:19

---

### Daniel 6:20

**Q1. When the king came to the den, he cried with what kind of voice?**  
**Answer:** With a lamentable voice.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:20

**Q2. Whom did the king ask if he was able to deliver him?**  
**Answer:** Daniel.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:20

**Q3. What did the king call Daniel in the den?**  
**Answer:** Servant of the living God.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:20

---

### Daniel 6:21

**Q1. Who said unto the king, O king, live for ever?**  
**Answer:** Daniel.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:21

**Q2. To whom did Daniel speak, saying, O king, live for ever?**  
**Answer:** The king.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:21

**Q3. Who spoke in Daniel 6:21?**  
**Answer:** Daniel.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:21

---

### Daniel 6:22

**Q1. Who hath sent his angel?**  
**Answer:** My God.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:22

**Q2. What did God's angel shut?**  
**Answer:** The lions' mouths.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:22

**Q3. Why did the lions not hurt Daniel?**  
**Answer:** Forasmuch as before him innocency was found in me; and also before thee, O king, have I done no hurt.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:22

---

### Daniel 6:23

**Q1. How did the king feel when he commanded that they should take Daniel up out of the den?**  
**Answer:** Exceeding glad.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:23

**Q2. When Daniel was taken up out of the den, what manner of hurt was found upon him?**  
**Answer:** No manner of hurt was found upon him.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:23

**Q3. Why was no manner of hurt found upon Daniel?**  
**Answer:** Because he believed in his God.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:23

---

### Daniel 6:24

**Q1. And the king commanded, and they brought whom?**  
**Answer:** Those men which had accused Daniel.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:24

**Q2. Who were cast into the den of lions along with the accusers?**  
**Answer:** Their children, and their wives.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:24

**Q3. What did the lions do to the accusers, their children, and their wives before they reached the bottom of the den?**  
**Answer:** Brake all their bones in pieces.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:24

---

### Daniel 6:25

**Q1. To whom did king Darius write?**  
**Answer:** All people, nations, and languages, that dwell in all the earth.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:25

**Q2. What was the greeting in the writing of king Darius?**  
**Answer:** Peace be multiplied unto you.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:25

**Q3. Who wrote unto all people, nations, and languages?**  
**Answer:** King Darius.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:25

---

### Daniel 6:26

**Q1. I make a decree, That in every dominion of my kingdom men tremble and fear before whom?**  
**Answer:** The God of Daniel.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:26

**Q2. What kind of God is the God of Daniel?**  
**Answer:** The living God, and stedfast for ever.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:26

**Q3. Whose kingdom shall not be destroyed, and whose dominion shall be even unto the end?**  
**Answer:** His kingdom.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:26

---

### Daniel 6:27

**Q1. Who delivereth and rescueth?**  
**Answer:** He.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:27

**Q2. Where does God work signs and wonders?**  
**Answer:** In heaven and in earth.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:27

**Q3. From the power of whom hath God delivered Daniel?**  
**Answer:** The lions.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:27

---

### Daniel 6:28

**Q1. So this Daniel prospered in the reign of Darius, and in the reign of whom else?**  
**Answer:** Cyrus the Persian.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:28

**Q2. In whose reign did Daniel prosper?**  
**Answer:** Darius and Cyrus the Persian.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:28

**Q3. What did Daniel do in the reign of Darius and Cyrus the Persian?**  
**Answer:** Prospered.  
**Verse:** Daniel 6:28
