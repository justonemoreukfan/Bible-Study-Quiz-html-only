# Daniel 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Daniel 12:1

**Q1. Who shall stand up at that time according to verse 1?**  
**Answer:** Michael.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:1

**Q2. What is Michael described as in relation to the children of thy people?**  
**Answer:** The great prince which standeth for the children of thy people.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:1

**Q3. Who shall be delivered among the people written in the book?**  
**Answer:** Every one that shall be found written in the book.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:1

---

### Daniel 12:2

**Q4. Where shall many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth awake?**  
**Answer:** In the dust of the earth.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:2

**Q5. To what shall some awake?**  
**Answer:** To everlasting life.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:2

**Q6. To what shall some awake and be?**  
**Answer:** To shame and everlasting contempt.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:2

---

### Daniel 12:3

**Q7. What shall they that be wise do?**  
**Answer:** Shine as the brightness of the firmament.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:3

**Q8. What shall they that turn many to righteousness do?**  
**Answer:** Shine as the stars for ever and ever.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:3

**Q9. How long shall they shine as the stars?**  
**Answer:** For ever and ever.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:3

---

### Daniel 12:4

**Q10. What was Daniel told to do with the words and seal the book?**  
**Answer:** Shut up the words, and seal the book.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:4

**Q11. Until what time was the book to be sealed?**  
**Answer:** Even to the time of the end.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:4

**Q12. What shall many do while knowledge shall be increased?**  
**Answer:** Many shall run to and fro.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:4

---

### Daniel 12:5

**Q13. Who did Daniel look and see in verse 5?**  
**Answer:** Two other.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:5

**Q14. Where did the two other stand?**  
**Answer:** The one on the other side of the bank of the river, and the other on that side of the bank of the river.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:5

**Q15. What was the location mentioned in connection with the two other?**  
**Answer:** The bank of the river.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:5

---

### Daniel 12:6

**Q16. What was one man clothed in?**  
**Answer:** Linen.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:6

**Q17. Where was the man clothed in linen located?**  
**Answer:** Upon the waters of the river.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:6

**Q18. What question did one ask the man clothed in linen?**  
**Answer:** How long shall it be to the end of these wonders?  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:6

---

### Daniel 12:7

**Q19. How did the man clothed in linen hold up his hands?**  
**Answer:** He held up his right hand and his left hand unto heaven.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:7

**Q20. By whom did the man swear?**  
**Answer:** By him that liveth for ever.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:7

**Q21. What would happen when the power of the holy people was scattered?**  
**Answer:** All these things shall be finished.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:7

---

### Daniel 12:8

**Q22. Did Daniel understand what he heard?**  
**Answer:** No ("I heard, but I understood not").  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:8

**Q23. What question did Daniel ask?**  
**Answer:** O my Lord, what shall be the end of these things?  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:8

**Q24. Whose response was Daniel asking for?**  
**Answer:** O my Lord.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:8

---

### Daniel 12:9

**Q25. Where was Daniel told to go?**  
**Answer:** Thy way, Daniel.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:9

**Q26. What was to be done with the words?**  
**Answer:** The words are closed up and sealed.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:9

**Q27. Until what period were the words closed up and sealed?**  
**Answer:** Till the time of the end.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:9

---

### Daniel 12:10

**Q28. Who shall be purified, and made white, and tried?**  
**Answer:** Many.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:10

**Q29. Who shall do wickedly?**  
**Answer:** The wicked.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:10

**Q30. Who shall understand?**  
**Answer:** The wise.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:10

---

### Daniel 12:11

**Q31. From what time shall the daily sacrifice be taken away?**  
**Answer:** From the time that the daily sacrifice shall be taken away.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:11

**Q32. What event is associated with the taking away of the daily sacrifice?**  
**Answer:** The abomination that maketh desolate set up.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:11

**Q33. How many days shall there be according to verse 11?**  
**Answer:** A thousand two hundred and ninety days.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:11

---

### Daniel 12:12

**Q34. Who is pronounced blessed?**  
**Answer:** Blessed is he that waiteth.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:12

**Q35. How many days does the person wait to reach the specified number?**  
**Answer:** To the thousand three hundred and five and thirty days.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:12

**Q36. What is the total number of days mentioned in verse 12?**  
**Answer:** A thousand three hundred and five and thirty days.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:12

---

### Daniel 12:13

**Q37. Where was Daniel told to go his way?**  
**Answer:** Thy way till the end be.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:13

**Q38. What would happen to Daniel after going his way?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt rest.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:13

**Q39. When would Daniel stand in his lot?**  
**Answer:** In the end of the days.  
**Verse:** Daniel 12:13
