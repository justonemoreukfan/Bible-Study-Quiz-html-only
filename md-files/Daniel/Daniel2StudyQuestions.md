# Daniel 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Daniel 2:1

**Q1. In what year of his reign did Nebuchadnezzar have dreams?**  
**Answer:** In the second year of the reign of Nebuchadnezzar.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:1

**Q2. What happened to Nebuchadnezzar's spirit during this time?**  
**Answer:** His spirit was troubled.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:1

**Q3. What happened to Nebuchadnezzar's sleep because of his dreams?**  
**Answer:** His sleep brake from him.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:1

---

### Daniel 2:2

**Q4. Who did the king command to be called to show him his dreams?**  
**Answer:** The magicians, and the astrologers, and the sorcerers, and the Chaldeans.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:2

**Q5. For what purpose were these men called before the king?**  
**Answer:** To shew the king his dreams.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:2

**Q6. What did they do after coming and standing before the king?**  
**Answer:** They came and stood before the king.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:2

---

### Daniel 2:3

**Q7. What did the king say unto them regarding his mind?**  
**Answer:** I have dreamed a dream, and my spirit was troubled to know the dream.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:3

**Q8. What state was the king's spirit in concerning the dream?**  
**Answer:** Troubled to know the dream.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:3

**Q9. What did the king state that he had done?**  
**Answer:** I have dreamed a dream.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:3

---

### Daniel 2:4

**Q10. In what language did the Chaldeans speak to the king?**  
**Answer:** In Syriack.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:4

**Q11. How did the Chaldeans address the king?**  
**Answer:** O king, live for ever.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:4

**Q12. What did the Chaldeans ask the king to tell his servants?**  
**Answer:** Tell thy servants the dream, and we will shew the interpretation.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:4

---

### Daniel 2:5

**Q13. What did the king answer and say to the Chaldeans?**  
**Answer:** The thing is gone from me.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:5

**Q14. What warning did the king give if they did not make known the dream and its interpretation?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall be cut in pieces, and your houses shall be made a dunghill.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:5

**Q15. What would the king give them if they showed the dream and the interpretation?**  
**Answer:** Gifts and rewards and great honour.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:5

---

### Daniel 2:6

**Q16. What did the king reiterate that they must show him?**  
**Answer:** The dream, and the interpretation thereof.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:6

**Q17. What specific things would the king give them for showing the dream and interpretation?**  
**Answer:** Gifts and rewards and great honour.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:6

**Q18. Therefore, what did the king command them to show him?**  
**Answer:** Therefore shew me the dream and the interpretation thereof.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:6

---

### Daniel 2:7

**Q19. How did they answer the king the second time?**  
**Answer:** Let the king tell his servants the dream, and we will shew the interpretation of it.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:7

**Q20. Who did they ask the king to tell the dream to?**  
**Answer:** His servants.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:7

**Q21. What did they promise to do once the dream was told?**  
**Answer:** We will shew the interpretation of it.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:7

---

### Daniel 2:8

**Q22. What did the king answer and say regarding their actions?**  
**Answer:** I know of certainty that ye would gain the time.  
**Verse:** Daniel 2:8
