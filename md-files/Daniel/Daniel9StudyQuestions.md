# Daniel 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Daniel 9:1

**Q1. In what year of Darius's reign was he made king over the realm of the Chaldeans?**  
**Answer:** The first year.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:1

**Q2. Whose son was Darius the king?**  
**Answer:** Ahasuerus.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:1

**Q3. Of what seed was Darius?**  
**Answer:** Of the seed of the Medes.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:1

---

### Daniel 9:2

**Q1. What did Daniel understand by books?**  
**Answer:** The number of the years, whereof the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah the prophet.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:2

**Q2. Concerning what city was the desolation to accomplish seventy years?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:2

**Q3. Whose word came to Jeremiah the prophet?**  
**Answer:** The word of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:2

---

### Daniel 9:3

**Q1. To whom did Daniel set his face?**  
**Answer:** The Lord God.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:3

**Q2. What did Daniel seek by prayer and supplications?**  
**Answer:** The Lord God.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:3

**Q3. With what physical practices did Daniel seek the Lord God?**  
**Answer:** By fasting, and sackcloth, and ashes.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:3

---

### Daniel 9:4

**Q1. To whom did Daniel pray and make his confession?**  
**Answer:** The LORD my God.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:4

**Q2. How did Daniel describe the Lord God in his prayer?**  
**Answer:** The great and dreadful God.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:4

**Q3. To whom does God keep covenant and mercy?**  
**Answer:** To them that love him, and to them that keep his commandments.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:4

---

### Daniel 9:5

**Q1. What did Daniel confess that they had done?**  
**Answer:** We have sinned, and have committed iniquity, and have done wickedly, and have rebelled.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:5

**Q2. From what did they depart?**  
**Answer:** From thy precepts and from thy judgments.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:5

**Q3. Whose precepts and judgments did they depart from?**  
**Answer:** Thine.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:5

---

### Daniel 9:6

**Q1. To whom would they not hearken?**  
**Answer:** Thy servants the prophets.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:6

**Q2. In whose name did the prophets speak?**  
**Answer:** In thy name.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:6

**Q3. To whom did the prophets speak in the king's name?**  
**Answer:** To our kings, our princes, and our fathers, and to all the people of the land.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:6

---

### Daniel 9:7

**Q1. What belongeth unto the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Righteousness.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:7

**Q2. What belongeth unto them this day?**  
**Answer:** Confusion of faces.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:7

**Q3. Who is included in those that have confusion of faces?**  
**Answer:** The men of Judah, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and all Israel, that are near, and that are far off, through all the countries whither thou hast driven them, because of their trespass that they have trespassed against thee.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:7

---

### Daniel 9:8

**Q1. What belongeth to us, our kings, our princes, and our fathers?**  
**Answer:** Confusion of face.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:8

**Q2. Why does confusion of face belong to them?**  
**Answer:** Because we have sinned against thee.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:8

**Q3. Who belongs to the group that has confusion of face?**  
**Answer:** O Lord, to us, to our kings, to our princes, and to our fathers.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:8

---

### Daniel 9:9

**Q1. What belongeth to the Lord our God?**  
**Answer:** Mercies and forgivenesses.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:9

**Q2. Against whom have they rebelled?**  
**Answer:** Against him.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:9

**Q3. Whose mercies and forgivenesses are these?**  
**Answer:** The Lord our God's.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:9

---

### Daniel 9:10

**Q1. Whose voice have they not obeyed?**  
**Answer:** The voice of the LORD our God.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:10

**Q2. What did they fail to walk in?**  
**Answer:** His laws, which he set before us.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:10

**Q3. By whose ministry were the laws set before them?**  
**Answer:** By his servants the prophets.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:10

---

### Daniel 9:11

**Q1. Who has transgressed God's law?**  
**Answer:** All Israel.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:11

**Q2. By departing from where did they transgress?**  
**Answer:** By departing, that they might not obey thy voice.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:11

**Q3. What has been poured upon them?**  
**Answer:** The curse and the oath that is written in the law of Moses the servant of God.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:11

---

### Daniel 9:12

**Q1. Whose words did God confirm?**  
**Answer:** His words which he spake against us, and against our judges that judged us.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:12

**Q2. Upon whom did God bring a great evil?**  
**Answer:** Upon us.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:12

**Q3. Under the whole heaven, what was never done as hath been done upon what city?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:12

---

### Daniel 9:13

**Q1. As it is written in what, has all this evil come upon us?**  
**Answer:** The law of Moses.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:13

**Q2. What did they not make before the LORD their God?**  
**Answer:** Their prayer.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:13

**Q3. From what did they not turn?**  
**Answer:** From their iniquities, and understand thy truth.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:13

---

### Daniel 9:14

**Q1. What did the LORD watch upon?**  
**Answer:** The evil.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:14

**Q2. Upon whom did the LORD bring the evil?**  
**Answer:** Upon us.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:14

**Q3. How did Daniel describe the LORD our God in relation to his works?**  
**Answer:** Righteous in all his works which he doeth.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:14

---

### Daniel 9:15

**Q1. Out of what land did the Lord bring his people with a mighty hand?**  
**Answer:** The land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:15

**Q2. What did they get themselves this day?**  
**Answer:** Renown.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:15

**Q3. What have they done as at this day?**  
**Answer:** We have sinned, we have done wickedly.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:15

---

### Daniel 9:16

**Q1. According to all thy righteousness, what did Daniel pray be turned away?**  
**Answer:** Thine anger and thy fury.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:16

**Q2. From what city was God's anger and fury to be turned away?**  
**Answer:** From thy city Jerusalem, thy holy mountain.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:16

**Q3. Why are Jerusalem and God's people a reproach to all that are about them?**  
**Answer:** For our sins, and for the iniquities of our fathers.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:16

---

### Daniel 9:17

**Q1. To whose prayer and supplications did Daniel ask God to hearken?**  
**Answer:** His servant.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:17

**Q2. For whose sake did Daniel ask God to cause His face to shine?**  
**Answer:** For the Lord's sake.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:17

**Q3. Upon what sanctuary did Daniel ask God's face to shine that is desolate?**  
**Answer:** Upon thy sanctuary that is desolate.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:17

---

### Daniel 9:18

**Q1. What did Daniel ask God to incline and hear?**  
**Answer:** Thine ear, and hear.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:18

**Q2. What did Daniel ask God to open and behold?**  
**Answer:** Thine eyes, and behold our desolations.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:18

**Q3. Upon what city is God's name called?**  
**Answer:** The city which is called by thy name.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:18

---

### Daniel 9:19

**Q1. What did Daniel tell the Lord to hear and forgive?**  
**Answer:** O Lord, hear; O Lord, forgive.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:19

**Q2. What did Daniel tell the Lord to hearken and do?**  
**Answer:** O Lord, hearken and do.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:19

**Q3. For whose sake did Daniel ask the Lord not to defer?**  
**Answer:** For thine own sake, O my God.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:19

---

### Daniel 9:20

**Q1. What was Daniel doing while he was speaking, praying, and confessing?**  
**Answer:** Praying, and confessing my sin and the sin of my people Israel.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:20

**Q2. Before whose mountain did Daniel present his supplication?**  
**Answer:** The mountain of my God.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:20

**Q3. Whose God is mentioned?**  
**Answer:** My God.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:20

---

### Daniel 9:21

**Q1. While Daniel was speaking in prayer, who flew swiftly?**  
**Answer:** The man Gabriel.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:21

**Q2. Where had Daniel seen Gabriel previously in the vision?**  
**Answer:** In the beginning.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:21

**Q3. At what time did Gabriel touch Daniel?**  
**Answer:** About the time of the evening oblation.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:21

---

### Daniel 9:22

**Q1. What did Gabriel inform Daniel to give him?**  
**Answer:** Skill and understanding.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:22

**Q2. With whom did Gabriel talk?**  
**Answer:** Daniel.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:22

**Q3. What was the purpose of Gabriel coming forth?**  
**Answer:** To give thee skill and understanding.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:22

---

### Daniel 9:23

**Q1. When did the commandment come forth?**  
**Answer:** At the beginning of thy supplications.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:23

**Q2. What was Daniel declared to be?**  
**Answer:** Greatly beloved.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:23

**Q3. What did Gabriel tell Daniel to consider and understand?**  
**Answer:** The matter, and understand the vision.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:23

---

### Daniel 9:24

**Q1. Upon whom and upon what holy city is seventy weeks determined?**  
**Answer:** Upon thy people and upon thy holy city.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:24

**Q2. To what end are the seventy weeks determined regarding transgression and sin?**  
**Answer:** To finish the transgression, and to make an end of sins.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:24

**Q3. What is to be brought in during the seventy weeks?**  
**Answer:** Everlasting righteousness.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:24

---

### Daniel 9:25

**Q1. From the going forth of the commandment to restore and to build Jerusalem unto whom is the measurement?**  
**Answer:** Unto the Messiah the Prince.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:25

**Q2. How many weeks are specified for the street and the wall to be built again?**  
**Answer:** Seven weeks, and threescore and two weeks.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:25

**Q3. In what kind of times shall the street and wall be built again?**  
**Answer:** In troublous times.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:25

---

### Daniel 9:26

**Q1. After threescore and two weeks, what shall happen to Messiah?**  
**Answer:** Shall Messiah be cut off, but not for himself.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:26

**Q2. Who shall destroy the city and the sanctuary?**  
**Answer:** The people of the prince that shall come.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:26

**Q3. With what shall the end of the city and sanctuary be?**  
**Answer:** With a flood.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:26

---

### Daniel 9:27

**Q1. For how many weeks shall he confirm the covenant with many?**  
**Answer:** One week.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:27

**Q2. In the midst of the week, what shall he cause to cease?**  
**Answer:** The sacrifice and the oblation.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:27

**Q3. What shall be poured upon the desolate?**  
**Answer:** The overspreading of abominations.  
**Verse:** Daniel 9:27
