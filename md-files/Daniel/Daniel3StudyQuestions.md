# Daniel 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Daniel 3:1

**Q1. What did Nebuchadnezzar the king make?**  
**Answer:** An image of gold.  
**Verse:** Daniel 3:1

**Q2. What were the dimensions of the image?**  
**Answer:** Whose height was threescore cubits, and the breadth thereof six cubits.  
**Verse:** Daniel 3:1

**Q3. Where did the king set up the image?**  
**Answer:** In the plain of Dura, in the province of Babylon.  
**Verse:** Daniel 3:1

---

### Daniel 3:2

**Q1. Whom did Nebuchadnezzar the king send to gather together?**  
**Answer:** The princes, the governors, and the captains, the judges, the treasurers, the counsellors, the sheriffs, and all the rulers of the provinces.  
**Verse:** Daniel 3:2

**Q2. For what purpose were these officials gathered together?**  
**Answer:** To come to the dedication of the image which Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up.  
**Verse:** Daniel 3:2

**Q3. Who sent to gather these officials together?**  
**Answer:** Nebuchadnezzar the king.  
**Verse:** Daniel 3:2

---

### Daniel 3:3

**Q1. Who were gathered together unto the dedication of the image?**  
**Answer:** The princes, the governors, and captains, the judges, the treasurers, the counsellors, the sheriffs, and all the rulers of the provinces.  
**Verse:** Daniel 3:3

**Q2. What event were they gathered together for?**  
**Answer:** The dedication of the image which Nebuchadnezzar had set up.  
**Verse:** Daniel 3:3

**Q3. Where did they stand in relation to the image?**

**Verse:** Daniel 3:3

**Answer:** The golden image that Nebuchadnezzar the king hath set up.  
**Verse:** Daniel 3:5
