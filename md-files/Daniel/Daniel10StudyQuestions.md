# Daniel 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Daniel 10:1

**Q1. In the third year of which king was a thing revealed unto Daniel?**  
**Answer:** Cyrus king of Persia.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:1

**Q2. What was the name whose name was called Daniel?**  
**Answer:** Belteshazzar.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:1

**Q3. What was true concerning the thing revealed unto Daniel, which had a time appointed?**  
**Answer:** The thing was true, but the time appointed was long.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:1

---

### Daniel 10:2

**Q1. In those days, how long was Daniel mourning?**  
**Answer:** Three full weeks.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:2

---

### Daniel 10:3

**Q1. What kind of bread did Daniel eat during the three full weeks?**  
**Answer:** Pleasant bread.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:3

**Q2. What did Daniel not come into until three whole weeks were fulfilled?**  
**Answer:** Flesh nor wine.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:3

**Q3. With what did Daniel not anoint himself at all until the three weeks were fulfilled?**  
**Answer:** With oil.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:3

---

### Daniel 10:4

**Q1. On what day of the first month was Daniel by the side of the great river?**  
**Answer:** The four and twentieth day of the first month.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:4

**Q2. By the side of which great river was Daniel?**  
**Answer:** Hiddekel.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:4

---

### Daniel 10:5

**Q1. When Daniel lifted up his eyes and looked, what was a certain man clothed in?**  
**Answer:** Linen.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:5

**Q2. What kind of gold was the man's loins girded with?**  
**Answer:** Fine gold of Uphaz.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:5

---

### Daniel 10:6

**Q1. What was the body of the man like, similar to the beryl?**  
**Answer:** His body also was like the beryl.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:6

**Q2. What was the appearance of his face like?**  
**Answer:** As the appearance of lightning.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:6

**Q3. What were his eyes and his arms and feet like?**  
**Answer:** His eyes as lamps of fire, and his arms and his feet like in colour to polished brass, and the voice of his words like the voice of a multitude.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:6

---

### Daniel 10:7

**Q1. Who alone saw the vision while Daniel was with them?**  
**Answer:** Daniel alone saw the vision.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:7

**Q2. What fell upon the men that were with Daniel?**  
**Answer:** A great quaking.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:7

**Q3. What did the men that were with Daniel do because of the great quaking?**  
**Answer:** They fled to hide themselves.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:7

---

### Daniel 10:8

**Q1. When Daniel was left alone and saw this great vision, what remained in him?**  
**Answer:** No strength.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:8

**Q2. What was turned in Daniel when he saw the vision?**  
**Answer:** His comeliness was turned in him into corruption.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:8

**Q3. What did Daniel retain in him when his comeliness was turned into corruption?**  
**Answer:** No strength.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:8

---

### Daniel 10:9

**Q1. What did Daniel hear when he heard the voice of the man's words?**  
**Answer:** The voice of his words.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:9

**Q2. What happened to Daniel when he heard the voice of the man's words?**  
**Answer:** Then was I in a deep sleep on my face, and my face toward the ground.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:9

---

### Daniel 10:10

**Q1. What touched Daniel when he was in a deep sleep on his face?**  
**Answer:** A hand.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:10

**Q2. How did the hand set Daniel?**  
**Answer:** Upon his knees and upon the palms of his hands.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:10

---

### Daniel 10:11

**Q1. What did the speaker call Daniel?**  
**Answer:** O Daniel, a man greatly beloved.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:11

**Q2. What did the speaker tell Daniel to understand?**  
**Answer:** The words that I speak unto thee.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:11

**Q3. How did the speaker tell Daniel to stand?**  
**Answer:** Stand upright.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:11

---

### Daniel 10:12

**Q1. What did the speaker tell Daniel not to do from the first day?**  
**Answer:** Fear not.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:12

**Q2. What did Daniel set his heart to do from the first day?**  
**Answer:** To understand.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:12

**Q3. Before whom did Daniel chasten himself?**  
**Answer:** Before thy God.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:12

---

### Daniel 10:13

**Q1. Who withstood the speaker for one and twenty days?**  
**Answer:** The prince of the kingdom of Persia.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:13

**Q2. Who came to help the speaker because he remained there with the kings of Persia?**  
**Answer:** Michael, one of the chief princes.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:13

**Q3. For how many days did the prince of the kingdom of Persia withstand the speaker?**  
**Answer:** One and twenty days.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:13

---

### Daniel 10:14

**Q1. Why had the speaker come to make Daniel understand?**  
**Answer:** What shall befall thy people in the latter days.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:14

**Q2. When shall the things befall Daniel's people?**  
**Answer:** In the latter days.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:14

**Q3. What yet is for the vision?**  
**Answer:** For yet the vision is for many days.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:14

---

### Daniel 10:15

**Q1. When the speaker had spoken such words unto Daniel, what did Daniel do?**  
**Answer:** I set my face toward the ground.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:15

**Q2. What happened to Daniel as he set his face toward the ground?**  
**Answer:** I became dumb.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:15

---

### Daniel 10:16

**Q1. What did one like the similitude of the sons of men do?**  
**Answer:** Touched my lips.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:16

**Q2. What did Daniel say was opened in him?**  
**Answer:** My mouth.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:16

**Q3. What did Daniel say came upon him in the vision?**  
**Answer:** My sorrows are turned upon me, and I have retained no strength.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:16

---

### Daniel 10:17

**Q1. How could the servant of my lord talk with this my lord?**  
**Answer:** For as for me, straightway there remained no strength in me, neither is there breath left in me.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:17

---

### Daniel 10:18

**Q1. What touched Daniel again?**  
**Answer:** One like the appearance of a man.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:18

**Q2. What did the one like the appearance of a man do to Daniel?**  
**Answer:** He strengthened him.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:18

---

### Daniel 10:19

**Q1. What did the man say to Daniel, calling him a man greatly beloved?**  
**Answer:** O man greatly beloved, fear not.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:19

**Q2. What did the man wish for Daniel concerning peace?**  
**Answer:** Peace be unto thee.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:19

**Q3. What did Daniel say happened when the man had spoken unto him?**  
**Answer:** I was strengthened, and said, Let my lord speak; for thou hast strengthened me.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:19

---

### Daniel 10:20

**Q1. Where did the man ask Daniel if he knew why he came unto him?**  
**Answer:** Knowest thou wherefore I come unto thee?  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:20

**Q2. With whom must the man return to fight?**  
**Answer:** With the prince of Persia.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:20

**Q3. Who shall come after the man is gone forth with the prince of Persia?**  
**Answer:** The prince of Grecia.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:20

---

### Daniel 10:21

**Q1. What did the man say he will show Daniel?**  
**Answer:** That which is noted in the scripture of truth.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:21

**Q2. Who is the only one that holdeth with the man in these things except Michael your prince?**  
**Answer:** None.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:21

**Q3. Who is the prince belonging to Daniel's people mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Michael your prince.  
**Verse:** Daniel 10:21
