# Daniel 7 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Daniel 7:1

**Q1. In what year of Belshazzar king of Babylon did Daniel have a dream and visions of his head upon his bed?**  
**Answer:** In the first year of Belshazzar king of Babylon.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:1

**Q2. Who wrote the dream and told the sum of the matters?**  
**Answer:** Daniel.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:1

**Q3. Where were the visions of Daniel's head when he saw them?**  
**Answer:** Upon his bed.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:1

---

### Daniel 7:2

**Q1. Who spake and said what follows?**  
**Answer:** Daniel.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:2

**Q2. What did Daniel see in his vision by night?**  
**Answer:** The four winds of the heaven strove upon the great sea.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:2

---

### Daniel 7:3

**Q1. How many great beasts came up from the sea?**  
**Answer:** Four.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:3

**Q2. Where did the four great beasts come up from?**  
**Answer:** The sea.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:3

**Q3. How were the four great beasts described in relation to one another?**  
**Answer:** Diverse one from another.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:3

---

### Daniel 7:4

**Q1. What was the first beast like?**  
**Answer:** Like a lion.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:4

**Q2. What wings did the first beast have?**  
**Answer:** Eagle's wings.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:4

**Q3. What happened to the wings thereof until it was lifted up from the earth and made to stand upon the feet as a man, and a man's heart was given to it?**  
**Answer:** They were plucked.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:4

---

### Daniel 7:5

**Q1. What was the second beast like?**  
**Answer:** A bear.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:5

**Q2. How was the second beast raised up?**  
**Answer:** On one side.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:5

**Q3. What was in the mouth of the second beast between the teeth of it?**  
**Answer:** Three ribs.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:5

---

### Daniel 7:6

**Q1. What was the third beast like?**  
**Answer:** Like a leopard.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:6

**Q2. What did the third beast have upon the back of it?**  
**Answer:** Four wings of a fowl.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:6

**Q3. How many heads did the third beast have, and what was given to it?**  
**Answer:** Four heads; and dominion was given to it.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:6

---

### Daniel 7:7

**Q1. What followed the visions of the night after the third beast?**  
**Answer:** A fourth beast.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:7

**Q2. How is the fourth beast described in the text?**  
**Answer:** Dreadful and terrible, and strong exceedingly.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:7

**Q3. What did the fourth beast have, and what did it do with the residue?**  
**Answer:** Great iron teeth; it devoured and brake in pieces, and stamped the residue with the feet of it.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:7

---

### Daniel 7:8

**Q1. What did Daniel consider among the horns of the fourth beast?**  
**Answer:** The horns.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:8

**Q2. What came up among the horns of the fourth beast?**  
**Answer:** Another little horn.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:8

**Q3. What was before the little horn, and what were in this horn?**  
**Answer:** Three of the first horns plucked up by the roots; and eyes like the eyes of man, and a mouth speaking great things.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:8

---

### Daniel 7:9

**Q1. What were cast down until the thrones were placed?**  
**Answer:** The thrones.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:9

**Q2. Who did sit, whose garment was white as snow, and the hair of his head like the pure wool?**  
**Answer:** The Ancient of days.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:9

**Q3. What was like the fiery flame, and his wheels as burning fire?**  
**Answer:** His throne.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:9

---

### Daniel 7:10

**Q1. What issued and came forth from before him?**  
**Answer:** A fiery stream.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:10

**Q2. How many ministered unto him, and how many stood before him?**  
**Answer:** Thousand thousands ministered unto him, and ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:10

**Q3. What was set, and what were opened?**  
**Answer:** The judgment was set, and the books were opened.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:10

---

### Daniel 7:11

**Q1. Because of what did Daniel behold at that time?**  
**Answer:** Because of the voice of the great words which the horn spake.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:11

**Q2. What happened to the beast until it was slain?**  
**Answer:** It was beast slain, and his body destroyed.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:11

**Q3. To what was the destroyed body given to be burned?**  
**Answer:** To the burning flame.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:11

---

### Daniel 7:12

**Q1. What happened to the rest of the beasts?**  
**Answer:** Their dominion was taken away.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:12

**Q2. What was given to the rest of the beasts regarding their lives?**  
**Answer:** Their lives were prolonged.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:12

**Q3. For how long was their lives prolonged?**  
**Answer:** For a season and time.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:12

---

### Daniel 7:13

**Q1. What did Daniel see in the night visions?**  
**Answer:** One like the Son of man coming with the clouds of heaven.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:13

**Q2. To whom did the Son of man come?**  
**Answer:** To the Ancient of days.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:13

**Q3. Where was the Son of man brought before the Ancient of days?**  
**Answer:** Near before him.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:13

---

### Daniel 7:14

**Q1. What was given unto the Son of man?**  
**Answer:** Dominion, and glory, and a kingdom.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:14

**Q2. Who should serve him?**  
**Answer:** All people, nations, and languages.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:14

**Q3. How is his dominion and kingdom described?**  
**Answer:** An everlasting dominion, which shall not pass away, and his kingdom that which shall not be destroyed.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:14

---

### Daniel 7:15

**Q1. Who was grieved in his spirit in the midst of his body?**  
**Answer:** I Daniel.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:15

**Q2. What troubled Daniel?**  
**Answer:** The visions of his head.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:15

---

### Daniel 7:16

**Q1. Unto whom did Daniel come near?**  
**Answer:** One of them that stood by.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:16

**Q2. What did Daniel ask him?**  
**Answer:** The truth of all this.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:16

**Q3. What did the one standing by do for Daniel?**  
**Answer:** Told him, and made him know the interpretation of the things.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:16

---

### Daniel 7:17

**Q1. What are these great beasts?**  
**Answer:** Four kings.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:17

**Q2. From where shall these four kings arise?**  
**Answer:** Out of the earth.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:17

---

### Daniel 7:18

**Q1. Who shall take the kingdom?**  
**Answer:** The saints of the most High.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:18

**Q2. For how long shall the saints possess the kingdom?**  
**Answer:** For ever, even for ever and ever.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:18

---

### Daniel 7:19

**Q1. What did Daniel desire to know concerning the fourth beast?**  
**Answer:** The truth of the fourth beast.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:19

**Q2. How is the fourth beast described by Daniel in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Diverse from all the others, exceeding dreadful, whose teeth were of iron, and his nails of brass.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:19

**Q3. What did the fourth beast do with what it devoured and brake in pieces?**  
**Answer:** Stamped the residue with his feet.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:19

---

### Daniel 7:20

**Q1. What did Daniel desire to know about the ten horns that were in his head?**  
**Answer:** The truth of the ten horns.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:20

**Q2. What did Daniel desire to know about the other horn which came up, and before which three fell?**  
**Answer:** Of the other which came up, and before whom three fell.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:20

**Q3. What was the appearance and speech of that horn?**  
**Answer:** Had eyes, and a mouth that spake very great things, whose look was more stout than his fellows.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:20

---

### Daniel 7:21

**Q1. What did Daniel behold?**  
**Answer:** The same horn made war with the saints.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:21

**Q2. What did the same horn prevail against?**  
**Answer:** The saints.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:21

---

### Daniel 7:22

**Q1. Until when did the Ancient of days come?**  
**Answer:** Until the Ancient of days came.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:22

**Q2. What was given to the saints of the most High?**  
**Answer:** Judgment.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:22

**Q3. What happened when the time came that the saints possessed the kingdom?**  
**Answer:** The time came that the saints possessed the kingdom.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:22

### Daniel 7:23

**Q1. What shall the fourth beast be?**  
**Answer:** The fourth kingdom upon earth.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:23

**Q2. How will the fourth kingdom compare to other kingdoms?**  
**Answer:** Shall be diverse from all kingdoms.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:23

**Q3. What will the fourth kingdom do to the whole earth?**  
**Answer:** Shall devour the whole earth, and shall tread it down, and break it in pieces.  
**Verse:** Daniel 7:23

---

**Q1. What are the ten horns out of this kingdom?**

**Verse:** Daniel 7:24
