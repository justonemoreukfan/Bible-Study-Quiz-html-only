# Daniel 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Daniel 4:1

**Q1. Who wrote the message to all people, nations, and languages in all the earth?**  
**Answer:** Nebuchadnezzar the king.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:1

**Q2. How is the peace wished upon the people described in the greeting?**  
**Answer:** Peace be multiplied.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:1

**Q3. To whom are peace and the message addressed in this verse?**  
**Answer:** All people, nations, and languages, that dwell in all the earth.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:1

---

### Daniel 4:2

**Q1. What did the king think it good to declare?**  
**Answer:** The signs and wonders that the high God hath wrought toward me.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:2

**Q2. Who wrought the signs and wonders toward the king?**  
**Answer:** The high God.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:2

**Q3. What kind of acts did the high God perform toward the king?**  
**Answer:** Signs and wonders.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:2

---

### Daniel 4:3

**Q1. How great are God's signs described as being?**

**Verse:** Daniel 4:3

**Q2. How mighty are God's wonders?**  
**Answer:** And how mighty are his wonders!  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:3

**Q3. What describes the duration of His kingdom and dominion?**  
**Answer:** His kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and his dominion is from generation to generation.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:3

### Daniel 4:4

**Q1. Where was Nebuchadnezzar at ease?**

**Verse:** Daniel 4:4

**Q2. What was the king's condition in his palace?**

**Verse:** Daniel 4:4

**Answer:** I Nebuchadnezzar.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:4

### Daniel 4:5

**Q1. What did the king see that made him afraid?**  
**Answer:** A dream.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:5

**Q2. Where were the thoughts upon my bed that troubled the king?**  
**Answer:** The thoughts upon my bed and the visions of my head.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:5

**Q3. What effect did the visions of his head have upon him?**  
**Answer:** Troubled me.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:5

---

### Daniel 4:6

**Q1. Who did the king make a decree to bring in before him?**  
**Answer:** All the wise men of Babylon.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:6

**Q2. Why were the wise men of Babylon brought in before the king?**  
**Answer:** That they might make known unto me the interpretation of the dream.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:6

**Q3. What did the king issue to bring in the wise men?**  
**Answer:** A decree.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:6

---

### Daniel 4:7

**Q1. Who came in together after the decree?**

**Verse:** Daniel 4:7

**Q2. What did the king tell before them?**  
**Answer:** The dream.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:7

**Q3. What were they unable to make known unto the king?**  
**Answer:** The interpretation thereof.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:7

---

### Daniel 4:8

**Q1. Who came in before the king last in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Daniel, whose name was Belteshazzar.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:8

**Q2. After whose name was Daniel named Belteshazzar?**

**Q3. What is within Daniel according to the king?**

**Verse:** Daniel 4:8

### Daniel 4:9

**Verse:** Daniel 4:9

**Q2. What did the king know was in Daniel?**  
**Answer:** That the spirit of the holy gods is in thee.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:9

**Q3. What did the king ask Daniel to tell him?**

**Verse:** Daniel 4:9

**Verse:** Daniel 4:10

### Daniel 4:11

**Q1. What happened to the tree in terms of its growth?**  
**Answer:** The tree grew, and was strong.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:11

**Q2. How high did the height of the tree reach?**  
**Answer:** And the height thereof reached unto heaven.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:11

**Q3. To what places was the tree visible?**

**Verse:** Daniel 4:11

### Daniel 4:12

**Q1. What was fair about the tree?**  
**Answer:** The leaves thereof were fair.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:12

**Q2. What was abundant on the tree for all?**  
**Answer:** And the fruit thereof much.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:12

**Q3. What creatures found shelter under and in the tree?**

**Verse:** Daniel 4:12

### Daniel 4:13

**Q1. What did the king see in the visions of his head upon his bed?**  
**Answer:** A watcher and an holy one came down from heaven.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:13

**Q2. From where did the watcher and holy one come?**  
**Answer:** Came down from heaven.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:13

**Q3. What phrase is used to describe the heavenly visitor alongside "watcher"?**  
**Answer:** An holy one.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:13

---

### Daniel 4:14

**Q1. How did the watcher cry aloud?**  
**Answer:** He cried aloud, and said thus.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:14

**Q2. What specific actions were commanded regarding the tree?**  
**Answer:** Hew down the tree, and cut off his branches, shake off his leaves, and scatter his fruit.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:14

**Q3. What must move away from under the tree?**  
**Answer:** Let the beasts get away from under it, and the fowls from his branches.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:14

---

### Daniel 4:15

**Q1. What part of the tree was to be left in the earth?**  
**Answer:** Nevertheless leave the stump of his roots in the earth.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:15

**Q2. With what material was the stump bound?**  
**Answer:** With a band of iron and brass.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:15

**Q3. In what environment was the stump to be let wet?**  
**Answer:** In the tender grass of the field; and let it be wet with the dew of heaven.  
**Verse:** Daniel 4:15

### Daniel 4:18
