# Daniel 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Daniel 5:1

**Q1. Who made a great feast to a thousand of his lords?**  
**Answer:** Belshazzar the king.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:1

**Q2. Before how many of his lords did Belshazzar make a great feast?**  
**Answer:** A thousand.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:1

**Q3. What did Belshazzar drink before the thousand?**  
**Answer:** Wine.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:1

---

### Daniel 5:2

**Q1. While he tasted the wine, what did Belshazzar command to be brought?**  
**Answer:** The golden and silver vessels.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:2

**Q2. Who had taken the golden and silver vessels out of the temple which was in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** His father Nebuchadnezzar.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:2

**Q3. Who was to drink in the vessels brought from the temple in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The king, and his princes, his wives, and his concubines.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:2

---

### Daniel 5:3

**Q1. What vessels were brought from the temple of the house of God which was at Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The golden vessels.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:3

**Q2. Who drank in the golden vessels?**  
**Answer:** The king, and his princes, his wives, and his concubines.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:3

---

### Daniel 5:4

**Q1. What did they drink, and praise the gods of?**  
**Answer:** Wine.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:4

**Q2. The gods of what materials did they praise?**  
**Answer:** Gold, and of silver, of brass, of iron, of wood, and of stone.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:4

---

### Daniel 5:5

**Q1. What came forth in the same hour?**  
**Answer:** Fingers of a man's hand.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:5

**Q2. Where did the fingers of a man's hand write?**  
**Answer:** Over against the candlestick upon the plaister of the wall of the king's palace.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:5

**Q3. What part of the king did see the part of the hand that wrote?**  
**Answer:** The king saw the part of the hand that wrote.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:5

---

### Daniel 5:6

**Q1. What happened to the king's countenance when he saw the hand?**  
**Answer:** His countenance was changed in him.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:6

**Q2. What happened to the king's thoughts?**  
**Answer:** His thoughts troubled him.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:6

**Q3. What physical reaction did the king experience in his joints and knees?**  
**Answer:** The joints of his loins were loosed, and his knees smote one against another.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:6

---

### Daniel 5:7

**Q1. Who did the king cry aloud to bring in?**  
**Answer:** The astrologers, the Chaldeans, and the soothsayers.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:7

**Q2. What was promised to the man that could read the writing and show the interpretation thereof?**  
**Answer:** Clothed with scarlet, and have a chain of gold about his neck, and be the third ruler in the kingdom.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:7

---

### Daniel 5:8

**Q1. Who came in at the king's command?**  
**Answer:** All the king's wise men.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:8

**Q2. What could the wise men not do with the writing?**  
**Answer:** Could not read the writing.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:8

**Q3. What could the wise men not make known to the king?**  
**Answer:** The interpretation thereof.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:8

---

### Daniel 5:9

**Q1. How was King Belshazzar affected after this?**  
**Answer:** Was greatly troubled.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:9

**Q2. What happened to the king's countenance in him?**  
**Answer:** His countenance was changed in him.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:9

**Q3. How were his lords affected?**  
**Answer:** Were astonished.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:9

---

### Daniel 5:10

**Q1. Who came into the banquet house by reason of the words of the king and his lords?**  
**Answer:** The queen.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:10

**Q2. What did the queen speak and say?**  
**Answer:** O king, live for ever.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:10

**Q3. What did the queen advise the king not to do regarding his thoughts?**  
**Answer:** Let not thy thoughts trouble thee, nor let thy countenance be changed.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:10

---

### Daniel 5:11

**Q1. Who is in the king's kingdom, in whom is the spirit of the holy gods?**  
**Answer:** A man.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:11

**Q2. What was found in the days of the king's father in this man?**  
**Answer:** Light and understanding and wisdom, like the wisdom of the gods.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:11

**Q3. Who did the king's father, Nebuchadnezzar the king, make master of the magicians, astrologers, Chaldeans, and soothsayers?**  
**Answer:** Master of the magicians.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:11

---

### Daniel 5:12

**Q1. What was found in Daniel whom the king named Belteshazzar?**  
**Answer:** An excellent spirit, and knowledge, and understanding.  
**Verse:** Daniel 5:12
