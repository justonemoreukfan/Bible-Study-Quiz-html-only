# Daniel 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Daniel 1:1

**Q1. In what year of the reign of Jehoiakim did Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon come unto Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The third year.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:1

**Q2. Who came unto Jerusalem and besieged it in the third year of the reign of Jehoiakim?**  
**Answer:** Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:1

**Q3. What city did Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon come unto and besiege?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:1

---

### Daniel 1:2

**Q1. Whom did the Lord give into Nebuchadnezzar's hand?**  
**Answer:** Jehoiakim king of Judah.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:2

**Q2. Where did Nebuchadnezzar carry the part of the vessels of the house of God?**  
**Answer:** Into the land of Shinar to the house of his god.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:2

**Q3. Into whose treasure house did Nebuchadnezzar bring the vessels?**  
**Answer:** The treasure house of his god.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:2

---

### Daniel 1:3

**Q1. Whom did the king speak unto to bring certain of the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Master of his eunuchs, Ashpenaz.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:3

**Q2. Of whose seed were the children to be brought unto the king?**  
**Answer:** Of the king's seed, and of the princes.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:3

**Q3. Besides the king's seed, what other group of children was Ashpenaz told to bring?**  
**Answer:** Children of the princes.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:3

---

### Daniel 1:4

**Q1. What kind of blemish were the children to be free from?**  
**Answer:** No blemish.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:4

**Q2. What kind of well-favoured appearance were they to have?**  
**Answer:** Well-favoured.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:4

**Q3. What were they to be taught in the learning and the tongue of the Chaldeans?**  
**Answer:** The learning and the tongue of the Chaldeans.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:4

---

### Daniel 1:5

**Q1. What did the king appoint for them daily?**  
**Answer:** A daily provision of the king's meat, and of the wine which he drank.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:5

**Q2. How long were they to be nourished before they stood before the king?**  
**Answer:** Three years.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:5

**Q3. After being nourished, where were they to stand?**  
**Answer:** Before the king.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:5

---

### Daniel 1:6

**Q1. Among the children of Judah, what were the names of the four persons mentioned?**  
**Answer:** Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:6

**Q2. To which tribe did Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah belong?**  
**Answer:** Judah.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:6

**Q3. How many children of the tribe of Judah are specifically named in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Four.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:6

---

### Daniel 1:7

**Q1. Unto whom did the prince of the eunuchs give names?**  
**Answer:** Unto them.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:7

**Q2. What name did the prince of the eunuchs give unto Daniel?**  
**Answer:** Belteshazzar.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:7

**Q3. What names were given to Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah respectively?**  
**Answer:** Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:7

---

### Daniel 1:8

**Q1. What did Daniel purpose in his heart?**  
**Answer:** That he would not defile himself with the portion of the king's meat, nor with the wine which he drank.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:8

**Q2. Whom did Daniel request that he might not defile himself?**  
**Answer:** The prince of the eunuchs.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:8

**Q3. What did Daniel ask the prince of the eunuchs for regarding his defilement?**  
**Answer:** That he might not defile himself.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:8

---

### Daniel 1:9

**Q1. What did God bring Daniel into?**  
**Answer:** Favour and tender love.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:9

**Q2. In whose sight did God bring Daniel into favour and tender love?**  
**Answer:** The prince of the eunuchs.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:9

**Q3. Who brought Daniel into favour and tender love with the prince of the eunuchs?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:9

---

### Daniel 1:10

**Q1. What did the prince of the eunuchs fear concerning the king?**  
**Answer:** My lord the king, who hath appointed your meat and your drink.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:10

**Q2. If the king saw their faces worse liking than the other youths, what did the prince fear he would endanger?**  
**Answer:** His head.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:10

**Q3. Whose faces did the prince of the eunuchs fear would be seen worse liking?**  
**Answer:** The children which are your sort.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:10

---

### Daniel 1:11

**Q1. To whom did the prince of the eunuchs set over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah speak?**  
**Answer:** Melzar.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:11

**Q2. Who had set Melzar over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah?**  
**Answer:** The prince of the eunuchs.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:11

**Q3. What are the names of the four men whom the prince of the eunuchs set Melzar over?**  
**Answer:** Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:11

---

### Daniel 1:12

**Q1. How many days did Daniel ask Melzar to prove them?**  
**Answer:** Ten days.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:12

**Q2. What did Daniel ask to be given them to eat?**  
**Answer:** Pulse.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:12

**Q3. What did Daniel ask to be given them to drink?**  
**Answer:** Water.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:12

---

### Daniel 1:13

**Q1. What did Daniel ask Melzar to look upon after the ten days?**  
**Answer:** Their countenances and the countenance of the children that eat of the portion of the king's meat.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:13

**Q2. According to their countenances, how should Melzar deal with his servants?**  
**Answer:** As thou seest.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:13

**Q3. Whose meat was compared to the pulse eaten by Daniel and his companions?**  
**Answer:** The king's meat.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:13

---

### Daniel 1:14

**Q1. Whose consent did Melzar heed in this matter?**  
**Answer:** Their consent.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:14

**Q2. For how many days did Melzar prove them?**  
**Answer:** Ten days.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:14

**Q3. Who consented to them in this matter and proved them ten days?**  
**Answer:** Melzar.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:14

---

### Daniel 1:15

**Q1. At the end of ten days, how did their countenances appear compared to the children who ate the king's meat?**  
**Answer:** Fairer and fatter in flesh.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:15

**Q2. At the end of what time period were their countenances fairer and fatter?**  
**Answer:** Ten days.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:15

**Q3. Who ate the portion of the king's meat while Daniel and his friends ate pulse?**  
**Answer:** All the children which did eat the portion of the king's meat.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:15

---

### Daniel 1:16

**Q1. What did Melzar take away from them?**  
**Answer:** The portion of their meat, and the wine that they should drink.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:16

**Q2. What did Melzar give them instead of the king's meat and wine?**  
**Answer:** Pulse.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:16

**Q3. Who took away the portion of meat and wine and gave them pulse?**  
**Answer:** Melzar.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:16

---

### Daniel 1:17

**Q1. What did God give to the four children?**  
**Answer:** Knowledge and skill in all learning and wisdom.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:17

**Q2. In what specific area did God give Daniel understanding?**  
**Answer:** All visions and dreams.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:17

**Q3. Who gave knowledge and skill in all learning and wisdom to the four children?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:17

### Daniel 1:18

**Q1. At the end of the days that the king had said he should bring them in, who brought them before Nebuchadnezzar?**  
**Answer:** The prince of the eunuchs.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:18

**Q2. Before whom were the children brought at the end of the appointed days?**  
**Answer:** Nebuchadnezzar.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:18

**Q3. When were the children brought in by the prince of the eunuchs?**  
**Answer:** At the end of the days that the king had said he should bring them in.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:18

---

### Daniel 1:19

**Q1. With how many of the children did the king commune?**  
**Answer:** With them.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:19

**Q2. Among them all, who were found like unto Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah?**  
**Answer:** None.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:19

**Q3. After communing with them, where did Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah stand?**  
**Answer:** Before the king.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:19

### Daniel 1:20

**Q1. In all matters of wisdom and understanding that the king inquired of them, how many times better were they than all the magicians and astrologers?**  
**Answer:** Ten times better.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:20

**Q2. Throughout whose realm were the magicians and astrologers found?**  
**Answer:** All his realm.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:20

**Q3. Who inquired of them in all matters of wisdom and understanding?**  
**Answer:** The king.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:20

### Daniel 1:21

**Q1. Until what year of King Cyrus did Daniel continue?**  
**Answer:** The first year of king Cyrus.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:21

**Q2. Who continued unto the first year of king Cyrus?**  
**Answer:** Daniel.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:21

**Q3. Whose first year is mentioned as the time Daniel continued until?**  
**Answer:** King Cyrus.  
**Verse:** Daniel 1:21
