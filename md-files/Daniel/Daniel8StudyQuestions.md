# Daniel 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Daniel 8:1

**Q1. In the third year of the reign of which king did a vision appear unto Daniel?**  
**Answer:** Belshazzar.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:1

**Q2. To whom did a vision appear after that which appeared unto him at the first?**  
**Answer:** Unto Daniel.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:1

**Q3. What appeared unto Daniel in the third year of the reign of king Belshazzar?**  
**Answer:** A vision.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:1

---

### Daniel 8:2

**Q1. And I saw in a vision; and it came to pass, when I saw, that I was at where?**  
**Answer:** At Shushan in the palace.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:2

**Q2. In the province of what place was Shushan the palace where Daniel saw the vision?**  
**Answer:** Elam.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:2

**Q3. And I saw in a vision, and I was by what river?**  
**Answer:** Of Ulai.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:2

---

### Daniel 8:3

**Q1. Then I lifted up mine eyes, and saw, and, behold, there stood before the river what animal?**  
**Answer:** A ram.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:3

**Q2. What did the ram which Daniel saw have?**  
**Answer:** Two horns.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:3

**Q3. Which of the two horns of the ram was higher, and came up last?**  
**Answer:** The higher one came up last.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:3

---

### Daniel 8:4

**Q1. I saw the ram pushing in which directions?**  
**Answer:** Westward, and northward, and southward.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:4

**Q2. So that no beasts might stand before the ram, neither was there any what that could deliver out of his hand?**  
**Answer:** Any that could deliver out of his hand.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:4

**Q3. What did the ram do according to his will, and become great?**  
**Answer:** He did according to his will.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:4

---

### Daniel 8:5

**Q1. And as I was considering, behold, what came from the west?**  
**Answer:** An he goat.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:5

**Q2. Over the face of what did the he goat come without touching the ground?**  
**Answer:** The whole earth.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:5

**Q3. What was on the he goat between his eyes?**  
**Answer:** A notable horn.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:5

---

### Daniel 8:6

**Q1. And he came to the ram that had what?**  
**Answer:** Two horns.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:6

**Q2. Where did Daniel see the ram standing before the he goat came unto him?**  
**Answer:** Which I had seen standing before the river.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:6

**Q3. In what manner did the he goat run unto the ram in the fury of his power?**  
**Answer:** In the fury of his power.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:6

---

### Daniel 8:7

**Q1. And I saw him come close unto the ram, and he was what against him?**  
**Answer:** Moved with choler against him.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:7

**Q2. What did the he goat do to the ram and break his two horns?**  
**Answer:** Smote the ram.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:7

**Q3. Was there any power in the ram to stand before the he goat?**  
**Answer:** No.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:7

---

### Daniel 8:8

**Q1. Therefore the he goat waxed very what?**  
**Answer:** Great.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:8

**Q2. When the he goat was strong, what happened to the great horn?**  
**Answer:** It was broken.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:8

**Q3. What came up instead of the great horn toward the four winds of heaven?**  
**Answer:** Four notable ones.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:8

---

### Daniel 8:9

**Q1. And out of one of them came forth what kind of horn?**  
**Answer:** A little horn.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:9

**Q2. How did the little horn wax exceedingly great?**  
**Answer:** Toward the south, and toward the east, and toward the pleasant land.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:9

**Q3. Toward which directions did the little horn wax exceedingly great?**  
**Answer:** Toward the south, and toward the east, and toward the pleasant land.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:9

---

**Verse:** Daniel 8:10

**Q2. What did the little horn cast down to the ground?**  
**Answer:** Some of the host and of the stars.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:10

**Q3. What did the little horn do to some of the host and of the stars?**  
**Answer:** Stamped upon them.  
**Verse:** Daniel 8:10

### Daniel 8:13

**Q1. Then I heard one saint speaking, and another saint said unto whom that spake?**

**Q2. What shall be cleansed after two thousand and three hundred days?**
