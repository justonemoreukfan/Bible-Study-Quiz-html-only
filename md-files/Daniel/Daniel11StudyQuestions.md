# Daniel 11 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Daniel 11:1

**Q1. In what year of Darius the Mede did the speaker stand to confirm and to strengthen him?**  
**Answer:** The first year.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:1

**Q2. Who stood to confirm and to strengthen Darius the Mede?**  
**Answer:** I (referring to the angel/speaker).  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:1

**Q3. Whose first year was it when the speaker stood to confirm and strengthen Darius?**  
**Answer:** Darius the Mede.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:1

---

### Daniel 11:2

**Q1. What did the speaker say he would show Daniel?**  
**Answer:** The truth.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:2

**Q2. How many more kings shall stand up in Persia?**  
**Answer:** Three kings.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:2

**Q3. Which king shall be far richer than they all, and stir up all against the realm of Grecia?**  
**Answer:** The fourth.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:2

---

### Daniel 11:3

**Q1. What kind of king shall stand up that shall rule with great dominion?**  
**Answer:** A mighty king.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:3

**Q2. How shall the mighty king rule?**  
**Answer:** According to his will.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:3

**Q3. When he shall stand up, what will happen to his kingdom?**  
**Answer:** It shall be broken, and shall be divided toward the four winds of heaven.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:3

---

### Daniel 11:4

**Q1. To whom shall the kingdom not be divided?**  
**Answer:** Not to his posterity.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:4

**Q2. According to what shall the kingdom be plucked up?**  
**Answer:** According to his dominion which he had.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:4

**Q3. Among whom shall the kingdom be divided, besides others?**  
**Answer:** Even for those other than his posterity (toward the four winds).  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:4

---

### Daniel 11:5

**Q1. Who shall be strong, even one of the princes of the king of the south?**  
**Answer:** The king of the south.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:5

**Q2. Who else shall be strong above the king of the south?**  
**Answer:** And another of his princes.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:5

**Q3. Whose dominion shall be a great dominion?**  
**Answer:** His dominion (the prince's dominion).  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:5

---

### Daniel 11:6

**Q1. In the end of years, what shall the king of the south and the king of the north join themselves to?**  
**Answer:** They shall join themselves together.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:6

**Q2. Who shall come to the king of the north to make an agreement?**  
**Answer:** The king's daughter of the south.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:6

**Q3. What will happen to the power of the arm (or the king's seed) after she comes?**  
**Answer:** She shall not retain the power of the arm; neither shall he stand, nor his arm.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:6

---

### Daniel 11:7

**Q1. Out of a branch of her roots shall one stand up in his estate, who shall come with an army and do what?**  
**Answer:** Enter into the fortress of the king of the north.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:7

---

### Daniel 11:7 (Continued)

**Q2. What shall this one deal against the king of the north?**  
**Answer:** Deal against them.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:7

**Q3. What shall he prevail against?**  
**Answer:** Shall prevail.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:7

---

### Daniel 11:8

**Q1. What else shall he carry captive into Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Their gods, with their princes, and with their precious vessels of silver and of gold.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:8

**Q2. How long shall he continue more than the king of the north?**  
**Answer:** He shall continue more years than the king of the north.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:8

**Q3. What shall he carry captive into Egypt besides princes and vessels?**  
**Answer:** Their gods.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:8

---

### Daniel 11:9

**Q1. Into whose kingdom shall the king of the south come?**  
**Answer:** Into the kingdom of the king of the north.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:9

**Q2. What shall the king of the south do after entering the kingdom?**  
**Answer:** Shall return into his own land.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:9

**Q3. Who shall come into the kingdom?**  
**Answer:** The king of the south.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:9

---

### Daniel 11:10

**Q1. Who shall be stirred up, and assemble a multitude of great forces?**  
**Answer:** His sons (the sons of the king of the north).  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:10

**Q2. What shall one come on and do, like a flood?**  
**Answer:** Overflow and pass through.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:10

**Q3. To where shall he return and be stirred up, even to his fortress?**  
**Answer:** Then shall he return, and be stirred up, even to his fortress.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:10

---

### Daniel 11:11

**Q1. Who shall be moved with choler, and come forth and fight with him?**  
**Answer:** The king of the south.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:11

**Q2. With whom shall the king of the south fight?**  
**Answer:** With the king of the north.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:11

**Q3. What shall the king of the north set forth, which shall be given into the king of the south's hand?**  
**Answer:** A great multitude.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:11

---

### Daniel 11:12

**Q1. When the multitude is taken away, how shall his heart be lifted up?**  
**Answer:** His heart shall be lifted up.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:12

**Q2. How many shall he cast down?**  
**Answer:** Many ten thousands.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:12

**Q3. Even though he casts down many ten thousands, what shall he not do?**  
**Answer:** He shall not be strengthened by it.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:12

---

### Daniel 11:13

**Q1. Who shall return and set forth a multitude greater than the former?**  
**Answer:** The king of the north.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:13

**Q2. After certain times, how shall the king of the north come?**  
**Answer:** With a great army and with much riches.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:13

**Q3. What kind of army shall he come with?**  
**Answer:** A great army.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:13

---

### Daniel 11:14

**Q1. In those times, who shall stand up against the king of the south?**  
**Answer:** Many.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:14

**Q2. Who also shall exalt themselves to establish the vision?**  
**Answer:** Also the robbers of thy people.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:14

**Q3. What will happen to those who exalt themselves to establish the vision?**  
**Answer:** They shall fall.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:14

---

### Daniel 11:15

**Q1. Who shall come, and cast up a mount, and take the most fenced cities?**  
**Answer:** The king of the north.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:15

**Q2. What shall the arms of the south not withstand?**  
**Answer:** Neither his chosen people.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:15

**Q3. What shall neither withstand the king of the north?**  
**Answer:** Neither shall there be any strength to withstand.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:15

---

### Daniel 11:16

**Q1. According to what shall he that cometh against him do unto himself?**  
**Answer:** According to his own will.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:16

**Q2. Who shall stand before him?**  
**Answer:** None.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:16

**Q3. In whose land shall he stand, which by his hand shall be consumed?**  
**Answer:** In the glorious land.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:16

---

### Daniel 11:17

**Q1. With whose strength shall he set his face to enter?**  
**Answer:** With the strength of his whole kingdom.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:17

**Q2. What shall he do with the king of the south, giving him the daughter of women to corrupt her?**  
**Answer:** Upright ones with him (or: thus shall he do).  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:17

**Q3. Did she stand on his side, or be for him?**  
**Answer:** She shall not stand on his side, neither be for him.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:17

---

### Daniel 11:18

**Q1. Unto what shall he turn his face?**  
**Answer:** Unto the isles.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:18

**Q2. What shall he take?**  
**Answer:** Many.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:18

**Q3. What shall a prince cause to cease against him, without his own reproach?**  
**Answer:** The reproach offered by him.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:18

---

### Daniel 11:19

**Q1. To what shall he turn his face after causing the reproach to cease?**  
**Answer:** Toward the fort of his own land.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:19

**Q2. What will happen to him when he turns toward the fort of his land?**  
**Answer:** He shall stumble and fall, and not be found.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:19

**Q3. Shall he be found after he stumbles and falls?**  
**Answer:** And not be found.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:19

---

### Daniel 11:20

**Q1. Who shall stand up in his estate, raising a raiser of taxes in the glory of the kingdom?**  
**Answer:** Then shall stand up in his estate one.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:20

**Q2. Within how many days shall he be destroyed?**  
**Answer:** Within a few days.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:20

**Q3. How shall he be destroyed, though not in anger nor in battle?**  
**Answer:** Not in anger, nor in battle.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:20

---

**Answer:** A vile person.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:21

**Q2. How shall the vile person come in?**  
**Answer:** Peaceably.

**Verse:** Daniel 11:21

**Answer:** Strong.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:23

### Daniel 11:24

**Q1. How shall he enter even upon the peaceable and fat places of the province?**  
**Answer:** Peaceably.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:24

**Q2. What shall he do that which his fathers have not done, nor his fathers' fathers?**  
**Answer:** He shall do that which his fathers have not done, nor his fathers' fathers.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:24

**Answer:** Prey, and spoil, and riches.

**Answer:** Against the king of the south.  
**Verse:** Daniel 11:25

**Verse:** Daniel 11:25
