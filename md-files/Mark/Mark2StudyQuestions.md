# Mark 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Mark 2:1

**Q1. Where did Jesus enter into after some days?**  
**Answer:** Into Capernaum.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:1

**Q2. What was noised about concerning Jesus?**  
**Answer:** That he was in the house.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:1

---

### Mark 2:2

**Q3. How many were gathered together according to verse 2?**  
**Answer:** Straightway many were gathered together.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:2

**Q4. Where was there no room left, no, not so much as about the door?**  
**Answer:** Insomuch that there was no room to receive them.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:2

**Q5. What did Jesus do unto them?**  
**Answer:** He preached the word unto them.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:2

---

### Mark 2:3

**Q6. Who came unto Jesus bringing one sick of the palsy?**  
**Answer:** Certain men bringing one sick of the palsy.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:3

**Q7. How many men bore the man sick of the palsy?**  
**Answer:** Four.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:3

---

### Mark 2:4

**Q8. Why could they not come nigh unto Jesus for the press?**  
**Answer:** Because of the press.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:4

**Q9. What did they uncover where Jesus was?**  
**Answer:** The roof.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:4

**Q10. What did they let down when they had broken it up?**  
**Answer:** The bed wherein the sick of the palsy lay.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:4

---

### Mark 2:5

**Q11. What did Jesus see when he saw their faith?**  
**Answer:** Their faith.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:5

**Q12. What did Jesus say unto the sick of the palsy?**  
**Answer:** Son, thy sins be forgiven thee.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:5

---

### Mark 2:6

**Q13. Who were sitting there and reasoning in their hearts?**  
**Answer:** Certain of the scribes.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:6

---

### Mark 2:7

**Q14. What did the scribes say this man doth speak thus?**  
**Answer:** Blasphemies.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:7

**Q15. Who can forgive sins but one?**  
**Answer:** God only.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:7

---

### Mark 2:8

**Q16. What did Jesus perceive in his spirit that they so reasoned within themselves?**  
**Answer:** Immediately.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:8

**Q17. What did Jesus ask them regarding their thoughts?**  
**Answer:** Why reason ye these things in your hearts?  
**Verse:** Mark 2:8

---

### Mark 2:9

**Q18. What was easier to say to the sick of the palsy?**  
**Answer:** Thy sins be forgiven thee; or to say, Arise, and take up thy bed, and walk?  
**Verse:** Mark 2:9

---

### Mark 2:10

**Q19. Who has power on earth to forgive sins?**  
**Answer:** The Son of man.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:10

**Q20. What did Jesus say to the sick of the palsy regarding this power?**  
**Answer:** He saith to the sick of the palsy.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:10

---

### Mark 2:11

**Q21. What three commands did Jesus give to the sick of the palsy?**  
**Answer:** Arise, and take up thy bed, and go thy way into thine house.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:11

---

### Mark 2:12

**Q22. What did the man do immediately after the command?**  
**Answer:** Arose, took up the bed, and went forth before them all.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:12

**Q23. How did the people react to this miracle?**  
**Answer:** Insomuch that they were all amazed, and glorified God.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:12

**Q24. What did the people say because they never saw it on this fashion?**  
**Answer:** We never saw it on this fashion.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:12

---

### Mark 2:13

**Q25. Where did Jesus go again?**  
**Answer:** Forth by the sea side.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:13

**Q26. Who resorted unto Jesus?**  
**Answer:** All the multitude.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:13

**Q27. What did Jesus do when they resorted unto him?**  
**Answer:** He taught them.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:13

---

### Mark 2:14

**Q28. As Jesus passed by, whom did he see sitting at the receipt of custom?**  
**Answer:** Levi the son of Alphaeus.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:14

**Q29. What did Jesus say unto Levi?**  
**Answer:** Follow me.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:14

**Q30. What did Levi do after Jesus spoke to him?**  
**Answer:** He arose and followed him.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:14

---

### Mark 2:15

**Q31. Where did Jesus sit at meat in Levi's house?**  
**Answer:** In his house.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:15

**Q32. Who sat also together with Jesus and his disciples?**  
**Answer:** Many publicans and sinners.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:15

**Q33. Why did many publicans and sinners sit together with Jesus and his disciples?**  
**Answer:** For there were many, and they followed him.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:15

---

### Mark 2:16

**Q34. When the scribes and Pharisees saw Jesus eat with publicans and sinners, what did they say unto his disciples?**  
**Answer:** How is it that he eateth and drinketh with publicans and sinners?  
**Verse:** Mark 2:16

---

### Mark 2:17

**Q35. When Jesus heard it, what did he saith unto them?**  
**Answer:** They that are whole have no need of the physician, but they that are sick.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:17

**Q36. Whom did Jesus say he came not to call?**  
**Answer:** The righteous.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:17

**Q37. Whom did Jesus say he came to call?**  
**Answer:** Sinners to repentance.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:17

---

### Mark 2:18

**Q38. Who used to fast?**  
**Answer:** The disciples of John and of the Pharisees.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:18

**Q39. What question did they come and ask Jesus regarding fasting?**  
**Answer:** Why do the disciples of John and of the Pharisees fast, but thy disciples fast not?  
**Verse:** Mark 2:18

---

### Mark 2:19

**Q40. Who cannot fast while the bridegroom is with them?**  
**Answer:** The children of the bridechamber.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:19

**Q41. How long do they have the bridegroom with them?**  
**Answer:** As long as they have the bridegroom with them.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:19

---

### Mark 2:20

**Q42. When will the days come that the bridegroom shall be taken away from them?**  
**Answer:** But the days will come, when the bridegroom shall be taken away from them.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:20

**Q43. What will the children of the bridechamber do in those days?**  
**Answer:** And then shall they fast in those days.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:20

---

### Mark 2:21

**Q44. What do men not sew upon an old garment?**  
**Answer:** A piece of new cloth on an old garment.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:21

**Q45. What happens if the new piece that filled it up takes away from the old?**  
**Answer:** The rent is made worse.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:21

---

### Mark 2:22

**Q46. Where do men not put new wine?**  
**Answer:** Into old bottles.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:22

**Q47. What will happen to the new wine if put into old bottles?**  
**Answer:** The new wine doth burst the bottles, and the wine is spilled, and the bottles will be marred.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:22

**Q48. Where must new wine be put?**  
**Answer:** Into new bottles.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:22

---

### Mark 2:23

**Q49. Through what did Jesus go on the sabbath day?**  
**Answer:** Through the corn fields.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:23

**Q50. What did his disciples begin to do as they went?**  
**Answer:** To pluck the ears of corn.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:23

---

### Mark 2:24

**Q51. What did the Pharisees say unto Jesus on the sabbath day?**  
**Answer:** Behold, why do they on the sabbath day that which is not lawful?  
**Verse:** Mark 2:24

---

### Mark 2:25

**Q52. What did Jesus say unto them about what David did?**  
**Answer:** Have ye never read what David did, when he had need, and was an hungred, he, and they that were with him?  
**Verse:** Mark 2:25

---

### Mark 2:26

**Q53. In whose days did David go into the house of God?**  
**Answer:** In the days of Abiathar the high priest.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:26

**Q54. What did David eat that was not lawful to eat save for the priests?**  
**Answer:** The shewbread.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:26

**Q55. To whom else did David give the shewbread?**  
**Answer:** To them which were with him.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:26

---

### Mark 2:27

**Q56. For whom was the sabbath made?**  
**Answer:** For man.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:27

**Q57. Was man made for the sabbath?**  
**Answer:** Not man for the sabbath.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:27

---

### Mark 2:28

**Q58. Who is Lord also of the sabbath?**  
**Answer:** The Son of man.  
**Verse:** Mark 2:28
