# Mark 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Mark 12:1

**Q1. What kind of parable did Jesus begin to speak unto them?**  
**Answer:** By parables.  
**Verse:** Mark 12:1

**Q2. What did a certain man plant around his vineyard?**  
**Answer:** A hedge.  
**Verse:** Mark 12:1

**Q3. What did the man build in his vineyard and to whom let he it out?**  
**Answer:** A tower, and let it out to husbandmen.  
**Verse:** Mark 12:1

---

### Mark 12:2

**Q1. When did the man send a servant to the husbandmen?**  
**Answer:** At the season.  
**Verse:** Mark 12:2

**Q2. From whom did the servant receive the fruit of the vineyard?**  
**Answer:** From the husbandmen.  
**Verse:** Mark 12:2

**Q3. Whose fruit was the servant sent to receive?**  
**Answer:** Of the fruit of the vineyard.  
**Verse:** Mark 12:2

---

### Mark 12:3

**Q1. What did they catch when they took the servant?**  
**Answer:** Him.  
**Verse:** Mark 12:3

**Q2. What did they do to the servant after catching him?**  
**Answer:** Beat him.  
**Verse:** Mark 12:3

**Q3. How did they send the servant away?**  
**Answer:** Away empty.  
**Verse:** Mark 12:3

---

### Mark 12:4

**Q1. Whom did the man send unto them again?**  
**Answer:** Another servant.  
**Verse:** Mark 12:4

**Q2. At him they did what, casting stones or something else?**  
**Answer:** Cast stones at him.  
**Verse:** Mark 12:4

**Q3. What else did they do unto him besides wounding him at the head?**  
**Answer:** Sent him away shamefully handled.  
**Verse:** Mark 12:4

---

### Mark 12:5

**Q1. Whom else did the man send after the others?**  
**Answer:** Another.  
**Verse:** Mark 12:5

**Answer:** Killed him.  
**Verse:** Mark 12:5

**Answer:** Many others.  
**Verse:** Mark 12:5

---

### Mark 12:6

**Verse:** Mark 12:6

### Mark 12:7

**Verse:** Mark 12:7

### Mark 12:8

**Verse:** Mark 12:8
