# Mark 13 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Mark 13:1

**Q1. As he went out of the temple, what did one of his disciples say unto him?**  
**Answer:** Master, see what manner of stones and what buildings are here!  
**Verse:** Mark 13:1

**Q2. Where was Jesus going when this was said to him?**  
**Answer:** Out of the temple.  
**Verse:** Mark 13:1

**Q3. Who spoke to Jesus about the stones and buildings?**  
**Answer:** One of his disciples.  
**Verse:** Mark 13:1

---

### Mark 13:2

**Q1. What did Jesus answering say unto him concerning the great buildings?**  
**Answer:** Seest thou these great buildings? there shall not be left one stone upon another, that shall not be thrown down.  
**Verse:** Mark 13:2

**Q2. What would not be left one upon another?**  
**Answer:** One stone.  
**Verse:** Mark 13:2

**Q3. What would happen to every stone of the buildings?**  
**Answer:** It shall not be left one stone upon another, that shall not be thrown down.  
**Verse:** Mark 13:2

---

### Mark 13:3

**Q1. Where did Jesus sit as he sat upon the mount of Olives?**  
**Answer:** Over against the temple.  
**Verse:** Mark 13:3

**Answer:** Peter and James and John and Andrew.  
**Verse:** Mark 13:3

**Q3. When did Peter, James, John, and Andrew ask him privately?**  
**Answer:** As he sat upon the mount of Olives over against the temple.  
**Verse:** Mark 13:3
