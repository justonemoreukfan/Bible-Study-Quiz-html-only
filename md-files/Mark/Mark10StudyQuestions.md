# Mark 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Mark 10:1

**Q1. From whence did Jesus arise and come into the coasts of Judaea by the farther side of Jordan?**  
**Answer:** From thence.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:1

**Q2. Who resorted unto Jesus again as he was accustomed?**  
**Answer:** The people.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:1

**Q3. What did Jesus do again unto the people, as he was wont?**  
**Answer:** He taught them.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:1

---

### Mark 10:2

**Q1. Who came to Jesus and asked him a question?**  
**Answer:** The Pharisees.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:2

**Q2. To tempt Jesus, what did the Pharisees ask him if it was lawful for a man to do?**  
**Answer:** To put away his wife.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:2

**Q3. What was the purpose of the Pharisees asking Jesus this question?**  
**Answer:** To tempt him.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:2

---

### Mark 10:3

**Q1. What did Jesus answer and say unto the Pharisees?**  
**Answer:** What did Moses command you?  
**Verse:** Mark 10:3

---

### Mark 10:4

**Q1. What did the Pharisees say Moses suffered to write?**  
**Answer:** A bill of divorcement, and to put her away.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:4

---

### Mark 10:5

**Q1. And Jesus answered and said unto them, For the hardness of your heart he wrote you what?**  
**Answer:** This precept.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:5

---

### Mark 10:6

**Q1. At the beginning of the creation, what did God make them?**  
**Answer:** Male and female.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:6

---

### Mark 10:7

**Q1. For this cause shall a man leave what?**  
**Answer:** His father and mother.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:7

**Q2. To whom shall a man cleave?**  
**Answer:** His wife.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:7

---

### Mark 10:8

**Q1. What shall the twain be?**  
**Answer:** One flesh.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:8

**Q2. Because they are no more twain, what are they?**  
**Answer:** One flesh.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:8

---

### Mark 10:9

**Q1. What therefore God hath joined together, who should not put asunder?**  
**Answer:** Let not man put asunder.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:9

---

### Mark 10:10

**Q1. When they were in the house, who asked Jesus again of the same matter?**  
**Answer:** His disciples.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:10

---

### Mark 10:11

**Q1. Whosoever shall put away his wife, and marry another, committeth adultery against whom?**  
**Answer:** Her.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:11

---

### Mark 10:12

**Q1. If a woman shall put away her husband, and be married to another, what does she commit?**  
**Answer:** Adultery.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:12

---

### Mark 10:13

**Q1. Who were brought to Jesus that he should touch them?**  
**Answer:** Young children.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:13

**Q2. What did his disciples do to those that brought the young children?**  
**Answer:** Rebuked them.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:13

---

### Mark 10:14

**Q1. When Jesus saw it, how was he affected?**  
**Answer:** Much displeased.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:14

**Q2. What did Jesus tell them to suffer the little children to do?**  
**Answer:** Come unto me.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:14

**Q3. Of such is what?**  
**Answer:** The kingdom of God.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:14

---

### Mark 10:15

**Q1. Verily I say unto you, Whosoever shall not receive the kingdom of God as what, he shall not enter therein?**  
**Answer:** A little child.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:15

---

### Mark 10:16

**Q1. How did Jesus take the children up?**  
**Answer:** In his arms.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:16

**Q2. What did Jesus put upon the children?**  
**Answer:** His hands.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:16

**Q3. What did Jesus do to the children after putting his hands upon them?**  
**Answer:** Blessed them.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:16

---

### Mark 10:17

**Q1. When he was gone forth into the way, who came running and kneeled to him?**  
**Answer:** One.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:17

**Q2. What did the man ask Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Good Master, what shall I do that I may inherit eternal life?  
**Verse:** Mark 10:17

---

### Mark 10:18

**Q1. What did Jesus say unto him regarding being good?**  
**Answer:** Why callest thou me good? there is none good but one, that is, God.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:18

---

### Mark 10:19

**Q1. Which commandments does Jesus list to the man?**  
**Answer:** Do not commit adultery, Do not kill, Do not steal, Do not bear false witness, Defraud not, Honour thy father and mother.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:19

---

### Mark 10:20

**Q1. What did the man answer and say unto Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Master, all these have I observed from my youth.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:20

---

### Mark 10:21

**Q1. Then Jesus beholding him did what?**  
**Answer:** Loved him.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:21

**Q2. What did Jesus tell the man he lacked?**  
**Answer:** One thing.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:21

**Q3. What did Jesus tell him to go and do with his goods?**  
**Answer:** Sell whatsoever thou hast, and give to the poor.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:21

---

### Mark 10:22

**Q1. How was the man affected by that saying?**  
**Answer:** Sad.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:22

**Q2. Why went the man away grieved?**  
**Answer:** For he had great possessions.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:22

---

### Mark 10:23

**Q1. When Jesus looked round about, what did he say unto his disciples?**  
**Answer:** How hardly shall they that have riches enter into the kingdom of God!  
**Verse:** Mark 10:23

---

### Mark 10:24

**Q1. Who were astonished at Jesus' words?**  
**Answer:** The disciples.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:24

**Q2. What did Jesus answer again and say unto them?**  
**Answer:** Children, how hard is it for them that trust in riches to enter into the kingdom of God!  
**Verse:** Mark 10:24

---

### Mark 10:25

**Q1. What is easier for a camel to go through than for a rich man to enter into the kingdom of God?**  
**Answer:** The eye of a needle.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:25

---

### Mark 10:26

**Q1. How were the disciples affected beyond measure?**  
**Answer:** Astonished out of measure.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:26

**Q2. What did the disciples say among themselves?**  
**Answer:** Who then can be saved?  
**Verse:** Mark 10:26

---

### Mark 10:27

**Q1. Looking upon them, what did Jesus say?**  
**Answer:** With men it is impossible, but not with God.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:27

**Q2. What are possible with God?**  
**Answer:** All things.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:27

---

### Mark 10:28

**Q1. Who began to say unto him, Lo, we have left all, and have followed thee?**  
**Answer:** Peter.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:28

---

### Mark 10:29

**Q1. Jesus answered and said, Verily I say unto you, There is no man that has left what for my sake, and the gospel's?**  
**Answer:** House, or brethren, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or lands.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:29

---

### Mark 10:30

**Q1. How much shall he receive now in this time?**  
**Answer:** An hundredfold.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:30

**Q2. What specific things shall he receive now in this time, with persecutions?**  
**Answer:** Houses, and brethren, and sisters, and mothers, and children, and lands.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:30

---

### Mark 10:31

**Q1. Who shall be first?**  
**Answer:** Many that are first.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:31

**Q2. Who shall be last?**  
**Answer:** The last.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:31

---

### Mark 10:32

**Q1. As they were in the way going up to Jerusalem, who went before them?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:32

**Q2. How were they affected as they followed?**  
**Answer:** They were amazed, and as they followed, they were afraid.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:32

**Q3. Whom did Jesus take again, and begin to tell what things should happen unto him?**  
**Answer:** The twelve.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:32

---

### Mark 10:33

**Q1. Where did Jesus say they were going up?**  
**Answer:** To Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:33

**Q2. To whom shall the Son of man be delivered?**  
**Answer:** Unto the chief priests, and unto the scribes.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:33

**Q3. What sentence shall they condemn the Son of man to?**  
**Answer:** Death.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:33

---

### Mark 10:34

**Q1. Who shall mock the Son of man, and scourge him, and spit upon him, and kill him?**  
**Answer:** The Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:34

**Q2. On what day shall the Son of man rise again?**  
**Answer:** The third day.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:34

---

### Mark 10:35

**Q1. Who came unto Jesus, saying, Master, we would that thou shouldest do for us whatsoever we shall desire?**  
**Answer:** James and John, the sons of Zebedee.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:35

---

### Mark 10:36

**Q1. What did Jesus say unto James and John?**  
**Answer:** What would ye that I should do for you?  
**Verse:** Mark 10:36

---

### Mark 10:37

**Q1. Where did James and John ask to sit in his glory?**  
**Answer:** One on thy right hand, and the other on thy left hand.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:37

---

### Mark 10:38

**Q1. What did Jesus say they knew not?**  
**Answer:** What ye ask.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:38

**Q2. What did Jesus ask if they were able to drink?**  
**Answer:** The cup that I drink of.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:38

**Q3. What did Jesus ask if they were able to be baptized with?**  
**Answer:** With the baptism that I am baptized with.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:38

---

### Mark 10:39

**Q1. What did James and John say unto Jesus regarding being able?**  
**Answer:** We can.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:39

**Q2. What did Jesus say they shall indeed drink of?**  
**Answer:** The cup that I drink of.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:39

**Q3. With what shall they be baptized?**  
**Answer:** With the baptism that I am baptized withal.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:39

---

### Mark 10:40

**Q1. Whose is it not to give to sit on Jesus' right hand and left hand?**  
**Answer:** Mine to give.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:40

**Q2. For whom is it prepared?**  
**Answer:** For them for whom it is prepared.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:40

---

### Mark 10:41

**Q1. When the ten heard it, how did they begin to be displeased?**  
**Answer:** With James and John.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:41

---

### Mark 10:42

**Q1. Calling them to him, what did Jesus say they which are accounted to rule over the Gentiles do?**

**Verse:** Mark 10:42

**Q2. What do their great ones exercise upon them?**  
**Answer:** Authority upon them.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:42

### Mark 10:43

**Q1. Among the disciples, how should it not be?**  
**Answer:** So.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:43

**Q2. Whosoever will be great among you, what shall he be?**  
**Answer:** Your minister.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:43

---

### Mark 10:44

**Q1. Whosoever of you will be the chief, what shall he be?**  
**Answer:** Servant of all.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:44

---

### Mark 10:45

**Q1. What did the Son of man not come to be ministered unto?**  
**Answer:** To be ministered unto.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:45

---

### Mark 10:46

**Q1. To what city did they come?**  
**Answer:** To Jericho.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:46

**Q2. Who sat by the highway side begging as Jesus went out of Jericho?**  
**Answer:** Blind Bartimaeus, the son of Timæus.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:46

---

### Mark 10:47

**Q1. When Bartimaeus heard that it was Jesus of Nazareth, what did he begin to cry out?**  
**Answer:** Jesus, thou son of David, have mercy on me.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:47

---

### Mark 10:48

**Q1. Who charged Bartimaeus that he should hold his peace?**  
**Answer:** Many.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:48

**Q2. How much more did Bartimaeus cry?**  
**Answer:** A great deal more.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:48

**Q3. What did Bartimaeus repeat when he cried a great deal more?**  
**Answer:** Thou son of David, have mercy on me.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:48

---

### Mark 10:49

**Q1. What did Jesus do and command Bartimaeus to be called?**  
**Answer:** Stood still, and commanded him to be called.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:49

**Q2. What did they say unto the blind man?**  
**Answer:** Be of good comfort, rise; he calleth thee.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:49

---

### Mark 10:50

**Q1. What did Bartimaeus cast away?**  
**Answer:** His garment.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:50

**Q2. How did Bartimaeus rise and come to Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Rose, and came to Jesus.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:50

---

### Mark 10:51

**Q1. What did Jesus answer and say unto Bartimaeus?**  
**Answer:** What wilt thou that I should do unto thee?  
**Verse:** Mark 10:51

**Q2. What did the blind man say unto him?**  
**Answer:** Lord, that I might receive my sight.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:51

---

### Mark 10:52

**Q1. What did Jesus say unto Bartimaeus?**  
**Answer:** Go thy way; thy faith hath made thee whole.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:52

**Q2. Immediately what happened to Bartimaeus's sight?**  
**Answer:** He received his sight.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:52

**Q3. Whom did Bartimaeus follow in the way?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Mark 10:52
