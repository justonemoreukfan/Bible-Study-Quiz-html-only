# Mark 16 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Mark 16:1

**Q1. When the sabbath was past, who bought sweet spices?**  
**Answer:** Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James, and Salome.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:1

**Q2. Why did Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome buy sweet spices?**  
**Answer:** That they might come and anoint him.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:1

**Q3. When did they buy the sweet spices?**  
**Answer:** When the sabbath was past.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:1

---

### Mark 16:2

**Q4. When did they come unto the sepulchre?**  
**Answer:** Very early in the morning the first day of the week.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:2

**Q5. What time of day did they arrive at the sepulchre?**  
**Answer:** At the rising of the sun.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:2

**Q6. What day of the week was it when they came unto the sepulchre?**  
**Answer:** The first day of the week.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:2

---

### Mark 16:3

**Q7. What did the women say among themselves on their way?**  
**Answer:** Who shall roll us away the stone from the door of the sepulchre?  
**Verse:** Mark 16:3

**Q8. What object were they concerned about rolling away?**  
**Answer:** The stone.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:3

**Q9. Where was the stone located?**  
**Answer:** From the door of the sepulchre.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:3

---

### Mark 16:4

**Q10. When they looked, what did they see?**  
**Answer:** That the stone was rolled away.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:4

**Q11. How does the verse describe the stone?**  
**Answer:** It was very great.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:4

**Q12. What was the state of the stone when they looked?**  
**Answer:** It was rolled away.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:4

---

### Mark 16:5

**Q13. Where did they enter?**  
**Answer:** Into the sepulchre.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:5

**Q14. Whom did they see sitting on the right side?**  
**Answer:** A young man clothed in a long white garment.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:5

**Q15. How did the young man affect the women?**  
**Answer:** They were affrighted.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:5

---

### Mark 16:6

**Q16. What did the young man tell the women not to be?**  
**Answer:** Affrighted.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:6

**Q17. Whom did the young man say they seek?**  
**Answer:** Jesus of Nazareth, which was crucified.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:6

**Q18. What did the young man declare about Jesus?**  
**Answer:** He is risen; he is not here.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:6

---

### Mark 16:7

**Q19. Whom did the young man tell them to go and tell?**  
**Answer:** His disciples and Peter.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:7

**Q20. Where did the young man say Jesus goeth before them?**  
**Answer:** Into Galilee.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:7

**Q21. What did the young man say they should see?**  
**Answer:** Him, as he said unto you.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:7

---

### Mark 16:8

**Q22. How did the women go out quickly from the sepulchre?**  
**Answer:** With trembling and amazement.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:8

**Q23. To whom did the women say nothing?**  
**Answer:** Any man.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:8

**Q24. Why did they say nothing to any man?**  
**Answer:** For they were afraid.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:8

---

### Mark 16:9

**Q25. When Jesus was risen early, on what day did he appear first?**  
**Answer:** The first day of the week.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:9

**Q26. To whom did Jesus appear first after rising?**  
**Answer:** Mary Magdalene.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:9

**Q27. What had Jesus cast out of Mary Magdalene previously?**  
**Answer:** Seven devils.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:9

---

### Mark 16:10

**Q28. What did Mary Magdalene do after seeing Jesus?**  
**Answer:** She went and told them that had been with him.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:10

**Q29. What state were those with him in when she told them?**  
**Answer:** They mourned and wept.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:10

**Q30. Who told them that Jesus was alive?**  
**Answer:** Mary Magdalene.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:10

---

### Mark 16:11

**Q31. When they had heard that he was alive and had been seen of her, did they believe?**  
**Answer:** They believed not.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:11

**Q32. Who told them that Jesus had been seen of her?**  
**Answer:** Mary Magdalene.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:11

**Q33. What did they hear regarding Jesus?**  
**Answer:** That he was alive, and had been seen of her.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:11

---

### Mark 16:12

**Q34. After that, how did Jesus appear unto two of them?**  
**Answer:** In another form.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:12

**Q35. To how many did he appear in another form?**  
**Answer:** Two of them.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:12

**Q36. Where were the two walking?**  
**Answer:** As they walked, and went into the country.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:12

---

### Mark 16:13

**Q37. What did the two do upon returning?**  
**Answer:** Told it unto the residue.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:13

**Q38. Did the residue believe the report of the two?**  
**Answer:** Neither believed they them.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:13

**Q39. What did they do regarding the report?**  
**Answer:** Told it unto the residue: neither believed they them.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:13

---

### Mark 16:14

**Q40. Afterward, unto whom did Jesus appear?**  
**Answer:** As they sat at meat.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:14

**Q41. How many disciples were present when he appeared unto them as they sat at meat?**  
**Answer:** The eleven.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:14

**Q42. What did Jesus upbraid them for?**  
**Answer:** Their unbelief and hardness of heart.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:14

---

### Mark 16:15

**Q43. What did Jesus command them to go into?**  
**Answer:** All the world.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:15

**Q44. What did he tell them to preach?**  
**Answer:** The gospel.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:15

**Q45. To whom were they to preach the gospel?**  
**Answer:** Every creature.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:15

---

### Mark 16:16

**Q46. Who shall be saved?**  
**Answer:** He that believeth and is baptized.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:16

**Q47. What happens to him that believeth not?**  
**Answer:** He shall be damned.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:16

**Q48. What is required along with belief for salvation in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Baptism.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:16

---

### Mark 16:17

**Q49. What signs shall follow them that believe?**  
**Answer:** In my name shall they cast out devils; they shall speak with new tongues.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:17

**Q50. Whose name shall they use to cast out devils?**  
**Answer:** In my name.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:17

**Q51. What kind of tongues shall they speak with?**  
**Answer:** New tongues.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:17

---

### Mark 16:18

**Q52. What shall they take up?**  
**Answer:** Serpents.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:18

**Q53. What happens if they drink any deadly thing?**  
**Answer:** It shall not hurt them.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:18

**Q54. Upon whom shall they lay hands, and what shall happen?**  
**Answer:** They shall lay hands on the sick, and they shall recover.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:18

---

### Mark 16:19

**Q55. After the Lord had spoken unto them, where was he received?**  
**Answer:** Up into heaven.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:19

**Q56. Where did Jesus sit after being received up into heaven?**  
**Answer:** On the right hand of God.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:19

**Q57. Who was received up into heaven?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:19

---

### Mark 16:20

**Q58. Where did they go forth and preach?**  
**Answer:** Everywhere.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:20

**Q59. Who worked with them and confirmed the word?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:20

**Q60. What followed the word to confirm it?**  
**Answer:** Signs following.  
**Verse:** Mark 16:20
