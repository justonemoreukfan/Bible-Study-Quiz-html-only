# Mark 14 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Mark 14:1

**Q1. After how many days was the feast of the passover, and of unleavened bread?**  
**Answer:** Two days.  
**Verse:** Mark 14:1

**Q2. What did the chief priests and the scribes seek to do by craft?**  
**Answer:** Take him [Jesus] by craft, and put him to death.  
**Verse:** Mark 14:1

**Q3. When did the chief priests and the scribes say they should not take him?**  
**Answer:** On the feast day.  
**Verse:** Mark 14:1

---

### Mark 14:2

**Q4. What did they say they should not do on the feast day?**  
**Answer:** Not on the feast day.  
**Verse:** Mark 14:2

**Q5. Why did they say they should not take him on the feast day?**  
**Answer:** Lest there be an uproar of the people.  
**Verse:** Mark 14:2

**Q6. What event are the feast day and the people associated with in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The feast day.  
**Verse:** Mark 14:2

---

### Mark 14:3

**Q7. Where was Jesus while being in the house of Simon the leper?**  
**Answer:** In Bethany.  
**Verse:** Mark 14:3

**Answer:** A woman.  
**Verse:** Mark 14:3

**Verse:** Mark 14:3
