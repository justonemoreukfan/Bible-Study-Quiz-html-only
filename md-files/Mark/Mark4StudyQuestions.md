# Mark 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Mark 4:1

**Q1. Where did Jesus begin again to teach?**  
**Answer:** By the sea side.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:1

**Q2. What was gathered unto Jesus?**  
**Answer:** A great multitude.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:1

**Q3. Where did Jesus enter and sit?**  
**Answer:** Into a ship.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:1

---

### Mark 4:2

**Q1. What did Jesus teach the multitude by?**  
**Answer:** Parables.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:2

**Q2. When did Jesus teach them many things by parables?**  
**Answer:** In his doctrine.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:2

**Q3. What did Jesus say unto them in his doctrine?**  
**Answer:** Hearken.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:2

---

### Mark 4:3

**Q1. What instruction did Jesus give at the beginning of verse 3?**  
**Answer:** Hearken.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:3

**Q2. Who went out to sow?**  
**Answer:** A sower.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:3

**Q3. What did the sower go out to do?**  
**Answer:** To sow.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:3

---

### Mark 4:4

**Q1. What happened as the sower sowed?**  
**Answer:** Some fell by the way side.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:4

**Q2. What came and devoured the seed that fell by the way side?**  
**Answer:** The fowls of the air.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:4

**Q3. Where did the fowls of the air devour the seed?**  
**Answer:** Up.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:4

---

### Mark 4:5

**Q1. Where did some seed fall besides the way side?**  
**Answer:** On stony ground.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:5

**Q2. What did the stony ground lack where the seed fell?**  
**Answer:** Much earth.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:5

**Q3. What happened immediately because the earth was not deep?**  
**Answer:** It sprang up.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:5

---

### Mark 4:6

**Q1. What happened when the sun was up?**  
**Answer:** It was scorched.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:6

**Q2. What happened to the seed because it had no root?**  
**Answer:** It withered away.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:6

**Q3. What caused the seed to scorch and wither?**  
**Answer:** The sun.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:6

---

### Mark 4:7

**Q1. Where did some seed fall among?**  
**Answer:** Thorns.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:7

**Q2. What grew up with the seed and choked it?**  
**Answer:** The thorns.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:7

**Q3. What did the seed yield no fruit for?**  
**Answer:** It yielded no fruit.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:7

---

### Mark 4:8

**Q1. Where did other seed fall?**  
**Answer:** On good ground.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:8

**Q2. What did the seed yield that fell on good ground?**  
**Answer:** Fruit.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:8

**Q3. What were the degrees of increase yielded by the fruit?**  
**Answer:** Some thirty, and some sixty, and some an hundred.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:8

---

### Mark 4:9

**Q1. What did Jesus say unto them?**  
**Answer:** He that hath ears to hear, let him hear.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:9

**Q2. What body part is mentioned in relation to hearing?**  
**Answer:** Ears.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:9

**Q3. What action is requested of him that has ears?**  
**Answer:** Let him hear.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:9

---

### Mark 4:10

**Q1. When was Jesus asked about the parable?**  
**Answer:** When he was alone.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:10

**Q2. Who asked Jesus about the parable along with the twelve?**  
**Answer:** They that were about him.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:10

**Q3. What did they ask him about?**  
**Answer:** The parable.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:10

---

### Mark 4:11

**Q1. What was given unto them?**  
**Answer:** The mystery of the kingdom of God.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:11

**Q2. How are those outside the inner group described?**  
**Answer:** Them that are without.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:11

**Q3. How are all things done to them that are without?**  
**Answer:** In parables.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:11

---

### Mark 4:12

**Q1. What might those seeing see and not perceive?**  
**Answer:** Seeing they may see, and not perceive.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:12

**Q2. What might those hearing hear and not understand?**  
**Answer:** And hearing they may hear, and not understand.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:12

**Q3. What would happen if they saw, perceived, heard, and understood?**  
**Answer:** Lest at any time they should be converted, and their sins should be forgiven them.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:12

---

**Verse:** Mark 4:13

**Q2. What did Jesus ask how they would know?**  
**Answer:** How then will ye know all parables?  
**Verse:** Mark 4:13

**Q3. How many parables did Jesus mention in this question?**

**Verse:** Mark 4:13

**Verse:** Mark 4:14

**Verse:** Mark 4:14

**Q2. What happens when they have heard?**  
**Answer:** Satan cometh immediately.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:15

### Mark 4:16

**Q2. What do they do when they have heard the word?**  
**Answer:** Immediately receive it with gladness.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:16

**Q3. With what attitude do they receive the word?**  
**Answer:** Gladness.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:16

### Mark 4:17

**Verse:** Mark 4:17

**Q2. How long do they endure?**  
**Answer:** For a time.  
**Verse:** Mark 4:17

### Mark 4:18
