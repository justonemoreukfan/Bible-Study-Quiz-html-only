# Mark 7 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Mark 7:1

**Q1. Who came together unto Jesus?**  
**Answer:** The Pharisees, and certain of the scribes, which came from Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:1

**Q2. Where did the scribes come from?**  
**Answer:** From Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:1

**Q3. Who came together unto Jesus from Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The Pharisees, and certain of the scribes.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:1

---

### Mark 7:2

**Q1. What did they see some of his disciples eat bread with?**  
**Answer:** With defiled, that is to say, with unwashen, hands.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:2

**Q2. How did they find fault with the disciples' hands?**  
**Answer:** They found fault.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:2

**Q3. Whose hands were unwashen when they saw them eat bread?**  
**Answer:** Some of his disciples.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:2

---

### Mark 7:3

**Q1. What do the Pharisees and all the Jews not eat unless they wash?**  
**Answer:** Except they wash their hands oft.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:3

**Q2. Whose tradition did the Pharisees hold?**  
**Answer:** The tradition of the elders.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:3

**Q3. How did the Pharisees and all the Jews wash their hands?**  
**Answer:** Oft.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:3

---

### Mark 7:4

**Q1. What do they not eat when they come from the market, except they wash?**  
**Answer:** Except they wash.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:4

**Q2. What other things besides meat do they hold to wash?**  
**Answer:** The washing of cups, and pots, brasen vessels, and of tables.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:4

**Q3. What do they hold as the tradition received to wash?**  
**Answer:** The washing of cups, and pots, brasen vessels, and of tables.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:4

---

### Mark 7:5

**Q1. Who asked Jesus why his disciples walked not according to the tradition of the elders?**  
**Answer:** The Pharisees and scribes.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:5

**Q2. What did the disciples eat with that prompted the question?**  
**Answer:** With unwashen hands.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:5

**Q3. Whose tradition were the disciples not walking according to?**  
**Answer:** The tradition of the elders.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:5

---

### Mark 7:6

**Q1. Who did Jesus answer and say they were hypocrites about?**  
**Answer:** Well hath Esaias prophesied of you hypocrites.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:6

**Q2. What does this people honor God with, according to the prophecy?**  
**Answer:** With their lips.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:6

**Q3. Where is the heart of this people while they honor God with their lips?**  
**Answer:** Far from me.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:6

---

### Mark 7:7

**Q1. How do they worship Jesus in vain?**  
**Answer:** Teaching for doctrines the commandments of men.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:7

**Q2. What do they teach for doctrines?**  
**Answer:** The commandments of men.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:7

**Q3. In what manner do they worship Jesus?**  
**Answer:** In vain.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:7

---

### Mark 7:8

**Q1. What have they laid aside while holding the tradition of men?**  
**Answer:** The commandment of God.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:8

**Q2. What tradition of men do they hold?**  
**Answer:** The washing of pots and cups: and many other such like things ye do.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:8

**Q3. What have they left instead of the commandment of God?**  
**Answer:** The tradition of men.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:8

---

### Mark 7:9

**Q1. What did Jesus say they full well do to the commandment of God?**  
**Answer:** Reject the commandment of God.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:9

**Q2. Why do they reject the commandment of God?**  
**Answer:** That ye may keep your own tradition.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:9

**Q3. Whose tradition do they keep by rejecting God's commandment?**  
**Answer:** Your own tradition.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:9

---

### Mark 7:10

**Q1. Who said, Honour thy father and thy mother?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:10

**Q2. What must happen to whosoever curseth father or mother?**  
**Answer:** Let him die the death.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:10

**Q3. Whose commandment includes honouring father and mother?**  
**Answer:** Moses said.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:10

---

### Mark 7:11

**Q1. What do you say a man shall say to his father or mother?**  
**Answer:** Corban, that is to say, a gift, by whatsoever thou mightest be profited by me.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:11

**Q2. What does the word "Corban" mean?**  
**Answer:** A gift.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:11

**Q3. Who might have been profited by the man?**  
**Answer:** His father or mother.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:11

---

### Mark 7:12

**Q1. What are you no more suffered to do for father or mother?**  
**Answer:** To do ought for his father or his mother.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:12

**Q2. Who are you no more suffered to do anything for?**  
**Answer:** His father or his mother.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:12

**Q3. Is the man suffered to do anything after saying Corban?**  
**Answer:** No more.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:12

### Mark 7:14

### Mark 7:15

**Q1. What is there from without a man that entering into him can defile him?**  
**Answer:** There is nothing from without a man, that entering into him can defile him.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:15

**Q2. What things are they which defile a man?**  
**Answer:** The things which come out of him, those are they that defile the man.

**Answer:** Out of him.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:15

### Mark 7:21

**Q1. Where do evil thoughts proceed from?**  
**Answer:** From within, out of the heart of men.

**Answer:** Adulteries, fornications, murders,  
**Verse:** Mark 7:21

**Answer:** Out of the heart of men.  
**Verse:** Mark 7:21
