# Mark 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Mark 3:1

**Q1. Into what place did he enter again?**  
**Answer:** Into the synagogue.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:1

**Q2. Who was there which had a withered hand?**  
**Answer:** A man.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:1

**Q3. What condition was the man's hand in?**  
**Answer:** Withered.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:1

---

### Mark 3:2

**Q1. Who watched him?**  
**Answer:** They.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:2

**Q2. What were they watching to see if he would do on the sabbath day?**  
**Answer:** Whether he would heal him on the sabbath day.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:2

**Q3. For what purpose were they watching him?**  
**Answer:** That they might accuse him.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:2

---

### Mark 3:3

**Q1. To whom did he saith, Stand forth?**  
**Answer:** Unto the man which had the withered hand.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:3

**Q2. What did he tell the man with the withered hand to do?**  
**Answer:** Stand forth.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:3

**Q3. Whose hand was withered?**  
**Answer:** The man's.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:3

---

### Mark 3:4

**Q1. What did he saith unto them?**  
**Answer:** Is it lawful to do good on the sabbath days, or to do evil? to save life, or to kill?  
**Verse:** Mark 3:4

**Q2. How did they respond to his question?**  
**Answer:** They held their peace.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:4

**Q3. What actions were contrasted in his question regarding the sabbath?**  
**Answer:** To do good or to do evil, to save life or to kill.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:4

---

### Mark 3:5

**Q1. How did he look round about on them?**  
**Answer:** With anger.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:5

**Q2. What feeling caused him to look round about on them with anger?**  
**Answer:** Being grieved for the hardness of their hearts.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:5

**Q3. What did he say to the man?**  
**Answer:** Stretch forth thine hand.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:5

---

### Mark 3:6

**Q1. Who went forth straightway?**  
**Answer:** The Pharisees.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:6

**Q2. With whom did the Pharisees take counsel?**  
**Answer:** The Herodians.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:6

**Q3. Against him, what did the Pharisees and Herodians take counsel to do?**  
**Answer:** How they might destroy him.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:6

---

### Mark 3:7

**Q1. Where did Jesus withdraw himself with his disciples?**  
**Answer:** To the sea.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:7

**Q2. From what region did a great multitude follow him?**  
**Answer:** From Galilee, and from Judaea.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:7

**Q3. Name other places besides Galilee and Judaea from which a great multitude followed him.**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem, and Idumaea, and from beyond Jordan; and they about Tyre and Sidon.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:7

---

### Mark 3:8

**Answer:** Came unto him.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:8

**Q2. From where did people come unto him upon hearing of his great works?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem, and Idumaea, and from beyond Jordan; and they about Tyre and Sidon.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:8

**Q3. What did the great multitude do regarding what he did?**  
**Answer:** Heard what great things he did.  
**Verse:** Mark 3:8
