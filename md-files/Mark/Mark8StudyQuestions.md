# Mark 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Mark 8:1

**Q1. In those days, when what was very great?**  
**Answer:** A great multitude being assembled.  
**Verse:** Mark 8:1

**Q2. When the multitude had nothing to eat, who did Jesus call unto him?**  
**Answer:** His disciples.  
**Verse:** Mark 8:1

**Q3. What did Jesus say he had unto his disciples?**  
**Answer:** Compassion on the multitude.  
**Verse:** Mark 8:1

---

### Mark 8:2

**Q1. How long had the multitude continued with Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Three days.  
**Verse:** Mark 8:2

**Q2. What did the multitude currently have?**  
**Answer:** Nothing to eat.  
**Verse:** Mark 8:2

---

### Mark 8:3

**Q1. Where did Jesus say he would send them away if he fasted them to their own houses?**  
**Answer:** Away to their own houses.  
**Verse:** Mark 8:3

**Q2. What would happen by the way if they were sent away fasting?**  
**Answer:** They will faint by the way.  
**Verse:** Mark 8:3

**Q3. Where did divers of them come from?**  
**Answer:** Far.  
**Verse:** Mark 8:3

---

### Mark 8:4

**Q2. Where did the disciples ask about getting bread?**  
**Answer:** Here in the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Mark 8:4

**Answer:** Satisfy these men with bread.  
**Verse:** Mark 8:4
