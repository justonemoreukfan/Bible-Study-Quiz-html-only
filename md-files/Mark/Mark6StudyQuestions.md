# Mark 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Mark 6:1

**Q1. From whence did Jesus depart?**  
**Answer:** He came out from thence.  
**Verse:** Mark 6:1

**Q2. Into what region did Jesus come?**  
**Answer:** Into his own country.  
**Verse:** Mark 6:1

**Q3. Who followed Jesus into his own country?**  
**Answer:** His disciples.  
**Verse:** Mark 6:1

---

### Mark 6:2

**Q1. When what day was come, did Jesus begin to teach in the synagogue?**  
**Answer:** The sabbath day.  
**Verse:** Mark 6:2

**Q2. How were many hearing Jesus astonished?**  
**Answer:** Saying, From whence hath this man these things?  
**Verse:** Mark 6:2

**Q3. What kind of wisdom was given unto him?**  
**Answer:** Wisdom which was given unto him, that even such mighty works are wrought by his hands.  
**Verse:** Mark 6:2

---

### Mark 6:3

**Q1. What trade is the person in this verse said to be?**  
**Answer:** The carpenter.  
**Verse:** Mark 6:3

**Q2. Whose son is he called in relation to Mary?**  
**Answer:** The son of Mary.  
**Verse:** Mark 6:3

**Q3. Whose brother is he specifically named alongside?**  
**Answer:** The brother of James, and Joses, and of Juda, and Simon.  
**Verse:** Mark 6:3

---

### Mark 6:4

**Q1. What did Jesus say to them regarding a prophet?**  
**Answer:** A prophet is not without honour, but in his own country, and among his own kin, and in his own house.  
**Verse:** Mark 6:4

---

### Mark 6:5

**Verse:** Mark 6:5

**Verse:** Mark 6:5

---

**Verse:** Mark 6:6

### Mark 6:7
