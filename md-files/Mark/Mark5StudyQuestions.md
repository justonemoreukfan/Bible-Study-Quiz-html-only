# Mark 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Mark 5:1

**Q1. Where did they come over unto?**  
**Answer:** The other side of the sea, into the country of the Gadarenes.  
**Verse:** Mark 5:1

**Q2. Who came over unto the other side of the sea?**  
**Answer:** Jesus and his disciples.  
**Verse:** Mark 5:1

**Q3. Into what country did they come?**  
**Answer:** The country of the Gadarenes.  
**Verse:** Mark 5:1

---

### Mark 5:2

**Q1. Who immediately met him when he was come out of the ship?**  
**Answer:** A man with an unclean spirit.  
**Verse:** Mark 5:2

**Q2. Where did this man come out of?**  
**Answer:** The tombs.  
**Verse:** Mark 5:2

**Q3. What kind of spirit did the man have?**  
**Answer:** An unclean spirit.  
**Verse:** Mark 5:2

---

### Mark 5:3

**Q1. Where had the man his dwelling?**  
**Answer:** Among the tombs.  
**Verse:** Mark 5:3

**Q2. What could no man bind him with?**  
**Answer:** No, not with chains.  
**Verse:** Mark 5:3

**Q3. What condition was the man in regarding binding?**  
**Answer:** No man could bind him, no, not with chains.  
**Verse:** Mark 5:3

---

### Mark 5:4

**Q1. With what had he been often bound?**  
**Answer:** Fetters and chains.  
**Verse:** Mark 5:4

**Q2. What had he done to the chains?**  
**Answer:** Plucked them asunder.  
**Verse:** Mark 5:4

**Q3. What had he done to the fetters?**  
**Answer:** Broken them in pieces.  
**Verse:** Mark 5:4

---

### Mark 5:5

**Q1. Where was he always, day and night?**  
**Answer:** In the mountains, and in the tombs.  
**Verse:** Mark 5:5

**Q2. What was he doing continually day and night?**  
**Answer:** Crying, and cutting himself with stones.  
**Verse:** Mark 5:5

**Q3. With what did he cut himself?**  
**Answer:** Stones.  
**Verse:** Mark 5:5

---

### Mark 5:6
