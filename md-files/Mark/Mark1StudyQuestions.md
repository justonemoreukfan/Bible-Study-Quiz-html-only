# Mark 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Mark 1:1

**Q1. What is the beginning of the gospel?**  
**Answer:** The gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:1

**Q2. Whose gospel is it?**  
**Answer:** Jesus Christ's.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:1

**Q3. What title is given to Jesus Christ in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The Son of God.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:1

---

### Mark 1:2

**Q1. In whom is it written?**  
**Answer:** In the prophets.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:2

**Q2. Who did God send before His face?**  
**Answer:** His messenger.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:2

**Q3. What shall the messenger prepare before Him?**  
**Answer:** Thy way before thee.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:2

---

### Mark 1:3

**Q1. Where is the voice of one crying?**  
**Answer:** In the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:3

**Q2. What are people told to prepare?**  
**Answer:** The way of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:3

**Q3. What are people told to make straight?**  
**Answer:** His paths.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:3

---

### Mark 1:4

**Q1. Who did John come into the wilderness baptizing?**  
**Answer:** John did baptize in the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:4

**Q2. What kind of baptism did John preach?**  
**Answer:** The baptism of repentance.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:4

**Q3. What was the baptism for the remission of?**  
**Answer:** Sins.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:4

---

### Mark 1:5

**Q1. What land went out unto John?**  
**Answer:** All the land of Judaea.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:5

**Q2. Who of Jerusalem went out unto John?**  
**Answer:** They of Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:5

**Q3. Where were they all baptized of him?**  
**Answer:** In the river of Jordan, confessing their sins.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:5

---

### Mark 1:6

**Q1. What was John clothed with?**  
**Answer:** Camel's hair.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:6

**Q2. What was about his loins?**  
**Answer:** A girdle of a skin.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:6

**Q3. What did he eat?**  
**Answer:** Locusts and wild honey.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:6

---

### Mark 1:7

**Q1. Who preached, saying there cometh one mightier than I?**  
**Answer:** John.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:7

**Q2. What was John not worthy to stoop down and unloose?**  
**Answer:** The latchet of his shoes.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:7

**Q3. How is the one coming after John described in terms of might?**  
**Answer:** Mightier than I.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:7

---

### Mark 1:8

**Q1. With what did John baptize?**  
**Answer:** With water.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:8

**Q2. With what shall the coming one baptize?**  
**Answer:** With the Holy Ghost.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:8

**Q3. Whose action is baptizing with water contrasted with?**  
**Answer:** John's.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:8

---

### Mark 1:9

**Q1. In those days, from where did Jesus come?**  
**Answer:** From Nazareth of Galilee.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:9

**Q2. Where was Jesus baptized by John?**  
**Answer:** In Jordan.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:9

**Q3. Who baptized Jesus in Jordan?**  
**Answer:** John.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:9

---

### Mark 1:10

**Q1. What did Jesus straightway see coming up out of the water?**  
**Answer:** The heavens opened.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:10

**Q2. What descended upon Him like a dove?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:10

**Q3. In what form did the Spirit descend?**  
**Answer:** Like a dove.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:10

---

### Mark 1:11

**Q1. What came from heaven?**  
**Answer:** A voice.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:11

**Q2. How did the voice address Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Thou art my beloved Son.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:11

**Q3. How did God feel toward His Son?**  
**Answer:** In whom I am well pleased.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:11

---

### Mark 1:12

**Q1. Immediately after, where did the spirit drive Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Into the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:12

**Q2. Who drove Jesus into the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** The spirit.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:12

**Q3. What word describes how quickly the spirit drove Him?**  
**Answer:** Immediately.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:12

---

### Mark 1:13

**Q1. How many days was He there in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** Forty days.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:13

**Q2. By whom was He tempted?**  
**Answer:** Satan.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:13

**Q3. Who was with Him in the wilderness besides the beasts?**  
**Answer:** The angels ministered unto him.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:13

---

### Mark 1:14

**Q1. After John was put in prison, into where did Jesus come?**  
**Answer:** Into Galilee.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:14

**Q2. What did Jesus preach?**  
**Answer:** The gospel of the kingdom of God.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:14

**Q3. Whose kingdom is mentioned in the gospel preached by Jesus?**  
**Answer:** The kingdom of God.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:14

---

### Mark 1:15

**Q1. What time was fulfilled?**  
**Answer:** The time is fulfilled.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:15

**Q2. What is at hand?**  
**Answer:** The kingdom of God.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:15

**Q3. What two actions are commanded regarding the gospel?**  
**Answer:** Repent ye, and believe the gospel.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:15

---

### Mark 1:16

**Q1. By what sea did Jesus walk?**  
**Answer:** The sea of Galilee.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:16

**Q2. Whom did Jesus see casting a net into the sea?**  
**Answer:** Simon and Andrew his brother.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:16

**Q3. What was their occupation?**  
**Answer:** They were fishers.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:16

---

### Mark 1:17

**Q1. What did Jesus say He would make them?**  
**Answer:** Fishers of men.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:17

**Q2. What command did Jesus give to Simon and Andrew?**  
**Answer:** Come ye after me.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:17

**Q3. Whose instructions were they told to follow?**  
**Answer:** Me (Jesus).  
**Verse:** Mark 1:17

---

### Mark 1:18

**Q1. What did they straightway forsake?**  
**Answer:** Their nets.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:18

**Q2. What did they do after forsaking their nets?**  
**Answer:** Followed him.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:18

**Q3. Who followed Him?**  
**Answer:** Simon and Andrew (implied by context of preceding verses).  
**Verse:** Mark 1:18

---

### Mark 1:19

**Q1. When Jesus had gone a little further, whom did He see?**  
**Answer:** James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:19

**Q2. Where were James and John when Jesus saw them?**  
**Answer:** In a ship.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:19

**Q3. What were they doing in the ship?**  
**Answer:** Mending their nets.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:19

---

### Mark 1:20

**Q1. Straightway Jesus called whom?**  
**Answer:** Them.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:20

**Q2. Whom did they leave in the ship?**  
**Answer:** Their father Zebedee.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:20

**Q3. Who was with Zebedee in the ship?**  
**Answer:** The hired servants.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:20

---

### Mark 1:21

**Q1. Into what city did they go?**  
**Answer:** Capernaum.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:21

**Q2. When did He enter into the synagogue and teach?**  
**Answer:** Straightway on the sabbath day.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:21

**Q3. Where did He teach on the sabbath day?**  
**Answer:** Into the synagogue.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:21

---

### Mark 1:22

**Q1. How were the people affected by His doctrine?**  
**Answer:** They were astonished at his doctrine.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:22

**Q2. How did He teach them?**  
**Answer:** As one that had authority.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:22

**Q3. Unlike whom did He teach?**  
**Answer:** Not as the scribes.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:22

---

### Mark 1:23

**Q1. Where was there a man with an unclean spirit?**  
**Answer:** In their synagogue.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:23

**Q2. What did the man with the unclean spirit do?**  
**Answer:** He cried out.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:23

**Q3. What kind of spirit did the man have?**  
**Answer:** An unclean spirit.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:23

---

### Mark 1:24

**Q1. What question did the unclean spirit ask Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Let us alone; what have we to do with thee, thou Jesus of Nazareth?  
**Verse:** Mark 1:24

**Q2. What did the unclean spirit say Jesus came to do?**  
**Answer:** Art thou come to destroy us?  
**Verse:** Mark 1:24

**Q3. What did the unclean spirit know Jesus to be?**  
**Answer:** The Holy One of God.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:24

---

### Mark 1:25

**Q1. Who rebuked the unclean spirit?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:25

**Q2. What did Jesus tell the spirit to do?**  
**Answer:** Hold thy peace.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:25

**Answer:** Come out of him.  
**Verse:** Mark 1:25

**Verse:** Mark 1:26

**Answer:** Cried with a loud voice.

**Q3. What did the spirit finally do?**
