# Mark 11 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Mark 11:1

**Q1. When they came nigh to Jerusalem, unto Bethphage and Bethany, at what mount did they arrive?**  
**Answer:** The mount of Olives.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:1

**Q2. Who did Jesus send forth?**  
**Answer:** Two of his disciples.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:1

**Q3. To what place did Jesus send forth two of his disciples?**  
**Answer:** Over against you.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:1

---

### Mark 11:2

**Q1. What did Jesus tell the disciples they would find as soon as they entered the village?**  
**Answer:** A colt tied, whereon never man sat.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:2

**Q2. What did Jesus instruct the disciples to do with the colt?**  
**Answer:** Loose him, and bring him.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:2

**Q3. If any man said unto them, "Why do ye this?", what were they to say?**  
**Answer:** That the Lord hath need of him; and straightway he will send him hither.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:2

---

### Mark 11:3

**Q1. What did Jesus say the owner would do if someone asked why they were loosing the colt?**  
**Answer:** Straightway he will send him hither.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:3

**Q2. Who did Jesus say hath need of the colt?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:3

**Q3. What reason were the disciples to give for loosing the colt?**  
**Answer:** The Lord hath need of him.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:3

---

### Mark 11:4

**Q1. Where did the disciples go?**  
**Answer:** Their way.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:4

**Q2. What did the disciples find where the street met?**  
**Answer:** The colt tied by the door without.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:4

**Q3. What did the disciples do to the colt?**  
**Answer:** They loose him.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:4

---

### Mark 11:5

**Q1. Who stood there?**  
**Answer:** Certain of them that stood there.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:5

**Q2. What did the certain of them that stood there say to the disciples?**  
**Answer:** What do ye, loosing the colt?  
**Verse:** Mark 11:5

**Q3. What action of the disciples prompted the bystanders to speak?**  
**Answer:** Loosing the colt.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:5

---

### Mark 11:6

**Q1. What did the disciples say unto them?**  
**Answer:** Even as Jesus had commanded.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:6

**Q2. Whose instructions did the disciples repeat?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:6

**Q3. What happened after the disciples spoke as Jesus commanded?**  
**Answer:** They let them go.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:6

---

### Mark 11:7

**Q1. To whom did they bring the colt?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:7

**Q2. What did they cast on the colt?**  
**Answer:** Their garments.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:7

**Q3. Who sat upon the colt?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:7

---

### Mark 11:8

**Q1. What did many spread in the way?**  
**Answer:** Their garments.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:8

**Q2. What else did others spread down instead of garments?**  
**Answer:** Branches off the trees.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:8

**Q3. Where did others cut the branches from?**  
**Answer:** The fields.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:8

---

### Mark 11:9

**Q1. What did they that went before, and they that followed, do?**  
**Answer:** Cried.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:9

**Q2. What phrase was shouted in the cries?**  
**Answer:** Hosanna.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:9

**Q3. In whose name was he blessed that cometh?**  
**Answer:** In the name of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:9

---

### Mark 11:10

**Q1. Whose kingdom was said to come?**  
**Answer:** The kingdom of our father David.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:10

**Q2. What kingdom was blessed?**  
**Answer:** The kingdom of our father David, that cometh in the name of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:10

**Q3. What exclamation was used at the highest point?**  
**Answer:** Hosanna in the highest.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:10

---

### Mark 11:11

**Q1. Into what city did Jesus enter?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:11

**Q2. Where did Jesus go after entering the temple?**  
**Answer:** Into the temple.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:11

**Q3. When did Jesus go out unto Bethany with the twelve?**  
**Answer:** When he had looked round about upon all things, and now the eventide was come.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:11

---

### Mark 11:12

**Q1. When they were come from where did this event take place?**  
**Answer:** Bethany.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:12

**Q2. Who was hungry the next day after they were come from Bethany?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:12

**Q3. What was Jesus' physical state on the morrow?**  
**Answer:** He was hungry.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:12

---

### Mark 11:13

**Q1. What tree did Jesus see afar off having leaves?**  
**Answer:** A fig tree.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:13

**Q2. What did Jesus hope to find on the fig tree?**  
**Answer:** Anything thereon.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:13

**Q3. Why did Jesus find nothing on the fig tree except leaves?**  
**Answer:** For the time of figs was not yet.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:13

---

### Mark 11:14

**Q1. Who answered and said unto the fig tree?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:14

**Q2. What did Jesus say no man would ever eat of hereafter?**  
**Answer:** Fruit of thee.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:14

**Q3. Who heard Jesus speak to the fig tree?**  
**Answer:** His disciples.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:14

---

### Mark 11:15

**Q1. To what city did they come?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:15

**Q2. Where did Jesus go after entering the temple?**  
**Answer:** Into the temple.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:15

**Q3. Whom did Jesus cast out of the temple?**  
**Answer:** Them that bought and sold in the temple.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:15

---

### Mark 11:16

**Q1. What would Jesus not suffer any man to carry through?**  
**Answer:** Any vessel.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:16

**Q2. Through what place would Jesus not suffer any man to carry a vessel?**  
**Answer:** The temple.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:16

**Q3. Whose actions were restricted concerning vessels in the temple?**  
**Answer:** Any man.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:16

---

### Mark 11:17

**Q1. What did Jesus teach and say unto them?**  
**Answer:** Is it not written, My house shall be called of all nations the house of prayer?  
**Verse:** Mark 11:17

**Q2. What had they made the house of prayer into?**  
**Answer:** A den of thieves.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:17

**Q3. Of what nations was the house supposed to be a house of prayer?**  
**Answer:** All nations.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:17

---

### Mark 11:18

**Q1. Who heard what Jesus said and did?**  
**Answer:** The scribes and chief priests.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:18

**Q2. What did the scribes and chief priests seek?**  
**Answer:** How they might destroy him.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:18

**Q3. Why did the scribes and chief priests fear Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Because all the people was astonished at his doctrine.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:18

---

### Mark 11:19

**Q1. When even was come, what did Jesus do?**  
**Answer:** He went out of the city.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:19

**Q2. Out of what city did Jesus go?**  
**Answer:** The city.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:19

**Q3. Who went out of the city when even was come?**  
**Answer:** He (Jesus).  
**Verse:** Mark 11:19

---

### Mark 11:20

**Q1. In the morning, as they passed by, what did they see?**  
**Answer:** The fig tree dried up from the roots.

**Q2. From where was the fig tree dried up?**  
**Answer:** From the roots.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:20

**Q3. When did they see the fig tree dried up?**

**Verse:** Mark 11:21

**Verse:** Mark 11:21

**Q1. Who answering saith unto them?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:22

**Q2. What attribute did Jesus tell them to have?**  
**Answer:** Have faith in God.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:22

**Q3. In whom should they have faith?**  
**Answer:** In God.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:22

---

### Mark 11:23

**Q1. What did Jesus say whosoever shall say unto this mountain involves?**  
**Answer:** Be thou removed, and be thou cast into the sea.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:23

**Q2. What must a person not do in their heart concerning what they say?**  
**Answer:** Not doubt in his heart.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:23

**Q3. What will happen if someone believes those things which he saith shall come to pass?**  
**Answer:** He shall have whatsoever he saith.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:23

---

### Mark 11:24

**Q1. What did Jesus say they should believe when they pray?**  
**Answer:** That ye receive them.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:24

**Q2. What will happen to those who believe they receive what they pray for?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall have them.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:24

**Q3. What things did Jesus say ye shall have when ye desire them in prayer?**  
**Answer:** What things soever ye desire, when ye pray.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:24

---

### Mark 11:25

**Q1. When ye stand praying, what are ye to do if ye have aught against any?**  
**Answer:** Forgive.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:25

**Q2. Why should you forgive when you stand praying?**  
**Answer:** That your Father also which is in heaven may forgive you your trespasses.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:25

**Q3. Who in heaven may forgive your trespasses if you forgive others?**  
**Answer:** Your Father which is in heaven.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:25

---

### Mark 11:26

**Q1. If ye do not forgive, what will your Father which is in heaven not do?**  
**Answer:** Neither will your Father which is in heaven forgive your trespasses.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:26

**Q2. Whose trespasses will not be forgiven if they do not forgive?**  
**Answer:** Your trespasses.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:26

**Q3. Where is the Father located who will not forgive if you do not forgive?**  
**Answer:** In heaven.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:26

### Mark 11:28

**Q1. What did the authorities ask Jesus about his actions?**  
**Answer:** By what authority doest thou these things?  
**Verse:** Mark 11:28

**Q2. What did they ask about who gave him this authority?**  
**Answer:** And who gave thee this authority to do these things?  
**Verse:** Mark 11:28

**Answer:** Authority to do these things.  
**Verse:** Mark 11:28

**Verse:** Mark 11:29
