# Mark 15 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Mark 15:1

**Q1. Who held a consultation straightway in the morning?**  
**Answer:** The chief priests with the elders and scribes and the whole council.  
**Verse:** Mark 15:1

**Q2. What did the council do with Jesus after binding him?**  
**Answer:** They carried him away, and delivered him to Pilate.  
**Verse:** Mark 15:1

**Q3. When did the chief priests hold their consultation?**  
**Answer:** Straightway in the morning.  
**Verse:** Mark 15:1

---

### Mark 15:2

**Q4. Who asked Jesus, "Art thou the King of the Jews?"**  
**Answer:** Pilate.  
**Verse:** Mark 15:2

**Q5. How did Jesus answer Pilate's question?**  
**Answer:** Thou sayest it.  
**Verse:** Mark 15:2

**Q6. To whom did Jesus answer "Thou sayest it"?**  
**Answer:** Pilate.  
**Verse:** Mark 15:2

---

### Mark 15:3

**Q7. Who accused Jesus of many things?**

**Verse:** Mark 15:3

**Verse:** Mark 15:3
