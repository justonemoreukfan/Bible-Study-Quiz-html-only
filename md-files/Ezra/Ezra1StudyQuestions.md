# Ezra 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezra 1:1

**Q1. In what year of Cyrus king of Persia was the word of the LORD by the mouth of Jeremiah fulfilled?**  
**Answer:** The first year.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:1

**Q2. Whose spirit did the LORD stir up in the first year of Cyrus king of Persia?**  
**Answer:** The spirit of Cyrus king of Persia.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:1

**Q3. How did Cyrus make a proclamation throughout all his kingdom, and put it also in writing?**  
**Answer:** By the mouth of Jeremiah. / He made a proclamation throughout all his kingdom, and put it also in writing.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:1

---

### Ezra 1:2

**Q1. Who did Cyrus king of Persia say had given him all the kingdoms of the earth?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God of heaven.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:2

**Q2. Who charged Cyrus to build him an house at Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God of heaven.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:2

**Q3. Where is the house that the LORD charged Cyrus to build located?**  
**Answer:** In Jerusalem, which is in Judah.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:2

---

### Ezra 1:3

**Q1. Who among all the people of the LORD is his God with?**  
**Answer:** His God be with him.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:3

**Q2. Where is the person exhorted to go up to?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem, which is in Judah.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:3

**Q3. What is the person supposed to build in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The house of the LORD God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:3

---

### Ezra 1:4

**Q1. What should the men of every place where any person sojourneth help him with?**  
**Answer:** With silver, and with gold, and with goods, and with beasts.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:4

**Q2. Besides silver, gold, goods, and beasts, what else should be given to the people who sojourn in any place?**  
**Answer:** Freewill offering for the house of God that is in Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:4

**Q3. Who is instructed to help the people of the places where they sojourn?**  
**Answer:** The men of his place.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:4

---

### Ezra 1:5

**Q1. Who rose up to go up to build the house of the LORD which is in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The chief of the fathers of Judah and Benjamin, and the priests, and the levites.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:5

**Q2. Whose spirit had God raised up to go up to build the house of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** All them whose spirit God had raised.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:5

**Q3. Where is the house of the LORD that these people rose up to build located?**  
**Answer:** In Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:5

---

### Ezra 1:6

**Q1. How did they that were about them strengthen their hands?**  
**Answer:** With vessels of silver, with gold, with goods, and with beasts, and with precious things.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:6

**Q2. What were included in the things that strengthened their hands besides silver, gold, goods, and beasts?**  
**Answer:** Precious things.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:6

**Q3. What was given besides all the willingly offered things?**  
**Answer:** All that was willingly offered.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:6

---

### Ezra 1:7

**Q1. What did Cyrus the king bring forth?**  
**Answer:** The vessels of the house of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:7

**Q2. Who had brought forth the vessels of the house of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Nebuchadnezzar.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:7

**Q3. Where had Nebuchadnezzar brought the vessels from and where had he put them?**  
**Answer:** From Jerusalem, and had put them in the house of his gods.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:7

---

### Ezra 1:8

**Q1. By the hand of whom did Cyrus king of Persia bring forth the vessels of the house of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Mithredath the treasurer.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:8

**Q2. To whom did Mithredath the treasurer number the vessels?**  
**Answer:** Sheshbazzar, the prince of Judah.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:8

**Q3. What title is given to Sheshbazzar in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The prince of Judah.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:8

---

### Ezra 1:9

**Q1. How many chargers of gold were there?**  
**Answer:** Thirty.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:9

**Q2. How many chargers of silver were there?**  
**Answer:** A thousand.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:9

**Q3. How many knives were counted among the numbered vessels?**  
**Answer:** Nine and twenty.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:9

---

### Ezra 1:10

**Q1. How many basons of gold were there?**  
**Answer:** Thirty.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:10

**Q2. How many silver basons of a second sort were there?**  
**Answer:** Four hundred and ten.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:10

**Q3. How many other vessels were counted?**  
**Answer:** A thousand.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:10

---

### Ezra 1:11

**Q1. What was the total number of all the vessels of gold and of silver?**  
**Answer:** Five thousand and four hundred.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:11

**Q2. Who brought all the vessels up with them of the captivity?**  
**Answer:** Sheshbazzar.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:11

**Q3. From where were the vessels brought up when they were brought from Babylon unto Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Babylon.  
**Verse:** Ezra 1:11
