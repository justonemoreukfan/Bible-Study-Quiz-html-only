# Ezra 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezra 5:1

**Q1. Who prophesied unto the Jews that were in Judah and Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Haggai the prophet, and Zechariah the son of Iddo.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:1

**Q2. In whose name did the prophets prophesy unto the Jews?**  
**Answer:** In the name of the God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:1

**Q3. Upon whom was the prophesying directed?**  
**Answer:** Upon the Jews that were in Judah and Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:1

---

### Ezra 5:2

**Q1. Who rose up and began to build the house of God which is at Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, and Jeshua the son of Jozadak.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:2

**Q2. With the two leaders, who else was with them helping to build the house of God?**  
**Answer:** The prophets of God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:2

**Q3. What were the prophets doing while the house of God was being built?**  
**Answer:** Helping them.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:2

---

### Ezra 5:3

**Q1. Who came to Zerubbabel, Jeshua, and the builders at the same time?**  
**Answer:** Tatnai, governor on this side the river, and Shetharboznai, and their companions.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:3

**Q2. What question did the governors ask the builders?**  
**Answer:** Who hath commanded you to build this house, and to make up this wall?  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:3

**Q3. Where was Tatnai the governor stationed?**  
**Answer:** On this side the river.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:3

---

### Ezra 5:4

**Q1. What else did the authorities ask the builders in addition to who commanded the building?**  
**Answer:** What are the names of the men that make this building?  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:4

**Q2. Whose names were the questioners asking for?**  
**Answer:** The names of the men that make this building.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:4

**Q3. What building activity were the men making according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** This building.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:4

---

### Ezra 5:5

**Q1. Upon whom was the eye of their God?**  
**Answer:** Upon the elders of the Jews.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:5

**Q2. What did the elders of the Jews not cause them to do?**  
**Answer:** That they could not cause them to cease.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:5

**Q3. Until what event was the matter brought back?**  
**Answer:** Until the matter came to Darius: and then they returned answer by letter concerning this matter.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:5

---

### Ezra 5:6

**Q1. What is the title of the text describing the letter sent to Darius?**  
**Answer:** The copy of the letter that Tatnai, governor on this side the river, and Shetharboznai, and his companions the Apharsachites, which were on this side the river, sent unto Darius the king.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:6

**Q2. Who sent the letter to King Darius?**  
**Answer:** Tatnai, governor on this side the river, and Shetharboznai, and his companions the Apharsachites.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:6

**Q3. Where were Tatnai, Shetharboznai, and their companions located?**  
**Answer:** On this side the river.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:6

---

### Ezra 5:7

**Q1. To whom was the message sent?**  
**Answer:** Unto Darius the king.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:7

**Q2. What kind of greeting was sent unto Darius?**  
**Answer:** All peace.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:7

**Q3. How was the message received by Darius described?**  
**Answer:** They sent a letter unto him, wherein was written thus.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:7

---

### Ezra 5:8

**Q1. To what province did the governor go in the letter?**  
**Answer:** To the province of Judea.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:8

**Q2. To the house of what great God did they go?**  
**Answer:** The house of the great God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:8

**Q3. With what material was the house being built, and how were the walls set?**  
**Answer:** With great stones, and timber is laid in the walls.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:8

---

### Ezra 5:9

**Q1. What question did the elders ask regarding the building?**  
**Answer:** Who commanded you to build this house, and to make up these walls?  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:9

**Q2. What did the builders make up besides the house?**  
**Answer:** These walls.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:9

**Q3. Who asked these questions to the elders?**  
**Answer:** Then asked we those elders, and thus said we unto them.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:9

---

### Ezra 5:10

**Q1. What else did the questioners ask the elders for, besides their authority?**  
**Answer:** Their names also.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:10

**Q2. Whose names did they ask to know?**  
**Answer:** The names of the men that were chief among them.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:10

**Q3. Why did they ask for the names of the chief men?**  
**Answer:** That we might write the names of the men that were the chief of them.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:10

---

### Ezra 5:11

**Q1. How did the elders describe themselves in their return answer?**  
**Answer:** We are the servants of the God of heaven and earth.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:11

**Q2. What did the elders say they were building?**  
**Answer:** The house that was builded these many years ago.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:11

**Q3. What kind of king had builded and set up the house many years ago?**  
**Answer:** A great king of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:11

---

### Ezra 5:12

**Q1. After what event did the fathers provoke the God of heaven?**  
**Answer:** After that our fathers had provoked the God of heaven unto wrath.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:12

**Q2. Into whose hand did God give the fathers?**  
**Answer:** Into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, the Chaldean.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:12

**Q3. What did Nebuchadnezzar do to this house and the people?**  
**Answer:** Who destroyed this house, and carried the people away into Babylon.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:12

---

### Ezra 5:13

**Q1. In what year of Cyrus the king of Babylon were the decrees given?**  
**Answer:** In the first year of Cyrus the king of Babylon.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:13

**Q2. Who made a decree to build this house of God?**  
**Answer:** King Cyrus.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:13

**Q3. Of what place was Cyrus king?**  
**Answer:** Babylon.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:13

---

### Ezra 5:14

**Q1. What kind of vessels of the house of God were taken out of the temple?**  
**Answer:** The vessels also of gold and silver.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:14

**Q2. Who had taken the gold and silver vessels out of the temple at Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Nebuchadnezzar.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:14

**Q3. Where did Nebuchadnezzar bring the vessels after taking them from the temple?**  
**Answer:** Into the temple of Babylon.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:14

---

### Ezra 5:15

**Q1. What did King Cyrus take out of the temple of Babylon?**  
**Answer:** The same vessels.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:15

**Q2. To whom did Cyrus deliver the vessels?**  
**Answer:** Unto one whose name was Sheshbazzar, whom he had made governor.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:15

**Q3. Where did Cyrus command Sheshbazzar to carry the vessels?**  
**Answer:** Go, carry these vessels, into the temple that is in Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:15

---

### Ezra 5:16

**Q1. Who came and laid the foundation of the house of God which is in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Then came the same Sheshbazzar, and laid the foundation of the house of God which is in Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:16

**Q2. From what time until then had the building been continuing?**  
**Answer:** And since that time even until now hath it been in building.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:16

**Q3. Was the house finished by that time?**  
**Answer:** And yet it is not finished.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:16

---

### Ezra 5:17

**Q1. What did the writers ask the king to do after this?**  
**Answer:** Now therefore, if it seem good to the king, let there be search made in the king's treasure house.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:17

**Q2. Where should the search be made in Babylon?**  
**Answer:** In the king's treasure house, which is there at Babylon.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:17

**Q3. What were they seeking to find out regarding King Cyrus?**  
**Answer:** Whether it be so, that a decree was made of Cyrus the king to build this house of God at Jerusalem, and let the king send his pleasure concerning this matter.  
**Verse:** Ezra 5:17
