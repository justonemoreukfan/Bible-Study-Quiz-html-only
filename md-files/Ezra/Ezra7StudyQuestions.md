# Ezra 7 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezra 7:1

**Q1. After these things, in the reign of which king did Ezra go up from Babylon?**  
**Answer:** Artaxerxes king of Persia.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:1

**Q2. Whose son was Ezra, according to verse 1?**  
**Answer:** Seraiah.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:1

**Q3. Whose son was Seraiah?**  
**Answer:** Azariah.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:1

---

### Ezra 7:2

**Q1. Whose son was Azariah?**  
**Answer:** Hilkiah.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:2

**Q2. Whose son was Hilkiah?**  
**Answer:** Shallum.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:2

**Q3. Whose son was Shallum?**  
**Answer:** Zadok.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:2

---

### Ezra 7:3

**Q1. Whose son was Zadok?**  
**Answer:** Meraioth.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:3

**Q2. Whose son was Meraioth?**  
**Answer:** Amariah.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:3

**Q3. Whose son was Amariah?**  
**Answer:** Azariah.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:3

---

### Ezra 7:4

**Q1. Whose son was the second Azariah mentioned in the lineage?**  
**Answer:** Meraioth.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:4

**Q2. Whose son was Meraioth in verse 4?**  
**Answer:** Zerahiah.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:4

**Q3. Whose son was Zerahiah?**  
**Answer:** Uzzi.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:4

---

### Ezra 7:5

**Q1. Whose son was Uzzi?**  
**Answer:** Bukki.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:5

**Q2. Whose son was Bukki?**  
**Answer:** Abishua.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:5

**Q3. Whose son was Abishua?**  
**Answer:** Phinehas, Eleazar, and Aaron the chief priest.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:5

---

### Ezra 7:6

**Q1. What kind of scribe was Ezra that went up from Babylon?**  
**Answer:** A ready scribe in the law of Moses.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:6

**Q2. Who granted Ezra all his request?**  
**Answer:** The king.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:6

**Q3. What was upon Ezra according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** The hand of the LORD his God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:6

---

### Ezra 7:7

**Q1. In which year of King Artaxerxes did some of the children of Israel go up?**  
**Answer:** The seventh year.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:7

**Q2. Who went up to Jerusalem along with the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The priests, the Levites, the singers, the porters, and the Nethinims.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:7

**Q3. Who else went up with them besides the priests and Levites?**  
**Answer:** Some of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:7

---

### Ezra 7:8

**Q1. In what month did Ezra come to Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The fifth month.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:8

**Q2. What year of the king's reign was it when he came to Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The seventh year.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:8

---

### Ezra 7:9

**Q1. On what day of the first month did he begin to go up from Babylon?**  
**Answer:** The first day of the first month.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:9

**Q2. On what day of the fifth month did he come to Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The first day of the fifth month.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:9

**Q3. What was upon him that enabled him to come to Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The good hand of his God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:9

---

### Ezra 7:10

**Q1. What had Ezra prepared his heart to seek?**  
**Answer:** The law of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:10

**Q2. What else had Ezra prepared his heart to do?**  
**Answer:** To do it.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:10

**Q3. What did Ezra prepare his heart to teach in Israel?**  
**Answer:** Statutes and judgments.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:10

---

### Ezra 7:11

**Q1. Who is Ezra described as in this verse?**  
**Answer:** A priest and a scribe.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:11

**Q2. Whose words and commandments were the basis of the scribe's matters concerning Israel?**  
**Answer:** The words of the commandments of the LORD, and of his statutes to Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:11

**Q3. To whom was this copy of the letter given by King Artaxerxes?**  
**Answer:** Ezra the priest, the scribe.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:11

---

### Ezra 7:12

**Q1. Who is sending this letter according to verse 12?**  
**Answer:** Artaxerxes, king of kings.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:12

**Q2. To whom is Artaxerxes sending this letter?**  
**Answer:** Ezra the priest, a scribe of the law of the God of heaven.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:12

**Q3. What greeting does the king give in the letter?**  
**Answer:** Perfect peace, and at such a time.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:12

---

### Ezra 7:13

**Q1. Who is granted permission to go with Ezra out of the realm of Artaxerxes?**  
**Answer:** All they of the people of Israel, and of his priests and Levites.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:13

**Q2. Where did these people have the option to go of their own free will?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:13

---

### Ezra 7:14

**Q1. By whom is Ezra sent from the king and his seven counsellors?**  
**Answer:** The king and his seven counsellors.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:14

**Q2. What was Ezra sent to inquire concerning?**  
**Answer:** Concerning Judah and Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:14

**Q3. According to what law was Ezra sent to inquire?**  
**Answer:** The law of thy God which is in thine hand.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:14

---

### Ezra 7:15

**Q1. What were Ezra and the others to carry with them?**  
**Answer:** The silver and gold.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:15

**Q2. Who freely offered the silver and gold mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The king and his counsellors.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:15

**Q3. To whom was this silver and gold offered?**  
**Answer:** The God of Israel, whose habitation is in Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:15

---

### Ezra 7:16

**Q1. What kind of silver and gold was Ezra to carry from the province of Babylon?**  
**Answer:** All the silver and gold that thou canst find in all the province of Babylon.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:16

**Q2. Who else besides the king and counsellors offered a freewill offering?**  
**Answer:** The people, and the priests.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:16

**Q3. For whose house were these freewill offerings willingly offered?**  
**Answer:** The house of their God which is in Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:16

---

### Ezra 7:17

**Q1. What specific items were Ezra to buy speedily with the money?**  
**Answer:** Bullocks, rams, lambs, with their meat offerings and their drink offerings.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:17

**Q2. Upon what altar were these items to be offered?**  
**Answer:** The altar of the house of your God which is in Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:17

---

### Ezra 7:18

**Q1. What should be done with the rest of the silver and the gold?**  
**Answer:** That do after the will of your God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:18

**Q2. Who was to decide what to do with the rest of the silver and gold?**  
**Answer:** Ezra and his brethren.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:18

---

### Ezra 7:19

**Q1. What vessels were given to Ezra for the service of the house of God?**  
**Answer:** The vessels that are given thee for the service of the house of thy God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:19

**Q2. Before whose face were these vessels to be delivered?**  
**Answer:** Before the God of Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:19

---

### Ezra 7:20

**Q1. What else besides vessels was Ezra authorized to bestow for the house of his God?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever more shall be needful for the house of thy God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:20

**Q2. From where was Ezra to bestow these needful things?**  
**Answer:** Out of the king's treasure house.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:20

---

### Ezra 7:21

**Q1. Who made a decree to all the treasurers beyond the river?**  
**Answer:** Artaxerxes the king.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:21

**Q2. What did Artaxerxes command the treasurers to do for Ezra?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever Ezra the priest, the scribe of the law of the God of heaven, shall require of you.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:21

---

### Ezra 7:22

**Q1. Up to how much silver was to be given speedily?**  
**Answer:** An hundred talents.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:22

**Q2. Up to how much wheat, wine, and oil were to be given?**  
**Answer:** An hundred measures of wheat, an hundred baths of wine, and an hundred baths of oil.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:22

**Q3. Was there a limit set on the amount of salt to be given?**  
**Answer:** And salt without prescribing how much.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:22

---

### Ezra 7:23

**Q1. By whom should whatever is commanded be diligently done?**  
**Answer:** By the God of heaven.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:23

**Q2. For whose house should this command be diligently done?**  
**Answer:** For the house of the God of heaven.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:23

**Q3. What reason does the king give for this diligence regarding the house of God?**  
**Answer:** For why should there be wrath against the realm of the king and his sons?  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:23

---

### Ezra 7:24

**Q1. Concerning which group of people was it certified that no toll, tribute, or custom should be imposed?**  
**Answer:** The priests and Levites, singers, porters, Nethinims, or ministers of this house of God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:24

**Q2. Who was forbidden to impose toll, tribute, or custom on these ministers?**  
**Answer:** Any of the priests and Levites, singers, porters, Nethinims, or ministers. (The text states: "It shall not be lawful to impose toll, tribute, or custom, upon them.")  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:24

---

### Ezra 7:25

**Q1. According to what did Ezra set magistrates and judges?**  
**Answer:** The wisdom of thy God, that is in thine hand.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:25

**Q2. Over whom were these magistrates and judges to judge?**  
**Answer:** All the people that are beyond the river.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:25

**Q3. Who were these judges to teach?**  
**Answer:** All such as know the laws of thy God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:25

---

### Ezra 7:26

**Q1. What law must whosoever will not do be executed speedily unto?**  
**Answer:** The law of thy God, and the law of the king.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:26

**Q2. What kind of judgment was to be executed speedily upon those who would not do the law?**  
**Answer:** Whether it be unto death, or to banishment, or to confiscation of goods, or to imprisonment.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:26

---

### Ezra 7:27

**Q1. Who put such a thing as this in the king's heart?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God of our fathers.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:27

**Q2. What did the LORD God put in the king's heart to beautify?**  
**Answer:** The house of the LORD which is in Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:27

---

### Ezra 7:28

**Q1. What did God extend toward Ezra before the king and his counsellors?**  
**Answer:** Mercy.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:28

**Q2. Who strengthened Ezra?**  
**Answer:** The hand of the LORD my God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:28

**Q3. Who gathered together out of Israel to go up with Ezra?**  
**Answer:** Chief men.  
**Verse:** Ezra 7:28
