# Ezra 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezra 4:1

**Q1. Who heard that the children of the captivity builded the temple unto the LORD God of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Now when the adversaries of Judah and Benjamin heard that the children of the captivity builded the temple unto the LORD God of Israel;  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:1

**Q2. Whose adversaries heard that the children of the captivity were building the temple?**  
**Answer:** Judah and Benjamin  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:1

**Q3. To whom did the adversaries come after hearing about the building of the temple?**  
**Answer:** Then they came to Zerubbabel, and to the chief of the fathers,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:1

---

### Ezra 4:2

**Q1. To whom did the adversaries say, "Let us build with you"?**  
**Answer:** Zerubbabel, and to the chief of the fathers,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:2

**Q2. What reason did the adversaries give for wanting to build with them?**  
**Answer:** For we seek your God, as ye do;  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:2

**Q3. To whom did the adversaries say they had sacrificed since the days of Esar-haddon king of Assur?**  
**Answer:** and we do sacrifice unto him since the days of Esar-haddon king of Assur, which brought us up hither.  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:2

---

### Ezra 4:3

**Q1. Who said unto the adversaries, "Ye have nothing to do with us to build an house unto our God"?**  
**Answer:** Zerubbabel, and Jeshua, and the rest of the chief of the fathers of Israel,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:3

**Q2. Who alone did they say should build unto the LORD God of Israel?**  
**Answer:** but we ourselves together will build unto the LORD God of Israel,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:3

**Q3. According to whose commandment was the house to be built?**  
**Answer:** as king Cyrus the king of Persia hath commanded us.  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:3

---

### Ezra 4:4

**Q1. What did the people of the land do to the people of Judah?**  
**Answer:** Then the people of the land weakened the hands of the people of Judah,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:4

**Q2. What did the people of the land do while the people of Judah were building?**  
**Answer:** and troubled them in building,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:4

**Q3. Whose hands did the people of the land weaken?**  
**Answer:** the hands of the people of Judah  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:4

---

### Ezra 4:5

**Q1. Whom did the people of the land hire against them to frustrate their purpose?**  
**Answer:** And hired counsellors against them, to frustrate their purpose,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:5

**Q2. During whose entire reign did the people of the land hire counsellors against them?**  
**Answer:** all the days of Cyrus king of Persia,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:5

**Q3. Until the reign of which king did this opposition last?**  
**Answer:** even until the reign of Darius king of Persia.  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:5

---

### Ezra 4:6

**Q1. In the reign of which king did they write an accusation against the inhabitants of Judah and Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** And in the reign of Ahasuerus, in the beginning of his reign,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:6

**Q2. Against whom did they write an accusation in the reign of Ahasuerus?**  
**Answer:** wrote they unto him an accusation against the inhabitants of Judah and Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:6

**Q3. When in the reign of Ahasuerus was this accusation written?**  
**Answer:** in the beginning of his reign  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:6

---

### Ezra 4:7

**Q1. In whose days did Bishlam, Mithredath, Tabeel, and the rest of their companions write unto Artaxerxes king of Persia?**  
**Answer:** And in the days of Artaxerxes wrote Bishlam, Mithredath, Tabeel, and the rest of their companions, unto Artaxerxes king of Persia;  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:7

**Q2. In what script or tongue was the writing of the letter written?**  
**Answer:** and the writing of the letter was written in the Syrian tongue, and interpreted in the Syrian tongue.  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:7

**Q3. Who wrote the letter along with Bishlam, Mithredath, and Tabeel?**  
**Answer:** and the rest of their companions  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:7

---

### Ezra 4:8

**Q1. Who wrote a letter against Jerusalem to Artaxerxes the king?**  
**Answer:** Rehum the chancellor and Shimshai the scribe  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:8

**Q2. Along with Rehum and Shimshai, who else wrote the letter against Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** and the rest of their companions; the Dinaites, the Apharsathchites, the Tarpelites, the Apharsites, the Archavites, the Babylonians, the Susanchites, the Dehavites, and the Elamites,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:8

**Q3. Against what place was the letter written?**  
**Answer:** against Jerusalem  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:8

---

### Ezra 4:9

**Q1. Who are named as writing this letter along with the Dinaites and Apharsathchites?**  
**Answer:** Then wrote Rehum the chancellor, and Shimshai the scribe, and the rest of their companions;  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:9

**Q2. Which groups from verse 9 are listed after the Tarpelites and Apharsites?**  
**Answer:** the Archavites, the Babylonians, the Susanchites, the Dehavites, and the Elamites,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:9

**Q3. To whom did these various companions write?**  
**Answer:** (Implied from context / previous verse: Artaxerxes the king; though verse 9 focuses on the senders, the letter is to the king). *Note: Verse 9 lists the senders.*  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:9

---

### Ezra 4:10

**Q1. Who was the great and noble king whom the rest of the nations brought over and set in the city of Samaria?**  
**Answer:** And the rest of the nations whom the great and noble Asnapper brought over, and set in the cities of Samaria,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:10

**Q2. Where did Asnapper set the rest of the nations?**  
**Answer:** in the cities of Samaria  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:10

**Q3. What phrase describes the region beyond the river where the rest of the nations dwelt?**  
**Answer:** and the rest that are on this side the river, and at such a time.  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:10

---

### Ezra 4:11

**Q1. What is the opening greeting of the copy of the letter that they sent to King Artaxerxes?**  
**Answer:** This is the copy of the letter that they sent unto him, even unto Artaxerxes the king;  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:11

**Q2. Who are the senders of the letter addressed to Artaxerxes?**  
**Answer:** Thy servants the men on this side the river, and at such a time.  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:11

**Q3. To whom was the copy of the letter sent?**  
**Answer:** unto Artaxerxes the king  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:11

---

### Ezra 4:12

**Q1. From where did the Jews come up to the king's location?**  
**Answer:** Be it known unto the king, that the Jews which came up from thee to us  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:12

**Q2. To what city are the Jews described as coming?**  
**Answer:** are come unto Jerusalem,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:12

**Q3. What are the Jews described as building?**  
**Answer:** building the rebellious and the bad city, and have set up the walls thereof, and joined the foundations.  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:12

---

### Ezra 4:13

**Q1. What did the writers say would happen if this city were built and the walls set up again?**  
**Answer:** Be it known now unto the king, that, if this city be builded, and the walls set up again, then will they not pay toll, tribute, and custom,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:13

**Q2. What would the rebuilding of the city eventually end up doing to the revenue of the kings?**  
**Answer:** and so thou shalt endamage the revenue of the kings.  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:13

**Q3. Because the writers had maintenance from the king's palace, what did they feel it was not meet to do?**  
**Answer:** Now because we have maintenance from the king's palace, and it was not meet for us to see the king's dishonour, therefore have we sent and certified the king;  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:13

---

### Ezra 4:14

**Q1. Because the writers had maintenance from the king's palace, what was not meet for them to see?**  
**Answer:** the king's dishonour  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:14

**Q2. What action did they take because it was not meet to see the king's dishonour?**  
**Answer:** therefore have we sent and certified the king;  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:14

**Q3. What did they have from the king's palace?**  
**Answer:** maintenance  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:14

---

### Ezra 4:15

**Q1. Where was search to be made in the book of the records of thy fathers?**  
**Answer:** That search may be made in the book of the records of thy fathers:  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:15

**Q2. What kind of city did the writers call Jerusalem in their letter?**  
**Answer:** a rebellious city, hurtful unto kings and provinces,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:15

**Q3. What did they say had been done within the city of Jerusalem in old time?**  
**Answer:** and that they have moved sedition within the same of old time:  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:15

---

### Ezra 4:16

**Q1. What did the king get certified of regarding the city of Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** We certify the king that, if this city be builded again, and the walls thereof set up,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:16

**Q2. What would happen on this side the river if Jerusalem's walls were set up?**  
**Answer:** by this means thou shalt have no portion on this side the river.  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:16

**Q3. What was the fate of the city according to this verse if it is built again?**  
**Answer:** if this city be builded again, and the walls thereof set up...  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:16

### Ezra 4:17

**Q1. To whom did the king send an answer?**  
**Answer:** Unto Rehum the chancellor, and to Shimshai the scribe, and to the rest of their companions that dwell in Samaria,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:17

**Q2. Where did the rest of their companions dwell?**  
**Answer:** in Samaria, and unto the rest beyond the river,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:17

**Q3. What greeting or title did the king use in his reply?**  
**Answer:** Peace, and at such a time.  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:17

---

### Ezra 4:18

**Q1. What had been plainly read before the king?**  
**Answer:** The letter which ye sent unto us hath been plainly read before me.  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:18

**Q2. By whom was the letter plainly read?**  
**Answer:** Read before me (the king)  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:18

**Answer:** ye (Rehum, Shimshai, and companions)

**Answer:** And I commanded, and search hath been made,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:19

**Q2. What had this city made of old against kings?**  
**Answer:** and it is found that this city of old time hath made insurrection against kings,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:19

**Answer:** and that rebellion and sedition have been made therein.

**Answer:** Give ye now commandment to cause these men to cease,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:21

**Answer:** and that this city be not builded, until another commandment shall be given from me.

**Answer:** Take heed now that ye fail not to do this:

### Ezra 4:23

**Answer:** Now when the copy of king Artaxerxes' letter was read before Rehum, and Shimshai the scribe, and their companions,  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:23

**Q2. Where did they go in haste?**  
**Answer:** they went up in haste to Jerusalem  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:23

**Answer:** unto the Jews, and made them to cease by force and power.  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:23

### Ezra 4:24

**Q1. What work ceased upon the house of God which is at Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Then ceased the work of the house of God which is at Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:24

**Q2. Until what year of the reign of Darius king of Persia did the work cease?**  
**Answer:** So it ceased unto the second year of the reign of Darius king of Persia.  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:24

**Q3. Where was the house of God located?**  
**Answer:** at Jerusalem  
**Verse:** Ezra 4:24
