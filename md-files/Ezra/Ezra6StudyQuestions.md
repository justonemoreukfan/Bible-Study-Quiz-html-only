# Ezra 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezra 6:1

**Q1. What did Darius the king make?**  
**Answer:** A decree.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:1

**Q2. Where was search made?**  
**Answer:** In the house of the rolls, where the treasures were laid up in Babylon.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:1

**Q3. In what place was the house where the search was made?**  
**Answer:** Babylon.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:1

---

### Ezra 6:2

**Q1. Where was there found a roll?**  
**Answer:** At Achmetha, in the palace that is in the province of the Medes.  
**Answer reference:** In the palace that is in the province of the Medes.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:2

**Q2. What was written in the roll?**  
**Answer:** A record.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:2

**Q3. What was contained in the record concerning the house of God at Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** A memorandum.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:2

---

### Ezra 6:3

**Q1. In what year of king Cyrus was the decree made concerning the house of God at Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The first year of Cyrus the king.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:3

**Q2. What was to be the place where they offered sacrifices?**  
**Answer:** The place where they offered sacrifices, and let the foundations thereof be strongly laid.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:3

**Q3. What were the height and breadth of the house?**  
**Answer:** The height thereof threescore cubits, and the breadth thereof threescore cubits.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:3

---

### Ezra 6:4

**Q1. How many rows of great stones were to be set?**  
**Answer:** Three rows of great stones.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:4

**Q2. What kind of new timber was to be used?**  
**Answer:** A row of new timber.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:4

**Q3. From where was the expenses to be given?**  
**Answer:** Out of the king's house.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:4

---

### Ezra 6:5

**Q1. What vessels of the house of God were to be restored?**  
**Answer:** The golden and silver vessels of the house of God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:5

**Q2. Who took the golden and silver vessels out of the temple which was at Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Nebuchadnezzar.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:5

**Q3. Where were the vessels brought?**  
**Answer:** Unto Babylon.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:5

---

### Ezra 6:6

**Q1. Who was told to be far from thence?**  
**Answer:** Tatnai, governor beyond the river, Shetharboznai, and their companions the Apharsachites, which are beyond the river.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:6

**Q2. Who were the companions mentioned alongside Tatnai and Shetharboznai?**  
**Answer:** The Apharsachites, which are beyond the river.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:6

**Q3. What instruction was given to Tatnai and his companions regarding the work?**  
**Answer:** Be ye far from thence.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:6

---

### Ezra 6:7

**Q1. What were the governor of the Jews and the elders of the Jews permitted to do with the house of God?**  
**Answer:** Let the work of this house of God alone.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:7

**Q2. Who was to build the house of God on his place?**  
**Answer:** The governor of the Jews and the elders of the Jews.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:7

**Q3. Whose house was allowed to be built on his place?**  
**Answer:** The house of God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:7

---

### Ezra 6:8

**Q1. What did the king make a decree concerning?**  
**Answer:** What ye shall do to the elders of these Jews for the building of this house of God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:8

**Q2. From what source were the expenses to be given with speed unto these men?**  
**Answer:** Of the king's goods, even of the tribute beyond the river.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:8

**Q3. Why were the expenses to be given unto these men?**  
**Answer:** That they be not hindered.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:8

---

### Ezra 6:9

**Q1. What was to be given day by day without fail?**  
**Answer:** Both young bullocks, and rams, and lambs, for the burnt offerings of the God of heaven, wheat, salt, wine, and oil.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:9

**Q2. Who was to appoint the things to be given?**  
**Answer:** According to the appointment of the priests which are at Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:9

**Q3. To whom were the things to be given?**  
**Answer:** The elders of the Jews.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:9

---

### Ezra 6:10

**Q1. What kind of sacrifices were they to offer unto the God of heaven?**  
**Answer:** Sacrifices of sweet savours.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:10

**Q2. For whom were they to pray?**  
**Answer:** The life of the king, and of his sons.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:10

**Q3. Who were they praying for when they prayed for the king and his sons?**  
**Answer:** The life of the king, and of his sons.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:10

---

### Ezra 6:11

**Q1. What was made concerning whoever would alter this word?**  
**Answer:** A decree.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:11

**Q2. What was to be pulled down from his house?**  
**Answer:** Timber.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:11

**Q3. What was to be done upon the timber pulled down from his house?**  
**Answer:** Let him be hanged thereon.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:11

---

### Ezra 6:12

**Q1. Who has caused his name to dwell there to destroy any king and people?**  
**Answer:** The God that hath caused his name to dwell there.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:12

**Q2. What might God do to any king and people that shall put to their hand to alter and to destroy this house of God which is at Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Destroy them.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:12

**Q3. Who issued the decree that was executed with speed?**  
**Answer:** I Darius.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:12

---

### Ezra 6:13

**Q1. Who did according to that which Darius the king had sent?**  
**Answer:** Tatnai, governor on this side the river, Shetharboznai, and their companions.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:13

**Q2. Who was the king that sent the message?**  
**Answer:** Darius the king.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:13

**Q3. How did Tatnai, Shetharboznai, and their companions act regarding the message?  
**Answer:** They did accordingly with speed.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:13

---

### Ezra 6:14

**Q1. Who builded, and prospered through the prophesying of Haggai the prophet and Zechariah the son of Iddo?**  
**Answer:** The elders of the Jews.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:14

**Q2. Whose prophesying did the elders of the Jews prosper through?**  
**Answer:** Haggai the prophet and Zechariah the son of Iddo.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:14

**Q3. By whose commandment was the house builded?**  
**Answer:** The commandment of the God of Israel, and according to the commandment of Cyrus, and Darius, and Artaxerxes king of Persia.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:14

---

### Ezra 6:15

**Q1. When was this house finished?**  
**Answer:** On the third day of the month Adar.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:15

**Q2. In what year of the reign of Darius the king was the house finished?**  
**Answer:** The sixth year of the reign of Darius the king.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:15

**Q3. What month is mentioned for the completion of the house?**  
**Answer:** Adar.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:15

---

### Ezra 6:16

**Q1. Who kept the dedication of this house of God with joy?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel, the priests, and the Levites, and the rest of the children of the captivity.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:16

**Q2. What did the children of Israel, the priests, the Levites, and the rest of the children of the captivity keep with joy?**  
**Answer:** The dedication of this house of God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:16

**Q3. With what attitude did they keep the dedication of this house of God?**  
**Answer:** With joy.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:16

---

### Ezra 6:17

**Q1. What was offered at the dedication of the house of God?**  
**Answer:** One hundred bullocks, two hundred rams, four hundred lambs.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:17

**Q2. For a sin offering for all Israel, what was offered?**  
**Answer:** Twelve he goats, according to the number of the tribes of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:17

**Q3. According to what number were the twelve he goats offered?**  
**Answer:** According to the number of the tribes of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:17

---

### Ezra 6:18

**Q1. Where did they set the priests in their divisions?**  
**Answer:** In their service for God, which is at Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:18

**Q2. Where did they set the Levites in their courses?**  
**Answer:** For the service of God, which is at Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:18

**Q3. According to what was it written in the book of Moses?**  
**Answer:** As it is written in the book of Moses.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:18

---

### Ezra 6:19

**Q1. Who kept the passover?**  
**Answer:** The children of the captivity.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:19

**Q2. On what day of the first month was the passover kept?**  
**Answer:** Upon the fourteenth day of the first month.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:19

**Q3. What event was kept upon the fourteenth day of the first month?**  
**Answer:** The passover.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:19

---

### Ezra 6:20

**Q1. Who were purified together?**  
**Answer:** The priests and the Levites.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:20

**Q2. Who killed the passover for all the children of the captivity?**  
**Answer:** The Levites.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:20

**Q3. For whom did the Levites kill the passover?**  
**Answer:** For their brethren the priests, and for themselves.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:20

---

### Ezra 6:21

**Q1. Who did eat the passover?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel which were come again out of captivity.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:21

**Q2. Who else joined with them to eat the passover?**  
**Answer:** All such as had separated themselves unto them from the filthiness of the heathen of the land.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:21

**Q3. Whom did they seek?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:21

---

### Ezra 6:22

**Q1. What feast did they keep with joy?**  
**Answer:** The feast of unleavened bread seven days.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:22

**Q2. What did the LORD make them?**  
**Answer:** Joyful.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:22

**Q3. Whose heart did the LORD turn unto them to strengthen their hands in the work of the house of God?**  
**Answer:** The heart of the king of Assyria.  
**Verse:** Ezra 6:22
