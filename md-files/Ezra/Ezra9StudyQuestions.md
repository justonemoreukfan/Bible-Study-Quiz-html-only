# Ezra 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezra 9:1

**Q1. Who came to Ezra after these things were done?**  
**Answer:** The princes.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:1

**Q2. From whom had the people of Israel, and the priests, and the Levites not separated themselves?**  
**Answer:** The people of the lands.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:1

**Q3. According to verse 1, whose abominations did the people do according to?**  
**Answer:** The Canaanites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Jebusites, the Ammonites, the Moabites, the Egyptians, and the Amorites.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:1

---

### Ezra 9:2

**Q1. What have they taken of their daughters for themselves and for their sons?**  
**Answer:** Wives.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:2

**Q2. Who have the holy seed mixed themselves with?**  
**Answer:** The people of those lands.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:2

**Q3. Who were chief in this trespass?**  
**Answer:** The hand of the princes and rulers.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:2

---

### Ezra 9:3

**Q1. What did Ezra do when he heard this thing?**  
**Answer:** Rent his garment and his mantle.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:3

**Q2. What did Ezra pluck off from his head and from his beard?**  
**Answer:** Hair of his head and of his beard.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:3

**Q3. How did Ezra sit down?**  
**Answer:** Astonished.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:3

---

### Ezra 9:4

**Q1. Who were assembled unto Ezra?**  
**Answer:** Every one that trembled at the words of the God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:4

**Q2. Because of whose transgression were they assembled unto Ezra?**  
**Answer:** The transgression of those that had been carried away.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:4

**Q3. Until when did Ezra sit astonished?**  
**Answer:** Unto the evening sacrifice.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:4

---

### Ezra 9:5

**Q1. When did Ezra rise up from his heaviness?**  
**Answer:** At the evening sacrifice.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:5

**Q2. What did Ezra have rent when he rose up?**  
**Answer:** His garment and his mantle.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:5

**Q3. In what posture did Ezra fall upon his knees?**  
**Answer:** Spread out his hands unto the LORD his God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:5

---

### Ezra 9:6

**Q1. What did Ezra say he was ashamed and blush to do?**  
**Answer:** Lift up his face to his God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:6

**Q2. What are our iniquities increased over?**  
**Answer:** Our head.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:6

**Q3. Up unto what place is our trespass grown?**  
**Answer:** Up unto the heavens.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:6

---

### Ezra 9:7

**Q1. From what days have we been in a great trespass unto this day?**  
**Answer:** The days of our fathers.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:7

**Q2. Because of our iniquities, to what have we, our kings, and our priests been delivered?**  
**Answer:** The hand of the kings of the lands, to the sword, to captivity, and to a spoil, and to confusion of face.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:7

**Q3. As of what day is the confusion of face as it is this day?**  
**Answer:** This day.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:7

---

### Ezra 9:8

**Q1. For how brief a moment has grace been showed from the LORD our God?**  
**Answer:** A little space.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:8

**Q2. What did the LORD our God leave us to remain?**  
**Answer:** A remnant.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:8

**Q3. What did the LORD give us in his holy place, to lighten our eyes and revive us?**  
**Answer:** A nail in his holy place.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:8

---

### Ezra 9:9

**Q1. What were we, though we were bondmen?**  
**Answer:** Our God hath not forsaken us in our bondage.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:9

**Q2. What did our God extend unto us in the sight of the kings of Persia?**  
**Answer:** Mercy.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:9

**Q3. What did our God give us to set us up, to repair the desolations thereof, and to give us a wall?**  
**Answer:** A reviving.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:9

---

### Ezra 9:10

**Q1. After all this, what have we done, O our God?**  
**Answer:** Forsaken thy commandments.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:10

**Q2. Whose voice have we forsaken?**  
**Answer:** Thy commandments.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:10

**Q3. By whose hand hast thou commanded?**  
**Answer:** Thy servants the prophets.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:10

---

### Ezra 9:11

**Q1. By whom hast thou commanded, saying, The land, unto which ye go to possess it, is an unclean land?**  
**Answer:** Thy servants the prophets.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:11

**Q2. With what have the people of the lands filled the land from one end to another by their filthiness?**  
**Answer:** Their uncleanness.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:11

**Q3. What did the people of the lands do by their filthiness?**  
**Answer:** Filled it from one end to another with their uncleanness.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:11

---

### Ezra 9:12

**Q1. What are ye not to give unto their sons?**  
**Answer:** Your daughters.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:12

**Q2. What are ye not to take for your sons or for yourselves?**  
**Answer:** Their daughters.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:12

**Q3. What are ye never to seek?**  
**Answer:** Their peace or their wealth.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:12

---

### Ezra 9:13

**Q1. After all that is come upon us for our evil deeds, and for what else?**  
**Answer:** Our great trespass.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:13

**Q2. Seeing that thou our God hast punished us less than our iniquities deserve, what else hast thou given us?**  
**Answer:** Such deliverance as this.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:13

**Q3. What did God punish us less than?**  
**Answer:** Our iniquities deserve.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:13

---

### Ezra 9:14

**Q1. Should we again break thy commandments, and join in affinity with the people of these abominations, what wouldst thou be angry with us till?**  
**Answer:** Thou be angry with us till thou hadst consumed us, so that there should be no remnant nor escaping.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:14

**Q2. What kind of people are those whose abominations the people joined in affinity with?**  
**Answer:** The people of these abominations.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:14

**Q3. What would there be none of if the Lord consumed them?**  
**Answer:** Remnant nor escaping.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:14

---

### Ezra 9:15

**Q1. O LORD God of Israel, what are we?**  
**Answer:** Righteous.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:15

**Q2. What are we left remaining, as it is this day?**  
**Answer:** A escaped remnant.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:15

**Q3. Before whom are we in our trespasses?**  
**Answer:** Before thee.  
**Verse:** Ezra 9:15
