# Ezra 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezra 3:1

**Q1. When was the seventh month come and the children of Israel were in the cities?**  
**Answer:** When the seventh month was come, and the children of Israel were in the cities.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:1

**Q2. As one man, where did the people gather themselves together?**  
**Answer:** To Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:1

**Q3. Who gathered themselves together as one man to Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The people.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:1

---

### Ezra 3:2

**Q1. Who stood up and builded the altar of the God of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Jeshua the son of Jozadak, and his brethren the priests, and Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, and his brethren.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:2

**Q2. What did they stand up and build?**  
**Answer:** The altar of the God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:2

**Q3. To offer burnt offerings thereon, as it is written in what?**  
**Answer:** The law of Moses the man of God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:2

---

### Ezra 3:3

**Q1. Why did they set the altar upon his bases?**  
**Answer:** Because fear was upon them because of the people of those countries.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:3

**Q2. What did they offer thereon unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Burnt offerings morning and evening.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:3

**Q3. When did they offer burnt offerings unto the LORD upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** Morning and evening.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:3

---

### Ezra 3:4

**Q1. What feast did they keep, as it is written?**  
**Answer:** The feast of tabernacles.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:4

**Q2. How did they offer the daily burnt offerings by number, according to what?**  
**Answer:** The custom, as the duty of every day required.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:4

**Q3. What did they offer according to the custom, as the duty of every day required?**  
**Answer:** The daily burnt offerings by number.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:4

---

### Ezra 3:5

**Q1. And afterward what did they offer?**  
**Answer:** The continual burnt offering, both of the new moons, and of all the set feasts of the LORD that were consecrated.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:5

**Q2. What did every man offer that willingly offered a freewill offering unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** A freewill offering unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:5

**Q3. Whose set feasts were consecrated?**  
**Answer:** The LORD'S.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:5

---

### Ezra 3:6

**Q1. From what day began they to offer burnt offerings unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** From the first day of the seventh month.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:6

**Q2. What was not yet laid at this time?**  
**Answer:** The foundation of the temple of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:6

**Q3. Who began to offer burnt offerings unto the LORD from the first day of the seventh month?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:6

---

### Ezra 3:7

**Q1. To whom did they give money?**  
**Answer:** The masons and to the carpenters.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:7

**Q2. What did they give to the masons and to the carpenters?**  
**Answer:** Money.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:7

**Q3. Meat, and drink, and oil, unto whom did they give them, that they might bring cedar trees from Lebanon to the sea of Joppa?**  
**Answer:** Them of Sidon, and to them of Tyre.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:7

---

### Ezra 3:8

**Q1. In what year of their coming unto the house of God at Jerusalem did they begin?**  
**Answer:** The second year.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:8

**Q2. Who began, along with Jeshua the son of Jozadak, and the remnant of their brethren the priests and the Levites?**  
**Answer:** Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, and Jeshua the son of Jozadak.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:8

**Q3. Over what did they appoint the Levites, from twenty years old and upward, to set forward the work of the house of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The work of the house of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:8

---

### Ezra 3:9

**Q1. Who stood together with his sons and his brethren to set forward the workmen in the house of God?**  
**Answer:** Jeshua with his sons and his brethren, Kadmiel and his sons, the sons of Judah.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:9

**Q2. Who were the sons of Henadad, with their sons and their brethren the Levites?**  
**Answer:** The Levites.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:9

**Q3. To do what did Jeshua, Kadmiel, and the sons of Henadad stand together?**  
**Answer:** To set forward the workmen in the house of God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:9

---

### Ezra 3:10

**Q1. When the builders laid the foundation of the temple of the LORD, who did they set in their apparel with trumpets?**  
**Answer:** The priests.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:10

**Q2. Who stood with cymbals, to praise the LORD, after the ordinance of David king of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The Levites the sons of Asaph.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:10

**Q3. According to whose ordinance did the sons of Asaph stand with cymbals to praise the LORD?**  
**Answer:** David king of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:10

---

### Ezra 3:11

**Q1. And they sang together by course in praising and giving thanks unto whom?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:11

**Q2. For what reason did all the people shout with a great shout, when they praised the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Because the foundation of the house of the LORD was laid.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:11

**Q3. Toward the LORD, for his mercy endureth how long toward Israel?**  
**Answer:** For ever.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:11

---

### Ezra 3:12

**Q1. Who wept with a loud voice, when they saw the foundation of this house laid before their eyes?**  
**Answer:** Many of the priests and Levites and chief of the fathers, who were ancient men.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:12

**Q2. Who shouted aloud for joy?**  
**Answer:** Many.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:12

**Q3. What did the ancient men do when they saw the foundation of this house laid before their eyes?**  
**Answer:** Wept with a loud voice.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:12

---

### Ezra 3:13

**Q1. So that the people could not discern the noise of the shout of joy from what?**  
**Answer:** The noise of the weeping of the people.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:13

**Q2. Why could the people not discern the noise of the shout of joy from the noise of the weeping?**  
**Answer:** For the people shouted with a loud shout, and the noise was heard afar off.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:13

**Q3. How far was the noise heard?**  
**Answer:** Afar off.  
**Verse:** Ezra 3:13
