# Ezra 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezra 8:1

**Q1. Who are listed at the beginning of Ezra 8?**  
**Answer:** The chief of their fathers, and their genealogy.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:1

**Q2. During whose reign did these chiefs go up from Babylon?**  
**Answer:** Artaxerxes the king.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:1

**Q3. From where did they go up with Ezra?**  
**Answer:** Babylon.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:1

---

### Ezra 8:2

**Q1. Of the sons of Phinehas, who is named?**  
**Answer:** Gershom.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:2

**Q2. Of the sons of Ithamar, who is named?**  
**Answer:** Daniel.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:2

**Q3. Of the sons of David, who is named?**  
**Answer:** Hattush.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:2

---

### Ezra 8:3

**Q1. Of the sons of Shechaniah, who is mentioned?**  
**Answer:** The sons of Pharosh.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:3

**Q2. Who else was reckoned by genealogy of the males along with Zechariah?**  
**Answer:** An hundred and fifty persons.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:3

**Answer:** Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:3

### Ezra 8:15

**Q1. Where did Ezra gather the people together?**  
**Answer:** To the river that runeth to Ahava.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:15

**Q2. How long did they abide in tents near Ahava?**  
**Answer:** Three days.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:15

**Q3. What did Ezra find lacking among the people and priests upon viewing them?**  
**Answer:** None of the sons of Levi.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:15

---

### Ezra 8:16

**Q1. Whom did Ezra send for unto Eliezer, Ariel, Shemaiah, Elnathan, Jarib, Elnathan, Nathan, Zechariah, and Meshullam?**  
**Answer:** Men of understanding.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:16

**Q2. Who were the chief men sent by Ezra?**  
**Answer:** Eliezer, Ariel, Shemaiah, Elnathan, Jarib, Elnathan, Nathan, Zechariah, and Meshullam.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:16

**Q3. To whom were these chief men sent?**  
**Answer:** Iddo the place at Casiphia.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:16

---

### Ezra 8:17

**Q1. With whom did Ezra give commandments concerning Iddo and his brethren?**  
**Answer:** Iddo the chief at the place Casiphia.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:17

**Q2. What did Ezra tell them to bring unto us?**  
**Answer:** Ministers for the house of our God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:17

**Q3. Where was Iddo located?**  
**Answer:** At the place Casiphia.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:17

---

### Ezra 8:18

**Q1. Who brought them as a good hand of God upon them?**  
**Answer:** A man of understanding, of the sons of Mahli, the son of Levi, the son of Israel.

**Q2. Who was Sherebiah?**  
**Answer:** A man of understanding, of the sons of Mahli.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:18

**Q3. Who were with Sherebiah and his sons and brethren?**  
**Answer:** Eighteen.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:18

### Ezra 8:21

**Q1. Where did Ezra proclaim a fast?**  
**Answer:** At the river of Ahava.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:21

**Q2. Why did they afflict themselves before God?**  
**Answer:** To seek of him a right way for us, and for our little ones, and for all our substance.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:21

**Q3. What did Ezra seek from God during this fast?**  
**Answer:** A right way for them, their little ones, and all their substance.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:21

---

### Ezra 8:22

**Q1. Why was Ezra ashamed to require a band of soldiers and horsemen from the king?**  
**Answer:** Because they had spoken unto the king, saying, The hand of our God is upon all them for good that seek him.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:22

**Q2. What did Ezra tell the king concerning God's power and wrath?**  
**Answer:** His power and his wrath is against all them that forsake him.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:22

**Q3. What did the king's enemies on the way cause Ezra to refuse to ask for?**  
**Answer:** A band of soldiers and horsemen to help us against the enemy in the way.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:22

---

### Ezra 8:23

**Q1. What did they do regarding their safe journey?**  
**Answer:** Fasted and besought our God for this.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:23

**Q2. How did God answer their fasting and prayer?**  
**Answer:** He was entreated of them.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:23

**Q3. Where did they entreat God?**  
**Answer:** For this.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:23

---

### Ezra 8:24

**Q1. Whom did Ezra separate?**  
**Answer:** Twelve of the chief of the priests.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:24

**Q2. Who were separated along with the twelve chief priests?**  
**Answer:** Sherebiah, Hashabiah, and ten of their brethren with them.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:24

**Q3. What were Sherebiah and Hashabiah?**  
**Answer:** Chief of the priests (along with ten brethren).  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:24

---

### Ezra 8:25

**Q1. What did Ezra weigh unto them?**  
**Answer:** The silver, and the gold, and the vessels.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:25

**Q2. Whose offering was this silver, gold, and vessels?**  
**Answer:** The offering of the house of our God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:25

**Q3. Who offered these items?**  
**Answer:** The king, and his counsellors, and his lords, and all Israel there present.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:25

---

**Q1. How much silver did Ezra weigh into their hand?**

**Verse:** Ezra 8:26

**Q2. How many silver vessels were there?**  
**Answer:** An hundred talents.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:26

**Q3. How much gold was weighed out?**  
**Answer:** An hundred talents.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:26

---

### Ezra 8:27

**Q1. How many basons of gold were there?**  
**Answer:** Twenty basons.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:27

**Q2. What was the value of the twenty gold basons?**  
**Answer:** A thousand drams.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:27

**Q3. What kind of vessels were also included that were fine as copper?**  
**Answer:** Two vessels of fine copper, precious as gold.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:27

---

### Ezra 8:28

**Q1. What did Ezra say unto the priests regarding the vessels?**  
**Answer:** Ye are holy unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:28

**Q2. What else did Ezra say was holy?**  
**Answer:** The vessels.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:28

**Q3. To whom was the silver and the gold a freewill offering?**  
**Answer:** Unto the LORD God of your fathers.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:28

---

### Ezra 8:33

**Q1. On the fourth day, what was weighed in the house of God?**  
**Answer:** The silver and the gold and the vessels.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:33

**Q2. Into whose hand was the weighed treasure delivered?**  
**Answer:** Meremoth the son of Uriah the priest.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:33

**Q3. Who was with Meremoth the priest when the treasure was delivered?**  
**Answer:** Eleazar the son of Phinehas, and with them Jozabad the son of Jeshua, and Noadiah the son of Binnui, Levites.

---

### Ezra 8:34

**Q1. How was everything accounted for?**  
**Answer:** By number and by weight.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:34

**Q2. When was all the weight written?**  
**Answer:** At that time.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:34

**Q3. What was written at that time?**  
**Answer:** All the weight.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:34

---

### Ezra 8:35

**Q1. What did the children of those that had been carried away do upon arriving?**  
**Answer:** Offered burnt offerings unto the God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:35

**Q2. How many bullocks were offered for all Israel?**  
**Answer:** Twelve bullocks.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:35

**Q3. How many lambs were offered as a burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** Seventy and six lambs.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:35

---

### Ezra 8:36

**Q1. To whom did they deliver the king's commissions?**  
**Answer:** The king's lieutenants.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:36

**Q2. To whom else were the king's commissions delivered?**  
**Answer:** The governors on this side the river.  
**Verse:** Ezra 8:36

**Q3. How did these officials help the people and the house of God?**  
**Answer:** They furthered the people, and the house of God.
