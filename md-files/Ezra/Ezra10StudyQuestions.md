# Ezra 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezra 10:1

**Q1. What did Ezra do while he prayed and confessed, weeping and casting himself down?**  
**Answer:** He had prayed and when he had confessed, weeping and casting himself down before the house of God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:1

**Q2. Who assembled unto Ezra out of Israel during this time?**  
**Answer:** A very great congregation of men and women and children.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:1

**Q3. How did the people of Israel respond to Ezra's weeping?**  
**Answer:** The people wept sore.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:1

---

### Ezra 10:2

**Q4. Who answered and said unto Ezra, "We have trespassed against our God"?**  
**Answer:** Shechaniah the son of Jehiel, one of the sons of Elam.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:2

**Q5. What kind of wives had the people of Israel taken from the people of the land?**  
**Answer:** Strange wives.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:2

**Q6. Despite taking strange wives, what was still there concerning Israel?**  
**Answer:** Yet now there is hope in Israel concerning this thing.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:2

---

### Ezra 10:3

**Q7. What did Shechaniah propose they should make with their God?**  
**Answer:** A covenant.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:3

**Q8. What action should be taken regarding the wives and those born of them?**  
**Answer:** To put away all the wives, and such as are born of them.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:3

**Q9. According to whose counsel should this action be done?**  
**Answer:** According to the counsel of my lord, and of those that tremble at the commandment of our God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:3

---

### Ezra 10:4

**Q10. What did Shechaniah tell Ezra to do regarding this matter?**  
**Answer:** Arise; for this matter belongeth unto thee.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:4

**Q11. Who was told to be with Ezra?**  
**Answer:** We also will be with thee.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:4

**Q12. What encouragement was given to Ezra at the end of the verse?**  
**Answer:** Be of good courage, and do it.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:4

---

### Ezra 10:5

**Q13. Who arose and made the chief priests, the Levites, and all Israel to swear?**  
**Answer:** Ezra.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:5

**Q14. What did Ezra make the people swear to do?**  
**Answer:** That they should do according to this word.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:5

**Q15. Did the people take the oath?**  
**Answer:** And they sware.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:5

---

### Ezra 10:6

**Q16. From before where did Ezra rise up?**  
**Answer:** From before the house of God.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:6

**Q17. Into whose chamber did Ezra go?**  
**Answer:** The chamber of Johanan the son of Eliashib.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:6

**Q18. Did Ezra eat bread or drink water while he went thither?**  
**Answer:** He did eat no bread, nor drink water.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:6

---

### Ezra 10:7

**Q19. What was made throughout Judah and Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** A proclamation.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:7

**Q20. Unto whom was the proclamation made?**  
**Answer:** Unto all the children of the captivity.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:7

**Q21. Where were the children of the captivity gathered unto?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:7

---

### Ezra 10:8

**Q22. Within how many days was everyone to come according to the princes and elders?**  
**Answer:** Within three days.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:8

**Q23. What would happen to the substance of anyone who did not come within three days?**  
**Answer:** His substance should be forfeited.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:8

**Q24. Who would be separated from the congregation of the captivity upon failure to come?**  
**Answer:** Himself.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:8

---

### Ezra 10:9

**Q25. Who were gathered together within three days?**  
**Answer:** All the men of Judah and Benjamin.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:9

**Q26. On what day of the month did the men gather in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The twentieth day of the month.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:9

**Q27. How did the people sit in the street of the house of God?**  
**Answer:** Trembling for this matter, and for the great rain.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:9

---

### Ezra 10:10

**Q28. Who stood up and said unto the people?**  
**Answer:** Ezra the priest.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:10

**Q29. What did Ezra accuse the people of doing?**  
**Answer:** Ye have transgressed, and have taken strange wives.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:10

**Q30. What was the result of taking strange wives, according to Ezra?**  
**Answer:** To increase the trespass of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:10

---

### Ezra 10:11

**Q31. What did Ezra tell the people to make unto the LORD God of their fathers?**  
**Answer:** Confession.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:11

**Q32. Whose pleasure were the people told to do?**  
**Answer:** His pleasure.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:11

**Q33. From whom were the people told to separate themselves?**  
**Answer:** From the people of the land, and from the strange wives.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:11

---

### Ezra 10:12

**Q34. Who answered and said with a loud voice?**  
**Answer:** All the congregation.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:12

**Q35. What did the congregation say they must do regarding Ezra's voice?**  
**Answer:** As thou hast said, so must we do.  
**Verse:** Ezra 10:12
