### Nahum 1:2
**Q4. What is stated in the opening of Nahum 1:2?**  
**Answer:** God is jealous, and the LORD revengeth.  
**Verse:** Nahum 1:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Nahum 1:2?**  
**Answer:** the LORD revengeth, and is furious.  
**Verse:** Nahum 1:2

**Q6. What further truth is recorded in Nahum 1:2?**

**Verse:** Nahum 1:2

### Nahum 1:3

**Verse:** Nahum 1:3

**Q8. What additional detail is declared in Nahum 1:3?**
