# Nahum 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Nahum 2:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Nahum 2:1?**  
**Answer:** He that dasheth in pieces is come up before thy face.  
**Verse:** Nahum 2:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Nahum 2:1?**  
**Answer:** keep the munition, watch the way, make thy loins strong, fortify thy power mightily.  
**Verse:** Nahum 2:1

**Q3. According to Nahum 2:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** He that dasheth in pieces is come up before thy face: keep the munition, watch the way, make thy loins strong, fortify thy power mightily.  
**Verse:** Nahum 2:1
