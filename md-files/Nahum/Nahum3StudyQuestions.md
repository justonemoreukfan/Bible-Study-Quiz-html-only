# Nahum 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Nahum 3:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Nahum 3:1?**  
**Answer:** Woe to the bloody city! it is all full of lies and robbery.  
**Verse:** Nahum 3:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Nahum 3:1?**  
**Answer:** the prey departeth not.  
**Verse:** Nahum 3:1

**Q3. According to Nahum 3:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Woe to the bloody city! it is all full of lies and robbery; the prey departeth not;.  
**Verse:** Nahum 3:1

---

### Nahum 3:2
**Q4. According to Nahum 3:2, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** The noise of a whip.  
**Verse:** Nahum 3:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Nahum 3:2?**

**Verse:** Nahum 3:2

**Verse:** Nahum 3:2

**Verse:** Nahum 3:4
