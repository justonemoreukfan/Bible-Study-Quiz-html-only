### Zechariah 13:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Zechariah 13:2?**  
**Answer:** And it shall come to pass in that day, saith the LORD of hosts, that I will cut off the names of the idols out of the land, and they shall no more be remembered.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 13:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Zechariah 13:2?**  
**Answer:** and also I will cause the prophets and the unclean spirit to pass out of the land.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 13:2

**Q6. According to Zechariah 13:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And it shall come to pass in that day, saith the LORD of hosts, that I will cut off the names of the idols out of the land, and they shall no more be remembered: and also I will cause the prophets and the unclean spirit to pass out of the land.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 13:2
