# Zechariah 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Zechariah 9:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Zechariah 9:1?**  
**Answer:** The burden of the word of the LORD in the land of Hadrach, and Damascus shall be the rest thereof.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 9:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Zechariah 9:1?**  
**Answer:** when the eyes of man, as of all the tribes of Israel, shall be toward the LORD.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 9:1

**Q3. According to Zechariah 9:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** The burden of the word of the LORD in the land of Hadrach, and Damascus shall be the rest thereof: when the eyes of man, as of all the tribes of Israel, shall be toward the LORD.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 9:1
