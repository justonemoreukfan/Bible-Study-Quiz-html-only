### Zechariah 7:1
**Q1. According to Zechariah 7:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And it came to pass in the fourth year of king Darius.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 7:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Zechariah 7:1?**  
**Answer:** that the word of the LORD came unto Zechariah in the fourth day of the ninth month.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 7:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Zechariah 7:1?**  
**Answer:** even in Chisleu.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 7:1
