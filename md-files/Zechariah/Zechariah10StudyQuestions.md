# Zechariah 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Zechariah 10:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Zechariah 10:1?**  
**Answer:** Ask ye of the LORD rain in the time of the latter rain.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 10:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Zechariah 10:1?**  
**Answer:** so the LORD shall make bright clouds, and give them showers of rain, to every one grass in the field.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 10:1

**Q3. According to Zechariah 10:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Ask ye of the LORD rain in the time of the latter rain; so the LORD shall make bright clouds, and give them showers of rain, to every one grass in the field.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 10:1
