# Zechariah 11 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Zechariah 11:1
**Q1. According to Zechariah 11:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Open thy doors.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 11:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Zechariah 11:1?**  
**Answer:** O Lebanon.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 11:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Zechariah 11:1?**  
**Answer:** that the fire may devour thy cedars.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 11:1

---

### Zechariah 11:2
**Q4. What is stated in the opening of Zechariah 11:2?**  
**Answer:** Howl, fir tree.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 11:2

**Verse:** Zechariah 11:2
