# Zechariah 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Zechariah 5:1
**Q1. According to Zechariah 5:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Then I turned.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 5:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Zechariah 5:1?**  
**Answer:** and lifted up mine eyes.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 5:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Zechariah 5:1?**  
**Answer:** and looked, and behold a flying roll.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 5:1

---

### Zechariah 5:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Zechariah 5:2?**  
**Answer:** And he said unto me, What seest thou? And I answered, I see a flying roll.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 5:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Zechariah 5:2?**  
**Answer:** the length thereof is twenty cubits, and the breadth thereof ten cubits.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 5:2

**Q6. According to Zechariah 5:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And he said unto me, What seest thou? And I answered, I see a flying roll; the length thereof is twenty cubits, and the breadth thereof ten cubits.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 5:2

---

### Zechariah 5:3
**Q7. What is stated in the opening of Zechariah 5:3?**  
**Answer:** Then said he unto me, This is the curse that goeth forth over the face of the whole earth.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 5:3

**Q8. What is declared next in Zechariah 5:3?**  
**Answer:** for every one that stealeth shall be cut off as on this side according to it.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 5:3

**Q9. What further truth is recorded in Zechariah 5:3?**  
**Answer:** and every one that sweareth shall be cut off as on that side according to it.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 5:3

---

### Zechariah 5:4
**Q10. What is stated in the first clause of Zechariah 5:4?**  
**Answer:** I will bring it forth, saith the LORD of hosts, and it shall enter into the house of the thief, and into the house of him that sweareth falsely by my name.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 5:4

**Q11. What additional detail is declared in Zechariah 5:4?**  
**Answer:** and it shall remain in the midst of his house, and shall consume it with the timber thereof and the stones thereof.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 5:4

**Q12. According to Zechariah 5:4, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** I will bring it forth, saith the LORD of hosts, and it shall enter into the house of the thief, and into the house of him that sweareth falsely by my name: and it shall remain in the midst of his house, and shall consume it with the timber thereof and the stones thereof.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 5:4
