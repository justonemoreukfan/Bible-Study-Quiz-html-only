# Zechariah 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Zechariah 4:1
**Q1. According to Zechariah 4:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And the angel that talked with me came again.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 4:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Zechariah 4:1?**  
**Answer:** and waked me.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 4:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Zechariah 4:1?**  
**Answer:** as a man that is wakened out of his sleep.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 4:1
