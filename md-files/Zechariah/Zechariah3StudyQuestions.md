# Zechariah 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Zechariah 3:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Zechariah 3:1 state?**  
**Answer:** And he shewed me Joshua the high priest standing before the angel of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 3:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Zechariah 3:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** and Satan standing at his right hand to resist him.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 3:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Zechariah 3:1?**  
**Answer:** And he shewed me Joshua the high priest standing before the angel of the LORD, and Satan standing at his right hand to resist him.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 3:1

### Zechariah 3:2
**Q4. Who spake unto Satan in Zechariah 3:2?**  
**Answer:** LORD.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 3:2

**Q5. What did LORD declare or say in Zechariah 3:2?**  
**Answer:** The LORD rebuke thee, O Satan; even the LORD that hath chosen Jerusalem rebuke thee: is not this a brand plucked out of the fire?

**Q6. What is recorded in Zechariah 3:2?**  
**Answer:** And the LORD said unto Satan, The LORD rebuke thee, O Satan; even the LORD that hath chosen Jerusalem rebuke thee: is not this a brand plucked out of the fire?

### Zechariah 3:7
**Q19. What is stated in the first clause of Zechariah 3:7?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD of hosts.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 3:7

**Q20. What additional detail is declared in Zechariah 3:7?**  
**Answer:** If thou wilt walk in my ways, and if thou wilt keep my charge, then thou shalt also judge my house, and shalt also keep my courts, and I will give thee places to walk among these that stand by.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 3:7

**Q21. According to Zechariah 3:7, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD of hosts; If thou wilt walk in my ways, and if thou wilt keep my charge, then thou shalt also judge my house, and shalt also keep my courts, and I will give thee places to walk among these that stand by.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 3:7
