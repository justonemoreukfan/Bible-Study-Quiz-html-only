# Zechariah 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Zechariah 6:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Zechariah 6:1?**  
**Answer:** And I turned, and lifted up mine eyes, and looked, and, behold, there came four chariots out from between two mountains.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 6:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Zechariah 6:1?**  
**Answer:** and the mountains were mountains of brass.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 6:1

**Q3. According to Zechariah 6:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And I turned, and lifted up mine eyes, and looked, and, behold, there came four chariots out from between two mountains; and the mountains were mountains of brass.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 6:1
