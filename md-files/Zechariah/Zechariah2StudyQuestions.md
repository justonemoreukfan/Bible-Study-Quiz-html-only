# Zechariah 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Zechariah 2:1
**Q1. According to Zechariah 2:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** I lifted up mine eyes again.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 2:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Zechariah 2:1?**  
**Answer:** and looked.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 2:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Zechariah 2:1?**  
**Answer:** and behold a man with a measuring line in his hand.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 2:1
