# Zechariah 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Zechariah 12:1
**Q1. According to Zechariah 12:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** The burden of the word of the LORD for Israel.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 12:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Zechariah 12:1?**  
**Answer:** saith the LORD.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 12:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Zechariah 12:1?**  
**Answer:** which stretcheth forth the heavens, and layeth the foundation of the earth, and formeth the spirit of man within him.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 12:1

---

### Zechariah 12:2
**Q4. What does the first part of Zechariah 12:2 state?**  
**Answer:** I will make Jerusalem a cup of trembling unto all the people round about.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 12:2

**Q5. What does the second part of Zechariah 12:2 declare?**  
**Answer:** when they shall be in the siege both against Judah and against Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 12:2

**Q6. What is recorded in Zechariah 12:2?**  
**Answer:** Behold, I will make Jerusalem a cup of trembling unto all the people round about, when they shall be in the siege both against Judah and against Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 12:2

### Zechariah 12:3
**Q7. What is stated in the first clause of Zechariah 12:3?**  
**Answer:** And in that day will I make Jerusalem a burdensome stone for all people.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 12:3

**Q8. What additional detail is declared in Zechariah 12:3?**  
**Answer:** all that burden themselves with it shall be cut in pieces, though all the people of the earth be gathered together against it.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 12:3

**Q9. According to Zechariah 12:3, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And in that day will I make Jerusalem a burdensome stone for all people: all that burden themselves with it shall be cut in pieces, though all the people of the earth be gathered together against it.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 12:3
