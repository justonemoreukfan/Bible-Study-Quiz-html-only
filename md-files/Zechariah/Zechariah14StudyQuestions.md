# Zechariah 14 Study Questions (KJV)

### Zechariah 14:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Zechariah 14:1 state?**  
**Answer:** the day of the LORD cometh.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 14:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Zechariah 14:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** and thy spoil shall be divided in the midst of thee.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 14:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Zechariah 14:1?**  
**Answer:** Behold, the day of the LORD cometh, and thy spoil shall be divided in the midst of thee.  
**Verse:** Zechariah 14:1
