# Joshua 17 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Joshua 17:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Joshua 17:1?**  
**Answer:** There was also a lot for the tribe of Manasseh.  
**Verse:** Joshua 17:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Joshua 17:1?**  
**Answer:** for he was the firstborn of Joseph.  
**Verse:** Joshua 17:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Joshua 17:1?**  
**Answer:** to wit, for Machir the firstborn of Manasseh, the father of Gilead; because he was a man of war, therefore he had Gilead and Bashan.  
**Verse:** Joshua 17:1

---

### Joshua 17:2
**Q4. What is stated in the opening of Joshua 17:2?**  
**Answer:** There was also a lot for the rest of the children of Manasseh by their families.  
**Verse:** Joshua 17:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Joshua 17:2?**  
**Answer:** for the children of Abiezer, and for the children of Helek, and for the children of Asriel, and for the children of Shechem, and for the children of Hepher, and for the children of Shemida.  
**Verse:** Joshua 17:2

**Q6. What further truth is recorded in Joshua 17:2?**  
**Answer:** these were the male children of Manasseh the son of Joseph by their families.  
**Verse:** Joshua 17:2

---

### Joshua 17:3
**Q7. What is stated in the first clause of Joshua 17:3?**  
**Answer:** But Zelophehad, the son of Hepher, the son of Gilead, the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh, had no sons, but daughters.  
**Verse:** Joshua 17:3

**Q8. What additional detail is declared in Joshua 17:3?**  
**Answer:** and these are the names of his daughters, Mahlah, and Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.  
**Verse:** Joshua 17:3

**Q9. According to Joshua 17:3, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** But Zelophehad, the son of Hepher, the son of Gilead, the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh, had no sons, but daughters: and these are the names of his daughters, Mahlah, and Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.  
**Verse:** Joshua 17:3
