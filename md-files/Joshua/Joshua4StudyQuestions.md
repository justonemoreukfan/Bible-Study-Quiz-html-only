# Joshua 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Joshua 4:1

**Q1. What happened after all the people were clean passed over Jordan?**  
**Answer:** The LORD spake unto Joshua.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:1

**Q2. Who spoke unto Joshua after all the people were clean passed over Jordan?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:1

**Q3. Which river had all the people passed over when the LORD spake unto Joshua?**  
**Answer:** Jordan.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:1

---

### Joshua 4:2

**Q1. How many men were taken out of the people?**  
**Answer:** Twelve men, out of every tribe a man.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:2

**Q2. From what grouping was a man taken out of for each of the twelve?**  
**Answer:** Out of every tribe a man.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:2

---

### Joshua 4:3

**Q1. What command were the men given concerning where they were to stand?**  
**Answer:** Take you hence out of the midst of Jordan, out of the place where the priests' feet stood firm.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:3

**Q2. How many stones were the men commanded to take up?**  
**Answer:** Twelve stones.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:3

**Q3. Where were the men to carry the stones, and where were they to lodge them?**  
**Answer:** Carry them over with you, and leave them in the lodging place, where ye shall lodge this night.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:3

---

### Joshua 4:4

**Q1. Whom did Joshua call?**  
**Answer:** The twelve men, whom he had prepared of the children of Israel, out of every tribe a man.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:4

**Q2. From whom were the twelve men prepared?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:4

---

### Joshua 4:5

**Q1. Where did Joshua tell the men to pass over unto?**  
**Answer:** Pass over before the ark of the LORD your God into the midst of Jordan.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:5

**Q2. What were the men to take up upon his shoulder?**  
**Answer:** Every man of you take up a stone upon his shoulder.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:5

**Q3. According to the tribes of whom were the stones to be taken up?**  
**Answer:** According unto the number of the tribes of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:5

---

### Joshua 4:6

**Q1. What purpose should the stones serve among the people?**  
**Answer:** That this may be a sign among you.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:6

**Q2. Who would ask their fathers what the stones mean?**  
**Answer:** Your children.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:6

**Q3. When would the children ask about the meaning of the stones?**  
**Answer:** In time to come.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:6

---

### Joshua 4:7

**Q1. What answer should be given to the children concerning the waters of Jordan?**  
**Answer:** That the waters of Jordan were cut off before the ark of the covenant of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:7

**Q2. What happened to the waters of Jordan when it passed over Jordan?**  
**Answer:** The waters of Jordan were cut off.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:7

**Q3. What shall these stones be unto the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** For a memorial for ever.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:7

---

### Joshua 4:8

**Q1. How did the children of Israel do as Joshua commanded?**  
**Answer:** They did so.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:8

**Q2. How many stones did they take up out of the midst of Jordan?**  
**Answer:** Twelve stones.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:8

**Q3. According to what did they take up the stones?**  
**Answer:** According to the number of the tribes of the children of Israel, as the LORD spake unto Joshua.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:8

---

### Joshua 4:9

**Q1. What did Joshua set up in the midst of Jordan?**  
**Answer:** Twelve stones.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:9

**Q2. In what specific spot in the midst of Jordan were the stones set up?**  
**Answer:** In the place where the feet of the priests which bare the ark of the covenant stood.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:9

**Q3. Unto this day, where are the stones that Joshua set up?**  
**Answer:** There unto this day.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:9

---

### Joshua 4:10

**Q1. Where did the priests which bare the ark stand?**  
**Answer:** In the midst of Jordan.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:10

**Q2. Until what point did the priests stand in the midst of Jordan?**  
**Answer:** Until every thing was finished that the LORD commanded Joshua to speak unto the people.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:10

**Q3. According to what did the people haste and pass over?**  
**Answer:** According to all that Moses commanded Joshua.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:10

---

### Joshua 4:11

**Q1. What happened when all the people were clean passed over?**  
**Answer:** That the ark of the LORD passed over, and the priests, in the presence of the people.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:11

**Q2. Who passed over in the presence of the people?**  
**Answer:** The ark of the LORD, and the priests.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:11

---

### Joshua 4:12

**Q1. Who passed over armed before the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The children of Reuben, and the children of Gad, and half the tribe of Manasseh.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:12

**Q2. How did the children of Reuben, Gad, and half the tribe of Manasseh pass over?**  
**Answer:** Armed.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:12

**Q3. Who had spoken thus unto them?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:12

---

### Joshua 4:13

**Q1. About how many men prepared for war passed over before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** About forty thousand.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:13

**Q2. To where did the men prepared for war pass over?**  
**Answer:** Unto battle.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:13

**Q3. Over what plains did they pass?**  
**Answer:** The plains of Jericho.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:13

---

### Joshua 4:14

**Q1. On that day whom did the LORD magnify?**  
**Answer:** Joshua.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:14

**Q2. In the sight of whom did the LORD magnify Joshua?**  
**Answer:** All Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:14

**Q3. How long did the people fear Joshua?**  
**Answer:** All the days of his life.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:14

---

### Joshua 4:15

**Q1. Who spake unto Joshua?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:15

---

### Joshua 4:16

**Q1. What did the LORD command Joshua to command the priests?**  
**Answer:** Command the priests that bear the ark of the testimony, that they come up out of Jordan.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:16

**Q2. What are the priests bearing called in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The ark of the testimony.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:16

---

### Joshua 4:17

**Q1. Therefore what did Joshua command the priests?**  
**Answer:** Saying, Come ye up out of Jordan.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:17

---

### Joshua 4:18

**Q1. What happened when the priests that bare the ark of the covenant of the LORD were come up out of the midst of Jordan?**  
**Answer:** The soles of the priests' feet were plucking up unto the dry land.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:18

**Q2. Where did the waters of Jordan return?**  
**Answer:** Unto their place.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:18

**Q3. How did the waters of Jordan flow after returning to their place?**  
**Answer:** Over all his banks, as they did before.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:18

---

### Joshua 4:19

**Q1. On what day of the month did the people come up out of Jordan?**  
**Answer:** The tenth day of the first month.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:19

**Q2. Where did the people encamp after coming up out of Jordan?**  
**Answer:** In Gilgal.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:19

**Q3. In the border of what place was Gilgal?**  
**Answer:** In the east border of Jericho.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:19

---

### Joshua 4:20

**Q1. Which twelve stones did Joshua pitch in Gilgal?**  
**Answer:** Those twelve stones, which they took out of Jordan.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:20

---

### Joshua 4:21

**Q1. To whom did Joshua speak regarding the stones?**  
**Answer:** Unto the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:21

**Q2. What will the children ask their fathers in time to come?**  
**Answer:** What mean these stones?  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:21

---

### Joshua 4:22

**Q1. What were the children to let know regarding Israel?**  
**Answer:** Israel came over this Jordan on dry land.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:22

**Q2. How did Israel come over Jordan?**  
**Answer:** On dry land.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:22

---

### Joshua 4:23

**Q1. What did the LORD your God do to the waters of Jordan before you?**  
**Answer:** Dried up the waters of Jordan from before you, until ye were passed over.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:23

**Q2. What did the LORD your God do to the Red sea?**  
**Answer:** Dried up the Red sea from before us, until we were gone over.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:23

---

### Joshua 4:24

**Q1. Why did the LORD dry up the waters of Jordan?**  
**Answer:** That all the people of the earth might know the hand of the LORD, that it is mighty.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:24

**Q2. Who should know that the hand of the LORD is mighty?**  
**Answer:** All the people of the earth.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:24

**Q3. Whom should ye fear for ever?**  
**Answer:** The LORD your God.  
**Verse:** Joshua 4:24
