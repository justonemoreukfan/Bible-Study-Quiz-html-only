### Joshua 18:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Joshua 18:1 state?**  
**Answer:** And the whole congregation of the children of Israel assembled together at Shiloh.  
**Verse:** Joshua 18:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Joshua 18:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** and set up the tabernacle of the congregation there. And the land was subdued before them.
