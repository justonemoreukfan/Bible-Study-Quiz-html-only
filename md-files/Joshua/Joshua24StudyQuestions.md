### Joshua 24:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Joshua 24:1?**  
**Answer:** And Joshua gathered all the tribes of Israel to Shechem, and called for the elders of Israel, and for their heads, and for their judges, and for their officers.

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Joshua 24:1?**  
**Answer:** and they presented themselves before God.  
**Verse:** Joshua 24:1
