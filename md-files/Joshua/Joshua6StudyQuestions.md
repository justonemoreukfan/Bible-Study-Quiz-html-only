# Joshua 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Joshua 6:1

**Q1. What was straitly shut up because of the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Jericho.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:1

**Q2. Who went out and who came in?**  
**Answer:** None.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:1

**Q3. How was Jericho described in relation to the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Straitly shut up because of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:1

---

### Joshua 6:2

**Q4. What did the LORD say unto Joshua?**  
**Answer:** See, I have given into thine hand Jericho, and the king thereof, and the mighty men of valour.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:2

**Q5. Who was given into Joshua's hand along with Jericho?**  
**Answer:** The king thereof, and the mighty men of valour.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:2

**Q6. What did the LORD tell Joshua to do regarding Jericho, its king, and its mighty men of valour?**  
**Answer:** See.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:2

---

### Joshua 6:3

**Q7. Who was to compass the city?**  
**Answer:** All ye men of war.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:3

**Q8. How were the men of war to go around the city?**  
**Answer:** Go round about the city once.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:3

**Q9. For how many days were they to do this?**  
**Answer:** Six days.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:3

---

### Joshua 6:4

**Q10. How many priests were to bear the seven trumpets before the ark?**  
**Answer:** Seven priests.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:4

**Q11. What kind of trumpets were the priests to bear?**  
**Answer:** Seven trumpets of rams' horns.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:4

**Q12. On what day were the people to compass the city seven times, and what were the priests to do?**  
**Answer:** On the seventh day, and the priests shall blow with the trumpets.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:4

---

### Joshua 6:5

**Q13. What sound was to cause the wall of the city to fall down flat?**  
**Answer:** When they make a long blast with the ram's horn, and when ye hear the sound of the trumpet.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:5

**Q14. What were all the people to do when they heard the sound of the trumpet?**  
**Answer:** Shout with a great shout.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:5

**Q15. What would happen to the wall of the city after the people shouted?**  
**Answer:** Fall down flat.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:5

---

### Joshua 6:6

**Q16. Who called the priests?**  
**Answer:** Joshua the son of Nun.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:6

**Q17. What did Joshua say unto the priests?**  
**Answer:** Take up the ark of the covenant.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:6

**Q18. How many priests were to take up seven trumpets of rams' horns before the ark of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Seven priests.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:6

---

### Joshua 6:7

**Q19. What did Joshua say to the people?**  
**Answer:** Pass on, and compass the city.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:7

**Q20. Who was to pass on before the ark of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** He that is armed.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:7

**Q21. What were they to do as they passed on?**  
**Answer:** Compass the city.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:7

---

### Joshua 6:8

**Q22. When had Joshua spoken unto the people?**  
**Answer:** When Joshua had spoken unto the people.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:8

**Q23. How many priests bearing the seven trumpets passed on before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The seven priests bearing the seven trumpets of rams' horns.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:8

**Q24. What did the priests do as they passed on before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Blew with the trumpets.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:8

---

### Joshua 6:9

**Q25. Who went before the priests that blew with the trumpets?**  
**Answer:** The armed men.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:9

**Q26. Who came after the ark?**  
**Answer:** The rereward.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:9

**Q27. What did the priests do as they went on?**  
**Answer:** Blew with the trumpets.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:9

---

### Joshua 6:10

**Q28. What command did Joshua give the people?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall not shout, nor make any noise with your voice.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:10

**Q29. What was not to proceed out of their mouth?**  
**Answer:** Any word.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:10

**Q30. Until what day were they commanded not to shout or speak?**  
**Answer:** Until the day I bid you shout.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:10

---

### Joshua 6:11

**Q31. What did the ark of the LORD compass?**  
**Answer:** The city.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:11

**Q32. How many times did the ark compass the city?**  
**Answer:** Going about it once.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:11

**Q33. Where did the people come into to lodge?**  
**Answer:** Into the camp.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:11

---

### Joshua 6:12

**Q34. Who rose early in the morning?**  
**Answer:** Joshua.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:12

**Q35. Who took up the ark of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The priests.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:12

**Q36. What day of the morning events does this verse describe?**  
**Answer:** The second day (referencing rising early).  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:12

---

### Joshua 6:13

**Q37. How many priests bearing seven trumpets of rams' horns went before the ark of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Seven priests.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:13

**Q38. What did the seven priests do continually as they went?**  
**Answer:** Blew with the trumpets.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:13

**Q39. Who went before them and who came after the ark of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The armed men went before them; but the rereward came after the ark of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:13

---

### Joshua 6:14

**Q40. On the second day, how many times did they compass the city?**  
**Answer:** Once.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:14

**Q41. Where did they return after compassing the city on the second day?**  
**Answer:** Into the camp.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:14

**Q42. For how many days did they do this?**  
**Answer:** Six days.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:14

---

### Joshua 6:15

**Q43. On what day did they rise early, about the dawning of the day?**  
**Answer:** The seventh day.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:15

**Q44. How did they compass the city after the same manner?**  
**Answer:** Seven times.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:15

**Q45. What was the only difference on this day regarding compassing the city?**  
**Answer:** They compassed the city after the same manner seven times.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:15

---

### Joshua 6:16

**Q46. At what time did the priests blow with the trumpets the seventh time?**  
**Answer:** At the seventh time.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:16

**Q47. What did Joshua say unto the people?**  
**Answer:** Shout; for the LORD hath given you the city.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:16

**Q48. Who had given the city to the people?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:16

---

### Joshua 6:17

**Q49. What was the city to be?**  
**Answer:** Accursed.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:17

**Q50. Who was to be destroyed along with all that are in it?**  
**Answer:** Even it, and all that are therein, to the LORD.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:17

**Q51. Who alone was to live, and why?**  
**Answer:** Rahab the harlot shall live, she and all that are with her in the house, because she hid the messengers that we sent.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:17

---

### Joshua 6:18

**Q52. What did Joshua warn the people to keep themselves from?**  
**Answer:** The accursed thing.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:18

**Q53. What might happen if they made themselves accursed by taking of the accursed thing?**  
**Answer:** Ye make the camp of Israel a curse, and trouble it.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:18

**Q54. What would happen to the camp of Israel if they took of the accursed thing?**  
**Answer:** Trouble it.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:18

---

### Joshua 6:19

**Q55. What kind of metals were consecrated unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Silver, and gold, and vessels of brass and iron.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:19

**Q56. To whom were the silver, gold, and vessels of brass and iron holy?**  
**Answer:** Unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:19

**Q57. Where were these consecrated treasures to come into?**  
**Answer:** Into the treasury of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:19

---

### Joshua 6:20

**Q58. What did the people do when the priests blew with the trumpets?**  
**Answer:** Shouted with a great shout.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:20

**Q59. What happened when the people shouted with a great shout?**  
**Answer:** The wall fell down flat.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:20

**Q60. How did the people go up into the city, every man straight before him?**  
**Answer:** They went up into the city, every man straight before him, and they took the city.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:20

---

### Joshua 6:21

**Q61. What did they utterly destroy with the edge of the sword?**  
**Answer:** All that was in the city, both man and woman, young and old, and ox, and sheep, and ass.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:21

**Q62. What age groups of people were utterly destroyed?**  
**Answer:** Both man and woman, young and old.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:21

**Q63. Which animals were utterly destroyed?**  
**Answer:** Ox, and sheep, and ass.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:21

---

### Joshua 6:22

**Q63 (Note: adjusted numbering sequence). What did Joshua say to the two men that had spied out the country?**  
**Answer:** Go into the harlot's house, and bring out thence the woman, and all that she hath, as ye sware unto her.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:22

**Q64. Whose house were the two spies told to go into?**  
**Answer:** The harlot's house.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:22

**Q65. Who was to be brought out of the harlot's house?**  
**Answer:** The woman, and all that she hath.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:22

---

### Joshua 6:23

**Q66. Who went in and brought out Rahab, her father, mother, brethren, and all that she had?**  
**Answer:** The young men that were spies.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:23

**Q67. Where did they bring all her kindred?**  
**Answer:** Without the camp of Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:23

**Q68. What did they do with Rahab and her family after bringing them out?**  
**Answer:** Left them without the camp of Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:23

---

### Joshua 6:24

**Q69. What did they burn with fire?**  
**Answer:** The city, and all that was therein.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:24

**Q70. Where was the silver, gold, and vessels of brass and iron put?**  
**Answer:** Into the treasury of the house of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:24

**Q71. What was burned with fire along with the city?**  
**Answer:** All that was therein.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:24

---

### Joshua 6:25

**Q72. Who did Joshua save alive?**  
**Answer:** Rahab the harlot, and her father's household, and all that she had.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:25

**Q73. Where did Rahab dwell from that day to this?**  
**Answer:** In the midst of Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:25

**Q74. Why did Rahab dwell in the midst of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Because she hid the messengers, which Joshua sent to spy out Jericho.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:25

---

### Joshua 6:26

**Q75. What did Joshua adjure the people at that time with?**  
**Answer:** An oath.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:26

**Q76. What was pronounced upon the man that riseth up and buildeth this city Jericho?**  
**Answer:** Cursed be the man before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:26

**Q77. At what cost to his sons would a man lay the foundation and set up the gates of Jericho?**  
**Answer:** He shall lay the foundation thereof in his firstborn, and in his youngest son shall he set up the gates of it.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:26

---

### Joshua 6:27

**Q78. Who was with Joshua?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:27

**Q79. What happened to Joshua's fame throughout all the country?**  
**Answer:** His fame was noised throughout all the country.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:27

**Q80. Whose presence was with Joshua?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Joshua 6:27
