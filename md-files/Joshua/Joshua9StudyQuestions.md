# Joshua 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Joshua 9:1

**Q1. Which kings heard of the events in Joshua 9:1?**  
**Answer:** All the kings which were on this side Jordan, in the hills, and in the valleys, and in all the coasts of the great sea over against Lebanon.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:1

**Q2. Who specifically is mentioned among the groups of kings in Joshua 9:1?**  
**Answer:** The Hittite, and the Amorite, the Canaanite, the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:1

**Q3. Where were the coasts located in relation to Lebanon in Joshua 9:1?**  
**Answer:** Over against Lebanon.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:1

---

### Joshua 9:2

**Q4. What did the kings gather themselves together to do in Joshua 9:2?**  
**Answer:** To fight with Joshua and with Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:2

**Q5. How did the kings gather themselves together in Joshua 9:2?**  
**Answer:** With one accord.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:2

**Q6. Who were the kings gathering to fight against according to Joshua 9:2?**  
**Answer:** Joshua and with Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:2

---

### Joshua 9:3

**Q7. Who heard what Joshua had done unto Jericho and to Ai in Joshua 9:3?**  
**Answer:** The inhabitants of Gibeon.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:3

**Q8. What actions of Joshua did the inhabitants of Gibeon hear about in Joshua 9:3?**  
**Answer:** What Joshua had done unto Jericho and to Ai.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:3

---

### Joshua 9:4

**Q9. What did the inhabitants of Gibeon do to carry out their work in Joshua 9:4?**  
**Answer:** They did work wilily.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:4

**Q10. What did they take and make as if they had been ambassadors in Joshua 9:4?**  
**Answer:** Old sacks upon their asses, and wine bottles, old, and rent, and bound up.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:4

**Q11. What kind of bottles did they take in Joshua 9:4?**  
**Answer:** Wine bottles, old, and rent, and bound up.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:4

### Joshua 9:5

**Q12. What kind of shoes did they have on their feet in Joshua 9:5?**  
**Answer:** Old and clouted upon their feet.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:5

**Q13. What kind of garments were upon them in Joshua 9:5?**  
**Answer:** Old garments.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:5

**Q14. What was the condition of all the bread of their provision in Joshua 9:5?**

**Answer:** Unto the camp at Gilgal.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:6

**Answer:** We be come from a far country: now therefore make ye a league with us.

**Answer:** The men of Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:7

**Q19. What did they say unto Joshua in Joshua 9:8?**  
**Answer:** We are thy servants.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:8

**Q20. What question did Joshua ask them in Joshua 9:8?**  
**Answer:** Who are ye? and from whence come ye?  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:8

---

### Joshua 9:9

**Q21. From where did they say they came in Joshua 9:9?**  
**Answer:** From a very far country.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:9

**Q22. Because of what did they come from a very far country in Joshua 9:9?**  
**Answer:** Because of the name of the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:9

**Q23. What had they heard about the LORD in Joshua 9:9?**  
**Answer:** His fame, and all that he did in Egypt.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:9

### Joshua 9:10

**Q24. What did they say the LORD did to the two kings of the Amorites in Joshua 9:10?**  
**Answer:** All that he did to the two kings of the Amorites, that were beyond Jordan.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:10

**Q25. Who were the two specific kings of the Amorites mentioned in Joshua 9:10?**  
**Answer:** Sihon king of Heshbon, and Og king of Bashan, which was at Ashtaroth.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:10

**Q26. Where was Og king of Bashan located in Joshua 9:10?**  
**Answer:** At Ashtaroth.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:10

---

### Joshua 9:11

**Answer:** Our elders and all the inhabitants of our country.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:11

**Verse:** Joshua 9:11

**Q36. What did Joshua make with them in Joshua 9:15?**

**Verse:** Joshua 9:15

**Answer:** A league with them, to let them live.

### Joshua 9:16

**Q39. How long after making a league with them did Israel hear that they were neighbors in Joshua 9:16?**  
**Answer:** At the end of three days after they had made a league with them.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:16

**Q40. What did Israel hear about these people at the end of three days in Joshua 9:16?**  
**Answer:** That they were their neighbours, and that they dwelt among them.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:16

**Verse:** Joshua 9:23

**Q55. What did the Gibeonites answer Joshua concerning why they did this in Joshua 9:24?**

**Verse:** Joshua 9:24

**Q56. How did the Gibeonites feel regarding their lives because of the Israelites in Joshua 9:24?**  
**Answer:** Therefore we were sore afraid of our lives because of you, and have done this thing.  
**Verse:** Joshua 9:24

**Verse:** Joshua 9:26

**Verse:** Joshua 9:26

**Q61. What did Joshua make the Gibeonites that day in Joshua 9:27?**

**Verse:** Joshua 9:27
