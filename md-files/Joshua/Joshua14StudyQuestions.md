# Joshua 14 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Joshua 14:1
**Q1. According to Joshua 14:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And these are the countries which the children of Israel inherited in the land of Canaan.  
**Verse:** Joshua 14:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Joshua 14:1?**  
**Answer:** which Eleazar the priest.  
**Verse:** Joshua 14:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Joshua 14:1?**  
**Answer:** and Joshua the son of Nun, and the heads of the fathers of the tribes of the children of Israel, distributed for inheritance to them.  
**Verse:** Joshua 14:1

---

### Joshua 14:2
**Q4. According to Joshua 14:2, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** By lot was their inheritance.  
**Verse:** Joshua 14:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Joshua 14:2?**

**Verse:** Joshua 14:2

**Verse:** Joshua 14:2

### Joshua 14:3
**Q7. What is stated in the first clause of Joshua 14:3?**  
**Answer:** For Moses had given the inheritance of two tribes and an half tribe on the other side Jordan.  
**Verse:** Joshua 14:3

**Q8. What additional detail is declared in Joshua 14:3?**  
**Answer:** but unto the Levites he gave none inheritance among them.  
**Verse:** Joshua 14:3

**Q9. According to Joshua 14:3, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** For Moses had given the inheritance of two tribes and an half tribe on the other side Jordan: but unto the Levites he gave none inheritance among them.  
**Verse:** Joshua 14:3
