# Joshua 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Joshua 3:1

**Q1. Who rose early in the morning?**  
**Answer:** Joshua.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:1

**Q2. From where did Joshua and all the children of Israel remove, and to where did they come?**  
**Answer:** They removed from Shittim, and came to Jordan.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:1

**Q3. Who came to Jordan and lodged there before they passed over?**  
**Answer:** Joshua and all the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:1

---

### Joshua 3:2

**Q1. After how many days did the officers go through the host?**  
**Answer:** Three days.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:2

---

### Joshua 3:3

**Q1. What did the officers command the people, telling them to remove from where they were and go after it?**  
**Answer:** The ark of the covenant of the LORD your God, and the priests the Levites bearing it.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:3

**Q2. Who was to bear the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God?**  
**Answer:** The priests the Levites.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:3

**Q3. When the people saw the ark moving, what were they commanded to do?**  
**Answer:** Remove from your place, and go after it.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:3

---

### Joshua 3:4

**Q1. What was to be the distance between you and the ark?**  
**Answer:** A space of about two thousand cubits by measure.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:4

**Q2. Why were the people not to come near unto the ark?**  
**Answer:** That ye may know the way by which ye must go.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:4

**Q3. What reason is given regarding why they had not passed this way heretofore?**  
**Answer:** For ye have not passed this way heretofore.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:4

---

### Joshua 3:5

**Q1. What did Joshua say unto the people in preparation for the next day?**  
**Answer:** Sanctify yourselves.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:5

**Q2. What event did Joshua say the LORD would do among the people tomorrow?**  
**Answer:** Wonders.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:5

**Q3. Who would do wonders among the people on the morrow?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:5

---

### Joshua 3:6

**Q1. To whom did Joshua speak concerning taking up the ark?**  
**Answer:** The priests.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:6

**Q2. What did Joshua command the priests to take up?**  
**Answer:** The ark of the covenant.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:6

**Q3. In relation to the people, where were the priests to pass over?**  
**Answer:** Before the people.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:6

---

### Joshua 3:7

**Q1. What did the LORD say unto Joshua?**  
**Answer:** This day will I begin to magnify thee in the sight of all Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:7

**Q2. In whose sight would the LORD begin to magnify Joshua?**  
**Answer:** All Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:7

**Q3. What would the people know through God magnifying Joshua?**  
**Answer:** That as I was with Moses, so I will be with thee.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:7

---

### Joshua 3:8

**Q1. To whom was Joshua to command the things regarding the brink of the water?**  
**Answer:** The priests that bear the ark of the covenant.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:8

**Q2. Where were the priests commanded to stand when they came to the brink of the water?**  
**Answer:** In Jordan.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:8

**Q3. Who was addressed in the command to stand in Jordan?**  
**Answer:** The priests that bear the ark of the covenant.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:8

---

### Joshua 3:9

**Q1. Whom did Joshua tell to come hither?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:9

**Q2. Whose words were the children of Israel to hear?**  
**Answer:** The words of the LORD your God.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:9

**Q3. Who spoke the words instructing the children of Israel to come hither and hear?**  
**Answer:** Joshua.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:9

---

### Joshua 3:10

**Q1. By what sign did Joshua say they would know that the living God is among them?**  
**Answer:** Hereby ye shall know that the living God is among you.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:10

**Q2. What group of people would the living God without fail drive out from before them?**  
**Answer:** The Canaanites, and the Hittites, and the Hivites, and the Perizzites, and the Girgashites, and the Amorites, and the Jebusites.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:10

**Q3. What description is given of God in relation to His presence among them?**  
**Answer:** The living God.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:10

---

### Joshua 3:11

**Q1. What passes over before you into Jordan?**  
**Answer:** The ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:11

**Q2. Whose covenant ark passes over before them?**  
**Answer:** The Lord of all the earth.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:11

**Q3. Where does the ark pass over into?**  
**Answer:** Jordan.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:11

---

### Joshua 3:12

**Q1. How many men were to be taken out of the tribes of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Twelve men.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:12

**Q2. From what group were the twelve men to be taken?**  
**Answer:** Out of the tribes of Israel, out of every tribe a man.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:12

**Q3. How many men were chosen from every tribe?**  
**Answer:** A man.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:12

---

### Joshua 3:13

**Q1. Whose soles of the feet shall rest in the waters of Jordan?**  
**Answer:** The priests that bear the ark of the LORD, the Lord of all the earth.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:13

**Q2. What will happen to the waters of Jordan when the soles of the priests' feet rest in them?**  
**Answer:** The waters of Jordan shall be cut off.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:13

**Q3. How will the waters coming down from above behave when cut off?**  
**Answer:** They shall stand upon an heap.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:13

---

### Joshua 3:14

**Q1. When did the people remove from their tents to pass over Jordan?**  
**Answer:** When the people removed from their tents to pass over Jordan.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:14

**Q2. Who bore the ark of the covenant before the people?**  
**Answer:** The priests bearing the ark of the covenant.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:14

**Q3. What were the people doing as they moved to pass over Jordan?**  
**Answer:** Removing from their tents.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:14

---

### Joshua 3:15

**Q1. Who bear the ark came unto Jordan?**  
**Answer:** They that bare the ark.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:15

**Q2. What condition were the feet of the priests that bare the ark in regarding the water?**  
**Answer:** Were dipped in the brim of the water.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:15

**Q3. During what time period does Jordan overflow all his banks?**  
**Answer:** All the time of harvest.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:15

---

### Joshua 3:16

**Q1. Where did the waters which came down from above stand and rise up?**  
**Answer:** Upon an heap very far from the city Adam.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:16

**Q2. Near what city was the city Adam located?**  
**Answer:** Zaretan.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:16

**Q3. What sea was completely failed and cut off by the waters coming down toward the sea of the plain?**  
**Answer:** The salt sea.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:16

---

### Joshua 3:17

**Q1. Where did the priests that bare the ark of the covenant of the LORD stand firm?**  
**Answer:** On dry ground in the midst of Jordan.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:17

**Q2. Who passed over on dry ground until all the people were passed clean over Jordan?**  
**Answer:** All the Israelites.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:17

**Q3. How did all the people pass over Jordan?**  
**Answer:** On dry ground.  
**Verse:** Joshua 3:17
