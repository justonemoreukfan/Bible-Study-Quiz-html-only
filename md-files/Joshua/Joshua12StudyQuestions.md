# Joshua 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Joshua 12:1
**Q1. According to Joshua 12:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Now these are the kings of the land.  
**Verse:** Joshua 12:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Joshua 12:1?**  
**Answer:** which the children of Israel smote.  
**Verse:** Joshua 12:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Joshua 12:1?**  
**Answer:** and possessed their land on the other side Jordan toward the rising of the sun, from the river Arnon unto mount Hermon, and all the plain on the east.  
**Verse:** Joshua 12:1

**Q4. According to Joshua 12:2, what is stated first?**

**Q5. What is declared next in Joshua 12:2?**

**Verse:** Joshua 12:2
