# Joshua 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Joshua 8:1

**Q1. Who spoke unto Joshua according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:1

**Q2. What did the Lord tell Joshua not to do?**  
**Answer:** Fear not, neither be thou dismayed.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:1

**Q3. Who was delivered into Joshua's hand along with the king of Ai?**  
**Answer:** All the people of war with thee, and his city, and his land.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:1

---

### Joshua 8:2

**Q1. What was Joshua told to do to Ai and her king?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt do to Ai and her king as thou didst unto Jericho and her king.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:2

**Q2. What were the Israelites allowed to take for themselves out of Ai?**  
**Answer:** The spoil thereof, and the cattle thereof.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:2

**Q3. Where did the Lord command Joshua to set an ambush for the city?**  
**Answer:** Behind the city.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:2

---

### Joshua 8:3

**Q1. Who arose to go up against Ai?**  
**Answer:** Joshua, and all the people of war.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:3

**Q2. How many mighty men of valour did Joshua choose?**  
**Answer:** Thirty thousand.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:3

**Q3. Where did Joshua send these chosen mighty men?**  
**Answer:** Away by night.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:3

---

### Joshua 8:4

**Q1. What did Joshua command the men, telling them to lie in wait against?**  
**Answer:** The city.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:4

**Q2. How far behind the city were the men told to go?**  
**Answer:** Not very far off.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:4

**Q3. What must all the men be ready to do?**  
**Answer:** Be ye all ready.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:4

---

### Joshua 8:5

**Q1. Who did Joshua and all the people with him approach?**  
**Answer:** The city.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:5

**Q2. What would happen when the men of Ai came out against them?**  
**Answer:** We will flee before them.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:5

---

### Joshua 8:6

**Q1. Why would the men of Ai come out after the Israelites?**  
**Answer:** For they will come out after us till we have drawn them from the city.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:6

**Q2. What reason would the men of Ai give for the Israelites fleeing?**  
**Answer:** They flee before us as at the first.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:6

**Q3. What would the Israelites do while the men of Ai pursued them?**  
**Answer:** Therefore we will flee before them.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:6

---

### Joshua 8:7

**Q1. When were the men in ambush commanded to rise up?**  
**Answer:** Then ye shall rise up from the ambush, and seize the city.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:7

**Q2. Who would deliver the city into their hand?**  
**Answer:** For the LORD your God will deliver it into your hand.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:7

---

### Joshua 8:8

**Q1. What were the people commanded to do after taking the city?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall set the city on fire.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:8

**Q2. According to whose commandment shall they do this thing?**  
**Answer:** According to the commandment of the LORD shall ye do.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:8

**Q3. What did Joshua tell them to see?**  
**Answer:** See, I have commanded you.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:8

---

### Joshua 8:9

**Q1. Where did Joshua send the people forth?**  
**Answer:** To lie in ambush.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:9

**Q2. Where did the ambush take place regarding Bethel and Ai?**  
**Answer:** Between Bethel and Ai, on the west side of Ai.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:9

**Q3. Where did Joshua lodge that night?**  
**Answer:** Joshua lodged that night among the people.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:9

---

### Joshua 8:10

**Q1. What did Joshua do early in the morning?**  
**Answer:** Rose up early in the morning.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:10

**Q2. Who did Joshua number before going up to Ai?**  
**Answer:** Numbered the people.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:10

**Q3. Who went up before the people to Ai?**  
**Answer:** Joshua and the elders of Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:10

---

### Joshua 8:11

**Q1. Who went up with Joshua and came before the city?**  
**Answer:** All the people, even the people of war that were with him.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:11

**Q2. Where did they pitch?**  
**Answer:** On the north side of Ai.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:11

**Q3. What was between them and Ai?**  
**Answer:** A valley.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:11

---

### Joshua 8:12

**Q1. How many men did Joshua take and set in ambush?**  
**Answer:** About five thousand men.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:12

**Q2. Where did he set them?**  
**Answer:** Between Bethel and Ai, on the west side of the city.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:12

---

### Joshua 8:13

**Q1. How did they set the people, even all the host that was on the north of the city?**  
**Answer:** Their liars in wait on the west of the city.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:13

**Q2. Where did Joshua go that night?**  
**Answer:** Joshua went that night into the midst of the valley.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:13

---

### Joshua 8:14

**Q1. Who saw the arrangement when the king of Ai and his men rose up early?**  
**Answer:** The king of Ai, he and all his people.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:14

**Q2. To what place did the men of the city go out to battle against Israel?**  
**Answer:** Toward the time appointed, before the plain.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:14

**Q3. Who was ignorant that there was an ambush against him behind the city?**  
**Answer:** He wist not that there were liars in ambush against him behind the city.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:14

---

### Joshua 8:15

**Q1. How did Joshua and all Israel act before the men of Ai?**  
**Answer:** Made themselves beaten before them.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:15

**Q2. In what direction did they flee?**  
**Answer:** Fled by the way of the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:15

---

### Joshua 8:16

**Q1. Who were called together to pursue after Joshua?**  
**Answer:** All the people that were in Ai.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:16

**Q2. What did they do as they pursued Joshua?**  
**Answer:** They pursued after Joshua, and were drawn away from the city.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:16

---

### Joshua 8:17

**Q1. Who was left in Ai or Bethel that did not go out after Israel?**  
**Answer:** There was not a man left in Ai or Bethel, that went not out after Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:17

**Q2. What did they leave the city open to do?**  
**Answer:** Left the city open, and pursued after Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:17

---

### Joshua 8:18

**Q1. What did the LORD say unto Joshua regarding the spear?**  
**Answer:** Stretch out the spear that is in thy hand toward Ai.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:18

**Q2. Into whose hand would Joshua give the city?**  
**Answer:** For I will give it into thine hand.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:18

**Q3. What did Joshua stretch out toward the city?**  
**Answer:** Joshua stretched out the spear that he had in his hand toward the city.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:18

---

### Joshua 8:19

**Q1. Who arose quickly out of their place when Joshua stretched out his hand?**  
**Answer:** The ambush.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:19

**Q2. What did the ambush do as soon as he stretched out his hand?**  
**Answer:** Ran as soon as he had stretched out his hand.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:19

**Q3. What did they enter, take, and immediately do to the city?**  
**Answer:** Entered into the city, and took it, and hasted and set the city on fire.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:19

---

### Joshua 8:20

**Q1. When the men of Ai looked behind them, what did they see?**  
**Answer:** Behold, the smoke of the city ascended up to heaven.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:20

**Q2. What power did the men of Ai have to flee this way or that way?**  
**Answer:** They had no power to flee this way or that way.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:20

**Q3. Where did the people that fled to the wilderness turn back to?**  
**Answer:** Turned back upon the pursuers.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:20

---

### Joshua 8:21

**Q1. When Joshua and all Israel saw that the ambush had taken the city, what did they do?**  
**Answer:** Turned again, and slew the men of Ai.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:21

**Q2. Who had taken the city?**  
**Answer:** The ambush.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:21

---

### Joshua 8:22

**Q1. Who issued out of the city against the men of Ai?**  
**Answer:** The other issued out of the city against them.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:22

**Q2. Where were the men of Ai caught in the middle of Israel?**  
**Answer:** So they were in the midst of Israel, some on this side, and some on that side.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:22

**Q3. How many survivors or escapees were left?**  
**Answer:** They smote them, so that they let none of them remain or escape.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:22

---

### Joshua 8:23

**Q1. Who was taken alive and brought to Joshua?**  
**Answer:** The king of Ai.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:23

---

### Joshua 8:24

**Q1. Where did Israel slay all the inhabitants of Ai?**  
**Answer:** In the field and in the wilderness wherein they chased them.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:24

**Q2. How did every single one of them fall?**  
**Answer:** Fell on the edge of the sword, until they were consumed.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:24

**Q3. Who returned to Ai and smote it with the edge of the sword?**  
**Answer:** All the Israelites returned unto Ai, and smote it with the edge of the sword.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:24

---

### Joshua 8:25

**Q1. How many fell that day, both of men and women?**  
**Answer:** All that fell that day, both of men and women, were twelve thousand, even all the men of Ai.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:25

---

### Joshua 8:26

**Q1. What did Joshua not draw back with which he stretched out the spear?**  
**Answer:** His hand wherewith he stretched out the spear.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:26

---

### Joshua 8:27

**Q1. What did Israel take for themselves out of the spoil of the city?**  
**Answer:** The cattle and the spoil of that city.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:27

**Q2. According to what did Israel take these things?**  
**Answer:** According unto the word of the LORD which he commanded Joshua.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:27

---

### Joshua 8:28

**Q1. What did Joshua burn?**  
**Answer:** Ai.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:28

**Q2. What did Joshua make Ai into?**  
**Answer:** An heap for ever.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:28

**Q3. What is Ai described as unto this day?**  
**Answer:** A desolation unto this day.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:28

---

### Joshua 8:29

**Q1. Where was the king of Ai hanged?**  
**Answer:** On a tree.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:29

**Q2. Until what time was the king of Ai hanged on the tree?**  
**Answer:** Until eventide.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:29

**Q3. What did Joshua command be done to the king's carcass at the going down of the sun?**  
**Answer:** Cast his carcase down at the entrance of the gate of the city, and raise thereon a great heap of stones.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:29

---

### Joshua 8:30

**Q1. Who did Joshua build an altar unto?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:30

**Q2. Where did Joshua build this altar?**  
**Answer:** In mount Ebal.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:30

---

### Joshua 8:31

**Q1. How was the altar built according to the book of the law of Moses?**  
**Answer:** As Moses the servant of the LORD commanded the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:31

**Q2. What kind of stones were used to build the altar?**  
**Answer:** An altar of whole stones, over which no man hath lift up any iron.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:31

**Q3. What offerings were offered upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** Offered burnt offerings thereon unto the LORD, and sacrificed peace offerings.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:31

---

### Joshua 8:32

**Q1. What did Joshua write upon the stones?**  
**Answer:** A copy of the law of Moses.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:32

**Q2. Before whose eyes did Joshua write this law?**  
**Answer:** In the presence of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:32

---

### Joshua 8:33

**Q1. Who stood on this side and on that side of the ark before the priests the Levites?**  
**Answer:** All Israel, and their elders, and officers, and their judges.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:33

**Q2. Who did the ark of the covenant of the LORD bear?**  
**Answer:** The priests the Levites.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:33

**Q3. Who was included among the people blessing the people of Israel as Moses had commanded?**  
**Answer:** As well the stranger, as he that was born among them; half of them over against mount Gerizim, and half of them over against mount Ebal.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:33

---

### Joshua 8:34

**Q1. What did Joshua read afterward?**  
**Answer:** All the words of the law.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:34

**Q2. What two things were included in the reading?**  
**Answer:** The blessing and cursing.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:34

**Q3. According to what were all these things written?**  
**Answer:** According to all that is written in the book of the law.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:34

---

### Joshua 8:35

**Q1. Was there any word of all that Moses commanded that Joshua read not?**  
**Answer:** There was not a word of all that Moses commanded, which Joshua read not.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:35

**Q2. Before whom did Joshua read all the words?**  
**Answer:** Before all the congregation of Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:35

**Q3. Who else was present with the women and little ones?**  
**Answer:** With the women, and the little ones, and the strangers that were conversant among them.  
**Verse:** Joshua 8:35
