# Joshua 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Joshua 2:1

**Q1. Who sent out of Shittim two men to spy secretly?**  
**Answer:** Joshua the son of Nun.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:1

**Q2. Where did the two spies go to view the land, even Jericho?**  
**Answer:** Into Jericho.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:1

**Q3. Whose house did they enter, whose name was Rahab?**  
**Answer:** An harlot's house.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:1

---

### Joshua 2:2

**Q4. Who was told that men came in thither that night of the children of Israel to search out the country?**  
**Answer:** The king of Jericho.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:2

---

### Joshua 2:3

**Q5. Who sent to Rahab, saying, Bring forth the men that are come to thee?**  
**Answer:** The king of Jericho.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:3

**Q6. Why had the men come to Rahab's house according to the king's message?**  
**Answer:** To search out all the country.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:3

---

### Joshua 2:4

**Q7. Who took the two men, and hid them?**  
**Answer:** The woman (Rahab).  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:4

**Q8. What did Rahab say concerning the men coming unto her?**  
**Answer:** There came men unto me, but I wist not whence they were.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:4

---

### Joshua 2:5

**Q9. When did the men go out about the time of shutting of the gate?**  
**Answer:** About the time of shutting of the gate, when it was dark.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:5

**Q10. What did Rahab tell the messengers to do in order to pursue the men quickly?**  
**Answer:** Pursue them quickly; for ye shall overtake them.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:5

---

### Joshua 2:6

**Q11. Where had she brought them up?**  
**Answer:** To the roof of the house.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:6

**Q12. With what did she hide them on the roof?**  
**Answer:** With the stalks of flax.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:6

**Q13. How were the stalks of flax laid out?**  
**Answer:** Laid in order upon the roof.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:6

---

### Joshua 2:7

**Q14. Who pursued after them the way to Jordan?**  
**Answer:** The men.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:7

**Q15. To what places did they pursue the spies?**  
**Answer:** Unto the fords.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:7

**Q16. What was shut as soon as they which pursued after them were gone out?**  
**Answer:** The gate.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:7

---

### Joshua 2:8

**Q17. Before the men were laid down, who came up unto them upon the roof?**  
**Answer:** She (Rahab).  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:8

---

### Joshua 2:9

**Q18. What did she say unto the men regarding the LORD?**  
**Answer:** I know that the LORD hath given you the land.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:9

**Q19. What had fallen upon them because of the Israelites?**  
**Answer:** Your terror.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:9

**Q20. What had happened to all the inhabitants of the land because of them?**  
**Answer:** All the inhabitants of the land faint because of you.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:9

---

### Joshua 2:10

**Q21. What body of water did the LORD dry up for them out of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** The water of the Red sea.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:10

**Q22. When did the LORD dry up the water of the Red sea?**  
**Answer:** When ye came out of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:10

**Q23. Whose two kings on the other side of Jordan were utterly destroyed?**  
**Answer:** Sihon and Og.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:10

---

### Joshua 2:11

**Q24. What happened to the hearts of the people when they heard these things?**  
**Answer:** Our hearts did melt.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:11

**Q25. What remained in any man because of you?**  
**Answer:** Neither did there remain any more courage in any man.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:11

**Q26. Where is the LORD your God stated to be God?**  
**Answer:** In heaven above, and in earth beneath.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:11

---

### Joshua 2:12

**Q27. By what did Rahab ask the men to swear unto her?**  
**Answer:** By the LORD.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:12

**Q28. What kindness did Rahab show to the men?**  
**Answer:** She shewed you kindness.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:12

**Q29. What did she ask the men to show unto her father's house?**  
**Answer:** A true token.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:12

---

### Joshua 2:13

**Q30. Who did Rahab ask to be saved alive?**  
**Answer:** My father, and my mother, and my brethren, and my sisters, and all that they have.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:13

**Q31. From what did she want her family to be delivered?**  
**Answer:** Deliver our lives from death.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:13

---

### Joshua 2:14

**Q32. What did the men answer her?**  
**Answer:** Our life for yours, if ye utter not this our business.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:14

**Q33. What would the LORD do when he gave them the land?**  
**Answer:** Deal kindly and truly with thee.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:14

---

### Joshua 2:15

**Q34. How did she let them down?**  
**Answer:** By a cord through the window.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:15

**Q35. Where was her house situated?**  
**Answer:** Upon the town wall.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:15

**Q36. Where did she dwell in relation to the wall?**  
**Answer:** She dwelt upon the wall.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:15

---

### Joshua 2:16

**Q37. Where did she tell them to get to avoid the pursuers?**  
**Answer:** To the mountain.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:16

**Q38. How long should the pursuers meet them there?**  
**Answer:** Three days.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:16

**Q39. What should they do afterward?**  
**Answer:** Go your way.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:16

---

### Joshua 2:17

**Q40. What did the men say unto her regarding their oath?**  
**Answer:** We will be blameless of this thine oath which thou hast made us swear.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:17

---

### Joshua 2:18

**Q41. What sign did they require when they came into the land?**  
**Answer:** This line of scarlet thread in the window which thou didst let us down by.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:18

**Q42. Who did she need to bring home unto her?**  
**Answer:** Thy father, and thy mother, and thy brethren, and all thy father's household.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:18

---

### Joshua 2:19

**Q43. Whose blood shall be upon his own head if he goes out of the doors of the house?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever shall go out of the doors of thy house into the street.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:19

**Q44. Who shall be with Rahab in the house if any hand be upon them?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever shall be with thee in the house.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:19

**Q45. Whose blood shall be on their head if any hand is upon them inside the house?**  
**Answer:** His blood shall be on our head.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:19

---

### Joshua 2:20

**Q46. What would happen if she uttered their business?**  
**Answer:** We will be unheld of thine oath which thou hast made us to swear.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:20

---

### Joshua 2:21

**Q47. What did Rahab say in response to their words?**  
**Answer:** According unto your words, so be it.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:21

**Q48. What did she send them away to do?**  
**Answer:** And they departed.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:21

**Q49. What did she bind in the window?**  
**Answer:** The scarlet line.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:21

---

### Joshua 2:22

**Q50. Where did the men go and abide?**  
**Answer:** Unto the mountain.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:22

**Q51. How long did the pursuers seek them throughout all the way?**  
**Answer:** Three days.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:22

**Q52. What was the outcome of the pursuers seeking them?**  
**Answer:** They found them not.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:22

---

### Joshua 2:23

**Q53. Who returned and descended from the mountain?**  
**Answer:** The two men.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:23

**Q54. Whose son did they pass over to?**  
**Answer:** Joshua the son of Nun.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:23

**Q55. What did they tell him regarding all things that befell them?**  
**Answer:** Told him all things that befell them.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:23

---

### Joshua 2:24

**Q56. What did they say unto Joshua concerning the LORD delivering the country?**  
**Answer:** Truly the LORD hath delivered into our hands all the land.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:24

**Q57. What did all the inhabitants of the country do because of them?**  
**Answer:** Do faint because of us.  
**Verse:** Joshua 2:24
