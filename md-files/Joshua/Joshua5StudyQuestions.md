# Joshua 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Joshua 5:1

**Q1. Who heard that the Lord had dried up the waters of Jordan from before the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** All the kings of the Amorites, which were on the side of Jordan westward, and all the kings of the Canaanites, which were by the sea.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:1

**Q2. What happened to the heart of the kings mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Their heart melted.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:1

**Q3. Because of the children of Israel, what did the kings no longer have?**  
**Answer:** Neither was there spirit in them any more.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:1

---

### Joshua 5:2

**Q1. What did the Lord say unto Joshua at that time?**  
**Answer:** Make thee sharp knives, and circumcise again the children of Israel the second time.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:2

**Q2. What kind of knives did the Lord tell Joshua to make?**  
**Answer:** Sharp knives.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:2

**Q3. Whom did the Lord tell Joshua to circumcise again?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel the second time.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:2

---

### Joshua 5:3

**Q1. What did Joshua make for himself?**  
**Answer:** Sharp knives.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:3

**Q2. Where did Joshua circumcise the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The hill of the foreskins.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:3

---

### Joshua 5:4

**Q1. Who was all the people that came out of Egypt that were males?**  
**Answer:** All the men of war.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:4

**Q2. When did all the men of war that were males die?**  
**Answer:** By the way in the wilderness, after they came out of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:4

**Q3. Where did they die?**  
**Answer:** In the wilderness by the way, after they came out of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:4

---

### Joshua 5:5

**Q1. What was the state of all the people that came out?**  
**Answer:** They were circumcised.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:5

**Q2. What about all the people born in the wilderness by the way as they came forth out of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** They had not been circumcised.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:5

**Q3. Where were the people born who had not been circumcised?**  
**Answer:** In the wilderness by the way as they came forth out of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:5

---

### Joshua 5:6

**Q1. How long did the children of Israel walk in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** Forty years.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:6

**Q2. What happened to all the people that were men of war, which came out of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** They were consumed.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:6

**Q3. Why were the men of war consumed?**  
**Answer:** Because they obeyed not the voice of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:6

---

### Joshua 5:7

**Q1. Whom did the Lord raise up in their stead?**  
**Answer:** Their children.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:7

**Q2. What did Joshua circumcise the children with?**  
**Answer:** They were uncircumcised, because they had not been circumcised by the way.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:7

**Q3. What did Joshua do to them?**  
**Answer:** He circumcised them.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:7

---

### Joshua 5:8

**Q1. When they had done circumcising all the people, what did they do?**  
**Answer:** They abode in their places in the camp, till they were whole.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:8

**Q2. Where did the people abide until they were whole?**  
**Answer:** In their places in the camp.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:8

**Q3. How long did they abide in their places?**  
**Answer:** Till they were whole.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:8

---

### Joshua 5:9

**Q1. What did the Lord say unto Joshua?**  
**Answer:** This day have I rolled away the reproach of Egypt from off you.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:9

**Q2. What was rolled away from off them?**  
**Answer:** The reproach of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:9

**Q3. What is the name of the place where this happened unto this day?**  
**Answer:** Gilgal.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:9

---

### Joshua 5:10

**Q1. Where did the children of Israel encamp?**  
**Answer:** In Gilgal.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:10

**Q2. What did the children of Israel keep in Gilgal?**  
**Answer:** The passover.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:10

**Q3. On what day of the month did they keep the passover?**  
**Answer:** On the fourteenth day of the month at even.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:10

---

### Joshua 5:11

**Q1. What did they eat on the morrow after the passover?**  
**Answer:** Of the old corn of the land.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:11

**Q2. What else did they eat on the selfsame day?**  
**Answer:** Unleavened cakes, and parched corn.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:11

---

### Joshua 5:12

**Q1. When did the manna cease?**  
**Answer:** On the morrow after they had eaten of the old corn of the land.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:12

**Q2. What did the children of Israel have no more after the manna ceased?**  
**Answer:** Manna.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:12

**Q3. What did they eat that year?**  
**Answer:** Of the fruit of the land of Canaan.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:12

---

### Joshua 5:13

**Q1. When Joshua was by Jericho, what did he do when he lifted up his eyes and looked?**  
**Answer:** Behold, there stood a man over against him with his sword drawn in his hand.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:13

**Q2. What was in the man's hand?**  
**Answer:** His sword drawn.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:13

**Q3. What did Joshua ask the man?**  
**Answer:** Art thou for us, or for our adversaries?  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:13

---

### Joshua 5:14

**Q1. How did the man answer Joshua?**  
**Answer:** Nay; but as captain of the host of the Lord am I now come.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:14

**Q2. What did Joshua do upon the earth when he heard this?**  
**Answer:** Fell on his face to the earth, and did worship.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:14

**Q3. What did Joshua ask his lord?**  
**Answer:** What saith my lord unto his servant?  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:14

---

### Joshua 5:15

**Q1. What did the captain of the Lord's host say unto Joshua?**  
**Answer:** Loose thy shoe from off thy foot.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:15

**Q2. Why did the captain tell Joshua to loose his shoe from off his foot?**  
**Answer:** For the place whereon thou standest is holy.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:15

**Q3. What did Joshua do?**  
**Answer:** And Joshua did so.  
**Verse:** Joshua 5:15
