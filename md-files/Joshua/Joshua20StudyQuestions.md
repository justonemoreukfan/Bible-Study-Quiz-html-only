### Joshua 20:1

**Q2. According to Joshua 20:1, what truth is stated?**  
**Answer:** The LORD also spake unto Joshua, saying,.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Joshua 20:1?**  
**Answer:** The LORD also spake unto Joshua, saying,.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:1

### Joshua 20:2
**Q4. According to Joshua 20:2, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Speak to the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Joshua 20:2?**  
**Answer:** Appoint out for you cities of refuge.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:2

**Q6. What further truth is given in Joshua 20:2?**  
**Answer:** whereof I spake unto you by the hand of Moses.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:2

---

### Joshua 20:3
**Q7. What is stated in the first clause of Joshua 20:3?**  
**Answer:** That the slayer that killeth any person unawares and unwittingly may flee thither.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:3

**Q8. What additional detail is declared in Joshua 20:3?**  
**Answer:** and they shall be your refuge from the avenger of blood.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:3

**Q9. According to Joshua 20:3, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** That the slayer that killeth any person unawares and unwittingly may flee thither: and they shall be your refuge from the avenger of blood.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:3

---

### Joshua 20:4
**Q10. According to Joshua 20:4, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And when he that doth flee unto one of those cities shall stand at the entering of the gate of the city.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:4

**Q11. What is declared next in Joshua 20:4?**  
**Answer:** and shall declare his cause in the ears of the elders of that city.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:4

**Q12. What further truth is given in Joshua 20:4?**  
**Answer:** they shall take him into the city unto them, and give him a place, that he may dwell among them.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:4

### Joshua 20:5
**Q13. What is stated in the first clause of Joshua 20:5?**  
**Answer:** And if the avenger of blood pursue after him, then they shall not deliver the slayer up into his hand.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:5

**Q14. What additional detail is declared in Joshua 20:5?**  
**Answer:** because he smote his neighbour unwittingly, and hated him not beforetime.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:5

**Q15. According to Joshua 20:5, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And if the avenger of blood pursue after him, then they shall not deliver the slayer up into his hand; because he smote his neighbour unwittingly, and hated him not beforetime.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:5

### Joshua 20:6
**Q16. What is stated in the first clause of Joshua 20:6?**  
**Answer:** And he shall dwell in that city, until he stand before the congregation for judgment, and until the death of the high priest that shall be in those days.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:6

**Q17. What additional detail is declared in Joshua 20:6?**  
**Answer:** then shall the slayer return, and come unto his own city, and unto his own house, unto the city from whence he fled.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:6

**Q18. According to Joshua 20:6, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And he shall dwell in that city, until he stand before the congregation for judgment, and until the death of the high priest that shall be in those days: then shall the slayer return, and come unto his own city, and unto his own house, unto the city from whence he fled.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:6

### Joshua 20:7
**Q19. According to Joshua 20:7, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And they appointed Kedesh in Galilee in mount Naphtali.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:7

**Q20. What is declared next in Joshua 20:7?**  
**Answer:** and Shechem in mount Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:7

**Q21. What further truth is given in Joshua 20:7?**  
**Answer:** and Kirjatharba, which is Hebron, in the mountain of Judah.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:7

---

### Joshua 20:8
**Q22. According to Joshua 20:8, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And on the other side Jordan by Jericho eastward.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:8

**Q23. What is declared next in Joshua 20:8?**  
**Answer:** they assigned Bezer in the wilderness upon the plain out of the tribe of Reuben.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:8

**Q24. What further truth is given in Joshua 20:8?**  
**Answer:** and Ramoth in Gilead out of the tribe of Gad, and Golan in Bashan out of the tribe of Manasseh.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:8

---

### Joshua 20:9
**Q25. According to Joshua 20:9, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** These were the cities appointed for all the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:9

**Q26. What is declared next in Joshua 20:9?**  
**Answer:** and for the stranger that sojourneth among them.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:9

**Q27. What further truth is given in Joshua 20:9?**  
**Answer:** that whosoever killeth any person at unawares might flee thither, and not die by the hand of the avenger of blood, until he stood before the congregation.  
**Verse:** Joshua 20:9
