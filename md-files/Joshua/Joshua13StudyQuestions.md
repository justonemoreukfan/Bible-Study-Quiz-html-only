# Joshua 13 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Joshua 13:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Joshua 13:1?**  
**Answer:** Now Joshua was old and stricken in years.  
**Verse:** Joshua 13:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Joshua 13:1?**  
**Answer:** and the LORD said unto him, Thou art old and stricken in years, and there remaineth yet very much land to be possessed.  
**Verse:** Joshua 13:1

**Q3. According to Joshua 13:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Now Joshua was old and stricken in years; and the LORD said unto him, Thou art old and stricken in years, and there remaineth yet very much land to be possessed.  
**Verse:** Joshua 13:1
