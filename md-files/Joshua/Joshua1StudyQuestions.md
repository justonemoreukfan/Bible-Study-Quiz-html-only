# Joshua 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Joshua 1:1

**Q1. After whose death did the LORD speak unto Joshua the son of Nun?**  
**Answer:** Moses the servant of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:1

**Q2. Whose minister was Joshua the son of Nun?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:1

**Q3. Who did the LORD speak unto after the death of Moses?**  
**Answer:** Joshua the son of Nun, Moses' minister.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:1

---

### Joshua 1:2

**Q4. Who did the LORD say is dead?**  
**Answer:** Moses my servant.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:2

**Q5. What did the LORD tell Joshua to arise and go over?**  
**Answer:** This Jordan.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:2

**Q6. Who were Joshua and the people to go into the land which the LORD gave them?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:2

---

### Joshua 1:3

**Q7. What did the LORD say He had given unto them?**  
**Answer:** Every place that the sole of your foot shall tread upon.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:3

**Q8. To whom did the LORD say He had given every place the sole of their foot shall tread upon?**  
**Answer:** Unto you.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:3

**Q9. According to what did the LORD say He had given these places to Moses?**  
**Answer:** As I said unto Moses.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:3

---

### Joshua 1:4

**Q10. From what wilderness and river shall their border be?**  
**Answer:** From the wilderness and this Lebanon.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:4

**Q11. Unto what great river shall the border extend?**  
**Answer:** Even unto the great river, the river Euphrates.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:4

**Q12. Unto what sea shall the coast be toward the going down of the sun?**  
**Answer:** Unto the great sea.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:4

---

### Joshua 1:5

**Q13. Who shall be able to stand before Joshua all the days of his life?**  
**Answer:** Any man.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:5

**Q14. As the LORD was with Moses, how will He be with Joshua?**  
**Answer:** I will be with thee.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:5

**Q15. What will the LORD not do to Joshua?**  
**Answer:** I will not fail thee, nor forsake thee.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:5

---

### Joshua 1:6

**Q16. What did the LORD command Joshua to be?**  
**Answer:** Strong and of a good courage.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:6

**Q17. Unto whom shall Joshua divide for an inheritance the land?**  
**Answer:** Unto this people.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:6

**Q18. What did the LORD swear unto their fathers to give them?**  
**Answer:** The land.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:6

---

### Joshua 1:7

**Q19. How did the LORD tell Joshua to be strong and very courageous?**  
**Answer:** Only be thou strong and very courageous.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:7

**Q20. According to what law, which Moses the servant of the LORD commanded him, should Joshua observe to do?**  
**Answer:** All the law.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:7

**Q21. From what direction should Joshua not turn away from it, that he may prosper whithersoever he goeth?**  
**Answer:** From it to the right hand or to the left.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:7

---

### Joshua 1:8

**Q22. What shall not depart out of thy mouth?**  
**Answer:** This book of the law.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:8

**Q23. When should Joshua meditate in the book of the law?**  
**Answer:** Day and night.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:8

**Q24. What will happen if Joshua observes to do according to all that is written therein?**  
**Answer:** Then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:8

---

### Joshua 1:9

**Q25. What question does the LORD ask regarding His command?**  
**Answer:** Have not I commanded thee?  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:9

**Q26. What two things did the LORD tell Joshua not to be?**  
**Answer:** Be not afraid, neither be thou dismayed.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:9

**Q27. Who is with Joshua whithersoever he goeth?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:9

---

### Joshua 1:10

**Q28. Whom did Joshua command?**  
**Answer:** The officers of the people.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:10

**Q29. What did Joshua command the officers to pass through?**  
**Answer:** The host.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:10

**Q30. What did Joshua command the officers to command the people to prepare?**  
**Answer:** Victuals.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:10

---

### Joshua 1:11

**Q31. Within how many days did Joshua tell the people they shall pass over this Jordan?**  
**Answer:** Yet three days.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:11

**Q32. To do what were the people to go to possess the land?**  
**Answer:** To go in to possess the land.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:11

**Q33. Who is giving them the land to possess?**  
**Answer:** The LORD your God giveth you to possess it.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:11

---

### Joshua 1:12

**Q34. To whom else did Joshua speak besides the officers?**  
**Answer:** To the Reubenites, and to the Gadites, and to half the tribe of Manasseh.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:12

**Q35. Whose tribes were the Reubenites, Gadites, and half the tribe of Manasseh?**  
**Answer:** Tribe of Manasseh.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:12

**Q36. What did Joshua speak to these specific tribes?**  
**Answer:** Saying...  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:12

---

### Joshua 1:13

**Q37. What word did Moses the servant of the LORD command them to remember?**  
**Answer:** The word which Moses the servant of the LORD commanded you.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:13

**Q38. Who did the LORD your God give rest to?**  
**Answer:** You.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:13

**Q39. What did the LORD give unto them?**  
**Answer:** This land.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:13

---

### Joshua 1:14

**Q40. Who shall pass before their brethren armed?**  
**Answer:** Your wives, your little ones, and your cattle. (Wait, let's look closer: "Your wives, your little ones, and your cattle, shall remain in the land which Moses gave you on this side Jordan; but ye shall pass before your brethren armed...")  
**Answer corrected:** All the mighty men of valour.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:14

**Q41. Who shall remain in the land which Moses gave them on this side Jordan?**  
**Answer:** Your wives, your little ones, and your cattle.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:14

**Q42. In what state shall they pass before their brethren?**  
**Answer:** Armed.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:14

---

### Joshua 1:15

**Q43. Until when shall the LORD have given rest to their brethren?**  
**Answer:** Until the LORD have given rest to your brethren, as well as unto you.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:15

**Q44. Who else shall also have possessions?**  
**Answer:** They also have the land which the LORD your God giveth them.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:15

**Q45. Unto which side of Jordan shall they return?**  
**Answer:** Unto your land, and enjoy it, which Moses the LORD's servant gave you on this side Jordan toward the sunrising.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:15

---

### Joshua 1:16

**Q46. What did they answer Joshua?**  
**Answer:** All that thou commandest us we will do.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:16

**Q47. Whithersoever Joshua sends them, what will they do?**  
**Answer:** Whithersoever thou sendest us, we will go.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:16

**Q48. Who did they say they would hearken unto just as they hearkened unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** Even so will we hearken unto thee.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:16

---

### Joshua 1:17

**Q49. As they hearkened unto whom in all things, will they hearken unto Joshua?**  
**Answer:** Unto Moses.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:17

**Q50. Who did they pray that the LORD their God would be with?**  
**Answer:** Thee.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:17

**Q51. With whom did they compare the LORD's presence with Joshua?**  
**Answer:** Even as he was with Moses.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:17

---

### Joshua 1:18

**Q52. What will happen to whosoever doth rebel against thy commandment?**  
**Answer:** He shall be put to death.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:18

**Q53. Whose words shall not be hearkened unto in all that thou commandest him?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever will not hearken unto thy words in all that thou commandest him.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:18

**Q54. What did they command Joshua to be?**  
**Answer:** Only be strong and of a good courage.  
**Verse:** Joshua 1:18
