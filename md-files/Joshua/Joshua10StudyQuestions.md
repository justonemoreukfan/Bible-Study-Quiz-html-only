# Joshua 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Joshua 10:1

**Q1.** What did Adonizedek, king of Jerusalem, hear that Joshua had done to Ai?  
**Answer:** How he had taken Ai, and had utterly destroyed it; as he had done to Jericho and her king, so he had done to Ai and her king.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:1

**Q2.** What did Adonizedek hear about the inhabitants of Gibeon?  
**Answer:** That they had made peace with Israel, and were among them.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:1

**Q3.** What was the reaction of Adonizedek and his people to this news?  
**Answer:** They feared greatly, because Gibeon was a great city, as one of the royal cities, and because it was greater than Ai, and all the men thereof were mighty.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:1

---

### Joshua 10:2

**Q4.** Whom did Adonizedek send a message to regarding this matter?  
**Answer:** Hoham king of Hebron, and unto Piram king of Jarmuth, and unto Japhia king of Lachish, and unto Debir king of Eglon.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:2

**Q5.** Why were the people of Jerusalem and their king afraid of Gibeon?  
**Answer:** Because Gibeon was a great city, as one of the royal cities, and because it was greater than Ai, and all the men thereof were mighty.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:2

**Q6.** What kind of men were the inhabitants of Gibeon said to be?  
**Answer:** Mighty.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:2

---

### Joshua 10:3

**Q7.** Who was the king of Jerusalem that gathered the other kings?  
**Answer:** Adonizedek.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:3

**Q8.** Name two of the other kings that Adonizedek sent unto.  
**Answer:** Hoham king of Hebron, and Piram king of Jarmuth. *(Also Japhia king of Lachish and Debir king of Eglon)*  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:3

**Q9.** What did Adonizedek ask these kings to do?  
**Answer:** Come up unto me, and help me, that we may smite Gibeon.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:3

---

### Joshua 10:4

**Q10.** What did the five kings agree to do together?  
**Answer:** Come up, and help me, that we may smite Gibeon.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:4

**Q11.** Why did the kings want to smite Gibeon?  
**Answer:** For it hath made peace with Joshua and with the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:4

**Q12.** Who were the five kings gathered together called in this verse?  
**Answer:** The five kings of the Amorites.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:4

---

### Joshua 10:5

**Q13.** How many kings of the Amorites gathered themselves together and went up?  
**Answer:** Five.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:5

**Q14.** Where did the five kings and all their hosts encamp?  
**Answer:** Before Gibeon, and made war against it.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:5

**Answer:** Made war against it.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:5

### Joshua 10:6

**Answer:** Unto Joshua.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:6

**Q17:** What was the plea of the men of Gibeon to Joshua?  
**Answer:** Slack not thy hand from thy servants; come up to us quickly, and save us, and help us.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:6

**Q18:** Who gathered together against Gibeon to cause this plea?  
**Answer:** All the kings of the Amorites that dwell in the mountains.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:6

**Answer:** And the Lord discomfited them before Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:10

**Q29:** Where did Israel chase the enemy along the way?

### Joshua 10:11

**Q31:** While the enemy fled from Israel, what did the Lord cast down upon them from heaven?  
**Answer:** Great stones from heaven upon them unto Azekah, and they died.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:11

**Q32:** What other weapon of destruction did the Lord use besides great stones?  
**Answer:** Hailstones.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:11

**Q33:** Who died more from the hailstones than from the sword?  
**Answer:** They which died with hailstones were more than they whom the children of Israel slew with the sword.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:11

---

### Joshua 10:12

**Q34:** To whom did Joshua speak on the day the Lord delivered up the Amorites?  
**Answer:** To the Lord.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:12

**Answer:** In the valley of Ajalon.  
**Verse:** Joshua 10:12

### Joshua 10:13
