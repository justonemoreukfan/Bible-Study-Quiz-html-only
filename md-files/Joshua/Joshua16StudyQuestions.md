# Joshua 16 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Joshua 16:1
**Q1. According to Joshua 16:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And the lot of the children of Joseph fell from Jordan by Jericho.  
**Verse:** Joshua 16:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Joshua 16:1?**  
**Answer:** unto the water of Jericho on the east.  
**Verse:** Joshua 16:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Joshua 16:1?**  
**Answer:** to the wilderness that goeth up from Jericho throughout mount Bethel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 16:1

---

### Joshua 16:2
**Q4. What does the first part of Joshua 16:2 state?**  
**Answer:** And goeth out from Bethel to Luz.  
**Verse:** Joshua 16:2

**Q5. What does the second part of Joshua 16:2 declare?**  
**Answer:** and passeth along unto the borders of Archi to Ataroth.  
**Verse:** Joshua 16:2

**Q6. What is recorded in Joshua 16:2?**  
**Answer:** And goeth out from Bethel to Luz, and passeth along unto the borders of Archi to Ataroth,.  
**Verse:** Joshua 16:2

### Joshua 16:3
**Q7. What is stated in the first clause of Joshua 16:3?**  
**Answer:** And goeth down westward to the coast of Japhleti, unto the coast of Bethhoron the nether, and to Gezer.  
**Verse:** Joshua 16:3

**Q8. What additional detail is declared in Joshua 16:3?**  
**Answer:** and the goings out thereof are at the sea.  
**Verse:** Joshua 16:3

**Q9. According to Joshua 16:3, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And goeth down westward to the coast of Japhleti, unto the coast of Bethhoron the nether, and to Gezer; and the goings out thereof are at the sea.  
**Verse:** Joshua 16:3

### Joshua 16:5
**Q13. What is stated in the first clause of Joshua 16:5?**  
**Answer:** And the border of the children of Ephraim according to their families was thus.  
**Verse:** Joshua 16:5

**Q14. What additional detail is declared in Joshua 16:5?**  
**Answer:** even the border of their inheritance on the east side was Atarothaddar, unto Bethhoron the upper.  
**Verse:** Joshua 16:5

**Q15. According to Joshua 16:5, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And the border of the children of Ephraim according to their families was thus: even the border of their inheritance on the east side was Atarothaddar, unto Bethhoron the upper;.  
**Verse:** Joshua 16:5
