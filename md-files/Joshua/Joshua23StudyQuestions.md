# Joshua 23 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Joshua 23:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Joshua 23:1 state?**  
**Answer:** And it came to pass a long time after that the LORD had given rest unto Israel from all their enemies round about.  
**Verse:** Joshua 23:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Joshua 23:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** that Joshua waxed old and stricken in age.  
**Verse:** Joshua 23:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Joshua 23:1?**  
**Answer:** And it came to pass a long time after that the LORD had given rest unto Israel from all their enemies round about, that Joshua waxed old and stricken in age.  
**Verse:** Joshua 23:1

---

### Joshua 23:2
**Q4. According to Joshua 23:2, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And Joshua called for all Israel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 23:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Joshua 23:2?**  
**Answer:** and for their elders.  
**Verse:** Joshua 23:2

**Q6. What further truth is given in Joshua 23:2?**  
**Answer:** and for their heads, and for their judges, and for their officers, and said unto them, I am old and stricken in age.  
**Verse:** Joshua 23:2
