**Verse:** Joshua 7:2

**Answer:** Beside Beth-aven, on the east side of Bethel.  
**Verse:** Joshua 7:2

**Q3. What did Joshua command the men to do?**

**Verse:** Joshua 7:2

### Joshua 7:3

**Q1. To whom did the men return after viewing Ai?**  
**Answer:** To Joshua.  
**Verse:** Joshua 7:3

**Q2. How many men did they suggest should go up against Ai?**  
**Answer:** About two or three thousand men.  
**Verse:** Joshua 7:3
