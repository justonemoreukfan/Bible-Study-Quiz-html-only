# Philemon 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Philemon 1:1

**Q1. Whose prisoner is described in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:1

**Q2. Who is joined with Paul in writing this epistle?**  
**Answer:** Timothy our brother.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:1

**Q3. To whom is this epistle written?**  
**Answer:** Philemon our dearly beloved, and fellowlabourer.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:1

---

### Philemon 1:2

**Q1. Who is specifically named alongside Philemon in this greeting?**  
**Answer:** Our beloved Apphia.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:2

**Q2. Who is mentioned as our fellowsoldier in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Archippus.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:2

**Q3. What meets in their house?**  
**Answer:** The church.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:2

---

### Philemon 1:3

**Q1. What is desired from God our Father?**  
**Answer:** Grace to you, and peace.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:3

**Q2. Whose grace and peace are desired?**  
**Answer:** From God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:3

**Q3. From whom besides the Father is grace and peace desired?**  
**Answer:** The Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:3

---

### Philemon 1:4

**Q1. What does Paul do for Philemon in his prayers?**  
**Answer:** Making mention of thee always in my prayers.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:4

**Q2. When does Paul make mention of Philemon?**  
**Answer:** Always.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:4

**Q3. To whom does Paul give thanks concerning Philemon?**  
**Answer:** My God.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:4

---

### Philemon 1:5

**Q1. What has Paul heard of regarding Philemon?**  
**Answer:** Thy love and faith.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:5

**Q2. Toward whom are Philemon's love and faith directed?**  
**Answer:** Toward the Lord Jesus, and toward all saints.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:5

**Q3. What is directed toward all saints?**  
**Answer:** Love.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:5

---

### Philemon 1:6

**Q1. What is the communication of Philemon's faith expected to become?**  
**Answer:** Effectual.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:6

**Q2. By what means is the communication of faith to become effectual?**  
**Answer:** By the acknowledging of every good thing which is in you in Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:6

**Q3. In whom are every good thing acknowledged?**  
**Answer:** Christ Jesus.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:6

---

### Philemon 1:7

**Q1. What did Paul have regarding Philemon's love?**  
**Answer:** Great joy and consolation.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:7

**Q2. What was refreshed by Philemon?**  
**Answer:** The bowels of the saints.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:7

**Q3. Who refreshed the bowels of the saints?**  
**Answer:** Thee, brother.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:7

---

### Philemon 1:8

**Q1. How much boldness did Paul have in Christ?**  
**Answer:** Much boldness.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:8

**Q2. What could Paul enjoin Philemon to do?**  
**Answer:** That which is convenient.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:8

**Q3. In whom did Paul have much boldness?**  
**Answer:** In Christ.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:8

---

### Philemon 1:9

**Q1. For what reason does Paul rather beseech Philemon?**  
**Answer:** For love's sake.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:9

**Q2. How does Paul describe himself in age?**  
**Answer:** Being such an one as Paul the aged.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:9

**Q3. What else is Paul currently, besides being aged?**  
**Answer:** Now also a prisoner of Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:9

---

### Philemon 1:10

**Q1. Whom does Paul beseech for his son?**  
**Answer:** For my son Onesimus.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:10

**Q2. How is Onesimus described in relation to Paul?**  
**Answer:** Whom I have begotten in my bonds.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:10

**Q3. Where was Onesimus begotten by Paul?**  
**Answer:** In my bonds.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:10

---

### Philemon 1:11

**Q1. How is Onesimus described in regard to past time?**  
**Answer:** Which in time past was to thee unprofitable.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:11

**Q2. How is Onesimus described in regard to the present time?**  
**Answer:** But now profitable to thee and to me.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:11

**Q3. To whom is Onesimus now profitable?**  
**Answer:** To thee and to me.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:11

---

### Philemon 1:12

**Q1. Whom has Paul sent back?**  
**Answer:** Onesimus.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:12

**Q2. How is Onesimus described upon being sent back?**  
**Answer:** Thou therefore receive him, that is, mine own bowels.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:12

**Q3. What is said of Onesimus regarding Paul?**  
**Answer:** Mine own bowels.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:12

---

### Philemon 1:13

**Q1. What did Paul desire to retain with himself?**  
**Answer:** Him.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:13

**Q2. In what context would Paul have retained him?**  
**Answer:** In thy stead he might have ministered unto me in the bonds of the gospel.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:13

**Q3. Where was Paul experiencing bonds while preaching?**  
**Answer:** In the bonds of the gospel.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:13

---

### Philemon 1:14

**Q1. Without whose mind would Paul do nothing?**  
**Answer:** Thy mind.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:14

**Q2. What kind of thing was Philemon's benefit not to be?**  
**Answer:** Not as it were of necessity.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:14

**Q3. How should Philemon's benefit willingly be?**  
**Answer:** But willingly.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:14

---

### Philemon 1:15

**Q1. For what purpose perhaps did Onesimus depart for a season?**  
**Answer:** That thou shouldest receive him for ever.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:15

**Q2. How long did Onesimus depart?**  
**Answer:** For a season.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:15

**Q3. How long should Philemon receive him back?**  
**Answer:** For ever.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:15

---

### Philemon 1:16

**Q1. How is Onesimus no longer to be received?**  
**Answer:** Not now as a servant.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:16

**Q2. What is Onesimus above a servant?**  
**Answer:** A brother beloved.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:16

**Q3. To whom is he especially a beloved brother?**  
**Answer:** Specially to me, but how much more unto thee, both in the flesh, and in the Lord?  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:16

---

### Philemon 1:17

**Q1. If Philemon counts Paul as what, should he receive Onesimus?**  
**Answer:** A partner.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:17

**Q2. How should Philemon receive Onesimus?**  
**Answer:** Receive him as myself.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:17

**Q3. Who is to be received as Paul himself?**  
**Answer:** Onesimus.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:17

---

### Philemon 1:18

**Q1. What has Onesimus done if he has wronged Philemon?**  
**Answer:** If he hath wronged thee.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:18

**Q2. What has Onesimus done if he owes Philemon anything?**  
**Answer:** Or oweth thee ought.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:18

**Q3. To whose account does Paul say to put that?**  
**Answer:** Put that on mine account.  
**Verse:** Philemon 1:18

**Verse:** Philemon 1:19
