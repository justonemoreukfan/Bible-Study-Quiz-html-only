# Isaiah 17 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 17:1
**Q1. According to Isaiah 17:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** The burden of Damascus. Behold.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 17:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 17:1?**  
**Answer:** Damascus is taken away from being a city.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 17:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Isaiah 17:1?**  
**Answer:** and it shall be a ruinous heap.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 17:1

---

### Isaiah 17:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 17:2?**  
**Answer:** The cities of Aroer are forsaken.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 17:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 17:2?**  
**Answer:** they shall be for flocks, which shall lie down, and none shall make them afraid.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 17:2

**Q6. According to Isaiah 17:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** The cities of Aroer are forsaken: they shall be for flocks, which shall lie down, and none shall make them afraid.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 17:2

### Isaiah 17:5
**Q13. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 17:5?**

**Verse:** Isaiah 17:5

**Q14. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 17:5?**  
**Answer:** and it shall be as he that gathereth ears in the valley of Rephaim.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 17:5

**Verse:** Isaiah 17:5
