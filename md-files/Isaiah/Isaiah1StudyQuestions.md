# Isaiah 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 1:1
**Q1. According to Isaiah 1:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** The vision of Isaiah the son of Amoz.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 1:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 1:1?**  
**Answer:** which he saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem in the days of Uzziah.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 1:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Isaiah 1:1?**  
**Answer:** and Hezekiah, kings of Judah.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 1:1
