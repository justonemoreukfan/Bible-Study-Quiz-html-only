# Isaiah 61 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 61:1

**Q1. Who hath anointed the speaker to preach good tidings unto the meek?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit of the Lord GOD.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:1

**Q2. To whom hath the Lord sent the speaker to bind up?**  
**Answer:** The brokenhearted.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:1

**Q3. What liberty is proclaimed to the captives?**  
**Answer:** The opening of the prison to them that are bound.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:1

---

### Isaiah 61:2

**Q4. What year of the LORD is proclaimed?**  
**Answer:** The acceptable year of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:2

**Q5. What day of our God is proclaimed alongside the acceptable year of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The day of vengeance of our God.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:2

**Q6. Who is to be comforted according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** All that mourn.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:2

---

### Isaiah 61:3

**Q7. For whom in Zion is appointment made to give unto them beauty for ashes?**  
**Answer:** Them that mourn in Zion.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:3

**Q8. What is given instead of ashes?**  
**Answer:** Beauty.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:3

**Q9. What kind of garment is given for the spirit of heaviness?**  
**Answer:** The garment of praise.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:3

---

### Isaiah 61:4

**Q10. What shall they build according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** The old wastes.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:4

**Q11. What shall they raise up?**  
**Answer:** The former desolations.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:4

**Q12. What shall they repair?**  
**Answer:** The waste cities, the desolations of many generations.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:4

---

### Isaiah 61:5

**Q13. Who shall stand and feed your flocks?**  
**Answer:** Strangers.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:5

**Q14. Who shall be your plowmen?**  
**Answer:** The sons of the alien.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:5

**Q15. Who shall be your vinedressers?**  
**Answer:** The sons of the alien.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:5

---

### Isaiah 61:6

**Q16. By what title shall ye yourselves be named?**  
**Answer:** The Priests of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:6

**Q17. What shall men call you concerning our God?**  
**Answer:** The Ministers of our God.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:6

**Q18. What shall ye eat, and in what shall ye boast yourselves?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall eat the riches of the Gentiles, and in their glory shall ye boast yourselves.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:6

---

### Isaiah 61:7

**Q19. Instead of your shame, what shall ye have?**  
**Answer:** Double.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:7

**Q20. Instead of confusion, what shall they rejoice in?**  
**Answer:** Their portion.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:7

**Q21. What shall they possess in their land?**  
**Answer:** Double.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:7

---

### Isaiah 61:8

**Q22. What does the LORD love?**  
**Answer:** Judgment.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:8

**Q23. What does the LORD hate?**  
**Answer:** Robbery for burnt offering.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:8

**Q24. What will the LORD direct in truth and make with them?**  
**Answer:** Their work in truth, and make an everlasting covenant with them.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:8

---

### Isaiah 61:9

**Q25. Where shall their seed be known?**  
**Answer:** Among the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:9

**Q26. Where shall their offspring be known?**  
**Answer:** Among the people.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:9

**Q27. What shall all that see them acknowledge about them?**  
**Answer:** That they are the seed which the LORD hath blessed.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:9

---

### Isaiah 61:10

**Q28. In whom will the speaker greatly rejoice?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:10

**Q29. In whom shall my soul be joyful?**  
**Answer:** My God.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:10

**Q30. With what has the Lord clothed and covered the speaker?**  
**Answer:** The garments of salvation and the robe of righteousness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:10

---

### Isaiah 61:11

**Q31. As what brings forth her bud, how does the earth bring forth her bud?**  
**Answer:** As the earth bringeth forth her bud, and as the garden causeth the things that are sown in it to spring forth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:11

**Q32. What shall the Lord GOD cause to spring forth before all the nations?**  
**Answer:** Righteousness and praise.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:11

**Q33. Before what group shall the Lord GOD cause righteousness and praise to spring forth?**  
**Answer:** All the nations.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 61:11
