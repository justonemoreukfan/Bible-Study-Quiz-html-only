# Isaiah 49 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 49:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 49:1?**  
**Answer:** Listen, O isles, unto me.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 49:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 49:1?**  
**Answer:** and hearken, ye people, from far.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 49:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 49:1?**  
**Answer:** The LORD hath called me from the womb; from the bowels of my mother hath he made mention of my name.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 49:1
