# Isaiah 59 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 59:1

**Q1. What is the Lord's hand not shortened that it cannot do?**  
**Answer:** Save.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:1

**Q2. What is the Lord's ear not heavy that it cannot do?**  
**Answer:** Hear.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:1

**Q3. Whose hand is not shortened and whose ear is not heavy?**  
**Answer:** The Lord's.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:1

---

### Isaiah 59:2

**Q1. What have separated between you and your God?**  
**Answer:** Your iniquities.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:2

**Q2. What have hid his face from you?**  
**Answer:** Your sins.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:2

**Q3. Because of sins, what will the Lord not do?**  
**Answer:** Hear.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:2

---

### Isaiah 59:3

**Q1. What are defiled with blood?**  
**Answer:** Your hands.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:3

**Q2. What are defiled with iniquity?**  
**Answer:** Your fingers.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:3

**Q3. What have your lips spoken?**  
**Answer:** Lies.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:3

---

### Isaiah 59:4

**Q1. Who calls for justice?**  
**Answer:** None.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:4

**Q2. Who pleads for truth?**  
**Answer:** None.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:4

**Q3. What do they trust in, conceive, and bring forth?**  
**Answer:** Vanity, mischief, and iniquity.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:4

---

### Isaiah 59:5

**Q1. What kind of eggs do they hatch?**  
**Answer:** Cockatrice' eggs.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:5

**Q2. What do they weave?**  
**Answer:** The spider's web.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:5

**Q3. What happens if one eats of their eggs?**  
**Answer:** He dieth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:5

---

### Isaiah 59:6

**Q1. What shall their webs not become?**  
**Answer:** Garments.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:6

**Q2. What shall they not cover themselves with?**  
**Answer:** Their works.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:6

**Q3. What are the works of their hands?**  
**Answer:** Works of iniquity.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:6

---

### Isaiah 59:7

**Q1. To what do their feet run?**  
**Answer:** Evil.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:7

**Q2. What do they make haste to shed?**  
**Answer:** Innocent blood.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:7

**Q3. What are their thoughts?**  
**Answer:** Thoughts of iniquity.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:7

---

### Isaiah 59:8

**Q1. What do they not know?**  
**Answer:** The way of peace.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:8

**Q2. What is not in their goings?**  
**Answer:** Judgment.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:8

**Q3. What have they made for themselves?**  
**Answer:** Crooked paths.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:8

---

### Isaiah 59:9

**Q1. What is far from us?**  
**Answer:** Judgment.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:9

**Q2. What does not overtake us?**  
**Answer:** Justice.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:9

**Q3. What do we wait for, but find only darkness?**  
**Answer:** Light.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:9

---

### Isaiah 59:10

**Q1. For what do we grope like the blind?**  
**Answer:** The wall.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:10

**Q2. As who do we grope, as if we had no eyes?**  
**Answer:** As those that have no eyes.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:10

**Q3. When do we stumble at noonday as in the night?**  
**Answer:** In desolate places.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:10

---

### Isaiah 59:11

**Q1. How do we roar all like bears?**  
**Answer:** Sore.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:11

**Q2. How do we mourn sore like doves?**  
**Answer:** Mourn sore.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:11

**Q3. What do we look for that is far off?**  
**Answer:** Salvation.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:11

---

### Isaiah 59:12

**Q1. What are multiplied before God?**  
**Answer:** Our transgressions.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:12

**Q2. What testifies against us?**  
**Answer:** Our sins.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:12

**Q3. What are with us and known by us?**  
**Answer:** Our transgressions and our iniquities.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:12

---

### Isaiah 59:13

**Q1. What have we transgressed and lied against?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:13

**Q2. What have we departed away from?**  
**Answer:** Our God.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:13

**Q3. What are uttered from the heart?**  
**Answer:** Words of oppression and revolt.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:13

---

### Isaiah 59:14

**Q1. What is turned away backward?**  
**Answer:** Judgment.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:14

**Q2. What standeth afar off?**  
**Answer:** Justice.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:14

**Q3. What is fallen in the street?**  
**Answer:** Truth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:14

---

### Isaiah 59:15

**Q1. What is failing?**  
**Answer:** Truth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:15

**Q2. What makes himself a prey?**  
**Answer:** He that departeth from evil.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:15

**Q3. Who saw it, and it displeased him that there was no judgment?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:15

---

### Isaiah 59:16

**Q1. What did the Lord see when there was no man?**  
**Answer:** That there was no man.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:16

**Q2. At what did the Lord wonder?**  
**Answer:** That there was no intercessor.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:16

**Q3. What brought salvation unto him?**  
**Answer:** His own arm.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:16

---

### Isaiah 59:17

**Q1. What did he put on as a breastplate?**  
**Answer:** Righteousness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:17

**Q2. What was upon his head?**  
**Answer:** An helmet of salvation.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:17

**Q3. What did he put on for clothing?**  
**Answer:** The garments of vengeance.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:17

---

### Isaiah 59:18

**Q1. According to whose deeds will he accordingly repay?**  
**Answer:** Their deeds.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:18

**Q2. What will he repay to his adversaries?**  
**Answer:** Fury.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:18

**Q3. To whom will he repay recompence?**  
**Answer:** His enemies.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:18

---

### Isaiah 59:19

**Q1. From where shall they fear the name of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** From the west.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:19

**Q2. From where shall they fear his glory?**  
**Answer:** From the rising of the sun.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:19

**Q3. When the enemy shall come in like a flood, what shall lift up a standard against him?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:19

---

### Isaiah 59:20

**Q1. Who shall come to Zion?**  
**Answer:** The Redeemer.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:20

**Q2. Unto whom shall the Redeemer come?**  
**Answer:** Unto them that turn from transgression in Jacob.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:20

**Q3. Who saith this?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:20

---

### Isaiah 59:21

**Q1. Whose covenant is this with them, saith the Lord?**  
**Answer:** My covenant.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:21

**Q2. What shall not depart out of thy mouth?**  
**Answer:** My words.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:21

**Q3. From henceforth and for ever, whose mouth shall not depart out of the mouth of thy seed?**  
**Answer:** The Lord's words.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 59:21
