# Isaiah 52 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 52:1

**Q1. What does the speaker tell Zion to do in verse 1?**  
**Answer:** Awake, awake.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:1

**Q2. What is Zion told to put on along with her strength?**  
**Answer:** Thy beautiful garments.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:1

**Q3. Who shall no more come into Jerusalem, the holy city?**  
**Answer:** The uncircumcised and the unclean.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:1

---

### Isaiah 52:2

**Q1. What is Jerusalem commanded to shake herself from?**  
**Answer:** The dust.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:2

**Q2. What should Jerusalem arise and sit down on?**  
**Answer:** Loose thyself from the bands of thy neck.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:2

**Q3. Who is told to loose themselves from the bands of their neck?**  
**Answer:** O captive daughter of Zion.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:2

---

### Isaiah 52:3

**Q1. How did the Lord say His people had sold themselves?**  
**Answer:** For nought.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:3

**Q2. How shall the people be redeemed according to verse 3?**  
**Answer:** Without money.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:3

**Q3. Who said that the people sold themselves for nought?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:3

---

### Isaiah 52:4

**Q1. Where did the Lord God's people go down at the first to sojourn there?**  
**Answer:** Egypt.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:4

**Q2. Who oppressed the people without cause?**  
**Answer:** The Assyrian.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:4

**Q3. To which place did the people go down to sojourn?**  
**Answer:** Egypt.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:4

---

### Isaiah 52:5

**Q1. What question does the Lord ask regarding His people being taken away?**  
**Answer:** What have I here, saith the Lord, that my people is taken away for nought?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:5

**Q2. What do they that rule over the people do?**  
**Answer:** Make them to howl, saith the Lord.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:5

**Q3. What is continually blasphemed all the day long?**  
**Answer:** My name.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:5

---

### Isaiah 52:6

**Q1. What shall the Lord's people know in that day?**  
**Answer:** My name.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:6

**Q2. Who shall know that the Lord is He that doth speak?**  
**Answer:** Therefore they shall know in that day that I am he that doth speak.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:6

**Q3. What declaration does the Lord make regarding Himself at the end of verse 6?**  
**Answer:** Behold, it is I.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:6

---

### Isaiah 52:7

**Q1. What are the feet of him upon the mountains described as?**  
**Answer:** Beautiful upon the mountains.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:7

**Q2. What does the messenger bring that publishes peace?**  
**Answer:** Good tidings of good.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:7

**Q3. What does the messenger say unto Zion?**  
**Answer:** Thy God reigneth!  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:7

---

### Isaiah 52:8

**Q1. What do the watchmen lift up?**  
**Answer:** Their voice.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:8

**Q2. What do the watchmen do together with their voice?**  
**Answer:** Sing.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:8

**Q3. What shall the watchmen see eye to eye?**  
**Answer:** When the Lord shall bring again Zion.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:8

---

### Isaiah 52:9

**Q1. What are the waste places of Jerusalem commanded to do?**  
**Answer:** Break forth into joy, sing together.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:9

**Q2. What has the Lord comforted?**  
**Answer:** His people.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:9

**Q3. What has the Lord redeemed?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:9

---

### Isaiah 52:10

**Q1. What has the Lord made bare?**  
**Answer:** His holy arm.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:10

**Q2. In the eyes of what shall the Lord make bare His holy arm?**  
**Answer:** All the nations.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:10

**Q3. What shall all the ends of the earth see?**  
**Answer:** The salvation of our God.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:10

---

### Isaiah 52:11

**Q1. What action are the people told to take regarding Babylon?**  
**Answer:** Depart ye, depart ye.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:11

**Q2. What are the people told not to touch?**  
**Answer:** Any unclean thing.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:11

**Q3. Who are commanded to be clean, that bear the vessels of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Ye that bear the vessels of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:11

---

### Isaiah 52:12

**Q1. How shall the people not go out?**  
**Answer:** With haste.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:12

**Q2. How shall the people not go by?**  
**Answer:** By flight.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:12

**Q3. Who will go before the people and be their rereward?**  
**Answer:** The Lord will go before you; and the God of Israel will be your rereward.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:12

---

### Isaiah 52:13

**Q1. How shall the Lord's servant deal according to verse 13?**  
**Answer:** Prudently.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:13

**Q2. What shall happen to the servant in terms of being exalted?**  
**Answer:** He shall be exalted and extolled.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:13

**Q3. To what extent shall the servant be exalted and extolled?**  
**Answer:** And be very high.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:13

---

### Isaiah 52:14

**Q1. What were many astonished at regarding the servant?**  
**Answer:** At thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:14

**Q2. How was the servant's visage described?**  
**Answer:** His visage was so marred more than any man.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:14

**Q3. How was the servant's form described more than the sons of men?**  
**Answer:** And his form more than the sons of men.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:14

---

### Isaiah 52:15

**Q1. What shall the servant sprinkle?**  
**Answer:** Many nations.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:15

**Q2. What shall kings do at the servant because of this?**  
**Answer:** The kings shall shut their mouths at him.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:15

**Q3. What shall men see and consider that had not been told them?**  
**Answer:** That which had not been told them shall they see; and that which they had not heard shall they consider.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 52:15
