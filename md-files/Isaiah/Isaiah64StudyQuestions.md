# Isaiah 64 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 64:1
**Q1. According to Isaiah 64:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Oh that thou wouldest rend the heavens.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 64:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 64:1?**  
**Answer:** that thou wouldest come down.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 64:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Isaiah 64:1?**  
**Answer:** that the mountains might flow down at thy presence.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 64:1

---

### Isaiah 64:2
**Q4. According to Isaiah 64:2, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** As when the melting fire burneth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 64:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Isaiah 64:2?**  
**Answer:** the fire causeth the waters to boil.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 64:2

**Q6. What further truth is given in Isaiah 64:2?**  
**Answer:** to make thy name known to thine adversaries, that the nations may tremble at thy presence!  
**Verse:** Isaiah 64:2

### Isaiah 64:4
**Q10. According to Isaiah 64:4, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** For since the beginning of the world men have not heard.

### Isaiah 64:5
**Q13. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 64:5?**  
**Answer:** Thou meetest him that rejoiceth and worketh righteousness, those that remember thee in thy ways.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 64:5

**Q14. What is declared next in Isaiah 64:5?**  
**Answer:** behold, thou art wroth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 64:5

**Q15. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 64:5?**  
**Answer:** for we have sinned; in those is continuance, and we shall be saved.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 64:5

### Isaiah 64:6
**Q16. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 64:6?**  
**Answer:** But we are all as an unclean thing, and all our righteousnesses are as filthy rags.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 64:6

**Q17. What is declared next in Isaiah 64:6?**  
**Answer:** and we all do fade as a leaf.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 64:6

**Q18. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 64:6?**  
**Answer:** and our iniquities, like the wind, have taken us away.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 64:6

---

### Isaiah 64:7
**Q19. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 64:7?**  
**Answer:** And there is none that calleth upon thy name, that stirreth up himself to take hold of thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 64:7

**Q20. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 64:7?**  
**Answer:** for thou hast hid thy face from us, and hast consumed us, because of our iniquities.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 64:7

**Q21. According to Isaiah 64:7, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And there is none that calleth upon thy name, that stirreth up himself to take hold of thee: for thou hast hid thy face from us, and hast consumed us, because of our iniquities.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 64:7
