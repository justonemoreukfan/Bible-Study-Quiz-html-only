# Isaiah 46 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 46:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 46:1?**  
**Answer:** Bel boweth down, Nebo stoopeth, their idols were upon the beasts, and upon the cattle.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 46:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 46:1?**  
**Answer:** your carriages were heavy loaden.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 46:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 46:1?**  
**Answer:** they are a burden to the weary beast.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 46:1

---

### Isaiah 46:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 46:2?**  
**Answer:** They stoop, they bow down together.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 46:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 46:2?**  
**Answer:** they could not deliver the burden, but themselves are gone into captivity.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 46:2

**Q6. According to Isaiah 46:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** They stoop, they bow down together; they could not deliver the burden, but themselves are gone into captivity.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 46:2
