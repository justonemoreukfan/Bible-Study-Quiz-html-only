**Q1. What is declared in Isaiah 2:1?**

### Isaiah 2:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 2:2?**  
**Answer:** And it shall come to pass in the last days, that the mountain of the LORD’s house shall be established in the top of the mountains, and shall be exalted above the hills.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 2:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 2:2?**  
**Answer:** and all nations shall flow unto it.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 2:2

**Q6. According to Isaiah 2:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And it shall come to pass in the last days, that the mountain of the LORD’s house shall be established in the top of the mountains, and shall be exalted above the hills; and all nations shall flow unto it.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 2:2
