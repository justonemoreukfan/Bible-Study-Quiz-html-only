# Isaiah 14 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 14:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 14:1?**  
**Answer:** For the LORD will have mercy on Jacob, and will yet choose Israel, and set them in their own land.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 14:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 14:1?**  
**Answer:** and the strangers shall be joined with them, and they shall cleave to the house of Jacob.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 14:1

**Q3. According to Isaiah 14:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** For the LORD will have mercy on Jacob, and will yet choose Israel, and set them in their own land: and the strangers shall be joined with them, and they shall cleave to the house of Jacob.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 14:1
