# Isaiah 11 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 11:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Isaiah 11:1 state?**  
**Answer:** And there shall come forth a rod out of the stem of Jesse.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 11:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Isaiah 11:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** and a Branch shall grow out of his roots.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 11:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Isaiah 11:1?**  
**Answer:** And there shall come forth a rod out of the stem of Jesse, and a Branch shall grow out of his roots:.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 11:1

---

### Isaiah 11:2
**Q4. According to Isaiah 11:2, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And the spirit of the LORD shall rest upon him.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 11:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Isaiah 11:2?**  
**Answer:** the spirit of wisdom and understanding.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 11:2

**Q6. What further truth is given in Isaiah 11:2?**  
**Answer:** the spirit of counsel and might, the spirit of knowledge and of the fear of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 11:2
