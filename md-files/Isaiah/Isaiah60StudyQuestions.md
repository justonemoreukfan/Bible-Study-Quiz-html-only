# Isaiah 60 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 60:1

**Q1. What is commanded to happen to the person addressed in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Arise, shine.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:1

**Q2. What has come to the person addressed?**  
**Answer:** Thy light.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:1

**Q3. What has risen upon the person addressed?**  
**Answer:** The glory of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:1

---

### Isaiah 60:2

**Q1. What shall cover the earth according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Darkness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:2

**Q2. What shall cover the people?**  
**Answer:** Gross darkness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:2

**Q3. Who shall arise upon the person addressed?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:2

---

### Isaiah 60:3

**Q1. Who shall come to the light mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:3

**Q2. Whose brightness shall kings come to?**  
**Answer:** Thy brightness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:3

**Q3. What is the rising upon the person addressed compared to in relation to kings?**  
**Answer:** The brightness of thy rising.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:3

---

### Isaiah 60:4

**Q1. Where should the eyes be lifted up and looked round about?**  
**Answer:** Lift up thine eyes round about, and see.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:4

**Q2. Who gathers themselves together and comes to the person addressed?**  
**Answer:** They all gather themselves together, they come to thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:4

**Q3. How shall sons and daughters be carried and nursed?**  
**Answer:** Thy sons shall come from far, and thy daughters shall be nursed at thy side.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:4

---

### Isaiah 60:5

**Q1. What action shall be taken with the heart upon seeing these things?**  
**Answer:** Then thou shalt see, and flow together.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:5

**Q2. How shall the heart feel and expand?**  
**Answer:** Thine heart shall fear, and be enlarged.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:5

**Q3. What abundance shall be converted and come unto thee?**  
**Answer:** The abundance of the sea shall be converted unto thee, the forces of the Gentiles shall come unto thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:5

---

### Isaiah 60:6

**Q1. What multitude of camels shall cover the land?**  
**Answer:** The multitude of camels.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:6

**Q2. Which specific lands are mentioned as covering the camels?**  
**Answer:** The dromedaries of Midian and Ephah.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:6

**Q3. What shall they from Sheba bring?**  
**Answer:** They shall bring gold and incense.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:6

---

### Isaiah 60:7

**Q1. All the flocks of whom shall be gathered together unto thee?**  
**Answer:** Kedar.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:7

**Q2. Whose rams shall minister unto thee?**  
**Answer:** The rams of Nebaioth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:7

**Q3. Upon what shall they come up with acceptance?**  
**Answer:** Mine altar.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:7

---

### Isaiah 60:8

**Q1. Who are these that fly as what creature?**  
**Answer:** As a cloud.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:8

**Q2. How else are they described as flying besides a cloud?**  
**Answer:** As doves to their windows.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:8

**Q3. What are they doing in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Who are these that fly as a cloud, and as the doves to their windows?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:8

---

### Isaiah 60:9

**Q1. Who shall wait for me according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** The isles.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:9

**Q2. What ships are mentioned as coming first?**  
**Answer:** The ships of Tarshish.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:9

**Q3. From where are they bringing their silver and gold?**  
**Answer:** From far.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:9

---

### Isaiah 60:10

**Q1. Who shall build up thy walls?**  
**Answer:** The sons of the stranger.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:10

**Q2. Whose kings shall minister unto thee?**  
**Answer:** Their kings.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:10

**Q3. In what manner did the LORD smite thee, and in what manner has he had mercy?**  
**Answer:** In my wrath I smote thee, but in my favour have I had mercy on thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:10

---

### Isaiah 60:11

**Q1. When shall thy gates be open continually?**  
**Answer:** Therefore thy gates shall be open continually.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:11

**Q2. How often shall the gates be open?**  
**Answer:** Continually.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:11

**Q3. What shall not be shut?**  
**Answer:** They shall not be shut day nor night.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:11

---

### Isaiah 60:12

**Q1. Which nation and kingdom shall perish?**  
**Answer:** That nation and kingdom that will not serve thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:12

**Q2. What will happen to those nations and kingdoms?**  
**Answer:** They shall perish.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:12

**Q3. How thoroughly shall those nations be wasted?**  
**Answer:** Yea, those nations shall be utterly wasted.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:12

---

### Isaiah 60:13

**Q1. What specific trees shall come unto thee?**  
**Answer:** The glory of Lebanon.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:13

**Q2. Which trees are specifically named to come together?**  
**Answer:** The fir tree, the pine tree, and the box together.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:13

**Q3. To beautify what place are these trees brought?**  
**Answer:** The place of my sanctuary.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:13

---

### Isaiah 60:14

**Q1. Who shall come bending unto thee?**  
**Answer:** The sons of them that afflicted thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:14

**Q2. Who shall bow themselves down at the soles of thy feet?**  
**Answer:** All they that despised thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:14

**Q3. What shall they call thee?**  
**Answer:** The city of the LORD, The Zion of the Holy One of Israel.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:14

---

### Isaiah 60:15

**Q1. From what state has the city been, where no man went through?**  
**Answer:** Whereas thou hast been forsaken and hated.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:15

**Q2. What will the LORD make thee an eternal excellency of?**  
**Answer:** I will make thee an eternal excellency.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:15

**Q3. For how many generations shall the joy last?**  
**Answer:** A joy of many generations.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:15

---

### Isaiah 60:16

**Q1. What shall thou suck from the Gentiles?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt also suck the milk of the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:16

**Q2. What shall thou suck along with the Gentiles?**  
**Answer:** And shalt suck the breast of kings.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:16

**Q3. What shall thou know after experiencing this?**  
**Answer:** And thou shalt know that I the LORD am thy Saviour and thy Redeemer, the mighty One of Jacob.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:16

---

### Isaiah 60:17

**Q1. Instead of brass, what will be brought?**  
**Answer:** For brass I will bring gold.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:17

**Q2. Instead of iron, what will be brought?**  
**Answer:** And for iron I will bring silver.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:17

**Q3. What shall God make thine officers and exactors?**  
**Answer:** I will also make thy officers peace, and thine exactors righteousness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:17

---

### Isaiah 60:18

**Q1. What kind of violence shall no more be heard in thy land?**  
**Answer:** Violence shall no more be heard in thy land.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:18

**Q2. What other negative things shall not be heard within thy borders?**  
**Answer:** Wasting nor destruction within thy borders.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:18

**Q3. What shall thou call thy walls and thy gates?**  
**Answer:** But thou shalt call thy walls Salvation, and thy gates Praise.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:18

---

### Isaiah 60:19

**Q1. What shall no longer be thy light by day?**  
**Answer:** The sun shall be no more thy light by day.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:19

**Q2. What shall not give light unto thee by brightness?**  
**Answer:** Neither for brightness shall the moon give light unto thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:19

**Q3. Who shall be unto thee an everlasting light?**  
**Answer:** But the LORD shall be unto thee an everlasting light.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:19

---

### Isaiah 60:20

**Q1. When shall thy sun no more go down?**  
**Answer:** Thy sun shall no more go down.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:20

**Q2. What shall thy moon not do?**  
**Answer:** Neither shall thy moon withdraw itself.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:20

**Q3. What days shall be ended?**  
**Answer:** And the days of thy mourning shall be ended.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:20

---

### Isaiah 60:21

**Q1. What shall all thy people be?**  
**Answer:** All thy people shall be righteous.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:21

**Q2. What shall they inherit for ever?**  
**Answer:** They shall inherit the land for ever.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:21

**Q3. How is the people described as a planting of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The branch of my planting, the work of my hands, that I may be glorified.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:21

---

### Isaiah 60:22

**Q1. Into what shall a little one become?**  
**Answer:** A little one shall become a thousand.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:22

**Q2. Into what shall a small one become?**  
**Answer:** And a small one a strong nation.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:22

**Q3. Who will hasten this thing in his time?**  
**Answer:** I the LORD will hasten it in his time.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 60:22
