# Isaiah 25 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 25:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 25:1?**  
**Answer:** O Lord, thou art my God.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 25:1?**  
**Answer:** I will exalt thee, I will praise thy name.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 25:1?**  
**Answer:** for thou hast done wonderful things; thy counsels of old are faithfulness and truth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:1

---

### Isaiah 25:2
**Q4. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 25:2?**  
**Answer:** For thou hast made of a city an heap.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Isaiah 25:2?**  
**Answer:** of a defenced city a ruin.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:2

**Q6. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 25:2?**  
**Answer:** a palace of strangers to be no city; it shall never be built.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:2

---

### Isaiah 25:3
**Q7. What does the first part of Isaiah 25:3 state?**  
**Answer:** Therefore shall the strong people glorify thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:3

**Q8. What does the second part of Isaiah 25:3 declare?**  
**Answer:** the city of the terrible nations shall fear thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:3

**Q9. What is recorded in Isaiah 25:3?**  
**Answer:** Therefore shall the strong people glorify thee, the city of the terrible nations shall fear thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:3

---

### Isaiah 25:4
**Q10. According to Isaiah 25:4, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** For thou hast been a strength to the poor.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:4

**Q11. What is declared next in Isaiah 25:4?**  
**Answer:** a strength to the needy in his distress.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:4

**Q12. What further truth is given in Isaiah 25:4?**  
**Answer:** a refuge from the storm, a shadow from the heat, when the blast of the terrible ones is as a storm against the wall.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:4

---

### Isaiah 25:5
**Q13. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 25:5?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt bring down the noise of strangers, as the heat in a dry place.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:5

**Q14. What is declared next in Isaiah 25:5?**  
**Answer:** even the heat with the shadow of a cloud.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:5

**Q15. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 25:5?**  
**Answer:** the branch of the terrible ones shall be brought low.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:5

---

### Isaiah 25:6
**Q16. According to Isaiah 25:6, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And in this mountain shall the LORD of hosts make unto all people a feast of fat things.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:6

**Q17. What is declared next in Isaiah 25:6?**  
**Answer:** a feast of wines on the lees.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:6

**Q18. What further truth is given in Isaiah 25:6?**  
**Answer:** of fat things full of marrow, of wines on the lees well refined.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:6

---

### Isaiah 25:7
**Q19. What does the first part of Isaiah 25:7 state?**  
**Answer:** And he will destroy in this mountain the face of the covering cast over all people.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:7

**Q20. What does the second part of Isaiah 25:7 declare?**  
**Answer:** and the vail that is spread over all nations.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:7

**Q21. What is recorded in Isaiah 25:7?**  
**Answer:** And he will destroy in this mountain the face of the covering cast over all people, and the vail that is spread over all nations.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:7

---

### Isaiah 25:8
**Q22. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 25:8?**  
**Answer:** He will swallow up death in victory.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:8

**Q23. What is declared next in Isaiah 25:8?**  
**Answer:** and the Lord GOD will wipe away tears from off all faces.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:8

**Q24. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 25:8?**  
**Answer:** and the rebuke of his people shall he take away from off all the earth; for the LORD hath spoken it.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:8

---

### Isaiah 25:9
**Q25. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 25:9?**  
**Answer:** And it shall be said in that day, Lo, this is our God.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:9

**Q26. What is declared next in Isaiah 25:9?**  
**Answer:** we have waited for him, and he will save us.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:9

**Q27. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 25:9?**  
**Answer:** this is the LORD; we have waited for him, we will be glad and rejoice in his salvation.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:9

---

### Isaiah 25:10
**Q28. According to Isaiah 25:10, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** For in this mountain shall the hand of the LORD rest.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:10

**Q29. What is declared next in Isaiah 25:10?**  
**Answer:** and Moab shall be trodden down under him.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:10

**Q30. What further truth is given in Isaiah 25:10?**  
**Answer:** even as straw is trodden down for the dunghill.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:10

---

### Isaiah 25:11
**Q31. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 25:11?**  
**Answer:** And he shall spread forth his hands in the midst of them, as he that swimmeth spreadeth forth his hands to swim.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:11

**Q32. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 25:11?**  
**Answer:** and he shall bring down their pride together with the spoils of their hands.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:11

**Q33. According to Isaiah 25:11, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And he shall spread forth his hands in the midst of them, as he that swimmeth spreadeth forth his hands to swim: and he shall bring down their pride together with the spoils of their hands.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:11

---

### Isaiah 25:12
**Q34. According to Isaiah 25:12, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And the fortress of the high fort of thy walls shall he bring down.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:12

**Q35. What is declared next in Isaiah 25:12?**  
**Answer:** lay low.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:12

**Q36. What further truth is given in Isaiah 25:12?**  
**Answer:** and bring to the ground, even to the dust.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 25:12
