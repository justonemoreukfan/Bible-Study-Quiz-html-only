# Isaiah 15 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 15:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 15:1?**  
**Answer:** The burden of Moab. Because in the night Ar of Moab is laid waste, and brought to silence.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 15:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 15:1?**  
**Answer:** because in the night Kir of Moab is laid waste, and brought to silence.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 15:1

**Q3. According to Isaiah 15:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** The burden of Moab. Because in the night Ar of Moab is laid waste, and brought to silence; because in the night Kir of Moab is laid waste, and brought to silence;.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 15:1

---

### Isaiah 15:2
**Q4. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 15:2?**  
**Answer:** He is gone up to Bajith, and to Dibon, the high places, to weep.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 15:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Isaiah 15:2?**  
**Answer:** Moab shall howl over Nebo, and over Medeba.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 15:2

**Q6. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 15:2?**  
**Answer:** on all their heads shall be baldness, and every beard cut off.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 15:2

### Isaiah 15:3
**Q7. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 15:3?**  
**Answer:** In their streets they shall gird themselves with sackcloth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 15:3

**Q8. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 15:3?**  
**Answer:** on the tops of their houses, and in their streets, every one shall howl, weeping abundantly.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 15:3

**Q9. According to Isaiah 15:3, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** In their streets they shall gird themselves with sackcloth: on the tops of their houses, and in their streets, every one shall howl, weeping abundantly.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 15:3

### Isaiah 15:4
**Q10. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 15:4?**  
**Answer:** And Heshbon shall cry, and Elealeh.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 15:4

**Q11. What is declared next in Isaiah 15:4?**  
**Answer:** their voice shall be heard even unto Jahaz.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 15:4

**Q12. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 15:4?**  
**Answer:** therefore the armed soldiers of Moab shall cry out; his life shall be grievous unto him.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 15:4

### Isaiah 15:5
**Q13. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 15:5?**  
**Answer:** My heart shall cry out for Moab.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 15:5

**Q14. What is declared next in Isaiah 15:5?**  
**Answer:** his fugitives shall flee unto Zoar, an heifer of three years old.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 15:5

**Q15. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 15:5?**  
**Answer:** for by the mounting up of Luhith with weeping shall they go it up; for in the way of Horonaim they shall raise up a cry of destruction.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 15:5
