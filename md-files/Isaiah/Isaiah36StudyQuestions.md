# Isaiah 36 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 36:1
**Q1. According to Isaiah 36:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Now it came to pass in the fourteenth year of king Hezekiah.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 36:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 36:1?**  
**Answer:** that Sennacherib king of Assyria came up against all the defenced cities of Judah.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 36:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Isaiah 36:1?**  
**Answer:** and took them.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 36:1
