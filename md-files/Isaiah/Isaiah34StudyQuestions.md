# Isaiah 34 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 34:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 34:1?**  
**Answer:** Come near, ye nations, to hear.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 34:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 34:1?**  
**Answer:** and hearken, ye people.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 34:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 34:1?**  
**Answer:** let the earth hear, and all that is therein; the world, and all things that come forth of it.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 34:1

---

### Isaiah 34:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 34:2?**  
**Answer:** For the indignation of the LORD is upon all nations, and his fury upon all their armies.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 34:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 34:2?**  
**Answer:** he hath utterly destroyed them, he hath delivered them to the slaughter.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 34:2

**Q6. According to Isaiah 34:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** For the indignation of the LORD is upon all nations, and his fury upon all their armies: he hath utterly destroyed them, he hath delivered them to the slaughter.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 34:2
