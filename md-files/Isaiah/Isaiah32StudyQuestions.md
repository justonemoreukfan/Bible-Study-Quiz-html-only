# Isaiah 32 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 32:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Isaiah 32:1 state?**  
**Answer:** a king shall reign in righteousness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 32:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Isaiah 32:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** and princes shall rule in judgment.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 32:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Isaiah 32:1?**  
**Answer:** Behold, a king shall reign in righteousness, and princes shall rule in judgment.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 32:1

---

### Isaiah 32:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 32:2?**  
**Answer:** And a man shall be as an hiding place from the wind, and a covert from the tempest.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 32:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 32:2?**  
**Answer:** as rivers of water in a dry place, as the shadow of a great rock in a weary land.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 32:2

**Q6. According to Isaiah 32:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And a man shall be as an hiding place from the wind, and a covert from the tempest; as rivers of water in a dry place, as the shadow of a great rock in a weary land.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 32:2
