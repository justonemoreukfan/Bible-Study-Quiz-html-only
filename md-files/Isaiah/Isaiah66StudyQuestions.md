# Isaiah 66 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 66:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 66:1?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD, The heaven is my throne, and the earth is my footstool.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 66:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 66:1?**  
**Answer:** where is the house that ye build unto me? and where is the place of my rest?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 66:1

**Q3. According to Isaiah 66:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD, The heaven is my throne, and the earth is my footstool: where is the house that ye build unto me? and where is the place of my rest?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 66:1
