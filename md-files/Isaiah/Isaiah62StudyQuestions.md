# Isaiah 62 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 62:1

**Q1. For whose sake will the speaker not hold his peace?**  
**Answer:** For Zion's sake.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:1

**Q2. For whose sake will the speaker not rest?**  
**Answer:** For Jerusalem's sake.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:1

**Q3. Until what point will the righteousness thereof go forth?**  
**Answer:** Until the righteousness thereof go forth as brightness, and the salvation thereof as a lamp that burneth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:1

---

### Isaiah 62:2

**Q4. What shall the Gentiles see?**  
**Answer:** Thy righteousness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:2

**Q5. What shall all kings see?**  
**Answer:** Thy glory.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:2

**Q6. By what shall thou be called?**  
**Answer:** By a new name, which the mouth of the LORD shall name.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:2

---

### Isaiah 62:3

**Q7. In whose hand shall thou also be a crown of glory?**  
**Answer:** In the hand of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:3

**Q8. In whose hand shall thou be a royal diadem?**  
**Answer:** In the hand of thy God.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:3

**Q9. What shall thou also be in the hand of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** A crown of glory.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:3

---

### Isaiah 62:4

**Q10. What shall thou no more be termed?**  
**Answer:** Forsaken.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:4

**Q11. What shall thy land no more be termed?**  
**Answer:** Desolate.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:4

**Q12. What shall thou be called, and thy land?**  
**Answer:** Hephzibah, and thy land Beulah.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:4

---

### Isaiah 62:5

**Q13. As a young man marrieth a virgin, who shall marry thee?**  
**Answer:** Thy sons.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:5

**Q14. As the bridegroom rejoiceth over the bride, over whom shall thy God rejoice?**  
**Answer:** Over thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:5

**Q15. Who shall marry thee as a young man marrieth a virgin?**  
**Answer:** Thy sons.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:5

---

### Isaiah 62:6

**Q16. Upon what places have I set watchmen?**  
**Answer:** Upon thy walls, O Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:6

**Q17. When shall the watchmen never hold their peace?**  
**Answer:** All day nor all night.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:6

**Q18. Who are commanded to make mention of the LORD and not keep silence?**  
**Answer:** Ye that make mention of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:6

---

### Isaiah 62:7

**Q19. Give him no rest until what happens?**  
**Answer:** Until he establish, and until he make Jerusalem a praise in the earth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:7

**Q20. What is Jerusalem to become in the earth?**  
**Answer:** A praise.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:7

**Q21. What are the watchmen told not to give him?**  
**Answer:** Rest.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:7

---

### Isaiah 62:8

**Q22. By what has the LORD sworn?**  
**Answer:** By his right hand, and by the arm of his strength.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:8

**Q23. What will the LORD no more give to be meat for thine enemies?**  
**Answer:** Thy corn.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:8

**Q24. Whose sons shall no more drink thy wine?**  
**Answer:** The sons of the stranger.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:8

---

### Isaiah 62:9

**Q25. Who shall eat the corn and praise the LORD?**  
**Answer:** They that have gathered it.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:9

**Q26. Who shall drink the wine?**  
**Answer:** They that have gathered it.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:9

**Q27. Where shall they drink the wine?**  
**Answer:** In the courts of my holiness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:9

---

### Isaiah 62:10

**Q28. Through what are the people commanded to go through?**  
**Answer:** Through the gates.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:10

**Q29. What are the people told to prepare?**  
**Answer:** The way of the people.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:10

**Q30. What are the people told to lift up for the people?**  
**Answer:** An standard.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:10

---

### Isaiah 62:11

**Q31. Unto what end of the world has the LORD proclaimed?**  
**Answer:** Behold, the LORD hath proclaimed unto the end of the world.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:11

**Q32. To whom is the message said: "Behold, thy salvation cometh"?**  
**Answer:** To the daughter of Zion.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:11

**Q33. What is with him, and his work before him?**  
**Answer:** His reward.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:11

---

### Isaiah 62:12

**Q34. What shall they call them?**  
**Answer:** The holy people, The redeemed of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:12

**Q35. What shall thou be called?**  
**Answer:** Sought out, A city not forsaken.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:12

**Q36. Who shall call them the holy people?**  
**Answer:** They.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 62:12
