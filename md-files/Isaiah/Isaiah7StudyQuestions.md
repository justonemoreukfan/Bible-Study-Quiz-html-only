### Isaiah 7:1
**Q1. According to Isaiah 7:1, what is stated first?**

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 7:1?**  
**Answer:** the son of Uzziah.

**Answer:** king of Judah, that Rezin the king of Syria, and Pekah the son of Remaliah, king of Israel, went up toward Jerusalem to war against it, but could not prevail against it.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 7:1
