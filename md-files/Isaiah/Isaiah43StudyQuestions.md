# Isaiah 43 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 43:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 43:1?**  
**Answer:** But now thus saith the LORD that created thee, O Jacob, and he that formed thee, O Israel, Fear not.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 43:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 43:1?**  
**Answer:** for I have redeemed thee, I have called thee by thy name.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 43:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 43:1?**  
**Answer:** thou art mine.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 43:1
