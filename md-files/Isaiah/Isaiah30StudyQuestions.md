### Isaiah 30:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 30:1?**  
**Answer:** Woe to the rebellious children, saith the LORD, that take counsel, but not of me.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 30:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 30:1?**  
**Answer:** and that cover with a covering, but not of my spirit, that they may add sin to sin.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 30:1

**Q3. According to Isaiah 30:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Woe to the rebellious children, saith the LORD, that take counsel, but not of me; and that cover with a covering, but not of my spirit, that they may add sin to sin:.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 30:1
