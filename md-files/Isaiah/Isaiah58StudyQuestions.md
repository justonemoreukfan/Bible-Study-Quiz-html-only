# Isaiah 58 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 58:1

**Q1. What are we commanded to cry aloud and not to spare?**  
**Answer:** Cry aloud, spare not, lift up thy voice like a trumpet.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:1

**Q2. Whose transgressions are we to show unto them?**  
**Answer:** My people.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:1

**Q3. Whose sins are we to show unto the house of Jacob?**  
**Answer:** Their sins.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:1

---

### Isaiah 58:2

**Q1. Who seek God daily and delight to know His ways?**  
**Answer:** They seek me daily, and delight to know my ways.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:2

**Q2. What kind of nation do they appear to be that did not forsake the ordinance of their God?**  
**Answer:** As a nation that did righteousness, and forsook not the ordinance of their God.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:2

**Q3. What do they ask of God and take delight in?**  
**Answer:** They ask of me the ordinances of justice; they take delight in approaching to God.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:2

---

### Isaiah 58:3

**Q1. What do the people ask why God has not seen when they have fasted?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore have we fasted, say they, and thou seest not?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:3

**Q2. What do they ask why God has not taken knowledge of when they have afflicted their soul?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore have we afflicted our soul, and thou takest no knowledge?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:3

**Q3. What do they find in the day of their fast?**  
**Answer:** Behold, in the day of your fast ye find pleasure, and exact all your labours.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:3

---

### Isaiah 58:4

**Q1. For what do the people fast?**  
**Answer:** For strife and debate, and to smite with the fist of wickedness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:4

**Q2. Why should they not fast as they do this day?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall not fast as ye do this day, to make your voice to be heard on high.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:4

**Q3. What kind of fast is described as a day for a man to afflict his soul?**  
**Answer:** Is it such a fast that I have chosen? a day for a man to afflict his soul?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:4

---

### Isaiah 58:5

**Q1. What is described as bowing down an head as a bulrush?**  
**Answer:** Is it to bow down his head as a bulrush, and to spread sackcloth and ashes under him?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:5

**Q2. What will the people call this, and an acceptable day to the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Wilt thou call this a fast, and an acceptable day to the Lord?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:5

**Q3. What are spread under him in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Sackcloth and ashes.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:5

---

### Isaiah 58:6

**Q1. What is the fast that God has chosen regarding wickedness?**  
**Answer:** To loose the bands of wickedness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:6

**Q2. What are we told to undo regarding heavy burdens?**  
**Answer:** Undo the heavy burdens.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:6

**Q3. What are we to let go free, and what are we to break?**  
**Answer:** Let the oppressed go free, and that ye break every yoke.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:6

---

### Isaiah 58:7

**Q1. What are we instructed to do with our bread to the hungry?**  
**Answer:** Deal thy bread to the hungry.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:7

**Q2. Whom are we to bring to our house when they are cast out?**  
**Answer:** The poor that are cast out.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:7

**Q3. What must we do when we see the naked?**  
**Answer:** Cover him.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:7

---

### Isaiah 58:8

**Q1. What shall break forth as the morning when we do these things?**  
**Answer:** Then shall thy light break forth as the morning.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:8

**Q2. What shall spring forth speedily?**  
**Answer:** Thine health shall spring forth speedily.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:8

**Q3. What shall go before thee, and what shall be thy rereward?**  
**Answer:** Thy righteousness shall go before thee; the glory of the Lord shall be thy rereward.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:8

---

### Isaiah 58:9

**Q1. Who shall answer when thou shalt call?**  
**Answer:** Then shalt thou call, and the Lord shall answer.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:9

**Q2. What shall God say when thou criest?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt cry, and he shall say, Here I am.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:9

**Q3. What two things must we take away from the midst of us?**  
**Answer:** The yoke from the midst of thee, the putting forth of the finger, and speaking vanity.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:9

---

### Isaiah 58:10

**Q1. To whom must thou draw out thy soul?**  
**Answer:** To the hungry.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:10

**Q2. Whom must thou satisfy?**  
**Answer:** The afflicted soul.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:10

**Q3. When shall thy light rise in obscurity, and thy darkness be as the noonday?**  
**Answer:** Then shall thy light rise in obscurity, and thy darkness be as the noonday.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:10

---

### Isaiah 58:11

**Q1. Who shall guide thee continually?**  
**Answer:** The Lord shall guide thee continually.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:11

**Q2. What shall God satisfy in drought?**  
**Answer:** Thy soul in drought.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:11

**Q3. What shall God make fat?**  
**Answer:** Thy bones.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:11

---

### Isaiah 58:12

**Q1. What shall they that be of thee build?**  
**Answer:** The old waste places.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:12

**Q2. What shall thou raise up?**  
**Answer:** The foundations of many generations.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:12

**Q3. What shall thou be called?**  
**Answer:** The repairer of the breach, The restorer of paths to dwell in.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:12

---

### Isaiah 58:13

**Q1. What must thou turn away from regarding thy foot?**  
**Answer:** If thou turn away thy foot from the sabbath.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:13

**Q2. What must thou not do on God's holy day?**  
**Answer:** From doing thy pleasure on my holy day.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:13

**Q3. How must one call the sabbath and the holy of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Call the sabbath a delight, the holy of the Lord, honourable.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:13

---

### Isaiah 58:14

**Q1. In whom then shalt thou delight thyself?**  
**Answer:** Then shalt thou delight thyself in the Lord.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:14

**Q2. Upon what places of the earth will God cause thee to ride?**  
**Answer:** Upon the high places of the earth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:14

**Q3. With whose heritage will God feed thee?**  
**Answer:** With the heritage of Jacob thy father.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 58:14
