# Isaiah 63 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 63:1
**Q1. According to Isaiah 63:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Who is this that cometh from Edom.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 63:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 63:1?**  
**Answer:** with dyed garments from Bozrah? this that is glorious in his apparel.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 63:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Isaiah 63:1?**  
**Answer:** travelling in the greatness of his strength? I that speak in righteousness, mighty to save.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 63:1

---

### Isaiah 63:2
**Q4. What does the first part of Isaiah 63:2 state?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore art thou red in thine apparel.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 63:2

**Q5. What does the second part of Isaiah 63:2 declare?**  
**Answer:** and thy garments like him that treadeth in the winefat?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 63:2

**Q6. What is recorded in Isaiah 63:2?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore art thou red in thine apparel, and thy garments like him that treadeth in the winefat?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 63:2
