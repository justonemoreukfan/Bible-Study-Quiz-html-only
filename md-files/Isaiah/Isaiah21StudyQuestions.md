# Isaiah 21 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 21:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 21:1?**  
**Answer:** The burden of the desert of the sea. As whirlwinds in the south pass through.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 21:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 21:1?**  
**Answer:** so it cometh from the desert, from a terrible land.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 21:1

**Q3. According to Isaiah 21:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** The burden of the desert of the sea. As whirlwinds in the south pass through; so it cometh from the desert, from a terrible land.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 21:1
