# Isaiah 55 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 55:1

**Q1. Who is invited to come to the waters?**  
**Answer:** Every one that thirsteth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:1

**Q2. What are those without money told to do?**  
**Answer:** Come, buy, and eat.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:1

**Q3. What can be bought without money and without price?**  
**Answer:** Wine and milk.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:1

---

### Isaiah 55:2

**Q1. What is spent for that which is not bread?**  
**Answer:** Money.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:2

**Q2. What is labored for that which satisfieth not?**  
**Answer:** Your labour.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:2

**Q3. What kind of food should be eaten so that the soul may delight itself in fatness?**  
**Answer:** That which is good.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:2

---

### Isaiah 55:3

**Q1. Where should one incline their ear and come?**  
**Answer:** Unto me.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:3

**Q2. What will happen to your soul if you hear?**  
**Answer:** Your soul shall live.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:3

**Q3. What kind of covenant will be made with you?**  
**Answer:** An everlasting covenant, even the sure mercies of David.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:3

---

### Isaiah 55:4

**Q1. Who has been given for a witness to the people?**  
**Answer:** Him (David/a witness).  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:4

**Q2. What two roles regarding the people are mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Leader and commander.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:4

---

### Isaiah 55:5

**Q1. What nation will be called that is not known?**  
**Answer:** A nation that thou knowest not.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:5

**Q2. What shall nations that did not know thee do?**  
**Answer:** Shall run unto thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:5

**Q3. For whose sake will the nations run unto thee?**  
**Answer:** Because of the LORD thy God, and for the Holy One of Israel; for he hath glorified thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:5

---

### Isaiah 55:6

**Q1. While whom may the LORD be found?**  
**Answer:** While he may be found.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:6

**Q2. What action should be taken while the LORD is near?**  
**Answer:** Call ye upon him.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:6

---

### Isaiah 55:7

**Q1. What should the wicked forsake?**  
**Answer:** His way.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:7

**Q2. What should the unrighteous man forsake?**  
**Answer:** His thoughts.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:7

**Q3. To whom should the unrighteous man return for abundant pardon?**  
**Answer:** Unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:7

---

### Isaiah 55:8

**Q1. Whose thoughts are declared not to be your thoughts?**  
**Answer:** The LORD's thoughts.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:8

**Q2. Whose ways are declared not to be the LORD's ways?**  
**Answer:** Your ways.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:8

---

### Isaiah 55:9

**Q1. What is higher than the earth according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** The heavens.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:9

**Q2. What are higher than your ways?**  
**Answer:** My ways.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:9

**Q3. What are higher than your thoughts?**  
**Answer:** My thoughts.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:9

---

### Isaiah 55:10

**Q1. What comes down from heaven?**  
**Answer:** The rain and the snow.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:10

---

### Isaiah 55:11

**Q1. What shall my word be that goeth forth out of my mouth?**  
**Answer:** It shall not return unto me void.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:11

**Q2. What shall the word accomplish?**  
**Answer:** That which I please.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:11

**Q3. In what thing shall the word prosper?**  
**Answer:** In the thing whereto I sent it.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:11

---

### Isaiah 55:12

**Q1. With what shall ye go out?**  
**Answer:** With joy.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:12

**Q2. How shall ye be led forth?**  
**Answer:** With peace.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:12

**Q3. What bodies of nature shall break forth before you into singing?**  
**Answer:** The mountains and the hills.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:12

---

### Isaiah 55:13

**Q1. What shall come up instead of the thorn?**  
**Answer:** The fir tree.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:13

**Q2. What shall come up instead of the brier?**  
**Answer:** The myrtle tree.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:13

**Q3. What shall the event be to the LORD for a name?**  
**Answer:** It shall be to the LORD for a name, for an everlasting sign that shall not be cut off.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 55:13
