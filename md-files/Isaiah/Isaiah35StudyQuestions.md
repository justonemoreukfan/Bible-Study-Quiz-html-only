# Isaiah 35 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 35:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 35:1?**  
**Answer:** The wilderness and the solitary place shall be glad for them.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 35:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 35:1?**  
**Answer:** and the desert shall rejoice, and blossom as the rose.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 35:1

**Q3. According to Isaiah 35:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** The wilderness and the solitary place shall be glad for them; and the desert shall rejoice, and blossom as the rose.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 35:1

---
