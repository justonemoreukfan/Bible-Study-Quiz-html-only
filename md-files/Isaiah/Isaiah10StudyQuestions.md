# Isaiah 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 10:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Isaiah 10:1 state?**  
**Answer:** Woe unto them that decree unrighteous decrees.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 10:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Isaiah 10:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** and that write grievousness which they have prescribed.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 10:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Isaiah 10:1?**  
**Answer:** Woe unto them that decree unrighteous decrees, and that write grievousness which they have prescribed;.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 10:1

---

### Isaiah 10:2
**Q4. According to Isaiah 10:2, what is stated first?**
