# Isaiah 23 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 23:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 23:1?**  
**Answer:** The burden of Tyre. Howl, ye ships of Tarshish.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 23:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 23:1?**  
**Answer:** for it is laid waste, so that there is no house, no entering in.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 23:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 23:1?**  
**Answer:** from the land of Chittim it is revealed to them.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 23:1

---

### Isaiah 23:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 23:2?**  
**Answer:** Be still, ye inhabitants of the isle.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 23:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 23:2?**  
**Answer:** thou whom the merchants of Zidon, that pass over the sea, have replenished.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 23:2

**Q6. According to Isaiah 23:2, what is the full truth recorded?**
