# Isaiah 18 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 18:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Isaiah 18:1 state?**  
**Answer:** Woe to the land shadowing with wings.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Isaiah 18:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** which is beyond the rivers of Ethiopia.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Isaiah 18:1?**  
**Answer:** Woe to the land shadowing with wings, which is beyond the rivers of Ethiopia:.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:1

---

### Isaiah 18:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 18:2?**  
**Answer:** That sendeth ambassadors by the sea, even in vessels of bulrushes upon the waters, saying, Go, ye swift messengers, to a nation scattered and peeled, to a people terrible from their beginning hitherto.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 18:2?**  
**Answer:** a nation meted out and trodden down, whose land the rivers have spoiled!  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:2

**Q6. According to Isaiah 18:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** That sendeth ambassadors by the sea, even in vessels of bulrushes upon the waters, saying, Go, ye swift messengers, to a nation scattered and peeled, to a people terrible from their beginning hitherto; a nation meted out and trodden down, whose land the rivers have spoiled!  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:2

### Isaiah 18:3
**Q7. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 18:3?**  
**Answer:** All ye inhabitants of the world, and dwellers on the earth, see ye, when he lifteth up an ensign on the mountains.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:3

**Q8. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 18:3?**  
**Answer:** and when he bloweth a trumpet, hear ye.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:3

**Q9. According to Isaiah 18:3, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** All ye inhabitants of the world, and dwellers on the earth, see ye, when he lifteth up an ensign on the mountains; and when he bloweth a trumpet, hear ye.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:3

### Isaiah 18:4
**Q10. According to Isaiah 18:4, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** For so the LORD said unto me.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:4

**Q11. What is declared next in Isaiah 18:4?**  
**Answer:** I will take my rest.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:4

**Q12. What further truth is given in Isaiah 18:4?**  
**Answer:** and I will consider in my dwelling place like a clear heat upon herbs, and like a cloud of dew in the heat of harvest.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:4

---

### Isaiah 18:5
**Q13. According to Isaiah 18:5, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** For afore the harvest.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:5

**Q14. What is declared next in Isaiah 18:5?**  
**Answer:** when the bud is perfect.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:5

**Q15. What further truth is given in Isaiah 18:5?**  
**Answer:** and the sour grape is ripening in the flower, he shall both cut off the sprigs with pruning hooks, and take away and cut down the branches.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:5

### Isaiah 18:6
**Q16. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 18:6?**  
**Answer:** They shall be left together unto the fowls of the mountains, and to the beasts of the earth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:6

**Q17. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 18:6?**  
**Answer:** and the fowls shall summer upon them, and all the beasts of the earth shall winter upon them.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:6

**Q18. According to Isaiah 18:6, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** They shall be left together unto the fowls of the mountains, and to the beasts of the earth: and the fowls shall summer upon them, and all the beasts of the earth shall winter upon them.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:6

---

### Isaiah 18:7
**Q19. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 18:7?**  
**Answer:** In that time shall the present be brought unto the LORD of hosts of a people scattered and peeled, and from a people terrible from their beginning hitherto.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:7

**Q20. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 18:7?**  
**Answer:** a nation meted out and trodden under foot, whose land the rivers have spoiled, to the place of the name of the LORD of hosts, the mount Zion.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:7

**Q21. According to Isaiah 18:7, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** In that time shall the present be brought unto the LORD of hosts of a people scattered and peeled, and from a people terrible from their beginning hitherto; a nation meted out and trodden under foot, whose land the rivers have spoiled, to the place of the name of the LORD of hosts, the mount Zion.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 18:7
