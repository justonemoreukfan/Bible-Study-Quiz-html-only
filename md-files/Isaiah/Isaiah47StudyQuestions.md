# Isaiah 47 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 47:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 47:1?**  
**Answer:** Come down, and sit in the dust, O virgin daughter of Babylon, sit on the ground.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 47:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 47:1?**  
**Answer:** there is no throne, O daughter of the Chaldeans.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 47:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 47:1?**  
**Answer:** for thou shalt no more be called tender and delicate.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 47:1

---

### Isaiah 47:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 47:2?**  
**Answer:** Take the millstones, and grind meal.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 47:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 47:2?**  
**Answer:** uncover thy locks, make bare the leg, uncover the thigh, pass over the rivers.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 47:2

**Q6. According to Isaiah 47:2, what is the full truth recorded?**

**Verse:** Isaiah 47:2
