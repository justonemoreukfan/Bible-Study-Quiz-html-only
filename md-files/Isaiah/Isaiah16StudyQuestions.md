# Isaiah 16 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 16:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Isaiah 16:1 state?**  
**Answer:** Send ye the lamb to the ruler of the land from Sela to the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Isaiah 16:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** unto the mount of the daughter of Zion.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Isaiah 16:1?**  
**Answer:** Send ye the lamb to the ruler of the land from Sela to the wilderness, unto the mount of the daughter of Zion.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:1

---

### Isaiah 16:2
**Q4. According to Isaiah 16:2, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** For it shall be.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Isaiah 16:2?**  
**Answer:** as a wandering bird cast out of the nest.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:2

**Q6. What further truth is given in Isaiah 16:2?**  
**Answer:** so the daughters of Moab shall be at the fords of Arnon.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:2

---

### Isaiah 16:3
**Q7. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 16:3?**  
**Answer:** Take counsel, execute judgment.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:3

**Q8. What is declared next in Isaiah 16:3?**  
**Answer:** make thy shadow as the night in the midst of the noonday.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:3

**Q9. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 16:3?**  
**Answer:** hide the outcasts; bewray not him that wandereth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:3

---

### Isaiah 16:4
**Q10. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 16:4?**  
**Answer:** Let mine outcasts dwell with thee, Moab.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:4

**Q11. What is declared next in Isaiah 16:4?**  
**Answer:** be thou a covert to them from the face of the spoiler.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:4

**Q12. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 16:4?**  
**Answer:** for the extortioner is at an end, the spoiler ceaseth, the oppressors are consumed out of the land.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:4

---

### Isaiah 16:5
**Q13. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 16:5?**  
**Answer:** And in mercy shall the throne be established.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:5

**Q14. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 16:5?**  
**Answer:** and he shall sit upon it in truth in the tabernacle of David, judging, and seeking judgment, and hasting righteousness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:5

**Q15. According to Isaiah 16:5, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And in mercy shall the throne be established: and he shall sit upon it in truth in the tabernacle of David, judging, and seeking judgment, and hasting righteousness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:5

### Isaiah 16:6
**Q16. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 16:6?**  
**Answer:** We have heard of the pride of Moab.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:6

**Q17. What is declared next in Isaiah 16:6?**  
**Answer:** he is very proud.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:6

**Q18. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 16:6?**

**Verse:** Isaiah 16:6

### Isaiah 16:7
**Q19. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 16:7?**  
**Answer:** Therefore shall Moab howl for Moab, every one shall howl.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:7

**Q20. What is declared next in Isaiah 16:7?**  
**Answer:** for the foundations of Kirhareseth shall ye mourn.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:7

**Q21. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 16:7?**  
**Answer:** surely they are stricken.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:7

### Isaiah 16:8
**Q22. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 16:8?**  
**Answer:** For the fields of Heshbon languish, and the vine of Sibmah.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:8

**Q23. What is declared next in Isaiah 16:8?**  
**Answer:** the lords of the heathen have broken down the principal plants thereof, they are come even unto Jazer, they wandered through the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:8

**Q24. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 16:8?**  
**Answer:** her branches are stretched out, they are gone over the sea.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:8

---

### Isaiah 16:9
**Q25. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 16:9?**  
**Answer:** Therefore I will bewail with the weeping of Jazer the vine of Sibmah.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:9

**Q26. What is declared next in Isaiah 16:9?**  
**Answer:** I will water thee with my tears, O Heshbon, and Elealeh.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:9

**Q27. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 16:9?**  
**Answer:** for the shouting for thy summer fruits and for thy harvest is fallen.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:9

---

### Isaiah 16:10
**Q28. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 16:10?**  
**Answer:** And gladness is taken away, and joy out of the plentiful field.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:10

**Q29. What is declared next in Isaiah 16:10?**  
**Answer:** and in the vineyards there shall be no singing, neither shall there be shouting.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:10

**Q30. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 16:10?**  
**Answer:** the treaders shall tread out no wine in their presses; I have made their vintage shouting to cease.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 16:10
