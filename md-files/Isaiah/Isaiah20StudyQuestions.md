# Isaiah 20 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 20:1
**Q1. According to Isaiah 20:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** In the year that Tartan came unto Ashdod.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 20:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 20:1?**  
**Answer:** (when Sargon the king of Assyria sent him).  
**Verse:** Isaiah 20:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Isaiah 20:1?**  
**Answer:** and fought against Ashdod, and took it.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 20:1

---

### Isaiah 20:2
**Q4. According to Isaiah 20:2, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** At the same time spake the LORD by Isaiah the son of Amoz.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 20:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Isaiah 20:2?**  
**Answer:** Go and loose the sackcloth from off thy loins.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 20:2

**Q6. What further truth is given in Isaiah 20:2?**  
**Answer:** and put off thy shoe from thy foot. And he did so, walking naked and barefoot.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 20:2

---

### Isaiah 20:3
**Q7. What does the first part of Isaiah 20:3 state?**  
**Answer:** And the LORD said.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 20:3

**Q8. What does the second part of Isaiah 20:3 declare?**  
**Answer:** Like as my servant Isaiah hath walked naked and barefoot three years for a sign and wonder upon Egypt and upon Ethiopia.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 20:3

**Q9. What is recorded in Isaiah 20:3?**  
**Answer:** And the LORD said, Like as my servant Isaiah hath walked naked and barefoot three years for a sign and wonder upon Egypt and upon Ethiopia;.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 20:3

---

### Isaiah 20:4
**Q10. According to Isaiah 20:4, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** So shall the king of Assyria lead away the Egyptians prisoners.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 20:4

**Q11. What is declared next in Isaiah 20:4?**  
**Answer:** and the Ethiopians captives.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 20:4

**Q12. What further truth is given in Isaiah 20:4?**  
**Answer:** young and old, naked and barefoot, even with their buttocks uncovered, to the shame of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 20:4

---

### Isaiah 20:5
**Q13. What does the first part of Isaiah 20:5 state?**  
**Answer:** And they shall be afraid and ashamed of Ethiopia their expectation.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 20:5

**Q14. What does the second part of Isaiah 20:5 declare?**  
**Answer:** and of Egypt their glory.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 20:5

**Q15. What is recorded in Isaiah 20:5?**  
**Answer:** And they shall be afraid and ashamed of Ethiopia their expectation, and of Egypt their glory.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 20:5

---

### Isaiah 20:6
**Q16. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 20:6?**  
**Answer:** And the inhabitant of this isle shall say in that day, Behold, such is our expectation, whither we flee for help to be delivered from the king of Assyria.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 20:6

**Q17. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 20:6?**  
**Answer:** and how shall we escape?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 20:6

**Q18. According to Isaiah 20:6, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And the inhabitant of this isle shall say in that day, Behold, such is our expectation, whither we flee for help to be delivered from the king of Assyria: and how shall we escape?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 20:6
