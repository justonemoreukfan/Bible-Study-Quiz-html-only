# Isaiah 48 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 48:1
**Q1. According to Isaiah 48:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Hear ye this.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 48:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 48:1?**  
**Answer:** O house of Jacob.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 48:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Isaiah 48:1?**  
**Answer:** which are called by the name of Israel, and are come forth out of the waters of Judah, which swear by the name of the LORD, and make mention of the God of Israel, but not in truth, nor in righteousness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 48:1

**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 48:2?**
