# Isaiah 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 4:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 4:1?**  
**Answer:** And in that day seven women shall take hold of one man, saying, We will eat our own bread, and wear our own apparel.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 4:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 4:1?**  
**Answer:** only let us be called by thy name, to take away our reproach.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 4:1

**Q3. According to Isaiah 4:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And in that day seven women shall take hold of one man, saying, We will eat our own bread, and wear our own apparel: only let us be called by thy name, to take away our reproach.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 4:1

---

### Isaiah 4:2
**Q4. What does the first part of Isaiah 4:2 state?**  
**Answer:** In that day shall the branch of the LORD be beautiful and glorious.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 4:2

**Q5. What does the second part of Isaiah 4:2 declare?**  
**Answer:** and the fruit of the earth shall be excellent and comely for them that are escaped of Israel.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 4:2

**Q6. What is recorded in Isaiah 4:2?**  
**Answer:** In that day shall the branch of the LORD be beautiful and glorious, and the fruit of the earth shall be excellent and comely for them that are escaped of Israel.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 4:2

---

### Isaiah 4:3
**Q7. According to Isaiah 4:3, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And it shall come to pass.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 4:3

**Q8. What is declared next in Isaiah 4:3?**  
**Answer:** that he that is left in Zion.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 4:3

**Q9. What further truth is given in Isaiah 4:3?**  
**Answer:** and he that remaineth in Jerusalem, shall be called holy, even every one that is written among the living in Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 4:3

---

### Isaiah 4:4
**Q10. According to Isaiah 4:4, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** When the Lord shall have washed away the filth of the daughters of Zion.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 4:4

**Q11. What is declared next in Isaiah 4:4?**  
**Answer:** and shall have purged the blood of Jerusalem from the midst thereof by the spirit of judgment.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 4:4

**Q12. What further truth is given in Isaiah 4:4?**  
**Answer:** and by the spirit of burning.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 4:4

---

### Isaiah 4:5
**Q13. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 4:5?**  
**Answer:** And the LORD will create upon every dwelling place of mount Zion, and upon her assemblies, a cloud and smoke by day, and the shining of a flaming fire by night.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 4:5

**Q14. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 4:5?**  
**Answer:** for upon all the glory shall be a defence.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 4:5

**Q15. According to Isaiah 4:5, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And the LORD will create upon every dwelling place of mount Zion, and upon her assemblies, a cloud and smoke by day, and the shining of a flaming fire by night: for upon all the glory shall be a defence.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 4:5

---

### Isaiah 4:6
**Q16. According to Isaiah 4:6, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And there shall be a tabernacle for a shadow in the day time from the heat.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 4:6

**Q17. What is declared next in Isaiah 4:6?**  
**Answer:** and for a place of refuge.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 4:6

**Q18. What further truth is given in Isaiah 4:6?**  
**Answer:** and for a covert from storm and from rain.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 4:6
