### Isaiah 28:1
**Q1. According to Isaiah 28:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Woe to the crown of pride.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 28:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 28:1?**  
**Answer:** to the drunkards of Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 28:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Isaiah 28:1?**  
**Answer:** whose glorious beauty is a fading flower, which are on the head of the fat valleys of them that are overcome with wine!  
**Verse:** Isaiah 28:1
