# Isaiah 42 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 42:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 42:1?**  
**Answer:** Behold my servant, whom I uphold.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 42:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 42:1?**  
**Answer:** mine elect, in whom my soul delighteth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 42:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 42:1?**  
**Answer:** I have put my spirit upon him; he shall bring forth judgment to the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 42:1

---
