# Isaiah 53 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 53:1

**Q1. Who hath believed our report?**  
**Answer:** Our report.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:1

**Q2. To whom is what revealed?**  
**Answer:** The arm of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:1

**Q3. To whom is the arm of the LORD revealed?**  
**Answer:** To whom is the arm of the LORD revealed?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:1

---

### Isaiah 53:2

**Q4. For he shall grow up before him as what?**  
**Answer:** A tender plant.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:2

**Q5. He shall grow up as what out of a dry ground?**  
**Answer:** A root.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:2

**Q6. What does he have no form or comeliness regarding?**  
**Answer:** When we shall see him.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:2

---

### Isaiah 53:3

**Q7. What is he described as being?**  
**Answer:** Despised and rejected of men.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:3

**Q8. What kind of man is he described as being?**  
**Answer:** A man of sorrows, and acquainted with grief.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:3

**Q9. What did we do as it were our faces from him?**  
**Answer:** We hid as it were our faces from him.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:3

---

### Isaiah 53:4

**Q10. Whose griefs hath he borne?**  
**Answer:** Our griefs.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:4

**Q11. Whose sorrows did he carry?**  
**Answer:** Our sorrows.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:4

**Q12. How did we did esteem him?**  
**Answer:** Stricken, smitten of God, and afflicted.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:4

---

### Isaiah 53:5

**Q13. For what was he wounded?**  
**Answer:** For our transgressions.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:5

**Q14. For what was he bruised?**  
**Answer:** For our iniquities.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:5

**Q15. By whose stripes are we healed?**  
**Answer:** With his stripes.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:5

---

### Isaiah 53:6

**Q16. Like what have all we gone astray?**  
**Answer:** Like sheep.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:6

**Q17. What have we turned every one to?**  
**Answer:** His own way.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:6

**Q18. What hath the LORD laid on him?**  
**Answer:** The iniquity of us all.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:6

---

### Isaiah 53:7

**Q19. How was he oppressed and afflicted?**  
**Answer:** He was oppressed, and he was afflicted.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:7

**Q20. What did he do regarding the opening of his mouth?**  
**Answer:** He opened not his mouth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:7

**Q21. As what is he brought to the slaughter?**  
**Answer:** As a lamb.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:7

---

### Isaiah 53:8

**Q22. From what was he taken?**  
**Answer:** From prison and from judgment.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:8

**Q23. Who shall declare his generation?**  
**Answer:** Who shall declare his generation?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:8

**Q24. For the transgression of whom was he stricken?**  
**Answer:** For the transgression of my people.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:8

---

### Isaiah 53:9

**Q25. With whom was his grave made?**  
**Answer:** With the wicked.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:9

**Q26. With whom was he in his death?**  
**Answer:** With the rich.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:9

**Q27. Because he had done what?**  
**Answer:** No violence.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:9

---

### Isaiah 53:10

**Q28. What did it please the LORD to do to him?**  
**Answer:** To bruise him.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:10

**Q29. When thou shalt make his soul an offering for sin, what shall he see?**  
**Answer:** His seed.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:10

**Q30. What shall prosper in his hand?**  
**Answer:** The pleasure of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:10

---

### Isaiah 53:11

**Q31. Of what shall he see and be satisfied?**  
**Answer:** Of the travail of his soul.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:11

**Q32. By his knowledge shall whom justify many?**  
**Answer:** My righteous servant.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:11

**Q33. What shall he bear concerning them?**  
**Answer:** Their iniquities.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:11

---

### Isaiah 53:12

**Q34. Therefore will I divide him a portion with whom?**  
**Answer:** The great.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:12

**Q35. With whom shall he divide the spoil?**  
**Answer:** The strong.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:12

**Q36. He bare the sin of whom?**  
**Answer:** Many.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 53:12
