# Isaiah 44 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 44:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 44:1?**  
**Answer:** Yet now hear, O Jacob my servant.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 44:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 44:1?**  
**Answer:** and Israel, whom I have chosen.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 44:1

**Q3. According to Isaiah 44:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Yet now hear, O Jacob my servant; and Israel, whom I have chosen:.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 44:1

---
