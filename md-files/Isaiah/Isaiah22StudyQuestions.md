# Isaiah 22 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 22:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Isaiah 22:1 state?**  
**Answer:** The burden of the valley of vision. What aileth thee now.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 22:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Isaiah 22:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** that thou art wholly gone up to the housetops?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 22:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Isaiah 22:1?**  
**Answer:** The burden of the valley of vision. What aileth thee now, that thou art wholly gone up to the housetops?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 22:1
