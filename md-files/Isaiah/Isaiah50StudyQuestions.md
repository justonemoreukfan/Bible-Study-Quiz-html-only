# Isaiah 50 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 50:1
**Q1. According to Isaiah 50:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 50:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 50:1?**  
**Answer:** Where is the bill of your mother’s divorcement.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 50:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Isaiah 50:1?**  
**Answer:** whom I have put away? or which of my creditors is it to whom I have sold you? Behold, for your iniquities have ye sold yourselves, and for your transgressions is your mother put away.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 50:1

---

### Isaiah 50:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 50:2?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore, when I came, was there no man? when I called, was there none to answer? Is my hand shortened at all, that it cannot redeem? or have I no power to deliver? behold, at my rebuke I dry up the sea, I make the rivers a wilderness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 50:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 50:2?**  
**Answer:** their fish stinketh, because there is no water, and dieth for thirst.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 50:2

**Q6. According to Isaiah 50:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore, when I came, was there no man? when I called, was there none to answer? Is my hand shortened at all, that it cannot redeem? or have I no power to deliver? behold, at my rebuke I dry up the sea, I make the rivers a wilderness: their fish stinketh, because there is no water, and dieth for thirst.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 50:2

**Verse:** Isaiah 50:4

**Q11. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 50:4?**

**Verse:** Isaiah 50:4

**Q12. According to Isaiah 50:4, what is the full truth recorded?**
