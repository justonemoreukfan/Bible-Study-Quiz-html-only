# Isaiah 29 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 29:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 29:1?**  
**Answer:** Woe to Ariel, to Ariel, the city where David dwelt! add ye year to year.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 29:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 29:1?**  
**Answer:** let them kill sacrifices.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 29:1

**Q3. According to Isaiah 29:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Woe to Ariel, to Ariel, the city where David dwelt! add ye year to year; let them kill sacrifices.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 29:1
