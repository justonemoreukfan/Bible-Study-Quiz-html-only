# Isaiah 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 9:1
**Q1. According to Isaiah 9:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Nevertheless the dimness shall not be such as was in her vexation.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 9:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 9:1?**  
**Answer:** when at the first he lightly afflicted the land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 9:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Isaiah 9:1?**  
**Answer:** and afterward did more grievously afflict her by the way of the sea, beyond Jordan, in Galilee of the nations.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 9:1

---

### Isaiah 9:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 9:2?**  
**Answer:** The people that walked in darkness have seen a great light.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 9:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 9:2?**  
**Answer:** they that dwell in the land of the shadow of death, upon them hath the light shined.

**Q6. According to Isaiah 9:2, what is the full truth recorded?**

**Verse:** Isaiah 9:2
