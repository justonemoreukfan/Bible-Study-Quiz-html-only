# Isaiah 31 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 31:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 31:1?**  
**Answer:** Woe to them that go down to Egypt for help.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 31:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 31:1?**  
**Answer:** and stay on horses, and trust in chariots, because they are many.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 31:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 31:1?**  
**Answer:** and in horsemen, because they are very strong; but they look not unto the Holy One of Israel, neither seek the LORD!  
**Verse:** Isaiah 31:1

---

### Isaiah 31:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 31:2?**  
**Answer:** Yet he also is wise, and will bring evil, and will not call back his words.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 31:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 31:2?**  
**Answer:** but will arise against the house of the evildoers, and against the help of them that work iniquity.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 31:2

**Q6. According to Isaiah 31:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Yet he also is wise, and will bring evil, and will not call back his words: but will arise against the house of the evildoers, and against the help of them that work iniquity.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 31:2

**Answer:** and their horses flesh, and not spirit. When the LORD shall stretch out his hand, both he that helpeth shall fall, and he that is holpen shall fall down, and they all shall fail together.

**Q9. According to Isaiah 31:3, what is the full truth recorded?**

### Isaiah 31:4
**Q10. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 31:4?**  
**Answer:** For thus hath the LORD spoken unto me, Like as the lion and the young lion roaring on his prey, when a multitude of shepherds is called forth against him, he will not be afraid of their voice, nor abase himself for the noise of them.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 31:4

**Q11. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 31:4?**  
**Answer:** so shall the LORD of hosts come down to fight for mount Zion, and for the hill thereof.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 31:4

**Q12. According to Isaiah 31:4, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** For thus hath the LORD spoken unto me, Like as the lion and the young lion roaring on his prey, when a multitude of shepherds is called forth against him, he will not be afraid of their voice, nor abase himself for the noise of them: so shall the LORD of hosts come down to fight for mount Zion, and for the hill thereof.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 31:4
