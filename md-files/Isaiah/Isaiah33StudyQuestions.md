# Isaiah 33 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 33:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 33:1?**  
**Answer:** Woe to thee that spoilest, and thou wast not spoiled.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 33:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 33:1?**  
**Answer:** and dealest treacherously, and they dealt not treacherously with thee! when thou shalt cease to spoil, thou shalt be spoiled.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 33:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 33:1?**  
**Answer:** and when thou shalt make an end to deal treacherously, they shall deal treacherously with thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 33:1
