# Isaiah 39 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 39:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 39:1?**  
**Answer:** At that time Merodachbaladan, the son of Baladan, king of Babylon, sent letters and a present to Hezekiah.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 39:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 39:1?**  
**Answer:** for he had heard that he had been sick, and was recovered.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 39:1

**Q3. According to Isaiah 39:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** At that time Merodachbaladan, the son of Baladan, king of Babylon, sent letters and a present to Hezekiah: for he had heard that he had been sick, and was recovered.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 39:1

### Isaiah 39:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 39:2?**  
**Answer:** And Hezekiah was glad of them, and shewed them the house of his precious things, the silver, and the gold, and the spices, and the precious ointment, and all the house of his armour, and all that was found in his treasures.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 39:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 39:2?**  
**Answer:** there was nothing in his house, nor in all his dominion, that Hezekiah shewed them not.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 39:2

**Q6. According to Isaiah 39:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And Hezekiah was glad of them, and shewed them the house of his precious things, the silver, and the gold, and the spices, and the precious ointment, and all the house of his armour, and all that was found in his treasures: there was nothing in his house, nor in all his dominion, that Hezekiah shewed them not.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 39:2

### Isaiah 39:3
**Q7. According to Isaiah 39:3, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Then came Isaiah the prophet unto king Hezekiah.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 39:3

**Q8. What is declared next in Isaiah 39:3?**  
**Answer:** and said unto him.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 39:3

**Q9. What further truth is given in Isaiah 39:3?**  
**Answer:** What said these men? and from whence came they unto thee? And Hezekiah said, They are come from a far country unto me, even from Babylon.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 39:3

### Isaiah 39:4
**Q10. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 39:4?**  
**Answer:** Then said he, What have they seen in thine house? And Hezekiah answered, All that is in mine house have they seen.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 39:4

**Q11. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 39:4?**  
**Answer:** there is nothing among my treasures that I have not shewed them.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 39:4

**Q12. According to Isaiah 39:4, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Then said he, What have they seen in thine house? And Hezekiah answered, All that is in mine house have they seen: there is nothing among my treasures that I have not shewed them.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 39:4

### Isaiah 39:7
**Q19. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 39:7?**

**Verse:** Isaiah 39:7

**Q20. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 39:7?**  
**Answer:** and they shall be eunuchs in the palace of the king of Babylon.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 39:7

**Q21. According to Isaiah 39:7, what is the full truth recorded?**

**Verse:** Isaiah 39:7
