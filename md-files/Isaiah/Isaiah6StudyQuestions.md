# Isaiah 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 6:1
**Q1. According to Isaiah 6:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** In the year that king Uzziah died I saw also the LORD sitting upon a throne.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 6:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 6:1?**  
**Answer:** high and lifted up.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 6:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Isaiah 6:1?**  
**Answer:** and his train filled the temple.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 6:1

---

### Isaiah 6:2
**Q4. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 6:2?**  
**Answer:** Above it stood the seraphims.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 6:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Isaiah 6:2?**  
**Answer:** each one had six wings.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 6:2

**Q6. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 6:2?**  
**Answer:** with twain he covered his face, and with twain he covered his feet, and with twain he did fly.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 6:2

---

### Isaiah 6:3
**Q7. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 6:3?**  
**Answer:** And one cried unto another, and said, Holy, holy, holy, is the LORD of hosts.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 6:3

**Q8. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 6:3?**  
**Answer:** the whole earth is full of his glory.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 6:3

**Q9. According to Isaiah 6:3, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And one cried unto another, and said, Holy, holy, holy, is the LORD of hosts: the whole earth is full of his glory.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 6:3

---

### Isaiah 6:5
**Q13. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 6:5?**

**Verse:** Isaiah 6:5

**Q14. What is declared next in Isaiah 6:5?**  
**Answer:** because I am a man of unclean lips, and I dwell in the midst of a people of unclean lips.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 6:5

**Q15. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 6:5?**

**Verse:** Isaiah 6:5

### Isaiah 6:7
**Q19. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 6:7?**  
**Answer:** And he laid it upon my mouth, and said, Lo, this hath touched thy lips.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 6:7

**Q20. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 6:7?**

**Verse:** Isaiah 6:7

**Q21. According to Isaiah 6:7, what is the full truth recorded?**

### Isaiah 6:10

**Verse:** Isaiah 6:10

**Q29. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 6:10?**

**Verse:** Isaiah 6:10

**Q30. According to Isaiah 6:10, what is the full truth recorded?**

**Verse:** Isaiah 6:10

### Isaiah 6:11
**Q31. According to Isaiah 6:11, what is stated first?**

**Verse:** Isaiah 6:11
