# Isaiah 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 3:1
**Q1. According to Isaiah 3:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** the Lord.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 3:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 3:1?**  
**Answer:** the LORD of hosts.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 3:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Isaiah 3:1?**  
**Answer:** doth take away from Jerusalem and from Judah the stay and the staff, the whole stay of bread, and the whole stay of water.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 3:1
