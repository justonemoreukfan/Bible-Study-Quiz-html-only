# Isaiah 41 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 41:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 41:1?**  
**Answer:** Keep silence before me, O islands.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 41:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 41:1?**  
**Answer:** and let the people renew their strength.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 41:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 41:1?**  
**Answer:** let them come near; then let them speak; let us come near together to judgment.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 41:1
