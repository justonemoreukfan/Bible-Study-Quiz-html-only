# Isaiah 56 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 56:1

**Q1. What are the people told to keep and do?**  
**Answer:** Judgment, and do justice.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:1

**Q2. What is near to come?**  
**Answer:** My salvation.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:1

**Q3. What is near to be revealed?**  
**Answer:** My righteousness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:1

---

### Isaiah 56:2

**Q1. Who is the man that is blessed?**  
**Answer:** That doeth this, and the son of man that layeth hold on it.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:2

**Q2. What does this man keep from doing?**  
**Answer:** Keeping the sabbath from polluting it.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:2

**Q3. What does this man keep his hand from doing?**  
**Answer:** Keeping his hand from doing any evil.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:2

---

### Isaiah 56:3

**Q1. Who should not speak, saying the Lord hath utterly separated me from his people?**  
**Answer:** The son of the stranger, that hath joined himself to the Lord.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:3

**Q2. Who should not say, Behold, I am a dry tree?**  
**Answer:** Neither let the eunuch say.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:3

**Q3. What does the son of the stranger have to do regarding the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Joined himself to the Lord.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:3

---

### Isaiah 56:4

**Q1. Whose eunuchs are spoken of in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The eunuchs.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:4

**Q2. What are the eunuchs to keep?**  
**Answer:** My sabbaths.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:4

**Q3. What are the eunuchs to take hold of?**  
**Answer:** My covenant.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:4

---

### Isaiah 56:5

**Q1. What will the Lord give unto them in His house and within His walls?**  
**Answer:** A place and a name.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:5

**Q2. To whom will the Lord give this place and name?**  
**Answer:** Unto the eunuchs.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:5

**Q3. What kind of name will the Lord give them that shall not be cut off?**  
**Answer:** Better than of sons and of daughters: I will give them an everlasting name.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:5

---

### Isaiah 56:6

**Q1. Who are the people that join themselves to the Lord in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The sons of the stranger.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:6

**Q2. What are these strangers to serve the Lord with?**  
**Answer:** To serve him, and to love the name of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:6

**Q3. What are they to be unto Him besides servants?**  
**Answer:** To be his servants.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:6

---

### Isaiah 56:7

**Q1. To what place will the Lord bring them?**  
**Answer:** Even them will I bring to my holy mountain.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:7

**Q2. How will the Lord make them joyful?**  
**Answer:** In my house of prayer.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:7

**Q3. What will be accepted upon His altar?**  
**Answer:** Their burnt offerings and their sacrifices.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:7

---

### Isaiah 56:8

**Q1. Who does the Lord God gather?**  
**Answer:** The outcasts of Israel.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:8

**Q2. What does the Lord God say He will gather besides the outcasts?**  
**Answer:** Yet will I gather others to him, besides those that are gathered unto him.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:8

**Q3. Who is speaking in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The Lord GOD which gathereth the outcasts of Israel.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:8

---

### Isaiah 56:9

**Q1. Who are called to come to devour?**  
**Answer:** All ye beasts of the field, come to devour.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:9

**Q2. Where are the beasts of the field and the forest?**  
**Answer:** Yea, all ye beasts in the forest.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:9

**Q3. Who is commanded to come to devour?**  
**Answer:** All ye beasts of the field, yea, all ye beasts in the forest.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:9

---

### Isaiah 56:10

**Q1. What are his watchmen described as?**  
**Answer:** Blind.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:10

**Q2. What are they all without knowledge of?**  
**Answer:** They are all ignorant.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:10

**Q3. What are they compared to that cannot bark?**  
**Answer:** They are all dumb dogs, they cannot bark.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:10

---

### Isaiah 56:11

**Q1. What kind of dogs are they described as being?**  
**Answer:** Greedy dogs which can never have enough.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:11

**Q2. What are the shepherds that cannot understand?**  
**Answer:** They are shepherds that cannot understand.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:11

**Q3. Where do they all look?**  
**Answer:** They all look to their own way.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:11

---

### Isaiah 56:12

**Q1. What do they say they will fetch?**  
**Answer:** Come ye, say they, I will fetch wine.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:12

**Q2. What will they fill themselves with?**  
**Answer:** We will fill ourselves with strong drink.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:12

**Q3. How do they describe tomorrow?**  
**Answer:** And to-morrow shall be as this day, and much more abundant.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 56:12
