# Isaiah 57 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 57:1

**Q1. Who perishes according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** The righteous man.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:1

**Q2. Who is taken away while no man considers it?**  
**Answer:** Merciful men.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:1

**Q3. From what is the righteous taken away?**  
**Answer:** From the evil to come.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:1

---

### Isaiah 57:2

**Q4. Where shall he enter?**  
**Answer:** Into peace.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:2

**Q5. How shall they rest?**  
**Answer:** In their beds.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:2

**Q6. Who walks in his uprightness?**  
**Answer:** Each one.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:2

---

### Isaiah 57:3

**Q7. Who are commanded to draw near here?**  
**Answer:** The sons of the sorceress.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:3

**Q8. Whose seed are they described as being?**  
**Answer:** The seed of the adulterer and the whore.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:3

**Q9. Who are they called the sons of?**  
**Answer:** The sorceress.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:3

---

### Isaiah 57:4

**Q10. Against whom do they sport themselves?**  
**Answer:** Against whom do ye sport yourselves?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:4

**Q11. Against whom do they make a wide mouth?**  
**Answer:** Against whom make ye a wide mouth, and draw out the tongue?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:4

**Q12. What kind of children are they said to be?**  
**Answer:** Children of transgression, a seed of falsehood.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:4

---

### Isaiah 57:5

**Q13. With whom do they enflame themselves?**  
**Answer:** With idols.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:5

**Q14. Under what kind of tree do they enflame themselves?**  
**Answer:** Every green tree.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:5

**Q15. Where do they slay the children?**  
**Answer:** In the valleys under the clifts of the rocks.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:5

---

### Isaiah 57:6

**Q16. Among what portion are the smooth stones of the stream?**  
**Answer:** Among the smooth stones of the stream.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:6

**Q17. What is thy portion?**  
**Answer:** Thy portion.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:6

**Q18. To what kind of offering hast thou poured a drink offering?**  
**Answer:** To a drink offering.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:6

---

### Isaiah 57:7

**Q19. Upon what kind of mountain have they set their bed?**  
**Answer:** Upon a lofty and high mountain.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:7

**Q20. Where else did they go up to offer sacrifice?**  
**Answer:** Thither wentest thou up to offer sacrifice.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:7

**Q21. Whose bed was set upon a lofty and high mountain?**  
**Answer:** Their bed.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:7

---

### Isaiah 57:8

**Q22. Behind what have they set up their remembrance?**  
**Answer:** Behind the doors and the posts.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:8

**Q23. From whom hast thou discovered thyself?**  
**Answer:** From me.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:8

**Q24. Whose bed hast thou enlarged for thyself?**  
**Answer:** A wide bed for thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:8

---

### Isaiah 57:9

**Q25. To whom wentest thou with ointment?**  
**Answer:** To the king.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:9

**Q26. With what didst thou increase thy perfumes?**  
**Answer:** With ointment.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:9

**Q27. Where didst thou send thy messengers?**  
**Answer:** Far off.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:9

---

### Isaiah 57:10

**Q28. In what were thou wearied?**  
**Answer:** In the greatness of thy way.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:10

**Q29. What didst thou not say concerning thy way?**  
**Answer:** There is no hope.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:10

**Q30. What didst thou find of thine hand?**  
**Answer:** The life of thine hand.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:10

---

### Isaiah 57:11

**Q31. Of whom hast thou been afraid or feared?**  
**Answer:** Of whom hast thou been afraid or feared, that thou hast lied?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:11

**Q32. Who has not been remembered?**  
**Answer:** Me.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:11

**Q33. What has been held in the heart?**  
**Answer:** Nor laid it to thy heart.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:11

---

### Isaiah 57:12

**Q34. What will I declare?**  
**Answer:** Thy righteousness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:12

**Q35. What will thy works not profit thee?**  
**Answer:** And thy works.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:12

**Q36. Whose works will not profit them?**  
**Answer:** Thy works.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:12

---

### Isaiah 57:13

**Q37. Who shall deliver thee when thou criest?**  
**Answer:** Thy companies.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:13

**Q38. What shall the wind carry away?**  
**Answer:** All of them.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:13

**Q39. Who shall inherit the land?**  
**Answer:** He that putteth his trust in me.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:13

---

### Isaiah 57:14

**Q40. What is commanded to be said?**  
**Answer:** Cast ye up, cast ye up, prepare the way.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:14

**Q41. What must be taken out of the way?**  
**Answer:** The stumblingblock.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:14

**Q42. Out of whose way must the stumblingblock be taken?**  
**Answer:** Out of the way of my people.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:14

---

### Isaiah 57:15

**Q43. Who is the high and lofty One?**  
**Answer:** The high and lofty One that inhabiteth eternity.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:15

**Q44. What is the name of the high and lofty One?**  
**Answer:** Whose name is Holy.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:15

**Q45. With whom does the high and lofty One dwell?**  
**Answer:** With him also that is of a contrite and humble spirit.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:15

---

### Isaiah 57:16

**Q46. For how long will the Lord not contend?**  
**Answer:** For ever.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:16

**Q47. For how long will He not be always wroth?**  
**Answer:** Neither will I be always wroth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:16

**Q48. What would fail before Him?**  
**Answer:** The spirit should fail before me.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:16

---

### Isaiah 57:17

**Q49. For the iniquity of whose covetousness was He wroth?**  
**Answer:** For the iniquity of his covetousness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:17

**Q50. What did He do when He smote him?**  
**Answer:** Smote him.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:17

**Q51. How did the backslider go?**  
**Answer:** On frowardly in the way of his heart.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:17

---

### Isaiah 57:18

**Q52. What has been seen of his ways?**  
**Answer:** I have seen his ways.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:18

**Q53. What will He do to him?**  
**Answer:** Heal him.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:18

**Q54. To whom will He restore comforts?**  
**Answer:** Unto him and to his mourners.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:18

---

### Isaiah 57:19

**Q55. What does He create?**  
**Answer:** The fruit of the lips.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:19

**Q56. To whom is peace spoken?**  
**Answer:** To him that is far off, and to him that is near.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:19

**Q57. What will the Lord do to him?**  
**Answer:** I will heal him.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:19

---

### Isaiah 57:20

**Q58. Who are like the troubled sea?**  
**Answer:** The wicked.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:20

**Q59. When cannot the wicked rest?**  
**Answer:** When it cannot rest.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:20

**Q60. What do the waters of the wicked cast up?**  
**Answer:** Mire and dirt.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:20

---

### Isaiah 57:21

**Q61. For whom is there no peace?**  
**Answer:** To the wicked.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:21

**Q62. Who says there is no peace to the wicked?**  
**Answer:** Saith my God.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:21

**Q63. What is stated to be absent for the wicked?**  
**Answer:** No peace.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 57:21
