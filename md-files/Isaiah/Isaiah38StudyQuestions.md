### Isaiah 38:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 38:1?**  
**Answer:** In those days was Hezekiah sick unto death. And Isaiah the prophet the son of Amoz came unto him, and said unto him, Thus saith the LORD, Set thine house in order.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 38:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 38:1?**  
**Answer:** for thou shalt die, and not live.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 38:1

**Q3. According to Isaiah 38:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** In those days was Hezekiah sick unto death. And Isaiah the prophet the son of Amoz came unto him, and said unto him, Thus saith the LORD, Set thine house in order: for thou shalt die, and not live.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 38:1
