# Isaiah 26 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 26:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 26:1?**  
**Answer:** In that day shall this song be sung in the land of Judah.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 26:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 26:1?**  
**Answer:** We have a strong city.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 26:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 26:1?**  
**Answer:** salvation will God appoint for walls and bulwarks.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 26:1

---

### Isaiah 26:2
**Q4. What does the first part of Isaiah 26:2 state?**  
**Answer:** Open ye the gates.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 26:2

**Q5. What does the second part of Isaiah 26:2 declare?**  
**Answer:** that the righteous nation which keepeth the truth may enter in.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 26:2

**Q6. What is recorded in Isaiah 26:2?**  
**Answer:** Open ye the gates, that the righteous nation which keepeth the truth may enter in.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 26:2
