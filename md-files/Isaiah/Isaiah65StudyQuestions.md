# Isaiah 65 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 65:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 65:1?**  
**Answer:** I am sought of them that asked not for me.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 65:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 65:1?**  
**Answer:** I am found of them that sought me not.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 65:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 65:1?**  
**Answer:** I said, Behold me, behold me, unto a nation that was not called by my name.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 65:1

---
