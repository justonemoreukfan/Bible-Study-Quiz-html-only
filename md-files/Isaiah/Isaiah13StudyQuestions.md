# Isaiah 13 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 13:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Isaiah 13:1 state?**  
**Answer:** The burden of Babylon.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 13:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Isaiah 13:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** which Isaiah the son of Amoz did see.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 13:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Isaiah 13:1?**  
**Answer:** The burden of Babylon, which Isaiah the son of Amoz did see.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 13:1

---
