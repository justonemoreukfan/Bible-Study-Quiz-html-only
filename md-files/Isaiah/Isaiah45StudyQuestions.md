# Isaiah 45 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 45:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 45:1?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD to his anointed, to Cyrus, whose right hand I have holden, to subdue nations before him.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 45:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 45:1?**  
**Answer:** and I will loose the loins of kings, to open before him the two leaved gates.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 45:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 45:1?**  
**Answer:** and the gates shall not be shut.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 45:1

---

### Isaiah 45:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 45:2?**

**Verse:** Isaiah 45:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 45:2?**
