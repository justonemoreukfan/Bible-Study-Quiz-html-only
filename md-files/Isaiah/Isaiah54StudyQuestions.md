# Isaiah 54 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 54:1

**Q1. Who is commanded to sing, according to the verse?**  
**Answer:** The barren that did not bear.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:1

**Q2. What should she break forth into, and cry aloud?**  
**Answer:** Singing.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:1

**Q3. Who has more children than the married wife?**  
**Answer:** The desolate.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:1

---

### Isaiah 54:2

**Q4. What is commanded to be enlarged?**  
**Answer:** The place of thy tent.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:2

**Q5. What should be stretched forth?**  
**Answer:** The curtains of thine habitations.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:2

**Q6. What should be lengthened and strengthened?**  
**Answer:** Thy cords, and strengthen thy stakes.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:2

---

### Isaiah 54:3

**Q7. In what directions shall you break forth?**  
**Answer:** On the right hand and on the left.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:3

**Q8. What shall thy seed inherit?**  
**Answer:** The Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:3

**Q9. What shall be made to be inhabited?**  
**Answer:** The desolate cities.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:3

---

### Isaiah 54:4

**Q10. What should one not fear?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not fear.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:4

**Q11. What should one not be?**  
**Answer:** Ashamed or confounded.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:4

**Q12. What shame of one's youth shall be forgotten?**  
**Answer:** The shame of thy youth and the reproach of thy widowhood.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:4

---

### Isaiah 54:5

**Q13. Who is thy Maker?**  
**Answer:** Thine husband.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:5

**Q14. What is the name of the LORD of hosts?**  
**Answer:** The LORD of hosts.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:5

**Q15. Who is thy Redeemer and the God of the whole earth?**  
**Answer:** The Holy One of Israel.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:5

---

### Isaiah 54:6

**Q16. As what kind of woman has the LORD called thee?**  
**Answer:** As a woman forsaken and grieved in spirit.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:6

**Q17. What kind of wife was she of youth?**  
**Answer:** A wife of youth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:6

**Q18. What does the LORD ask if she was?**  
**Answer:** When thou wast refused.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:6

---

### Isaiah 54:7

**Q19. For how long did the LORD forsake thee?**  
**Answer:** For a small moment.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:7

**Q20. With what will the LORD gather thee?**  
**Answer:** With great mercies.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:7

**Q21. Whose actions are described here?**  
**Answer:** The LORD's.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:7

---

### Isaiah 54:8

**Q22. In what did the LORD hide His face from thee?**  
**Answer:** In a little wrath.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:8

**Q23. For how long did He hide His face?**  
**Answer:** For a moment.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:8

**Q24. With what will He have mercy on thee?**  
**Answer:** With everlasting kindness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:8

---

### Isaiah 54:9

**Q25. To what days is this likened?**  
**Answer:** The waters of Noah unto me.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:9

**Q26. What has the LORD sworn?**  
**Answer:** That the waters of Noah should no more go over the earth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:9

**Q27. What has the LORD sworn that He would not do?**  
**Answer:** That I would not be wroth with thee, nor rebuke thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:9

---

### Isaiah 54:10

**Q28. What shall depart from thee?**  
**Answer:** The mountains shall depart, and the hills be removed.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:10

**Q29. What shall not depart from thee?**  
**Answer:** My kindness, neither shall the covenant of my peace be removed.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:10

**Q30. Who has mercy on thee?**  
**Answer:** The LORD that hath mercy on thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:10

---

### Isaiah 54:11

**Q31. How is the afflicted one described?**  
**Answer:** O thou afflicted, tossed with tempest, and not comforted.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:11

**Q32. With what will He lay thy stones?**  
**Answer:** With fair colours.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:11

**Q33. With what will He lay thy foundations?**  
**Answer:** With sapphires.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:11

---

### Isaiah 54:12

**Q34. What will He make thy windows of?**  
**Answer:** Agates.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:12

**Q35. What will He make thy gates of?**  
**Answer:** Carbuncles.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:12

**Q36. What will He make all thy borders of?**  
**Answer:** Pleasant stones.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:12

---

### Isaiah 54:13

**Q37. Who shall be taught of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** All thy children.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:13

**Q38. What shall be great?**  
**Answer:** The peace of thy children.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:13

**Q39. Whose children are taught of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Thy children.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:13

---

### Isaiah 54:14

**Q40. In what shall thou be established?**  
**Answer:** In righteousness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:14

**Q41. What shall thou be far from?**  
**Answer:** Oppression.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:14

**Q42. What shall not come near thee?**  
**Answer:** Terror, for it shall not come near thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:14

---

### Isaiah 54:15

**Q43. If they shall surely gather together, what will happen?**  
**Answer:** Not by me.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:15

**Q44. What will happen to whosoever shall gather together against thee?**  
**Answer:** Shall fall for thy sake.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:15

**Q45. Who is speaking about those who gather against thee?**  
**Answer:** The LORD (implied by "by me").  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:15

---

### Isaiah 54:16

**Q46. Who has created the smith?**  
**Answer:** Behold, I have created the smith.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:16

**Q47. What does the smith blow the coals in?**  
**Answer:** The fire.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:16

**Q48. Who has created the waster to destroy?**  
**Answer:** I have created the waster to destroy.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:16

---

### Isaiah 54:17

**Q49. What weapon formed against thee shall prosper?**  
**Answer:** No weapon that is formed against thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:17

**Q50. What shall every tongue that rises against thee in judgment be shown to be?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt condemn.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:17

**Q51. Whose heritage is this?**  
**Answer:** The heritage of the servants of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 54:17
