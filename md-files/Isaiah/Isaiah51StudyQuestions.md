# Isaiah 51 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 51:1

**Q1. Who are instructed to hearken to the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Ye that follow after righteousness, ye that seek the Lord.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:1

**Q2. To what are they told to look?**  
**Answer:** The rock whence ye are hewn, and to the hole of the pit whence ye are digged.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:1

### Isaiah 51:2

**Q3. Who are they to look unto?**  
**Answer:** Abraham your father, and unto Sarah that bare you.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:2

**Q4. What was Abraham’s state when the Lord called him?**  
**Answer:** Alone.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:2

**Q5. What did the Lord do to Abraham after calling him?**  
**Answer:** Blessed him, and increased him.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:2

### Isaiah 51:3

**Q6. What will the Lord comfort?**  
**Answer:** Zion.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:3

**Q7. What will the Lord comfort all of?**  
**Answer:** Her waste places.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:3

**Q8. What will the Lord make her wilderness and desert like?**  
**Answer:** Eden, and her desert like the garden of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:3

### Isaiah 51:4

**Q9. To whom should my people hearken and give ear?**  
**Answer:** Unto me, O my nation; and give ear unto me, O my people.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:4

**Q10. What shall proceed from the Lord?**  
**Answer:** A law.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:4

**Q11. What will the Lord make to rest for a light of the people?**  
**Answer:** My judgment.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:4

### Isaiah 51:5

**Q12. What is near?**  
**Answer:** My righteousness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:5

**Q13. What is gone forth?**  
**Answer:** My salvation.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:5

**Q14. What shall mine arms judge?**  
**Answer:** The people.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:5

### Isaiah 51:6

**Q15. Where should you lift up your eyes?**  
**Answer:** To the heavens, and look upon the earth beneath.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:6

**Q16. How shall the heavens vanish away and the earth wax old?**  
**Answer:** Like smoke.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:6

**Q17. What shall not be abolished?**  
**Answer:** My salvation, and my righteousness.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:6

### Isaiah 51:7

**Q18. Who are addressed as knowing righteousness?**  
**Answer:** Ye that know righteousness, the people in whose heart is my law.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:7

**Q19. Whose reproach should you not fear?**  
**Answer:** The reproach of men.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:7

**Q20. Whose revilings should you not be afraid of?**  
**Answer:** Their revilings.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:7

### Isaiah 51:8

**Q21. Who shall eat them up like a garment?**  
**Answer:** The moth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:8

**Q22. Who shall eat them up like wool?**  
**Answer:** The worm.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:8

**Q23. What shall endure forever?**  
**Answer:** My righteousness, and my salvation from generation to generation.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:8

### Isaiah 51:9

**Q24. What is asked of the arm of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Awake, awake, put on strength, O arm of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:9

**Q25. In what days of old did the arm of the Lord awake?**  
**Answer:** In the ancient days.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:9

**Q26. Who was cut in pieces and wounded by the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Rahab, and the dragon.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:9

### Isaiah 51:10

**Q27. What did the Lord dry up?**  
**Answer:** The sea, the waters of the great deep.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:10

**Q28. What did the Lord make the depths of the sea?**  
**Answer:** A way for the ransomed to pass over.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:10

### Isaiah 51:11

**Q29. Who shall return and come with singing unto Zion?**  
**Answer:** The redeemed of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:11

**Q30. What shall be upon their head?**  
**Answer:** Everlasting joy.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:11

**Q31. What shall flee away?**  
**Answer:** Sorrow and mourning.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:11

### Isaiah 51:12

**Q32. Who is the one that comforts you?**  
**Answer:** I, even I.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:12

**Q33. Whom should you be afraid of?**  
**Answer:** A man that shall die, and of the son of man which shall be made as grass.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:12

### Isaiah 51:13

**Q34. Whom didst thou forget?**  
**Answer:** The Lord thy maker.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:13

**Q35. What did the Lord stretch forth?**  
**Answer:** The heavens, and laid the foundations of the earth.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:13

**Q36. What did the oppressor ready to destroy fear continually?**  
**Answer:** The fury of the oppressor, as if he were ready to destroy.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:13

### Isaiah 51:14

**Q37. Who hasteneth to be loosed?**  
**Answer:** The captive exile.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:14

**Q38. Shall the captive exile die in the pit?**  
**Answer:** No.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:14

**Q39. Shall his bread fail?**  
**Answer:** No.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:14

### Isaiah 51:15

**Q40. Who am I that divides the sea?**  
**Answer:** The Lord thy God.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:15

**Q41. What do the waves of the sea do?**  
**Answer:** Roar.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:15

**Q42. What is the name of the Lord of hosts?**  
**Answer:** The Lord of hosts is his name.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:15

### Isaiah 51:16

**Q43. What has the Lord put in thy mouth?**  
**Answer:** My words.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:16

**Q44. With what has the Lord covered thee?**  
**Answer:** In the shadow of mine hand.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:16

**Q45. What is said to Zion concerning her identity?**  
**Answer:** Thou art my people.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:16

### Isaiah 51:17

**Q46. What is commanded of Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Awake, awake, stand up, O Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:17

**Q47. Whose hand has Jerusalem drunk at?**  
**Answer:** The hand of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:17

**Q48. What cup has Jerusalem drunk and wrung out?**  
**Answer:** The cup of his fury, the dregs of the cup of trembling.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:17

### Isaiah 51:18

**Q49. Who is there to guide among all the sons she has brought forth?**  
**Answer:** None.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:18

**Q50. Who is there to take her by the hand among all the sons she has brought up?**  
**Answer:** None.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:18

### Isaiah 51:19

**Q51. What two things happened unto thee?**  
**Answer:** Desolation, and destruction, and the famine, and the sword.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:19

**Q52. Who shall comfort thee?**  
**Answer:** By whom shall I comfort thee?  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:19

### Isaiah 51:20

**Q53. Where have thy sons fainted?**  
**Answer:** At the head of all the streets.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:20

**Q54. What are thy sons compared to in the streets?**  
**Answer:** As a wild bull in a net.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:20

**Q55. Of what are they full?**  
**Answer:** The fury of the Lord, the rebuke of thy God.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:20

### Isaiah 51:21

**Q56. Who is afflicted?**  
**Answer:** Thou afflicted.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:21

**Q57. What condition is she in without wine?**  
**Answer:** And drunken, but not with wine.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:21

### Isaiah 51:22

**Q58. Who pleadeth the cause of his people?**  
**Answer:** Thy Lord the Lord, and thy God that pleadeth the cause of his people.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:22

**Q59. What has the Lord taken out of her hand?**  
**Answer:** The cup of trembling.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:22

**Q60. Will she ever drink the cup of his fury again?**  
**Answer:** No more.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:22

### Isaiah 51:23

**Q61. Into whose hand will the Lord put the cup?**  
**Answer:** Into the hand of them that afflict thee.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:23

**Q62. What did they say to thy soul?**  
**Answer:** Bow down, that we may go over.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:23

**Q63. What didst thou lay your body like to the ground?**  
**Answer:** As the ground, and as the street, to them that went over.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 51:23
