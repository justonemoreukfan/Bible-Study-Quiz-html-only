# Isaiah 27 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 27:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 27:1?**  
**Answer:** In that day the LORD with his sore and great and strong sword shall punish leviathan the piercing serpent, even leviathan that crooked serpent.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 27:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 27:1?**  
**Answer:** and he shall slay the dragon that is in the sea.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 27:1

**Q3. According to Isaiah 27:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** In that day the LORD with his sore and great and strong sword shall punish leviathan the piercing serpent, even leviathan that crooked serpent; and he shall slay the dragon that is in the sea.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 27:1

**Verse:** Isaiah 27:3
