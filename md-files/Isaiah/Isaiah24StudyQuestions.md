# Isaiah 24 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 24:1
**Q1. According to Isaiah 24:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** the LORD maketh the earth empty.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 24:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 24:1?**  
**Answer:** and maketh it waste.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 24:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Isaiah 24:1?**  
**Answer:** and turneth it upside down, and scattereth abroad the inhabitants thereof.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 24:1

---

### Isaiah 24:2
**Q4. What is stated in the opening of Isaiah 24:2?**  
**Answer:** And it shall be, as with the people, so with the priest.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 24:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Isaiah 24:2?**  
**Answer:** as with the servant, so with his master.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 24:2

**Q6. What further truth is recorded in Isaiah 24:2?**  
**Answer:** as with the maid, so with her mistress; as with the buyer, so with the seller; as with the lender, so with the borrower; as with the taker of usury, so with the giver of usury to him.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 24:2

---
