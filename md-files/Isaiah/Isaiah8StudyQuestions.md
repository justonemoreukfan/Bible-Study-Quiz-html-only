### Isaiah 8:1
**Q1. According to Isaiah 8:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Moreover the LORD said unto me.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 8:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Isaiah 8:1?**  
**Answer:** Take thee a great roll.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 8:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Isaiah 8:1?**  
**Answer:** and write in it with a man’s pen concerning Mahershalalhashbaz.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 8:1
