# Isaiah 19 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Isaiah 19:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Isaiah 19:1?**  
**Answer:** The burden of Egypt. Behold, the LORD rideth upon a swift cloud, and shall come into Egypt.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 19:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Isaiah 19:1?**  
**Answer:** and the idols of Egypt shall be moved at his presence, and the heart of Egypt shall melt in the midst of it.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 19:1

**Q3. According to Isaiah 19:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** The burden of Egypt. Behold, the LORD rideth upon a swift cloud, and shall come into Egypt: and the idols of Egypt shall be moved at his presence, and the heart of Egypt shall melt in the midst of it.  
**Verse:** Isaiah 19:1
