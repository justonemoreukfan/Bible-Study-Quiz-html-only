# 2 Chronicles 27 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Chronicles 27:1
**Q1. According to 2 Chronicles 27:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Jotham was twenty and five years old when he began to reign.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 27:1

**Q2. What is declared next in 2 Chronicles 27:1?**  
**Answer:** and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name also was Jerushah.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 27:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in 2 Chronicles 27:1?**  
**Answer:** the daughter of Zadok.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 27:1

### 2 Chronicles 27:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of 2 Chronicles 27:2?**  
**Answer:** And he did that which was right in the sight of the LORD, according to all that his father Uzziah did.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 27:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in 2 Chronicles 27:2?**  
**Answer:** howbeit he entered not into the temple of the LORD. And the people did yet corruptly.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 27:2

**Q6. According to 2 Chronicles 27:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And he did that which was right in the sight of the LORD, according to all that his father Uzziah did: howbeit he entered not into the temple of the LORD. And the people did yet corruptly.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 27:2
