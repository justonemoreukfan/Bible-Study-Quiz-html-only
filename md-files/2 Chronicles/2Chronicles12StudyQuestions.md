### 2 Chronicles 12:1
**Q1. According to 2 Chronicles 12:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And it came to pass.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 12:1

**Q2. What is declared next in 2 Chronicles 12:1?**  
**Answer:** when Rehoboam had established the kingdom.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 12:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in 2 Chronicles 12:1?**  
**Answer:** and had strengthened himself, he forsook the law of the LORD, and all Israel with him.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 12:1
