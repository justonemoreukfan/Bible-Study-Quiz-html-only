# 2 Chronicles 35 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Chronicles 35:1

**Q1. Where did Josiah keep a passover unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** In Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:1

**Q2. When did they kill the passover?**  
**Answer:** On the fourteenth day of the first month.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:1

**Q3. Unto whom did Josiah keep a passover?**  
**Answer:** Unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:1

---

### 2 Chronicles 35:2

**Q1. Who set the priests in their charges?**  
**Answer:** Josiah.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:2

**Q2. Whom did Josiah encourage?**  
**Answer:** To the service of the house of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:2

**Q3. What did Josiah set the priests in?**  
**Answer:** Their charges.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:2

---

### 2 Chronicles 35:3

**Q1. To whom did Josiah speak?**  
**Answer:** Unto the Levites that taught all Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:3

**Q2. What did Josiah tell the Levites to put in the holy house?**  
**Answer:** The holy ark.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:3

**Q3. Who built the house that was mentioned?**  
**Answer:** Solomon the son of David king of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:3

---

### 2 Chronicles 35:4

**Q1. What did Josiah tell the Levites to prepare themselves by?**  
**Answer:** The houses of your fathers, after your courses.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:4

**Q2. According to whose writing were the Levites to prepare themselves?**  
**Answer:** According to the writing of David king of Israel, and according to the writing of Solomon his son.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:4

**Q3. Whose courses were they to follow?**  
**Answer:** After your courses.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:4

---

### 2 Chronicles 35:5

**Q1. Where were the Levites to stand?**  
**Answer:** In the holy place.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:5

**Q2. According to the division of the families of whom were they to stand?**  
**Answer:** Of the fathers' houses of your brethren the people.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:5

**Q3. After whose division were the families of the Levites to be?**  
**Answer:** After the division of the families of the Levites.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:5

---

### 2 Chronicles 35:6

**Q1. What did Josiah tell them to kill?**  
**Answer:** The passover.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:6

**Q2. What were they to sanctify?**  
**Answer:** Themselves.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:6

**Q3. For whom were they to prepare the passover?**  
**Answer:** Your brethren.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:6

---

### 2 Chronicles 35:7

**Q1. What did Josiah give to the people?**  
**Answer:** Of the flock, lambs and kids, all for the passover offerings.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:7

**Q2. How many lambs and kids did Josiah give?**  
**Answer:** Thirty thousand, and three thousand bullocks.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:7

**Q3. To how many people present did Josiah give these offerings?**  
**Answer:** To all that were present.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:7

---

### 2 Chronicles 35:8

**Q1. Who willingly gave unto the people, the priests, and the Levites?**  
**Answer:** His princes.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:8

**Q2. Who gave unto the Levites for the passover offerings?**  
**Answer:** Hilkiah and Zechariah and Jehiel.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:8

**Q3. What were Hilkiah, Zechariah, and Jehiel described as?**  
**Answer:** Rulers of the house of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:8

---

### 2 Chronicles 35:9

**Q1. Who gave unto the Levites for passover offerings?**  
**Answer:** Conaniah also, and Shemaiah and Nethaneel, his brethren, and Hashabiah and Jeiel and Jozabad.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:9

**Q2. What were Conaniah, Shemaiah, Nethaneel, Hashabiah, Jeiel, and Jozabad described as?**  
**Answer:** Chief of the Levites.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:9

**Q3. How many small cattle and bullocks did they give?**  
**Answer:** Five thousand small cattle, and five hundred oxen.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:9

---

### 2 Chronicles 35:13

**Q1. How did they roast the passover with fire?**  
**Answer:** With fire according to the ordinance.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:13

**Q2. What did they seethe in pots, caldrons, and pans?**  
**Answer:** The other holy offerings.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:13

**Q3. How did they divide the holy offerings among the people?**  
**Answer:** Speedily.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:13

### 2 Chronicles 35:14

**Q1. Who made ready for themselves, and for the priests?**  
**Answer:** Afterward they made ready for themselves, and for the priests.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:14

**Q2. Why did the priests make ready until night?**  
**Answer:** Because the priests the sons of Aaron were busied in offering of burnt offerings and the fat until night.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:14

**Q3. For whom did the Levites prepare?**  
**Answer:** For themselves, and for the priests the sons of Aaron.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:14

---

### 2 Chronicles 35:15

**Q1. Who were in their place?**  
**Answer:** The singers the sons of Asaph.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:15

**Q2. According to whose commandment were the singers in their place?**  
**Answer:** According to the commandment of David, and Asaph, and Heman, and Jeduthun the king's seer.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:15

**Q3. Why did the porters not depart from every gate?**  
**Answer:** Because they might not depart from their service.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:15

---

### 2 Chronicles 35:16

**Q1. What was prepared on that same day?**  
**Answer:** All the service of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:16

**Q2. What did they keep on that day?**  
**Answer:** The passover.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:16

**Q3. According to whose commandment was the passover kept?**  
**Answer:** To keep the passover, and to offer burnt offerings upon the altar of the LORD, according to the commandment of king Josiah.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:16

---

### 2 Chronicles 35:17

**Q1. Who were present at that time?**  
**Answer:** And the children of Israel that were present.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:17

**Q2. What did the children of Israel keep at that time?**  
**Answer:** The passover.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:17

**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:17

### 2 Chronicles 35:18

**Q1. Like unto what passover was there no passover kept like it in Israel?**  
**Answer:** From the days of Samuel the prophet.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:18

**Q2. Which of the kings of Israel kept such a passover?**  
**Answer:** Neither did all the kings of Israel keep such a passover as Josiah kept.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:18

**Q3. Who besides the king kept this passover?**  
**Answer:** And the priests, and the Levites, and all Judah and Israel that were present, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 35:18

---
