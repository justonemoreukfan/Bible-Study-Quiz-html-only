# 2 Chronicles 16 Study Questions (KJV)

### 2 Chronicles 16:1
**Q1. According to 2 Chronicles 16:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** In the six and thirtieth year of the reign of Asa Baasha king of Israel came up against Judah.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 16:1

**Q2. What is declared next in 2 Chronicles 16:1?**  
**Answer:** and built Ramah.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 16:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in 2 Chronicles 16:1?**  
**Answer:** to the intent that he might let none go out or come in to Asa king of Judah.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 16:1
