### 2 Chronicles 4:1
**Q1. According to 2 Chronicles 4:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Moreover he made an altar of brass.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 4:1

**Q2. What is declared next in 2 Chronicles 4:1?**  
**Answer:** twenty cubits the length thereof.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 4:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in 2 Chronicles 4:1?**  
**Answer:** and twenty cubits the breadth thereof, and ten cubits the height thereof.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 4:1
