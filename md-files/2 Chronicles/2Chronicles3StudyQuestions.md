### 2 Chronicles 3:1
**Q1. According to 2 Chronicles 3:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Then Solomon began to build the house of the LORD at Jerusalem in mount Moriah.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 3:1

**Q2. What is declared next in 2 Chronicles 3:1?**  
**Answer:** where the Lord appeared unto David his father.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 3:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in 2 Chronicles 3:1?**  
**Answer:** in the place that David had prepared in the threshingfloor of Ornan the Jebusite.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 3:1
