# 2 Chronicles 34 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Chronicles 34:1

**Q1. How old was Josiah when he began to reign?**  
**Answer:** Eight years old.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:1

**Q2. How long did Josiah reign in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** One and thirty years.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:1

**Q3. In what city did Josiah reign?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:1

---

### 2 Chronicles 34:2

**Q1. In whose sight did Josiah do that which was right?**  
**Answer:** In the sight of the Lord.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:2

**Q2. Whose ways did Josiah walk in?**  
**Answer:** In the ways of David his father.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:2

**Q3. What did Josiah not decline to, either to the right hand or to the left?**  
**Answer:** To the right hand or to the left.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:2

---

### 2 Chronicles 34:3

**Q1. In what year of his reign did Josiah begin to seek after the God of David his father?**  
**Answer:** In the eighth year of his reign.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:3

**Q2. In what year of his reign did he begin to purge Judah and Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** In the twelfth year.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:3

**Q3. What did he purge Judah and Jerusalem from?**  
**Answer:** The high places, and the groves, and the carved images, and the molten images.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:3

---

### 2 Chronicles 34:4

**Q1. What did they break down in his presence?**  
**Answer:** The altars of Baalim.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:4

**Q2. What did Josiah cut down that were on high above them?**  
**Answer:** The images.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:4

**Q3. What did he do to the groves, the carved images, and the molten images?**  
**Answer:** He brake them in pieces, and made dust of them, and strowed it upon the graves of them that had sacrificed unto them.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:4

---

### 2 Chronicles 34:5

**Q1. Whose bones did Josiah burn upon the altars?**  
**Answer:** The bones of the priests.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:5

**Q2. Where did he cleanse Judah and Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Judah and Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:5

**Q3. What did he do to Judah and Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Cleansed them.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:5

---

### 2 Chronicles 34:6

**Q1. In what cities did he do this?**  
**Answer:** In the cities of Manasseh, and Ephraim, and Simeon, even unto Naphtali.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:6

**Q2. What were those cities surrounded with?**  
**Answer:** Their mattocks, round about. (Note: text says "with their mattocks round about", or "in their ruins round about" depending on variant phrasing; using exact text: "with their mattocks round about").  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:6

**Q3. What region is specifically mentioned alongside Manasseh, Ephraim, and Simeon?**  
**Answer:** Naphtali.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:6

---

### 2 Chronicles 34:7

**Q1. What did he break down when he had beaten down the altars and groves?**  
**Answer:** The altars and the groves.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:7

**Q2. What did he beat into powder?**  
**Answer:** The graven images.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:7

**Q3. Through all the land of Israel, what did he do after breaking down the altars and groves?**  
**Answer:** He cut down all the idols throughout all the land of Israel, and returned to Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 34:7
