# 2 Chronicles 22 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Chronicles 22:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of 2 Chronicles 22:1?**  
**Answer:** And the inhabitants of Jerusalem made Ahaziah his youngest son king in his stead.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 22:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in 2 Chronicles 22:1?**  
**Answer:** for the band of men that came with the Arabians to the camp had slain all the eldest. So Ahaziah the son of Jehoram king of Judah reigned.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 22:1

**Q3. According to 2 Chronicles 22:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And the inhabitants of Jerusalem made Ahaziah his youngest son king in his stead: for the band of men that came with the Arabians to the camp had slain all the eldest. So Ahaziah the son of Jehoram king of Judah reigned.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 22:1
