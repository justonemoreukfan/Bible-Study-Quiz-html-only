# 2 Chronicles 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Chronicles 2:1
**Q1. What does the first part of 2 Chronicles 2:1 state?**  
**Answer:** And Solomon determined to build an house for the name of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 2:1

**Q2. What does the second part of 2 Chronicles 2:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** and an house for his kingdom.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 2:1

**Q3. What is recorded in 2 Chronicles 2:1?**  
**Answer:** And Solomon determined to build an house for the name of the LORD, and an house for his kingdom.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 2:1
