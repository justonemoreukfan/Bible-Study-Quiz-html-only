# 2 Chronicles 36 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:1

**Q1. Who took Jehoahaz the son of Josiah?**  
**Answer:** The people of the land.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:1

**Q2. Whom did the people of the land make king in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Jehoahaz his son.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:1

**Q3. In whose stead was Jehoahaz made king?**  
**Answer:** In his father's stead.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:1

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:2

**Q1. How old was Jehoahaz when he began to reign?**  
**Answer:** Twenty and three years old.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:2

**Q2. How long did Jehoahaz reign in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Three months.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:2

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:3

**Q1. Who put Jehoahaz down in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The king of Egypt.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:3

**Q2. What did the king of Egypt condemn the land in?**  
**Answer:** An hundred talents of silver and a talent of gold.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:3

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:4

**Q1. Whom did the king of Egypt make king over Judah and Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Eliakim his brother.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:4

**Q2. Whose name did the king of Egypt turn to Jehoiakim?**  
**Answer:** Eliakim's.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:4

**Q3. Who took Jehoahaz and carried him to Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Necho.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:4

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:5

**Q1. How old was Jehoiakim when he began to reign?**  
**Answer:** Twenty and five years old.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:5

**Q2. How long did Jehoiakim reign in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Eleven years.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:5

**Q3. In the sight of whom did Jehoiakim do that which was evil?**  
**Answer:** In the sight of the LORD his God.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:5

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:6

**Q1. Who came up against Jehoiakim?**  
**Answer:** Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:6

**Q2. With what did Nebuchadnezzar bind Jehoiakim?**  
**Answer:** With fetters.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:6

**Q3. To where did Nebuchadnezzar carry Jehoiakim?**  
**Answer:** To Babylon.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:6

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:7

**Q1. What did Nebuchadnezzar carry of the vessels of the house of the LORD to Babylon?**  
**Answer:** Some of them.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:7

**Q2. Where did Nebuchadnezzar put the vessels he carried to Babylon?**  
**Answer:** In his temple at Babylon.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:7

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:8

**Q1. What are the rest of the acts of Jehoiakim written in?**  
**Answer:** The book of the kings of Israel and Judah.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:8

**Q2. What abominations which Jehoiakim did are written in the book?**  
**Answer:** And his abominations which he did.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:8

**Q3. Who reigned in Jehoiakim's stead?**  
**Answer:** Jehoiachin his son.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:8

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:9

**Q1. How old was Jehoiachin when he began to reign?**  
**Answer:** Eight years old.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:9

**Q2. How long and ten days did Jehoiachin reign in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Three months and ten days.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:9

**Q3. In the sight of whom did Jehoiachin do that which was evil?**  
**Answer:** In the sight of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:9

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:10

**Q1. When the year was expired, who sent and brought Jehoiachin to Babylon?**  
**Answer:** King Nebuchadnezzar.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:10

**Q2. With the goodly vessels of the house of the LORD, whom did Nebuchadnezzar bring to Babylon?**  
**Answer:** Jehoiachin.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:10

**Q3. Whom did Nebuchadnezzar make king over Judah and Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Zedekiah his brother.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:10

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:11

**Q1. How old was Zedekiah when he began to reign?**  
**Answer:** One and twenty years old.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:11

**Q2. How long did Zedekiah reign in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Eleven years.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:11

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:12

**Q1. In the sight of whom did Zedekiah do that which was evil?**  
**Answer:** In the sight of the LORD his God.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:12

**Q2. Before whom did Zedekiah not humble himself?**  
**Answer:** Before Jeremiah the prophet speaking from the mouth of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:12

**Q3. Whose mouth did Jeremiah the prophet speak from?**  
**Answer:** The mouth of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:12

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:13

**Q1. Against whom also did Zedekiah rebel?**  
**Answer:** King Nebuchadnezzar.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:13

**Q2. By whom had Nebuchadnezzar made Zedekiah swear?**  
**Answer:** By God.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:13

**Q3. What did Zedekiah stiffen and harden against turning unto the LORD God of Israel?**  
**Answer:** His neck, and hardened his heart.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:13

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:14

**Q1. Who transgressed very much among the chief of the priests and the people?**  
**Answer:** All the chief of the priests, and the people.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:14

**Q2. After all the abominations of whom did the people transgress?**  
**Answer:** Of the heathen.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:14

**Q3. What did the people pollute which the LORD had hallowed in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The house of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:14

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:15

**Q1. Who sent to them by his messengers?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God of their fathers.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:15

**Q2. How did the LORD God of their fathers send to them?**  
**Answer:** Rising up betimes, and sending.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:15

**Q3. Why did the LORD God of their fathers send his messengers?**  
**Answer:** Because he had compassion on his people, and on his dwelling place.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:15

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:16

**Q1. What did the people mock in relation to the messengers of God?**  
**Answer:** The messengers of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:16

**Q2. What did the people despise?**  
**Answer:** His words.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:16

**Q3. Until when was there no remedy for the wrath of the LORD against his people?**  
**Answer:** Till there was no remedy.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:16

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:17

**Q1. Whom did the LORD bring upon them?**  
**Answer:** The king of the Chaldees.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:17

**Q2. Who was slain with the sword in the house of their sanctuary?**  
**Answer:** Their young men.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:17

**Q3. Into whose hand was the king of the Chaldees given?**  
**Answer:** He gave them all into his hand.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:17

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:18

**Q1. What vessels of the house of God, great and small, were brought to Babylon?**  
**Answer:** All the vessels of the house of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:18

**Q2. Whose treasures were brought to Babylon?**  
**Answer:** The treasures of the house of the LORD, and the treasures of the king, and of his princes.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:18

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:19

**Q1. What did they burn?**  
**Answer:** The house of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:19

**Q2. What did they break down?**  
**Answer:** The wall of Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:19

**Q3. What was burned with fire?**  
**Answer:** All the palaces thereof with fire, and all the goodly vessels thereof.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:19

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:20

**Q1. Whom did Nebuchadnezzar carry away from the sword to Babylon?**  
**Answer:** Them that escaped from the sword.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:20

**Q2. Whose servants were they made?**  
**Answer:** Servants to him and his sons.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:20

**Q3. Until the reign of what kingdom did they serve him and his sons?**  
**Answer:** Until the reign of the kingdom of Persia.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:20

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:21

**Q1. To fulfill the word of the LORD by whose mouth was the land to enjoy her sabbaths?**  
**Answer:** By the mouth of Jeremiah.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:21

**Q2. How long did the land rest?**  
**Answer:** As long as she lay desolate.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:21

**Q3. To fulfill what was the land to keep sabbath, to fulfil threescore and ten years?**  
**Answer:** To fulfil threescore and ten years.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:21

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:22

**Q1. In what year of Cyrus king of Persia was the word of the LORD spoken by the mouth of Jeremiah stirred up?**  
**Answer:** In the first year of Cyrus king of Persia.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:22

**Q2. Whose spirit did the LORD stir up?**  
**Answer:** The spirit of Cyrus king of Persia.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:22

**Q3. What did Cyrus make throughout all his kingdom, including by writing?**  
**Answer:** A proclamation.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:22

---

### 2 Chronicles 36:23

**Q1. Who said, "All the kingdoms of the earth hath the LORD God of heaven given me"?**  
**Answer:** Cyrus king of Persia.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:23

**Q2. Who charged Cyrus to build him an house in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God of heaven.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:23

**Q3. Who among all his people is commanded to go up, and with whom is the LORD his God?**  
**Answer:** Who is there among you of all his people? The LORD his God be with him, and let him go up.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 36:23
