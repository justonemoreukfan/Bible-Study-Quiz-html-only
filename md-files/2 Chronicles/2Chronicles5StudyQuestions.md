# 2 Chronicles 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Chronicles 5:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of 2 Chronicles 5:1?**  
**Answer:** Thus all the work that Solomon made for the house of the LORD was finished.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 5:1

**Q2. What is declared next in 2 Chronicles 5:1?**  
**Answer:** and Solomon brought in all the things that David his father had dedicated.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 5:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in 2 Chronicles 5:1?**  
**Answer:** and the silver, and the gold, and all the instruments, put he among the treasures of the house of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 5:1
