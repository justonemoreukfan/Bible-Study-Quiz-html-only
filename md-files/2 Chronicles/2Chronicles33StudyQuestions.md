# 2 Chronicles 33 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:1

**Q1. How old was Manasseh when he began to reign?**  
**Answer:** Twelve years old.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:1

**Q2. How long did Manasseh reign in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Fifty and five years.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:1

**Q3. Where did Manasseh reign?**  
**Answer:** In Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:1

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:2

**Q1. What did Manasseh do in the sight of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** That which was evil in the sight of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:2

**Q2. Whose abominations did Manasseh follow?**  
**Answer:** The abominations of the heathen.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:2

**Q3. Whom did the heathen cast out before the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Whom the LORD had cast out before the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:2

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:3

**Q1. What did Manasseh build again?**  
**Answer:** The high places which his father Hezekiah had broken down.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:3

**Q2. What did Manasseh rear up for Baalim?**  
**Answer:** Altars.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:3

**Q3. What did Manasseh make and worship?**  
**Answer:** Groves, and worshipped all the host of heaven, and served them.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:3

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:4

**Q1. What did Manasseh build in the house of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Altars.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:4

**Q2. Concerning what house had the LORD said, "In Jerusalem shall my name be for ever"?**  
**Answer:** The house of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:4

**Q3. Where did the LORD say His name should be for ever?**  
**Answer:** In Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:4

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:5

**Q1. What did Manasseh build for all the host of heaven?**  
**Answer:** Altars.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:5

**Q2. In which courts of the house of the LORD did Manasseh build these altars?**  
**Answer:** In the two courts of the house of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:5

**Q3. What did Manasseh build altars for in the two courts?**  
**Answer:** For all the host of heaven.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:5

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:6

**Q1. Where did Manasseh cause his children to pass through the fire?**  
**Answer:** In the valley of the son of Hinnom.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:6

**Q2. What did Manasseh observe besides witchcraft and enchantment?**  
**Answer:** Times.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:6

**Q3. Whom did Manasseh deal with, provoking the LORD to anger?**  
**Answer:** With a familiar spirit, and with wizards.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:6

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:7

**Q1. What did Manasseh set in the house of God?**  
**Answer:** A carved image, the idol which he had made.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:7

**Q2. What had God said to David and to Solomon concerning the house?**  
**Answer:** In this house, and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen before all the tribes of Israel, will I put my name for ever.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:7

**Q3. Out of all the tribes of Israel, what had God chosen?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:7

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:8

**Q1. What will the LORD no more remove from out of the land if Israel will walk carefully?**  
**Answer:** The foot of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:8

**Q2. From out of what place would their foot no more be removed?**  
**Answer:** From out of the land which I have appointed for your fathers.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:8

**Q3. What condition was attached to Israel keeping their land according to all that God commanded?**  
**Answer:** So that they will take heed to do all that I have commanded them.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:8

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:9

**Q1. Who did Manasseh make to err?**  
**Answer:** Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:9

**Q2. To do what did Manasseh cause Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem to err?**  
**Answer:** To do worse than the heathen.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:9

**Q3. Whom had the LORD destroyed before the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The heathen whom the LORD had destroyed before the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:9

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:10

**Q1. Who spake to Manasseh and his people?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:10

**Q2. How did Manasseh and his people respond to the LORD speaking?**  
**Answer:** They would not hearken.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:10

**Q3. Who spake to Manasseh and his people?**  
**Answer:** The LORD (or implied: through His servants/prophets, though text states "And the LORD spake").  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:10

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:11

**Q1. Whom did the LORD bring upon Manasseh and his people?**  
**Answer:** The captains of the host of the king of Assyria.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:11

**Q2. How did they take Manasseh?**  
**Answer:** With thorns.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:11

**Q3. With what else was Manasseh bound and carried to Babylon?**  
**Answer:** With fetters of brass.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:11

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:12

**Q1. When Manasseh was in affliction, what did he do to the face of the LORD his God?**  
**Answer:** Besought the face of the LORD his God.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:12

**Q2. Before whom did Manasseh humble himself greatly?**  
**Answer:** Before the God of his fathers.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:12

**Q3. When did Manasseh humble himself greatly?**  
**Answer:** When he was in affliction.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:12

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:13

**Q1. What did God do to Manasseh's supplication?**  
**Answer:** Was intreated of him.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:13

**Q2. Whose supplication did God hear?**  
**Answer:** Manasseh's supplication.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:13

**Q3. Where did God bring Manasseh back to after hearing his supplication?**  
**Answer:** To Jerusalem into his kingdom.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:13

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:14

**Q1. What did Manasseh build without the city of David?**  
**Answer:** A wall.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:14

**Q2. On the west side of what did Manasseh build the wall?**  
**Answer:** Gihon.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:14

**Q3. Where did the wall extend to, even to the entering in of?**  
**Answer:** The fish gate.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:14

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:15

**Q1. What did Manasseh take away out of the house of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The strange gods, and the idol.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:15

**Q2. What did Manasseh take away that he had built in the mount of the house of the LORD, and in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** All the altars.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:15

**Q3. Where did Manasseh cast all the altars after he took them away?**  
**Answer:** Without the city.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:15

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:16

**Q1. What did Manasseh repair belonging to the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The altar of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:16

**Q2. What did Manasseh sacrifice upon the altar of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Peace offerings and thank offerings.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:16

**Q3. Whom did Manasseh command to serve the LORD God of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Judah.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:16

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:17

**Q1. Where did the people still sacrifice?**  
**Answer:** In the high places.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:17

**Q2. Unto whom did the people sacrifice in the high places?**  
**Answer:** Unto the LORD their God only.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:17

**Q3. Did the people sacrifice unto the LORD their God only in the high places?**  
**Answer:** Yet the people did sacrifice still in the high places.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:17

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:18

**Q1. What acts of Manasseh are written in the book of the kings of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The rest of the acts of Manasseh, and his prayer unto his God.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:18

**Q2. Who spake in the name of the LORD God of Israel to Manasseh?**  
**Answer:** The seers.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:18

**Q3. In whose name did the seers speak to Manasseh?**  
**Answer:** In the name of the LORD God of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:18

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:19

**Q1. Whose prayer is written among the sayings of the seers?**  
**Answer:** Manasseh's prayer.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:19

**Q2. How was God entreated regarding Manasseh's prayer and his entreaty?**  
**Answer:** God was intreated of him.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:19

**Q3. What is written concerning Manasseh's sin and trespass, and the places where he built what?**  
**Answer:** His sin, and his trespass, and the places wherein he built high places, and set up groves and graven images.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:19

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:20

**Q1. With whom did Manasseh sleep?**  
**Answer:** With his fathers.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:20

**Q2. Where was Manasseh buried?**  
**Answer:** In his own house.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:20

**Q3. Who reigned in Manasseh's stead?**  
**Answer:** Amon his son.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:20

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:21

**Q1. How old was Amon when he began to reign?**  
**Answer:** Two and twenty years old.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:21

**Q2. How long did Amon reign in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Two years.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:21

**Q3. Where did Amon reign?**  
**Answer:** In Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:21

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:22

**Q1. What did Amon do in the sight of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** That which was evil in the sight of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:22

**Q2. Who had done as Amon did regarding carved images?**  
**Answer:** Manasseh his father.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:22

**Q3. Whom did Amon sacrifice unto and serve?**  
**Answer:** All the carved images which Manasseh his father had made.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:22

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:23

**Q1. Before whom did Amon not humble himself?**  
**Answer:** Before the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:23

**Q2. As who had humbled himself, did Amon humble himself?**  
**Answer:** As Manasseh his father had humbled himself.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:23

**Q3. What did Amon trespass more and more?**  
**Answer:** Amon trespassed more and more.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:23

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:24

**Q1. Against whom did Amon's servants conspire?**  
**Answer:** Against him.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:24

**Q2. Where did Amon's servants slay him?**  
**Answer:** In his own house.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:24

**Q3. Who conspired against Amon?**  
**Answer:** His servants.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:24

---

### 2 Chronicles 33:25

**Q1. Who slew all them that had conspired against king Amon?**  
**Answer:** The people of the land.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:25

**Q2. Whom did the people of the land make king in Amon's stead?**  
**Answer:** Josiah his son.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:25

**Q3. Who was made king in Amon's stead?**  
**Answer:** Josiah his son.  
**Verse:** 2 Chronicles 33:25
