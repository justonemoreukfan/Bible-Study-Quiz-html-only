# 2 Samuel 22 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 22:1

**Q1. Whose words are these that David spake unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The words of this song.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 22:1

**Q2. To whom did David speak the words of this song?**  
**Answer:** Unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 22:1

**Q3. On what occasion did David speak these words unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** In the day that the LORD had delivered him out of the hand of all his enemies, and out of the hand of Saul.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 22:1

---

### 2 Samuel 22:2

**Q4. What did David call the LORD in verse 2?**  
**Answer:** My rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 22:2

---

### 2 Samuel 22:3

**Q5. What metaphor does David use for God of my rock in whom he will trust?**  
**Answer:** My shield, and the horn of my salvation.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 22:3

**Q6. What else does David call his high tower and his refuge?**  
**Answer:** My high tower, and my refuge, my saviour.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 22:3

**Q7. From what does David say God saves him?**  
**Answer:** Thou savest me from violence.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 22:3

---

### 2 Samuel 22:4

**Q8. Whom did David call upon?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 22:4

**Q9. How is the LORD described in relation to being worthy?**  
**Answer:** Who is worthy to be praised.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 22:4

**Q10. From whom shall David be saved when he calls upon the LORD?**  
**Answer:** So shall I be saved from mine enemies.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 22:4
