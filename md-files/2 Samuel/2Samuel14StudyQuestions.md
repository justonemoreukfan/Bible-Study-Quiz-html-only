# 2 Samuel 14 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 14:1

**Q1.** Whose son did Joab perceive to be against Absalom?  
**Answer:** David's son.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:1

**Q2.** Who perceived that the king's heart was toward Absalom?  
**Answer:** Joab the son of Zeruiah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:1

**Q3.** Where did Joab send to, and whom did he fetch from thence?  
**Answer:** To Tekoah, and fetched thence a wise woman.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:1

---

### 2 Samuel 14:2

**Q1.** To whom did Joab say, I pray thee, feign thyself to be a mourner?  
**Answer:** To the wise woman.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:2

**Q2.** What did Joab tell the woman to put on?  
**Answer:** Mourning weeds.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:2

**Q3.** What did Joab tell the woman not to anoint herself with?  
**Answer:** With oil.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:2

---

### 2 Samuel 14:3

**Q1.** To whom did Joab tell the woman to go?  
**Answer:** To the king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:3

**Q2.** What did Joab tell the woman to speak to the king?  
**Answer:** On this manner.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:3

**Q3.** What did Joab put in the woman's mouth?  
**Answer:** The words.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:3

---

### 2 Samuel 14:4

**Q1.** Who spoke to the king?  
**Answer:** The woman of Tekoah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:4

**Q2.** What did the woman do when she spoke to the king?  
**Answer:** She fell on her face to the ground, and did obeisance.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:4

**Q3.** What did the woman say to the king?  
**Answer:** Help, O king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:4

---

### 2 Samuel 14:5

**Q1.** What did the king ask the woman?  
**Answer:** What aileth thee?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:5

**Q2.** What did the woman answer?  
**Answer:** I am indeed a widow woman, and mine husband is dead.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:5

---

### 2 Samuel 14:6

**Q1.** How many sons did the woman have?  
**Answer:** Two sons.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:6

---

### 2 Samuel 14:7

**Q1.** Who rose up against the woman's family?  
**Answer:** The whole family.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:7

---

### 2 Samuel 14:8

**Q1.** What did the king say unto the woman?  
**Answer:** Go to thine house, and I will give charge concerning thee.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:8

---

### 2 Samuel 14:9

**Q1.** What did the woman of Tekoah say unto the king?  
**Answer:** My lord, O king, the iniquity be on me, and on my father's house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:9

---

### 2 Samuel 14:10

**Q1.** What did the king say concerning whosoever speaks to the woman?  
**Answer:** Bring him to me, and he shall not touch thee any more.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:10

---

### 2 Samuel 14:11

**Q1.** What did the woman say the king should remember?  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:11

---

### 2 Samuel 14:12

**Q1.** What did the woman ask permission to speak?  
**Answer:** One word unto my lord the king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:12

---

### 2 Samuel 14:13

**Q1.** Why did the woman ask why the king thought such a thing against the people of God?  
**Answer:** Because the king doth speak this thing as one which is faulty.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:13

---

### 2 Samuel 14:14

**Q1.** What did the woman say we must needs die as?  
**Answer:** Water spilt on the ground, which cannot be gathered up again.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:14

---

### 2 Samuel 14:15

**Q1.** Why did the woman come to speak of this thing unto my lord the king?  
**Answer:** Because the people have made me afraid.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:15

---

### 2 Samuel 14:16

**Q1.** For what purpose did the king hear the woman?  
**Answer:** To deliver his handmaid out of the hand of the man that would destroy me and my son together out of the inheritance of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:16

---

### 2 Samuel 14:17

**Q1.** What did the woman say the word of my lord the king shall be as?  
**Answer:** Comfortable.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:17

---

### 2 Samuel 14:18

**Q1.** What did the king answer and say unto the woman?  
**Answer:** Hide not from me, I pray thee, the thing that I shall ask thee.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:18

---

### 2 Samuel 14:19

**Answer:** The hand of Joab.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:19

---

### 2 Samuel 14:20

**Q1.** To fetch about this form of speech has thy servant Joab done what?

**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:20

**Q1.** What did the king say unto Joab?

**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:21

**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:22

**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:23

**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:24

---

### 2 Samuel 14:25

**Q1.** Who was praised for his beauty in all Israel?  
**Answer:** Absalom.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:25

---

### 2 Samuel 14:26

**Q1.** What was the weight of the hair on Absalom's head when he polled it?  
**Answer:** Two hundred shekels after the king's weight.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:26

---

### 2 Samuel 14:27

**Q1.** How many sons were born unto Absalom?  
**Answer:** Three sons.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:27

---

### 2 Samuel 14:28

**Q1.** How long did Absalom dwell two full years in Jerusalem without doing what?  
**Answer:** Without seeing the king's face.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:28

---

### 2 Samuel 14:29

**Q1. To whom did Absalom send to send him to the king?**  
**Answer:** Joab.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:29

**Q2. How many times did Absalom send to Joab?**  
**Answer:** Twice.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:29

**Q3. What did Joab refuse to do when Absalom sent for him?**  
**Answer:** He would not come to him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:29

---

### 2 Samuel 14:30

**Q1. What did Absalom say unto his servants concerning Joab's field?**  
**Answer:** See, Joab's field is near mine, and he hath barley there; go and set it on fire.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:30

**Q2. Whose servants set Absalom's field on fire?**  
**Answer:** Absalom's servants.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:30

**Q3. What did Absalom's servants do to Joab's field?**  
**Answer:** They set the field on fire.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:30

---

### 2 Samuel 14:31

**Q1. Who arose and came to Absalom unto his house?**  
**Answer:** Joab.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:31

**Q2. What did Joab ask Absalom when he came to his house?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore have thy servants set my field on fire?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:31

**Q3. Whose servants did Joab ask about setting his field on fire?**  
**Answer:** Absalom's servants.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:31

---

### 2 Samuel 14:32

**Q1. What did Absalom answer Joab about sending messengers to him?**  
**Answer:** Behold, I sent unto thee, saying, Come hither, that I may send thee to the king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:32

**Q2. From where did Absalom say it had been good for him to be there still?**  
**Answer:** From Geshur.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:32

**Q3. What did Absalom ask to see?**  
**Answer:** The king's face.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:32

---

### 2 Samuel 14:33

**Q1. To whom did Joab come and tell these things?**  
**Answer:** To the king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:33

**Q2. Whom did the king call for?**  
**Answer:** Absalom.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:33

**Q3. What did Absalom do to the king when he came to him?**  
**Answer:** He bowed himself on his face to the ground before the king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 14:33
