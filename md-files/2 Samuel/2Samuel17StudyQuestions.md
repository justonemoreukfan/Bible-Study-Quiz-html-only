# 2 Samuel 17 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 17:1

**Q1. What did Ahithophel say to Absalom regarding twelve thousand men?**  
**Answer:** Let me now choose out twelve thousand men, and I will arise and pursue after David this night.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 17:1

**Q2. Whose help did Ahithophel want to enlist with his twelve thousand men?**  
**Answer:** David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 17:1

**Q3. When did Ahithophel propose to arise and pursue David?**  
**Answer:** This night.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 17:1

---

### 2 Samuel 17:2

**Q1. How did Ahithophel plan to find David while he was weary and weak handed?**  
**Answer:** And I will come upon him while he is weary and weak handed.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 17:2

**Q2. What effect did Ahithophel say his attack would have on David?**  
**Answer:** And will make him afraid.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 17:2

**Q3. What would happen to all the people who were with David?**  
**Answer:** And all the people that are with him shall flee.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 17:2

### 2 Samuel 17:3
