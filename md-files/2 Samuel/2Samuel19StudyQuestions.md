# 2 Samuel 19 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 19:1

**Q1. Who was told that the king weeps and mourns for Absalom?**  
**Answer:** Joab.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 19:1

**Q2. For whom does the king weep and mourn?**  
**Answer:** Absalom.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 19:1

**Q3. What did the king do for Absalom?**  
**Answer:** Weep and mourn.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 19:1

---

### 2 Samuel 19:2

**Q1. Into what was the victory turned that day?**  
**Answer:** Mourning.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 19:2

**Q2. For whom was the victory turned into mourning unto all the people?**  
**Answer:** The king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 19:2

**Q3. What did the people hear that day concerning the king?**  
**Answer:** That the king was grieved for his son.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 19:2

**Verse:** 2 Samuel 19:3
