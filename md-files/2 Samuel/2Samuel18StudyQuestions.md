# 2 Samuel 18 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 18:1

**Q1. Who numbered the people that were with him?**  
**Answer:** David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:1

**Q2. Whom did David set over the people?**  
**Answer:** Captains of thousands and captains of hundreds.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:1

---

### 2 Samuel 18:2

**Q3. Into how many parts did David send forth the people?**  
**Answer:** A third part.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:2

**Q4. Under whose hand did David send a third part of the people?**  
**Answer:** Joab, Abishai, and Ittai.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:2

**Q5. What did the king say he would do with the people?**  
**Answer:** "I will surely go forth with you myself also."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:2

---

### 2 Samuel 18:3

**Q6. What did the people say the king should not do?**  
**Answer:** Go forth.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:3

**Q7. If they fled away, what would the enemy not care for?**  
**Answer:** Them.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:3

**Q8. How many of them would the enemy not care for even if half died?**  
**Answer:** "neither if half of us die will they care for us."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:3

---

### 2 Samuel 18:4

**Q9. What did the king say unto them?**  
**Answer:** "What seemeth you best I will do."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:4

**Q10. Where did the king stand?**  
**Answer:** By the gate side.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:4

**Q11. How did all the people come out?**  
**Answer:** By hundreds and by thousands.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:4

---

### 2 Samuel 18:5

**Q12. Whom did the king command Joab and Abishai and Ittai?**  
**Answer:** "Deal gently for my sake with the young man, even with Absalom."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:5

**Q13. Who heard when the king gave all the captains charge concerning Absalom?**  
**Answer:** All the people.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:5

**Q14. Whose name was mentioned in the king's charge to the captains?**  
**Answer:** Absalom.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:5

---

### 2 Samuel 18:6

**Q15. Into the field where did the people go out against Israel?**  
**Answer:** Into the wood.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:6

**Q16. Where did the battle take place?**  
**Answer:** In the wood of Ephraim.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:6

**Q17. Who went out into the field against Israel?**  
**Answer:** The people.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:6

---

### 2 Samuel 18:7

**Q18. Who were slain there by the servants of David?**  
**Answer:** The people of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:7

**Q19. What was there on that day?**  
**Answer:** A great slaughter of twenty thousand men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:7

**Q20. How many men were slain?**  
**Answer:** Twenty thousand men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:7

---

### 2 Samuel 18:8

**Q21. Where was the battle scattered?**  
**Answer:** Over the face of all the country.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:8

**Q22. What devoured more people that day?**  
**Answer:** The wood.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:8

**Q23. What devoured more people than the sword devoured?**  
**Answer:** The wood.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:8

---

### 2 Samuel 18:9

**Q24. Whom did Absalom meet?**  
**Answer:** The servants of David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 18:9
