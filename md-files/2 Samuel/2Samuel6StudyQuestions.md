# 2 Samuel 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 6:1

**Q1. Who did David gather together again?**  
**Answer:** All the chosen men of Israel, thirty thousand.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:1

**Q2. How many chosen men of Israel did David gather together?**  
**Answer:** Thirty thousand.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:1

**Q3. What did David gather again out of Israel?**  
**Answer:** All the chosen men of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:1

---

### 2 Samuel 6:2

**Q1. And what did David arise and go with?**  
**Answer:** All the people that were with him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:2

**Q2. Where did David and the people go from Baale of Judah?**  
**Answer:** To bring up from thence the ark of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:2

**Q3. Whose name is called on the ark of God mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The name of the LORD of hosts that dwelleth between the cherubims.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:2

---

### 2 Samuel 6:3

**Q1. Upon what did they set the ark of God?**  
**Answer:** Upon a new cart.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:3

**Q2. From where did they bring the ark of God out of?**  
**Answer:** Out of the house of Abinadab that was in Gibeah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:3

**Q3. Who drave the new cart?**  
**Answer:** Uzzah and Ahio, the sons of Abinadab.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:3

---

### 2 Samuel 6:4

**Q1. From where did they bring the new cart with the ark of God?**  
**Answer:** Out of the house of Abinadab which was at Gibeah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:4

**Q2. Who went before the ark of God?**  
**Answer:** Ahio.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:4

---

### 2 Samuel 6:5

**Q1. Who played before the LORD on all manner of instruments made of fir wood?**  
**Answer:** David and all the house of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:5

**Q2. What instruments were played before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Harps, psalteries, timbrels, cornets, and cymbals.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:5

**Q3. Before whom did David and all the house of Israel play?**  
**Answer:** Before the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:5

---

### 2 Samuel 6:6

**Q1. To whose threshingfloor did they come?**  
**Answer:** Nachon's threshingfloor.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:6

**Q2. What did Uzzah put forth to the ark of God?**  
**Answer:** His hand.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:6

**Q3. Why did Uzzah put forth his hand to the ark of God?**  
**Answer:** For the oxen shook it.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:6

---

### 2 Samuel 6:7

**Q1. Whose anger was kindled against Uzzah?**  
**Answer:** The anger of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:7

**Q2. Why did God smite Uzzah there?**  
**Answer:** For his error.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:7

**Q3. Where did Uzzah die?**  
**Answer:** By the ark of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:7

---

### 2 Samuel 6:8

**Q1. How did David feel because the LORD had made a breach upon Uzzah?**  
**Answer:** Displeased.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:8

**Q2. What name was the place called to this day?**  
**Answer:** Perezuzzah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:8

**Q3. What had the LORD made upon Uzzah?**  
**Answer:** A breach.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:8

---

### 2 Samuel 6:9

**Q1. How did David feel toward the LORD that day?**  
**Answer:** Afraid.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:9

**Q2. What question did David ask concerning the ark of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** How shall the ark of the LORD come to me?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:9

**Q3. Who did David say the ark of the LORD should come to?**  
**Answer:** Me.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:9

---

### 2 Samuel 6:10

**Q1. What would David not do with the ark of the LORD unto him into the city of David?**  
**Answer:** Would not remove it.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:10

**Q2. Where did David carry the ark aside?**  
**Answer:** Into the house of Obededom the Gittite.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:10

**Q3. Whose house was the ark carried aside into?**  
**Answer:** Obededom the Gittite.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:10

---

### 2 Samuel 6:11

**Q1. Where did the ark of the LORD continue?**  
**Answer:** In the house of Obededom the Gittite.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:11

**Q2. How long did the ark continue in the house of Obededom the Gittite?**  
**Answer:** Three months.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:11

**Q3. What did the LORD do to Obededom, and all his household?**  
**Answer:** Blessed Obededom, and all his household.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:11

---

### 2 Samuel 6:12

**Q1. Who was told that the LORD had blessed the house of Obededom?**  
**Answer:** King David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:12

**Q2. What did the LORD bless because of the ark of God?**  
**Answer:** The house of Obededom, and all that pertained unto him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:12

**Q3. From where did David go and bring up the ark of God with gladness?**  
**Answer:** From the house of Obededom into the city of David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:12

---

### 2 Samuel 6:13

**Q1. How many paces did they that bare the ark of the LORD go?**  
**Answer:** Six paces.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:13

**Q2. What did David sacrifice?**  
**Answer:** Oxen and fatlings.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:13

**Q3. Who bare the ark of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** They that bare the ark of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:13

---

### 2 Samuel 6:14

**Q1. How did David dance before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** With all his might.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:14

**Q2. What was David girded with?**  
**Answer:** A linen ephod.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:14

**Q3. Before whom did David dance?**  
**Answer:** Before the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:14

---

### 2 Samuel 6:15

**Q1. Who brought up the ark of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** David and all the house of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:15

**Q2. With what noise was the ark of the LORD brought up?**  
**Answer:** With shouting, and with the sound of the trumpet.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:15

**Q3. Who brought up the ark with shouting and sound of trumpet along with David?**  
**Answer:** All the house of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:15

---

### 2 Samuel 6:16

**Q1. What came into the city of David?**  
**Answer:** The ark of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:16

**Q2. Who looked through a window?**  
**Answer:** Michal Saul's daughter.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:16

**Q3. What did Michal see King David doing, and how did she feel?**  
**Answer:** She saw king David leaping and dancing before the LORD; and she despised him in her heart.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:16

---

### 2 Samuel 6:17

**Q1. Where did they bring in the ark of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Into his place.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:17

**Q2. What was set in the midst of the tabernacle that David had pitched for it?**  
**Answer:** The ark of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:17

**Q3. What did David offer before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Burnt offerings and peace offerings.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:17

---

### 2 Samuel 6:18

**Q1. As soon as David had made an end of offering burnt offerings and peace offerings, what did he do?**  
**Answer:** He blessed the people in the name of the LORD of hosts.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:18

**Q2. In whose name did David bless the people?**  
**Answer:** In the name of the LORD of hosts.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:18

**Q3. Who did David bless?**  
**Answer:** The people.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:18

---

### 2 Samuel 6:19

**Q1. To whom did David deal among all the people?**  
**Answer:** Among the whole multitude of Israel, as well to the women as men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:19

**Q2. What did David divide to every one of the people?**  
**Answer:** A cake of bread, and a good piece of flesh, and a flagon of wine.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:19

**Q3. After distributing gifts, where did all the people depart to?**  
**Answer:** Every one to his house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:19

---

### 2 Samuel 6:20

**Q1. To whom did David return to bless his household?**  
**Answer:** Michal the daughter of Saul.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:20

**Q2. How did Michal come out to meet David?**  
**Answer:** Out to meet David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:20

**Q3. What did Michal say the king of Israel had done to himself today?**  
**Answer:** Glorified himself.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:20

---

### 2 Samuel 6:21

**Q1. Before whom did David say he was playing?**  
**Answer:** Before the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:21

**Q2. Who did David say the LORD chose before Michal's father, and before all his house?**  
**Answer:** David himself.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:21

**Q3. What was David appointed to be over?**  
**Answer:** Ruler over the people of the LORD, over Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:21

---

### 2 Samuel 6:22

**Q1. How much more vile would David yet be?**  
**Answer:** Yet more vile than this.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:22

**Q2. In whose sight would David be base?**  
**Answer:** In mine own sight.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:22

**Q3. By the maidservants which Michal spoke of, what would David be had in?**  
**Answer:** Of them shall I be had in honour.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:22

---

**Answer:** Michal the daughter of Saul.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:23

**Q2. Until what day did Michal have no child?**  
**Answer:** Unto the day of her death.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 6:23
