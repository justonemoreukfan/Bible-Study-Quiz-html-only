# 2 Samuel 7 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 7:1

**Q1. Who sat in his house?**  
**Answer:** The king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:1

**Q2. From whom had the Lord given the king rest round about?**  
**Answer:** From all his enemies.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:1

**Q3. Who had given the king rest round about from all his enemies?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:1

---

### 2 Samuel 7:2

**Q1. To whom did the king say, See now, I dwell in an house of cedar?**  
**Answer:** Unto Nathan the prophet.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:2

**Q2. What did the king say he dwelt in?**  
**Answer:** An house of cedar.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:2

**Q3. Where did the ark of God dwell?**  
**Answer:** Within curtains.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:2

---

### 2 Samuel 7:3

**Q1. To whom did Nathan say, Go, do all that is in thine heart?**  
**Answer:** To the king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:3

**Q2. Why was Nathan able to tell the king to do all that was in his heart?**  
**Answer:** For the Lord is with thee.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:3

**Q3. Whose heart was mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The king's heart.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:3

---

### 2 Samuel 7:4

**Q1. When did the word of the Lord come unto Nathan?**  
**Answer:** That night.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:4

**Q2. Whose word came unto Nathan?**  
**Answer:** The word of the Lord.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:4

**Q3. To whom did the word of the Lord come that night?**  
**Answer:** Unto Nathan.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:4

---

### 2 Samuel 7:5

**Q1. Who was told to go and tell my servant David?**  
**Answer:** Nathan.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:5

**Q2. What did the Lord ask about building Him an house?**  
**Answer:** Shalt thou build me an house for me to dwell in?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:5

**Q3. For how long had the Lord not dwelt in an house?**  
**Answer:** Since the time that I brought up the children of Israel out of Egypt, even to this day.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:5

---

### 2 Samuel 7:6

**Q1. In what did the Lord walk?**  
**Answer:** In a tent and in a tabernacle.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:6

**Q2. Where did the Lord walk since the time He brought up the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** In a tent and in a tabernacle.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:6

**Q3. Out of what land were the children of Israel brought?**  
**Answer:** Out of Egypt.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:6

---

### 2 Samuel 7:7

**Q1. In all the places wherein I have walked with all the children of Israel, did I speak a word to whom?**  
**Answer:** To any of the tribes of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:7

**Q2. Whom did the Lord command to feed His people Israel?**  
**Answer:** The judges of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:7

**Q3. What did the Lord ask the judges of Israel to build Him?**  
**Answer:** Why build ye not me an house of cedar?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:7

---

### 2 Samuel 7:8

**Q1. Now therefore so shalt thou say unto whom?**  
**Answer:** Unto my servant David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:8

**Q2. From where did the Lord take David?**  
**Answer:** From the sheepcote.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:8

**Q3. To what position was David appointed after being taken from the sheepcote?**  
**Answer:** To be ruler over my people, over Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:8

---

### 2 Samuel 7:9

**Q1. With whom was the Lord whithersoever he went?**  
**Answer:** With David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:9

**Q2. What had the Lord cut off out of the sight of David?**  
**Answer:** All his enemies.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:9

**Q3. What did the Lord promise to make unto David?**  
**Answer:** A great name, like unto the name of the great men that are in the earth.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:9

---

### 2 Samuel 7:10

**Q1. Moreover what will the Lord appoint for His people Israel?**  
**Answer:** A place.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:10

**Q2. Where will the Lord plant His people Israel so they may dwell in a place of their own?**  
**Answer:** In their own place.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:10

**Q3. Who shall afflict the children of Israel no more?**  
**Answer:** The children of wickedness.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:10

---

### 2 Samuel 7:11

**Q1. Since what time were the judges commanded to be over My people Israel?**  
**Answer:** Since the time that I commanded judges to be over my people Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:11

**Q2. Who caused David to rest from all his enemies?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:11

**Q3. What did the Lord tell David that He would make him?**  
**Answer:** An house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:11

**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:12

**Verse:** 2 Samuel 7:12

### 2 Samuel 7:14
