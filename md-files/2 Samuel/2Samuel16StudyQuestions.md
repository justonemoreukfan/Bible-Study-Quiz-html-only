# 2 Samuel 16 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 16:1

**Q1. When David was a little past the top of the hill, who met him?**  
**Answer:** Ziba the servant of Mephibosheth.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:1

**Q2. What did Ziba bring with him besides a couple of asses saddled?**  
**Answer:** Two hundred loaves of bread, and an hundred bunches of raisins, and an hundred of summer fruits, and a bottle of wine.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:1

**Q3. What was the purpose of the wine that Ziba brought?**  
**Answer:** That such as be faint in the wilderness might drink.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:1

---

### 2 Samuel 16:2

**Q4. What did the king ask Ziba?**  
**Answer:** What meanest thou by these?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:2

**Q5. According to Ziba, what were the asses intended for?**  
**Answer:** For the king's household to ride on.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:2

**Q6. What were the bread and summer fruit intended for?**  
**Answer:** For the young men to eat.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:2

---

### 2 Samuel 16:3

**Q7. What did the king ask concerning his master's son?**  
**Answer:** And where is thy master's son?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:3

**Q8. What did Ziba say Mephibosheth said at Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** To day shall the house of Israel restore me the kingdom of my father.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:3

**Q9. Whose kingdom did Mephibosheth expect to be restored?**  
**Answer:** The kingdom of his father.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:3

---

### 2 Samuel 16:4

**Q10. What did the king say to Ziba regarding Mephibosheth's possessions?**  
**Answer:** Behold, thine are all that pertained unto Mephibosheth.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:4

**Q11. What was Ziba's response to the king?**  
**Answer:** I humbly beseech thee that I may find grace in thy sight, my lord, O king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:4

**Q12. By what title did Ziba address David?**  
**Answer:** My lord, O king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:4

---

### 2 Samuel 16:5

**Q13. When King David came to Bahurim, who came out from there?**  
**Answer:** Shimei the son of Gera.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:5

**Q14. Of whose family was Shimei?**  
**Answer:** Of the family of the house of Saul.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:5

**Q15. What did Shimei do as he came out?**  
**Answer:** He came forth, and cursed still as he came.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:5

---

### 2 Samuel 16:6

**Q16. What did Shimei cast at David and his servants?**  
**Answer:** Stones.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:6

**Q17. Who else was Shimei casting stones at besides David?**  
**Answer:** All the servants of king David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:6

**Q18. Who was with David besides his servants?**  
**Answer:** All the people and all the mighty men were on his right hand and on his left.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:6

---

### 2 Samuel 16:7

**Q19. What did Shimei say as he cursed David?**  
**Answer:** Come out, come out, thou bloody man, and thou man of Belial.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:7

**Q20. By what terms of insult did Shimei address David?**  
**Answer:** Thou bloody man, and thou man of Belial.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:7

---

### 2 Samuel 16:8

**Q21. Whose blood did Shimei say the LORD had returned upon David?**  
**Answer:** The blood of the house of Saul.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:8

**Q22. In whose stead did Shimei say David now reigned?**  
**Answer:** In whose stead thou hast reigned.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:8

**Q23. To whom did Shimei say the LORD had delivered the kingdom?**  
**Answer:** Into the hand of Absalom thy son.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:8

---

### 2 Samuel 16:9

**Q24. Who spoke to the king concerning Shimei's cursing?**  
**Answer:** Abishai the son of Zeruiah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:9

**Q25. What did Abishai call Shimei?**  
**Answer:** This dead dog.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:9

**Q26. What did Abishai ask permission to do?**  
**Answer:** Let me go over, I pray thee, and take off his head.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:9

---

### 2 Samuel 16:10

**Q27. How did the king address Abishai and the sons of Zeruiah?**  
**Answer:** What have I to do with you, ye sons of Zeruiah?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:10

**Q28. Why did the king say Shimei cursed?**  
**Answer:** Because the LORD hath said unto him, Curse David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:10

**Q29. What question did David ask about who shall say, Wherefore hast thou done so?**  
**Answer:** Who shall then say, Wherefore hast thou done so?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:10

---

### 2 Samuel 16:11

**Q30. Whom did David say sought his life?**  
**Answer:** My son, which came forth of my bowels.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:11

**Q31. Who did David say now did much more seek his life?**  
**Answer:** How much more now may this Benjamite do it?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:11

**Q32. What command did David give regarding Shimei?**  
**Answer:** Let him alone, and let him curse; for the LORD hath bidden him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:11

---

### 2 Samuel 16:12

**Q33. What did David hope the LORD would look on?**  
**Answer:** Mine affliction.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:12

**Q34. What did David hope the LORD would requite him with instead of Shimei's cursing?**  
**Answer:** Good for his cursing this day.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:12

---

### 2 Samuel 16:13

**Q35. How did David and his men go along the way?**  
**Answer:** And as David and his men went by the way.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:13

**Q36. Where did Shimei go alongside him?**  
**Answer:** Shimei went along on the hill's side over against him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:13

**Q37. What did Shimei do as he went along the hill's side?**  
**Answer:** Curser as he went, and threw stones at him, and cast dust.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:13

---

### 2 Samuel 16:14

**Q38. Who came to Jordan weary?**  
**Answer:** The king, and all the people that were with him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:14

**Q39. Where did they refresh themselves?**  
**Answer:** And refreshed themselves there.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:14

---

### 2 Samuel 16:15

**Q40. Who came to Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Absalom, and all the people the men of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:15

**Q41. Who else came with Absalom to Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Ahithophel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:15

---

### 2 Samuel 16:16

**Q42. Who came to Absalom?**  
**Answer:** Hushai the Archite, David's friend.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:16

**Q43. What did Hushai say to Absalom?**  
**Answer:** God save the king, God save the king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:16

---

### 2 Samuel 16:17

**Q44. What question did Absalom ask Hushai?**  
**Answer:** Is this thy kindness to thy friend?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:17

**Q45. Whose friend did Absalom mention?**  
**Answer:** Thy friend David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:17

**Q46. What did Absalom ask about why Hushai went not with his friend?**  
**Answer:** Why wentest thou not with thy friend?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:17

---

### 2 Samuel 16:18

**Q47. To whom did Hushai say he would belong?**  
**Answer:** Nay; but whom the LORD, and this people, and all the men of Israel, choose, his will I be, and with him will I abide.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:18

**Q48. Whose choice of king did Hushai mention alongside the LORD's?**  
**Answer:** This people, and all the men of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:18

---

### 2 Samuel 16:19

**Q49. Whom did Hushai say he should serve?**  
**Answer:** And again, whom should I serve? should I not serve in the presence of his son?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:19

**Q50. Whose presence did Hushai compare to serving David?**  
**Answer:** As I have served in thy father's presence, so will I be in thy presence.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:19

---

### 2 Samuel 16:20

**Q51. To whom did Absalom speak?**  
**Answer:** Then said Absalom to Ahithophel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:20

**Q52. What did Absalom ask Ahithophel to give?**  
**Answer:** Give counsel among you what we shall do.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:20

---

### 2 Samuel 16:21

**Q53. What did Ahithophel advise Absalom to do with his father's concubines?**  
**Answer:** Go in unto thy father's concubines, which he hath left to keep the house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:21

**Q54. What did Ahithophel say all Israel would hear?**  
**Answer:** That thou art abhorred of thy father.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:21

**Q55. What would happen to the hands of all that were with Absalom if he followed this counsel?**  
**Answer:** Then shall the hands of all that are with thee be strong.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:21

---

### 2 Samuel 16:22

**Q56. What did they pitch for Absalom?**  
**Answer:** A tent upon the top of the house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:22

**Q57. In whose sight did Absalom go in unto his father's concubines?**  
**Answer:** In the sight of all Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:22

---

### 2 Samuel 16:23

**Q58. How was the counsel of Ahithophel esteemed in those days?**  
**Answer:** As if a man had enquired at the oracle of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:23

**Q59. Who regarded Ahithophel's counsel as the oracle of God?**  
**Answer:** Both David and Absalom.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 16:23
