# 2 Samuel 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 8:1

**Q1. Who did David smite and subdue?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:1

**Q2. Whose hands did David take from the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** Methegammah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:1

**Q3. What did David take out of the hand of the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** Methegammah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:1

---

### 2 Samuel 8:2

**Q2. Who did David smite, and how did he measure them?**  
**Answer:** Moab, measuring them with a line, casting them down to the ground.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:2

**Q3. How many lines of people did David measure to be put to death?**  
**Answer:** With two lines measured he to put to death.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:2

**Q4. How many full lines did David keep alive?**  
**Answer:** With one full line to keep alive.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:2

**Q5. What became of the Moabites after this?**  
**Answer:** They became David's servants, and brought gifts.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:2

---

### 2 Samuel 8:3

**Q6. Who was the son of Rehob that David smote?**  
**Answer:** Hadadezer.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:3

**Q7. Whose king was Hadadezer when David smote him?**  
**Answer:** King of Zobah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:3

**Q8. Where was Hadadezer going when David smote him?**  
**Answer:** As he went to recover his border at the river Euphrates.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:3

---

### 2 Samuel 8:4

**Q9. How many horsemen did David take from Hadadezer?**  
**Answer:** Seven hundred horsemen.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:4

**Q10. How many footmen did David take from him?**  
**Answer:** Twenty thousand footmen.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:4

**Q11. What did David do to the chariot horses, except for how many?**  
**Answer:** He houghed all the chariot horses, but reserved of them for an hundred chariots.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:4

---

### 2 Samuel 8:5

**Q12. When the Syrians of Damascus came to succor Hadadezer, how many of them did David slay?**  
**Answer:** Two and twenty thousand men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:5

**Q13. Whose king was Hadadezer, whom the Syrians came to help?**  
**Answer:** King of Zobah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:5

**Q14. From what place were the Syrians who came to help?**  
**Answer:** Damascus.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:5

---

### 2 Samuel 8:6

**Q15. Where did David put garrisons?**  
**Answer:** In Syria of Damascus.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:6

**Q16. What did the Syrians become to David?**  
**Answer:** Servants, and brought gifts.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:6

**Q17. Who preserved David whithersoever he went?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:6

---

### 2 Samuel 8:7

**Q18. What did David take from the servants of Hadadezer?**  
**Answer:** The shields of gold.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:7

**Q19. To where did David bring the shields of gold?**  
**Answer:** To Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:7

**Q20. What material were the shields made of that David took?**  
**Answer:** Gold.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:7

---

### 2 Samuel 8:8

**Q21. From which cities of Hadadezer did King David take exceeding much brass?**  
**Answer:** Betah and Berothai.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:8

**Q22. Who took exceeding much brass from Betah and Berothai?**  
**Answer:** King David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:8

---

### 2 Samuel 8:9

**Q23. Who was the king of Hamath that heard David had smitten all the host of Hadadezer?**  
**Answer:** Toi.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:9

**Q24. Whose host had David smitten?**  
**Answer:** Hadadezer's.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:9

**Q25. What was the name of the king of Hamath?**  
**Answer:** Toi.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:9

---

### 2 Samuel 8:10

**Q26. Who did Toi send to King David?**  
**Answer:** Joram his son.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:10

**Q27. For what purposes did Joram come to King David?**  
**Answer:** To salute him, and to bless him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:10

**Q28. What materials were brought in Joram's hand?**  
**Answer:** Vessels of silver, and vessels of gold, and vessels of brass.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:10

---

### 2 Samuel 8:11

**Q29. Which king did King David dedicate unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** King David dedicated these also unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:11

**Q30. Whose silver and gold did David dedicate unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The silver and gold of all nations which he subdued.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:11

**Q31. From which nations did David dedicate silver and gold?**  
**Answer:** Syria, of Moab, of the children of Ammon, of the Philistines, and of Amalek.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:11

---

### 2 Samuel 8:12

**Q32. From whom did David dedicate the spoil of Hadadezer?**  
**Answer:** Hadadezer, son of Rehob, king of Zobah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:12

**Q33. What specific victory is mentioned alongside the spoil of the Syrians?**  
**Answer:** The valley of salt.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:12

**Q34. How many Syrians did David smite in the valley of salt?**  
**Answer:** Eighteen thousand.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:12

---

### 2 Samuel 8:13

**Q35. What name did David get him when he returned?**  
**Answer:** A name.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:13

**Q36. Where did David smite the Syrians upon his return?**  
**Answer:** In the valley of salt.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:13

**Q37. How many Syrians were smitten in the valley of salt according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Eighteen thousand.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:13

---

### 2 Samuel 8:14

**Q38. Where did David put garrisons?**

**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:14

**Q39. What did all they of Edom become?**  
**Answer:** David's servants.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:14

**Q40. Who preserved David whithersoever he went?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:14

### 2 Samuel 8:15

**Q41. Over whom did David reign?**  
**Answer:** Over all Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:15

**Q42. What did David execute unto all his people?**  
**Answer:** Judgment and justice.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:15

**Q43. Who reigned over all Israel executing judgment and justice?**  
**Answer:** David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:15

**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:16

**Q45. What was Jehoshaphat's office?**  
**Answer:** Recorder.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:16

**Q46. Who were the priests mentioned alongside Ahitub's son?**  
**Answer:** Zadok the son of Ahitub, and Ahimelech the son of Abiathar.

**Q47. Who was the son of Ahitub?**

**Verse:** 2 Samuel 8:17

**Q48. Who was the son of Abiathar?**

**Answer:** Chief rulers.
