# 2 Samuel 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 9:1

**Q1. Who did David ask about remaining in the house of Saul?**  
**Answer:** Any that left of the house of Saul.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:1

**Q2. For whose sake did David want to show kindness?**  
**Answer:** For Jonathan's sake.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:1

**Q3. How long did David want to show kindness to that person?**  
**Answer:** For Jonathan's sake. (Note: verse wording specifies "for Jonathan's sake" in relation to the kindness).  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:1

---

### 2 Samuel 9:2

**Q1. Whose servant was called unto David?**  
**Answer:** The servant of the house of Saul.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:2

**Q2. What was the name of the servant who was called unto David?**  
**Answer:** Ziba.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:2

**Q3. What did the king say to Ziba when he was called unto him?**  
**Answer:** Art thou Ziba?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:2

---

### 2 Samuel 9:3

**Q1. What question did the king ask Ziba regarding the house of Saul?**  
**Answer:** Is there not yet any of the house of Saul, that I may shew the kindness of God unto him?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:3

**Q2. What did Ziba say unto the king?**  
**Answer:** Jonathan hath yet a son, which is lame on his feet.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:3

**Q3. Whose kindness did the king want to show to someone of the house of Saul?**  
**Answer:** The kindness of God.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:3

---

### 2 Samuel 9:4

**Q1. Where did the king ask Ziba where the son was?**  
**Answer:** Where is he?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:4

**Q2. Who answered the king concerning where the son was?**  
**Answer:** Ziba.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:4

**Q3. In whose house was Jonathan's son?**  
**Answer:** In the house of Machir, the son of Ammiel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:4

---

### 2 Samuel 9:5

**Q1. Who sent and fetched Jonathan's son?**  
**Answer:** King David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:5

**Q2. From whose house was Jonathan's son fetched?**  
**Answer:** Out of the house of Machir, the son of Ammiel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:5

**Q3. Where was Machir's house located according to the text?**  
**Answer:** Lo-debar.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:5

---

### 2 Samuel 9:6

**Q1. Who came unto King David?**  
**Answer:** Mephibosheth, the son of Jonathan, the son of Saul.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:6

**Q2. What did Mephibosheth do when he was come unto David?**  
**Answer:** He fell on his face, and did reverence.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:6

**Q3. What did David say when Mephibosheth answered him?**  
**Answer:** Mephibosheth.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:6

---

### 2 Samuel 9:7

**Q1. What did David tell Mephibosheth not to do?**  
**Answer:** Fear not.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:7

**Q2. For whose sake did David say he would surely show him kindness?**  
**Answer:** For Jonathan thy father's sake.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:7

**Q3. What would David restore unto Mephibosheth?**  
**Answer:** All the land of Saul thy father.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:7

---

### 2 Samuel 9:8

**Q1. What did Mephibosheth do after David spoke to him?**  
**Answer:** He bowed himself.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:8

**Q2. How did Mephibosheth describe himself to the king?**  
**Answer:** What is thy servant, that thou shouldest look upon such a dead dog as I am?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:8

**Q3. Whose son was Mephibosheth?**  
**Answer:** Jonathan.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:8

---

### 2 Samuel 9:9

**Q1. Whom did the king call to Saul's servant?**  
**Answer:** Ziba.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:9

**Q2. What did the king call Ziba?**  
**Answer:** Saul's servant.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:9

**Q3. What did the king say he had given unto his master's son?**  
**Answer:** All that pertained to Saul and to all his house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:9

---

### 2 Samuel 9:10

**Q1. Who was told to till the land?**  
**Answer:** Ziba and his sons and his servants.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:10

**Q2. Who was to bring in the fruits?**  
**Answer:** Ziba's sons and his servants.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:10

**Q3. Who was to eat bread at the king's table continually?**  
**Answer:** Mephibosheth thy master's son.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:10

---

### 2 Samuel 9:11

**Q1. Who spoke to the king according to all that the lord the king had commanded?**  
**Answer:** Ziba.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:11

**Q2. How did Ziba say he would act toward the king?**  
**Answer:** So will thy servant do.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:11

**Q3. At whose table did Mephibosheth eat?**  
**Answer:** At David's table.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:11

---

### 2 Samuel 9:12

**Q1. Who did Mephibosheth have?**  
**Answer:** A young son.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:12

**Q2. What was the name of Mephibosheth's young son?**  
**Answer:** Micha.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:12

**Q3. Who dwelt in the house of Ziba?**  
**Answer:** All that dwelt in the house of Ziba were servants unto Mephibosheth. (Or: Mephibosheth dwelt in Jerusalem, for he did eat continually at the king's table; though Ziba's household served him).  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:12

---

### 2 Samuel 9:13

**Q1. Where did Mephibosheth dwell?**  
**Answer:** In Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:13

**Q2. Why did Mephibosheth dwell in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** For he did eat continually at the king's table.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:13

**Q3. What physical condition is mentioned about Mephibosheth in this verse?**  
**Answer:** He was lame on both his feet.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 9:13
