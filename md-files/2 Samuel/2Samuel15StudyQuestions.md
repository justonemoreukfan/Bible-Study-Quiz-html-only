# 2 Samuel 15 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 15:1

**Q1. What did Absalom prepare for himself?**  
**Answer:** Absalom prepared him chariots and horses.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:1

**Q2. How many men did Absalom have to run before him?**  
**Answer:** Fifty men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:1

**Q3. What did Absalom do regarding his preparation of chariots, horses, and runners?**  
**Answer:** He prepared them.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:1

---

### 2 Samuel 15:2

**Q1. When did Absalom rise up early?**  
**Answer:** Beside the way of the gate.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:2

**Q2. What did Absalom do when any man had a controversy to come to the king for judgment?**  
**Answer:** Absalom called unto him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:2

**Q3. What did Absalom ask the man who came for judgment?**  
**Answer:** Of what city art thou?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:2

---

### 2 Samuel 15:3

**Q1. What did Absalom say unto the man after hearing his city?**  
**Answer:** Thy matters are good and right.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:3

**Q2. Who did Absalom say there was no man deputed by?**  
**Answer:** The king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:3

**Q3. According to Absalom, what was the status of the man's matters from the king?**  
**Answer:** There is no man deputed of the king to hear thee.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:3

---

### 2 Samuel 15:4

**Q1. What did Absalom wish he was made in the land?**  
**Answer:** Judge in the land.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:4

**Q2. Who did Absalom say should come unto him?**  
**Answer:** Every man which has any suit or cause.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:4

**Q3. What would Absalom do for any man with a suit or cause if he were judge?**  
**Answer:** Do him justice.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:4

---

### 2 Samuel 15:5

**Q1. What did any man do when he came near to do obeisance to Absalom?**  
**Answer:** He put forth his hand.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:5

**Q2. What did Absalom do with his hand when a man came near?**  
**Answer:** Took him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:5

**Q3. What did Absalom do to the man after taking him by the hand?**  
**Answer:** Kissed him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:5

---

### 2 Samuel 15:6

**Q1. To whom did Absalom act in this manner?**  
**Answer:** All Israel that came to the king for judgment.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:6

**Q2. What did Absalom steal?**  
**Answer:** The hearts of the men of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:6

**Q3. Whose hearts did Absalom steal?**  
**Answer:** The hearts of the men of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:6

---

### 2 Samuel 15:7

**Q1. After how many years did Absalom speak to the king?**  
**Answer:** Forty years.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:7

**Q2. What did Absalom ask the king to let him go and perform?**  
**Answer:** His vow, which he vowed unto the LORD, in Hebron.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:7

**Q3. Where did Absalom vow to perform his vow unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** In Hebron.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:7

---

### 2 Samuel 15:8

**Q1. When did Absalom vow a vow while he abode at Geshur in Syria?**  
**Answer:** While thy servant abode at Geshur in Syria.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:8

**Q2. What did Absalom say the LORD shall do if He brought him again indeed?**  
**Answer:** Then I will serve the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:8

**Q3. Where did Absalom say he would serve the LORD upon being brought again?**  
**Answer:** In Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:8

---

### 2 Samuel 15:9

**Q1. Who said unto Absalom, Go in peace?**  
**Answer:** The king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:9

**Q2. What did Absalom arise and do?**  
**Answer:** Went to Hebron.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:9

**Q3. Who told Absalom to go in peace?**  
**Answer:** The king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:9

---

### 2 Samuel 15:10

**Q1. Who did Absalom send spies throughout?**  
**Answer:** All the tribes of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:10

**Q2. What were the spies commanded to say as soon as the people heard the sound of the trumpet?**  
**Answer:** Absalom reigneth in Hebron.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:10

---

### 2 Samuel 15:11

**Q1. How many men went with Absalom from Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Two hundred men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:11

**Q2. In what condition did the two hundred men go with Absalom?**  
**Answer:** Called, and went in their simplicity.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:11

**Q3. What did the two hundred men know?**  
**Answer:** They knew not any thing.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:11

---

### 2 Samuel 15:12

**Q1. Whom did Absalom send for from Giloh?**  
**Answer:** Ahithophel the Gilonite, David's counsellor.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:12

**Q2. Where was Ahithophel when he was sent for?**  
**Answer:** In his city, even in Giloh.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:12

**Q3. What happened to the conspiracy after Ahithophel was called?**  
**Answer:** The conspiracy was strong; for the people increased continually with Absalom.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:12

---

### 2 Samuel 15:13

**Q1. Who came to David to report the situation?**  
**Answer:** A messenger.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:13

**Q2. What did the messenger say about the hearts of the men of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The hearts of the men of Israel are after Absalom.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:13

**Q3. To whom did the messenger bring this news?**  
**Answer:** David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:13

---

### 2 Samuel 15:14

**Q1. What did David say to all his servants that were with him at Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Arise, and let us flee; for we shall not else escape from Absalom.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:14

**Q2. What did David command his servants to do quickly?**  
**Answer:** Make speed to depart.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:14

**Q3. What reason did David give for needing to flee?**  
**Answer:** Lest he overtake us suddenly, and bring evil upon us, and smite the city with the edge of the sword.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:14

---

### 2 Samuel 15:15

**Q1. What did the king's servants say to the king?**  
**Answer:** Behold, thy servants are ready to do whatsoever my lord the king shall appoint.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:15

**Q2. Whose servants spoke these words to the king?**  
**Answer:** The king's servants.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:15

**Q3. What was the attitude of the king's servants?**  
**Answer:** They were ready to do whatsoever the king appointed.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:15

---

### 2 Samuel 15:16

**Q1. Who went forth upon his departure?**  
**Answer:** The king, and all his household after him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:16

**Q2. How many women, being concubines, did the king leave behind?**  
**Answer:** Ten women.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:16

**Q3. What purpose did the king leave the ten concubines for?**  
**Answer:** To keep the house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:16

---

### 2 Samuel 15:17

**Q1. Who went forth after the king?**  
**Answer:** All the people.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:17

**Q2. Where did the king and all the people tarry?**  
**Answer:** In a place that was far off.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:17

---

### 2 Samuel 15:18

**Q1. Who passed on beside the king?**  
**Answer:** All his servants.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:18

**Q2. Who passed on under the king's leadership?**  
**Answer:** All the Cherethites, and all the Pelethites, and all the Gittites.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:18

**Q3. How many men came after the king from Gath?**  
**Answer:** Six hundred men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:18

---

### 2 Samuel 15:19

**Q1. To whom did the king speak regarding returning?**  
**Answer:** Ittai the Gittite.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:19

**Q2. What did the king ask Ittai about going with them?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore goest thou also with us?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:19

**Q3. Where did the king tell Ittai to return and abide?**  
**Answer:** Return to thy place, and abide with the king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:19

---

### 2 Samuel 15:20

**Q1. When did Ittai come?**  
**Answer:** Yesterday.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:20

**Q2. What did the king ask Ittai about making him go up and down with them?**  
**Answer:** Should I this day make thee go up and down with us?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:20

**Q3. What did the king tell Ittai to do with his brethren?**  
**Answer:** Return thou, and take back thy brethren.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:20

---

### 2 Samuel 15:21

**Q1. What did Ittai answer the king?**  
**Answer:** As the LORD liveth, and as my lord the king liveth.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:21

**Q2. In what place did Ittai swear he would be?**  
**Answer:** Surely in what place my lord the king shall be, whether in death or life, even there also will thy servant be.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:21

**Q3. Whose life did Ittai swear by?**  
**Answer:** The LORD liveth, and as my lord the king liveth.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:21

---

### 2 Samuel 15:22

**Q1. What did David say to Ittai?**  
**Answer:** Go and pass over.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:22

**Q2. Who passed over with Ittai the Gittite?**  
**Answer:** Ittai the Gittite passed over, and all his men, and all the little ones that were with him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:22

---

**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:23

**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:23

**Answer:** Toward the way of the wilderness.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 15:23
