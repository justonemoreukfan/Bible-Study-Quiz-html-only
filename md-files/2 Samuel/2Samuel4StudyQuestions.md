# 2 Samuel 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 4:1

**Q1. Whose son heard that Abner was dead in Hebron?**  
**Answer:** Saul's son.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:1

**Q2. What happened to Saul's son's hands?**  
**Answer:** They were feeble.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:1

**Q3. Who was troubled regarding the events in 2 Samuel 4:1?**  
**Answer:** All the Israelites.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:1

---

### 2 Samuel 4:2

**Q1. How many captains of bands did Saul's son have?**  
**Answer:** Two.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:2

**Q2. What was the name of one of the captains whose son was Rimmon?**  
**Answer:** Baanah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:2

**Q3. Of what tribe were Rechab and Baanah, the sons of Rimmon a Beerothite?**  
**Answer:** Benjamin.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:2

---

### 2 Samuel 4:3

**Q1. To where did the Beerothites flee?**  
**Answer:** Gittaim.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:3

**Q2. How long did the Beerothites sojourn in Gittaim?**  
**Answer:** Until then.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:3

---

### 2 Samuel 4:4

**Q1. Whose son had a son that was lame of his feet?**  
**Answer:** Jonathan, Saul's son.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:4

**Q2. How old was Mephibosheth when the tidings came of Saul and Jonathan out of Jezreel?**  
**Answer:** Five years old.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:4

**Q3. What happened to Mephibosheth because his nurse made haste to flee?**  
**Answer:** He fell, and became lame.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:4

---

### 2 Samuel 4:5

**Q1. Who went and came to the house of Ishbosheth?**  
**Answer:** The sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, Rechab and Baanah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:5

**Q2. When did Rechab and Baanah come to the house of Ishbosheth?**  
**Answer:** In the heat of the day.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:5

**Q3. What was Ishbosheth doing when they came to his house?**  
**Answer:** He lay on a bed at noon.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:5

---

### 2 Samuel 4:6

**Q1. Under what pretense did they come into the midst of the house?**  
**Answer:** As though they would have fetched wheat.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:6

**Q2. Where did they smite Ishbosheth?**  
**Answer:** Under the fifth rib.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:6

**Q3. Who escaped after striking Ishbosheth?**  
**Answer:** Rechab and Baanah his brother.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:6

---

### 2 Samuel 4:7

**Q1. Where was Ishbosheth when they came into the house?**  
**Answer:** On his bed in his bedchamber.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:7

**Q2. What did they do to Ishbosheth after smiting him?**  
**Answer:** Slew him, and beheaded him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:7

**Q3. What did they do with Ishbosheth's head after taking it?**  
**Answer:** Gat away through the plain all night.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:7

---

### 2 Samuel 4:8

**Q1. Whose head did they bring unto David to Hebron?**  
**Answer:** The head of Ishbosheth the son of Saul.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:8

**Q2. What did they say Ishbosheth was to the king?**  
**Answer:** Thine enemy.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:8

**Q3. Who sought the life of David according to their statement to the king?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:8

---

### 2 Samuel 4:9

**Q1. Who answered Rechab and Baanah his brother?**  
**Answer:** David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:9

**Q2. By whom did David swear?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:9

**Q3. What did the LORD have redeemed out of all adversity?**  
**Answer:** His soul.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:9

---

### 2 Samuel 4:10

**Q1. What did the messenger think he brought to David as good tidings?**  
**Answer:** Saul was dead.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:10

**Q2. What did David do to the man who brought the news of Saul's death in Ziklag?**  
**Answer:** Took hold of him, and slew him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:10

**Q3. What was supposed to have been the reward for the messenger's tidings?**  
**Answer:** Who thought that I would have given him a reward for his tidings.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:10

---

### 2 Samuel 4:11

**Q1. Who were the wicked men that slew a righteous person in his own house upon his bed?**  
**Answer:** Rechab and Baanah his brother.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:11

**Q2. Whose blood did David require from their hand?**  
**Answer:** The blood of Ishbosheth.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:11

**Q3. What did David say he would do to them from the earth?**  
**Answer:** Take you away from the earth.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:11

---

### 2 Samuel 4:12

**Q1. Whom did David command his young men to slay?**  
**Answer:** Rechab and Baanah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:12

**Q2. What did the young men cut off from Rechab and Baanah?**  
**Answer:** Their hands and their feet.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:12

**Q3. Where did they hang the head of Ishbosheth?**  
**Answer:** In the pool in Hebron.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 4:12
