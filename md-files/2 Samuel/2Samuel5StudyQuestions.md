# 2 Samuel 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 5:1

**Q1. Who came to David unto Hebron?**  
**Answer:** All the tribes of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:1

**Q2. What did the tribes of Israel say to David when they came to Hebron?**  
**Answer:** Behold, we are thy bone and thy flesh.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:1

---

### 2 Samuel 5:2

**Q3. Who was it that previously led out and brought in Israel when Saul was king?**  
**Answer:** He that leddest out and broughtest in Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:2

**Q4. What did the LORD say to David regarding His people Israel?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt feed my people Israel, and thou shalt be a captain over Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:2

---

### 2 Samuel 5:3

**Q5. Where did all the elders of Israel come to the king?**  
**Answer:** To Hebron.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:3

**Q6. With whom did King David make a league at Hebron before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The elders of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:3

**Q7. What did they do to David after the league was made?**  
**Answer:** They anointed David king over Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:3

---

### 2 Samuel 5:4

**Q8. How old was David when he began to reign?**  
**Answer:** Thirty years old.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:4

**Q9. How long did David reign in total?**  
**Answer:** Forty years.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:4

---

### 2 Samuel 5:5

**Q10. Where did David reign over Judah seven years and six months?**  
**Answer:** In Hebron.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:5

**Q11. Where did David reign thirty and three years over all Israel and Judah?**  
**Answer:** In Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:5

---

### 2 Samuel 5:6

**Q12. Against whom did the king and his men go to Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The Jebusites, the inhabitants of the land.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:6

**Q13. What did the Jebusites say to David?**  
**Answer:** Except thou take away the blind and the lame, thou shalt not come in hither.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:6

**Q14. What did the Jebusites think about David entering Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** David cannot come in hither.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:6

---

### 2 Samuel 5:7

**Q15. What stronghold did David take?**  
**Answer:** The stronghold of Zion.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:7

**Q16. What else is the stronghold of Zion called?**  
**Answer:** The city of David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:7

---

### 2 Samuel 5:8

**Q17. What did David say on that day regarding whoever gets up to the gutter?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever getteth up to the gutter, and smiteth the Jebusites, and the lame and the blind, that are hated of David's soul, he shall be chief and captain.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:8

**Q18. What proverb did they say?**  
**Answer:** The blind and the lame shall not come into the house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:8

---

### 2 Samuel 5:9

**Q19. Where did David dwell after taking the stronghold?**  
**Answer:** In the fort.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:9

**Q20. What did David call the fort?**  
**Answer:** The city of David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:9

**Q21. How did David build round about?**  
**Answer:** From Millo and inward.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:9

---

### 2 Samuel 5:10

**Q22. Who went on and grew great?**  
**Answer:** David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:10

**Q23. Who was with David to help him grow great?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God of hosts.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:10

---

### 2 Samuel 5:11

**Q24. Who was the king of Tyre that sent messengers to David?**  
**Answer:** Hiram.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:11

**Q25. What materials did Hiram send to David?**  
**Answer:** Cedar trees, and carpenters, and masons.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:11

**Q26. What did Hiram's messengers build for David?**  
**Answer:** An house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:11

---

### 2 Samuel 5:12

**Q27. What did David perceive that the LORD had established him as?**  
**Answer:** King over Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:12

**Q28. For whose sake did the LORD exalt David's kingdom?**  
**Answer:** For his people Israel's sake.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:12

---

### 2 Samuel 5:13

**Q29. Where did David take more concubines and wives from after he came from Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Out of Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:13

**Q30. What happened to more sons and daughters born to David?**  
**Answer:** They were born to David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:13

---

### 2 Samuel 5:14

**Q31. Name the first son born to David in Jerusalem from verse 14.**  
**Answer:** Shammua.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:14

**Q32. Name the second son listed in verse 14.**  
**Answer:** Shobab.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:14

**Q33. Name the third and fourth sons listed in verse 14.**  
**Answer:** Nathan, and Solomon.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:14

---

### 2 Samuel 5:15

**Q34. Who was the fifth son listed as born to David?**  
**Answer:** Ibhar.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:15

**Q35. Who was the sixth son listed in verse 15?**  
**Answer:** Elishua.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:15

**Q36. Who were the seventh and eighth sons listed in verse 15?**  
**Answer:** Nepheg, and Japhia.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:15

---

### 2 Samuel 5:16

**Q37. Who was the ninth son listed in verse 16?**  
**Answer:** Elishama.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:16

**Q38. Who was the tenth son listed in verse 16?**  
**Answer:** Eliada.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:16

**Q39. Who was the eleventh son listed in verse 16?**  
**Answer:** Eliphalet.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:16

---

### 2 Samuel 5:17

**Q40. What did the Philistines hear when they heard David was anointed king over Israel?**  
**Answer:** That they had anointed David king over Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:17

**Q41. What did all the Philistines do after hearing David was king?**  
**Answer:** They came up to seek David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:17

**Q42. Where did David go when he heard that the Philistines were seeking him?**  
**Answer:** He went down to the hold.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:17

---

### 2 Samuel 5:18

**Q43. Where did the Philistines come and spread themselves?**  
**Answer:** In the valley of Rephaim.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:18

---

### 2 Samuel 5:19

**Q44. Whom did David inquire of regarding the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:19

**Q45. What question did David ask the LORD about going up to the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** Shall I go up to the Philistines? wilt thou deliver them into mine hand?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:19

**Q46. What did the LORD answer David?**  
**Answer:** Go up: for I will doubtless deliver the Philistines into thine hand.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:19

---

### 2 Samuel 5:20

**Q47. Where did David come and smite the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** Baalperazim.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:20

**Q48. What did David say the LORD had broken forth upon his enemies like?**  
**Answer:** The breaking forth of waters.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:20

**Q49. What name did they call the place where he smote them?**  
**Answer:** Baalperazim.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:20

---

### 2 Samuel 5:21

**Q50. What did the Philistines leave behind in Baalperazim?**  
**Answer:** Their images.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:21

**Q51. Who burned the images left behind by the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** David and his men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:21

---

### 2 Samuel 5:22

**Q52. Who came up yet again against David?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:22

**Q53. Where did the Philistines spread themselves the second time?**  
**Answer:** In the valley of Rephaim.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:22

---

### 2 Samuel 5:23

**Q54. Whom did David inquire of when the Philistines came up again?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:23

**Q55. What did the LORD tell David not to do?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not go up; but fetch a compass behind them, and come upon them over against the mulberry trees.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:23

---

### 2 Samuel 5:24

**Q56. What sound was David to listen for in the tops of the mulberry trees?**  
**Answer:** The sound of a going in the tops of the mulberry trees.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:24

**Q57. When was David to bestir himself upon hearing the sound?**  
**Answer:** When thou hearest the sound of a going in the tops of the mulberry trees.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:24

**Q58. Why was David to bestir himself at that moment?**  
**Answer:** For then shall the LORD go out before thee, to smite the host of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:24

---

### 2 Samuel 5:25

**Q59. How did David do as the LORD commanded him?**  
**Answer:** So did David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:25

**Q60. From where to where did David smite the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** From Geba until thou come to Gazer.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 5:25
