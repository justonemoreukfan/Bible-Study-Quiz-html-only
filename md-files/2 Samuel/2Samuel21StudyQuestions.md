# 2 Samuel 21 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 21:1

**Q1. What happened in the days of David for three years, year after year?**  
**Answer:** There was a famine.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:1

**Q2. Whom did David inquire of during the famine?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:1

**Q3. For whose sake did the Lord say the famine was because of?**  
**Answer:** For Saul, and for his bloody house, because he slew the Gibeonites.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:1

---

### 2 Samuel 21:2

**Q1. Whom did the king call, and who were they not of?**  
**Answer:** The Gibeonites, who were not of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:2

**Q2. Of the remnant of whom were the Gibeonites?**  
**Answer:** The Amorites.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:2

**Q3. What had the children of Israel done concerning the Gibeonites?**  
**Answer:** Sworn unto them.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:2

---

### 2 Samuel 21:3

**Q1. What did David ask the Gibeonites?**  
**Answer:** "What shall I do for you?" and "Wherewith shall I make the atonement, that ye may bless the inheritance of the Lord?"  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:3

---

### 2 Samuel 21:4

**Q1. What did the Gibeonites answer the king regarding silver and gold?**  
**Answer:** "We will have no silver nor gold of Saul, nor of his house."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:4

**Q2. What did the Gibeonites say regarding any man of Israel?**  
**Answer:** "Neither for us shalt thou kill any man in Israel."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:4

**Q3. What did David say after they spoke?**  
**Answer:** "What ye shall say, that will I do for you."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:4

---

### 2 Samuel 21:5

**Q1. What did the men answer the king concerning Saul?**  
**Answer:** "The man that consumed us, and that devised against us that we should be destroyed from remaining in any of the coasts of Israel,"  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:5

---

### 2 Samuel 21:6

**Q1. How many men of Saul's sons were requested to be delivered unto them?**  
**Answer:** Seven men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:6

**Q2. Where did they want to hang them up unto the Lord?**  
**Answer:** In Gibeah of Saul, whom the Lord did choose.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:6

**Q3. What was the king's response to this request?**  
**Answer:** "I will give them."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:6

---

### 2 Samuel 21:7

**Q1. Whom did the king spare?**  
**Answer:** Mephibosheth, the son of Jonathan the son of Saul.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:7

**Q2. For whose oath's sake between them was Mephibosheth spared?**  
**Answer:** The Lord's oath's sake.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:7

**Q3. Between whom was the Lord's oath?**  
**Answer:** Between David and Jonathan the son of Saul.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:7

---

### 2 Samuel 21:8

**Q1. What were the names of the two sons of Rizpah the daughter of Aiah, whom she bare unto Saul?**  
**Answer:** Armoni and Mephibosheth.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:8

**Q2. Who were the five sons of Michal the daughter of Saul whom she brought up?**  
**Answer:** The sons of Adriel the son of Barzillai the Meholathite.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:8

---

### 2 Samuel 21:9

**Q1. Into whose hands did David deliver the seven men?**  
**Answer:** The hands of the Gibeonites.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:9

**Q2. What did the Gibeonites do to the seven men?**  
**Answer:** They hanged them in the hill before the Lord.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:9

**Q3. At what specific time did they fall together?**  
**Answer:** In the days of harvest, in the first days, in the beginning of barley harvest.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:9

---

### 2 Samuel 21:10

**Q1. What did Rizpah the daughter of Aiah take?**  
**Answer:** Sackcloth.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:10

**Q2. Where did Rizpah spread the sackcloth for her?**  
**Answer:** Upon the rock.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:10

**Q3. What did she suffer neither to drop on them by day, nor by night?**  
**Answer:** Neither the birds of the air to rest on them by day, nor the beasts of the field by night.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:10

---

### 2 Samuel 21:11

**Q1. What was told to David concerning what Rizpah the daughter of Aiah had done?**  
**Answer:** What Rizpah the daughter of Aiah, the concubine of Saul, had done.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:11

---

### 2 Samuel 21:12

**Q1. What did David go and take from the men of Jabeshgilead?**  
**Answer:** The bones of Saul and the bones of Jonathan his son.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:12

**Q2. From whom had the men of Jabeshgilead stolen the bones?**  
**Answer:** From the street of Bethshan.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:12

**Q3. Who had hanged them there, and where?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines, when they had slain Saul in Gilboa.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:12

---

### 2 Samuel 21:13

**Q1. Whose bones did David bring up from thence?**  
**Answer:** The bones of Saul and the bones of Jonathan his son.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:13

**Q2. Whose bones did they gather together?**  
**Answer:** The bones of them that were hanged.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:13

---

### 2 Samuel 21:14

**Q1. Where did they bury the bones of Saul and Jonathan his son?**  
**Answer:** In the country of Benjamin in Zelah, in the sepulchre of Kish his father.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:14

**Q2. What did they do after all that the king commanded?**  
**Answer:** They performed all that the king commanded.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:14

**Q3. After this, what did God answer for the land?**  
**Answer:** Entreaty for the land.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:14

---

### 2 Samuel 21:15

**Q1. Who had yet war again with the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines had yet war again with Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:15

**Q2. Who went down, and who with him?**  
**Answer:** David went down, and his servants with him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:15

**Q3. What happened to David as he fought against the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** David waxed faint.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:15

---

### 2 Samuel 21:16

**Q1. Who was of the sons of the giant?**  
**Answer:** Ishbibenob.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:16

**Q2. What was the weight of Ishbibenob's spear?**  
**Answer:** Three hundred shekels of brass in weight.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:16

**Q3. What was Ishbibenob girded with, and what did he think to do?**  
**Answer:** He was girded with a new sword, and thought to have slain David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:16

---

### 2 Samuel 21:17

**Q1. Who succoured David and smote the Philistine, killing him?**  
**Answer:** Abishai the son of Zeruiah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:17

**Q2. What did the men of David swear unto him?**  
**Answer:** "Thou shalt go no more out with us to battle, that thou quench not the light of Israel."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:17

---

### 2 Samuel 21:18

**Q1. Where did there happen to be yet a battle with the Philistines after this?**  
**Answer:** In Gob.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:18

**Q2. Who smote Saph, which was of the sons of the giant?**  
**Answer:** Sibbechai the Hushathite.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:18

---

### 2 Samuel 21:19

**Q1. Where was there yet another battle in Gob with the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** In Gob.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:19

**Q2. Who smote the brother of Goliath the Gittite?**  
**Answer:** Elhanan the son of Jaareoregim, a Bethlehemite.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:19

**Q3. What was the staff of the spear of the brother of Goliath like?**  
**Answer:** Like a weaver's beam.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:19

---

### 2 Samuel 21:20

**Q1. Where was there yet a battle?**  
**Answer:** In Gath.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:20

**Q2. How many fingers and toes did the man of great stature have?**  
**Answer:** Four and twenty; six on each hand, and six on each foot.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:20

**Q3. Whose son was this man of great stature?**  
**Answer:** The son of the giant.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:20

---

### 2 Samuel 21:21

**Q1. When this man defied Israel, who defied him and was slain?**  
**Answer:** Jonathan the son of Shimeah the brother of David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:21

---

### 2 Samuel 21:22

**Q1. Who were born to the giant in Gath?**  
**Answer:** These four.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:22

**Q2. By whose hand did the giant's sons fall?**  
**Answer:** By the hand of David, and by the hand of his servants.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 21:22
