# 2 Samuel 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 2:1

**Q1. After this, of whom did David inquire?**  
**Answer:** Of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:1

**Q2. What did David ask if he should go up into any of the cities of Judah?**  
**Answer:** "Shall I go up into any of the cities of Judah?"  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:1

**Q3. What was the LORD's answer to where David should go?**  
**Answer:** "Unto Hebron."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:1

---

### 2 Samuel 2:2

**Q4. Who went up thither with David?**  
**Answer:** His two wives also, Ahinoam the Jezreelitess, and Abigail Nabal's wife the Carmelite.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:2

---

### 2 Samuel 2:3

**Q5. Who did David bring up with him, with every man and his household?**  
**Answer:** His men that were with him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:3

**Q6. Where did the men dwell?**  
**Answer:** In the cities of Hebron.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:3

---

### 2 Samuel 2:4

**Q7. Who came and anointed David king over the house of Judah?**  
**Answer:** The men of Judah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:4

**Q8. Where did they anoint David king over the house of Judah?**  
**Answer:** In Hebron.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:4

**Q9. Whom did they tell David had buried Saul?**  
**Answer:** The men of Jabeshgilead.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:4

---

### 2 Samuel 2:5

**Q10. Whom did David send messengers unto?**  
**Answer:** The men of Jabeshgilead.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:5

**Q11. What did David say the men of Jabeshgilead had shown unto their lord, even Saul?**  
**Answer:** Kindness.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:5

**Q12. What did the men of Jabeshgilead do to Saul?**  
**Answer:** They buried him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:5

---

### 2 Samuel 2:6

**Q13. What did David pray that the LORD would show unto them?**  
**Answer:** Kindness and truth.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:6

**Q14. What did David say he also would requite them with, because they had done this thing?**  
**Answer:** This kindness.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:6

---

### 2 Samuel 2:7

**Q15. What did David instruct his hands to be?**  
**Answer:** Strengthened.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:7

**Q16. Who did David say was dead?**  
**Answer:** Saul your master.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:7

**Q17. Who has anointed David king over them?**  
**Answer:** The house of Judah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:7

---

### 2 Samuel 2:8

**Q18. Who was the captain of Saul's host?**  
**Answer:** Abner the son of Ner.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:8

**Q19. Whose son was Abner?**  
**Answer:** Ner.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:8

**Q20. Whom did Abner take, and bring him over to Mahanaim?**  
**Answer:** Ishbosheth the son of Saul.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:8

---

### 2 Samuel 2:9

**Q21. Over whom did Abner make Ishbosheth king?**  
**Answer:** Gilead, and the Ashurites, and Jezreel, and Ephraim, and Benjamin, and all Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:9

---

### 2 Samuel 2:10

**Q22. How old was Ishbosheth Saul's son when he began to reign over Israel?**  
**Answer:** Forty years old.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:10

**Q23. How long did Ishbosheth reign?**  
**Answer:** Two years.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:10

**Q24. Who followed the house of David?**  
**Answer:** But the house of Judah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:10

---

### 2 Samuel 2:11

**Q25. How long was the time that David was king in Hebron over the house of Judah?**  
**Answer:** Seven years and six months.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:11

---

### 2 Samuel 2:12

**Q26. Who went out from Mahanaim to Gibeon?**  
**Answer:** Abner the son of Ner, and the servants of Ishbosheth the son of Saul.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:12

---

### 2 Samuel 2:13

**Q27. Who went out, and met together by the pool of Gibeon?**  
**Answer:** Joab the son of Zeruiah, and the servants of David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:13

**Q28. Where did the servants of David and the servants of Ishbosheth meet together?**  
**Answer:** By the pool of Gibeon.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:13

**Q29. On which side of the pool did the two groups sit?**  
**Answer:** The one on the one side of the pool, and the other on the other side of the pool.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:13

---

### 2 Samuel 2:14

**Q30. What did Abner say to Joab?**  
**Answer:** "Let the young men now arise, and play before us."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:14

**Q31. What did Joab reply?**  
**Answer:** "Let them arise."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:14

---

### 2 Samuel 2:15

**Q32. How many men went over by number for Benjamin, which pertained to Ishbosheth the son of Saul?**  
**Answer:** Twelve.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:15

**Q33. How many men went over out of the servants of David?**  
**Answer:** Twelve.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:15

---

### 2 Samuel 2:16

**Q34. What did every one catch by his fellow?**  
**Answer:** His head.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:16

**Q35. What did every man thrust in his fellow's side?**  
**Answer:** His sword.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:16

**Q36. What was the name of that place which is in Gibeon?**  
**Answer:** Helkathhazzurim.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:16

---

### 2 Samuel 2:17

**Q37. What kind of battle was there that day?**  
**Answer:** A very sore battle.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:17

**Q38. Who was beaten before the servants of David?**  
**Answer:** Abner, and the men of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:17

---

### 2 Samuel 2:18

**Q39. Who were there in that place?**  
**Answer:** Three sons of Zeruiah there: Joab, and Abishai, and Asahel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:18

**Q40. Which of the sons of Zeruiah was as light of foot as a wild roe?**  
**Answer:** Asahel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:18

---

### 2 Samuel 2:19

**Q41. Whom did Asahel pursue after?**  
**Answer:** Abner.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:19

**Q42. Did Asahel turn to the right hand or to the left in following Abner?**  
**Answer:** He turned not to the right hand nor to the left from following Abner.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:19

---

### 2 Samuel 2:20

**Q43. When Abner looked behind him, what did he ask?**  
**Answer:** "Art thou Asahel?"  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:20

**Q44. What was Asahel's answer to Abner's question?**  
**Answer:** "I am."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:20

---

### 2 Samuel 2:21

**Q45. What did Abner tell Asahel to turn aside to?**  
**Answer:** To thy right hand or to thy left.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:21

**Q46. What did Abner tell Asahel to lay hold on?**  
**Answer:** One of the young men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:21

**Q47. What did Abner tell Asahel to take for himself?**  
**Answer:** His armor.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:21

**Q48. Did Asahel would not turn aside from following Abner?**  
**Answer:** No. (Implied: But Asahel would not turn aside from following of him.)  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:21

---

### 2 Samuel 2:22

**Q49. What did Abner say to Asahel the second time?**  
**Answer:** "Turn thee aside from following me:"  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:22

**Q50. What reason did Abner give for why he should smite Asahel to the ground?**  
**Answer:** "Wherefore should I smite thee to the ground? how then should I hold up my face to Joab thy brother?"  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:22

---

### 2 Samuel 2:23

**Q51. What did Asahel refuse to do?**  
**Answer:** To turn aside.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:23

---

### 2 Samuel 2:24

**Q52. Who pursued after Abner?**  
**Answer:** Joab and Abishai.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:24

**Q53. When the sun went down, where did Joab and Abishai come?**  
**Answer:** To the hill of Ammah, that lieth before Giah by the way of the wilderness of Gibeon.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:24

---

### 2 Samuel 2:25

**Q54. Who gathered themselves together after Abner?**  
**Answer:** The children of Benjamin.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:25

**Q55. What kind of troop did the children of Benjamin become?**  
**Answer:** One troop.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:25

**Q56. Where did they stand on the top of?**  
**Answer:** An hill.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:25

---

### 2 Samuel 2:26

**Q57. To whom did Abner call?**  
**Answer:** Joab.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:26

**Q58. What did Abner ask if the sword should devour forever?**  
**Answer:** "Shall the sword devour for ever?"  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:26

**Q59. What did Abner ask Joab if he knew not that it would be in what manner in the latter end?**  
**Answer:** Bitterness.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:26

**Q60. How long did Abner ask them not to return from following their brethren?**  
**Answer:** "How long shall it be then, that thou biddest not the people return from following their brethren?"  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:26

---

### 2 Samuel 2:27

**Q61. What did Joab swear by?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:27

**Q62. What did Joab say would have happened unless Abner had spoken?**  
**Answer:** "Surely then in the morning the people had gone up every one from following his brother."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:27

---

### 2 Samuel 2:28

**Q63. What did Joab blow?**  
**Answer:** A trumpet.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:28

**Q64. What did all the people do when the trumpet blew?**  
**Answer:** Stood still, and pursued after Israel no more, neither fought they any more.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:28

---

### 2 Samuel 2:29

**Q65. Through what region did Abner and his men walk all that night?**  
**Answer:** The plain.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:29

**Q66. What river did Abner and his men pass over?**  
**Answer:** Jordan.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:29

**Q67. Through what entire region did they go before arriving at Mahanaim?**  
**Answer:** All Bithron.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:29

---

### 2 Samuel 2:30

**Q68. Who returned from following Abner?**  
**Answer:** Joab.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:30

**Q69. When Joab had gathered all the people together, how many of David's servants lacked besides Asahel?**  
**Answer:** Nineteen men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:30

---

### 2 Samuel 2:31

**Q70. Whose men had smitten of Benjamin and of Abner's men?**  
**Answer:** The servants of David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:31

**Q71. How many men died among the servants of David's enemies?**  
**Answer:** Three hundred and sixty men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:31

---

### 2 Samuel 2:32

**Q72. Where did they take Asahel up, and bury him?**  
**Answer:** In the sepulchre of his father, which was in Bethlehem.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:32

**Q73. Who went all night?**  
**Answer:** Joab and his men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:32

**Q74. At what time of day did Joab and his men break day at Hebron?**  
**Answer:** The morning.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 2:32
