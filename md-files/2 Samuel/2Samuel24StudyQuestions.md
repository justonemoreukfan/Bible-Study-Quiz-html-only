# 2 Samuel 24 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 24:1

**Q1. Again against whom was the anger of the LORD kindled?**  
**Answer:** Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:1

**Q2. Who moved David against Israel according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Satan (or the LORD, depending on the phrasing, but the text says "he moved David against them").  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:1

**Q3. What did the moving entity say to David concerning Israel and Judah?**  
**Answer:** "Go, number Israel and Judah."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:1

---

### 2 Samuel 24:2

**Q4. To whom did the king speak regarding the numbering?**  
**Answer:** Joab the captain of the host.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:2

**Q5. From where to where were the people to be numbered?**  
**Answer:** From Dan even to Beersheba.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:2

**Q6. What was David's stated reason for numbering the people?**  
**Answer:** "That I may know the number of the people."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:2

---

### 2 Samuel 24:3

**Q7. What did Joab say the LORD God should add unto the people?**  
**Answer:** How many soever they be, an hundredfold more.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:3

**Q8. Whose eyes did Joab say should see it?**  
**Answer:** The eyes of my lord the king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:3

**Q9. What question did Joab ask regarding why the king had this thing in mind?**  
**Answer:** "But why doth my lord the king delight in this thing?"  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:3

---

### 2 Samuel 24:4

**Q10. Whose word prevailed against the king?**  
**Answer:** The king's word against Joab, and against the captains of the host.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:4

**Q11. Who departed from the presence of the king?**  
**Answer:** Joab and the captains of the host.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:4

**Q12. What did they depart to do?**  
**Answer:** To number the people of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:4

---

### 2 Samuel 24:5

**Q13. Over what river did they pass?**  
**Answer:** Jordan.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:5

**Q14. Where did they pitch their camp?**  
**Answer:** In Aroer.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:5

**Q15. On the right side of what city was the camp located?**  
**Answer:** The city that lieth in the midst of the river of Gad, and toward Jazer.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:5

---

### 2 Samuel 24:6

**Q16. To what land did they come?**  
**Answer:** The land of Tahtimhodshi.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:6

**Q17. To what place did they come after Tahtimhodshi?**  
**Answer:** Danjaan.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:6

**Q18. Around what region did they go after Danjaan?**  
**Answer:** About to Zidon.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:6

---

### 2 Samuel 24:7

**Q19. To what stronghold did they come?**  
**Answer:** The stronghold of Tyre.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:7

**Q20. What cities did they pass through?**  
**Answer:** All the cities of the Hivites and of the Canaanites.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:7

**Q21. Where did they go out after passing the Hivites and Canaanites?**  
**Answer:** Toward the south of Judah, even to Beersheba.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:7

---

### 2 Samuel 24:8

**Q22. When they had gone through all the land, to where did they come?**  
**Answer:** To Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:8

**Q23. How long did it take them to go through all the land?**  
**Answer:** Nine months and twenty days.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:8

---

### 2 Samuel 24:9

**Q24. What did Joab give up to the king?**  
**Answer:** The sum of the number of the people.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:9

**Q25. How many valiant men that drew the sword were found in Israel?**  
**Answer:** Eight hundred thousand.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:9

**Q26. How many men of Judah were there?**  
**Answer:** Five hundred thousand men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:9

---

### 2 Samuel 24:10

**Q27. Whose heart smote him after he had numbered the people?**  
**Answer:** David's heart.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:10

**Q28. What did David say he had done greatly?**  
**Answer:** "I have sinned greatly in that I have done."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:10

**Q29. What did David ask the LORD to take away?**  
**Answer:** The iniquity of his servant.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:10

---

### 2 Samuel 24:11

**Q30. When David was up in the morning, whose word came to the prophet Gad?**  
**Answer:** The word of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:11

**Q31. Who is Gad described as?**  
**Answer:** David's seer.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:11

**Q32. Whose word came to Gad concerning David?**  
**Answer:** The LORD's word.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:11

---

### 2 Samuel 24:12

**Q33. Whose name was Gad to speak with to David?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:12

**Q34. How many things did the LORD offer David to choose from?**  
**Answer:** Three things.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:12

**Q35. What did the LORD say He would do with one of those things?**  
**Answer:** "I will offer it unto thee."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:12

---

### 2 Samuel 24:13

**Q36. Who came to David and told him?**  
**Answer:** Gad.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:13

**Q37. What are the three options of punishment presented to David?**  
**Answer:** Seven years of famine, three months of fleeing before enemies, or three days' pestilence.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:13

**Q38. What was David asked to advise regarding what answer to return?**  
**Answer:** "Now advise, and see what answer I shall return to him that sent me."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:13

---

### 2 Samuel 24:14

**Q39. How did David describe his state of mind to Gad?**  
**Answer:** "I am in a great strait."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:14

**Q40. Into whose hand did David say they should fall?**  
**Answer:** Into the hand of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:14

**Q41. Why did David prefer falling into the hand of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** "For his mercies are great."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:14

---

### 2 Samuel 24:15

**Q42. What did the LORD send upon Israel?**  
**Answer:** A pestilence.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:15

**Q43. From what time to what time did the pestilence last?**  
**Answer:** From the morning even to the time appointed.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:15

**Q44. How many men died from Dan even to Beersheba?**  
**Answer:** Seventy thousand men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:15

---

### 2 Samuel 24:16

**Q45. Who stretched out his hand upon Jerusalem to destroy it?**  
**Answer:** The angel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:16

**Q46. What did the LORD repent of regarding the evil?**  
**Answer:** The evil.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:16

**Q47. What did the LORD say to the angel that destroyed the people?**  
**Answer:** "It is enough: stay now thine hand."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:16

---

### 2 Samuel 24:17

**Q48. Who spoke to the LORD when he saw the angel that smote the people?**  
**Answer:** David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:17

**Q49. What did David confess about his own actions?**  
**Answer:** "Lo, I have sinned, and I have done wickedly."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:17

**Q50. Against whom did David ask the hand of the LORD to be?**  
**Answer:** Against him, and against his father's house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:17

---

### 2 Samuel 24:18

**Q51. Who came that day to David?**  
**Answer:** Gad.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:18

---

### 2 Samuel 24:19

**Q52. According to whose saying did David go up?**  
**Answer:** According to the saying of Gad.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:19

**Q53. As the LORD commanded, what did David do?**  
**Answer:** Went up.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:19

---

### 2 Samuel 24:20

**Q54. Who looked, and saw the king and his servants coming toward him?**  
**Answer:** Araunah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:20

**Q55. What did Araunah do when he saw them?**  
**Answer:** Went out, and bowed himself before the king on his face upon the ground.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:20

---

### 2 Samuel 24:21

**Q56. What reason did Araunah give for the king and his servants coming to him?**  
**Answer:** "Wherefore is my lord the king come to his servant?"  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:21

**Q57. What did David say he wanted to buy from Araunah?**  
**Answer:** The threshingfloor.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:21

**Q58. Why did David want to build an altar unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** That the plague may be stayed from the people.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:21

---

### 2 Samuel 24:22

**Q59. What did Araunah tell David to take and offer?**  
**Answer:** Let my lord the king take and offer up what seemeth good unto him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:22

**Q60. What animals did Araunah offer for burnt sacrifices?**  
**Answer:** Oxen.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:22

**Q61. What instruments did Araunah offer for wood?**  
**Answer:** The threshing instruments and other instruments of the oxen.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:22

---

### 2 Samuel 24:23

**Q62. As a king, what did Araunah give unto the king?**  
**Answer:** All these things did Araunah, as a king, give unto the king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:23

**Q63. What did Araunah say the LORD his God would accept?**  
**Answer:** "The LORD thy God accept thee."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:23

---

### 2 Samuel 24:24

**Q64. What did the king say he would rather do instead of taking it for free?**  
**Answer:** "Nay; but I will surely buy it of thee at a price."  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:24

**Q65. What kind of offerings did David refuse to offer unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Burnt offerings that cost him nothing.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:24

**Q66. How much did David buy the threshingfloor and the oxen for?**  
**Answer:** Fifty shekels of silver.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:24

---

### 2 Samuel 24:25

**Q67. What did David build there unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** An altar.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:25

**Q68. What kind of offerings did David offer on the altar?**  
**Answer:** Burnt offerings and peace offerings.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:25

**Q69. How did the LORD respond to the land after these events?**  
**Answer:** The LORD was intreated for the land, and the plague was stayed from Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 24:25
