# 2 Samuel 11 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 11:1

**Q1. When was the time when kings go forth to battle?**  
**Answer:** After the year was expired, at the time when kings go forth to battle.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:1

**Q2. Whom did David send forth?**  
**Answer:** Joab, and his servants with him, and all Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:1

**Q3. Where did David stay while they destroyed the children of Ammon and besieged Rabbah?**  
**Answer:** David tarried still at Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:1

---

### 2 Samuel 11:2

**Q1. At what time of day did David arise from off his bed?**  
**Answer:** In an eventide.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:2

**Q2. Where did David walk upon?**  
**Answer:** Upon the roof of the king's house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:2

**Q3. Whom did David see washing herself from the roof?**  
**Answer:** A woman washed herself; and the woman was very beautiful to look upon.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:2

---

### 2 Samuel 11:3

**Q1. What did David do concerning the woman he saw?**  
**Answer:** David sent and enquired after the woman.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:3

**Q2. Whose daughter was the woman said to be?**  
**Answer:** Bathsheba, the daughter of Eliam.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:3

**Q3. Who was Bathsheba's husband?**  
**Answer:** Uriah the Hittite.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:3

---

### 2 Samuel 11:4

**Q1. What did David send messengers to do?**  
**Answer:** And took her.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:4

**Q2. Where did Bathsheba come unto David?**  
**Answer:** Unto him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:4

**Q3. How is Bathsheba's purification described after she came unto him?**  
**Answer:** For she was purified from her uncleanness.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:4

---

### 2 Samuel 11:5

**Q1. What did the woman conceive?**  
**Answer:** And conceived.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:5

**Q2. Whom did the woman send and tell?**  
**Answer:** And sent and told David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:5

**Q3. What specific message did she send to David?**  
**Answer:** I am with child.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:5

### 2 Samuel 11:6

**Q1. To whom did David send word regarding Uriah?**  
**Answer:** To Joab.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:6

**Q2. What did David instruct Joab to send him?**  
**Answer:** Send me Uriah the Hittite.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:6

**Q3. Who sent Uriah the Hittite unto David?**  
**Answer:** Joab sent Uriah to David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:6

---

### 2 Samuel 11:7

**Q1. Who came unto David when Uriah was sent?**  
**Answer:** Uriah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:7

**Q2. What did David demand of Uriah?**  
**Answer:** How Joab did, and how the people did, and how the war prospered.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:7

**Q3. Whose condition was David inquiring about alongside the war?**  
**Answer:** The people.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:7

---

### 2 Samuel 11:8

**Q1. What did David say to Uriah?**  
**Answer:** Go down to thy house, and wash thy feet.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:8

**Q2. Who departed out of the king's house?**  
**Answer:** Uriah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:8

**Q3. What followed Uriah after he departed out of the king's house?**  
**Answer:** And a mess of meat from the king followed him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:8

---

### 2 Samuel 11:9

**Q1. Where did Uriah sleep instead of going down to his house?**  
**Answer:** At the door of the king's house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:9

**Q2. With whom did Uriah sleep?**  
**Answer:** With all the servants of his lord.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:9

**Q3. Did Uriah go down to his house?**  
**Answer:** Went not down to his house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:9

---

### 2 Samuel 11:10

**Q1. Who was told that Uriah went not down to his house?**  
**Answer:** David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:10

**Q2. What question did David ask Uriah?**  
**Answer:** Camest thou not from thy journey?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:10

**Q3. Why did David ask Uriah why he did not go down to his house?**  
**Answer:** Why didst thou not go down unto thine house?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:10

---

### 2 Samuel 11:11

**Q1. What did Uriah say abode in tents?**  
**Answer:** The ark, and Israel, and Judah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:11

**Q2. Who were encamped in the open fields?**  
**Answer:** My lord Joab, and the servants of my lord.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:11

**Q3. What did Uriah swear he would not do?**  
**Answer:** Eat and to drink, and to lie with my wife.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:11

---

### 2 Samuel 11:12

**Q1. What did David tell Uriah to do for yet one day?**

**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:12

**Q2. When did David say he would let Uriah depart?**  
**Answer:** To morrow.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:12

**Q3. Who abode in Jerusalem that day and the next?**  
**Answer:** Uriah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:12

---

### 2 Samuel 11:13

**Q1. Whom did David call to eat and drink before him?**  
**Answer:** Uriah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:13

**Q2. What did David make Uriah do?**  
**Answer:** And he made him drunk.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:13

**Q3. Where did Uriah go out to sleep at even?**  
**Answer:** To lie on his bed with the servants of his lord, but went not down to his house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:13

### 2 Samuel 11:14

**Q1. When did David write a letter?**  
**Answer:** In the morning.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:14

**Q2. To whom did David write the letter?**  
**Answer:** To Joab.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:14

**Q3. By whose hand did David send the letter?**  
**Answer:** By the hand of Uriah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:14

---

**Answer:** Where he knew that valiant men were.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 11:16
