# 2 Samuel 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 1:1

**Q1. Who had died before the events of this chapter?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:1

**Q2. From where had David returned after defeating the Amalekites?**  
**Answer:** From the slaughter of the Amalekites.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:1

**Q3. How many days had David abode in Ziklag?**  
**Answer:** Two days.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:1

---

### 2 Samuel 1:2

**Q1. On what day did a man come out of the camp from Saul?**  
**Answer:** On the third day.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:2

**Q2. What condition were the man's clothes in when he came to David?**  
**Answer:** His clothes were rent.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:2

**Q3. What did the man have upon his head when he came to David?**  
**Answer:** Earth upon his head.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:2

---

### 2 Samuel 1:3

**Q1. When David asked the man where he came from, what was his reply?**  
**Answer:** Out of the camp of Israel am I escaped.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:3

**Q2. Who did David ask for information concerning the matter?**  
**Answer:** The man who came to him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:3

**Q3. What question did David specifically ask the man?**  
**Answer:** How went the matter?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:3

---

### 2 Samuel 1:4

**Q1. What did the man tell David had fled from the battle?**  
**Answer:** The people.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:4

**Q2. What happened to many of the people who fled?**  
**Answer:** Many of the people are fallen and dead.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:4

**Q3. Who else did the man report as dead besides the people?**  
**Answer:** Saul and Jonathan his son are dead also.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:4

### 2 Samuel 1:5

**Q1. To whom did David speak regarding the news of Saul and Jonathan's deaths?**  
**Answer:** The young man that told him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:5

**Q2. What specific question did David ask the young man?**  
**Answer:** How knowest thou that Saul and Jonathan his son be dead?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:5

**Q3. What was the identity of the person David questioned?**  
**Answer:** The young man that told him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:5

### 2 Samuel 1:6

**Q1. Upon what mountain did the young man say he happened by chance to be?**  
**Answer:** Mount Gilboa.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:6

**Q2. Whom did the young man see leaning upon his spear on Mount Gilboa?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:6

**Q3. What was pursuing Saul closely upon Mount Gilboa?**

### 2 Samuel 1:9

**Q1. What did Saul say had come upon him?**

**Q2. Why did Saul say his life was still whole in him?**  
**Answer:** Because his life is yet whole in him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:9

**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:9

### 2 Samuel 1:10

**Q1. What did the young man say he did after Saul fell?**  
**Answer:** So I stood upon him, and slew him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:10

**Q2. Why was the young man sure Saul could not live after he fell?**  
**Answer:** Because he was sure that he could not live after that he was fallen.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:10

**Q3. What two items did the young man take from Saul?**  
**Answer:** The crown that was upon his head, and the bracelet that was on his arm.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:10

### 2 Samuel 1:11

**Q1. What physical action did David take upon his own clothes?**  
**Answer:** David took hold on his clothes, and rent them.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:11

**Q2. Who joined David in renting their clothes?**  
**Answer:** All the men that were with him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:11

**Q3. Whose clothes were rent by David and the men with him?**  
**Answer:** Their own clothes.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:11

### 2 Samuel 1:12

**Q1. How long did David and his men mourn and weep?**  
**Answer:** Until even.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:12

**Q2. For whom did David and the men mourn and weep?**  
**Answer:** For Saul, and for Jonathan his son.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:12

**Q3. Who else did David and his men mourn for besides Saul and Jonathan?**  
**Answer:** For the people of the LORD, and for the house of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:12

### 2 Samuel 1:20

**Q1. Where should people not tell this news?**  
**Answer:** Tell it not in Gath, publish it not in the streets of Askelon.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:20

**Q2. Who did David say should not rejoice over the news?**  
**Answer:** Lest the daughters of the Philistines rejoice, lest the daughters of the uncircumcised triumph.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:20

**Answer:** Gath and Askelon.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 1:20
