# 2 Samuel 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 12:1

**Q1. Who did the Lord send unto David?**  
**Answer:** Nathan.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:1

**Q2. When Nathan came to David, what did he say unto him?**  
**Answer:** There were two men in one city; the one rich, and the other poor.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:1

**Q3. Regarding flocks and herds, what did the rich man have?**  
**Answer:** Exceeding many flocks and herds.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:1

---

### 2 Samuel 12:2

**Q1. What did the poor man have?**  
**Answer:** Save one little ewe lamb, which he had bought and nourished up.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:2

**Q2. How did the poor man acquire his ewe lamb?**  
**Answer:** He had bought and nourished it up.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:2

**Q3. How did the ewe lamb grow up with the poor man?**  
**Answer:** It grew up together with him, and with his children.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:2

---

### 2 Samuel 12:3

**Q1. Where did the little ewe lamb eat and drink?**  
**Answer:** Of his own meat, and drank of his own cup.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:3

**Q2. Where did the lamb lie?**  
**Answer:** In his bosom.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:3

**Q3. What was the lamb unto him?**  
**Answer:** As a daughter.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:3

---

### 2 Samuel 12:4

**Q1. Who came unto the rich man?**  
**Answer:** A traveller.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:4

**Q2. What did the rich man spare to take of his own flock and herd to dress for the wayfaring man?**  
**Answer:** To take of his own flock and of his own herd.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:4

**Q3. Whose lamb did the rich man take and dress?**  
**Answer:** The poor man's lamb.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:4

---

### 2 Samuel 12:5

**Q1. How was David's anger kindled against the man?**  
**Answer:** Greatly.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:5

**Q2. What did David say to Nathan?**  
**Answer:** As the LORD liveth, the man that hath done this thing shall surely die.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:5

**Q3. By whom did David swear when he spoke to Nathan?**  
**Answer:** As the LORD liveth.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:5

---

### 2 Samuel 12:6

**Q1. How many-fold did David say the lamb should be restored?**  
**Answer:** Fourfold.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:6

**Q2. Why did David say the lamb should be restored fourfold?**  
**Answer:** Because he did this thing, and because he had no pity.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:6

**Q3. What did the man lack regarding the poor man's lamb?**  
**Answer:** Pity.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:6

---

### 2 Samuel 12:7

**Q1. What did Nathan say to David after the parable?**  
**Answer:** Thou art the man.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:7

**Q2. Who said, "Thus saith the LORD God of Israel"?**  
**Answer:** Nathan.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:7

**Q3. What did the LORD anoint David king over?**  
**Answer:** Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:7

---

### 2 Samuel 12:8

**Q1. What did the Lord give David besides his master's house?**  
**Answer:** His master's wives into thy bosom.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:8

**Q2. Whose house did the Lord give to David?**  
**Answer:** Thy master's house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:8

**Q3. What houses of Israel and Judah did the Lord give David?**  
**Answer:** The house of Israel and Judah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:8

---

### 2 Samuel 12:9

**Q1. What did David despise?**  
**Answer:** The commandment of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:9

**Q2. Whose husband did David kill with the sword?**  
**Answer:** Uriah the Hittite's.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:9

**Q3. Whose wife did David take to be his wife?**  
**Answer:** The wife of Uriah the Hittite.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:9

---

### 2 Samuel 12:10

**Q1. What would not depart from David's house forever?**  
**Answer:** The sword.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:10

**Q2. Why would the sword not depart from David's house?**  
**Answer:** Because thou hast despised me, and hast taken the wife of Uriah the Hittite to be thy wife.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:10

**Q3. Whose wife did the Lord state David took?**  
**Answer:** The wife of Uriah the Hittite.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:10

---

### 2 Samuel 12:11

**Q1. What did the Lord say he would raise up against David?**  
**Answer:** Evil out of thine own house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:11

**Q2. What would the Lord take before David's eyes?**  
**Answer:** Thy wives.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:11

**Q3. To whom would the Lord give David's wives?**  
**Answer:** Unto thy neighbour.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:11

---

### 2 Samuel 12:12

**Q1. In what manner did David do the act of taking Uriah's wife?**  
**Answer:** Secretly.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:12

**Q2. Before whom would the Lord do this thing?**  
**Answer:** Before all Israel, and before the sun.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:12

**Q3. Who would have relations with David's wives?**  
**Answer:** This neighbour.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:12

---

### 2 Samuel 12:13

**Q1. What did David say to Nathan?**  
**Answer:** I have sinned against the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:13

**Q2. What did Nathan say unto David regarding his sin?**  
**Answer:** The LORD also hath put away thy sin; thou shalt not die.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:13

**Q3. What consequence did Nathan tell David he would avoid?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not die.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:13

---

### 2 Samuel 12:14

**Q1. What did David give great occasion to do by this deed?**  
**Answer:** To the enemies of the LORD to blaspheme.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:14

**Q2. Who would blaspheme because of this deed?**  
**Answer:** The enemies of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:14

**Q3. What would surely die?**  
**Answer:** The child also that is born unto thee.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:14

---

### 2 Samuel 12:15

**Q1. Where did Nathan depart?**  
**Answer:** Unto his house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:15

**Q2. Whom did the LORD strike?**  
**Answer:** The child that Uriah's wife bare unto David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:15

**Q3. How did the child become?**  
**Answer:** Very sick.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:15

---

### 2 Samuel 12:16

**Q1. For whom did David besought God?**  
**Answer:** For the child.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:16

**Q2. How did David fast?**  
**Answer:** And fasted, and went in and lay all night upon the earth.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:16

**Q3. Where did David lie all night?**  
**Answer:** Upon the earth.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:16

---

### 2 Samuel 12:17

**Q1. Who arose and went to David to raise him up from the earth?**  
**Answer:** The elders of his house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:17

**Q2. What would David not do with the elders of his house?**  
**Answer:** Neither did he eat bread with them.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:17

**Q3. With whom would David not eat bread?**  
**Answer:** With them.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:17

---

### 2 Samuel 12:18

**Q1. On what day did the child die?**  
**Answer:** On the seventh day.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:18

**Q2. What did David's servants fear to tell him?**  
**Answer:** That the child was dead.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:18

**Q3. Why did the servants fear to tell David the child was dead?**  
**Answer:** For they said, Behold, while the child was yet alive, we spake unto him, and he would not hearken unto our voice: how will he then vex himself, if we tell him that the child is dead?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:18

---

### 2 Samuel 12:19

**Q1. What did David perceive when his servants whispered?**  
**Answer:** That the child was dead.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:19

**Q2. What did David ask his servants?**  
**Answer:** Is the child dead?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:19

**Q3. What was the servants' answer to David's question?**  
**Answer:** He is dead.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:19

---

### 2 Samuel 12:20

**Q1. What did David do after the child was dead?**  
**Answer:** Then David arose from the earth, and washed, and anointed himself, and changed his apparel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:20

**Q2. Where did David go after changing his apparel?**  
**Answer:** And came into the house of the LORD, and worshipped.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:20

**Q3. What did David do when he came to his own house?**  
**Answer:** He required that they should set bread before him, and he did eat.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:20

---

### 2 Samuel 12:21

**Q1. What did the servants say unto David about his actions?**  
**Answer:** What thing is this that thou hast done? thou didst fast and weep for the child, while it was alive; but when the child was dead, thou didst rise and eat bread.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:21

**Q2. When did David fast and weep?**  
**Answer:** While it was alive.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:21

**Q3. When did David rise and eat bread?**  
**Answer:** When the child was dead.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:21

---

### 2 Samuel 12:22

**Q1. While the child was yet alive, what did David do?**  
**Answer:** I fasted and wept.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:22

**Q2. Why did David fast and weep while the child was alive?**  
**Answer:** For I said, Who can tell whether GOD will be gracious to me, that the child may live?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:22

**Q3. Whom did David hope might be gracious to him?**  
**Answer:** GOD.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:22

---

### 2 Samuel 12:23

**Q1. Why should David fast now that the child is dead?**  
**Answer:** Can I bring him back again?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:23

**Q2. Where did David say he shall go regarding the child?**  
**Answer:** I shall go to him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:23

**Q3. What did David say about the child returning to him?**  
**Answer:** He shall not return to me.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:23

---

**Verse:** 2 Samuel 12:24

**Answer:** Under saws, and under harrows of iron, and under axes of iron.
