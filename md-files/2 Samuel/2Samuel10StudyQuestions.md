# 2 Samuel 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 10:1

**Q1. Whose death is mentioned at the beginning of the chapter?**  
**Answer:** The king of the children of Ammon.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:1

**Q2. Who reigned in the stead of the king of the children of Ammon?**  
**Answer:** Hanun his son.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:1

### 2 Samuel 10:2

**Q3. What did David say he would show unto Hanun the son of Nahash?**  
**Answer:** Kindness.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:2

**Q4. Whose kindness did David say he would show to Hanun?**  
**Answer:** His father showed kindness unto me.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:2

**Q5. Whom did David send to comfort Hanun by the hand of his servants concerning his father?**  
**Answer:** His servants.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:2

### 2 Samuel 10:3

**Q6. Into whose land did the servants of David come?**  
**Answer:** Into the land of the children of Ammon.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:3

**Q7. What did the princes of the children of Ammon say unto Hanun their lord regarding David's comforters?**  
**Answer:** Thinkest thou that David doth honour thy father, that he hath sent comforters unto thee?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:3

**Q8. What did the princes say David had actually sent his servants to do unto the city?**  
**Answer:** To search the city, and to spy it out, and to overthrow it.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:3

### 2 Samuel 10:4

**Q9. What did Hanun take regarding David's servants?**  
**Answer:** David's servants.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:4

**Q10. What did Hanun do to the beards of David's servants?**  
**Answer:** Shaved off the one half of their beards.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:4

**Q11. What did Hanun do to their garments in the middle, even to their buttocks?**  
**Answer:** Cut off their garments in the middle.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:4

### 2 Samuel 10:5

**Q12. When they told David, what did he do because the men were greatly ashamed?**  
**Answer:** Sent to meet them.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:5

**Q13. What did the king say the men should do until their beards were grown?**  
**Answer:** Tarry at Jericho.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:5

**Q14. What instruction did the king give the men to do once their beards were grown?**  
**Answer:** Return.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:5

### 2 Samuel 10:6

**Q15. When the children of Ammon saw that they stank before David, what did they do?**  
**Answer:** Sent and hired the Syrians of Bethrehob, and the Syrians of Zoba, twenty thousand footmen, and of king Maacah a thousand men, and of Ishtob twelve thousand men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:6

**Q16. How many footmen of the Syrians of Bethrehob and Zoba did the children of Ammon hire?**  
**Answer:** Twenty thousand footmen.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:6

**Q17. How many men did they hire from king Maacah and from Ishtob respectively?**  
**Answer:** A thousand men from Maacah, and twelve thousand men from Ishtob.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:6

### 2 Samuel 10:7

**Q18. When David heard of it, whom did he send?**  
**Answer:** Joab, and all the host of the mighty men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:7

### 2 Samuel 10:8

**Q19. Where did the children of Ammon come out and put the battle in array?**  
**Answer:** At the entering in of the gate.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:8

**Q20. Who were by themselves in the field?**  
**Answer:** The Syrians of Zoba, and of Rehob, and Ishtob, and Maacah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:8

### 2 Samuel 10:9

**Q21. When Joab saw that the front of the battle was against him before and behind, what did he choose?**  
**Answer:** Of all the choice men of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:9

**Q22. Against whom did Joab put them in array?**  
**Answer:** Against the Syrians.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:9

### 2 Samuel 10:10

**Q23. To whom did Joab deliver the rest of the people?**  
**Answer:** Into the hand of Abishai his brother.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:10

**Q24. Against whom did Abishai put them in array?**  
**Answer:** Against the children of Ammon.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:10

### 2 Samuel 10:11

**Q25. According to Joab, if the Syrians be too strong for him, what should Abishai do?**  
**Answer:** Then thou shalt help me.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:11

**Q26. According to Joab, if the children of Ammon be too strong for Abishai, what would Joab do?**  
**Answer:** Then I will come and help thee.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:11

### 2 Samuel 10:12

**Q27. What did Joab tell them to be of?**  
**Answer:** Of good courage.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:12

**Q28. For what kind of things did Joab say they should play the men?**  
**Answer:** For our people, and for the cities of our God.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:12

**Q29. What did Joab say the Lord would do?**  
**Answer:** Let the Lord do that which seemeth him good.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:12

### 2 Samuel 10:13

**Q30. Who drew nigh, and the people that were with him, unto the battle against the Syrians?**  
**Answer:** Joab.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:13

**Q31. What did the Syrians do when Joab drew nigh?**  
**Answer:** Fled before him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:13

### 2 Samuel 10:14

**Q32. When the children of Ammon saw that the Syrians were fled, what did they do?**  
**Answer:** Fled also before Abishai, and entered into the city.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:14

**Q33. Where did Joab return from, and where did he come?**  
**Answer:** Returned from the children of Ammon, and came to Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:14

### 2 Samuel 10:15

**Q34. When the Syrians saw that they were smitten before Israel, what did they do?**  
**Answer:** Gathered themselves together.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:15

### 2 Samuel 10:16

**Q35. Whom did Hadarezer send and bring out?**  
**Answer:** The Syrians that were beyond the river.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:16

**Q36. Where did they come to Helam?**  
**Answer:** And Shobach the captain of the host of Hadarezer went before them.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:16

### 2 Samuel 10:17

**Q37. When it was told David, what did he gather?**  
**Answer:** All Israel together.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:17

**Q38. Where did David pass over, and where did he come?**  
**Answer:** Passed over Jordan, and came to Helam.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:17

**Q39. How did the Syrians set themselves in array against David and fight with him?**  
**Answer:** The Syrians set themselves in array against David, and fought with him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:17

### 2 Samuel 10:18

**Q40. Who fled before Israel?**  
**Answer:** The Syrians.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:18

**Q41. How many men of the chariots of the Syrians did David slay?**  
**Answer:** Seven hundred men of the chariots of the Syrians.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:18

**Q42. How many horsemen of the Syrians did David slay?**  
**Answer:** Forty thousand horsemen.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:18

### 2 Samuel 10:19

**Q43. When all the kings that were servants to Hadarezer saw that they were smitten before Israel, what did they do?**  
**Answer:** Made peace with Israel, and served them.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:19

**Q44. What did the Syrians fear to do anymore?**  
**Answer:** To help the children of Ammon any more.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 10:19
