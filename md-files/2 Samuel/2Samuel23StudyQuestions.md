# 2 Samuel 23 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 23:1

**Q1. Who is described as the last words of David?**  
**Answer:** David the son of Jesse.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 23:1

**Q2. How is the man who was raised up on high described in relation to the anointed of the God of Jacob?**  
**Answer:** The anointed of the God of Jacob.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 23:1

**Q3. What title is given to David regarding the sweet psalms of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The sweet psalmist of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 23:1
