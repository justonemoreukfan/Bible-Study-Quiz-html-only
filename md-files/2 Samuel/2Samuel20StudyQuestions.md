# 2 Samuel 20 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 20:1

**Q1. Who happened to be there when the man of Belial named Sheba was present?**  
**Answer:** A man of Belial, whose name was Sheba, the son of Bichri, a Benjamite.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:1

**Q2. What did Sheba do with the trumpet?**  
**Answer:** He blew a trumpet.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:1

**Q3. What did Sheba say the men of Israel should do regarding David?**  
**Answer:** Every man to his tents, O Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:1

---

### 2 Samuel 20:2

**Q2. Who went every man of Israel up from after David after Sheba spoke?**  
**Answer:** Every man of Israel went up from after David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:2

**Q2. Who followed Sheba the son of Bichri?**  
**Answer:** The men of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:2

**Q2. Whom did the men of Judah cleave unto from Jordan even to Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Unto their king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:2

---

### 2 Samuel 20:3

**Q2. To where did David come when he reached his house at Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** To his house at Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:3

**Q2. Whom did the king take, the ten women his concubines, to keep the house?**  
**Answer:** The ten women his concubines.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:3

**Q2. Where did the king put the ten women his concubines, and what did he do to them?**  
**Answer:** In ward, and fed them, but went not in unto them.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:3

---

### 2 Samuel 20:4

**Q2. To whom did the king say, "Assemble me the men of Judah within three days"?**  
**Answer:** To Amasa.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:4

**Q2. Within how many days was Amasa told to assemble the men of Judah?**  
**Answer:** Within three days.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:4

**Q2. What was Amasa commanded to do regarding himself within three days?**  
**Answer:** Be thou here present.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:4

---

### 2 Samuel 20:5

**Q2. Who went to assemble the men of Judah?**  
**Answer:** Amasa.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:5

**Q2. What did Amasa tarry beyond?**  
**Answer:** The set time which he had appointed him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:5

---

### 2 Samuel 20:6

**Q2. To whom did David say that Sheba the son of Bichri shall do us more harm than did Absalom?**  
**Answer:** To Abishai.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:6

**Q2. Whose servants were to take their lord's servants and pursue after Sheba?**  
**Answer:** Thy lord's servants.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:6

**Q2. Why did David say they should pursue after Sheba?**  
**Answer:** Lest he get him fenced cities, and escape us.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:6

---

### 2 Samuel 20:7

**Q2. Who went out after Abishai?**  
**Answer:** Joab's men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:7

**Q2. Who went out with Joab's men?**  
**Answer:** The Cherethites, and the Pelethites, and all the mighty men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:7

**Q2. From where did they go out to pursue after Sheba the son of Bichri?**  
**Answer:** Out of Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:7

---

### 2 Samuel 20:8

**Q2. Where were they when they were at the great stone which is in Gibeon?**  
**Answer:** By the great stone which is in Gibeon.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:8

**Q2. Who went before them at the great stone in Gibeon?**  
**Answer:** Amasa.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:8

**Q2. What was girded upon Joab as he was clad, and what happened when he went forth?**  
**Answer:** A girdle of a sword upon his loins fastened thereunto in the sheath thereof; and as he went forth it fell out.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:8

---

### 2 Samuel 20:9

**Q2. What did Joab say to Amasa?**  
**Answer:** Art thou in health, my brother?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:9

**Q2. With what hand did Joab take Amasa by the beard to kiss him?**  
**Answer:** The right hand.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:9

---

### 2 Samuel 20:10

**Q2. Of what did Amasa take no heed?**  
**Answer:** Of the sword that was in Joab's hand.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:10

**Q2. Where did Joab smite Amasa with the sword?**  
**Answer:** In the fifth rib.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:10

**Q2. What happened to Amasa's bowels after he was smitten, and did Joab strike him again?**  
**Answer:** Shed out to the ground, and struck him not again; and he died.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:10

---

### 2 Samuel 20:11

**Q2. Who stood by Amasa's body?**  
**Answer:** One of Joab's men.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:11

**Q2. What did the man standing by Amasa say?**  
**Answer:** He that favoureth Joab, and he that is for David, let him go after Joab.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:11

---

### 2 Samuel 20:12

**Q2. In what place did Amasa wallow in blood?**  
**Answer:** In the midst of the highway.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:12

**Q2. What did the man see when everyone that came by him stood still?**  
**Answer:** That every one that came by him stood still.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:12

**Q2. When the man saw this, what did he do with Amasa out of the highway into the field, and what did he cast upon him?**  
**Answer:** Removed Amasa out of the highway into the field, and cast a cloth upon him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:12

---

### 2 Samuel 20:13

**Q2. When Amasa was removed out of the highway, who went on after Joab?**  
**Answer:** All the people.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:13

**Q2. Why did all the people go on after Joab?**  
**Answer:** To pursue after Sheba the son of Bichri.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:13

---

### 2 Samuel 20:14

**Q2. Through what did Sheba go through all the tribes of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Through all the tribes of Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:14

**Q2. To where did Sheba go, and who were gathered together and went also after him?**  
**Answer:** Unto Abel, and to Bethmaachah, and all the Berites: and they were gathered together, and went also after him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:14

---

### 2 Samuel 20:15

**Q2. Who came and besieged Sheba in Abel of Bethmaachah?**  
**Answer:** They came and besieged him in Abel of Bethmaachah.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:15

**Q2. Against what did they cast up a bank?**  
**Answer:** Against the city.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:15

**Q2. What did the bank stand in, and what did all the people that were with Joab do to the wall?**  
**Answer:** It stood in the trench: and all the people that were with Joab battered the wall, to throw it down.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:15

---

### 2 Samuel 20:16

**Q2. Who cried out of the city?**  
**Answer:** A wise woman.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:16

**Q2. From what city did the wise woman cry?**  
**Answer:** From Abel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:16

**Q2. What did the wise woman ask Joab to do?**  
**Answer:** Hear, hear; say unto Joab, Come near hither, that I may speak with thee.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:16

---

### 2 Samuel 20:17

**Q2. When Joab was come near unto her, what did the woman ask?**  
**Answer:** Art thou Joab?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:17

**Q2. What was Joab's answer when the woman asked if he was Joab?**  
**Answer:** I am.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:17

**Q2. What did the woman tell Joab to do?**  
**Answer:** Hear the words of thine handmaid.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:17

---

### 2 Samuel 20:18

**Q2. What did the woman say they spake in old time?**  
**Answer:** They were wont to speak in old time, saying, They shall surely ask counsel at Abel: and so they ended the matter.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:18

---

### 2 Samuel 20:19

**Q2. How did the woman describe herself and her city in Israel?**  
**Answer:** I am one of them that are peaceable and faithful in Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:19

**Q2. What was Joab seeking to destroy or swallow up?**  
**Answer:** A city and a mother in Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:19

**Q2. What did the woman ask Joab why he would swallow up?**  
**Answer:** Why wilt thou swallow up the inheritance of the Lord?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:19

---

### 2 Samuel 20:20

**Q2. What was Joab's answer to the woman regarding swallowing up or destroying?**  
**Answer:** Far be it, far be it from me, that I should swallow up or destroy.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:20

---

### 2 Samuel 20:21

**Q2. Who did Joab say had lifted up his hand against the king, even against David?**  
**Answer:** A man of mount Ephraim, Sheba the son of Bichri by name.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:21

**Q2. What did Joab tell the woman to deliver only?**  
**Answer:** Deliver him only.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:21

**Q2. What did Joab say he would do if she delivered Sheba only?**  
**Answer:** I will depart from the city.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:21

---

### 2 Samuel 20:22

**Q2. To whom did the woman go in her wisdom?**  
**Answer:** Unto all the people.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:22

**Q2. What did they do with the head of Sheba the son of Bichri?**  
**Answer:** Cut off the head of Sheba the son of Bichri.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:22

**Q2. What did Joab do after the head of Sheba was cast out to him?**  
**Answer:** He blew a trumpet, and they retired from the city, every man to his tent.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:22

---

### 2 Samuel 20:23

**Q2. Who was over all the host of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Joab.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:23

**Q2. Who was over the Cherethites and over the Pelethites?**  
**Answer:** Benaiah the son of Jehoiada.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:23

---

### 2 Samuel 20:24

**Q2. Who was over the tribute?**  
**Answer:** Adoram.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:24

**Q2. Who was the recorder?**  
**Answer:** Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:24

---

### 2 Samuel 20:25

**Q2. Who was scribe?**  
**Answer:** Sheva.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:25

**Q2. Who were the priests?**  
**Answer:** Zadok and Abiathar.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 20:25

### 2 Samuel 20:26

**Answer:** Ira the Jairite.
