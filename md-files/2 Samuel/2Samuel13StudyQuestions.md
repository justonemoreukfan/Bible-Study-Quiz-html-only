# 2 Samuel 13 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 Samuel 13:1

**Q1. Who had a fair sister whose name was Tamar?**  
**Answer:** Absalom the son of David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:1

**Q2. What was the name of Absalom's fair sister?**  
**Answer:** Tamar.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:1

**Q3. Who loved Tamar?**  
**Answer:** Amnon the son of David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:1

---

### 2 Samuel 13:2

**Q1. How did Amnon feel regarding his sister Tamar?**  
**Answer:** So vexed, that he fell sick for her sister Tamar.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:2

**Q2. Why was Amnon vexed for Tamar?**  
**Answer:** For she was a virgin.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:2

**Q3. What seemed hard to Amnon to do to Tamar?**  
**Answer:** Anything.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:2

---

### 2 Samuel 13:3

**Q1. What was the name of Amnon's friend who was the son of Shimeah?**  
**Answer:** Jonadab.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:3

**Q2. Whose brother was Shimeah?**  
**Answer:** David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:3

**Q3. How is Jonadab described in relation to Amnon?**  
**Answer:** A very subtil man.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:3

---

### 2 Samuel 13:4

**Q1. How did Jonadab address Amnon day by day?**  
**Answer:** Why art thou, being the king's son, lean from day to day?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:4

**Q2. What question did Jonadab ask Amnon about his condition?**  
**Answer:** Wilt thou not tell me?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:4

**Q3. What was Amnon's reply to Jonadab?**  
**Answer:** I love Tamar, my brother Absalom's sister.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:4

---

### 2 Samuel 13:5

**Q1. What did Jonadab advise Amnon to lay himself down and do?**  
**Answer:** Make thyself sick.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:5

**Q2. When thy father cometh to see thee, what should Amnon say to him?**  
**Answer:** I pray thee, let my sister Tamar come, and give me meat, and dress the meat in my sight, that I may see it, and eat it at her hand.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:5

**Q3. Who was supposed to come and give Amnon meat?**  
**Answer:** His sister Tamar.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:5

---

### 2 Samuel 13:6

**Q1. What did Amnon do so that he appeared sick?**  
**Answer:** Lay down.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:6

**Q2. Who came to see Amnon?**  
**Answer:** The king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:6

**Q3. What did Amnon say unto the king?**  
**Answer:** I pray thee, let Tamar my sister come, and make me a couple of cakes in my sight, that I may eat at her hand.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:6

---

### 2 Samuel 13:7

**Q1. To whose house did David send home to Tamar?**  
**Answer:** To the house of Amnon.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:7

**Q2. What message did David send to Tamar?**  
**Answer:** Go now to thy brother Amnon's house, and dress him meat.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:7

**Q3. Whose house was Tamar told to go to?**  
**Answer:** Her brother Amnon's house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:7

---

### 2 Samuel 13:8

**Q1. To whose house did Tamar go?**  
**Answer:** Her brother Amnon's house.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:8

**Q2. What was Amnon doing when Tamar arrived?**  
**Answer:** He was laid down.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:8

**Q3. What did Tamar take and bake in his sight?**  
**Answer:** Flour, and kneaded it, and made cakes in his sight, and did bake the cakes.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:8

---

### 2 Samuel 13:9

**Q1. What did Tamar take out and pour before Amnon?**  
**Answer:** The pan, and poured them out before him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:9

**Q2. How did Amnon react to eating the food?**  
**Answer:** He refused to eat.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:9

**Q3. What command did Amnon give regarding the people?**  
**Answer:** Have out all men from me.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:9

---

### 2 Samuel 13:10

**Q1. What did Amnon say unto Tamar regarding the chamber?**  
**Answer:** Bring the meat into the chamber, that I may eat of thine hand.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:10

**Q2. Who brought the cakes into the chamber to Amnon?**  
**Answer:** Tamar.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:10

**Q3. Whose hand did Amnon want to eat from?**  
**Answer:** Thine hand (Tamar's hand).  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:10

---

### 2 Samuel 13:11

**Q1. When she had brought them near to him to eat, what did Amnon do?**  
**Answer:** Took hold of her.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:11

**Q2. What did Amnon say to Tamar when he took hold of her?**  
**Answer:** Come lie with me, my sister.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:11

**Q3. How did Amnon address Tamar as he asked her to lie with him?**  
**Answer:** My sister.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:11

---

### 2 Samuel 13:12

**Q1. What did Tamar answer Amnon?**  
**Answer:** Nay, my brother, do not force me.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:12

**Q2. What did Tamar say was not done in Israel?**  
**Answer:** No such thing ought to be done in Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:12

**Q3. What did Tamar call Amnon's action?**  
**Answer:** Folly.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:12

---

### 2 Samuel 13:13

**Q1. Where would Tamar go with her shame?**  
**Answer:** Whither shall I cause my shame to go?  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:13

**Q2. What would happen to Amnon if he committed this act?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt be as one of the fools in Israel.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:13

**Q3. What did Tamar tell Amnon to speak to the king about?**  
**Answer:** Speak unto the king; for he will not withhold me from thee.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:13

---

### 2 Samuel 13:14

**Q1. Did Amnon hearken unto the voice of Tamar?**  
**Answer:** Howbeit he would not hearken unto her voice.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:14

**Q2. Being stronger than she, what did Amnon do to Tamar?**  
**Answer:** Forced her, and lay with her.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:14

**Q3. Why was Amnon able to force Tamar?**  
**Answer:** Being stronger than she.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:14

---

### 2 Samuel 13:15

**Q1. How did Amnon feel toward Tamar after he lay with her?**  
**Answer:** Then Amnon hated her exceedingly.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:15

**Q2. How great was the hatred wherewith Amnon hated her?**  
**Answer:** Exceedingly.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:15

**Q3. How did the hatred wherewith he hated her compare to the love wherewith he had loved her?**  
**Answer:** The hatred wherewith he hated her was greater than the love wherewith he had loved her.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:15

---

### 2 Samuel 13:16

**Q1. What did Tamar say to Amnon when he told her to arise and be gone?**  
**Answer:** There is no cause: this evil in sending me away is greater than the other that thou didst unto me.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:16

**Q2. Did Amnon hearken unto Tamar's words?**  
**Answer:** But he would not hearken unto her.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:16

**Q3. What did Amnon tell Tamar to do?**  
**Answer:** Arise, be gone.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:16

---

### 2 Samuel 13:17

**Q1. Whom did Amnon call unto him?**  
**Answer:** His servant that ministered unto him.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:17

**Q2. What did Amnon tell the servant to put out from him?**  
**Answer:** Put now this woman out from me, and bolt the door after her.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:17

**Q3. What did Amnon instruct the servant to do with the door after putting her out?**  
**Answer:** Bolt the door after her.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:17

---

### 2 Samuel 13:18

**Q1. What kind of garment did Tamar have upon her?**  
**Answer:** A garment of divers colours.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:18

**Q2. What kind of robes were worn by the king's daughters that were virgins?**  
**Answer:** Such robes.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:18

**Q3. What did the servant do with Tamar after putting her out?**  
**Answer:** Bolted the door after her.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:18

---

### 2 Samuel 13:19

**Q1. What did Tamar put on her head?**  
**Answer:** Ashes.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:19

**Q2. What did Tamar rend?**  
**Answer:** Her garment of divers colours that was on her.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:19

**Q3. How did Tamar go, crying as she went?**  
**Answer:** Laid her hand on her head, and went on crying.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:19

---

### 2 Samuel 13:20

**Q1. Who asked Tamar if her brother Amnon had been with her?**  
**Answer:** Absalom her brother.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:20

**Q2. What counsel did Absalom give Tamar after she told him?**  
**Answer:** Hold now thy peace, my sister: he is thy brother; regard not this thing.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:20

**Q3. How did Tamar remain in her brother Absalom's house?**  
**Answer:** Desolate.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:20

---

### 2 Samuel 13:21

**Q1. When King David heard of all these things, how did he feel?**  
**Answer:** He was very wroth.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:21

**Q2. Who heard of all these things?**  
**Answer:** King David.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:21

**Q3. Did David hear of all these things?**  
**Answer:** Yes.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:21

---

### 2 Samuel 13:22

**Q1. How did Absalom speak unto his brother Amnon?**  
**Answer:** Neither good nor bad.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:22

**Q2. Why did Absalom hate Amnon?**  
**Answer:** Because he had forced his sister Tamar.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:22

**Q3. Did Absalom speak to Amnon about the matter?**  
**Answer:** No (spake neither good nor bad).  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:22

---

### 2 Samuel 13:23

**Q1. After how many full years did Absalom have sheepshearers?**  
**Answer:** Two full years.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:23

**Q2. Where were Absalom's sheepshearers located?**  
**Answer:** In Baalhazor, which is beside Ephraim.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:23

**Q3. Who did Absalom invite to come to the sheepshearing?**  
**Answer:** All the king's sons.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:23

---

### 2 Samuel 13:24

**Q1. To whom did Absalom come regarding the king's sons going with him?**  
**Answer:** The king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:24

**Q2. What did Absalom say the king and his servants should do?**  
**Answer:** Let the king, I beseech thee, and his servants go with thy servant.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:24

**Answer:** The king.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:24

**Q1. What did Absalom command his servants concerning Amnon?**  
**Answer:** Mark ye now when Amnon's heart is merry with wine, and when I say unto you, Smite Amnon; then kill him, fear not.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:28

**Q2. What instruction did Absalom give his servants about who commanded them?**  
**Answer:** Have not I commanded you? be courageous, and be valiant.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:28

**Answer:** When Amnon's heart is merry with wine.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:28

---

### 2 Samuel 13:29

**Q1. What did the servants of Absalom do unto Amnon?**  
**Answer:** Did unto Amnon as Absalom had commanded.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:29

**Answer:** Arose, and every man gat him up upon his mule, and fled.

**Answer:** His mule.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:29

**Answer:** Absalom hath slain all the king's sons, and there is not one of them left.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:30

**Q2. According to the first tidings, how many of the king's sons were left?**  
**Answer:** Not one of them left.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:30

**Answer:** Absalom.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:30

**Q1. What did the king do upon hearing the news?**  
**Answer:** Arose, and tare his garments, and lay on the earth.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:31

**Q2. Who stood by him with their clothes rent?**  
**Answer:** All his servants.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:31

**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:31

### 2 Samuel 13:32

**Q1. Who answered and spoke regarding the rumor?**  
**Answer:** Jonadab, the son of Shimeah David's brother.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:32

**Q2. What did Jonadab say the king should not suppose?**  
**Answer:** Let not my lord suppose that they have slain all the young men the king's sons; for Amnon only is dead.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:32

**Answer:** For by the appointment of Absalom this hath been determined from the day that he forced his sister Tamar.  
**Verse:** 2 Samuel 13:32
