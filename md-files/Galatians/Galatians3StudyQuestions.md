# Galatians 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Galatians 3:1

**Q1. Who hath bewitched whom, according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** O foolish Galatians, who hath bewitched you.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:1

**Q2. Before whose eyes Jesus Christ hath been evidently set forth?**  
**Answer:** Before whose eyes Jesus Christ hath been evidently set forth.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:1

**Q3. How has Jesus Christ been set forth among them?**  
**Answer:** Crucified among you.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:1

---

### Galatians 3:2

**Q4. What would Paul learn of them first?**  
**Answer:** This only would I learn of you.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:2

**Q5. By what means received ye the Spirit?**  
**Answer:** Received ye the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:2

**Q6. Did they receive the Spirit by the works of the law, or by what else?**  
**Answer:** By the works of the law, or by the hearing of faith?  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:2

---

### Galatians 3:3

**Q7. What kind of persons are they called in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Are ye so foolish?  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:3

**Q8. Having begun in what, are ye now made perfect by the flesh?**  
**Answer:** Having begun in the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:3

**Q9. By what are ye now made perfect?**  
**Answer:** By the flesh.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:3

---

### Galatians 3:4

**Q10. Have ye suffered how many things in vain?**  
**Answer:** Have ye suffered so many things in vain.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:4

**Q11. If it be yet how?**  
**Answer:** If it be yet in vain.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:4

**Q12. What action does the verse question regarding their suffering?**  
**Answer:** Have ye suffered so many things in vain?  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:4

---

### Galatians 3:5

**Q13. He therefore that ministereth to you what, does so by works or faith?**  
**Answer:** He therefore that ministereth to you the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:5

**Q14. And worketh what among you?**  
**Answer:** And worketh miracles among you.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:5

**Q15. Does he do this by the works of the law, or by what?**  
**Answer:** By the works of the law, or by the hearing of faith?  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:5

---

### Galatians 3:6

**Q16. Even as who believed God?**  
**Answer:** Even as Abraham believed God.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:6

**Q17. What was accounted to him?**  
**Answer:** And it was accounted to him.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:6

**Q18. Accounted to him for what?**  
**Answer:** For righteousness.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:6

---

### Galatians 3:7

**Q19. Know ye therefore that they which are of what are the children of Abraham?**  
**Answer:** They which are of faith.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:7

**Q20. Who are the children of Abraham?**  
**Answer:** The same are the children of Abraham.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:7

**Q21. What word is used to start the identification of these children?**  
**Answer:** Know ye therefore.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:7

---

### Galatians 3:8

**Q22. And the scripture, foreseeing what, preached before the gospel unto Abraham?**  
**Answer:** Foreseeing that God would justify the heathen through faith.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:8

**Q23. What did the scripture preach before unto Abraham?**  
**Answer:** Preached before the gospel unto Abraham.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:8

**Q24. What statement was preached to Abraham concerning all nations?**  
**Answer:** In thee shall all nations be blessed.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:8

---

### Galatians 3:9

**Q25. So then they which be of faith are blessed with whom?**  
**Answer:** With faithful Abraham.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:9

**Q26. Who are blessed with faithful Abraham?**  
**Answer:** They which be of faith.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:9

**Q27. How are they described in relation to Abraham?**  
**Answer:** Blessed with faithful Abraham.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:9

---

### Galatians 3:10

**Q28. For as many as are of the works of the law are under what?**  
**Answer:** Are under the curse.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:10

**Q29. What is written regarding everyone that continueth not in all things?**  
**Answer:** Cursed is every one that continueth not in all things which are written in the book of the law to do them.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:10

**Q30. Where must these things be written to be done?**  
**Answer:** In the book of the law.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:10

---

### Galatians 3:11

**Q31. But that no man is justified by the law in the sight of God, what is evident?**  
**Answer:** For, The just shall live by faith.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:11

**Q32. Where is it established that no man is justified by the law?**  
**Answer:** In the sight of God.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:11

**Q33. By what shall the just live?**  
**Answer:** By faith.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:11

---

### Galatians 3:12

**Q34. And the law is not of what?**  
**Answer:** The law is not of faith.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:12

**Q35. But what does the man that does them do?**  
**Answer:** But, The man that doeth them shall live in them.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:12

**Q36. Where shall the man live who does them?**  
**Answer:** Shall live in them.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:12

---

### Galatians 3:13

**Q37. Christ hath redeemed us from what of the law?**  
**Answer:** From the curse of the law.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:13

**Q38. How did Christ redeem us from the curse of the law?**  
**Answer:** Being made a curse for us.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:13

**Q39. What is written about everyone that hangeth on a tree?**  
**Answer:** Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:13

---

### Galatians 3:14

**Q40. That the blessing of whom might come on the Gentiles through Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** The blessing of Abraham.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:14

**Q41. Through whom might the blessing of Abraham come on the Gentiles?**  
**Answer:** Through Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:14

**Q42. That we might receive what through faith?**  
**Answer:** The promise of the Spirit through faith.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:14

---

### Galatians 3:15

**Q43. Brethren, I speak after the manner of what?**  
**Answer:** After the manner of men.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:15

**Q44. Though it be but a man's what, if it be confirmed?**  
**Answer:** A man's covenant.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:15

**Q45. What happens once a man's covenant is confirmed?**  
**Answer:** No man disannulleth, or addeth thereto.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:15

---

### Galatians 3:16

**Q46. To Abraham and his seed were what made?**  
**Answer:** Were the promises made.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:16

**Q47. He saith not, And to seeds, as of what?**  
**Answer:** As of many.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:16

**Q48. But as of whom, referring to thy seed?**  
**Answer:** And to thy seed, which is Christ.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:16

---

### Galatians 3:17

**Q49. And this I say, that the covenant, that was confirmed before of God in whom?**  
**Answer:** In Christ.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:17

**Q50. The law, which was how many years after, cannot disannul?**  
**Answer:** Four hundred and thirty years after.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:17

**Q51. What cannot the law do to the promise?**  
**Answer:** That it should make the promise of none effect.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:17

---

### Galatians 3:18

**Q52. For if the inheritance be of the law, is it of what anymore?**  
**Answer:** It is no more of promise.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:18

**Q53. But God gave it to Abraham by what?**  
**Answer:** By promise.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:18

**Q54. What is stated to be of the law in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The inheritance.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:18

---

### Galatians 3:19

**Q55. Wherefore then serveth what?**  
**Answer:** The law.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:19

**Q56. Why was the law added according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** It was added because of transgressions.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:19

**Q57. Until when should the law stay?**  
**Answer:** Till the seed should come to whom the promise was made.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:19

---

### Galatians 3:20

**Q58. Now a mediator is not a mediator of whom?**  
**Answer:** Of one.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:20

**Q59. But who is one?**  
**Answer:** But God is one.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:20

**Q60. What concept is a mediator said not to be?**  
**Answer:** A mediator is not a mediator of one.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:20

---

### Galatians 3:21

**Q61. Is the law then against what of God?**  
**Answer:** Against the promises of God.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:21

**Q62. God forbid: for if there had been a law given which could have given what?**  
**Answer:** Which could have given life.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:21

**Q63. Verily righteousness should have been by what?**  
**Answer:** By the law.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:21

---

### Galatians 3:22

**Q64. But the scripture hath concluded all under what?**  
**Answer:** Under sin.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:22

**Q65. Why has the scripture concluded all under sin?**  
**Answer:** That the promise by faith of Jesus Christ might be given to them that believe.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:22

**Q66. To whom might the promise by faith of Jesus Christ be given?**  
**Answer:** To them that believe.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:22

---

### Galatians 3:23

**Q67. But before faith came, how were we kept?**  
**Answer:** We were kept under the law.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:23

**Q68. Shut up unto the faith which should afterwards be what?**  
**Answer:** Afterwards be revealed.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:23

**Q69. Under what condition were we kept before faith came?**  
**Answer:** Shut up unto the faith which should afterwards be revealed.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:23

---

### Galatians 3:24

**Q70. Wherefore what was our schoolmaster?**  
**Answer:** The law was our schoolmaster.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:24

**Q71. What was the schoolmaster's purpose regarding Christ?**  
**Answer:** To bring us unto Christ.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:24

**Q72. Why were we brought unto Christ by this schoolmaster?**  
**Answer:** That we might be justified by faith.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:24

---

**Answer:** Under a schoolmaster.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:25

**Q74. What event marks when we are no longer under a schoolmaster?**  
**Answer:** After that faith is come.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:25

**Q75. What is no longer ruling over us after faith comes?**  
**Answer:** A schoolmaster.  
**Verse:** Galatians 3:25

**Q76. For ye are all the children of whom?**

**Verse:** Galatians 3:26

**Verse:** Galatians 3:26

**Q83. There is neither bond nor what?**

**Verse:** Galatians 3:28

**Verse:** Galatians 3:28
