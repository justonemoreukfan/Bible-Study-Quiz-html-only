# Galatians 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Galatians 6:1

**Q1. What should a man be overtaken in if he is overtaken in a fault?**  
**Answer:** A fault.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:1

**Q2. Who are ye which are spiritual to restore such an one?**  
**Answer:** Ye.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:1

**Q3. In what spirit should ye restore such an one?**  
**Answer:** The spirit of meekness.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:1

---

### Galatians 6:2

**Q4. What should ye bear one of another?**  
**Answer:** Burdens.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:2

**Q5. What law will ye fulfill by bearing one another's burdens?**  
**Answer:** The law of Christ.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:2

---

### Galatians 6:3

**Q6. What does a man think he is when he is nothing?**  
**Answer:** Something.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:3

**Q7. What does a man deceive?**  
**Answer:** Himself.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:3

**Q8. What is a man when he thinketh himself to be something, being nothing?**  
**Answer:** Nothing.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:3

---

### Galatians 6:4

**Q9. What should every man prove?**  
**Answer:** His own work.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:4

**Q10. In what should every man have rejoicing?**  
**Answer:** In himself alone.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:4

**Q11. In what should every man not have rejoicing?**  
**Answer:** In another.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:4

---

### Galatians 6:5

**Q12. What shall every man bear?**  
**Answer:** His own burden.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:5

---

### Galatians 6:6

**Q13. Who is taught in the word?**  
**Answer:** Him that is taught in the word.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:6

**Q14. Whom should he that is taught communicate unto him that teacheth?**  
**Answer:** In all good things.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:6

---

### Galatians 6:7

**Q15. Who is not mocked?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:7

**Q16. What shall a man whatsoever he soweth also do?**  
**Answer:** Reap.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:7

---

### Galatians 6:8

**Q17. Unto what does he that soweth to his flesh reap corruption?**  
**Answer:** His flesh.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:8

**Q18. Unto what does he that soweth to the Spirit reap life everlasting?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:8

---

### Galatians 6:9

**Q19. In what should we not be weary?**  
**Answer:** In well doing.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:9

**Q20. When shall we reap if we faint not?**  
**Answer:** In due season.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:9

---

### Galatians 6:10

**Q21. As we have therefore opportunity, what should we do unto all men?**  
**Answer:** Do good.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:10

**Q22. Especially unto whom should we do good?**  
**Answer:** Unto them who are of the household of faith.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:10

---

### Galatians 6:11

**Q23. With what kind of letters have I written unto you?**  
**Answer:** Large letters.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:11

**Q24. Who has written large letters unto you with his own hand?**  
**Answer:** Mine own hand.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:11

---

### Galatians 6:12

**Q25. As many as desire to make what sort of show in the flesh do constrain you to be circumcised?**  
**Answer:** A fair show.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:12

**Q26. Only lest they should suffer what for the cross of Christ?**  
**Answer:** Persecution.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:12

---

### Galatians 6:13

**Q27. Who themselves who are circumcised keep not the law?**  
**Answer:** Neither they themselves who are circumcised.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:13

**Q28. What do they desire to have you circumcised, that they may glory in?**  
**Answer:** Your flesh.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:13

---

### Galatians 6:14

**Q29. God forbid that I should glory, save in what?**  
**Answer:** In the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:14

**Q30. By whom is the world crucified unto me?**  
**Answer:** By whom (or by which / Christ).  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:14

---

### Galatians 6:15

**Q31. In Christ Jesus what availeth anything?**  
**Answer:** Neither circumcision any thing, nor uncircumcision.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:15

**Q32. What availeth anything in Christ Jesus?**  
**Answer:** A new creature.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:15

---

### Galatians 6:16

**Q33. According to what rule shall peace and mercy be on them?**  
**Answer:** This rule.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:16

**Q34. Upon whom shall peace and mercy be?**  
**Answer:** Upon the Israel of God.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:16

---

### Galatians 6:17

**Q35. From henceforth let no man do what to me?**  
**Answer:** Trouble me.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:17

**Q36. What do I bear in my body?**  
**Answer:** The marks of the Lord Jesus.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:17

---

### Galatians 6:18

**Q37. What be with your spirit?**  
**Answer:** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:18

**Q38. With whose spirit be the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** Your spirit.  
**Verse:** Galatians 6:18
