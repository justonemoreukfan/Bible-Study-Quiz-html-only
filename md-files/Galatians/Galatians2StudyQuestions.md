# Galatians 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Galatians 2:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Galatians 2:1 state?**  
**Answer:** Then fourteen years after I went up again to Jerusalem with Barnabas.  
**Verse:** Galatians 2:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Galatians 2:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** and took Titus with me also.  
**Verse:** Galatians 2:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Galatians 2:1?**  
**Answer:** Then fourteen years after I went up again to Jerusalem with Barnabas, and took Titus with me also.  
**Verse:** Galatians 2:1

**Q47. What additional detail is declared in Galatians 2:16?**  
**Answer:** for by the works of the law shall no flesh be justified.  
**Verse:** Galatians 2:16

**Q48. According to Galatians 2:16, what is the full truth recorded?**

**Verse:** Galatians 2:16
