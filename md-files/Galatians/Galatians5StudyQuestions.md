# Galatians 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Galatians 5:1

**Q1. Wherewith hath Christ made us free?**  
**Answer:** Stand fast therefore in the liberty wherewith Christ hath made us free.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:1

**Q2. What should we not be entangled again with?**  
**Answer:** And be not entangled again with the yoke of bondage.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:1

**Q3. In what should we stand fast?**  
**Answer:** Stand fast therefore.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:1

---

### Galatians 5:2

**Q4. Who speaks in this verse regarding circumcision?**  
**Answer:** Behold, I Paul say unto you.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:2

**Q5. What will profit you nothing if ye be circumcised?**  
**Answer:** That if ye be circumcised, Christ shall profit you nothing.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:2

**Q6. To whom is Paul speaking when he mentions circumcision?**  
**Answer:** Unto you.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:2

---

### Galatians 5:3

**Q7. To every man that is circumcised, what does Paul testify again?**  
**Answer:** That he is a debtor to do the whole law.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:3

**Q8. What is every man that is circumcised a debtor to do?**  
**Answer:** The whole law.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:3

**Q9. Who testifies this to every man that is circumcised?**  
**Answer:** I testify again.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:3

---

### Galatians 5:4

**Q10. Who are become of no effect unto you?**  
**Answer:** Christ is become of no effect unto you.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:4

**Q11. By what are ye fallen from grace, if you are justified by the law?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever of you are justified by the law; ye are fallen from grace.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:4

**Q12. What state have those fallen from who are justified by the law?**  
**Answer:** Grace.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:4

---

### Galatians 5:5

**Q13. Through what do we wait for the hope of righteousness?**  
**Answer:** Through the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:5

**Q14. What do we wait for through the Spirit?**  
**Answer:** The hope of righteousness.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:5

**Q15. By what means do we eagerly look for this hope?**  
**Answer:** By faith.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:5

---

### Galatians 5:6

**Q16. In Jesus Christ, what avails anything regarding circumcision or uncircumcision?**  
**Answer:** Neither circumcision availeth any thing, nor uncircumcision.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:6

**Q17. What kind of faith availeth in Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** Faith which worketh by love.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:6

**Q18. In whom do neither circumcision nor uncircumcision avail anything?**  
**Answer:** In Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:6

---

### Galatians 5:7

**Q19. Who did run well?**  
**Answer:** Ye did run well.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:7

**Q20. Who hindered you that ye should not obey the truth?**  
**Answer:** Who did hinder you that ye should not obey the truth?  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:7

**Q21. What did they fail to obey because they were hindered?**  
**Answer:** The truth.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:7

---

### Galatians 5:8

**Q22. This persuasion cometh not of whom?**  
**Answer:** Him that calleth you.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:8

**Q23. What does not come from him that calleth you?**  
**Answer:** This persuasion.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:8

**Q24. What is the action described concerning him in this verse?**  
**Answer:** That calleth you.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:8

---

### Galatians 5:9

**Q25. How much leaven leaveneth the whole lump?**  
**Answer:** A little leaven.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:9

**Q26. What does a little leaven do to the whole lump?**  
**Answer:** Leaveneth the whole lump.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:9

**Q27. What is being leavened by a little leaven?**  
**Answer:** The whole lump.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:9

---

### Galatians 5:10

**Q28. What confidence have I in you through the Lord?**  
**Answer:** That ye will be none otherwise minded.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:10

**Q29. What shall he bear who troubleth you, whosoever he be?**  
**Answer:** His judgment.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:10

**Q30. Who shall bear his judgment?**  
**Answer:** He that troubleth you.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:10

---

### Galatians 5:11

**Q31. If I yet preach circumcision, what do I yet suffer?**  
**Answer:** Persecution.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:11

**Q32. What is then ceased if I yet preach circumcision?**  
**Answer:** Then is the offence of the cross ceased.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:11

**Q33. What do I yet preach if I am still suffering persecution?**  
**Answer:** Circumcision.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:11

---

### Galatians 5:12

**Q34. What would I have those do who trouble you?**  
**Answer:** I would they were even cut off.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:12

**Q35. Who would I have even cut off?**  
**Answer:** They which trouble you.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:12

**Q36. What action does Paul wish upon those who trouble them?**  
**Answer:** Were even cut off.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:12

---

### Galatians 5:13

**Q37. Unto what were ye called, brethren?**  
**Answer:** Unto liberty.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:13

**Q38. What should liberty not be used as an occasion for?**  
**Answer:** An occasion to the flesh.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:13

**Q39. By what should ye serve one another?**  
**Answer:** By love.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:13

---

### Galatians 5:14

**Q40. How is all the law fulfilled in one word?**  
**Answer:** Even in this.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:14

**Q41. Whose neighbor should you love as yourself?**  
**Answer:** Thy neighbour.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:14

**Q42. How are you to love your neighbor?**  
**Answer:** As thyself.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:14

---

### Galatians 5:15

**Q43. What might you do if ye bite and devour one another?**  
**Answer:** Take heed that ye be not consumed one of another.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:15

**Q44. What actions might you do to one another besides devouring?**  
**Answer:** Bite.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:15

**Q45. What could happen to you if you bite and devour one another?**  
**Answer:** Ye be not consumed one of another.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:15

---

### Galatians 5:16

**Q46. By what should you walk?**  
**Answer:** In the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:16

**Q47. What will you not fulfill if you walk in the Spirit?**  
**Answer:** The lust of the flesh.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:16

**Q48. What command is given regarding the Spirit in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Walk in the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:16

---

### Galatians 5:17

**Q49. Against what does the flesh lust?**  
**Answer:** Against the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:17

**Q50. Against what does the Spirit lust?**  
**Answer:** Against the flesh.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:17

**Q51. Why are these two contrary the one to the other?**  
**Answer:** So that ye cannot do the things that ye would.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:17

---

### Galatians 5:18

**Q52. By what are ye not led if you are led of the Spirit?**  
**Answer:** Under the law.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:18

**Q53. By what are you led if you are not under the law?**  
**Answer:** Of the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:18

**Q54. What condition prevents you from being under the law?**  
**Answer:** If ye be led of the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:18

---

### Galatians 5:19

**Q55. What are manifest, which are the works of the flesh?**  
**Answer:** The works of the flesh.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:19

**Q56. Name one of the specific works of the flesh listed first in this verse.**  
**Answer:** Adultery.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:19

**Q57. Name another work of the flesh listed in this verse alongside fornication and uncleanness.**  
**Answer:** Lasciviousness.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:19

---

### Galatians 5:20

**Q58. What works of the flesh mentioned in this verse involve worship and magic?**  
**Answer:** Idolatry, witchcraft.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:20

**Q59. What works of the flesh relate to division and conflict?**  
**Answer:** Hatred, variance, emulations, wrath, strife, seditions, heresies.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:20

**Q60. Which of these works of the flesh are listed right after emulations?**  
**Answer:** Wrath, strife.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:20

---

### Galatians 5:21

**Q61. What works of the flesh are listed first in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Envyings, murders.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:21

**Q62. What else is listed among the works of the flesh along with drunkenness and revellings?**  
**Answer:** Murthers (or murders / drunkenness / revellings).  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:21

**Q63. What do they which do such things not inherit?**  
**Answer:** The kingdom of God.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:21

---

### Galatians 5:22

**Q64. What is the fruit of the Spirit?**  
**Answer:** Love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:22

**Q65. Which fruit of the Spirit is listed right after joy?**  
**Answer:** Peace.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:22

**Q66. Which fruit of the Spirit is listed right after gentleness?**  
**Answer:** Goodness.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:22

---

### Galatians 5:23

**Q67. What fruits of the Spirit are mentioned at the beginning of this verse?**  
**Answer:** Meekness, temperance.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:23

**Q68. Against such things, what is there?**  
**Answer:** There is no law.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:23

**Q69. Is there any law against such things?**  
**Answer:** There is no law.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:23

---

### Galatians 5:24

**Q70. Whose are they that are Christ's?**  
**Answer:** They that are Christ's.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:24

**Q71. What have they crucified with the affections and lusts?**  
**Answer:** The flesh.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:24

**Q72. With what have they crucified the flesh?**  
**Answer:** With the affections and lusts.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:24

---

### Galatians 5:25

**Q73. If we live in the Spirit, by what should we also walk?**  
**Answer:** In the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:25

**Q74. What must be true of us first if we are to walk in the Spirit?**  
**Answer:** If we live in the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:25

**Q75. What action are we instructed to take if we live in the Spirit?**  
**Answer:** Let us also walk in the Spirit.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:25

**Q76. What should we not be desirous of?**  
**Answer:** Vain glory.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:26

**Q77. What action should we not do to one another regarding provocation?**  
**Answer:** Provoking one another.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:26

**Q78. What action should we not do to one another regarding envy?**  
**Answer:** Envying one another.  
**Verse:** Galatians 5:26
