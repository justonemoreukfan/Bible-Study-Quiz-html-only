# Galatians 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Galatians 4:1

**Q1. For how long time does the heir differ nothing from a servant, though he be lord of all?**  
**Answer:** As long as he is a child.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:1

**Q2. Who differs nothing from a servant as long as he is a child?**  
**Answer:** The heir.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:1

**Q3. Although he is a child, what is the heir lord of?**  
**Answer:** All.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:1

---

### Galatians 4:2

**Q1. Under whom is the heir until the time appointed of the father?**  
**Answer:** Tutors and governors.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:2

**Q2. Until what time is the heir under tutors and governors?**  
**Answer:** The time appointed of the father.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:2

**Q3. Who appoints the time until which the heir is under tutors and governors?**  
**Answer:** The father.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:2

---

### Galatians 4:3

**Q1. Even so, when we were children, under what were we kept in bondage?**  
**Answer:** The elements of the world.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:3

**Q2. When were we kept in bondage under the elements of the world?**  
**Answer:** When we were children.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:3

**Q3. What was done to us under the elements of the world when we were children?**  
**Answer:** We were kept in bondage.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:3

---

### Galatians 4:4

**Q1. When the fulness of the time was come, what did God send forth?**  
**Answer:** His Son.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:4

**Q2. Of whom was the Son made?**  
**Answer:** A woman.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:4

**Q3. Under what was the Son made?**  
**Answer:** The law.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:4

---

### Galatians 4:5

**Q1. To redeem whom did God send forth His Son?**  
**Answer:** Them that were under the law.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:5

**Q2. For what purpose did God send forth His Son to redeem them that were under the law?**  
**Answer:** That we might receive the adoption of sons.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:5

**Q3. What might we receive through the redemption of the Son?**  
**Answer:** The adoption of sons.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:5

---

### Galatians 4:6

**Q1. Because ye are sons, what has God sent forth into your hearts?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit of His Son.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:6

**Q2. Into what has God sent forth the Spirit of His Son?**  
**Answer:** Your hearts.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:6

**Q3. What does the Spirit cry in our hearts?**  
**Answer:** Abba, Father.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:6

---

### Galatians 4:7

**Q1. Wherefore thou art no more a servant, but what?**  
**Answer:** A son.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:7

**Q2. If thou art a son, what else are you through Christ?**  
**Answer:** An heir of God.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:7

**Q3. Through whom are you an heir of God?**  
**Answer:** Christ.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:7

---

### Galatians 4:8

**Q1. When ye knew not God, whom did ye serve?**  
**Answer:** Them which by nature are no gods.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:8

**Q2. Did they know God at that time?**  
**Answer:** No ("When ye knew not God").  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:8

**Q3. What were those whom they served by nature?**  
**Answer:** No gods.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:8

---

### Galatians 4:9

**Q1. After that ye have known God, or rather are known of God, how turn ye again?**  
**Answer:** To the weak and beggarly elements.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:9

**Q2. To what do ye desire again to be in bondage?**  
**Answer:** The weak and beggarly elements.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:9

**Q3. How are the elements described to which they desire again to be in bondage?**  
**Answer:** Weak and beggarly.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:9

### Galatians 4:10

**Q1. What do ye observe regarding days?**  
**Answer:** Ye observe days.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:10

**Q2. What else do ye observe besides days and times?**  
**Answer:** Months and years.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:10

**Q3. Complete the list of things observed in this verse: days, months, times, and...**  
**Answer:** Years.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:10

### Galatians 4:11

**Q1. What is Paul afraid of concerning the Galatians?**  
**Answer:** Lest I have bestowed upon you labour in vain.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:11

**Q2. Upon whom does Paul fear he has bestowed labour in vain?**  
**Answer:** You.  
**Verse:** Galatians 4:11

**Q3. How does Paul describe his labor upon them if it is in vain?**

**Verse:** Galatians 4:11

**Verse:** Galatians 4:12
