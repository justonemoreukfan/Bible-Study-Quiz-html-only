### Galatians 1:1
**Q1. According to Galatians 1:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** an apostle.  
**Verse:** Galatians 1:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Galatians 1:1?**  
**Answer:** (not of men).  
**Verse:** Galatians 1:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Galatians 1:1?**  
**Answer:** neither by man, but by Jesus Christ, and God the Father, who raised him from the dead.  
**Verse:** Galatians 1:1
