# Matthew 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 10:1

**Q1. Whom did Jesus call unto him?**  
**Answer:** His twelve disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:1

**Q2. What power did Jesus give his disciples against unclean spirits?**  
**Answer:** Power to cast them out.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:1

**Q3. What else did Jesus give the disciples power to heal?**  
**Answer:** All manner of sickness and all manner of disease.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:1

---

### Matthew 10:2

**Q1. What are the names of the first two apostles mentioned?**  
**Answer:** Simon, who is called Peter, and Andrew his brother.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:2

**Q2. Who are the next two brothers mentioned after Peter and Andrew?**  
**Answer:** James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:2

---

### Matthew 10:3

**Q1. Who are listed after James and John?**  
**Answer:** Philip, and Bartholomew; Thomas, and Matthew the publican.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:3

**Q2. Who are the next two disciples listed in this verse?**  
**Answer:** James the son of Alphaeus, and Lebbaeus, whose surname was Thaddaeus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:3

---

### Matthew 10:4

**Q1. Who are the final two disciples mentioned by name?**  
**Answer:** Simon the Canaanite, and Judas Iscariot.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:4

**Q2. What did Judas Iscariot do to Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Who also betrayed him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:4

---

### Matthew 10:5

**Q1. How many disciples did Jesus send forth?**  
**Answer:** Twelve.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:5

**Q2. What did Jesus command them, saying, "Go not into..."?**  
**Answer:** The way of the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:5

**Q3. Into whose city shall ye not enter?**  
**Answer:** Any city of the Samaritans.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:5

---

### Matthew 10:6

**Q1. To whom rather should the disciples go?**  
**Answer:** To the lost sheep of the house of Israel.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:6

---

### Matthew 10:7

**Q1. As the disciples go, what should they preach?**  
**Answer:** Saying, The kingdom of heaven is at hand.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:7

---

### Matthew 10:8

**Q1. What should the disciples do with the sick, cleansed lepers, raised dead, and cast out devils?**  
**Answer:** Heal the sick, cleanse the lepers, raise the dead, cast out devils.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:8

**Q2. How did the disciples receive these powers?**  
**Answer:** Freely.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:8

**Q3. How should the disciples give these powers?**  
**Answer:** Freely.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:8

---

### Matthew 10:9

**Q1. What should the disciples not provide in their purses?**  
**Answer:** Neither gold, nor silver, nor brass.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:9

---

### Matthew 10:10

**Q1. What should they not take for their journey?**  
**Answer:** Neither scrip for your journey, neither two coats, neither shoes, nor yet staves.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:10

**Q2. Why is the workman worthy of his meat?**  
**Answer:** For the workman is worthy of his meat.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:10

---

### Matthew 10:11

**Q1. Into whatsoever city or town ye shall enter, what should ye inquire?**  
**Answer:** Who in it is worthy.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:11

**Q2. Where should the disciples abide until they go thence?**  
**Answer:** In that house.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:11

---

### Matthew 10:12

**Q1. When ye come into an house, what should ye do?**  
**Answer:** Salute it.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:12

---

### Matthew 10:13

**Q1. If the house be worthy, what should come upon it?**  
**Answer:** Your peace.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:13

**Q2. If the house be not worthy, what should return to you?**  
**Answer:** Your peace.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:13

---

### Matthew 10:14

**Q1. What should be done if anyone will not receive you or hear your words when ye depart out of that house or city?**  
**Answer:** Shake off the dust of your feet.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:14

---

### Matthew 10:15

**Q1. Verily I say unto you, it shall be more tolerable for what land in the day of judgment?**  
**Answer:** The land of Sodom and Gomorrha.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:15

**Q2. Compared to what city will it be more tolerable for Sodom and Gomorrha?**  
**Answer:** That city.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:15

---

### Matthew 10:16

**Q1. How does Jesus send forth his disciples?**  
**Answer:** As sheep in the midst of wolves.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:16

**Q2. Therefore, what should disciples be as?**  
**Answer:** Wise as serpents.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:16

**Q3. What else should disciples be as, alongside being wise?**  
**Answer:** Harmless as doves.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:16

---

### Matthew 10:17

**Q1. Of whom should the disciples beware?**  
**Answer:** Men.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:17

**Q2. To whom will men deliver you up?**  
**Answer:** The councils.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:17

**Q3. What will men do to you in their synagogues?**  
**Answer:** Scourge you.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:17

---

### Matthew 10:18

**Q1. Before whom shall ye be brought for Jesus' sake?**  
**Answer:** Governors and kings.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:18

**Q2. For whose sake shall ye be brought before governors and kings?**  
**Answer:** For my sake.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:18

**Q3. To whom shall ye bear testimony before governors and kings?**  
**Answer:** For a testimony against them and the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:18

---

### Matthew 10:19

**Q1. When they deliver you up, what should ye not take thought about?**  
**Answer:** How or what ye shall speak.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:19

**Q2. When shall it be given you what ye shall speak?**  
**Answer:** In that same hour.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:19

---

### Matthew 10:20

**Q1. Who is it that speaks in you?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit of your Father.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:20

---

### Matthew 10:21

**Q1. Who shall deliver up the brother to death?**  
**Answer:** The brother.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:21

**Q2. Who shall deliver up the child?**  
**Answer:** The father.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:21

**Q3. Who shall rise up against their parents and cause them to be put to death?**  
**Answer:** Children.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:21

---

### Matthew 10:22

**Q1. By whom shall ye be hated?**  
**Answer:** All men.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:22

**Q2. For whose name's sake shall ye be hated?**  
**Answer:** For my name's sake.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:22

**Q3. Who shall be saved?**  
**Answer:** He that endureth to the end.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:22

---

### Matthew 10:23

**Q1. When they persecute you in this city, where should ye flee?**  
**Answer:** Into another.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:23

**Q2. Verily I say unto you, ye shall not have gone over what cities?**  
**Answer:** The cities of Israel.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:23

**Q3. Until what event shall ye not have gone over the cities of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Till the Son of man be come.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:23

---

### Matthew 10:24

**Q1. What is the disciple not above?**  
**Answer:** His master.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:24

**Q2. What is the servant not above?**  
**Answer:** His lord.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:24

---

### Matthew 10:25

**Q1. What is enough for the disciple?**  
**Answer:** That he be as his master.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:25

**Q2. What is enough for the servant?**  
**Answer:** As his lord.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:25

**Q3. If they have called the master of the house Beelzebub, what shall they call them of his household?**  
**Answer:** Much more they of his household.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:25

---

### Matthew 10:26

**Q1. Whom should ye not fear therefore?**  
**Answer:** Them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:26

**Q2. What is covered that shall not be revealed?**  
**Answer:** Nothing.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:26

**Q3. What is hid that shall not be known?**  
**Answer:** Nothing.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:26

---

### Matthew 10:27

**Q1. What I tell you in darkness, what should ye speak?**  
**Answer:** In light.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:27

**Q2. What ye hear in the ear, what should ye preach?**  
**Answer:** Upon the housetops.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:27

---

### Matthew 10:28

**Q1. Whom should ye not fear?**  
**Answer:** Them which kill the body.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:28

**Q2. What are they not able to kill?**  
**Answer:** The soul.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:28

**Q3. Rather, whom should ye fear?**  
**Answer:** Him which is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:28

---

### Matthew 10:29

**Q1. For how much are two sparrows sold?**  
**Answer:** A farthing.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:29

**Q2. Which of the sparrows shall not fall on the ground without your Father?**  
**Answer:** One of them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:29

---

### Matthew 10:30

**Q1. What are all numbered on your head?**  
**Answer:** The very hairs of your head.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:30

---

### Matthew 10:31

**Q1. Therefore, what should ye not fear?**  
**Answer:** Ye are of more value than many sparrows.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:31

**Q2. How are ye compared to many sparrows?**  
**Answer:** Of more value.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:31

---

### Matthew 10:32

**Q1. Whosoever therefore shall confess me before whom?**  
**Answer:** Men.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:32

**Q2. Whom will I also confess before?**  
**Answer:** My Father which is in heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:32

---

### Matthew 10:33

**Q1. But whosoever shall deny me before whom?**  
**Answer:** Men.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:33

**Q2. Whom will I also deny?**  
**Answer:** Before my Father which is in heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:33

---

### Matthew 10:34

**Q1. Think not that I am come to send what on earth?**  
**Answer:** Peace on earth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:34

**Q2. I came not to send peace, but what?**  
**Answer:** A sword.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:34

---

### Matthew 10:35

**Q1. For I am come to set a man at variance against whom?**  
**Answer:** His father.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:35

**Q2. Against whom else shall the daughter be set at variance?**  
**Answer:** Her mother.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:35

**Q3. Against whom shall the daughter in law be set at variance?**  
**Answer:** Her mother in law.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:35

---

### Matthew 10:36

**Q1. Who shall be a man's foes?**  
**Answer:** They of his own household.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:36

---

### Matthew 10:37

**Q1. Who loves father or mother more than me?**  
**Answer:** He that loveth father or mother more than me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:37

**Q2. Who loves son or daughter more than me?**  
**Answer:** He that loveth son or daughter more than me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:37

**Q3. Is such a person worthy of Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Is not worthy of me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:37

---

### Matthew 10:38

**Q1. What does he that takes not his cross do?**  
**Answer:** Follow after me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:38

**Q2. Is such a person worthy of Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Is not worthy of me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:38

---

### Matthew 10:39

**Q1. What will he that finds his life do?**  
**Answer:** Lose it.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:39

**Q2. What will he that loses his life for my sake do?**  
**Answer:** Find it.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:39

---

### Matthew 10:40

**Q1. He that receiveth you, whom does he receive?**  
**Answer:** Me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:40

**Q2. He that receiveth me, whom does he receive?**  
**Answer:** Him that sent me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:40

---

### Matthew 10:41

**Q1. He that receiveth a prophet in the name of a prophet, what shall he receive?**  
**Answer:** A prophet's reward.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:41

**Q2. He that receiveth a righteous man in the name of a righteous man, what shall he receive?**  
**Answer:** A righteous man's reward.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:41

---

### Matthew 10:42

**Q1. Whosoever shall give to drink unto one of these little ones a cup of what?**  
**Answer:** Cold water only.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:42

**Q2. In whose name must this cup of cold water be given?**  
**Answer:** In the name of a disciple.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:42

**Q3. Verily I say unto you, what shall he in no wise lose?**  
**Answer:** His reward.  
**Verse:** Matthew 10:42
