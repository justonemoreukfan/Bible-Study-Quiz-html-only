# Matthew 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 1:1

**Q1. Whose generation is the book of?**  
**Answer:** Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Matthew 1:1

**Q2. Whose son is Jesus Christ said to be in this verse?**  
**Answer:** David.  
**Verse:** Matthew 1:1

**Q3. Whose son is David said to be in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Abraham.  
**Verse:** Matthew 1:1
