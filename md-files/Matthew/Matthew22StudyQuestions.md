# Matthew 22 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 22:1

**Q1. How did Jesus speak unto them again?**  
**Answer:** By parables.  
**Verse:** Matthew 22:1

**Q2. Who did Jesus speak parables unto?**  
**Answer:** The chief priests and the elders of the people.  
**Verse:** Matthew 22:1

**Q3. In what manner did Jesus speak unto them?**  
**Answer:** By parables.  
**Verse:** Matthew 22:1

---

### Matthew 22:2

**Q4. What is the kingdom of heaven like unto?**  
**Answer:** A certain king, which made a marriage for his son.  
**Verse:** Matthew 22:2

**Q5. Who made a marriage for his son?**  
**Answer:** A certain king.  
**Verse:** Matthew 22:2

**Q6. For whom did the king make a marriage?**  
**Answer:** His son.  
**Verse:** Matthew 22:2

---

### Matthew 22:3

**Q7. Whom did the king send forth?**  
**Answer:** His servants.  
**Verse:** Matthew 22:3

**Q8. What were the servants sent to call?**  
**Answer:** Them that were bidden to the wedding.  
**Verse:** Matthew 22:3

**Q9. Did they want to come when called?**  
**Answer:** And they would not come.  
**Verse:** Matthew 22:3

---

### Matthew 22:4

**Q10. What did the king do again?**  
**Answer:** Sent forth other servants.  
**Verse:** Matthew 22:4

**Q11. What instructions were given to those servants concerning the dinner?**  
**Answer:** Tell them which are bidden, Behold, I have prepared my dinner: my oxen and my fatlings are killed, and all things are ready: come unto the marriage.  
**Verse:** Matthew 22:4

**Q12. What was killed for the dinner?**  
**Answer:** My oxen and my fatlings.  
**Verse:** Matthew 22:4
