# Matthew 21 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 21:1

**Q1. When they drew nigh unto Jerusalem, and were come to Bethphage, unto the mount of Olives, what did Jesus do?**  
**Answer:** He sent two disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:1

**Q2. Where did Jesus send two of his disciples from?**  
**Answer:** Bethphage, unto the mount of Olives.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:1

**Q3. To what city were they drawing nigh when Jesus sent the two disciples?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:1

---

### Matthew 21:2

**Q4. What did Jesus tell the disciples to go into?**  
**Answer:** The village over against you.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:2

**Q5. What would the disciples find straightway in the village?**  
**Answer:** An ass tied, and a colt with her.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:2

**Q6. What did Jesus instruct the disciples to do with the ass and the colt?**  
**Answer:** Loose them, and bring them unto me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:2

---

### Matthew 21:3

**Q7. If any man say ought unto the disciples, what were they to say?**  
**Answer:** The Lord hath need of them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:3

**Q8. When would the man send the animals back?**  
**Answer:** Straightway.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:3

**Q9. Who did the disciples say had need of the animals?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:3

---

### Matthew 21:4

**Q10. All this was done that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by whom?**  
**Answer:** The prophet.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:4

**Q11. What happened so that the prophecy might be fulfilled?**  
**Answer:** All this was done.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:4

**Q12. By what means was the word spoken, according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** By the prophet.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:4

---

### Matthew 21:5

**Q13. Tell ye the daughter of what place to come?**  
**Answer:** Sion.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:5

**Q14. Behold, who cometh unto thee?**  
**Answer:** Thy King.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:5

**Q15. How does the King come unto the daughter of Sion?**  
**Answer:** Meek, and sitting upon an ass, and a colt the foal of an ass.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:5

---

### Matthew 21:6

**Q16. Who went and did as Jesus commanded them?**  
**Answer:** The disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:6

---

*(Note: In strict adherence to the instruction for 3 questions per verse, here are the remaining two questions for Matthew 21:6, though the text is short.)*

**Q17. Whose command did the disciples go and do?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:6

**Q18. What did the disciples do with regards to Jesus' command?**  
**Answer:** Went and did as Jesus commanded them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:6

---

### Matthew 21:7

**Q19. What animals did the disciples bring?**  
**Answer:** The ass, and the colt.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:7

**Q20. What did the disciples put on the animals?**  
**Answer:** Their clothes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:7

**Q21. Who sat thereon?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:7

---

### Matthew 21:8

**Q22. What did a very great multitude spread in the way?**  
**Answer:** Their garments.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:8

**Q23. What did others cut down from the trees and strew in the way?**  
**Answer:** Branches.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:8

**Q24. What did the very great multitude do with their garments and branches?**  
**Answer:** Spread them in the way.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:8

---

### Matthew 21:9

**Q25. What did the multitudes that went before, and that followed, cry?**  
**Answer:** Hosanna to the Son of David.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:9

**Q26. Blessed is he that cometh in the name of whom?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:9

**Q27. Where did the multitudes cry "Hosanna in the highest"?**  
**Answer:** In the highest.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:9

---

### Matthew 21:10

**Q28. When he was come into Jerusalem, what did the whole city do?**  
**Answer:** Was moved.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:10

**Q29. What question did the city ask when Jesus entered?**  
**Answer:** Who is this?  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:10

**Q30. Into what city was he come when the whole city was moved?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:10

---

### Matthew 21:11

**Q31. And the multitude said, Who is this?**  
**Answer:** Jesus the prophet of Nazareth of Galilee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:11

**Q32. From what place was Jesus the prophet said to be?**  
**Answer:** Nazareth of Galilee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:11

**Q33. What office was Jesus called by the multitude?**  
**Answer:** The prophet.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:11

---

### Matthew 21:12

**Q34. Into what building did Jesus go?**  
**Answer:** The temple of God.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:12

**Q35. Whom did Jesus cast out of the temple?**  
**Answer:** All them that sold and bought in the temple.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:12

**Q36. What did Jesus overthrow in the temple?**  
**Answer:** The tables of the moneychangers, and the seats of them that sold doves.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:12

---

### Matthew 21:13

**Q37. What is written regarding the house?**  
**Answer:** My house shall be called the house of prayer.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:13

**Q38. What had the people made the house?**  
**Answer:** A den of thieves.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:13

**Q39. Whose house was it said to be?**  
**Answer:** My house.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:13

---

### Matthew 21:14

**Q40. Who came to Jesus in the temple?**  
**Answer:** The blind and the lame.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:14

**Q41. Where did the blind and the lame come to Jesus?**  
**Answer:** In the temple.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:14

**Q42. What did Jesus do for the blind and the lame?**  
**Answer:** He healed them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:14

---

### Matthew 21:15

**Q43. When the chief priests and scribes saw the wonderful things that he did, and the children crying in the temple, how did they feel?**  
**Answer:** Sore displeased.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:15

**Q44. What were the children crying in the temple?**  
**Answer:** Hosanna to the Son of David.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:15

**Q45. Who saw the wonderful things and heard the children crying?**  
**Answer:** The chief priests and scribes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:15

---

### Matthew 21:16

**Q46. What question did the chief priests and scribes ask Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Hearest thou what these say?  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:16

**Q47. What did Jesus ask if they had ever read?**  
**Answer:** Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings thou hast perfected praise.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:16

**Q48. Who did Jesus say had perfected praise?**  
**Answer:** Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:16

---

### Matthew 21:17

**Q49. Whom did Jesus leave?**  
**Answer:** Them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:17

**Q50. Where did Jesus go out of the city?**  
**Answer:** Into Bethany.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:17

**Q51. Where did Jesus lodge?**  
**Answer:** There.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:17

---

### Matthew 21:18

**Q52. In the morning, as Jesus returned into the city, what did he experience?**  
**Answer:** He hungered.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:18

**Q53. When did Jesus return into the city?**  
**Answer:** In the morning.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:18

**Q54. Where was Jesus returning to?**  
**Answer:** Into the city.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:18

---

### Matthew 21:19

**Q55. When Jesus saw a fig tree in the way, what did he find on it?**  
**Answer:** Nothing thereon, but leaves only.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:19

---

*(Note: Additional questions to fulfill the 3-question requirement for verse 19)*

**Q56. What did Jesus say to the fig tree?**  
**Answer:** Let no fruit grow on thee henceforward for ever.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:19

**Q57. What happened to the fig tree presently after Jesus spoke?**  
**Answer:** The fig tree withered away.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:19

---

### Matthew 21:20

**Q58. When the disciples saw the fig tree wither away, what did they ask?**  
**Answer:** How soon is the fig tree withered away!  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:20

**Q59. Who saw the fig tree wither away?**  
**Answer:** The disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:20

**Q60. What was the state of the fig tree when the disciples saw it?**  
**Answer:** Withered away.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:20

---

### Matthew 21:21

**Q61. Jesus answered and said unto them, if ye have faith, and what else?**  
**Answer:** Doubt not.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:21

**Q62. What could they do to the fig tree, besides what was done?**  
**Answer:** Not only do this which is done to the fig tree.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:21

**Q63. What could they say to the mountain, and it would be done?**  
**Answer:** Be thou removed, and be thou cast into the sea.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:21

---

### Matthew 21:22

**Q64. All things, whatsoever ye shall ask in prayer, believing, what will happen?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall receive.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:22

**Q65. How must you ask in prayer to receive?**  
**Answer:** Believing.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:22

**Q66. What kind of things shall ye receive if ye ask believing in prayer?**  
**Answer:** All things, whatsoever ye shall ask.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:22

---

### Matthew 21:23

**Q67. When Jesus was come into the temple, who came unto him as he was teaching?**  
**Answer:** The chief priests and the elders of the people.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:23

**Q68. What did the chief priests and elders ask Jesus?**  
**Answer:** By what authority doest thou these things? and who gave thee this authority?  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:23

**Q69. What was Jesus doing when the chief priests and elders came unto him?**  
**Answer:** Teaching.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:23

---

### Matthew 21:24

**Q70. How many things did Jesus answer and say unto them?**  
**Answer:** One thing.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:24

**Q71. What would they do if they told Jesus that one thing?**  
**Answer:** I in like wise will tell you by what authority I do these things.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:24

**Q72. Who answered and said unto them, I also will ask you one thing?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:24

---

### Matthew 21:25

**Q73. From whence was the baptism of John?**  
**Answer:** From heaven, or of men.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:25

---

*(Note: Additional questions for verse 25 to meet the 3-question rule)*

**Q74. What did the chief priests and elders reason with themselves about?**  
**Answer:** If we shall say, From heaven; he will say unto us, Why did ye not then believe him?  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:25

**Q75. Whose baptism was being questioned regarding its origin?**  
**Answer:** The baptism of John.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:25

---

### Matthew 21:26

**Q76. But if they said, Of men, whom did they fear?**  
**Answer:** The people.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:26

**Q77. Why did they fear the people if they said "Of men"?**  
**Answer:** For all hold John as a prophet.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:26

**Q78. How did all hold John, according to the elders' reasoning?**  
**Answer:** As a prophet.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:26

---

### Matthew 21:27

**Q79. What did they answer Jesus and say?**  
**Answer:** We cannot tell.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:27

**Q80. What did Jesus say unto them after their answer?**  
**Answer:** Neither tell I you by what authority I do these things.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:27

**Q81. To whom did Jesus say "Neither tell I you"?**  
**Answer:** Them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:27

---

### Matthew 21:28

**Q82. What did Jesus ask the people to think about?**  
**Answer:** A certain man had two sons.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:28

**Q83. Who did the certain man have?**  
**Answer:** Two sons.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:28

**Q84. What did the father say to the first son?**  
**Answer:** Son, go work to day in my vineyard.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:28

---

### Matthew 21:29

**Q85. What was the first son's answer to his father?**  
**Answer:** I will not.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:29

**Q86. What did the first son do afterward?**  
**Answer:** He repented, and went.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:29

**Q87. Who answered and said, "I will not"?**  
**Answer:** He (the first son).  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:29

---

### Matthew 21:30

**Q88. To whom did the father come and say likewise?**  
**Answer:** The second son.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:30

**Q89. What did the second son answer?**  
**Answer:** I go, sir.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:30

**Q90. Where did the second son go after saying "I go, sir"?**  
**Answer:** He went not.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:30

---

### Matthew 21:31

**Q91. Which of the twain did the will of his father?**  
**Answer:** The first.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:31

**Q92. Who said unto them, Verily I say unto you, That the publicans and the harlots go into the kingdom of God before you?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:31

**Q93. Who goes into the kingdom of God before the chief priests and elders?**  
**Answer:** The publicans and the harlots.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:31

---

### Matthew 21:32

**Q94. Who came unto you in the way of righteousness, and you believed him not?**  
**Answer:** John.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:32

**Q95. Who believed John?**  
**Answer:** The publicans and the harlots.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:32

**Q96. What did the chief priests and elders do after seeing it, regarding repentance?**  
**Answer:** Repented not afterward, that ye might believe him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:32

---

### Matthew 21:33

**Q97. Hear another parable: What did a certain householder plant?**  
**Answer:** A vineyard.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:33

**Q98. What did the householder hedge around the vineyard, dig, and build?**  
**Answer:** Hedged it round about, and digged a winepress in it, and built a tower.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:33

**Q99. To whom did the householder let out the vineyard before going into a far country?**  
**Answer:** Husbandmen.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:33

---

### Matthew 21:34

**Q100. When the time of the fruit drew near, what did the householder do?**  
**Answer:** Sent his servants to the husbandmen, that they might receive the fruits of it.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:34

**Q101. Why did the householder send his servants?**  
**Answer:** That they might receive the fruits of it.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:34

**Q102. Whose servants were sent to the husbandmen?**  
**Answer:** The householder's.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:34

---

### Matthew 21:35

**Q103. What did the husbandmen do to the servants?**  
**Answer:** Took one, and beat another, and killed another.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:35

---

*(Note: Additional questions for verse 35 to fulfill the 3-question count)*

**Q104. Which servant was beaten by the husbandmen?**  
**Answer:** Another (one of the servants).  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:35

**Q105. What happened to one of the servants besides being beaten?**  
**Answer:** Killed another.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:35

---

### Matthew 21:36

**Q106. What did the householder do again after the first servants were treated poorly?**  
**Answer:** Sent other servants more than the first.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:36

**Q107. How did the husbandmen treat the second group of servants?**  
**Answer:** Did unto them likewise.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:36

**Q108. How many servants were sent the second time compared to the first?**  
**Answer:** More than the first.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:36

---

**Answer:** His son.  
**Verse:** Matthew 21:37
