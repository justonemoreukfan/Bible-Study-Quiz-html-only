# Matthew 19 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 19:1

**Q1. When Jesus had finished these sayings, where did he depart from?**  
**Answer:** Galilee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:1

**Q2. Into whose coasts did Jesus come after departing Galilee?**  
**Answer:** The coasts of Judaea beyond Jordan.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:1

**Q3. Who followed Jesus into the coasts of Judaea beyond Jordan?**  
**Answer:** Great multitudes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:1

---

### Matthew 19:2

**Q4. Who followed Jesus into the coasts of Judaea beyond Jordan?**  
**Answer:** Great multitudes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:2

**Q5. What did Jesus do for the great multitudes there?**  
**Answer:** He healed them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:2

---

### Matthew 19:3

**Q6. Who came unto Jesus tempting him?**  
**Answer:** The Pharisees.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:3

**Q7. What did the Pharisees ask Jesus concerning a man putting away his wife?**  
**Answer:** Is it lawful for a man to put away his wife for every cause?  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:3

---

### Matthew 19:4

**Q8. What did Jesus ask the Pharisees if they had not read?**  
**Answer:** That he which made them at the beginning made them male and female.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:4

---

### Matthew 19:5

**Q9. What did Jesus say a man shall leave for this cause?**  
**Answer:** Father and mother.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:5

**Q10. To whom shall a man cleave?**  
**Answer:** His wife.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:5

**Q11. What shall the twain become?**  
**Answer:** One flesh.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:5

---

### Matthew 19:6

**Q12. What are they no more after becoming one flesh?**  
**Answer:** Twayn, but one flesh.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:6

**Q13. What command is given concerning what God has joined together?**  
**Answer:** Let not man put asunder.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:6

---

### Matthew 19:7

**Q14. What did they say Moses did command?**  
**Answer:** To give a writing of divorcement, and to put her away.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:7

---

### Matthew 19:8

**Q15. Why did Moses suffer the Pharisees to put away their wives?**  
**Answer:** Because of the hardness of your hearts.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:8

**Q16. Was the putting away of wives so from the beginning?**  
**Answer:** From the beginning it was not so.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:8

---

### Matthew 19:9

**Q17. What does Jesus say whosoever shall put away his wife, except it be for fornication, and shall marry another, committeth?**  
**Answer:** Adultery.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:9

**Q18. What does whoso marry her which is put away commit?**  
**Answer:** Adultery.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:9

**Q19. What exception is given for putting away a wife without committing adultery upon remarriage?**  
**Answer:** Except it be for fornication.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:9

---

### Matthew 19:10

**Q20. Whose disciples say unto him, If the case of the man be so with his wife, it is not good to marry?**  
**Answer:** His disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:10

---

### Matthew 19:11

**Q21. What did Jesus say unto them concerning this saying?**  
**Answer:** All men cannot receive this saying, save they to whom it is given.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:11

---

### Matthew 19:12

**Q22. What kind of eunuchs are there from their mother's womb?**  
**Answer:** Some.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:12

**Q23. What kind of eunuchs are made of men?**  
**Answer:** Some.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:12

**Q24. What kind of eunuchs have made themselves eunuchs for the kingdom of heaven's sake?**  
**Answer:** There be eunuchs, which have made themselves eunuchs for the kingdom of heaven's sake.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:12

---

### Matthew 19:13

**Q25. Who were brought unto Jesus that he should put his hands on them, and pray?**  
**Answer:** Little children.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:13

**Q26. Who rebuked those that brought the little children?**  
**Answer:** The disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:13

---

### Matthew 19:14

**Q27. What did Jesus say concerning the little children?**  
**Answer:** Suffer little children, and forbid them not, to come unto me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:14

**Q28. Of such is what?**  
**Answer:** The kingdom of heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:14

---

### Matthew 19:15

**Q29. What did Jesus do after laying his hands on them?**  
**Answer:** He departed thence.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:15

---

### Matthew 19:16

**Q30. Who came and said unto him, Good Master, what good thing shall I do, that I may have eternal life?**  
**Answer:** One.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:16

---

### Matthew 19:17

**Q31. What did Jesus ask the man concerning why he called him good?**  
**Answer:** Why callest thou me good? there is none good but one, that is, God.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:17

**Q32. What did Jesus tell the man to do if he would enter into life?**  
**Answer:** Keep the commandments.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:17

---

### Matthew 19:18

**Q33. Which commandments did the man ask about?**  
**Answer:** Which?  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:18

**Q34. What specific commandments did Jesus list?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt do no murder, Thou shalt not commit adultery, Thou shalt not steal, Thou shalt not bear false witness.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:18

---

### Matthew 19:19

**Q35. Whose honor is commanded in the list of commandments?**  
**Answer:** Honour thy father and thy mother.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:19

**Q36. How is one's neighbor to be loved according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:19

---

### Matthew 19:20

**Q37. What did the young man say he had kept?**  
**Answer:** All these things from my youth up.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:20

**Q38. What question did the young man ask Jesus at the end?**  
**Answer:** What lack I yet?  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:20

---

### Matthew 19:21

**Q39. What did Jesus tell the young man to do if he would be perfect?**  
**Answer:** Go and sell that thou hast, and give to the poor.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:21

**Q40. Where did Jesus say the young man would have treasure?**  
**Answer:** In heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:21

**Q41. After selling and giving, what did Jesus tell the man to come and do?**  
**Answer:** Follow me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:21

---

### Matthew 19:22

**Q42. How did the young man go away when he heard that saying?**  
**Answer:** Sorrowful.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:22

**Q43. Why did the young man go away sorrowful?**  
**Answer:** For he had great possessions.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:22

---

### Matthew 19:23

**Q44. What did Jesus say unto his disciples regarding a rich man?**  
**Answer:** Verily I say unto you, That a rich man shall hardly enter into the kingdom of heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:23

---

### Matthew 19:24

**Q45. What did Jesus compare a rich man entering the kingdom of God to?**  
**Answer:** It is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:24

---

### Matthew 19:25

**Q46. How did his disciples feel when they heard it?**  
**Answer:** They were exceedingly amazed.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:25

**Q47. What question did the disciples ask?**  
**Answer:** Who then can be saved?  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:25

---

### Matthew 19:26

**Q48. Upon beholding them, what did Jesus say to them regarding men?**  
**Answer:** With men this is impossible.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:26

**Q49. What did Jesus say is possible with God?**  
**Answer:** With God all things are possible.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:26

---

### Matthew 19:27

**Q50. Who answered and said unto him, Behold, we have forsaken all, and followed thee?**  
**Answer:** Peter.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:27

**Q51. What question did Peter ask Jesus about what they shall have therefore?**  
**Answer:** What shall we have therefore?  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:27

---

### Matthew 19:28

**Q52. In what period did Jesus say the Son of man shall sit in the throne of his glory?**  
**Answer:** In the regeneration.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:28

**Q53. Upon how many thrones shall the disciples sit, judging the twelve tribes of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Twelve thrones.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:28

---

### Matthew 19:29

**Q54. What did Jesus say every one that hath forsaken houses, brethren, sisters, father, mother, wife, children, or lands for his name's sake shall receive?**  
**Answer:** An hundredfold.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:29

**Q55. What else shall they inherit besides an hundredfold?**  
**Answer:** Everlasting life.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:29

---

### Matthew 19:30

**Q56. What shall many that are first be?**  
**Answer:** Last.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:30

**Q57. What shall many that are last be?**  
**Answer:** First.  
**Verse:** Matthew 19:30
