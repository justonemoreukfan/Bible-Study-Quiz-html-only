# Matthew 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 9:1

**Q1. What did Jesus enter into?**  
**Answer:** A ship.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:1

**Q2. What did Jesus pass over?**  
**Answer:** The sea.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:1

**Q3. Whose city did Jesus come into?**  
**Answer:** His own city.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:1

---

### Matthew 9:2

**Q1. What did they bring to Jesus?**  
**Answer:** A man sick of the palsy, lying on a bed.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:2

**Q2. What did Jesus see when they brought the man?**  
**Answer:** Their faith.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:2

**Q3. What did Jesus say to the sick of the palsy?**  
**Answer:** Son, be of good cheer; thy sins be forgiven thee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:2

---

### Matthew 9:3

**Q1. What did certain of the scribes say within themselves?**  
**Answer:** This man blasphemeth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:3

---

### Matthew 9:4

**Q1. What did Jesus know?**  
**Answer:** Their thoughts.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:4

**Q2. What question did Jesus ask them regarding their thoughts?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore think ye evil in your hearts?  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:4

---

### Matthew 9:5

**Q1. What is easier to say according to Jesus' question?**  
**Answer:** Thy sins be forgiven thee; or to say, Arise, and walk?  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:5

---

### Matthew 9:6

**Q1. What authority did the Son of man have upon earth?**  
**Answer:** Power to forgive sins.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:6

**Q2. What did Jesus say to the sick of the palsy after speaking of His power?**  
**Answer:** Arise, take up thy bed, and go unto thine house.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:6

---

### Matthew 9:7

**Q1. What did the man do after Jesus spoke to him?**  
**Answer:** He arose, and departed to his house.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:7

---

### Matthew 9:8

**Q1. What did the multitudes see?**  
**Answer:** It.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:8

**Q2. How did the multitudes react when they saw it?**  
**Answer:** They marveled.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:8

**Q3. Whom did the multitudes glorify?**  
**Answer:** God, which had given such power unto men.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:8

---

### Matthew 9:9

**Q1. From where did Jesus pass forth?**  
**Answer:** From thence.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:9

**Q2. Whom did Jesus see sitting at the receipt of custom?**  
**Answer:** A man, named Matthew.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:9

**Q3. What did Jesus say unto him, and what did he do?**  
**Answer:** He saith unto him, Follow me. And he arose, and followed him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:9

---

### Matthew 9:10

**Q1. Where was Jesus sitting?**  
**Answer:** In the house.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:10

**Q2. Who came and sat down with Jesus and his disciples?**  
**Answer:** Many publicans and sinners.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:10

---

### Matthew 9:11

**Q1. Who saw the publicans and sinners eating with Jesus and his disciples?**  
**Answer:** The Pharisees.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:11

**Q2. What did the Pharisees say unto his disciples?**  
**Answer:** Why eateth your Master with publicans and sinners?  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:11

---

### Matthew 9:12

**Q1. Who heard the question asked by the Pharisees?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:12

**Q2. Who do not need a physician according to Jesus?**  
**Answer:** They that be whole.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:12

**Q3. Who do need a physician?**  
**Answer:** They that are sick.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:12

---

### Matthew 9:13

**Q1. What did Jesus tell them to go and learn the meaning of?**  
**Answer:** What that meaneth, I will have mercy, and not sacrifice.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:13

**Q2. Whom did Jesus say he is not come to call?**  
**Answer:** The righteous.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:13

**Q3. Whom did Jesus say he is come to call?**  
**Answer:** Sinners to repentance.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:13

---

### Matthew 9:14

**Q1. Who came to Jesus?**  
**Answer:** The disciples of John.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:14

**Q2. What question did the disciples of John ask Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Why do we and the Pharisees fast oft, but thy disciples fast not?  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:14

---

### Matthew 9:15

**Q1. Who cannot mourn as long as the bridegroom is with them?**  
**Answer:** The children of the bridechamber.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:15

**Q2. When will the days come when the bridegroom shall be taken from them?**  
**Answer:** Then shall they fast.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:15

---

### Matthew 9:16

**Q1. What is put unto an old garment?**  
**Answer:** A piece of new cloth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:16

**Q2. What does the new piece take from the garment?**  
**Answer:** The piece filled up from the old.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:16

**Q3. What happens to the rent after the new piece is put on?**  
**Answer:** The rent is made worse.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:16

---

### Matthew 9:17

**Q1. Into what do men put new wine?**  
**Answer:** Into new bottles.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:17

**Q2. What happens to the bottles if new wine is put into old bottles?**  
**Answer:** The bottles break, and the wine runneth out.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:17

**Q3. Where do they put both new wine and new bottles?**  
**Answer:** Into both new bottles, and both are preserved.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:17

---

### Matthew 9:18

**Q1. Who came and worshipped Jesus while he spoke these things unto them?**  
**Answer:** A certain ruler.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:18

**Q2. What condition was the ruler's daughter in?**  
**Answer:** My daughter is even now dead.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:18

**Q3. What did the ruler ask Jesus to do?**  
**Answer:** Come and lay thy hand upon her, and she shall live.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:18

---

### Matthew 9:19

**Q1. Who arose and followed the ruler?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:19

**Q2. Who else followed Jesus along with his disciples?**  
**Answer:** His disciples (noted as "and his disciples").  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:19

---

### Matthew 9:20

**Q1. What was the affliction of the woman who came behind Jesus?**  
**Answer:** A woman, which was diseased with an issue of blood twelve years.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:20

**Q2. What part of Jesus' garment did she touch?**  
**Answer:** The hem of his garment.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:20

---

### Matthew 9:21

**Q1. What did the woman say within herself regarding touching Jesus' garment?**  
**Answer:** If I may but touch his garment, I shall be whole.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:21

---

### Matthew 9:22

**Q1. What did Jesus do when he turned him about and saw her?**  
**Answer:** He said, Daughter, be of good comfort; thy faith hath made thee whole.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:22

**Q2. When was the woman made whole?**  
**Answer:** From that same hour.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:22

---

### Matthew 9:23

**Q1. Where did Jesus come into?**  
**Answer:** Into the ruler's house.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:23

**Q2. Whom did Jesus see in the ruler's house?**  
**Answer:** The minstrels and the people making a noise.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:23

---

### Matthew 9:24

**Q1. What did Jesus say to the people in the ruler's house?**  
**Answer:** Give place: for the maid is not dead, but sleepeth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:24

**Q2. How did the people react to what Jesus said?**  
**Answer:** They laughed him to scorn.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:24

---

### Matthew 9:25

**Q1. When the people were put forth, what did Jesus do?**  
**Answer:** He went in, and took her by the hand.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:25

**Q2. What happened to the maid after Jesus took her by the hand?**  
**Answer:** The maid arose.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:25

---

### Matthew 9:26

**Q1. What went abroad regarding this event?**  
**Answer:** The fame hereof.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:26

**Q2. Where did the fame go abroad into?**  
**Answer:** Into all that land.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:26

---

### Matthew 9:27

**Q1. Who departed from thence?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:27

**Q2. Who followed Jesus as he departed from thence?**  
**Answer:** Two blind men.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:27

**Q3. What did the two blind men cry out?**  
**Answer:** Thou son of David, have mercy on us.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:27

---

### Matthew 9:28

**Q1. Where did Jesus go?**  
**Answer:** Into the house.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:28

**Q2. Who came to Jesus in the house?**  
**Answer:** The blind men.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:28

**Q3. What question did Jesus ask the blind men?**  
**Answer:** Believe ye that I am able to do this?  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:28

---

### Matthew 9:29

**Q1. What part of the blind men did Jesus touch?**  
**Answer:** Their eyes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:29

**Q2. What words did Jesus use to describe how it should be done unto them?**  
**Answer:** According to your faith be it unto you.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:29

---

### Matthew 9:30

**Q1. What happened to the eyes of the blind men?**  
**Answer:** Their eyes were opened.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:30

**Q2. What did Jesus straitly charge them?**  
**Answer:** Saying, See that no man know it.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:30

---

### Matthew 9:31

**Q1. What did the men do after leaving?**  
**Answer:** Spread abroad his fame in all that country.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:31

---

### Matthew 9:32

**Q1. As they went out, who was brought to Jesus?**  
**Answer:** A dumb man possessed with a devil.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:32

---

### Matthew 9:33

**Q1. What happened when the devil was cast out?**  
**Answer:** The dumb spake.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:33

**Q2. How did the multitudes react to this miracle?**  
**Answer:** They marveled.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:33

**Q3. What did the multitudes say about Israel after seeing the miracle?**  
**Answer:** It was never so seen in Israel.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:33

---

### Matthew 9:34

**Q1. What did the Pharisees say?**  
**Answer:** He casteth out devils through the prince of the devils.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:34

---

### Matthew 9:35

**Q1. What places did Jesus go about teaching in?**  
**Answer:** All the cities and villages.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:35

**Q2. Where did Jesus teach the people?**  
**Answer:** In their synagogues.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:35

**Q3. What did Jesus preach and heal?**  
**Answer:** Preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every sickness and every disease among the people.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:35

---

### Matthew 9:36

**Q1. Whom did Jesus see when he looked at the multitudes?**  
**Answer:** The multitudes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:36

**Q2. How did Jesus feel when he saw the multitudes?**  
**Answer:** He was moved with compassion on them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:36

**Q3. What condition were the multitudes in?**  
**Answer:** Fainted, and were scattered abroad, as sheep having no shepherd.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:36

---

### Matthew 9:37

**Q1. What did Jesus say unto his disciples regarding the harvest?**  
**Answer:** The harvest truly is plenteous, but the labourers are few.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:37

---

### Matthew 9:38

**Q1. Whom did Jesus tell them to pray to?**  
**Answer:** The Lord of the harvest.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:38

**Q2. What did Jesus instruct them to pray for the Lord of the harvest to send forth?**  
**Answer:** Labourers into his harvest.  
**Verse:** Matthew 9:38
