# Matthew 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 12:1

**Q1. At that time, through what did Jesus go on the sabbath day?**  
**Answer:** The corn.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:1

**Q2. What were his disciples?**  
**Answer:** An hungred.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:1

**Q3. What did the disciples begin to do?**  
**Answer:** To pluck the ears of corn, and to eat.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:1

---

### Matthew 12:2

**Q4. When the Pharisees saw it, what did they say unto him?**  
**Answer:** Behold, thy disciples do that which is not lawful to do upon the sabbath day.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:2

---

### Matthew 12:3

**Q5. But he said unto them, have ye not read what David did when he did what?**  
**Answer:** When he was an hungred, and they that were with him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:3

---

### Matthew 12:4

**Q6. How did he enter into the house of God?**  
**Answer:** He entered.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:4

**Q7. What did he eat, which was not lawful for him to eat, neither for them which were with him?**  
**Answer:** The shewbread.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:4

**Q8. For whom only was the shewbread lawful?**  
**Answer:** Only for the priests.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:4

---

### Matthew 12:5

**Q9. Or have ye not read in the law, how that on the sabbath days what do in the temple?**  
**Answer:** The priests profane the sabbath, and are blameless.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:5

---

### Matthew 12:6

**Q10. But I say unto you, that in this place is what?**  
**Answer:** One greater than the temple.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:6

---

### Matthew 12:7

**Q11. But if ye had known what this meaneth, what would ye not have condemned?**  
**Answer:** The guiltless.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:7

**Q12. What did he desire rather than sacrifice?**  
**Answer:** Mercy.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:7

---

### Matthew 12:8

**Q13. Who is Lord even of the sabbath day?**  
**Answer:** The Son of man.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:8

---

### Matthew 12:9

**Q14. And when he was departed thence, into what did he go?**  
**Answer:** Into their synagogue.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:9

---

### Matthew 12:10

**Q15. And, behold, who was there, having which hand withered?**  
**Answer:** A man which had his hand withered.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:10

**Q16. Whom did they ask, saying, Is it lawful to heal on the sabbath days?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:10

**Q17. Why did they ask him whether it was lawful to heal on the sabbath days?**  
**Answer:** That they might accuse him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:10

---

### Matthew 12:11

**Q18. And he said unto them, What man shall there be among you, that shall have what?**  
**Answer:** One sheep.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:11

**Q19. If it fall into a pit on the sabbath day, what will he do?**  
**Answer:** Lay hold on it, and lift it out.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:11

---

### Matthew 12:12

**Q20. How much then is a man better than what?**  
**Answer:** A sheep.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:12

**Q21. Therefore, what is it lawful to do on the sabbath days?**  
**Answer:** To do well.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:12

---

### Matthew 12:13

**Q22. Then saith he to the man, what?**  
**Answer:** Stretch forth thine hand.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:13

**Q23. And he stretched it forth; and what happened to it?**  
**Answer:** It was restored whole, like as the other.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:13

---

### Matthew 12:14

**Q24. Then the Pharisees went out, and held a council against him, concerning what?**  
**Answer:** How they might destroy him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:14

---

### Matthew 12:15

**Q25. But when Jesus knew it, what did he do?**  
**Answer:** He withdrew himself from thence.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:15

**Q26. Who followed him?**  
**Answer:** Great multitudes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:15

**Q27. What did he do to them all?**  
**Answer:** He healed them all.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:15

---

### Matthew 12:16

**Q28. And what did he charge them?**  
**Answer:** That they should not make him known.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:16

---

### Matthew 12:17

**Q29. That it might be fulfilled which was spoken by whom?**  
**Answer:** Esaias the prophet.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:17

---

### Matthew 12:18

**Q30. Who is God's servant, whom he has chosen?**  
**Answer:** Behold my servant, whom I have chosen.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:18

**Q31. In whom is God's soul well pleased?**  
**Answer:** My beloved, in whom my soul is well pleased.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:18

**Q32. What will God put upon him, and what shall he show to the Gentiles?**  
**Answer:** I will put my spirit upon him, and he shall shew judgment to the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:18

---

### Matthew 12:19

**Q33. What will he not do regarding strife or crying?**  
**Answer:** He shall not strive, nor cry.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:19

**Q34. Where shall anyone not hear his voice?**  
**Answer:** In the streets.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:19

---

### Matthew 12:20

**Q35. What kind of bruised reed shall he not break?**  
**Answer:** A bruised reed shall he not break.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:20

**Q36. What kind of smoking flax shall he not quench?**  
**Answer:** Smoking flax shall he not quench.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:20

**Q37. Until when shall he send forth judgment unto victory?**  
**Answer:** Till he send forth judgment unto victory.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:20

---

### Matthew 12:21

**Q38. In whose name shall the Gentiles trust?**  
**Answer:** In his name.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:21

---

### Matthew 12:22

**Q39. Then was brought unto him one possessed with a devil, who was what?**  
**Answer:** Blind, and dumb.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:22

**Q40. And what did he do to him?**  
**Answer:** He healed him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:22

**Q41. What happened to the blind and dumb man after he was healed?**  
**Answer:** Insomuch that the blind and dumb both spake and saw.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:22

---

### Matthew 12:23

**Q42. And all the people were amazed, and said, what?**  
**Answer:** Is not this the son of David?  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:23

---

### Matthew 12:24

**Q43. But when the Pharisees heard it, what did they say?**  
**Answer:** This fellow doth not cast out devils, but by Beelzebub the prince of the devils.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:24

---

### Matthew 12:25

**Q44. And Jesus knew their thoughts, and said unto them, what kind of kingdom is divided against itself?**  
**Answer:** Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:25

**Q45. What kind of city or house divided against itself shall not stand?**  
**Answer:** Every city or house divided against itself shall not stand.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:25

---

### Matthew 12:26

**Q46. And if Satan cast out whom, is he divided against himself?**  
**Answer:** Satan.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:26

**Q47. How then shall his kingdom stand?**  
**Answer:** How shall then his kingdom stand?  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:26

---

### Matthew 12:27

**Q48. And if I by Beelzebub cast out devils, by whom do whom cast them out?**  
**Answer:** By whom do your children cast them out?  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:27

**Q49. Therefore, what shall they be?**  
**Answer:** They shall be your judges.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:27

---

### Matthew 12:28

**Q50. But if I cast out devils by what, then is the kingdom of God come unto you?**  
**Answer:** By the Spirit of God.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:28

---

### Matthew 12:29

**Q51. Or else how can one enter into a strong man's house, and do what?**  
**Answer:** Spoil his goods.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:29

**Q52. Except he first bind whom?**  
**Answer:** The strong man.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:29

**Q53. And then what can he do to his house?**  
**Answer:** He will then spoil his house.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:29

---

### Matthew 12:30

**Q51. He that is not with me is what?**  
**Answer:** Against me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:30

**Q52. And he that gathereth not with me what?**  
**Answer:** Scattereth abroad.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:30

---

### Matthew 12:31

**Q53. Wherefore I say unto you, what kind of sin and blasphemy shall be forgiven unto men?**  
**Answer:** All manner of sin and blasphemy.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:31

**Q54. But what shall not be forgiven unto men?**  
**Answer:** The blasphemy against the Holy Ghost.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:31

---

### Matthew 12:32

**Q55. And whosoever speaketh a word against the Son of man, what shall happen to him?**  
**Answer:** It shall be forgiven him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:32

**Q56. But whosoever speaketh against what, it shall not be forgiven him?**  
**Answer:** Against the Holy Ghost.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:32

**Q57. When shall it not be forgiven him, neither in this world, neither in the world to come?**  
**Answer:** Neither in this world, neither in the world to come.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:32

---

### Matthew 12:33

**Q58. Either make the tree good, and what?**  
**Answer:** And his fruit good.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:33

**Q59. Or else make the tree corrupt, and what?**  
**Answer:** And his fruit corrupt.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:33

**Q60. For how is the tree known?**  
**Answer:** By his fruit.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:33

---

### Matthew 12:34

**Q61. O generation of vipers, being evil, how can ye speak what?**  
**Answer:** Good things.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:34

**Q62. For out of the abundance of what the mouth speaketh?**  
**Answer:** The heart.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:34

---

### Matthew 12:35

**Q63. A good man out of what treasure bringeth forth good things?**  
**Answer:** The good treasure of the heart.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:35

**Q64. And an evil man out of what treasure bringeth forth evil things?**  
**Answer:** The evil treasure.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:35

---

### Matthew 12:36

**Q65. But I say unto you, That every idle word that men shall speak, what shall they give?**  
**Answer:** An account thereof.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:36

**Q66. When shall men give an account of every idle word?**  
**Answer:** In the day of judgment.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:36

---

### Matthew 12:37

**Q67. For by what shall thou be justified?**  
**Answer:** By thy words.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:37

**Q68. And by what shall thou be condemned?**  
**Answer:** By thy words.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:37

---

### Matthew 12:38

**Q69. Then certain of the scribes and of the Pharisees answered, saying, what?**  
**Answer:** Master, we would see a sign from thee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:38

---

### Matthew 12:39

**Q70. But he answered and said unto them, what kind of generation seeketh after a sign?**  
**Answer:** An evil and adulterous generation.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:39

**Q71. And what sign shall not be given to it?**  
**Answer:** There shall no sign be given to it.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:39

**Q72. But whose sign shall be given to it?**  
**Answer:** The sign of the prophet Jonas.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:39

---

### Matthew 12:40

**Q73. For as Jonas was how long in the whale's belly?**  
**Answer:** Three days and three nights.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:40

**Q74. So shall whom be how long in the heart of the earth?**  
**Answer:** The Son of man shall be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:40

---

### Matthew 12:41

**Q75. Who shall rise up in judgment with this generation, and shall condemn it?**  
**Answer:** The men of Nineveh.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:41

**Q76. Why shall the men of Nineveh rise up and condemn this generation?**  
**Answer:** Because they repented at the preaching of Jonas.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:41

**Q77. And, behold, what is in this place?**  
**Answer:** A greater than Jonas is here.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:41

---

### Matthew 12:42

**Q78. Who shall rise up in the judgment with this generation, and shall condemn it?**  
**Answer:** The queen of the south.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:42

**Q79. Why shall the queen of the south rise up and condemn this generation?**  
**Answer:** For she came from the uttermost parts of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:42

**Q80. And, behold, what is in this place?**  
**Answer:** A greater than Solomon is here.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:42

---

### Matthew 12:43

**Q81. When the unclean spirit is gone out of whom, where does he walk?**  
**Answer:** Out of a man, through dry places.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:43

**Q82. What does he seek while walking through dry places?**  
**Answer:** Rest.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:43

**Q83. What does he find?**  
**Answer:** None.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:43

---

### Matthew 12:44

**Q84. Then he saith, I will return into what place?**  
**Answer:** Into my house from whence I came out.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:44

**Q85. And when he is come, what does he find?**  
**Answer:** Empty, swept, and garnished.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:44

---

### Matthew 12:45

**Q86. Then goeth he, and taketh with himself how many other spirits?**  
**Answer:** Seven other spirits.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:45

**Q87. What kind of spirits are they compared to himself?**  
**Answer:** More wicked than himself.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:45

**Q88. And what happens to the last state of that man?**  
**Answer:** The last state of that man is worse than the first.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:45

---

### Matthew 12:46

**Q89. While he yet talked to the people, who stood without?**  
**Answer:** His mother and his brethren.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:46

**Q90. What did they desire?**  
**Answer:** To speak with him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:46

---

### Matthew 12:47

**Q91. Then one said unto him, Behold, who stand without?**  
**Answer:** Thy mother and thy brethren.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:47

**Q92. What do they desire?**  
**Answer:** Desiring to speak with thee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:47

---

### Matthew 12:48

**Q93. But he answered and said unto him that told him, who is what?**  
**Answer:** Who is my mother? and who are my brethren?  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:48

---

### Matthew 12:49

**Q94. And he stretched forth his hand toward whom?**  
**Answer:** Toward his disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:49

**Q95. What did he say about them?**  
**Answer:** Behold my mother and my brethren!  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:49

---

### Matthew 12:50

**Q96. For whosoever shall do the will of whom?**  
**Answer:** My Father which is in heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:50

**Q97. What relation is that person to him?**  
**Answer:** The same is my brother, and sister, and mother.  
**Verse:** Matthew 12:50
