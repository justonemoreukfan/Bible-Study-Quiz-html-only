# Matthew 23 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 23:1

**Q1. To whom did Jesus speak in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The multitude, and to his disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:1

**Q2. Who spake these words?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:1

**Q3. Who was addressed along with the disciples?**  
**Answer:** The multitude.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:1

---

### Matthew 23:2

**Q1. Who sits in Moses' seat?**  
**Answer:** The scribes and the Pharisees.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:2

**Q2. In whose seat do the scribes and Pharisees sit?**  
**Answer:** Moses' seat.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:2

**Q3. What group, besides the Pharisees, sits in Moses' seat?**  
**Answer:** The scribes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:2

---

### Matthew 23:3

**Q1. What should men observe and do?**  
**Answer:** All things whatsoever the scribes and Pharisees bid them observe and do.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:3

**Q2. Whose works should men not do?**  
**Answer:** The works of the scribes and Pharisees.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:3

**Q3. Why should men not do after the works of the scribes and Pharisees?**  
**Answer:** For they say, and do not.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:3

---

### Matthew 23:4

**Q1. What kind of burdens do they bind?**  
**Answer:** Heavy burdens and grievous to be borne.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:4

**Q2. On whose shoulders are these heavy burdens laid?**  
**Answer:** Men's shoulders.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:4

**Q3. What will the scribes and Pharisees not move with one of their fingers?**  
**Answer:** The burdens.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:4

---

### Matthew 23:5

**Q1. For what purpose do they do all their works?**  
**Answer:** To be seen of men.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:5

**Q2. What do they make broad?**  
**Answer:** Their phylacteries.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:5

**Q3. What do they enlarge?**  
**Answer:** The borders of their garments.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:5

---

### Matthew 23:6

**Q1. What do they love in the feasts?**  
**Answer:** The uppermost rooms.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:6

**Q2. What do they love in the synagogues?**  
**Answer:** The chief seats.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:6

**Q3. What do they love to have in the markets?**  
**Answer:** Greetings.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:6

---

### Matthew 23:7

**Q1. What do they love to be called in the markets?**

**Verse:** Matthew 23:7

**Q2. By what title should men not be called?**  
**Answer:** Rabbi.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:7

**Q3. Where are they greeted with the title Rabbi?**

**Verse:** Matthew 23:7

### Matthew 23:16

### Matthew 23:23

**Q1. What do they pay tithe of?**  
**Answer:** Mint and anise and cummin.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:23

**Q2. What have they omitted?**  
**Answer:** The weightier matters of the law.  
**Verse:** Matthew 23:23

**Q3. Name three weightier matters of the law mentioned in this verse.**

**Verse:** Matthew 23:23
