# Matthew 11 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 11:1

**Q1. When Jesus had made an end of commanding his disciples, where did he depart to?**  
**Answer:** To teach and to preach in their cities.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:1

**Q2. Whose cities did Jesus depart to teach and to preach in?**  
**Answer:** Their cities.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:1

**Q3. What had Jesus made an end of doing to his disciples?**  
**Answer:** Commanding his disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:1

---

### Matthew 11:2

**Q4. Now when John had heard in the prison the works of Christ, what did he send?**  
**Answer:** Two of his disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:2

**Q5. Where was John when he heard of the works of Christ?**  
**Answer:** In the prison.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:2

**Q6. What had John heard in the prison?**  
**Answer:** The works of Christ.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:2

---

### Matthew 11:3

**Q7. What did John say unto him through his disciples?**  
**Answer:** Art thou he that should come, or do we look for another?  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:3

**Q8. What did John ask if Jesus was?**  
**Answer:** He that should come.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:3

**Q9. If Jesus was not the one, what did John ask if they should do?**  
**Answer:** Look for another.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:3

---

### Matthew 11:4

**Q10. Who answered and said unto them, Go and shew John again those things which ye do hear and see?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:4

**Q11. What did Jesus tell them to go and shew John again?**  
**Answer:** Those things which ye do hear and see.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:4

**Q12. What two senses did the things to shew John involve?**  
**Answer:** Hear and see.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:4

---

### Matthew 11:5

**Q13. What class of people receive their sight according to verse 5?**  
**Answer:** The blind.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:5

**Q14. Who walks in verse 5?**  
**Answer:** The lame.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:5

**Q15. Who is raised up in verse 5?**  
**Answer:** The dead.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:5

---

### Matthew 11:6

**Q16. Who is pronounced blessed in verse 6?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever, whosoever shall not be offended in me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:6

**Q17. What should someone not be in Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Offended.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:6

**Q18. In whom should the blessed person not be offended?**  
**Answer:** In me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:6

---

### Matthew 11:7

**Q19. As they departed, what did Jesus begin to say unto the multitudes concerning John?**  
**Answer:** What went ye out into the wilderness to see?  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:7

**Q20. What did Jesus ask if the multitude went out into the wilderness to see?**  
**Answer:** A reed shaken with the wind?  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:7

**Q21. Where did the multitudes go out to see?**  
**Answer:** Into the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:7

---

### Matthew 11:8

**Q22. What did Jesus ask if they went out to see clothed in soft raiment?**  
**Answer:** A man.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:8

**Q23. Where are they that wear soft clothing found?**  
**Answer:** In kings' houses.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:8

**Q24. What kind of raiment were those in kings' houses wearing?**  
**Answer:** Soft raiment (or soft clothing).  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:8

---

### Matthew 11:9

**Q25. What did Jesus ask if they went out to see?**  
**Answer:** A prophet.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:9

**Q26. What did Jesus say John was in relation to a prophet?**  
**Answer:** Yea, I say unto you, and more than a prophet.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:9

**Q27. When Jesus asked if they went out to see a prophet, what was his affirmative response?**  
**Answer:** Yea, I say unto you.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:9

---

### Matthew 11:10

**Q28. Of whom is it written, Behold, I send my messenger before thy face?**  
**Answer:** Of this is he, of whom it is written.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:10

**Q29. What would the messenger prepare before Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Thy way before thee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:10

**Q30. Where did the messenger prepare the way?**  
**Answer:** Before thy face.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:10

---

### Matthew 11:11

**Q31. Among them that are born of women, who hath not risen a greater than?**  
**Answer:** John the Baptist.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:11

**Q32. How does he that is least in the kingdom of heaven compare to John the Baptist?**  
**Answer:** He is greater than he.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:11

**Q33. Among whom hath not risen a greater than John the Baptist?**  
**Answer:** Them that are born of women.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:11

---

### Matthew 11:12

**Q34. From what time hath the kingdom of heaven suffered violence?**  
**Answer:** From the days of John the Baptist until now.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:12

**Q35. What does the kingdom of heaven suffer?**  
**Answer:** Violence.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:12

**Q36. What do the violent take by force?**  
**Answer:** It (the kingdom of heaven).  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:12

---

### Matthew 11:13

**Q37. Until John, what did all the prophets and the law prophesy concerning?**  
**Answer:** Prophesied.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:13

**Q38. Until when did all the prophets and the law prophesy?**  
**Answer:** Until John.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:13

**Q39. Who prophesied until John?**  
**Answer:** All the prophets and the law.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:13

---

### Matthew 11:14

**Q40. If ye will receive it, what is John?**  
**Answer:** Elias, which was for to come.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:14

**Q41. Who was Elias to come?**  
**Answer:** John.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:14

**Q42. What condition is placed upon receiving John as Elias?**  
**Answer:** If ye will receive it.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:14

---

### Matthew 11:15

**Q43. Who has ears to hear?**  
**Answer:** He that has ears to hear.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:15

**Q44. What command is given concerning ears?**  
**Answer:** Let him hear.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:15

**Q45. Whose ears are referenced in verse 15?**  
**Answer:** He that hath ears to hear.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:15

---

### Matthew 11:16

**Q46. Unto what did Jesus liken this generation?**  
**Answer:** It is like unto children sitting in the markets.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:16

**Q47. Where were the children sitting?**  
**Answer:** In the markets.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:16

**Q48. To whom did the children call unto their fellows?**  
**Answer:** Their fellows.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:16

---

### Matthew 11:17

**Q49. What did the children say they have piped unto you?**  
**Answer:** And ye have not danced.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:17

**Q50. What did the children say they have mourned unto you?**  
**Answer:** And ye have not lamented.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:17

**Q51. What two musical or emotional actions were played/mourned?**  
**Answer:** Piped and mourned.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:17

---

### Matthew 11:18

**Q52. For who came neither eating nor drinking?**  
**Answer:** John.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:18

**Q53. What did they say about John because he came neither eating nor drinking?**  
**Answer:** He hath a devil.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:18

**Q54. How did John come regarding eating and drinking?**  
**Answer:** Neither eating nor drinking.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:18

---

### Matthew 11:19

**Q55. Who came eating and drinking?**  
**Answer:** The Son of man.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:19

**Q56. What did they say about the Son of man?**  
**Answer:** Behold a man gluttonous, and a winebibber, a friend of publicans and sinners.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:19

**Q57. What is wisdom justified of?**  
**Answer:** Her children.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:19

---

### Matthew 11:20

**Q58. What did Jesus begin to upbraid the cities in wherein most of his mighty works were done?**  
**Answer:** Because they repented not.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:20

**Q59. What was done in those cities that caused Jesus to upbraid them?**  
**Answer:** Most of his mighty works were done.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:20

**Q60. What did the cities fail to do?**  
**Answer:** Repented not.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:20

### Matthew 11:21

**Q61. Woe unto whom did Jesus proclaim in verse 21?**  
**Answer:** Chorazin and Bethsaida.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:21

**Q62. What cities, if they had been done in Tyre and Sidon, would have repented long ago in sackcloth and ashes?**  
**Answer:** The mighty works, which were done in you.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:21

**Q63. In what did Tyre and Sidon repent long ago?**  
**Answer:** In sackcloth and ashes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:21

**Answer:** The day of judgment.

**Q66. For whom will it be more tolerable than for Chorazin and Bethsaida?**  
**Answer:** Tyre and Sidon.  
**Verse:** Matthew 11:22

**Q80. Who knows the Son besides the Father?**

**Q85. What did Jesus tell them to take upon themselves?**

**Verse:** Matthew 11:29

**Q86. What should they learn of Jesus?**  
**Answer:** For I am meek and lowly in heart.

**Verse:** Matthew 11:29

**Q88. How is Jesus' yoke described?**  
**Answer:** Easy.

**Q89. How is Jesus' burden described?**  
**Answer:** Light.
