# Matthew 26 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 26:1

**Q1. What did Jesus finish when He had finished all these sayings?**  
**Answer:** All these sayings.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:1

**Q2. To whom did Jesus speak when He had finished all these sayings?**  
**Answer:** Unto his disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:1

**Q3. Who is the person that finished all these sayings unto his disciples?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:1

---

### Matthew 26:2

**Q1. What did Jesus say was after two days?**  
**Answer:** The feast of the passover.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:2

**Q2. What did Jesus say the Son of man would be betrayed to do?**  
**Answer:** Be crucified.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:2

**Q3. What event did Jesus tell His disciples was coming after two days?**  
**Answer:** The passover.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:2

---

### Matthew 26:3

**Q1. Who assembled together in the palace of the high priest?**  
**Answer:** The chief priests, and the scribes, and the elders of the people.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:3

**Q2. Who was the high priest at the time the chief priests, scribes, and elders assembled?**  
**Answer:** Caiaphas.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:3

**Q3. In whose palace did the chief priests, scribes, and elders assemble?**  
**Answer:** The high priest's.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:3

---

### Matthew 26:4

**Q1. What did the assembled leaders consult together to do?**  
**Answer:** That they might take Jesus by subtilty, and kill him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:4

**Q2. By what method did they consult to take Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Subtilty.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:4

**Q3. Besides taking Jesus by subtilty, what else did they consult to do to Him?**  
**Answer:** Kill him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:4

---

### Matthew 26:5

**Q1. When did they say not to take Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Not on the feast day.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:5

**Q2. Why did they say not to take Him on the feast day?**  
**Answer:** Lest there be an uproar among the people.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:5

**Q3. Among whom might there be an uproar if they took Him on the feast day?**  
**Answer:** The people.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:5

---

### Matthew 26:6

**Q1. Where was Jesus when this event took place?**  
**Answer:** In Bethany.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:6

**Q2. In whose house was Jesus while in Bethany?**  
**Answer:** Simon the leper's.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:6

**Q3. What was Simon known as?**  
**Answer:** The leper.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:6

---

### Matthew 26:7

**Q1. Who came unto Jesus having an alabaster box?**  
**Answer:** A woman.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:7

**Q2. What was inside the alabaster box?**  
**Answer:** Very precious ointment.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:7

**Q3. What did the woman do with the ointment while Jesus sat at meat?**  
**Answer:** Poured it on his head.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:7

---

### Matthew 26:8

**Q1. Who saw the woman pouring the ointment on Jesus?**  
**Answer:** His disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:8

**Q2. How did the disciples feel when they saw it?**  
**Answer:** Had indignation.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:8

**Q3. What question did the disciples ask regarding the ointment?**  
**Answer:** To what purpose is this waste?  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:8

---

### Matthew 26:9

**Q1. For how much did the disciples say this ointment might have been sold?**  
**Answer:** For much.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:9

**Q2. To whom did the disciples say the proceeds might have been given?**  
**Answer:** To the poor.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:9

**Q3. What could have been done with the ointment according to the disciples?**  
**Answer:** Sold for much, and given to the poor.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:9

---

### Matthew 26:10

**Q1. Who understood what the disciples were saying?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:10

**Q2. What did Jesus ask the disciples why they troubled?**  
**Answer:** The woman.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:10

**Q3. What did Jesus say the woman had wrought upon Him?**  
**Answer:** A good work.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:10

---

### Matthew 26:11

**Q1. Whom did Jesus say ye have always with you?**  
**Answer:** The poor.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:11

**Q2. Whom did Jesus say ye have not always?**  
**Answer:** Me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:11

**Q3. What does Jesus say regarding Himself and the poor?**  
**Answer:** For ye have the poor always with you; but me ye have not always.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:11

---

### Matthew 26:12

**Q1. What did Jesus say the woman poured on His body?**  
**Answer:** This ointment.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:12

**Q2. For what purpose did she pour the ointment on His body?**  
**Answer:** For my burial.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:12

**Q3. When did she pour this ointment on His body?**  
**Answer:** In that she hath poured this ointment on my body, she did it for my burial.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:12

---

### Matthew 26:13

**Q1. Where did Jesus say this gospel should be preached?**  
**Answer:** In all the world.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:13

**Q2. In what context did Jesus say this gospel shall be spoken of?**  
**Answer:** What this woman hath done.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:13

**Q3. For what purpose shall this be told as a memorial of her?**  
**Answer:** For a memorial of her.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:13

---

### Matthew 26:14

**Q1. Who went unto the chief priests?**  
**Answer:** One of the twelve.  
**Verse:** Matthew 26:14
