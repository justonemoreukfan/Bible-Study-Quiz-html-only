# Matthew 15 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 15:1

**Q1. Who came to Jesus from Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Scribes and Pharisees.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:1

**Q2. Where did the scribes and Pharisees come from?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:1

**Q3. To whom did the scribes and Pharisees speak?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:1

---

### Matthew 15:2

**Q1. What did the disciples not wash when they ate bread?**  
**Answer:** Their hands.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:2

**Q2. Whose tradition did the disciples transgress?**  
**Answer:** The tradition of the elders.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:2

**Q3. When did the disciples fail to wash their hands?**  
**Answer:** When they eat bread.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:2

---

### Matthew 15:3

**Q1. Who answered and said unto them?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:3

**Q2. What did Jesus ask them they also do by their tradition?**  
**Answer:** Transgress the commandment of God.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:3

**Q3. Whose commandment do they transgress by their tradition?**  
**Answer:** The commandment of God.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:3

---

### Matthew 15:4

**Q1. Who commanded, saying, Honour thy father and mother?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:4

**Q2. What must one do to their father and mother according to the commandment?**  
**Answer:** Honour them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:4

**Q3. What penalty is prescribed for him that curseth father or mother?**  
**Answer:** Let him die the death.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:4

---

### Matthew 15:5

**Q1. What do you say a man shall say to his father or mother?**  
**Answer:** It is a gift.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:5

**Q2. What is a gift by whatsoever thou mightest be profited by me?**  
**Answer:** That.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:5

**Q3. To whom does the person say "It is a gift"?**  
**Answer:** His father or his mother.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:5

---

### Matthew 15:6

**Q1. What shall he not do to his father or mother?**  
**Answer:** Honour not his father or his mother.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:6

**Q2. What have you made the commandment of God of none effect by?**  
**Answer:** Your tradition.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:6

---

### Matthew 15:7

**Q1. Who did Esaias prophesy of?**  
**Answer:** You hypocrites.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:7

**Q2. Who spoke the prophecy concerning them?**  
**Answer:** Esaias.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:7

**Q3. What did Esaias well prophesy of them?**  
**Answer:** Hypocrites.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:7

---

### Matthew 15:8

**Q1. With what do this people draw nigh unto me?**  
**Answer:** Their mouth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:8

**Q2. With what do they honour me?**  
**Answer:** Their lips.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:8

**Q3. Where is their heart while they honour me with their lips?**  
**Answer:** Far from me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:8

---

### Matthew 15:9

**Q1. In vain do they worship me, teaching for doctrines the what?**  
**Answer:** The commandments of men.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:9

**Q2. How do they worship me?**  
**Answer:** In vain.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:9

**Q3. What do they teach for doctrines?**  
**Answer:** The commandments of men.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:9

---

### Matthew 15:10

**Q1. Who did Jesus call?**  
**Answer:** The multitude.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:10

**Q2. What did Jesus say to the multitude?**  
**Answer:** Hear, and understand.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:10

**Q3. Who called the multitude and said, Hear, and understand?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:10

---

### Matthew 15:11

**Q1. What does not defile a man?**  
**Answer:** That which goeth into the mouth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:11

**Q2. What defiles a man?**  
**Answer:** That which cometh out of the mouth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:11

**Q3. Where does the defiling thing come out of?**  
**Answer:** The mouth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:11

---

### Matthew 15:12

**Q1. Who came to Jesus after this?**  
**Answer:** His disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:12

**Q2. What did the disciples ask Jesus if he knew?**  
**Answer:** That the Pharisees were offended.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:12

**Q3. Who was offended after they heard this saying?**  
**Answer:** The Pharisees.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:12

---

### Matthew 15:13

**Q1. Who answered and said?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:13

**Q2. What shall be rooted up?**  
**Answer:** Every plant, which my heavenly Father hath not planted.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:13

**Q3. Who has not planted every plant that shall be rooted up?**  
**Answer:** My heavenly Father.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:13

---

### Matthew 15:14

**Q1. What are the Pharisees?**  
**Answer:** Blind leaders of the blind.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:14

**Q2. What happens if the blind lead the blind?**  
**Answer:** Both shall fall into the ditch.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:14

**Q3. Where shall both fall if the blind lead the blind?**  
**Answer:** Into the ditch.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:14

---

### Matthew 15:15

**Q1. Who answered and said unto Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Peter.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:15

**Q2. What did Peter ask Jesus to declare unto them?**  
**Answer:** This parable.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:15

**Q3. Who spoke to Jesus asking Him to declare the parable?**  
**Answer:** Peter.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:15

---

### Matthew 15:16

**Q1. Who asked, Are ye also yet without understanding?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:16

**Q2. Who was asked if they were also yet without understanding?**  
**Answer:** The disciples (implied by context / "Are ye also").  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:16

**Q3. What condition were the disciples still in according to Jesus' question?**  
**Answer:** Without understanding.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:16

---

### Matthew 15:17

**Q1. Where does whatsoever enter in at the mouth go?**  
**Answer:** Into the belly.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:17

**Q2. Where is what enters the belly cast out?**  
**Answer:** Into the draught.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:17

**Q3. Do you not yet understand that whatever enters the mouth goes where?**  
**Answer:** Into the belly.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:17

---

### Matthew 15:18

**Q1. Where do those things which proceed out of the mouth come forth from?**  
**Answer:** The heart.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:18

**Q2. What do the things which proceed out of the mouth do to the man?**  
**Answer:** Defile the man.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:18

**Q3. Where do the things that defile a man proceed from?**  
**Answer:** The mouth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:18

---

### Matthew 15:19

**Q1. What proceeds out of the heart?**  
**Answer:** Evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false witness, blasphemies.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:19

**Q2. Which of the following is listed as coming from the heart: murders or good deeds?**  
**Answer:** Murders.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:19

**Q3. Are false testimonies (witness) mentioned as proceeding from the heart?**  
**Answer:** False witness.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:19

---

### Matthew 15:20

**Q1. What are the things which defile a man?**  
**Answer:** These are the things which defile a man.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:20

**Q2. What does eating with unwashen hands not do to a man?**  
**Answer:** Defileth not a man.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:20

**Q3. Whose hands eating with does not defile a man?**  
**Answer:** With unwashen hands.  
**Verse:** Matthew 15:20

**Verse:** Matthew 15:22
