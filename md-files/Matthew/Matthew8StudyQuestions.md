# Matthew 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 8:1

**Q1. When was the event described in this verse?**  
**Answer:** When he was come down from the mountain.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:1

**Q2. Who followed him when he came down from the mountain?**  
**Answer:** Great multitudes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:1

**Q3. Where did the great multitudes come from to follow him?**  
**Answer:** From the mountain.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:1

---

### Matthew 8:2

**Q1. Who came and worshipped him in this verse?**  
**Answer:** A leper.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:2

**Q2. What did the leper do when he came to him?**  
**Answer:** Worshipped him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:2

**Q3. What did the leper say regarding the Lord's will?**  
**Answer:** Lord, if thou wilt, thou canst make me clean.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:2

---

### Matthew 8:3

**Q1. What did Jesus put forth in this verse?**  
**Answer:** His hand.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:3

**Q2. Whom did Jesus touch?**  
**Answer:** The leper.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:3

**Q3. What did Jesus say immediately after touching him?**  
**Answer:** I will; be thou clean.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:3

---

### Matthew 8:4

**Q1. What instruction did Jesus give the man regarding telling others?**  
**Answer:** See thou tell no man.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:4

**Q2. Where was the man told to go show himself?**  
**Answer:** To the priest.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:4

**Q3. What was the man commanded to offer?**  
**Answer:** The gift that Moses commanded, for a testimony unto them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:4

---

### Matthew 8:5

**Q1. Into what city did Jesus enter?**  
**Answer:** Capernaum.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:5

**Q2. Who came unto Jesus when he entered Capernaum?**  
**Answer:** A centurion.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:5

**Q3. What did the centurion do when he came unto Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Besought him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:5

---

### Matthew 8:6

**Q1. What did the centurion say his servant was lying at home as?**  
**Answer:** Sick of the palsy.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:6

**Q2. How did the centurion describe his servant's condition at home?**  
**Answer:** Grievously tormented.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:6

**Q3. Where was the servant lying?**  
**Answer:** At home.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:6

---

### Matthew 8:7

**Q1. Who spoke in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:7

**Q2. What did Jesus say he would do?**  
**Answer:** Come and heal him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:7

**Q3. To whom did Jesus give this response?**  
**Answer:** The centurion.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:7

---

### Matthew 8:8

**Q1. Who answered and spoke in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The centurion.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:8

**Q2. What did the centurion say he was not worthy of?**  
**Answer:** That thou shouldest come under my roof.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:8

**Q3. What did the centurion ask Jesus to do instead?**  
**Answer:** Speak the word only.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:8

---

### Matthew 8:9

**Q1. What authority did the centurion say he was under?**  
**Answer:** Authority, having soldiers under me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:9

**Q2. What happens when the centurion says to one man, "Go"?**  
**Answer:** He goeth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:9

**Q3. What happens when the centurion says to his servant, "Do this"?**  
**Answer:** He doeth it.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:9

---

### Matthew 8:10

**Q1. When Jesus heard it, how did he respond?**  
**Answer:** He marvelled.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:10

**Q2. To whom did Jesus speak regarding this faith?**  
**Answer:** Them that followed.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:10

**Q3. What did Jesus say he had not found in Israel?**  
**Answer:** So great faith, no, not in Israel.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:10

---

### Matthew 8:11

**Q1. Where did Jesus say many shall come from?**  
**Answer:** From the east and west.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:11

**Q2. With whom shall they sit down in the kingdom of heaven?**  
**Answer:** Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:11

**Q3. In what kingdom shall they sit down?**  
**Answer:** The kingdom of heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:11

---

### Matthew 8:12

**Q1. Who shall be cast out into outer darkness?**  
**Answer:** The children of the kingdom.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:12

**Q2. Where shall the children of the kingdom be cast?**  
**Answer:** Into outer darkness.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:12

**Q3. What will be in the outer darkness?**  
**Answer:** There shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:12

---

### Matthew 8:13

**Q1. To whom did Jesus speak this verse?**  
**Answer:** Unto the centurion.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:13

**Q2. According to what was the centurion answered?**  
**Answer:** As thou hast believed.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:13

**Q3. When was his servant healed?**  
**Answer:** In the selfsame hour.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:13

---

### Matthew 8:14

**Q1. Into whose house did Jesus come?**  
**Answer:** Peter's house.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:14

**Q2. Whom did Jesus see in Peter's house?**  
**Answer:** His wife's mother laid.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:14

**Q3. What was the condition of Peter's wife's mother?**  
**Answer:** Sick of a fever.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:14

---

### Matthew 8:15

**Q1. Whose hand did Jesus touch?**  
**Answer:** Her hand.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:15

**Q2. What happened when Jesus touched her hand?**  
**Answer:** The fever left her.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:15

**Q3. What did she do after the fever left her?**  
**Answer:** She arose, and ministered unto them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:15

---

### Matthew 8:16

**Q1. When was the evening come?**  
**Answer:** When the even was come.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:16

**Q2. What was brought unto Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Many that were possessed with devils.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:16

**Q3. How did Jesus cast out the spirits and heal the sick?**  
**Answer:** With his word.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:16

---

### Matthew 8:17

**Q1. By whom was this fulfilled?**  
**Answer:** Esaias the prophet.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:17

**Q2. What did Esaias say he took?**  
**Answer:** Our infirmities.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:17

**Q3. What did Esaias say he bare?**  
**Answer:** Our sicknesses.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:17

---

### Matthew 8:18

**Q1. Who saw great multitudes about him?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:18

**Q2. What did Jesus give commandment to do?**  
**Answer:** To depart unto the other side.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:18

---

### Matthew 8:19

**Q1. Who came to Jesus in this verse?**  
**Answer:** A certain scribe.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:19

**Q2. What title did the scribe use when addressing Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Master.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:19

**Q3. What did the scribe say he would do?**  
**Answer:** Follow thee whithersoever thou goest.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:19

### Matthew 8:20

**Q1. What did Jesus say the foxes have?**  
**Answer:** Holes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:20

**Q2. What did Jesus say the birds of the air have?**  
**Answer:** Nests.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:20

**Q3. What did Jesus say the Son of man does not have?**  
**Answer:** Where to lay his head.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:20

---

### Matthew 8:21

**Q1. Who was another of his disciples?**  
**Answer:** Another of his disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:21

**Q2. How did this disciple address Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Lord.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:21

**Q3. What did the disciple ask permission to do first?**  
**Answer:** Suffer me first to go and bury my father.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:21

---

### Matthew 8:22

**Q1. What did Jesus say unto him?**  
**Answer:** Follow me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:22

**Q2. What did Jesus tell the dead to do?**  
**Answer:** Let the dead bury their dead.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:22

---

### Matthew 8:23

**Q1. When he was entered into a ship, who followed him?**  
**Answer:** His disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:23

**Q2. Into what did he enter?**  
**Answer:** A ship.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:23

---

### Matthew 8:24

**Q1. What arose in the sea?**  
**Answer:** A great tempest.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:24

**Q2. What was happening to the ship because of the tempest?**  
**Answer:** The ship was covered with the waves.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:24

**Q3. What was Jesus doing during this time?**  
**Answer:** He was asleep.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:24

---

### Matthew 8:25

**Q1. Who came to Jesus and awoke him?**  
**Answer:** His disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:25

**Q2. What title did the disciples use when waking him?**  
**Answer:** Lord.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:25

**Q3. What did the disciples say would happen to them?**  
**Answer:** Save us: we perish.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:25

---

### Matthew 8:26

**Q1. What did Jesus say to his disciples about their fear?**  
**Answer:** Why are ye fearful, O ye of little faith?  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:26

**Q2. What did Jesus rebuke?**  
**Answer:** The winds and the sea.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:26

**Q3. What followed the rebuking of the winds and the sea?**  
**Answer:** There was a great calm.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:26

---

### Matthew 8:27

**Q1. How did the men react?**  
**Answer:** Marvelled.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:27

**Q2. What question did the men ask about Jesus?**  
**Answer:** What manner of man is this?  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:27

**Q3. What obeyed Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Even the winds and the sea obey him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:27

---

### Matthew 8:28

**Q1. To what country did he come on the other side?**  
**Answer:** The country of the Gergesenes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:28

**Q2. Who met him coming out of the tombs?**  
**Answer:** Two possessed with devils.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:28

**Q3. How were the two men possessed with devils described?**  
**Answer:** Exceeding fierce, so that no man might pass by that way.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:28

---

### Matthew 8:29

**Q1. What did the men cry out, addressing Jesus?**  
**Answer:** What have we to do with thee, Jesus, thou Son of God?  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:29

**Q2. What did they ask if he had come to do before the time?**  
**Answer:** Art thou come hither to torment us before the time?  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:29

---

### Matthew 8:30

**Q1. What was feeding a good way off from them?**  
**Answer:** A great herd of swine.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:30

**Q2. Where was the great herd of swine feeding?**  
**Answer:** A good way off from them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:30

---

### Matthew 8:31

**Q1. Who besought Jesus in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The devils.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:31

**Q2. What did the devils ask Jesus to permit them to do?**  
**Answer:** Suffer us to go away into the herd of swine.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:31

---

### Matthew 8:32

**Q1. What single word did Jesus say to the devils?**  
**Answer:** Go.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:32

**Q2. Where did the devils go when they came out?**  
**Answer:** Into the herd of swine.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:32

**Q3. What happened to the herd of swine?**  
**Answer:** The whole herd of swine ran violently down a steep place into the sea, and perished in the waters.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:32

---

### Matthew 8:33

**Q1. Who fled after these events?**  
**Answer:** They that kept them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:33

**Q2. Where did the keepers go?**  
**Answer:** Their ways into the city.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:33

**Q3. What did the keepers tell in the city?**  
**Answer:** Every thing, and what was befitting the possessed of the devils.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:33

---

### Matthew 8:34

**Q1. Who came out to meet Jesus?**  
**Answer:** The whole city.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:34

**Q2. What did the whole city do when they saw him?**  
**Answer:** Came out to meet Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:34

**Q3. What did they besought him to do after seeing him?**  
**Answer:** That he would depart out of their coasts.  
**Verse:** Matthew 8:34
