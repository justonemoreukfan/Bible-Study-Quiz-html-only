# Matthew 24 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 24:1

**Q1. Who went out, and from where did he depart?**  
**Answer:** Jesus went out, and departed from the temple.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:1

**Q2. Who came to Jesus after his departure from the temple?**  
**Answer:** His disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:1

**Q3. What did the disciples come to him for?**  
**Answer:** To shew him the buildings of the temple.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:1

---

### Matthew 24:2

**Q1. What did Jesus say to them regarding the things they showed him?**  
**Answer:** See ye not all these things?  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:2

**Q2. What did Jesus declare would not be left upon another concerning the buildings?**  
**Answer:** One stone upon another.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:2

**Q3. What will happen to every stone that is not left upon another?**  
**Answer:** It shall not be thrown down.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:2

---

### Matthew 24:3

**Q1. Where did Jesus sit as the disciples came unto him privately?**  
**Answer:** Upon the mount of Olives.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:3

**Q2. What did the disciples ask Jesus to tell them?**  
**Answer:** When shall these things be? and what shall be the sign of thy coming, and of the end of the world?  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:3

**Q3. When did the disciples ask these things of Jesus privately?**  
**Answer:** As he sat upon the mount of Olives.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:3

---

### Matthew 24:4

**Q1. Who answered and said unto them?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:4

**Q2. What did Jesus tell them to take heed of?**  
**Answer:** That no man deceive you.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:4

**Q3. Why did Jesus warn them to take heed?**  
**Answer:** Lest any man deceive you.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:4

---

### Matthew 24:5

**Q1. Who would come in Jesus' name?**  
**Answer:** Many.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:5

**Q2. What would these many claim when they come in his name?**  
**Answer:** Saying, I am Christ.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:5

**Q3. Whom would these false claimants deceive?**  
**Answer:** Many.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:5

---

### Matthew 24:6

**Q1. What would the disciples hear of?**  
**Answer:** Wars and rumours of wars.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:6

**Q2. How did Jesus tell them they should feel about these things?**  
**Answer:** See that ye be not troubled.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:6

**Q3. What did Jesus say must needs happen, though the end is not yet?**  
**Answer:** All these things must come to pass.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:6

---

### Matthew 24:7

**Q1. What would rise against nation and kingdom?**  
**Answer:** Nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:7

**Q2. What conditions would occur in various places?**  
**Answer:** Famines, and pestilences, and earthquakes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:7

**Q3. Whose rising against whom is mentioned alongside kingdoms?**  
**Answer:** Nation against nation.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:7

---

**Q1. What kind of tribulation shall there be?**  
**Answer:** Great tribulation.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:21

**Answer:** Such as was not since the beginning of the world to this time, no, nor ever shall be.

**Q3. Will there ever be another tribulation like it?**  
**Answer:** No, nor ever shall be.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:21

### Matthew 24:22

**Q1. What would happen to those days if they were not shortened?**  
**Answer:** There should no flesh be saved.

**Answer:** For the elect's sake.  
**Verse:** Matthew 24:22

**Q3. Who shall shorten those days?**

**Verse:** Matthew 24:22
