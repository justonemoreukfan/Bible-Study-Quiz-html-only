# Matthew 20 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 20:1

**Q1. What is the kingdom of heaven likened unto?**  
**Answer:** A man that is an householder.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:1

**Q2. When did the householder go out early in the morning?**  
**Answer:** To hire labourers into his vineyard.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:1

**Q3. Whose vineyard did the labourers go into?**  
**Answer:** The householder's.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:1

---

### Matthew 20:2

**Q1. When the householder had agreed with the labourers for a penny a day, where did he send them?**  
**Answer:** Into his vineyard.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:2

**Q2. How much did the householder agree with the labourers for?**  
**Answer:** A penny a day.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:2

**Q3. For how long of a period was the penny agreed upon?**  
**Answer:** A day.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:2

---

### Matthew 20:3

**Q1. About what hour did he go out again?**  
**Answer:** About the third hour.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:3

**Q2. Whom did he see standing idle in the marketplace?**  
**Answer:** Others.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:3

**Q3. Where were the others standing idle?**  
**Answer:** In the marketplace.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:3

---

### Matthew 20:4

**Q1. What did he say unto them?**  
**Answer:** Go ye also into the vineyard.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:4

**Q2. What did he promise to give them?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever is right.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:4

**Q3. Where did they go?**  
**Answer:** Into the vineyard.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:4

---

### Matthew 20:5

**Q1. About which hours did he go out again?**  
**Answer:** The sixth and ninth hour.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:5

**Q2. What did he do when he went out at the sixth and ninth hour?**  
**Answer:** Did likewise.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:5

**Q3. Did he repeat this action?**  
**Answer:** Yes (he went out again).  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:5

---

### Matthew 20:6

**Q1. About what hour did he go out and find others standing idle?**  
**Answer:** About the eleventh hour.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:6

**Q2. What did he ask them?**  
**Answer:** Why stand ye here all the day idle?  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:6

**Q3. How long had they been standing idle?**  
**Answer:** All the day.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:6

---

### Matthew 20:7

**Q1. What answer did they give him?**  
**Answer:** Because no man hath hired us.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:7

**Q2. What did he say unto them?**  
**Answer:** Go ye also into the vineyard.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:7

**Q3. What would they receive at the end?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever is right, that shall ye receive.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:7

---

### Matthew 20:8

**Q1. When the even was come, what did the lord of the vineyard say unto his steward?**  
**Answer:** Call the labourers, and give them their hire.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:8

**Q2. Who was told to call the labourers?**  
**Answer:** His steward.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:8

**Q3. Beginning from where were the labourers to be paid?**  
**Answer:** From the last unto the first.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:8

---

### Matthew 20:9

**Q1. When they came that were hired about the eleventh hour, what did every man receive?**  
**Answer:** A penny.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:9

**Q2. About what hour were these particular men hired?**  
**Answer:** About the eleventh hour.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:9

**Q3. Did every man receive the same amount?**  
**Answer:** Yes (every man received a penny).  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:9

---

### Matthew 20:10

**Q1. When the first came, what did they suppose?**  
**Answer:** That they should have received more.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:10

**Q2. What did they likewise receive?**  
**Answer:** Every man a penny.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:10

**Q3. Who came first to receive payment?**  
**Answer:** The first.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:10

---

### Matthew 20:11

**Q1. When they had received it, what did they do?**  
**Answer:** Murmured against the goodman of the house.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:11

**Q2. Against whom did they murmur?**  
**Answer:** The goodman of the house.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:11

**Q3. What did they do after receiving the penny?**  
**Answer:** Murmured.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:11

---

### Matthew 20:12

**Q1. How many hours did these last have wrought?**  
**Answer:** But one hour.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:12

**Q2. With whom did the murmurers make the last equal?**  
**Answer:** Unto us.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:12

**Q3. What had the first borne during the day?**  
**Answer:** The burden and heat of the day.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:12

---

### Matthew 20:13

**Q1. How did the householder answer one of them?**  
**Answer:** Friend, I do thee no wrong.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:13

**Q2. What did he ask the labourer if he had agreed with him for?**  
**Answer:** For a penny.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:13

---

### Matthew 20:14

**Q1. What did he tell the man to take?**  
**Answer:** That thine is.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:14

**Q2. Where was the man told to go?**  
**Answer:** Thy way.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:14

**Q3. Unto whom did the householder will to give as much as unto this last?**  
**Answer:** Unto this last.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:14

---

### Matthew 20:15

**Q1. What did the householder ask if it was not lawful for him to do?**  
**Answer:** To do what I will with mine own?  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:15

**Q2. Why was his eye evil?**  
**Answer:** Because I am good.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:15

**Q3. Whose things did the householder have the right to do with as he willed?**  
**Answer:** Mine own.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:15

---

### Matthew 20:16

**Q1. Who shall be first?**  
**Answer:** The last.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:16

**Q2. Who shall be last?**  
**Answer:** The first.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:16

**Q3. How many are called, but how many are chosen?**  
**Answer:** Many be called, but few chosen.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:16

---

### Matthew 20:17

**Q1. As Jesus was going up to Jerusalem, what did he do to the twelve disciples?**  
**Answer:** Took them apart in the way.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:17

**Q2. Where did Jesus take the twelve disciples apart?**  
**Answer:** In the way.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:17

**Q3. What did Jesus say unto them?**  
**Answer:** Behold, we go up to Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:17

---

### Matthew 20:18

**Q1. To whom shall the Son of man be betrayed?**  
**Answer:** Unto the chief priests and unto the scribes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:18

**Q2. What will the chief priests and scribes condemn Him to?**  
**Answer:** Death.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:18

**Q3. Where is the Son of man going to be betrayed?**  
**Answer:** To Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:18

---

### Matthew 20:19

**Q1. To whom shall they deliver the Son of man?**  
**Answer:** To the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:19

**Q2. What will the Gentiles do to Him?**  
**Answer:** Mock, and to scourge, and to crucify him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:19

**Q3. What will happen on the third day?**  
**Answer:** He shall rise again.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:19

---

### Matthew 20:20

**Q1. Who came to Jesus with her sons?**  
**Answer:** The mother of Zebedee's children.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:20

**Q2. What did she do when she came to Him?**  
**Answer:** Worshipped him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:20

**Q3. What did she desire of Him?**  
**Answer:** A certain thing.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:20

---

### Matthew 20:21

**Q1. What did Jesus ask her?**  
**Answer:** What wilt thou?  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:21

**Q2. Where did she ask for her two sons to sit?**  
**Answer:** One on thy right hand, and the other on the left.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:21

**Q3. In what did she want them to sit in these positions?**  
**Answer:** In thy kingdom.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:21

---

### Matthew 20:22

**Q1. What did Jesus answer and say?**  
**Answer:** Ye know not what ye ask.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:22

**Q2. What question did Jesus ask the sons regarding the cup?**  
**Answer:** Are ye able to drink of the cup that I shall drink of?  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:22

**Q3. What did the sons say to Him?**  
**Answer:** We are able.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:22

---

### Matthew 20:23

**Q1. What did Jesus say they shall indeed drink of?**  
**Answer:** My cup.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:23

**Q2. Whose is it to give seats on His right hand and left?**  
**Answer:** It is not mine to give, but it shall be given to them for whom it is prepared of my Father.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:23

**Q3. Who prepared these positions?**  
**Answer:** My Father.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:23

---

### Matthew 20:24

**Q1. When the ten heard what happened, how were they moved regarding the two brethren?**  
**Answer:** They were moved with indignation.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:24

**Q2. How many disciples were moved with indignation?**  
**Answer:** The ten.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:24

**Q3. Against whom were the ten moved with indignation?**  
**Answer:** Against the two brethren.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:24

**Verse:** Matthew 20:32

**Verse:** Matthew 20:32

**Q1. What did the blind men say unto Him?**  
**Answer:** Lord, that our eyes may be opened.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:33

**Q2. Whose eyes did they want opened?**  
**Answer:** Our eyes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:33

**Q3. How did they address Him in this verse?**

**Verse:** Matthew 20:33

**Q1. How did Jesus feel toward them?**  
**Answer:** Had compassion on them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:34

**Q2. What did Jesus touch?**  
**Answer:** Their eyes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 20:34

**Q3. What happened immediately after their eyes were touched?**
