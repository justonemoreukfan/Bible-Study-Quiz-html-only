# Matthew 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 2:1

**Q1. Where was Jesus born?**  
**Answer:** In Bethlehem of Judaea.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:1

**Q2. In the days of which king was Jesus born?**  
**Answer:** Herod the king.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:1

**Q3. Who came from the east to Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Wise men.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:1

---

### Matthew 2:2

**Q4. What question did the wise men ask concerning him that was born?**  
**Answer:** Where is he that is born King of the Jews?  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:2

**Q5. What did the wise men see in the east?**  
**Answer:** His star.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:2

**Q6. What did the wise men say they had come to do?**  
**Answer:** To worship him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:2

---

### Matthew 2:3

**Q7. When Herod the king had heard these things, how did he feel?**  
**Answer:** He was troubled.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:3

**Q8. Who else besides Herod was troubled with him?**  
**Answer:** All Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:3

---

### Matthew 2:4

**Q9. Whom did Herod gather together?**  
**Answer:** All the chief priests and scribes of the people.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:4

**Q10. What did Herod demand of them?**  
**Answer:** Where Christ should be born.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:4

---

### Matthew 2:5

**Q11. Where did they say he should be born?**  
**Answer:** In Bethlehem of Judaea.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:5

**Q12. By what prophet was it written?**  
**Answer:** By the prophet.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:5

---

### Matthew 2:6

**Q13. Which land is not the least among the princes of Judah?**  
**Answer:** Thou Bethlehem, in the land of Judah.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:6

**Q14. What shall come out of Bethlehem?**  
**Answer:** A Governor.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:6

---

### Matthew 2:7

**Q15. Whom did Herod privily call?**  
**Answer:** The wise men.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:7

**Q16. What did Herod diligently inquire of them?**  
**Answer:** What time the star appeared.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:7

---

### Matthew 2:8

**Q17. Where did Herod send the wise men?**  
**Answer:** To Bethlehem.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:8

**Q18. What did Herod tell them to search for?**  
**Answer:** Diligently for the young child.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:8

---

### Matthew 2:9

**Q19. When they had heard the king, what did they do?**  
**Answer:** Departed.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:9

**Q20. What went before them?**  
**Answer:** The star, which they saw in the east.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:9

**Q21. Where did the star stand?**  
**Answer:** Over where the young child was.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:9

---

### Matthew 2:10

**Q22. When they saw the star, how did they feel?**  
**Answer:** With exceeding great joy.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:10

---

### Matthew 2:11

**Q23. Into what did they come when they were come into the house?**  
**Answer:** Into the house.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:11

**Q24. Whom did they see with Mary his mother?**  
**Answer:** The young child.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:11

**Q25. What did they present unto him?**  
**Answer:** Gifts; gold, and frankincense, and myrrh.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:11

---

### Matthew 2:12

**Q26. Being warned of God in a dream, what did they not do?**  
**Answer:** Return to Herod.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:12

**Q27. By what way did they depart into their own country?**  
**Answer:** Another way.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:12

---

### Matthew 2:13

**Q28. Who appeared to Joseph in a dream?**  
**Answer:** The angel of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:13

**Q29. Where did the angel tell Joseph to flee?**  
**Answer:** Into Egypt.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:13

**Q30. How long was Joseph to stay there?**  
**Answer:** Until I bring thee word.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:13

---

### Matthew 2:14

**Q31. When he arose, what did Joseph take?**  
**Answer:** The young child and his mother by night.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:14

**Q32. Where did Joseph depart unto?**  
**Answer:** Into Egypt.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:14

---

### Matthew 2:15

**Q33. Until when was Joseph there?**  
**Answer:** Until the death of Herod.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:15

**Q34. What was fulfilled that was spoken of the Lord by the prophet?**  
**Answer:** Out of Egypt have I called my son.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:15

---

### Matthew 2:16

**Q35. How did Herod feel when he was mocked of the wise men?**  
**Answer:** Exceeding wroth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:16

**Q36. Whom did Herod send forth to be slain?**  
**Answer:** All the children that were in Bethlehem, and in all the coasts thereof.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:16

**Q37. What was the age limit of the children slain?**  
**Answer:** From two years old and under.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:16

---

### Matthew 2:17

**Q38. What was fulfilled that was spoken by Jeremy the prophet?**  
**Answer:** That which was spoken by Jeremy the prophet.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:17

---

### Matthew 2:18

**Q39. Where was a voice heard?**  
**Answer:** In Rama.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:18

**Q40. Who was weeping for her children?**  
**Answer:** Rachel.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:18

**Q41. Why would she not be comforted?**  
**Answer:** Because they are not.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:18

---

### Matthew 2:19

**Q42. When Herod was dead, who appeared in a dream to Joseph in Egypt?**  
**Answer:** An angel of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:19

---

### Matthew 2:20

**Q43. What did the angel tell Joseph to take?**  
**Answer:** The young child and his mother.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:20

**Q44. To what land were they to go?**  
**Answer:** Into the land of Israel.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:20

**Q45. Why were they able to return?**  
**Answer:** For they are dead which sought the young child's life.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:20

---

### Matthew 2:21

**Q46. What did Joseph arise and take?**  
**Answer:** The young child and his mother.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:21

**Q47. Where did he come?**  
**Answer:** Into the land of Israel.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:21

---

### Matthew 2:22

**Q48. Who reigned in Judaea in the room of his father Herod?**  
**Answer:** Archelaus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:22

**Q49. When Joseph heard that Archelaus did reign, how did he feel about going thither?**  
**Answer:** He was afraid.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:22

**Q50. Being warned of God in a dream, where did Joseph turn aside?**  
**Answer:** Into the parts of Galilee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:22

---

### Matthew 2:23

**Q51. And he came and dwelt in a city called what?**  
**Answer:** Nazareth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:23

**Q52. What was fulfilled which was spoken by the prophets?**  
**Answer:** He shall be called a Nazarene.  
**Verse:** Matthew 2:23
