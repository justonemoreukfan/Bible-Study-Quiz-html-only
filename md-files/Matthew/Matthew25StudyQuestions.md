# Matthew 25 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 25:1

**Q1. Then shall the kingdom of heaven be likened unto what?**  
**Answer:** Ten virgins.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:1

**Q2. What did the ten virgins do with their lamps?**  
**Answer:** Took their lamps.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:1

**Q3. Whose coming did the ten virgins go forth to meet?**  
**Answer:** The bridegroom.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:1

---

### Matthew 25:2

**Q4. How many of the virgins were wise?**  
**Answer:** Five.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:2

**Q5. How many of the virgins were foolish?**  
**Answer:** Five.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:2

**Q6. What were the five foolish virgins described as besides foolish?**  
**Answer:** They took their lamps, and took no oil with them. (Or: They were foolish.)  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:2

---

### Matthew 25:3

**Q7. What did the foolish virgins take when they took their lamps?**  
**Answer:** No oil with them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:3

**Q8. Who were the ones that took no oil with them?**  
**Answer:** The foolish.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:3

**Q9. What did the foolish take with their lamps?**  
**Answer:** No oil.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:3

---

### Matthew 25:4

**Q10. What did the wise take in their vessels with their lamps?**  
**Answer:** Oil.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:4

**Q11. Who took oil in their vessels with their lamps?**  
**Answer:** The wise.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:4

**Q12. What did the wise virgins have along with their lamps?**  
**Answer:** Oil in their vessels.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:4

---

### Matthew 25:5

**Q13. While the bridegroom tarried, what did all the virgins do?**  
**Answer:** Slumbered and slept.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:5

**Q14. Who slumbered and slept while the bridegroom tarried?**  
**Answer:** All.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:5

**Q15. Why did they slumber and sleep?**  
**Answer:** While the bridegroom tarried.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:5

---

### Matthew 25:6

**Q16. At what time was there a cry made?**  
**Answer:** At midnight.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:6

**Q17. What was the cry that was made at midnight?**  
**Answer:** Behold, the bridegroom cometh; go ye out to meet him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:6

**Q18. Who were they told to go out to meet?**  
**Answer:** The bridegroom.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:6

---

### Matthew 25:7

**Q19. What did all those virgins do after the cry?**  
**Answer:** Arose.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:7

**Q20. What did the virgins trim?**  
**Answer:** Their lamps.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:7

**Q21. Who arose and trimmed their lamps?**  
**Answer:** All those virgins.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:7

---

### Matthew 25:8

**Q22. What did the foolish say unto the wise?**  
**Answer:** Give us of your oil.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:8

**Q23. Why did the foolish ask the wise for oil?**  
**Answer:** For our lamps are gone out.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:8

**Q24. Whose lamps were gone out?**  
**Answer:** The foolish.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:8

---

**Verse:** Matthew 25:9

**Answer:** Go ye rather to them that sell, and buy for yourselves.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:9

**Q27. Why did the wise refuse to give oil?**  
**Answer:** Lest there be not enough for us and you.  
**Verse:** Matthew 25:9

---

### Matthew 25:10

**Q28. While they went to buy, who came?**

**Verse:** Matthew 25:10

**Answer:** They went in with him to the marriage.
