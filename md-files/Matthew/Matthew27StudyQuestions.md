# Matthew 27 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 27:1

**Q1. When the morning was come, who took counsel against Jesus?**  
**Answer:** All the chief priests and elders of the people.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:1

**Q2. Against whom did the chief priests and elders take counsel?**  
**Answer:** Against Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:1

**Q3. What was the purpose of the counsel taken against Jesus?**  
**Answer:** To put him to death.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:1

---

### Matthew 27:2

**Q1. When they had bound him, where did they lead Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Away.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:2

**Q2. To whom did they deliver him after binding him?**  
**Answer:** Pontius Pilate the governor.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:2

**Q3. Who was Pontius Pilate?**  
**Answer:** The governor.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:2

---

### Matthew 27:3

**Q1. Who saw that Jesus was condemned?**  
**Answer:** Judas, which had betrayed him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:3

**Q2. What did Judas bring again to the chief priests and elders?**  
**Answer:** The thirty pieces of silver.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:3

**Q3. Why did Judas repent and bring back the silver?**  
**Answer:** Because he saw that Jesus was condemned.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:3

---

### Matthew 27:4

**Q1. What did Judas say he had sinned in?**  
**Answer:** In that he betrayed the innocent blood.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:4

**Q2. What was the response of the chief priests and elders to Judas's confession?**  
**Answer:** "What is that to us? see thou to that."  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:4

**Q3. How did Judas describe the blood he betrayed?**  
**Answer:** Innocent blood.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:4

---

### Matthew 27:5

**Q1. Where did Judas cast down the pieces of silver?**  
**Answer:** In the temple.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:5

**Q2. What did Judas do after casting down the pieces of silver in the temple?**  
**Answer:** He departed.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:5

**Q3. What did Judas do after he departed?**  
**Answer:** Went and hanged himself.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:5

---

### Matthew 27:6

**Q1. Who took the silver pieces?**  
**Answer:** The chief priests.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:6

**Q2. Why did the chief priests say it was not lawful to put the silver pieces into the treasury?**  
**Answer:** Because it is the price of blood.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:6

**Q3. What were the silver pieces called by the chief priests?**  
**Answer:** The price of blood.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:6

---

### Matthew 27:7

**Q1. When they took counsel, what did they buy with the pieces of silver?**  
**Answer:** The potter's field.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:7

**Q2. For what purpose was the potter's field bought?**  
**Answer:** To bury strangers in.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:7

---

### Matthew 27:8

**Q1. What has that field been called unto this day?**  
**Answer:** The field of blood.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:8

---

### Matthew 27:9

**Q1. Which prophet was spoken of regarding the thirty pieces of silver?**  
**Answer:** Jeremy the prophet.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:9

**Q2. Whose value was fixed by the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Him that was valued.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:9

---

### Matthew 27:10

**Q1. For what were the thirty pieces of silver given?**  
**Answer:** For the potter's field.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:10

**Q2. Who appointed the giving of the silver for the potter's field?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:10

---

### Matthew 27:11

**Q1. Before whom did Jesus stand?**  
**Answer:** The governor.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:11

**Q2. What question did the governor ask Jesus?**  
**Answer:** "Art thou the King of the Jews?"  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:11

**Q3. What was Jesus' answer to the governor?**  
**Answer:** "Thou sayest."  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:11

---

### Matthew 27:12

**Answer:** Nothing.  
**Verse:** Matthew 27:12

---

### Matthew 27:13

**Q1. What did Pilate say unto him regarding the accusations?**

**Verse:** Matthew 27:13

---

**Verse:** Matthew 27:14
