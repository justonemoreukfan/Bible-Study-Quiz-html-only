# Matthew 28 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 28:1

**Q1. In the end of the sabbath, as it began to dawn toward the first day of the week, who came to see the sepulchre?**  
**Answer:** Mary Magdalene and the other Mary.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:1

**Q2. When did Mary Magdalene and the other Mary come to see the sepulchre?**  
**Answer:** In the end of the sabbath, as it began to dawn toward the first day of the week.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:1

**Q3. What did Mary Magdalene and the other Mary come to see?**  
**Answer:** The sepulchre.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:1

---

### Matthew 28:2

**Q4. What occurred just before the angel of the Lord descended from heaven?**  
**Answer:** There was a great earthquake.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:2

**Q5. Who descended from heaven and came and rolled back the stone from the door?**  
**Answer:** The angel of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:2

**Q6. What did the angel do after coming to the sepulchre?**  
**Answer:** He rolled back the stone from the door, and sat upon it.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:2

---

### Matthew 28:3

**Q7. What was the countenance of the angel?**  
**Answer:** Like lightning.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:3

**Q8. What was the color of the angel's raiment?**  
**Answer:** White as snow.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:3

**Q9. From where did the angel descend in verse 2?**  
**Answer:** From heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:3

---

### Matthew 28:4

**Q10. How did the keepers shake for fear of the angel?**  
**Answer:** They did shake.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:4

**Q11. What happened to the keepers because of their fear?**  
**Answer:** They became as dead men.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:4

**Q12. Who shook and became as dead men out of fear?**  
**Answer:** The keepers.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:4

---

### Matthew 28:5

**Q13. Who answered and said unto the women?**  
**Answer:** The angel.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:5

**Q14. What instruction did the angel give the women regarding fear?**  
**Answer:** Fear not ye.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:5

**Q15. Whom did the angel know the women were seeking?**  
**Answer:** Jesus, which was crucified.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:5

---

### Matthew 28:6

**Q16. Where did the angel say Jesus was?**  
**Answer:** He is not here: for he is risen.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:6

**Q17. What proof did the angel offer the women to see?**  
**Answer:** The place where the Lord lay.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:6

**Q18. As whom did Jesus rise, according to the angel's statement?**  
**Answer:** As he said.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:6

---

### Matthew 28:7

**Q19. Where did the angel instruct the women to go quickly?**  
**Answer:** Tell his disciples that he is risen from the dead.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:7

**Q20. Where would the disciples go before the angel's mentioned destination?**  
**Answer:** Behold, he goeth before you into Galilee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:7

**Q21. What did the angel say the women would do there?**  
**Answer:** There shall ye see him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:7

---

### Matthew 28:8

**Q22. How did the women depart quickly from the sepulchre?**  
**Answer:** With fear and great joy.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:8

**Q23. Where did the women depart from?**  
**Answer:** The sepulchre.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:8

**Q24. Where did the women run to do?**  
**Answer:** Did run to bring his disciples word.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:8

---

### Matthew 28:9

**Q25. Who met the women as they went to tell his disciples?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:9

**Q26. What greeting did Jesus give the women?**  
**Answer:** All hail.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:9

**Q27. What physical action did the women take when they held him?**  
**Answer:** They came and held him by the feet, and worshipped him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:9

---

### Matthew 28:10

**Q28. What did Jesus tell the women not to do?**  
**Answer:** Be not afraid.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:10

**Q29. Who did Jesus tell the women to go and tell to go into Galilee?**  
**Answer:** My brethren.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:10

**Q30. Where did Jesus say his brethren would see him?**  
**Answer:** In Galilee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:10

---

### Matthew 28:11

**Q31. While the women were going, who came into the city?**  
**Answer:** Some of the watch.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:11

**Q32. What did the watch show unto the chief priests?**  
**Answer:** All the things that were done.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:11

**Q33. To whom did some of the watch report what happened?**  
**Answer:** The chief priests.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:11

---

### Matthew 28:12

**Q34. Who were assembled with the elders to take counsel?**  
**Answer:** The chief priests.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:12

**Q35. After taking counsel, what did they give to the soldiers?**  
**Answer:** Large money.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:12

---

### Matthew 28:13

**Q36. What phrase were the soldiers instructed to say?**  
**Answer:** His disciples came by night, and stole him away while we slept.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:13

**Q37. When did the soldiers say the disciples came?**  
**Answer:** By night.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:13

**Q38. What state were the soldiers in while the body was supposedly stolen?**  
**Answer:** While we slept.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:13

---

### Matthew 28:14

**Q39. If this come to the governor's ears, who would persuade him?**  
**Answer:** We will persuade him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:14

**Q40. What would the chief priests secure for the soldiers regarding the governor?**  
**Answer:** Secure you.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:14

**Q41. What would the soldiers be kept free from by this persuasion?**  
**Answer:** Fear.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:14

---

### Matthew 28:15

**Q42. What did the soldiers do with the money?**  
**Answer:** Took the money, and did as they were taught.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:15

**Q43. Among whom was this saying commonly reported?**  
**Answer:** Among the Jews.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:15

**Q44. Until when was this saying commonly reported among the Jews?**  
**Answer:** Until this day.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:15

---

### Matthew 28:16

**Q45. How many disciples went away into Galilee?**  
**Answer:** The eleven disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:16

**Q46. Into what region did the eleven disciples go?**  
**Answer:** Into Galilee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:16

**Q47. To what specific location in Galilee did they go, as Jesus had appointed?**  
**Answer:** Into a mountain where Jesus had appointed them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:16

---

### Matthew 28:17

**Q48. What did the disciples do when they saw him?**  
**Answer:** They worshipped him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:17

**Q49. What condition did some disciples experience upon seeing him?**  
**Answer:** But some doubted.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:17

**Q50. Whom did the disciples see on the mountain?**  
**Answer:** Him (Jesus).  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:17

---

### Matthew 28:18

**Q51. Who came and spoke unto them?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:18

**Q52. What power was given unto Jesus?**  
**Answer:** All power is given unto me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:18

**Q53. Where did Jesus state this power was given?**  
**Answer:** In heaven and in earth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:18

---

### Matthew 28:19

**Q54. What action were the disciples commanded to take regarding all nations?**  
**Answer:** Go ye therefore, and teach all nations.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:19

**Q55. In whose name were the disciples told to baptize?**  
**Answer:** In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:19

**Q56. What groups were the disciples to teach?**  
**Answer:** All nations.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:19

---

### Matthew 28:20

**Q57. What were the disciples to teach the nations to observe?**  
**Answer:** All things whatsoever I have commanded you.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:20

**Q58. How long did Jesus promise to be with them?**  
**Answer:** Alway, even unto the end of the world.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:20

**Q59. Amen concludes which book chapter?**  
**Answer:** Matthew 28.  
**Verse:** Matthew 28:20
