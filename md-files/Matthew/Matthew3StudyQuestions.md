# Matthew 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 3:1

**Q1. Who came in those days?**  
**Answer:** John the Baptist.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:1

**Q2. Where did John the Baptist come preaching?**  
**Answer:** In the wilderness of Judaea.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:1

**Q3. What did John the Baptist do in the wilderness of Judaea?**  
**Answer:** Preached.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:1

---

### Matthew 3:2

**Q4. What command did John give regarding the kingdom of heaven?**  
**Answer:** Repent ye.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:2

**Q5. Why were the people told to repent?**  
**Answer:** For the kingdom of heaven is at hand.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:2

**Q6. What is at hand according to John's preaching?**  
**Answer:** The kingdom of heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:2

---

### Matthew 3:3

**Q7. Who was spoken of by the prophet Esaias?**  
**Answer:** This is he that was spoken of by the prophet Esaias.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:3

**Q8. Whose voice crying in the wilderness was Esaias speaking of?**  
**Answer:** The voice of one crying in the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:3

**Q9. What were the people told to prepare in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make his paths straight.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:3

---

### Matthew 3:4

**Q10. What was the clothing of John the Baptist made of?**  
**Answer:** Camel's hair.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:4

**Q11. What did John wear about his loins?**  
**Answer:** A leathern girdle.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:4

**Q12. What was John's meat?**  
**Answer:** Locusts and wild honey.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:4

---

### Matthew 3:5

**Q13. Who went out to John?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem, and all Judaea, and all the region round about Jordan.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:5

**Q14. Where did the people go out to?**  
**Answer:** To him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:5

**Q15. From which regions did the people go out?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem, and all Judaea, and all the region round about Jordan.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:5

---

### Matthew 3:6

**Q16. What was done to the people who went out?**  
**Answer:** They were baptized of him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:6

**Q17. Where were the people baptized?**  
**Answer:** In Jordan.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:6

**Q18. What did the people do while being baptized?**  
**Answer:** Confessing their sins.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:6

---

### Matthew 3:7

**Q19. Who did John see coming to his baptism?**  
**Answer:** Many of the Pharisees and Sadducees.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:7

**Q20. What did John call the Pharisees and Sadducees?**  
**Answer:** Generation of vipers.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:7

**Q21. What question did John ask the Pharisees and Sadducees?**  
**Answer:** Who hath warned you to flee from the wrath to come?  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:7

---

### Matthew 3:8

**Q22. What did John tell them to bring forth?**  
**Answer:** Fruits meet for repentance.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:8

**Q23. What kind of fruits were they to bring forth?**  
**Answer:** Fruits meet for repentance.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:8

**Q24. In relation to what were these fruits to be brought forth?**  
**Answer:** Repentance.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:8

---

### Matthew 3:9

**Q25. What did John say they should not think to say within themselves?**  
**Answer:** We have Abraham to our father.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:9

**Q26. Who did they think was their father?**  
**Answer:** Abraham.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:9

**Q27. What did John say God is able to raise up of these stones?**  
**Answer:** Children unto Abraham.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:9

---

### Matthew 3:10

**Q28. Where is the axe laid?**  
**Answer:** Unto the root of the trees.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:10

**Q29. What happens to every tree that bringeth not forth good fruit?**  
**Answer:** It is hewn down, and cast into the fire.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:10

**Q30. What happens to the tree after it is hewn down?**  
**Answer:** It is cast into the fire.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:10

---

### Matthew 3:11

**Q31. With what did John baptize the people?**  
**Answer:** With water unto repentance.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:11

**Q32. Who cometh after John that is mightier than he?**  
**Answer:** He that cometh after me is mightier than I.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:11

**Q33. With what will he baptize the people?**  
**Answer:** With the Holy Ghost, and with fire.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:11

---

### Matthew 3:12

**Q34. What is in his hand?**  
**Answer:** His fan.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:12

**Q35. Where will he gather his wheat?**  
**Answer:** Into the garner.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:12

**Q36. With what will he burn up the chaff?**  
**Answer:** With unquenchable fire.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:12

---

### Matthew 3:13

**Q37. From where did Jesus come?**  
**Answer:** From Galilee to Jordan.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:13

**Q38. To whom did Jesus come at Jordan?**  
**Answer:** Unto John.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:13

**Q39. Why did Jesus come to John at Jordan?**  
**Answer:** To be baptized of him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:13

---

### Matthew 3:14

**Q40. Who forbade Jesus?**  
**Answer:** John.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:14

**Q41. What did John say to Jesus when he forbade him?**  
**Answer:** I have need to be baptized of thee, and comest thou to me?  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:14

**Q42. Who felt he had need to be baptized of the other?**  
**Answer:** John.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:14

---

### Matthew 3:15

**Q43. What did Jesus answer and say unto John?**  
**Answer:** Suffer it to be so now.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:15

**Q44. Why did Jesus say it is becoming to fulfill all righteousness?**  
**Answer:** For thus it becometh us to fulfill all righteousness.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:15

**Q45. After Jesus spoke, what did John do?**  
**Answer:** Then he suffered him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:15

---

### Matthew 3:16

**Q47. What did Jesus do straightway after he was baptized?**  
**Answer:** Went up straightway out of the water.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:16

**Q46. What were opened unto him when Jesus came up out of the water?**  
**Answer:** The heavens were opened unto him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:16

**Q48. What descended like a dove and lighted upon him?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit of God.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:16

---

### Matthew 3:17

**Q49. What came from heaven?**  
**Answer:** A voice from heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:17

**Q50. What did the voice from heaven say about Jesus?**  
**Answer:** This is my beloved Son.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:17

**Q51. In whom was the Father well pleased?**  
**Answer:** In whom I am well pleased.  
**Verse:** Matthew 3:17
