# Matthew 14 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 14:1

**Q1. Who heard of the fame of Jesus at that time?**  
**Answer:** Herod the tetrarch.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:1

**Q2. Whose fame did Herod the tetrarch hear of?**  
**Answer:** The fame of Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:1

**Q3. When did Herod hear of the fame of Jesus?**  
**Answer:** At that time.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:1

---

### Matthew 14:2

**Q1. What did Herod say unto his servants concerning John the Baptist?**  
**Answer:** This is John the Baptist.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:2

**Q2. What is said to be risen from the dead?**  
**Answer:** He is risen from the dead.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:2

**Q3. What is shown forth through him according to Herod?**  
**Answer:** Mighty works do show forth themselves in him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:2

---

### Matthew 14:3

**Q1. Who had laid hold on John?**  
**Answer:** Herod.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:3

**Q2. What did Herod do to John?**  
**Answer:** Bound him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:3

**Q3. Where did Herod put John because of Herodias?**  
**Answer:** Put him in prison.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:3

---

### Matthew 14:4

**Q1. What did John say unto Herod?**  
**Answer:** It is not lawful for thee to have her.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:4

**Q2. Whose wife was Herodias, whom Herod had?**  
**Answer:** Philip his brother's wife.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:4

**Q3. According to John, what was it not lawful for Herod to do?**  
**Answer:** To have her.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:4

---

### Matthew 14:5

**Q1. When Herod would have put John to death, what did he fear?**  
**Answer:** The multitude.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:5

**Q2. Why did Herod fear the multitude?**  
**Answer:** Because they counted him as a prophet.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:5

**Q3. Whom did the multitude count as a prophet?**  
**Answer:** John.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:5

---

### Matthew 14:6

**Q1. Whose birthday was kept?**  
**Answer:** Herod's.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:6

**Q2. Who danced before them during Herod's birthday?**  
**Answer:** The daughter of Herodias.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:6

**Q3. What did the daughter of Herodias do before them?**  
**Answer:** Danced.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:6

---

### Matthew 14:7

**Q1. What did Herod promise with an oath?**  
**Answer:** To give her whatsoever she would ask.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:7

**Q2. How did Herod make the promise to her?**  
**Answer:** With an oath.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:7

**Q3. Who was promised whatever she would ask?**  
**Answer:** The daughter of Herodias.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:7

---

### Matthew 14:8

**Q1. By whom was she being before instructed?**  
**Answer:** Her mother.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:8

**Q2. What did she say to give her here?**  
**Answer:** John Baptist's head in a charger.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:8

**Q3. In what was John Baptist's head to be given?**  
**Answer:** In a charger.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:8

---

### Matthew 14:9

**Q1. How did the king feel about the request?**  
**Answer:** He was sorry.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:9

**Q2. For whose sake did the king command it to be given?**  
**Answer:** For the oath's sake, and them which sat with him at meat.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:9

**Q3. What did the king command to be done?**  
**Answer:** It to be given her.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:9

---

### Matthew 14:10

**Q1. Who did the king send and behead?**  
**Answer:** John.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:10

**Q2. Where did the beheading take place?**  
**Answer:** In the prison.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:10

**Q3. Who sent to have John beheaded in the prison?**  
**Answer:** The king.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:10

---

### Matthew 14:11

**Q1. How was John's head brought?**  
**Answer:** In a charger.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:11

**Q2. To whom was the head brought?**  
**Answer:** The damsel.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:11

**Q3. To whom did the damsel bring the head?**  
**Answer:** Her mother.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:11

---

### Matthew 14:12

**Q1. Who came and took up the body?**  
**Answer:** His disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:12

**Q2. What did his disciples do after taking up the body?**  
**Answer:** Buried it.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:12

**Q3. After burying the body, what did they do?**  
**Answer:** Went and told Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:12

---

### Matthew 14:13

**Q1. When Jesus heard of it, what did he do?**  
**Answer:** Departed thence by ship.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:13

**Q2. Where did Jesus depart into?**  
**Answer:** A desert place apart.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:13

**Q3. Who followed Jesus on foot out of the cities when they heard thereof?**  
**Answer:** The people.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:13

---

### Matthew 14:14

**Q1. What did Jesus do when he went forth?**  
**Answer:** Saw a great multitude.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:14

**Q2. How did Jesus feel toward the great multitude?**  
**Answer:** Was moved with compassion toward them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:14

**Q3. What did Jesus do to their sick?**  
**Answer:** He healed their sick.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:14

---

### Matthew 14:15

**Q1. When it was evening, who came to Jesus?**  
**Answer:** His disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:15

**Q2. What kind of place did the disciples say it was?**  
**Answer:** This is a desert place.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:15

**Q3. What did the disciples want the multitude to do?**  
**Answer:** Send the multitude away, that they may go into the villages, and buy themselves victuals.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:15

---

### Matthew 14:16

**Q1. Who said, "They need not depart"?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:16

**Q2. What did Jesus tell the disciples to give the multitude?**  
**Answer:** Give ye them to eat.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:16

**Q3. Who spoke the words "They need not depart"?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:16

---

### Matthew 14:17

**Q1. What did the disciples say they had here?**  
**Answer:** Five loaves, and two fishes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:17

**Q2. How many loaves did they have?**  
**Answer:** Five loaves.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:17

**Q3. How many fishes did they have?**  
**Answer:** Two fishes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:17

---

### Matthew 14:18

**Q1. What did Jesus tell them to do with the food?**  
**Answer:** Bring them hither to me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:18

**Q2. Who said, "Bring them hither to me"?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:18

**Q3. What objects were to be brought hither to Jesus?**  
**Answer:** The loaves and fishes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:18

---

### Matthew 14:19

**Q1. Whom did Jesus command to sit down on the grass?**  
**Answer:** The multitude.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:19

**Q2. What did Jesus take when he looked up to heaven?**  
**Answer:** The five loaves, and the two fishes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:19

**Q3. To whom did the disciples give the loaves?**  
**Answer:** To the multitude.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:19

---

### Matthew 14:20

**Q1. Who did all eat?**  
**Answer:** They did all eat.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:20

**Q2. How were they filled?**  
**Answer:** And were filled.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:20

**Q3. How many baskets of the fragments that remained did they take up?**  
**Answer:** Twelve baskets full.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:20

---

### Matthew 14:21

**Q1. How many men had eaten, besides women and children?**  
**Answer:** About five thousand men.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:21

**Q2. Who besides men had eaten?**  
**Answer:** Women and children.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:21

**Q3. Approximately how many men were there that had eaten?**  
**Answer:** About five thousand.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:21

---

### Matthew 14:22

**Q1. What did Jesus straightway constrain his disciples to do?**  
**Answer:** To get into a ship.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:22

**Q2. Where were the disciples to go before him?**  
**Answer:** Unto the other side.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:22

**Q3. What did Jesus do while the disciples went to the other side?**  
**Answer:** Sent the multitudes away.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:22

---

### Matthew 14:23

**Q1. Where did Jesus go when he had sent the multitudes away?**  
**Answer:** Up into a mountain apart to pray.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:23

**Q2. When the evening was come, where was Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Alone.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:23

**Q3. Who was alone in the mountain when evening was come?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:23

---

### Matthew 14:24

**Q1. Where was the ship now?**  
**Answer:** In the midst of the sea.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:24

**Q2. What was the ship tossed with?**  
**Answer:** Waves.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:24

**Q3. Why was the ship tossed with waves?**  
**Answer:** For the wind was contrary.  
**Verse:** Matthew 14:24

**Verse:** Matthew 14:25

**Q2. How did Jesus go unto them?**

**Verse:** Matthew 14:27

**Q2. What did Jesus tell them to be?**

**Verse:** Matthew 14:27
