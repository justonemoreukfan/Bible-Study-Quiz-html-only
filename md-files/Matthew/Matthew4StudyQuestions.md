# Matthew 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 4:1

**Q1. Who was Jesus led up of into the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:1

**Q2. What was the purpose of Jesus being led into the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** To be tempted of the devil.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:1

**Q3. By whom was Jesus tempted in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** The devil.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:1

---

### Matthew 4:2

**Q1. How many days and nights had Jesus fasted?**  
**Answer:** Forty days and forty nights.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:2

**Q2. What happened after Jesus had fasted forty days and forty nights?**  
**Answer:** He was afterward an hungred.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:2

**Q3. What state was Jesus in after the fast?**  
**Answer:** An hungred.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:2

---

### Matthew 4:3

**Q1. Who came to Jesus during His hunger?**  
**Answer:** The tempter.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:3

---

*(Note: Matthew 4:3 contains a compound statement and questions are derived directly from the text within this verse).*

**Q2. What did the tempter say Jesus should command if He be the Son of God?**  
**Answer:** That these stones be made bread.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:3

**Q3. What identity did the tempter attribute to Jesus in his suggestion?**  
**Answer:** The Son of God.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:3

---

### Matthew 4:4

**Q1. What was written, according to Jesus' response?**  
**Answer:** Man shall not live by bread alone.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:4

**Q2. By what else does man live besides bread?**  
**Answer:** By every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:4

**Q3. Out of whose mouth does the alternative sustainer proceed?**  
**Answer:** The mouth of God.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:4

---

### Matthew 4:5

**Q1. Where did the devil take Jesus next?**  
**Answer:** Up into the holy city.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:5

**Q2. Where did the devil set Jesus in the holy city?**  
**Answer:** On a pinnacle of the temple.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:5

**Q3. What is the city referred to as in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The holy city.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:5

---

### Matthew 4:6

**Q1. What did the devil say Jesus should do if He be the Son of God?**  
**Answer:** Cast thyself down.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:6

**Q2. What was written concerning the angels in the devil's quote?**  
**Answer:** He shall give his angels charge concerning thee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:6

**Q3. In their hands, how would the angels bear Jesus up?**  
**Answer:** Lest at any time thou dash thy foot against a stone.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:6

---

### Matthew 4:7

**Q1. What did Jesus say is written regarding tempting the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:7

**Q2. Whose God is the Lord, according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Thy God.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:7

**Q3. What instruction does this verse give regarding the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:7

---

### Matthew 4:8

**Q1. Where did the devil take Jesus in the third temptation?**  
**Answer:** Up into an exceeding high mountain.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:8

**Q2. What did the devil show Jesus from the mountain?**  
**Answer:** All the kingdoms of the world.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:8

**Q3. What else did the devil show Jesus besides the kingdoms of the world?**  
**Answer:** The glory of them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:8

---

### Matthew 4:9

**Q1. What did the devil say he would give unto Jesus?**  
**Answer:** All these things.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:9

**Q2. Under what condition did the devil say he would give these things to Jesus?**  
**Answer:** If thou wilt fall down and worship me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:9

**Q3. What action did the devil require Jesus to perform?**  
**Answer:** Fall down and worship me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:9

---

### Matthew 4:10

**Q1. What did Jesus say unto the devil when He commanded him to get hence?**  
**Answer:** Get thee hence, Satan.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:10

**Q2. What is written that man shall worship?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:10

**Q3. Whom only shall man serve, according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Him only shalt thou serve.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:10

---

### Matthew 4:11

**Q1. Who left Jesus after the temptation?**  
**Answer:** The devil.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:11

**Q2. Who came and ministered unto Jesus after the devil departed?**  
**Answer:** Behold, angels.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:11

**Q3. What did the angels do unto Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Came and ministered unto him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:11

---

### Matthew 4:12

**Q1. What did Jesus hear had happened to John?**  
**Answer:** That John was cast into prison.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:12

**Q2. Where did Jesus depart into after hearing about John?**  
**Answer:** Into Galilee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:12

**Q3. Who was cast into prison, causing Jesus to depart?**  
**Answer:** John.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:12

---

### Matthew 4:13

**Q1. What city did Jesus leave to dwell in Capernaum?**  
**Answer:** Nazareth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:13

**Q2. Where did Jesus come and dwell?**  
**Answer:** Capernaum.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:13

**Q3. In what region are the borders of Zabulon and Nephthalim located?**  
**Answer:** Upon the sea coast.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:13

---

### Matthew 4:14

**Q1. Whose prophecy was fulfilled by these events?**  
**Answer:** Esaias the prophet.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:14

**Q2. What was spoken by Esaias the prophet?**  
**Answer:** That it might be fulfilled which was spoken.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:14

**Q3. Who spoke the words referred to in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Esaias the prophet.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:14

---

### Matthew 4:15

**Q1. Which land is mentioned first in the prophecy?**  
**Answer:** The land of Zabulon, and the land of Nephthalim.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:15

**Q2. By what other description is the way referred to in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The way of the sea, beyond Jordan.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:15

**Q3. What region is mentioned alongside the Gentiles?**  
**Answer:** Galilee of the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:15

---

### Matthew 4:16

**Q1. What did the people which sat in darkness see?**  
**Answer:** Great light.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:16

**Q2. Upon what group did light spring up?**  
**Answer:** Upon them which sat in the region and shadow of death.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:16

**Q3. What kind of light did the people in darkness see?**  
**Answer:** Great light.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:16

---

### Matthew 4:17

**Q1. From that time what did Jesus begin to do?**  
**Answer:** To preach.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:17

**Q2. What message did Jesus preach?**  
**Answer:** Repent: for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:17

**Q3. Why did Jesus say to repent?**  
**Answer:** For the kingdom of heaven is at hand.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:17

---

### Matthew 4:18

**Q1. By what sea was Jesus walking?**  
**Answer:** The sea of Galilee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:18

**Q2. How many brethren did Jesus see walking by the sea?**  
**Answer:** Two brethren.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:18

**Q3. What were Simon called Peter and Andrew casting into the sea?**  
**Answer:** A net.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:18

---

### Matthew 4:19

**Q1. What did Jesus say He would make the two brethren?**  
**Answer:** Fishers of men.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:19

**Q2. What instruction did Jesus give the two brethren?**  
**Answer:** Follow me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:19

**Q3. Whom were the brethren called to follow?**  
**Answer:** Me (Jesus).  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:19

---

### Matthew 4:20

**Q1. What did the brethren straightway leave?**  
**Answer:** Their nets.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:20

**Q2. What did the brethren do immediately after Jesus called them?**  
**Answer:** They straightway left their nets, and followed him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:20

**Q3. Whom did the brethren follow after leaving their nets?**  
**Answer:** Him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:20

---

### Matthew 4:21

**Q1. Whom did Jesus see going on from thence?**  
**Answer:** Other two brethren.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:21

**Q2. What were James and John doing in the ship?**  
**Answer:** Mending their nets.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:21

**Q3. Who was with James and John in the ship?**  
**Answer:** Zebedee their father.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:21

---

### Matthew 4:22

**Q1. What did James and John immediately leave?**  
**Answer:** The ship and their father.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:22

**Q2. What did James and John do immediately after being called?**  
**Answer:** They followed him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:22

**Q3. Whose father did they leave in the ship?**  
**Answer:** Their father Zebedee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:22

---

### Matthew 4:23

**Q1. Where did Jesus go about teaching?**  
**Answer:** All Galilee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:23

**Q2. Where did Jesus teach the people?**  
**Answer:** In their synagogues.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:23

**Q3. What did Jesus heal among the people?**  
**Answer:** All manner of sickness and all manner of disease among the people.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:23

---

### Matthew 4:24

**Q1. Where did Jesus' fame go throughout?**  
**Answer:** Throughout all Syria.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:24

**Q2. What did they bring unto Jesus?**  
**Answer:** All sick people that were taken with diverse diseases and torments.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:24

**Q3. Whom did Jesus heal alongside those possessed with devils?**  
**Answer:** Those which were lunatick, and those that had the palsy.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:24

---

### Matthew 4:25

**Q1. What great multitudes followed Jesus?**  
**Answer:** From Galilee, and from Decapolis, and from Jerusalem, and from Judaea, and from beyond Jordan.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:25

**Q2. From how many specific regions are the multitudes noted as coming from in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Five.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:25

**Q3. Who followed Jesus from Galilee, Decapolis, Jerusalem, Judaea, and beyond Jordan?**  
**Answer:** Great multitudes of people.  
**Verse:** Matthew 4:25
