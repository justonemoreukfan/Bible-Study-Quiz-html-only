# Matthew 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 6:1

**Q1. What should men not do before men to be seen of them?**  
**Answer:** Take heed that ye do not your alms before men, to be seen of them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:1

**Q2. Otherwise, what will ye have none of from your Father which is in heaven?**  
**Answer:** Otherwise ye have no reward of your Father which is in heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:1

**Q3. Where is your Father located who gives the reward?**  
**Answer:** In heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:1

---

### Matthew 6:2

**Q1. When thou doest thine alms, what shouldest thou not do before thee?**  
**Answer:** Do not sound a trumpet before thee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:2

**Q2. Who does this sounding of a trumpet in the synagogues and in the streets?**  
**Answer:** The hypocrites.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:2

**Q3. What do the hypocrites have as their reward?**  
**Answer:** Verily I say unto you, They have their reward.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:2

---

### Matthew 6:3

**Q1. When thou doest alms, what should thy left hand not know?**  
**Answer:** Let not thy left hand know what thy right hand doeth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:3

**Q2. Whose hand should not know what thy right hand doeth?**  
**Answer:** Thy left hand.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:3

**Q3. What action are you performing with your right hand in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Doest alms.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:3

---

### Matthew 6:4

**Q1. That thine alms may be in what condition?**  
**Answer:** In secret.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:4

**Q2. Who seeth in secret and shall reward thee openly?**  
**Answer:** Thy Father which seeth in secret.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:4

**Q3. How shall thy Father reward thee?**  
**Answer:** Himself shall reward thee openly.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:4

---

### Matthew 6:5

**Q1. Who should you not be as when you pray?**  
**Answer:** The hypocrites.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:5

**Q2. Where do the hypocrites love to pray?**  
**Answer:** Standing in the synagogues and in the corners of the streets.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:5

**Q3. Why do they stand to pray in these places?**  
**Answer:** That they may be seen of men.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:5

---

### Matthew 6:6

**Q1. When thou prayest, where shouldest thou enter?**  
**Answer:** Into thy closet.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:6

**Q2. When thou hast shut thy door, to whom shouldest thou pray?**  
**Answer:** To thy Father which is in secret.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:6

**Q3. What will thy Father which seeth in secret do?**  
**Answer:** Shall reward thee openly.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:6

---

### Matthew 6:7

**Q1. When ye pray, what should ye not use?**  
**Answer:** Vain repetitions.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:7

**Q2. Who uses vain repetitions in their prayer?**  
**Answer:** The heathen.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:7

**Q3. What do the heathen think they shall be heard for?**  
**Answer:** For their much speaking.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:7

---

### Matthew 6:8

**Q1. Should ye therefore be like the heathen?**

**Verse:** Matthew 6:8

**Q2. Who knoweth what things ye have need of?**  
**Answer:** Your Father.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:8

**Q3. When does your Father know what things ye have need of?**  
**Answer:** Before ye ask him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:8

### Matthew 6:14

**Q1. If ye forgive men what, will your heavenly Father also forgive you?**  
**Answer:** Their trespasses.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:14

**Q2. Whose trespasses must ye forgive?**  
**Answer:** Men their trespasses.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:14

**Q3. Who will also forgive you if you do this?**  
**Answer:** Your heavenly Father.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:14

---

### Matthew 6:15

**Q1. If ye forgive not men their trespasses, what will your Father not do?**  
**Answer:** Neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:15

**Q2. Whose trespasses will your Father not forgive?**  
**Answer:** Your trespasses.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:15

**Q3. Why will your Father not forgive your trespasses?**  
**Answer:** If ye forgive not men their trespasses.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:15

### Matthew 6:19

**Q1. Where should ye not lay up for yourselves treasures?**  
**Answer:** Upon earth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:19

**Q2. What corrupts treasures on earth?**  
**Answer:** Where moth and rust doth corrupt.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:19

**Q3. What do thieves do to treasures on earth?**  
**Answer:** Where thieves break through and steal.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:19

### Matthew 6:20

**Q1. Where should ye lay up for yourselves treasures?**  
**Answer:** In heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:20

**Q2. What does moth and rust not do in heaven?**  
**Answer:** Where neither moth nor rust doth corrupt.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:20

**Q3. What do thieves not do in heaven?**  
**Answer:** Where thieves do not break through nor steal.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:20

### Matthew 6:22

**Q1. What is the light of the body?**  
**Answer:** The light of the body is the eye.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:22

**Q2. If therefore thine eye be what, thy whole body shall be full of light?**  
**Answer:** Single.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:22

**Q3. What shall thy whole body be if thine eye be single?**  
**Answer:** Full of light.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:22

---

### Matthew 6:23

**Q1. If thine eye be evil, what shall thy whole body be?**  
**Answer:** Full of darkness.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:23

**Q2. If the light that is in thee be darkness, how great is what?**  
**Answer:** That darkness!  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:23

**Q3. What kind of eye makes the whole body full of darkness?**  
**Answer:** An evil eye.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:23

---

### Matthew 6:24

**Q1. How many masters can no man serve?**  
**Answer:** No man can serve two masters.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:24

**Q2. What will he either hate and love, or hold to and despise?**  
**Answer:** Either he will hate the one, and love the other; or else he will hold to the one, and despise the other.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:24

**Q3. What two entities cannot be served together?**  
**Answer:** Ye cannot serve God and mammon.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:24

---

**Answer:** What ye shall eat, or what ye shall drink.

**Q2. What should ye not take thought for concerning your body?**  
**Answer:** What ye shall put on.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:25

**Q3. Is the life more than meat, and the body than what?**  
**Answer:** Raiment.  
**Verse:** Matthew 6:25
