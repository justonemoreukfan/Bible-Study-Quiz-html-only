# Matthew 13 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 13:1

**Q1. Who went out of the house on the same day?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:1

**Q2. Where did Jesus sit?**  
**Answer:** By the sea side.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:1

**Q3. What did Jesus do after going out of the house?**  
**Answer:** He sat by the sea side.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:1

---

### Matthew 13:2

**Q1. Who gathered together unto Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Great multitudes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:2

**Q2. Where did Jesus go after the multitudes gathered?**  
**Answer:** Into a ship.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:2

**Q3. What did the whole multitude do while Jesus sat in the ship?**  
**Answer:** Stood on the shore.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:2

---

### Matthew 13:3

**Q1. How did Jesus speak to the multitudes?**  
**Answer:** In parables.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:3

**Q2. What did Jesus say went forth to sow?**  
**Answer:** A sower.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:3

**Q3. What did the sower do?**  
**Answer:** He went forth to sow.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:3

---

### Matthew 13:4

**Q1. Where did some seeds fall when he sowed?**  
**Answer:** By the way side.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:4

**Q2. What came and devoured the seeds that fell by the way side?**  
**Answer:** The fowls.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:4

**Q3. What happened to the seeds that fell by the way side?**  
**Answer:** The fowls came and devoured them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:4

---

### Matthew 13:5

**Q1. Where did some portions of seed fall?**  
**Answer:** Upon stony places.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:5

**Q2. Why did the seeds spring up quickly in stony places?**  
**Answer:** Because they had no deepness of earth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:5

**Q3. What did the seeds not have in the stony places?**  
**Answer:** Deepness of earth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:5

---

### Matthew 13:6

**Q1. What happened when the sun was up?**  
**Answer:** The plants were scorched.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:6

**Q2. Why did the plants wither away?**  
**Answer:** Because they had no root.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:6

**Q3. What happened to the plants because they had no root?**  
**Answer:** They withered away.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:6

---

### Matthew 13:7

**Q1. Where did some seeds fall among other things?**  
**Answer:** Among thorns.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:7

**Q2. What sprang up with the seeds that fell among thorns?**  
**Answer:** The thorns.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:7

**Q3. What did the thorns do to the seeds?**  
**Answer:** Choked them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:7

---

### Matthew 13:8

**Q1. Where did other seeds fall?**  
**Answer:** Into good ground.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:8

**Q2. What did the seeds bring forth?**  
**Answer:** Fruit.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:8

**Q3. What were the different yields of fruit produced?**  
**Answer:** Some an hundredfold, some sixtyfold, some thirtyfold.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:8

**Answer:** Seeing they see not.  
**Verse:** Matthew 13:13

**Verse:** Matthew 13:13
