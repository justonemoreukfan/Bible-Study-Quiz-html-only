# Matthew 17 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 17:1

**Q1. Who taketh Jesus after six days?**  
**Answer:** Peter, James, and John his brother.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:1

**Q2. Where does Jesus bring them up?**  
**Answer:** Into an high mountain apart.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:1

**Q3. Who is brought up into an high mountain apart?**  
**Answer:** Peter, James, and John.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:1

---

### Matthew 17:2

**Q4. What happened to Jesus before them?**  
**Answer:** He was transfigured.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:2

**Q5. What did His face look like?**  
**Answer:** It did shine as the sun.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:2

**Q6. What happened to His raiment?**  
**Answer:** It was white as the light.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:2

---

### Matthew 17:3

**Q7. Who appeared unto them?**  
**Answer:** Moses and Elias.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:3

---

### Matthew 17:4

**Q8. Who answered and said unto Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Peter.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:4

**Q9. What did Peter call it?**  
**Answer:** Good for us to be here.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:4

**Q10. How many tabernacles did Peter propose to make?**  
**Answer:** Three.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:4

---

### Matthew 17:5

**Q11. What kind of cloud overshadowed them?**  
**Answer:** A bright cloud.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:5

**Q12. What came out of the cloud?**  
**Answer:** A voice.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:5

**Q13. What is said of Jesus in the cloud's message?**  
**Answer:** This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased; hear ye him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:5

---

### Matthew 17:6

**Q14. What did the disciples do when they heard the voice?**  
**Answer:** They fell on their face.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:6

**Q15. How did the disciples feel?**  
**Answer:** Sore afraid.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:6

---

### Matthew 17:7

**Q16. Who came and touched them?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:7

**Q17. What did Jesus tell them to do?**  
**Answer:** Arise, and be not afraid.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:7

---

### Matthew 17:8

**Q18. When they had lifted up their eyes, whom did they see?**  
**Answer:** No man, save Jesus only.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:8

---

### Matthew 17:9

**Q19. As they came down from the mountain, what did Jesus charge them?**  
**Answer:** Tell the vision to no man, until the Son of man be risen again from the dead.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:9

---

### Matthew 17:10

**Q20. Who asked Jesus a question in this verse?**  
**Answer:** His disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:10

**Q21. What did the disciples ask Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Why then say the scribes that Elias must first come?  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:10

---

### Matthew 17:11

**Q22. Who answered and said unto them?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:11

**Q23. What does Elias truly first come and do?**  
**Answer:** Restore all things.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:11

---

### Matthew 17:12

**Q24. Did Elias come already?**  
**Answer:** Yes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:12

**Q25. Did they know him?**  
**Answer:** No.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:12

**Q26. What shall the Son of man suffer of them?**  
**Answer:** Likewise suffer of them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:12

---

### Matthew 17:13

**Q27. Who did the disciples understand Jesus spake unto them about?**  
**Answer:** John the Baptist.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:13

---

### Matthew 17:14

**Q28. To whom were they come when they were come to the multitude?**  
**Answer:** A certain man.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:14

**Q29. What did the man do to Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Kneeled down to him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:14

---

### Matthew 17:15

**Q30. What did the man ask Lord to have mercy on?**  
**Answer:** His son.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:15

**Q31. What is wrong with the man's son?**  
**Answer:** He is lunatick, and sore vexed.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:15

**Q32. Where does the son often fall?**  
**Answer:** Oftentimes he falleth into the fire, and oft into the water.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:15

---

### Matthew 17:16

**Q33. To whom did the father bring his son?**  
**Answer:** To thy disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:16

**Q34. What were the disciples unable to do?**  
**Answer:** Could not cure him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:16

---

### Matthew 17:17

**Q35. How did Jesus address the generation?**  
**Answer:** O faithless and perverse generation.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:17

**Q36. How long shall Jesus be with them and suffer them?**  
**Answer:** How long shall I be with you? how long shall I suffer you?  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:17

**Q37. What did Jesus command to bring unto Him?**  
**Answer:** Bring him hither to me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:17

---

### Matthew 17:18

**Q38. What did Jesus do to the devil?**  
**Answer:** He rebuked the devil.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:18

**Q39. What did the devil do upon being rebuked?**  
**Answer:** He departed out of him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:18

**Q40. When was the child cured?**  
**Answer:** From that very hour.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:18

---

### Matthew 17:19

**Q41. Who came to Jesus apart?**  
**Answer:** The disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:19

**Q42. What did the disciples ask Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Why could not we cast him out?  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:19

---

### Matthew 17:20

**Q43. Why did Jesus say the disciples could not cast him out?**  
**Answer:** Because of your unbelief.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:20

**Q44. How much faith did Jesus say is needed to move a mountain?**  
**Answer:** As a grain of mustard seed.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:20

**Q45. What did Jesus say shall be impossible unto them?**  
**Answer:** Nothing shall be impossible unto you.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:20

---

### Matthew 17:21

**Q46. How does this kind go out?**  
**Answer:** But by prayer and fasting.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:21

---

### Matthew 17:22

**Q47. While they abode in Galilee, what did Jesus say unto them?**  
**Answer:** The Son of man shall be betrayed into the hands of men.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:22

---

### Matthew 17:23

**Q48. What will they do to the Son of man?**  
**Answer:** They shall kill him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:23

**Q49. What will happen on the third day?**  
**Answer:** He shall be raised again.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:23

**Q50. How did the disciples feel upon hearing this?**  
**Answer:** They were exceeding sorry.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:23

---

### Matthew 17:24

**Q51. To what place did they come?**  
**Answer:** Capernaum.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:24

**Q52. Who received the tribute money?**  
**Answer:** They that received tribute money.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:24

**Q53. To whom did they that received tribute money come?**  
**Answer:** To Peter.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:24

---

### Matthew 17:25

**Q54. What did Peter say when asked if his master paid tribute?**  
**Answer:** Yes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:25

**Q55. Who prevented Peter when he entered into the house?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:25

**Q56. What did Jesus ask Peter about earthly kings?**  
**Answer:** Of whom do the kings of the earth take custom or tribute? of their own children, or of strangers?  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:25

---

### Matthew 17:26

**Q57. What did Peter say to strangers?**  
**Answer:** Of strangers.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:26

**Q58. What did Jesus say the children are?**  
**Answer:** Then are the children free.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:26

---

### Matthew 17:27

**Q59. Where did Jesus tell Peter to go lest they should offend them?**  
**Answer:** Go thou to the sea, and cast an hook.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:27

**Q60. What will Peter find in the first fish that comes up?**  
**Answer:** A piece of money.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:27

**Q61. What should Peter do with the money found in the fish?**  
**Answer:** Take that, and give unto them for me and thee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 17:27
