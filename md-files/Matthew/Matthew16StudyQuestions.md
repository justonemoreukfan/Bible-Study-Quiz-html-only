# Matthew 16 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 16:1

**Q1. Who came and tempted Jesus?**  
**Answer:** The Pharisees also with the Sadducees.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:1

**Q2. What did the Pharisees and Sadducees desire Jesus to show them?**  
**Answer:** A sign from heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:1

**Q3. Whom did the Pharisees and Sadducees desire to tempt?**  
**Answer:** Him (Jesus).  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:1

---

### Matthew 16:2

**Q1. How did Jesus answer and say unto them?**  
**Answer:** When it is evening, ye say, It will be fair weather: for the sky is red.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:2

---

### Matthew 16:3

**Q1. What did they say in the morning when the sky was red and lowering?**  
**Answer:** It will be foul weather to day.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:3

**Q2. What did Jesus say they know how to discern?**  
**Answer:** The face of the sky.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:3

**Q3. What could they not discern?**  
**Answer:** The signs of the times.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:3

---

### Matthew 16:4

**Q1. What kind of generation seeketh after a sign?**  
**Answer:** A wicked and adulterous generation.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:4

**Q2. What sign shall be given unto the generation?**  
**Answer:** The sign of the prophet Jonas.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:4

**Q3. What did Jesus do after speaking to them?**  
**Answer:** He left them, and departed.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:4

---

### Matthew 16:5

**Q1. When his disciples were come to the other side, what had they forgotten?**  
**Answer:** To take bread.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:5

---

### Matthew 16:6

**Q1. What did Jesus say unto his disciples?**  
**Answer:** Take heed and beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:6

---

### Matthew 16:7

**Q1. What did the disciples reason among themselves about?**  
**Answer:** It is because we have taken no bread.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:7

---

### Matthew 16:8

**Q1. What did Jesus perceive when he knew that?**  
**Answer:** O ye of little faith, why reason ye among yourselves, because ye have brought no bread?  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:8

---

### Matthew 16:9

**Q1. What did Jesus ask if they understood not yet?**  
**Answer:** Neither remember the five loaves of the five thousand, and how many baskets ye took up?  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:9

---

### Matthew 16:10

**Q1. What did Jesus ask about the seven loaves of the four thousand?**  
**Answer:** And how many baskets ye took up?  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:10

---

### Matthew 16:11

**Q1. What did Jesus ask how it was they did not understand?**  
**Answer:** That I spake it not to you concerning bread, that ye should beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees?  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:11

---

### Matthew 16:12

**Q1. What did they understand that he bade them not beware of?**  
**Answer:** Of the leaven of bread.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:12

**Q2. What did they understand that he bade them beware of?**  
**Answer:** Of the doctrine of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:12

---

### Matthew 16:13

**Q1. Into whose coasts did Jesus come?**  
**Answer:** The coasts of Caesarea Philippi.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:13

**Q2. Whom did Jesus ask his disciples?**  
**Answer:** Whom do men that I the Son of man am?  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:13

**Q3. How is Jesus referred to in the question to His disciples?**  
**Answer:** The Son of man.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:13

---

### Matthew 16:14

**Q1. Who did some say that Jesus was?**  
**Answer:** John the Baptist.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:14

**Q2. Who did others say that Jesus was?**  
**Answer:** Elias.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:14

**Q3. Who did still others say that Jesus was?**  
**Answer:** Jeremias, or one of the prophets.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:14

---

### Matthew 16:15

**Q1. Whom did Jesus ask the disciples that he was?**  
**Answer:** But whom say ye that I am?  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:15

---

### Matthew 16:16

**Q1. Who answered and said, Thou art the Christ?**  
**Answer:** Simon Peter.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:16

**Q2. What did Simon Peter call the Son of the living God?**  
**Answer:** The Christ.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:16

**Q3. Whose Son did Peter declare Jesus to be?**  
**Answer:** The Son of the living God.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:16

---

### Matthew 16:17

**Q1. What did Jesus answer and say unto Simon Peter?**  
**Answer:** Blessed art thou, Simon Bar-jona.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:17

**Q2. What did Jesus say had not revealed this unto Peter?**  
**Answer:** Flesh and blood.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:17

**Q3. Who did reveal it unto Peter?**  
**Answer:** My Father which is in heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:17

---

### Matthew 16:18

**Q1. Upon what rock did Jesus say he will build his church?**  
**Answer:** Upon this rock.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:18

**Q2. What shall not prevail against the church?**  
**Answer:** The gates of hell.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:18

**Q3. Who said, "I say also unto thee, That thou art Peter"?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:18

---

### Matthew 16:19

**Q1. What will Jesus give unto Peter?**  
**Answer:** The keys of the kingdom of heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:19

**Q2. What shall be bound in heaven regarding what Peter binds on earth?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever thou shalt bind on earth shall be bound in heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:19

**Q3. What shall be loosed in heaven regarding what Peter looses on earth?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever thou shalt loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:19

---

### Matthew 16:20

**Q1. What did Jesus charge his disciples?**  
**Answer:** That they should tell no man that he was Jesus the Christ.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:20

---

### Matthew 16:21

**Q1. From that time forth, what did Jesus begin to show unto his disciples?**  
**Answer:** How that he must go unto Jerusalem, and suffer many things of the elders and chief priests and scribes, and be killed, and be raised again the third day.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:21

---

### Matthew 16:22

**Q1. Who took Jesus and began to rebuke him?**  
**Answer:** Peter.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:22

**Q2. What did Peter say to Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Be it far from thee, Lord: this shall not be unto thee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:22

---

### Matthew 16:23

**Q1. What did Jesus turn and say unto Peter?**  
**Answer:** Get thee behind me, Satan.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:23

**Q2. What did Jesus say Peter was to him?**  
**Answer:** An offence.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:23

**Q3. What things did Peter savor not?**  
**Answer:** The things that be of God, but those that be of men.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:23

---

### Matthew 16:24

**Q1. What did Jesus say unto his disciples if any man will come after him?**  
**Answer:** Let him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:24

---

### Matthew 16:25

**Q1. What shall whosoever will save his life do?**  
**Answer:** Lose it.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:25

**Q2. What shall whosoever will lose his life for Jesus' sake do?**  
**Answer:** Find it.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:25

---

### Matthew 16:26

**Q1. What shall it profit a man if he gain the whole world?**  
**Answer:** And lose his own soul.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:26

**Q2. What shall a man give in exchange for his soul?**  
**Answer:** What shall a man give in exchange for his soul?  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:26

---

### Matthew 16:27

**Q1. In whose glory shall the Son of man come?**  
**Answer:** In the glory of his Father with his angels.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:27

**Q2. Then what shall the Son of man reward every man according to?**  
**Answer:** According to his works.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:27

---

### Matthew 16:28

**Q1. Who did Jesus say there be standing here, which shall not taste of death?**  
**Answer:** Some of them that stand here.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:28

**Q2. Until they see what coming in his kingdom?**  
**Answer:** The Son of man coming in his kingdom.  
**Verse:** Matthew 16:28
