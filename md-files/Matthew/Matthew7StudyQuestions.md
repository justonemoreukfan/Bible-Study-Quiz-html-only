# Matthew 7 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 7:1

**Q1. What command is given regarding others in the first part of the verse?**  
**Answer:** Judge not.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:1

**Q2. What is the stated consequence for the person who follows this command?**  
**Answer:** That ye be not judged.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:1

**Q3. Who is the implied subject being told not to judge?**  
**Answer:** Judge not.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:1

---

### Matthew 7:2

**Q1. What kind of judgment will be used when you judge?**  
**Answer:** With what judgment ye judge.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:2

**Q2. What will happen to you with the judgment you use?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall be judged.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:2

**Q3. What measure will be used to mete out to you again?**  
**Answer:** With what measure ye mete.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:2

---

### Matthew 7:3

**Q1. What object is beheld in thy brother's eye?**  
**Answer:** The mote.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:3

**Q2. What object is in thine own eye?**  
**Answer:** A beam.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:3

**Q3. What action does a person fail to consider regarding their own eye?**  
**Answer:** Considerest not the beam that is in thine own eye.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:3

---

### Matthew 7:4

**Q1. What question is asked about saying to thy brother?**  
**Answer:** How wilt thou say to thy brother?  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:4

**Q2. What action does the person offer to do for the brother?**  
**Answer:** Let me pull out the mote out of thine eye.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:4

**Q3. What is actually present in thine own eye while making this offer?**  
**Answer:** A beam is in thine own eye.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:4

---

### Matthew 7:5

**Q1. What title is given to the person in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Thou hypocrite.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:5

**Q2. What must first be cast out of thine own eye?**  
**Answer:** The beam out of thine own eye.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:5

**Q3. What will you then see clearly to do?**  
**Answer:** To cast out the mote out of thy brother's eye.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:5

---

### Matthew 7:6

**Q1. What should not be given to the dogs?**  
**Answer:** That which is holy.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:6

**Q2. What should not be cast before swine?**  
**Answer:** Your pearls.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:6

**Q3. What might the swine do after turning again?**  
**Answer:** Rend you.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:6

---

### Matthew 7:7

**Q1. What action is commanded first in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Ask.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:7

**Q2. What action is commanded regarding looking for something?**  
**Answer:** Seek.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:7

**Q3. What action is commanded regarding a barrier?**  
**Answer:** Knock.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:7

---

### Matthew 7:8

**Q1. What happens to everyone that asks?**  
**Answer:** Receiveth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:8

**Q2. What happens to him that seeks?**  
**Answer:** Findeth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:8

**Q3. What happens to him that knocks?**  
**Answer:** It shall be opened.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:8

---

### Matthew 7:9

**Q1. What man is mentioned in relation to his son?**  
**Answer:** What man is there of you.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:9

**Q2. What does the son ask the man for?**  
**Answer:** Bread.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:9

**Q3. What will the man give the son instead of bread?**  
**Answer:** A stone.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:9

---

### Matthew 7:10

**Q1. What does the son ask for in this verse?**  
**Answer:** A fish.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:10

**Q2. What will the man give the son instead of a fish?**  
**Answer:** A serpent.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:10

**Q3. Who is asking for the fish?**  
**Answer:** Or if he ask a fish.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:10

### Matthew 7:11

**Q1. What are the people addressed in this verse described as being?**  
**Answer:** Evil.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:11

**Q2. What do these people know how to give unto their children?**  
**Answer:** Good gifts.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:11

**Q3. Who shall give good things to them that ask him?**  
**Answer:** Your Father which is in heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:11

### Matthew 7:12

**Q1. What should men do to you?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever ye would that men should do to you.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:12

**Q2. What must you do even so to them?**  
**Answer:** Do ye even so to them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:12

**Q3. What represents the law and the prophets in this verse?**

**Verse:** Matthew 7:12

### Matthew 7:13

**Q1. Through which gate are men told to enter?**  
**Answer:** At the strait gate.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:13

**Q2. What is the path like that leads to destruction?**  
**Answer:** Broad is the way.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:13

**Q3. How many go in thereat?**  
**Answer:** Many there be which go in thereat.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:13

### Matthew 7:14

**Q1. What is the gate like that leads unto life?**  
**Answer:** Strait is the gate.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:14

**Q2. What is the way like that leads unto life?**  
**Answer:** Narrow is the way.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:14

**Q3. How many find the way that leads unto life?**  
**Answer:** Few there be that find it.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:14

### Matthew 7:15

**Q1. What kind of prophets should people beware of?**  
**Answer:** False prophets.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:15

**Q2. In what clothing do these prophets come to you?**  
**Answer:** In sheep's clothing.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:15

**Q3. What are they inwardly?**  
**Answer:** Inwardly they are ravening wolves.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:15

---

### Matthew 7:16

**Q1. By what will ye know these prophets?**  
**Answer:** By their fruits.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:16

**Q2. What do men gather from thorns?**  
**Answer:** Grapes.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:16

**Q3. What do men gather from thistles?**  
**Answer:** Figs.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:16

---

### Matthew 7:17

**Q1. What kind of tree brings forth good fruit?**  
**Answer:** Every good tree.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:17

**Q2. What kind of tree brings forth evil fruit?**  
**Answer:** A corrupt tree.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:17

**Q3. What kind of fruit does a corrupt tree bring forth?**  
**Answer:** Evil fruit.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:17

---

### Matthew 7:18

**Q1. What can a good tree not bring forth?**  
**Answer:** Evil fruit.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:18

**Q2. What can a corrupt tree not bring forth?**  
**Answer:** Good fruit.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:18

**Q3. What type of tree is unable to produce good fruit according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** A corrupt tree.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:18

---

### Matthew 7:19

**Q1. What happens to every tree that does not bring forth good fruit?**  
**Answer:** Is hewn down.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:19

**Q2. What happens to the tree after it is hewn down?**  
**Answer:** Cast into the fire.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:19

**Q3. What must a tree bring forth to avoid being cut down?**  
**Answer:** Good fruit.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:19

### Matthew 7:24

**Q1. What is likened unto a wise man?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:24

**Q2. Upon what did the wise man build his house?**  
**Answer:** Upon a rock.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:24

**Q3. Who is the house compared to in terms of wisdom?**  
**Answer:** A wise man, which built his house upon a rock.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:24

---

### Matthew 7:25

**Q1. What fell down upon the house?**  
**Answer:** The rain.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:25

**Q2. What came upon the house alongside the rain?**  
**Answer:** The floods came, and the winds blew.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:25

**Q3. Why did the house not fall?**  
**Answer:** For it was founded upon a rock.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:25

---

### Matthew 7:26

**Q1. What is likened unto a foolish man?**  
**Answer:** Every one that heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them not.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:26

**Q2. Upon what did the foolish man build his house?**  
**Answer:** Upon the sand.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:26

**Q3. What kind of man built his house upon the sand?**  
**Answer:** A foolish man.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:26

---

### Matthew 7:27

**Q1. What fell upon the house built on the sand?**  
**Answer:** The rain.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:27

**Q2. What beat upon that house?**  
**Answer:** The winds blew, and beat upon that house.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:27

**Q3. What was the result for the house?**  
**Answer:** It fell: and great was the fall of it.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:27

**Verse:** Matthew 7:28

**Q2. How were the people affected by his sayings?**  
**Answer:** The people were astonished at his doctrine.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:28

**Answer:** At his doctrine.  
**Verse:** Matthew 7:28
