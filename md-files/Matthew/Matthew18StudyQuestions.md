# Matthew 18 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Matthew 18:1

**Q1. Who came unto Jesus at the same time?**  
**Answer:** The disciples.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:1

**Q2. What question did the disciples ask Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Who is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven?  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:1

**Q3. When did the disciples come unto Jesus asking this question?**  
**Answer:** At the same time.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:1

---

### Matthew 18:2

**Q4. What did Jesus call unto him?**  
**Answer:** A little child.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:2

**Q5. Where did Jesus set the little child?**  
**Answer:** In the midst of them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:2

---

### Matthew 18:3

**Q6. What did Jesus say they must do except they be converted?**  
**Answer:** Become as little children.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:3

**Q7. What will happen if they are not converted and become as little children?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:3

---

### Matthew 18:4

**Q8. Who shall be greatest in the kingdom of heaven?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever therefore shall humble himself as this little child.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:4

---

### Matthew 18:5

**Q9. Who shall receive one such little child in Jesus' name?**  
**Answer:** Whoso shall receive one such little child in my name.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:5

**Q10. What does receiving one such little child in Jesus' name do?**  
**Answer:** Receiveth me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:5

---

### Matthew 18:6

**Q11. Who shall be offended by one of these little ones which believe in Jesus?**  
**Answer:** Whoso shall offend one of these little ones which believe in me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:6

**Q12. What would be better for someone who offends one of these little ones?**  
**Answer:** It were better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:6

**Q13. Where should he be drowned according to verse 6?**  
**Answer:** In the depth of the sea.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:6

---

### Matthew 18:7

**Q14. What does the world need to have because of offences?**  
**Answer:** Woe unto the world because of offences!  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:7

**Q15. Is it necessary that offences come?**  
**Answer:** For it must needs be that offences come.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:7

**Q16. What is said about the man by whom the offence cometh?**  
**Answer:** But woe to that man by whom the offence cometh!  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:7

---

### Matthew 18:8

**Q17. What body parts are mentioned as causing someone to offend in verse 8?**  
**Answer:** Thy hand or thy foot.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:8

**Q18. What should be done if your hand or foot offends you?**  
**Answer:** Cut them off, and cast them from thee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:8

**Q19. How is it better to enter into life rather than having two hands or two feet?**  
**Answer:** It is better for thee to enter into life halt or maimed.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:8

---

### Matthew 18:9

**Q20. What body part is mentioned as causing someone to offend in verse 9?**  
**Answer:** Thine eye.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:9

**Q21. What should be done if your eye offends you?**  
**Answer:** Pluck it out, and cast it from thee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:9

**Q22. Where will someone be cast if they enter with two eyes?**  
**Answer:** Into hell fire.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:9

---

### Matthew 18:10

**Q23. Who should you take heed that you do not despise?**  
**Answer:** One of these little ones.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:10

**Q24. Where are the angels of these little ones always beholding?**  
**Answer:** In heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:10

**Q25. Whose face do their angels always behold?**  
**Answer:** The face of my Father which is in heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:10

---

### Matthew 18:11

**Q26. Why is the Son of man come?**  
**Answer:** To save that which was lost.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:11

---

### Matthew 18:12

**Q27. How many sheep does a man have if a hundred go astray?**  
**Answer:** An hundred sheep.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:12

**Q28. What does the man leave on the mountains to go seek the stray sheep?**  
**Answer:** The ninety and nine.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:12

---

### Matthew 18:13

**Q29. What happens if the man finds the stray sheep?**  
**Answer:** He rejoiceth more of that sheep, than of the ninety and nine which went not astray.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:13

---

### Matthew 18:14

**Q30. Who is not willing that one of these little ones should perish?**  
**Answer:** Your Father which is in heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:14

---

### Matthew 18:15

**Q31. What should you do if your brother shall trespass against you?**  
**Answer:** Go and tell him his fault between thee and him alone.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:15

**Q32. What happens if your brother shall hear you?**  
**Answer:** Thou hast gained thy brother.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:15

---

### Matthew 18:16

**Q33. How many witnesses should you take with you if he will not hear you?**  
**Answer:** One or two more.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:16

**Q34. Why should you take one or two more witnesses?**  
**Answer:** That in the mouth of two or three witnesses every word may be established.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:16

---

### Matthew 18:17

**Q35. Whom should you tell if he shall neglect to hear them?**  
**Answer:** Tell it unto the church.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:17

**Q36. What should he be treated as if he neglect to hear the church?**  
**Answer:** Let him be unto thee as an heathen man and a publican.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:17

---

### Matthew 18:18

**Q37. What shall be bound in heaven according to verse 18?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever ye shall bind on earth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:18

**Q38. What shall be loosed in heaven according to verse 18?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever ye shall loose on earth.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:18

---

### Matthew 18:19

**Q39. How many on earth need to agree touching any thing they shall ask?**  
**Answer:** Two of you.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:19

**Q40. Who shall do it for them?**  
**Answer:** My Father which is in heaven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:19

---

### Matthew 18:20

**Q41. In whose name are two or three gathered together?**  
**Answer:** In my name.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:20

**Q42. Where is Jesus in the midst of them?**  
**Answer:** There am I in the midst of them.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:20

---

### Matthew 18:21

**Q43. Who came to Jesus and asked a question in verse 21?**  
**Answer:** Peter.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:21

**Q44. How many times did Peter ask if he should forgive his brother?**  
**Answer:** Till seven times?  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:21

---

### Matthew 18:22

**Q45. What did Jesus say to Peter regarding how many times to forgive?**  
**Answer:** Until seventy times seven.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:22

---

### Matthew 18:23

**Q46. What is the kingdom of heaven likened unto?**  
**Answer:** A certain king, which would take account of his servants.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:23

---

### Matthew 18:24

**Q47. Who was brought unto him when he had begun to reckon?**  
**Answer:** One which owed him ten thousand talents.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:24

---

### Matthew 18:25

**Q48. What did the lord command to be sold when the servant had not to pay?**  
**Answer:** His wife, and children, and all that he had.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:25

**Q49. Why were they to be sold?**  
**Answer:** And payment to be made.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:25

---

### Matthew 18:26

**Q50. What did the servant do when he fell down and worshipped him?**  
**Answer:** Lord, have patience with me, and I will pay thee all.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:26

---

### Matthew 18:27

**Q51. How did the lord of that servant feel when he was moved with compassion?**  
**Answer:** Moved with compassion.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:27

**Q52. What did the lord do for the servant?**  
**Answer:** Loosed him, and forgave him the debt.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:27

---

### Matthew 18:28

**Q53. Whom did the servant find after he went out?**  
**Answer:** One of his fellowservants, which owed him an hundred pence.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:28

**Q54. What did the servant do to his fellowservant?**  
**Answer:** Laid hands on him, and took him by the throat.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:28

**Q55. What demand did he make of his fellowservant?**  
**Answer:** Pay me that thou owest.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:28

---

### Matthew 18:29

**Q56. What did the fellowservant do at his feet?**  
**Answer:** Fell down at his feet, and besought him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:29

**Q57. What did the fellowservant say?**  
**Answer:** Have patience with me, and I will pay thee all.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:29

---

### Matthew 18:30

**Q58. What did the servant refuse to do?**  
**Answer:** Would not.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:30

**Q59. Where did he cast his fellowservant?**  
**Answer:** Into prison.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:30

**Q60. Until when did he cast him into prison?**  
**Answer:** Till he should pay the debt.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:30

---

### Matthew 18:31

**Q61. Who saw what was done and were very sorry?**  
**Answer:** His fellowservants.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:31

**Q62. To whom did the fellowservants come and tell all that was done?**  
**Answer:** Unto their lord.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:31

---

### Matthew 18:32

**Q63. What did his lord call him after he had called him?**  
**Answer:** O thou wicked servant.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:32

**Q64. Why did the lord forgive him his debt?**  
**Answer:** Because thou desiredst me.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:32

---

### Matthew 18:33

**Q65. What question did the lord ask the wicked servant about compassion?**  
**Answer:** Shouldest not thou also have had compassion on thy fellowservant?  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:33

**Q66. Who had pity on the wicked servant?**  
**Answer:** Even as I had pity on thee.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:33

---

### Matthew 18:34

**Q67. To whom was the wroth lord delivered the servant?**  
**Answer:** To the tormentors.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:34

**Q68. Until when was he delivered to the tormentors?**  
**Answer:** Till he should pay all that was due unto him.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:34

---

### Matthew 18:35

**Q69. What will my heavenly Father do likewise unto you?**  
**Answer:** If ye from your hearts forgive not every one his brother their trespasses.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:35

**Q70. From where must you forgive your brother?**  
**Answer:** From your hearts.  
**Verse:** Matthew 18:35
