# Song of Solomon 7 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Song of Solomon 7:1
**Q1. According to Song of Solomon 7:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** How beautiful are thy feet with shoes.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 7:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Song of Solomon 7:1?**  
**Answer:** O prince’s daughter! the joints of thy thighs are like jewels.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 7:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Song of Solomon 7:1?**  
**Answer:** the work of the hands of a cunning workman.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 7:1

### Song of Solomon 7:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Song of Solomon 7:2?**  
**Answer:** Thy navel is like a round goblet, which wanteth not liquor.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 7:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Song of Solomon 7:2?**  
**Answer:** thy belly is like an heap of wheat set about with lilies.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 7:2

**Q6. According to Song of Solomon 7:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Thy navel is like a round goblet, which wanteth not liquor: thy belly is like an heap of wheat set about with lilies.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 7:2
