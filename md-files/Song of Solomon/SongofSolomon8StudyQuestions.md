# Song of Solomon 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Song of Solomon 8:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Song of Solomon 8:1?**  
**Answer:** O that thou wert as my brother, that sucked the breasts of my mother! when I should find thee without, I would kiss thee.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 8:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Song of Solomon 8:1?**  
**Answer:** yea, I should not be despised.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 8:1

**Q3. According to Song of Solomon 8:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** O that thou wert as my brother, that sucked the breasts of my mother! when I should find thee without, I would kiss thee; yea, I should not be despised.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 8:1
