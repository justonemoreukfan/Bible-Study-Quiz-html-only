# Song of Solomon 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Song of Solomon 3:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Song of Solomon 3:1?**  
**Answer:** By night on my bed I sought him whom my soul loveth.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 3:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Song of Solomon 3:1?**  
**Answer:** I sought him, but I found him not.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 3:1

**Q3. According to Song of Solomon 3:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** By night on my bed I sought him whom my soul loveth: I sought him, but I found him not.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 3:1
