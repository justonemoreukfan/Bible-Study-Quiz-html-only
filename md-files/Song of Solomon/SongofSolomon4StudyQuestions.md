# Song of Solomon 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Song of Solomon 4:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Song of Solomon 4:1?**  
**Answer:** Behold, thou art fair, my love.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 4:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Song of Solomon 4:1?**  
**Answer:** behold, thou art fair.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 4:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Song of Solomon 4:1?**  
**Answer:** thou hast doves’ eyes within thy locks; thy hair is as a flock of goats, that appear from mount Gilead.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 4:1
