# Song of Solomon 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Song of Solomon 6:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Song of Solomon 6:1 state?**  
**Answer:** Whither is thy beloved gone.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 6:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Song of Solomon 6:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** O thou fairest among women? whither is thy beloved turned aside? that we may seek him with thee.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 6:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Song of Solomon 6:1?**  
**Answer:** Whither is thy beloved gone, O thou fairest among women? whither is thy beloved turned aside? that we may seek him with thee.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 6:1
