### Song of Solomon 2:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Song of Solomon 2:1 state?**  
**Answer:** I am the rose of Sharon.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 2:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Song of Solomon 2:1 declare?**

**Verse:** Song of Solomon 2:1
