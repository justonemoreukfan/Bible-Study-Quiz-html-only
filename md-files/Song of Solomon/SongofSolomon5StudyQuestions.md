# Song of Solomon 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Song of Solomon 5:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Song of Solomon 5:1?**  
**Answer:** I am come into my garden, my sister, my spouse.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 5:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Song of Solomon 5:1?**  
**Answer:** I have gathered my myrrh with my spice.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 5:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Song of Solomon 5:1?**  
**Answer:** I have eaten my honeycomb with my honey; I have drunk my wine with my milk; eat, O friends; drink, yea, drink abundantly, O beloved.  
**Verse:** Song of Solomon 5:1
