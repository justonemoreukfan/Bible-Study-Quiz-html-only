# 1 Samuel 19 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 19:1

**Q1. To whom did Saul speak regarding killing David?**  
**Answer:** Jonathan his son, and to all his servants.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 19:1

**Q2. How did Saul feel about David according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Saul's son Jonathan delighted much in David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 19:1

**Q3. What did Saul tell his son and servants that David should do?**  
**Answer:** That they should kill David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 19:1

---

### 1 Samuel 19:2

**Q4. Who told David about Saul's intention to kill him?**  
**Answer:** Jonathan Saul's son.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 19:2

**Q5. Where did Jonathan tell David to take heed?**  
**Answer:** Until the morning.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 19:2

**Q6. Where did Jonathan tell David to abide secretly and hide himself?**  
**Answer:** In a secret place.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 19:2

---

### 1 Samuel 19:3

**Q7. With whom did Jonathan say he would go out and stand?**  
**Answer:** His father.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 19:3

**Q8. Where did Jonathan say he would stand with his father?**  
**Answer:** In the field where David is.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 19:3

**Q9. What did Jonathan say he would do with his father regarding David?**  
**Answer:** Commune with his father of David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 19:3

---

### 1 Samuel 19:4

**Q10. What did Jonathan speak of David to Saul his father?**  
**Answer:** Good things.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 19:4

**Q11. What did Jonathan say the king should not do against his servant David?**  
**Answer:** Sin against his servant.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 19:4

**Q12. Against whom did Jonathan say David has not sinned?**  
**Answer:** Against thee (Saul).  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 19:4
