# 1 Samuel 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 1:1

**Q1. What was the name of the man from Ramathaimzophim?**  
**Answer:** Elkanah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:1

**Q2. Of what mountain and country was Elkanah's father?**  
**Answer:** Mount Ephraim.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:1

**Q3. What was the name of Elkanah's great-great-grandfather mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Zuph.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:1

---

### 1 Samuel 1:2

**Q1. How many wives did Elkanah have?**  
**Answer:** Two.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:2

**Q2. What was the name of the first wife?**  
**Answer:** Hannah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:2

**Q3. What was the name of the second wife?**  
**Answer:** Peninnah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:2

---

### 1 Samuel 1:3

**Q1. From where did this man go up out of his city yearly?**  
**Answer:** Yearly.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:3

**Q2. To whom did he go up to worship and sacrifice?**  
**Answer:** The LORD of hosts in Shiloh.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:3

**Q3. Who were the two sons of Eli that were the priests of the LORD there?**  
**Answer:** Hophni and Phinehas.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:3

---

### 1 Samuel 1:4

**Q1. When Elkanah offered sacrifice, what did he give to Peninnah his wife?**  
**Answer:** Portions.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:4

**Q2. What did he also give to all her sons and daughters?**  
**Answer:** Portions.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:4

**Q3. Who else did he give a worthy portion unto?**  
**Answer:** Hannah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:4

---

### 1 Samuel 1:5

**Q1. Whom did Elkanah love?**  
**Answer:** Hannah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:5

**Q2. Who had shut up Hannah's womb?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:5

**Q3. Why did he give Hannah a worthy portion?**  
**Answer:** Because he loved Hannah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:5

---

### 1 Samuel 1:6

**Q1. Who also provoked Hannah sore?**  
**Answer:** Her adversary.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:6

**Q2. For what purpose did her adversary provoke her?**  
**Answer:** For to make her fret.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:6

**Q3. Why was Hannah provoked in this manner?**  
**Answer:** Because the LORD had shut up her womb.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:6

---

### 1 Samuel 1:7

**Q1. How often did Peninnah provoke Hannah?**  
**Answer:** Year by year.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:7

**Q2. What happened when Hannah went up to the house of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** She provoked her.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:7

**Q3. What was Hannah's reaction to this provocation?**  
**Answer:** She wept, and did not eat.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:7

---

### 1 Samuel 1:8

**Q1. Who spoke to Hannah and asked why she wept?**  
**Answer:** Elkanah her husband.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:8

**Q2. What question did Elkanah ask Hannah about eating?**  
**Answer:** Why eatest thou not?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:8

**Q3. What did Elkanah say he was better to her than?**  
**Answer:** Ten sons.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:8

---

### 1 Samuel 1:9

**Q1. Where did Hannah rise up after they had eaten and drank in Shiloh?**  
**Answer:** After they had eaten in Shiloh, and after they had drunk.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:9

**Q2. Who sat upon a seat by a post of the temple of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Eli the priest.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:9

**Q3. What did Hannah do after eating and drinking?**  
**Answer:** She rose up.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:9

---

### 1 Samuel 1:10

**Q1. In what state of mind was Hannah as she prayed unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** In bitterness of soul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:10

**Q2. To whom did Hannah pray?**  
**Answer:** Unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:10

**Q3. What bodily action accompanied her prayer?**  
**Answer:** Wept sore.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:10

---

### 1 Samuel 1:11

**Q1. What did Hannah vow and ask the LORD of hosts to look on?**  
**Answer:** The affliction of his handmaid.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:11

**Q2. What did she ask the LORD to remember?**  
**Answer:** Remember me.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:11

**Q3. What did she promise to give unto the LORD all the days of his life?**  
**Answer:** A man child.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:11

---

### 1 Samuel 1:12

**Q1. Who continued praying long before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Hannah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:12

**Q2. Who marked Hannah's mouth?**  
**Answer:** Eli.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:12

**Q3. For how long did she continue praying?**  
**Answer:** Long.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:12

---

### 1 Samuel 1:13

**Q1. How did Hannah speak in her heart?**  
**Answer:** She spake in her heart.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:13

**Q2. What part of her body only moved?**  
**Answer:** Her lips.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:13

**Q3. What did Eli think Hannah was?**  
**Answer:** Drunken.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:13

---

### 1 Samuel 1:14

**Q1. What question did Eli ask Hannah regarding her wine?**  
**Answer:** How long wilt thou be drunken?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:14

**Q2. What did Eli tell Hannah to put away from her?**  
**Answer:** Thy wine.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:14

**Q3. Whose instruction was this?**  
**Answer:** Eli's.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:14

---

### 1 Samuel 1:15

**Q1. How did Hannah answer Eli?**  
**Answer:** No, my lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:15

**Q2. What kind of woman did Hannah say she was?**  
**Answer:** A woman of a sorrowful spirit.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:15

**Q3. What had she drunk neither of?**  
**Answer:** Wine nor strong drink.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:15

---

### 1 Samuel 1:16

**Q1. What did Hannah tell Eli not to count his handmaid for?**  
**Answer:** A daughter of Belial.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:16

**Q2. Out of the abundance of what did she speak?**  
**Answer:** Complaint and grief.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:16

**Q3. Why had she spoken until now?**  
**Answer:** Out of the abundance of my complaint and grief have I spoken hitherto.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:16

---

### 1 Samuel 1:17

**Q1. Who answered and said, Go in peace?**  
**Answer:** Eli.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:17

**Q2. What did Eli say the God of Israel grant her?**  
**Answer:** Thy petition.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:17

**Q3. What petition was to be granted?**  
**Answer:** That she had asked of him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:17

**Q2. What did the woman go her way and do?**

**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:18

### 1 Samuel 1:22

**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:22

**Answer:** That he may appear before the LORD, and there abide for ever.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:22

**Answer:** There abide for ever.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:22

### 1 Samuel 1:23

**Q1. What did Elkanah her husband say unto her regarding her plan?**  
**Answer:** Do what seemeth thee good.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:23

**Q2. Until when did Elkanah tell her to tarry?**  
**Answer:** Until ye have weaned him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:23

**Q3. What did the woman do and what did she feed her son with?**  
**Answer:** She abode, and gave her son suck until she weaned him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 1:23
