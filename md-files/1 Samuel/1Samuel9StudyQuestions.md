# 1 Samuel 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 9:1

**Q1. What was the name of the man of Benjamin described in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Kish.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:1

**Q2. Whose son was Kish?**  
**Answer:** Abiel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:1

**Q3. What was Kish's father's father's name?**  
**Answer:** Zeror.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:1

---

### 1 Samuel 9:2

**Q1. What was the name of Kish's son?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:2

**Q2. How was Saul described in terms of his appearance?**  
**Answer:** A choice young man, and a goodly.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:2

**Q3. Among the children of Israel, how tall was Saul compared to anyone else?**  
**Answer:** From his shoulders and upward he was higher than any of the people.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:2

---

### 1 Samuel 9:3

**Q1. Whose asses were lost?**  
**Answer:** Kish's asses.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:3

**Q2. What did Kish say to his son Saul concerning the asses?**  
**Answer:** Take now one of the servants with thee, and arise, go seek the asses.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:3

**Q3. Who was told to go with Saul to seek the asses?**  
**Answer:** One of the servants.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:3

---

### 1 Samuel 9:4

**Q1. What region did they pass through first in search of the asses?**  
**Answer:** Mount Ephraim.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:4

**Q2. After passing through the land of Shalisha, where did they go next?**  
**Answer:** The land of Shalim.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:4

**Q3. In whose land did they search where the asses were not found?**  
**Answer:** The land of the Benjamites.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:4

---

### 1 Samuel 9:5

**Q1. When they were come to the land of Zuph, what did Saul say to his servant?**  
**Answer:** Come, and let us return; lest my father leave caring for the asses, and take thought for us.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:5

**Q2. What did Saul fear his father might take besides caring for the asses?**  
**Answer:** Thought for us.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:5

**Q3. Where were they when Saul suggested returning?**  
**Answer:** The land of Zuph.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:5

---

### 1 Samuel 9:6

**Q1. What did the servant say was in this city?**  
**Answer:** A man of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:6

**Q2. How was the man of God described regarding his reputation?**  
**Answer:** An honourable man; all that he saith cometh surely to pass.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:6

**Q3. What did the servant suggest they do now?**  
**Answer:** Go thither; peradventure he can shew us our way that we should go.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:6

---

### 1 Samuel 9:7

**Q1. What did Saul ask the servant if they went?**  
**Answer:** But, behold, if we go, what shall we bring the man?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:7

**Q2. What was gone out of their vessels?**  
**Answer:** The bread.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:7

**Q3. What was there not a present to bring to the man of God?**  
**Answer:** Any present to bring to the man of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:7

---

### 1 Samuel 9:8

**Q1. How did the servant answer Saul a second time?**  
**Answer:** Behold, I have here at hand the fourth part of a shekel of silver.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:8

**Q2. What would this silver be given to the man of God for?**  
**Answer:** To tell us our way.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:8

**Q3. How much silver did the servant have at hand?**  
**Answer:** The fourth part of a shekel of silver.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:8

---

### 1 Samuel 9:9

**Q1. What was the former custom in Israel when a man went to enquire of God?**  
**Answer:** Come, and let us go to the seer.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:9

**Q2. What name was given in earlier times to a prophet?**  
**Answer:** A Seer.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:9

**Q3. What is the person called now who was formerly called a Seer?**  
**Answer:** A Prophet.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:9

**Answer:** Come, let us go.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:10

### 1 Samuel 9:11

**Q1. As they went up the hill to the city, who did they find?**

**Q2. What were the young maidens doing?**  
**Answer:** Going out to draw water.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:11

**Q3. Where were the maidens going to draw water?**  
**Answer:** To the city.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:11

---

### 1 Samuel 9:12

**Q1. What question did Saul and his servant ask the maidens?**  
**Answer:** Is the seer here?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:12

**Q2. How did the maidens answer them?**  
**Answer:** He is; behold, he is before you.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:12

**Q3. What reason did the maidens give for hastening now?**  
**Answer:** For he came today to the city; for there is a sacrifice of the people today in the high place.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:12

---

### 1 Samuel 9:13

**Q1. When ye come into the city, where shall ye straightway find him?**  
**Answer:** Before he go up to the high place to eat.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:13

**Q2. Why will the people not eat until the seer comes?**  
**Answer:** Because he doth bless the sacrifice.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:13

**Q3. What happens after the seer blesses the sacrifice?**  
**Answer:** They eat that be bidden.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 9:13
