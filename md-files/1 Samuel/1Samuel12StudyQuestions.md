# 1 Samuel 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 12:1

**Q1. To whom did Samuel say he had hearkened in all that they said unto him?**  
**Answer:** All Israel  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 12:1

**Q2. What did Samuel say he had made over the people?**  
**Answer:** A king  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 12:1

**Q3. According to Samuel, who was walking before the people?**  
**Answer:** The king  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 12:1

---

### 1 Samuel 12:2

**Q1. Who is described as walking before the people from childhood unto this day?**  
**Answer:** Samuel  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 12:2

**Q2. Whose age and gray hairs were present before the people?**  
**Answer:** Samuel's  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 12:2

**Q3. Who are described as being with the people?**  
**Answer:** Samuel's sons  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 12:2

---

### 1 Samuel 12:3

**Q1. Before whose anointed did Samuel ask them to bear witness against him?**  
**Answer:** The Lord's and his anointed  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 12:3

**Q2. Whose ox or ass did Samuel ask if he had taken?**  
**Answer:** Whose ox have I taken? or whose ass have I taken?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 12:3

**Q3. From whose hand did Samuel ask if he had received any bribe to blind his eyes therewith?**  
**Answer:** Any man's hand  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 12:3

---

### 1 Samuel 12:4

**Q1. What did the people say Samuel had not done to them?**  
**Answer:** Defrauded them, oppressed them, or taken ought of any man's hand  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 12:4

**Q2. Did Samuel have anything of theirs?**  
**Answer:** No  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 12:4

**Q3. What did the people say Samuel had not defrauded them of?**  
**Answer:** Ought  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 12:4
