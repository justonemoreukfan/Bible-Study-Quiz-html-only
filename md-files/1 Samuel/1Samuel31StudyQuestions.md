# 1 Samuel 31 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 31:1

**Q1. Who fought against Israel in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:1

**Q2. What happened to the men of Israel before the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** They fled.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:1

**Q3. Where did many men of Israel fall down slain?**  
**Answer:** In mount Gilboa.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:1

---

### 1 Samuel 31:2

**Q1. Whom did the Philistines follow hard upon?**  
**Answer:** Saul and his sons.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:2

**Q2. Which sons of Saul did the Philistines slay?**  
**Answer:** Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:2

---

### 1 Samuel 31:3

**Q1. Against whom went the battle sore?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:3

**Q2. Who overtook Saul?**  
**Answer:** The archers.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:3

**Q3. How was Saul affected by the archers?**  
**Answer:** He was sore wounded of the archers.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:3

---

### 1 Samuel 31:4

**Q1. What did Saul say to his armourbearer?**  
**Answer:** Draw thy sword, and thrust me through therewith.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:4

**Q2. Why did Saul want his armourbearer to thrust him through?**  
**Answer:** Lest these uncircumcised come and thrust me through, and abuse me.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:4

**Q3. What did the armourbearer do regarding Saul's request?**  
**Answer:** He would not; for he was sore afraid.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:4

---

### 1 Samuel 31:5

**Q1. What did Saul do when his armourbearer would not do it?**  
**Answer:** Saul took a sword, and fell upon it.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:5

---

### 1 Samuel 31:6

**Q1. Who died together with Saul on that mountain?**  
**Answer:** His armourbearer.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:6

**Q2. Who else died along with Saul and his armourbearer on that same day?**  
**Answer:** His three sons, and all his men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:6

---

### 1 Samuel 31:7

**Q1. Who saw that the men of Israel were fled and that Saul and his sons were dead?**  
**Answer:** The men of Israel that were on the other side of the valley, and they that were on the other side Jordan.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:7

**Q2. What did the men of Israel do when they saw these things?**  
**Answer:** They forsook the cities, and fled.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:7

**Q3. Who came and dwelt in the cities abandoned by Israel?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:7

---

### 1 Samuel 31:8

**Q1. What did the Philistines do on the morrow?**  
**Answer:** They came to strip the slain.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:8

**Q2. Whom did the Philistines find fallen in mount Gilboa?**  
**Answer:** Saul and his three sons.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:8

---

### 1 Samuel 31:9

**Q1. What did the Philistines cut off from Saul?**  
**Answer:** His head.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:9

**Q2. What did the Philistines strip off Saul?**  
**Answer:** His armour.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:9

**Q3. Where did the Philistines send word throughout the land of the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** Into the land of the Philistines round about, to publish it in the house of their idols, and among the people.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:9

---

### 1 Samuel 31:10

**Q1. Where did the Philistines put Saul's armour?**  
**Answer:** In the house of Ashtaroth.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:10

**Q2. Where did the Philistines fasten Saul's body?**  
**Answer:** To the wall of Bethshan.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:10

---

### 1 Samuel 31:11

**Q1. Who heard what the Philistines had done to Saul?**  
**Answer:** The inhabitants of Jabeshgilead.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:11

---

### 1 Samuel 31:12

**Q1. Who arose and went all night?**  
**Answer:** All the valiant men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:12

**Q2. What did the valiant men take down from the wall of Bethshan?**  
**Answer:** The body of Saul and the bodies of his sons.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:12

**Q3. To where did the valiant men bring the bodies after taking them down?**  
**Answer:** To Jabesh.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:12

---

### 1 Samuel 31:13

**Q1. What did the men of Jabesh take?**  
**Answer:** Their bones.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:13

**Q2. Where did they bury the bones?**  
**Answer:** Under a tree at Jabesh.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:13

**Q3. How long did the men of Jabesh fast after burying the bones?**  
**Answer:** Seven days.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 31:13
