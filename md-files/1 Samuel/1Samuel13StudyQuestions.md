# 1 Samuel 13 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 13:1

**Q1. How old was Saul when he began to reign?**  
**Answer:** Saul reigned one year.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:1

**Q2. For how long did Saul reign over Israel?**  
**Answer:** Two years.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:1

**Q3. Whose age is mentioned regarding when he reigned?**  
**Answer:** Saul's age when he began to reign.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:1

---

### 1 Samuel 13:2

**Q1. How many men of Israel did Saul choose for himself?**  
**Answer:** Three thousand men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:2

**Q2. How many men were with Saul in Michmash and in mount Bethel?**  
**Answer:** Two thousand.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:2

**Q3. How many men were with Jonathan in Gibeah of Benjamin?**  
**Answer:** A thousand men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:2

---

### 1 Samuel 13:3

**Q1. Who smote the garrison of the Philistines that was in Geba?**  
**Answer:** Jonathan.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:3

**Q2. Who heard about the smiting of the garrison?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:3

**Q3. What did Saul blow throughout all the land?**  
**Answer:** The trumpet.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:3

---

### 1 Samuel 13:4

**Q1. What did all Israel hear that Saul had done?**  
**Answer:** That Saul had smitten a garrison of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:4

**Q2. What did Israel also hear that happened with the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** That Israel was had in abomination with the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:4

**Q3. Where were the people called together after Saul?**  
**Answer:** Together to Gilgal.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:4

---

### 1 Samuel 13:5

**Q1. Who gathered themselves together to fight with Israel?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:5

**Q2. How many chariots did the Philistines have?**  
**Answer:** Thirty thousand.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:5

**Q3. Where did the Philistines pitch?**  
**Answer:** In Michmash, eastward from Beth-haven.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:5

---

### 1 Samuel 13:6

**Q1. What did the men of Israel see when they were in a strait?**  
**Answer:** That they were distressed.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:6

**Q2. Where did the people hide themselves?**  
**Answer:** In caves, and in thickets, and in rocks, and in high places, and in pits.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:6

**Q3. Who hid themselves?**  
**Answer:** The people of Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:6

---

### 1 Samuel 13:7

**Q1. Who went over Jordan to the land of Gad and Gilead?**  
**Answer:** Some of the Hebrews.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:7

**Q2. Where was Saul while the people fled?**  
**Answer:** In Gilgal.  
**Verse:** 1Samuel 13:7

**Q3. In what state were all the people that followed Saul?**  
**Answer:** They trembled.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:7

---

### 1 Samuel 13:8

**Q1. How many days did Saul wait, according to the set time that Samuel had appointed?**  
**Answer:** Seven days.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:8

**Q2. Who had not come to Gilgal?**  
**Answer:** Samuel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:8

**Q3. What did the people do from Saul?**  
**Answer:** They were scattered from him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:8

---

### 1 Samuel 13:9

**Q1. What did Saul tell them to bring hither to him?**  
**Answer:** A burnt offering and peace offerings.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:9

**Q2. Who offered the burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:9

**Q3. Where did Saul offer the burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** In Gilgal.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:9

---

### 1 Samuel 13:10

**Q1. When was it that Samuel came?**  
**Answer:** As soon as he had made an end of offering the burnt offering.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:10

**Q2. Who went out to meet Samuel?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:10

**Q3. Why did Saul go out to meet Samuel?**  
**Answer:** That he might salute him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:10

---

### 1 Samuel 13:11

**Q1. Who asked, "What hast thou done?"**  
**Answer:** Samuel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:11

**Q2. What did Saul see that made him justify his action?**  
**Answer:** That the people were scattered from him, and that Samuel came not within the days appointed, and that the Philistines gathered themselves together at Michmash.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:11

**Q3. Who said that the people were scattered from him?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:11

---

### 1 Samuel 13:12

**Q1. What did Saul fear the Philistines would come down upon?**  
**Answer:** Upon him to Gilgal.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:12

**Q2. What had Saul not made supplication unto?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:12

**Q3. What did Saul say he forced himself to do?**  
**Answer:** Offered a burnt offering.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:12

---

### 1 Samuel 13:13

**Q1. Who told Saul, "Thou hast done foolishly"?**  
**Answer:** Samuel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:13

**Q2. What commandment of the Lord thy God had Saul not kept?**  
**Answer:** The commandment which he commanded him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:13

**Q3. What would the Lord have established upon Israel for ever had Saul obeyed?**  
**Answer:** His kingdom.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:13

---

### 1 Samuel 13:14

**Q1. Whose kingdom shall not continue?**  
**Answer:** Saul's kingdom.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:14

**Q2. Whom has the Lord sought for himself?**  
**Answer:** A man after his own heart.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:14

**Q3. What has the Lord commanded the man after his own heart to be?**  
**Answer:** Captain over his people.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:14

---

### 1 Samuel 13:15

**Q1. Where did Saul arise and get him up from?**  
**Answer:** From Gilgal unto Gibeah of Benjamin.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:15

**Q2. Who numbered the people that were present with him?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:15

**Q3. How many people were present with Saul?**  
**Answer:** About six hundred men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:15

---

### 1 Samuel 13:16

**Q1. Who, along with Saul and Jonathan his son, abode in Gibeah of Benjamin?**  
**Answer:** The people that were present with them.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:16

**Q2. Where did the people that were present with Saul and Jonathan abide?**  
**Answer:** In Gibeah of Benjamin.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:16

**Q3. Who abode in Michmash?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:16

---

### 1 Samuel 13:17

**Q1. Who went out of the camp of the Philistines in three companies?**  
**Answer:** The spoilers.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:17

**Q2. In how many companies did the spoilers go out?**  
**Answer:** Three companies.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:17

**Q3. Toward what land did one company turn?**  
**Answer:** Unto the way of Ophrah, unto the land of Shual.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:17

---

### 1 Samuel 13:18

**Q1. Toward what place did another company turn?**  
**Answer:** The way of Beth-horon.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:18

**Q2. Toward what border did the other company turn?**  
**Answer:** The border that looketh to the valley of Zeboim toward the wilderness.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:18

**Q3. Who turned toward these directions?**  
**Answer:** The companies of spoilers.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:18

---

### 1 Samuel 13:19

**Q1. What was there none found throughout all the land of Israel?**  
**Answer:** A smith.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:19

**Q2. Why did the Philistines say the Hebrews must not make for themselves?**  
**Answer:** Swords or spears.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:19

**Q3. Who said that the Hebrews must not make swords or spears?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:19

---

### 1 Samuel 13:20

**Q1. To whom did all the Israelites go down?**  
**Answer:** To the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:20

**Q2. What tools did each Israelite go down to sharpen?**  
**Answer:** His share, and his coulter, and his axe, and his mattock.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:20

**Q3. Whose share and coulter were sharpened?**  
**Answer:** Every man's share, and coulter, and fork, and axe.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:20

---

### 1 Samuel 13:21

**Q1. What was the charge for sharpening mattocks, coulters, forks, and axes?**  
**Answer:** A pim.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:21

**Q2. What tools had a file charge of six shekels?**  
**Answer:** The goads.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:21

**Q3. For what were the goads sharpened?**  
**Answer:** For the forks.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:21

---

### 1 Samuel 13:22

**Q1. What was not found in the hand of any people with Saul and Jonathan?**  
**Answer:** Either sword or spear.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:22

**Q2. Who alone had a sword and a spear?**  
**Answer:** Saul and Jonathan his son.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:22

**Q3. In whose hand was a sword and spear found?**  
**Answer:** Saul and Jonathan his son.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:22

---

**Answer:** The passage of Michmash.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 13:23

**Q3. Whose garrison went out to the passage of Michmash?**  
**Answer:** The garrison of the Philistines.
