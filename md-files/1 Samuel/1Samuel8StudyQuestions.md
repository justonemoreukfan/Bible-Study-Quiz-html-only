# 1 Samuel 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 8:1

**Q1. Who was old when he made his sons judges over Israel?**  
**Answer:** Samuel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 8:1

**Q2. What did Samuel make his sons?**  
**Answer:** Judges over Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 8:1

**Q3. What was the name of Samuel's firstborn?**  
**Answer:** Joel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 8:1

---

### 1 Samuel 8:2

**Q1. What was the name of Samuel's firstborn son?**  
**Answer:** Vashni.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 8:2

**Q2. What was the name of Samuel's second son?**  
**Answer:** Abiah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 8:2

**Q3. Where did Samuel's sons judge?**  
**Answer:** In Beersheba.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 8:2
