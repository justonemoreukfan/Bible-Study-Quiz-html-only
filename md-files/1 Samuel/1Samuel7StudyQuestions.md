# 1 Samuel 7 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 7:1

**Q1. Who came and fetched the ark of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The men of Kirjathjearim.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:1

**Q2. In whose house did they sanctify Eleazar his son to keep the ark of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** In the house of Abinadab in the hill.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:1

**Q3. What did the men of Kirjathjearim do with the ark of the LORD when they brought it into the house?**  
**Answer:** They sanctified Eleazar his son to keep it.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:1

---

### 1 Samuel 7:2

**Q1. How long did the ark abide in Kirjathjearim?**  
**Answer:** A long time; namely, twenty years.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:2

**Q2. Who lamented after the LORD during the time the ark abode in Kirjathjearim?**  
**Answer:** All the house of Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:2

**Q3. What did all the house of Israel do after the LORD during this period?**  
**Answer:** They lamented.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:2

---

### 1 Samuel 7:3

**Q1. Who spake unto all the house of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Samuel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:3

**Q2. With what kind of heart were the Israelites to return unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** With all their hearts.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:3

**Q3. What two groups of foreign objects were the Israelites told to put away from among them?**  
**Answer:** The strange gods and Ashtaroth.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:3

---

### 1 Samuel 7:4

**Q1. What specific idols did the children of Israel put away?**  
**Answer:** Baalim and Ashtaroth.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:4

**Q2. Whom did the children of Israel serve only, after putting away Baalim and Ashtaroth?**  
**Answer:** The LORD only.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:4

**Q3. Where did the children of Israel put away Baalim and Ashtaroth?**  
**Answer:** From among them.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:4

---

### 1 Samuel 7:5

**Q1. Where did Samuel tell all Israel to gather?**  
**Answer:** To Mizpeh.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:5

**Q2. What did Samuel promise to do for the children of Israel at Mizpeh?**  
**Answer:** Pray for them unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:5

**Q3. Who was to be gathered to Mizpeh besides "all Israel"?**  
**Answer:** Samuel said, "I will pray for you unto the LORD." (Implies gathering Israel together).  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:5

---

### 1 Samuel 7:6

**Q1. Where did they gather together and draw water?**  
**Answer:** To Mizpeh.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:6

**Q2. What did they pour out before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Water.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:6

**Q3. What did the people confess at Mizpeh?**  
**Answer:** We have sinned against the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:6

---

### 1 Samuel 7:7

**Q1. Who heard that the children of Israel were gathered together to Mizpeh?**  
**Answer:** The lords of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:7

**Q2. What did the lords of the Philistines do when they heard Israel was gathered at Mizpeh?**  
**Answer:** They went up against Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:7

**Q3. How did the children of Israel feel when they heard that the Philistines were coming?**  
**Answer:** They were afraid of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:7

---

### 1 Samuel 7:8

**Q1. What did the children of Israel say to Samuel regarding the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** "Cease not to cry unto the LORD our God for us, that he will save us out of the hand of the Philistines."  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:8

**Q2. Whose hand did Israel want to be saved out of?**  
**Answer:** The hand of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:8

**Q3. Whom did the Israelites ask Samuel to cry unto?**  
**Answer:** The LORD our God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:8

---

### 1 Samuel 7:9

**Q1. What kind of lamb did Samuel take for a burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** A sucking lamb.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:9

**Q2. To whom did Samuel offer the sucking lamb as a whole burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** Unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:9

**Q3. What did Samuel do after offering the lamb and crying unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The LORD heard him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:9

---

### 1 Samuel 7:10

**Q1. What was Samuel doing as the Philistines drew near to battle against Israel?**  
**Answer:** Samuel was offering up the burnt offering.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:10

**Q2. How did the LORD answer the Philistines upon Israel?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thundered with a great thunder on that day upon the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:10

**Q3. What happened to the Philistines when the LORD thundered against them?**  
**Answer:** They were discomfited, and were smitten before Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:10

---

### 1 Samuel 7:11

**Q1. From what city did the men of Israel go out?**  
**Answer:** Mizpeh.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:11

**Q2. Whom did the men of Israel pursue?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:11

**Q3. Where did the men of Israel smite the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** Unto a place under Bethcar.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:11

---

### 1 Samuel 7:12

**Q1. What did Samuel take and set up between Mizpeh and Shen?**  
**Answer:** A stone.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:12

**Q2. What name did Samuel give to the stone?**  
**Answer:** Ebenezer.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:12

**Q3. What explanation did Samuel give for naming the stone Ebenezer?**  
**Answer:** "Hitherto hath the LORD helped us."  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:12

---

### 1 Samuel 7:13

**Q1. What happened to the Philistines after the stone was set up?**  
**Answer:** They were subdued, and came no more into the coast of Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:13

**Q2. Whose hand was against the Philistines all the days of Samuel?**  
**Answer:** The hand of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:13

**Q3. How long was the hand of the LORD against the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** All the days of Samuel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:13

---

### 1 Samuel 7:14

**Q1. Which cities that the Philistines had taken from Israel were restored?**  
**Answer:** The cities from Ekron even unto Gath.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:14

**Q2. Who delivered the coasts of Israel out of the hands of the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:14

**Q3. What was there between Israel and the Amorites?**  
**Answer:** Peace.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:14

---

### 1 Samuel 7:15

**Q1. How long did Samuel judge Israel?**  
**Answer:** All the days of his life.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:15

---

### 1 Samuel 7:16

**Q1. How often did Samuel go from year to year in circuit?**  
**Answer:** From year to year.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:16

**Q2. What three cities did Samuel go in circuit to judge Israel?**  
**Answer:** Bethel, Gilgal, and Mizpeh.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:16

**Q3. What did Samuel do in Bethel, Gilgal, and Mizpeh?**  
**Answer:** He judged Israel in all those places.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:16

---

### 1 Samuel 7:17

**Q1. To what place did Samuel return?**  
**Answer:** To Ramah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:17

**Q2. What was located in Ramah pertaining to Samuel?**  
**Answer:** His house.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:17

**Q3. What did Samuel build unto the LORD in Ramah?**  
**Answer:** An altar.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 7:17
