# 1 Samuel 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 4:1

**Q1. Whose word came to all Israel?**  
**Answer:** Samuel's word.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:1

**Q2. Against whom did Israel go out to battle?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:1

**Q3. Where did Israel pitch beside while the Philistines pitched in Aphek?**  
**Answer:** Eben-ezer.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:1

---

### 1 Samuel 4:2

**Q4. Who put themselves in array against Israel?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:2

**Q5. What happened when the battle was joined?**  
**Answer:** Israel was smitten before the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:2

**Q6. How many men of the army were slain in the field?**  
**Answer:** About four thousand men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:2

---

### 1 Samuel 4:3

**Q7. To where did the people return after the defeat?**  
**Answer:** Into the camp.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:3

**Q8. What did the elders of Israel ask after the Lord smote them?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore hath the Lord smitten us to day before the Philistines?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:3

**Q9. What did they suggest bringing from Shiloh unto them?**  
**Answer:** The ark of the covenant of the Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:3

---

### 1 Samuel 4:4

**Q10. Who sent to Shiloh from the people?**  
**Answer:** The people.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:4

**Q11. What did they bring from Shiloh?**  
**Answer:** The ark of the covenant of the Lord of hosts, which dwelleth between the cherubims.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:4

**Q12. Who were there with the ark of the covenant of God?**  
**Answer:** Hophni and Phinehas, the two sons of Eli.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:4

---

### 1 Samuel 4:5

**Q13. What came into the camp when the ark of the covenant of the Lord came?**  
**Answer:** All Israel shouted with a great shout.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:5

**Q14. What happened to the earth upon hearing the shout?**  
**Answer:** The earth rang again.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:5

**Q15. Who shouted?**  
**Answer:** All Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:5

---

### 1 Samuel 4:6

**Q16. What did the Philistines hear when the noise of the shout came?**  
**Answer:** The noise of the shout.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:6

**Q17. What did the Philistines ask upon hearing the shout?**  
**Answer:** What meaneth the noise of this great shout in the camp of the Hebrews?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:6

**Q18. What did they understand had come into the camp?**  
**Answer:** That the ark of the Lord was come into the camp.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:6

---

### 1 Samuel 4:7

**Q19. How did the Philistines feel when they understood?**  
**Answer:** They were afraid.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:7

**Q20. What did the Philistines say had come into the camp?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:7

**Q21. What did they exclaim regarding their previous state?**  
**Answer:** Woe unto us! for there hath not been such a thing heretofore.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:7

---

### 1 Samuel 4:8

**Q22. What did the Philistines say again?**  
**Answer:** Woe unto us!  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:8

**Q23. Who did they ask could deliver them out of the hand of these mighty Gods?**  
**Answer:** Who shall deliver us out of the hand of these mighty Gods?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:8

**Q24. Where did they say these Gods smote the Egyptians?**  
**Answer:** In the wilderness with all the plagues.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:8

---

### 1 Samuel 4:9

**Q25. What did the Philistines command one another to be?**  
**Answer:** Strong and quit yourselves like men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:9

**Q26. Whose servants did they say they should not become?**  
**Answer:** The servants unto the Hebrews.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:9

**Q27. How did they tell each other to act?**  
**Answer:** Quit yourselves like men, and fight.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:9

---

### 1 Samuel 4:10

**Q28. Who fought after these words?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:10

**Q29. What happened to Israel?**  
**Answer:** They were smitten.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:10

**Q30. Where did every man flee?**  
**Answer:** Into his tent.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:10

---

### 1 Samuel 4:11

**Q31. What was taken in the battle?**  
**Answer:** The ark of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:11

**Q32. Who were slain in the battle?**  
**Answer:** Hophni and Phinehas, the two sons of Eli.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:11

**Q33. What happened to the two sons of Eli?**  
**Answer:** They were dead.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:11

---

### 1 Samuel 4:12

**Q34. Who ran out of the army?**  
**Answer:** A man of Benjamin.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:12

**Q35. When did the man of Benjamin run out of the army to Shiloh?**  
**Answer:** The same day.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:12

**Q36. What were the physical signs of grief upon the man's arrival in Shiloh?**  
**Answer:** His clothes rent, and earth upon his head.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:12

---

### 1 Samuel 4:13

**Q37. Where was Eli sitting when the man arrived?**  
**Answer:** Upon a seat by the wayside watching.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:13

**Q38. Why did Eli's heart tremble?**  
**Answer:** For the ark of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:13

**Q39. What did the man do when he came into the city, causing all the city to cry out?**  
**Answer:** Told it.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:13

---

### 1 Samuel 4:14

**Q40. What did Eli hear when he heard the noise of the crying?**  
**Answer:** The noise of the crying.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:14

**Q41. What did Eli ask when he heard the noise?**  
**Answer:** What meaneth the noise of this tumult?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:14

**Q42. What did the man do regarding Eli?**  
**Answer:** Hasted, and came and told Eli.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:14

---

### 1 Samuel 4:15

**Q43. How old was Eli?**  
**Answer:** Ninety and eight years old.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:15

**Q44. What happened to Eli's eyes?**  
**Answer:** His eyes were dim, that he could not see.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:15

**Q45. What condition were his eyes in?**  
**Answer:** They were dim.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:15

---

### 1 Samuel 4:16

**Q46. What did the man say to Eli concerning his arrival?**  
**Answer:** I am he that came from the army, and I fled to day out of the army.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:16

**Q47. What did Eli ask the man?**  
**Answer:** What is there done, my son?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:16

**Q48. From where did the messenger say he fled that day?**  
**Answer:** Out of the army.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:16

---

### 1 Samuel 4:17

**Q49. Who answered and said Israel is fled before the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** The messenger.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:17

**Q50. What happened to the people regarding a great slaughter?**  
**Answer:** There hath been also a great slaughter among the people.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:17

**Q51. Who else were dead according to the messenger?**  
**Answer:** Thy two sons also, Hophni and Phinehas, and the ark of God is taken.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:17

---

### 1 Samuel 4:18

**Q52. What was made mention of when the messenger made mention of it?**  
**Answer:** The ark of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:18

**Q53. Where did Eli fall from upon the mention of the ark of God?**  
**Answer:** From off the seat backward by the side of the gate.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:18

**Q54. What happened to Eli because he was an old man and heavy?**  
**Answer:** His neck brake, and he died.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:18

---

### 1 Samuel 4:19

**Q55. Who was great with child, near to be delivered?**  
**Answer:** His daughter in law, Phinehas' wife.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:19

**Q56. What tidings did she hear that were taken?**  
**Answer:** That the ark of God was taken, and that her father in law and her husband were dead.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:19

**Q57. What happened to her when she heard the tidings?**  
**Answer:** She bowed herself and travailed; for her pains came upon her.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:19

---

### 1 Samuel 4:20

**Q58. Who stood about her while she was in travail?**  
**Answer:** The women that stood by her.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:20

**Q59. What did the women say to comfort her?**  
**Answer:** Fear not; for thou hast born a son.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:20

**Q60. Did she answer or regard the women?**  
**Answer:** She answered not, neither did she regard it.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:20

---

### 1 Samuel 4:21

**Q61. What did she name the child?**  
**Answer:** Ichabod.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:21

**Q62. What did she say regarding the glory?**  
**Answer:** The glory is departed from Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:21

**Q63. Why did she say the glory was departed?**  
**Answer:** Because the ark of God was taken, and because of her father in law and her husband.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:21

---

### 1 Samuel 4:22

**Q64. What did she repeat about the glory?**  
**Answer:** The glory is departed from Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:22

**Q65. Why did she say the glory was departed from Israel in the final verse?**  
**Answer:** For the ark of God is taken.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:22

**Q66. What specific item was taken according to her final statement?**  
**Answer:** The ark of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 4:22
