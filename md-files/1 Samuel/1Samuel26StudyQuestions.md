# 1 Samuel 26 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 26:1

**Q1. Who came unto Saul to Gibeah?**  
**Answer:** The Ziphites.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:1

**Q2. Where did the Ziphites say David hid himself?**  
**Answer:** In the hill of Hachilah, which is before Jeshimon.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:1

**Q3. To whom did the Ziphites come unto Gibeah saying David hid himself?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:1

---

### 1 Samuel 26:2

**Q1. Who arose and went down to the wilderness of Ziph?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:2

**Q2. How many chosen men of Israel went down with Saul?**  
**Answer:** Three thousand chosen men of Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:2

**Q3. Where did Saul seek David with his three thousand chosen men?**  
**Answer:** In the wilderness of Ziph.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:2

---

### 1 Samuel 26:3

**Q1. Where did Saul pitch his tent?**  
**Answer:** In the hill of Hachilah, which is before Jeshimon, by the way.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:3

**Q2. Who abode in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:3

**Q3. What did David see when Saul came after him into the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** That Saul came after him into the wilderness.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:3

---

### 1 Samuel 26:4

**Q1. Whom did David send out?**  
**Answer:** Spies.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:4

**Q2. What did David understand by sending out spies?**  
**Answer:** That Saul was come in very deed.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:4

---

### 1 Samuel 26:5

**Q1. And where did David arise and come?**  
**Answer:** To the place where Saul had pitched.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:5

**Q2. Whose place did David behold?**  
**Answer:** Saul's place, and Abner the son of Ner, the captain of his host.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:5

**Q3. Where was Saul lying in the trench?**  
**Answer:** In the trench, and the people pitched round about him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:5

---

### 1 Samuel 26:6

**Q1. To whom did David speak?**  
**Answer:** Ahimelech the Hittite, and to Abishai the son of Zeruiah, brother to Joab.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:6

**Q2. Whom did David ask to go down with him to Saul to the camp?**  
**Answer:** Abishai the son of Zeruiah, brother to Joab.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:6

**Q3. What was Abishai's reply to David?**  
**Answer:** I will go down with thee.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:6

---

### 1 Samuel 26:7

**Q1. Who came to the people by night?**  
**Answer:** David and Abishai.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:7

**Q2. Where was Saul lying and sleeping within the trench?**  
**Answer:** Saul lay sleeping within the trench.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:7

**Q3. Where was Saul's spear stuck in the ground?**  
**Answer:** At his bolster.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:7

---

### 1 Samuel 26:8

**Q1. What did Abishai say God had delivered into David's hand this day?**  
**Answer:** Thine enemy.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:8

**Q2. What did Abishai ask permission to smite Saul with?**  
**Answer:** The spear even to the earth at once.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:8

**Q3. How many times did Abishai say he would smite Saul if permitted?**  
**Answer:** I will not smite him the second time.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:8

---

### 1 Samuel 26:9

**Q1. What did David tell Abishai not to do to Saul?**  
**Answer:** Destroy him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:9

**Q2. Whose anointed can put forth his hand and be guiltless, according to David?**  
**Answer:** The Lord's anointed.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:9

**Q3. Why did David say not to destroy Saul?**  
**Answer:** For who can stretch forth his hand against the Lord's anointed, and be guiltless?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:9

---

### 1 Samuel 26:10

**Q1. By what did David swear the Lord liveth?**  
**Answer:** The Lord shall smite him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:10

**Q2. When might Saul's day come to die?**  
**Answer:** Or his day shall come to die; or he shall descend into battle, and perish.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:10

**Q3. What alternative fate for Saul did David mention besides his day to die?**  
**Answer:** He shall descend into battle, and perish.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:10

---

### 1 Samuel 26:11

**Q1. What did David forbid himself from stretching forth his hand against?**  
**Answer:** The Lord's anointed.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:11

**Q2. What did David tell Abishai to take from Saul's bolster?**  
**Answer:** The spear that is at his bolster, and the cruse of water.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:11

**Q3. What two items did David and Abishai take from Saul's bolster?**  
**Answer:** The spear and the cruse of water.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:11

---

### 1 Samuel 26:12

**Q1. What did David take from Saul's bolster?**  
**Answer:** The spear and the cruse of water.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:12

**Q2. Where did David and Abishai get away?**  
**Answer:** They gat them away, and no man saw it, nor knew it, neither awaked.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:12

**Q3. Why did no man see, know, or awake?**  
**Answer:** Because a deep sleep from the Lord was fallen upon them.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:12

---

### 1 Samuel 26:13

**Q1. Where did David go over?**  
**Answer:** To the other side.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:13

**Q2. Where did David stand on the top of?**  
**Answer:** On the top of an hill afar off.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:13

**Q3. What was the distance between David and the hill where he stood?**  
**Answer:** A great space being between them.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:13

---

### 1 Samuel 26:14

**Q1. To whom did David cry out?**  
**Answer:** To the people, and to Abner the son of Ner.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:14

**Q2. What did David ask Abner?**  
**Answer:** Answerest thou not, Abner?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:14

**Q3. What was Abner's answer when David called?**  
**Answer:** Who art thou that criest to the king?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:14

---

### 1 Samuel 26:15

**Q1. What did David ask Abner about his identity?**  
**Answer:** Art not thou a valiant man?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:15

**Q2. Whom did David say was like Abner in Israel?**  
**Answer:** Who is like to thee in Israel?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:15

**Q3. Who came in to destroy the king the lord?**  
**Answer:** One of the people.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:15

---

### 1 Samuel 26:16

**Q1. What did David say was not good that Abner had done?**  
**Answer:** This thing that thou hast done.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:16

**Q2. By what did David swear the Lord liveth regarding Abner and his men?**  
**Answer:** As the Lord liveth, ye are worthy to die.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:16

**Q3. Why did David say Abner and his men were worthy to die?**  
**Answer:** Because ye have not kept your master, the Lord's anointed.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:16

---

### 1 Samuel 26:17

**Q1. Whose voice did Saul recognize?**  
**Answer:** David's voice.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:17

**Q2. What did Saul call David in his question?**  
**Answer:** My son David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:17

**Q3. What was David's response to Saul's question?**  
**Answer:** It is my voice, my lord, O king.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:17

---

### 1 Samuel 26:18

**Q1. What did David ask why his lord thus pursued?**  
**Answer:** His servant.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:18

**Q2. What did David ask what he had done?**  
**Answer:** For what have I done?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:18

**Q3. What did David ask what evil was in?**  
**Answer:** In mine hand.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:18

---

### 1 Samuel 26:19

**Q1. Who did David ask the king to hear the words of?**  
**Answer:** His lord the king.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:19

**Q2. If the Lord have stirred thee up against me, what would He accept?**  
**Answer:** An offering.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:19

**Q3. If the children of men stirred Saul up, what were they cursed before?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:19

---

### 1 Samuel 26:20

**Q1. What did David say should not fall to the earth?**  
**Answer:** His blood.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:20

**Q2. Before whose face did David say his blood should not fall to the earth?**  
**Answer:** Before the face of the Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:20

**Q3. What did David compare the king of Israel coming out to seek as?**  
**Answer:** As when one doth hunt a partridge in the mountains.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:20

---

### 1 Samuel 26:21

**Q1. What did Saul confess he had done?**  
**Answer:** I have sinned.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:21

**Q2. What did Saul tell David to do after confessing?**  
**Answer:** Return, my son David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:21

**Q3. Why did Saul say he would no more do David harm?**  
**Answer:** Because my soul was precious in thine eyes this day.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:21

---

### 1 Samuel 26:22

**Q1. Whose spear did David answer and say to let one of the young men come over and fetch?**  
**Answer:** The king's spear.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:22

---

### 1 Samuel 26:23

**Q1. Who renders to every man his righteousness and his faithfulness?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:23

**Q2. Into whose hand did the Lord deliver Saul today?**  
**Answer:** Into thy hand.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:23

**Q3. What did David refuse to stretch forth his hand against?**  
**Answer:** The Lord's anointed.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:23

### 1 Samuel 26:24

**Q1. As David's life was much set by this day, how did he want his life set by in the eyes of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** So let my life be much set by in the eyes of the Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:24

**Q2. From what did David desire the Lord to deliver him?**  
**Answer:** Out of all tribulation.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:24

---

### 1 Samuel 26:25

**Q1. What did Saul say to David?**  
**Answer:** Blessed be thou, my son David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:25

**Q2. What did Saul declare David should both do and also prevail in?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt both do great things, and also shalt still prevail.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:25

**Q3. Where did David go, and where did Saul return to?**  
**Answer:** David went on his way, and Saul returned to his place.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 26:25
