# 1 Samuel 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 2:1

**Q1. What did Hannah do and say in her prayer?**  
**Answer:** My heart rejoiceth in the LORD, mine horn is exalted in the LORD: my mouth is enlarged over mine enemies; because I rejoice in thy salvation.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 2:1

**Q2. In what did Hannah's heart rejoice?**  
**Answer:** In the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 2:1

**Q3. What was enlarged over her enemies?**  
**Answer:** My mouth.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 2:1

---

### 1 Samuel 2:2

**Q4. Who is holy like the LORD?**  
**Answer:** None.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 2:2

**Q5. Who is beside the LORD?**  
**Answer:** None.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 2:2

**Q6. What is there none like besides the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Any rock like our God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 2:2

---

### 1 Samuel 2:3

**Q7. What should men talk no more of so exceedingly?**

**Verse:** 1 Samuel 2:3

**Q8. What must not come out of your mouth?**

**Verse:** 1 Samuel 2:3

**Q9. What kind of God is the LORD by whom actions are weighed?**  
**Answer:** A God of knowledge.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 2:3

---

### 1 Samuel 2:4

**Answer:** The bows of the mighty men.

**Verse:** 1 Samuel 2:5

**Verse:** 1 Samuel 2:5
