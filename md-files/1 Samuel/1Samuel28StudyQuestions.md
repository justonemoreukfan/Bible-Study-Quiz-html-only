# 1 Samuel 28 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 28:1

**Q1. Who gathered their armies together for warfare against Israel?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:1

**Q2. What did Achish say to David concerning going out to battle with him?**  
**Answer:** Know thou assuredly, that thou shalt go out with me to battle, thou and thy men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:1

**Q3. Whom did Achish tell David he and his men would go out with to battle?**  
**Answer:** Thou and thy men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:1

---

### 1 Samuel 28:2

**Q1. What did David say to Achish he would know?**  
**Answer:** Surely thou shalt know what thy servant can do.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:2

**Q2. What did Achish make David in consequence of his reply?**  
**Answer:** Keeper of mine head for ever.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:2

**Q3. For how long did Achish make David keeper of his head?**  
**Answer:** For ever.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:2

---

### 1 Samuel 28:3

**Q1. Who was dead?**  
**Answer:** Samuel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:3

**Q2. Who had lamented Samuel?**  
**Answer:** All Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:3

**Q3. Where had Saul put away those that had familiar spirits, and the wizards, out of the land?**  
**Answer:** Out of the land.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:3

---

### 1 Samuel 28:4

**Q1. Where did the Philistines gather themselves together?**  
**Answer:** And came and pitched in Shunem.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:4

**Q2. Who gathered all Israel together?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:4

**Q3. Where did Saul pitch?**  
**Answer:** In Gilboa.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:4

---

### 1 Samuel 28:5

**Q1. When Saul saw the host of the Philistines, what happened to him?**  
**Answer:** He was afraid.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:5

**Q2. What did his heart do when he saw the host of the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** His heart greatly trembled.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:5

**Q3. Whose host did Saul see?**  
**Answer:** The host of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:5

---

### 1 Samuel 28:6

**Q1. When Saul inquired of the LORD, did the LORD answer him?**  
**Answer:** The LORD answered him not.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:6

**Q2. By what did the LORD not answer Saul?**  
**Answer:** Neither by dreams, nor by Urim, nor by prophets.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:6

**Q3. Who inquired of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:6

---

### 1 Samuel 28:7

**Q1. What did Saul say to his servants?**  
**Answer:** Seek me a woman that hath a familiar spirit, that I may go to her, and enquire of her.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:7

**Q2. What did Saul's servants say to him?**  
**Answer:** Behold, there is a woman that hath a familiar spirit at Endor.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:7

**Q3. Where was the woman that had a familiar spirit located?**  
**Answer:** At Endor.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:7

---

### 1 Samuel 28:8

**Q1. What did Saul do to himself?**  
**Answer:** Disguised himself, and put on other raiment.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:8

**Q2. With how many men did Saul go?**  
**Answer:** Two men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:8

**Q3. When did Saul and his men come to the woman?**  
**Answer:** By night.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:8

---

### 1 Samuel 28:9

**Q1. What did the woman say Saul had laid?**  
**Answer:** A snare for my life, to cause me to die.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:9

**Q2. What did Saul swear by?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:9

**Q3. What did Saul promise the woman under oath?**  
**Answer:** As the LORD liveth, there shall no punishment happen to thee for this thing.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:9

---

### 1 Samuel 28:10

**Q1. By whom did Saul swear to the woman?**  
**Answer:** By the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:10

**Q2. What did Saul say would not happen to her?**  
**Answer:** There shall no punishment happen to thee for this thing.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:10

**Q3. Whose life was Saul addressing with an oath?**  
**Answer:** The woman's life.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:10

---

### 1 Samuel 28:11

**Q1. Whom did the woman ask Saul to bring up unto her?**  
**Answer:** Samuel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:11

**Q2. What did the woman say to Saul?**  
**Answer:** Whom shall I bring up unto thee?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:11

**Q3. Whose request was it to bring up Samuel?**  
**Answer:** Saul's.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:11

---

### 1 Samuel 28:12

**Q1. When the woman saw Samuel, what did she do?**  
**Answer:** She cried with a loud voice.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:12

**Q2. To whom did the woman speak with a loud voice?**  
**Answer:** To Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:12

**Q3. What did the woman say Saul had done to her?**  
**Answer:** Why hast thou deceived me? for thou art Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:12

---

### 1 Samuel 28:13

**Q1. What did the king say to her?**  
**Answer:** Be not afraid.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:13

**Q2. What did the woman say she saw?**  
**Answer:** I saw gods ascending out of the earth.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:13

**Q3. Who said, "I saw gods ascending out of the earth"?**  
**Answer:** The woman.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:13

---

### 1 Samuel 28:14

**Q1. What did Saul ask the woman?**  
**Answer:** What form is he of?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:14

**Q2. What did the woman answer about the old man?**  
**Answer:** An old man cometh up; and he is covered with a mantle.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:14

**Q3. When Saul perceived that it was Samuel, what did he do?**  
**Answer:** Stooped with his face to the ground, and bowed himself.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:14

---

### 1 Samuel 28:15

**Q1. What did Samuel say to Saul?**  
**Answer:** Why hast thou disquieted me, to bring me up?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:15

**Q2. Why did Saul answer that he was sore distressed?**  
**Answer:** For the Philistines make war against me, and God is departed from me.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:15

**Q3. By whom did Saul say God was no longer answering him?**  
**Answer:** Neither by prophets, nor by dreams.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:15

---

### 1 Samuel 28:16

**Q1. Why did Samuel ask, "Wherefore then dost thou ask of me"?**  
**Answer:** Seeing the LORD is departed from thee, and is become thine enemy.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:16

**Q2. Whom did Samuel say the LORD had become to?**  
**Answer:** Thine enemy.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:16

**Q3. From whom did Samuel say the LORD is departed?**  
**Answer:** Thee.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:16

---

### 1 Samuel 28:17

**Q1. What has the LORD done to him, as he spake by Samuel?**  
**Answer:** The LORD hath done to him, as he spake by me.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:17

**Q2. From whose hand has the LORD rent the kingdom?**  
**Answer:** Out of thine hand.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:17

**Q3. To whom has the LORD given the kingdom?**  
**Answer:** To thy neighbour, even to David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:17

---

### 1 Samuel 28:18

**Q1. Because Saul obeyed not what, was this done?**  
**Answer:** The voice of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:18

**Q2. Whose fierce wrath did Saul not execute?**  
**Answer:** Amalek.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:18

**Q3. What has the LORD done unto thee this day because of this?**  
**Answer:** Therefore hath the LORD done this thing unto thee this day.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:18

---

### 1 Samuel 28:19

**Q1. Into whose hand will the LORD also deliver Israel with Saul?**  
**Answer:** Into the hand of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:19

**Q2. Where will Saul and his sons be tomorrow?**  
**Answer:** With me.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:19

**Q3. Whose host shall the LORD deliver into the hand of the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** The host of Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:19

---

### 1 Samuel 28:20

**Q1. How did Saul fall straightway along the earth?**  
**Answer:** All along the earth.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:20

**Q2. Why was Saul sore afraid?**  
**Answer:** Because of the words of Samuel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:20

**Q3. What was there no strength in?**  
**Answer:** There was no strength in him; for he had eaten no bread all the day, nor all the night.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:20

---

### 1 Samuel 28:21

**Q1. Who came unto Saul and saw that he was sore troubled?**  
**Answer:** The woman.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:21

**Q2. What did the woman say she had obeyed?**  
**Answer:** Thy voice.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:21

**Q3. What did the woman put in her hand?**  
**Answer:** My life.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:21

---

### 1 Samuel 28:22

**Q1. What did the woman ask Saul to hearken unto?**  
**Answer:** The voice of thine handmaid.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:22

**Q2. What did the woman invite Saul to set before him?**  
**Answer:** A morsel of bread.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:22

**Q3. Why did the woman want Saul to eat?**  
**Answer:** That thou mayest have strength, when thou goest on thy way.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:22

---

### 1 Samuel 28:23

**Q1. What did Saul refuse to do?**  
**Answer:** He refused.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:23

**Q2. Who compelled Saul to eat?**  
**Answer:** His servants, together with the woman.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:23

**Q3. What did Saul do after hearkening unto their voice?**  
**Answer:** He arose from the earth, and sat upon the bed.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:23

---

### 1 Samuel 28:24

**Q1. What did the woman have in the house?**  
**Answer:** A fat calf.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:24

**Q2. What did the woman quickly do with the fat calf?**  
**Answer:** Killed it, and took flour, and kneaded it, and did bake unleavened bread thereof.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:24

---

### 1 Samuel 28:25

**Q1. Where did the woman bring the food?**  
**Answer:** Before Saul, and before his servants.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:25

**Q2. What did Saul and his servants do after the food was brought?**  
**Answer:** They did eat.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:25

**Q3. When did Saul and his servants rise up and depart?**  
**Answer:** That night.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 28:25
