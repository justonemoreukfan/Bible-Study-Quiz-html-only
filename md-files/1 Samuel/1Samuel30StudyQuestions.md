# 1 Samuel 30 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 30:1

**Q1. When David and his men came to Ziklag on the third day, who had invaded the south and Ziklag?**  
**Answer:** The Amalekites.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:1

**Q2. What had the Amalekites invaded in the south?**  
**Answer:** Ziklag.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:1

**Q3. What had the Amalekites done to Ziklag and burned it with fire?**  
**Answer:** Smitten Ziklag.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:1

---

### 1 Samuel 30:2

**Q4. Whose women had the Amalekites taken captive?**  
**Answer:** The women that were therein.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:2

**Q5. Whom did the Amalekites not slay, either small or great?**  
**Answer:** Any that were therein.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:2

**Q6. What did the Amalekites do with the captives after carrying them away?**  
**Answer:** Went on their way.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:2

---

### 1 Samuel 30:3

**Q7. Who came to the city when David and his men arrived?**  
**Answer:** David and his men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:3

**Q8. What condition was the city in when David and his men came to it?**  
**Answer:** Burned with fire.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:3

**Q9. Who had been taken captive and carried away in the city?**  
**Answer:** Their wives, and their sons, and their daughters.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:3

---

### 1 Samuel 30:4

**Q10. What did David and the people that were with him do?**  
**Answer:** Lifted up their voice and wept.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:4

**Q11. Until what point did David and the people weep?**  
**Answer:** Until they had no more power to weep.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:4

**Q12. What was the state of David and the people after they wept?**  
**Answer:** They had no more power to weep.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:4

---

### 1 Samuel 30:5

**Q11. Who were taken captive along with the others?**  
**Answer:** David's two wives.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:5

**Q12. Name the first of David's two wives who was taken captive.**  
**Answer:** Ahinoam the Jezreelitess.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:5

**Q13. Name the second of David's two wives who was taken captive.**  
**Answer:** Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:5

---

### 1 Samuel 30:6

**Q14. How was David greatly distressed because of what the people spoke?**  
**Answer:** The people spake of stoning him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:6

**Q15. Why was the soul of all the people grieved?**  
**Answer:** For the sons and for the daughters.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:6

**Q16. What did David do to encourage himself?**  
**Answer:** Encouraged himself in the LORD his God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:6

### 1 Samuel 30:11

**Q29. Whom did the men find in the field?**  
**Answer:** An Egyptian.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:11

**Q30. To whom did they bring the Egyptian?**  
**Answer:** To David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:11

**Q31. What did they give the Egyptian to eat and drink?**

**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:11

### 1 Samuel 30:12

**Q32. What happened to the Egyptian's spirit after he had eaten?**  
**Answer:** His spirit came again to him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:12

**Answer:** Three days and three nights.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 30:12

**Q34. What food items did they give him before he revived?**  
**Answer:** A piece of a cake of figs, and two clusters of raisins.

### 1 Samuel 30:16

**Q44. When the Egyptian had brought David down, how were the Amalekites spread abroad?**  
**Answer:** Upon all the earth.
