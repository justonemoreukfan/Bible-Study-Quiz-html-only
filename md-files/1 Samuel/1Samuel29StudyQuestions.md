# 1 Samuel 29 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 29:1

**Q1.** Who gathered all their armies together to Aphek?  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:1

**Q2.** Where did the Israelites pitch by a fountain?  
**Answer:** By a fountain which is in Jezreel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:1

**Q3.** Where was the fountain located?  
**Answer:** In Jezreel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:1

---

### 1 Samuel 29:2

**Q1.** Who passed on by hundreds and by thousands?  
**Answer:** The lords of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:2

**Q2.** Who passed on in the rear-ward?  
**Answer:** David and his men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:2

**Q3.** With whom did David and his men pass on?  
**Answer:** With Achish.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:2

---

### 1 Samuel 29:3

**Q1.** Who said, What do these Hebrews here?  
**Answer:** The princes of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:3

**Q2.** What did Achish say unto the princes of the Philistines regarding David?  
**Answer:** Is not this David, the servant of Saul the king of Israel?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:3

**Q3.** How long had David been with Achish, and what fault did Achish find in him?  
**Answer:** He had been with him these days, or these years, and Achish found no fault in him since he fell unto him unto this day.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:3

---

### 1 Samuel 29:4

**Q1.** Who were wroth with Achish?  
**Answer:** The princes of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:4

**Q2.** What did the princes of the Philistines command Achish to make David do?  
**Answer:** Make this fellow return, that he may go again to his place which thou hast appointed him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:4

**Q3.** Why did the princes say David should not go down with them to battle, lest he be what?  
**Answer:** Lest in the battle he be an adversary to us.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:4

---

### 1 Samuel 29:5

**Q1.** Of whom did they sing one to another in dances concerning David?  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:5

**Q2.** What did the people sing concerning Saul and David?  
**Answer:** Saul slew his thousands, and David his ten thousands.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:5

**Q3.** What question is asked about this David?  
**Answer:** Is not this David, of whom they sang one to another in dances, saying, Saul slew his thousands, and David his ten thousands?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:5

---

### 1 Samuel 29:6

**Q1.** Whom did Achish call to him?  
**Answer:** David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:6

**Q2.** By whom did Achish swear that David was upright?  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:6

**Q3.** How did Achish describe David's going out and coming in with him in the host?  
**Answer:** Thy going out and thy coming in with me in the host is good in my sight.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:6

---

### 1 Samuel 29:7

**Q1.** Wherefore now, what did Achish tell David to return and do?  
**Answer:** Return and go in peace.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:7

**Q2.** Whose eyes should David not displease?  
**Answer:** The lords of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:7

**Q3.** What kind of return did Achish command?  
**Answer:** Return and go in peace, that thou displease not the lords of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:7

---

### 1 Samuel 29:8

**Q1.** What did David ask Achish he had done?  
**Answer:** What have I done?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:8

**Q2.** What did David ask what Achish had found in his servant?  
**Answer:** What hast thou found in thy servant so long as I have been with thee unto this day?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:8

**Q3.** Why did David ask not to go, that he might not do what?  
**Answer:** That I may not go do fight against the enemies of my lord the king.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:8

---

### 1 Samuel 29:9

**Q1.** How did Achish answer and say unto David?  
**Answer:** I know that thou art good in my sight, as an angel of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:9

**Q2.** To what did Achish compare David?  
**Answer:** As an angel of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:9

**Q3.** Who did Achish say had not come with them to the battle?  
**Answer:** The princes of the Philistines have said, He shall not go up with us to the battle.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:9

---

### 1 Samuel 29:10

**Q1.** Who did Achish tell to rise up early in the morning?  
**Answer:** David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:10

**Q2.** Along with David, who else was told to rise up early with his master's servants?  
**Answer:** Thy master's servants that are come with thee.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:10

**Q3.** When should they depart?  
**Answer:** As soon as ye have light in the morning, and have light.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:10

---

### 1 Samuel 29:11

**Q1.** When did David and his men rise up early?  
**Answer:** In the morning.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:11

**Q2.** Where did they depart to return?  
**Answer:** Into the land of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:11

**Q3.** Where did the Philistines go?  
**Answer:** Up to Jezreel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 29:11
