# 1 Samuel 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 5:1

**Q1. Who took the ark of God?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:1

**Q2. From where did the Philistines bring the ark of God?**  
**Answer:** From Ebenezer.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:1

**Q3. Unto where did the Philistines bring the ark of God?**  
**Answer:** Unto Ashdod.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:1

---

### 1 Samuel 5:2

**Q1. When the Philistines took the ark of God, where did they bring it?**  
**Answer:** Into the house of Dagon.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:2

**Q2. Beside whom did the Philistines set the ark?**  
**Answer:** Beside Dagon.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:2

---

### 1 Samuel 5:3

**Q1. Who of Ashdod arose early on the morrow?**  
**Answer:** They of Ashdod.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:3

**Q2. How was Dagon found when they arose early?**  
**Answer:** Fallen upon his face to the earth before the ark of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:3

**Q3. What did the people of Ashdod do with Dagon?**  
**Answer:** Took Dagon, and set him in his place again.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:3

---

### 1 Samuel 5:4

**Q1. When they arose early on the morrow morning, how was Dagon found again?**  
**Answer:** Fallen upon his face to the ground before the ark of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:4

**Q2. What parts of Dagon were cut off upon the threshold?**  
**Answer:** The head of Dagon and both the palms of his hands.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:4

**Q3. What alone was left to Dagon?**  
**Answer:** The stump of Dagon.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:4

---

### 1 Samuel 5:5

**Q1. Neither Dagon's priests, nor any that come into Dagon's house, do what upon this day?**  
**Answer:** Tread on the threshold of Dagon in Ashdod.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:5

**Q2. Where is the threshold that they do not tread upon located?**  
**Answer:** In Ashdod.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:5

---

### 1 Samuel 5:6

**Q1. What was the hand of the LORD upon?**  
**Answer:** The men of Ashdod.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:6

**Q2. What did the LORD do to the men of Ashdod?**  
**Answer:** Destroyed them.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:6

**Q3. With what did the LORD smite Ashdod and the coasts thereof?**  
**Answer:** With emerods.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:6

---

### 1 Samuel 5:7

**Q1. When the men of Ashdod saw that it was so, what did they say?**  
**Answer:** The ark of the God of Israel shall not abide with us: for his hand is sore upon us, and upon Dagon our god.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:7

**Q2. Whose hand did the men of Ashdod say was sore upon them and upon Dagon?**  
**Answer:** The hand of the God of Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:7

---

### 1 Samuel 5:8

**Q1. Whom did the lords of the Philistines gather together?**  
**Answer:** All the lords of the Philistines unto them.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:8

**Q2. What question did they ask concerning the ark of the God of Israel?**  
**Answer:** What shall we do with the ark of the God of Israel?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:8

**Q3. Where did the men of Gath answer that the ark should be carried?**  
**Answer:** Unto Gath.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:8

---

### 1 Samuel 5:9

**Q1. After they had carried the ark away, what was the hand of the LORD against?**  
**Answer:** The city.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:9

**Q2. With what kind of destruction did the LORD smite the city?**  
**Answer:** A very great destruction.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:9

**Q3. Whom did the LORD smite, from the least to the greatest?**  
**Answer:** The men of the city.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:9

---

### 1 Samuel 5:10

**Q1. Where did they send the ark of God?**  
**Answer:** To Ekron.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:10

**Q2. When the ark of God came to Ekron, what did the Ekronites cry out?**  
**Answer:** They have brought about the ark of the God of Israel to us, to slay us and our people.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:10

**Q3. Whom did the Ekronites say the ark was brought to slay?**  
**Answer:** Us and our people.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:10

---

### 1 Samuel 5:11

**Q1. Whom did they send and gather together?**  
**Answer:** All the lords of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:11

**Q2. What did the lords of the Philistines say to do with the ark of the God of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Send it away, that it go again to his own place.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:11

**Q3. What reason did they give for wanting to send the ark away?**  
**Answer:** That it slay us not, and our people.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:11

---

### 1 Samuel 5:12

**Q1. Who were not slain with the emerods?**  
**Answer:** The men that died not.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:12

**Q2. With what were the men smitten if they died not?**  
**Answer:** With the emerods.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:12

**Q3. Up to where went the cry of the city?**  
**Answer:** Up to heaven.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 5:12
