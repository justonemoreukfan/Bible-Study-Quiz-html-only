# 1 Samuel 18 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 18:1

**Q1. When he had made an end of speaking unto Saul, whose soul was knit with the soul of David?**  
**Answer:** The soul of Jonathan.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:1

**Q2. How did Jonathan's soul feel toward David?**  
**Answer:** It was knit with the soul of David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:1

**Q3. How did Jonathan love David?**  
**Answer:** As his own soul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:1

---

### 1 Samuel 18:2

**Q4. Who took David that day?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:2

**Q5. What did Saul let David do no more?**  
**Answer:** Go no more to his father's house.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:2

---

### 1 Samuel 18:3

**Q6. Who made a covenant with David?**  
**Answer:** Jonathan.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:3

**Q7. Why did Jonathan make a covenant with David?**  
**Answer:** Because he loved him as his own soul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:3

---

### 1 Samuel 18:4

**Q8. What did Jonathan strip himself of?**  
**Answer:** The robe that was upon him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:4

**Q9. To whom did Jonathan give his garments?**  
**Answer:** To David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:4

**Q10. What specific items did Jonathan give to David?**  
**Answer:** His robe, his garments, even to his sword, and to his bow, and to his girdle.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:4

---

### 1 Samuel 18:5

**Q11. Whithersoever Saul sent him, how did David behave himself?**  
**Answer:** Wisely.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:5

**Q12. What did Saul set David over?**  
**Answer:** The men of war.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:5

**Q13. In whose sight was David accepted?**  
**Answer:** In the sight of all the people, and also in the sight of Saul's servants.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:5

---

### 1 Samuel 18:6

**Q14. As they came, when David was returned from the slaughter of the Philistine, who came out of all cities of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The women.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:6

**Q15. To meet whom did the women come out?**  
**Answer:** King Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:6

**Q16. With what did the women come out singing and dancing?**  
**Answer:** With tabrets, with joy, and with instruments of musick.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:6

---

### 1 Samuel 18:7

**Q17. As the women answered one another in play, what did they say Saul had slain?**  
**Answer:** His thousands.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:7

**Q18. What did the women say David had slain?**  
**Answer:** His ten thousands.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:7

---

### 1 Samuel 18:8

**Q19. How did Saul feel about the saying that David had slain his ten thousands?**  
**Answer:** He was very wroth, and the saying displeased him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:8

**Q20. What did Saul say they had ascribed unto David?**  
**Answer:** Ten thousands.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:8

**Q21. What did Saul say they had ascribed unto him?**  
**Answer:** But thousands.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:8

---

### 1 Samuel 18:9

**Q22. From what day forward did Saul eye David?**  
**Answer:** From that day and forward.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:9

---

### 1 Samuel 18:10

**Q23. What came upon Saul on the morrow?**  
**Answer:** The evil spirit from God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:10

**Q24. What did Saul do in the midst of the house?**  
**Answer:** He prophesied.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:10

**Q25. What was in Saul's hand as he prophesied?**  
**Answer:** A javelin.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:10

---

### 1 Samuel 18:11

**Q26. What did Saul cast?**  
**Answer:** The javelin.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:11

**Q27. What did Saul say when he cast the javelin?**  
**Answer:** I will smite David even to the wall with it.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:11

**Q28. How many times did David avoid Saul's javelin?**  
**Answer:** Twice.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:11

---

### 1 Samuel 18:12

**Q29. Why was Saul afraid of David?**  
**Answer:** Because the Lord was with him, and was departed from Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:12

---

### 1 Samuel 18:13

**Q30. Therefore what did Saul remove David from?**  
**Answer:** From him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:13

**Q31. What position did Saul make David?**  
**Answer:** His captain over a thousand.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:13

**Q32. Who went out and came in before the people?**  
**Answer:** David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:13

---

### 1 Samuel 18:14

**Q33. In all his business, how did David behave himself?**  
**Answer:** Wisely.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:14

**Q34. Who was with David?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:14

---

### 1 Samuel 18:15

**Q35. When Saul saw that David behaved himself very wisely, how did he feel?**  
**Answer:** He was afraid of him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:15

---

### 1 Samuel 18:16

**Q36. Who loved David?**  
**Answer:** All Israel and Judah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:16

**Q37. Why did all Israel and Judah love David?**  
**Answer:** Because he went out and came in before them.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:16

---

### 1 Samuel 18:17

**Q38. Whom did Saul say he would give to David to wife?**  
**Answer:** Merab his elder daughter.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:17

**Q39. What did Saul command David to be?**  
**Answer:** Valiant for him, and fight the Lord's battles.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:17

**Q40. Whose hand did Saul say should not be upon David?**  
**Answer:** His hand.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:17

---

### 1 Samuel 18:18

**Q41. What did David ask Saul about who he was?**  
**Answer:** Who am I?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:18

**Q42. What did David call his father's family?**  
**Answer:** My life, or my family in Israel. (Text: "what is my life, or my father's family in Israel")  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:18

**Q43. What did David ask he should be to the king's son in law?**  
**Answer:** That I should be son in law to the king.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:18

---

### 1 Samuel 18:19

**Q44. At the time when Merab Saul's daughter should have been given to David, to whom was she given?**  
**Answer:** To Adriel the Meholathite to wife.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:19

---

### 1 Samuel 18:20

**Q45. Who loved David?**  
**Answer:** Michal Saul's daughter.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:20

**Q46. Who was told that Michal loved David?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:20

**Q47. How did Saul feel when he heard that Michal loved David?**  
**Answer:** The thing pleased him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:20

---

### 1 Samuel 18:21

**Q48. What did Saul say he would give Michal to David for?**  
**Answer:** That she may be a snare to him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:21

**Q49. Whose hand did Saul say should be against David through Michal?**  
**Answer:** The hand of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:21

**Q50. Therefore, what did Saul say to David this second time?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt now be my son in law.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:21

---

### 1 Samuel 18:22

**Q51. With whom did Saul command his servants to speak?**  
**Answer:** With David secretly.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:22

**Q52. What did Saul's servants tell David the king had in?**  
**Answer:** Delight in thee. (Text: "Behold, the king hath delight in thee, and all his servants love thee")  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:22

**Q53. What did Saul's servants ask David to now be?**  
**Answer:** The king's son in law.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:22

---

### 1 Samuel 18:23

**Q54. In whose ears did Saul's servants speak these words?**  
**Answer:** In the ears of David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:23

**Q55. How did David respond regarding being the king's son in law?**  
**Answer:** Seemeth it to you a light thing to be a king's son in law?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:23

**Q56. What did David say about himself?**  
**Answer:** I am a poor man, and lightly esteemed.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:23

---

### 1 Samuel 18:24

**Q57. Who shewed Saul what David said?**  
**Answer:** His servants.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:24

---

### 1 Samuel 18:25

**Q58. What did Saul tell his servants to say to David?**  
**Answer:** The king desireth not any dowry.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:25

**Q59. What did Saul want a hundred of from the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** A hundred foreskins of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:25

**Q60. Why did Saul want a hundred foreskins of the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** To be avenged of the king's enemies.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:25

---

### 1 Samuel 18:26

**Q61. When his servants told David these words, how did it please David?**  
**Answer:** It pleased David well to be the king's son in law.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:26

**Q62. Before what event was the days not expired?**  
**Answer:** Before the days were expired.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:26

---

### 1 Samuel 18:27

**Q63. Who arose and went?**  
**Answer:** David and his men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:27

**Q64. How many Philistines did David and his men slay?**  
**Answer:** Two hundred men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:27

**Q65. What did David bring to the king to be his full son in law?**  
**Answer:** Their foreskins.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:27

---

### 1 Samuel 18:28

**Q66. What did Saul saw and know?**  
**Answer:** That the Lord was with David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:28

**Q67. Who loved David?**  
**Answer:** Michal Saul's daughter.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:28

---

### 1 Samuel 18:29

**Q68. How did Saul feel yet the more towards David?**  
**Answer:** He was yet the more afraid of David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:29

**Q69. Whose enemy did Saul become continually?**  
**Answer:** David's enemy.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:29

---

### 1 Samuel 18:30

**Q70. What did the princes of the Philistines do?**  
**Answer:** Went forth.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:30

**Q71. When the princes of the Philistines went forth, how did David behave himself?**  
**Answer:** More wisely than all the servants of Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:30

**Q72. Whose name was much set by?**  
**Answer:** The name of David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 18:30
