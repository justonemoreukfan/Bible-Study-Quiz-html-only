# 1 Samuel 16 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 16:1

**Q1. How long did the LORD say he would mourn for Saul?**  
**Answer:** Until when he had refused him from reigning over Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:1

**Q2. What did the LORD tell Samuel to fill with oil and go?**  
**Answer:** His horn.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:1

**Q3. To whose house was Samuel told to send him?**  
**Answer:** Jesse the Bethlehemite.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:1

### 1 Samuel 16:2

**Q4. What reason did Samuel give for why he could not go?**  
**Answer:** If Saul hear it, he will kill me.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:2

**Q5. What did the LORD tell Samuel to take with him?**  
**Answer:** An heifer.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:2

**Q6. What was Samuel to say he had come to do?**  
**Answer:** To sacrifice to the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:2

### 1 Samuel 16:3

**Q7. Whom was Samuel to call to the sacrifice?**  
**Answer:** Jesse.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:3

**Q8. What would the LORD show Samuel to do?**  
**Answer:** What thou shalt do.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:3

**Q9. Whom was Samuel to anoint unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Him whom I name unto thee.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:3

### 1 Samuel 16:4

**Q10. How did Samuel do that which the LORD spake?**  
**Answer:** He did that which the LORD spake.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:4

**Q11. Where did Samuel come to?**  
**Answer:** Bethlehem.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:4

**Q12. How did the elders of the town tremble when they met Samuel?**  
**Answer:** At his coming.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:4

### 1 Samuel 16:5

**Q13. What did Samuel say he had come to do?**  
**Answer:** Peaceably to sacrifice unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:5

**Q14. What were Jesse and his sons to do?**  
**Answer:** Sanctify yourselves, and come with me to the sacrifice.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:5

**Q15. Whom did Samuel sanctify and call to the sacrifice?**  
**Answer:** Jesse and his sons.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:5

### 1 Samuel 16:6

**Q16. Who came in when they were come?**  
**Answer:** Eliab.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:6

**Q17. What did Samuel look on when Eliab entered?**  
**Answer:** Eliab.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:6

**Q18. What did Samuel say regarding the LORD's anointed?**  
**Answer:** Surely the LORD's anointed is before him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:6

### 1 Samuel 16:7

**Q19. What did the LORD say to Samuel regarding appearance and stature?**  
**Answer:** Look not on his countenance, or on the height of his stature.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:7

**Q20. Why did the LORD say he had refused him?**  
**Answer:** Because I have refused him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:7

**Q21. What does man look on versus what the LORD looks on?**  
**Answer:** Man looketh on the outward appearance, but the LORD looketh on the heart.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:7

### 1 Samuel 16:8

**Q22. Whom did Jesse call and pass before Samuel?**  
**Answer:** Abinadab.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:8

**Q23. What did Samuel say about Abinadab?**  
**Answer:** Neither hath the LORD chosen this.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:8

### 1 Samuel 16:9

**Q24. Whom did Jesse cause to pass by next?**  
**Answer:** Shammah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:9

**Q25. What did Samuel say about Shammah?**  
**Answer:** Neither hath the LORD chosen this.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:9

### 1 Samuel 16:10

**Q26. How many of his sons did Jesse cause to pass before Samuel?**  
**Answer:** Seven.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:10

**Q27. What did Samuel say to Jesse concerning these seven?**  
**Answer:** The LORD hath not chosen these.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:10

### 1 Samuel 16:11

**Q28. What did Samuel ask Jesse regarding the young men?**  
**Answer:** Are here all thy children?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:11

**Q29. Who was the one left?**  
**Answer:** The youngest.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:11

**Q30. What was the youngest son doing?**  
**Answer:** Keeping the sheep.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:11

### 1 Samuel 16:12

**Q31. What instructions did Samuel give to Jesse concerning his youngest son?**  
**Answer:** Send and fetch him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:12

**Q32. How was the youth described in appearance?**  
**Answer:** Ruddy, and withal of a beautiful countenance, and goodly to look to.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:12

**Q33. What did the LORD say to Samuel when the youth arrived?**  
**Answer:** Arise, anoint him: for this is he.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:12

### 1 Samuel 16:13

**Q34. What horn did Samuel take to anoint him?**  
**Answer:** The horn of oil.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:13

**Q35. Where did Samuel anoint him?**  
**Answer:** In the midst of his brethren.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:13

**Q36. What came upon David from that day forward?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit of the LORD came upon David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:13

### 1 Samuel 16:14

**Q37. Whose Spirit departed from Saul?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:14

**Q38. What troubled Saul instead?**  
**Answer:** An evil spirit from the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:14

### 1 Samuel 16:15

**Q39. What did Saul's servants say was troubling him?**  
**Answer:** An evil spirit from God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:15

### 1 Samuel 16:16

**Q40. What did Saul's servants suggest their lord command?**  
**Answer:** Thy servants now before thee will seek out a man.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:16

**Q41. Who was the man to be skilled at playing?**  
**Answer:** A cunning player on an harp.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:16

**Q42. What would happen when the evil spirit from God was upon Saul?**  
**Answer:** He shall play with his hand, and thou shalt be well.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:16

### 1 Samuel 16:17

**Q43. What did Saul say to his servants?**  
**Answer:** Provide me now a man that can play well, and bring him to me.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:17

### 1 Samuel 16:18

**Q44. Whose son did one of the servants answer that he had seen?**  
**Answer:** Jesse the Bethlehemite.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:18

**Q45. How was David described by the servant?**  
**Answer:** Cunning in playing, and a mighty valiant man, and a man of war, and prudent in matters, and a comely person.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:18

**Q46. Who was with David?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:18

### 1 Samuel 16:19

**Q47. Where did Saul send messengers to Jesse?**  
**Answer:** To Jesse.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:19

**Q48. What did Saul ask Jesse to send him?**  
**Answer:** David thy son, which is with the sheep.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:19

### 1 Samuel 16:20

**Q49. What did Jesse take along with an ass laden with bread and a bottle of wine?**  
**Answer:** A kid.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:20

**Q50. By whose hand did Jesse send these things to Saul?**  
**Answer:** By David his son.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:20

### 1 Samuel 16:21

**Q51. To whom did David come and stand before?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:21

**Q52. How did Saul feel about David?**  
**Answer:** He loved him greatly.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:21

**Q53. What position did David attain with Saul?**  
**Answer:** He became his armourbearer.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:21

### 1 Samuel 16:22

**Q54. What message did Saul send to Jesse?**  
**Answer:** Let David, I pray thee, stand before me.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:22

**Q55. Why did Saul want David to stand before him?**  
**Answer:** For he hath found favour in my sight.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:22

### 1 Samuel 16:23

**Q56. When would the evil spirit from God come upon Saul?**  
**Answer:** When the evil spirit from God was upon Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:23

**Q57. What instrument did David take and play with his hand?**  
**Answer:** An harp.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:23

**Q58. What happened to Saul when David played?**  
**Answer:** Saul was refreshed, and was well, and the evil spirit departed from him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 16:23
