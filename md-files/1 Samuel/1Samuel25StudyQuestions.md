# 1 Samuel 25 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 25:1

**Q1. Who died in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Samuel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 25:1

**Q2. Who gathered together and lamented him?**  
**Answer:** All the Israelites.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 25:1

**Q3. Where did David go down after Samuel was buried?**  
**Answer:** To the wilderness of Paran.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 25:1
