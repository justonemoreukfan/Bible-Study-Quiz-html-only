# 1 Samuel 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 3:1

**Q1. Who ministered unto the LORD before Eli?**  
**Answer:** The child Samuel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:1

**Q2. How was the word of the LORD described in those days?**  
**Answer:** Precious.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:1

**Q3. What was there regarding open vision?**  
**Answer:** There was no open vision.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:1

---

### 1 Samuel 3:2

**Q1. Whose eyes began to wax dim at that time?**  
**Answer:** Eli's.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:2

**Q2. What was Eli unable to do because of his eyes?**  
**Answer:** He could not see.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:2

**Q3. Where was Eli laid?**  
**Answer:** In his place.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:2

---

### 1 Samuel 3:3

**Q1. Who was laid down to sleep before the lamp of God went out?**  
**Answer:** Samuel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:3

**Q2. In what specific place was Samuel laid down?**  
**Answer:** In the temple of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:3

**Q3. What else was in the temple of the LORD where the ark of God was?**  
**Answer:** The ark of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:3

---

### 1 Samuel 3:4

**Q1. Who called unto Samuel?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:4

**Q2. How did Samuel respond when called?**  
**Answer:** He answered, Here am I.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:4

---

### 1 Samuel 3:5

**Q1. To whom did Samuel run after being called?**  
**Answer:** Unto Eli.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:5

**Q2. What did Samuel say to Eli when he ran to him?**  
**Answer:** Here am I; for thou calledst me.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:5

**Q3. What did Eli say he did not do?**  
**Answer:** I called not; lie down again.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:5

---

### 1 Samuel 3:6

**Q1. What did the LORD do yet again?**  
**Answer:** Called yet again, Samuel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:6

**Q2. What did Samuel do upon hearing the second call?**  
**Answer:** Arose and went to Eli.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:6

**Q3. What did Eli answer the second time?**  
**Answer:** I called not, my son; lie down again.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:6

---

### 1 Samuel 3:7

**Q1. What had Samuel not yet known?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:7

**Q2. What had not yet been revealed unto Samuel?**  
**Answer:** The word of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:7

---

### 1 Samuel 3:8

**Q1. How many times did the LORD call Samuel?**  
**Answer:** The third time.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:8

**Q2. What did Samuel do when he arose and went to Eli the third time?**  
**Answer:** Said, Here am I; for thou didst call me.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:8

**Q3. What did Eli perceive?**  
**Answer:** That the LORD had called the child.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:8

---

### 1 Samuel 3:9

**Q1. What did Eli tell Samuel to do?**  
**Answer:** Go, lie down.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:9

**Q2. If He shall call thee, what did Eli say Samuel should answer?**  
**Answer:** Speak, LORD; for thy servant heareth.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:9

**Q3. What did Samuel do after receiving instructions from Eli?**  
**Answer:** Went and lay down in his place.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:9

---

### 1 Samuel 3:10

**Q1. Who came and stood and called as at other times?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:10

**Q2. What names or phrases did the LORD use when calling?**  
**Answer:** Samuel, Samuel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:10

**Q3. How did Samuel answer the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Speak; for thy servant heareth.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:10

---

### 1 Samuel 3:11

**Q1. What did the LORD say to Samuel?**  
**Answer:** Behold, I will do a thing in Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:11

**Q2. What effect will the thing have on everyone that hears it?**  
**Answer:** Both the ears of every one that heareth it shall tingle.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:11

---

### 1 Samuel 3:12

**Q1. Against whom will the LORD perform all His words?**  
**Answer:** Against Eli.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:12

**Q2. From where to where will the LORD perform His words against Eli?**  
**Answer:** From the beginning even unto the end.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:12

---

### 1 Samuel 3:13

**Q1. What did the LORD declare He would judge?**  
**Answer:** Eli's house for ever.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:13

**Q2. Why did the LORD say He would judge Eli's house?**  
**Answer:** For the iniquity which he knoweth.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:13

**Q3. What did Eli's sons do to themselves, and how did Eli react?**  
**Answer:** His sons made themselves vile, and he restrained them not.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:13

---

### 1 Samuel 3:14

**Q1. What did the LORD swear unto the house of Eli?**  
**Answer:** That the iniquity of Eli's house shall not be purged with sacrifice nor offering for ever.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:14

**Q2. What will not purge the iniquity of Eli's house?**  
**Answer:** Sacrifice nor offering.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:14

**Q3. For how long did the LORD swear this?**  
**Answer:** For ever.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:14

---

### 1 Samuel 3:15

**Q1. Until when did Samuel lie?**  
**Answer:** Until the morning.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:15

**Q2. What did Samuel open in the morning?**  
**Answer:** The doors of the house of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:15

**Q3. What was Samuel afraid to do with the vision?**  
**Answer:** Show Eli the vision.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:15

---

### 1 Samuel 3:16

**Q1. Who called Samuel?**  
**Answer:** Eli.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:16

**Q2. What did Eli call Samuel?**  
**Answer:** Samuel, my son.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:16

**Q3. How did Samuel answer Eli?**  
**Answer:** Here am I.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:16

---

### 1 Samuel 3:17

**Q1. What did Eli ask Samuel the LORD had said?**  
**Answer:** What is the thing that the LORD hath said unto thee?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:17

**Q2. What did Eli tell Samuel not to do?**  
**Answer:** I pray thee hide it not from me.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:17

**Q3. What warning did Eli give Samuel if he hid anything?**  
**Answer:** God do so to thee, and more also, if thou hide any thing from me of all the things that he said unto thee.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:17

---

### 1 Samuel 3:18

**Q1. What did Samuel tell Eli?**  
**Answer:** Every whit, and hid nothing from him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:18

**Q2. What did Eli say after hearing the words?**  
**Answer:** It is the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:18

**Q3. What else did Eli say regarding what the LORD should do?**  
**Answer:** Let him do what seemeth him good.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:18

---

### 1 Samuel 3:19

**Q1. Who grew as he was with him?**  
**Answer:** Samuel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:19

**Q2. Who was with Samuel as he grew?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:19

**Q3. What did the LORD let none of fall to the ground?**  
**Answer:** None of his words.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:19

---

### 1 Samuel 3:20

**Q1. Who from Dan to Beersheba knew that Samuel was established?**  
**Answer:** All Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:20

**Q2. As what was Samuel established?**  
**Answer:** A prophet of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:20

**Q3. What geographical bounds are mentioned for all Israel?**  
**Answer:** From Dan even to Beersheba.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:20

---

### 1 Samuel 3:21

**Q1. Where did the LORD appear again?**  
**Answer:** In Shiloh.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:21

**Q2. How did the LORD reveal Himself to Samuel in Shiloh?**  
**Answer:** By the word of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:21

**Q3. To whom did the LORD reveal Himself by His word in Shiloh?**  
**Answer:** To Samuel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 3:21
