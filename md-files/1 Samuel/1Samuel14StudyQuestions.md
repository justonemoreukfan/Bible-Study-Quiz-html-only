# 1 Samuel 14 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 14:1

**Q1. Who said unto the young man that bare his armour, Come, and let us go over to the Philistines' garrison?**  
**Answer:** Jonathan the son of Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 14:1

**Q2. Whose armourbearer was addressed by Jonathan to go over to the Philistines' garrison?**  
**Answer:** The young man that bare his armour.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 14:1

**Q3. Whose garrison did Jonathan propose to go over to?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines' garrison.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 14:1

### 1 Samuel 14:2

**Q24. Where did Saul tarry in the uttermost part of Gibeah?**  
**Answer:** Under a pomegranate tree which is in Migron.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 14:2

**Q25. About how many men were with Saul?**  
**Answer:** About six hundred men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 14:2

**Q26. Who was with Saul among the people, whose son was Ahiah?**  
**Answer:** Ahiah, the son of Ahitub, Ichabod's brother, the son of Phinehas, the son of Eli, the LORD'S priest in Shiloh.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 14:2

**Q28. Whose brother was Ahitub?**  
**Answer:** Ichabod's brother.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 14:3

### 1 Samuel 14:4

**Q30. What was between the passages, by which Jonathan sought to go over unto the Philistines' garrison?**  
**Answer:** A sharp rock on the one side, and a sharp rock on the other side.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 14:4

**Q31. What were the names of the two sharp rocks?**  
**Answer:** Bozez and Seneh.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 14:4

**Q32. Where was the forefront of the one rock situated?**  
**Answer:** Overward against Michmash.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 14:4
