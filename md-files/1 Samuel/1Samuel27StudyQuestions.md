# 1 Samuel 27 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 27:1

**Q1. What did David say in his heart concerning himself?**  
**Answer:** I shall now perish one day by the hand of Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:1

**Q2. What did David conclude was the best thing for him to do?**  
**Answer:** To escape safely into the land of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:1

**Q3. What would Saul then despair of, according to David?**  
**Answer:** Of seeking him any more in any coast of Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:1

---

### 1 Samuel 27:2

**Q1. Who arose and passed over with the six hundred men?**  
**Answer:** David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:2

**Q2. Unto whom did David pass over?**  
**Answer:** Achish, the son of Maoch, king of Gath.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:2

---

### 1 Samuel 27:3

**Q1. With whom did David dwell at Gath?**  
**Answer:** Achish.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:3

**Q2. Who was with David besides his men?**  
**Answer:** His two wives, Ahinoam the Jezreelitess, and Abigail the Carmelitess, Nabal's wife.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:3

---

### 1 Samuel 27:4

**Q1. What was told to Saul concerning David?**  
**Answer:** That David was fled to Gath.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:4

**Q2. What did Saul do after he was told David fled to Gath?**  
**Answer:** He sought no more again for him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:4

---

### 1 Samuel 27:5

**Q1. What did David say unto Achish?**  
**Answer:** If I have now found grace in thine eyes, let them give me a place in some town in the country.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:5

**Q2. Why did David ask Achish for a place in the country?**  
**Answer:** For why should thy servant dwell in the royal city with thee?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:5

---

### 1 Samuel 27:6

**Q1. What did Achish give him that day?**  
**Answer:** Ziklag.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:6

**Q2. What happened to Ziklag because it was given to David?**  
**Answer:** It pertaineth unto the kings of Judah unto this day.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:6

---

### 1 Samuel 27:7

**Q1. How long was the time that David dwelt in the country of the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** A full year and four months.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:7

---

### 1 Samuel 27:8

**Q1. Who went up and invaded the Geshurites, and the Gezrites, and the Amalekites?**  
**Answer:** David and his men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:8

**Q2. Of what nations were those inhabitants from of old?**  
**Answer:** The inhabitants of the land, as thou goest to Shur, even unto the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:8

---

### 1 Samuel 27:9

**Q1. What did David smite when he invaded the land?**  
**Answer:** Left neither man nor woman alive.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:9

**Q2. What possessions did David take away?**  
**Answer:** The sheep, and the oxen, and the asses, and the camels, and the apparel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:9

**Q3. To whom did David return after the smiting?**  
**Answer:** Achish.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:9

---

### 1 Samuel 27:10

**Q1. What did Achish ask David when he returned?**  
**Answer:** Where have ye made a road to day?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:10

**Q2. What did David say he had invaded?**  
**Answer:** The south of Judah, and the south of the Jerahmeelites, and the south of the Kenites.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:10

---

### 1 Samuel 27:11

**Q1. Why did David save neither man nor woman alive to bring tidings to Gath?**  
**Answer:** Lest they should tell on us, saying, So did David, and so will be his manner all the while he dwelleth in the country of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:11

---

### 1 Samuel 27:12

**Q1. What did Achish believe concerning David?**  
**Answer:** He hath made his people Israel utterly to abhor him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:12

**Q2. What conclusion did Achish reach about David's future relationship with him?**  
**Answer:** Therefore he shall be my servant for ever.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 27:12
