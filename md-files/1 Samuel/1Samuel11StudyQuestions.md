# 1 Samuel 11 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 11:1

**Q1. Who came up, and against what city did he encamp?**  
**Answer:** Nahash the Ammonite came up, and encamped against Jabeshgilead.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:1

**Q2. What did all the men of Jabesh say unto Nahash?**  
**Answer:** Make a covenant with us, and we will serve thee.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:1

**Q3. What condition did Nahash the Ammonite set for making a covenant with them?**  
**Answer:** That I may thrust out all your right eyes, and lay it for a reproach upon all Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:1

---

### 1 Samuel 11:2

**Q1. What did the elders of Jabesh say to Nahash regarding the terms?**  
**Answer:** Give us seven days' respite, that we may send messengers unto all the coasts of Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:2

**Q2. What would happen if there was no man to save them?**  
**Answer:** Then we will come out to thee.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:2

**Q3. To whom did the messengers come?**  
**Answer:** To Gibeah of Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:2

---

### 1 Samuel 11:3

**Q1. In whose ears did the messengers speak the tidings?**  
**Answer:** In the ears of the people.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:3

**Q2. What was the reaction of all the people?**  
**Answer:** All the people lifted up their voices, and wept.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:3

**Q3. Who came out of the field after the herd, and what did he ask?**  
**Answer:** Saul came after the herd out of the field; and Saul said, What aileth the people that they weep?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:3

---

### 1 Samuel 11:4

**Q1. Whose tidings did they tell Saul?**  
**Answer:** The tidings of the men of Jabesh.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:4

**Q2. Where did Saul come?**  
**Answer:** To Gibeah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:4

**Q3. Whose voices did the people lift up?**  
**Answer:** The voices of the men of Jabesh.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:4

---

### 1 Samuel 11:5

**Q1. Where was Saul coming from?**  
**Answer:** Out of the field.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:5

**Q2. What was Saul following?**  
**Answer:** The herd.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:5

**Q3. What question did Saul ask the people?**  
**Answer:** What aileth the people that they weep?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:5

---

### 1 Samuel 11:6

**Q1. What came upon Saul when he heard those tidings?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:6

**Q2. How did Saul's anger kindle?**  
**Answer:** His anger was kindled greatly.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:6

**Q3. Whose tidings caused Saul's anger to be kindled?**  
**Answer:** The tidings of the men of Jabesh.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:6

---

### 1 Samuel 11:7

**Q1. What did Saul take and hew in pieces?**  
**Answer:** A yoke of oxen.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:7

**Q2. How did Saul send the pieces throughout all the coasts of Israel?**  
**Answer:** By the hands of messengers.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:7

**Q3. What message went with the pieces concerning those who came not forth after Saul and Samuel?**  
**Answer:** So shall it be done unto his oxen.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:7

---

### 1 Samuel 11:8

**Q1. Where did Saul number the people?**  
**Answer:** In Bezek.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:8

**Q2. How many children of Israel were there?**  
**Answer:** Three hundred thousand.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:8

**Q3. How many men of Judah were there?**  
**Answer:** Thirty thousand.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:8

---

### 1 Samuel 11:9

**Q1. What did they say to the messengers that came?**  
**Answer:** Thus shall ye say unto the men of Jabeshgilead, Tomorrow, by that time the sun be hot, ye shall have help.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:9

**Q2. To whom were the messengers to speak these words?**  
**Answer:** Unto the men of Jabeshgilead.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:9

**Q3. When did the messengers return and tell the men of Jabesh?**  
**Answer:** When the messengers came and told the men of Jabesh.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:9

---

### 1 Samuel 11:10

**Q1. What did the men of Jabesh say to Nahash the Ammonite?**  
**Answer:** Tomorrow we will come out unto you, and ye shall do with us all that seemeth good unto you.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:10

**Q2. When did the men of Jabesh say they would come out to Nahash?**  
**Answer:** Tomorrow.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:10

**Q3. What did the men of Jabesh say Nahash could do with them?**  
**Answer:** All that seemeth good unto you.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:10

---

### 1 Samuel 11:11

**Q1. On the morrow, into how many companies did Saul put the people?**  
**Answer:** Three companies.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:11

**Q2. When did they come into the midst of the host?**  
**Answer:** In the morning watch.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:11

**Q3. What did they do to the Ammonites until the heat of the day?**  
**Answer:** Slew the Ammonites until the heat of the day.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:11

---

### 1 Samuel 11:12

**Q1. What did the people say to Samuel?**  
**Answer:** Who is he that said, Shall Saul reign over us? bring the men, that we may put them to death.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:12

**Q2. Whose reign was being questioned by those people?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:12

**Q3. What did the people want to do to those men?**  
**Answer:** Put them to death.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:12

---

### 1 Samuel 11:13

**Q1. Who spoke up and said no man should be put to death that day?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:13

**Q2. What reason did Saul give for not putting any man to death?**  
**Answer:** For to day the LORD hath wrought salvation in Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:13

**Q3. Who had wrought salvation in Israel that day?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:13

---

### 1 Samuel 11:14

**Q1. What did Samuel say to the people?**  
**Answer:** Come, and let us go to Gilgal, and renew the kingdom there.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:14

**Q2. Where did Samuel suggest they go?**  
**Answer:** To Gilgal.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:14

**Q3. What did they intend to do in Gilgal?**  
**Answer:** Renew the kingdom there.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:14

---

### 1 Samuel 11:15

**Q1. Where did all the people go?**  
**Answer:** To Gilgal.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:15

**Q2. Whom did they make king before the LORD in Gilgal?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:15

**Q3. What sacrifices of peace offerings did they sacrifice before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Saul and all the men of Israel rejoiced greatly.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 11:15
