# 1 Samuel 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 6:1

**Q1. Where was the ark of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** In the country of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:1

**Q2. How long was the ark of the LORD in the country of the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** Seven months.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:1

**Q3. Whose ark was in the country of the Philistines for seven months?**  
**Answer:** The ark of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:1

---

### 1 Samuel 6:2

**Q1. Who did the Philistines call for?**  
**Answer:** The priests and the diviners.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:2

**Q2. What did the Philistines ask the priests and the diviners?**  
**Answer:** What shall we do to the ark of the LORD?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:2

**Q3. What did the Philistines ask the priests and diviners to tell them regarding sending the ark away?**  
**Answer:** Wherewith shall we send it to his place?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:2

---

### 1 Samuel 6:3

**Q1. What did they say the Philistines should not send away?**  
**Answer:** The ark of the God of Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:3

**Q2. What should be returned to the ark if it is sent away?**  
**Answer:** A trespass offering.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:3

**Q3. What would happen if the Philistines returned a trespass offering?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall be healed, and it shall be known to you why his hand is not removed from you.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:3

---

### 1 Samuel 6:4

**Q1. What question did they ask concerning the trespass offering?**  
**Answer:** What shall be the trespass offering which we shall return to him?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:4

**Q2. What answer was given concerning the number of golden emerods?**  
**Answer:** Five golden emerods.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:4

**Q3. According to what number were the golden mice and golden emerods to be made?**  
**Answer:** According to the number of the lords of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:4

---

### 1 Samuel 6:5

**Q1. What kind of images were the Philistines commanded to make?**  
**Answer:** Images of your emerods, and images of your mice that mar the land.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:5

**Q2. Whose glory were the Philistines instructed to give?**  
**Answer:** Unto the God of Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:5

**Q3. What might God lighten from off the Philistines, their gods, and their land?**  
**Answer:** His hand.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:5

---

### 1 Samuel 6:6

**Q1. Why should the Philistines not harden their hearts?**  
**Answer:** As the Egyptians and Pharaoh hardened their hearts.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:6

**Q2. What did God do when the Egyptians and Pharaoh were hardened?**  
**Answer:** He wrought wonderfully among them.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:6

**Q3. After God wrought wonderfully among the Egyptians, what did they do?**  
**Answer:** They let the people go, and they departed.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:6

---

### 1 Samuel 6:7

**Q1. What did they advise to make?**  
**Answer:** A new cart.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:7

**Q2. What kind of cows were to be tied to the new cart?**  
**Answer:** Two milch kine, on which there hath come no yoke.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:7

**Q3. What were the kine to be tied to do with the cart?**  
**Answer:** Tie the kine to the cart, and bring their calves home from them.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:7

---

### 1 Samuel 6:8

**Q1. What was to be taken and laid upon the cart?**  
**Answer:** The ark of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:8

**Q2. Where were the jewels of gold to be put?**  
**Answer:** In a coffer by the side thereof.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:8

**Q3. Where were the jewels of gold returned as a trespass offering to be placed?**  
**Answer:** By the side of the ark.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:8

---

### 1 Samuel 6:9

**Q1. By what way was the ark to go up if it went by the way of his own coast?**  
**Answer:** To Bethshemesh.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:9

**Q2. Who did they say did this great evil to the Philistines if the ark did not go by its own coast?**  
**Answer:** It was not his hand that smote us.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:9

**Q3. If the ark went to Bethshemesh, what would it be a chance that happened to them?**  
**Answer:** It was a chance that happened to us.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:9

---

### 1 Samuel 6:10

**Q1. What did the men do according to the instructions?**  
**Answer:** Did so.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:10

**Q2. What kind of kine did the men take?**  
**Answer:** Two milch kine.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:10

**Q3. What did the men do to the calves?**  
**Answer:** Tied the kine to the cart, and shut up their calves at home.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:10

---

### 1 Samuel 6:11

**Q1. What was laid upon the cart?**  
**Answer:** The ark of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:11

**Q2. What else was put upon the cart besides the ark?**  
**Answer:** The coffer and the mice of gold and the images of their emerods.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:11

**Q3. Whose images were put in the coffer?**  
**Answer:** The mice of gold and the images of their emerods.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:11

---

### 1 Samuel 6:12

**Q1. Straight in what direction did the kine go?**  
**Answer:** The way to Bethshemesh.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:12

**Q2. What did the kine do along the highway as they went?**  
**Answer:** Went along the highway, lowing as they went, and turned not aside to the right hand or to the left.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:12

**Q3. Who followed after the kine unto the border of Bethshemesh?**  
**Answer:** The lords of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:12

---

### 1 Samuel 6:13

**Q1. Who of Bethshemesh were reaping their wheat harvest in the valley?**  
**Answer:** They of Bethshemesh.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:13

**Q2. What did the people of Bethshemesh do when they lifted up their eyes?**  
**Answer:** Saw the ark, and rejoiced to see it.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:13

**Q3. When the people of Bethshemesh lifted up their eyes and saw the ark, what did they do?**  
**Answer:** Rejoiced to see it.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:13

---

### 1 Samuel 6:14

**Q1. Into whose field did the cart come?**  
**Answer:** Into the field of Joshua a Bethshemite.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:14

**Q2. Where did the cart stand still?**  
**Answer:** There, where there was a great stone.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:14

**Q3. What did they break up and offer as a burnt offering to the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The wood of the cart, and offered the kine a burnt offering unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:14

---

### 1 Samuel 6:15

**Q1. Who took down the ark of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The Levites.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:15

**Q2. What else was taken down with the ark?**  
**Answer:** The coffer that was with it, wherein the jewels of gold were.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:15

**Q3. Where were the ark and the coffer put?**  
**Answer:** Upon the great stone.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:15

---

### 1 Samuel 6:16

**Q1. When the five lords of the Philistines saw it, what did they do?**  
**Answer:** Returned to Ekron the same day.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:16

**Q2. To where did the five lords of the Philistines return?**  
**Answer:** To Ekron.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:16

**Q3. On what day did the five lords of the Philistines return to Ekron?**  
**Answer:** The same day.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:16

---

### 1 Samuel 6:17

**Q1. What golden emerods did the Philistines return for a trespass offering?**  
**Answer:** These are the golden emerods which the Philistines returned for a trespass offering.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:17

**Q2. For Ashdod, Gaza, Askelon, Gath, and Ekron, how many emerods of gold were returned?**  
**Answer:** One for Ashdod, one for Gaza, one for Askelon, one for Gath, one for Ekron.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:17

**Q3. How many cities of the Philistines were represented by the golden emerods?**  
**Answer:** Five.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:17

---

### 1 Samuel 6:18

**Q1. According to what were the golden mice numbered?**  
**Answer:** According to the number of all the cities of the Philistines belonging to the five lords.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:18

**Q2. What kind of cities did the Philistines have based on the golden mice?**  
**Answer:** Both of fenced cities, and of country villages.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:18

**Q3. Unto what great stone did the great stone remain unto this day?**  
**Answer:** Unto the great stone of Abel, whereon they set down the ark of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:18

---

### 1 Samuel 6:19

**Q1. Whom did the LORD smite because they had looked into the ark of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The men of Bethshemesh.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:19

**Q2. How many men of the people did the LORD smite?**  
**Answer:** Fifty thousand and threescore and ten men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:19

**Q3. How did the people mourn after the LORD smote them?**  
**Answer:** Because the LORD had smitten many of the people with a great slaughter.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:19

---

### 1 Samuel 6:20

**Q1. What question did the men of Bethshemesh ask?**  
**Answer:** Who is able to stand before this holy LORD God?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:20

**Q2. To whom shall the ark go up from us?**  
**Answer:** To whom shall he go up from us?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:20

**Q3. Whose presence was questioned by the men of Bethshemesh?**  
**Answer:** The holy LORD God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:20

---

### 1 Samuel 6:21

**Q1. To whom did the inhabitants of Kirjathjearim send messengers?**  
**Answer:** The inhabitants of Kirjathjearim.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:21

**Q2. Who did the messengers say had brought again the ark of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:21

**Q3. Where did the messengers tell the inhabitants of Kirjathjearim to come down and fetch the ark?**  
**Answer:** Come ye down, fetch it up to you.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 6:21
