# 1 Samuel 15 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 15:1

**Q1. Who sent Samuel to anoint Saul to be king?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:1

**Q2. Over whom did the LORD send Samuel to anoint Saul to be king?**  
**Answer:** Over his people, over Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:1

**Q3. What did Samuel tell Saul to do regarding the message he was about to hear?**  
**Answer:** Hearken thou unto the voice of the words of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:1

---

### 1 Samuel 15:2

**Q1. Whose words did the LORD of hosts say he would remember?**  
**Answer:** That which Amalek did to Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:2

**Q2. How did Amalek lay wait for Israel?**  
**Answer:** In the way, when he came up from Egypt.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:2

---

### 1 Samuel 15:3

**Q1. What did the LORD tell Saul to go and smite?**  
**Answer:** Amalek.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:3

**Q2. What did the LORD command to be utterly destroyed?**  
**Answer:** All that they have.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:3

**Q3. Name the specific groups the LORD commanded to be slain.**  
**Answer:** Both man and woman, infant and suckling, ox and sheep, camel and ass.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:3

---

### 1 Samuel 15:4

**Q1. Whom did Saul gather together?**  
**Answer:** The people.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:4

**Q2. Where did Saul number the people?**  
**Answer:** In Telaim.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:4

**Q3. How many footmen were numbered from Judah?**  
**Answer:** Ten thousand men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:4

---

### 1 Samuel 15:5

**Q1. To what city of Amalek did Saul come?**  
**Answer:** A city of Amalek.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:5

**Q2. What did Saul do in the valley?**  
**Answer:** Laid wait in the valley.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:5

---

### 1 Samuel 15:6

**Q1. What did Saul say to the Kenites?**  
**Answer:** Go, depart, get you down from among the Amalekites.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:6

**Q2. Why did Saul show kindness to the Kenites?**  
**Answer:** Because ye showed kindness to all the children of Israel, when they came up out of Egypt.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:6

**Q3. Who departed from among the Amalekites?**  
**Answer:** The Kenites.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:6

---

### 1 Samuel 15:7

**Q1. What did Saul smite?**  
**Answer:** The Amalekites.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:7

**Q2. From where did Saul smite the Amalekites?**  
**Answer:** From Havilah until thou comest to Shur, that is over against Egypt.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:7

---

### 1 Samuel 15:8

**Q1. Whom did Saul take alive?**  
**Answer:** Agag the king of the Amalekites.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:8

**Q2. What did Saul utterly destroy with the edge of the sword?**  
**Answer:** All the people.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:8

---

### 1 Samuel 15:9

**Q1. Whom did Saul and the people spare?**  
**Answer:** Agag, and the best of the sheep, and of the oxen, and of the fatlings, and the lambs, and all that was good.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:9

---

### 1 Samuel 15:10

**Q1. To whom came the word of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Unto Samuel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:10

---

### 1 Samuel 15:11

**Q1. What did the LORD say He repented of making?**  
**Answer:** That I have set up Saul to be king.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:11

---

### 1 Samuel 15:12

**Q1. Where did Samuel rise early to meet Saul in the morning?**  
**Answer:** To Carmel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:12

---

### 1 Samuel 15:13

**Q1. What did Samuel say to Saul?**  
**Answer:** Blessed be thou of the LORD: I have performed the commandment of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:13

---

### 1 Samuel 15:14

**Q1. What did Samuel ask when he heard the bleating of the sheep in his ears?**  
**Answer:** What meaneth then this bleating of the sheep in mine ears, and the lowing of the oxen which I hear?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:14

---

### 1 Samuel 15:15

**Q1. From where did Saul say they had brought the best of the sheep and oxen?**  
**Answer:** From the Amalekites.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:15

---

### 1 Samuel 15:16

**Q1. What did Samuel say to Saul?**  
**Answer:** Stay, and I will tell thee what the LORD hath said to me this night.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:16

---

### 1 Samuel 15:17

**Q1. When Saul was little in his own sight, who made him the head?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God of Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:17

---

### 1 Samuel 15:18

**Q1. Who did the LORD send Saul on a journey to utterly destroy?**  
**Answer:** The sinners the Amalekites.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:18

---

### 1 Samuel 15:19

**Q1. Why did Saul fly upon the spoil instead of obeying the voice of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** And didst evil in the sight of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:19

---

### 1 Samuel 15:20

**Q1. What did Saul say he had obeyed?**  
**Answer:** The voice of the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:20

---

### 1 Samuel 15:21

**Q1. What did the people take of the spoil to sacrifice unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The chief of the things which should have been utterly destroyed, sheep and oxen.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:21

---

### 1 Samuel 15:22

**Q1. What does the LORD have as great delight in, as in obeying the voice of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** In burnt offerings and sacrifices.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:22

---

### 1 Samuel 15:23

**Q1. What is rebellion as?**  
**Answer:** As the sin of witchcraft.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:23

---

### 1 Samuel 15:24

**Q1. What did Saul say he had sinned against because he feared the people?**  
**Answer:** The commandment of the LORD, and thy words.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:24

---

### 1 Samuel 15:25

**Q1. What did Saul ask Samuel to turn again with him for?**  
**Answer:** That I may worship the LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:25

---

### 1 Samuel 15:26

**Q1. Whom did Samuel refuse to turn again with?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:26

---

### 1 Samuel 15:27

**Q1. What happened as Samuel turned about to go away?**  
**Answer:** Saul laid hold upon the skirt of his mantle, and it rent.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:27

---

### 1 Samuel 15:28

**Q1. From whom has the LORD rent the kingdom of Israel this day?**  
**Answer:** From thee.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:28

---

### 1 Samuel 15:29

**Q1. Who is the Strength of Israel that will not lie nor repent?**  
**Answer:** The Strength of Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:29

---

### 1 Samuel 15:30

**Q1. Whom did Saul ask Samuel to honor before the elders of his people?**  
**Answer:** Me now.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:30

---

### 1 Samuel 15:31

**Q1. Who turned again after Saul?**  
**Answer:** Samuel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:31

---

### 1Samuel 15:32

**Q1. Who came unto Samuel delicately?**  
**Answer:** Agag king of the Amalekites.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:32

---

### 1 Samuel 15:33

**Q1. What did Samuel's sword do in Gilgal before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Hewed Agag in pieces.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:33

**Q1. Where did Samuel go?**  
**Answer:** To Ramah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 15:34

**Answer:** Samuel.
