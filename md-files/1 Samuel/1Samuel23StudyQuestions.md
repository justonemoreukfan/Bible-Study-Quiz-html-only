# 1 Samuel 23 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 23:1

**Q1. Who told David that the Philistines were fighting against Keilah?**  
**Answer:** They told David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 23:1

**Q2. What were the Philistines robbing?**  
**Answer:** The threshingfloors.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 23:1

**Q3. Against what place were the Philistines fighting?**  
**Answer:** Keilah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 23:1

---

### 1 Samuel 23:2

**Q1. Whom did David inquire of?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 23:2

**Q2. What did David ask if he should go and smite?**  
**Answer:** These Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 23:2

**Q3. What did the LORD say to David regarding the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** Go and smite the Philistines, and save Keilah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 23:2

---

### 1 Samuel 23:3

**Q1. What did David's men say they were in?**

**Verse:** 1 Samuel 23:3

**Answer:** Fear.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 23:3

**Q3. To where did David's men say they would go?**  
**Answer:** To Keilah against the armies of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 23:3

**Verse:** 1 Samuel 23:4
