# 1 Samuel 22 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 22:1

**Q1. Where did David depart from?**  
**Answer:** From thence.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:1

**Q2. Where did David escape to?**  
**Answer:** To the cave Adullam.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:1

**Q3. Who heard of David's escape and went down thither to him?**  
**Answer:** His brethren and all his father's house.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:1

---

### 1 Samuel 22:2

**Q4. Who gathered themselves unto him?**  
**Answer:** Every one that was in distress, every one that was in debt, and every one that was discontented.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:2

**Q5. What role did David become over them?**  
**Answer:** A captain over them.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:2

**Q6. About how many men were with him?**  
**Answer:** About four hundred men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:2

---

### 1 Samuel 22:3

**Q7. Where did David go from thence?**  
**Answer:** To Mizpeh of Moab.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:3

**Q8. What did David say unto the king of Moab?**  
**Answer:** Let my father and my mother, I pray thee, come forth, and be with you, till I know what God will do for me.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:3

---

### 1 Samuel 22:4

**Q9. Who did David bring before the king of Moab?**  
**Answer:** His father and his mother.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:4

**Q10. Where did they dwell all the while that David was in the hold?**  
**Answer:** With him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:4

---

### 1 Samuel 22:5

**Q11. Who said unto David, Abide not in the hold?**  
**Answer:** The prophet Gad.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:5

**Q12. Where did the prophet Gad tell David to depart and go?**  
**Answer:** Into the land of Judah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:5

**Q13. What did David do after Gad spoke to him?**  
**Answer:** David departed, and came into the forest of Hareth.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:5

---

### 1 Samuel 22:6

**Q14. Who heard that David was discovered, and the men that were with him?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:6

**Q15. Where was Saul abiding when he heard this?**  
**Answer:** In Gibeah under a tree in Ramah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:6

---

### 1 Samuel 22:7

**Q16. What did Saul say unto his servants that stood about him?**  
**Answer:** Hear now, ye Benjamites; will the son of Jesse give every one of you fields and vineyards, and make you all captains of thousands, and captains of hundreds?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:7

---

### 1 Samuel 22:8

**Q17. What did Saul accuse all of them of doing?**  
**Answer:** Conspiracy against him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:8

**Q18. What did Saul claim no one showed him?**  
**Answer:** When his son has made a league with the son of Jesse.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:8

**Q19. Why did Saul say there was none of them that was sorry for him?**  
**Answer:** Because his son has stirred up his servant against him, to lie in wait, as at this day.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:8

---

### 1 Samuel 22:9

**Q20. Who answered and said, I saw the son of Jesse coming to Nob, to Ahimelech the son of Ahitub?**  
**Answer:** Doeg the Edomite.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:9

---

### 1 Samuel 22:10

**Q21. Who did Ahimelech inquire of the Lord for?**  
**Answer:** David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:10

**Q22. What else did Ahimelech give David?**  
**Answer:** Victuals.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:10

**Q23. What weapon did Ahimelech give David?**  
**Answer:** The sword of Goliath the Philistine.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:10

---

### 1 Samuel 22:11

**Q24. Who did the king send to call Ahimelech the priest?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:11

**Q25. Who besides Ahimelech was sent for by the king?**  
**Answer:** All his father's house, the priests that were in Nob.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:11

**Q26. Where did they come?**  
**Answer:** All of them to the king.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:11

---

### 1 Samuel 22:12

**Q27. What did Saul say when Ahimelech answered?**  
**Answer:** Hear now, thou son of Ahitub.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:12

**Q28. What was Ahimelech's reply to the king?**  
**Answer:** Here I am, my lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:12

---

### 1 Samuel 22:13

**Q29. What did Saul ask Ahimelech about why they had conspired against him?**  
**Answer:** Why have ye conspired against me, thou and the son of Jesse, in that thou hast given him bread, and a sword, and hast inquired of God for him, that he should rise against me, to lie in wait, as at this day?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:13

---

### 1 Samuel 22:14

**Q30. How did Ahimelech answer the king?**  
**Answer:** And who is so faithful among all thy servants as David, which is the king's son in law, and goeth at thy bidding, and is honourable in thine house?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:14

---

### 1 Samuel 22:15

**Q31. Did Ahimelech begin to inquire of God for David for the first time that day?**  
**Answer:** Did I then begin to inquire of God for him? be it far from me.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:15

**Q32. What did Ahimelech tell the king not to impute?**  
**Answer:** Anything unto his servant, nor to all the house of his father.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:15

**Q33. What did Ahimelech state he knew nothing of?**  
**Answer:** The king's servant knew nothing of all this, less or more.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:15

---

### 1 Samuel 22:16

**Q34. What did the king say Ahimelech would surely die for?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt surely die, Ahimelech, thou, and all thy father's house.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:16

---

### 1 Samuel 22:17

**Q35. Who did the king say to turn and slay?**  
**Answer:** The priests of the Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:17

**Q36. Why did Saul say their hand also is with David?**  
**Answer:** Because they knew when he fled, and did not shew it to him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:17

**Q37. Whose servants would not put forth their hand to fall upon the priests of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** The servants of the king.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:17

---

### 1 Samuel 22:18

**Q38. Who did the king turn and command to fall upon the priests?**  
**Answer:** Doeg.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:18

**Q39. What did Doeg the Edomite do to the priests?**  
**Answer:** He turned, and fell upon the priests.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:18

**Q40. How many persons that wore a linen ephod were slain that day?**  
**Answer:** Fourscore and five persons.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:18

---

### 1 Samuel 22:19

**Q41. What city of the priests did Doeg smite with the edge of the sword?**  
**Answer:** Nob.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:19

**Q42. Who did Doeg smite in the city of Nob?**  
**Answer:** Both men and women, children and sucklings, and oxen, and asses, and sheep.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:19

---

### 1 Samuel 22:20

**Q43. Who was the son of Ahimelech that escaped?**  
**Answer:** Abiathar.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:20

---

### 1 Samuel 22:21

**Q44. What did Abiathar shew David?**  
**Answer:** That Saul had slain the Lord's priests.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:21

---

### 1 Samuel 22:22

**Q45. Who did David say he knew was there that day?**  
**Answer:** Doeg the Edomite.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:22

---

### 1 Samuel 22:23

**Q46. What did David tell Abiathar not to fear?**  
**Answer:** Fear not.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:23

**Q47. What did David say sought his life?**  
**Answer:** He that seeketh my life seeketh thy life.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:23

**Q48. What did David promise Abiathar if he stayed with him?**  
**Answer:** With me thou shalt be in safeguard.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 22:23
