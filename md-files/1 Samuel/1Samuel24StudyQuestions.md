# 1 Samuel 24 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 24:1

**Q1. Who returned from following the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** Saul  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 24:1

**Q2. Who was it told that Saul had returned from following the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** David  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 24:1

**Q3. Where was David when he was told that Saul had returned?**  
**Answer:** In the wilderness of En-gedi  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 24:1

### 1 Samuel 24:2

**Q1. How many chosen men did Saul take with him?**  
**Answer:** Three thousand  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 24:2

**Q2. From where did Saul take three thousand chosen men?**  
**Answer:** Out of all Israel  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 24:2

**Q3. Whom did Saul and his men go to seek?**  
**Answer:** David and his men  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 24:2

### 1 Samuel 24:3

**Q1. To whose sheepcotes by the way did Saul come?**  
**Answer:** The sheepcotes by the way  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 24:3

**Q2. What was there by the sheepcotes?**  
**Answer:** A cave  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 24:3

**Q3. Who went into the cave?**  
**Answer:** Saul  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 24:3

### 1 Samuel 24:4

**Q1. Where were David and his men remaining?**  
**Answer:** In the sides of the cave  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 24:4

**Q2. What did the men of David say unto him?**  
**Answer:** Behold the day of which the LORD said unto thee...  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 24:4

**Q3. What did David do secretly?**  
**Answer:** Cut off the skirt of Saul's robe  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 24:4
