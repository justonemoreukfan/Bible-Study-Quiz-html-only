# 1 Samuel 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 10:1

**Q1. What did Samuel take in his hand?**  
**Answer:** A vial of oil.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:1

**Q2. Whose head did Samuel pour the oil upon?**  
**Answer:** Saul's head.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:1

**Q3. Whom did the Lord anoint to be captain over His inheritance?**  
**Answer:** Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:1

---

### 1 Samuel 10:2

**Q1. When thou art departed from me to day, where shalt thou find two men?**  
**Answer:** By Rachel's sepulchre in the border of Benjamin to Zelzah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:2

**Q2. What will the two men say unto thee concerning the asses?**  
**Answer:** That the asses which thou wentest to seek are found.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:2

**Q3. Whom will thy father leave the care of and sorrow for?**  
**Answer:** The asses.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:2

---

### 1 Samuel 10:3

**Q1. Where shalt thou go on forward from thence?**  
**Answer:** To the plain of Tabor.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:3

**Q2. How many men shall meet thee there going up to God to Bethel?**  
**Answer:** Three men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:3

**Q3. What three things will the men be carrying?**  
**Answer:** Three kids, three loaves of bread, and a bottle of wine.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:3

---

### 1 Samuel 10:4

**Q1. What will the men salute thee with?**  
**Answer:** Two loaves.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:4

**Q2. What will the men give unto thee out of their hands?**  
**Answer:** Two loaves of bread.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:4

---

### 1 Samuel 10:5

**Q1. After that, to what hill of God shalt thou come?**  
**Answer:** The hill of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:5

**Q2. Who is at the hill of God where the garrison of the Philistines is?**  
**Answer:** The Philistines (the garrison).  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:5

**Q3. What instruments shall come down from the high place preceding a company of prophets?**  
**Answer:** A psaltery, and a tabret, and a pipe, and a harp.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:5

---

### 1 Samuel 10:6

**Q1. What Spirit of the Lord will come upon thee?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit of the Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:6

**Q2. What shall thou do when the Spirit of the Lord comes upon thee?**  
**Answer:** Prophesy with them.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:6

**Q3. Into what kind of man shalt thou be turned?**  
**Answer:** Another man.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:6

---

### 1 Samuel 10:7

**Q1. What shouldst thou do when these signs are come unto thee?**  
**Answer:** That thou do as occasion serve thee.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:7

**Q2. Who is with thee when thou doest as occasion serve thee?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:7

---

### 1 Samuel 10:8

**Q1. Whither shalt thou go down before me?**  
**Answer:** To Gilgal.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:8

**Q2. What will I come down unto thee to offer?**  
**Answer:** Burnt offerings, and to sacrifice sacrifices of peace offerings.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:8

**Q3. How many days shalt thou tarry till I come to thee?**  
**Answer:** Seven days.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:8

---

### 1 Samuel 10:9

**Q1. When Saul had turned his back to go from Samuel, what did God give him?**  
**Answer:** Another heart.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:9

**Q2. What happened to all those signs that came to pass that day?**  
**Answer:** They came to pass that day.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:9

---

### 1 Samuel 10:10

**Q1. When they came thither to the hill, what met him?**  
**Answer:** A company of prophets.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:10

**Q2. What Spirit came upon him?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit of God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:10

**Q3. What did Saul do among the prophets?**  
**Answer:** He prophesied among them.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:10

---

### 1 Samuel 10:11

**Q1. What did all that knew him before see that he did?**  
**Answer:** Prophesy among the prophets.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:11

**Q2. What did the people say one to another?**  
**Answer:** What is this that is come unto the son of Kish?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:11

**Q3. Who else is among the prophets?**  
**Answer:** Is Saul also among the prophets?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:11

---

### 1 Samuel 10:12

**Q1. Who answered and said, But who is their father?**  
**Answer:** One of the same place.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:12

**Q2. What became a proverb?**  
**Answer:** Is Saul also among the prophets?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:12

---

### 1 Samuel 10:13

**Q1. When he had made an end of prophesying, where did he come?**  
**Answer:** To the high place.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:13

---

### 1 Samuel 10:14

**Q1. Whom did Saul's uncle ask, Whither went ye?**  
**Answer:** Saul and his servant.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:14

**Q2. What did Saul say they went to seek?**  
**Answer:** The asses.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:14

**Q3. When they saw that the asses were nowhere, where did they come?**  
**Answer:** To Samuel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:14

---

### 1 Samuel 10:15

**Q1. Who said, Tell me, I pray thee, what Samuel said unto you?**  
**Answer:** Saul's uncle.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:15

---

### 1 Samuel 10:16

**Q1. What did Saul tell his uncle plainly?**  
**Answer:** That the asses were found.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:16

**Q2. What matter of the kingdom did Saul not tell him?**  
**Answer:** The matter of the kingdom, whereof Samuel spake.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:16

---

### 1 Samuel 10:17

**Q1. Whom did Samuel call together unto the Lord?**  
**Answer:** The people.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:17

**Q2. Where did Samuel call the people together?**  
**Answer:** To Mizpeh.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:17

---

### 1 Samuel 10:18

**Q1. What did Samuel say the Lord God of Israel brought up out of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Israel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:18

**Q2. Out of whose hand did the Lord deliver you?**  
**Answer:** The hand of the Egyptians, and out of the hand of all kingdoms that oppressed you.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:18

---

### 1 Samuel 10:19

**Q1. Whom have ye this day rejected?**  
**Answer:** Your God.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:19

**Q2. From who did God save you out of all your adversities and your tribulations?**  
**Answer:** Himself (Who himself saved you out of all your adversities and your tribulations).  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:19

**Q3. What did the people say to do unto them?**  
**Answer:** Set a king over us.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:19

---

### 1 Samuel 10:20

**Q1. When Samuel had caused all the tribes of Israel to come near, which tribe was taken?**  
**Answer:** The tribe of Benjamin.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:20

---

### 1 Samuel 10:21

**Q1. When he had caused the tribe of Benjamin to come near by their families, which family was taken?**  
**Answer:** The family of Matri.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:21

**Q2. Who was taken when the family of Matri was brought near?**  
**Answer:** Saul the son of Kish.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:21

**Q3. When they sought him, could he be found?**  
**Answer:** He could not be found.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:21

---

### 1 Samuel 10:22

**Q1. Whom did they enquire of further?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:22

**Q2. What did the Lord answer concerning the man?**  
**Answer:** Behold, he hath hid himself among the stuff.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:22

---

### 1 Samuel 10:23

**Q1. When they ran and fetched him from thence, where did he stand?**  
**Answer:** Among the people.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:23

**Q2. How much higher was he than any of the people from his shoulders and upward?**  
**Answer:** Higher than any of the people.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:23

---

### 1 Samuel 10:24

**Q1. What did Samuel say to all the people?**  
**Answer:** See ye him whom the Lord hath chosen?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:24

**Q2. Who is like him among all the people?**  
**Answer:** None like him.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:24

**Q3. What did all the people shout and say?**  
**Answer:** God save the king.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:24

---

### 1 Samuel 10:25

**Q1. What did Samuel tell the people?**  
**Answer:** The manner of the kingdom.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:25

**Q2. Where did Samuel write the manner of the kingdom?**  
**Answer:** In a book.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:25

**Q3. Where did Samuel lay up the book before the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Before the Lord.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:25

---

### 1 Samuel 10:26

**Q1. To where did Saul also go?**  
**Answer:** Home to Gibeah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:26

**Q2. Who went with Saul?**  
**Answer:** A band of men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:26

**Q3. Whose hearts had God touched?**  
**Answer:** The band of men whose hearts God had touched.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:26

---

### 1 Samuel 10:27

**Q1. Who said, How shall this man save us?**  
**Answer:** Certain children of Belial.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:27

**Q2. What did they despise him and bring him?**  
**Answer:** No presents.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:27

**Q3. What did Saul do as though he were deaf?**  
**Answer:** He held his peace.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 10:27
