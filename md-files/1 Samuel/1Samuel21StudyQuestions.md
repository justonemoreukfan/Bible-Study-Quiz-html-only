# 1 Samuel 21 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Samuel 21:1

**Q1. To what place came David to Ahimelech the priest?**  
**Answer:** Nob.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:1

**Q2. How did Ahimelech the priest react when he met David?**  
**Answer:** He was afraid at the meeting of David.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:1

**Q3. What question did Ahimelech ask David upon meeting him?**  
**Answer:** Why art thou alone, and no man with thee?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:1

---

### 1 Samuel 21:2

**Q1. What did David say the king had commanded him?**  
**Answer:** A business, and said unto me, Let no man know any thing of the business whereabout I send thee, and what I have commanded thee.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:2

**Q2. Who did David say he had appointed his servants to?**  
**Answer:** To such and such a place.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:2

---

### 1 Samuel 21:3

**Q1. What did David ask Ahimelech to give him under his hand?**  
**Answer:** Five loaves of bread.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:3

**Q2. What alternative to five loaves did David ask for?**  
**Answer:** What there is present.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:3

---

### 1 Samuel 21:4

**Q1. What did the priest answer David and say he did not have under his hand?**  
**Answer:** Common bread.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:4

**Q2. What kind of bread did the priest have under his hand?**  
**Answer:** Hallowed bread.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:4

**Q3. Under what condition did the priest say the hallowed bread was available?**  
**Answer:** If the young men have kept themselves at least from women.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:4

---

### 1 Samuel 21:5

**Q1. What did David answer the priest concerning women?**  
**Answer:** Of a truth women have been kept from us about these three days.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:5

**Q2. How did David describe the vessels (or clothes) of the young men when he came out?**  
**Answer:** Holy.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:5

**Q3. How was the bread described even though it was in a manner common?**  
**Answer:** Yea, though it were sanctified this day in the vessel.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:5

---

### 1 Samuel 21:6

**Q1. What did the priest give David since there was no bread there?**  
**Answer:** Hallowed bread.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:6

**Q2. What specific bread was given to David?**  
**Answer:** The shewbread.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:6

**Q3. When was the shewbread taken from before the Lord?**  
**Answer:** When it was taken away, to put hot bread in the day when it was taken away.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:6

---

### 1 Samuel 21:7

**Q1. Who was detained before the Lord on that day?**  
**Answer:** A certain man of the servants of Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:7

---

### 1 Samuel 21:8

**Q1. What did David ask Ahimelech if he had under his hand?**  
**Answer:** Spear or sword?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:8

**Q2. Why did David say he had neither brought his sword nor his weapons with him?**  
**Answer:** Because the king's business required haste.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:8

---

### 1 Samuel 21:9

**Q1. What weapon did the priest say was there?**  
**Answer:** The sword of Goliath the Philistine.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:9

**Q2. Where was Goliath's sword slain by David?**  
**Answer:** In the valley of Elah.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:9

**Q3. How was Goliath's sword wrapped and where was it kept?**  
**Answer:** It is here wrapped in a cloth behind the ephod.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:9

---

### 1 Samuel 21:10

**Q1. What did David do that day because of his fear of Saul?**  
**Answer:** Arose, and fled that day for fear of Saul.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:10

**Q2. To whom did David go when he fled from Saul?**  
**Answer:** Achish the king of Gath.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:10

---

### 1 Samuel 21:11

**Q1. What did the servants of Achish say about David?**  
**Answer:** Is not this David the king of the land?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:11

**Q2. What did they recall singing in dances concerning David?**  
**Answer:** Saul hath slain his thousands, and David his ten thousands.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:11

---

### 1 Samuel 21:12

**Q1. How did David take these words to heart?**  
**Answer:** And David laid up these words in his heart.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:12

**Q2. How did David feel regarding Achish the king of Gath?**  
**Answer:** Was sore afraid of Achish the king of Gath.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:12

---

### 1 Samuel 21:13

**Q1. What did David change before them?**  
**Answer:** His behaviour.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:13

**Q2. How did David feign himself in their hands?**  
**Answer:** Mad.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:13

**Q3. What actions did David perform on the doors of the gate?**  
**Answer:** Scrabbled on the doors of the gate, and let his spittle fall down upon his beard.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:13

---

### 1 Samuel 21:14

**Q1. What did Achish say to his servants after seeing David?**  
**Answer:** Lo, ye see the man is mad.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:14

**Q2. What question did Achish ask his servants about bringing David to him?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore then have ye brought him to me?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:14

---

### 1 Samuel 21:15

**Q1. What did Achish ask if he had a lack of?**  
**Answer:** Mad men.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:15

**Q2. What did Achish say David had done in his presence?**  
**Answer:** Played the mad man in my presence.  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:15

**Q3. What did Achish declare about David coming into his house?**  
**Answer:** Shall this fellow come into my house?  
**Verse:** 1 Samuel 21:15
