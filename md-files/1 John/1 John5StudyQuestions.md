# 1 John 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 John 5:1

**Q1. What is believed about Jesus in relation to Christ?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever believeth that Jesus is the Christ is born of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:1

**Q2. What is the relationship between loving him that begat and loving him also that is begotten of him?**  
**Answer:** Every one that loveth him that begat loveth him also that is begotten of him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:1

**Q3. Who is born of God according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever believeth that Jesus is the Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:1

---

### 1 John 5:2

**Q1. By what do we know that we love the children of God?**  
**Answer:** By this we know that we love the children of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:2

**Q2. When do we know that we love the children of God?**  
**Answer:** When we love God, and keep his commandments.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:2

**Q3. What must we do along with loving God to know we love His children?**  
**Answer:** Keep his commandments.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:2

---

### 1 John 5:3

**Q1. What is the love of God?**  
**Answer:** That we keep his commandments.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:3

**Q2. How are God's commandments described in relation to being grievous?**  
**Answer:** His commandments are not grievous.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:3

**Q3. What is defined as the love of God in this verse?**  
**Answer:** That we keep his commandments.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:3

---

### 1 John 5:4

**Q1. What is whatsoever is born of God said to do?**  
**Answer:** Overcometh the world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:4

**Q2. What is the victory that overcometh the world?**  
**Answer:** Even our faith.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:4

**Q3. What overcomes the world?**  
**Answer:** Our faith.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:4

---

### 1 John 5:5

**Q1. Who is he that overcometh the world?**  
**Answer:** He that believeth that Jesus is the Son of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:5

**Q2. What must a person believe to overcome the world?**  
**Answer:** That Jesus is the Son of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:5

**Q3. What does the person who believes Jesus is the Son of God do?**  
**Answer:** Overcometh the world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:5

---

### 1 John 5:6

**Q1. Who is he that came by water and blood?**  
**Answer:** This is he that came by water and blood, even Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:6

**Q2. Did he come by water only, or by water and blood?**  
**Answer:** Not by water only, but by water and blood.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:6

**Q3. What bears witness because the Spirit is truth?**  
**Answer:** And it is the Spirit that beareth witness.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:6

---

### 1 John 5:7

**Q1. How many are there that bear record in heaven?**  
**Answer:** There are three that bear record in heaven.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:7

**Q2. Who are the three that bear record in heaven?**  
**Answer:** The Father, the Word, and the Holy Ghost.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:7

**Q3. What are these three said to be?**  
**Answer:** These three are one.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:7

---

### 1 John 5:8

**Q1. How many are there that bear witness in earth?**  
**Answer:** There are three that bear witness in earth.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:8

**Q2. What are the three that bear witness in earth?**  
**Answer:** The spirit, and the water, and the blood.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:8

**Q3. What do these three agree in?**  
**Answer:** These three agree in one.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:8

---

### 1 John 5:9

**Q1. Whose witness do we receive?**  
**Answer:** If we receive the witness of men.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:9

**Q2. Whose witness is greater?**  
**Answer:** The witness of God is greater.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:9

**Q3. Of whom has God testified in this witness?**  
**Answer:** For this is the witness of God which he hath testified of his Son.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:9

---

### 1 John 5:10

**Q1. What does he that believeth on the Son of God have in him?**  
**Answer:** He hath the witness in himself.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:10

**Q2. What has he that believeth not God made Him?**  
**Answer:** He hath made him a liar.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:10

**Q3. Why has the unbeliever made God a liar?**  
**Answer:** Because he believeth not the record that God gave of his Son.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:10

---

### 1 John 5:11

**Q1. What is the record that God hath given to us?**  
**Answer:** That God hath given to us eternal life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:11

**Q2. Where is this eternal life located?**  
**Answer:** And this life is in his Son.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:11

**Q3. What has God given to us in His Son?**  
**Answer:** Eternal life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:11

---

### 1 John 5:12

**Q1. Who has life?**  
**Answer:** He that hath the Son hath life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:12

**Q2. Who does not have life?**  
**Answer:** He that hath not the Son of God hath not life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:12

**Q3. What is possessed by him that hath the Son?**  
**Answer:** Life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:12

---

### 1 John 5:13

**Q1. To whom are these things written that they may know they have eternal life?**  
**Answer:** Unto you that believe on the name of the Son of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:13

**Q2. What may those who believe on the name of the Son of God know?**  
**Answer:** That ye have eternal life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:13

**Q3. Who should believe on the name of the Son of God according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** That ye may believe on the name of the Son of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:13

---

### 1 John 5:14

**Q1. What is the confidence that we have in him?**  
**Answer:** That, if we ask any thing according to his will, he heareth us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:14

**Q2. Under what condition does He hear us when we ask anything?**  
**Answer:** If we ask any thing according to his will.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:14

**Q3. What do we have in him regarding hearing our requests?**  
**Answer:** This is the confidence that we have in him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:14

---

### 1 John 5:15

**Q1. What do we know that He does if we know that He hear us?**  
**Answer:** We know that we have the petitions that we desired of him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:15

**Q2. What do we know about His hearing us?**  
**Answer:** And if we know that he hear us, whatsoever we ask.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:15

**Q3. What do we have concerning the petitions desired of him?**  
**Answer:** We have the petitions that we desired of him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:15

---

### 1 John 5:16

**Q1. What shall a man do if he see his brother sin a sin which is not unto death?**  
**Answer:** He shall ask, and he shall give him life for them that sin not unto death.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:16

**Q2. Is there a sin unto death?**  
**Answer:** There is a sin unto death.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:16

**Q3. What is said regarding praying for a sin unto death?**  
**Answer:** I do not say that he shall pray for it.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:16

---

### 1 John 5:17

**Q1. What is unrighteousness?**  
**Answer:** All unrighteousness is sin.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:17

**Q2. Is there a sin not unto death?**  
**Answer:** And there is a sin not unto death.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:17

**Q3. How is all unrighteousness categorized?**  
**Answer:** As sin.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:17

---

### 1 John 5:18

**Q1. What do we know about whosoever is born of God?**  
**Answer:** We know that whosoever is born of God sinneth not.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:18

**Q2. Who keeps him that is begotten of God?**  
**Answer:** But he that is begotten of God keepeth himself.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:18

**Q3. What does the wicked one do to him?**  
**Answer:** And that wicked one toucheth him not.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:18

---

### 1 John 5:19

**Q1. What do we know about ourselves concerning God?**  
**Answer:** And we know that we are of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:19

**Q2. How is the whole world described in this verse?**  
**Answer:** And the whole world lieth in wickedness.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:19

**Q3. Where does the whole world lie?**  
**Answer:** In wickedness.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:19

---

### 1 John 5:20

**Q1. What do we know the Son of God has come and given us?**  
**Answer:** And we know that the Son of God is come, and hath given us an understanding.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:20

**Q2. Why has He given us an understanding?**  
**Answer:** That we may know him that is true.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:20

**Q3. In whom are we, and who is He?**  
**Answer:** And we are in him that is true, even in his Son Jesus Christ. This is the true God, and eternal life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:20

---

**Q2. What must little children keep themselves from?**  
**Answer:** Keep yourselves from idols.

**Q3. What instruction is given regarding idols?**  
**Answer:** Keep yourselves from idols.
