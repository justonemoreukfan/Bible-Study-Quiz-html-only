# 1 John 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 John 1:1

**Q1. What was from the beginning?**  
**Answer:** That which was from the beginning.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:1

**Q2. What have the writers heard, seen, looked upon, and handled?**  
**Answer:**The Word of life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:1

**Q3. What did their hands handle?**  
**Answer:**The Word of life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:1

---

### 1 John 1:2

**Q4. What was manifested?**  
**Answer:**The life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:2

**Q5. What did the writers do with the eternal life they saw?**  
**Answer:**Bear witness, and shew unto you.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:2

**Q6. With whom was the eternal life, and to whom was it manifested?**  
**Answer:**With the Father, and was manifested unto us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:2

---

### 1 John 1:3

**Q7. What do the writers declare unto you?**  
**Answer:**That which we have seen and heard.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:3

**Q8. Why do they declare it?**  
**Answer:**That ye also may have fellowship with us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:3

**Q9. With whom is the writers' fellowship?**  
**Answer:**The Father, and with his Son Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:3

---

### 1 John 1:4

**Q10. Why do the writers write these things?**  
**Answer:**That your joy may be full.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:4

**Q11. To whom do the writers write?**  
**Answer:**You.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:4

**Q12. What is the desired result of writing these things?**  
**Answer:**Your joy may be full.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:4

---

### 1 John 1:5

**Q13. What is the message the writers heard and declare?**  
**Answer:**God is light, and in him is no darkness at all.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:5

**Q14. From whom did the writers hear this message?**  
**Answer:**Him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:5

**Q15. What is said about darkness in God?**  
**Answer:**In him is no darkness at all.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:5

---

### 1 John 1:6

**Q16. What happens if we say we have fellowship with him but walk in darkness?**  
**Answer:**We lie, and do not the truth.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:6

**Q17. What two things are contrasted in this verse?**  
**Answer:**Fellowship with him and walk in darkness.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:6

**Q18. What is the result of claiming fellowship while walking in darkness?**  
**Answer:**We lie, and do not the truth.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:6

---

### 1 John 1:7

**Q19. What happens if we walk in the light as he is in the light?**  
**Answer:**We have fellowship one with another.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:7

**Q20. What cleanses us from all sin?**  
**Answer:**The blood of Jesus Christ his Son.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:7

**Q21. From what does the blood of Jesus Christ cleanse us?**  
**Answer:**All sin.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:7

---

### 1 John 1:8

**Q22. What happens if we say we have no sin?**  
**Answer:**We deceive ourselves, and the truth is not in us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:8

**Q23. Who do we deceive if we say we have no sin?**  
**Answer:**Ourselves.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:8

**Q24. What is not in us if we say we have no sin?**  
**Answer:**The truth.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:8

---

### 1 John 1:9

**Q25. What happens if we confess our sins?**  
**Answer:**He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:9

**Q26. What two things does God do when we confess our sins?**  
**Answer:**Forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:9

**Q27. From what does God cleanse us?**  
**Answer:**All unrighteousness.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:9

---

### 1 John 1:10

**Q28. What happens if we say we have not sinned?**  
**Answer:**We make him a liar, and his word is not in us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:10

**Q29. Whom do we make a liar if we say we have not sinned?**  
**Answer:**Him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:10

**Q30. What is not in us if we say we have not sinned?**  
**Answer:**His word.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:10
