# 1 John 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 John 3:1

**Q1. What manner of love hath the Father bestowed upon us?**  
**Answer:** That we should be called the sons of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:1

**Q2. Therefore what does the world not know us for?**  
**Answer:** Because it knew him not.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:1

**Q3. Who are we at the present time?**  
**Answer:** The sons of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:1

---

### 1 John 3:2

**Q4. What are we now?**  
**Answer:** The sons of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:2

**Q5. What does it not yet appear?**  
**Answer:** What we shall be.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:2

**Q6. When he shall appear, what do we know we shall be?**  
**Answer:** Like him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:2

---

### 1 John 3:3

**Q7. Every man that hath this hope in him purifieth himself, even as who is pure?**  
**Answer:** He.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:3

**Q8. What does every man that hath this hope in him do?**  
**Answer:** Purifieth himself.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:3

**Q9. As whom is he that hath this hope required to purify himself?**  
**Answer:** Even as he is pure.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:3

---

### 1 John 3:4

**Q10. Whosoever committeth sin transgresseth also what?**  
**Answer:** The law.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:4

**Q11. What is sin?**  
**Answer:** The transgression of the law.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:4

**Q12. Who transgresseth also the law?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever committeth sin.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:4

---

### 1 John 3:5

**Q13. Why was he manifested?**  
**Answer:** To take away our sins.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:5

**Q14. What is not in him?**  
**Answer:** Sin.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:5

**Q15. To take away what was he manifested?**  
**Answer:** Our sins.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:5

---

### 1 John 3:6

**Q16. Whosoever abideth in him does what?**  
**Answer:** Sinneth not.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:6

**Q17. Whosoever sinneth has not seen him, neither known what?**  
**Answer:** Him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:6

**Q18. What has whosoever sinneth neither done?**  
**Answer:** Neither known him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:6

---

### 1 John 3:7

**Q19. Little children, let no man do what?**  
**Answer:** Deceive you.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:7

**Q20. He that doeth righteousness is righteous, even as who is righteous?**  
**Answer:** He.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:7

**Q21. What is he that doeth righteousness?**  
**Answer:** Righteous.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:7

---

### 1 John 3:8

**Q22. He that committeth sin is of whom?**  
**Answer:** The devil.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:8

**Q23. From the beginning, what has the devil done?**  
**Answer:** Sinned.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:8

**Q24. For this purpose what was manifested?**  
**Answer:** The Son of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:8

---

### 1 John 3:9

**Q25. Whosoever is born of God doth not commit what?**  
**Answer:** Sin.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:9

**Q26. What remaineth in him?**  
**Answer:** His seed.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:9

**Q27. Why can he not sin?**  
**Answer:** Because he is born of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:9

---

### 1 John 3:10

**Q28. In this the children of God are manifest, and who else?**  
**Answer:** The children of the devil.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:10

**Q29. Whosoever doeth not righteousness is not of whom?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:10

**Q30. Who else is not of God besides he that doeth not righteousness?**  
**Answer:** He that loveth not his brother.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:10

---

### 1 John 3:11

**Q31. What is the message that ye have heard from the beginning?**  
**Answer:** That we should love one another.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:11

**Q32. From when have ye heard this message?**  
**Answer:** From the beginning.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:11

**Q33. What should we do to one another according to the message?**  
**Answer:** Love one another.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:11

---

### 1 John 3:12

**Q34. Not as who was of that wicked one, and slew his brother?**  
**Answer:** Cain.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:12

**Q35. Wherefore slew he his brother?**  
**Answer:** Because his own works were evil, and his brother's righteous.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:12

**Q36. Whose works were righteous?**  
**Answer:** His brother's.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:12

---

### 1 John 3:13

**Q37. Marvel not, my brethren, if whom hate you?**  
**Answer:** The world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:13

**Q38. Who is addressed as being hated by the world?**  
**Answer:** My brethren.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:13

**Q39. What are the brethren told not to do if the world hate them?**  
**Answer:** Marvel not.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:13

---

### 1 John 3:14

**Q40. We know that we have passed from death unto life, because we love whom?**  
**Answer:** The brethren.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:14

**Q41. What does he that loveth not his brother abide in?**  
**Answer:** Death.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:14

**Q42. From what unto what have we passed because we love the brethren?**  
**Answer:** From death unto life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:14

---

### 1 John 3:15

**Q43. Whosoever hateth his brother is what?**  
**Answer:** A murderer.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:15

**Q44. What do you know that no murderer hath abiding in him?**  
**Answer:** Eternal life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:15

**Q45. Who is a murderer?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever hateth his brother.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:15

---

### 1 John 3:16

**Q46. Hereby perceive we the love of God, because he laid down what for us?**  
**Answer:** His life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:16

**Q47. What ought we also to lay down for the brethren?**  
**Answer:** Our lives.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:16

**Q48. For whom ought we also to lay down our lives?**  
**Answer:** The brethren.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:16

---

### 1 John 3:17

**Q49. But whoso hath this world's good, and seeth his brother have what, and shutteth up his bowels of compassion from him?**  
**Answer:** Need.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:17

**Q50. How does the love of God dwell in him?**  
**Answer:** How dwelleth the love of God in him?  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:17

**Q51. What does he shut up from his brother?**  
**Answer:** His bowels of compassion.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:17

---

### 1 John 3:18

**Q52. My little children, let us not love in what way?**  
**Answer:** In word, neither in tongue.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:18

**Q53. In what two ways should we love?**  
**Answer:** In deed and in truth.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:18

**Q54. Whom is this command addressed to?**  
**Answer:** My little children.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:18

---

### 1 John 3:19

**Q55. And hereby what do we know?**  
**Answer:** That we are of the truth.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:19

**Q56. What shall we assure before him?**  
**Answer:** Our hearts.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:19

**Q57. Where shall we assure our hearts?**  
**Answer:** Before him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:19

---

### 1 John 3:20

**Q58. For if our heart condemn us, who is greater than our heart?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:20

**Q59. What does God know?**  
**Answer:** All things.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:20

**Q60. Who is greater than our heart?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:20

---

### 1 John 3:21

**Q61. Beloved, if our heart condemn us not, then what do we have?**  
**Answer:** Confidence toward God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:21

**Q62. Toward whom do we have confidence if our heart condemn us not?**  
**Answer:** Toward God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:21

**Q63. What must our heart do or not do for us to have this confidence?**  
**Answer:** Condemn us not.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:21

---

### 1 John 3:22

**Q64. And whatsoever we ask, what do we receive of him?**  
**Answer:** We receive of him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:22

**Q65. Why do we receive whatsoever we ask of him?**  
**Answer:** Because we keep his commandments, and do those things that are pleasing in his sight.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:22

**Q66. What things do we do that are pleasing in his sight?**  
**Answer:** Those things that are pleasing in his sight.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:22

---

### 1 John 3:23

**Q67. And what is his commandment?**  
**Answer:** That we should believe on the name of his Son Jesus Christ, and love one another, as he gave us commandment.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:23

**Q68. On whose name should we believe?**  
**Answer:** The name of his Son Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:23

**Q69. As whom gave us commandment, are we to love one another?**  
**Answer:** As he gave us commandment.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:23

---

### 1 John 3:24

**Q70. And he that keepeth his commandments dwelleth in whom, and he in him?**  
**Answer:** In him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:24

**Q71. And hereby we know that he abideth in us, by what?**  
**Answer:** By the Spirit which he hath given us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:24

**Q72. What has he given us by which we know that he abideth in us?**  
**Answer:** The Spirit.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:24
