# 1 John 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 John 3:1

**Q1. What manner of love has the Father bestowed upon us?**  
**Answer:**That we should be called the sons of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:1

**Q2. Why does the world not know us?**  
**Answer:**Because it knew him not.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:1

**Q3. What are we called?**  
**Answer:**The sons of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:1

---

### 1 John 3:2

**Q4. What are we now?**  
**Answer:**The sons of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:2

**Q5. What does not yet appear?**  
**Answer:**What we shall be.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:2

**Q6. What do we know will happen when He shall appear?**  
**Answer:**We shall be like him; for we shall see him as he is.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:2

---

### 1 John 3:3

**Q7. What does every man that has this hope in him do?**  
**Answer:**Purifieth himself.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:3

**Q8. How does he purify himself?**  
**Answer:**Even as he is pure.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:3

**Q9. Who purifies himself?**  
**Answer:**Every man that hath this hope in him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:3

---

### 1 John 3:4

**Q10. What does whosoever committeth sin also do?**  
**Answer:**Transgresseth the law.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:4

**Q11. What is sin?**  
**Answer:**The transgression of the law.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:4

**Q12. What does sin transgress?**  
**Answer:**The law.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:4

---

### 1 John 3:5

**Q13. What do ye know?**  
**Answer:**That he was manifested to take away our sins.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:5

**Q14. Why was He manifested?**  
**Answer:**To take away our sins.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:5

**Q15. What is in Him?**  
**Answer:**No sin.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:5

---

### 1 John 3:6

**Q16. What does whosoever abideth in Him not do?**  
**Answer:**Sinneth not.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:6

**Q17. What has whosoever sinneth not done?**  
**Answer:**Hath not seen him, neither known him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:6

**Q18. What two things has the one who sins not done?**  
**Answer:**Hath not seen him, neither known him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:6

---

### 1 John 3:7

**Q19. What are the little children told not to let any man do?**  
**Answer:**Deceive you.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:7

**Q20. Who is righteous?**  
**Answer:**He that doeth righteousness.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:7

**Q21. How is the one who does righteousness righteous?**  
**Answer:**Even as he is righteous.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:7

---

### 1 John 3:8

**Q22. Who is of the devil?**  
**Answer:**He that committeth sin.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:8

**Q23. Why is he that committeth sin of the devil?**  
**Answer:**For the devil sinneth from the beginning.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:8

**Q24. For what purpose was the Son of God manifested?**  
**Answer:**That he might destroy the works of the devil.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:8

---

### 1 John 3:9

**Q25. What does whosoever is born of God not do?**  
**Answer:**Commit sin.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:9

**Q26. Why can he not sin?**  
**Answer:**Because he is born of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:9

**Q27. What remains in him?**  
**Answer:**His seed.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:9

---

### 1 John 3:10

**Q28. In what are the children of God manifest?**  
**Answer:**In this.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:10

**Q29. Who is not of God?**  
**Answer:**Whosoever doeth not righteousness.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:10

**Q30. Who else is not of God?**  
**Answer:**He that loveth not his brother.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:10

---

### 1 John 3:11

**Q31. What is the message that ye heard from the beginning?**  
**Answer:**That we should love one another.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:11

**Q32. From when have ye heard this message?**  
**Answer:**The beginning.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:11

**Q33. What should we do?**  
**Answer:**Love one another.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:11

---

### 1 John 3:12

**Q34. Who was Cain of?**  
**Answer:**That wicked one.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:12

**Q35. What did Cain do?**  
**Answer:**Slew his brother.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:12

**Q36. Why did Cain slay his brother?**  
**Answer:**Because his own works were evil, and his brother's righteous.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:12

---

### 1 John 3:13

**Q37. What are the brethren told not to marvel at?**  
**Answer:**If the world hate you.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:13

**Q38. Who might hate the brethren?**  
**Answer:**The world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:13

**Q39. What should the brethren not do?**  
**Answer:**Marvel.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:13

---

### 1 John 3:14

**Q40. How do we know that we have passed from death unto life?**  
**Answer:**Because we love the brethren.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:14

**Q41. Who abides in death?**  
**Answer:**He that loveth not his brother.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:14

**Q42. What is the evidence of having passed from death unto life?**  
**Answer:**We love the brethren.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:14

---

### 1 John 3:15

**Q43. What is whosoever hateth his brother?**  
**Answer:**A murderer.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:15

**Q44. What does no murderer have abiding in him?**  
**Answer:**Eternal life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:15

**Q45. What do ye know about murderers?**  
**Answer:**No murderer hath eternal life abiding in him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:15

---

### 1 John 3:16

**Q46. How do we perceive the love of God?**  
**Answer:**Because he laid down his life for us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:16

**Q47. What did He do for us?**  
**Answer:**Laid down his life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:16

**Q48. What ought we to do for the brethren?**  
**Answer:**Lay down our lives.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:16

---

### 1 John 3:17

**Q49. Who has this world's good?**  
**Answer:**Whoso.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:17

**Q50. What does he see?**  
**Answer:**His brother have need.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:17

**Q51. What does he do?**  
**Answer:**Shutteth up his bowels of compassion from him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:17

---

### 1 John 3:17 (continued)

**Q52. What is asked about such a person?**  
**Answer:**How dwelleth the love of God in him?  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:17

**Q53. What does the person shut up?**  
**Answer:**His bowels of compassion.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:17

**Q54. From whom does he shut up his compassion?**  
**Answer:**His brother.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:17

---

### 1 John 3:18

**Q55. What are the little children told not to love in?**  
**Answer:**Word, neither in tongue.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:18

**Q56. In what are we to love?**  
**Answer:**Deed and in truth.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:18

**Q57. What two things are contrasted as ways to love?**  
**Answer:**Word/tongue vs. deed/truth.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:18

---

### 1 John 3:19

**Q58. How do we know that we are of the truth?**  
**Answer:**Hereby.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:19

**Q59. What shall we do before Him?**  
**Answer:**Assure our hearts.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:19

**Q60. What does "hereby" refer to?**  
**Answer:**We know that we are of the truth.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:19

---

### 1 John 3:20

**Q61. If our heart condemn us, who is greater than our heart?**  
**Answer:**God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:20

**Q62. What does God know?**  
**Answer:**All things.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:20

**Q63. What is God greater than?**  
**Answer:**Our heart.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:20

---

### 1 John 3:21

**Q64. If our heart condemn us not, what do we have?**  
**Answer:**Confidence toward God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:21

**Q65. Toward whom do we have confidence?**  
**Answer:**God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:21

**Q66. What is the condition for having confidence toward God?**  
**Answer:**If our heart condemn us not.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:21

---

### 1 John 3:22

**Q67. What do we receive of Him?**  
**Answer:**Whatsoever we ask.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:22

**Q68. Why do we receive what we ask?**  
**Answer:**Because we keep his commandments, and do those things that are pleasing in his sight.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:22

**Q69. What two things do we do that result in receiving what we ask?**  
**Answer:**Keep his commandments, and do those things that are pleasing in his sight.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:22

---

### 1 John 3:23

**Q70. What is His commandment?**  
**Answer:**That we should believe on the name of his Son Jesus Christ, and love one another.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:23

**Q71. On whose name should we believe?**  
**Answer:**His Son Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:23

**Q72. What are the two parts of His commandment?**  
**Answer:**Believe on the name of his Son Jesus Christ, and love one another.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:23

---

### 1 John 3:24

**Q73. Who dwelleth in Him?**  
**Answer:**He that keepeth his commandments.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:24

**Q74. Who dwelleth in the one who keeps His commandments?**  
**Answer:**He (God).  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:24

**Q75. How do we know that He abides in us?**  
**Answer:**By the Spirit which he hath given us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 3:24
