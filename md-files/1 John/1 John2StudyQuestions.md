# 1 John 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 John 2:1

**Q1. To whom are these things written that they sin not?**  
**Answer:** My little children.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:1

**Q2. What is the provision if any man sin?**  
**Answer:** We have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:1

**Q3. What kind of person is Jesus Christ before the Father in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The righteous.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:1

---

### 1 John 2:2

**Q4. What is Jesus Christ Himself in relation to our sins?**  
**Answer:** The propitiation for our sins.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:2

**Q5. Whose sins are covered besides the sins of the writers?**  
**Answer:** Not for ours only, but also for the sins of the whole world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:2

---

### 1 John 2:3

**Q6. By what do we know that we know him?**  
**Answer:** If we keep his commandments.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:3

---

### 1 John 2:4

**Q7. What does he who says, "I know him," and keeps not his commandments, do?**  
**Answer:** He is a liar, and the truth is not in him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:4

---

### 1 John 2:5

**Q8. In whom is the love of God perfected?**  
**Answer:** Whoso keepeth his word.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:5

**Q9. By what do we know that we are in him?**  
**Answer:** Hereby know we that we are in him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:5

---

### 1 John 2:6

**Q10. He that says he abideth in him ought himself to do what?**  
**Answer:** Even as he walked.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:6

---

### 1 John 2:7

**Q11. What kind of commandment is written to the brethren?**  
**Answer:** No new commandment, but an old commandment which ye had from the beginning.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:7

**Q12. What is the old commandment defined as in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The word which ye have heard from the beginning.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:7

---

### 1 John 2:8

**Q13. What kind of commandment do I write unto you, which thing is true in him and in you?**  
**Answer:** A new commandment.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:8

**Q14. What is passing away according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** The darkness is past.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:8

**Q15. What kind of light now shines?**  
**Answer:** The true light now shineth.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:8

---

### 1 John 2:9

**Q16. What does a person who says he is in the light and hates his brother do?**  
**Answer:** He is in darkness even until now.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:9

---

### 1 John 2:10

**Q17. Where does he that loves his brother abide?**  
**Answer:** In the light.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:10

**Q18. What is not in him who loves his brother?**  
**Answer:** There is none occasion of stumbling in him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:10

---

### 1 John 2:11

**Q19. Where is he that hates his brother?**  
**Answer:** In darkness, and walketh in darkness.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:11

**Q20. What does the person who hates his brother not know?**  
**Answer:** Whither he goeth.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:11

**Q21. Why does this person not know where he is going?**  
**Answer:** Because that darkness hath blinded his eyes.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:11

---

### 1 John 2:12

**Q22. To whom does the writer write because their sins are forgiven?**  
**Answer:** Little children.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:12

**Q23. Why are their sins forgiven?**  
**Answer:** For his name's sake.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:12

---

### 1 John 2:13

**Q24. To whom does the writer write because they have known him that is from the beginning?**  
**Answer:** Fathers.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:13

**Q25. To whom does the writer write because they have overcome the wicked one?**  
**Answer:** Young men.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:13

**Q26. To whom does the writer write because they have known the Father?**  
**Answer:** Little children.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:13

---

### 1 John 2:14

**Q27. Why does the writer write unto you, fathers?**  
**Answer:** Because ye have known him that is from the beginning.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:14

**Q28. Why does the writer write unto you, young men?**  
**Answer:** Because ye are strong, and the word of God abideth in you, and ye have overcome the wicked one.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:14

---

### 1 John 2:15

**Q29. What should we not love?**  
**Answer:** The world, neither the things that are in the world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:15

**Q30. What is not in the man who loves the world?**  
**Answer:** The love of the Father.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:15

---

### 1 John 2:16

**Q31. What are the three things that are all that is in the world?**  
**Answer:** The lust of the flesh, and the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:16

**Q32. From where are these three things of the world?**  
**Answer:** Is not of the Father, but is of the world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:16

---

### 1 John 2:17

**Q33. What is passing away?**  
**Answer:** The world passeth away, and the lust thereof.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:17

**Q34. What does he that doeth the will of God do?**  
**Answer:** Abideth for ever.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:17

---

### 1 John 2:18

**Q35. What time is it according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** It is the last time.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:18

**Q36. What did they hear that should come?**  
**Answer:** That antichrist shall come.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:18

**Q37. What do we know by the fact that many antichrists are risen?**  
**Answer:** Whereby we know that it is the last time.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:18

---

### 1 John 2:19

**Q38. From whom did they go out?**  
**Answer:** They went out from us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:19

**Q39. What does their going out manifest?**  
**Answer:** That they were not all of us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:19

---

### 1 John 2:20

**Q40. What do you have from the Holy One?**  
**Answer:** An unction from the Holy One.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:20

**Q41. What is the result of having this unction?**  
**Answer:** And ye know all things.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:20

---

### 1 John 2:21

**Q42. Why have I not written unto you?**  
**Answer:** Because ye know not the truth. (Note: verse says "Because ye know not the truth" as what is absent, or rather "because ye know it").  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:21

**Q43. What do we know about any lie?**  
**Answer:** That no lie is of the truth.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:21

---

### 1 John 2:22

**Q44. Who is a liar?**  
**Answer:** He that denieth that Jesus is the Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:22

**Q45. Who is the antichrist?**  
**Answer:** He that denieth the Father and the Son.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:22

---

### 1 John 2:23

**Q46. What does whosoever denies the Son not have?**  
**Answer:** The same hath not the Father.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:23

**Q47. What does he that acknowledges the Son have?**  
**Answer:** He hath the Father also.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:23

---

### 1 John 2:24

**Q48. What should let abide in you?**  
**Answer:** Let that therefore abide in you, which ye have heard from the beginning.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:24

**Q49. In what shall ye continue if that which ye heard from the beginning remains in you?**  
**Answer:** Ye also shall continue in the Son, and in the Father.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:24

---

### 1 John 2:25

**Q50. What is the promise that he has promised us?**  
**Answer:** Even eternal life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:25

---

### 1 John 2:26

**Q51. Concerning whom have I written these things to you?**  
**Answer:** Concerning them that seduce you.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:26

---

### 1 John 2:27

**Q52. What abideth in you?**  
**Answer:** The anointing which ye have received of him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:27

**Q53. Do you need any man to teach you according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Ye need not that any man teach you.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:27

**Q54. What does the same anointing teach you?**  
**Answer:** All things, and is truth, and is no lie.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:27

---

### 1 John 2:28

**Q55. Whereby should little children abide in him?**  
**Answer:** That, when he shall appear, we may have confidence, and not be ashamed before him at his coming.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:28

---

### 1 John 2:29

**Q56. What do you know if you know that he is righteous?**  
**Answer:** That every one that doeth righteousness is born of him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:29
