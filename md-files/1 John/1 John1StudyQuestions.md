# 1 John 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 John 1:1

**Q1. What was from the beginning?**  
**Answer:** That which was from the beginning.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:1

**Q2. What did they hear with their eyes?**  
**Answer:** That which they have heard, which they have seen with their eyes.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:1

**Q3. What did their hands handle concerning the Word of life?**  
**Answer:** The Word of life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:1

---

### 1 John 1:2

**Q4. What was manifested?**  
**Answer:** The life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:2

**Q5. What did they bear witness of?**  
**Answer:** That eternal life, which was with the Father, and was manifested unto us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:2

**Q6. Where was the eternal life before it was manifested unto us?**  
**Answer:** With the Father.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:2

---

### 1 John 1:3

**Q7. What have they seen and heard?**  
**Answer:** That which we have seen and heard declare we unto you.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:3

**Q8. Why do they declare these things unto you?**  
**Answer:** That ye also may have fellowship with us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:3

**Q9. With whom is our fellowship?**  
**Answer:** With the Father, and with his Son Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:3

---

### 1 John 1:4

**Q10. What do they write unto you?**  
**Answer:** These things write we unto you.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:4

**Q11. Why do they write these things unto you?**  
**Answer:** That your joy may be full.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:4

**Q12. Whose joy is intended to be full?**  
**Answer:** Your joy.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:4

---

### 1 John 1:5

**Q13. What is the message which they have heard of him?**  
**Answer:** That God is light, and in him is no darkness at all.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:5

**Q14. What is God declared to be in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Light.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:5

**Q15. How much darkness is in God?**  
**Answer:** No darkness at all.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:5

---

### 1 John 1:6

**Q16. What do we say if we say we have fellowship with him?**  
**Answer:** That we have fellowship with him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:6

**Q17. Where do we walk if we say we have fellowship with him and walk in darkness?**  
**Answer:** In darkness.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:6

**Q18. What do we do if we say we have fellowship with him, and walk in darkness?**  
**Answer:** We lie, and do not the truth.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:6

---

### 1 John 1:7

**Q19. Where should we walk as he is in the light?**  
**Answer:** In the light.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:7

**Q20. What do we have one with another if we walk in the light?**  
**Answer:** Fellowship one with another.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:7

**Q21. What cleanseth us from all sin?**  
**Answer:** The blood of Jesus Christ his Son.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:7

---

### 1 John 1:8

**Q22. What do we say if we say that we have no sin?**  
**Answer:** That we have no sin.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:8

**Q23. What do we do to ourselves if we say we have no sin?**  
**Answer:** We deceive ourselves.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:8

**Q24. What is not in us if we say we have no sin?**  
**Answer:** The truth.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:8

---

### 1 John 1:9

**Q25. What should we do if we confess our sins?**  
**Answer:** Confess our sins.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:9

**Q26. How is God described when He forgives our sins and cleanses us?**  
**Answer:** Faithful and just.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:9

**Q27. From what does He cleanse us if we confess our sins?**  
**Answer:** From all unrighteousness.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:9

---

### 1 John 1:10

**Q28. What do we say if we say that we have not sinned?**  
**Answer:** That we have not sinned.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:10

**Q29. What do we make Him if we say we have not sinned?**  
**Answer:** A liar.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:10

**Q30. What is not in us if we say we have not sinned?**  
**Answer:** His word.  
**Verse:** 1 John 1:10
