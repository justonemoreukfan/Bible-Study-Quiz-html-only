# 1 John 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 John 4:1

**Q1. What are the beloved told not to believe?**  
**Answer:**Every spirit.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:1

**Q2. What are the beloved told to do?**  
**Answer:**Try the spirits whether they are of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:1

**Q3. Why should we try the spirits?**  
**Answer:**Because many false prophets are gone out into the world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:1

---

### 1 John 4:2

**Q4. How do we know the Spirit of God?**  
**Answer:**Every spirit that confesseth that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh is of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:2

**Q5. What must a spirit confess to be of God?**  
**Answer:**That Jesus Christ is come in the flesh.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:2

**Q6. What spirit is of God?**  
**Answer:**Every spirit that confesseth that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:2

---

### 1 John 4:3

**Q7. What spirit is not of God?**  
**Answer:**Every spirit that confesseth not that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:3

**Q8. What is this spirit called?**  
**Answer:**Spirit of antichrist.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:3

**Q9. Where is this spirit now?**  
**Answer:**In the world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:3

---

### 1 John 4:4

**Q10. Of whom are the little children?**  
**Answer:**God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:4

**Q11. What have the little children done?**  
**Answer:**Overcome them.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:4

**Q12. Why have they overcome them?**  
**Answer:**Because greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:4

---

### 1 John 4:5

**Q13. Of whom are they?**  
**Answer:**The world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:5

**Q14. Of what do they speak?**  
**Answer:**The world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:5

**Q15. Who hears them?**  
**Answer:**The world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:5

---

### 1 John 4:6

**Q16. Of whom are we?**  
**Answer:**God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:6

**Q17. Who hears us?**  
**Answer:**He that knoweth God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:6

**Q18. Who hears not us?**  
**Answer:**He that is not of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:6

---

### 1 John 4:6 (continued)

**Q19. What do we know hereby?**  
**Answer:**The spirit of truth, and the spirit of error.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:6

**Q20. What two spirits are distinguished?**  
**Answer:**The spirit of truth, and the spirit of error.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:6

**Q21. How do we know the spirit of truth and the spirit of error?**  
**Answer:**Hereby.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:6

---

### 1 John 4:7

**Q22. What are the beloved told to do?**  
**Answer:**Let us love one another.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:7

**Q23. Why should we love one another?**  
**Answer:**For love is of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:7

**Q24. What is true of everyone that loveth?**  
**Answer:**Is born of God, and knoweth God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:7

---

### 1 John 4:8

**Q25. What does he that loveth not do?**  
**Answer:**Knoweth not God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:8

**Q26. Why does he that loveth not know not God?**  
**Answer:**For God is love.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:8

**Q27. What is God?**  
**Answer:**Love.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:8

---

### 1 John 4:9

**Q28. In what was the love of God manifested toward us?**  
**Answer:**God sent his only begotten Son into the world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:9

**Q29. Why did God send His only begotten Son?**  
**Answer:**That we might live through him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:9

**Q30. What did God send into the world?**  
**Answer:**His only begotten Son.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:9

---

### 1 John 4:10

**Q31. What is love (herein)?**  
**Answer:**Not that we loved God, but that he loved us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:10

**Q32. What did God do?**  
**Answer:**Loved us, and sent his Son to be the propitiation for our sins.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:10

**Q33. What did God send His Son to be?**  
**Answer:**The propitiation for our sins.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:10

---

### 1 John 4:11

**Q34. If God so loved us, what ought we to do?**  
**Answer:**Love one another.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:11

**Q35. Who loved us?**  
**Answer:**God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:11

**Q36. What ought we to do?**  
**Answer:**Love one another.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:11

---

### 1 John 4:12

**Q37. What has no man seen at any time?**  
**Answer:**God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:12

**Q38. If we love one another, what happens?**  
**Answer:**God dwelleth in us, and his love is perfected in us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:12

**Q39. What two things happen if we love one another?**  
**Answer:**God dwelleth in us, and his love is perfected in us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:12

---

### 1 John 4:13

**Q40. How do we know that we dwell in Him, and He in us?**  
**Answer:**Because he hath given us of his Spirit.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:13

**Q41. What has He given us?**  
**Answer:**Of his Spirit.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:13

**Q42. What does "hereby" refer to?**  
**Answer:**We know that we dwell in him, and he in us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:13

---

### 1 John 4:14

**Q43. What have we seen and do testify?**  
**Answer:**That the Father sent the Son to be the Saviour of the world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:14

**Q44. Who sent the Son?**  
**Answer:**The Father.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:14

**Q45. What was the Son sent to be?**  
**Answer:**The Saviour of the world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:14

---

### 1 John 4:15

**Q46. What happens if a man confesses that Jesus is the Son of God?**  
**Answer:**God dwelleth in him, and he in God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:15

**Q47. Who dwells in whom?**  
**Answer:**God dwelleth in him, and he in God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:15

**Q48. What must be confessed?**  
**Answer:**That Jesus is the Son of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:15

---

### 1 John 4:16

**Q49. What have we known and believed?**  
**Answer:**The love that God hath to us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:16

**Q50. What is God?**  
**Answer:**Love.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:16

**Q51. Who dwelleth in God, and God in him?**  
**Answer:**He that dwelleth in love.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:16

---

### 1 John 4:17

**Q52. What is made perfect herein?**  
**Answer:**Our love.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:17

**Q53. Why is our love made perfect?**  
**Answer:**That we may have boldness in the day of judgment.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:17

**Q54. What is the basis for boldness in the day of judgment?**  
**Answer:**Because as he is, so are we in this world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:17

---

### 1 John 4:18

**Q55. What is there no fear in?**  
**Answer:**Love.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:18

**Q56. What casts out fear?**  
**Answer:**Perfect love.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:18

**Q57. Why does perfect love cast out fear?**  
**Answer:**Because fear hath torment.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:18

---

### 1 John 4:18 (continued)

**Q58. Who is not made perfect in love?**  
**Answer:**He that feareth.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:18

**Q59. What does fear have?**  
**Answer:**Torment.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:18

**Q60. What is the relationship between fear and perfect love?**  
**Answer:**Perfect love casteth out fear.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:18

---

### 1 John 4:19

**Q61. Why do we love Him?**  
**Answer:**Because he first loved us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:19

**Q62. Who loved first?**  
**Answer:**He.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:19

**Q63. Whom did He love first?**  
**Answer:**Us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:19

---

### 1 John 4:20

**Q64. If a man says "I love God" and hates his brother, what is he?**  
**Answer:**A liar.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:20

**Q65. Why is he a liar?**  
**Answer:**For he that loveth not his brother whom he hath seen, how can he love God whom he hath not seen?  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:20

**Q66. Whom has the man seen?**  
**Answer:**His brother.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:20

---

### 1 John 4:20 (continued)

**Q67. Whom has the man not seen?**  
**Answer:**God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:20

**Q68. What is the rhetorical question asked?**  
**Answer:**How can he love God whom he hath not seen?  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:20

**Q69. What does the man claim?**  
**Answer:**I love God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:20

---

### 1 John 4:21

**Q70. What commandment have we from Him?**  
**Answer:**That he who loveth God love his brother also.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:21

**Q71. Who must love his brother?**  
**Answer:**He who loveth God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:21

**Q72. From whom do we have this commandment?**  
**Answer Him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:21
