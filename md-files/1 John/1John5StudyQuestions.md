# 1 John 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 John 5:1

**Q1. Who is born of God?**  
**Answer:**Whosoever believeth that Jesus is the Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:1

**Q2. What does everyone that loves him that begat also do?**  
**Answer:**Loveth him also that is begotten of him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:1

**Q3. What is the condition for being born of God?**  
**Answer:**Believeth that Jesus is the Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:1

---

### 1 John 5:2

**Q4. By what do we know that we love the children of God?**  
**Answer:**When we love God, and keep his commandments.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:2

**Q5. What two things indicate we love the children of God?**  
**Answer:**Love God, and keep his commandments.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:2

**Q6. When do we know we love the children of God?**  
**Answer:**When we love God, and keep his commandments.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:2

---

### 1 John 5:3

**Q7. What is the love of God?**  
**Answer:**That we keep his commandments.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:3

**Q8. What are His commandments not?**  
**Answer:**Grievous.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:3

**Q9. What do we do if we love God?**  
**Answer:**Keep his commandments.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:3

**Verse:** 1 John 5:4

**Q11. What is the victory that overcomes the world?**

**Verse:** 1 John 5:4

**Q12. Who overcomes the world?**  
**Answer:**Whatsoever is born of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:4

**Q14. What does the one who overcomes the world believe?**  
**Answer:**That Jesus is the Son of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:5

**Q15. What is the condition for overcoming the world?**  
**Answer:**Believeth that Jesus is the Son of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:5

### 1 John 5:6

**Q16. Who came by water and blood?**  
**Answer:**Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:6

**Q17. By what did He come?**  
**Answer:**Water and blood.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:6

**Q18. Not by what only?**

**Verse:** 1 John 5:6

### 1 John 5:6 (continued)

**Q19. What bears witness?**  
**Answer:**The Spirit.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:6

**Q20. Why does the Spirit bear witness?**  
**Answer:**Because the Spirit is truth.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:6

**Q21. What is the Spirit?**  
**Answer:**Truth.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:6

---

### 1 John 5:7

**Q22. How many bear record in heaven?**  
**Answer:**Three.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:7

**Q23. Who are the three that bear record in heaven?**  
**Answer:**The Father, the Word, and the Holy Ghost.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:7

**Q24. What are these three?**  
**Answer:**One.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:7

---

### 1 John 5:8

**Q25. How many bear witness in earth?**  
**Answer:**Three.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:8

**Q26. Who are the three that bear witness in earth?**  
**Answer:**The Spirit, and the water, and the blood.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:8

**Q27. What do these three do?**  
**Answer:**Agree in one.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:8

---

### 1 John 5:9

**Q28. If we receive the witness of men, what is greater?**  
**Answer:**The witness of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:9

**Q29. What is the witness of God?**  
**Answer:**This is the witness of God which he hath testified of his Son.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:9

**Q30. Of whom has God testified?**  
**Answer:**His Son.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:9

---

### 1 John 5:10

**Q31. Who has the witness in himself?**  
**Answer:**He that believeth on the Son of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:10

**Q32. Who has made God a liar?**  
**Answer:**He that believeth not God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:10

**Q33. Why has he made God a liar?**  
**Answer:**Because he believeth not the record that God gave of his Son.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:10

---

### 1 John 5:11

**Q34. What is the record?**  
**Answer:**That God hath given to us eternal life, and this life is in his Son.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:11

**Q35. What has God given to us?**  
**Answer:**Eternal life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:11

**Q36. Where is this life?**  
**Answer:**In his Son.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:11

**Verse:** 1 John 5:12

**Q39. What does he that hath the Son have?**  
**Answer:**Life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:12

### 1 John 5:13

**Q40. To whom has the writer written these things?**  
**Answer:**You that believe on the name of the Son of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:13

**Q41. Why has the writer written these things?**  
**Answer:**That ye may know that ye have eternal life.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:13

**Q42. What else is the purpose of writing?**  
**Answer:**That ye may believe on the name of the Son of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:13

---

### 1 John 5:14

**Q43. What is the confidence that we have in Him?**  
**Answer:**That, if we ask any thing according to his will, he heareth us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:14

**Q44. Under what condition does He hear us?**  
**Answer:**If we ask any thing according to his will.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:14

**Q45. What does He do if we ask according to His will?**  
**Answer:**Heareth us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:14

---

### 1 John 5:15

**Q46. If we know that He hear us, what do we know?**  
**Answer:**Whatsoever we ask, we know that we have the petitions that we desired of him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:15

**Q47. What do we have if we know He hears us?**  
**Answer:**The petitions that we desired of him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:15

**Q48. What is the condition for having the petitions we desired?**  
**Answer:**If we know that he hear us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:15

---

### 1 John 5:16

**Q49. If any man see his brother sin a sin which is not unto death, what shall he do?**  
**Answer:**He shall ask, and he shall give him life for them that sin not unto death.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:16

**Q50. What is there a sin unto?**  
**Answer:**Death.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:16

**Q51. What does the writer not say to do for the sin unto death?**  
**Answer:**I do not say that he shall pray for it.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:16

---

### 1 John 5:17

**Q52. What is all unrighteousness?**  
**Answer:**Sin.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:17

**Q53. Is there a sin not unto death?**  
**Answer:**There is a sin not unto death.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:17

**Q54. What two categories of sin are mentioned?**  
**Answer:**Sin unto death, and sin not unto death.  
**Verse:** 1 John 5:17
