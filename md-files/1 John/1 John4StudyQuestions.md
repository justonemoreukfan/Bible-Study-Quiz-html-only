# 1 John 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 John 4:1

**Q1. Who are we told not to believe in 1 John 4:1?**  
**Answer:** Every spirit.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:1

**Q2. What should we do to the spirits according to verse 1?**  
**Answer:** Try the spirits whether they are of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:1

**Q3. Why must we try the spirits as stated in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Because many false prophets are gone out into the world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:1

---

### 1 John 4:2

**Q4. What do we know the Spirit of God by, according to 1 John 4:2?**  
**Answer:** Hereby know ye the Spirit of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:2

**Q5. What does every spirit that confesses this fact acknowledge?**  
**Answer:** That Jesus Christ is come in the flesh.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:2

**Q6. Of whom is the spirit that confesses Jesus Christ is come in the flesh?**  
**Answer:** Of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:2

---

### 1 John 4:3

**Q7. What does every spirit that does not confess that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh not do?**  
**Answer:** Confess not that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:3

**Q8. Of whom is every spirit that does not confess Jesus is come in the flesh?**  
**Answer:** Not of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:3

**Q9. Whose spirit is the spirit that confesses not Jesus, which they have heard should come and is already in the world?**  
**Answer:** That spirit of antichrist.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:3

---

### 1 John 4:4

**Q10. Whose children are you, and whom have you overcome in 1 John 4:4?**  
**Answer:** Ye are of God, little children, and have overcome them.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:4

**Q11. Why is the one who is in you greater than the one who is in the world?**  
**Answer:** Because greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:4

**Q12. Where is the one who is greater than the one in the world?**  
**Answer:** In you.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:4

---

### 1 John 4:5

**Q13. Of what are they, according to 1 John 4:5?**  
**Answer:** Of the world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:5

**Q14. How do they speak?**  
**Answer:** Of the world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:5

**Q15. Who hears them?**  
**Answer:** The world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:5

---

### 1 John 4:6

**Q16. Of whom are we, according to 1 John 4:6?**  
**Answer:** Of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:6

**Q17. Who hears us and who does not hear us?**  
**Answer:** He that knoweth God heareth us; he that is not of God heareth not us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:6

**Q18. By what do we know the spirit of truth and the spirit of error?**  
**Answer:** Hereby know we the spirit of truth, and the spirit of error.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:6

---

### 1 John 4:7

**Q19. What are we instructed to do one another in 1 John 4:7?**  
**Answer:** Love one another.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:7

**Q20. From whom does love come?**  
**Answer:** Of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:7

**Q21. What happens to everyone that loves?**  
**Answer:** Is born of God, and knoweth God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:7

---

### 1 John 4:8

**Q22. What does the person who does not love fail to do?**  
**Answer:** Knoweth not God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:8

**Q23. Why does the person who does not love not know God?**  
**Answer:** For God is love.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:8

**Q24. What is God declared to be in 1 John 4:8?**  
**Answer:** Love.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:8

---

### 1 John 4:9

**Q25. How was the love of God manifested toward us?**  
**Answer:** In this was manifested the love of God toward us, because that God sent his only begotten Son into the world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:9

**Q26. Why did God send His only begotten Son into the world?**  
**Answer:** That we might live through him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:9

**Q27. What kind of Son did God send into the world?**  
**Answer:** His only begotten Son.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:9

---

### 1 John 4:10

**Q28. In what does love consist, according to 1 John 4:10?**  
**Answer:** Herein is love, not that we loved God, but that he loved us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:10

**Q29. What did God send His Son to be?**  
**Answer:** The propitiation for our sins.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:10

**Q30. Did we love God first, or did He love us first?**  
**Answer:** Not that we loved God, but that he loved us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:10

---

### 1 John 4:11

**Q31. How ought we to love one another, given how God loved us?**  
**Answer:** We ought also to love one another.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:11

**Q32. What did God do to us that prompts our love for one another?**  
**Answer:** God so loved us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:11

**Q33. What is the main command given in 1 John 4:11?**  
**Answer:** Beloved, if God so loved us, we ought also to love one another.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:11

---

### 1 John 4:12

**Q34. Has anyone seen God at any time?**  
**Answer:** No man hath seen God at any time.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:12

**Q35. What happens if we love one another?**  
**Answer:** God dwelleth in us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:12

**Q36. What is perfected in us if we love one another?**  
**Answer:** His love is perfected in us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:12

---

### 1 John 4:13

**Q37. By what do we know that we dwell in him, and he in us?**  
**Answer:** Hereby know we that we dwell in him, and he in us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:13

**Q38. What has He given us to prove we dwell in Him?**  
**Answer:** Because he hath given us of his Spirit.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:13

**Q39. Who dwells in us according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** He (God) in us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:13

---

### 1 John 4:14

**Q40. What have we seen and do testify in 1 John 4:14?**  
**Answer:** And we have seen and do testify that the Father sent the Son to be the Saviour of the world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:14

**Q41. Who sent the Son?**  
**Answer:** The Father.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:14

**Q42. What is the Son to be, according to the Father's sending?**  
**Answer:** The Saviour of the world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:14

---

### 1 John 4:15

**Q43. What must a person confess in order for God to dwell in them and they in God?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever shall confess that Jesus is the Son of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:15

**Q44. Who dwells in the person who confesses that Jesus is the Son of God?**  
**Answer:** God dwelleth in him, and he in God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:15

**Q45. Whose Son must be confessed?**  
**Answer:** The Son of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:15

---

### 1 John 4:16

**Q46. What have we known and believed?**  
**Answer:** And we have known and believed the love that God hath to us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:16

**Q47. What is God declared to be in 1 John 4:16?**  
**Answer:** God is love.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:16

**Q48. Where does he that dwelleth in love dwell?**  
**Answer:** In God, and God in him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:16

---

### 1 John 4:17

**Q49. What is made perfect with us so that we may have boldness in the day of judgment?**  
**Answer:** Herein is our love made perfect.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:17

**Q50. When may we have boldness according to verse 17?**  
**Answer:** In the day of judgment.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:17

**Q51. How is our relationship in this world described in relation to Him?**  
**Answer:** As he is, so are we in this world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:17

---

### 1 John 4:18

**Q52. What is not in love?**  
**Answer:** There is no fear in love.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:18

**Q53. What casts out fear?**  
**Answer:** Perfect love casteth out fear.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:18

**Q54. What does fear have, and why is he that fears not made perfect in love?**  
**Answer:** Because fear hath torment. He that feareth is not made perfect in love.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:18

---

### 1 John 4:19

**Q55. Why do we love him?**  
**Answer:** Because he first loved us.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:19

**Q56. Who loved first?**  
**Answer:** He (God).  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:19

**Q57. What do we do in response?**  
**Answer:** We love him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:19

---

### 1 John 4:20

**Q58. What does a person who says "I love God" and hates his brother become?**  
**Answer:** A liar.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:20

**Q59. Why can't a man who does not love his brother whom he has seen love God whom he has not seen?**  
**Answer:** For he that loveth not his brother whom he has seen, how can he love God whom he has not seen?  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:20

**Q60. Have we seen our brother and have we seen God, according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Whom he hath seen / whom he hath not seen.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:20

---

### 1 John 4:21

**Q61. What commandment do we have from him?**  
**Answer:** And this commandment have we from him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:21

**Q62. Who must love his brother according to this commandment?**  
**Answer:** That he who loveth God love his brother also.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:21

**Q63. What is the dual requirement stated in verse 21?**  
**Answer:** That he who loveth God love his brother also.  
**Verse:** 1 John 4:21
