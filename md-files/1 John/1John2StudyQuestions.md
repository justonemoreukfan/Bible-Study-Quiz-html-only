# 1 John 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 John 2:1

**Q1. To whom does the writer address as "my little children"?**  
**Answer:**You.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:1

**Q2. Why does the writer write these things?**  
**Answer:**That ye sin not.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:1

**Q3. If any man sin, what do we have?**  
**Answer:**An advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:1

---

### 1 John 2:2

**Q4. For whose sins is Jesus Christ the propitiation?**  
**Answer:**Our sins.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:2

**Q5. For whose sins only is He not the propitiation?**  
**Answer:**Ours only.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:2

**Q6. For whose sins also is He the propitiation?**  
**Answer:**The sins of the whole world.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:2

---

### 1 John 2:3

**Q7. How do we know that we know Him?**  
**Answer:**If we keep His commandments.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:3

**Q8. What is the condition for knowing that we know Him?**  
**Answer:**Keep His commandments.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:3

**Q9. What does "hereby" refer to in this verse?**  
**Answer:**We do know that we know him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:3

---

### 1 John 2:4

**Q10. What is said of the one who says "I know him" but keeps not His commandments?**  
**Answer:**Is a liar, and the truth is not in him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:4

**Q11. What two things are true of the one who claims to know Him but keeps not His commandments?**  
**Answer:**Is a liar, and the truth is not in him.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:4

**Q12. What is not in the one who keeps not His commandments?**  
**Answer:**The truth.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:4

---

### 1 John 2:5

**Q13. In whom is the love of God perfected?**  
**Answer:**Whoso keepeth His word.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:5

**Q14. How do we know that we are in Him?**  
**Answer:**Whoso keepeth His word, in him verily is the love of God perfected.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:5

**Q15. What is perfected in the one who keeps His word?**  
**Answer:**The love of God.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:5

---

### 1 John 2:6

**Q16. How ought the one who says he abides in Him to walk?**  
**Answer:**Even as He walked.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:6

**Q17. What is the obligation of the one who says he abides in Him?**  
**Answer:**Ought himself also so to walk, even as He walked.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:6

**Q18. After whose example should the one abiding in Him walk?**  
**Answer:**He (Jesus Christ).  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:6

---

### 1 John 2:7

**Q19. What does the writer write to the brethren?**  
**Answer:**No new commandment.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:7

**Q20. What commandment does the writer write?**  
**Answer:**An old commandment which ye had from the beginning.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:7

**Q21. What is the old commandment?**  
**Answer:**The word which ye have heard from the beginning.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:7

---

### 1 John 2:8

**Q22. What does the writer write again?**  
**Answer:**A new commandment.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:8

**Q23. In whom is the new commandment true?**  
**Answer:**In him and in you.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:8

**Q24. Why is the new commandment true in him and in you?**  
**Answer:**Because the darkness is past, and the true light now shineth.  
**Verse:** 1 John 2:8
