# Leviticus 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Leviticus 10:1

**Q1. Who were the sons of Aaron that took either of them his censer?**  
**Answer:** Nadab and Abihu.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:1

**Q2. What did Nadab and Abihu put in their censers?**  
**Answer:** Fire, and put incense thereon.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:1

**Q3. What kind of fire did they offer before the LORD, which he commanded them not?**  
**Answer:** Strange fire.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:1

---

### Leviticus 10:2

**Q4. What went out from the LORD to devour Nadab and Abihu?**  
**Answer:** There went out fire from the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:2

---

### Leviticus 10:2 (cont.)

**Q5. What happened to Nadab and Abihu when the fire went out from the LORD?**  
**Answer:** They died before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:2

---

### Leviticus 10:2 (cont.)

**Q6. Where did they die before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:2

---

### Leviticus 10:3

**Q7. To whom did Moses speak regarding this event?**  
**Answer:** Unto Aaron.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:3

---

### Leviticus 10:3 (cont.)

**Q8. Whom did the LORD say He will be sanctified in?**  
**Answer:** In them that come nigh me.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:3

---

### Leviticus 10:3 (cont.)

**Q9. Before whom did the LORD say He will be glorified?**  
**Answer:** Before all the people.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:3

---

### Leviticus 10:4

**Q10. Whom did Moses call?**  
**Answer:** Mishael and Elzaphan, the sons of Uzziel the uncle of Aaron.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:4

---

### Leviticus 10:4 (cont.)

**Q11. Whose sons were Mishael and Elzaphan?**  
**Answer:** The sons of Uzziel the uncle of Aaron.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:4

---

### Leviticus 10:4 (cont.)

**Q12. What did Moses tell Mishael and Elzaphan to carry?**  
**Answer:** Carry your brethren from before the sanctuary out of the camp.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:4

---

### Leviticus 10:5

**Q13. How did Mishael and Elzaphan carry Nadab and Abihu?**  
**Answer:** In their coats out of the camp.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:5

---

### Leviticus 10:5 (cont.)

**Q14. In what were Nadab and Abihu carried out of the camp?**  
**Answer:** In their coats.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:5

---

### Leviticus 10:5 (cont.)

**Q15. As Moses had said, where did they carry Nadab and Abihu?**  
**Answer:** Out of the camp.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:5

---

### Leviticus 10:6

**Q16. Who did Moses tell not to uncover their heads, neither rend their clothes?**  
**Answer:** Aaron, and unto Eleazar and unto Ithamar, his sons.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:6

---

### Leviticus 10:6 (cont.)

**Q17. What warning did Moses give about dying and wrath?**  
**Answer:** Lest ye die, and lest wrath come upon all the people.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:6

---

### Leviticus 10:6 (cont.)

**Q18. Who was allowed to bewail the burning which the LORD had kindled?**  
**Answer:** All the house of Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:6

---

### Leviticus 10:7

**Q19. Where were Aaron and his sons told not to go out from?**  
**Answer:** From the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:7

---

### Leviticus 10:7 (cont.)

**Q20. What would happen to Aaron and his sons if they went out from the tabernacle door?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall die.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:7

---

### Leviticus 10:7 (cont.)

**Q21. What was upon Aaron and his sons?**  
**Answer:** The anointing oil of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:7

---

### Leviticus 10:8

**Q22. Who spoke unto Aaron?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:8

---

### Leviticus 10:8 (cont.)

**Q23. What did the LORD speak unto Aaron?**  
**Answer:** Saying.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:8

---

### Leviticus 10:8 (cont.)

**Q24. Who was the direct recipient of this speech from the LORD in verse 8?**  
**Answer:** Aaron.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:8

---

### Leviticus 10:9

**Q25. What were Aaron and his sons forbidden to drink when they went into the tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** Wine nor strong drink.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:9

---

### Leviticus 10:9 (cont.)

**Q26. What was the consequence of drinking wine or strong drink when entering the tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** That ye die.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:9

---

### Leviticus 10:9 (cont.)

**Q27. How long was this statute to be throughout their generations?**  
**Answer:** A statute for ever.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:9

---

### Leviticus 10:10

**Q28. What distinction were the priests required to make regarding holy and unholy?**  
**Answer:** That ye may put difference between holy and unholy.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:10

---

### Leviticus 10:10 (cont.)

**Q29. What other distinction were they required to make besides holy and unholy?**  
**Answer:** Between unclean and clean.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:10

---

### Leviticus 10:10 (cont.)

**Q30. To whom should the children of Israel be taught all the statutes by the hand of Moses?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:10

---

### Leviticus 10:11

**Q31. What were they to teach the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** All the statutes which the LORD hath spoken unto them by the hand of Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:11

---

### Leviticus 10:11 (cont.)

**Q32. By whose hand did the LORD speak the statutes to the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** By the hand of Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:11

---

### Leviticus 10:11 (cont.)

**Q33. Who spoke the statutes to Moses?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:11

---

### Leviticus 10:12

**Q34. To whom did Moses speak concerning the meat offering?**  
**Answer:** Unto Aaron, and unto Eleazar and unto Ithamar, his sons that were left.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:12

---

### Leviticus 10:12 (cont.)

**Q35. How was the meat offering to be eaten beside the altar?**  
**Answer:** Without leaven.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:12

---

### Leviticus 10:12 (cont.)

**Q36. Where was the meat offering to be eaten?**  
**Answer:** Beside the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:12

---

### Leviticus 10:13

**Q37. Where must the holy place be used to eat the meat offering?**  
**Answer:** In the holy place.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:13

---

### Leviticus 10:13 (cont.)

**Q38. Whose due is the meat offering out of the sacrifices of the LORD made by fire?**  
**Answer:** It is thy due, and thy sons' due.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:13

---

### Leviticus 10:13 (cont.)

**Q39. What did the LORD command concerning this matter?**  
**Answer:** For so I am commanded.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:13

---

### Leviticus 10:14

**Q40. In what place shall the wave breast and heave shoulder be eaten?**  
**Answer:** In a clean place.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:14

---

### Leviticus 10:14 (cont.)

**Q41. Who besides Aaron was allowed to eat the wave breast and heave shoulder?**  
**Answer:** Thy sons, and thy daughters with thee.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:14

---

### Leviticus 10:14 (cont.)

**Q42. Out of whose sacrifices were these given as thy due?**  
**Answer:** Out of the sacrifices of peace offerings of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:14

---

### Leviticus 10:15

**Q43. What offerings must be brought with the offerings made by fire of the fat?**  
**Answer:** The heave shoulder and the wave breast.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:15

---

### Leviticus 10:15 (cont.)

**Q44. What action is performed with the breast before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** It shall be waved for a wave offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:15

---

### Leviticus 10:15 (cont.)

**Q45. Whose perpetual due shall the wave breast and heave shoulder be?**  
**Answer:** Thine, and thy sons' with thee.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:15

---

### Leviticus 10:16

**Q46. What did Moses diligently seek concerning the goat of the sin offering?**  
**Answer:** The goat of the sin offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:16

---

### Leviticus 10:16 (cont.)

**Q47. What had happened to the goat of the sin offering?**  
**Answer:** Behold, it was burnt.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:16

---

### Leviticus 10:16 (cont.)

**Q48. Upon whom was Moses angry regarding Eleazar and Ithamar?**  
**Answer:** Upon Eleazar and Ithamar, the sons of Aaron which were left alive.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:16

---

### Leviticus 10:17

**Q49. Where should the sin offering have been eaten according to Moses?**  
**Answer:** In the holy place.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:17

---

### Leviticus 10:17 (cont.)

**Q50. Why did God give the sin offering to the priests?**  
**Answer:** To bear the iniquity of the congregation, to make atonement for them before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:17

---

### Leviticus 10:17 (cont.)

**Q51. What was the sin offering declared to be?**  
**Answer:** Most holy.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:17

---

### Leviticus 10:18

**Q52. Was the blood of the sin offering brought within the holy place?**  
**Answer:** No.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:18

---

### Leviticus 10:18 (cont.)

**Q53. Where should the sin offering indeed have been eaten?**  
**Answer:** In the holy place.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:18

---

### Leviticus 10:18 (cont.)

**Q54. By whose command should the sin offering have been eaten in the holy place?**  
**Answer:** As I commanded.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:18

---

### Leviticus 10:19

**Q55. What did Aaron say to Moses they had offered today?**  
**Answer:** Their sin offering and their burnt offering before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:19

---

### Leviticus 10:19 (cont.)

**Q56. What things had befallen Aaron today?**  
**Answer:** Such things have befallen me.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:19

---

### Leviticus 10:19 (cont.)

**Q57. If Aaron had eaten the sin offering today, would it have been accepted in the sight of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Would it have been accepted in the sight of the LORD?  
**Verse:** Leviticus 10:19
