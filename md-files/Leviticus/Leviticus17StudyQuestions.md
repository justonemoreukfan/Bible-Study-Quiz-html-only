# Leviticus 17 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Leviticus 17:1

**Q1. Who spake unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:1

**Q2. To whom did the Lord speak regarding this matter?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:1

**Q3. Whose word is recorded in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The word of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:1

---

### Leviticus 17:2

**Q1. Unto whom else was Moses commanded to speak in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Aaron, and unto his sons, and unto all the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:2

**Q2. What did the Lord say when He spake unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** This is the thing which the Lord hath commanded.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:2

**Q3. Who are the groups of people mentioned to be spoken to besides Moses?**  
**Answer:** Aaron, his sons, and all the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:2

---

### Leviticus 17:3

**Q1. What man of the house of Israel is addressed in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever there is of the house of Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:3

**Q2. What animals does this verse specifically mention killing?**  
**Answer:** An ox, or lamb, or goat.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:3

**Q3. Where are these animals killed according to the action described?**  
**Answer:** Either in the camp, or that killeth it out of the camp.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:3

---

### Leviticus 17:4

**Q1. Where should the man have brought the offering instead of the camp?**  
**Answer:** Unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:4

**Q2. To what specific location before the tabernacle must it be brought?**  
**Answer:** Before the tabernacle of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:4

**Q3. What shall be imputed unto that man if he does not bring it to the tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** Blood shall be imputed unto that man; he hath shed blood; and that man shall be cut off from among his people.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:4

---

### Leviticus 17:5

**Q1. To the end that the children of Israel may bring what sacrifices?**  
**Answer:** Their sacrifices, which they offer in the open field.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:5

**Q2. Unto whom must the children of Israel bring these sacrifices?**  
**Answer:** Unto the Lord.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:5

**Q3. To whose door must they bring them, and unto whom must they offer them for peace offerings?**  
**Answer:** Unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, unto the priest, and offer them for peace offerings unto the Lord.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:5

---

### Leviticus 17:6

**Q1. What will the priest sprinkle upon the altar of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** The blood.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:6

**Q2. Where is the altar of the Lord located in this verse?**  
**Answer:** At the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:6

**Q3. What shall the priest burn for a sweet savour unto the Lord?**  
**Answer:** The fat.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:6

---

### Leviticus 17:7

**Q1. What kind of sacrifices shall they no more offer their sacrifices unto?**  
**Answer:** Devils.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:7

**Q2. After whom have they gone a whoring in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Devils.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:7

**Q3. For whom shall this be a statute for ever throughout their generations?**  
**Answer:** Unto them.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:7

---

### Leviticus 17:8

**Q1. What house of Israel or strangers is addressed here?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever man there be of the house of Israel, or of the strangers which sojourn among you.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:8

**Q2. What actions do these individuals perform regarding burnt offerings or sacrifices?**  
**Answer:** That offereth a burnt offering or sacrifice.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:8

**Q3. What must happen to them if they do not bring it to the door of the tabernacle of the congregation?**  
**Answer:** Even that man shall be cut off from his people.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:8

### Leviticus 17:10

**Q1. Who of the house of Israel, or of the strangers that sojourn among them, is addressed?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever man there be of the house of Israel, or of the strangers that sojourn among you.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:10

**Q2. What does this man eat any manner of?**  
**Answer:** Blood.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:10

**Q3. What will the Lord set His face against, and what will happen to that soul?**  
**Answer:** I will even set my face against that soul that eateth blood, and will cut him off from among his people.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:10

---

### Leviticus 17:11

**Q1. What is the life of the flesh in?**  
**Answer:** In the blood.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:11

**Q2. Where has the Lord given the blood to the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Upon the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:11

**Q3. For what purpose is the blood given upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** To make an atonement for your souls.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:11

---

### Leviticus 17:12

**Q1. Therefore what did the Lord say unto the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** No soul of you shall eat blood.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:12

**Q2. Who among them shall not eat blood?**  
**Answer:** Neither any stranger that sojourneth among you.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:12

**Q3. What substance is forbidden to be eaten by any soul or stranger?**  
**Answer:** Blood.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:12

### Leviticus 17:13

**Q1. What persons of the children of Israel, or of the strangers that sojourn among them, are mentioned?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever man there be of the children of Israel, or of the strangers that sojourn among you.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:13

**Q2. What action does he do to any beast or fowl that may be eaten?**  
**Answer:** Which hunteth and catcheth any beast or fowl that may be eaten.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:13

**Q3. What must he pour out and cover with dust regarding that animal?**  
**Answer:** He shall even pour out the blood thereof, and cover it with dust.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:13

---

### Leviticus 17:14

**Q1. For what is the life of all flesh?**  
**Answer:** The blood of it is for the life thereof.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:14

**Q2. What did the Lord say unto the children of Israel regarding the blood of any flesh?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall eat the blood of no manner of flesh.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:14

**Q3. What will happen to whosoever eateth it?**  
**Answer:** Shall be cut off.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:14

---

### Leviticus 17:15

**Q1. What kind of animal may every soul eat that died of itself?**  
**Answer:** That which died of itself, or that which was torn with beasts.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:15

**Q2. Who is permitted to eat the animal that died of itself or was torn with beasts?**  
**Answer:** Whether it be one of your own country, or a stranger.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:15

**Q3. What must the person wash and do until the even after eating it?**  
**Answer:** He shall both wash his clothes, and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until the even: then shall he be clean.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:15

---

### Leviticus 17:16

**Q1. What will happen if the person does not wash his clothes and bathe his flesh?**  
**Answer:** Then he shall bear his iniquity.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:16

**Q2. Whose iniquity shall he bear?**  
**Answer:** His own iniquity.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:16

**Q3. What failure leads to bearing this iniquity according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** If he wash them not, nor bathe his flesh.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 17:16
