### Leviticus 13:1

**Q1. Who did the LORD speak unto in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Moses and Aaron.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 13:1

**Q2. Who was the speaker to Moses and Aaron?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.
