# Leviticus 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Leviticus 2:1

**Q1. What offering shall any man offer unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** A meat offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:1

**Q2. What must the offering consist of?**  
**Answer:** Fine flour.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:1

**Q3. What must be poured upon it, and what put thereon?**  
**Answer:** Oil poured upon it, and frankincense put thereon.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:1

---

### Leviticus 2:2

**Q1. To whom shall he bring the offering?**  
**Answer:** Unto Aaron's sons the priests.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:2

**Q2. What shall the priest take his handful of?**  
**Answer:** Of the flour thereof, and of the oil thereof, with all the frankincense thereof.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:2

**Q3. What shall the priest burn upon the altar as an offering made by fire?**  
**Answer:** The memorial of it.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:2

---

### Leviticus 2:3

**Q1. What shall be left of the meat offering?**  
**Answer:** Aaron's and his sons'.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:3

**Q2. What kind of thing is it among the offerings of the LORD made by fire?**  
**Answer:** A thing most holy.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:3

---

### Leviticus 2:4

**Q1. When thou bringest an oblation of a meat offering baked in the oven, what shall it be of?**  
**Answer:** Unleavened cakes of fine flour mingled with oil, or unleavened wafers anointed with oil.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:4

---

### Leviticus 2:5

**Q1. If thy oblation be a meat offering baked in a pan, what shall it be of?**  
**Answer:** Fine flour unleavened, mingled with oil.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:5

---

### Leviticus 2:6

**Q1. How shall the meat offering baked in a pan be divided?**  
**Answer:** In pieces.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:6

**Q2. What shall be poured thereon?**  
**Answer:** Oil.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:6

**Q3. What kind of offering is it?**  
**Answer:** A meat offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:6

---

### Leviticus 2:7

**Q1. If thy oblation be a meat offering baked in the frying pan, what shall it be made of?**  
**Answer:** Of fine flour with oil.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:7

---

### Leviticus 2:8

**Q1. What shall you bring unto the LORD of these things?**  
**Answer:** The meat offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:8

**Q2. To whom shall it be presented when brought?**  
**Answer:** Unto the priest.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:8

**Q3. Where shall the priest bring it?**  
**Answer:** Unto the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:8

---

### Leviticus 2:9

**Q1. What shall the priest take from the meat offering?**  
**Answer:** The memorial thereof.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:9

**Q2. Where shall the priest burn the memorial?**  
**Answer:** Upon the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:9

**Q3. What kind of offering is made by fire of a sweet savour unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** An offering made by fire.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:9

---

### Leviticus 2:10

**Q1. What remains of the meat offering?**  
**Answer:** Aaron's and his sons'.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:10

**Q2. What is this portion considered among the offerings of the LORD made by fire?**  
**Answer:** A thing most holy.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:10

---

### Leviticus 2:11

**Q1. What must no meat offering brought unto the LORD be made with?**  
**Answer:** Leaven.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:11

**Q2. What two things shall ye not burn any of as an offering made by fire unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Leaven, nor any honey.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:11

---

### Leviticus 2:12

**Q1. As for the oblation of the firstfruits, what may ye offer them unto the LORD for?**  
**Answer:** An oblation of the firstfruits.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:12

**Q2. Shall they come upon the altar for a sweet savour?**  
**Answer:** They shall not be burnt on the altar for a sweet savour.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:12

---

### Leviticus 2:13

**Q1. With what shall every oblation of thy meat offering be seasoned?**  
**Answer:** Salt.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:13

**Q2. What covenant of thy God shall not be lacked upon thy meat offering?**  
**Answer:** The salt of the covenant of thy God.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:13

**Q3. What must you offer with all your offerings?**  
**Answer:** Salt.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:13

---

### Leviticus 2:14

**Q1. If thou offer a meat offering of thy firstfruits unto the LORD, what must you offer for the meat offering of thy firstfruits?**  
**Answer:** Green ears of corn dried by the fire, even corn beaten out of full ears.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:14

---

### Leviticus 2:15

**Q1. What shall you put upon the corn?**  
**Answer:** Oil.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:15

**Q2. What shall you lay thereon?**  
**Answer:** Frankincense.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:15

**Q3. What kind of offering is it?**  
**Answer:** A meat offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:15

---

### Leviticus 2:16

**Q1. What shall the priest burn as the memorial of it?**  
**Answer:** Part of the beaten corn thereof, and part of the oil thereof, with all the frankincense thereof.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:16

**Q2. What kind of offering is this unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** An offering made by fire unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 2:16
