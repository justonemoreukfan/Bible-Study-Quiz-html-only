# Leviticus 20 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Leviticus 20:1

**Q1. Who spake unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 20:1

**Q2. To whom did the LORD speak?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 20:1

**Q3. What did the LORD say to Moses?**  
**Answer:** Saying.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 20:1

---

### Leviticus 20:2

**Q1. To whom else shalt thou say something besides the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever there is of the children of Israel, or of the strangers that sojourn in Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 20:2

**Q2. To whom shall he give of his seed, which is what is forbidden?**  
**Answer:** Molech.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 20:2

**Q3. What shall surely happen to him that gives of his seed unto Molech?**  
**Answer:** He shall surely be put to death.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 20:2

---

### Leviticus 20:3

**Q1. Against whom will the LORD set His face?**  
**Answer:** Against that man.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 20:3

**Q2. From among whom will the LORD cut him off?**  
**Answer:** From among his people.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 20:3

**Q3. Why will the LORD cut him off from among his people?**  
**Answer:** Because he hath given of his seed unto Molech, to defile my sanctuary, and to profane my holy name.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 20:3

---

### Leviticus 20:4

**Q1. And if the people of the land do what regarding the man who gives of his seed unto Molech?**  
**Answer:** Hide their eyes in any way from the man.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 20:4

**Q2. What action do the people fail to take against that man?**  
**Answer:** When he giveth of his seed unto Molech, and kill him not.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 20:4

**Q3. What will the LORD do in that case?**  
**Answer:** Then I will set my face against that man, and against his family.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 20:4

---

### Leviticus 20:5

**Q1. Against whom else will the LORD set His face besides that man?**  
**Answer:** And against his family.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 20:5

**Q2. Whom will the LORD cut off, and all that go a whoring after him?**  
**Answer:** Him.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 20:5

**Q3. Whom will they go a whoring after to commit whoredom with him?**  
**Answer:** Even to commit whoredom with Molech, from among their people.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 20:5

---

**Answer:** With his neighbour's wife.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 20:10

**Answer:** Even he that committeth adultery with his neighbour's wife.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 20:10

**Verse:** Leviticus 20:11
