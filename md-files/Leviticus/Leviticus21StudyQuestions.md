# Leviticus 21 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Leviticus 21:1

**Q1. Who did the LORD speak unto concerning the defilement among the people?**  
**Answer:** Moses  
**Verse:** Leviticus 21:1

**Q2. Whose sons were spoken to regarding the dead?**  
**Answer:** The sons of Aaron  
**Verse:** Leviticus 21:1

**Q3. Who among his kin shall a priest not defile himself for?**  
**Answer:** The dead  
**Verse:** Leviticus 21:1

---

### Leviticus 21:2

**Q1. Who are the kin that a priest may defile himself for?**  
**Answer:** His kin, that is near unto him  
**Verse:** Leviticus 21:2

**Q2. Which female relative is listed first as someone he may defile himself for?**  
**Answer:** His mother  
**Verse:** Leviticus 21:2

**Q3. Which male relative is listed among those he may defile himself for?**  
**Answer:** His father  
**Verse:** Leviticus 21:2

---

### Leviticus 21:3

**Q1. Which female relative of his own house may he defile himself for?**  
**Answer:** His sister  
**Verse:** Leviticus 21:3

**Q2. What condition must his sister be in for him to defile himself for her?**  
**Answer:** A virgin  
**Verse:** Leviticus 21:3

**Q3. Why is she noted as being a suitable relative for defilement?**  
**Answer:** Because she hath had no husband  
**Verse:** Leviticus 21:3

---

### Leviticus 21:4

**Q1. What term is used to describe a chief man among his people?**  
**Answer:** A chief man  
**Verse:** Leviticus 21:4

**Q2. What should a priest not defile himself for, considering him as a chief man?**  
**Answer:** Being a chief man among his people  
**Verse:** Leviticus 21:4

**Q3. What is the consequence mentioned regarding defiling himself for a chief man?**  
**Answer:** To defile himself  
**Verse:** Leviticus 21:4

---

### Leviticus 21:5

**Answer:** Baldness upon their head  
**Verse:** Leviticus 21:5

**Answer:** The corner of their beard  
**Verse:** Leviticus 21:5

**Q3. What incision are they forbidden to make in their flesh?**  
**Answer:** Any cuttings in their flesh  
**Verse:** Leviticus 21:5

**Verse:** Leviticus 21:6

**Verse:** Leviticus 21:6

**Verse:** Leviticus 21:6

**Answer:** A woman put away from her husband  
**Verse:** Leviticus 21:7
