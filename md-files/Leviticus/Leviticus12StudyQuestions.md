# Leviticus 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Leviticus 12:1

**Q1. Who spoke unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:1

**Q2. To whom did the LORD speak?**  
**Answer:** Unto Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:1

**Q3. Whose instructions are recorded in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The LORD'S instructions.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:1

---

### Leviticus 12:2

**Q4. Unto whom shall Moses speak?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:2

**Q5. What shall a woman conceive and bear?**  
**Answer:** A man child.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:2

**Q6. How long shall the woman be unclean after bearing a man child, as in the days of her separation?**  
**Answer:** Seven days.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:2

---

### Leviticus 12:3

**Q7. What shall be done in the eighth day?**  
**Answer:** The flesh of his foreskin shall be circumcised.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:3

**Q8. Whose flesh shall be circumcised?**  
**Answer:** The child's.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:3

**Q9. On which day shall the child be circumcised?**  
**Answer:** In the eighth day.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:3

---

### Leviticus 12:4

**Q10. How many days shall she then continue in the blood of her purifying?**  
**Answer:** Three and thirty days.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:4

**Q11. What shall she touch during the days of her purifying?**  
**Answer:** No hallowed thing.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:4

**Q12. Where shall she not come until the days of her purifying be fulfilled?**  
**Answer:** Into the sanctuary.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:4

---

### Leviticus 12:5

**Q13. What shall the woman bear if she yield a maid?**  
**Answer:** Two weeks.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:5

**Q14. How long shall she be unclean when she bears a maid, as in her separation?**  
**Answer:** Two weeks.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:5

**Q15. How many days shall she continue in the blood of her purifying after bearing a maid?**  
**Answer:** Threescore and six days.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:5

---

### Leviticus 12:6

**Q16. When are the days of her purifying fulfilled, for a son or for a daughter?**  
**Answer:** When the days of her purifying are fulfilled.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:6

**Q17. What shall she bring for a burnt offering unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation?**  
**Answer:** A lamb of the first year.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:6

**Q18. What shall she bring for a sin offering unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation?**  
**Answer:** A young pigeon, or a turtledove.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:6

---

### Leviticus 12:7

**Q19. To whom shall the priest offer it?**  
**Answer:** Before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:7

**Q20. What shall the priest make for her?**  
**Answer:** An atonement for her.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:7

**Q21. What shall be cleansed from the issue of her blood?**  
**Answer:** She shall be cleansed.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:7

---

### Leviticus 12:8

**Q22. What shall she bring if her hand be not able to bring a lamb?**  
**Answer:** Two turtles, or two young pigeons.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:8

**Q23. Which one shall be for the burnt offering and which for the sin offering?**  
**Answer:** The one for the burnt offering, and the other for the sin offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:8

**Q24. Who shall make an atonement for her?**  
**Answer:** The priest.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 12:8
