# Leviticus 22 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Leviticus 22:1

**Q1. Who did the Lord speak unto?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:1

**Q2. What did the Lord tell Moses to speak unto?**  
**Answer:** Aaron and to his sons.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:1

**Q3. What instructions should Aaron and his sons concerning the holy things of the children of Israel be given?**  
**Answer:** That they separate themselves from the holy things of the children of Israel, and that they profane not my holy name in those things which they hallow unto me.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:1

---

### Leviticus 22:2

**Q1. Who should Aaron and his sons speak unto regarding the holy things?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:2

**Q2. What should the children of Israel be separated from while handling the holy things?**  
**Answer:** That they separate themselves from the holy things of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:2

**Q3. Whose name should not be profaned by the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The Lord's holy name.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:2

---

### Leviticus 22:3

**Q1. What phrase describes the descendants of Aaron in future generations who approach the holy things?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever he be of all your seed among your generations.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:3

**Q2. What state must a person be in when he goes unto the holy things?**  
**Answer:** Having his uncleanness upon him. (Wait, he shall be cut off from my presence: that is the negative; the state of uncleanness is what incurs being cut off).  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:3

**Q3. What will happen to the soul that approaches the holy things having his uncleanness upon him?**  
**Answer:** That soul shall be cut off from my presence.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:3

---

### Leviticus 22:4

**Q1. Which of the seed of Aaron is forbidden to eat of the holy things if he has a specific condition?**  
**Answer:** What man soever of the seed of Aaron is a leper, or hath a running issue.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:4

**Q2. When may a leper or one with a running issue eat of the holy things?**  
**Answer:** Until he be clean.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:4

**Q3. What other things make a person unclean, preventing them from eating of the holy things?**  
**Answer:** Whoso toucheth any thing that is unclean by the dead, or a man whose seed goeth from him; Or whosoever toucheth any creeping thing, whereby he may be made unclean, or a man of whom he may take uncleanness, whatsoever uncleanness he hath.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:4

---

### Leviticus 22:5

**Q1. What specific objects might a person touch to become unclean?**  
**Answer:** Any creeping thing, whereby he may be made unclean.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:5

**Q2. Whose uncleanness can also make a person unclean by touch?**  
**Answer:** A man of whom he may take uncleanness, whatsoever uncleanness he hath.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:5

---

### Leviticus 22:6

**Q1. Until when shall the soul who has touched any such unclean thing remain unclean?**  
**Answer:** Until even.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:6

**Q2. What must the person not eat of until he is clean?**  
**Answer:** Of the holy things.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:6

**Q3. What must a person do with his flesh before he eats of the holy things?**  
**Answer:** Wash his flesh with water.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:6

---

### Leviticus 22:7

**Q1. What time of day must arrive before the priest is clean?**  
**Answer:** When the sun is down.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:7

**Q2. After he is clean, what may the priest afterward eat?**  
**Answer:** The holy things.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:7

**Q3. What term is used to describe the holy things in relation to the priest's food?**  
**Answer:** Because it is his food.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:7

---

### Leviticus 22:8

**Q1. What kind of animal death shall a priest not eat of to defile himself therewith?**  
**Answer:** That which dieth of itself, or is torn with beasts.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:8

---

### Leviticus 22:9

**Q1. What ordinance of the Lord shall they keep?**  
**Answer:** Mine ordinance.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:9

---

### Leviticus 22:10

**Q1. Who shall not eat of the holy thing?**  
**Answer:** There shall no stranger eat of the holy thing.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:10

**Q2. What about a sojourner of the priest, or a hired servant?**  
**Answer:** Shall not eat of the holy thing.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:10

---

### Leviticus 22:11

**Q1. If the priest buy any soul with his money, who may eat of it?**  
**Answer:** He shall eat of it.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:11

**Q2. Who else born in the priest's house may eat of his meat?**  
**Answer:** They that are born in his house.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:11

---

### Leviticus 22:12

**Q1. If a priest's daughter be married unto a stranger, what can she not eat of?**  
**Answer:** She may not eat of an offering of the holy things.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:12

---

### Leviticus 22:13

**Q1. Whose daughter may return unto her father's house, as in her youth, and eat of her father's meat?**  
**Answer:** But if the priest's daughter be a widow, or divorced, and have no child.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:13

**Q2. What condition must be met regarding her children for her to return and eat of her father's meat?**  
**Answer:** And have no child.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:13

---

### Leviticus 22:14

**Q1. What must a man add to the holy thing if he eats it unwittingly?**  
**Answer:** He shall put the fifth part thereof unto it.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:14

**Q2. To whom shall the fifth part of the holy thing be given along with the holy thing?**  
**Answer:** Unto the priest.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:14

---

### Leviticus 22:15

**Q1. What shall the children of Israel not profane regarding the holy things?**  
**Answer:** The holy things of the children of Israel, which they offer unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:15

---

### Leviticus 22:16

**Q1. What do they suffer upon themselves when they eat their holy things?**  
**Answer:** The iniquity of trespass.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:16

**Q2. Who does the sanctifying of the holy things?**  
**Answer:** I the LORD do sanctify them.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:16

---

### Leviticus 22:17

**Q1. Who did the Lord speak unto in verse 17?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:17

---

### Leviticus 22:18

**Q1. Who should Moses speak to regarding their oblations and vows?**  
**Answer:** Aaron, and his sons, and all the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:18

**Q2. What kind of offerings are mentioned in this verse that the children of Israel may offer?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever he be of the house of Israel, or of the strangers in Israel, that will offer his oblation for all his vows, and for all his freewill offerings.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:18

---

### Leviticus 22:19

**Q1. What kind of male must the offering be for it to be accepted?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall offer at your own will a male without blemish.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:19

**Q2. What types of animals from the herd or flock are specified?**  
**Answer:** Of the beeves, of the sheep, or of the goats.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:19

---

### Leviticus 22:20

**Q1. What kind of animal shall ye not offer?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever hath a blemish.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:20

**Q2. Why shall a blemished animal not be offered?**  
**Answer:** For that shall not be acceptable for you.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:20

---

### Leviticus 22:21

**Q1. What kind of offering is brought unto the LORD to accomplish a vow or as a freewill offering?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever offereth a sacrifice of peace offerings unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:21

**Q2. How must the animal be in order to be accepted?**  
**Answer:** It shall be perfect to be accepted; there shall be no blemish therein.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:21

---

### Leviticus 22:22

**Q1. What physical conditions make an animal blind, broken, or maimed?**  
**Answer:** Blind, or broken, or maimed, or having a wen, or scurvy, or scabbed.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:22

**Q2. Where shall these blemished animals not be offered unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Upon the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:22

**Q3. What shall not be made upon the altar by fire for the LORD from these blemished animals?**  
**Answer:** Nor make an offering by fire of them upon the altar unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:22

---

### Leviticus 22:23

**Q1. Which animals may be offered for a freewill offering?**  
**Answer:** Either a bull or a lamb that hath anything superfluous or lacking in his parts.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:23

**Q2. Which type of offering will a bull or lamb with anything superfluous or lacking not be accepted for?**  
**Answer:** For a vow it shall not be accepted.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:23

---

### Leviticus 22:24

**Q1. What conditions of the testicles disqualify an animal from being offered?**  
**Answer:** That which is bruised, or crushed, or broken, or cut.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:24

**Q2. Where shall you not offer these bruised, crushed, broken, or cut animals?**  
**Answer:** In your land.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:24

---

### Leviticus 22:25

**Q1. Whose hand shall you not offer the bread of your God from?**  
**Answer:** Neither from a stranger's hand.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:25

**Q2. Why are these things (from a stranger's hand or having corruption/blemish) not accepted?**  
**Answer:** Because their corruption is in them, and blemishes be in them.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:25

---

### Leviticus 22:26

**Q1. Who spoke unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:26

---

### Leviticus 22:27

**Q1. When a bullock, or a sheep, or a goat is brought forth, how many days must it be under its dam?**  
**Answer:** Seven days.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:27

**Q2. From what day onward shall an offering made by fire be accepted for the LORD?**  
**Answer:** And from the eighth day and thereon it shall be accepted for an offering made by fire unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:27

---

### Leviticus 22:28

**Q1. What specific animal pair shall you not kill on the same day?**  
**Answer:** Whether it be cow or ewe, ye shall not kill it and her young both in one day.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:28

---

### Leviticus 22:29

**Q1. When you will offer a sacrifice of thanksgiving unto the LORD, how should you offer it?**  
**Answer:** Offer it at your own will.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:29

---

### Leviticus 22:30

**Q1. On the same day the thanksgiving sacrifice is offered, when shall it be eaten?**  
**Answer:** On the same day it shall be eaten.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:30

**Q2. What part of the sacrifice shall be left until the morrow?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall leave none of it until the morrow.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:30

**Q3. Who is speaking the command to leave none of it until the morrow?**  
**Answer:** I am the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:30

---

### Leviticus 22:31

**Q1. What commandments shall you keep and do?**  
**Answer:** Therefore shall ye keep my commandments, and do them.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:31

**Q2. Whose commandments are they?**  
**Answer:** Mine (the LORD'S).  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:31

---

### Leviticus 22:32

**Q1. What shall not be profaned?**  
**Answer:** Neither shall ye profane my holy name.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:32

**Q2. Among whom will the Lord be hallowed?**  
**Answer:** But I will be hallowed among the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:32

**Q3. What does the Lord do to the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** I am the LORD which hallow you.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:32

---

### Leviticus 22:33

**Q1. From what land did the Lord bring the children of Israel out?**  
**Answer:** Out of the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:33

**Q2. For what purpose did He bring them out of the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** To be your God.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:33

**Q3. How does the Lord identify Himself at the end of the chapter?**  
**Answer:** I am the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 22:33
