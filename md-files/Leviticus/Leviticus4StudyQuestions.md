# Leviticus 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Leviticus 4:1

**Q1. Who spake unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:1

**Q2. To whom did the LORD speak regarding these matters?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:1

**Q3. Where did the LORD speak unto Moses from?**  
**Answer:** Saying.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:1

---

### Leviticus 4:2

**Q1. To the children of whom shall Moses speak?**  
**Answer:** Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:2

**Q2. What kind of sins are addressed if a soul sins through ignorance?**  
**Answer:** Against any of the commandments of the LORD concerning things which ought not to be done.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:2

**Q3. Against what things shall the soul have sinned ignorantly?**  
**Answer:** Against any of the commandments of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:2

---

### Leviticus 4:3

**Q1. According to whose sin should a young bullock without blemish be offered?**  
**Answer:** According to the sin of the priest that is anointed.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:3

**Q2. What kind of priest is specified in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The priest that is anointed.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:3

**Q3. What offering is to be brought unto the LORD for the priest's sin?**  
**Answer:** A young bullock without blemish unto the LORD for his sin, which he hath sinned.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:3

---

### Leviticus 4:4

**Q1. Unto the door of what place shall the bullock be brought?**  
**Answer:** The tabernacle of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:4

**Q2. Before what object shall the bullock be brought?**  
**Answer:** Before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:4

**Q3. Whose hands shall be laid upon the bullock's head?**  
**Answer:** The priest's hands.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:4

---

### Leviticus 4:5

**Q1. Who shall take of the bullock's blood?**  
**Answer:** The priest that is anointed.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:5

**Q2. Where shall the anointed priest bring the bullock's blood?**  
**Answer:** To the tabernacle of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:5

**Q3. What kind of priest takes the blood to the tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** The priest that is anointed.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:5

---

### Leviticus 4:6

**Q1. With what shall the priest dip his finger?**  
**Answer:** In the blood.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:6

**Q2. How many times shall the priest sprinkle of the blood?**  
**Answer:** Seven times.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:6

**Q3. Before what object shall the blood be sprinkled?**  
**Answer:** Before the LORD, before the vail of the sanctuary.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:6

---

### Leviticus 4:7

**Q1. Upon the horns of what altar shall the priest put some of the blood?**  
**Answer:** The altar of sweet incense before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:7

**Q2. Where is the altar of sweet incense located?**  
**Answer:** In the tabernacle of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:7

**Q3. What shall be poured at the bottom of the altar of the burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** All the rest of the blood of the bullock.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:7

---

### Leviticus 4:8

**Q1. What shall he take off from the bullock for the sin offering?**  
**Answer:** All the fat of the bullock for the sin offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:8

**Q2. What fat covers what part of the body?**  
**Answer:** The fat that covereth the inwards, and all the fat that is upon the inwards.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:8

---

### Leviticus 4:9

**Q1. What are the two kidneys mentioned with?**  
**Answer:** The fat that is upon them.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:9

**Q2. Where are the kidneys and their fat located?**  
**Answer:** Which is by the flanks.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:9

**Q3. What shall be taken away with the kidneys?**  
**Answer:** The caul above the liver.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 4:9

### Leviticus 4:10

**Answer:** Upon the altar of the burnt offering.

### Leviticus 4:11

**Q1. What shall be carried forth without the camp?**  
**Answer:** The skin of the bullock, and all his flesh, with his head, and with his legs, and his inwards, and his dung.

**Verse:** Leviticus 4:11

### Leviticus 4:12
