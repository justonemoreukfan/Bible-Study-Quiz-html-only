### Leviticus 19:1

**Q1. Who spoke unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 19:1

**Q2. To whom did the LORD speak?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 19:1

**Q3. Who was commanded to speak to all the congregation of the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 19:1
