# Leviticus 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Leviticus 6:1

**Q1. Who spake unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:1

**Q2. Unto whom did the Lord speak?**  
**Answer:** Unto Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:1

**Q3. What did the Lord say?**  
**Answer:** Saying.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:1

---

### Leviticus 6:2

**Q1. If a soul sin, and commit a trespass against whom?**  
**Answer:** Against the Lord.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:2

**Q2. What might someone lie unto his neighbour about?**  
**Answer:** In that which was delivered him to keep, or in fellowship, or in a thing taken away by violence, or have deceived his neighbour.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:2

**Q3. What else might someone do regarding his neighbour?**  
**Answer:** Have deceived his neighbour.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:2

---

### Leviticus 6:3

**Q1. What might someone have found, and lied concerning it?**  
**Answer:** That which was lost.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:3

**Q2. When someone swears falsely, what does he do in any of all these that a man doeth?**  
**Answer:** Sinneth therein.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:3

**Q3. Besides finding that which was lost and swearing falsely, what else is mentioned?**  
**Answer:** Sweareth falsely.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:3

---

### Leviticus 6:4

**Q1. Because he hath sinned, and is guilty, what shall he restore?**  
**Answer:** That which he took violently away, or the thing which he hath deceitfully gotten, or that which was delivered him to keep, or the lost thing which he found.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:4

**Q2. What kind of thing might he have gotten deceitfully?**  
**Answer:** The thing which he hath deceitfully gotten.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:4

**Q3. What kind of thing might he have found?**  
**Answer:** The lost thing which he found.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:4

---

### Leviticus 6:5

**Q1. About what shall he restore it in the principal?**  
**Answer:** All that about which he hath sworn falsely.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:5

**Q2. How much shall he add to it?**  
**Answer:** The fifth part more thereto.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:5

**Q3. To whom shall he give it in the day of his trespass offering?**  
**Answer:** Unto him to whom it appertaineth.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:5

### Leviticus 6:9

**Q1. Whom shall Moses command?**  
**Answer:** Aaron and his sons.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:9

**Q2. What law is this concerning?**  
**Answer:** The law of the burnt offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:9

**Q3. Where shall the burnt offering be all night unto the morning?**  
**Answer:** Upon the hearth upon the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:9

---

### Leviticus 6:10

**Q1. What shall the priest put on?**  
**Answer:** His linen garment, and his linen breeches shall he put upon his flesh.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:10

**Q2. What shall he take up?**  
**Answer:** The ashes which the fire hath consumed with the burnt offering on the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:10

**Q3. Where shall he put the ashes?**  
**Answer:** Beside the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:10

---

### Leviticus 6:11

**Q1. What shall the priest put off after taking up the ashes?**  
**Answer:** His garments.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:11

**Q2. What shall he put on before carrying the ashes?**  
**Answer:** Other garments.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:11

**Q3. Where shall he carry the ashes?**  
**Answer:** Without the camp unto a clean place.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:11

---

### Leviticus 6:12

**Q1. What shall be burning upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** The fire.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:12

**Q2. What must not happen to the fire on the altar?**  
**Answer:** It shall not be put out.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:12

**Q3. What shall the priest burn on it every morning?**  
**Answer:** Wood.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:12

---

### Leviticus 6:13

**Q1. What kind of fire shall ever be burning upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** A fire shall ever be burning upon the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:13

**Q2. What must happen to the fire?**  
**Answer:** It shall never go out.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:13

**Q3. Is the fire allowed to go out?**  
**Answer:** No ("shall never go out").  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:13

### Leviticus 6:14

**Q1. What is this the law of?**  
**Answer:** The meat offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:14

**Q2. Who shall offer the meat offering?**  
**Answer:** The sons of Aaron.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:14

**Q3. Before whom shall they offer it?**  
**Answer:** Before the Lord, before the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:14

---

### Leviticus 6:15

**Q1. What shall he take of it?**  
**Answer:** His handful, of the flour of the meat offering, and of the oil thereof.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:15

**Q2. What else shall he take from the meat offering?**  
**Answer:** All the frankincense which is upon the meat offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:15

**Q3. Where shall he burn it upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** Upon the altar for a sweet savour, even the memorial of it, unto the Lord.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:15

---

### Leviticus 6:16

**Q1. What shall Aaron and his sons eat of the remainder?**  
**Answer:** The remainder thereof shall Aaron and his sons eat.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:16

**Q2. How shall it be eaten?**  
**Answer:** With unleavened bread.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:16

**Q3. Where shall they eat it?**  
**Answer:** In the holy place; in the court of the tabernacle of the congregation they shall eat it.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:16

### Leviticus 6:30

**Q1. What sin offering shall not be eaten?**  
**Answer:** And no sin offering, whereof any of the blood is brought into the tabernacle of the congregation to reconcile withal in the holy place, shall be eaten.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:30

**Q2. Where shall the sin offering not to be eaten be burned?**  
**Answer:** It shall be burnt in the fire.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:30

**Q3. Where is the blood brought in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Into the tabernacle of the congregation to reconcile withal in the holy place.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 6:30
