# Leviticus 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Leviticus 9:1

**Q1. On what day did Moses call Aaron, his sons, and the elders of Israel?**  
**Answer:** On the eighth day.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:1

**Q2. Whom did Moses call together in Leviticus 9:1?**  
**Answer:** Aaron, his sons, and the elders of Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:1

**Q3. Who called Aaron, his sons, and the elders of Israel on the eighth day?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:1

---

### Leviticus 9:2

**Q1. What did Moses tell Aaron to take for a sin offering?**  
**Answer:** A young calf.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:2

**Q2. What did Moses tell Aaron to take for a burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** A ram.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:2

**Q3. Without what blemish were the calf and the ram to be?**  
**Answer:** Without blemish.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:2

---

### Leviticus 9:3

**Q1. Unto whom was Moses to speak regarding the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Unto the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:3

**Q2. What kind of animal were the children of Israel to take for a sin offering?**  
**Answer:** A kid of the goats.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:3

**Q3. What animals were the children of Israel to take for a burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** A calf and a lamb, both of the first year, without blemish.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:3

---

### Leviticus 9:4

**Q1. What else were the children of Israel to take for a peace offering before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** A bullock and a ram.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:4

**Q2. With what was the meat offering to be mingled?**  
**Answer:** Oil.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:4

**Q3. Why were these offerings to be brought before the LORD on that day?**  
**Answer:** For to day the LORD will appear unto you.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:4

---

### Leviticus 9:5

**Q1. Where did the congregation bring the things that Moses commanded?**  
**Answer:** Before the tabernacle of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:5

**Q2. Who commanded the things that the congregation brought?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:5

**Q3. Who drew near and stood before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** All the congregation.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:5

---

### Leviticus 9:6

**Q1. Who said, "This is the thing which the LORD commanded that ye should do"?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:6

**Q2. What would appear unto the congregation when they did what the LORD commanded?**  
**Answer:** The glory of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:6

**Q3. Who said that the glory of the LORD would appear unto them?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:6

---

### Leviticus 9:7

**Q1. To whom did Moses say, "Go unto the altar, and offer thy sin offering, and thy burnt offering"?**  
**Answer:** Unto Aaron.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:7

**Q2. For whom was Aaron to make an atonement besides himself?**  
**Answer:** For the people.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:7

**Q3. Whose offering was Aaron to offer after making an atonement for himself and the people?**  
**Answer:** The offering of the people.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:7

---

### Leviticus 9:8

**Q1. Who went unto the altar in Leviticus 9:8?**  
**Answer:** Aaron.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:8

**Q2. What did Aaron do to the calf of the sin offering?**  
**Answer:** He slew it.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:8

**Q3. For whom was the calf of the sin offering which Aaron slew?**  
**Answer:** For himself.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:8

---

### Leviticus 9:9

**Q1. Who brought the blood unto Aaron?**  
**Answer:** The sons of Aaron.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:9

**Q2. What did Aaron dip his finger in?**  
**Answer:** The blood.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:9

**Q3. Where did Aaron put the blood after dipping his finger in it?**  
**Answer:** Upon the horns of the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:9

---

### Leviticus 9:10

**Q1. What parts of the sin offering did Aaron burn upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** The fat, and the kidneys, and the caul above the liver.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:10

**Q2. Whose command did Aaron follow when burning these parts upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** As the LORD commanded Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:10

**Q3. Upon what did Aaron burn the fat, kidneys, and caul?**  
**Answer:** Upon the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:10

---

### Leviticus 9:11

**Q1. What did Aaron burn with fire without the camp?**  
**Answer:** The flesh and the hide.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:11

**Q2. Where were the flesh and the hide burned?**  
**Answer:** Without the camp.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:11

**Q3. Whose command was followed regarding the burning of the flesh and the hide?**  
**Answer:** As the LORD commanded Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:11

---

### Leviticus 9:12

**Q1. Who slew the burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** Aaron.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:12

**Q2. Who presented the blood unto Aaron?**  
**Answer:** Aaron's sons.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:12

**Q3. What did Aaron do with the blood round about the altar?**  
**Answer:** Sprinkled it round about upon the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:12

---

### Leviticus 9:13

**Q1. What did they bring unto Aaron regarding the burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** The burnt offering unto him, with the pieces thereof, and the head.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:13

**Q2. Where did Aaron burn the pieces and the head?**  
**Answer:** Upon the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:13

**Q3. What was the burnt offering burned with?**  
**Answer:** Fire upon the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:13

---

### Leviticus 9:14

**Q1. What did Aaron wash before burning them on the burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** The inwards and the legs.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:14

**Q2. Upon what did Aaron burn the inwards and the legs?**  
**Answer:** Upon the burnt offering on the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:14

**Q3. Whose burnt offering were the inwards and the legs burned upon?**  
**Answer:** Upon the burnt offering on the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:14

---

### Leviticus 9:15

**Q1. Whose offering did Aaron bring next?**  
**Answer:** The people's offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:15

**Q2. What specific animal did Aaron take for the people's sin offering?**  
**Answer:** The goat which was the sin offering for the people.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:15

**Q3. What did Aaron do with the goat for the people's sin offering?**  
**Answer:** Slew it, and offered it for sin, as the first.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:15

---

### Leviticus 9:16

**Q1. What did Aaron bring?**  
**Answer:** The burnt offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:16

**Q2. How did Aaron offer the burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** According to the manner.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:16

**Q3. Whose command or custom was followed for offering the burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** As was ordained / according to the manner.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:16

---

### Leviticus 9:17

**Q1. What did Aaron bring alongside the meat offering?**  
**Answer:** The meat offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:17

**Q2. What did Aaron do with his hand with the meat offering?**  
**Answer:** Took an handful thereof.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:17

**Q3. Where did Aaron burn the handful of the meat offering?**  
**Answer:** Upon the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:17

### Leviticus 9:18

**Q1. What animals did Aaron slay as a sacrifice of peace offerings?**  
**Answer:** The bullock and the ram.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:18

**Q2. For whom were the bullock and the ram slain?**  
**Answer:** For the people.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:18

**Q3. Who delivered the blood of the peace offerings unto Aaron?**  
**Answer:** Aaron's sons.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:18

---

### Leviticus 9:19

**Q1. What was taken from the bullock and from the ram regarding fat?**  
**Answer:** The fat.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:19

**Q2. What parts were included with the fat from the bullock and ram?**  
**Answer:** The rump, and that which covereth the inwards, and the kidneys, and the caul above the liver.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:19

---

### Leviticus 9:20

**Q1. Where did they put the fat?**  
**Answer:** Upon the breasts.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:20

**Q2. What did Aaron do with the fat upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** He burned the fat upon the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:20

---

**Q1. What did Aaron wave before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The breasts and the right shoulder.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:21

**Answer:** For a wave offering before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:21

**Answer:** As Moses commanded.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:21

**Answer:** Aaron.

**Answer:** After he had offered the sin offering, and the burnt offering, and peace offerings.

**Q3. Toward whom did Aaron lift up his hand?**  
**Answer:** Toward the people.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:22

**Q1. Who went into the tabernacle of the congregation with Moses?**  
**Answer:** Aaron.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:23

**Q2. What happened after Moses and Aaron came out and blessed the people?**  
**Answer:** The glory of the LORD appeared unto all the people.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:23

**Answer:** Moses and Aaron.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:23

### Leviticus 9:24

**Q1. What came out from before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** There came a fire out from before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:24

**Q2. What did the fire consume upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** The burnt offering and the fat.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:24

**Q3. What did all the people do when they saw the fire consume the offering?**  
**Answer:** They shouted, and fell on their faces.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 9:24
