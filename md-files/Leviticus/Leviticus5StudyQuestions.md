# Leviticus 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Leviticus 5:1

**Q1. What kind of voice is a person adjured by in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The voice of swearing.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:1

**Q2. What is the person who witnessed or knew of the matter described as being?**  
**Answer:** A witness.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:1

**Q3. What consequence will the person bear if he does not utter it?**  
**Answer:** His iniquity.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:1

---

### Leviticus 5:2

**Q1. What unclean thing might a person touch?**  
**Answer:** Any unclean thing, whether it be a carcass of an unclean beast, or a carcass of unclean cattle, or the carcass of unclean creeping things.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:2

**Q2. What is the person's status after touching an unclean thing, even if he is unaware of it?**  
**Answer:** He also is unclean, and guilty.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:2

**Q3. What happens regarding the person's awareness of touching the unclean thing?**  
**Answer:** It be hidden from him.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:2

---

### Leviticus 5:3

**Q1. What kind of uncleanness might a man touch?**  
**Answer:** The uncleanness of man.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:3

**Q2. What happens to the person when he knows it, having forgotten or not realized it at first?**  
**Answer:** Then he is guilty.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:3

**Q3. How is the man's uncleanness described as being similar to touching an unclean beast?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever uncleanness *it be* that a man shall be defiled withal, and it be hid from him.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:3

---

### Leviticus 5:4

**Q1. What does a person do thoughtlessly with his lips?**  
**Answer:** Swear, pronouncing to do evil, or to do good.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:4

**Q2. What kind of an oath is it when a man pronounces it with an oath?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever *it be* that a man shall pronounce with an oath.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:4

**Q3. What happens when the person knows of it after the fact?**  
**Answer:** Then he shall be guilty in one of these.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:4

---

### Leviticus 5:5

**Q1. What must a person do when he is guilty in one of these things?**  
**Answer:** He shall confess that he hath sinned in that *thing*.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:5

**Q2. When is the confession to be made?**  
**Answer:** When he shall be guilty in one of *these things*.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:5

**Q3. What specific action does the sinner confess to?**  
**Answer:** That he hath sinned.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:5

---

### Leviticus 5:6

**Q1. What must the person bring to the Lord for his trespass which he hath sinned?**  
**Answer:** A female from the flock, a lamb or a kid of the goats, for a sin offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:6

**Q2. What gender must the sheep or goat be that is brought?**  
**Answer:** A female.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:6

**Q3. Who shall make an atonement for the person concerning his sin?**  
**Answer:** The priest.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:6

---

### Leviticus 5:7

**Q1. If a person is not able to bring a lamb, what should he bring to the Lord for his trespass?**  
**Answer:** Two turtledoves, or two young pigeons.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:7

**Q2. What is the limit of the person's financial ability mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** If he be not able to bring a lamb.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:7

**Q3. What are the two types of birds specified for the offering?**  
**Answer:** Two turtledoves, or two young pigeons.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:7

---

### Leviticus 5:8

**Q1. Who shall bring the birds to the priest?**  
**Answer:** He shall bring them unto the priest.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:8

**Q2. What must the priest do to the head of the first bird?**  
**Answer:** Wring off his head from his neck.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:8

**Q3. How far should the head be divided from the body?**  
**Answer:** But shall not divide *it* asunder.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:8

---

### Leviticus 5:9

**Q1. Upon what part of the altar shall the priest sprinkle some of the blood of the sin offering?**  
**Answer:** Upon the side of the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:9

**Q2. What happens to the rest of the blood?**  
**Answer:** The rest of the blood shall be wrung out at the bottom of the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:9

**Q3. What kind of offering is this blood associated with?**  
**Answer:** A sin offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:9

---

### Leviticus 5:10

**Q1. What must be done with the second bird after the first?**  
**Answer:** He shall offer the second *for* a burnt offering, according to the manner.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:10

**Q2. According to what standard should the second bird be offered?**  
**Answer:** According to the manner.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:10

**Q3. Who shall make an atonement for the person for his sin which he hath sinned?**  
**Answer:** The priest.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:10

---

### Leviticus 5:11

**Q1. What if the person is not able to bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons?**  
**Answer:** Then he that sinned shall bring for his offering the tenth part of an ephah of fine flour for a sin offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:11

**Q2. How much fine flour must he bring?**  
**Answer:** The tenth part of an ephah.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:11

**Q3. What must the person not put upon the fine flour offering?**  
**Answer:** He shall put no oil upon it, neither shall he put any frankincense thereon.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:11

---

### Leviticus 5:12

**Q1. To whom shall the person bring the handful of fine flour?**  
**Answer:** To the priest.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:12

**Q2. What part of the flour shall the priest take as a memorial?**  
**Answer:** His handful of it.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:12

**Q3. Where shall the priest burn the memorial upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** Upon the altar, according to the offerings made by fire unto the Lord.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:12

---

### Leviticus 5:13

**Q1. Who shall make an atonement for the person touching his sin in one of these?**  
**Answer:** The priest.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:13

**Q2. What will happen to the person after the atonement is made?**  
**Answer:** It shall be forgiven him.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:13

**Q3. To whom shall the remnant of the offering belong, just as in the meat offering?**  
**Answer:** The remnant shall be the priest's.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:13

---

### Leviticus 5:14

**Q1. To whom did the Lord speak in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Unto Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:14

**Q2. What did the Lord say?**  
**Answer:** Saying.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:14

**Q3. Whose instructions were given to Moses regarding this matter?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:14

---

### Leviticus 5:15

**Q1. What kind of trespass does a person commit if he commits a trespass through ignorance in the holy things of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** A trespass.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:15

**Q2. What must the person bring to the Lord for his trespass?**  
**Answer:** A ram without blemish out of the flocks.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:15

**Q3. How is the value of the ram measured for estimation?**  
**Answer:** With thy estimation by shekels of silver, after the shekel of the sanctuary.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:15

---

### Leviticus 5:16

**Q1. What must the person make amends for regarding the holy thing?**  
**Answer:** That which he hath done amiss in the holy thing.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:16

**Q2. What additional fraction must the person add to the amount?**  
**Answer:** The fifth part.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:16

**Q3. To whom shall the priest give the ram of the trespass offering?**  
**Answer:** Unto the priest.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:16

---

### Leviticus 5:17

**Q1. What if a soul sin, and commit any of these things which are forbidden to be done by what?**  
**Answer:** The commandments of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:17

**Q2. What is true of the soul even if he wist *it* not?**  
**Answer:** Yet he is guilty, and shall bear his iniquity.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:17

**Q3. What does the soul bear for committing the forbidden act unknowingly?**  
**Answer:** His iniquity.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:17

---

**Q1. What must the person bring to the priest from the flock?**  
**Answer:** A ram without blemish.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:18

**Q2. According to what valuation must the ram be brought?**  
**Answer:** With thy estimation, for a trespass offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:18

**Q1. What kind of offering is this described as being?**  
**Answer:** A trespass offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:19

**Q2. Against whom has the person clearly trespassed in this context?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:19

**Q3. What did the person actually do against the Lord?**  
**Answer:** He hath certainly trespassed against the Lord.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 5:19
