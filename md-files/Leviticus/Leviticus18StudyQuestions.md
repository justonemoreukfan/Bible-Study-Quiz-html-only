# Leviticus 18 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Leviticus 18:1

**Q1. Who spake unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 18:1

**Q2. To whom did the LORD speak?**  
**Answer:** Unto Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 18:1

**Q3. What action did the LORD take regarding Moses?**  
**Answer:** Spake unto him.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 18:1

---

### Leviticus 18:2

**Q1. Whom was Moses told to speak unto?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 18:2

**Q2. What did the LORD identify Himself as to them?**  
**Answer:** The LORD your God.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 18:2

**Q3. Whose children were instructed to be spoken to?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 18:2
