# Leviticus 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Leviticus 1:1

**Q1. Who called unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:1

**Q2. From where did the LORD speak unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** Out of the tabernacle of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:1

**Q3. To whom did the LORD speak this message?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:1

---

### Leviticus 1:2

**Q1. Who was Moses commanded to speak unto?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:2

**Q2. What were the children of Israel to bring when they brought an offering unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** An offering of the cattle, even of the herd, and of the flock.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:2

**Q3. Whose cattle were they to bring their offering from?**  
**Answer:** The cattle, even of the herd, and of the flock.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:2

---

### Leviticus 1:3

**Q1. If his offering be a burnt sacrifice of what kind must it be?**  
**Answer:** Of the herd.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:3

**Q2. What kind of animal of the herd must he offer?**  
**Answer:** A male without blemish.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:3

**Q3. Where shall he offer it, and for what purpose?**  
**Answer:** He shall offer it of his own voluntary will at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:3

---

### Leviticus 1:4

**Q1. What shall the offerer put upon the head of the burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** His hand.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:4

**Q2. What will the burnt offering be accepted for?**  
**Answer:** To make atonement for him.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:4

**Q3. Before whom shall the offerer put his hand upon the head of the offering?**  
**Answer:** Before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:4

---

### Leviticus 1:5

**Q1. Who shall kill the bullock before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The priest's sons.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:5

**Q2. What shall Aaron's sons bring and sprinkle round about?**  
**Answer:** The blood.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:5

**Q3. Where shall Aaron's sons sprinkle the blood?**  
**Answer:** Upon the altar that is by the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:5

---

### Leviticus 1:6

**Q1. What shall the offerer do to the burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** Flay the burnt offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:6

**Q2. Into what parts shall the burnt offering be cut?**  
**Answer:** His pieces.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:6

**Q3. What is done to the burnt offering after it is flayed?**  
**Answer:** Cut it into his pieces.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:6

---

### Leviticus 1:7

**Q1. Whose sons shall put fire upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** The sons of Aaron the priest.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:7

**Q2. What shall be put upon the altar along with the fire?**  
**Answer:** Wood in order upon the fire.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:7

**Q3. Who are the sons mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Aaron the priest.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:7

---

### Leviticus 1:8

**Q1. Who shall lay the parts, the head, and the fat in order?**  
**Answer:** The priests, Aaron's sons.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:8

**Q2. Where shall the parts, the head, and the fat be laid?**  
**Answer:** Upon the wood that is on the fire which is upon the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:8

**Q3. What specific parts of the animal are listed to be laid in order?**  
**Answer:** The parts, the head, and the fat.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:8

---

### Leviticus 1:9

**Q1. What parts of the animal shall be washed with water?**  
**Answer:** His inwards and his legs.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:9

**Q2. Who shall burn all on the altar?**  
**Answer:** The priest.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:9

**Q3. What kind of sacrifice is this described to be unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** A burnt sacrifice, an offering made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:9

---

### Leviticus 1:10

**Q1. If his offering be of the flocks, what kind of animals may he bring?**  
**Answer:** Of the sheep, or of the goats.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:10

**Q2. For a burnt sacrifice from the flocks, what gender must the animal be?**  
**Answer:** A male without blemish.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:10

**Q3. From what group must this animal be taken?**  
**Answer:** The flocks.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:10

---

### Leviticus 1:11

**Q1. On which side of the altar shall he kill it?**  
**Answer:** On the northward side of the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:11

**Q2. Before whom shall he kill the animal on the northward side of the altar?**  
**Answer:** Before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:11

**Q3. What shall Aaron's sons do with the blood of the animal?**  
**Answer:** Sprinkle his blood round about upon the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:11

---

### Leviticus 1:12

**Q1. Into what parts shall he cut his offering?**  
**Answer:** His head and his fat.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:12

**Q2. Where shall the priest lay the head and the fat?**  
**Answer:** In order on the wood that is on the fire which is upon the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:12

**Q3. What else must be laid in order with the head and the fat?**  
**Answer:** His pieces.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:12

---

### Leviticus 1:13

**Q1. What parts shall be washed with water?**  
**Answer:** The inwards and the legs.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:13

**Q2. What shall the priest bring and burn upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** All it, and burn it upon the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:13

**Q3. What kind of offering is this described to be at the end of the verse?**  
**Answer:** A burnt sacrifice, an offering made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:13

---

### Leviticus 1:14

**Q1. If the burnt sacrifice for the LORD is of fowls, what kind of fowls may be brought?**  
**Answer:** Turtledoves, or of young pigeons.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:14

**Q2. To whom is the burnt sacrifice of fowls brought?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:14

**Q3. What are the specific birds mentioned for this offering?**  
**Answer:** Turtledoves, or of young pigeons.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:14

---

### Leviticus 1:15

**Q1. Who shall bring the fowl unto the altar?**  
**Answer:** The priest.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:15

**Q2. What shall the priest do to the head of the fowl at the altar?**  
**Answer:** Wring off his head, and burn it on the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:15

**Q3. What shall be wrung out at the side of the altar?**  
**Answer:** The blood thereof.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:15

---

### Leviticus 1:16

**Q1. What part of the fowl shall the priest take away?**  
**Answer:** His crop with his feathers.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:16

**Q2. Where shall the priest cast what he took away?**  
**Answer:** Beside the altar on the east part.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:16

**Q3. In what place, specifically, shall it be cast?**  
**Answer:** By the place of the ashes.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:16

---

### Leviticus 1:17

**Q1. What shall the priest do with the wings of the fowl?**  
**Answer:** Cleave it with the wings thereof.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:17

**Q2. To what extent should the wings be cleaved?**  
**Answer:** But shall not divide it asunder.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:17

**Q3. Where shall the priest burn the fowl upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** Upon the wood that is upon the fire.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 1:17
