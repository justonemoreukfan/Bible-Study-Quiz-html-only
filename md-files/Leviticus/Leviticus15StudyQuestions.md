# Leviticus 15 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Leviticus 15:1

**Q1. Unto whom did the Lord speak?**  
**Answer:** Moses and to Aaron.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 15:1

**Q2. Who spoke unto Moses and Aaron?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 15:1

**Q3. Whose instructions were given to Moses and Aaron?**  
**Answer:** The Lord's.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 15:1

---

### Leviticus 15:2

**Q1. Who were the children of Israel to speak unto?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 15:2

**Q2. What condition pertaining to a man's flesh is described?**  
**Answer:** When any man hath a running issue out of his flesh.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 15:2

**Q3. Because of his running issue, what is the man declared to be?**  
**Answer:** Because of his issue he is unclean.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 15:2

---

### Leviticus 15:3

**Q1. What is this uncleanness in his flesh concerning?**  
**Answer:** His issue.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 15:3

**Q2. What can his flesh do with his issue?**  
**Answer:** Run with his issue.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 15:3

**Q3. What else can his flesh do regarding his issue?**  
**Answer:** Be stopped from his issue.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 15:3
