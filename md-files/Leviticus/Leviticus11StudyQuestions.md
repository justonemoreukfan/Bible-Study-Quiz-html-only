# Leviticus 11 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Leviticus 11:1

**Q1. Who did the LORD speak unto in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Moses and to Aaron.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 11:1

**Q2. Who spoke unto Moses and Aaron?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 11:1

**Q3. Along with Moses, who else did the LORD speak to?**  
**Answer:** Aaron.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 11:1

---

### Leviticus 11:2

**Q1. To whom was Moses and Aaron to speak regarding these things?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 11:2

**Q2. What kind of beasts are the children of Israel to eat?**  
**Answer:** The beasts which ye shall eat among all the beasts that are on the earth.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 11:2

**Q3. Where are the beasts mentioned located?**  
**Answer:** On the earth.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 11:2

### Leviticus 11:4

**Q1. Which animals should ye not eat of them that chew the cud?**

**Verse:** Leviticus 11:4

**Q2. What is said about the camel in this verse?**  
**Answer:** As the camel, because he cheweth the cud, but divideth not the hoof; he is unclean unto you.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 11:4

**Q3. Why is the camel unclean unto you?**  
**Answer:** Because he cheweth the cud, but divideth not the hoof.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 11:4
