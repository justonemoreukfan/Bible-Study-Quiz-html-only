# Leviticus 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Leviticus 3:1

**Q1. What kind of offering is described if a man's oblation be a sacrifice of peace offering?**  
**Answer:** Of the herd.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 3:1

**Q2. What must the animal offered from the herd be, whether it be a male or female?**  
**Answer:** Without blemish.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 3:1

**Q3. Before whom must the animal be offered?**  
**Answer:** Before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 3:1

---

### Leviticus 3:2

**Q1. Upon whose head shall the offerer lay his hand?**  
**Answer:** The head of his offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 3:2

**Q2. Where shall the offering be killed?**  
**Answer:** At the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 3:2

**Q3. What shall Aaron's sons the priests sprinkle upon the altar round about?**  
**Answer:** The blood.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 3:2

---

### Leviticus 3:3

**Q1. What shall be offered of the sacrifice of the peace offering as an offering made by fire unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The fat that covereth the inwards, and all the fat that is upon the inwards.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 3:3

---

### Leviticus 3:4

**Q1. What are the two kidneys that shall be taken away with the fat?**  
**Answer:** The two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, which is by the flanks.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 3:4

**Q2. What part above the liver shall be taken away with the kidneys?**  
**Answer:** The caul above the liver.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 3:4

**Q3. With what shall the caul above the liver be taken away?**  
**Answer:** The kidneys.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 3:4

---

### Leviticus 3:5

**Q1. Where shall Aaron's sons burn the fat on the altar?**  
**Answer:** Upon the burnt sacrifice.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 3:5

**Q2. Upon what wood shall the burnt sacrifice be placed?**  
**Answer:** The wood that is on the fire.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 3:5

**Q3. What kind of savour is this offering made by fire unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** A sweet savour.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 3:5

---

### Leviticus 3:6

**Answer:** The fat thereof.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 3:9

**Q2. Near to what bone shall the whole rump be taken off?**  
**Answer:** The backbone.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 3:9

**Answer:** All the fat that covereth the inwards, and all the fat that is upon the inwards.
