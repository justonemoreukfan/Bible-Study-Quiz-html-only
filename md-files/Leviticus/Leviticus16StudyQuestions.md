# Leviticus 16 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Leviticus 16:1

**Q1. After whose death did the Lord speak unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** After the death of the two sons of Aaron.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:1

**Q2. When did the two sons of Aaron die?**  
**Answer:** When they offered before the Lord.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:1

**Q3. To whom did the Lord speak after the death of Aaron's sons?**  
**Answer:** Unto Moses.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:1

---

### Leviticus 16:2

**Q1. Who did the Lord say unto Moses that Aaron should not come at all times into the holy place?**  
**Answer:** Aaron his brother.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:2

**Q2. Where should Aaron not come at all times?**  
**Answer:** Within the vail before the mercy seat, which is upon the ark.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:2

**Q3. Why should Aaron not come at all times into the holy place?**  
**Answer:** That he die not.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:2

---

### Leviticus 16:3

**Q1. With what shall Aaron come into the holy place?**  
**Answer:** With a young bullock for a sin offering, and a ram for a burnt offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:3

---

### Leviticus 16:4

**Q1. What holy linen coat shall Aaron put on?**  
**Answer:** The holy linen coat.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:4

**Q2. What shall be upon his flesh?**  
**Answer:** The linen breeches.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:4

**Q3. With what shall he be girded and mitred?**  
**Answer:** With the linen girdle, and with the linen mitre.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:4

---

### Leviticus 16:5

**Q1. What shall Aaron take of the congregation of the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Two kids of the goats for a sin offering, and one ram for a burnt offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:5

---

### Leviticus 16:6

**Q1. Whose bullock of the sin offering shall Aaron offer first?**  
**Answer:** His own.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:6

**Q2. What shall Aaron make for himself, and for his house?**  
**Answer:** An atonement.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:6

---

### Leviticus 16:7

**Q1. What shall Aaron take?**  
**Answer:** The two goats.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:7

**Q2. Where shall Aaron present the two goats?**  
**Answer:** Before the Lord at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:7

---

### Leviticus 16:8

**Q1. What shall Aaron cast upon the two goats?**  
**Answer:** Lots.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:8

**Q2. For whom shall one lot be?**  
**Answer:** For the Lord.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:8

**Q3. For whom shall the other lot be?**  
**Answer:** For the scapegoat.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:8

---

### Leviticus 16:9

**Q1. Whose goat shall Aaron bring and offer for a sin offering?**  
**Answer:** The goat upon which the Lord's lot fell.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:9

---

### Leviticus 16:10

**Q1. Where shall the goat be presented alive before the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Upon which the lot fell to be the scapegoat.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:10

**Q2. Why shall the goat be presented alive before the Lord?**  
**Answer:** To make an atonement with him.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:10

**Q3. Whither shall the goat be sent?**  
**Answer:** Into the wilderness for a scapegoat.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:10

---

### Leviticus 16:11

**Q1. Whose bullock of the sin offering shall Aaron bring?**  
**Answer:** The bullock of the sin offering which is his own.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:11

**Q2. What shall Aaron make for himself, and for his house?**  
**Answer:** An atonement.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:11

**Q3. What shall Aaron do with the bullock of the sin offering which is his?**  
**Answer:** Kill it.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:11

---

### Leviticus 16:12

**Q1. What shall Aaron take full of?**  
**Answer:** A censer full of burning coals of fire from off the altar before the Lord.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:12

**Q2. What shall he take his hands full of?**  
**Answer:** Sweet incense beaten small.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:12

**Q3. Where shall he bring the censer and the sweet incense?**  
**Answer:** Within the vail.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:12

---

### Leviticus 16:13

**Q1. What shall he put upon the fire before the Lord?**  
**Answer:** The incense.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:13

**Q2. What shall the cloud of the incense cover?**  
**Answer:** The mercy seat that is upon the testimony.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:13

**Q3. Why shall the incense cover the mercy seat?**  
**Answer:** That he die not.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:13

---

### Leviticus 16:14

**Q1. What shall Aaron take of the blood of the bullock?**  
**Answer:** Some of the blood.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:14

**Q2. With what shall he sprinkle the blood?**  
**Answer:** With his finger upon the mercy seat eastward.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:14

**Q3. Before what shall he sprinkle the blood with his finger seven times?**  
**Answer:** Before the mercy seat.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:14

---

### Leviticus 16:15

**Q1. Whose goat of the sin offering shall Aaron kill?**  
**Answer:** The goat of the sin offering, that is for the people.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:15

**Q2. Where shall he bring the blood of the goat?**  
**Answer:** Within the vail.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:15

**Q3. What shall he do with that blood as he did with the blood of the bullock?**  
**Answer:** Sprinkle it upon the mercy seat, and before the mercy seat.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:15

---

### Leviticus 16:16

**Q1. What shall Aaron make an atonement for?**  
**Answer:** The holy place.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:16

**Q2. Because of what uncleanness shall he make atonement?**  
**Answer:** Because of the uncleanness of the children of Israel, and because of their transgressions in all their sins.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:16

**Q3. Where shall he do this for the tabernacle of the congregation?**  
**Answer:** Among them in the midst of their uncleanness.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:16

---

### Leviticus 16:17

**Q1. Who shall be in the tabernacle of the congregation when he goeth in to make an atonement in the holy place?**  
**Answer:** There shall be no man.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:17

**Q2. Until when shall no man be in the tabernacle of the congregation?**  
**Answer:** Until he come out.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:17

**Q3. For whom shall he make an atonement?**  
**Answer:** For himself, and for his household, and for all the congregation of Israel.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:17

---

### Leviticus 16:18

**Q1. Unto what altar shall he go out?**  
**Answer:** Unto the altar that is before the Lord.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:18

**Q2. What shall he make for the altar?**  
**Answer:** An atonement.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:18

**Q3. What blood shall he take and put upon the horns of the altar round about?**  
**Answer:** The blood of the bullock, and of the goat.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:18

---

### Leviticus 16:19

**Q1. What shall he sprinkle upon the altar with his finger?**  
**Answer:** Of the blood upon it seven times.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:19

**Q2. What shall he do to the altar from the uncleanness of the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Cleanse it.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:19

**Q3. What else shall he do to the altar besides cleansing it?**  
**Answer:** Hallow it.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:19

---

### Leviticus 16:20

**Q1. When has he made an end of reconciling the holy place, and the tabernacle of the congregation, and the altar?**  
**Answer:** When he hath made an end of reconciling them.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:20

**Q2. What goat shall he bring?**  
**Answer:** The live goat.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:20

---

### Leviticus 16:21

**Q1. What shall Aaron lay upon the head of the live goat?**  
**Answer:** Both his hands.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:21

**Q2. What shall he confess over the live goat?**  
**Answer:** All the iniquities of the children of Israel, and all their transgressions in all their sins.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:21

**Q3. Upon whose head shall he put them?**  
**Answer:** Upon the head of the goat.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:21

---

### Leviticus 16:22

**Q1. Whither shall the goat bear upon him?**  
**Answer:** All their iniquities.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:22

**Q2. Unto what kind of land shall the goat bear their iniquities?**  
**Answer:** Unto a land not inhabited.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:22

**Answer:** The man appointed for the same.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:22

**Answer:** An atonement shall be made for you.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:30

**Q2. From what shall ye be clean before the Lord?**  
**Answer:** From all your sins.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:30

---

### Leviticus 16:31

**Q1. What kind of sabbath shall it be unto you?**  
**Answer:** A sabbath of rest.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:31

**Q2. What shall ye afflict?**  
**Answer:** Your souls.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:31

**Q3. By what sort of statute shall ye afflict your souls?**  
**Answer:** By a statute for ever.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:31

---

### Leviticus 16:32

**Q1. Who shall make the atonement?**  
**Answer:** The priest, whom he shall anoint.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:32

**Q2. Whose stead shall the priest be consecrated in?**  
**Answer:** In his father's stead.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:32

**Q3. What garments shall the priest put on?**  
**Answer:** The linen clothes, even the holy garments.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:32

---

### Leviticus 16:33

**Q1. For what three main entities shall he make an atonement?**  
**Answer:** The holy sanctuary, the tabernacle of the congregation, and the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:33

**Q2. For whom else shall he make an atonement?**  
**Answer:** For the priests, and for all the people of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:33

---

### Leviticus 16:34

**Q1. What shall this be an everlasting statute unto you?**  
**Answer:** To make an atonement for the children of Israel for all their sins once a year.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:34

**Q2. Who did as the Lord commanded Moses?**  
**Answer:** Aaron.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 16:34
