# Leviticus 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Leviticus 8:1

**Q1. Who did the LORD speak unto?**  
**Answer:** Moses  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:1

**Q2. What did the LORD say?**  
**Answer:** "Take Aaron and his sons with him"  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:1

**Q3. Whose garments and anointing oil were to be taken?**  
**Answer:** Aaron's garments, and the anointing oil  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:1

---

### Leviticus 8:2

**Q1. Who was to be taken along with Aaron and his sons?**  
**Answer:** His sons with him  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:2

**Q2. What garments were to be taken?**  
**Answer:** The garments  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:2

**Q3. What else was to be taken besides the garments and the anointing oil?**  
**Answer:** A bull for the sin offering, and two rams, and a basket of unleavened bread  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:2

---

### Leviticus 8:3

**Q1. Who was to be gathered together?**  
**Answer:** All the congregation  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:3

**Q2. Where was the congregation to be gathered?**  
**Answer:** Unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:3

---

### Leviticus 8:4

**Q1. Who did as the LORD commanded him?**  
**Answer:** Moses  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:4

**Q2. What was assembled unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation?**  
**Answer:** The congregation  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:4

---

### Leviticus 8:5

**Q1. Who spoke unto the congregation?**  
**Answer:** Moses  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:5

**Q2. What did Moses say?**  
**Answer:** "This is the thing which the LORD commanded to be done"  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:5

---

### Leviticus 8:6

**Q1. Who did Moses bring near?**  
**Answer:** Aaron and his sons  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:6

**Q2. What did Moses wash them with?**  
**Answer:** Water  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:6

---

### Leviticus 8:7

**Q1. What did Moses put upon Aaron?**  
**Answer:** The coat  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:7

**Q2. What did Moses gird him with?**  
**Answer:** The girdle  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:7

**Q3. What else was put upon Aaron in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The robe, and the ephod, and the curious girdle of the ephod  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:7

---

### Leviticus 8:8

**Q1. What did Moses put upon Aaron?**  
**Answer:** The breastplate  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:8

**Q2. What did Moses put in the breastplate?**  
**Answer:** The Urim and the Thummim  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:8

---

### Leviticus 8:9

**Q1. What did Moses put upon Aaron's head?**  
**Answer:** The mitre  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:9

**Q2. What was put upon the mitre, even upon his forefront?**  
**Answer:** The golden plate, the holy crown  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:9

**Q3. According to what did Moses do this?**  
**Answer:** As the LORD commanded Moses  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:9

---

### Leviticus 8:10

**Q1. What did Moses take?**  
**Answer:** The anointing oil  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:10

**Q2. What did Moses anoint?**  
**Answer:** The tabernacle and all that was therein  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:10

**Q3. What did Moses do to sanctify them?**  
**Answer:** Anointed them  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:10

---

### Leviticus 8:11

**Q1. How many times did Moses sprinkle oil upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** Seven times  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:11

**Q2. What did Moses anoint along with the altar?**  
**Answer:** All its vessels, both the laver and his foot  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:11

---

### Leviticus 8:12

**Q1. What did Moses pour upon Aaron's head?**  
**Answer:** The anointing oil  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:12

**Q2. What was Aaron anointed to?**  
**Answer:** To sanctify him  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:12

---

### Leviticus 8:13

**Q1. Who did Moses bring near after Aaron?**  
**Answer:** Aaron's sons  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:13

**Q2. What did Moses put upon Aaron's sons?**  
**Answer:** Coats, and girded them with girdles, and put bonnets upon them  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:13

**Q3. Who commanded these things concerning Aaron's sons?**  
**Answer:** The LORD  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:13

---

### Leviticus 8:14

**Q1. What did Moses bring?**  
**Answer:** The bull for the sin offering  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:14

**Q2. Who laid their hands upon the head of the bull for the sin offering?**  
**Answer:** Aaron and his sons  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:14

---

### Leviticus 8:15

**Q1. Who killed the bull?**  
**Answer:** Moses  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:15

**Q2. What did Moses take and put upon the horns of the altar round about with his finger?**  
**Answer:** The blood  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:15

**Q3. What did Moses purify with the blood?**  
**Answer:** The altar  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:15

---

### Leviticus 8:16

**Q1. What did Moses take that was upon the inwards?**  
**Answer:** All the fat that was upon the inwards, and the caul above the liver, and the two kidneys, and their fat  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:16

**Q2. Where did Moses burn the fat and parts?**  
**Answer:** Upon the altar  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:16

---

### Leviticus 8:17

**Q1. What was burned with fire outside the camp?**  
**Answer:** The bull, his hide, his flesh, and his dung  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:17

**Q2. According to what was this done?**  
**Answer:** As the LORD commanded Moses  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:17

---

### Leviticus 8:18

**Q1. What ram was brought?**  
**Answer:** The ram for the burnt offering  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:18

**Q2. Who laid their hands upon the head of the ram?**  
**Answer:** Aaron and his sons  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:18

---

### Leviticus 8:19

**Q1. Who killed the ram?**  
**Answer:** Moses  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:19

**Q2. What did Moses sprinkle upon the altar round about?**  
**Answer:** The blood  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:19

---

### Leviticus 8:20

**Q1. Into what pieces did Moses cut the ram?**  
**Answer:** Into the pieces thereof  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:20

**Q2. What did Moses burn?**  
**Answer:** The head, and the pieces, and the fat  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:20

---

### Leviticus 8:21

**Q1. With what did Moses wash the inwards and the legs?**  
**Answer:** With water  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:21

**Q2. Where did Moses burn the whole ram?**  
**Answer:** Upon the altar  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:21

**Q3. What kind of sacrifice was this ram made into?**  
**Answer:** A burnt sacrifice for a sweet savour, an offering made by fire unto the LORD  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:21

---

### Leviticus 8:22

**Q1. What other ram was brought?**  
**Answer:** The other ram, the ram of consecration  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:22

**Q2. Who laid their hands upon the head of the ram?**  
**Answer:** Aaron and his sons  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:22

---

### Leviticus 8:23

**Q1. Who killed the ram of consecration?**  
**Answer:** Moses  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:23

**Q2. Whose tip of the right ear did Moses take blood from?**  
**Answer:** Aaron's  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:23

**Q3. What other body parts of Aaron were touched with the blood of the ram?**  
**Answer:** The thumb of his right hand, and the great toe of his right foot  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:23

---

### Leviticus 8:24

**Q1. Who did Moses bring near after Aaron?**  
**Answer:** Aaron's sons  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:24

**Q2. What did Moses put on the tip of the sons' right ears, right thumbs, and right toes?**  
**Answer:** Of the blood  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:24

**Q3. Where did Moses sprinkle the blood upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** Round about  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:24

---

### Leviticus 8:25

**Q1. What fat did Moses take?**  
**Answer:** The fat, and the rump, and all the fat that was upon the inwards, and the caul above the liver, and the two kidneys, and their fat  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:25

**Q2. Whose shoulder was taken along with the fat?**  
**Answer:** The right shoulder  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:25

---

### Leviticus 8:26

**Q1. What was taken out of the basket of unleavened bread?**  
**Answer:** One unleavened cake, and a cake of oiled bread, and one wafer  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:26

**Q2. Where were these cakes and wafer put?**  
**Answer:** On the fat, and upon the right shoulder  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:26

---

### Leviticus 8:27

**Q1. Upon whose hands were all these things put?**  
**Answer:** Upon Aaron's hands, and upon his sons' hands  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:27

**Q2. How were these things waved?**  
**Answer:** For a wave offering before the LORD  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:27

---

### Leviticus 8:28

**Q1. Who took them from off their hands?**  
**Answer:** Moses  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:28

**Q2. Where did Moses burn them?**  
**Answer:** Upon the altar upon the burnt offering  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:28

**Q3. What kind of offering were they for a sweet savour?**  
**Answer:** Consecrations for a sweet savour: it is an offering made by fire unto the LORD  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:28

---

### Leviticus 8:29

**Q1. What did Moses take?**  
**Answer:** The breast  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:29

**Q2. For what was the breast waved?**  
**Answer:** For a wave offering before the LORD  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:29

**Q3. Whose part of the ram of consecration was this breast?**  
**Answer:** Moses' part  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:29

---

### Leviticus 8:30

**Q1. What did Moses take of the anointing oil, and of the blood which was upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** Some  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:30

**Q2. Upon whom did Moses sprinkle the oil and blood?**  
**Answer:** Upon Aaron, and upon his garments, and upon his sons, and upon his sons' garments with him  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:30

**Q3. What was sanctified by this sprinkling?**  
**Answer:** Aaron, and his garments, and his sons, and his sons' garments with him  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:30

---

**Q1. What did Moses say to Aaron and to his sons?**  
**Answer:** "Boil the flesh at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation"  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:31

**Q2. Where was the flesh to be eaten?**  
**Answer:** There  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:31

**Q3. With what was the flesh and bread to be eaten?**  
**Answer:** With the bread that is in the basket of consecrations  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:31

---

### Leviticus 8:32

**Q1. What was to be burned with fire if anything remained?**  
**Answer:** That which remaineth of the flesh and of the bread  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:32

---

### Leviticus 8:33

**Q1. From where were they not to go out for seven days?**  
**Answer:** Out of the door of the tabernacle of the congregation  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:33

**Q2. Until what was accomplished were they not to go out?**  
**Answer:** Until the days of your consecration be at an end  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:33

**Q3. How many days were the days of their consecration?**  
**Answer:** Seven days  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:33

---

### Leviticus 8:34

**Q1. As what has been done this day?**  
**Answer:** What has been done this day  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:34

**Q2. Who commanded to do this, to make an atonement for you?**  
**Answer:** The LORD  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:34

---

### Leviticus 8:35

**Q1. Where were they to abide day and night?**  
**Answer:** At the door of the tabernacle of the congregation  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:35

**Q2. What were they to keep?**  
**Answer:** The charge of the LORD  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:35

**Q3. Why must they keep the charge of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** That ye die not  
**Verse:** Leviticus 8:35
