# Leviticus 7 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Leviticus 7:1

**Q1. What kind of offering is described in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The trespass offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 7:1

**Q2. What is said about the trespass offering in this verse?**  
**Answer:** It is most holy.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 7:1

**Q3. Where is the trespass offering to be killed according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** In the place where they kill the burnt offering.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 7:1

---

### Leviticus 7:2

**Q2. Where shall they kill the blood of the trespass offering?**  
**Answer:** Round about the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 7:2

**Q3. What shall be done with the blood of the trespass offering?**  
**Answer:** It shall be sprinkled round about upon the altar.  
**Verse:** Leviticus 7:2
