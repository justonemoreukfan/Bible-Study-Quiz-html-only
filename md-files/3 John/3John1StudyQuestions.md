# III John 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### III John 1:1

**Q1. Unto whom does the elder write in III John 1:1?**  
**Answer:** Unto the wellbeloved Gaius.  
**Verse:** III John 1:1

**Q2. How does the elder say he loves Gaius?**  
**Answer:** In the truth.  
**Verse:** III John 1:1

**Q3. Who writes this epistle according to III John 1:1?**  
**Answer:** The elder.  
**Verse:** III John 1:1

---

### III John 1:2

**Q4. What does the writer wish above all things for the beloved?**  
**Answer:** That thou mayest prosper and be in health.  
**Verse:** III John 1:2

**Q5. What is to prosper according to III John 1:2?**  
**Answer:** Thy soul.  
**Verse:** III John 1:2

**Q6. In what two ways does the writer wish the beloved well?**  
**Answer:** To prosper and be in health.  
**Verse:** III John 1:2

---

### III John 1:3

**Q7. What made the writer rejoice greatly?**  
**Answer:** The brethren came and testified of the truth that is in thee.  
**Verse:** III John 1:3

**Q8. In what does Gaius walk according to III John 1:3?**  
**Answer:** In the truth.  
**Verse:** III John 1:3

**Q9. Who came and bore testimony in III John 1:3?**  
**Answer:** The brethren.  
**Verse:** III John 1:3

---

### III John 1:4

**Q10. What does the writer say he has no greater joy than?**  
**Answer:** To hear that my children walk in truth.  
**Verse:** III John 1:4

**Q11. Who are said to walk in truth in III John 1:4?**  
**Answer:** My children.  
**Verse:** III John 1:4

**Q12. What kind of joy is mentioned in III John 1:4?**  
**Answer:** No greater joy.  
**Verse:** III John 1:4

---

### III John 1:5

**Q13. How does the beloved act in what he does?**  
**Answer:** Thou doest faithfully.  
**Verse:** III John 1:5

**Q14. To whom does he do this faithful work?**  
**Answer:** To the brethren, and to strangers.  
**Verse:** III John 1:5

**Q15. What is said of the beloved's deeds in III John 1:5?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever thou doest, thou doest faithfully.  
**Verse:** III John 1:5

---

### III John 1:6

**Q16. What did these brethren bear witness of before the church?**  
**Answer:** Thy charity.  
**Verse:** III John 1:6

**Q17. How should they be brought forward on their journey?**  
**Answer:** After a godly sort.  
**Verse:** III John 1:6

**Q18. What will happen if thou bring them forward after a godly sort?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt do well.  
**Verse:** III John 1:6

---

### III John 1:7

**Q19. For whose sake did they go forth?**  
**Answer:** For his name's sake.  
**Verse:** III John 1:7

**Q20. What did they take from the Gentiles?**  
**Answer:** Nothing.  
**Verse:** III John 1:7

**Q21. From whom did they take nothing in III John 1:7?**  
**Answer:** Of the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** III John 1:7

---

### III John 1:8

**Q22. What ought we to do for such people?**  
**Answer:** We ought to receive such.  
**Verse:** III John 1:8

**Q23. What may we be if we receive such?**  
**Answer:** Fellowhelpers to the truth.  
**Verse:** III John 1:8

**Q24. To what are we fellowhelpers according to III John 1:8?**  
**Answer:** To the truth.  
**Verse:** III John 1:8

---

### III John 1:9

**Q25. Unto whom had the writer written?**  
**Answer:** Unto the church.  
**Verse:** III John 1:9

**Q26. Who loveth to have the preeminence among them?**  
**Answer:** Diotrephes.  
**Verse:** III John 1:9

**Q27. What does Diotrephes do concerning the writer and his companions?**  
**Answer:** He receiveth us not.  
**Verse:** III John 1:9

---

### III John 1:10

**Q28. What does the writer say he will remember if he comes?**  
**Answer:** His deeds which he doeth.  
**Verse:** III John 1:10

**Q29. How does Diotrephes speak against them?**  
**Answer:** Prating against us with malicious words.  
**Verse:** III John 1:10

**Q30. What does Diotrephes do to those who would receive the brethren?**  
**Answer:** He forbiddeth them, and casteth them out of the church.  
**Verse:** III John 1:10

---

### III John 1:11

**Q31. What should the beloved not follow?**  
**Answer:** That which is evil.  
**Verse:** III John 1:11

**Q32. What should the beloved follow instead?**  
**Answer:** That which is good.  
**Verse:** III John 1:11

**Q33. Who is of God according to III John 1:11?**  
**Answer:** He that doeth good.  
**Verse:** III John 1:11

---

### III John 1:12

**Q34. Who has good report of all men?**  
**Answer:** Demetrius.  
**Verse:** III John 1:12

**Q35. Of what else does Demetrius have good report?**  
**Answer:** Of the truth itself.  
**Verse:** III John 1:12

**Q36. What do the writers also bear concerning Demetrius?**  
**Answer:** Record.  
**Verse:** III John 1:12

---

### III John 1:13

**Q37. How many things did the writer have to write?**  
**Answer:** I had many things to write.  
**Verse:** III John 1:13

**Q38. With what did the writer choose not to write?**  
**Answer:** With ink and pen.  
**Verse:** III John 1:13

**Q39. What two writing tools are named in III John 1:13?**  
**Answer:** Ink and pen.  
**Verse:** III John 1:13

---

### III John 1:14

**Q40. What does the writer trust he will do shortly?**  
**Answer:** I shall shortly see thee.  
**Verse:** III John 1:14

**Q41. How does the writer hope to speak with the reader?**  
**Answer:** Face to face.  
**Verse:** III John 1:14

**Q42. How should the friends be greeted?**  
**Answer:** Greet the friends by name.  
**Verse:** III John 1:14
