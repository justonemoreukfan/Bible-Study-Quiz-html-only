# 3 John 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 3 John 1:1

**Q1. Who is writing the letter?**  
**Answer:** The elder.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:1

**Q2. To whom is the letter written?**  
**Answer:** Unto the wellbeloved Gaius.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:1

**Q3. In what does the writer love Gaius?**  
**Answer:** In the truth.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:1

---

### 3 John 1:2

**Q1. What does the writer wish above all things for the beloved?**  
**Answer:** That thou mayest prosper and be in health.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:2

**Q2. In what specific way should the beloved prosper and be in health?**  
**Answer:** Even as thy soul prospereth.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:2

**Q3. What two things are mentioned as goals for the beloved's wellbeing?**  
**Answer:** To prosper and be in health.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:2

---

### 3 John 1:3

**Q1. Why did the writer rejoice greatly?**  
**Answer:** When the brethren came and testified of the truth that is in thee.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:3

**Q2. What did the brethren testify of?**  
**Answer:** Of the truth that is in thee.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:3

**Q3. How is the beloved described as walking?**  
**Answer:** Even as thou walkest in the truth.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:3

---

### 3 John 1:4

**Q1. What is the writer's greatest joy?**  
**Answer:** To hear that my children walk in truth.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:4

**Q2. What does the writer have no greater joy than to hear?**  
**Answer:** That my children walk in truth.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:4

**Q3. In what are the children walking?**  
**Answer:** In truth.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:4

---

### 3 John 1:5

**Q1. How does the beloved do whatsoever he doest to the brethren and to strangers?**  
**Answer:** Faithfully.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:5

**Q2. To whom does the beloved do things faithfully?**  
**Answer:** To the brethren, and to strangers.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:5

**Q3. What word is used to describe the actions of the beloved toward others?**  
**Answer:** Faithfully.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:5

---

### 3 John 1:6

**Q1. Before whom have the strangers borne witness of the beloved's charity?**  
**Answer:** Before the church.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:6

**Q2. What have the strangers borne witness of?**  
**Answer:** Of thy charity.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:6

**Q3. What should the beloved do to the strangers to do well?**  
**Answer:** Bring forward on their journey after a godly sort.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:6

---

### 3 John 1:7

**Q1. For whose name's sake did they go forth?**  
**Answer:** For his name's sake.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:7

**Q2. What did they take from the Gentiles?**  
**Answer:** Nothing.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:7

**Q3. From whom did they take nothing?**  
**Answer:** The Gentiles.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:7

---

### 3 John 1:8

**Q1. Why ought we to receive such people?**  
**Answer:** That we might be fellowhelpers to the truth.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:8

**Q2. What should we be if we receive them?**  
**Answer:** Fellowhelpers to the truth.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:8

**Q3. What is the action that makes us fellowhelpers to the truth?**  
**Answer:** To receive such.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:8

---

### 3 John 1:9

**Q1. To whom did the writer write?**  
**Answer:** Unto the church.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:9

**Q2. Who loveth to have the preeminence among the church?**  
**Answer:** Diotrephes.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:9

**Q3. What does Diotrephes do regarding the writers?**  
**Answer:** Receiveth us not.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:9

---

### 3 John 1:10

**Q1. What will the writer remember if he comes?**  
**Answer:** His deeds which he doeth.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:10

**Q2. How does Diotrephes speak against the writers?**  
**Answer:** Prating against us with malicious words.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:10

**Q3. What else does Diotrephes do besides not receiving the writers?**  
**Answer:** Neither doth he himself receive the brethren, and forbiddeth them that would, and casteth them out of the church.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:10

---

### 3 John 1:11

**Q1. What is the beloved told not to follow?**  
**Answer:** Follow not that which is evil.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:11

**Q2. What is the beloved told to follow?**  
**Answer:** That which is good.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:11

**Q3. Who is he that doeth good?**  
**Answer:** He is of God.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:11

---

### 3 John 1:12

**Q1. From whom does Demetrius have a good report?**  
**Answer:** Of all men, and of the truth itself.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:12

**Q2. What does the writer and others bear record of?**  
**Answer:** That Demetrius hath good report.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:12

**Q3. What do the readers know about the record of the writer?**  
**Answer:** That our record is true.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:12

---

### 3 John 1:13

**Q1. What did the writer have many things to write?**  
**Answer:** I had many things to write.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:13

**Q2. How will the writer NOT write to the beloved?**  
**Answer:** Not with ink and pen.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:13

**Q3. What did the writer avoid using to write?**  
**Answer:** Ink and pen.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:13

---

### 3 John 1:14

**Q1. What does the writer trust shall happen shortly?**  
**Answer:** I trust I shall shortly see thee.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:14

**Q2. How shall the writer and the beloved speak?**  
**Answer:** Speak face to face.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:14

**Q3. What closing greetings are given?**  
**Answer:** Peace be to thee. Our friends salute thee. Greet the friends by name.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:14
