# 3 John 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 3 John 1:1

**Q1. Who writes the epistle?**  
**Answer:** The elder.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:1

**Q2. To whom is the epistle written?**  
**Answer:** Unto the wellbeloved Gaius.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:1

**Q3. How does the elder love Gaius?**  
**Answer:** In the truth.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:1

### 3 John 1:2

**Q4. What does the elder wish above all things for Gaius?**  
**Answer:** That he may prosper and be in health.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:2

**Q5. What else does the elder wish for Gaius's health in addition to his prosperity?**  
**Answer:** Even as thy soul prospereth.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:2

**Q6. Whose soul does the elder mention as prospering?**  
**Answer:** Even as thy soul prospereth (Gaius's soul).  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:2

### 3 John 1:3

**Q7. Who rejoiced greatly when the brethren came?**  
**Answer:** The elder.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:3

**Q8. What did the brethren testify of concerning Gaius?**  
**Answer:** Thy truth.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:3

**Q9. How does Gaius walk?**  
**Answer:** Even as thou walkest in the truth.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:3

### 3 John 1:4

**Q10. What does the elder have that brings him greater joy than anything else?**  
**Answer:** I have no greater joy.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:4

**Q11. What specifically causes the elder's greatest joy?**  
**Answer:** To hear that my children walk in truth.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:4

**Q12. Whose walk in truth does the elder hear about?**  
**Answer:** That my children walk in truth.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:4

### 3 John 1:5

**Q13. How does the elder address Gaius in this verse regarding his actions?**  
**Answer:** Beloved.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:5

**Q14. What does Gaius faithfully do whatsoever he does?**  
**Answer:** Thou doest faithfully whatsoever thou doest.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:5

**Q15. To whom does Gaius show this faithful action?**  
**Answer:** To the brethren, and to strangers.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:5

### 3 John 1:6

**Q16. Who bore witness of Gaius's charity?**  
**Answer:** Which have borne witness of thy charity before the church.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:6

**Q17. Before whom did they bear witness of Gaius's charity?**  
**Answer:** Before the church.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:6

**Q18. How does the elder ask Gaius to bring the travelers forward on their journey?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt do well to bring forward on their journey after a godly sort.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:6

### 3 John 1:7

**Q19. For whose name's sake did the travelers go forth?**  
**Answer:** For his name's sake.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:7

**Q20. What did the travelers take from the Gentiles?**  
**Answer:** Taking nothing of the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:7

**Q21. Whose name's sake is mentioned as the reason they went forth?**  
**Answer:** For his name's sake.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:7

### 3 John 1:8

**Q22. Whom ought we to receive according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** We therefore ought to receive such.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:8

**Q23. For what purpose should we receive such persons?**  
**Answer:** That we might be fellowhelpers to the truth.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:8

**Q24. What do we become when we receive them?**  
**Answer:** Fellowhelpers to the truth.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:8

### 3 John 1:9

**Q25. To whom did the elder write unto the church?**  
**Answer:** I wrote unto the church.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:9

**Q26. Who loveth to have the preeminence among them?**  
**Answer:** Diotrephes.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:9

**Q27. What does Diotrephes not receive?**  
**Answer:** Receiveth us not.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:9

### 3 John 1:10

**Q28. What will the elder do if he comes?**  
**Answer:** I will remember his deeds which he doeth.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:10

**Q29. With what does Diotrephes prat against the elder?**  
**Answer:** Prating against us with malicious words.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:10

**Q30. What does Diotrephes do to the brethren, besides not content with that?**  
**Answer:** Neither doth he himself receive the brethren, and forbiddeth them that would, and casteth them out of the church.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:10

### 3 John 1:11

**Q31. What should the beloved not follow?**  
**Answer:** Follow not that which is evil.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:11

**Q32. What should the beloved follow?**  
**Answer:** But that which is good.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:11

**Q33. What is said of him that doeth good?**  
**Answer:** He that doeth good is of God.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:11

### 3 John 1:12

**Q34. Who has good report of all men?**  
**Answer:** Demetrius.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:12

**Q35. What else has good report alongside Demetrius?**  
**Answer:** And of the truth itself.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:12

**Q36. Who also bears record of Demetrius?**  
**Answer:** Yea, and we also bear record.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:12

### 3 John 1:13

**Q37. How many things did the elder have to write?**  
**Answer:** I had many things to write.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:13

**Q38. With what tools did the elder not want to write unto Demetrius/Gaius?**  
**Answer:** I will not with ink and pen write unto thee.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:13

**Q39. What are ink and pen used for in this verse?**  
**Answer:** To write unto thee.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:13

### 3 John 1:14

**Q40. What does the elder trust he shall shortly see?**  
**Answer:** But I trust I shall shortly see thee.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:14

**Q41. How will they speak when they see each other?**  
**Answer:** And we shall speak face to face.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:14

**Q42. What is the final salutation given at the end of the chapter?**  
**Answer:** Peace be to thee. Our friends salute thee. Greet the friends by name.  
**Verse:** 3 John 1:14
