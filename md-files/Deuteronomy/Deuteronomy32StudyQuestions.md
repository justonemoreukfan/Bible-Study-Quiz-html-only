### Deuteronomy 32:1

**Q1. What does the speaker ask the heaven to do?**  
**Answer:** Give ear.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 32:1

**Q2. What does the speaker call the earth to hear?**  
**Answer:** The words of his mouth.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 32:1

**Q3. Who is speaking in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Moses (contextually, addressing heaven and earth).  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 32:1
