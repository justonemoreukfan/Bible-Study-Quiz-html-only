# Deuteronomy 21 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 21:1

**Q1. What land which the LORD thy God giveth thee to possess is spoken of?**  
**Answer:** The land.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:1

**Q2. Who might be found slain in the land?**  
**Answer:** One.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:1

**Q3. Where might the slain person be lying?**  
**Answer:** In the field.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:1

---

### Deuteronomy 21:2

**Q1. Who shall go forth?**  
**Answer:** Thy elders and thy judges.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:2

**Q2. What shall they measure?**  
**Answer:** The cities which are round about him that is slain.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:2

**Q3. What direction shall they measure to the cities?**  
**Answer:** Round about him that is slain.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:2

---

### Deuteronomy 21:3

**Q1. What city's elders are mentioned first in this process?**  
**Answer:** The city which is next unto the slain man.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:3

**Q2. What kind of animal shall the elders of that city take?**  
**Answer:** An heifer.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:3

**Q3. What characteristic must the heifer have regarding labor?**  
**Answer:** Which hath not been wrought with, and which hath not drawn in the yoke.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:3

---

### Deuteronomy 21:4

**Q1. Where shall the elders of that city bring down the heifer?**  
**Answer:** Unto a rough valley.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:4

**Q2. What condition must be true of the valley regarding cultivation and water?**  
**Answer:** Which is neither eared nor sown.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:4

**Q3. What action shall they do unto the heifer in the valley?**  
**Answer:** Strike off the heifer's neck there in the valley.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:4

---

### Deuteronomy 21:5

**Q1. Who shall come near?**  
**Answer:** The priests the sons of Levi.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:5

**Q2. Whom has the LORD thy God chosen to minister unto him?**  
**Answer:** The priests the sons of Levi.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:5

**Q3. Whose name shall every controversy and stroke be by?**  
**Answer:** By their word.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:5

---

### Deuteronomy 21:6

**Q1. Who shall wash their hands over the heifer?**  
**Answer:** All the elders of that city.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:6

**Q2. What body part shall be washed over the heifer?**  
**Answer:** Their hands.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:6

**Q3. Over what specific animal shall their hands be washed?**  
**Answer:** Over the heifer that is beheaded in the valley.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:6

---

### Deuteronomy 21:7

**Q1. What shall the elders answer and say?**  
**Answer:** Our hands have not shed this blood, neither have our eyes seen it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:7

**Q2. Whose blood shall they state was not shed by their hands?**  
**Answer:** This blood.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:7

**Q3. What sense shall they state did not see the shedding of blood?**  
**Answer:** Neither have our eyes seen it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:7

---

### Deuteronomy 21:8

**Q1. Whom shall the elders ask the LORD to be merciful unto?**  
**Answer:** Thy people Israel.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:8

**Q2. Who has the LORD redeemed?**  
**Answer:** Thy people Israel.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:8

**Q3. What blood shall not be laid upon the people of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Innocent blood.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:8

---

### Deuteronomy 21:9

**Q1. What shall thou put away from among you?**  
**Answer:** The guilt of innocent blood.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:9

**Q2. When shall you put away the guilt of innocent blood?**  
**Answer:** When thou shalt do that which is right in the sight of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:9

**Q3. In whose sight must thou do that which is right?**  
**Answer:** In the sight of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:9

---

### Deuteronomy 21:10

**Q1. Against whom shalt thou go forth to war?**  
**Answer:** Thine enemies.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:10

**Q2. Who hath delivered them into thine hands?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:10

**Q3. Whom shalt thou take captive?**  
**Answer:** Captives.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:10

---

### Deuteronomy 21:11

**Q1. What kind of woman might a man see among the captives?**  
**Answer:** A beautiful woman.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:11

**Q2. What feeling might the man have toward the woman?**  
**Answer:** Have a desire unto her.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:11

**Q3. What might the man take her for?**  
**Answer:** To have her to his wife.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:11

---

### Deuteronomy 21:12

**Q1. Where shall he bring her home?**  
**Answer:** To his house.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:12

**Q2. What shall she do to her head?**  
**Answer:** Shave her head.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:12

**Q3. What shall she pare?**  
**Answer:** Her nails.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:12

---

### Deuteronomy 21:13

**Q1. What shall she put off from her?**  
**Answer:** The raiment of her captivity.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:13

**Q2. Where shall she remain?**  
**Answer:** In thine house.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:13

**Q3. For how long shall she bewail her father and her mother?**  
**Answer:** A full month.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:13

---

### Deuteronomy 21:14

**Q1. What must the man have if he has no delight in her?**  
**Answer:** Let her go whither she will.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:14

**Q2. What shall he not sell at all for money?**  
**Answer:** Her.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:14

**Q3. How shall he not treat her because he has humbled her?**  
**Answer:** Make merchandise of her.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:14

---

### Deuteronomy 21:15

**Q1. How many wives might a man have?**  
**Answer:** Two wives.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:15

**Q2. What attitude might the man have toward one wife compared to the other?**  
**Answer:** One beloved, and another hated.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:15

**Q3. Who might bear him children besides the beloved?**  
**Answer:** The hated.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:15

---

### Deuteronomy 21:16

**Q1. When the man divides his inheritance among his sons, what shall he not make?**  
**Answer:** The son of the beloved firstborn before the son of the hated.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:16

**Q2. Which son is acknowledged as the firstborn?**  
**Answer:** Indeed the firstborn, which is the son of the hated.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:16

**Q3. Whose son must not be preferred before the true firstborn?**  
**Answer:** The son of the beloved.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:16

---

### Deuteronomy 21:17

**Q1. Whom shall he acknowledge as the firstborn?**  
**Answer:** The son of the hated.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:17

**Q2. What fraction of all that he has shall he give to the firstborn?**  
**Answer:** A double portion.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:17

**Q3. What is said to be the beginning of his strength regarding the firstborn?**  
**Answer:** The right of the firstborn is his.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:17

### Deuteronomy 21:18

**Q1. What kind of son might a man have who is stubborn and rebellious?**  
**Answer:** A stubborn and rebellious son.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:18

**Q2. Whose voice will the son not obey?**  
**Answer:** The voice of his father, or the voice of his mother.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:18

**Q3. How do they respond when his parents chasten him?**  
**Answer:** He will not hearken unto them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:18

**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:19

**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:19

**Q1. What shall the elders of his city be told about the son?**  
**Answer:** This our son is stubborn and rebellious.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:20

**Q2. Whose voice will the son not obey according to his parents?**  
**Answer:** He will not obey our voice.

**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:21

**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:21

**Q2. What shall happen to the man after he is put to death?**

**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:22

**Verse:** Deuteronomy 21:22
