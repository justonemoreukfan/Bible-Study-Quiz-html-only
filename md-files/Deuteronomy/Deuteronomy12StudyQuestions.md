# Deuteronomy 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 12:1

**Q1. What statutes and judgments are the children of Israel commanded to observe?**  
**Answer:** All the statutes and judgments which ye shall observe to do in the land which the LORD God of thy fathers giveth thee to possess it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:1

**Q2. Whose land is given to the children of Israel to possess?**  
**Answer:** The land of the LORD God of thy fathers.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:1

**Q3. For how long are the children of Israel to observe these statutes and judgments?**  
**Answer:** All the days that ye live upon the earth.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:1

---

### Deuteronomy 12:2

**Q1. What must ye utterly destroy?**  
**Answer:** All the places, wherein the nations which ye shall possess served their gods.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:2

**Q2. Where did the nations serve their gods upon?**  
**Answer:** Upon the high mountains, and upon the hills, and under every green tree.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:2

**Q3. What kind of trees are specifically mentioned where these nations served their gods?**  
**Answer:** Every green tree.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:2

---

### Deuteronomy 12:3

**Q1. What should be done to the altars of the nations?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall overthrow their altars.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:3

**Q2. What should be broken and burned with fire?**  
**Answer:** Break their pillars, and burn their groves with fire.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:3

**Q3. What should be hewn down concerning the graven images of their gods?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall hew down the graven images of their gods.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:3

---

### Deuteronomy 12:4

**Q1. Who are the children of Israel commanded not to do so unto?**  
**Answer:** The LORD your God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:4

**Q2. What should not be done unto the LORD your God?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall not do so unto the LORD your God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:4

**Q3. Who is addressed as "the LORD your God" in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The LORD your God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:4

---

### Deuteronomy 12:5

**Q1. Unto what place shall ye seek?**  
**Answer:** Unto the place which the LORD your God shall choose out of all your tribes.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:5

**Q2. What will the LORD your God put in that place?**  
**Answer:** His name.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:5

**Q3. For what purpose shall ye seek unto His habitation?**  
**Answer:** To his habitation shall ye seek, and thither thou shalt come.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:5

---

### Deuteronomy 12:6

**Q1. What offerings are the children of Israel to bring to that place?**  
**Answer:** Burnt offerings, and your sacrifices, and your tithes, and heave offerings of your hand, and your vows, and your freewill offerings.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:6

**Q2. What kind of offerings from your hand are mentioned?**  
**Answer:** Heave offerings of your hand.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:6

**Q3. What else should be brought besides burnt offerings, sacrifices, and tithes?**  
**Answer:** The firstlings of your herds and of your flocks.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:6

---

### Deuteronomy 12:7

**Q1. Who shall eat before the LORD your God?**  
**Answer:** And there ye shall eat before the LORD your God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:7

**Q2. Who shall rejoice in all that ye put your hand unto?**  
**Answer:** Ye and your households.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:7

**Q3. Who hath blessed thee in your rejoicing?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God hath blessed thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:7

---

### Deuteronomy 12:8

**Q1. What must ye not do after all the things that we do here this day?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall not do after all the things that we do here this day.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:8

**Q2. How is every man doing what is right described?**  
**Answer:** Every man whatsoever is right in his own eyes.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:8

---

### Deuteronomy 12:9

**Q1. To what have ye not as yet come?**  
**Answer:** Ye are not as yet come to the rest and to the inheritance.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:9

**Q2. Who is giving the rest and the inheritance?**  
**Answer:** The LORD your God giveth you.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:9

---

### Deuteronomy 12:10

**Q1. Over what body of water shall ye go?**  
**Answer:** Jordan.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:10

**Q2. Where shall ye dwell after going over Jordan?**  
**Answer:** In the land which the LORD your God giveth you to inherit.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:10

**Q3. What will the LORD give you rest from?**  
**Answer:** From all your enemies round about.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:10

---

### Deuteronomy 12:11

**Q1. What place shall there be?**  
**Answer:** A place which the LORD your God shall choose to cause his name to dwell there.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:11

**Q2. What shall ye bring unto the place which the LORD chooses?**  
**Answer:** All that I command you; your burnt offerings, and your sacrifices, your tithes, and the heave offering of your hand, and all your choice vows which ye vow unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:11

**Q3. What kind of vows are to be brought unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** All your choice vows which ye vow unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:11

---

### Deuteronomy 12:12

**Q1. Who shall rejoice before the LORD your God?**  
**Answer:** Ye, and your sons, and your daughters, and your menservants, and your maidservants.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:12

**Q2. Who else is mentioned as rejoicing along with the household?**  
**Answer:** The Levite that is within your gates.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:12

**Q3. Why has the Levite no part nor inheritance with you?**  
**Answer:** Forasmuch as he hath no part nor inheritance with you.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:12

---

### Deuteronomy 12:13

**Q1. What warning is given concerning the burnt offerings?**  
**Answer:** Take heed to thyself that thou offer not thy burnt offerings in every place that thou seest.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:13

**Q2. Where should a man not offer his burnt offerings?**  
**Answer:** In every place that thou seest.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:13

---

### Deuteronomy 12:14

**Q1. Where shall thou offer thy burnt offerings?**  
**Answer:** In the place which the LORD shall choose in one of thy tribes.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:14

**Q2. What shall thou do there regarding thy offerings?**  
**Answer:** There thou shalt offer thy burnt offerings, and there thou shalt do all that I command thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:14

**Q3. In how many of thy tribes will the LORD choose this place?**  
**Answer:** In one of thy tribes.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:14

---

### Deuteronomy 12:15

**Q1. What may thou kill and eat in all thy gates?**  
**Answer:** Flesh, whatsoever thy soul lusteth after.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:15

**Q2. According to what blessing may thou kill and eat flesh?**  
**Answer:** According to the blessing of the LORD thy God which he hath given thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:15

**Q3. Who may eat the unclean and the clean flesh alike?**  
**Answer:** The unclean and the clean may eat thereof, as of the roebuck, and as of the hart.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:15

---

### Deuteronomy 12:16

**Q1. What must ye not eat?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall not eat the blood.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:16

**Q2. How shall ye pour out the blood?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall pour it upon the earth as water.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:16

---

### Deuteronomy 12:17

**Q1. What are you forbidden to eat within your gates?**  
**Answer:** The tithe of thy corn, or of thy wine, or of thy oil, or the firstlings of thy herds or of thy flock.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:17

**Q2. What kind of vows are also forbidden to be eaten within your gates?**  
**Answer:** Nor any of thy vows which thou vowest, nor thy freewill offerings, or heave offerings of thine hand.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 12:17

---
