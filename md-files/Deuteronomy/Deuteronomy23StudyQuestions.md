# Deuteronomy 23 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 23:1

**Q1. What is not permitted to enter into the congregation of the Lord according to Deuteronomy 23:1?**  
**Answer:** He that is wounded in the stones, or hath his privy member cut off.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:1

**Q2. Whose stones must be wounded for this exclusion to apply?**  
**Answer:** He that is wounded in the stones.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:1

**Q3. What specific part of the body being cut off excludes a man from the congregation?**  
**Answer:** His privy member.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:1

---

### Deuteronomy 23:2

**Q1. Who shall not enter into the congregation of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** A bastard.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:2

**Q2. Up to what generation shall a bastard not enter into the congregation of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Even to his tenth generation.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:2

**Q3. Who else, along with a bastard, shall not enter into the congregation of the Lord to his tenth generation?**  
**Answer:** Even any of his.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:2

---

### Deuteronomy 23:3

**Q1. Who shall not enter into the congregation of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** An Ammonite or Moabite.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:3

**Q2. Up to what generation shall an Ammonite or Moabite not enter into the congregation of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Even to their tenth generation.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:3

**Q3. For how long shall they not enter into the congregation of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Forever.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:3

---

### Deuteronomy 23:4

**Q1. Why did they not meet you with bread and with water in the way?**  
**Answer:** Because they met you not with bread and with water in the way, when ye came forth out of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:4

**Q2. Whom did they hire against thee?**  
**Answer:** Balaam the son of Beor of Pethor of Mesopotamia.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:4

**Q3. What did Balaam do regarding thee?**  
**Answer:** To curse thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:4

---

### Deuteronomy 23:5

**Q1. Whom would the Lord thy God not hearken unto?**  
**Answer:** Balaam.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:5

**Q2. Into what did the Lord thy God turn the curse?**  
**Answer:** A blessing unto thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:5

**Q3. Why did the Lord thy God turn the curse into a blessing unto thee?**  
**Answer:** Because the Lord thy God loved thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:5

---

### Deuteronomy 23:6

**Q1. What shall you not seek all your days forever?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not seek their peace nor their prosperity.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:6

**Q2. How long shall you not seek their peace nor their prosperity?**  
**Answer:** All thy days for ever.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:6

**Q3. Whose peace and prosperity shall you not seek?**  
**Answer:** The Ammonite or Moabite (implied by the context of preceding verses).  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:6

---

### Deuteronomy 23:7

**Q1. Whom shall you not abhor?**  
**Answer:** An Edomite.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:7

**Q2. Why is an Edomite not to be abhorred?**  
**Answer:** For he is thy brother.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:7

**Q3. Whom else shall you not abhor, and why?**  
**Answer:** An Egyptian, because thou wast a stranger in his land.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:7

---

### Deuteronomy 23:8

**Q1. Who are permitted to enter into the congregation of the Lord in their children?**  
**Answer:** The children that are begotten of them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:8

**Q2. Up to what generation may their children enter into the congregation of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** In their third generation.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:8

**Q3. Which specific groups' children are referred to here?**  
**Answer:** Edomites and Egyptians (from the context of verse 7).  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:8

---

### Deuteronomy 23:9

**Q1. When the host goes forth against thine enemies, what must you keep yourself from?**  
**Answer:** Keep thee from every wicked thing.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:9

**Q2. What situation is specifically addressed where enemies are involved?**  
**Answer:** When the host goeth forth against thine enemies.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:9

**Q3. What kind of thing are you commanded to keep yourself from?**  
**Answer:** Every wicked thing.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:9

---

### Deuteronomy 23:10

**Q1. Who among you shall not be clean?**  
**Answer:** Any man, that is not clean by reason of uncleanness that chanceth him by night.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:10

**Q2. When does this uncleanness chance to the man?**  
**Answer:** By night.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:10

**Q3. Where must the man go because of this uncleanness?**  
**Answer:** Then shall he go abroad out of the camp.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:10

---

### Deuteronomy 23:11

**Q1. When shall he come into the camp again?**  
**Answer:** When evening cometh on.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:11

**Q2. What must he do with water when evening comes on?**  
**Answer:** He shall wash himself with water.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:11

**Q3. When can he come into the camp after washing?**  
**Answer:** When the sun is down.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:11

---

### Deuteronomy 23:12

**Q1. What must you have outside of the camp?**  
**Answer:** A place also without the camp.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:12

**Q2. Where shall you go forth to by that place?**  
**Answer:** Whither thou shalt go forth abroad.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:12

**Q3. What is the purpose of this place without the camp?**  
**Answer:** To go thither abroad.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:12

---

### Deuteronomy 23:13

**Q1. What must you have upon your weapon?**  
**Answer:** A paddle upon thy weapon.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:13

**Q2. When you ease yourself abroad, what must you dig with the paddle?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt dig therewith.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:13

**Q3. After digging, what must you do and cover?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt turn back and cover that which cometh from thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:13

---

### Deuteronomy 23:14

**Q1. Who walks in the midst of your camp?**  
**Answer:** The Lord thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:14

**Q2. Why does the Lord walk in the midst of your camp?**  
**Answer:** To deliver thee, and to give up thine enemies before thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:14

**Q3. What kind of camp must your camp therefore be?**  
**Answer:** Holy.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:14

---

### Deuteronomy 23:15

**Q1. What type of servant is not to be delivered unto his master?**  
**Answer:** The servant which is escaped from his master unto thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:15

**Q2. From whom has the servant escaped?**  
**Answer:** His master.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:15

**Q3. To whom has the servant escaped?**  
**Answer:** Unto thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:15

---

### Deuteronomy 23:16

**Q1. Where shall the escaped servant dwell with you?**  
**Answer:** Even among you.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:16

**Q2. In which place shall he dwell?**  
**Answer:** In that place which he shall choose in one of thy gates.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:16

**Q3. What must you not do to him?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not oppress him.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:16

---

### Deuteronomy 23:17

**Q1. Who shall not be a whore?**  
**Answer:** There shall be no whore of the daughters of Israel.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:17

**Q2. Who else shall not be a sodomite?**  
**Answer:** Nor a sodomite of the sons of Israel.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:17

**Q3. Whose daughters and sons are mentioned in this prohibition?**  
**Answer:** The daughters of Israel and sons of Israel.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:17

---

### Deuteronomy 23:18

**Q1. What kind of money shall not be brought into the house of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** The hire of a whore, or the price of a dog.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:18

**Q2. For the vow of whom are these things an abomination unto the Lord?**  
**Answer:** For any vow.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:18

**Q3. Who are both of these things to the Lord thy God?**  
**Answer:** An abomination.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:18

---

### Deuteronomy 23:19

**Q1. What kind of usury shall you not lend to your brother?**  
**Answer:** Usury of money, usury of victuals, usury of any thing that is lent upon usury.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:19

**Q2. To whom shall you not lend upon usury?**  
**Answer:** Thy brother.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:19

**Q3. What specific items are listed regarding usury?**  
**Answer:** Money, victuals, or any thing that is lent upon usury.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:19

---

### Deuteronomy 23:20

**Q1. Unto whom may you lend upon usury?**  
**Answer:** Unto a stranger.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:20

**Q2. Unto whom shall you not lend upon usury?**  
**Answer:** Unto thy brother.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:20

**Q3. What will the Lord thy God do in all that you set your hand to in the land?**  
**Answer:** Bless thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:20

### Deuteronomy 23:21

**Q1. What must you not slack to pay when you vow a vow?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not slack to pay it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:21

**Q2. Whose vow must you not slack to pay?**  
**Answer:** Unto the Lord thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:21

**Q3. What will the Lord thy God surely require of you?**  
**Answer:** It.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:21

---

### Deuteronomy 23:22

**Q1. What is the consequence if you shall forbear to vow?**  
**Answer:** It shall be no sin in thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:22

**Q2. Is it a sin if you do not make a vow at all?**  
**Answer:** No ("it shall be no sin in thee").  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:22

**Q3. What is not a sin regarding vows?**  
**Answer:** To forbear to vow.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:22

---

### Deuteronomy 23:23

**Q1. What must you keep and perform that has gone out of your lips?**  
**Answer:** That which is gone out of thy lips.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:23

**Q2. What kind of offering must you keep and perform?**  
**Answer:** A freewill offering.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:23

**Q3. How must you perform the freewill offering?**  
**Answer:** According as thou hast vowed unto the Lord thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:23

---

### Deuteronomy 23:24

**Q1. Into whose vineyard may you come?**  
**Answer:** Into thy neighbour's vineyard.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:24

**Q2. What may you eat at your own pleasure?**  
**Answer:** Grapes thy fill.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:24

**Q3. Where are you not permitted to put any grapes?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not put any in thy vessel.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:24

---

### Deuteronomy 23:25

**Q1. Into whose standing corn may you come?**  
**Answer:** Into the standing corn of thy neighbour.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:25

**Q2. What may you pluck with your hands?**  
**Answer:** The ears.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:25

**Q3. What must you not move against your neighbour's standing corn?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not move a sickle.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 23:25
