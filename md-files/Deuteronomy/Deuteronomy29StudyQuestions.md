# Deuteronomy 29 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 29:1

**Q1. What are the words of the covenant which the Lord commanded Moses to make called?**  
**Answer:** The words of the covenant, which the Lord commanded Moses to make with the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:1

**Q2. Where did the Lord command Moses to make this covenant besides the covenant he made with them in Horeb?**  
**Answer:** In the land of Moab.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:1

**Q3. With whom did the Lord command Moses to make this covenant in the land of Moab?**  
**Answer:** With the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:1

---

### Deuteronomy 29:2

**Q1. Whom did Moses call unto all Israel and say unto them?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:2

**Q2. What had all Israel seen that the Lord did before their eyes in the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** All that the Lord did before their eyes in the land of Egypt unto Pharaoh, and unto all his servants, and unto all his land.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:2

**Q3. Unto whom did the Lord do these things in the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Unto Pharaoh, and unto all his servants, and unto all his land.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:2

---

### Deuteronomy 29:3

**Q1. What had the eyes of the people seen?**  
**Answer:** The great temptations, the signs, and those great miracles.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:3

**Q2. Who had seen the great temptations, signs, and miracles?**  
**Answer:** Your eyes.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:3

**Q3. What kind of temptations and miracles were they?**  
**Answer:** Great temptations... and those great miracles.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:3

---

### Deuteronomy 29:4

**Q1. What did the Lord not give unto the people until that day?**  
**Answer:** An heart to perceive, and eyes to see, and ears to hear.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:4

**Q2. Who is mentioned as not having given an heart to perceive unto that day?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:4

**Q3. Until when did the Lord not give them eyes to see and ears to hear?**  
**Answer:** Unto this day.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:4

---

### Deuteronomy 29:5

**Q1. How many years did Moses lead the people through the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** Forty years.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:5

**Q2. What happened to the clothes upon the people during the forty years?**  
**Answer:** Your clothes are not waxen old upon you.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:5

**Q3. What happened to the shoe upon the foot of the people?**  
**Answer:** Thy shoe is not waxen old upon thy foot.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:5

---

### Deuteronomy 29:6

**Q1. What did the people not eat?**  
**Answer:** Bread.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:6

**Q2. What did the people not drink?**  
**Answer:** Wine or strong drink.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:6

**Q3. Why did they not eat bread and drink wine or strong drink?**  
**Answer:** That ye might know that I am the Lord your God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:6

---

### Deuteronomy 29:7

**Q1. Who came out against the people unto this place?**  
**Answer:** Sihon the king of Heshbon, and Og the king of Bashan.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:7

**Q2. What did Sihon and Og do when they came out against the people?**  
**Answer:** Came out unto battle.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:7

**Q3. What did the people do to Sihon and Og?**  
**Answer:** We smote them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:7

---

### Deuteronomy 29:8

**Q1. Whose land did the people take?**  
**Answer:** Their land.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:8

**Q2. For whom was the land given for an inheritance?**  
**Answer:** For the Reubenites, and for the Gadites, and for the half tribe of Manasseh.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:8

**Q3. To whom did they give the land for an inheritance?**  
**Answer:** Unto the Reubenites, and to the Gadites, and to the half tribe of Manasseh.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:8

---

### Deuteronomy 29:9

**Q1. What are the people instructed to keep?**  
**Answer:** The words of this covenant.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:9

**Q2. What are the people instructed to do with the words of this covenant?**  
**Answer:** Keep therefore the words of this covenant, and do them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:9

**Q3. Why should they keep and do the words of this covenant?**  
**Answer:** That ye may prosper in all that ye do.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:9

---

### Deuteronomy 29:10

**Q1. Who stands this day before the Lord your God?**  
**Answer:** Ye stand this day all of you before the Lord your God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:10

**Q2. Who specifically is listed as standing before the Lord besides the captains of your tribes and your elders?**  
**Answer:** Your captains of your tribes, your elders, and your officers, with all the men of Israel.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:10

**Q3. Who else is mentioned as standing before the Lord alongside the men of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Your little ones, your wives, and thy stranger that is in thy camp.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:10

---

### Deuteronomy 29:11

**Q1. Who is included from the camp along with the hewers of wood?**  
**Answer:** Thy stranger that is in thy camp, from the hewer of thy wood unto the drawer of thy water.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:11

**Q2. From whom to whom is included in passing into the covenant?**  
**Answer:** From the hewer of thy wood unto the drawer of thy water.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:11

**Q3. What are they entering into with the Lord your God?**  
**Answer:** Into his covenant, and into his oath.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:11

---

### Deuteronomy 29:12

**Q1. Into what is the person passing in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Into covenant of the Lord thy God, and into his oath.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:12

**Q2. With whom does the Lord thy God make this covenant and oath?**  
**Answer:** With thee this day.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:12

**Q3. Whose action is it to make this covenant and oath with thee this day?**  
**Answer:** The Lord thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:12

---

### Deuteronomy 29:13

**Q1. To what end would the Lord establish the people today for a people unto himself?**  
**Answer:** That he may establish thee to day for a people unto himself.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:13

**Q2. Who may be a God unto the people, as he hath said unto them?**  
**Answer:** That he may be unto thee a God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:13

**Q3. Unto whom did the Lord swear to the fathers?**  
**Answer:** Unto thy fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:13

**Answer:** Wood and stone, silver and gold.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:17

**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:17

### Deuteronomy 29:18

**Q1. Who must not be among the people, man or woman, family or tribe?**  
**Answer:** Lest there should be among you man, or woman, or family, or tribe, whose heart turneth away this day from the Lord our God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:18

**Q2. Whose heart might turn away this day from the Lord our God to go and serve certain gods?**  
**Answer:** The gods of these nations.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:18

**Q3. What kind of root is described as bearing gall and wormwood?**  
**Answer:** A root that beareth gall and wormwood.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:18

### Deuteronomy 29:19

**Q1. What might a person hear when he hears the words of this curse?**  
**Answer:** The words of this curse.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:19

**Q2. What does such a person bless himself in?**  
**Answer:** In his heart.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:19

**Q3. What does the person say in his heart regarding peace?**  
**Answer:** I shall have peace, though I walk in the imagination of mine heart, to add drunkenness to thirst.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:19

### Deuteronomy 29:20

**Q1. What will the Lord not do for such a man?**  
**Answer:** The Lord will not spare him.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:20

**Q2. What will smoke against that man?**  
**Answer:** The anger of the Lord and his jealousy.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:20

**Answer:** All the curses that are written in this book.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:20

**Q1. Out of what tribes of Israel will the Lord separate that man?**  
**Answer:** Out of all the tribes of Israel.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:21

**Q2. For what purpose will the Lord separate that man?**  
**Answer:** Unto evil.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:21

**Answer:** According to all the curses of the covenant that are written in this book of the law.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:21

**Answer:** The generation to come of your children that shall rise up after you.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:22

**Q2. From where shall the stranger come that shall say these things?**  
**Answer:** From a far land.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:22

**Q3. What shall the stranger and the generation to come see?**  
**Answer:** The plagues of that land, and the sicknesses which the Lord hath laid upon it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:22

### Deuteronomy 29:23

**Q1. What is the whole land described as being burned with?**  
**Answer:** Brimstone, and salt, and burning.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:23

**Q2. Is the whole land sown?**  
**Answer:** It is not sown, nor beareth, nor any grass groweth thereon.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:23

**Q3. Like what cities is the overthrow described?**  
**Answer:** Like the overthrow of Sodom, and Gomorrah, Admah, and Zeboim, which the Lord overthrew in his anger, and in his wrath.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 29:23
