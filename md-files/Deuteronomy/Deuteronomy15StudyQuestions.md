# Deuteronomy 15 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 15:1

**Q1. At the end of every seven years, what are you to make?**  
**Answer:** A release.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:1

**Q2. Who is this release to be made concerning?**  
**Answer:** Every creditor that lendeth ought unto his neighbour.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:1

**Q3. What shall the creditor not do to his neighbour, or to his brother?**  
**Answer:** Exact it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:1

---

### Deuteronomy 15:2

**Q1. What is the manner of the release?**  
**Answer:** Every creditor that lendeth ought unto his neighbour shall release it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:2

**Q2. When shall the creditor not exact it from his neighbour, or from his brother?**  
**Answer:** Because it is called the LORD'S release.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:2

**Q3. Who shall the creditor not exact it of?**  
**Answer:** His neighbour, or his brother.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:2

---

### Deuteronomy 15:3

**Q1. Of whom mayest thou exact a release again?**  
**Answer:** Of a foreigner.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:3

**Q2. What is that which is thine with thy brother?**  
**Answer:** That which is thine with thy brother thine hand shall release.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:3

**Q3. Whose hand shall release what is thine with thy brother?**  
**Answer:** Thine hand.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:3

---

### Deuteronomy 15:4

**Q1. Save when there shall be what among you?**  
**Answer:** No poor among you.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:4

**Q2. Who will greatly bless thee in the land which the LORD thy God giveth thee for an inheritance to possess it?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:4

**Q3. What is the land given to thee for?**  
**Answer:** An inheritance to possess it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:4

---

### Deuteronomy 15:5

**Q1. If thou carefully hearken unto what voice?**  
**Answer:** The voice of the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:5

**Q2. What must thou observe to do?**  
**Answer:** All these commandments which I command thee this day.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:5

**Q3. When must thou observe to do all these commandments?**  
**Answer:** This day.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:5

---

### Deuteronomy 15:6

**Q1. Who will bless thee, as he promised thee?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:6

**Q2. What shall thou lend unto many nations?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt lend unto many nations.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:6

**Q3. Over how many nations shalt thou not reign?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not borrow.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:6

---

### Deuteronomy 15:7

**Q1. If there be among you what?**  
**Answer:** A poor man of one of thy brethren within any of thy gates in thy land which the LORD thy God giveth thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:7

**Q2. What shall thou not harden, nor shut from thy poor brother?**  
**Answer:** Thine heart, nor thine hand.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:7

**Q3. What shall thou not do concerning thy poor brother?**  
**Answer:** Harden thine heart, nor shut thine hand from thy poor brother.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:7

---

### Deuteronomy 15:8

**Q1. What shall thou open unto him?**  
**Answer:** Thine hand wide.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:8

**Q2. What shall thou surely lend him sufficient for?**  
**Answer:** His need, in that which he wanteth.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:8

**Q3. What shall thou surely lend him?**  
**Answer:** Sufficient for his need.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:8

---

### Deuteronomy 15:9

**Q1. Beware that there be not what in thy wicked heart?**  
**Answer:** A thought.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:9

**Q2. What year is at hand?**  
**Answer:** The seventh year, the year of release.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:9

**Q3. Against whom is thine eye evil, and what dost thou not give him?**  
**Answer:** Against thy poor brother, and thou givest him nought.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:9

---

### Deuteronomy 15:10

**Q1. What shall thou surely give him?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt surely give him.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:10

**Q2. What shall not be when thou givest unto him?**  
**Answer:** Thine heart shall not be grieved.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:10

**Q3. For what thing will the LORD thy God bless thee?**  
**Answer:** For this thing the LORD thy God shall bless thee in all thy works, and in all that thou puttest thine hand unto.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:10

---

### Deuteronomy 15:11

**Q1. Who shall never cease out of the land?**  
**Answer:** The poor.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:11

**Q2. Therefore, what do I command thee?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt open thine hand wide unto thy brother, to thy poor, and to thy needy, in thy land.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:11

**Q3. To whom shall you open your hand wide?**  
**Answer:** Unto thy brother, to thy poor, and to thy needy, in thy land.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:11

---

### Deuteronomy 15:12

**Q1. If thy brother, an Hebrew man, or an Hebrew woman, be sold unto thee, how many years shall he serve thee?**  
**Answer:** Six years.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:12

**Q2. In what year shall thou let him go free from thee?**  
**Answer:** In the seventh year.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:12

**Q3. What shall you do unto him when you let him go free?**  
**Answer:** Let him go free from thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:12

---

### Deuteronomy 15:13

**Q1. When thou sendest him out free from thee, what shall thou not do?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not let him go away empty.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:13

**Q2. What shall thou not let him go away?**  
**Answer:** Empty.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:13

**Q3. Who is being sent out free?**  
**Answer:** Thy servant (or thy brother, an Hebrew man/woman).  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:13

---

### Deuteronomy 15:14

**Q1. What shall thou furnish him liberally out of?**  
**Answer:** Thy flock, and out of thy floor, and out of thy winepress.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:14

**Q2. Wherewith shall thou bless him?**  
**Answer:** Wherewith the LORD thy God hath blessed thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:14

**Q3. What must thou remember that thou wast?**  
**Answer:** A bondman in the land of Egypt, and the LORD thy God redeemed thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:14

---

### Deuteronomy 15:15

**Q1. What must thou remember that thou wast in the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** A bondman in the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:15

**Q2. Who redeemed thee from Egypt?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:15

**Q3. Therefore, what do I command thee this thing today?**  
**Answer:** Therefore I command thee this thing to day.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:15

---

### Deuteronomy 15:16

**Q1. If the servant says unto thee, I will not go away from thee, what is the reason?**  
**Answer:** Because he loveth thee and thine house, because he is well with thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:16

**Q2. Whom does the servant love?**  
**Answer:** Thee and thine house.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:16

**Q3. Why is it well with him?**  
**Answer:** Because he is well with thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:16

---

### Deuteronomy 15:17

**Q1. What shall thou take and thrust through his ear unto the door?**  
**Answer:** An awl.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:17

**Q2. Who shall be thy servant for ever?**  
**Answer:** He (the servant).  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:17

**Q3. Unto whom also shalt thou do likewise?**  
**Answer:** Unto thy maidservant.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:17

---

### Deuteronomy 15:18

**Q1. When thou sendest him away free from thee, let it not seem what unto thee?**  
**Answer:** Hard.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:18

**Q2. For how much has he been worth to thee in serving thee six years?**  
**Answer:** Worth a double hired servant to thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:18

**Q3. Who shall bless thee in all that thou doest?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:18

---

### Deuteronomy 15:19

**Q1. What firstling males that come of thy herd and of thy flock shall thou sanctify?**  
**Answer:** All the firstling males.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:19

**Q2. Unto whom shalt thou sanctify all the firstling males?**  
**Answer:** Unto the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:19

**Q3. What must you not do with the firstling of your bullock, nor shear what?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt do no work with the firstling of thy bullock, nor shear the firstling of thy sheep.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:19

---

### Deuteronomy 15:20

**Q1. Where shall thou eat it before the LORD thy God year by year?**  
**Answer:** In the place which the LORD shall choose.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:20

**Q2. Who shall eat it with thee?**  
**Answer:** Thou and thy household.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:20

**Q3. How often shall thou eat it before the LORD thy God?**  
**Answer:** Year by year.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:20

---

### Deuteronomy 15:21

**Q1. If there be any blemish in the firstling, such as what?**  
**Answer:** As if it be lame, or blind, or have any ill blemish.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:21

**Q2. Unto whom shall thou not sacrifice it?**  
**Answer:** Unto the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:21

**Q3. What kind of blemish must it not have?**  
**Answer:** Any ill blemish.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:21

---

### Deuteronomy 15:22

**Q1. Where shall thou eat the blemished firstling?**  
**Answer:** Within thy gates.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:22

**Q2. Who shall eat it together?**  
**Answer:** The unclean and the clean person.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:22

**Q3. What animals are likened to the clean and unclean persons regarding eating it?**  
**Answer:** As the roebuck, and as the hart.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:22

---

### Deuteronomy 15:23

**Q1. What must thou not do with the blood thereof?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not eat the blood thereof.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:23

**Q2. What must thou do with the blood?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt pour it upon the ground as water.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:23

**Q3. How shall you pour the blood upon the ground?**  
**Answer:** As water.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 15:23
