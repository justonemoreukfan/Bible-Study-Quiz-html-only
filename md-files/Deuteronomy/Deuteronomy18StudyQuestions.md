# Deuteronomy 18 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 18:1

**Q1. Who shall have no part nor inheritance with Israel?**  
**Answer:** The priests the Levites, and all the tribe of Levi.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:1

**Q2. What shall the priests the Levites, and all the tribe of Levi, not have with Israel?**  
**Answer:** No part nor inheritance.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:1

**Q3. Whose offerings made by fire, and their inheritance, shall they eat?**  
**Answer:** The offerings of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:1

---

### Deuteronomy 18:2

**Q4. What shall they have among their brethren?**  
**Answer:** No inheritance.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:2

**Q5. Who is their inheritance?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:2

**Q6. As what has the LORD said unto them?**  
**Answer:** As he hath said unto them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:2

---

### Deuteronomy 18:3

**Q7. What shall be the priest's due from the people?**  
**Answer:** From them that offer a sacrifice, whether it be ox or sheep.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:3

**Q8. What parts of the animal shall they give unto the priest?**  
**Answer:** The shoulder, and the two cheeks, and the maw.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:3

**Q9. From whom shall the priest receive this due?**  
**Answer:** From them that offer a sacrifice.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:3

---

### Deuteronomy 18:4

**Q10. What firstfruit shall you give him of your corn, your wine, and your oil?**  
**Answer:** The firstfruit.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:4

**Q11. What else shall you give him of the first of the fleece of your sheep?**  
**Answer:** The first of the fleece.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:4

**Q12. Out of what shall you give him these things?**  
**Answer:** Of your flock.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:4

---

### Deuteronomy 18:5

**Q13. Whom has the LORD thy God chosen out of all thy tribes?**  
**Answer:** Him.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:5

**Q14. To stand to minister in whose name shall he and his sons be chosen?**  
**Answer:** In the name of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:5

**Q15. How long shall he and his sons stand to minister in the name of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** For ever.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:5

---

### Deuteronomy 18:6

**Q16. If a Levite come from any of thy gates out of all Israel where he sojourned, how shall he come?**  
**Answer:** With all the desire of his mind.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:6

**Q17. To what place shall the Levite come?**  
**Answer:** Unto the place which the LORD shall choose.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:6

**Q18. When may the Levite come unto the place which the LORD shall choose?**  
**Answer:** Whensoever he cometh.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:6

---

### Deuteronomy 18:7

**Q19. In whose name shall he minister?**  
**Answer:** In the name of the LORD his God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:7

**Q20. Like whom shall he minister?**  
**Answer:** As all his brethren the Levites do, which stand there before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:7

**Q21. Where do his brethren the Levites stand?**  
**Answer:** Before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:7

---

### Deuteronomy 18:8

**Q22. What portion shall they have to eat?**  
**Answer:** Like portions.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:8

**Q23. Besides what shall they have like portions?**  
**Answer:** Beside that which cometh of the sale of his patrimony.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:8

**Q24. What are those that come of the sale of his patrimony called?**  
**Answer:** His patrimony.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:8

---

### Deuteronomy 18:9

**Q25. Into what land art thou come?**  
**Answer:** The land which the LORD thy God giveth thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:9

**Q26. What must thou not learn to do after?**  
**Answer:** After the abominations of those nations.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:9

**Q27. Whose abominations must thou not learn to do after?**  
**Answer:** Those nations.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:9

---

### Deuteronomy 18:10

**Q28. Who shall not be found among you that makes his son or his daughter to pass through the fire?**  
**Answer:** Anyone that maketh his son or his daughter to pass through the fire.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:10

**Q29. Who shall not be found among you that uses divination?**  
**Answer:** Anyone that useth divination.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:10

**Q30. Who else shall not be found among you regarding times and enchantments?**  
**Answer:** An observer of times, or an enchanter, or a witch.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:10

---

### Deuteronomy 18:11

**Q31. Who shall not be found among you that charms?**  
**Answer:** A charmer.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:11

**Q32. Who shall not be found among you that consults familiar spirits?**  
**Answer:** A consulter with familiar spirits.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:11

**Q33. Who shall not be found among you that is a wizard or seeks the dead?**  
**Answer:** A wizard, or a necromancer.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:11

---

### Deuteronomy 18:12

**Q34. What are all that do these things unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** An abomination unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:12

**Q35. Because of what abominations does the LORD thy God drive them out from before thee?**  
**Answer:** These abominations.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:12

**Q36. From before whom does the LORD thy God drive them out?**  
**Answer:** Thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:12

---

### Deuteronomy 18:13

**Q37. How shall thou be with the LORD thy God?**  
**Answer:** Perfect.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:13

**Q38. With whom shall thou be perfect?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:13

**Q39. Whose God is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:13

---

### Deuteronomy 18:14

**Q40. To whom shall these nations hearken unto?**  
**Answer:** Observers of times, and unto diviners.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:14

**Q41. Whose nations are these that thou shalt possess?**  
**Answer:** Nations which thou shalt possess.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:14

**Q42. Whom has the LORD thy God not suffered thee to do like unto?**  
**Answer:** As for thee, the LORD thy God hath not suffered thee so to do.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:14

---

### Deuteronomy 18:15

**Q43. Whom will the LORD thy God raise up unto thee?**  
**Answer:** A Prophet.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:15

**Q44. From where shall this Prophet be raised up?**  
**Answer:** From the midst of thee, of thy brethren.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:15

**Q45. Like unto whom shall this Prophet be?**  
**Answer:** Unto me.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:15

---

### Deuteronomy 18:16

**Q46. According to all that thou desiredst of the LORD thy God in Horeb in what day?**  
**Answer:** In the day of the assembly.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:16

**Q47. What did the people say they would not hear again?**  
**Answer:** The voice of the LORD my God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:16

**Q48. What did they say they would not see anymore so that they die not?**  
**Answer:** This great fire any more.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:16

---

### Deuteronomy 18:17

**Q49. What did the LORD say unto me regarding what they have spoken?**  
**Answer:** They have well spoken that which they have spoken.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:17

**Q50. Who said "They have well spoken that which they have spoken"?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:17

**Q51. What did the people do well in according to the LORD?**  
**Answer:** That which they have spoken.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:17

---

### Deuteronomy 18:18

**Q52. Whom will the LORD raise up from among their brethren?**  
**Answer:** A Prophet.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:18

**Q53. Like unto whom shall this Prophet be?**  
**Answer:** Unto thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:18

**Q54. Whose words will the LORD put in his mouth?**  
**Answer:** My words.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:18

---

### Deuteronomy 18:19

**Q55. Whose words shall come to pass if a man will not hearken unto them?**  
**Answer:** My words which he shall speak in my name.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:19

**Q56. Who will require it of that man?**  
**Answer:** I will require it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:19

**Q57. In whose name shall the prophet speak the words?**  
**Answer:** In my name.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:19

---

### Deuteronomy 18:20

**Q58. What shall happen to the prophet which shall presume to speak a word in my name which I have not commanded him to speak?**  
**Answer:** Even that prophet shall die.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:20

**Q59. What else shall happen to the prophet that speaks in the name of other gods?**  
**Answer:** Even that prophet shall die.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:20

**Q60. Who has not commanded the prophet to speak that word?**  
**Answer:** I.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:20

---

### Deuteronomy 18:21

**Q61. What question might thou say in thine heart?**  
**Answer:** How shall we know the word which the LORD hath not spoken?  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:21

**Q62. What does thou ask about the word in thine heart?**  
**Answer:** How shall we know the word which the LORD hath not spoken?  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:21

**Q63. Who is the word spoken by in this question?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:21

---

### Deuteronomy 18:22

**Q64. When a prophet speaks in the name of the LORD, if the thing follow not nor come to pass, what is that?**  
**Answer:** That is the thing which the LORD hath not spoken.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:22

**Q65. How has the prophet spoken that thing?**  
**Answer:** Presumptuously.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:22

**Q66. Whom shall thou not be afraid of regarding that prophet?**  
**Answer:** Him.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:22
