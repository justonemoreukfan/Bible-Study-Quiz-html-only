# Deuteronomy 11 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 11:1

**Q1. Therefore what should thou love?**  
**Answer:** The Lord thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:1

**Q2. What shouldst thou keep?**  
**Answer:** His charge, and his statutes, and his judgments, and his commandments.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:1

**Q3. How long should one keep these things?**  
**Answer:** Alway.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:1

---

### Deuteronomy 11:2

**Q1. And know ye this day with whom must ye not speak?**  
**Answer:** With your children which have not known, and which have not seen.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:2

**Q2. Whose chastisement have the children not known nor seen?**  
**Answer:** The chastisement of the Lord your God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:2

**Q3. What else have they not known nor seen besides his chastisement?**  
**Answer:** His greatness, his mighty hand, and his stretched out arm.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:2

---

### Deuteronomy 11:3

**Q1. What did his miracles and acts do in the midst of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Did he do.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:3

**Q2. Against whom were his miracles and acts done in Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh the king of Egypt, and unto all his land.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:3

**Q3. Who was Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** The king of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:3

---

### Deuteronomy 11:4

**Q1. What did he do unto the army of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Unto their horses and unto their chariots.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:4

**Q2. How did he make the water of the Red sea overflow them?**  
**Answer:** When they pursued after you.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:4

**Q3. What did the Lord destroy unto this day concerning the Egyptian army?**  
**Answer:** He destroyed them unto this day.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:4

---

### Deuteronomy 11:5

**Q1. What did he do unto you in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** What he did unto you in the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:5

**Q2. Until what point did he do this unto you in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** Until ye came into this place.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:5

**Q3. Who did these things unto you in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** The Lord your God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:5

---

### Deuteronomy 11:6

**Q1. What did he do unto Dathan and Abiram?**  
**Answer:** The sons of Eliab, the son of Reuben.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:6

**Q2. How did the earth open its mouth against Dathan and Abiram?**  
**Answer:** And swallowed them up.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:6

**Q3. Who else was swallowed up along with their households and tents?**  
**Answer:** And their households, and their tents, and all the substance that was in their possession.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:6

---

### Deuteronomy 11:7

**Q1. Whose eyes have seen all the great acts of the Lord?**

**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:7

**Q2. What acts of the Lord have your eyes seen?**  
**Answer:** All the great acts of the Lord which he did.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:7

**Q3. When did the Lord do these great acts?**  
**Answer:** Which he did.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:7

---

### Deuteronomy 11:8

**Q1. Therefore what commandments shall ye keep?**  
**Answer:** All the commandments which I command you this day.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:8

**Q2. For what purpose should ye keep these commandments?**  
**Answer:** That ye may be strong.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:8

**Q3. What else should happen when ye go in and possess the land?**  
**Answer:** And go in and possess the land, whither ye go to possess it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:8

### Deuteronomy 11:9

**Q1. And that ye may prolong what?**  
**Answer:** Your days.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:9

**Q2. In what land shall ye prolong your days?**  
**Answer:** In the land which the Lord sware unto your fathers to give unto them and to their seed.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:9

**Q3. What kind of land is it that flows with milk and honey?**  
**Answer:** A land that floweth with milk and honey.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:9

---

### Deuteronomy 11:10

**Q1. For what kind of land is not the land whither thou goest?**  
**Answer:** The land of Egypt, from whence ye came out.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:10

**Q2. How didst thou sow thy seed in the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** And wateredst it with thy foot.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:10

**Q3. What kind of garden was the land of Egypt compared to?**  
**Answer:** As a garden of herbs.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:10

---

### Deuteronomy 11:11

**Q1. But the land, whither ye go to possess it, what kind of land is it?**

**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:11

**Q2. By what does this land drink water?**  
**Answer:** Drinketh water of the rain of heaven.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:11

**Q3. Where does the water of heaven come from for this land?**

**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:11

### Deuteronomy 11:12

**Answer:** A land which the Lord thy God careth for.

**Answer:** The eyes of the Lord thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:12

**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:12

### Deuteronomy 11:14

**Q1. What will I give you in his season?**

**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:14

**Verse:** Deuteronomy 11:14
