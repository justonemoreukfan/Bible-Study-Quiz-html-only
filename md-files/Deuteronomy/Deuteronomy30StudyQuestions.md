# Deuteronomy 30 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 30:1

**Q1. What things will come upon the children of Israel, the blessing and the curse?**  
**Answer:** All these things.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:1

**Q2. Where shall the children of Israel call these things to mind?**  
**Answer:** Among all the nations, whither the LORD thy God hath driven them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:1

**Q3. When are these things to be called to mind?**  
**Answer:** When all these things are come upon thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:1

---

### Deuteronomy 30:2

**Q4. To whom shall the children of Israel return?**  
**Answer:** Unto the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:2

**Q5. Whose voice shall they obey according to all that is commanded this day?**  
**Answer:** His voice.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:2

**Q6. With what should they return to the LORD?**  
**Answer:** With all thine heart, and with all thy soul.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:2

---

### Deuteronomy 30:3

**Q7. What will the LORD thy God turn?**  
**Answer:** Thy captivity.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:3

**Q8. What will the LORD have upon the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Compassion.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:3

**Q9. Where will the LORD gather them from?**  
**Answer:** All the nations, whither the LORD thy God hath scattered thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:3

---

### Deuteronomy 30:4

**Q10. If any of thine be driven out, where will they be?**  
**Answer:** In the utmost parts of heaven.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:4

**Q11. From where will the LORD thy God gather thee?**  
**Answer:** From thence.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:4

**Q12. From where will the LORD fetch thee?**  
**Answer:** From thence.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:4

---

### Deuteronomy 30:5

**Q13. Into what land will the LORD thy God bring thee?**  
**Answer:** The land which thy fathers possessed.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:5

**Q14. Who shall possess the land?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt possess it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:5

**Q15. What will the LORD do unto thee regarding thy fathers?**  
**Answer:** Do thee good, and multiply thee above thy fathers.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:5

---

### Deuteronomy 30:6

**Q16. What will the LORD thy God circumcise?**  
**Answer:** Thine heart, and the heart of thy seed.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:6

---

### Deuteronomy 30:6 (continued)

**Q17. For what purpose will He circumcise thine heart and the heart of thy seed?**  
**Answer:** To love the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:6

**Q18. With what part of thee shall thou love the LORD thy God?**  
**Answer:** With all thine heart, and with all thy soul.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:6

**Answer:** The voice of the LORD.

**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:8

### Deuteronomy 30:9

**Q25. In what will the LORD thy God make thee plenteous?**  
**Answer:** In every work of thine hand.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:9

**Q26. What parts of thy body will the LORD make plenteous?**  
**Answer:** In the fruit of thy body, and in the fruit of thy cattle, and in the fruit of thy land.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:9

**Q27. How will the LORD rejoice over thee?**  
**Answer:** For good, as he rejoiced over thy fathers.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:9

**Answer:** To hearken unto the voice of the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 30:10

**Q29. What must be kept written in this book of the law?**  
**Answer:** His commandments and his statutes.
