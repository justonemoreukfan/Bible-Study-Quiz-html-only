# Deuteronomy 31 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 31:1

**Q1. What did Moses do after speaking these words unto all Israel?**  
**Answer:** He went and spake these words unto all Israel.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 31:1

**Q2. To whom did Moses speak these words?**  
**Answer:** Unto all Israel.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 31:1

**Q3. What did Moses go and do?**  
**Answer:** He went and spake these words.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 31:1

---

### Deuteronomy 31:2

**Q1. What age did Moses state he was?**  
**Answer:** An hundred and twenty years old this day.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 31:2

**Q2. What could Moses no more go out and come in by?**  
**Answer:** I can no more go out and come in.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 31:2

**Q3. What did the LORD say unto Moses concerning crossing jordan?**  
**Answer:** The LORD hath said unto me, Thou shalt not go over this Jordan.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 31:2

### Deuteronomy 31:3
