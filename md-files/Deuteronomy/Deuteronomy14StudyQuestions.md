# Deuteronomy 14 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 14:1

**Q1. Whose children are the people addressed as in Deuteronomy 14:1?**  
**Answer:** The children of the LORD your God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:1

**Q2. What are the people commanded not to do to themselves for the dead?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall not cut yourselves.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:1

**Q3. What else are the people commanded not to make between their eyes for the dead?**  
**Answer:** Nor make any baldness between your eyes.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:1

---

### Deuteronomy 14:2

**Q4. What kind of people are the children of Israel unto the LORD their God?**  
**Answer:** An holy people unto the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:2

**Q5. Whom has the LORD chosen Israel to be above all the nations that are upon the earth?**  
**Answer:** A peculiar people unto himself.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:2

**Q6. Where are the nations located that Israel was chosen above?**  
**Answer:** Upon the earth.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:2

---

### Deuteronomy 14:3

**Q7. What general instruction is given in Deuteronomy 14:3?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not eat any abominable thing.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:3

---

### Deuteronomy 14:4

**Q8. Which specific animals are listed that the people may eat?**  
**Answer:** The ox, the sheep, and the goat.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:4

---

### Deuteronomy 14:5

**Q9. Which wild animal with horns is listed first among those that may be eaten?**  
**Answer:** The hart.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:5

**Q10. What kind of roe is listed as permissible to eat?**  
**Answer:** The roebuck.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:5

**Q11. What other animals are included in the list of allowable food in Deuteronomy 14:5?**  
**Answer:** The fallow deer, and the wild goat, and the pygarg, and the wild ox, and the chamois.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:5

---

### Deuteronomy 14:6

**Q12. What two physical characteristics must every beast have to be eaten?**  
**Answer:** Parteth the hoof, and cleaveth the cleft into two claws, and cheweth the cud among the beasts.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:6

**Q13. Among what group of animals must these eating criteria be found?**  
**Answer:** Among the beasts.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:6

**Q14. What may be done with beasts that meet these criteria?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall eat.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:6

---

### Deuteronomy 14:7

**Q15. Which animals that chew the cud or divide the cloven hoof may ye not eat?**  
**Answer:** The camel, and the hare, and the conen.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:7

**Q16. What do these specific animals (camel, hare, coney) chew, according to verse 7?**  
**Answer:** They chew the cud.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:7

**Q17. What characteristic do these animals (camel, hare, coney) lack regarding their hooves?**  
**Answer:** They divide not the hoof.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:7

---

### Deuteronomy 14:8

**Q18. What is said about the swine because it divideth the hoof yet cheweth not the cud?**  
**Answer:** It is unclean unto you.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:8

**Q19. What part of the swine's body is divided?**  
**Answer:** The hoof.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:8

**Q20. What instruction is given regarding the flesh of these unclean beasts?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall not eat of their flesh, nor touch their dead carcass.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:8

---

### Deuteronomy 14:9

**Q21. What can be eaten out of all that are in the waters?**  
**Answer:** All that have fins and scales shall ye eat.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:9

**Q22. Where must these creatures with fins and scales be located?**  
**Answer:** In the waters, in the seas, and in the rivers.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:9

**Q23. What specific body parts must water creatures have in order to be eaten?**  
**Answer:** Fins and scales.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:9

---

### Deuteronomy 14:10

**Q24. What must not be eaten that are in the seas and in the rivers?**  
**Answer:** All that have not fins and scales.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:10

**Q25. How are creatures without fins and scales classified in verse 10?**  
**Answer:** Unclean unto you.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:10

**Q26. Where are the creatures without fins and scales located?**  
**Answer:** In the seas, and in the rivers.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:10

---

### Deuteronomy 14:11

**Q27. What general category of creatures is addressed in Deuteronomy 14:11?**  
**Answer:** Of all clean birds ye shall eat.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:11

**Q28. What status must the birds have to be eaten?**  
**Answer:** Clean.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:11

**Q29. What action is permitted regarding these clean birds?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall eat.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:11

---

### Deuteronomy 14:12

**Q30. Which specific birds are listed as things ye shall not eat?**  
**Answer:** The eagle, and the ossifrage, and the ospray.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:12

---

### Deuteronomy 14:13

**Q31. Which specific bird of prey is listed first in Deuteronomy 14:13?**  
**Answer:** The glede.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:13

**Q32. What other birds follow the glede in the list of unclean birds?**  
**Answer:** The kite, and the vulture after his kind.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:13

---

### Deuteronomy 14:14

**Q33. What kind of raven is listed among the unclean birds?**  
**Answer:** Every raven after his kind.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:14

---

### Deuteronomy 14:15

**Q34. Which birds are listed together in Deuteronomy 14:15?**  
**Answer:** The owl, and the night hawk, and the cuckow.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:15

**Q35. What kind of hawk is mentioned in this verse along with its kind?**  
**Answer:** The hawk after his kind.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:15

**Answer:** The little owl, and the great owl.

**Q37. What other bird is listed alongside the owls in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The swan.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:16

**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:17

### Deuteronomy 14:18

**Answer:** The stork, and the heron after her kind.

**Answer:** The glead, and the bat.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:18

**Q41. How are every creeping thing that flyeth classified in Deuteronomy 14:19?**  
**Answer:** They are unclean unto you.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:19

**Q42. What action must be taken regarding every creeping thing that flyeth?**  
**Answer:** They shall not be eaten.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:19

**Answer:** They fly.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:19

### Deuteronomy 14:21

**Q46. What must not be eaten that dies of itself?**  
**Answer:** Any thing that dieth of itself.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:21

**Q47. To whom may the thing that dieth of itself be given, or who may sell it?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt give it unto the stranger that is in thy gates, that he may eat it; or thou mayest sell it unto an alien.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:21

**Q48. What kind of people are the children of Israel unto the LORD their God in this verse?**  
**Answer:** An holy people unto the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:21

---

### Deuteronomy 14:22

**Q49. What must the people truly tithe?**  
**Answer:** All the increase of thy seed.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:22

**Q50. How often must the increase of thy seed be tithed?**  
**Answer:** Year by year.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:22

**Q51. What comes forth from the field?**  
**Answer:** That the field bringeth forth year by year.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:22

---

### Deuteronomy 14:23

**Q52. Where shall the people eat the tithe of their corn, wine, oil, and firstlings?**  
**Answer:** Before the LORD thy God, in the place which he shall choose to place his name there.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:23

**Q53. What specific agricultural items are to be eaten before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The tithe of thy corn, of thy wine, and of thine oil.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:23

**Q54. What animal firstlings are mentioned in connection with this tithe?**  
**Answer:** The firstlings of thy herds and of thy flocks.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:23

---

### Deuteronomy 14:24

**Q55. What condition might prevent the people from carrying their tithe?**  
**Answer:** If the way be too long for thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:24

**Q56. Why might the way be too long to carry the tithe?**  
**Answer:** Because the place is too far from thee, which the LORD thy God shall choose to set his name there.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:24

**Q57. What has the LORD done regarding the place where He sets His name?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God hath blessed thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:24

---

### Deuteronomy 14:25

**Q58. What action is the person to take if the way is too long?**  
**Answer:** Then shalt thou turn it into money, and bind up the money in thine hand.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:25

**Q59. Where shall the person go with the money?**  
**Answer:** Unto the place which the LORD thy God shall choose.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:25

**Q60. What is bound up in thine hand?**  
**Answer:** The money.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:25

---

### Deuteronomy 14:26

**Q61. For what kinds of food or drink may the money be bestowed?**  
**Answer:** For oxen, or for sheep, or for wine, or for strong drink.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:26

**Q62. What else may the money be bestowed for, according to whatsoever thy soul desireth?**  
**Answer:** For whatsoever thy soul desireth.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:26

**Q63. Where shall thou eat before the LORD thy God and rejoice?**  
**Answer:** There before the LORD thy God, and thou shalt rejoice, thou, and thine household.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:26

---

### Deuteronomy 14:27

**Q64. Who must not be forsaken within thy gates?**  
**Answer:** The Levite that is within thy gates.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:27

**Q65. Why must the Levite not be forsaken?**  
**Answer:** For he hath no part nor inheritance with thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:27

---

### Deuteronomy 14:28

**Q66. At the end of how many years shall all the tithe of thine increase be brought forth?**  
**Answer:** At the end of three years.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:28

**Q67. Where shall the tithe of increase be laid up?**  
**Answer:** And shalt lay it up within thy gates.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:28

---

### Deuteronomy 14:29

**Q68. Who shall come, and eat, and be satisfied along with the Levite?**  
**Answer:** The stranger, and the fatherless, and the widow, which are within thy gates.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:29

**Q69. Why are the Levite, stranger, fatherless, and widow to come and eat?**  
**Answer:** That they may come, and shall eat and be satisfied.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:29

**Q70. What will the LORD thy God do in all the work of thine hand that thou doest?**  
**Answer:** That the LORD thy God may bless thee in all the work of thine hand which thou doest.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 14:29
