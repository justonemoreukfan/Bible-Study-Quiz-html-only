# Deuteronomy 26 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 26:1

**Q1. When shall thou come in unto the land which the LORD thy God giveth thee for an inheritance?**  
**Answer:** When thou hast possessed it, and dwellest therein.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:1

**Q2. Whose land is it that the LORD giveth thee for an inheritance?**  
**Answer:** The land which the LORD thy God giveth thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:1

**Q3. What shall thou do after possessing and dwelling in the land?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt take of the first of all the fruit of the earth.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:1

---

### Deuteronomy 26:2

**Q4. What shall thou take of the earth, which thou bringest of thy land?**  
**Answer:** Some of the first of all the fruit of the earth.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:2

**Q5. Where shall thou put the fruit that thou takest of the earth?**  
**Answer:** In a basket.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:2

**Q6. Whither shall thou go with the basket?**  
**Answer:** Unto the place which the LORD thy God shall choose to place his name there.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:2

---

### Deuteronomy 26:3

**Q7. Unto whom shall thou go when thou comest unto the priest?**  
**Answer:** Unto the priest that shall be in those days.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:3

**Q8. What shall thou say unto the priest?**  
**Answer:** I profess this day unto the LORD thy God, that I am come unto the country which the LORD sware unto our fathers for to give us.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:3

**Q9. To whom did the LORD swear to give the country?**  
**Answer:** Unto our fathers.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:3

---

### Deuteronomy 26:4

**Q10. What shall the priest take out of thine hand?**  
**Answer:** The basket.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:4

**Q11. Where shall the priest set the basket?**  
**Answer:** Down before the altar of the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:4

**Q12. Whose altar is the basket set before?**  
**Answer:** The altar of the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:4

---

### Deuteronomy 26:5

**Q13. What shall thou speak and say before the LORD thy God?**  
**Answer:** A Syrian ready to perish was my father.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:5

**Q14. Down into what country did his father go?**  
**Answer:** Egypt.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:5

**Q15. With how many men did he go down into Egypt and sojourn there?**  
**Answer:** A few.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:5

---

### Deuteronomy 26:6

**Q16. What did the Egyptians do to us?**  
**Answer:** Evil entreated us, and afflicted us.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:6

**Q17. What did the Egyptians lay upon us?**  
**Answer:** Hard bondage.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:6

**Q18. Who laid hard bondage upon us?**  
**Answer:** The Egyptians.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:6

---

### Deuteronomy 26:7

**Q19. To whom did we cry when we cried unto the LORD God of our fathers?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God of our fathers.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:7

**Q20. What did the LORD hear?**  
**Answer:** Our voice.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:7

**Q21. What did the LORD look on?**  
**Answer:** Our affliction, and our labour, and our oppression.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:7

---

### Deuteronomy 26:8

**Q22. By what means did the LORD bring us forth out of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** With a mighty hand, and with an outstretched arm.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:8

**Q23. With what kind of terrors did the LORD bring us forth?**  
**Answer:** With great terribleness, and with signs, and with wonders.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:8

**Q24. From where did the LORD bring us forth?**  
**Answer:** Out of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:8

---

### Deuteronomy 26:9

**Q25. Whither hath the LORD brought us?**  
**Answer:** Into this place.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:9

**Q26. What hath the LORD given us?**  
**Answer:** This land, even a land that floweth milk and honey.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:9

**Q27. What does the land flow with?**  
**Answer:** Milk and honey.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:9

---

### Deuteronomy 26:10

**Q28. What now have I brought?**  
**Answer:** The firstfruits of the land, which thou, O LORD, hast given me.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:10

**Q29. What shall thou do with the basket before the LORD thy God?**  
**Answer:** Set it before the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:10

**Q30. Before whom shall thou worship?**  
**Answer:** Before the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:10

---

### Deuteronomy 26:11

**Q31. In what shall thou rejoice?**  
**Answer:** In every good thing which the LORD thy God hath given unto thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:11

**Q32. Who shall rejoice with thee in every good thing?**  
**Answer:** Thou, and thine house, and the Levite, and the stranger that is among you.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:11

**Q33. Who gave unto thee the every good thing?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:11

---

### Deuteronomy 26:12

**Q34. When thou hast made an end of tithing all the tithes of thine increase, in what year is it?**  
**Answer:** The third year, which is the year of tithing.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:12

**Q35. To whom shall thou give the tithe of thine increase?**  
**Answer:** Unto the Levite, the stranger, the fatherless, and the widow.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:12

**Q36. Where may they eat within thy gates, and be filled?**  
**Answer:** Within thy gates.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:12

---

### Deuteronomy 26:13

**Q37. What shall thou say before the LORD thy God?**  
**Answer:** I have brought away the hallowed things out of mine house, and also have given them unto the Levite, and unto the stranger, to the fatherless, and to the widow.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:13

**Q38. Whose commandments have I not transgressed or forgotten?**  
**Answer:** According to all thy commandments which thou hast commanded me.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:13

**Q39. What have I not done concerning the commandments?**  
**Answer:** I have not transgressed thy commandments, neither have I forgotten them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:13

---

### Deuteronomy 26:14

**Q40. What have I not eaten of in my mourning?**  
**Answer:** Thereof.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:14

**Q41. What have I not taken away thereof for any unclean use?**  
**Answer:** Neither have I taken away ought thereof for any unclean use.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:14

**Q42. For whose use have I not given ought thereof when dead?**  
**Answer:** Nor given ought thereof for the dead.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:14

---

### Deuteronomy 26:15

**Q43. Look down from what holy habitation?**  
**Answer:** From thy holy habitation, from heaven.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:15

**Q44. Whose people Israel have you blessed?**  
**Answer:** Thy people Israel.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:15

**Q45. What kind of land hast thou given us, as thou swarest unto our fathers?**  
**Answer:** A land which floweth with milk and honey.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:15

---

### Deuteronomy 26:16

**Q46. This day who hath commanded thee to do these statutes and judgments?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:16

**Q47. With what shall thou keep and do these statutes and judgments?**  
**Answer:** With all thine heart, and with all thy soul.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:16

**Q48. What shall thou do with all thine heart and all thy soul?**  
**Answer:** Keep and do them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:16

---

### Deuteronomy 26:17

**Q49. Whom hast thou avouched this day to be thy God?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:17

**Q50. To walk in whose ways hast thou avouched to walk?**  
**Answer:** In his ways.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:17

**Q51. What must thou keep when hearkening unto his voice?**  
**Answer:** His statutes, and his commandments, and his judgments.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:17

---

### Deuteronomy 26:18

**Q52. Whom hath the LORD avouched thee this day to be?**  
**Answer:** His peculiar people.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:18

**Q53. As what hath the LORD avouched thee?**  
**Answer:** As he hath promised thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:18

**Q54. What must thou keep in order to be his peculiar people?**  
**Answer:** All his commandments.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:18

---

### Deuteronomy 26:19

**Q55. High above all nations hath he made thee in what ways?**  
**Answer:** In praise, and in name, and in honour.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:19

**Q56. Unto whom shall thou be a holy people?**  
**Answer:** Unto the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:19

**Q57. As what hath he spoken?**  
**Answer:** As he hath spoken.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 26:19
