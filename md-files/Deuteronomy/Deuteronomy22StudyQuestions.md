# Deuteronomy 22 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 22:1

**Q1. Whose sheep or ox should not be seen go astray?**  
**Answer:** Thy brother's.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:1

**Q2. What should one not do when seeing a brother's sheep or ox go astray?**  
**Answer:** Hide thyself from them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:1

**Q3. To whom shalt thou bring them again?**  
**Answer:** Again unto thy brother.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:1

---

### Deuteronomy 22:2

**Q1. What should be done if the brother is not nigh unto thee, or if thou know him not?**  
**Answer:** Bring it unto thine own house, and it shall be with thee until thy brother seek after it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:2

**Q2. When shalt thou restore the animal to him?**  
**Answer:** When thy brother seek after it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:2

---

### Deuteronomy 22:3

**Q1. In what manner shalt thou do with his ass, his raiment, and every lost thing of thy brother's?**  
**Answer:** Likewise.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:3

**Q2. What are you forbidden to do with lost things?**  
**Answer:** Thou mayest not hide thyself.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:3

---

### Deuteronomy 22:4

**Q1. Whose ass or ox shouldst thou not see fall down by the way?**  
**Answer:** Thy brother's.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:4

**Q2. What should you not do when you see them fall down?**  
**Answer:** Hide thyself from them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:4

**Q3. With whom shalt thou surely help him to lift them up again?**  
**Answer:** With him.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:4

---

### Deuteronomy 22:5

**Q1. What shall not a woman wear pertaining unto a man?**  
**Answer:** That which pertaineth unto a man.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:5

**Q2. What shall a man not put on?**  
**Answer:** A woman's garment.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:5

**Q3. What is all that do these things unto the Lord thy God?**  
**Answer:** An abomination.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:5

---

### Deuteronomy 22:6

**Q1. What might chance to be before thee in the way in any tree, or on the ground?**  
**Answer:** A bird's nest.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:6

**Q2. What must be in the nest concerning young ones or eggs?**  
**Answer:** Young ones, or eggs.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:6

**Q3. Who should be sitting upon the young, or upon the eggs?**  
**Answer:** The dam.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:6

---

### Deuteronomy 22:7

**Q1. What must you surely let go when you find a bird's nest?**  
**Answer:** The dam.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:7

**Q2. What may you take to yourself?**  
**Answer:** The young.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:7

**Q3. Why should you do this, that it may be with you?**  
**Answer:** That it may be well with thee, and that thou mayest prolong thy days.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:7

---

### Deuteronomy 22:8

**Q1. What kind of new house must thou build?**  
**Answer:** A new house.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:8

**Q2. What must thou make for thy roof?**  
**Answer:** A battlement.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:8

**Answer:** Blood.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:8

### Deuteronomy 22:9

**Answer:** Divers seeds.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:9

**Q2. What happens if you sow divers seeds in your vineyard?**

**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:9

**Verse:** Deuteronomy 22:10

### Deuteronomy 22:11
