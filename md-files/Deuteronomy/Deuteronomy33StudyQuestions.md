# Deuteronomy 33 Study Questions (KJV)

### Deuteronomy 33:1

**Q1. Who is described as the man of God in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 33:1

**Q2. Before whose death did Moses bless the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Before his death.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 33:1

**Q3. Whom did Moses bless before his death?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 33:1
