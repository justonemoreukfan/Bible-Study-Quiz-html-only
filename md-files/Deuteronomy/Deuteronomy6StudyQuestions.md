# Deuteronomy 6 Study Questions (KJV)

### Deuteronomy 6:1
**Q1. According to Deuteronomy 6:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Now these are the commandments.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 6:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Deuteronomy 6:1?**  
**Answer:** the statutes.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 6:1

**Q3. What conclusion or detail is given in Deuteronomy 6:1?**  
**Answer:** and the judgments, which the LORD your God commanded to teach you, that ye might do them in the land whither ye go to possess it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 6:1
