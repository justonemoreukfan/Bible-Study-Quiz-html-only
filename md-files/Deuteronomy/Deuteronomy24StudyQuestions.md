# Deuteronomy 24 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 24:1

**Q1. When a man hath taken a wife, and married her, what might happen if she find no favour in his eyes?**  
**Answer:** He may write her a bill of divorcement, and give it in her hand, and send her out of his house.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 24:1

**Q2. What happens after the woman departs out of his house according to verse 1?**  
**Answer:** She may go and be another man's wife.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 24:1

**Q3. Where is the bill of divorcement placed when the man sends her out of his house?**  
**Answer:** In her hand.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 24:1

---

### Deuteronomy 24:2

**Q1. What does the latter husband do when he takes her to be his wife?**  
**Answer:** Hates her.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 24:2

**Q2. What does the latter husband write in her hand after he hates her?**  
**Answer:** A bill of divorcement.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 24:2

**Q3. Where does the latter husband send her out of?**  
**Answer:** His house.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 24:2

### Deuteronomy 24:3

**Answer:** The latter husband.

### Deuteronomy 24:4

**Q1. What is her former husband not permitted to do after she is defiled?**  
**Answer:** Take her again to be his wife.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 24:4

**Q2. How is the woman described before she is taken again by her former husband?**  
**Answer:** Defiled.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 24:4

**Q3. What is said to be an abomination before the Lord regarding this matter?**  
**Answer:** That which is defiled.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 24:4

### Deuteronomy 24:5

**Q1. How long is a newly married man exempt from going out to war?**  
**Answer:** One year.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 24:5

**Q2. What burden is to be charged upon a newly married man during this year?**  
**Answer:** Neither shall he be charged with any business.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 24:5

**Q3. What is the man free to do at home during this year?**  
**Answer:** Cheer up his wife which he hath taken.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 24:5

### Deuteronomy 24:6

**Q1. What two items are forbidden to be taken as a pledge for a debt?**  
**Answer:** The nether or the upper millstone.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 24:6

**Q2. Why is taking a millstone as a pledge compared to taking a man's life?**  
**Answer:** For he taketh a man's life to pledge.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 24:6

**Q3. What specific part of the mill is mentioned alongside the upper millstone?**  
**Answer:** The nether millstone.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 24:6

### Deuteronomy 24:7

**Q1. What action against a brother of the children of Israel leads to the thief's death?**  
**Answer:** If a man be found stealing any of his brethren of the children of Israel, and maketh merchandise of him, or selleth him.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 24:7

**Q2. What must be put away from among the Israelites when this crime is committed?**  
**Answer:** Evil.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 24:7

**Q3. Who are the people the thief might steal from among his brethren?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 24:7
