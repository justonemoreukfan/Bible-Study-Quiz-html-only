# Deuteronomy 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 10:1

**Q1. What did the LORD say unto Moses in that time?**  
**Answer:** Hew thee two tables of stone like unto the first, and come up unto me into the mount, and make thee an ark of wood.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:1

**Q2. What were the two tables to be made like?**  
**Answer:** Like unto the first.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:1

**Q3. Where was Moses told to come up?**  
**Answer:** Into the mount.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:1

---

### Deuteronomy 10:2

**Q4. What would the LORD write upon the tables?**  
**Answer:** The words that were in the first tables which thou brakest.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:2

**Q5. Where were the tables to be put after writing?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt put them in the ark.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:2

**Q6. Whose words were written on the tables?**  
**Answer:** The words that were in the first tables.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:2

---

### Deuteronomy 10:3

**Q7. What kind of ark did Moses make?**  
**Answer:** An ark of shittim wood.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:3

**Q8. What did Moses hew out like unto the first?**  
**Answer:** Two tables of stone.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:3

**Q9. Where did Moses go up, having the two tables in his hand?**  
**Answer:** Into the mount.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:3

---

### Deuteronomy 10:4

**Q10. What did the LORD write on the tables according to the first writing?**  
**Answer:** The ten commandments.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:4

**Q11. When did the LORD speak the ten commandments to the assembly?**  
**Answer:** In the mount out of the midst of the fire in the day of the assembly.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:4

**Q12. What did the LORD give unto Moses after writing them?**  
**Answer:** The LORD gave them unto me.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:4

---

### Deuteronomy 10:5

**Q13. Where did Moses turn and come down from?**  
**Answer:** From the mount.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:5

**Q14. Where did Moses put the tables?**  
**Answer:** In the ark which I had made.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:5

**Q15. How long did the tables stay in the ark as the LORD commanded?**  
**Answer:** There they be.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:5

---

### Deuteronomy 10:6

**Q16. From where did the children of Israel take their journey?**  
**Answer:** From the wells of the children of Jaakan to Mosera.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:6

**Q17. Who died and was buried at Mosera?**  
**Answer:** Aaron.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:6

**Q18. Who ministered in the priest's office in Aaron's place?**  
**Answer:** Eleazar his son.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:6

---

### Deuteronomy 10:7

**Q19. From whence did the people journey unto Gudgodah?**  
**Answer:** From thence.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:7

**Q20. From Gudgodah, where did they journey?**  
**Answer:** To Jotbath, a land of rivers of waters.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:7

**Q21. What kind of land was Jotbath described as?**  
**Answer:** A land of rivers of waters.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:7

---

### Deuteronomy 10:8

**Q22. At that time, what did the LORD separate?**  
**Answer:** The tribe of Levi.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:8

**Q23. What ark was the tribe of Levi commanded to bear?**  
**Answer:** The ark of the covenant of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:8

**Q24. Before whom were the Levites appointed to stand, minister, and bless?**  
**Answer:** To stand before the LORD to minister unto him, and to bless in his name.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:8

---

### Deuteronomy 10:9

**Q25. Who hath no part nor inheritance with his brethren?**  
**Answer:** Levi.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:9

**Q26. Who is the inheritance of Levi?**  
**Answer:** The LORD is his inheritance.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:9

**Q27. According to what did the LORD God promise Levi?**  
**Answer:** According as the LORD thy God promised him.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:9

---

### Deuteronomy 10:10

**Q28. How long did Moses stay in the mount, as at the first time?**  
**Answer:** Forty days and forty nights.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:10

**Q29. Who hearkened unto Moses at that time also?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:10

**Q30. What did the LORD not destroy concerning Moses?**  
**Answer:** The LORD would not destroy thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:10

---

### Deuteronomy 10:11

**Q31. What did the LORD say unto Moses to arise and do?**  
**Answer:** Take thy journey before the people.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:11

**Q32. Where were the people to go and possess?**  
**Answer:** That they may go in and possess the land.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:11

**Q33. To whom did the LORD swear unto their fathers to give the land?**  
**Answer:** Which I sware unto their fathers to give unto them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:11

---

### Deuteronomy 10:12

**Q34. What does the LORD thy God require of thee?**  
**Answer:** To fear the LORD thy God, to walk in all his ways, and to love him, and to serve the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:12

**Q35. With what manner of heart and soul should one serve the LORD?**  
**Answer:** With all thy heart and with all thy soul.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:12

**Q36. How many ways of God are we instructed to walk in?**  
**Answer:** All his ways.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:12

---

### Deuteronomy 10:13

**Q37. To keep what did the LORD give commandments and statutes?**  
**Answer:** The commandments of the LORD, and his statutes.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:13

**Q38. For whose good were the commandments and statutes commanded this day?**  
**Answer:** For thy good.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:13

**Q39. What are we to do with the commandments and statutes?**  
**Answer:** To keep them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:13

---

### Deuteronomy 10:14

**Q40. What belongs to the LORD thy God?**  
**Answer:** The heaven and the heaven of heavens.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:14

**Q41. What else belongs to the LORD besides the heavens?**  
**Answer:** The earth also, with all that therein is.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:14

**Q42. Who is the owner of the earth and the heavens?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:14

---

### Deuteronomy 10:15

**Q43. In whom had the LORD a delight to love them?**  
**Answer:** Only in thy fathers.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:15

**Q44. What did the LORD choose after the fathers?**  
**Answer:** Their seed after them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:15

**Q45. Who did the LORD choose above all people?**  
**Answer:** You above all people.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:15

---

### Deuteronomy 10:16

**Q46. What part of the body are the children of Israel told to circumcise?**  
**Answer:** The foreskin of your heart.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:16

**Q47. What else should they no longer be?**  
**Answer:** Stiffnecked.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:16

**Q48. Whose heart should be circumcised?**  
**Answer:** The foreskin of your heart.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:16

---

### Deuteronomy 10:17

**Q49. Who is the God of gods and Lord of lords?**  
**Answer:** The LORD your God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:17

**Q50. What kind of God is the LORD described as in this verse?**  
**Answer:** A great God, a mighty, and a terrible.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:17

**Q51. What does the LORD not regard or take?**  
**Answer:** Which regardeth not persons, nor taketh reward.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:17

---

### Deuteronomy 10:18

**Q52. Whose judgment does the LORD execute?**  
**Answer:** The fatherless and widow.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:18

**Q53. Whom does the LORD love?**  
**Answer:** The stranger.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:18

**Q54. What does the LORD give to the stranger?**  
**Answer:** Food and raiment.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:18

---

### Deuteronomy 10:19

**Q55. Who are the people commanded to love?**  
**Answer:** The stranger.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:19

**Q56. Why were the children of Israel told to love the stranger?**  
**Answer:** For ye were strangers in the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:19

**Q57. Where had the Israelites been strangers?**  
**Answer:** In the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:19

---

### Deuteronomy 10:20

**Q58. Whom shalt thou fear?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:20

**Q59. Whom shalt thou serve and cleave to?**  
**Answer:** Him shalt thou serve, and to him shalt thou cleave.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:20

**Q60. By whose name shalt thou swear?**  
**Answer:** Swear by his name.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:20

---

### Deuteronomy 10:21

**Q61. Who is your praise?**  
**Answer:** He is thy praise.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:21

**Q62. Who is your God?**  
**Answer:** He is thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:21

**Q63. What has the LORD done for you that your eyes have seen?**  
**Answer:** That have done for thee these great and terrible things, which thine eyes have seen.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:21

---

### Deuteronomy 10:22

**Q64. How many souls were the fathers when they went down into Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Threescore and ten persons.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:22

**Q65. Who made the children of Israel as the stars of heaven for multitude?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God hath made thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:22

**Q66. What are the children of Israel now compared to in number?**  
**Answer:** As the stars of heaven for multitude.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 10:22
