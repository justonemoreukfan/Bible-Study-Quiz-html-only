# Deuteronomy 34 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 34:1

**Q1. Where did Moses go from the plains of Moab?**  
**Answer:** To the mountain of Nebo, to the top of Pisgah, that is over against Jericho.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:1

**Q2. What did the LORD shew him from the top of Pisgah?**  
**Answer:** All the land of Gilead, unto Dan.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:1

**Q3. What land was shewed unto him after Gilead unto Dan?**  
**Answer:** All Naphtali, and the land of Ephraim, and Manasseh, and all the land of Judah, unto the utmost sea.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:1

---

### Deuteronomy 34:2

**Q4. What else did the LORD shew unto Moses besides Naphtali, Ephraim, Manasseh, and Judah?**  
**Answer:** And the south, and the plain of the valley of Jericho, the city of palm trees, unto Zoar.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:2

**Q5. To which city were the plain of the valley of Jericho and the city of palm trees shewed?**  
**Answer:** Unto Zoar.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:2

**Q6. What was Jericho called in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The city of palm trees.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:2

---

### Deuteronomy 34:3

**Q7. What did the LORD say unto Moses concerning this land?**  
**Answer:** This is the land which I sware unto Abraham, unto Isaac, and unto Jacob.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:3

**Q8. To whom did the LORD swear regarding this land?**  
**Answer:** Unto Abraham, unto Isaac, and unto Jacob.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:3

**Q9. What did the LORD say He would give unto their seed?**  
**Answer:** I will give it unto thy seed.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:3

---

### Deuteronomy 34:4

**Q10. What did the LORD cause Moses to see with his eyes?**  
**Answer:** I have caused thee to see it with thine eyes.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:4

**Q11. Will Moses go over thither into the land?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not go over thither.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:4

**Q12. Who stated that Moses would not go over thither?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:4

---

### Deuteronomy 34:5

**Q13. Who died in the land of Moab?**  
**Answer:** Moses the servant of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:5

**Q14. Where did Moses the servant of the LORD die?**  
**Answer:** In the land of Moab.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:5

**Q15. According to the word of whom did Moses die?**  
**Answer:** According to the word of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:5

---

### Deuteronomy 34:6

**Q16. Where did He bury him?**  
**Answer:** In a valley in the land of Moab, over against Bethpeor.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:6

**Q17. Does any man know his sepuichre unto this day?**  
**Answer:** No man knoweth of his sepulchre unto this day.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:6

**Q18. Who buried him?**  
**Answer:** He [the LORD] buried him in a valley.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:6

---

### Deuteronomy 34:7

**Q19. How old was Moses when he died?**  
**Answer:** An hundred and twenty years old.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:7

**Q20. What was the state of his eye when he died?**  
**Answer:** His eye was not dim.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:7

**Q21. What was the state of his natural force when he died?**  
**Answer:** Nor his natural force abated.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:7

---

### Deuteronomy 34:8

**Q22. How many days did the children of Israel weep for Moses in the plains of Moab?**  
**Answer:** Thirty days.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:8

**Q23. Where did the children of Israel weep for Moses?**  
**Answer:** In the plains of Moab.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:8

**Q24. What days were ended after the weeping for Moses?**  
**Answer:** The days of weeping and mourning for Moses.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:8

---

### Deuteronomy 34:9

**Q25. With what was Joshua the son of Nun full?**  
**Answer:** With the spirit of wisdom.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:9

**Q26. Why was Joshua full of the spirit of wisdom?**  
**Answer:** For Moses had laid his hands upon him.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:9

**Q27. How did the children of Israel hearken unto Joshua?**  
**Answer:** And the children of Israel hearkened unto him, and did as the LORD commanded Moses.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:9

---

### Deuteronomy 34:10

**Q28. Has there arisen a prophet since in Israel like unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** And there arose not a prophet since in Israel like unto Moses.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:10

**Q29. Whom did the LORD know face to face?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:10

**Q30. Who knew Moses face to face?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:10

---

### Deuteronomy 34:11

**Q31. In all the signs and the wonders, by whom were they sent?**  
**Answer:** Which the LORD sent him to do in the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:11

**Q32. Against whom were the signs and wonders done in the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** To Pharaoh, and to all his servants, and to all his land.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:11

**Q33. Where did the LORD send Moses to do signs and wonders?**  
**Answer:** In the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:11

---

### Deuteronomy 34:12

**Q34. What was in all that mighty hand which Moses showed?**  
**Answer:** All the mighty hand, and all the great terror.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:12

**Q35. In whose sight did Moses show all the mighty hand and great terror?**  
**Answer:** In the sight of all Israel.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:12

**Q36. Who showed all the great terror?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 34:12
