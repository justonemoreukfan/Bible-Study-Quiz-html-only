# Deuteronomy 20 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 20:1

**Q1. What might Israel see when they go out to battle against their enemies?**  
**Answer:** Horses, and chariots, and a people more than thou.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:1

**Q2. Who should Israel not be afraid of in battle?**  
**Answer:** Them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:1

**Q3. Who is with Israel, bringing them up out of the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:1

---

### Deuteronomy 20:2

**Q1. Who is to approach unto the people when they are come nigh unto the battle?**  
**Answer:** The priest.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:2

**Q2. When is the priest to approach unto the people?**  
**Answer:** When ye are come nigh unto the battle.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:2

**Q3. What action does the priest take toward the people?**  
**Answer:** Shall approach and speak unto the people.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:2

---

### Deuteronomy 20:3

**Q1. What question does the priest ask Israel at the start of his speech?**  
**Answer:** Hear, O Israel, ye approach this day unto battle against your enemies: let not your hearts faint, fear not, and do not tremble, neither be ye terrified because of them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:3

**Q2. How should Israel's hearts not be described as they approach battle?**  
**Answer:** Faint.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:3

**Q3. Because of whom should the people not be terrified?**  
**Answer:** Because of them (their enemies).  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:3

---

### Deuteronomy 20:4

**Q1. Who goes with Israel to fight for them against their enemies?**  
**Answer:** The LORD your God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:4

**Q2. What is the purpose of the LORD going with Israel?**  
**Answer:** To fight for you against your enemies, to save you.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:4

**Q3. What will the LORD do in addition to fighting for Israel?**  
**Answer:** Save you.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:4

---

### Deuteronomy 20:5

**Q1. Who shall speak unto the people after the priests?**  
**Answer:** The officers.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:5

**Q2. What specific new possession is mentioned regarding a man's house?**  
**Answer:** A new house.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:5

**Q3. Where should the man who has built a new house go, and what might happen if he goes to the battle?**  
**Answer:** Let him go and return to his house, lest he die in the battle, and another man dedicate it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:5

---

### Deuteronomy 20:6

**Q1. What has a man potentially planted that is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** A vineyard.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:6

**Q2. What status must the vineyard have for this rule to apply?**  
**Answer:** Has not yet eaten of it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:6

**Q3. What should the man who planted the vineyard do?**  
**Answer:** Let him also go and return unto his house, lest he die in the battle, and another man eat of it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:6

---

### Deuteronomy 20:7

**Q1. What has a man potentially betrothed that is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** A wife.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:7

**Q2. What must the man not have done yet with the betrothed wife?**  
**Answer:** Not taken her.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:7

**Q3. Where should this man go, and what should he do?**  
**Answer:** Let him go and return unto his house, lest he die in battle, and another man take her.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:7

---

### Deuteronomy 20:8

**Q1. Who shall speak further unto the people after the officers?**  
**Answer:** The officers.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:8

**Q2. What kind of man should return to his house according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** What man is there that is fearful and fainthearted?  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:8

**Q3. Why should the fearful and fainthearted man go and return unto his house?**  
**Answer:** Lest his brethren's heart faint as well as his heart.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:8

---

### Deuteronomy 20:9

**Q1. When shall the officers have made an end of speaking unto the people?**  
**Answer:** When the officers have made an end of speaking unto the people.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:9

**Q2. Who shall make captains of the armies to lead the people?**  
**Answer:** They shall make captains of the armies.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:9

**Q3. Who leads the armies after the captains are made?**  
**Answer:** Captains of the armies to lead the people.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:9

### Deuteronomy 20:10

**Q1. When thou comest nigh unto what shouldst thou do?**  
**Answer:** A city to fight against it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:10

**Q2. What must you proclaim to the city when you come nigh to fight against it?**  
**Answer:** Peace unto it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:10

**Q3. Whom are you proclaiming peace to?**  
**Answer:** The city.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:10

---

### Deuteronomy 20:11

**Q1. What must the city answer if it makes you an answer of peace?**  
**Answer:** And open unto thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:11

**Q2. What shall be unto thee of all the people that is found therein?**  
**Answer:** Then it shall be, that all the people that is found therein shall be tributaries unto thee, and they shall serve thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:11

**Q3. What will the people of the city do for you?**  
**Answer:** Shall serve thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:11

---

### Deuteronomy 20:12

**Q1. What will the city do if it will not make peace with thee?**  
**Answer:** Will make no peace with thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:12

**Q2. What will the city do with thee instead of making peace?**  
**Answer:** Will make war against thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:12

**Q3. What action must you take when the city makes war against you?**  
**Answer:** Then thou shalt besiege it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:12

**Q2. What must you smite with the edge of the sword?**  
**Answer:** Every male thereof.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:13

### Deuteronomy 20:14

**Q1. What can you take unto yourselves from the city besides the men?**  
**Answer:** The women, and the little ones, and the cattle.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:14

**Q2. What else can you take out of the city besides women, little ones, and cattle?**  
**Answer:** All that is in the city, even all the spoil thereof.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:14

**Q3. Whose gift is the spoil of all thine enemies that thou shalt eat?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God giveth it thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:14

### Deuteronomy 20:15

**Q1. Concerning what kind of cities must you follow this rule?**  
**Answer:** All the cities which are very far off from thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:15

**Q2. What type of cities are excluded from being "cities which are very far off"?**  
**Answer:** Which are not of the cities of these nations.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:15

**Q3. Where are these cities located relative to the speaker?**  
**Answer:** Which are very far off from thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:15

### Deuteronomy 20:16

**Q1. Of the cities of these people, what must you save alive?**  
**Answer:** Nothing that breatheth.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:16

**Q2. Which specific people groups' cities are referred to here?**  
**Answer:** The cities of these people, which the LORD thy God doth give thee for an inheritance.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:16

**Q3. What kind of inheritance does the LORD thy God give thee?**  
**Answer:** For an inheritance.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:16

### Deuteronomy 20:17

**Q1. Which specific nations shall you utterly destroy?**  
**Answer:** The Hittites, and the Amorites, the Canaanites, and the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:17

**Q2. Whose command must be followed in utterly destroying these nations?**  
**Answer:** As the LORD thy God hath commanded thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:17

**Q3. How thoroughly must these nations be destroyed?**  
**Answer:** Utterly destroy them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:17

---

### Deuteronomy 20:18

**Q1. What might these nations teach Israel to do?**  
**Answer:** To do after all their abominations, which they have done unto their gods.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:18

**Q2. Against whom would Israel sin if they learned the abominations of these nations?**  
**Answer:** Against the LORD your God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:18

**Q3. Why has the LORD commanded that nothing that breathes be saved alive in these cities?**  
**Answer:** That they teach you not to do after all their abominations.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:18

---

### Deuteronomy 20:19

**Q1. What might you besiege a city for a long time in making war against it?**  
**Answer:** When thou shalt besiege a city a long time, in making war against it to take it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:19

**Q2. What specific kind of trees must you not destroy by forcing an axe against them?**  
**Answer:** The trees thereof.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:19

**Q3. Why must you not cut down the fruit-yielding trees of the city?**  
**Answer:** For thou mayest eat of them, and thou shalt not cut them down (for the tree of the field is man's life) to employ them in the siege.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:19

---

### Deuteronomy 20:20

**Q1. What kind of trees may you destroy and cut down?**  
**Answer:** Only the trees which thou knowest that they be not trees for meat.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:20

**Q2. What can you build against the city that makes war with you using the cut-down trees?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt destroy and cut them down; and they shall build bulwarks against the city that maketh war with thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:20

**Q3. Until when must the bulwarks be built against the city?**  
**Answer:** Until it be subdued.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 20:20
