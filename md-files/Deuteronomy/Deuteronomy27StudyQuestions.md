# Deuteronomy 27 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 27:1

**Q1. Who commanded the people along with the elders of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Moses  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 27:1

**Q2. What did Moses and the elders of Israel command the people to keep?**  
**Answer:** All the commandments which I command you this day  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 27:1

**Q3. When did Moses and the elders command the people to keep the commandments?**  
**Answer:** This day  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 27:1

### Deuteronomy 27:2

**Q1. When should the people pass over Jordan unto the land which the LORD giveth them?**  
**Answer:** On the day when ye shall pass over Jordan unto the land which the LORD thy God giveth thee  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 27:2

**Q2. What should be set up on the day they pass over Jordan?**  
**Answer:** Great stones  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 27:2

**Q3. What must be done to the great stones after setting them up?**  
**Answer:** Plaster them with plaster  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 27:2
