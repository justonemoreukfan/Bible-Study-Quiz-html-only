# Deuteronomy 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 9:1

**Q1. Hear, O Israel: Thou art to pass over what today?**  
**Answer:** Jordan.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:1

**Q2. What are the nations greater and mightier than you said to have?**  
**Answer:** Cities great and fenced up to heaven.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:1

---

### Deuteronomy 9:2

**Q3. What kind of people are described as great and tall?**  
**Answer:** The children of the Anakims.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:2

**Q4. Who is asked, "Who can stand before the children of Anak!"?**  
**Answer:** Thou.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:2

---

### Deuteronomy 9:3

**Q5. Who is it that goeth over before thee as a consuming fire?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:3

---

### Deuteronomy 9:4

**Q6. What does the LORD thy God cast out from before thee when He drives them out?**  
**Answer:** These nations.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:4

**Q7. What reason does the LORD say you should not think in your heart for bringing you in to possess this land?**  
**Answer:** For my righteousness the LORD hath brought me in to possess this land.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:4

---

### Deuteronomy 9:5

**Q8. For whose wickedness does the LORD drive out these nations from before thee?**  
**Answer:** For the wickedness of these nations.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:5

---

### Deuteronomy 9:6

**Q9. What kind of people does the LORD thy God say you are?**  
**Answer:** A stiffnecked people.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:6

---

### Deuteronomy 9:7

**Q10. What should you remember, and not forget, that provoked the LORD thy God to wrath in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** How thou provokest the LORD thy God to wrath in the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:7

---

### Deuteronomy 9:8

**Q11. At what place did you provoke the LORD to wrath so that He was angry enough to have destroyed you?**  
**Answer:** In Horeb.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:8

---

### Deuteronomy 9:9

**Q12. Into the mount did I go up to receive what items?**  
**Answer:** The tables of stone, even the tables of the covenant which the LORD made with you.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:9

**Q13. How many days and nights did I abide in the mount while neither eating bread nor drinking water?**  
**Answer:** Forty days and forty nights.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:9

---

### Deuteronomy 9:10

**Q14. What did the LORD deliver unto me written with the finger of God?**  
**Answer:** Two tables of stone.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:10

**Q15. Out of what did the LORD speak unto you in the mount out of the midst of the fire on the day of the assembly?**  
**Answer:** The fire.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:10

---

### Deuteronomy 9:11

**Q12. At the end of how many days and nights did the LORD give me the two tables of stone?**  
**Answer:** Forty days and forty nights.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:11

---

### Deuteronomy 9:12

**Q13. Who told me to arise and get me down quickly from hence?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:12

**Q14. What have thy people which thou hast brought forth out of Egypt done?**  
**Answer:** They have corrupted themselves.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:12

---

### Deuteronomy 9:13

**Q15. Furthermore the LORD spake unto me, saying, what kind of people have I seen?**  
**Answer:** A stiffnecked people.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:13

---

### Deuteronomy 9:14

**Q16. What does the LORD say He will do to them and blot out their name from under heaven?**  
**Answer:** Let me alone, that I may destroy them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:14

**Q17. What kind of nation does the LORD say He will make of me that is mightier and greater than they?**  
**Answer:** A nation mightier and greater than they.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:14

---

### Deuteronomy 9:15

**Q18. So I turned and came down from the mount, and what was in my hand?**  
**Answer:** The two tables of the covenant.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:15

---

### Deuteronomy 9:16

**Q19. What had the people made for themselves when I looked and saw they had sinned against the LORD?**  
**Answer:** A molten calf.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:16

---

### Deuteronomy 9:17

**Q20. What did I do with the two tables that were in my two hands?**  
**Answer:** Cast them out of my two hands, and brake them before your eyes.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:17

---

### Deuteronomy 9:18

**Q21. How long did I fall down before the LORD, as at the first, without eating bread or drinking water?**  
**Answer:** Forty days and forty nights.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:18

**Q22. Because of all your sins which ye sinned, what did you do in doing wickedly in the sight of the LORD to provoke him to anger?**  
**Answer:** Did wickedly in the sight of the LORD, to provoke him to anger.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:18

---

### Deuteronomy 9:19

**Q23. Of what was I afraid because of the fierce anger of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The anger and hot displeasure.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:19

**Q24. Who hearkened unto me at that time also?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:19

---

### Deuteronomy 9:20

**Q25. With whom was the LORD very angry to have destroyed him?**  
**Answer:** Aaron.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:20

**Q26. What did I pray for at the same time?**  
**Answer:** For Aaron also.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:20

---

### Deuteronomy 9:21

**Q27. What did I take, your sin, the calf which ye had made, and burn with fire?**  
**Answer:** Your sin, the calf which ye had made.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:21

**Q28. What did I do to the dust thereof, casting it into the brook?**  
**Answer:** Stamped it, and ground it small even until it was as small as dust.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:21

---

### Deuteronomy 9:22

**Q29. At what three places did ye provoke the LORD to wrath?**  
**Answer:** At Taberah, and at Massah, and at Kibrothhattaavah.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:22

---

### Deuteronomy 9:23

**Q30. From what place did the LORD send you up, saying, Go up and possess the land which I have given you?**  
**Answer:** Kadeshbarnea.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:23

**Q31. Whose commandment did you rebel against?**  
**Answer:** The commandment of the LORD your God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:23

---

### Deuteronomy 9:24

**Q32. Since what day have ye been rebellious against the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Since the day that I knew you.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:24

---

### Deuteronomy 9:25

**Q33. How long did I fall down before the LORD because the LORD had said he would destroy you?**  
**Answer:** Forty days and forty nights.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:25

---

### Deuteronomy 9:26

**Q34. Unto whom did I pray and say, O Lord GOD, destroy not thy people and thine inheritance?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:26

---

### Deuteronomy 9:27

**Q35. Whose servants shouldst thou remember, Abraham, Isaac, and whom else?**  
**Answer:** Jacob.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:27

**Q36. What shouldst thou not look unto regarding this people?**  
**Answer:** The stubbornness of this people, nor to their wickedness, nor to their sin.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:27

---

### Deuteronomy 9:28

**Q37. From what land did the people say the LORD was not able to bring them?**  
**Answer:** The land whence ye brought us out.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:28

**Q38. Because the LORD hated them, where did the land's inhabitants say He brought them out to slay them?**  
**Answer:** In the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:28

---

### Deuteronomy 9:29

**Q39. What are they described as, being thy people and whom?**  
**Answer:** Thine inheritance.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:29

**Q40. By what power didst thou bring them out?**  
**Answer:** Thy mighty power and by thy stretched out arm.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 9:29
