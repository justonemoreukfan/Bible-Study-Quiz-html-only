# Deuteronomy 16 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 16:1

**Q1. What month are the children of Israel commanded to observe?**  
**Answer:** The month of Abib.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:1

**Q2. What feast are the children of Israel to keep unto the LORD their God in the month of Abib?**  
**Answer:** The passover.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:1

**Q3. Who brought the children of Israel forth out of Egypt by night?**  
**Answer:** The LORD their God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:1

---

### Deuteronomy 16:2

**Q1. What are the children of Israel to sacrifice unto the LORD their God beside the passover?**  
**Answer:** The flock and the herd.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:2

**Q2. Where is the sacrifice to be offered?**  
**Answer:** In the place which the LORD shall choose to place his name there.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:2

**Q3. What kind of offerings are the flock and herd to be in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The passover.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:2

---

### Deuteronomy 16:3

**Q1. What must the people not eat with the passover?**  
**Answer:** Leavened bread.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:3

**Q2. What must they eat with the passover for seven days?**  
**Answer:** Unleavened bread, even the bread of affliction.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:3

**Q3. Why did they come forth out of the land of Egypt in haste?**  
**Answer:** That thou mayest remember the day when thou camest forth out of the land of Egypt all the days of thy life.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:3

---

### Deuteronomy 16:4

**Q1. How much leaven is allowed to be seen with the children of Israel in all their coasts for seven days?**  
**Answer:** There shall be no leavened bread seen with thee in all thy coast seven days.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:4

**Q2. When must any of the flesh remain overnight until the morning from the first day?**  
**Answer:** None of the flesh which thou sacrificedst the first day at even shall remain all night until the morning.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:4

**Q3. What is prohibited regarding the sacrifice from the first day until morning?**  
**Answer:** Neither shall any of the flesh remain all night until the morning.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:4

---

### Deuteronomy 16:5

**Q1. Where are the people forbidden to sacrifice the passover?**  
**Answer:** Within any of thy gates, which the LORD thy God giveth thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:5

**Q2. Who gives the gates within which the passover may not be sacrificed?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:5

**Q3. What specific sacrifice is mentioned in relation to the gates?**  
**Answer:** The passover.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:5

---

### Deuteronomy 16:6

**Q1. In what location must the passover be sacrificed?**  
**Answer:** In the place which the LORD thy God shall choose to place his name in.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:6

**Q2. At what time of day shall they sacrifice the passover?**  
**Answer:** At even.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:6

**Q3. At what specific time of day shall they sacrifice the passover relative to the sun?**  
**Answer:** At the going down of the sun, at the season that thou camest forth out of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:6

---

### Deuteronomy 16:7

**Q1. Where are the people to roast and eat the sacrifice?**  
**Answer:** In the place which the LORD thy God shall choose.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:7

**Q2. What are the people to do in the morning after eating the passover?**  
**Answer:** Turn, and go unto thy tents.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:7

**Q3. What action must be done to the meat before eating it?**  
**Answer:** Roast and eat it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:7

### Deuteronomy 16:8

**Q1. How many days shall the people eat unleavened bread?**  
**Answer:** Six days.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:8

**Q2. What is to be held on the seventh day?**  
**Answer:** A solemn assembly to the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:8

**Q3. What work is to be done on the seventh day?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt do no work therein.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:8

### Deuteronomy 16:9

**Q1. How many weeks shall the people number unto themselves?**  
**Answer:** Seven weeks.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:9

**Q2. When does the numbering of the seven weeks begin?**  
**Answer:** Begin to number the seven weeks from such time as thou beginnest to put the sickle to the corn.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:9

**Q3. What is to begin when the sickle is put to the corn?**  
**Answer:** Begin to number the seven weeks.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:9

### Deuteronomy 16:10

**Q1. What feast shall the people keep unto the LORD their God?**  
**Answer:** The feast of weeks.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:10

**Q2. What kind of tribute of a freewill offering is to be given?**  
**Answer:** A tribute of a freewill offering of thine hand.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:10

**Q3. According to what standard should the freewill offering be given?**  
**Answer:** According as the LORD thy God hath blessed thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:10

### Deuteronomy 16:11

**Q1. Where shall the people rejoice before the LORD their God?**  
**Answer:** In the place which the LORD thy God hath chosen to place his name there.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:11

**Q2. Who besides the family is to rejoice before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Thy son, and thy daughter, and thy manservant, and thy maidservant, and the Levite that is within thy gates, and the stranger, and the fatherless, and the widow.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:11

**Q3. Where are the Levite, stranger, fatherless, and widow located?**  
**Answer:** Among you, in the midst of you / within thy gates.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:11

---

### Deuteronomy 16:12

**Q1. What must the people remember regarding Egypt?**  
**Answer:** That thou wast a bondman in Egypt.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:12

**Q2. What must the people observe and do?**  
**Answer:** These statutes.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:12

**Q3. What kind of bondman were they in Egypt?**  
**Answer:** A bondman in Egypt.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:12

### Deuteronomy 16:13

**Q1. What feast shall the people observe for seven days?**  
**Answer:** The feast of tabernacles.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:13

**Q2. When is the feast of tabernacles to be observed?**  
**Answer:** After that thou hast gathered in thy corn and thy wine.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:13

**Q3. From what locations are the corn and wine gathered?**  
**Answer:** Thy corn and thy wine (from thy floor and thy winepress).  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:13

---

### Deuteronomy 16:14

**Q1. Who shall rejoice in the feast of tabernacles?**  
**Answer:** Thou, and thy son, and thy daughter, and thy manservant, and thy maidservant, and the Levite, the stranger, and the fatherless, and the widow.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:14

**Q2. Who are the people to rejoice with during this feast?**  
**Answer:** The Levite, and the stranger, and the fatherless, and the widow.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:14

**Q3. Where are these various groups located relative to the person rejoicing?**  
**Answer:** That are within thy gates.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:14

### Deuteronomy 16:16

**Q1. How many times in a year shall all males appear before the LORD thy God?**  
**Answer:** Three times in a year.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:16

**Q2. In what place shall all males appear before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** In the place which he shall choose.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:16

**Q3. What are the three feasts named in which the males must appear?**  
**Answer:** In the feast of unleavened bread, and in the feast of weeks, and in the feast of tabernacles.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:16

**Answer:** According to the blessing of the LORD thy God which he hath given thee.

**Answer:** They shall not appear before the LORD empty.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 16:17
