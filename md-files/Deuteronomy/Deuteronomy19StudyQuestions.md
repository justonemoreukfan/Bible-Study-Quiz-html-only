# Deuteronomy 19 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 19:1

**Q1. What has the LORD thy God cut off?**  
**Answer:** The nations.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:1

**Q2. Whose land doth the LORD thy God give thee?**  
**Answer:** The land of the nations.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:1

**Q3. What are you to do after you succeed them and dwell in their cities and houses?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt succeed them, and dwell in their cities, and in their houses.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:1

---

### Deuteronomy 19:2

**Q1. How many cities are you to separate for yourself in the midst of your land?**  
**Answer:** Three cities.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:2

**Q2. Whose land are these cities to be separated in?**  
**Answer:** The land, which the LORD thy God giveth thee to possess it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:2

---

### Deuteronomy 19:3

**Q1. What are you to prepare?**  
**Answer:** Thee an way.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:3

**Q2. What are you to divide into three parts?**  
**Answer:** The coasts of thy land, which the LORD thy God giveth thee to inherit.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:3

**Q3. What is the purpose of dividing the coasts and preparing the way?**  
**Answer:** That every slayer may flee thither.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:3

---

### Deuteronomy 19:4

**Q1. Who is the case concerning that shall flee to the city?**  
**Answer:** The slayer.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:4

**Q2. How must the slayer kill his neighbour?**  
**Answer:** Unawares.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:4

**Q3. What did the slayer not have in times past toward his neighbour?**  
**Answer:** Hatred.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:4

---

### Deuteronomy 19:5

**Q1. Where do a man and his neighbour go in this example?**  
**Answer:** Into the wood to hew wood.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:5

**Q2. What action does the man's hand do with the axe?**  
**Answer:** Fetcheth a stroke with the axe to cut down the tree.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:5

**Q3. What happens to the head from the haft?**  
**Answer:** The head slippeth from the helve.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:5

---

### Deuteronomy 19:6

**Q1. Who might pursue the slayer while hot with anger?**  
**Answer:** The avenger of blood.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:6

**Q2. What might happen if the way is too long?**  
**Answer:** Overtake him, because the way is long.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:6

**Q3. What does the slayer not deserve since he killed his neighbour ignorantly and without past hatred?**  
**Answer:** He was not worthy of death, inasmuch as he hated him not in time past.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:6

---

### Deuteronomy 19:7

**Q1. What did I command you to do?**  
**Answer:** Separate three cities for thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:7

---

### Deuteronomy 19:8

**Q1. What will the LORD thy God enlarge if He keeps His oath to thy fathers?**  
**Answer:** Thy coast.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:8

**Q2. What land will the LORD give unto thee?**  
**Answer:** All the land which He promised to give unto thy fathers.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:8

---

### Deuteronomy 19:9

**Q1. What must you keep to do all these commandments?**  
**Answer:** All this commandment.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:9

**Q2. What are you to do to the commandments this day?**  
**Answer:** Which I command thee this day.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:9

**Q3. What must you do in addition to loving the LORD your God?**  
**Answer:** Walk ever in His ways.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:9

---

### Deuteronomy 19:10

**Q1. What kind of blood is not to be shed in your land?**  
**Answer:** Innocent blood.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:10

**Q2. Who gives you the land for an inheritance?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God giveth thee for an inheritance.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:10

**Q3. What would be upon you if innocent blood were shed?**  
**Answer:** Bloodguiltiness be upon thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:10

---

### Deuteronomy 19:11

**Q1. What does a man have if he hates his neighbour?**  
**Answer:** Hatred.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:11

**Q2. What does the man do to his neighbour out of hatred?**  
**Answer:** Lie in wait for him, and rise up against him, and smite him mortally that he die.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:11

**Q3. Where does the man flee after smiting his neighbour mortally?**  
**Answer:** Into one of these cities.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:11

---

### Deuteronomy 19:12

**Q1. Who shall send and fetch the murderer out of his city?**  
**Answer:** The elders of his city.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:12

**Q2. Into whose hand shall the murderer be delivered?**  
**Answer:** Into the hand of the avenger of blood.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:12

**Q3. What shall happen to the murderer so that he may die?**  
**Answer:** That he may die.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:12

---

### Deuteronomy 19:13

**Q1. What must your eye not do regarding the murderer?**  
**Answer:** Thine eye shall not pity him.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:13

**Q2. What kind of blood must you put away from Israel?**  
**Answer:** The guilt of innocent blood.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:13

**Q3. What will happen as a result of putting away innocent blood?**  
**Answer:** That it may go well with thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:13

---

### Deuteronomy 19:14

**Q1. What must you not remove or alter?**  
**Answer:** Thy neighbour's landmark.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:14

**Q2. When were these landmarks set?**  
**Answer:** Which they of old time have set.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:14

**Q3. In whose inheritance must you not remove your neighbour's landmark?**  
**Answer:** In thine inheritance, which thou shalt inherit.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:14

---

### Deuteronomy 19:15

**Q1. How many witnesses shall rise up against a man for any iniquity or sin?**  
**Answer:** One witness shall not rise up against a man for any iniquity, or for any sin.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:15

**Q2. At the mouth of how many witnesses shall a matter be established?**  
**Answer:** At the mouth of two witnesses, or at the mouth of three witnesses.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:15

---

### Deuteronomy 19:16

**Q1. What kind of witness rises up against any man?**  
**Answer:** A false witness.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:16

**Q2. What does the false witness testify against a man?**  
**Answer:** To testify against him that which is wrong.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:16

---

### Deuteronomy 19:17

**Q1. Who are the two men involved in the controversy?**  
**Answer:** Both the men, between whom the controversy is.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:17

**Q2. Before whom shall the two men stand?**  
**Answer:** Before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:17

**Q3. Who else shall stand before the LORD and the judges?**  
**Answer:** Before the priests and the judges, which shall be in those days.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:17

---

### Deuteronomy 19:18

**Q1. What shall the judges make diligent inquisition into?**  
**Answer:** The matter.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:18

**Q2. What kind of witness is identified if he has testified falsely?**  
**Answer:** Behold, if the witness be a false witness.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:18

**Q3. What has the false witness testified against?**  
**Answer:** Hath testified falsely against his brother.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:18

---

### Deuteronomy 19:19

**Q1. What shall be done unto the false witness?**  
**Answer:** Then shall ye do unto him, as he had thought to have done unto his brother.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:19

**Q2. What must you put away from the midst of you?**  
**Answer:** The evil.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:19

---

### Deuteronomy 19:20

**Q1. Who shall hear and fear when this judgment is executed?**  
**Answer:** And those which remain shall hear, and fear.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:20

**Q2. What shall henceforth not be committed among you?**  
**Answer:** Shall no more commit any such evil among you.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:20

---

### Deuteronomy 19:21

**Q1. What shall your eye not do regarding the offender?**  
**Answer:** Thine eye shall not pity.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:21

**Q2. What is the penalty for life?**  
**Answer:** Life for life.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:21

**Q3. What other punishments are listed for offenses?**  
**Answer:** Eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 19:21
