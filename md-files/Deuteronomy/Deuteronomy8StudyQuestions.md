# Deuteronomy 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 8:1

**Q1. What should the children of Israel observe to do?**  
**Answer:** All the commandments which I command thee this day.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:1

**Q2. What will the children of Israel do if they observe and do the commandments?**  
**Answer:** Live, and multiply, and go in and possess the land which the LORD sware unto your fathers.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:1

**Q3. Unto whose fathers did the LORD swear regarding the land?**  
**Answer:** Your fathers.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:1

---

### Deuteronomy 8:2

**Q1. What should the children of Israel remember?**  
**Answer:** All the way which the LORD thy God led thee these forty years in the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:2

**Q2. How long did the LORD thy God lead thee in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** These forty years.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:2

**Q3. To what purpose did the LORD thy God lead thee these forty years in the wilderness, to humble thee, and to prove thee, to know what was in thine heart, whether thou wouldest keep his commandments or no?**  
**Answer:** To humble thee, and to prove thee, to know what was in thine heart, whether thou wouldest keep his commandments, or no.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:2

---

### Deuteronomy 8:3

**Q1. And how did he humble thee, and suffer thee to hunger?**  
**Answer:** And fed thee with manna.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:3

**Q2. Who did not know the manna, neither did thy fathers know it?**  
**Answer:** Thou knewest not, neither did thy fathers know.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:3

**Q3. That he might make thee know that man doth not live by bread only, but by what does man live?**  
**Answer:** By every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of the LORD doth man live.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:3

---

### Deuteronomy 8:4

**Q1. What waxed not old upon thee?**  
**Answer:** Thy raiment.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:4

**Q2. What did not swell upon thee?**  
**Answer:** Thy foot.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:4

**Q3. For how long did thy raiment not wax old upon thee, neither did thy foot swell?**  
**Answer:** These forty years.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:4

---

### Deuteronomy 8:5

**Q1. What shouldst thou consider in thine heart?**  
**Answer:** That, as a man chasteneth his son, so the LORD thy God chasteneth thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:5

**Q2. As what does a man chasteneth his son?**  
**Answer:** As a man chasteneth his son.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:5

**Q3. Who chasteneth thee as a man chasteneth his son?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:5

---

### Deuteronomy 8:6

**Q1. Whose commandments shalt thou keep?**  
**Answer:** The commandments of the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:6

**Q2. How should you walk in relation to his ways?**  
**Answer:** To walk in his ways.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:6

**Q3. What attitude should you hold toward the LORD thy God?**  
**Answer:** And to fear him.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:6

---

### Deuteronomy 8:7

**Q1. Into what kind of land does the LORD thy God bring thee?**  
**Answer:** A good land.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:7

**Q2. What is in the land regarding water?**  
**Answer:** A land of brooks of water, of fountains and depths that spring out of valleys and hills.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:7

**Q3. Where do the fountains and depths spring out of?**  
**Answer:** Valleys and hills.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:7

---

### Deuteronomy 8:8

**Q1. What kind of grain and fruit are in the land?**  
**Answer:** A land of wheat, and barley, and vines, and fig trees, and pomegranates.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:8

**Q2. What kind of oil is in the land?**  
**Answer:** A land of oil olive.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:8

**Q3. What kind of sweet food is in the land?**  
**Answer:** And honey.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:8

---

### Deuteronomy 8:9

**Q1. In what kind of bread shalt thou eat bread without scarceness?**  
**Answer:** A land wherein thou shalt eat bread without scarceness.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:9

**Q2. What shalt thou not lack in the land?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not lack any thing in it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:9

**Q3. What are the stones of the land, and out of whose hills mayest thou dig brass?**  
**Answer:** A land whose stones are iron, and out of whose hills thou mayest dig brass.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:9

---

### Deuteronomy 8:10

**Q1. When shalt thou eat and be full?**  
**Answer:** When thou hast eaten and art full.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:10

**Q2. What must thou do for the good land which he hath given thee?**  
**Answer:** Then thou shalt bless the LORD thy God for the good land which he hath given thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:10

**Q3. Who gave thee the good land?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:10

---

### Deuteronomy 8:11

**Q1. What warning is given regarding the LORD thy God?**  
**Answer:** Beware that thou forget not the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:11

**Q2. In what manner are you warned not to forget the LORD thy God?**  
**Answer:** In not keeping his commandments, and his judgments, and his statutes, which I command thee this day.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:11

**Q3. When are these commandments, judgments, and statutes commanded?**  
**Answer:** This day.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:11

---

### Deuteronomy 8:12

**Q1. What happens when thou hast eaten and art full?**  
**Answer:** Thou hast built goodly houses, and dwelt therein.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:12

**Q2. What kind of houses have you built?**  
**Answer:** Goodly houses.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:12

**Q3. What have you done in those goodly houses?**  
**Answer:** And dwelt therein.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:12

---

### Deuteronomy 8:13

**Q1. What happens to your herds and flocks?**  
**Answer:** And when thy herds and thy flocks multiply.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:13

**Q2. What happens to your silver and gold?**  
**Answer:** And thy silver and thy gold is multiplied.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:13

**Q3. What happens to all that you have?**  
**Answer:** And all that thou hast is multiplied.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:13

---

### Deuteronomy 8:14

**Q1. What might happen to thine heart when your herds, flocks, silver, gold, and all that you have are multiplied?**  
**Answer:** Then thine heart be lifted up.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:14

**Q2. Who might thou forget in that time?**  
**Answer:** And thou forget the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:14

**Q3. From where did the LORD thy God bring thee forth?**  
**Answer:** Which brought thee forth out of the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:14

---

### Deuteronomy 8:15

**Q1. Through what kind of wilderness did He lead thee?**  
**Answer:** Through that great and terrible wilderness.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:15

**Q2. What creatures were in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** Wherein were fiery serpents, and scorpions, and drought.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:15

**Q3. Where there was no water, what did He bring forth for thee?**  
**Answer:** Who brought thee forth water out of the rock of flint.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:15

---

### Deuteronomy 8:16

**Q1. With what did He feed thee in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** Who fed thee in the wilderness with manna.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:16

**Q2. Did your fathers know this food?**  
**Answer:** Which thy fathers knew not.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:16

**Q3. To what end did He humble thee and prove thee?**  
**Answer:** That he might humble thee, and that he might prove thee, to do thee good at thy latter end.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:16

---

### Deuteronomy 8:17

**Q1. What might thou say in thine heart?**  
**Answer:** And thou say in thine heart.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:17

**Q2. To whom is power attributed in this thought?**  
**Answer:** My power and the might of mine hand hath gotten me this wealth.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:17

**Q3. What has your power and the might of your hand gotten you?**  
**Answer:** This wealth.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:17

---

### Deuteronomy 8:18

**Q1. But whom shall thou remember?**  
**Answer:** But thou shalt remember the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:18

**Q2. Who gives thee power to get wealth?**  
**Answer:** For it is he that giveth thee power to get wealth.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:18

**Q3. Why does He give thee power to get wealth?**  
**Answer:** That he may establish his covenant which he sware unto thy fathers, as it is this day.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:18

---

### Deuteronomy 8:19

**Q1. What warning is given if thou shalt do at all?**  
**Answer:** And it shall be, if thou do at all forget the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:19

**Q2. After what other things might you walk?**  
**Answer:** And walk after other gods, and serve them, and worship them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:19

**Q3. What testimony is given against you if you do this?**  
**Answer:** I testify against you this day that ye shall surely perish.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:19

---

### Deuteronomy 8:20

**Q1. As what nations shall ye perish?**  
**Answer:** As the nations which the LORD destroyeth before your face.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:20

**Q2. Why shall ye perish?**  
**Answer:** Because ye would not be obedient unto the voice of the LORD your God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:20

**Q3. Whose voice would ye not be obedient unto?**  
**Answer:** The voice of the LORD your God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 8:20
