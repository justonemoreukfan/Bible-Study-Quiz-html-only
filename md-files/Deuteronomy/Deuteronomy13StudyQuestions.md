# Deuteronomy 13 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 13:1

**Q1. What may arise among you, or a dreamer of dreams?**  
**Answer:** A prophet.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:1

**Q2. What shall such a prophet or dreamer of dreams give thee?**  
**Answer:** A sign or a wonder.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:1

**Q3. What shall happen regarding the sign or the wonder whereof he spake unto thee?**  
**Answer:** It shall come to pass.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:1

---

### Deuteronomy 13:2

**Q2. When shall the sign or the wonder come to pass?**  
**Answer:** When the sign or the wonder come to pass, whereof he spake unto thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:2

**Q3. What words shall the prophet or dreamer speak concerning other gods?**  
**Answer:** Saying, Let us go after other gods, which thou hast not known, and let us serve them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:2

**Q4. What is the description given of these other gods in relation to the people?**  
**Answer:** Which thou hast not known.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:2

---

### Deuteronomy 13:3

**Q4. Whose words shall ye not hearken unto?**  
**Answer:** The words of that prophet, or that dreamer of dreams.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:3

**Q5. Who proveth you according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** The LORD your God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:3

**Q6. How does the LORD your God love you, for what purpose is He proving you?**  
**Answer:** To know whether ye love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:3

---

### Deuteronomy 13:4

**Q7. After whom shall ye walk?**  
**Answer:** The LORD your God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:4

**Q8. Whom shall ye fear?**  
**Answer:** Him.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:4

**Q9. Whose commandments shall ye keep, obey, serve, and cleave unto?**  
**Answer:** His commandments, and ye shall obey his voice, and ye shall serve him, and cleave unto him.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:4

---

### Deuteronomy 13:5

**Q10. What shall be done to that prophet, or that dreamer of dreams?**  
**Answer:** That prophet, or that dreamer of dreams, shall be put to death.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:5

**Q11. Why has that prophet spoken to turn you away from the LORD your God?**  
**Answer:** Because he hath spoken to turn you away from the LORD your God, which brought you out of the land of Egypt, and redeemed you out of the house of bondage.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:5

**Q12. What action should be taken to put the evil away from the midst of thee?**  
**Answer:** So shalt thou put the evil away from the midst of thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:5

---

### Deuteronomy 13:6

**Q13. Who might entice thee secretly, saying, Let us go and serve other gods?**  
**Answer:** Thy brother, the son of thy mother, or thy son, or thy daughter, or the wife of thy bosom, or thy friend.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:6

**Q14. How might this person entice thee?**  
**Answer:** Secretly.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:6

**Q15. Whose names are used when they say, "Let us go and serve other gods"?**  
**Answer:** Gods, which thou hast not known, thou, nor thy fathers.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:6

---

### Deuteronomy 13:7

**Q16. From among the gods of the people which are round about you, whether near or far off, where might they be located?**  
**Answer:** Namely, the gods of the people which are round about you, nigh unto thee, or far off from thee, from the one end of the earth even unto the other end of the earth.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:7

**Q17. What are these gods described as being to you?**  
**Answer:** Gods of the people which are round about you, nigh unto thee, or far off from thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:7

**Q18. What span of the earth is mentioned regarding where these people and gods might be?**  
**Answer:** From the one end of the earth even unto the other end of the earth.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:7

---

### Deuteronomy 13:8

**Q19. What should you not do unto the person who entices you secretly?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not consent unto him, nor hearken unto him.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:8

**Q20. What shall your eye not do toward him?**  
**Answer:** Neither shall thine eye pity him.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:8

**Q21. What shall you not do regarding sparing or concealing him?**  
**Answer:** Neither shalt thou spare, neither shalt thou conceal him.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:8

---

### Deuteronomy 13:9

**Q22. What must you surely do to the person?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt surely kill him.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:9

**Q23. Whose hand shall be first upon him to put him to death?**  
**Answer:** Thine hand shall be first upon him to put him to death.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:9

**Q24. Whose hand shall follow afterwards to put him to death?**  
**Answer:** And afterwards the hand of all the people.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:9

---

### Deuteronomy 13:10

**Q25. With what shall you stone him with stones?**  
**Answer:** That he die.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:10

**Q26. Why must you stone him with stones until he dies?**  
**Answer:** Because he hath sought to thrust thee away from the LORD thy God, which brought thee out of the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:10

**Q27. From where did the LORD thy God bring thee out?**  
**Answer:** Out of the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:10

---

### Deuteronomy 13:11

**Q28. Who shall hear, and fear, and shall do no more any such wickedness as this among you?**  
**Answer:** And all Israel shall hear, and fear.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:11

**Q29. What effect shall this hearing have on all Israel?**  
**Answer:** And shall hear, and fear.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:11

**Q30. What shall they do no more among you after hearing this?**  
**Answer:** And shall do no more any such wickedness as this among you.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:11

---

### Deuteronomy 13:12

**Q31. In which of thy cities, which the LORD thy God hath given thee to dwell there, mightest thou hear this said?**  
**Answer:** In one of thy cities, which the LORD thy God hath given thee to dwell there.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:12

**Q32. Who has given thee these cities to dwell there?**  
**Answer:** The LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:12

**Q33. What might you hear said in one of these cities?**  
**Answer:** Certain men, the children of Belial, are gone out from among you...  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:12

---

### Deuteronomy 13:13

**Q34. What kind of men are described as going out from among you?**  
**Answer:** Certain men, the children of Belial.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:13

**Q35. What have these men withdrawn the inhabitants of their city to do?**  
**Answer:** Saying, Let us go and serve other gods, which ye have not known.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:13

**Q36. How are the gods described that these men lead the inhabitants to serve?**  
**Answer:** Which ye have not known.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:13

---

### Deuteronomy 13:14

**Q37. What must you do upon hearing this report?**  
**Answer:** Then shalt thou enquire, and make search, and ask diligently.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:14

**Q38. If the thing is found certain, what must be true about the abomination?**  
**Answer:** And, behold, if it be truth, and the thing certain, that such abomination is wrought among you;  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:14

**Q39. What is the specific word used in the verse to describe this thing wrought among you?**  
**Answer:** Abomination.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:14

---

### Deuteronomy 13:15

**Q40. With the edge of the sword, what must you surely smite?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt surely smite the inhabitants of that city.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:15

**Q41. What must you destroy utterly, along with all that is therein and the cattle thereof?**  
**Answer:** Destroying it utterly, and all that is therein, and the cattle thereof, with the edge of the sword.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:15

**Q42. With what weapon shall you smite the city and its cattle?**  
**Answer:** With the edge of the sword.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:15

---

### Deuteronomy 13:16

**Q43. What shall you gather into the midst of the street thereof?**  
**Answer:** And thou shalt gather all the spoil of it into the midst of the street thereof.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:16

**Q44. What shall you burn with fire?**  
**Answer:** And shalt burn with fire the city, and all the spoil thereof every whit, for the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:16

**Q45. For whom shall the city and all its spoil be burned with fire as a burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** For the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:16

---

### Deuteronomy 13:17

**Q46. What shall not cleave to thine hand from the cursed thing?**  
**Answer:** And there shall cleave nought of the cursed thing to thine hand.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:17

**Q47. From what entity shall the LORD turn away?**  
**Answer:** That the LORD may turn from the fierceness of his anger.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:17

**Q48. What feelings and actions shall the LORD show thee according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** And shew thee mercy, and have compassion upon thee, and multiply thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:17

---

### Deuteronomy 13:18

**Q49. When shall the LORD shew thee mercy, have compassion upon thee, and multiply thee?**  
**Answer:** When thou shalt hearken to the voice of the LORD thy God, to keep all his commandments which I command thee this day, to do that which is right in the eyes of the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:18

**Q50. Whose commandments are you to keep?**  
**Answer:** All his commandments which I command thee this day.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:18

**Q51. In whose eyes must you do that which is right?**  
**Answer:** In the eyes of the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 13:18
