# Deuteronomy 25 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 25:1

**Q1. If there be a controversy between men, where do they come?**  
**Answer:** Unto judgment.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:1

**Q2. What are the judges to do concerning the men in controversy?**  
**Answer:** Judge them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:1

**Q3. Whom shall the judges justify and whom shall they condemn?**  
**Answer:** They shall justify the righteous, and condemn the wicked.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:1

---

### Deuteronomy 25:2

**Q1. If the wicked man be worthy to be beaten, what shall the judge cause him to be?**  
**Answer:** Brought before him, and to be beaten before his face.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:2

**Q2. According to what shall the judge cause him to be beaten?**  
**Answer:** According to his fault, by a certain number.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:2

---

### Deuteronomy 25:3

**Q1. How many stripes may the judge give him, and not exceed?**  
**Answer:** Forty stripes.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:3

**Q2. What would happen to thy brother if he should exceed and beat him above these with many stripes?**  
**Answer:** Thy brother should seem vile unto thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:3

---

### Deuteronomy 25:4

**Q1. What animal shall thou not muzzle?**  
**Answer:** The ox.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:4

**Q2. What is the ox doing when it treads out the corn?**  
**Answer:** Treading out the corn.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:4

---

### Deuteronomy 25:5

**Q1. If brethren dwell together, and one of them die, and have no child, what shall the wife of the dead not do?**  
**Answer:** Marry without unto a stranger.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:5

**Q2. Who shall go in unto her and take her to him to wife?**  
**Answer:** Her husband's brother.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:5

**Q3. What duty shall the husband's brother perform unto her?**  
**Answer:** The duty of an husband's brother unto her.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:5

---

### Deuteronomy 25:6

**Q1. Whom shall the firstborn which she beareth succeed?**  
**Answer:** In the name of his brother which is dead.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:6

**Q2. What is the purpose of succeeding in his brother's name?**  
**Answer:** That his name be not put out of Israel.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:6

---

### Deuteronomy 25:7

**Q1. If the man like not to take his brother's wife, where shall she go up?**  
**Answer:** To the gate unto the elders.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:7

---

### Deuteronomy 25:8

**Q1. Who shall call the man and speak unto him?**  
**Answer:** The elders of his city.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:8

---

### Deuteronomy 25:9

**Q1. What shall his brother's wife come unto him in the presence of the elders?**  
**Answer:** Loose his shoe from off his foot.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:9

**Q2. What shall she spit in?**  
**Answer:** In his face.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:9

**Q3. What shall she answer and say?**  
**Answer:** So shall it be done unto that man that will not build up his brother's house.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:9

---

### Deuteronomy 25:10

**Q1. What shall his name be called in Israel?**  
**Answer:** The house of him that hath his shoe loosed.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:10

---

### Deuteronomy 25:11

**Q1. When men strive together one with another, who draws near to deliver her husband out of the hand of him that smiteth him?**  
**Answer:** The wife of the one.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:11

**Q2. How does the wife put forth her hand?**  
**Answer:** And taketh him by the secrets.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:11

---

### Deuteronomy 25:12

**Q1. What shall thou do to her hand when she does this?**  
**Answer:** Then thou shalt cut off her hand.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:12

**Q2. How shall thine eye regard her?**  
**Answer:** Thine eye shall not pity her.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:12

---

### Deuteronomy 25:13

**Q1. What shall thou not have in thy bag?**  
**Answer:** Divers weights, a great and a small.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:13

---

### Deuteronomy 25:14

**Q1. What shall thou not have in thine house?**  
**Answer:** Divers measures, a great and a small.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:14

---

### Deuteronomy 25:15

**Q1. What kind of weight and measure shall thou have?**  
**Answer:** A perfect and just weight, a perfect and just measure shalt thou have.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:15

**Q2. What will happen to thy days in the land which the LORD thy God giveth thee?**  
**Answer:** That thy days may be lengthened in the land which the LORD thy God giveth thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:15

---

### Deuteronomy 25:16

**Q1. Who is an abomination unto the LORD thy God?**  
**Answer:** All that do such things, all that do unrighteously.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:16

---

### Deuteronomy 25:17

**Q1. What did Amalek do unto thee by the way, when ye were come forth out of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Remember what Amalek did unto thee by the way, when ye were come forth out of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:17

---

### Deuteronomy 25:18

**Q1. How did Amalek meet thee by the way?**  
**Answer:** And met thee by the way.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:18

**Q2. Whom did Amalek smite in the rear?**  
**Answer:** And smote the hindmost of thee, even all that were feeble behind thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:18

**Q3. What was Amalek's attitude toward God?**  
**Answer:** When thou wast faint and weary; and he feared not God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:18

---

### Deuteronomy 25:19

**Q1. When the LORD thy God hath given thee rest from all thine enemies round about, what shall thou blot out?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt blot out the remembrance of Amalek from under heaven.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:19

**Q2. What shall thou not forget?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not forget it.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 25:19
