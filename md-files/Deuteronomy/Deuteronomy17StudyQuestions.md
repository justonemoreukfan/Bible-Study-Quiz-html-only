# Deuteronomy 17 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Deuteronomy 17:1

**Q1. What kind of animal should not be sacrificed unto the Lord thy God?**  
**Answer:** Any bull, or sheep, wherein is blemish, or any evilfavouredness.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:1

**Q2. What is such an offering considered unto the Lord thy God?**  
**Answer:** An abomination unto the Lord thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:1

**Q3. Among whom should there not be found any bull or sheep with a blemish?**  
**Answer:** Within any of thy gates which the Lord thy God giveth thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:1

---

### Deuteronomy 17:2

**Q1. Where might there be found among you man or woman that hath wrought wickedness?**  
**Answer:** Within any of thy gates which the Lord thy God giveth thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:2

**Q2. In whose sight has the man or woman wrought wickedness?**  
**Answer:** In the sight of the Lord thy God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:2

**Q3. What has the man or woman transgressed in doing this wickedness?**  
**Answer:** His covenant.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:2

---

### Deuteronomy 17:3

**Q1. And hath gone and served what other entities?**  
**Answer:** Other gods, and worshipped them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:3

**Q2. What specific celestial bodies are mentioned as being worshipped?**  
**Answer:** The sun, or moon, or any of the host of heaven.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:3

**Q3. Whose command does this worship violate?**  
**Answer:** Which I have not commanded.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:3

---

### Deuteronomy 17:4

**Q1. To whom must this thing be told when it is heard?**  
**Answer:** Unto thee, and thou hast heard it, and enquired diligently.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:4

**Q2. What kind of thing is found, according to the truth?**  
**Answer:** Behold, it be true, and the thing certain.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:4

**Q3. What specific abomination has been wrought in Israel?**  
**Answer:** That such abomination is wrought in Israel.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:4

---

### Deuteronomy 17:5

**Q1. Who shall be brought forth unto thy gates?**  
**Answer:** That man or that woman, which have committed that wicked thing.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:5

**Q2. What kind of person is to be brought to the gates?**  
**Answer:** That man or that woman.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:5

**Q3. By what action shall they be stoned with stones until they die?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt stone them with stones, till they die.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:5

---

### Deuteronomy 17:6

**Q1. By the mouth of how many witnesses shall he that is worthy of death be put to death?**  
**Answer:** Two or three witnesses.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:6

**Q2. By the mouth of how many witnesses shall he NOT be put to death?**  
**Answer:** One witness.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:6

**Q3. What kind of witness is specified as insufficient on his own?**  
**Answer:** One witness.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:6

---

### Deuteronomy 17:7

**Q1. Whose hands shall be first upon him to put him to death?**  
**Answer:** The hands of the witnesses.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:7

**Q2. Whose hands shall be last upon him after the witnesses?**  
**Answer:** The hands of all the people.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:7

**Q3. What action are you instructed to take to put the evil away from among you?**  
**Answer:** So thou shalt put the evil away from among you.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:7

---

### Deuteronomy 17:8

**Q1. What kind of matter might be too hard for thee in judgment?**  
**Answer:** If there arise a matter too hard for thee in judgment.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:8

**Q2. What types of disputes are listed as potential matters of judgment?**  
**Answer:** Between blood and blood, between plea and plea, and between stroke and stroke.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:8

**Q3. Where shall you arise and go up regarding these matters of controversy?**  
**Answer:** Unto the place which the Lord thy God shall choose.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:8

---

### Deuteronomy 17:9

**Q1. Unto whom specifically shall you come with your matters of controversy?**  
**Answer:** Unto the priests the Levites, and unto the judge that shall be in those days.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:9

**Q2. To whom shall you enquire when you go to these officials?**  
**Answer:** And enquire.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:9

**Q3. What shall these officials show you?**  
**Answer:** And they shall show thee the sentence of judgment.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:9

---

### Deuteronomy 17:10

**Q1. According to what standard shall you do upon the sentence which they show you?**  
**Answer:** According to the sentence of the law which they shall teach thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:10

**Q2. According to what standard shall you do regarding the judgment which they tell you?**  
**Answer:** And according to the judgment which they shall tell thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:10

**Q3. From what place shall you not decline, to the right hand nor to the left?**  
**Answer:** From the sentence which they shall show thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:10

---

### Deuteronomy 17:11

**Q1. According to the sentence of what law shall they teach you?**  
**Answer:** The law which they shall teach thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:11

**Q2. According to what judgment shall they tell you?**  
**Answer:** The judgment which they shall tell thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:11

**Q3. What must you not decline from, to the right hand nor to the left?**  
**Answer:** From the sentence which they shall show thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:11

---

### Deuteronomy 17:12

**Q1. What will the man do who acts presumptuously?**  
**Answer:** And the man that will do presumptuously.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:12

**Q2. Whose ministering presence will this presumptuous man refuse to hearken unto?**  
**Answer:** Unto the priest that standeth to minister there before the Lord thy God, or unto the judge.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:12

**Q3. What must happen to that man who acts presumptuously?**  
**Answer:** Even that man shall die.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:12

---

### Deuteronomy 17:13

**Q1. What will all the people do when they hear of this punishment?**  
**Answer:** And all the people shall hear, and fear.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:13

**Q2. What will the people no longer do after hearing and fearing?**  
**Answer:** And do no more presumptuously.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:13

**Q3. What attitude will the people have regarding doing presumptuously again?**  
**Answer:** And fear, and do no more presumptuously.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:13

---

### Deuteronomy 17:14

**Q1. Into what land shall you come, which the Lord thy God giveth thee?**  
**Answer:** When thou art come unto the land which the Lord thy God giveth thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:14

**Q2. What will you possess and dwell in within that land?**  
**Answer:** And shalt possess it, and shalt dwell therein.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:14

**Q3. What statement will you say regarding a king?**  
**Answer:** I will set a king over me, like as all the nations that are about me.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:14

---

### Deuteronomy 17:15

**Q1. Whom shall you in any wise set king over you?**  
**Answer:** Him whom the Lord thy God shall choose.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:15

**Q2. From among whom must this king be chosen?**  
**Answer:** From among thy brethren shalt thou set king over thee.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:15

**Q3. Who is not permitted to be set as king over you?**  
**Answer:** Thou mayest not set a stranger over thee, which is not thy brother.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:15

---

### Deuteronomy 17:16

**Q1. What must the king not multiply unto himself?**  
**Answer:** Horses to himself.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:16

**Q2. To what place must the king not cause the people to return, to multiply horses?**  
**Answer:** Nor cause the people to return to Egypt, to the end that he should multiply horses.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:16

---

### Deuteronomy 17:17

**Q1. What else must the king not multiply unto himself?**  
**Answer:** Neither shall he multiply wives to himself, that his heart turn not away.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:17

**Q2. What else must the king not greatly multiply unto himself?**  
**Answer:** Neither shall he greatly multiply to himself silver and gold.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:17

**Q3. Why must he not multiply wives to himself?**  
**Answer:** That his heart turn not away.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:17

### Deuteronomy 17:18

**Q1. What shall he write upon a book when he sitteth upon the throne of his kingdom?**  
**Answer:** He shall write him a copy of this law in a book out of that which is before the priests the Levites.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:18

**Q2. Out of what source shall he write a copy of this law?**  
**Answer:** Out of that which is before the priests the Levites.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:18

---

### Deuteronomy 17:19

**Q1. With the king all the days of his life, where shall the book be?**  
**Answer:** And it shall be with him, and he shall read therein all the days of his life.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:19

**Q2. Why shall he read in the book all the days of his life?**  
**Answer:** That he may learn to fear the Lord his God.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:19

**Q3. What must he keep to do, according to the words of this law and these statutes?**  
**Answer:** To keep all the words of this law and these statutes, to do them.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:19

---

### Deuteronomy 17:20

**Q1. What must his heart not do above his brethren?**  
**Answer:** That his heart be not lifted up above his brethren.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:20

**Q2. From what part of the commandment must he not turn aside?**  
**Answer:** To the right hand, or to the left.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:20

**Q3. What will this ensure regarding his kingdom in Israel?**  
**Answer:** To the end that he may prolong his days in his kingdom, he, and his children, in the midst of Israel.  
**Verse:** Deuteronomy 17:20
