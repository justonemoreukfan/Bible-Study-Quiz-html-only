# Micah 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Micah 6:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Micah 6:1?**  
**Answer:** Hear ye now what the LORD saith.  
**Verse:** Micah 6:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Micah 6:1?**  
**Answer:** Arise, contend thou before the mountains, and let the hills hear thy voice.  
**Verse:** Micah 6:1

**Q3. According to Micah 6:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Hear ye now what the LORD saith; Arise, contend thou before the mountains, and let the hills hear thy voice.  
**Verse:** Micah 6:1
