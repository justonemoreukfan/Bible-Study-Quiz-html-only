# Micah 7 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Micah 7:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Micah 7:1?**  
**Answer:** Woe is me! for I am as when they have gathered the summer fruits, as the grapegleanings of the vintage.  
**Verse:** Micah 7:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Micah 7:1?**  
**Answer:** there is no cluster to eat.  
**Verse:** Micah 7:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Micah 7:1?**  
**Answer:** my soul desired the firstripe fruit.  
**Verse:** Micah 7:1
