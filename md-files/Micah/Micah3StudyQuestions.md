# Micah 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Micah 3:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Micah 3:1?**  
**Answer:** And I said, Hear, I pray you, O heads of Jacob, and ye princes of the house of Israel.  
**Verse:** Micah 3:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Micah 3:1?**  
**Answer:** Is it not for you to know judgment?  
**Verse:** Micah 3:1

**Q3. According to Micah 3:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And I said, Hear, I pray you, O heads of Jacob, and ye princes of the house of Israel; Is it not for you to know judgment?  
**Verse:** Micah 3:1

### Micah 3:6
**Q16. What is stated in the opening of Micah 3:6?**  
**Answer:** Therefore night shall be unto you, that ye shall not have a vision.  
**Verse:** Micah 3:6

**Q17. What is declared next in Micah 3:6?**  
**Answer:** and it shall be dark unto you, that ye shall not divine.  
**Verse:** Micah 3:6

**Q18. What further truth is recorded in Micah 3:6?**  
**Answer:** and the sun shall go down over the prophets, and the day shall be dark over them.  
**Verse:** Micah 3:6
