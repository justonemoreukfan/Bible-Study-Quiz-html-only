# Micah 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Micah 5:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Micah 5:1?**  
**Answer:** Now gather thyself in troops, O daughter of troops.  
**Verse:** Micah 5:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Micah 5:1?**  
**Answer:** he hath laid siege against us.  
**Verse:** Micah 5:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Micah 5:1?**  
**Answer:** they shall smite the judge of Israel with a rod upon the cheek.  
**Verse:** Micah 5:1

### Micah 5:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Micah 5:2?**  
**Answer:** But thou, Bethlehem Ephratah, though thou be little among the thousands of Judah, yet out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel.  
**Verse:** Micah 5:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Micah 5:2?**  
**Answer:** whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting.  
**Verse:** Micah 5:2

**Q6. According to Micah 5:2, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** But thou, Bethlehem Ephratah, though thou be little among the thousands of Judah, yet out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel; whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting.  
**Verse:** Micah 5:2
