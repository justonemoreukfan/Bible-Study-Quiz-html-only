# Micah 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Micah 2:1
**Q1. According to Micah 2:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Woe to them that devise iniquity.  
**Verse:** Micah 2:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Micah 2:1?**  
**Answer:** and work evil upon their beds! when the morning is light.  
**Verse:** Micah 2:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Micah 2:1?**  
**Answer:** they practise it, because it is in the power of their hand.  
**Verse:** Micah 2:1

---

### Micah 2:2
**Q4. What is stated in the opening of Micah 2:2?**  
**Answer:** And they covet fields, and take them by violence.  
**Verse:** Micah 2:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Micah 2:2?**  
**Answer:** and houses, and take them away.  
**Verse:** Micah 2:2

**Q6. What further truth is recorded in Micah 2:2?**  
**Answer:** so they oppress a man and his house, even a man and his heritage.  
**Verse:** Micah 2:2

### Micah 2:3
**Q7. What is stated in the opening of Micah 2:3?**  
**Answer:** Therefore thus saith the LORD.  
**Verse:** Micah 2:3

**Q8. What is declared next in Micah 2:3?**  
**Answer:** Behold, against this family do I devise an evil, from which ye shall not remove your necks.  
**Verse:** Micah 2:3

**Q9. What further truth is recorded in Micah 2:3?**  
**Answer:** neither shall ye go haughtily; for this time is evil.  
**Verse:** Micah 2:3

### Micah 2:4
**Q10. What is stated in the opening of Micah 2:4?**  
**Answer:** In that day shall one take up a parable against you, and lament with a doleful lamentation, and say, We be utterly spoiled.  
**Verse:** Micah 2:4

**Q11. What is declared next in Micah 2:4?**

**Verse:** Micah 2:4

**Q12. What further truth is recorded in Micah 2:4?**

**Verse:** Micah 2:4

### Micah 2:6
**Q16. What is stated in the first clause of Micah 2:6?**  
**Answer:** Prophesy ye not, say they to them that prophesy.  
**Verse:** Micah 2:6

**Q17. What additional detail is declared in Micah 2:6?**  
**Answer:** they shall not prophesy to them, that they shall not take shame.  
**Verse:** Micah 2:6

**Q18. According to Micah 2:6, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Prophesy ye not, say they to them that prophesy: they shall not prophesy to them, that they shall not take shame.  
**Verse:** Micah 2:6
