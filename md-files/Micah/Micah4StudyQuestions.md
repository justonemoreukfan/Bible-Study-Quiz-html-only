# Micah 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Micah 4:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Micah 4:1?**  
**Answer:** But in the last days it shall come to pass, that the mountain of the house of the LORD shall be established in the top of the mountains, and it shall be exalted above the hills.  
**Verse:** Micah 4:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Micah 4:1?**  
**Answer:** and people shall flow unto it.  
**Verse:** Micah 4:1

**Q3. According to Micah 4:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** But in the last days it shall come to pass, that the mountain of the house of the LORD shall be established in the top of the mountains, and it shall be exalted above the hills; and people shall flow unto it.  
**Verse:** Micah 4:1

---

### Micah 4:2
**Q4. What is stated in the opening of Micah 4:2?**  
**Answer:** And many nations shall come, and say, Come, and let us go up to the mountain of the LORD, and to the house of the God of Jacob.  
**Verse:** Micah 4:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Micah 4:2?**  
**Answer:** and he will teach us of his ways, and we will walk in his paths.  
**Verse:** Micah 4:2

**Q6. What further truth is recorded in Micah 4:2?**  
**Answer:** for the law shall go forth of Zion, and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Micah 4:2

### Micah 4:5
**Q13. What does the first part of Micah 4:5 state?**  
**Answer:** For all people will walk every one in the name of his god.  
**Verse:** Micah 4:5

**Q14. What does the second part of Micah 4:5 declare?**  
**Answer:** and we will walk in the name of the LORD our God for ever and ever.  
**Verse:** Micah 4:5

**Q15. What is recorded in Micah 4:5?**  
**Answer:** For all people will walk every one in the name of his god, and we will walk in the name of the LORD our God for ever and ever.  
**Verse:** Micah 4:5
