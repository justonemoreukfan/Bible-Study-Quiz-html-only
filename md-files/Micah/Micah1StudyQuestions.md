# Micah 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Micah 1:1
**Q1. According to Micah 1:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** The word of the LORD that came to Micah the Morasthite in the days of Jotham.  
**Verse:** Micah 1:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Micah 1:1?**  
**Answer:** and Hezekiah.  
**Verse:** Micah 1:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Micah 1:1?**  
**Answer:** kings of Judah, which he saw concerning Samaria and Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Micah 1:1

### Micah 1:6

**Verse:** Micah 1:6

**Q17. What additional detail is declared in Micah 1:6?**  
**Answer:** and I will pour down the stones thereof into the valley, and I will discover the foundations thereof.  
**Verse:** Micah 1:6

**Q18. According to Micah 1:6, what is the full truth recorded?**
