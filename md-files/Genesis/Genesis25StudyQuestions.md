# Genesis 25 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 25:1

**Q1. Whom did Abraham take as wife?**  
**Answer:** Keturah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 25:1  

**Q2. Who was Keturah?**  
**Answer:** Abraham's wife.  
**Verse:** Genesis 25:1  

**Q3. What did Abraham do after Sarah's death?**  
**Answer:** Took a wife.  
**Verse:** Genesis 25:1
