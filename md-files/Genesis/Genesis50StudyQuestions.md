# Genesis 50 Study Questions (KJV)

### Genesis 50:1

**Q1. What did Joseph do when his father died?**  
**Answer:** He fell upon his father's face, and kissed him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 50:1

**Q2. Upon whose face did Joseph fall?**  
**Answer:** His father's.  
**Verse:** Genesis 50:1

**Q3. What action did Joseph take toward his father?**  
**Answer:** He kissed him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 50:1
