### Genesis 32:1

**Q1. Who came up to meet Jacob?**  
**Answer:** The angels of God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 32:1

**Q2. What happened when the angels of God met Jacob?**  
**Answer:** Jacob saw the angels of God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 32:1

**Q3. Where did this meeting take place?**  
**Answer:** As Jacob passed through.  
**Verse:** Genesis 32:1
