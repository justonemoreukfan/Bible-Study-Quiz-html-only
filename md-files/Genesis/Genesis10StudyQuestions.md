# Genesis 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 10:1
**Q1. Whose generations are recorded?**  
**Answer:** The sons of Noah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 10:1

**Q2. Who were Noah's sons?**  
**Answer:** Shem, Ham, and Japheth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 10:1

**Q3. When were their generations recorded?**  
**Answer:** After the flood.  
**Verse:** Genesis 10:1
