# Genesis 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 1:1
**Q1. Who created the heaven and the earth?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 1:1

**Q2. What did God create?**  
**Answer:** The heaven and the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 1:1

**Q3. When did God create them?**  
**Answer:** In the beginning.  
**Verse:** Genesis 1:1
