# Genesis 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 2:1
**Q1. What were finished?**  
**Answer:** The heavens and the earth, and all the host of them.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:1

**Q2. What was finished with them?**  
**Answer:** All the host of them.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:1

**Q3. Who finished them?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:1

---

### Genesis 2:2
**Q4. On what day did God end his work?**  
**Answer:** The seventh day.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:2

**Q5. What did God do on the seventh day?**  
**Answer:** He rested from all his work.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:2

**Q6. What did God finish?**  
**Answer:** His work which he had made.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:2

---

### Genesis 2:3
**Q7. What did God bless?**  
**Answer:** The seventh day.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:3

**Q8. What did God sanctify?**  
**Answer:** The seventh day.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:3

**Q9. Why did God bless and sanctify it?**  
**Answer:** Because he had rested from all his work.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:3

---

### Genesis 2:4
**Q10. What are the generations of?**  
**Answer:** The heavens and the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:4

**Q11. When were they created?**  
**Answer:** In the day that the LORD God made the earth and the heavens.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:4

**Q12. Who made the earth and the heavens?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:4

---

### Genesis 2:5
**Q13. What had not yet grown?**  
**Answer:** Every plant of the field.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:5

**Q14. What had not yet sprung up?**  
**Answer:** Every herb of the field.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:5

**Q15. Why had they not grown?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God had not caused it to rain.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:5

---

### Genesis 2:6
**Q16. What went up from the earth?**  
**Answer:** A mist.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:6

**Q17. What did the mist do?**  
**Answer:** It watered the whole face of the ground.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:6

**Q18. What did the mist water?**  
**Answer:** The whole face of the ground.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:6

---

### Genesis 2:7
**Q19. How was man formed?**  
**Answer:** Of the dust of the ground.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:7

**Q20. What did God breathe into his nostrils?**  
**Answer:** The breath of life.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:7

**Q21. What did man become?**  
**Answer:** A living soul.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:7

---

### Genesis 2:8
**Q22. Where did the LORD God plant a garden?**  
**Answer:** Eastward in Eden.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:8

**Q23. Whom did God put there?**  
**Answer:** The man whom he had formed.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:8

**Q24. What garden did God plant?**  
**Answer:** A garden eastward in Eden.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:8

---

### Genesis 2:9
**Q25. What did the LORD God make to grow?**  
**Answer:** Every tree pleasant to the sight and good for food.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:9

**Q26. What was in the midst of the garden?**  
**Answer:** The tree of life and the tree of knowledge of good and evil.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:9

**Q27. Which tree was in the midst of the garden?**  
**Answer:** The tree of life.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:9

---

### Genesis 2:10
**Q28. What went out of Eden?**  
**Answer:** A river.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:10

**Q29. What did the river do?**  
**Answer:** It watered the garden.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:10

**Q30. Into what did it become divided?**  
**Answer:** Four heads.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:10

---

### Genesis 2:11
**Q31. What was the name of the first river?**  
**Answer:** Pison.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:11

**Q32. Where did Pison compass?**  
**Answer:** The whole land of Havilah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:11

**Q33. What land did it compass?**  
**Answer:** Havilah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:11

---

### Genesis 2:12
**Q34. What is good in the land of Havilah?**  
**Answer:** The gold.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:12

**Q35. What is there besides gold?**  
**Answer:** Bdellium and the onyx stone.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:12

**Q36. What kind of stone is named?**  
**Answer:** The onyx stone.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:12

---

### Genesis 2:13
**Q37. What was the name of the second river?**  
**Answer:** Gihon.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:13

**Q38. Where did Gihon compass?**  
**Answer:** The whole land of Ethiopia.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:13

**Q39. What land did Gihon compass?**  
**Answer:** Ethiopia.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:13

---

### Genesis 2:14
**Q40. What was the name of the third river?**  
**Answer:** Hiddekel.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:14

**Q41. Where did Hiddekel go?**  
**Answer:** Toward the east of Assyria.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:14

**Q42. What was the fourth river?**  
**Answer:** Euphrates.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:14

---

### Genesis 2:15
**Q43. Where did the LORD God put the man?**  
**Answer:** Into the garden of Eden.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:15

**Q44. Why was he put there?**  
**Answer:** To dress it and to keep it.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:15

**Q45. What was man to keep?**  
**Answer:** The garden.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:15

---

### Genesis 2:16
**Q46. Of what could the man freely eat?**  
**Answer:** Every tree of the garden.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:16

**Q47. What did God command the man?**  
**Answer:** Thou mayest freely eat.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:16

**Q48. Who gave the command?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:16

---

### Genesis 2:17
**Q49. Of what tree might man not eat?**  
**Answer:** The tree of the knowledge of good and evil.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:17

**Q50. What would happen in the day he ate?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt surely die.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:17

**Q51. Who gave this command?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 2:17

**Q53. What would God make for him?**

**Verse:** Genesis 2:18

**Q56. To whom were they brought?**
