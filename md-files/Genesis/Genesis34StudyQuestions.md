# Genesis 34 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 34:1

**Q1. Who was Dinah, the daughter of Leah?**  
**Answer:** Leah bare Jacob a daughter Dinah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 34:1

**Q2. What did Dinah do?**  
**Answer:** She went out to see the daughters of the land.  
**Verse:** Genesis 34:1

**Q3. Whom did Dinah go to see?**  
**Answer:** The daughters of the land.  
**Verse:** Genesis 34:1

---

### Genesis 34:2

**Q4. Who saw Dinah?**  
**Answer:** Shechem the son of Hamor.  
**Verse:** Genesis 34:2

**Q5. Who was Shechem?**  
**Answer:** The son of Hamor.  
**Verse:** Genesis 34:2

**Q6. What did Shechem do when he saw Dinah?**  
**Answer:** He took her, and lay with her.  
**Verse:** Genesis 34:2
