# Genesis 11 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 11:1
**Q1. What was of one language?**  
**Answer:** The whole earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 11:1

**Q2. How many languages did the earth have?**  
**Answer:** One language.  
**Verse:** Genesis 11:1

**Q3. What did all the earth have?**  
**Answer:** One speech.  
**Verse:** Genesis 11:1
