# Genesis 37 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 37:1

**Q1. Where did Jacob dwell?**  
**Answer:** In the land of Canaan.  
**Verse:** Genesis 37:1

**Q2. Whose father was Jacob?**  
**Answer:** Isaac's.  
**Verse:** Genesis 37:1

**Q3. What did Jacob do in the land of Canaan?**  
**Answer:** Dwelt.  
**Verse:** Genesis 37:1

---

### Genesis 37:2

**Q4. Who was Jacob?**  
**Answer:** The father of twelve sons.  
**Verse:** Genesis 37:2

**Q5. Who told his father Joseph's evil report?**  
**Answer:** Joseph.  
**Verse:** Genesis 37:2

**Q6. What did Joseph bring to his father?**  
**Answer:** An evil report of his brethren.  
**Verse:** Genesis 37:2

---

### Genesis 37:3

**Q7. Whom did Israel love more than all his children?**  
**Answer:** Joseph.  
**Verse:** Genesis 37:3

**Q8. Why did Israel love Joseph more?**  
**Answer:** Because he was the son of his old age.  
**Verse:** Genesis 37:3

**Q9. What did Israel make for Joseph?**  
**Answer:** A coat of many colours.  
**Verse:** Genesis 37:3

---

### Genesis 37:4

**Q10. What did Joseph's brethren see?**  
**Answer:** That their father loved him more than all their brethren.  
**Verse:** Genesis 37:4

**Q11. How did the brethren feel toward Joseph?**  
**Answer:** They hated him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 37:4

**Q12. Could the brethren speak peaceably to him?**  
**Answer:** They could not speak peaceably to him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 37:4

---

### Genesis 37:5

**Q13. What happened to Joseph in a dream?**  
**Answer:** He dreamed a dream.  
**Verse:** Genesis 37:5

**Q14. To whom did Joseph tell his dream?**  
**Answer:** To his brethren.  
**Verse:** Genesis 37:5

**Q15. What did Joseph do after telling his dream?**  
**Answer:** His brethren hated him yet more.  
**Verse:** Genesis 37:5

### Genesis 37:6

**Q16. What was the first part of Joseph's dream?**  
**Answer:** We were binding sheaves in the field.  
**Verse:** Genesis 37:6

**Q17. What happened to Joseph's sheaf?**  
**Answer:** My sheaf stood up.  
**Verse:** Genesis 37:6

**Q18. What did the other sheaves do?**

**Verse:** Genesis 37:6

**Answer:** Joseph.  
**Verse:** Genesis 37:7
