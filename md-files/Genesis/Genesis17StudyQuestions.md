# Genesis 17 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 17:1
**Q1. How old was Abram?**  
**Answer:** Ninety years old and nine.  
**Verse:** Genesis 17:1

**Q2. Who appeared to Abram?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 17:1

**Q3. What did God tell Abram to do?**  
**Answer:** Walk before him and be thou perfect.  
**Verse:** Genesis 17:1

---

### Genesis 17:2
**Q4. What would God make between him and Abram?**

**Verse:** Genesis 17:2

**Verse:** Genesis 17:2

**Q6. Who promised the covenant?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 17:2

### Genesis 17:10
**Q28. What covenant was to be kept?**  
**Answer:** God's covenant.  
**Verse:** Genesis 17:10

**Q29. Who was to be circumcised?**  
**Answer:** Every man child among them.  
**Verse:** Genesis 17:10

**Q30. What was the token of the covenant?**  
**Answer:** Circumcision.  
**Verse:** Genesis 17:10

---

### Genesis 17:11
**Q31. What were they to circumcise?**  
**Answer:** The flesh of their foreskin.  
**Verse:** Genesis 17:11

**Q32. What would circumcision be?**  
**Answer:** A token of the covenant between God and them.  
**Verse:** Genesis 17:11

**Q33. Between whom was the token?**  
**Answer:** God and Abraham's seed.  
**Verse:** Genesis 17:11

---

### Genesis 17:12
**Q34. When was a man child to be circumcised?**  
**Answer:** When he was eight days old.  
**Verse:** Genesis 17:12

**Q35. Who was to be circumcised?**  
**Answer:** Every man child in their generations.  
**Verse:** Genesis 17:12

**Q36. Who else was to be circumcised?**  
**Answer:** He that is bought with money of any stranger.  
**Verse:** Genesis 17:12
