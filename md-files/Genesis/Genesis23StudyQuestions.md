# Genesis 23 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 23:1
**Q1. How old was Sarah when she died?**  
**Answer:** An hundred and seven and twenty years old.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:1  

**Q2. Who was Sarah?**  
**Answer:** The wife of Abraham.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:1  

**Q3. Where did Sarah die?**  
**Answer:** In Kirjath-arba.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:1  

---

### Genesis 23:2
**Q4. Where did Sarah die?**  
**Answer:** In Kirjath-arba; the same is Hebron.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:2  

**Q5. In what land was Hebron?**  
**Answer:** In the land of Canaan.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:2  

**Q6. What did Abraham do for Sarah?**  
**Answer:** Mourned for her, and to weep for her.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:2  

---

### Genesis 23:3
**Q7. From whom did Abraham come?**  
**Answer:** From before his dead.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:3  

**Q8. To whom did Abraham speak?**  
**Answer:** Unto the sons of Heth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:3  

**Q9. What had died?**  
**Answer:** His dead.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:3  

---

### Genesis 23:4
**Q10. What did Abraham call himself?**  
**Answer:** A stranger and a sojourner.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:4  

**Q11. Among whom did Abraham dwell?**  
**Answer:** Among the sons of Heth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:4  

**Q12. What did Abraham ask to buy?**  
**Answer:** A possession of a buryingplace.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:4  

---

### Genesis 23:5
**Q13. Who answered Abraham?**  
**Answer:** The children of Heth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:5  

**Q14. What did they say Abraham was among them?**  
**Answer:** A mighty prince.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:5  

**Q15. What did they offer Abraham?**  
**Answer:** The choice of their sepulchres.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:5  

---

### Genesis 23:6
**Q16. What did the children of Heth call Abraham?**  
**Answer:** A mighty prince.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:6  

**Q17. Where was Abraham to bury his dead?**  
**Answer:** In the choice of their sepulchres.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:6  

**Q18. Who would withhold a sepulchre from Abraham?**  
**Answer:** None of them.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:6  

---

### Genesis 23:7
**Q19. What did Abraham do before the people?**  
**Answer:** Bowed himself down.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:7  

**Q20. Before whom did Abraham bow?**  
**Answer:** Before the people of the land.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:7  

**Q21. Who were present before Abraham?**  
**Answer:** The people of the land.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:7  

---

### Genesis 23:8
**Q22. With whom did Abraham commune?**  
**Answer:** With the children of Heth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:8  

**Q23. What did Abraham ask them to hear?**  
**Answer:** If it be your mind that I should bury my dead out of my sight.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:8  

**Q24. Whom did Abraham ask them to entreat?**  
**Answer:** Ephron the son of Zohar.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:8  

---

### Genesis 23:9
**Q25. Whose cave did Abraham request?**  
**Answer:** The cave of Machpelah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:9  

**Q26. Where was the cave of Machpelah?**  
**Answer:** In the end of his field.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:9  

**Q27. What did Abraham offer to pay?**  
**Answer:** The full price.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:9  

---

### Genesis 23:10
**Q28. Where did Ephron sit?**  
**Answer:** Among the children of Heth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:10  

**Q29. Who heard Ephron's answer?**  
**Answer:** All that went in at the gate of his city.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:10  

**Q30. What did Ephron answer Abraham?**  
**Answer:** In the audience of the children of Heth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:10  

---

### Genesis 23:11
**Q31. What did Ephron offer Abraham?**  
**Answer:** The field, and the cave that is therein.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:11  

**Q32. In whose presence did Ephron give the field?**  
**Answer:** In the presence of the sons of his people.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:11  

**Q33. What did Ephron say Abraham could do?**  
**Answer:** Bury his dead.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:11  

---

### Genesis 23:12
**Q34. What did Abraham do before the people?**  
**Answer:** Bowed down himself.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:12  

**Q35. Before whom did Abraham bow down?**  
**Answer:** Before the people of the land.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:12  

**Q36. What did Abraham do after Ephron spoke?**  
**Answer:** Bowed down himself.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:12  

---

### Genesis 23:13
**Q37. To whom did Abraham speak?**  
**Answer:** Unto Ephron.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:13  

**Q38. What did Abraham ask Ephron to hear?**  
**Answer:** If thou wilt give it, I pray thee, hear me.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:13  

**Q39. What did Abraham say he would give Ephron?**  
**Answer:** Money for the field.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:13  

---

### Genesis 23:14
**Q40. What did Ephron answer Abraham?**  
**Answer:** Saying unto him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:14  

**Q41. To whom did Ephron give his answer?**  
**Answer:** Unto Abraham.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:14  

**Q42. What was Ephron doing in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Answering Abraham.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:14  

---

### Genesis 23:15
**Q43. How much was the land worth?**  
**Answer:** Four hundred shekels of silver.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:15  

**Q44. What did Ephron say was between him and Abraham?**  
**Answer:** What is that betwixt me and thee?  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:15  

**Q45. What did Ephron tell Abraham to do?**  
**Answer:** Bury therefore thy dead.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:15  

---

### Genesis 23:16
**Q46. Who hearkened unto Ephron?**  
**Answer:** Abraham.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:16  

**Q47. What did Abraham weigh to Ephron?**  
**Answer:** The silver.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:16  

**Q48. How much silver did Abraham weigh?**  
**Answer:** Four hundred shekels of silver.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:16  

---

### Genesis 23:17
**Q49. Whose field was made sure?**  
**Answer:** Ephron's field.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:17  

**Q50. Where was the field?**  
**Answer:** In Machpelah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:17  

**Q51. What else was made sure besides the field?**  
**Answer:** The cave and all the trees in the field.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:17  

---

### Genesis 23:18
**Q52. Before whom was the field made sure?**  
**Answer:** Before all the people of the city.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:18  

**Q53. Where were the people gathered?**  
**Answer:** In the gate of his city.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:18  

**Q54. What was made sure to Abraham?**  
**Answer:** The field and the cave therein.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:18  

---

### Genesis 23:19
**Q55. Where did Abraham bury Sarah?**  
**Answer:** In the cave of the field of Machpelah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:19  

**Q56. Where was Machpelah?**  
**Answer:** Before Mamre.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:19  

**Q57. What is Mamre called?**  
**Answer:** Hebron.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:19  

---

### Genesis 23:20
**Q58. What did the field and the cave become?**  
**Answer:** Made sure unto Abraham for a possession of a buryingplace.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:20  

**Q59. Who made the field and cave sure?**  
**Answer:** The sons of Heth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:20  

**Q60. For what purpose was the possession made sure?**  
**Answer:** For a buryingplace.  
**Verse:** Genesis 23:20
