# Genesis 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 9:1
**Q1. Whom did God bless?**  
**Answer:** Noah and his sons.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:1

**Q2. What were they told to do?**  
**Answer:** Be fruitful, multiply, and replenish the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:1

**Q3. Who received this command?**  
**Answer:** Noah and his sons.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:1

---

### Genesis 9:2
**Q4. Upon whom would the fear of man be?**  
**Answer:** Every beast, fowl, and creature upon the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:2

**Q5. Into whose hand were creatures delivered?**  
**Answer:** Man's hand.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:2

**Q6. What would fear man?**  
**Answer:** Every beast of the earth and every fowl of the air.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:2

---

### Genesis 9:3
**Q7. What may be meat?**  
**Answer:** Every moving thing that liveth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:3

**Q8. What was given as meat?**  
**Answer:** Every moving thing that liveth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:3

**Q9. What was given besides the green herb?**  
**Answer:** Every moving thing that liveth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:3

---

### Genesis 9:4
**Q10. What must not be eaten?**  
**Answer:** Flesh with the life thereof, which is the blood thereof.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:4

**Q11. What is the life of the flesh?**  
**Answer:** The blood thereof.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:4

**Q12. With what must flesh not be eaten?**  
**Answer:** The blood thereof.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:4

---

### Genesis 9:5
**Q13. Whose blood would God require?**  
**Answer:** Man's blood.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:5

**Q14. At whose hand would God require it?**  
**Answer:** At the hand of every beast and man.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:5

**Q15. What would God require?**  
**Answer:** The life of man.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:5

---

### Genesis 9:6
**Q16. What happens to one who sheds man's blood?**  
**Answer:** By man shall his blood be shed.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:6

**Q17. In whose image was man made?**  
**Answer:** In the image of God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:6

**Q18. What is the reason given for shedding man's blood?**  
**Answer:** God made man in his own image.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:6

---

### Genesis 9:7
**Q19. What were Noah and his sons told again?**  
**Answer:** Be fruitful, multiply, and bring forth abundantly.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:7

**Q20. What were they to multiply in?**  
**Answer:** The earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:7

**Q21. What were they to do with the earth?**  
**Answer:** Replenish it.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:7

---

### Genesis 9:8
**Q22. Who spake unto Noah and his sons?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:8

**Q23. To whom did God speak?**  
**Answer:** Noah and his sons with him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:8

**Q24. What did God do?**  
**Answer:** God spake unto Noah and his sons.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:8

---

### Genesis 9:9

**Answer:** Noah, his sons, and their seed after them.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:9

**Q26. What would God establish?**  
**Answer:** His covenant.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:9

**Q27. Whose seed was included?**  
**Answer:** Their seed after them.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:9

### Genesis 9:10
**Q28. With whom else was the covenant made?**

**Verse:** Genesis 9:10

**Q29. What creatures were named?**  
**Answer:** Fowl, cattle, and every beast of the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:10

**Q30. What was included besides Noah's family?**  
**Answer:** Every beast of the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:10

---

### Genesis 9:11
**Q31. What would God establish?**  
**Answer:** His covenant.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:11

**Q32. What would never again be destroyed by a flood?**  
**Answer:** All flesh.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:11

**Q33. What would never again be a flood?**  
**Answer:** A flood to destroy the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:11

---

### Genesis 9:12
**Q34. What token did God give?**  
**Answer:** The token of the covenant.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:12

**Q35. Between whom was the token?**  
**Answer:** God and every living creature.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:12

**Q36. For how many generations was the token?**  
**Answer:** Perpetual generations.  
**Verse:** Genesis 9:12

**Verse:** Genesis 9:13
