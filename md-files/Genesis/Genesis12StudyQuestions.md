# Genesis 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 12:1
**Q1. What did the LORD say to Abram?**  
**Answer:** Get thee out of thy country, kindred, and father's house.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:1

**Q2. Where was Abram to go?**  
**Answer:** Unto a land that God would shew him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:1

**Q3. Whose house was Abram to leave?**  
**Answer:** His father's house.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:1

---

### Genesis 12:2
**Q4. What would God make of Abram?**  
**Answer:** A great nation.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:2

**Q5. What would God do to Abram's name?**  
**Answer:** Make it great.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:2

**Q6. What would Abram be?**  
**Answer:** A blessing.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:2

---

### Genesis 12:3
**Q7. Whom would God bless?**  
**Answer:** Them that bless Abram.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:3

**Q8. Whom would God curse?**  
**Answer:** Him that curseth Abram.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:3

**Q9. In whom would all families of the earth be blessed?**  
**Answer:** Abram.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:3

---

### Genesis 12:4
**Q10. How old was Abram when he departed?**  
**Answer:** Seventy and five years old.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:4

**Q11. Who departed as the LORD had spoken?**  
**Answer:** Abram.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:4

**Q12. Who went with Abram?**  
**Answer:** Lot.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:4

---

### Genesis 12:5
**Q13. Whom did Abram take?**  
**Answer:** Sarai his wife, Lot his brother's son, and all their substance and souls.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:5

**Q14. Where did they go?**  
**Answer:** Into the land of Canaan.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:5

**Q15. What did they do in Canaan?**  
**Answer:** Into the land of Canaan came they.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:5

---

### Genesis 12:6
**Q16. Where did Abram pass through the land?**  
**Answer:** Unto the place of Sichem, unto the plain of Moreh.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:6

**Q17. Who was then in the land?**  
**Answer:** The Canaanite.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:6

**Q18. Who was in the land when Abram passed through?**  
**Answer:** The Canaanite.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:6

---

### Genesis 12:7
**Q19. Who appeared unto Abram?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:7

**Q20. What did the LORD promise?**  
**Answer:** Unto Abram's seed he would give this land.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:7

**Q21. What did Abram build?**  
**Answer:** An altar unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:7

---

### Genesis 12:8
**Q22. Where did Abram remove?**  
**Answer:** Unto a mountain on the east of Beth-el.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:8

**Q23. What did Abram pitch?**  
**Answer:** His tent.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:8

**Q24. What did Abram call upon?**  
**Answer:** The name of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:8

---

### Genesis 12:9
**Q25. Where did Abram journey?**  
**Answer:** Going on still toward the south.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:9

**Q26. In what direction did Abram go?**  
**Answer:** Toward the south.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:9

**Q27. Who journeyed?**  
**Answer:** Abram.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:9

---

### Genesis 12:10
**Q28. What was in the land?**  
**Answer:** A famine.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:10

**Q29. Where did Abram go?**  
**Answer:** Down into Egypt.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:10

**Q30. Why did Abram go into Egypt?**  
**Answer:** The famine was grievous in the land.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:10

---

### Genesis 12:11
**Q31. What did Abram say to Sarai?**  
**Answer:** Behold now, I know that thou art a fair woman to look upon.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:11

**Q32. What did Abram know about Sarai?**  
**Answer:** She was a fair woman.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:11

**Q33. Who was fair to look upon?**  
**Answer:** Sarai.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:11

---

### Genesis 12:12
**Q34. What would the Egyptians do?**  
**Answer:** They would see Sarai.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:12

**Q35. Whom would the Egyptians kill?**  
**Answer:** Abram.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:12

**Q36. Why would Abram be killed?**  
**Answer:** Because Sarai was his wife.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:12

---

### Genesis 12:13
**Q37. What did Abram ask Sarai to say?**  
**Answer:** I pray thee, thou art my sister.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:13

**Q38. Why did Abram ask this?**  
**Answer:** That it may be well with him for her sake.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:13

**Q39. What did Abram want to save?**  
**Answer:** His life.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:13

---

### Genesis 12:14
**Q40. What did the Egyptians behold?**  
**Answer:** The woman was very fair.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:14

**Q41. Who saw Sarai?**  
**Answer:** The Egyptians.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:14

**Q42. How was Sarai described?**  
**Answer:** Very fair.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:14

---

### Genesis 12:15
**Q43. Who saw Sarai?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh's princes.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:15

**Q44. Before whom was Sarai commended?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:15

**Q45. Where was Sarai taken?**  
**Answer:** Into Pharaoh's house.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:15

---

### Genesis 12:16
**Q46. What did Pharaoh do for Abram?**  
**Answer:** He entreated Abram well.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:16

**Q47. What did Abram receive?**  
**Answer:** Sheep, oxen, he asses, menservants, maidservants, she asses, and camels.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:16

**Q48. Why did Pharaoh entreat Abram well?**  
**Answer:** For Sarai's sake.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:16

---

### Genesis 12:17
**Q49. What did the LORD plague?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh and his house.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:17

**Q50. Why did the LORD plague Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** Because of Sarai, Abram's wife.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:17

**Q51. Who was plagued?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh and his house.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:17

---

### Genesis 12:18
**Q52. Whom did Pharaoh call?**  
**Answer:** Abram.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:18

**Q53. What did Pharaoh ask Abram?**  
**Answer:** What is this that thou hast done unto me?  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:18

**Q54. Why had Abram not told Pharaoh Sarai was his wife?**  
**Answer:** The verse records Pharaoh's question about it.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:18

---

### Genesis 12:19
**Q55. What did Pharaoh ask about Sarai?**  
**Answer:** Why saidst thou, She is my sister?  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:19

**Q56. Whom had Pharaoh taken to be his wife?**  
**Answer:** Sarai.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:19

**Q57. What did Pharaoh tell Abram to do?**  
**Answer:** Behold thy wife, take her, and go thy way.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:19

---

### Genesis 12:20
**Q58. What did Pharaoh command concerning Abram?**  
**Answer:** His men restored him, and they sent him away.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:20

**Q59. Whom did Pharaoh send away?**  
**Answer:** Abram and his wife, with all that he had.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:20

**Q60. What went with Abram?**  
**Answer:** All that he had.  
**Verse:** Genesis 12:20
