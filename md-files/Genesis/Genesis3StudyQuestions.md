# Genesis 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 3:1
**Q1. What was more subtil than any beast?**  
**Answer:** The serpent.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:1

**Q2. To whom did the serpent speak?**  
**Answer:** The woman.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:1

**Q3. What did the serpent ask?**  
**Answer:** Yea, hath God said?  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:1

---

### Genesis 3:2
**Q4. Of what might the woman eat?**  
**Answer:** Of the fruit of the trees of the garden.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:2

**Q5. Who answered the serpent?**  
**Answer:** The woman.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:2

**Q6. Where were the trees?**  
**Answer:** In the garden.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:2

---

### Genesis 3:3
**Q7. Of what tree might they not eat?**  
**Answer:** The fruit of the tree in the midst of the garden.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:3

**Q8. What did God say?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall not eat of it.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:3

**Q9. What would happen if they touched it?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall die.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:3

---

### Genesis 3:4
**Q10. What did the serpent say?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall not surely die.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:4

**Q11. Who would not surely die?**  
**Answer:** The woman.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:4

**Q12. What did the serpent deny?**  
**Answer:** That they would surely die.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:4

---

### Genesis 3:5
**Q13. What would happen when they ate?**  
**Answer:** Their eyes would be opened.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:5

**Q14. Whom would they be as?**  
**Answer:** As gods.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:5

**Q15. What would they know?**  
**Answer:** Good and evil.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:5

---

### Genesis 3:6
**Q16. What did the woman see?**  
**Answer:** The tree was good for food, pleasant to the eyes, and desired to make one wise.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:6

**Q17. What did she take?**  
**Answer:** Of the fruit thereof.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:6

**Q18. To whom did she give it?**  
**Answer:** Her husband with her.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:6

---

### Genesis 3:7
**Q19. What were opened?**  
**Answer:** The eyes of them both.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:7

**Q20. What did they know?**  
**Answer:** That they were naked.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:7

**Q21. What did they make?**  
**Answer:** Aprons of fig leaves.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:7

---

### Genesis 3:8
**Q22. What did Adam and his wife hear?**  
**Answer:** The voice of the LORD God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:8

**Q23. Where did they hide?**  
**Answer:** Among the trees of the garden.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:8

**Q24. When did they hear the voice?**  
**Answer:** In the cool of the day.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:8

---

### Genesis 3:9
**Q25. Whom did the LORD God call?**  
**Answer:** Adam.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:9

**Q26. What did God ask?**  
**Answer:** Where art thou?  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:9

**Q27. Who called Adam?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:9

---

### Genesis 3:10
**Q28. What had Adam heard?**  
**Answer:** God's voice in the garden.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:10

**Q29. Why was Adam afraid?**  
**Answer:** He was naked.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:10

**Q30. What did Adam do?**  
**Answer:** He hid himself.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:10

---

### Genesis 3:11
**Q31. What did God ask Adam?**  
**Answer:** Who told thee that thou wast naked?  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:11

**Q32. Of what did God ask if Adam had eaten?**  
**Answer:** The tree.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:11

**Q33. What had God commanded?**  
**Answer:** That he should not eat.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:11

---

### Genesis 3:12
**Q34. Whom did Adam blame?**  
**Answer:** The woman.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:12

**Q35. Who gave Adam the fruit?**  
**Answer:** The woman.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:12

**Q36. What did Adam say she gave him?**  
**Answer:** Of the tree, and he did eat.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:12

---

### Genesis 3:13
**Q37. Whom did the woman blame?**  
**Answer:** The serpent.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:13

**Q38. What did the serpent do?**  
**Answer:** Beguiled her.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:13

**Q39. What did the woman eat?**  
**Answer:** The fruit.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:13

---

### Genesis 3:14
**Q40. What was the serpent cursed above?**  
**Answer:** Every beast and cattle.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:14

**Q41. Upon what was it to go?**  
**Answer:** Its belly.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:14

**Q42. What was it to eat?**  
**Answer:** Dust.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:14

---

### Genesis 3:15
**Q43. Between whom would God put enmity?**  
**Answer:** The serpent and the woman.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:15

**Q44. What would bruise the serpent's head?**  
**Answer:** The seed of the woman.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:15

**Q45. What would the serpent bruise?**  
**Answer:** His heel.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:15

---

### Genesis 3:16
**Q46. What would God multiply?**  
**Answer:** The woman's sorrow and conception.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:16

**Q47. To whom would her desire be?**  
**Answer:** Her husband.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:16

**Q48. Who would rule over her?**  
**Answer:** Her husband.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:16

---

### Genesis 3:17
**Q49. Why was the ground cursed?**  
**Answer:** Because Adam hearkened unto his wife's voice.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:17

**Q50. What would Adam eat?**  
**Answer:** The herb of the field.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:17

**Q51. What would he eat all the days of his life?**  
**Answer:** The herb of the field.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:17

---

### Genesis 3:18

**Answer:** Thorns and thistles.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:18

**Q53. What would Adam eat?**  
**Answer:** The herb of the field.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:18

**Q54. What would grow from the ground?**

**Verse:** Genesis 3:18

---

### Genesis 3:19
**Q55. In what would Adam eat bread?**  
**Answer:** The sweat of his face.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:19

**Q56. Where would he return?**  
**Answer:** Unto the ground.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:19

**Q57. Of what was he taken?**  
**Answer:** Dust.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:19

---

### Genesis 3:20
**Q58. What did Adam call his wife's name?**  
**Answer:** Eve.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:20

**Q59. Why was she called Eve?**  
**Answer:** She was the mother of all living.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:20

**Q60. Who was the mother of all living?**

**Verse:** Genesis 3:20

---

### Genesis 3:21
**Q61. What did the LORD God make?**  
**Answer:** Coats of skins.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:21

**Q62. Whom did he clothe?**  
**Answer:** Adam and his wife.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:21

**Q63. Who made the coats?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:21

---

### Genesis 3:22
**Q64. What had man become?**  
**Answer:** As one of us, to know good and evil.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:22

**Q65. What tree might man take from?**  
**Answer:** The tree of life.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:22

**Q66. What might man eat and live for ever?**  
**Answer:** The fruit of the tree of life.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:22

---

### Genesis 3:23
**Q67. From where did God send man?**  
**Answer:** The garden of Eden.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:23

**Q68. Why was man sent forth?**  
**Answer:** To till the ground from whence he was taken.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:23

**Q69. Who sent him forth?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:23

---

### Genesis 3:24
**Q70. What did God place at Eden's east?**  
**Answer:** Cherubims and a flaming sword.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:24

**Q71. What did the sword keep?**  
**Answer:** The way of the tree of life.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:24

**Q72. Where did God place the cherubims?**  
**Answer:** At the east of the garden of Eden.  
**Verse:** Genesis 3:24
