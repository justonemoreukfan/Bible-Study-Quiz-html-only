# Genesis 19 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 19:1
**Q1. Who came to Sodom at even?**  
**Answer:** Two angels.  
**Verse:** Genesis 19:1

**Q2. Where did Lot sit?**  
**Answer:** In the gate of Sodom.  
**Verse:** Genesis 19:1

**Q3. What did Lot do when he saw them?**  
**Answer:** He rose up to meet them and bowed himself.  
**Verse:** Genesis 19:1

---

### Genesis 19:2
**Q4. What did Lot ask the angels to do?**  
**Answer:** Turn in to his house and lodge there.  
**Verse:** Genesis 19:2

**Q5. What did Lot offer to wash?**  
**Answer:** Their feet.  
**Verse:** Genesis 19:2

**Q6. Where did Lot invite them?**  
**Answer:** Into his house.  
**Verse:** Genesis 19:2

---

### Genesis 19:3
**Q7. What did Lot do for the angels?**

**Verse:** Genesis 19:3

**Q8. What did the angels eat?**  
**Answer:** They did eat.  
**Verse:** Genesis 19:3

**Q9. How did Lot persuade them?**  
**Answer:** He pressed upon them greatly.  
**Verse:** Genesis 19:3
