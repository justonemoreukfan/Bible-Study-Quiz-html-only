# Genesis 26 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 26:1

**Q1. What arose in the land?**  
**Answer:** A famine.  
**Verse:** Genesis 26:1  

**Q2. Besides the first famine, what was this?**  
**Answer:** The second famine.  
**Verse:** Genesis 26:1  

**Q3. Where did Isaac go?**  
**Answer:** Unto Abimelech king of the Philistines unto Gerar.  
**Verse:** Genesis 26:1  

---

### Genesis 26:2

**Q4. Who appeared unto Isaac?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 26:2  

**Q5. Where was Isaac told not to go?**  
**Answer:** Down into Egypt.  
**Verse:** Genesis 26:2  

**Q6. Where was Isaac to dwell?**  
**Answer:** In the land which I shall tell thee of.  
**Verse:** Genesis 26:2
