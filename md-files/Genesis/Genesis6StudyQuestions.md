# Genesis 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 6:1
**Q1. What began to multiply?**  
**Answer:** Men.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:1

**Q2. What were born unto them?**  
**Answer:** Daughters.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:1

**Q3. When did daughters appear?**  
**Answer:** When men began to multiply.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:1

---

### Genesis 6:2
**Q4. Who saw the daughters of men?**  
**Answer:** The sons of God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:2

**Q5. What did they take?**  
**Answer:** Wives of all which they chose.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:2

**Q6. Whom did the sons of God take?**  
**Answer:** Wives of all which they chose.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:2

---

### Genesis 6:3
**Q7. What did the LORD say would not always strive with man?**  
**Answer:** His spirit.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:3

**Q8. How long would man's days be?**  
**Answer:** An hundred and twenty years.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:3

**Q9. With whom would God's spirit strive?**  
**Answer:** Man.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:3

---

### Genesis 6:4
**Q10. What were in the earth?**  
**Answer:** Giants.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:4

**Q11. Who were mighty men?**  
**Answer:** Men of renown.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:4

**Q12. When were mighty men born?**  
**Answer:** When the sons of God came in unto the daughters of men.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:4

---

### Genesis 6:5
**Q13. What did God see?**  
**Answer:** The wickedness of man.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:5

**Q14. What was continually evil?**  
**Answer:** Every imagination of the thoughts of man's heart.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:5

**Q15. Where was evil found?**  
**Answer:** In man's heart.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:5

---

### Genesis 6:6
**Q16. What did the LORD repent?**  
**Answer:** That he had made man on the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:6

**Q17. What was grieved?**  
**Answer:** The LORD at his heart.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:6

**Q18. Where was the LORD grieved?**  
**Answer:** At his heart.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:6

---

### Genesis 6:7
**Q19. What did the LORD say he would destroy?**  
**Answer:** Man and beast, creeping thing and fowl of the air.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:7

**Q20. Why would he destroy them?**  
**Answer:** It repenteth me that I have made them.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:7

**Q21. Who found grace?**  
**Answer:** Noah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:7

---

### Genesis 6:8
**Q22. Who found grace in the eyes of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Noah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:8

**Q23. Where did Noah find grace?**  
**Answer:** In the eyes of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:8

**Q24. What did Noah find?**  
**Answer:** Grace.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:8

---

### Genesis 6:9
**Q25. What are the generations of?**  
**Answer:** Noah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:9

**Q26. How did Noah walk?**  
**Answer:** With God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:9

**Q27. What kind of man was Noah?**  
**Answer:** A just man and perfect in his generations.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:9

---

### Genesis 6:10
**Q28. Whom did Noah beget?**  
**Answer:** Shem, Ham, and Japheth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:10

**Q29. How many sons did Noah have?**  
**Answer:** Three.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:10

**Q30. What were their names?**  
**Answer:** Shem, Ham, and Japheth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:10

---

### Genesis 6:11
**Q31. What was corrupt before God?**  
**Answer:** The earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:11

**Q32. What was the earth filled with?**  
**Answer:** Violence.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:11

**Q33. Before whom was the earth corrupt?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:11

---

### Genesis 6:12
**Q34. Who had corrupted his way?**  
**Answer:** All flesh.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:12

**Q35. What did God look upon?**  
**Answer:** The earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:12

**Q36. How had all flesh corrupted its way?**  
**Answer:** Upon the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:12

---

### Genesis 6:13
**Q37. What did God say was come before him?**  
**Answer:** The end of all flesh.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:13

**Q38. Why was the earth filled with violence?**  
**Answer:** Through them.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:13

**Q39. What would God destroy?**  
**Answer:** Them with the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:13

---

### Genesis 6:14
**Q40. What was Noah to make?**  
**Answer:** An ark.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:14

**Q41. Of what wood was it to be made?**  
**Answer:** Gopher wood.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:14

**Q42. With what was the ark to be pitched?**  
**Answer:** Pitch.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:14

---

### Genesis 6:15
**Q43. How long was the ark?**  
**Answer:** Three hundred cubits.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:15

**Q44. How broad was it?**  
**Answer:** Fifty cubits.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:15

**Q45. How high was it?**  
**Answer:** Thirty cubits.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:15

---

### Genesis 6:16
**Q46. What was Noah to make in the ark?**  
**Answer:** A window.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:16

**Q47. Where was the door to be set?**  
**Answer:** In the side of the ark.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:16

**Q48. How many stories was the ark to have?**  
**Answer:** Three.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:16

---

### Genesis 6:17
**Q49. What would God bring upon the earth?**  
**Answer:** A flood of waters.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:17

**Q50. What would be destroyed?**  
**Answer:** All flesh wherein is the breath of life.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:17

**Q51. What would die?**  
**Answer:** Every thing that is in the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:17

---

### Genesis 6:18
**Q52. With whom would God establish his covenant?**  
**Answer:** Noah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:18

**Q53. Who would enter the ark?**  
**Answer:** Noah, his sons, his wife, and his sons' wives.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:18

**Q54. What would God establish?**  
**Answer:** His covenant.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:18

---

### Genesis 6:19
**Q55. What was Noah to bring into the ark?**  
**Answer:** Two of every sort of living thing.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:19

**Q56. How many of every sort?**  
**Answer:** Two.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:19

**Q57. Why were they brought?**  
**Answer:** To keep them alive.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:19

---

### Genesis 6:20
**Q58. What was to come to Noah?**  
**Answer:** Two of every sort.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:20

**Q59. What kinds were named?**  
**Answer:** Fowl, cattle, and creeping thing.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:20

**Q60. Why would they come?**  
**Answer:** To keep them alive.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:20

---

### Genesis 6:21
**Q61. What was Noah to take?**  
**Answer:** All food that is eaten.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:21

**Q62. For whom was food gathered?**  
**Answer:** Noah and the animals.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:21

**Q63. What would the food be?**  
**Answer:** For food.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:21

---

### Genesis 6:22
**Q64. What did Noah do?**  
**Answer:** According to all that God commanded him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:22

**Q65. How did Noah do?**  
**Answer:** So did he.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:22

**Q66. Who commanded Noah?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 6:22
