# Genesis 21 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 21:1
**Q1. Whom did the LORD visit?**  
**Answer:** Sarah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 21:1

**Q2. What did the LORD do for Sarah?**  
**Answer:** He did unto Sarah as he had spoken.  
**Verse:** Genesis 21:1

**Q3. What had God promised Sarah?**  
**Answer:** A son.  
**Verse:** Genesis 21:1

---

### Genesis 21:2
**Q4. What did Sarah conceive and bear?**  
**Answer:** A son.  
**Verse:** Genesis 21:2

**Q5. When did Sarah bear the son?**  
**Answer:** At the set time of which God had spoken.  
**Verse:** Genesis 21:2

**Q6. Who bore Abraham a son?**  
**Answer:** Sarah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 21:2

**Verse:** Genesis 21:3
