# Genesis 18 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 18:1
**Q1. Who appeared unto Abraham?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:1

**Q2. Where was Abraham sitting?**  
**Answer:** In the tent door in the heat of the day.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:1

**Q3. When did the LORD appear?**  
**Answer:** In the heat of the day.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:1

---

### Genesis 18:2
**Q4. What did Abraham see?**  
**Answer:** Three men.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:2

**Q5. What did Abraham do?**  
**Answer:** He ran to meet them and bowed himself toward the ground.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:2

**Q6. Where did Abraham meet them?**  
**Answer:** By the tent door.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:2

---

### Genesis 18:3
**Q7. What did Abraham call one of the visitors?**  
**Answer:** My Lord.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:3

**Q8. What did Abraham ask not to do?**  
**Answer:** Not to pass away from his servant.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:3

**Q9. What did Abraham find?**  
**Answer:** Favour in his sight.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:3

---

### Genesis 18:4
**Q10. What did Abraham ask them to do?**  
**Answer:** Rest under the tree.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:4

**Q11. What would be brought for their feet?**  
**Answer:** A little water.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:4

**Q12. Where were they to rest?**  
**Answer:** Under the tree.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:4

---

### Genesis 18:5
**Q13. What did Abraham offer to fetch?**  
**Answer:** A morsel of bread.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:5

**Q14. Why did he offer food?**  
**Answer:** To comfort their hearts.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:5

**Q15. What did they say?**  
**Answer:** So do, as thou hast said.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:5

---

### Genesis 18:6
**Q16. To whom did Abraham go?**  
**Answer:** Unto Sarah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:6

**Q17. What was Sarah told to make?**  
**Answer:** Cakes upon the hearth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:6

**Q18. How much meal was used?**  
**Answer:** Three measures of fine meal.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:6

---

### Genesis 18:7
**Q19. What did Abraham fetch?**  
**Answer:** A calf, tender and good.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:7

**Q20. To whom did he give the calf?**  
**Answer:** Unto a young man.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:7

**Q21. What was done with the calf?**  
**Answer:** It was dressed.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:7

---

### Genesis 18:8
**Q22. What did Abraham set before them?**  
**Answer:** Butter, milk, and the dressed calf.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:8

**Q23. Where did Abraham stand?**  
**Answer:** By them under the tree.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:8

**Q24. What did the men do?**  
**Answer:** They did eat.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:8

---

### Genesis 18:9
**Q25. What did they ask Abraham?**  
**Answer:** Where is Sarah thy wife?  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:9

**Q26. Where was Sarah?**  
**Answer:** In the tent.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:9

**Q27. Who answered the men?**  
**Answer:** Abraham.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:9

---

### Genesis 18:10
**Q28. What would Sarah have?**  
**Answer:** A son.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:10

**Q29. When would the visitor return?**  
**Answer:** According to the time of life.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:10

**Q30. Who heard the promise?**  
**Answer:** Sarah in the tent door.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:10

---

### Genesis 18:11
**Q31. How were Abraham and Sarah?**  
**Answer:** Old and well stricken in age.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:11

**Q32. What had ceased with Sarah?**  
**Answer:** The manner of women.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:11

**Q33. Who was old?**  
**Answer:** Abraham and Sarah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:11

---

### Genesis 18:12
**Q34. What did Sarah do?**  
**Answer:** She laughed within herself.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:12

**Q35. Why did Sarah laugh?**  
**Answer:** She was old and her lord was old.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:12

**Q36. What did Sarah say was past?**  
**Answer:** Her pleasure.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:12

---

### Genesis 18:13
**Q37. Who asked Abraham about Sarah's laughter?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:13

**Q38. What had Sarah done?**  
**Answer:** Laughed.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:13

**Q39. What question did the LORD ask?**  
**Answer:** Shall I of a surety bear a child, which am old?  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:13

---

### Genesis 18:14
**Q40. What is too hard for the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Nothing.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:14

**Q41. When would the LORD return?**  
**Answer:** At the appointed time.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:14

**Q42. What would Sarah have?**  
**Answer:** A son.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:14

---

### Genesis 18:15
**Q43. What did Sarah do?**  
**Answer:** She denied, saying, I laughed not.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:15

**Q44. Why did Sarah deny laughing?**  
**Answer:** She was afraid.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:15

**Q45. What did the LORD say?**  
**Answer:** Nay; but thou didst laugh.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:15

---

### Genesis 18:16
**Q46. What did the men do?**  
**Answer:** They rose up from thence.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:16

**Q47. Where did they look?**  
**Answer:** Toward Sodom.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:16

**Q48. Who went with them?**  
**Answer:** Abraham.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:16

---

### Genesis 18:17
**Q49. What did the LORD ask?**  
**Answer:** Shall I hide from Abraham that thing which I do?  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:17

**Q50. From whom would the LORD not hide?**  
**Answer:** Abraham.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:17

**Q51. What did the LORD consider?**  
**Answer:** Whether to hide his work from Abraham.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:17

---

### Genesis 18:18
**Q52. What would Abraham surely become?**  
**Answer:** A great and mighty nation.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:18

**Q53. In whom would all nations be blessed?**  
**Answer:** Abraham.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:18

**Q54. Who would be blessed in Abraham?**  
**Answer:** All the nations of the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:18

---

### Genesis 18:19
**Q55. What did God know about Abraham?**  
**Answer:** That he would command his children and household.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:19

**Q56. What way would Abraham keep?**  
**Answer:** The way of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:19

**Q57. Why did the LORD know Abraham?**  
**Answer:** To keep the way of the LORD and do justice and judgment.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:19

---

### Genesis 18:20
**Q58. What was great?**  
**Answer:** The cry of Sodom and Gomorrah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:20

**Q59. What was very grievous?**  
**Answer:** Their sin.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:20

**Q60. Where was the cry great?**  
**Answer:** Sodom and Gomorrah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:20

---

### Genesis 18:21
**Q61. What would God do?**  
**Answer:** Go down and see whether they had done altogether according to the cry.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:21

**Q62. What would God see?**  
**Answer:** Whether their deeds matched the cry.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:21

**Q63. Who told God the cry?**  
**Answer:** The cry that came unto him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:21

---

### Genesis 18:22
**Q64. Where did the men go?**  
**Answer:** Toward Sodom.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:22

**Q65. Where did Abraham remain?**  
**Answer:** Before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:22

**Q66. Who remained before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Abraham.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:22

---

### Genesis 18:23
**Q67. What did Abraham draw near to ask?**  
**Answer:** Wilt thou also destroy the righteous with the wicked?  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:23

**Q68. Who did Abraham approach?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:23

**Q69. What two groups did Abraham mention?**  
**Answer:** The righteous and the wicked.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:23

---

### Genesis 18:24
**Q70. What did Abraham ask about fifty righteous?**  
**Answer:** If the city would be spared for their sake.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:24

**Q71. What would be spared?**  
**Answer:** The place.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:24

**Q72. For whose sake would it be spared?**  
**Answer:** The fifty righteous.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:24

---

### Genesis 18:25
**Q73. What did Abraham say the Judge of all the earth would do?**  
**Answer:** Do right.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:25

**Q74. What did Abraham say was far from God?**  
**Answer:** To slay the righteous with the wicked.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:25

**Q75. Who is the Judge of all the earth?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:25

---

### Genesis 18:26
**Q76. What would the LORD do for fifty righteous?**  
**Answer:** Spare all the place.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:26

**Q77. Whom would the LORD spare?**  
**Answer:** All the place for their sakes.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:26

**Q78. Where would the righteous be found?**  
**Answer:** Within the city.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:26

---

### Genesis 18:27
**Q79. What did Abraham confess?**  
**Answer:** He was dust and ashes.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:27

**Q80. To whom did Abraham speak?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:27

**Q81. What did Abraham do?**  
**Answer:** Took upon himself to speak unto the Lord.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:27

---

### Genesis 18:28
**Q82. What if five were lacking from fifty?**  
**Answer:** The LORD would not destroy the city.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:28

**Q83. For how many would the LORD spare the city?**  
**Answer:** Forty and five.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:28

**Q84. What did Abraham ask?**  
**Answer:** Wilt thou destroy all the city for lack of five?  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:28

---

### Genesis 18:29
**Q85. What did Abraham ask about forty?**  
**Answer:** If the LORD would do it for forty's sake.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:29

**Q86. What did the LORD say?**  
**Answer:** He would not do it.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:29

**Q87. How many did Abraham mention?**  
**Answer:** Forty.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:29

---

### Genesis 18:30
**Q88. What did Abraham ask the LORD not to be?**  
**Answer:** Angry.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:30

**Q89. How many did Abraham mention next?**  
**Answer:** Thirty.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:30

**Q90. What did Abraham ask?**  
**Answer:** If thirty were found there.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:30

---

### Genesis 18:31
**Q91. What did Abraham ask about twenty?**  
**Answer:** If the LORD would spare it for twenty's sake.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:31

**Q92. What did the LORD say about twenty?**  
**Answer:** He would not destroy it.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:31

**Q93. How did Abraham describe his speaking?**  
**Answer:** He had taken upon himself to speak unto the Lord.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:31

---

### Genesis 18:32
**Q94. What final number did Abraham mention?**  
**Answer:** Ten.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:32

**Q95. For whose sake would the LORD not destroy the city?**  
**Answer:** For ten's sake.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:32

**Q96. What did the LORD do after speaking?**  
**Answer:** He went his way.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:32

---

### Genesis 18:33
**Q97. When did the LORD go his way?**  
**Answer:** As soon as he had left communing with Abraham.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:33

**Q98. Where did Abraham return?**  
**Answer:** Unto his place.  
**Verse:** Genesis 18:33

**Q99. Who left Abraham?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.
