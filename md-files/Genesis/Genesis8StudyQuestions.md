# Genesis 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 8:1
**Q1. Whom did God remember?**  
**Answer:** Noah, and every living thing with him in the ark.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:1

**Q2. What did God make to pass over the earth?**  
**Answer:** A wind.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:1

**Q3. What were the waters doing?**  
**Answer:** They were asswaged.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:1

---

### Genesis 8:2
**Q4. What were stopped?**  
**Answer:** The fountains of the deep and the windows of heaven.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:2

**Q5. What was restrained?**  
**Answer:** The rain from heaven.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:2

**Q6. What happened to the waters?**  
**Answer:** The waters returned from off the earth continually.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:2

---

### Genesis 8:3
**Q7. How did the waters return?**  
**Answer:** They returned continually from off the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:3

**Q8. At what time did the waters abate?**  
**Answer:** At the end of the hundred and fifty days.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:3

**Q9. What happened to the waters?**  
**Answer:** They abated.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:3

---

### Genesis 8:4
**Q10. Where did the ark rest?**  
**Answer:** Upon the mountains of Ararat.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:4

**Q11. In what month did the ark rest?**  
**Answer:** The seventh month.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:4

**Q12. On what day did the ark rest?**  
**Answer:** The seventeenth day of the month.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:4

---

### Genesis 8:5
**Q13. When were the tops of the mountains seen?**  
**Answer:** In the tenth month.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:5

**Q14. On what day were the tops of the mountains seen?**  
**Answer:** The first day of the month.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:5

**Q15. What appeared?**  
**Answer:** The tops of the mountains.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:5

---

### Genesis 8:6
**Q16. What did Noah open?**  
**Answer:** The window of the ark.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:6

**Q17. When did Noah open the window?**  
**Answer:** At the end of forty days.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:6

**Q18. Who opened the window?**  
**Answer:** Noah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:6

---

### Genesis 8:7
**Q19. What bird did Noah send forth?**  
**Answer:** A raven.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:7

**Q20. What did the raven do?**  
**Answer:** It went forth to and fro.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:7

**Q21. Until when did the raven remain?**  
**Answer:** Until the waters were dried up from off the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:7

---

### Genesis 8:8
**Q22. What did Noah send forth?**  
**Answer:** A dove.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:8

**Q23. Why did Noah send the dove?**  
**Answer:** To see if the waters were abated from off the face of the ground.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:8

**Q24. What was Noah checking?**  
**Answer:** Whether the waters were abated.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:8

---

### Genesis 8:9
**Q25. Where did the dove find no rest?**  
**Answer:** For the sole of her foot.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:9

**Q26. Where did the dove return?**  
**Answer:** Unto him into the ark.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:9

**Q27. What did Noah put forth?**  
**Answer:** His hand.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:9

---

### Genesis 8:10
**Q28. How long did Noah wait?**  
**Answer:** Yet other seven days.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:10

**Q29. What did Noah do after waiting?**  
**Answer:** He sent forth the dove again.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:10

**Q30. What bird was sent again?**  
**Answer:** The dove.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:10

---

### Genesis 8:11
**Q31. When did the dove come in?**  
**Answer:** In the evening.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:11

**Q32. What did the dove have in her mouth?**  
**Answer:** An olive leaf pluckt off.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:11

**Q33. What did Noah know?**  
**Answer:** That the waters were abated from off the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:11

---

### Genesis 8:12
**Q34. How long did Noah wait?**  
**Answer:** Yet other seven days.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:12

**Q35. What did Noah send forth?**  
**Answer:** The dove.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:12

**Q36. What did the dove not do?**  
**Answer:** She returned not again unto him any more.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:12

---

### Genesis 8:13
**Q37. When was the earth dried?**  
**Answer:** In the six hundredth and first year, in the first month, the first day.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:13

**Q38. What did Noah remove?**  
**Answer:** The covering of the ark.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:13

**Q39. What did Noah see?**  
**Answer:** The face of the ground was dry.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:13

---

### Genesis 8:14
**Q40. When was the earth dried?**  
**Answer:** In the second month, on the seven and twentieth day.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:14

**Q41. What was completely dry?**  
**Answer:** The earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:14

**Q42. On what day was the earth dry?**  
**Answer:** The seven and twentieth day of the month.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:14

---

### Genesis 8:15
**Q43. Who spake unto Noah?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:15

**Q44. To whom did God speak?**  
**Answer:** Noah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:15

**Q45. What did God do?**  
**Answer:** God spake unto Noah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:15

---

### Genesis 8:16
**Q46. What was Noah told to do?**  
**Answer:** Go forth of the ark.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:16

**Q47. Who was to go forth with Noah?**  
**Answer:** His wife, his sons, and his sons' wives.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:16

**Q48. Where was Noah to go?**  
**Answer:** Forth of the ark.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:16

---

### Genesis 8:17
**Q49. What was Noah to bring forth?**  
**Answer:** Every living thing.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:17

**Q50. What were the creatures to do?**  
**Answer:** Be fruitful, multiply, and replenish the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:17

**Q51. What kinds of creatures were to go forth?**  
**Answer:** Fowl, cattle, and every creeping thing.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:17

---

### Genesis 8:18
**Q52. Who went forth?**  
**Answer:** Noah, his sons, his wife, and his sons' wives.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:18

**Q53. Who went out of the ark?**  
**Answer:** Noah and his family.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:18

**Q54. Where did they go?**  
**Answer:** Out of the ark.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:18

---

### Genesis 8:19
**Q55. What went forth from the ark?**  
**Answer:** Every beast, every creeping thing, and every fowl.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:19

**Q56. How did the creatures go forth?**  
**Answer:** After their kinds.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:19

**Q57. Where did they go?**  
**Answer:** Forth out of the ark.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:19

---

### Genesis 8:20
**Q58. What did Noah build?**  
**Answer:** An altar unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:20

**Q59. What did Noah offer?**  
**Answer:** Of every clean beast and every clean fowl.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:20

**Q60. What did Noah offer upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** Burnt offerings.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:20

---

### Genesis 8:21
**Q61. What did the LORD smell?**  
**Answer:** A sweet savour.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:21

**Q62. What would the LORD not again do?**  
**Answer:** Curse the ground for man's sake.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:21

**Q63. What is man's heart like from his youth?**  
**Answer:** Evil.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:21

---

### Genesis 8:22
**Q64. What shall not cease?**  
**Answer:** Seedtime and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, and day and night.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:22

**Q65. What seasons are named?**  
**Answer:** Summer and winter.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:22

**Q66. What times are named?**  
**Answer:** Seedtime and harvest.  
**Verse:** Genesis 8:22
