# Genesis 7 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 7:1
**Q1. Whom did the LORD tell to come into the ark?**  
**Answer:** Noah and all his house.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:1

**Q2. Why was Noah to enter?**  
**Answer:** For thee have I seen righteous before me in this generation.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:1

**Q3. Who was seen righteous?**  
**Answer:** Noah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:1

---

### Genesis 7:2
**Q4. How many of every clean beast was Noah to take?**  
**Answer:** By sevens.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:2

**Q5. How many of unclean beasts?**  
**Answer:** By two.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:2

**Q6. What was to be taken by sevens?**  
**Answer:** The clean beasts.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:2

---

### Genesis 7:3
**Q7. How many of fowls was Noah to take?**  
**Answer:** By sevens.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:3

**Q8. Why were fowls taken?**  
**Answer:** To keep seed alive upon the face of all the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:3

**Q9. What was to be kept alive?**  
**Answer:** Seed.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:3

---

### Genesis 7:4
**Q10. In how many days would God cause rain?**  
**Answer:** Seven days.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:4

**Q11. How long would rain be upon the earth?**  
**Answer:** Forty days and forty nights.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:4

**Q12. What would God destroy?**  
**Answer:** Every living substance.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:4

---

### Genesis 7:5
**Q13. What did Noah do?**  
**Answer:** According unto all that the LORD commanded him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:5

**Q14. Who commanded Noah?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:5

**Q15. How did Noah obey?**  
**Answer:** According to all that was commanded.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:5

---

### Genesis 7:6
**Q16. How old was Noah when the flood came?**  
**Answer:** Six hundred years old.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:6

**Q17. What came upon the earth?**  
**Answer:** The flood of waters.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:6

**Q18. Who was six hundred years old?**  
**Answer:** Noah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:6

---

### Genesis 7:7
**Q19. Who went into the ark?**  
**Answer:** Noah, his sons, his wife, and his sons' wives.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:7

**Q20. Why did they enter?**  
**Answer:** Because of the waters of the flood.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:7

**Q21. What waters caused them to enter?**  
**Answer:** The waters of the flood.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:7

---

### Genesis 7:8
**Q22. What animals went into the ark?**  
**Answer:** Clean beasts, unclean beasts, fowls, and creeping things.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:8

**Q23. What kinds of beasts entered?**  
**Answer:** Clean and unclean.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:8

**Q24. What creeping things entered?**  
**Answer:** Every creeping thing.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:8

---

### Genesis 7:9
**Q25. By what did the animals enter?**  
**Answer:** Two and two.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:9

**Q26. To whom did they come?**  
**Answer:** Unto Noah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:9

**Q27. Who brought them into the ark?**  
**Answer:** Noah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:9

---

### Genesis 7:10
**Q28. What happened after seven days?**  
**Answer:** The waters of the flood were upon the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:10

**Q29. When did the flood waters come?**  
**Answer:** After seven days.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:10

**Q30. What was upon the earth?**  
**Answer:** The waters of the flood.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:10

---

### Genesis 7:11
**Q31. How old was Noah?**  
**Answer:** Six hundred years old.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:11

**Q32. In what month did the flood begin?**  
**Answer:** The second month.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:11

**Q33. What was opened?**  
**Answer:** The windows of heaven.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:11

---

### Genesis 7:12
**Q34. How long did rain fall?**  
**Answer:** Forty days and forty nights.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:12

**Q35. From where did rain come?**  
**Answer:** Upon the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:12

**Q36. What fell upon the earth?**  
**Answer:** Rain.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:12

---

### Genesis 7:13
**Q37. Who entered the ark that day?**  
**Answer:** Noah, Shem, Ham, Japheth, and their wives.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:13

**Q38. Who were Noah's sons?**  
**Answer:** Shem, Ham, and Japheth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:13

**Q39. Whose wives entered?**  
**Answer:** Noah's sons' wives.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:13

---

### Genesis 7:14
**Q40. What went into the ark?**  
**Answer:** Every beast, cattle, creeping thing, and fowl.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:14

**Q41. What kinds of creatures entered?**  
**Answer:** Every kind.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:14

**Q42. What was in the ark?**  
**Answer:** Every beast after his kind.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:14

---

### Genesis 7:15
**Q43. How did creatures enter?**  
**Answer:** Two and two.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:15

**Q44. To whom did they come?**  
**Answer:** Unto Noah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:15

**Q45. What entered the ark?**  
**Answer:** Every thing in whose nostrils was the breath of life.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:15

---

### Genesis 7:16
**Q46. Who went in?**  
**Answer:** Male and female of all flesh.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:16

**Q47. Who shut Noah in?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:16

**Q48. What was shut?**  
**Answer:** The ark.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:16

---

### Genesis 7:17
**Q49. How long was the flood upon the earth?**  
**Answer:** Forty days.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:17

**Q50. What did the waters bear up?**  
**Answer:** The ark.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:17

**Q51. What was lifted up?**  
**Answer:** The ark above the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:17

---

### Genesis 7:18
**Q52. What prevailed upon the earth?**  
**Answer:** The waters.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:18

**Q53. What did the ark do?**  
**Answer:** Went upon the face of the waters.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:18

**Q54. What covered the earth?**  
**Answer:** The waters.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:18

---

### Genesis 7:19
**Q55. What was covered?**  
**Answer:** All the high hills under the whole heaven.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:19

**Q56. What prevailed exceedingly?**  
**Answer:** The waters.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:19

**Q57. What hills were covered?**  
**Answer:** All the high hills.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:19

---

### Genesis 7:20
**Q58. How high did the waters prevail?**  
**Answer:** Fifteen cubits upward.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:20

**Q59. What mountains were covered?**  
**Answer:** The mountains.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:20

**Q60. What prevailed above the mountains?**  
**Answer:** The waters.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:20

---

### Genesis 7:21
**Q61. What died?**  
**Answer:** All flesh that moved upon the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:21

**Q62. What kinds died?**  
**Answer:** Fowl, cattle, beasts, creeping things, and man.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:21

**Q63. What moved upon the earth?**  
**Answer:** All flesh.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:21

---

### Genesis 7:22
**Q64. What died?**  
**Answer:** All in whose nostrils was the breath of life.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:22

**Q65. Where was the breath of life?**  
**Answer:** In their nostrils.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:22

**Q66. What was in the nostrils of every creature?**  
**Answer:** The breath of life.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:22

---

### Genesis 7:23
**Q67. What was destroyed?**  
**Answer:** Every living substance upon the face of the ground.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:23

**Q68. Who remained alive?**  
**Answer:** Noah and they that were with him in the ark.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:23

**Q69. What remained besides Noah?**  
**Answer:** They that were with him in the ark.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:23

---

### Genesis 7:24
**Q70. How long did the waters prevail?**  
**Answer:** An hundred and fifty days.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:24

**Q71. What prevailed upon the earth?**  
**Answer:** The waters.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:24

**Q72. How many days did the waters prevail?**  
**Answer:** One hundred fifty days.  
**Verse:** Genesis 7:24
