# Genesis 40 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 40:1

**Q1. Who came into prison to Joseph?**  
**Answer:** The butler of the king of Egypt and the baker.  
**Verse:** Genesis 40:1

**Q2. What was the position of the butler?**  
**Answer:** Butler of the king of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Genesis 40:1

**Q3. Who else came into prison?**  
**Answer:** The baker.  
**Verse:** Genesis 40:1

---

**Q4. Who was the keeper of the prison?**
