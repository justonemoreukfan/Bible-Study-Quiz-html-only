# Genesis 16 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 16:1
**Q1. What had Sarai not borne Abram?**  
**Answer:** Children.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:1

**Q2. What was Sarai's maid called?**  
**Answer:** Hagar.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:1

**Q3. Where was Hagar from?**  
**Answer:** Egypt.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:1

---

### Genesis 16:2
**Q4. What did Sarai say the LORD had restrained?**  
**Answer:** Her from bearing.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:2

**Q5. Whom did Sarai give to Abram?**  
**Answer:** Hagar her maid.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:2

**Q6. Why did Sarai give Hagar to Abram?**  
**Answer:** That she might obtain children by her.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:2

---

### Genesis 16:3
**Q7. How long had Abram dwelt in Canaan?**  
**Answer:** Ten years.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:3

**Q8. Whom did Sarai take?**  
**Answer:** Hagar her maid, the Egyptian.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:3

**Q9. To whom did Sarai give Hagar?**  
**Answer:** Abram her husband.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:3

---

### Genesis 16:4
**Q10. What did Hagar do after conceiving?**  
**Answer:** She despised her mistress.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:4

**Q11. Whom did Hagar despise?**  
**Answer:** Sarai.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:4

**Q12. What happened to Hagar?**  
**Answer:** She conceived.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:4

---

### Genesis 16:5
**Q13. Who did Sarai say was wrong?**  
**Answer:** Abram.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:5

**Q14. Who had given Hagar to Abram?**  
**Answer:** Sarai.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:5

**Q15. Into whose hand did Sarai say the LORD should judge?**  
**Answer:** Abram's hand.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:5

---

### Genesis 16:6
**Q16. What did Abram tell Sarai?**  
**Answer:** Behold, thy maid is in thy hand; do to her as it pleaseth thee.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:6

**Q17. Whom did Sarai deal hardly with?**  
**Answer:** Hagar.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:6

**Q18. Where did Hagar flee?**  
**Answer:** From Sarai's face.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:6

---

### Genesis 16:7
**Q19. Who found Hagar?**  
**Answer:** The angel of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:7

**Q20. Where was Hagar found?**  
**Answer:** By a fountain of water in the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:7

**Q21. By what fountain was she found?**  
**Answer:** The fountain in the way to Shur.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:7

---

### Genesis 16:8
**Q22. What did the angel ask Hagar?**  
**Answer:** Whence camest thou, and whither wilt thou go?  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:8

**Q23. From where had Hagar come?**  
**Answer:** From Sarai her mistress.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:8

**Q24. From whom had Hagar fled?**  
**Answer:** Sarai her mistress.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:8

---

### Genesis 16:9
**Q25. What did the angel tell Hagar to do?**  
**Answer:** Return to her mistress and submit herself under her hands.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:9

**Q26. To whom was Hagar to return?**  
**Answer:** Her mistress.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:9

**Q27. Under whose hands was Hagar to submit?**  
**Answer:** Sarai's hands.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:9

---

### Genesis 16:10
**Q28. What would God multiply?**  
**Answer:** Hagar's seed.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:10

**Q29. How would Hagar's seed be numbered?**  
**Answer:** It would not be numbered for multitude.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:10

**Q30. Who promised to multiply Hagar's seed?**  
**Answer:** The angel of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:10

---

### Genesis 16:11
**Q31. What was Hagar to call her son?**  
**Answer:** Ishmael.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:11

**Q32. Why was he named Ishmael?**  
**Answer:** The LORD had heard her affliction.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:11

**Q33. Who heard Hagar's affliction?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:11

---

### Genesis 16:12
**Q34. What kind of man would Ishmael be?**  
**Answer:** A wild man.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:12

**Q35. Against whom would his hand be?**  
**Answer:** Every man's hand.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:12

**Q36. Before whom would Ishmael dwell?**  
**Answer:** Before all his brethren.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:12

---

### Genesis 16:13
**Q37. What did Hagar call the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Thou God seest me.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:13

**Q38. What did Hagar say she had seen?**  
**Answer:** Him that seeth me.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:13

**Q39. Where did Hagar call upon the LORD?**  
**Answer:** In the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:13

---

### Genesis 16:14
**Q40. What was the well called?**  
**Answer:** Beer-lahai-roi.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:14

**Q41. Where was the well?**  
**Answer:** Between Kadesh and Bered.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:14

**Q42. What did the name mean?**  
**Answer:** The well of him that liveth and seeth me.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:14

---

### Genesis 16:15
**Q43. Whom did Hagar bear to Abram?**  
**Answer:** A son.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:15

**Q44. What did Abram call his son?**  
**Answer:** Ishmael.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:15

**Q45. Who named the son Ishmael?**  
**Answer:** Abram.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:15

---

### Genesis 16:16
**Q46. How old was Abram when Hagar bare Ishmael?**  
**Answer:** Fourscore and six years old.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:16

**Q47. Who bare Ishmael?**  
**Answer:** Hagar.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:16

**Q48. How old was Abram?**  
**Answer:** Eighty-six years old.  
**Verse:** Genesis 16:16
