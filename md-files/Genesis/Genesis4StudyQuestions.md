# Genesis 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 4:1
**Q1. Who conceived and bare Cain?**  
**Answer:** Eve.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:1

**Q2. What did Eve say she had gotten?**  
**Answer:** A man from the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:1

**Q3. Who was born after Adam knew Eve?**  
**Answer:** Cain.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:1

---

### Genesis 4:2
**Q4. Whom did Eve again bear?**  
**Answer:** Abel.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:2

**Q5. What was Abel's occupation?**  
**Answer:** A keeper of sheep.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:2

**Q6. What was Cain's occupation?**  
**Answer:** A tiller of the ground.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:2

---

### Genesis 4:3
**Q7. What did Cain bring?**  
**Answer:** Of the fruit of the ground.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:3

**Q8. When did he bring it?**  
**Answer:** In process of time.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:3

**Q9. To whom did Cain bring an offering?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:3

---

### Genesis 4:4
**Q10. What did Abel bring?**  
**Answer:** The firstlings of his flock and the fat thereof.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:4

**Q11. Whose offering had respect?**  
**Answer:** Abel's.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:4

**Q12. To what did the LORD have respect?**  
**Answer:** Abel and his offering.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:4

---

### Genesis 4:5
**Q13. To whom did God not have respect?**  
**Answer:** Cain and his offering.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:5

**Q14. How did Cain feel?**  
**Answer:** Very wroth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:5

**Q15. What was fallen?**  
**Answer:** Cain's countenance.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:5

---

### Genesis 4:6
**Q16. What did the LORD ask Cain?**  
**Answer:** Why art thou wroth?  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:6

**Q17. What else did the LORD ask?**  
**Answer:** Why is thy countenance fallen?  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:6

**Q18. Who spoke to Cain?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:6

---

### Genesis 4:7
**Q19. What would happen if Cain did well?**  
**Answer:** He would be accepted.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:7

**Q20. What lieth at the door?**  
**Answer:** Sin.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:7

**Q21. Over whom should Cain rule?**  
**Answer:** Sin.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:7

---

### Genesis 4:8
**Q22. To whom did Cain talk?**  
**Answer:** Abel his brother.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:8

**Q23. Where did Cain kill Abel?**  
**Answer:** In the field.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:8

**Q24. Who rose up against Abel?**  
**Answer:** Cain.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:8

---

### Genesis 4:9
**Q25. What did the LORD ask Cain?**  
**Answer:** Where is Abel thy brother?  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:9

**Q26. What did Cain answer?**  
**Answer:** I know not.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:9

**Q27. What did Cain deny being?**  
**Answer:** His brother's keeper.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:9

---

### Genesis 4:10
**Q28. What cried unto God?**  
**Answer:** The voice of Abel's blood.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:10

**Q29. From where did it cry?**  
**Answer:** From the ground.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:10

**Q30. Whose blood cried?**  
**Answer:** Abel's.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:10

---

### Genesis 4:11
**Q31. What was cursed?**  
**Answer:** The ground.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:11

**Q32. What had opened her mouth?**  
**Answer:** The earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:11

**Q33. What had the earth received?**  
**Answer:** Thy brother's blood.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:11

---

### Genesis 4:12
**Q34. What would the ground not yield?**  
**Answer:** Her strength unto Cain.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:12

**Q35. What would Cain be?**  
**Answer:** A fugitive and a vagabond.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:12

**Q36. What would happen when Cain tilled the ground?**  
**Answer:** It would not yield her strength.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:12

---

### Genesis 4:13
**Q37. What did Cain say was greater than he could bear?**  
**Answer:** His punishment.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:13

**Q38. What did Cain call his punishment?**  
**Answer:** Greater than he could bear.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:13

**Q39. Who spoke of the punishment?**  
**Answer:** Cain.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:13

---

### Genesis 4:14
**Q40. From where was Cain driven?**  
**Answer:** From the face of the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:14

**Q41. From what was Cain hid?**  
**Answer:** God's face.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:14

**Q42. Who might kill Cain?**  
**Answer:** Every one that findeth him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:14

---

### Genesis 4:15
**Q43. What did the LORD say about Cain?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever slayeth Cain, vengeance shall be taken on him sevenfold.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:15

**Q44. What did God set upon Cain?**  
**Answer:** A mark.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:15

**Q45. Why was the mark set?**  
**Answer:** Lest any finding him should kill him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:15

---

### Genesis 4:16
**Q46. From whose presence did Cain go?**  
**Answer:** The presence of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:16

**Q47. Where did Cain dwell?**  
**Answer:** In the land of Nod.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:16

**Q48. Where was Nod?**  
**Answer:** On the east of Eden.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:16

---

### Genesis 4:17
**Q49. Who did Cain know?**  
**Answer:** His wife.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:17

**Q50. What did Cain build?**  
**Answer:** A city.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:17

**Q51. What did Cain call the city?**  
**Answer:** Enoch.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:17

---

### Genesis 4:18
**Q52. Who was born to Enoch?**  
**Answer:** Irad.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:18

**Q53. Who begat Mehujael?**  
**Answer:** Irad.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:18

**Q54. Who begat Methusael?**  
**Answer:** Mehujael.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:18

---

### Genesis 4:19
**Q55. Who had two wives?**  
**Answer:** Lamech.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:19

**Q56. What were their names?**  
**Answer:** Adah and Zillah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:19

**Q57. Who took two wives?**  
**Answer:** Lamech.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:19

---

### Genesis 4:20
**Q58. Who was the father of such as dwell in tents?**  
**Answer:** Jabal.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:20

**Q59. Who was the father of cattle?**  
**Answer:** Jabal.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:20

**Q60. What did Jabal's descendants dwell in?**  
**Answer:** Tents.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:20

---

### Genesis 4:21
**Q61. Who was the father of those who handle the harp and organ?**  
**Answer:** Jubal.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:21

**Q62. What did Jubal handle?**  
**Answer:** The harp and organ.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:21

**Q63. Who was Jubal's brother?**  
**Answer:** Jabal.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:21

---

### Genesis 4:22
**Q64. Who was an instructor of every artificer in brass and iron?**  
**Answer:** Tubal-cain.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:22

**Q65. Who was the sister of Tubal-cain?**  
**Answer:** Naamah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:22

**Q66. What did Tubal-cain work?**  
**Answer:** Brass and iron.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:22

---

### Genesis 4:23
**Q67. To whom did Lamech speak?**  
**Answer:** Adah and Zillah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:23

**Q68. What had Lamech done?**  
**Answer:** Slain a man in his wound.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:23

**Q69. What had a young man done?**  
**Answer:** Hurt Lamech.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:23

---

### Genesis 4:24
**Q70. If Cain shall be avenged sevenfold, what shall Lamech be?**  
**Answer:** Seventy and sevenfold.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:24

**Q71. How many times would Lamech be avenged?**  
**Answer:** Seventy and sevenfold.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:24

**Q72. Whose vengeance was compared with Lamech's?**  
**Answer:** Cain's.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:24

---

### Genesis 4:25
**Q73. Whom did Adam know again?**  
**Answer:** His wife.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:25

**Q74. What did Eve call her son?**  
**Answer:** Seth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:25

**Q75. Why was Seth given?**  
**Answer:** God had appointed another seed instead of Abel.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:25

---

### Genesis 4:26
**Q76. What was born to Seth?**  
**Answer:** A son.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:26

**Q77. What was his name?**  
**Answer:** Enos.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:26

**Q78. What began to be called upon?**  
**Answer:** The name of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 4:26
