# Genesis 33 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 33:1

**Q1. What did Jacob see when he lifted up his eyes?**  
**Answer:** Esau coming with four hundred men.  
**Verse:** Genesis 33:1

**Q2. How many men were with Esau?**  
**Answer:** Four hundred men.  
**Verse:** Genesis 33:1

**Q3. What action did Jacob take with his wives, children, and handmaids?**  
**Answer:** Divided them.  
**Verse:** Genesis 33:1

---

### Genesis 33:2

**Q4. Whom did Jacob set first?**  
**Answer:** The handmaids, and the children.  
**Verse:** Genesis 33:2

**Q5. Whom did he set next?**  
**Answer:** Leah, and her children.  
**Verse:** Genesis 33:2

**Q6. Whom did Jacob set last of all?**  
**Answer:** Rachel, and Joseph.  
**Verse:** Genesis 33:2
