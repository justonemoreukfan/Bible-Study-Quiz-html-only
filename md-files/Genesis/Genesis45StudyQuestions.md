# Genesis 45 Study Questions (KJV)

### Genesis 45:1

**Q1. What did Joseph do when he could no longer restrain himself?**  
**Answer:** He wept aloud.  
**Verse:** Genesis 45:1

**Q2. Who did Joseph command to leave the room?**  
**Answer:** All the men.  
**Verse:** Genesis 45:1

**Q3. What did Joseph say to his brethren in verse 1?**  
**Answer:** I am Joseph; doth my father yet live?  
**Verse:** Genesis 45:1
