# Genesis 15 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 15:1
**Q1. Who came to Abram in a vision?**  
**Answer:** The word of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:1

**Q2. What was the LORD to Abram?**  
**Answer:** His shield and exceeding great reward.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:1

**Q3. What was Abram told not to do?**  
**Answer:** Fear.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:1

---

### Genesis 15:2
**Q4. What did Abram say he lacked?**  
**Answer:** A child.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:2

**Q5. Who was Abram's steward?**  
**Answer:** Eliezer of Damascus.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:2

**Q6. What did Abram call his steward?**  
**Answer:** The steward of his house.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:2

---

### Genesis 15:3
**Q7. What did Abram say God had not given him?**  
**Answer:** Seed.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:3

**Q8. Who was heir of Abram's house?**  
**Answer:** Eliezer of Damascus.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:3

**Q9. What would a servant be in Abram's house?**  
**Answer:** His heir.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:3

---

### Genesis 15:4
**Q10. Who would be Abram's heir?**  
**Answer:** He that shall come forth out of thine own bowels.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:4

**Q11. Who spoke to Abram?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:4

**Q12. What would not be Abram's heir?**  
**Answer:** Eliezer.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:4

---

### Genesis 15:5
**Q13. What did the LORD bring Abram forth to see?**  
**Answer:** The stars.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:5

**Q14. Like what would Abram's seed be?**  
**Answer:** The stars.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:5

**Q15. What did Abram do?**  
**Answer:** He believed in the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:5

---

### Genesis 15:6
**Q16. What did Abram believe?**  
**Answer:** In the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:6

**Q17. What was counted to Abram for righteousness?**  
**Answer:** His belief in the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:6

**Q18. What was counted for righteousness?**  
**Answer:** Faith.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:6

---

### Genesis 15:7
**Q19. Who brought Abram out of Ur?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:7

**Q20. What land was given to Abram?**  
**Answer:** This land, to inherit it.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:7

**Q21. Where was Abram brought from?**  
**Answer:** Ur of the Chaldees.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:7

---

### Genesis 15:8
**Q22. What did Abram ask the Lord GOD?**  
**Answer:** Whereby shall I know that I shall inherit it?  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:8

**Q23. What did Abram want to know?**  
**Answer:** How he would inherit the land.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:8

**Q24. Who asked this question?**  
**Answer:** Abram.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:8

---

### Genesis 15:9
**Q25. What animals was Abram told to bring?**  
**Answer:** A heifer, she goat, ram, turtledove, and young pigeon.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:9

**Q26. How old were the heifer, she goat, and ram?**  
**Answer:** Three years old.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:9

**Q27. What birds were to be brought?**  
**Answer:** A turtledove and a young pigeon.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:9

---

### Genesis 15:10
**Q28. What did Abram divide?**  
**Answer:** The animals.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:10

**Q29. What did Abram not divide?**  
**Answer:** The birds.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:10

**Q30. Who laid each piece one against another?**  
**Answer:** Abram.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:10

---

### Genesis 15:11
**Q31. What came down upon the carcases?**  
**Answer:** The fowls.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:11

**Q32. What did Abram do to the fowls?**  
**Answer:** He drove them away.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:11

**Q33. Who drove away the fowls?**  
**Answer:** Abram.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:11

---

### Genesis 15:12
**Q34. What fell upon Abram?**  
**Answer:** A deep sleep.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:12

**Q35. What came upon Abram?**  
**Answer:** An horror of great darkness.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:12

**Q36. When did this happen?**  
**Answer:** When the sun was going down.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:12

---

### Genesis 15:13
**Q37. What would Abram's seed be?**  
**Answer:** A stranger in a land that is not theirs.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:13

**Q38. How long would they serve and be afflicted?**  
**Answer:** Four hundred years.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:13

**Q39. What would happen to Abram's seed?**  
**Answer:** They would be afflicted.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:13

---

### Genesis 15:14
**Q40. What would God judge?**  
**Answer:** That nation whom they shall serve.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:14

**Q41. With what would Abram's seed come out?**  
**Answer:** Great substance.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:14

**Q42. Whom would God judge?**  
**Answer:** The nation that served Abram's seed.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:14

---

### Genesis 15:15
**Q43. To whom would Abram go?**  
**Answer:** To his fathers in peace.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:15

**Q44. How would Abram be buried?**  
**Answer:** In a good old age.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:15

**Q45. What would Abram enjoy?**  
**Answer:** Peace.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:15

---

### Genesis 15:16
**Q46. In what generation would Abram's seed return?**  
**Answer:** The fourth generation.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:16

**Q47. Why would they return?**  
**Answer:** The iniquity of the Amorites was not yet full.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:16

**Q48. Whose iniquity was not yet full?**  
**Answer:** The Amorites'.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:16

---

### Genesis 15:17
**Q49. What appeared when the sun went down?**  
**Answer:** A smoking furnace and a burning lamp.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:17

**Q50. What passed between the pieces?**  
**Answer:** A smoking furnace and a burning lamp.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:17

**Q51. When did this happen?**  
**Answer:** When the sun went down and it was dark.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:17

---

### Genesis 15:18
**Q52. With whom did the LORD make a covenant?**  
**Answer:** Abram.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:18

**Q53. To whom did God give the land?**  
**Answer:** Abram's seed.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:18

**Q54. What land was given?**  
**Answer:** From the river of Egypt unto the great river, the river Euphrates.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:18

---

### Genesis 15:19
**Q55. Whose land was included?**  
**Answer:** The Kenites, Kenizzites, and Kadmonites.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:19

**Q56. What land was promised?**  
**Answer:** The land of the named peoples.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:19

**Q57. Who were the first peoples named?**  
**Answer:** The Kenites, Kenizzites, and Kadmonites.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:19

---

### Genesis 15:20
**Q58. Whose land was also included?**  
**Answer:** The Hittites, Perizzites, and Rephaim.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:20

**Q59. Who were named after the Kadmonites?**  
**Answer:** The Hittites, Perizzites, and Rephaim.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:20

**Q60. What did God promise to Abram's seed?**  
**Answer:** The land of these nations.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:20

---

### Genesis 15:21
**Q61. Whose land was last named?**  
**Answer:** The Amorites, Canaanites, Girgashites, and Jebusites.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:21

**Q62. How many peoples are named in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Four.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:21

**Q63. To whom would their land be given?**  
**Answer:** Abram's seed.  
**Verse:** Genesis 15:21
