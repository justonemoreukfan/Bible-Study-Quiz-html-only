# Genesis 39 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 39:1

**Q1. Where was Joseph carried?**  
**Answer:** Into Egypt.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:1

**Q2. Who bought Joseph?**  
**Answer:** Potiphar.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:1

**Q3. Who was Potiphar?**  
**Answer:** An officer of Pharaoh, captain of the guard.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:1

---

### Genesis 39:2

**Q4. Who was with Joseph?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:2

**Q5. What was Joseph because the LORD was with him?**  
**Answer:** A prosperous man.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:2

**Q6. In whose house was Joseph a prosperous man?**  
**Answer:** In the house of his master, which was an Egyptian.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:2

---

### Genesis 39:3

**Q7. What did Potiphar see?**  
**Answer:** That the LORD was with him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:3

**Q8. What did Potiphar see the LORD do for Joseph?**  
**Answer:** That the LORD made all that he did to prosper in his hand.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:3

**Q9. Who prospered Joseph in his hand?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:3

---

### Genesis 39:4

**Q10. What did Potiphar do for Joseph?**  
**Answer:** Joseph found grace in his sight.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:4

**Q11. What did Potiphar make Joseph?**  
**Answer:** His steward.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:4

**Q12. What did Potiphar commit to Joseph's hand?**  
**Answer:** All that he had.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:4

---

### Genesis 39:5

**Q13. What did the LORD bless for Joseph's sake?**  
**Answer:** The house of his master.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:5

**Q14. What did the LORD bring upon the house?**  
**Answer:** A blessing.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:5

**Q15. On whose sake did the LORD bless Potiphar's house?**  
**Answer:** Joseph's.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:5

---

### Genesis 39:6

**Q16. How much of his house did Potiphar commit to Joseph?**  
**Answer:** All.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:6

**Q17. What did Potiphar no longer attend to?**  
**Answer:** Any thing save the meat which is in his mouth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:6

**Q18. Who was in charge of everything in Potiphar's house?**  
**Answer:** Joseph.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:6

---

### Genesis 39:7

**Q19. What happened after Joseph had made himself prosperous?**  
**Answer:** Potiphar's wife cast her eyes upon Joseph.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:7

**Q20. Who cast her eyes upon Joseph?**  
**Answer:** Potiphar's wife.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:7

**Q21. What did Potiphar's wife ask Joseph?**  
**Answer:** Lie with me.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:7

---

### Genesis 39:8

**Q22. How did Joseph respond to Potiphar's wife?**  
**Answer:** He refused.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:8

**Q23. Why did Joseph refuse?**  
**Answer:** Because my master hath committed all which is his into my hand.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:8

**Q24. What did Joseph say his master had not reserved from him?**  
**Answer:** Only thee.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:8

---

### Genesis 39:9

**Q25. What did Joseph say was the greatest sin?**

**Verse:** Genesis 39:9

**Q26. Against whom would the sin be?**  
**Answer:** Against God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:9

**Q27. What did Joseph call the act of lying with Potiphar's wife?**

**Verse:** Genesis 39:9

### Genesis 39:10

**Q28. How often did Potiphar's wife speak to Joseph?**

**Verse:** Genesis 39:10

**Q29. What did she ask Joseph to do?**

**Verse:** Genesis 39:10

**Verse:** Genesis 39:10

---

### Genesis 39:11

**Q31. When did Potiphar's wife catch Joseph?**

**Q32. Where was no man in the house?**

**Verse:** Genesis 39:11

**Verse:** Genesis 39:11

**Q63. Who was over all the prisoners?**  
**Answer:** The keeper of the prison.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:22

**Q64. What did the keeper do for Joseph?**  
**Answer:** He committed to Joseph all the prisoners.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:22

**Q65. What was Joseph's role in prison?**  
**Answer:** He oversaw all that was done in the prison.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:22

---

### Genesis 39:23

**Q66. Why did the keeper not attend to any thing?**  
**Answer:** Because the LORD was with Joseph.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:23

**Q67. What did the LORD do for Joseph?**  
**Answer:** The LORD made all that he did to prosper.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:23

**Q68. What was the result of the LORD being with Joseph?**  
**Answer:** The keeper committed all that was in the prison to Joseph's hand.  
**Verse:** Genesis 39:23
