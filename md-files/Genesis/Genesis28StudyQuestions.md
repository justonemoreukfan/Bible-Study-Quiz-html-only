# Genesis 28 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 28:1

**Q1. Whom did Isaac call?**  
**Answer:** Jacob.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:1  

**Q2. What did Isaac charge Jacob?**  
**Answer:** Take not a wife of the daughters of Canaan.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:1  

**Q3. What was Jacob forbidden to take?**  
**Answer:** A wife of Canaan.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:1  

---

### Genesis 28:2

**Q4. Where was Jacob told to go?**  
**Answer:** Padan-aram.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:2  

**Q5. Whom was Jacob to take to wife?**  
**Answer:** One of the daughters of Laban.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:2  

**Q6. Who was Laban?**  
**Answer:** Bethuel's son.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:2  

---

### Genesis 28:3

**Q7. Who would bless Jacob?**  
**Answer:** God Almighty.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:3  

**Q8. What would God make Jacob?**  
**Answer:** Fruitful and multiply him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:3  

**Q9. What would Jacob become?**  
**Answer:** A multitude of people.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:3  

---

### Genesis 28:4

**Q10. What blessing was given to Jacob?**  
**Answer:** The blessing of Abraham.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:4  

**Q11. What would Jacob inherit?**  
**Answer:** The land of his sojournings.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:4  

**Q12. To whom was the land promised?**  
**Answer:** Abraham.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:4  

---

### Genesis 28:5

**Q15. Who was Laban's sister?**

**Verse:** Genesis 28:5

### Genesis 28:6

**Verse:** Genesis 28:6  

**Q17. Where had Isaac sent Jacob?**

**Verse:** Genesis 28:6  

**Q18. Why was Jacob sent there?**

**Verse:** Genesis 28:6

### Genesis 28:7

**Q19. Whose voice did Jacob obey?**  
**Answer:** His father and mother.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:7  

**Q20. Where did Jacob go?**  
**Answer:** Padan-aram.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:7  

**Q21. What did Jacob do?**  
**Answer:** Obeyed his parents.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:7  

---

### Genesis 28:8

**Q22. What did Esau see?**  
**Answer:** The daughters of Canaan pleased not Isaac.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:8  

**Q23. Whom did the daughters not please?**  
**Answer:** Isaac.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:8  

**Q24. What did Esau observe?**  
**Answer:** His father's displeasure.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:8  

---

### Genesis 28:9

**Answer:** Ishmael.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:9  

**Q26. Whom did Esau take?**  
**Answer:** Mahalath.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:9  

**Q27. Who was Mahalath?**

**Verse:** Genesis 28:9  

---

### Genesis 28:10

**Q28. From where did Jacob go?**  
**Answer:** From Beer-sheba.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:10  

**Q29. Where did Jacob go toward?**  
**Answer:** Haran.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:10  

**Q30. Who journeyed toward Haran?**  
**Answer:** Jacob.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:10  

---

### Genesis 28:11

**Q31. Where did Jacob light?**  
**Answer:** Upon a certain place.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:11  

**Q32. What did Jacob do there?**  
**Answer:** Tarried all night.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:11  

**Q33. What did Jacob use for pillows?**  
**Answer:** The stones of that place.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:11  

---

### Genesis 28:12

**Q34. What did Jacob dream?**  
**Answer:** A ladder set up on the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:12  

**Q35. Where did the ladder reach?**  
**Answer:** To heaven.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:12  

**Q36. Who ascended and descended?**  
**Answer:** The angels of God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:12  

---

### Genesis 28:13

**Q37. Who stood above the ladder?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:13  

**Q38. Whose God was the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Abraham's and Isaac's God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:13  

**Q39. What land would Jacob receive?**  
**Answer:** The land whereon thou liest.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:13  

---

### Genesis 28:14

**Q40. What would Jacob's seed be like?**  
**Answer:** The dust of the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:14  

**Q41. In whom would families be blessed?**  
**Answer:** Jacob and his seed.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:14  

**Q42. In what directions would Jacob spread?**  
**Answer:** West, east, north, and south.  
**Verse:** Genesis 28:14

### Genesis 28:15

**Q43. Who would be with Jacob?**

**Verse:** Genesis 28:15
