# Genesis 20 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 20:1
**Q1. Where did Abraham journey?**  
**Answer:** From thence toward the south country.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:1

**Q2. Where did Abraham dwell?**  
**Answer:** Between Kadesh and Shur, and he sojourned in Gerar.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:1

**Q3. What did Abraham say about Sarah?**  
**Answer:** She is my sister.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:1

---

### Genesis 20:2
**Q4. Who took Sarah?**  
**Answer:** Abimelech king of Gerar.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:2

**Q5. What did Abraham say Sarah was?**  
**Answer:** His sister.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:2

**Q6. Where was Sarah taken?**  
**Answer:** Into Abimelech's house.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:2

---

### Genesis 20:3
**Q7. Who came to Abimelech in a dream?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:3

**Q8. What did God call Sarah?**  
**Answer:** A man's wife.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:3

**Q9. What would Abimelech do?**  
**Answer:** Die.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:3

---

### Genesis 20:4
**Q10. Had Abimelech come near Sarah?**  
**Answer:** No.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:4

**Q11. What did Abimelech say he had not done?**  
**Answer:** Touched her.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:4

**Q12. Whom did Abimelech call righteous?**  
**Answer:** His nation.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:4

---

### Genesis 20:5
**Q13. What had Abraham said Sarah was?**  
**Answer:** His sister.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:5

**Q14. What had Sarah also said?**  
**Answer:** He is my brother.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:5

**Q15. How had Abimelech acted?**  
**Answer:** In the integrity of his heart and innocency of his hands.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:5

---

### Genesis 20:6
**Q16. What did God know about Abimelech?**  
**Answer:** He acted in the integrity of his heart.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:6

**Q17. Who kept Abimelech from sinning?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:6

**Q18. What was Abimelech kept from doing?**  
**Answer:** Touching Sarah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:6

---

### Genesis 20:7
**Q19. Whom was Abimelech to restore?**  
**Answer:** Abraham's wife.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:7

**Q20. What was Abraham?**  
**Answer:** A prophet.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:7

**Q21. What would Abraham do for Abimelech?**  
**Answer:** Pray for him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:7

---

### Genesis 20:8
**Q22. When did Abimelech rise?**  
**Answer:** Early in the morning.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:8

**Q23. Whom did Abimelech call?**  
**Answer:** All his servants.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:8

**Q24. What did Abimelech tell them?**  
**Answer:** All these things.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:8

---

### Genesis 20:9
**Q25. Whom did Abimelech call?**  
**Answer:** Abraham.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:9

**Q26. What did Abimelech ask Abraham?**  
**Answer:** What hast thou done unto us?  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:9

**Q27. What had Abraham brought upon Abimelech?**  
**Answer:** A great sin.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:9

---

### Genesis 20:10
**Q28. What did Abimelech ask Abraham?**  
**Answer:** What sawest thou, that thou hast done this thing?  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:10

**Q29. What did Abimelech want to know?**  
**Answer:** Why Abraham did this thing.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:10

**Q30. Who asked Abraham this?**  
**Answer:** Abimelech.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:10

---

### Genesis 20:11
**Q31. What did Abraham say he thought?**  
**Answer:** Surely the fear of God is not in this place.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:11

**Q32. What did Abraham fear?**  
**Answer:** They would kill him for Sarah's sake.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:11

**Q33. Why did Abraham fear?**  
**Answer:** He thought there was no fear of God in the place.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:11

---

### Genesis 20:12
**Q34. What was Sarah also?**  
**Answer:** Abraham's sister.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:12

**Q35. Whose daughter was Sarah?**  
**Answer:** His father's daughter, but not his mother's daughter.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:12

**Q36. Whom had Abraham married?**  
**Answer:** Sarah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:12

---

### Genesis 20:13
**Q37. What did God cause Abraham to do?**  
**Answer:** Wander from his father's house.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:13

**Q38. What did Abraham ask Sarah to say?**  
**Answer:** He is my brother.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:13

**Q39. What did Abraham ask Sarah to show him?**  
**Answer:** Kindness.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:13

---

### Genesis 20:14
**Q40. What did Abimelech give Abraham?**  
**Answer:** Sheep, oxen, menservants, and womenservants.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:14

**Q41. Whom did Abimelech restore?**  
**Answer:** Sarah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:14

**Q42. What did Abimelech give besides servants?**  
**Answer:** Sheep and oxen.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:14

---

### Genesis 20:15
**Q43. What did Abimelech say was before Abraham?**  
**Answer:** His land.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:15

**Q44. Where could Abraham dwell?**  
**Answer:** Where it pleased him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:15

**Q45. Who offered Abraham the land?**  
**Answer:** Abimelech.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:15

---

### Genesis 20:16
**Q46. What did Abimelech give Sarah?**  
**Answer:** A thousand pieces of silver.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:16

**Q47. What was it to Sarah?**  
**Answer:** A covering of the eyes unto all that were with her.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:16

**Q48. What did Abimelech say Sarah was?**  
**Answer:** Reproved.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:16

---

### Genesis 20:17
**Q49. Who prayed unto God?**  
**Answer:** Abraham.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:17

**Q50. Whom did God heal?**  
**Answer:** Abimelech, his wife, and his maidservants.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:17

**Q51. What happened after Abraham prayed?**  
**Answer:** God healed Abimelech and his household.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:17

---

### Genesis 20:18
**Q52. What had the LORD fast closed?**  
**Answer:** All the wombs of Abimelech's house.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:18

**Q53. Why had the LORD closed the wombs?**  
**Answer:** Because of Sarah, Abraham's wife.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:18

**Q54. Whose house was affected?**  
**Answer:** Abimelech's.  
**Verse:** Genesis 20:18
