# Genesis 22 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 22:1
**Q1. Whom did God tempt?**  
**Answer:** Abraham.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:1

**Q2. What did God say to Abraham?**  
**Answer:** Take now thy son.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:1

**Q3. What did Abraham answer?**  
**Answer:** Behold, here I am.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:1

---

### Genesis 22:2
**Q4. Whom was Abraham told to take?**  
**Answer:** Isaac, his only son whom he loved.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:2

**Q5. Where was Abraham to offer Isaac?**  
**Answer:** Upon one of the mountains God would tell him of.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:2

**Q6. What was Abraham to do with Isaac?**  
**Answer:** Offer him for a burnt offering.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:2

---

### Genesis 22:3
**Q7. When did Abraham rise?**  
**Answer:** Early in the morning.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:3

**Q8. What did Abraham prepare?**  
**Answer:** The wood for the burnt offering.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:3

**Q9. Who went with Abraham?**  
**Answer:** Isaac and two young men.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:3

---

### Genesis 22:4
**Q10. On what day did Abraham see the place?**  
**Answer:** The third day.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:4

**Q11. What did Abraham see?**  
**Answer:** The place afar off.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:4

**Q12. Who saw the place?**  
**Answer:** Abraham.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:4

---

### Genesis 22:5
**Q13. Whom did Abraham tell to abide with the ass?**  
**Answer:** The young men.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:5

**Q14. Where would Abraham and Isaac go?**  
**Answer:** Yonder to worship.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:5

**Q15. What did Abraham say they would do?**  
**Answer:** Come again to the young men.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:5

---

### Genesis 22:6
**Q16. Who carried the wood?**  
**Answer:** Abraham laid it upon Isaac.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:6

**Q17. What did Abraham carry?**  
**Answer:** The fire and a knife.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:6

**Q18. Who went together?**  
**Answer:** Abraham and Isaac.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:6

---

### Genesis 22:7
**Q19. What did Isaac ask?**  
**Answer:** Where is the lamb for a burnt offering?  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:7

**Q20. Whom did Isaac call?**  
**Answer:** His father.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:7

**Q21. What did Abraham answer?**  
**Answer:** Here am I, my son.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:7

---

### Genesis 22:8
**Q22. Who would provide the lamb?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:8

**Q23. What would God provide?**  
**Answer:** A lamb for a burnt offering.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:8

**Q24. Who went together?**  
**Answer:** Abraham and Isaac.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:8

---

### Genesis 22:9
**Q25. What did Abraham build?**  
**Answer:** An altar.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:9

**Q26. What did Abraham bind?**  
**Answer:** Isaac his son.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:9

**Q27. Where did Abraham lay Isaac?**  
**Answer:** Upon the altar upon the wood.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:9

---

### Genesis 22:10
**Q28. What did Abraham take?**  
**Answer:** The knife.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:10

**Q29. What was Abraham preparing to do?**  
**Answer:** Slay his son.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:10

**Q30. Who was to be slain?**  
**Answer:** Isaac.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:10

---

### Genesis 22:11
**Q31. Who called to Abraham?**  
**Answer:** The angel of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:11

**Q32. From where did the angel call?**  
**Answer:** Out of heaven.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:11

**Q33. What did Abraham answer?**  
**Answer:** Here am I.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:11

---

### Genesis 22:12
**Q34. What was Abraham told not to do?**  
**Answer:** Lay his hand upon the lad.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:12

**Q35. What did God know about Abraham?**  
**Answer:** That he feared God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:12

**Q36. What had Abraham not withheld?**  
**Answer:** His son, his only son.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:12

---

### Genesis 22:13
**Q37. What did Abraham see behind him?**  
**Answer:** A ram caught in a thicket by his horns.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:13

**Q38. What did Abraham offer?**  
**Answer:** The ram for a burnt offering.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:13

**Q39. In whose stead was the ram offered?**  
**Answer:** In the stead of his son.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:13

---

### Genesis 22:14
**Q40. What did Abraham call the place?**  
**Answer:** Jehovah-jireh.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:14

**Q41. What would be said in the mount of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** It shall be seen.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:14

**Q42. Who named the place?**  
**Answer:** Abraham.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:14

---

### Genesis 22:15
**Q43. Who called to Abraham the second time?**  
**Answer:** The angel of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:15

**Q44. From where did the angel call?**  
**Answer:** Out of heaven.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:15

**Q45. How many times did the angel call?**  
**Answer:** The second time.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:15

---

### Genesis 22:16
**Q46. By whom did God swear?**  
**Answer:** By himself.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:16

**Q47. Why would God bless Abraham?**  
**Answer:** Because he had obeyed God's voice.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:16

**Q48. What had Abraham done?**  
**Answer:** He had not withheld his son.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:16

---

### Genesis 22:17
**Q49. How would God multiply Abraham's seed?**  
**Answer:** As the stars of heaven and the sand upon the sea shore.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:17

**Q50. What would Abraham's seed possess?**  
**Answer:** The gate of his enemies.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:17

**Q51. What would be multiplied?**  
**Answer:** Abraham's seed.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:17

---

### Genesis 22:18
**Q52. In whom would all nations be blessed?**  
**Answer:** In Abraham's seed.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:18

**Q53. Why would the nations be blessed?**  
**Answer:** Because Abraham obeyed God's voice.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:18

**Q54. Whose voice had Abraham obeyed?**  
**Answer:** God's voice.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:18

---

### Genesis 22:19
**Q55. Where did Abraham return?**  
**Answer:** Unto his young men.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:19

**Q56. Where did Abraham dwell?**  
**Answer:** At Beer-sheba.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:19

**Q57. Who went with Abraham?**  
**Answer:** His young men and Isaac.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:19

---

### Genesis 22:20
**Q58. Who told Abraham about Nahor?**  
**Answer:** It was told him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:20

**Q59. Who had borne children to Nahor?**  
**Answer:** Milcah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:20

**Q60. Who was Nahor's wife?**  
**Answer:** Milcah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:20

---

### Genesis 22:21
**Q61. Who was Uz's brother?**  
**Answer:** Buz.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:21

**Q62. Who was the father of Aram?**  
**Answer:** Kemuel.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:21

**Q63. Who were named after Uz and Buz?**  
**Answer:** Kemuel, Chesed, Hazo, Pildash, Jidlaph, and Bethuel.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:21

---

### Genesis 22:22
**Q64. Who were the sons of Nahor?**  
**Answer:** Chesed, Hazo, Pildash, Jidlaph, and Bethuel.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:22

**Q65. Who was named last?**  
**Answer:** Bethuel.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:22

**Q66. Who was Bethuel's father?**  
**Answer:** Nahor.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:22

---

### Genesis 22:23
**Q67. Who begat Rebekah?**  
**Answer:** Bethuel.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:23

**Q68. Who was Rebekah's father?**  
**Answer:** Bethuel.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:23

**Q69. Who were the eight children borne to Nahor?**  
**Answer:** The children of Milcah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:23

---

### Genesis 22:24
**Q70. Who was Nahor's concubine?**  
**Answer:** Reumah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:24

**Q71. Who did Reumah bear?**  
**Answer:** Tebah, Gaham, Thahash, and Maachah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:24

**Q72. How many children did Reumah bear?**  
**Answer:** Four.  
**Verse:** Genesis 22:24
