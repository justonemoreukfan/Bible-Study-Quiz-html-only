# Genesis 14 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 14:1
**Q1. Who were the kings named?**  
**Answer:** Amraphel, Arioch, Chedorlaomer, and Tidal.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:1

**Q2. What did these men have in common?**  
**Answer:** They were kings.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:1

**Q3. Who was king of nations?**  
**Answer:** Tidal.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:1

---

### Genesis 14:2
**Q4. Against whom did these kings make war?**  
**Answer:** Bera, Birsha, Shinab, Shemeber, and the king of Bela.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:2

**Q5. Who was king of Sodom?**  
**Answer:** Bera.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:2

**Q6. Who was king of Gomorrah?**  
**Answer:** Birsha.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:2

---

### Genesis 14:3
**Q7. Where did the kings join together?**  
**Answer:** In the vale of Siddim, which is the salt sea.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:3

**Q8. What is the vale of Siddim called?**  
**Answer:** The salt sea.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:3

**Q9. Who joined together?**  
**Answer:** The kings named in the passage.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:3

---

### Genesis 14:4
**Q10. How long had the kings served Chedorlaomer?**  
**Answer:** Twelve years.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:4

**Q11. What happened in the thirteenth year?**  
**Answer:** They rebelled.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:4

**Q12. Against whom did they rebel?**  
**Answer:** Chedorlaomer.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:4

---

### Genesis 14:5
**Q13. What did Chedorlaomer do in the fourteenth year?**  
**Answer:** He came and smote the Rephaim, Zuzims, Emims, and Horites.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:5

**Q14. Whom did he smite in Ashteroth Karnaim?**  
**Answer:** The Rephaim.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:5

**Q15. Whom did he smite in Shaveh Kiriathaim?**  
**Answer:** The Emims.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:5

---

### Genesis 14:6
**Q16. Whom did he smite in mount Seir?**  
**Answer:** The Horites.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:6

**Q17. Where did the Horites dwell?**  
**Answer:** In mount Seir.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:6

**Q18. Unto what wilderness did the kings pursue them?**  
**Answer:** The wilderness of El-paran.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:6

---

### Genesis 14:7
**Q19. Where did the kings return?**  
**Answer:** To En-mishpat, which is Kadesh.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:7

**Q20. Whom did they smite?**  
**Answer:** All the country of the Amalekites and the Amorites.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:7

**Q21. Where did the Amorites dwell?**  
**Answer:** In Hazezon-tamar.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:7

---

### Genesis 14:8
**Q22. Who went out to battle?**  
**Answer:** The king of Sodom, the king of Gomorrah, and their allies.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:8

**Q23. Where did they battle?**  
**Answer:** In the vale of Siddim.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:8

**Q24. Against whom did they battle?**  
**Answer:** Chedorlaomer and the kings with him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:8

---

### Genesis 14:9
**Q25. Who fought against the kings of Sodom and Gomorrah?**  
**Answer:** Chedorlaomer, Tidal, Amraphel, and Arioch.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:9

**Q26. How many kings fought against them?**  
**Answer:** Four kings against five.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:9

**Q27. Who were four against five?**  
**Answer:** The four kings fought the five kings.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:9

---

### Genesis 14:10
**Q28. What was full of slimepits?**  
**Answer:** The vale of Siddim.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:10

**Q29. What happened to the kings of Sodom and Gomorrah?**  
**Answer:** They fled and fell there.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:10

**Q30. Where did the remnant flee?**  
**Answer:** To the mountain.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:10

---

### Genesis 14:11
**Q31. What did the victorious kings take?**  
**Answer:** All the goods of Sodom and Gomorrah and all their victuals.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:11

**Q32. Whose goods were taken?**  
**Answer:** The goods of Sodom and Gomorrah.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:11

**Q33. What else did they take?**  
**Answer:** Their victuals.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:11

---

### Genesis 14:12
**Q34. Whom did they take?**  
**Answer:** Lot and his goods.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:12

**Q35. Where was Lot dwelling?**  
**Answer:** In Sodom.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:12

**Q36. Who was taken captive?**  
**Answer:** Abram's brother's son, Lot.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:12

---

### Genesis 14:13
**Q37. Who escaped and told Abram?**  
**Answer:** One that escaped.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:13

**Q38. Where did Abram dwell?**  
**Answer:** By the plain of Mamre the Amorite.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:13

**Q39. What was Abram's relation to Lot?**  
**Answer:** Lot was his brother's son.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:13

---

### Genesis 14:14
**Q40. What did Abram do when he heard Lot was taken?**  
**Answer:** He armed his trained servants and pursued them.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:14

**Q41. How many trained servants did Abram lead?**  
**Answer:** Three hundred and eighteen.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:14

**Q42. Unto what place did Abram pursue them?**  
**Answer:** Unto Dan.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:14

---

### Genesis 14:15
**Q43. How did Abram divide his servants?**  
**Answer:** He divided himself and his servants by night.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:15

**Q44. When did Abram smite the enemy?**  
**Answer:** By night.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:15

**Q45. Where did he pursue them?**  
**Answer:** Unto Hobah, which is on the left hand of Damascus.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:15

---

### Genesis 14:16
**Q46. What did Abram bring back?**  
**Answer:** All the goods, Lot, the women, and the people.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:16

**Q47. Who was brought back?**  
**Answer:** Lot.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:16

**Q48. What other people were brought back?**  
**Answer:** The women and the people.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:16

---

### Genesis 14:17
**Q49. Who went out to meet Abram?**  
**Answer:** The king of Sodom.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:17

**Q50. Where did he meet Abram?**  
**Answer:** At the valley of Shaveh, which is the king's dale.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:17

**Q51. When did he meet Abram?**  
**Answer:** After Abram returned from the slaughter of Chedorlaomer and the kings.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:17

---

### Genesis 14:18
**Q52. Who brought forth bread and wine?**  
**Answer:** Melchizedek.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:18

**Q53. What was Melchizedek?**  
**Answer:** King of Salem and priest of the most high God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:18

**Q54. What did Melchizedek bring?**  
**Answer:** Bread and wine.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:18

---

### Genesis 14:19
**Q55. Whom did Melchizedek bless?**  
**Answer:** Abram.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:19

**Q56. Who is the possessor of heaven and earth?**  
**Answer:** The most high God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:19

**Q57. What blessing did Abram receive?**  
**Answer:** Blessed be Abram of the most high God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:19

---

### Genesis 14:20
**Q58. Who delivered Abram's enemies into his hand?**  
**Answer:** The most high God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:20

**Q59. What did Abram give Melchizedek?**  
**Answer:** Tithes of all.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:20

**Q60. What did Abram give?**  
**Answer:** Tithes.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:20

---

### Genesis 14:21
**Q61. What did the king of Sodom offer Abram?**  
**Answer:** The persons, and that he should take the goods.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:21

**Q62. What did the king want returned?**  
**Answer:** The persons.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:21

**Q63. What could Abram take?**  
**Answer:** The goods.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:21

---

### Genesis 14:22
**Q64. To whom did Abram lift up his hand?**  
**Answer:** The LORD, the most high God.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:22

**Q65. What did Abram call the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The most high God, the possessor of heaven and earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:22

**Q66. What did Abram lift up?**  
**Answer:** His hand.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:22

---

### Genesis 14:23
**Q67. What would Abram not take?**  
**Answer:** From a thread even to a shoelatchet.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:23

**Q68. Why would Abram not take the goods?**  
**Answer:** Lest the king of Sodom should say he had made Abram rich.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:23

**Q69. What would Abram not take besides a thread?**  
**Answer:** A shoelatchet.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:23

---

### Genesis 14:24
**Q70. What would Abram take?**  
**Answer:** Only what the young men had eaten and the portion of Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:24

**Q71. Who would take their portion?**  
**Answer:** Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:24

**Q72. Who had eaten?**  
**Answer:** The young men.  
**Verse:** Genesis 14:24
