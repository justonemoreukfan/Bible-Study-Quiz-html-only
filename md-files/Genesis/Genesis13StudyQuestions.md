# Genesis 13 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Genesis 13:1
**Q1. Who went up out of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Abram, his wife, and Lot.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:1

**Q2. Where did Abram go?**  
**Answer:** Into the south.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:1

**Q3. What did Abram have?**  
**Answer:** Cattle, silver, and gold.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:1

---

### Genesis 13:2
**Q4. How rich was Abram?**  
**Answer:** Very rich in cattle, silver, and gold.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:2

**Q5. In what was Abram rich?**  
**Answer:** Cattle, silver, and gold.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:2

**Q6. Who was very rich?**  
**Answer:** Abram.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:2

---

### Genesis 13:3
**Q7. Where did Abram go from the south?**  
**Answer:** Unto Beth-el.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:3

**Q8. Where had his tent been at the first?**  
**Answer:** Between Beth-el and Hai.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:3

**Q9. Who journeyed?**  
**Answer:** Abram.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:3

---

### Genesis 13:4
**Q10. Where was the altar Abram made at the first?**  
**Answer:** Between Beth-el and Hai.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:4

**Q11. What did Abram do there?**  
**Answer:** Called on the name of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:4

**Q12. Whose name did Abram call upon?**  
**Answer:** The LORD's.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:4

---

### Genesis 13:5
**Q13. Who had flocks, herds, and tents?**  
**Answer:** Lot.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:5

**Q14. What did Lot have?**  
**Answer:** Flocks, herds, and tents.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:5

**Q15. Who went with Abram?**  
**Answer:** Lot.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:5

---

### Genesis 13:6
**Q16. What could not bear to dwell together?**  
**Answer:** The land.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:6

**Q17. Why could they not dwell together?**  
**Answer:** Their substance was great.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:6

**Q18. Who could not dwell together?**  
**Answer:** Abram and Lot.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:6

---

### Genesis 13:7
**Q19. What was between Abram's herdmen and Lot's herdmen?**  
**Answer:** Strife.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:7

**Q20. Who dwelt in the land then?**  
**Answer:** The Canaanite and the Perizzite.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:7

**Q21. What arose between the herdmen?**  
**Answer:** Strife.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:7

---

### Genesis 13:8
**Q22. What did Abram say should not be between them?**  
**Answer:** Strife.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:8

**Q23. Between whom should there be no strife?**  
**Answer:** Abram and Lot, and their herdmen.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:8

**Q24. What were Abram and Lot?**  
**Answer:** Brethren.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:8

---

### Genesis 13:9
**Q25. What was before Lot?**  
**Answer:** The whole land.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:9

**Q26. Where did Abram tell Lot to go?**  
**Answer:** To the left or right, as he chose.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:9

**Q27. What would Abram do if Lot went left?**  
**Answer:** He would go to the right.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:9

---

### Genesis 13:10
**Q28. What did Lot lift up his eyes and behold?**  
**Answer:** All the plain of Jordan.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:10

**Q29. How was the plain of Jordan?**  
**Answer:** Well watered every where.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:10

**Q30. Before what did the LORD destroy Sodom and Gomorrah?**  
**Answer:** Before the LORD destroyed them.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:10

---

### Genesis 13:11
**Q31. What did Lot choose?**  
**Answer:** All the plain of Jordan.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:11

**Q32. Which way did Lot journey?**  
**Answer:** East.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:11

**Q33. What happened to Abram and Lot?**  
**Answer:** They separated themselves.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:11

---

### Genesis 13:12
**Q34. Where did Abram dwell?**  
**Answer:** In the land of Canaan.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:12

**Q35. Where did Lot dwell?**  
**Answer:** In the cities of the plain.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:12

**Q36. Toward what did Lot pitch his tent?**  
**Answer:** Sodom.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:12

---

### Genesis 13:13
**Q37. How were the men of Sodom?**  
**Answer:** Wicked and sinners before the LORD exceedingly.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:13

**Q38. Before whom were they sinners?**  
**Answer:** Before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:13

**Q39. What was exceedingly great?**  
**Answer:** Their wickedness and sin.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:13

---

### Genesis 13:14
**Q40. Who said to Abram, Lift up now thine eyes?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:14

**Q41. In what directions was Abram to look?**  
**Answer:** Northward, southward, eastward, and westward.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:14

**Q42. When did the LORD speak?**  
**Answer:** After Lot was separated from him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:14

---

### Genesis 13:15
**Q43. What land would God give Abram?**  
**Answer:** All the land that he saw.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:15

**Q44. To whom would the land be given?**  
**Answer:** Abram and his seed for ever.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:15

**Q45. How long would Abram's seed possess the land?**  
**Answer:** For ever.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:15

---

### Genesis 13:16
**Q46. What would God make Abram's seed like?**  
**Answer:** The dust of the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:16

**Q47. Who could number the dust?**  
**Answer:** If any man could number the dust of the earth.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:16

**Q48. What would Abram's seed do?**  
**Answer:** Be numbered.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:16

---

### Genesis 13:17
**Q49. What was Abram told to arise and walk through?**  
**Answer:** The land.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:17

**Q50. In what directions was Abram to walk?**  
**Answer:** The length and breadth of the land.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:17

**Q51. Why was Abram to walk through the land?**  
**Answer:** For God would give it unto him.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:17

---

### Genesis 13:18
**Q52. Where did Abram move his tent?**  
**Answer:** To the plain of Mamre, which is in Hebron.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:18

**Q53. What did Abram build?**  
**Answer:** An altar unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:18

**Q54. Where was the altar?**  
**Answer:** In Hebron.  
**Verse:** Genesis 13:18
