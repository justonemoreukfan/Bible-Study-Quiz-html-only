# Obadiah 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Obadiah 1:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Obadiah 1:1?**  
**Answer:** The vision of Obadiah. Thus saith the Lord GOD concerning Edom.  
**Verse:** Obadiah 1:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Obadiah 1:1?**  
**Answer:** We have heard a rumour from the LORD, and an ambassador is sent among the heathen, Arise ye, and let us rise up against her in battle.  
**Verse:** Obadiah 1:1

**Q3. According to Obadiah 1:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** The vision of Obadiah. Thus saith the Lord GOD concerning Edom; We have heard a rumour from the LORD, and an ambassador is sent among the heathen, Arise ye, and let us rise up against her in battle.  
**Verse:** Obadiah 1:1
