# Exodus 20 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 20:1

**Q1. Who spake all these words?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:1

**Q2. What did God speak?**  
**Answer:** All these words.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:1

**Q3. Who spoke the words after saying "spake all these words"?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:1

---

### Exodus 20:2

**Q1. Who is speaking in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The Lord thy God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:2

**Q2. From what land did the Lord bring them out?**  
**Answer:** The land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:2

**Q3. From what condition did the Lord bring them out of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** The house of bondage.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:2

---

### Exodus 20:3

**Q1. Whose presence are you commanded not to have other gods before?**  
**Answer:** Me (the Lord's presence).  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:3

**Q2. What kind of gods are prohibited before Him?**  
**Answer:** Other gods.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:3

**Q3. How many other gods should be before Him?**  
**Answer:** None ("Thou shalt have no other gods before me").  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:3

---

### Exodus 20:4

**Q1. What are you commanded not to make unto yourself?**  
**Answer:** Any graven image.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:4

**Q2. What likeness should the graven image not resemble that is in heaven above?**  
**Answer:** Any likeness of anything that is in heaven above.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:4

**Q3. What likeness should the graven image not resemble that is in the earth beneath or in the water?**  
**Answer:** Anything that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the water under the earth.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:4

---

### Exodus 20:5

**Q1. What actions are forbidden regarding the graven images?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not bow down thyself to them, nor serve them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:5

**Q2. What kind of God is the Lord thy God declared to be in this verse?**  
**Answer:** A jealous God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:5

**Q3. Upon whom does the Lord visit the iniquity of the fathers?**  
**Answer:** Upon the children unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate me.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:5

---

### Exodus 20:6

**Q1. To how many generations does the Lord show mercy?**  
**Answer:** Unto thousands.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:6

**Q2. Who are the recipients of this mercy?**  
**Answer:** Them that love me, and keep my commandments.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:6

**Q3. What actions must be done by those who receive this mercy?**  
**Answer:** Love me, and keep my commandments.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:6

---

### Exodus 20:7

**Q1. Whose name shall you not take in vain?**  
**Answer:** The name of the Lord thy God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:7

**Q2. What will the Lord not hold him guiltless for?**  
**Answer:** That taketh his name in vain.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:7

**Q3. How will the Lord treat the person who takes His name in vain?**  
**Answer:** He will not hold him guiltless.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:7

---

### Exodus 20:8

**Q1. What day are you commanded to remember?**  
**Answer:** The sabbath day.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:8

**Q2. What must be done to the sabbath day?**  
**Answer:** Keep it holy.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:8

**Q3. Whose sabbath day is it mentioned to be?**  
**Answer:** The sabbath day (implied context of the Lord's commandments).  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:8

---

### Exodus 20:9

**Q1. How many days shall you labor?**  
**Answer:** Six days.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:9

**Q2. What should you do during those six days?**  
**Answer:** Labour, and do all thy work.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:9

**Q3. How much of your work should be done in those six days?**  
**Answer:** All thy work.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:9

---

### Exodus 20:10

**Q1. Whose sabbath is the seventh day?**  
**Answer:** The sabbath of the Lord thy God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:10

**Q2. Who is forbidden from doing any work on the seventh day?**  
**Answer:** Thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, thy manservant, nor thy maidservant, nor thy cattle, nor thy stranger that is within thy gates.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:10

**Q3. What kind of work is allowed to be done by these people on the seventh day?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not do any work.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:10

---

### Exodus 20:11

**Q1. In how many days did the Lord make heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them is?**  
**Answer:** In six days.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:11

**Q2. What did the Lord do on the seventh day?**  
**Answer:** He rested.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:11

**Q3. What did the Lord do to the sabbath day?**  
**Answer:** The Lord blessed the sabbath day, and hallowed it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:11

---

### Exodus 20:12

**Q1. Whom are you commanded to honor?**  
**Answer:** Thy father and thy mother.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:12

**Q2. What may be long upon the land?**  
**Answer:** Thy days.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:12

**Q3. Which land is specified in the promise?**  
**Answer:** The land which the Lord thy God giveth thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:12

---

### Exodus 20:13

**Q1. What is the command given in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not kill.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:13

**Q2. Who is commanded not to kill?**  
**Answer:** "Thou" (the person being addressed).  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:13

**Q3. Is there any exception listed in this verse for killing?**  
**Answer:** No.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:13

### Exodus 20:16

**Q1. What kind of witness shall you not bear?**  
**Answer:** False witness.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:16

**Q2. Against whom shall you not bear false witness?**  
**Answer:** Against thy neighbour.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:16

**Q3. Who is commanded not to bear false witness?**  
**Answer:** Thou.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:16

---

### Exodus 20:17

**Q1. What shall you not covet concerning your neighbor's house?**  
**Answer:** Thy neighbour's house.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:17

**Q2. What specific people belonging to your neighbor are you commanded not to covet?**  
**Answer:** Thy neighbour's wife, nor his manservant, nor his maidservant.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:17

**Q3. What animals and property belonging to your neighbor are you commanded not to covet?**  
**Answer:** Nor his ox, nor his ass, nor any thing that is thy neighbour's.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:17

### Exodus 20:18

**Q1. What sensory experiences did all the people perceive?**  
**Answer:** The thunderings, and the lightnings, and the noise of the trumpet, and the mountain smoking.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:18

**Q2. How did the people react when they saw these things?**  
**Answer:** They removed, and stood afar off.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:18

**Q3. What was the mountain doing?**  
**Answer:** Smoking.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:18

---

### Exodus 20:19

**Q1. To whom did the people speak?**  
**Answer:** Unto Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:19

**Q2. Who did the people want to speak with them instead of God?**  
**Answer:** Speak thou with us.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:19

**Q3. What did the people fear would happen if God spoke with them?**  
**Answer:** We will hear: but let not God speak with us, lest we die.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:19

---

### Exodus 20:20

**Q1. What did Moses tell the people not to do?**  
**Answer:** Fear not.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:20

**Q2. For what purpose did God come to prove them?**  
**Answer:** That his fear may be before your faces, that ye sin not.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:20

**Q3. Whose fear did Moses say should be before their faces?**  
**Answer:** His fear (God's fear).  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:20

**Q1. Where did the people stand?**

**Q2. Where did Moses draw near?**  
**Answer:** Unto the thick darkness where God was.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:21

**Q3. What was in the place where God was?**  
**Answer:** Thick darkness.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:21

### Exodus 20:22

**Q1. To whom did the Lord tell Moses to say these things?**  
**Answer:** Unto the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:22

**Q2. Who had talked with them from heaven?**  
**Answer:** Ye have seen that I have talked with you from heaven.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:22

**Q3. What were they forbidden to make with Him?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall not make with me gods of silver, neither shall ye make unto you gods of gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:22

---

### Exodus 20:23

**Q1. What kind of gods shall ye not make with Me?**  
**Answer:** Gods of silver, neither shall ye make unto you gods of gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:23

**Q2. In relation to God, where are these gods forbidden to be made?**  
**Answer:** With me (ye shall not make with me).  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:23

**Q3. Are both silver and gold gods prohibited?**  
**Answer:** Yes (gods of silver... gods of gold).  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:23

---

### Exodus 20:24

**Q1. What kind of altar of earth are you commanded to make unto Me?**  
**Answer:** An altar of earth.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:24

**Q2. What offerings are mentioned to be sacrificed on this altar?**  
**Answer:** Thy burnt offerings, and thy peace offerings, thy sheep, and thine oxen.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:24

**Q3. What will God do in all places where He records His name?**  
**Answer:** I will come unto thee, and I will bless thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:24

---

### Exodus 20:25

**Q1. If you make an altar of stone, what kind of stones must you not use?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not build it of hewn stone.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:25

**Q2. What happens to the altar if you lift up your tool upon it?**  
**Answer:** Thou hast polluted it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:25

**Q3. What tool is specifically mentioned as being lifted up upon the stones?**  
**Answer:** Thy tool.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:25

---

### Exodus 20:26

**Q1. What should you not go up by unto My altar?**  
**Answer:** By steps.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:26

**Q2. What is the reason given for not going up by steps to the altar?**  
**Answer:** That thy nakedness be not discovered thereon.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:26

**Q3. Whose nakedness might be discovered if steps are used?**  
**Answer:** Thy nakedness.  
**Verse:** Exodus 20:26
