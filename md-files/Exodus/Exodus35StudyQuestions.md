# Exodus 35 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 35:1

**Q1. Who did Moses gather together?**  
**Answer:** All the congregation of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 35:1

**Q2. What did Moses say unto them?**  
**Answer:** These are the words which the Lord hath commanded, that ye should do them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 35:1

**Q3. Who commanded the words that Moses spoke to the congregation?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 35:1

---

### Exodus 35:2

**Q1. How many days shall work be done?**  
**Answer:** Six days.  
**Verse:** Exodus 35:2

**Q2. What shall be to you on the seventh day?**  
**Answer:** An holy day, a sabbath of rest to the Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 35:2

**Q3. What shall happen to whosoever doeth work therein?**  
**Answer:** He shall be put to death.  
**Verse:** Exodus 35:2

---
