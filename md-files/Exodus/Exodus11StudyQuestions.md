# Exodus 11 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 11:1

**Q1. What did the LORD say He would yet bring upon Pharaoh and upon Egypt?**  
**Answer:** One plague more.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:1

**Q2. After bringing the plague upon them, what did the LORD say Pharaoh would do?**  
**Answer:** He will let you go hence.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:1

**Q3. When Pharaoh lets the people go, what will he do altogether?**  
**Answer:** Thrust you out hence altogether.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:1

---

### Exodus 11:2

**Q1. Who was told to speak in the ears of the people?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:2

**Q2. What were every man and every woman to borrow of his neighbor?**  
**Answer:** Jewels of silver, and jewels of gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:2

**Q3. From whom were the people to borrow these jewels?**  
**Answer:** His neighbour.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:2

---

### Exodus 11:3

**Q1. What did the LORD give the people in the sight of the Egyptians?**  
**Answer:** Favour.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:3

**Q2. What was the man Moses in the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Very great.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:3

**Q3. In whose sight was Moses very great?**  
**Answer:** In the sight of Pharaoh's servants, and in the sight of the people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:3

---

### Exodus 11:4

**Q1. Who spoke about going out into the midst of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:4

**Q2. At what time did Moses say he would go out into the midst of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** About midnight.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:4

**Q3. Who said, "About midnight will I go out"?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:4

---

### Exodus 11:5

**Q1. Who shall die in the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** All the firstborn in the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:5

**Q2. Whose firstborn shall die, sitting upon his throne?**  
**Answer:** The firstborn of Pharaoh that sitteth upon his throne.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:5

**Q3. Whose firstborn shall also die, who is behind the mill?**  
**Answer:** The firstborn of the maidservant that is behind the mill.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:5

---

### Exodus 11:6

**Q1. What shall be throughout all the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** A great cry.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:6

**Q2. How did Moses describe the cry throughout all the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Such as there was none like it, nor shall be like it any more.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:6

**Q3. What kind of cry shall there be?**  
**Answer:** A great cry.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:6

---

### Exodus 11:7

**Q1. Against whom shall not a dog move his tongue?**  
**Answer:** Against any of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:7

**Q2. Against whom else shall not a dog move his tongue?**  
**Answer:** Against man or beast.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:7

**Q3. What might ye know that the LORD doth?**  
**Answer:** How that the LORD doth put a difference between the Egyptians and Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:7

---

### Exodus 11:8

**Q1. Who shall come down unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** All these thy servants.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:8

**Q2. What shall these servants do unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** Bow down themselves unto me.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:8

**Q3. After this, what did Moses say he would do?**  
**Answer:** Get thee out.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:8

---

### Exodus 11:9

**Q1. What did the LORD say unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh shall not hearken unto you.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:9

**Q2. Why would Pharaoh not hearken unto them?**  
**Answer:** That my wonders may be multiplied in the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:9

**Q3. Whose wonders were to be multiplied?**  
**Answer:** My wonders.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:9

---

### Exodus 11:10

**Q1. What did Moses and Aaron do before Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** All these wonders.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:10

**Q2. What did the LORD harden?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh's heart.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:10

**Q3. What did Pharaoh not do because his heart was hardened?**  
**Answer:** Would not let the children of Israel go out of his land.  
**Verse:** Exodus 11:10
