# Exodus 7 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 7:1

**Q1. Who did the Lord say He had made unto Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** A god.  
**Verse:** Exodus 7:1

**Q2. Who did the Lord say was Aaron?**  
**Answer:** Thy prophet.  
**Verse:** Exodus 7:1

**Q3. Whose prophet was Aaron to be?**  
**Answer:** Moses'.  
**Verse:** Exodus 7:1

---

### Exodus 7:2

**Q4. What was Moses to speak?**  
**Answer:** All that I command thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 7:2

**Q5. What was Aaron's duty regarding Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** Speak unto Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 7:2

**Q6. What must Pharaoh send out of his land according to the verse?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 7:2

---

### Exodus 7:3

**Q7. Whose heart will the Lord harden?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh's heart.  
**Verse:** Exodus 7:3

**Q8. What will the Lord multiply in the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** His signs and His wonders.  
**Verse:** Exodus 7:3

**Q9. Where will the Lord multiply His signs and wonders?**  
**Answer:** In the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 7:3

---

### Exodus 7:4

**Q10. Who will not hearken unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 7:4

**Q11. Upon whom will the Lord lay His hand?**  
**Answer:** Upon Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 7:4

**Q12. What will the Lord bring forth out of the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** His armies, and His people the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 7:4

---

### Exodus 7:5

**Q13. Who shall know that the Lord is the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The Egyptians.  
**Verse:** Exodus 7:5

**Q14. When shall the Egyptians know that the Lord is the LORD?**  
**Answer:** When I stretch forth mine hand upon Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 7:5

**Q15. Whom will the Lord bring out from among the Egyptians?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 7:5

**Answer:** By the river's brink.

**Verse:** Exodus 7:15

**Answer:** The Lord God of the Hebrews.  
**Verse:** Exodus 7:16

**Answer:** Let my people go, that they may serve me in the wilderness.

**Verse:** Exodus 7:16

### Exodus 7:17

**Q49. By what will Pharaoh know that the Lord is the LORD?**  
**Answer:** By this.  
**Verse:** Exodus 7:17

**Q50. What will Moses smite with the rod in his hand?**  
**Answer:** The waters which are in the river.

**Verse:** Exodus 7:17
