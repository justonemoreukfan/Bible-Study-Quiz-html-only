# Exodus 34 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 34:1

**Q1. What did the LORD say unto Moses to hew out?**  
**Answer:** Two tables of stone like unto the first.  
**Verse:** Exodus 34:1

**Q2. What would the LORD write upon these tables?**  
**Answer:** The words that were in the first tables, which thou brakest.  
**Verse:** Exodus 34:1

**Q3. When was Moses to be ready in the morning?**  
**Answer:** In the morning.  
**Verse:** Exodus 34:1

---
