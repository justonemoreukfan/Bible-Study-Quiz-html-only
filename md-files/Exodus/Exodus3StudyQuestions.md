# Exodus 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 3:1

**Q1. Whose flock did Moses keep?**  
**Answer:** Jethro his father in law, the priest of Midian.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:1

**Q2. Where did Moses lead the flock to?**  
**Answer:** To the backside of the desert.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:1

**Q3. To what mountain did Moses come?**  
**Answer:** The mountain of God, even to Horeb.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:1

---

### Exodus 3:2

**Q4. Who appeared unto Moses in a flame of fire?**  
**Answer:** The angel of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:2

**Q5. Out of the midst of what object did the angel of the Lord appear?**  
**Answer:** Out of the midst of a bush.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:2

**Q6. What was unique about the bush as it burned with fire?**  
**Answer:** The bush was not consumed.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:2

---

### Exodus 3:3

**Q7. What did Moses say he would now turn aside and see?**  
**Answer:** This great sight.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:3

**Q8. What reason did Moses give for turning aside to see this sight?**  
**Answer:** Why the bush is not burnt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:3

---

### Exodus 3:4

**Q9. Who saw that Moses turned aside to see?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:4

**Q10. From out of the midst of what did God call unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** Out of the midst of the bush.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:4

**Q11. What did God call Moses?**  
**Answer:** Moses, Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:4

---

### Exodus 3:5

**Q12. What did God tell Moses not to do?**  
**Answer:** Draw not nigh hither.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:5

**Q13. What did God tell Moses to put off from his feet?**  
**Answer:** Thy shoes.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:5

**Q14. Why was Moses told to put off his shoes?**  
**Answer:** Because the place whereon thou standest is holy ground.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:5

---

### Exodus 3:6

**Q15. How did God identify Himself to Moses by family?**  
**Answer:** I am the God of thy father, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:6

**Q16. What did Moses do with his face?**  
**Answer:** Moses hid his face.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:6

**Q17. Why did Moses hide his face?**  
**Answer:** For he was afraid to look upon God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:6

---

### Exodus 3:7

**Q18. What had the Lord surely seen?**  
**Answer:** The affliction of my people which are in Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:7

**Q19. What had the Lord heard regarding His people?**  
**Answer:** Their cry by reason of their taskmasters.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:7

**Q20. What did the Lord say He knew?**  
**Answer:** Their sorrows.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:7

---

### Exodus 3:8

**Q21. To do what had the Lord come down?**  
**Answer:** To deliver them out of the hand of the Egyptians.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:8

**Q22. Where was the Lord going to bring them up out of?**  
**Answer:** Out of that land.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:8

**Q23. Unto what kind of land was He going to bring them?**  
**Answer:** A good land and a large, a land flowing with milk and honey.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:8

---

### Exodus 3:9

**Q24. What had now come unto the Lord?**  
**Answer:** The cry of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:9

**Q25. What else had the Lord seen?**  
**Answer:** The oppression wherewith the Egyptians oppress them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:9

---

### Exodus 3:10

**Q26. Who was the Lord going to send unto Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:10

**Q27. Why was Moses being sent unto Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** That thou mayest bring forth my people the children of Israel out of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:10

---

### Exodus 3:11

**Q28. What question did Moses ask God regarding going unto Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** Who am I, that I should go unto Pharaoh?  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:11

**Q29. What second question did Moses ask God regarding the Israelites?**  
**Answer:** And that I should bring forth the children of Israel out of Egypt?  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:11

---

### Exodus 3:12

**Q30. Who did God say would certainly be with Moses?**  
**Answer:** I will be with thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:12

**Q31. What would be a token unto Moses that God had sent him?**  
**Answer:** When thou hast brought forth the people out of Egypt, ye shall serve God upon this mountain.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:12

---

### Exodus 3:13

**Q32. What did Moses say the children of Israel would ask him?**  
**Answer:** What is his name?  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:13

---

### Exodus 3:14

**Q33. What did God say unto Moses concerning His name?**  
**Answer:** I AM THAT I AM.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:14

**Q34. What did God tell Moses to say to the children of Israel regarding "I AM"?**  
**Answer:** I AM has sent me unto you.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:14

---

### Exodus 3:15

**Q35. Whose God did God say He was to the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:15

**Q36. What did God call His name for ever?**  
**Answer:** This is my name for ever.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:15

**Q37. What did God call His memorial unto all generations?**  
**Answer:** This is my memorial unto all generations.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:15

---

### Exodus 3:16

**Q38. Who was Moses to gather together?**  
**Answer:** The elders of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:16

**Q39. Whose God was the Lord that appeared unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** The God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, of Isaac, and of Jacob.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:16

**Q40. What did the Lord say He had surely visited?**  
**Answer:** You, and that which is done to you in Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:16

---

### Exodus 3:17

**Q41. What did the Lord promise to bring them up out of?**  
**Answer:** Out of the affliction of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:17

**Q42. Unto the land of whom was the Lord going to bring them?**  
**Answer:** The Canaanites, and the Hittites, and the Amorites, and the Perizzites, and the Hivites, and the Jebusites.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:17

**Q43. What did the land flow with?**  
**Answer:** Milk and honey.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:17

---

### Exodus 3:18

**Q44. Whose voice would hearken unto the voice of Moses?**  
**Answer:** The elders of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:18

**Q45. Unto whom were they to go together?**  
**Answer:** Unto the king of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:18

**Q46. What distance did they want to go into the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** Three days' journey.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:18

---

### Exodus 3:19

**Q47. What did the king of Egypt not let them do?**  
**Answer:** Not go.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:19

**Q48. Under what condition would the king of Egypt let them go?**  
**Answer:** No, not by a mighty hand.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:19

---

### Exodus 3:20

**Q49. What did the Lord say He would stretch out?**  
**Answer:** His hand.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:20

**Q50. Upon whom would the Lord smite with all His wonders?**  
**Answer:** Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:20

**Q51. What would happen after the wonders?**  
**Answer:** After that he will let you go.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:20

---

### Exodus 3:21

**Q52. What would the Lord give this people in the sight of the Egyptians?**  
**Answer:** Favour.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:21

**Q53. What would happen when the people go?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall not go empty.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:21

---

### Exodus 3:22

**Q54. Every woman was to borrow of whom?**  
**Answer:** Her neighbour, and of her that sojourneth in her house.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:22

**Q55. What specific items were the women to borrow?**  
**Answer:** Jewels of silver, and jewels of gold, and raiment.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:22

**Q56. Upon whom were they to put the borrowed items?**  
**Answer:** Upon your sons, and upon your daughters.  
**Verse:** Exodus 3:22
