# Exodus 29 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 29:1

**Q1. What are Moses and Aaron to take to hallow them for the priest's office?**  
**Answer:** One young bullock, and two rams without blemish.  
**Verse:** Exodus 29:1

**Q2. What kind of bread shall be taken with the animals?**  
**Answer:** Unleavened bread.  
**Verse:** Exodus 29:1

**Q3. What kind of things shall be made with oil?**  
**Answer:** Cakes unleavened tempered with oil, and wafers unleavened anointed with oil.  
**Verse:** Exodus 29:1
