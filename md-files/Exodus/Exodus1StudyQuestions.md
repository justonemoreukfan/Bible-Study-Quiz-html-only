# Exodus 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 1:1

**Q1. Whose names came with Jacob into Egypt?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:1

**Q2. With how many sons did every man come with Jacob?**  
**Answer:** His household.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:1

**Q3. Into what place did the children of Israel come with Jacob?**  
**Answer:** Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:1

---

### Exodus 1:2

**Q4. Who are named first among those who came with Jacob?**  
**Answer:** Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:2

**Q5. Which sons of Jacob are listed together in Exodus 1:2?**  
**Answer:** Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:2

**Q6. Whose sons are Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Jacob's.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:2

---

### Exodus 1:3

**Q7. Which sons of Jacob are mentioned in Exodus 1:3?**  
**Answer:** Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:3

**Q8. Whose sons are listed in Exodus 1:3 alongside Issachar and Zebulun?**  
**Answer:** Benjamin.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:3

**Q9. Who is the father of Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin according to the context?**  
**Answer:** Jacob.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:3

---

### Exodus 1:4

**Q10. Which sons of Jacob are named in Exodus 1:4?**  
**Answer:** Dan, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:4

**Q11. Who are grouped together with Dan and Naphtali in verse 4?**  
**Answer:** Gad and Asher.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:4

**Q12. Whose children or sons are Dan, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher?**  
**Answer:** Jacob's.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:4

---

### Exodus 1:5

**Q13. All the souls that came out of the loins of whom were seventy souls?**  
**Answer:** Jacob.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:5

**Q14. Who was already in Egypt when the seventy souls came?**  
**Answer:** Joseph.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:5

**Q15. How many souls came out of the loins of Jacob in total?**  
**Answer:** Seventy souls.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:5

---

### Exodus 1:6

**Q16. Who died in Exodus 1:6?**  
**Answer:** Joseph, and all his brethren, and all that generation.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:6

**Q17. Besides Joseph, who else died?**  
**Answer:** All his brethren, and all that generation.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:6

**Q18. What happened to Joseph, all his brethren, and all that generation?**  
**Answer:** They died.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:6

---

### Exodus 1:7

**Q19. What did the children of Israel do regarding their population?**  
**Answer:** Were fruitful, and increased abundantly, and multiplied, and waxed exceeding mighty.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:7

**Q20. What happened to the land with the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The land was filled with them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:7

**Q21. How strong did the children of Israel wax?**  
**Answer:** Exceeding mighty.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:7

---

### Exodus 1:8

**Q22. Who arose over Egypt in Exodus 1:8?**  
**Answer:** A new king.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:8

**Q23. What did the new king not know?**  
**Answer:** Joseph.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:8

**Q24. Over what country did the new king arise?**  
**Answer:** Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:8

---

### Exodus 1:9

**Q25. To whom did the king speak concerning the people?**  
**Answer:** His people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:9

**Q26. Whose people did the king say were more and mightier than they?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:9

**Q27. Compared to the Egyptians, how did the king describe the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** More and mightier.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:9

---

### Exodus 1:10

**Q28. What did the king suggest they do with the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Deal wisely with them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:10

**Q29. What reason did the king give for dealing wisely with them, lest they do what?**  
**Answer:** Multiply.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:10

**Q30. What might happen in the event of war if they are not dealt with wisely?**  
**Answer:** They join also unto our enemies, and fight against us, and so get them up out of the land.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:10

---

### Exodus 1:11

**Q31. Whom did the Egyptians set over the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Taskmasters.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:11

**Q32. With what did the taskmasters afflict the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Their burdens.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:11

**Q33. What treasure cities did the children of Israel build for Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** Pithom and Raamses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:11

---

### Exodus 1:12

**Q34. What happened the more the Egyptians afflicted the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The more they multiplied and grew.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:12

**Q35. How did the Egyptians feel because of the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** They were grieved.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:12

**Q36. Because the Egyptians were grieved, what did they do regarding the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Dreaded them / were grieved because of them. *(Note: KJV text says "And they were grieved because of the children of Israel.")*  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:12

---

### Exodus 1:13

**Q37. How did the Egyptians make the children of Israel serve?**  
**Answer:** With rigour.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:13

**Q38. Who made the children of Israel serve with rigour?**  
**Answer:** The Egyptians.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:13

**Q39. Whom did the Egyptians make serve with rigour?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:13

---

### Exodus 1:14

**Q40. What kind of life did the Egyptians make their lives bitter with?**  
**Answer:** Hard bondage.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:14

**Q41. In what types of work did the Egyptians make the children of Israel serve?**  
**Answer:** In morter, and in brick, and in all manner of service in the field.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:14

**Q42. How were all their service wherein they made them serve characterized?**  
**Answer:** With rigour.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:14

---

### Exodus 1:15

**Q43. To whom did the king of Egypt speak?**  
**Answer:** The Hebrew midwives.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:15

**Q44. What was the name of the first midwife mentioned?**  
**Answer:** Shiphrah.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:15

**Q5. What was the name of the second midwife mentioned?**  
**Answer:** Puah.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:15

---

### Exodus 1:16

**Q45. When the midwives perform the office of a midwife to the Hebrew women, what should they look upon?**  
**Answer:** The stools.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:16

**Q46. If the child is a son, what should the midwives do?**  
**Answer:** Kill him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:16

**Q47. If the child is a daughter, what should happen?**  
**Answer:** Then she shall live.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:16

---

### Exodus 1:17

**Q48. Whom did the midwives fear?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:17

**Q49. What did the midwives not do as the king of Egypt commanded them?**  
**Answer:** They did not as the king of Egypt commanded them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:17

**Q50. What did the midwives save alive?**  
**Answer:** The men children.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:17

---

### Exodus 1:18

**Q51. Who called for the midwives?**  
**Answer:** The king of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:18

**Q52. What did the king ask the midwives?**  
**Answer:** Why have ye done this thing, and have saved the men children alive?  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:18

**Q53. Whose children did the king accuse the midwives of saving alive?**  
**Answer:** The men children.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:18

---

### Exodus 1:19

**Q54. What did the midwives say about the Hebrew women compared to the Egyptian women?**  
**Answer:** They are not as the Egyptian women.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:19

**Q55. How are the Hebrew women described in relation to giving birth?**  
**Answer:** They are lively.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:19

**Q56. When do the Hebrew women give birth according to the midwives?**  
**Answer:** Ere the midwives come in unto them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:19

---

### Exodus 1:20

**Q57. How did God deal with the midwives?**  
**Answer:** Well.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:20

**Q58. What did the people do in the land?**  
**Answer:** Multiplied, and waxed very mighty.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:20

**Q59. Who multiplied and waxed very mighty because God dealt well with the midwives?**  
**Answer:** The people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:20

---

### Exodus 1:21

**Q60. Why did God make houses for the midwives?**  
**Answer:** Because the midwives feared God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:21

**Q61. What did God make for the midwives?**  
**Answer:** Houses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:21

**Q62. Who made houses for the midwives?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:21

---

### Exodus 1:22

**Q63. To whom did Pharaoh charge all his people?**  
**Answer:** All his people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:22

**Q64. Where were the people commanded to cast every son that is born?**  
**Answer:** Into the river.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:22

**Q65. What were the people commanded to save alive?**  
**Answer:** Every daughter.  
**Verse:** Exodus 1:22
