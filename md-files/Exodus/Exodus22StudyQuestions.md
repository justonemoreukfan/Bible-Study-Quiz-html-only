# Exodus 22 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 22:1

**Q1. What must a man do if he shall steal an ox, or a sheep, and kill it, or sell it?**  
**Answer:** He shall restore five oxen for an ox, and four sheep for a sheep.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:1

**Q2. What animals are specifically mentioned in this verse regarding theft?**  
**Answer:** An ox, or a sheep.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:1

**Q3. What actions regarding the stolen animal require a multiple restitution?**  
**Answer:** If he kill it, or sell it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:1

---

### Exodus 22:2

**Q1. Where should a thief be found if he is smitten that he die?**  
**Answer:** Breaking up.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:2

**Q2. Whose blood shall not be shed for the death of a thief caught breaking up?**  
**Answer:** No blood shall be shed for him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:2

**Q3. What happens to the thief if he is found breaking up and is smitten so that he dies?**  
**Answer:** There shall no blood be shed for him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:2

---

### Exodus 22:3

**Q1. When shall blood be shed for a thief?**  
**Answer:** If the sun be risen upon him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:3

**Q2. What should the thief make full restitution for?**  
**Answer:** If he have nothing, then he shall be sold for his theft.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:3

**Q3. What happens if the thief has nothing with which to make restitution?**  
**Answer:** He shall be sold for his theft.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:3

---

### Exodus 22:4

**Q1. Where must the theft be found alive in the thief's hand?**  
**Answer:** If the theft be certainly found in his hand alive.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:4

**Q2. Which specific animals are listed among the possible living thefts found in the hand?**  
**Answer:** Whether ox, or ass, or sheep.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:4

**Q3. How much shall the thief restore if the stolen animal is found alive?**  
**Answer:** He shall restore double.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:4

---

### Exodus 22:5

**Q1. What action does a man do to a field or vineyard that causes a beast to feed?**  
**Answer:** Shall cause a field or vineyard to be eaten.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:5

**Q2. Where shall the beast feed when let loose by a man?**  
**Answer:** In another man's field.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:5

**Q3. With what shall the restitution be made for the beast feeding in another man's field?**  
**Answer:** Of the best of his own field, and of the best of his own vineyard, shall he make restitution.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:5

---

### Exodus 22:6

**Q1. What causes the fire to catch thorns?**  
**Answer:** If fire break out, and catch in thorns.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:6

**Q2. What gets consumed along with the thorns when the fire catches?**  
**Answer:** So that the stacks of corn, or the standing corn, or the field, be consumed therewith.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:6

**Q3. Who shall surely make restitution for the fire that consumed the corn or field?**  
**Answer:** He that kindled the fire.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:6

---

### Exodus 22:7

**Q1. What does a man deliver unto his neighbour to keep?**  
**Answer:** Money or stuff.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:7

**Q2. From where is the money or stuff stolen when delivered to a neighbour?**  
**Answer:** Out of the man's house.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:7

**Q3. What must the thief do if he is found regarding the stolen money or stuff?**  
**Answer:** If the thief be found, let him pay double.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:7

---

### Exodus 22:8

**Q1. What happens if the thief is not found?**  
**Answer:** Then the master of the house shall be brought unto the judges.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:8

**Q2. Before whom shall the master of the house be brought?**  
**Answer:** Unto the judges.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:8

**Q3. To find out what shall the master of the house be brought before the judges?**  
**Answer:** To see whether he have put his hand unto his neighbour's goods.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:8

---

### Exodus 22:9

**Q1. For what kinds of trespass does this verse apply?**  
**Answer:** For all manner of trespass, whether it be for ox, for ass, for sheep, for raiment, or for any manner of lost thing.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:9

**Q2. Before whom shall the cause of both parties come?**  
**Answer:** The cause of both parties shall come before the judges.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:9

**Q3. What shall the judges do to whom the parties are condemned?**  
**Answer:** Whom the judges shall condemn, he shall pay double unto his neighbour.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:9

---

### Exodus 22:10

**Q1. What animal does a man deliver unto his neighbour to keep?**  
**Answer:** An ass, or an ox, or a sheep, or any beast.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:10

**Q2. What can happen to the beast while in the neighbour's keeping?**  
**Answer:** Die, or be hurt, or driven away.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:10

**Q3. Who sees no man when the beast dies, is hurt, or driven away?**  
**Answer:** No man seeing it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:10

---

### Exodus 22:11

**Q1. By what shall an oath of the Lord be between them?**  
**Answer:** That he hath not put his hand unto his neighbour's goods.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:11

**Q2. Who shall accept the oath of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** The owner of it shall accept thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:11

**Q3. What shall the owner not do if the oath is taken?**  
**Answer:** He shall not make it good.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:11

---

### Exodus 22:12

**Q1. From whom shall the keeper make restitution if the animal is stolen from him?**  
**Answer:** Unto the owner thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:12

**Q2. What happened to the animal for this restitution to be required?**  
**Answer:** If it be stolen from him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:12

**Q3. Whose property must the stolen animal be?**  
**Answer:** Stolen from him. (Belonging to the owner)  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:12

---

### Exodus 22:13

**Q1. What happens to the animal if it be torn in pieces?**  
**Answer:** Then let him bring it for witness.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:13

**Q2. What shall the keeper not make good if the animal is torn in pieces?**  
**Answer:** He shall not make good that which was torn.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:13

**Q3. What must be brought as witness for the torn animal?**  
**Answer:** Let him bring it for witness.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:13

---

### Exodus 22:14

**Q1. What does a man borrow of his neighbour?**  
**Answer:** Ought of his neighbour.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:14

**Q2. What condition must be met for the borrower to not make it good?**  
**Answer:** If the owner thereof be not with it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:14

**Q3. What happens if the borrowed thing be hurt, or die?**  
**Answer:** The owner thereof being not with it, he shall surely make it good.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:14

---

### Exodus 22:15

**Q1. When shall the borrower not make it good if the thing is hurt or dies?**  
**Answer:** If the owner thereof be with it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:15

**Q2. What was it if it came with the hire?**  
**Answer:** It came for his hire.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:15

**Q3. What condition applies if the thing was hired?**  
**Answer:** It came for his hire.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:15

**Verse:** Exodus 22:16

**Answer:** He shall surely endow her to be his wife.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:16

**Q3. What status must the maid have regarding betrothal?**  
**Answer:** Not betrothed.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:16

**Verse:** Exodus 22:17

**Q2. What must the man pay according to the dowry of virgins?**  
**Answer:** He shall pay money.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:17

**Verse:** Exodus 22:17

**Answer:** He shall be utterly destroyed.

**Verse:** Exodus 22:20

**Answer:** If thou afflict them in any wise.

**Answer:** I will surely hear their cry.

**Verse:** Exodus 22:23

**Answer:** I will kill you with the sword.  
**Verse:** Exodus 22:24
