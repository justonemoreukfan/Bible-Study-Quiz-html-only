# Exodus 10 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 10:1

**Q1. Whose heart did the Lord say He had hardened?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh's heart.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:1

**Q2. Whose heart did the Lord also say He had hardened, and why?**  
**Answer:** The heart of his servants, that I might show these my signs among them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:1

**Q3. What did the Lord want to tell in the ears of thy son, and thy son's son?**  
**Answer:** What things I have wrought in Egypt, and my signs which I have done among them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:1

---

### Exodus 10:2

**Q1. What did the Lord want every man to tell in the ears of his son and his son's son?**  
**Answer:** What things I have wrought in Egypt, and my signs which I have done among them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:2

**Q2. Why did the Lord want these things told to their sons and grandsons?**  
**Answer:** That ye may know how that I am the Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:2

**Q3. What would they know after these signs were told and done?**  
**Answer:** That I am the Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:2

---

### Exodus 10:3

**Q1. Who went in unto Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** Moses and Aaron.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:3

**Q2. What did Moses and Aaron say the Lord God of the Hebrews asked?**  
**Answer:** How long wilt thou refuse to humble thyself before me?  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:3

**Q3. What did the Lord God command Pharaoh to do so that they might serve Him?**  
**Answer:** Let my people go, that they may serve me.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:3

---

### Exodus 10:4

**Q1. What did Moses and Aaron warn would happen if Pharaoh refused to let the people go?**  
**Answer:** Tomorrow will I bring the locusts into thy coast.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:4

---

### Exodus 10:5

**Q1. What would the locusts cover?**  
**Answer:** The face of the earth.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:5

**Q2. What would happen to that which remained after the hail?**  
**Answer:** They shall eat the residue of that which is escaped, which remaineth unto you from the hail.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:5

**Q3. What trees would the locusts eat?**  
**Answer:** Every tree which groweth for you out of the field.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:5

---

### Exodus 10:6

**Q1. Whose houses would be filled with the locusts?**  
**Answer:** Thy houses, and the houses of all thy servants, and the houses of all the Egyptians.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:6

**Q2. What did the text say neither thy fathers nor thy fathers' fathers had seen?**  
**Answer:** Which neither thy fathers have seen, nor thy fathers' fathers, since the day that they were upon the earth unto this day.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:6

**Q3. What did Moses do after speaking to Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** And he turned himself, and went out from Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:6

---

### Exodus 10:7

**Q1. Who spoke to Pharaoh and asked how long this man would be a snare unto them?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh's servants.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:7

**Q2. What did Pharaoh's servants ask him to let the men do?**  
**Answer:** Let the men go, that they may serve the Lord their God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:7

**Q3. What question did Pharaoh's servants ask regarding Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Knowest thou not yet that Egypt is destroyed?  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:7

---

### Exodus 10:8

**Q1. Who was brought again unto Moses and Aaron?**  
**Answer:** Moses and Aaron.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:8

**Q2. What did Pharaoh say to Moses and Aaron when they were brought back?**  
**Answer:** Go, serve the Lord your God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:8

**Q3. What specific question did Pharaoh ask regarding who shall go?**  
**Answer:** But who are they that shall go?  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:8

---

### Exodus 10:9

**Q1. Who did Moses say must go with them?**  
**Answer:** With our young and with our old, with our sons and with our daughters, with our flocks and with our herds.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:9

**Q2. Why did Moses say they must go with all their people and cattle?**  
**Answer:** For we must hold a feast unto the Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:9

---

### Exodus 10:10

**Q1. What did Pharaoh say the Lord would be if he let them go with their little ones?**  
**Answer:** Let the Lord be so with you, as I will let you go, and your little ones.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:10

**Q2. What did Pharaoh accuse Moses of intending before him?**  
**Answer:** Look to it; for evil is before you.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:10

---

### Exodus 10:11

**Q1. Who did Pharaoh say should go and serve the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Not so: go now ye that are men, and serve the Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:11

**Q2. What reason did Pharaoh give for allowing only the men to go?**  
**Answer:** For that ye did desire.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:11

**Q3. What happened to Moses and Aaron after Pharaoh spoke?**  
**Answer:** And they were driven out from Pharaoh's presence.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:11

---

### Exodus 10:12

**Q1. What did the Lord say unto Moses regarding the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Stretch out thine hand over the land of Egypt for the locusts.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:12

**Q2. What would the locusts come up upon?**  
**Answer:** Upon the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:12

**Q3. What would the locusts eat in the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** And eat every herb of the land, even all that the hail hath left.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:12

---

### Exodus 10:13

**Q1. What did Moses stretch forth over the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** His rod over the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:13

**Q2. What wind did the Lord bring upon the land all that day and all that night?**  
**Answer:** An east wind.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:13

**Q3. What did the east wind bring when it was morning?**  
**Answer:** Brought the locusts.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:13

---

### Exodus 10:14

**Q1. Where did the locusts go up?**  
**Answer:** Over all the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:14

**Q2. Where did the locusts rest?**  
**Answer:** In all the coasts of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:14

---

### Exodus 10:15

**Q1. What did the locusts cover?**  
**Answer:** The face of the whole earth.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:15

**Q2. What happened to the land because of the locusts?**  
**Answer:** So that the land was darkened.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:15

**Q3. What did the locusts eat from the land and trees?**  
**Answer:** Did eat every herb of the land, and all the fruit of the trees which the hail had left.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:15

---

### Exodus 10:16

**Q1. Who did Pharaoh call for in haste?**  
**Answer:** Moses and Aaron.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:16

**Q2. What did Pharaoh confess he had sinned against?**  
**Answer:** Against the Lord your God, and against you.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:16

---

### Exodus 10:17

**Q1. What did Pharaoh ask Moses and Aaron to forgive?**  
**Answer:** Now therefore forgive, I pray thee, my sin only this once.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:17

**Q2. What did Pharaoh ask Moses and Aaron to entreat?**  
**Answer:** And intreat the Lord your God, that he may take away from me this death only.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:17

---

### Exodus 10:18

**Q1. Who did Moses go out from?**  
**Answer:** From Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:18

**Q2. Who did Moses entreat after leaving Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** And intreated the Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:18

---

### Exodus 10:19

**Q1. What kind of wind did the Lord turn?**  
**Answer:** A mighty strong west wind.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:19

**Q2. Where did the west wind take the locusts?**  
**Answer:** And cast them into the Red sea.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:19

**Q3. How many locusts remained in all the coasts of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** There remained not one locust in all the coasts of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:19

---

### Exodus 10:20

**Q1. Whose heart did the Lord harden?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh's heart.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:20

**Q2. Would Pharaoh let the children of Israel go?**  
**Answer:** So that he would not let the children of Israel go.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:20

---

### Exodus 10:21

**Q1. What did the Lord say unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** Stretch out thine hand toward heaven.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:21

**Q2. What would come over the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** That there may be darkness over the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:21

**Q3. What kind of darkness was it described to be?**  
**Answer:** Even darkness which may be felt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:21

---

### Exodus 10:22

**Q1. What did Moses stretch forth toward heaven?**  
**Answer:** His hand toward heaven.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:22

**Q2. How long was there a thick darkness in all the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** In all the land of Egypt three days.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:22

---

### Exodus 10:23

**Q1. What could the people not do during the three days?**  
**Answer:** They saw not one another, neither rose any from his place for three days.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:23

**Q2. Where did the children of Israel have light?**  
**Answer:** But all the children of Israel had light in their dwellings.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:23

---

### Exodus 10:24

**Q1. Who did Pharaoh call unto?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:24

**Q2. What did Pharaoh tell Moses they could do?**  
**Answer:** Go ye, serve the Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:24

**Verse:** Exodus 10:24

**Answer:** Thou must give us also sacrifices and burnt offerings.

**Answer:** That we may sacrifice unto the Lord our God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:25

**Q1. What did Moses say must go with them?**  
**Answer:** Our cattle also shall go with us.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:26

**Q2. Would a hoof be left behind?**  
**Answer:** There shall not an hoof be left behind.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:26

**Q3. Why did they need to take their cattle to serve the Lord?**  
**Answer:** For thereof must we take to serve the Lord our God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:26

### Exodus 10:27

**Q1. Whose heart did the Lord harden this time?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh's heart.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:27

**Q2. Would Pharaoh let them go after the darkness?**  
**Answer:** And he would not let them go.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:27

---

### Exodus 10:28

**Q1. What did Pharaoh say unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** Get thee from me, take heed to thyself, see my face no more.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:28

**Q2. What did Pharaoh say would happen on the day Moses saw his face again?**  
**Answer:** For in that day thou seest my face thou shalt die.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:28

---

**Q1. How did Moses respond to Pharaoh's warning?**  
**Answer:** Thou hast spoken well.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:29

**Q2. What did Moses promise Pharaoh regarding seeing his face again?**  
**Answer:** I will see thy face again no more.  
**Verse:** Exodus 10:29
