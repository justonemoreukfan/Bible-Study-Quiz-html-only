# Exodus 25 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 25:1

**Q1. Unto whom did the LORD speak?**  
**Answer:** Moses  
**Verse:** Exodus 25:1

**Q2. What did the LORD do to Moses?**  
**Answer:** Spake unto him  
**Verse:** Exodus 25:1

**Q3. Whose word is spoken to Moses in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The LORD's  
**Verse:** Exodus 25:1

---

**Q4. What were the children of Israel instructed to do?**  
**Answer:** Bring an offering  
**Verse:** Exodus 25:2

**Q5. From whom shall ye take the offering?**  
**Answer:** Every man that giveth it willingly with his heart  
**Verse:** Exodus 25:2

**Q6. What kind of offering should be taken from every man?**  
**Answer:** An offering  
**Verse:** Exodus 25:2

### Exodus 25:8

**Verse:** Exodus 25:8

### Exodus 25:9

**Q25. According to what pattern should they make the tabernacle and its instruments?**  
**Answer:** According to all that I shew thee  
**Verse:** Exodus 25:9

**Q26. What two things are explicitly mentioned as being made after the pattern?**  
**Answer:** The tabernacle, and the pattern of all the instruments thereof  
**Verse:** Exodus 25:9

**Q27. Who showed Moses the pattern?**  
**Answer:** The LORD (implied by "I shew thee")  
**Verse:** Exodus 25:9

### Exodus 25:10

**Q28. What should they make of shittim wood?**  
**Answer:** An ark  
**Verse:** Exodus 25:10

**Answer:** Two cubits and a half  
**Verse:** Exodus 25:10

**Q30. What are the breadth and height of the ark?**

**Verse:** Exodus 25:10

### Exodus 25:31

**Q91. What shall be made of pure gold?**

**Q92. How shall the candlestick be made?**  
**Answer:** Of beaten work  
**Verse:** Exodus 25:31

**Q94. How many branches shall come out of the sides of the candlestick?**
