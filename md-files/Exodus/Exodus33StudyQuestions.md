# Exodus 33 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 33:1

**Q1. Who was told to depart from the place spoken of?**  
**Answer:** Moses and the people which he had brought up out of the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:1

**Q2. Unto whose land was Moses commanded to go?**  
**Answer:** The land which the LORD sware unto Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:1

**Q3. To whom did the LORD say He would give the land?**  
**Answer:** Unto thy seed.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:1

---

### Exodus 33:2

**Q1. Who did the LORD say He would send before Moses?**  
**Answer:** An angel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:2

**Q2. Whose inhabitants were to be driven out?**  
**Answer:** The Canaanite, the Amorite, the Hittite, and the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:2

**Q3. What did the LORD promise to drive out before them?**  
**Answer:** The Canaanite, the Amorite, the Hittite, and the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:2

---

### Exodus 33:3

**Q1. What kind of land was it described to flow with?**  
**Answer:** Milk and honey.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:3

**Q2. Why would the LORD not go up in the midst of them?**  
**Answer:** For thou art a stiffnecked people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:3

**Q3. What did the LORD say He might do to them in the way?**  
**Answer:** Consume thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:3

---

### Exodus 33:4

**Q1. What did the people hear when they heard these evil tidings?**  
**Answer:** These evil tidings.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:4

**Q2. How did the people react to these tidings?**  
**Answer:** They mourned.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:4

**Q3. What did no man put on him after hearing these tidings?**  
**Answer:** His ornaments.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:4

---

### Exodus 33:5

**Q1. What did the LORD say unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** Say unto the children of Israel, Ye are a stiffnecked people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:5

**Q2. How long did the LORD say He would come up into the midst of them?**  
**Answer:** In a moment.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:5

**Q3. What did the LORD command the people to put off from them?**  
**Answer:** Thy ornaments from thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:5

---

### Exodus 33:6

**Q1. What did the children of Israel strip themselves of?**  
**Answer:** Their ornaments.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:6

**Q2. Where did they strip themselves of their ornaments?**  
**Answer:** By the mount Horeb.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:6

**Q3. When did they strip themselves of their ornaments?**  
**Answer:** From the mount Horeb onward.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:6

---

### Exodus 33:7

**Q1. What did Moses take and pitch without the camp?**  
**Answer:** His tabernacle.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:7

**Q2. What was the tabernacle called?**  
**Answer:** The Tabernacle of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:7

**Q3. Who went out unto the tabernacle of the congregation?**  
**Answer:** Every one which sought the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:7

---

### Exodus 33:8

**Q1. When did Moses go out unto the tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** When Moses went out unto the tabernacle.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:8

**Q2. What did all the people do when Moses went out?**  
**Answer:** Rose up, and stood every man at his tent door.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:8

**Q3. Whose tents did the people look toward until Moses was gone into the tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** After Moses, until he was gone into the tabernacle.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:8

---

### Exodus 33:9

**Q1. What descended as Moses entered into the tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** The cloudy pillar.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:9

**Q2. Where did the cloudy pillar stand?**  
**Answer:** At the door of the tabernacle.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:9

**Q3. Who did the LORD talk with at the tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:9

---

### Exodus 33:10

**Q1. What did all the people see when the cloudy pillar stood at the tabernacle door?**  
**Answer:** The cloudy pillar stand at the tabernacle door.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:10

**Q2. What did all the people do when they saw the cloudy pillar?**  
**Answer:** Rose up and worshipped.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:10

**Q3. Where did every man worship?**  
**Answer:** Every man in his tent door.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:10

---

### Exodus 33:11

**Q1. How did the LORD speak unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** Face to face.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:11

**Q2. To whom did the LORD speak face to face, as a man speaketh unto his friend?**  
**Answer:** Unto Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:11

**Q3. Who was Moses' servant that departed not out of the tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** Joshua, the son of Nun, a young man.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:11

---

### Exodus 33:12

**Q1. What did Moses say unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** See, thou sayest unto me, Bring up this people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:12

**Q2. Whom did the LORD say He had known by name?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:12

**Q3. What did the LORD say Moses had found in His sight?**  
**Answer:** Grace in my sight.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:12

---

### Exodus 33:13

**Q1. What did Moses ask to find if he had found grace in God's sight?**  
**Answer:** Shew me now thy way.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:13

**Q2. What did Moses desire to know so that he might find grace?**  
**Answer:** That I may know thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:13

**Q3. What did Moses remind the LORD that this nation is?**  
**Answer:** Thy people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:13

---

### Exodus 33:14

**Q1. Whose presence did the LORD say shall go with Moses?**  
**Answer:** My presence.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:14

**Q2. What did the LORD promise to give Moses?**  
**Answer:** Rest.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:14

**Q3. Whose presence did the LORD state shall go with him?**  
**Answer:** My presence.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:14

---

### Exodus 33:15

**Q1. What did Moses say to the LORD regarding His presence?**  
**Answer:** If thy presence go not with me, carry us not up hence.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:15

**Q2. Who did Moses ask not to be carried up if God's presence didn't go?**  
**Answer:** Us.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:15

**Q3. What was conditional upon God's presence going with them?**  
**Answer:** Carry us not up hence.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:15

---

### Exodus 33:16

**Q1. What would indicate that God had found grace in His people's sight?**  
**Answer:** Wherein shall it be known here that I and thy people have found grace in thy sight?  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:16

**Q2. What action by the LORD distinguishes them from all other people?**  
**Answer:** Is it not in that thou goest with us?  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:16

**Q3. From whom shall Moses and God's people be separated by His going with them?**  
**Answer:** All the people that are upon the face of the earth.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:16

---

### Exodus 33:17

**Q1. What did the LORD say He would do regarding Moses' request?**  
**Answer:** I will do this thing also that thou hast spoken.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:17

**Q2. Why did the LORD agree to do this thing for Moses?**  
**Answer:** For thou hast found grace in my sight.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:17

**Q3. How did the LORD know Moses?**  
**Answer:** I know thee by name.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:17

---

### Exodus 33:18

**Q1. What did Moses ask the LORD to shew him?**  
**Answer:** I beseech thee, shew me thy glory.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:18

**Q2. Who asked to see God's glory?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:18

**Q3. How did Moses plead when asking to see the glory?**  
**Answer:** I beseech thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:18

---

### Exodus 33:19

**Q1. What did the LORD say He would make pass before Moses?**  
**Answer:** All my goodness.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:19

**Q2. What did the LORD say He would proclaim before him?**  
**Answer:** The name of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:19

**Q3. Upon whom will the LORD have mercy and compassion?**  
**Answer:** On whom I will have mercy, and will have compassion on whom I will have compassion.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:19

---

### Exodus 33:20

**Q1. What did the LORD say Moses could not see?**  
**Answer:** My face.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:20

**Q2. Why could no man see God's face and live?**  
**Answer:** For there shall no man see me, and live.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:20

**Q3. Who is excluded from seeing God's face and living?**  
**Answer:** No man.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:20

---

### Exodus 33:21

**Q1. Where did the LORD say a place was?**  
**Answer:** By me.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:21

**Q2. Where was Moses commanded to stand?**  
**Answer:** Upon a rock.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:21

**Q3. What specific place did the LORD point out for Moses?**  
**Answer:** Behold, there is a place by me, and thou shalt stand upon a rock.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:21

---

### Exodus 33:22

**Q1. What would happen while God's glory passed by?**  
**Answer:** While my glory passeth by.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:22

**Q2. Where would the LORD put Moses?**  
**Answer:** In a clift of the rock.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:22

**Q3. With what would the LORD cover Moses while passing by?**  
**Answer:** With my hand.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:22

---

### Exodus 33:23

**Q1. Whose hand did the LORD say He would take away?**  
**Answer:** Mine hand.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:23

**Q2. What parts of God would Moses see?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt see my back parts.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:23

**Q3. What parts of God would not be seen?**  
**Answer:** My face shall not be seen.  
**Verse:** Exodus 33:23
