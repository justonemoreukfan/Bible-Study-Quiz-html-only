# Exodus 23 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 23:1

**Q1. What are you commanded not to raise?**  
**Answer:** A false report.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:1

**Q2. Whose hand are you not to put with the wicked?**  
**Answer:** An unrighteous witness.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:1

**Q3. What kind of witness are you forbidden to be?**  
**Answer:** An unrighteous witness.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:1

---

### Exodus 23:2

**Q1. Whom shall you not follow to do evil?**  
**Answer:** A multitude.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:2

**Q2. To what should you not decline to wrest judgment?**  
**Answer:** After many.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:2

**Q3. What must you not speak in a cause?**  
**Answer:** To decline after many to wrest judgment.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:2

---

### Exodus 23:3

**Q1. Whose cause shall you not countenance?**  
**Answer:** A poor man's cause.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:3

**Q2. In what should you not countenance a poor man?**  
**Answer:** In his cause.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:3

**Q3. Is the poor man's cause favored to wrest justice in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not countenance a poor man in his cause.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:3

---

### Exodus 23:4

**Q1. Whose ox or ass should you bring back if you meet it?**  
**Answer:** Thine enemy's ox or his ass.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:4

**Q2. What animals are specifically mentioned to be brought back to their owner?**  
**Answer:** Ox or his ass.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:4

**Q3. To whom should the animal be brought back?**  
**Answer:** To him again.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:4

---

### Exodus 23:5

**Q1. Whose ass should you see lying under his burden?**  
**Answer:** The ass of him that hateth thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:5

**Q2. What should you refrain from doing to the ass?**  
**Answer:** Forbear to help him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:5

**Q3. What must you surely do with him?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt surely help with him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:5

---

### Exodus 23:6

**Q1. What must you not wrest in his cause?**  
**Answer:** The judgment of thy poor.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:6

**Q2. Whose judgment must not be wrested?**  
**Answer:** Thy poor.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:6

**Q3. Is the poor person's judgment to be perverted?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt not wrest the judgment of thy poor in his cause.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:6

---

### Exodus 23:7

**Q1. What kind of matter should you keep far from?**  
**Answer:** A false matter.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:7

**Q2. Whom should you not slay?**  
**Answer:** The innocent and righteous.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:7

**Q3. Whom will God not justify?**  
**Answer:** The wicked.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:7

---

### Exodus 23:8

**Q1. What must you not take?**  
**Answer:** A gift.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:8

**Q2. What does a gift blind?**  
**Answer:** The wise.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:8

**Q3. Whose words does a gift pervert?**  
**Answer:** The words of the righteous.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:8

---

### Exodus 23:9

**Q1. Whom shall you not oppress?**  
**Answer:** A stranger.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:9

**Q2. What do the children of Israel know about the heart of a stranger?**  
**Answer:** The heart of a stranger.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:9

**Q3. Where were the children of Israel strangers?**  
**Answer:** In the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:9

---

### Exodus 23:10

**Q1. For how many years shall you sow your land?**  
**Answer:** Six years.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:10

**Q2. What shall you gather in the land during those years?**  
**Answer:** The fruits thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:10

**Q3. How long do you sow and gather before resting the land?**  
**Answer:** Six years.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:10

---

### Exodus 23:11

**Q1. What should be done in the seventh year?**  
**Answer:** Let it rest and lie still.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:11

**Q2. Who may eat the fruit of the resting land?**  
**Answer:** The poor of thy people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:11

**Q3. Who shall eat what the poor leave?**  
**Answer:** The beasts of the field.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:11

---

### Exodus 23:12

**Q1. How many days shall you do your work?**  
**Answer:** Six days.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:12

**Q2. On which day shall you rest?**  
**Answer:** The seventh day.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:12

**Q3. Which animals and persons are specifically mentioned to rest?**  
**Answer:** Thine ox and thine ass may rest, and the son of thy handmaid, and the stranger may be refreshed.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:12

---

### Exodus 23:13

**Q1. What must you be circumspect in concerning all things?**  
**Answer:** All things that I have said unto you.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:13

**Q2. Whose name shall you not make mention of?**  
**Answer:** The name of other gods.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:13

**Q3. From where shall the names of other gods not be heard?**  
**Answer:** Out of thy mouth.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:13

---

### Exodus 23:14

**Q1. How many times in a year shall you keep a feast unto God?**  
**Answer:** Three times.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:14

**Q2. To whom shall you keep a feast three times in a year?**  
**Answer:** Unto me.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:14

**Q3. How often is the feast mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Three times in a year.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:14

---

### Exodus 23:15

**Q1. What feast shall you keep according to command?**  
**Answer:** The feast of unleavened bread.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:15

**Q2. How long shall you eat unleavened bread in the appointed time of what month?**  
**Answer:** Seven days... in the time appointed of the month Abib.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:15

**Q3. What must not be seen before God empty?**  
**Answer:** None shall appear before me empty.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:15

---

### Exodus 23:16

**Q1. What feast shall you keep of the firstfruits of your labors?**  
**Answer:** The feast of harvest.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:16

**Q2. What feast shall you keep at the end of the year?**  
**Answer:** The feast of ingathering.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:16

**Q3. Where is the feast of ingathering gathered from?**  
**Answer:** Out of the field.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:16

---

### Exodus 23:17

**Q1. How many times in a year shall all your males appear?**  
**Answer:** Three times.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:17

**Q2. Before whom shall all your males appear?**  
**Answer:** Before the Lord GOD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:17

**Q3. Who is required to appear before the Lord GOD three times in the year?**  
**Answer:** All thy males.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:17

---

### Exodus 23:18

**Q1. With what must you not offer the blood of my sacrifice?**  
**Answer:** With leavened bread.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:18

**Q2. Until when shall the fat of my feast remain?**  
**Answer:** Until the morning.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:18

**Q3. Whose sacrifice and feast are mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** My sacrifice... my feast.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:18

---

### Exodus 23:19

**Q1. What should you bring into the house of the LORD your God?**  
**Answer:** The first of the firstfruits of thy land.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:19

**Q2. What must you not seethe a kid in?**  
**Answer:** In his mother's milk.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:19

**Q3. Whose house should the firstfruits be brought into?**  
**Answer:** The house of the LORD thy God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:19

---

### Exodus 23:20

**Q1. Whom will God send before Israel?**  
**Answer:** An Angel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:20

**Q2. What is the Angel's purpose regarding the way?**  
**Answer:** To keep thee in the way.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:20

**Q3. To what place will the Angel bring Israel?**  
**Answer:** Into the place which I have prepared.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:20

---

### Exodus 23:21

**Q1. What must Israel beware of regarding the Angel?**  
**Answer:** Beware of him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:21

**Q2. Whose voice must Israel obey?**  
**Answer:** Obey his voice.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:21

**Q3. Why will the Angel not pardon transgressions?**  
**Answer:** For my name is in him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:21

---

### Exodus 23:22

**Q1. Whose voice shall you indeed obey?**  
**Answer:** His voice.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:22

**Q2. What shall God do to all that spake against Israel?**  
**Answer:** An enemy unto thine enemies, and an adversary unto thine adversaries.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:22

**Q3. What will God do to all that do all that God speaks?**  
**Answer:** I will be an enemy unto thine enemies, and an adversary unto thine adversaries.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:22

---

### Exodus 23:23

**Q1. Who shall go before thee?**  
**Answer:** Mine Angel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:23

**Q2. Into whose land shall the Angel bring Israel?**  
**Answer:** The Amorites, and the Hittites, and the Perizzites, and the Canaanites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:23

**Q3. What will God do to those nations?**  
**Answer:** Cut them off.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:23

---

**Q1. Before whose gods shall you not bow down?**

**Verse:** Exodus 23:24

**Q2. What must you not serve concerning their gods?**  
**Answer:** Nor serve them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 23:24

**Q3. What must you do to their gods' images?**

**Verse:** Exodus 23:24

**Verse:** Exodus 23:25

**Q2. What shall God bless?**

**Verse:** Exodus 23:25

**Q3. What will God take away from the midst of Israel?**

**Verse:** Exodus 23:25

**Verse:** Exodus 23:32
