# Exodus 38 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 38:1

**Q1. What altar was made of shittim wood?**  
**Answer:** The altar of burnt offering.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:1

**Q2. What shape was the altar of burnt offering described to be?**  
**Answer:** Foursquare.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:1

**Q3. What was the length and breadth of the altar?**  
**Answer:** Five cubits the length thereof, and five cubits the breadth thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:1

---

### Exodus 38:2

**Q1. What were made upon the four corners of the altar?**  
**Answer:** The horns thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:2

**Q2. The horns of the altar were of the same substance as what?**  
**Answer:** The same.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:2

**Q3. With what was the altar overlaid?**  
**Answer:** Brass.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:2

---

### Exodus 38:3

**Q1. What vessels of the altar were made of brass?**  
**Answer:** All the vessels of the altar.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:3

**Q2. Name the specific pots and pans mentioned in this verse.**  
**Answer:** The pots, and the shovels, and the basons, and the fleshhooks, and the firepans.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:3

**Q3. What were all these vessels made of?**  
**Answer:** Brass.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:3

---

### Exodus 38:4

**Q1. What did he make for the altar?**  
**Answer:** A grate of network of brass.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:4

**Q2. Under what part of the altar was the grate placed?**  
**Answer:** Under the compass thereof beneath.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:4

**Q3. How far did the grate reach into the midst of the altar?**  
**Answer:** Even unto the midst thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:4

---

### Exodus 38:5

**Q1. What were cast for the four corners of the brasen grate?**  
**Answer:** Four rings.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:5

**Q2. What were the four rings cast to hold?**  
**Answer:** For the staves.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:5

**Q3. Where were these four rings placed?**  
**Answer:** At the four corners of the brasen grate.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:5

---

### Exodus 38:6

**Q1. What wood were the staves made of?**  
**Answer:** Shittim wood.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:6

**Q2. With what were the staves overlaid?**  
**Answer:** Brass.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:6

---

### Exodus 38:7

**Q1. Where were the staves put?**  
**Answer:** Into the rings on the sides of the altar.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:7

**Q2. What were the staves used for with the altar?**  
**Answer:** To bear it withal.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:7

**Q3. How was the altar constructed regarding its hollowness?**  
**Answer:** Hollow with boards.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:7

**Verse:** Exodus 38:8

**Answer:** Bezaleel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:22

---

**Answer:** Aholiab, son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:23

**Q2. What was Aholiab described as being?**  
**Answer:** An engraver, and a cunning workman, and an embroiderer in blue, and in purple, and in scarlet, and in fine linen.

**Q1. What was the total amount of the gold used for the work?**  
**Answer:** Twenty and nine talents, and seven hundred and thirty shekels.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:24

**Q2. What standard of measurement was used for the shekels of gold?**  
**Answer:** The shekel of the sanctuary.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:24

---

**Answer:** Silver.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:25

**Q2. What was the weight of the silver of them that were numbered?**  
**Answer:** An hundred talents, and a thousand seven hundred and threescore and fifteen shekels.

**Answer:** The shekel of the sanctuary.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:25

### Exodus 38:26

**Q1. What was the amount required for every man included in the poll?**  
**Answer:** A bekah for every man.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:26

**Q2. A bekah was equal to what fraction of a shekel, according to the sanctuary?**  
**Answer:** Half a shekel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:26

**Answer:** Six hundred thousand and three thousand and five hundred and fifty men.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:26

**Answer:** An hundred sockets.

### Exodus 38:29

**Answer:** Seventy talents, and two thousand and four hundred shekels.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:29

---

### Exodus 38:30

**Q1. What was made with the brass of the offering?**  
**Answer:** The sockets to the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:30

**Q2. What else was made with the brass besides the door sockets?**  
**Answer:** The brasen altar, and the brasen grate for it, and all the vessels of the altar.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:30

---

### Exodus 38:31

**Q1. What brass items were made for the court round about?**  
**Answer:** The sockets of the court round about, and the sockets of the court gate.  
**Verse:** Exodus 38:31

**Q2. What else was made of brass regarding the pins?**  
**Answer:** All the pins of the tabernacle, and all the pins of the court round about.
