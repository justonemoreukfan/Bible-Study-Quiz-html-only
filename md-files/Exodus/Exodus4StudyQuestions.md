# Exodus 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 4:1

**Q1. What did Moses answer and say?**  
**Answer:** But, behold, they will not believe me, nor hearken unto my voice: for they will say, The LORD hath not appeared unto thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:1

**Q2. What did Moses anticipate the people would not do to his voice?**  
**Answer:** Hearken unto my voice.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:1

**Q3. What did Moses say the people would claim the LORD had not done?**  
**Answer:** Appeared unto thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:1

---

### Exodus 4:2

**Q1. What did the LORD say unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** What is that in thine hand?  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:2

**Q2. What did Moses reply that he had in his hand?**  
**Answer:** A rod.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:2

**Q3. Who asked Moses what was in his hand?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:2

---

### Exodus 4:3

**Q1. What did the LORD say to cast on the ground?**  
**Answer:** Cast it on the ground.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:3

**Q2. What did the rod become when it was cast on the ground?**  
**Answer:** A serpent.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:3

**Q3. What did Moses do when the rod became a serpent?**  
**Answer:** Moses fled from before it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:3

---

### Exodus 4:4

**Q1. What did the LORD say unto Moses to put forth?**  
**Answer:** Put forth thine hand, and take it by the tail.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:4

**Q2. Where was Moses told to take the serpent?**  
**Answer:** By the tail.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:4

**Q3. What did the serpent become in Moses' hand when he caught it?**  
**Answer:** It became a rod in his hand.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:4

---

### Exodus 4:5

**Q1. Why were they to believe that the LORD God appeared unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** That they may believe that the LORD God of their fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, hath appeared unto thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:5

**Q2. Whose God is specifically mentioned as appearing unto Moses besides the God of their fathers?**  
**Answer:** The God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:5

**Q3. Whose fathers' God is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Their fathers.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:5

---

### Exodus 4:6

**Q1. What did the LORD say furthermore unto him?**  
**Answer:** Put now thine hand into thy bosom.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:6

**Q2. Where did the LORD tell Moses to put his hand?**  
**Answer:** Into thy bosom.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:6

**Q3. How did Moses' hand look when he took it out of his bosom?**  
**Answer:** Behold, his hand was leprous as snow.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:6

---

### Exodus 4:7

**Q1. Where did the LORD tell Moses to put his hand into again?**  
**Answer:** Into thy bosom again.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:7

**Q2. What happened to Moses' hand when he plucked it out of his bosom again?**  
**Answer:** It was turned again as his other flesh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:7

**Q3. What did the hand become like when restored?**  
**Answer:** As his other flesh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:7

---

### Exodus 4:8

**Q1. What will happen if they will not believe Moses, neither hearken to the voice of the first sign?**  
**Answer:** That they will believe the voice of the latter sign.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:8

**Q2. What might they not believe or hearken to according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** The voice of the first sign.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:8

**Q3. What may happen regarding the voice of either the first or latter sign?**  
**Answer:** They will believe the voice of the latter sign.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:8

---

### Exodus 4:9

**Q1. What will happen if they will not believe these two signs, neither hearken unto thy voice?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt take of the water of the river, and pour it upon the dry land.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:9

**Q2. What shall the water taken from the river become upon the dry land?**  
**Answer:** Shall become blood upon the dry land.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:9

**Q3. From where is the water to be taken?**  
**Answer:** Of the water of the river.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:9

---

### Exodus 4:10

**Q1. What did Moses say unto the LORD regarding his speech?**  
**Answer:** O my LORD, I am not eloquent, neither heretofore, nor since thou hast spoken unto thy servant.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:10

**Q2. How did Moses describe his mouth and tongue?**  
**Answer:** I am slow of speech, and of a slow tongue.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:10

**Q3. Since when did Moses state he was not eloquent?**  
**Answer:** Neither heretofore, nor since thou hast spoken unto thy servant.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:10

---

### Exodus 4:11

**Q1. Who did the LORD say made man's mouth?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:11

**Q2. Who makes the dumb, deaf, seeing, or blind?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:11

**Q3. What rhetorical question does the LORD ask about who makes these conditions?**  
**Answer:** Who hath made man's mouth? or who maketh the dumb, or deaf, or the seeing, or the blind? have not I the LORD?  
**Verse:** Exodus 4:11

---

### Exodus 4:12
