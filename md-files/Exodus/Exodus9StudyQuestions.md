# Exodus 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 9:1

**Q1. Who did the Lord instruct Moses to go and speak unto?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:1

**Q2. What message was Moses told to deliver to Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD God of the Hebrews, Let my people go, that they may serve me.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:1

**Q3. What would happen if Pharaoh refused to let the people go and still held them?**  
**Answer:** The hand of the LORD is upon thy cattle which is in the field.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:1

---

### Exodus 9:2

**Q1. What did the Lord say would happen if Pharaoh refused to let them go and still held them?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt refuse to let them go, and wilt hold them still.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:2

**Q2. Whose hand would be upon the cattle in the field?**  
**Answer:** The hand of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:2

**Q3. Where were the cattle located that the Lord's hand would be upon?**  
**Answer:** In the field.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:2

---

### Exodus 9:3

**Q1. What specific animals were listed as being affected by the hand of the LORD upon the horses?**  
**Answer:** The horses, upon the asses, upon the camels, upon the oxen, and upon the sheep.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:3

**Q2. Where were the horses, asses, camels, oxen, and sheep located?**  
**Answer:** In the field.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:3

**Q3. What kind of disease was specified to be upon the cattle?**  
**Answer:** A very grievous murrain.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:3

---

### Exodus 9:4

**Q1. What would the LORD do between the cattle of Israel and the cattle of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Sever.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:4

**Q2. Whose cattle would suffer no death out of all that belonged to them?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:4

**Q3. How much of the cattle belonging to the children of Israel would die?**  
**Answer:** There shall nothing die of all that is the children's of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:4

---

### Exodus 9:5

**Q1. What did the LORD appoint?**  
**Answer:** A set time.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:5

**Q2. When did the LORD say He would do this thing?**  
**Answer:** Tomorrow.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:5

**Q3. Where would the LORD do this thing?**  
**Answer:** In the land.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:5

---

### Exodus 9:6

**Q1. When did the LORD do that thing?**  
**Answer:** On the morrow.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:6

**Q2. What happened to all the cattle of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** All the cattle of Egypt died.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:6

**Q3. How many of the cattle of the children of Israel died?**  
**Answer:** Of the cattle of the children of Israel died not one.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:6

---

### Exodus 9:7

**Q1. Who did Pharaoh send to see what happened to the cattle of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:7

**Q2. What did Pharaoh find out about the cattle of the Israelites?**  
**Answer:** There was not one of the cattle of the Israelites dead.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:7

**Q3. What was the state of Pharaoh's heart, and what did he refuse to do?**  
**Answer:** The heart of Pharaoh was hardened, and he did not let the people go.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:7

---

### Exodus 9:8

**Q1. What did the LORD say unto Moses and unto Aaron?**  
**Answer:** Take to you handfuls of ashes of the furnace.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:8

**Q2. Who was to sprinkle the ashes toward the heaven?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:8

**Q3. In whose sight was Moses to sprinkle the ashes toward the heaven?**  
**Answer:** In the sight of Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:8

---

### Exodus 9:9

**Q1. What would the ashes become in all the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Small dust.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:9

**Q2. Upon whom and what would the small dust become a boil?**  
**Answer:** Upon man, and upon beast.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:9

**Q3. Throughout how much of the land of Egypt would the boil break forth with blains?**  
**Answer:** Throughout all the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:9

---

### Exodus 9:10

**Q1. What did Moses and Aaron take?**  
**Answer:** Ashes of the furnace.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:10

**Q2. Before whom did they stand?**  
**Answer:** Before Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:10

**Q3. What did Moses sprinkle up toward the heaven?**  
**Answer:** It. (The ashes)  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:10

---

### Exodus 9:11

**Q1. Who could not stand before Moses because of the boils?**  
**Answer:** The magicians.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:11

**Q2. Why could the magicians not stand before Moses?**  
**Answer:** Because of the boil.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:11

**Q3. Upon whom was the boil, including the magicians?**  
**Answer:** Upon the magicians, and upon all the Egyptians.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:11

---

### Exodus 9:12

**Q1. Who hardened the heart of Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:12

**Q2. Whom did Pharaoh refuse to hearken unto?**  
**Answer:** Unto them. (Moses and Aaron)  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:12

**Q3. As what did the LORD have spoken unto Moses, did Pharaoh hearken unto them?**  
**Answer:** As the LORD had spoken unto Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:12

---

### Exodus 9:13

**Q1. What did the LORD say unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** Rise up early in the morning, and stand before Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:13

**Q2. What was Moses to say unto Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD God of the Hebrews, Let my people go, that they may serve me.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:13

**Q3. When was Moses told to rise up and stand before Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** Early in the morning.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:13

---

### Exodus 9:14

**Q1. What would the LORD send at this time upon Pharaoh's heart?**  
**Answer:** All his plagues.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:14

**Q2. Upon whom else would the plagues be sent besides Pharaoh's heart?**  
**Answer:** Upon his servants, and upon his people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:14

**Q3. Why would the plagues be sent, so that Pharaoh might know what?**  
**Answer:** That there is none like me in all the earth.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:14

---

### Exodus 9:15

**Q1. What could the LORD have stretched out and smitten Pharaoh and his people with?**  
**Answer:** My hand.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:15

**Q2. With what would Pharaoh and his people have been smitten if the hand had been stretched out?**  
**Answer:** Pestilence.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:15

**Q3. What would have happened to Pharaoh and his people from the earth if they were smitten?**  
**Answer:** Thou hadst been cut off from the earth.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:15

---

### Exodus 9:16

**Q1. For this cause have I raised thee up, for to show in thee what?**  
**Answer:** My power.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:16

**Q2. What might be declared throughout all the earth?**  
**Answer:** My name.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:16

**Q3. Whose power was to be shown in Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** Mine. (God's)  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:16

---

### Exodus 9:17

**Q1. What was Pharaoh still exalting himself against?**  
**Answer:** My people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:17

**Q2. That thou what?**  
**Answer:** That thou wilt not let them go.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:17

**Q3. Who was exalting himself against God's people?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:17

---

### Exodus 9:18

**Q1. When would the LORD cause it to rain?**  
**Answer:** Tomorrow about this time.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:18

**Q2. What kind of rain would the LORD cause to rain?**  
**Answer:** A very grievous hail.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:18

**Q3. Like what hail would this hail be, such as has not been in Egypt since when?**  
**Answer:** Since the foundation thereof even until now.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:18

---

### Exodus 9:19

**Q1. What did the LORD command Pharaoh to gather?**  
**Answer:** Thy cattle, and all that thou hast in the field.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:19

**Q2. Where would every man and beast be brought to escape the hail?**  
**Answer:** Into the house.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:19

**Q3. What would happen to every man and beast which was found in the field and not brought home?**  
**Answer:** The hail shall come down upon them, and they shall die.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:19

---

**Answer:** He that feared the word of the LORD among the servants of Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:20

**Q2. Where did those servants make their servants and cattle flee?**  
**Answer:** Into the houses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:20

**Q3. Whose servants and cattle were left in the field?**  
**Answer:** His servants and his cattle that regarded not the word of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:20

---

**Q1. Who regarded not the word of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** He that regarded not the word of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 9:21
