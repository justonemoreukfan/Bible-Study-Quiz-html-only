# Exodus 17 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 17:1

**Q1. According to their journeys, from where did all the congregation of the children of Israel journey?**  
**Answer:** From the wilderness of Sin.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:1

**Q2. By what did the children of Israel journey according to the commandment of the LORD?**  
**Answer:** By their journeys.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:1

**Q3. Where did the people pitch, and where was there no water for them to drink?**  
**Answer:** In Rephidim.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:1

---

### Exodus 17:2

**Q24. With whom did the people chide, and what did they say?**  
**Answer:** With Moses, saying, Give us water that we may drink.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:2

**Q25. What did Moses say unto them, Why chide ye with me?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore do ye tempt the LORD?  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:2

**Q26. What question did Moses ask about tempting the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore do ye tempt the LORD?  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:2

---

### Exodus 17:3

**Q4. What did the people thirst for in that place?**  
**Answer:** Water.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:3

**Q5. Against whom did the people murmur?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:3

**Q6. What did the people ask Moses regarding bringing them up out of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore is this that thou hast brought us up out of Egypt, to kill us and our children and our cattle with thirst?  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:3

---

### Exodus 17:4

**Q7. Unto whom did Moses cry, asking what he should do unto this people?**  
**Answer:** Unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:4

**Q8. What did Moses say the people were almost ready to do unto him?**  
**Answer:** Stone him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:4

**Q9. What did Moses ask the LORD about what he should do unto this people?**  
**Answer:** What shall I do unto this people?  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:4

---

### Exodus 17:5

**Q10. What did the LORD say unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** Go on before the people, and take with thee of the elders of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:5

**Q11. What was Moses to take in his hand?**  
**Answer:** Thy rod wherewith thou smotest the river.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:5

**Q12. Whose rod was Moses to take in his hand?**  
**Answer:** His rod.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:5

---

### Exodus 17:6

**Q13. Where did the LORD say He will stand before Moses upon the rock?**  
**Answer:** In Horeb.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:6

**Q14. What was Moses to do to the rock?**  
**Answer:** Smite the rock.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:6

**Q15. What would come out of the rock when Moses smote it?**  
**Answer:** Water that the people may drink.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:6

---

### Exodus 17:7

**Q16. What name did Moses call the place because of the chiding of the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Massah, and Meribah.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:7

**Q17. Why did Moses call the name of the place Massah and Meribah?**  
**Answer:** Because of the chiding of the children of Israel, and because they tempted the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:7

**Q18. What question did the people ask regarding the LORD's presence among them?**  
**Answer:** Is the LORD among us, or not?  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:7

---

### Exodus 17:8

**Q19. Who came and fought with Israel in Rephidim?**  
**Answer:** Amalek.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:8

**Q20. Where did Amalek come and fight with Israel?**  
**Answer:** In Rephidim.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:8

**Q21. Who fought with Israel in Rephidim?**  
**Answer:** Amalek.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:8

---

### Exodus 17:9

**Q22. What did Moses say unto Joshua?**  
**Answer:** Choose us out men, and go out, fight with Amalek.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:9

**Q23. Where would Moses stand with the rod of God in his hand?**  
**Answer:** On the top of the hill.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:9

**Q27. What was in the hand of Moses when he stood on the top of the hill?**  
**Answer:** The rod of God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:9

---

### Exodus 17:10

**Q28. As Moses had said to him, what did Joshua do?**  
**Answer:** Fought with Amalek.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:10

**Q29. Who went up to the top of the hill with Moses?**  
**Answer:** Aaron and Hur.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:10

**Q30. Who went up to the top of the hill?**  
**Answer:** Moses, Aaron, and Hur.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:10

---

### Exodus 17:11

**Q31. What happened when Moses held up his hand?**  
**Answer:** Israel prevailed.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:11

**Q32. What happened when Moses let down his hand?**  
**Answer:** Amalek prevailed.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:11

**Q33. Whose hand being held up caused Israel to prevail?**  
**Answer:** Moses' hand.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:11

---

### Exodus 17:12

**Q34. What were Moses' hands?**  
**Answer:** Heavy.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:12

**Q35. What did they take and put under Moses so he could sit thereon?**  
**Answer:** A stone.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:12

**Q36. Who stayed up Moses' hands, the one on the one side and the other on the other side?**  
**Answer:** Aaron and Hur.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:12

---

### Exodus 17:13

**Q37. Who discomfited Amalek and his people?**  
**Answer:** Joshua.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:13

**Q38. With what did Joshua discomfit Amalek and his people?**  
**Answer:** The edge of the sword.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:13

**Q39. Whom did Joshua discomfit with the edge of the sword?**  
**Answer:** Amalek and his people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:13

---

### Exodus 17:14

**Q40. What did the LORD say unto Moses to write for a memorial in a book?**  
**Answer:** Write this for a memorial in a book.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:14

**Q41. In whose ears was Moses to rehearse it?**  
**Answer:** Joshua's ears.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:14

**Q42. What will the LORD utterly put out from under heaven concerning Amalek?**  
**Answer:** The remembrance of Amalek.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:14

---

### Exodus 17:15

**Q43. What did Moses build?**  
**Answer:** An altar.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:15

**Q44. What name did Moses call the altar?**  
**Answer:** Jehovah-nissi.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:15

**Q45. Who built an altar and called the name of it Jehovah-nissi?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:15

---

### Exodus 17:16

**Q46. For what reason did Moses say the LORD has sworn?**  
**Answer:** Because the LORD hath sworn that the LORD will have war with Amalek from generation to generation.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:16

**Q47. With whom did Moses say the LORD will have war?**  
**Answer:** With Amalek.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:16

**Q48. From what to what did Moses say the LORD will have war with Amalek?**  
**Answer:** From generation to generation.  
**Verse:** Exodus 17:16
