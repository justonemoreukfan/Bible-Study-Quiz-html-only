# Exodus 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 6:1

**Q1. What did the LORD tell Moses he would see with regard to Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** What I will do to Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 6:1

**Q2. With what kind of hand did the LORD say Pharaoh would let the children of Israel go?**  
**Answer:** With a strong hand.  
**Verse:** Exodus 6:1

**Q3. With what kind of hand did the LORD say Pharaoh would drive the children of Israel out of his land?**  
**Answer:** With a strong hand.  
**Verse:** Exodus 6:1

---

### Exodus 6:2

**Q1. Who spoke unto Moses in this verse?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 6:2

**Q2. What did God say unto Moses after speaking to him?**  
**Answer:** I am the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 6:2

---

### Exodus 6:3

**Q1. Unto whom did God appear?**  
**Answer:** Unto Abraham, unto Isaac, and unto Jacob.  
**Verse:** Exodus 6:3

**Q2. By what name did God appear unto Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob?**  
**Answer:** By the name of God Almighty.  
**Verse:** Exodus 6:3

**Q3. By what name was God not known to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob?**  
**Answer:** By my name JEHOVAH.  
**Verse:** Exodus 6:3

---

### Exodus 6:4

**Q1. What did God establish with them?**  
**Answer:** My covenant.  
**Verse:** Exodus 6:4

**Q2. To give them what land did God establish His covenant?**  
**Answer:** The land of Canaan.  
**Verse:** Exodus 6:4

**Q3. What were the days of their pilgrimage described as?**  
**Answer:** The land of their pilgrimage, wherein they were strangers.  
**Verse:** Exodus 6:4

---

### Exodus 6:5

**Answer:** The groaning of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 6:5

**Q2. Whom did the Egyptians keep in bondage?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 6:5

**Q3. What did God remember?**  
**Answer:** My covenant.  
**Verse:** Exodus 6:5

### Exodus 6:6

**Q1. Who is speaking to the children of Israel in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 6:6

**Q2. From whose burdens did the LORD promise to bring them out?**  
**Answer:** From under the burdens of the Egyptians.  
**Verse:** Exodus 6:6

**Answer:** With great judgments.  
**Verse:** Exodus 6:6

### Exodus 6:7

**Q1. For what purpose would the LORD take the children of Israel to Him?**

**Verse:** Exodus 6:7

**Answer:** A God to you.
