# Exodus 36 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 36:1

**Q1. Who is stated to work in the sanctuary?**  
**Answer:** Bezaleel and Aholiab, and every wise hearted man.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:1

**Q2. What did the LORD put in these men?**  
**Answer:** Wisdom and understanding.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:1

**Q3. To what extent did they know how to work?**  
**Answer:** To know how to work all work for the service of the sanctuary.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:1

---

### Exodus 36:2

**Q1. Whom did Moses call to work?**  
**Answer:** Bezaleel and Aholiab, and every wise hearted man.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:2

**Q2. Whose heart had the LORD wisdom given to?**  
**Answer:** Every one whose heart stirred him up to come unto the work to do it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:2

**Q3. What was stirred up in these people?**  
**Answer:** Their heart.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:2

---

### Exodus 36:3

**Q1. What did Moses receive from the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** All the offering.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:3

**Q2. What had the children of Israel brought for the work of the service of the sanctuary?**  
**Answer:** The offering.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:3

**Q3. What did the people bring yet entirely every morning?**  
**Answer:** Free offerings.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:3

---

### Exodus 36:4

**Q1. What manner of men came every man from his work?**  
**Answer:** All the wise men, that wrought all the work of the sanctuary.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:4

**Q2. From what did the wise men come?**  
**Answer:** Every man from his work which they made.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:4

**Q3. Where did these wise men come?**  
**Answer:** They came.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:4

---

### Exodus 36:5

**Q1. To whom did the wise men speak?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:5

**Q2. What did they say the people brought?**  
**Answer:** Much more than enough for the service of the work, which the LORD commanded to make.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:5

**Q3. Whose command was the work made for?**

**Verse:** Exodus 36:5

### Exodus 36:6

**Q1. What did Moses give commandment for?**  
**Answer:** They caused it to be proclaimed throughout the camp.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:6

**Q2. What were the people told not to make?**  
**Answer:** Any more work for the offering of the sanctuary.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:6

**Q3. What was restrained from the people?**  
**Answer:** The people were restrained from bringing.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:6

### Exodus 36:7

**Verse:** Exodus 36:7

**Verse:** Exodus 36:7

### Exodus 36:8

**Q2. What was the breadth of one curtain?**  
**Answer:** Four cubits.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:9

**Verse:** Exodus 36:9

---

**Answer:** One to another.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:10

**Q2. How many curtains were coupled one to another?**  
**Answer:** Five curtains.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:10

**Q3. What did the other five curtains do?**

### Exodus 36:11

**Answer:** On the edge of one curtain from the selvage in the coupling.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:11

**Answer:** In the uttermost edge of another curtain, in the coupling of the second.  
**Verse:** Exodus 36:11
