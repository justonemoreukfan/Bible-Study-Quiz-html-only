# Exodus 32 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 32:1

**Q1. When the people saw that Moses delayed to come down out of the mount, to whom did they gather themselves together?**  
**Answer:** Unto Aaron.  
**Verse:** Exodus 32:1

**Q2. What did the people say unto Aaron concerning Moses?**  
**Answer:** Up, make us gods, which shall go before us; for as for this Moses, the man that brought us up out of the land of Egypt, we wot not what is become of him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 32:1

**Q3. Who did the people say brought them up out of the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** This Moses, the man that brought us up out of the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 32:1

---

### Exodus 32:2

**Q1. What did Aaron say unto them regarding their golden earrings?**  
**Answer:** Break off the golden earrings, which are in the ears of your wives, of your sons, and of your daughters, and bring them unto me.  
**Verse:** Exodus 32:2

**Q2. Whose golden earrings did Aaron tell them to break off?**  
**Answer:** The earrings of your wives, of your sons, and of your daughters.  
**Verse:** Exodus 32:2

**Q3. To whom were they to bring the golden earrings?**  
**Answer:** Unto me (Aaron).  
**Verse:** Exodus 32:2

---
