# Exodus 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 2:1

**Q1. What house did a man of the house of Levi go and take to wife?**  
**Answer:** A daughter of Levi.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:1

**Q2. Of what house was the man who went and took a wife?**  
**Answer:** The house of Levi.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:1

**Q3. Whom did the man of the house of Levi take to wife?**  
**Answer:** A daughter of Levi.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:1

---

### Exodus 2:2

**Q1. What did the woman conceive and bear?**  
**Answer:** A son.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:2

**Q2. What did the woman see when she saw him?**  
**Answer:** That he was a goodly child.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:2

**Q3. How long did she hide him?**  
**Answer:** Three months.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:2

---

### Exodus 2:3

**Q1. When she could not longer hide him, what did she take for him?**  
**Answer:** An ark of bulrushes.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:3

**Q2. With what did she daub the ark?**  
**Answer:** Slime and with pitch.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:3

**Q3. Where did she put the child in the ark?**  
**Answer:** In the flags by the river's brink.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:3

---

### Exodus 2:4

**Q1. Who stood afar off?**  
**Answer:** His sister.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:4

**Q2. To know what did his sister stand afar off?**  
**Answer:** To wit what would be done to him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:4

**Q3. Where did his sister stand?**  
**Answer:** Afar off.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:4

---

### Exodus 2:5

**Q1. Who came down to wash herself at the river?**  
**Answer:** The daughter of Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:5

**Q2. Where did the daughter of Pharaoh walk?**  
**Answer:** Along by the river's side.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:5

**Q3. When her maidens walked along by the river's side, what did she see?**  
**Answer:** The ark among the flags.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:5

---

### Exodus 2:6

**Q1. Whom did Pharaoh's daughter send to fetch the ark?**  
**Answer:** Her maid.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:6

**Q2. When she had opened it, what did she see?**  
**Answer:** The child.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:6

**Q3. What did the child do when she saw him?**  
**Answer:** Wept.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:6

---

### Exodus 2:7

**Q1. Who spoke to Pharaoh's daughter?**  
**Answer:** His sister.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:7

**Q2. What did his sister ask Pharaoh's daughter?**  
**Answer:** Shall I go and call to thee a nurse of the Hebrew women?  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:7

**Q3. For whom did the nurse need to nurse the child?**  
**Answer:** For thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:7

---

### Exodus 2:8

**Q1. What did Pharaoh's daughter say to her?**  
**Answer:** Go.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:8

**Q2. Who went and called the child's mother?**  
**Answer:** The maid.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:8

**Q3. Whose mother did the maid call?**  
**Answer:** The child's mother.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:8

---

### Exodus 2:9

**Q1. What did Pharaoh's daughter say unto her?**  
**Answer:** Take this child away, and nurse it for me.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:9

**Q2. Who would give the woman her wages?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh's daughter.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:9

**Q3. What did the woman take and nurse?**  
**Answer:** The child.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:9

---

### Exodus 2:10

**Q1. When the child grew, unto whom was he brought?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh's daughter.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:10

**Q2. Whose son did he become?**  
**Answer:** Her son.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:10

**Q3. What did she call his name?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:10

---

### Exodus 2:11

**Q1. When Moses was grown, what did he go out unto?**  
**Answer:** His brethren.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:11

**Q2. What did Moses look on?**  
**Answer:** Their burdens.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:11

**Q3. What did he spy an Egyptian doing?**  
**Answer:** Smiting an Hebrew, one of his brethren.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:11

---

### Exodus 2:12

**Q1. How did Moses look this way and that way?**  
**Answer:** He looked this way and that way.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:12

**Q2. When he saw that there was no man, what did he do to the Egyptian?**  
**Answer:** Slew him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:12

**Q3. Where did Moses hide the Egyptian?**  
**Answer:** In the sand.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:12

---

### Exodus 2:13

**Q1. When he went out the second day, what did he behold?**  
**Answer:** Two men of the Hebrews striving together.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:13

**Q2. What did he say to him that did the wrong?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore smitest thou thy fellow?  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:13

**Q3. Whom did the Hebrew smite?**  
**Answer:** His fellow.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:13

---

### Exodus 2:14

**Q1. Who said, "Who made thee a prince and a judge over us?"**  
**Answer:** He that did his neighbour wrong.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:14

**Q2. What did the man ask if Moses intended to do?**  
**Answer:** Kill me, as thou killest the Egyptian?  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:14

**Q3. When Moses feared, what did he say?**  
**Answer:** Surely this thing is known.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:14

---

### Exodus 2:15

**Q1. When Pharaoh heard this thing, what did he seek to do to Moses?**  
**Answer:** Slay Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:15

**Q2. From whose face did Moses flee?**  
**Answer:** The face of Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:15

**Q3. In the land of Midian, where did Moses dwell?**  
**Answer:** By a well.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:15

---

### Exodus 2:16

**Q1. How many daughters had the priest of Midian?**  
**Answer:** Seven.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:16

**Q2. What did the daughters of the priest of Midian come and draw?**  
**Answer:** Water.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:16

**Q3. To fill what did they fill the troughs?**  
**Answer:** To water their father's flock.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:16

---

### Exodus 2:17

**Q1. Who came and drove the daughters away?**  
**Answer:** The shepherds.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:17

**Q2. Who stood up and helped them?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:17

**Q3. Whose flock did Moses water?**  
**Answer:** Their flock.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:17

---

### Exodus 2:18

**Q1. When they came to Reuel their father, what did he ask?**  
**Answer:** How is it that ye are come so soon to day?  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:18

**Q2. Who was Reuel?**  
**Answer:** Their father / the priest of Midian.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:18

---

### Exodus 2:19

**Q1. What did they say an Egyptian delivered them out of?**  
**Answer:** The hand of the shepherds.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:19

**Q2. What else did the Egyptian do for them besides delivering them?**  
**Answer:** Drew water enough for them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:19

**Q3. Whose flock did the Egyptian water?**  
**Answer:** The flock.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:19

---

### Exodus 2:20

**Q1. What did their father ask his daughters?**  
**Answer:** And where is he?  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:20

**Q2. What did he ask why they had left?**  
**Answer:** Why is it that ye have left the man?  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:20

**Q3. What did he tell them to call him to do?**  
**Answer:** Eat bread.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:20

---

### Exodus 2:21

**Q1. With whom was Moses content to dwell?**  
**Answer:** The man.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:21

**Q2. Whom did Reuel give to Moses?**  
**Answer:** Zipporah his daughter.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:21

**Q3. Who was Zipporah?**  
**Answer:** Reuel's daughter.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:21

---

### Exodus 2:22

**Q1. What did Zipporah bear?**  
**Answer:** A son.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:22

**Q2. What did Moses call his name?**  
**Answer:** Gershom.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:22

**Q3. Why did Moses call his name Gershom?**  
**Answer:** For he said, I have been a stranger in a strange land.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:22

---

### Exodus 2:23

**Q1. In process of time, who died?**  
**Answer:** The king of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:23

**Q2. Why did the children of Israel sigh and cry?**  
**Answer:** By reason of the bondage.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:23

**Q3. Unto whom came their cry up by reason of the bondage?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:23

---

### Exodus 2:24

**Q1. What did God hear?**  
**Answer:** Their groaning.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:24

**Q2. What did God remember?**  
**Answer:** His covenant with Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:24

**Q3. With whom did God remember His covenant?**  
**Answer:** Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:24

---

### Exodus 2:25

**Q1. Upon whom did God look?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:25

**Q2. What did God have unto the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Respect.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:25

**Q3. Who had respect unto the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 2:25
