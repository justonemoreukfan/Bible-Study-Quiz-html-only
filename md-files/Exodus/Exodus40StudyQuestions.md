# Exodus 40 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 40:1

**Q1. Who spake unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 40:1

**Q2. What did the LORD do unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** Spake unto him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 40:1

**Q3. Whose instructions are recorded in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The LORD's.  
**Verse:** Exodus 40:1

---

### Exodus 40:2

**Q1. What specific object does the LORD tell Moses to set up?**  
**Answer:** The tabernacle of the tent of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Exodus 40:2

**Q2. On what day of the first month is Moses to set up the tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** On the first day of the first month.  
**Verse:** Exodus 40:2

**Q3. What kind of tent is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The tent of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Exodus 40:2

---

### Exodus 40:3

**Q1. What is Moses to put in the tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** The ark of the testimony.  
**Verse:** Exodus 40:3

**Q2. What is Moses to do with the vail?**  
**Answer:** Cover the ark with the vail.  
**Verse:** Exodus 40:3

**Q3. What specific ark is referenced in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The ark of the testimony.  
**Verse:** Exodus 40:3

---

### Exodus 40:4

**Q1. What is Moses to bring in and set in order?**  
**Answer:** The table.  
**Verse:** Exodus 40:4

**Q2. What is Moses to bring in and light the lamps thereof?**  
**Answer:** The candlestick.  
**Verse:** Exodus 40:4

**Q3. Where are the things to be brought in and set in order according to the context of the verse?**  
**Answer:** In the tent (implied by setting them in order).  
**Verse:** Exodus 40:4

---

**Answer:** The incense.  
**Verse:** Exodus 40:5

---

**Verse:** Exodus 40:7

### Exodus 40:9

**Q1. What oil is Moses to take?**  
**Answer:** The anointing oil.  
**Verse:** Exodus 40:9

**Q2. What is Moses to do with the anointing oil regarding the tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** Anoint the tabernacle, and all that is therein.  
**Verse:** Exodus 40:9

**Q3. What will happen to the tabernacle and its vessels when anointed?**  
**Answer:** They shall be holy.  
**Verse:** Exodus 40:9

---

### Exodus 40:10

**Q1. What altar is Moses to anoint?**  
**Answer:** The altar of the burnt offering.  
**Verse:** Exodus 40:10

**Q2. What else is Moses to anoint along with the altar?**  
**Answer:** All his vessels.  
**Verse:** Exodus 40:10

**Q3. What status will the altar attain after being sanctified?**  
**Answer:** It shall be an altar most holy.  
**Verse:** Exodus 40:10

**Answer:** The laver and his foot.

**Answer:** Moses.

**Verse:** Exodus 40:13

### Exodus 40:15

**Q1. What is Moses to do to Aaron's sons just as he did to their father?**  
**Answer:** Anoint them.

**Answer:** Minister unto the LORD in the priest's office.

### Exodus 40:16
