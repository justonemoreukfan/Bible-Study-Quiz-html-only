# Exodus 18 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 18:1

**Q1. Who heard of all that God had done for Moses, and for Israel his people?**  
**Answer:** Jethro, the priest of Midian, Moses' father in law.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:1

**Q2. Whose relation to Moses was Jethro?**  
**Answer:** Moses' father in law.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:1

**Q3. Who had brought Israel out of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:1

---

### Exodus 18:2

**Q1. Who did Jethro, Moses' father in law, take?**  
**Answer:** Zipporah, Moses' wife, after he had sent her back.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:2

---

### Exodus 18:3

**Q1. Who were the two sons of Moses mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Gershom and Eliezer.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:3

---

### Exodus 18:4

**Q1. What was the name of the other son?**  
**Answer:** Eliezer.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:4

---

### Exodus 18:5

**Q1. Where did Jethro come with Moses' sons and his wife unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** Into the wilderness, where he encamped at the mount of God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:5

---

### Exodus 18:6

**Q1. What message was sent unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** I thy father in law Jethro am come to thee, and thy wife, and her two sons with her.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:6

---

### Exodus 18:7

**Q1. How did Moses go out to meet his father in law?**  
**Answer:** He went out to meet his father in law, and did obeisance, and kissed him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:7

**Q2. What did they ask each other after they kissed?**  
**Answer:** They asked each other of their welfare.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:7

**Q3. Where did they come into?**  
**Answer:** Into the tent.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:7

---

### Exodus 18:8

**Q1. What did Moses tell his father in law?**  
**Answer:** All that the Lord had done unto Pharaoh and to the Egyptians for Israel's sake.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:8

**Q2. What else did Moses tell Jethro about?**  
**Answer:** All the travail that had come upon them by the way, and how the Lord delivered them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:8

**Q3. Who delivered them by the way?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:8

---

### Exodus 18:9

**Q1. How did Jethro feel for all the goodness which the Lord had done to Israel?**  
**Answer:** He rejoiced.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:9

**Q2. Whom had the Lord delivered out of the hand of the Egyptians?**  
**Answer:** Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:9

---

### Exodus 18:10

**Q1. What did Jethro say blessed be?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:10

**Q2. Out of whose hand had the Lord delivered the people?**  
**Answer:** Out of the hand of the Egyptians, and out of the hand of Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:10

**Q3. From under whose hand had he delivered the people?**  
**Answer:** From under the hand of the Egyptians.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:10

---

### Exodus 18:11

**Q1. What did Jethro know after hearing these things?**  
**Answer:** That the Lord is greater than all gods.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:11

**Q2. What did the Egyptians do wherein Jethro said God was greater?**  
**Answer:** Wherein they dealt proudly against them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:11

---

### Exodus 18:12

**Q1. What did Jethro, Moses' father in law, take?**  
**Answer:** A burnt offering and sacrifices for God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:12

**Q2. Who came to eat bread with Moses' father in law?**  
**Answer:** Aaron and all the elders of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:12

**Q3. Where did they eat bread before God?**  
**Answer:** Before God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:12

---

### Exodus 18:13

**Q1. What did Moses sit down to do on the morrow?**  
**Answer:** To judge the people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:13

**Q2. How long did the people stand by Moses?**  
**Answer:** From the morning unto the evening.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:13

---

### Exodus 18:14

**Q1. What did Jethro see that Moses did to the people?**  
**Answer:** All that he did to the people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:14

**Q2. What question did Jethro ask Moses about his judging?**  
**Answer:** What is this thing that thou doest to the people?  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:14

**Q3. Why did Jethro ask why Moses sat by himself?**  
**Answer:** Why sittest thou thyself alone, and all the people stand by thee from morning unto even?  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:14

---

### Exodus 18:15

**Q1. What was Moses' answer to his father in law regarding why the people came to him?**  
**Answer:** Because the people come unto me to enquire of God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:15

---

### Exodus 18:16

**Q1. What did Moses do when they had a matter?**  
**Answer:** They come unto me; and I judge between one and another.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:16

**Q2. What else did Moses make them know?**  
**Answer:** The statutes of God, and his laws.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:16

---

### Exodus 18:17

**Q1. What did Moses' father in law say about the thing that he did?**  
**Answer:** The thing that thou doest is not good.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:17

---

### Exodus 18:18

**Q1. What will surely happen to Moses and the people with him?**  
**Answer:** Thou wilt surely wear away, both thou, and this people that is with thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:18

**Q2. Why is this thing too heavy for Moses?**  
**Answer:** For this thing is too heavy for thee; thou art not able to perform it thyself alone.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:18

---

### Exodus 18:19

**Q1. Whose voice did Jethro tell Moses to hearken unto?**  
**Answer:** My voice.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:19

**Q2. What advice did Jethro give Moses concerning God?**  
**Answer:** Be thou for the people to God-ward, that thou mayest bring the causes unto God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:19

---

### Exodus 18:20

**Q1. What did Jethro advise Moses to teach the people?**  
**Answer:** Ordinances and laws.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:20

**Q2. What else did Jethro say Moses should show them?**  
**Answer:** The way wherein they must walk, and the work that they must do.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:20

---

### Exodus 18:21

**Q1. What kind of men should Moses provide out of all the people?**  
**Answer:** Able men, such as fear God, men of truth, hating covetousness.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:21

**Q2. Over what groups of people did Jethro suggest setting these men as rulers?**  
**Answer:** Rulers of thousands, and rulers of hundreds, rulers of fifties, and rulers of tens.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:21

---

### Exodus 18:22

**Q1. When should these rulers judge the people at all seasons?**  
**Answer:** Let them judge the people at all seasons.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:22

**Q2. What kind of matters were they to bring unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** Every great matter they shall bring unto thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:22

**Q3. What kind of matters were the rulers to judge themselves?**  
**Answer:** Every small matter they shall judge.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:22

---

### Exodus 18:23

**Q1. What did Jethro say would happen if Moses did this thing?**  
**Answer:** Then thou shalt be able to endure.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:23

**Q2. What would all this people also be able to do?**  
**Answer:** Go to their place in peace.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:23

**Q3. Who commanded this?**  
**Answer:** God shall command thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:23

---

### Exodus 18:24

**Q1. Whose voice did Moses hearken to?**  
**Answer:** The voice of his father in law.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:24

**Q2. What did Moses do after hearing his father in law's voice?**  
**Answer:** He did all that he had said.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:24

---

### Exodus 18:25

**Q1. What kind of men did Moses choose out of all Israel?**  
**Answer:** Able men.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:25

**Q2. Whom did Moses make heads over the people?**  
**Answer:** Rulers of thousands, rulers of hundreds, rulers of fifties, and rulers of tens.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:25

---

### Exodus 18:26

**Q1. Whom did the rulers judge at all seasons?**  
**Answer:** The people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:26

**Q2. What kind of causes did they bring unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** The hard causes.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:26

**Q3. What kind of every small matter did they judge themselves?**  
**Answer:** Every small matter they judged themselves.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:26

---

### Exodus 18:27

**Q1. Who let his father in law depart?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:27

**Q2. Where did Jethro go after Moses let him depart?**  
**Answer:** He went his way into his own land.  
**Verse:** Exodus 18:27
