# Exodus 39 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 39:1

**Q1. What kind of cloths were made for service, to do service in the holy place?**  
**Answer:** Clothes of service.  
**Verse:** Exodus 39:1

**Q2. What did they make alongside the cloths of service?**  
**Answer:** Holy garments for Aaron.  
**Verse:** Exodus 39:1

**Q3. As what, were these things commanded by the LORD to Moses?**  
**Answer:** As the LORD commanded Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 39:1

---

### Exodus 39:2

**Q1. What was made from gold?**  
**Answer:** The ephod.  
**Verse:** Exodus 39:2

**Q2. What colors of yarn were used alongside the gold?**  
**Answer:** Blue, and purple, and scarlet.  
**Verse:** Exodus 39:2

**Q3. What else was used to make the ephod besides gold, blue, purple, and scarlet?**  
**Answer:** Fine twined linen.  
**Verse:** Exodus 39:2

---

### Exodus 39:3

**Q1. Into what kind of pieces did they beat the gold?**  
**Answer:** Thin plates.  
**Verse:** Exodus 39:3

**Q2. How did they divide the gold to work it into the blue, purple, scarlet, and fine linen?**  
**Answer:** Into wires.  
**Verse:** Exodus 39:3

**Q3. Who did the work with cunning work?**  
**Answer:** Cunning work.  
**Verse:** Exodus 39:3

---

### Exodus 39:4

**Q1. What were made for the ephod?**  
**Answer:** The shoulder pieces thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 39:4

**Q2. Where were these shoulder pieces joined?**  
**Answer:** By the two edges.  
**Verse:** Exodus 39:4

**Q3. Where were the two edges located where it was joined?**  
**Answer:** In the two edges thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 39:4

---
