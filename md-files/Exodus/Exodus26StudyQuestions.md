# Exodus 26 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 26:1

**Q1. What is the tabernacle to be made with?**  
**Answer:** Ten curtains.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:1

**Q2. What material are the curtains to be made of?**  
**Answer:** Fine twined linen, and blue, and purple, and scarlet.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:1

**Q3. What work is to be in the curtains?**  
**Answer:** Cherubims of cunning work.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:1

---

### Exodus 26:2

**Q1. What shall the length of one curtain be?**  
**Answer:** Eight and twenty cubits.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:2

**Q2. What shall the breadth of one curtain be?**  
**Answer:** Four cubits.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:2

**Q3. What measure shall every one of the curtains have?**  
**Answer:** One measure.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:2

---

### Exodus 26:3

**Q1. How many curtains shall be coupled together one to another?**  
**Answer:** Five curtains.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:3

**Q2. What shall be done with the other five curtains?**  
**Answer:** Coupled one to another.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:3

**Q3. How many sets of five curtains are coupled together?**  
**Answer:** Two sets.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:3

---

### Exodus 26:4

**Q1. What loops of what color shall be made on the edge of the one curtain?**  
**Answer:** Loops of blue.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:4

**Q2. Where shall the loops of blue be set?**  
**Answer:** On the edge of the one curtain from the selvedge in the coupling.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:4

**Q3. Where shall the second edge have loops?**  
**Answer:** In the edge of the curtain in the coupling of the second.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:4

---

### Exodus 26:5

**Q1. How many loops shall be in the one curtain?**  
**Answer:** Fifty loops.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:5

**Q2. How many loops shall be in the edge of the curtain that is in the coupling of the second?**  
**Answer:** Fifty loops.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:5

**Q3. How shall the loops be positioned relative to one another?**  
**Answer:** The loops may take hold one of another.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:5

---

### Exodus 26:6

**Q1. What shall be made to couple the curtains together?**  
**Answer:** Fifty taches of gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:6

**Q2. What shall be coupled together with the taches?**  
**Answer:** The curtains one to another.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:6

**Q3. What will the tabernacle become when coupled together?**  
**Answer:** One tabernacle.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:6

---

### Exodus 26:7

**Q1. What shall be made of goats' hair?**  
**Answer:** Curtains to be a covering upon the tabernacle.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:7

**Q2. How many curtains shall be made of goats' hair?**  
**Answer:** Eleven curtains.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:7

**Q3. What is the purpose of these curtains?**  
**Answer:** To be a covering upon the tabernacle.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:7

---

### Exodus 26:8

**Q1. What shall the length of one curtain be?**  
**Answer:** Thirty cubits.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:8

**Q2. What shall the breadth of one curtain be?**  
**Answer:** Four cubits.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:8

**Q3. What measure shall the eleven curtains have?**  
**Answer:** One measure.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:8

---

### Exodus 26:9

**Q1. How many curtains shall be coupled by themselves?**  
**Answer:** Five curtains.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:9

**Q2. How many curtains shall be coupled by themselves in the second group?**  
**Answer:** Six curtains.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:9

**Q3. Where shall the sixth curtain be doubled?**  
**Answer:** In the forefront of the tabernacle.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:9

---

### Exodus 26:10

**Q1. How many loops shall be on the edge of the one curtain that is outmost in the coupling?**  
**Answer:** Fifty loops.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:10

**Q2. How many loops shall be in the edge of the curtain which coupleth the second?**  
**Answer:** Fifty loops.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:10

**Q3. Where are the fifty loops placed on the second curtain?**  
**Answer:** In the edge of the curtain which coupleth the second.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:10

---

### Exodus 26:11

**Q1. What shall be made to couple the tent together?**  
**Answer:** Fifty taches of brass.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:11

**Q2. Where shall the taches of brass be put?**  
**Answer:** Into the loops.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:11

**Q3. What will the tent become when coupled together?**  
**Answer:** One tent.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:11

---

### Exodus 26:12

**Q1. What is the remnant that remaineth of the curtains of the tent?**  
**Answer:** The half curtain that remaineth.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:12

**Q2. Where shall the remnant of the curtains hang?**  
**Answer:** Over the backside of the tabernacle.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:12

**Q3. What is the remnant composed of?**  
**Answer:** The half curtain that remaineth.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:12

---

### Exodus 26:13

**Q1. How much shall be on the one side and on the other side of the surplus in length?**  
**Answer:** A cubit on the one side, and a cubit on the other side.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:13

**Q2. Where shall the surplus hang?**  
**Answer:** Over the sides of the tabernacle on this side and on that side.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:13

**Q3. What is the purpose of hanging it over the sides?**  
**Answer:** To cover it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:13

---

### Exodus 26:14

**Q1. What shall be made for the tent?**  
**Answer:** A covering for the tent of rams' skins dyed red.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:14

**Q2. What kind of skins shall be made above that?**  
**Answer:** A covering of badgers' skins.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:14

**Q3. What are the rams' skins dyed?**  
**Answer:** Red.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:14

---

### Exodus 26:15

**Q1. What shall be made for the tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** Boards.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:15

**Q2. What material shall the boards be made of?**  
**Answer:** Shittim wood.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:15

**Q3. How shall the boards stand?**  
**Answer:** Upward.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:15

---

### Exodus 26:16

**Q1. What shall the length of a board be?**  
**Answer:** Ten cubits.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:16

**Q2. What shall the breadth of a board be?**  
**Answer:** A cubit and a half.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:16

---

### Exodus 26:17

**Q1. How many tenons shall there be in one board?**  
**Answer:** Two tenons.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:17

---

### Exodus 26:18

**Q1. How many boards shall be made for the tabernacle on the south side southward?**  
**Answer:** Twenty boards.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:18

---

**Q1. How many sockets of silver shall be made under the twenty boards?**  
**Answer:** Forty sockets of silver.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:19

---

### Exodus 26:20

**Q1. For what side of the tabernacle shall there be twenty boards?**  
**Answer:** The second side on the north side.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:20

---

### Exodus 26:21

**Q1. How many sockets of silver shall be under the twenty boards of the north side?**  
**Answer:** Forty sockets of silver.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:21

---

### Exodus 26:22

**Answer:** For the sides of the tabernacle westward.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:22

---

### Exodus 26:23

**Q1. How many boards shall be made for the corners of the tabernacle in the two sides?**  
**Answer:** Two boards.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:23

---

### Exodus 26:24

**Q1. How shall the corners be coupled together beneath?**  
**Answer:** They shall be coupled together beneath.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:24

---

### Exodus 26:25

**Q1. How many boards shall there be in all for the western side?**  
**Answer:** Eight boards.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:25

**Verse:** Exodus 26:26

**Q1. How many bars shall be for the boards of the other side of the tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** Five bars.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:27

**Answer:** From end to end.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:28

### Exodus 26:29

**Q1. What shall the boards be overlaid with?**  
**Answer:** Gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:29

**Answer:** According to the fashion thereof which was shewed thee in the mount.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:30

---

### Exodus 26:31

**Answer:** A vail.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:31

**Answer:** Four pillars of shittim wood.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:32

---

**Q1. Where shall ye hang up the vail under the taches?**  
**Answer:** Within the vail.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:33

**Answer:** Upon the ark of the testimony in the most holy place.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:34

**Answer:** Without the vail.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:35

**Answer:** An hanging.  
**Verse:** Exodus 26:36
