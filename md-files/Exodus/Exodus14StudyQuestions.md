# Exodus 14 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 14:1

**Q1. Who spake unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:1

**Q2. What did the LORD do after speaking unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** He spake unto Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:1

**Q3. Who was the recipient of the LORD's word in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:1

---

### Exodus 14:2

**Q1. Whom should the children of Israel speak to and turn back?**  
**Answer:** Phinehas / Pharaoh? No, the text says "Speak unto the children of Israel, that they turn and encamp before Piha-hiroth".  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:2

**Q2. Where were the children of Israel to encamp between?**  
**Answer:** Between Migdol and the sea.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:2

**Q3. Before what place were they to encamp over against Baal-zephon?**  
**Answer:** Piha-hiroth.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:2

---

### Exodus 14:3

**Q1. What will Pharaoh say of the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** They are entangled in the land, the wilderness hath shut them in.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:3

**Q2. Where did Pharaoh think the children of Israel were entangled?**  
**Answer:** In the land.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:3

**Q3. What did Pharaoh think had shut them in?**  
**Answer:** The wilderness.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:3

---

### Exodus 14:4

**Q1. Whose heart will the LORD harden?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh's heart.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:4

**Q2. What will Pharaoh follow after?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:4

**Q3. Upon whom and all his host will the LORD be honored?**  
**Answer:** Upon Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:4

---

### Exodus 14:5

**Q1. What was told the king of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** That the people fled.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:5

**Q2. Whose heart was turned against the people?**  
**Answer:** The heart of Pharaoh and of his servants.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:5

**Q3. What did Pharaoh and his servants say they had done regarding letting Israel go?**  
**Answer:** Why have we done this, that we have let Israel go from serving us?  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:5

---

### Exodus 14:6

**Q1. What did Pharaoh make ready?**  
**Answer:** His chariot.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:6

**Q2. Whom did Pharaoh take with him?**  
**Answer:** His people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:6

---

### Exodus 14:7

**Q1. How many choice chariots did he take?**  
**Answer:** Six hundred choice chariots.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:7

**Q2. What was upon every one of the chariots?**  
**Answer:** Captains over every one of them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:7

**Q3. Who else besides the choice chariots did Pharaoh take?**  
**Answer:** All the chariots of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:7

---

### Exodus 14:8

**Q1. Whose heart did the LORD harden?**  
**Answer:** The heart of Pharaoh king of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:8

**Q2. Whom did Pharaoh pursue?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:8

**Q3. With what did the children of Israel go out?**  
**Answer:** An high hand.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:8

---

### Exodus 14:9

**Q1. Who pursued after the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The Egyptians.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:9

**Q2. What did the Egyptians overtake?**  
**Answer:** All the horses and chariots of Pharaoh, and his horsemen, and his army.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:9

**Q3. Where did they overtake the children of Israel encamping?**  
**Answer:** By the sea, beside Piha-hiroth, before Baal-zephon.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:9

---

### Exodus 14:10

**Q1. Who drew nigh when Pharaoh drew nigh?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:10

**Q2. How did the children of Israel feel when they saw the Egyptians?**  
**Answer:** Sore afraid.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:10

**Q3. To whom did the children of Israel cry out?**  
**Answer:** Unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:10

---

### Exodus 14:11

**Q1. What did the children of Israel say to Moses about graves?**  
**Answer:** Because there were no graves in Egypt, hast thou taken us away to die in the wilderness?  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:11

**Q2. What did they ask Moses about why he had dealt with them?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore hast thou dealt thus with us, to carry us forth out of Egypt?  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:11

**Q3. Where did they ask if there were no graves?**  
**Answer:** In Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:11

---

### Exodus 14:12

**Q1. What did the children of Israel say to Moses in Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Let us alone, that we may serve the Egyptians.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:12

**Q2. Why did they say it had been better for them to serve the Egyptians?**  
**Answer:** For it had been better for us to serve the Egyptians, than that we should die in the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:12

**Q3. Where did they say it would be better to die or serve?**  
**Answer:** In the wilderness / Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:12

---

### Exodus 14:13

**Q1. What did Moses tell the people?**  
**Answer:** Fear ye not, stand still, and see the salvation of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:13

**Q2. What kind of salvation would the LORD show them today?**  
**Answer:** The salvation of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:13

**Q3. Will they see the Egyptians again after this day?**  
**Answer:** For the Egyptians whom ye have seen to day, ye shall see them again no more for ever.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:13

---

### Exodus 14:14

**Q1. Who shall fight for you?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:14

**Q2. What shall ye do?**  
**Answer:** Hold your peace.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:14

**Q3. Does the LORD fight for them while they hold their peace?**  
**Answer:** Yes ("The LORD shall fight for you, and ye shall hold your peace.")  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:14

---

**Q3. Who was told to speak unto the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:15

**Verse:** Exodus 14:16

**Answer:** The hearts of the Egyptians.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:17

**Q2. What will the Egyptians do after their hearts are hardened?**  
**Answer:** They shall follow them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:17

**Q2. Who shall know that the LORD is the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The Egyptians.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:18

**Answer:** Upon Pharaoh, upon his chariots, and upon his horsemen.

### Exodus 14:19

**Q1. Who went before the camp of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The angel of God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:19

**Q2. Where did the angel of God remove to?**  
**Answer:** Behind them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:19

**Q3. What went from before their face and stood behind them?**  
**Answer:** The pillar of the cloud.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:19

---

### Exodus 14:20

**Q1. Between what two camps did the pillar come?**  
**Answer:** The camp of the Egyptians and the camp of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:20

**Q2. What was the cloud and darkness to one side?**  
**Answer:** It was a cloud and darkness to them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:20

**Q3. What did the cloud give by night to the other side?**  
**Answer:** Light by night.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:20

---

**Q1. What did Moses stretch out over the sea?**  
**Answer:** His hand.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:21

**Q2. What did the LORD cause the sea to go back by?**  
**Answer:** A strong east wind all that night.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:21

**Answer:** Dry land.  
**Verse:** Exodus 14:21

**Answer:** A wall unto them.
