# Exodus 19 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 19:1

**Q1. When were the children of Israel come forth out of the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** In the third month.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:1

**Q2. On what day of the third month were the children of Israel come forth?**  
**Answer:** The same day.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:1

**Q3. Where did the children of Israel come into?**  
**Answer:** The wilderness of Sinai.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:1

---

### Exodus 19:2

**Q1. From where were they departed?**  
**Answer:** Rephidim.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:2

**Q2. Where were they come?**  
**Answer:** The desert of Sinai.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:2

**Q3. Where did Israel pitch?**  
**Answer:** In the wilderness before the mount.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:2

---

### Exodus 19:3

**Q1. Who went up unto God?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:3

**Q2. Who called unto Moses out of the mountain?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:3

**Q3. What was Moses told to say to the house of Jacob and tell the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Thus shalt thou say to the house of Jacob, and tell the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:3

---

### Exodus 19:4

**Q1. What did ye see that I did unto the Egyptians?**  
**Answer:** What I did unto the Egyptians.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:4

**Q2. How did the LORD bear them?**  
**Answer:** On eagles' wings.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:4

**Q3. Where did the LORD bring them?**  
**Answer:** Unto myself.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:4

---

### Exodus 19:5

**Q1. If ye will obey my voice indeed, what will ye keep?**  
**Answer:** My covenant.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:5

**Q2. What shall ye be unto me above all people?**  
**Answer:** A peculiar treasure.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:5

**Q3. Whose is all the earth?**  
**Answer:** Mine.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:5

---

### Exodus 19:6

**Q1. What shall ye be unto me?**  
**Answer:** A kingdom of priests.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:6

**Q2. What kind of nation shall ye be?**  
**Answer:** An holy nation.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:6

**Q3. What words shall Moses speak unto the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** These are the words which thou shalt speak unto the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:6

---

### Exodus 19:7

**Q1. Who came and called for the elders of the people?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:7

**Q2. What did Moses lay before their faces?**  
**Answer:** All these words which the LORD commanded him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:7

**Q3. Who commanded Moses these words?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:7

---

### Exodus 19:8

**Q1. Who answered together?**  
**Answer:** All the people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:8

**Q2. What did all the people say?**  
**Answer:** All that the LORD hath spoken we will do.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:8

**Q3. Who returned the words of the people unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:8

---

### Exodus 19:9

**Q1. In what will the LORD come unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** In a thick cloud.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:9

**Q2. Why will the LORD come in a thick cloud that the people may hear?**  
**Answer:** That the people may hear when I speak with thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:9

**Q3. What did Moses do with the words of the people unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** He told the words of the people unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:9

---

### Exodus 19:10

**Q1. Who said unto Moses, Go unto the people?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:10

**Q2. What are the people to be sanctified today and tomorrow?**  
**Answer:** Sanctify them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:10

**Q3. What must the people wash?**  
**Answer:** Their clothes.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:10

---

### Exodus 19:11

**Q1. Be ready against what day?**  
**Answer:** The third day.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:11

**Q2. What will come down in the sight of all the people upon mount Sinai?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:11

**Q3. Where will the LORD come down?**  
**Answer:** Upon mount Sinai.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:11

---

### Exodus 19:12

**Q1. What shall Moses set round about unto the people?**  
**Answer:** Bounds.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:12

**Q2. What are the people told not to go up into, or touch?**  
**Answer:** The mount, or touch the border of it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:12

**Q3. What shall surely happen to whosoever toucheth the mount?**  
**Answer:** He shall be put to death.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:12

---

### Exodus 19:13

**Q1. Who shall not touch the hand?**  
**Answer:** There shall not an hand touch it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:13

**Q2. What shall happen to he that touches it, whether it be beast or man?**  
**Answer:** He shall be stoned, or shot through.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:13

**Q3. When may they come up to the mount?**  
**Answer:** When the trumpet soundeth long.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:13

---

### Exodus 19:14

**Q1. Who went down from the mount unto the people?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:14

**Q2. What did Moses do to the people?**  
**Answer:** Sanctified the people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:14

**Q3. What did the people wash?**  
**Answer:** Their clothes.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:14

---

### Exodus 19:15

**Q1. What did Moses say unto the people?**  
**Answer:** Be ready against the third day.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:15

**Q2. What did Moses tell them not to come at?**  
**Answer:** Your wives.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:15

**Q3. Against what day were they to be ready?**  
**Answer:** The third day.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:15

---

### Exodus 19:16

**Q1. What happened on the third day in the morning?**  
**Answer:** There were thunders and lightnings, and a thick cloud upon the mount.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:16

**Q2. What sounded exceeding loud?**  
**Answer:** The voice of the trumpet.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:16

**Q3. How did all the people that was in the camp react?**  
**Answer:** Trembled.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:16

---

### Exodus 19:17

**Q1. Who brought forth the people out of the camp to meet with God?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:17

**Q2. Where did they stand?**  
**Answer:** At the nether part of the mount.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:17

**Q3. Who did they go to meet with?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:17

---

### Exodus 19:18

**Q1. What was mount Sinai all on?**  
**Answer:** A smoke.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:18

**Q2. Why was mount Sinai on a smoke?**  
**Answer:** Because the LORD descended upon it in fire.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:18

**Q3. How did the smoke thereof ascend?**  
**Answer:** As the smoke of a furnace, and the whole mount quaked greatly.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:18

---

### Exodus 19:19

**Q1. What waxed louder and louder?**  
**Answer:** The voice of the trumpet.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:19

**Q2. Who spake?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:19

**Q3. Who answered Moses by a voice?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:19

---

### Exodus 19:20

**Q1. Where did the LORD come down?**  
**Answer:** Upon mount Sinai, on the top of the mount.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:20

**Q2. Who called Moses up to the top of the mount?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:20

**Q3. Who went up?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:20

---

### Exodus 19:21

**Q1. Who said unto Moses, Go down?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:21

**Q2. What did the LORD tell Moses to charge the people?**  
**Answer:** Charge the people, lest they break through unto the LORD to gaze.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:21

**Q3. What might happen to many of them if they break through?**  
**Answer:** Many of them perish.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:21

---

### Exodus 19:22

**Q1. Who also shall come near to the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The priests which come near to the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:22

**Q2. What must the priests do to themselves?**  
**Answer:** Let them sanctify themselves.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:22

**Q3. Why must the priests sanctify themselves?**  
**Answer:** Lest the LORD break forth upon them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:22

---

### Exodus 19:23

**Q1. What did Moses say unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The people cannot come up to mount Sinai.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:23

**Q2. What did the LORD charge them to do?**  
**Answer:** Set bounds about the mount, and sanctify it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:23

**Q3. Who charged them to set bounds about the mount?**  
**Answer:** Thou chargedst us.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:23

---

### Exodus 19:24

**Q1. Who said unto him, Away, get thee down?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:24

**Q2. Who shall come up with Moses?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt come up, thou, and Aaron with thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:24

**Q3. What must the priests and the people not let themselves do?**  
**Answer:** Break through to come up unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:24

---

### Exodus 19:25

**Q1. Who went down unto the people?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 19:25

**Q2. What did Moses do after going down?**  
**Answer:** Spake unto them.
