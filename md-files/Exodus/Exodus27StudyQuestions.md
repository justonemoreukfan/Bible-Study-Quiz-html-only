# Exodus 27 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 27:1

**Q1. What specific type of wood is to be used to make the altar?**  
**Answer:** Shittim wood.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:1

**Q2. How long is the altar to be?**  
**Answer:** Five cubits.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:1

**Q3. What shape is the altar to be regarding its length and breadth?**  
**Answer:** Foursquare.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:1

---

### Exodus 27:2

**Q1. What shall be made upon the four corners of the altar?**  
**Answer:** Horns.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:2

**Q2. The horns shall be of the same what?**  
**Answer:** Of the same.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:2

**Q3. With what shall the altar be overlaid?**  
**Answer:** Brass.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:2

---

### Exodus 27:3

**Q1. What pans shall be made to receive his ashes?**  
**Answer:** His pans to receive his ashes.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:3

**Q2. What tools are listed alongside shovels?**  
**Answer:** Basons, fleshhooks, and firepans.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:3

**Q3. All the vessels of the altar shall be made of what material?**  
**Answer:** Brass.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:3

---

### Exodus 27:4

**Q1. What kind of grate shall be made for the altar?**  
**Answer:** A grate of network.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:4

**Q2. What material shall be used for the network?**  
**Answer:** Brass.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:4

**Q3. What shall be upon the four corners of the net?**  
**Answer:** Four brasen rings.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:4

---

### Exodus 27:5

**Q1. Where shall the grate be placed in relation to the altar?**  
**Answer:** Under the compass of the altar beneath.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:5

**Q2. How far down shall the net reach?**  
**Answer:** Even to the midst of the altar.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:5

---

### Exodus 27:6

**Q1. What shall be made for the altar?**  
**Answer:** Staves for the altar.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:6

**Q2. What kind of wood shall be used for the staves?**  
**Answer:** Shittim wood.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:6

**Q3. With what shall the staves be overlaid?**  
**Answer:** Brass.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:6

---

### Exodus 27:7

**Q1. Where shall the staves be put?**  
**Answer:** Into the rings.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:7

**Q2. On how many sides of the altar shall the staves be?**  
**Answer:** Upon the two sides of the altar.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:7

**Q3. What purpose do the staves serve when the altar is borne?**  
**Answer:** To bear it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:7

---

### Exodus 27:8

**Q1. How shall the altar be made?**  
**Answer:** Hollow with boards.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:8

**Q2. According to what pattern shall the altar be made?**  
**Answer:** As it was shewed thee in the mount.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:8

**Q3. How shall the altar be shown to Moses?**  
**Answer:** So shall they make it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:8

---

### Exodus 27:9

**Q1. What shall be made for the tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** The court of the tabernacle.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:9

**Q2. For which direction shall the south side have hangings?**  
**Answer:** The south side southward.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:9

**Q3. What material shall the hangings for the south side be made of?**  
**Answer:** Fine twined linen.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:9

---

### Exodus 27:10

**Q1. How many pillars shall there be for the south side?**  
**Answer:** Twenty pillars.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:10

**Q2. How many brasen sockets shall there be for the pillars?**  
**Answer:** Twenty brasen sockets.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:10

**Q3. Of what material shall the hooks of the pillars and their fillets be made?**  
**Answer:** Silver.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:10

---

### Exodus 27:11

**Q1. Along which side shall there be hangings in the length of an hundred cubits?**  
**Answer:** The north side in the length.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:11

**Q2. How many pillars shall be on the north side?**  
**Answer:** Twenty pillars.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:11

**Q3. What shall the sockets and hooks be made of for the north side?**  
**Answer:** Sockets of brass and hooks and fillets of silver.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:11

---

### Exodus 27:16

**Q1. What shall be for the gate of the court?**  
**Answer:** An hanging of twenty cubits.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:16

**Q2. What colors are used in the hanging for the gate?**  
**Answer:** Blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine twined linen.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:16

**Q3. How many pillars and sockets shall the gate have?**  
**Answer:** Four pillars, and four sockets.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:16

---

**Q1. All the pillars round about the court shall be filleted with what?**  
**Answer:** Silver.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:17

**Q2. What shall the hooks of the pillars be made of?**  
**Answer:** Silver.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:17

**Q3. What shall the sockets of the pillars be made of?**  
**Answer:** Brass.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:17

---

### Exodus 27:18

**Q1. What shall be the length of the court?**  
**Answer:** An hundred cubits.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:18

**Q2. What shall be the breadth of the court everywhere?**  
**Answer:** Fifty every where.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:18

**Q3. What shall be the height of the fine twined linen hangings?**  
**Answer:** Five cubits.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:18

### Exodus 27:19

**Q1. All the vessels of the tabernacle in all the service thereof shall be made of what?**  
**Answer:** Brass.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:19

**Q2. All the pins of the tabernacle shall be made of what?**  
**Answer:** Brass.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:19

**Q3. What shall be made of brass along with the pins of the tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** All the pins of the court.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:19

---

### Exodus 27:20

**Q1. Whom shall thou command to bring unto thee pure oil olive beaten for the light?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:20

**Q2. What is the oil to be beaten for?**  
**Answer:** For the light.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:20

**Q3. What shall be caused to burn always?**  
**Answer:** The lamp.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:20

---

### Exodus 27:21

**Q1. Where shall the lamps be set up?**  
**Answer:** In the tabernacle of the congregation without the vail.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:21

**Q2. Which testimony shall the lamps be before?**  
**Answer:** Which is before the testimony.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:21

**Q3. Who shall order the lamps from evening to morning before the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Aaron and his sons.  
**Verse:** Exodus 27:21
