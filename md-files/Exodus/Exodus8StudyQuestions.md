# Exodus 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 8:1

**Q1. Who spoke unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:1

**Q2. Where was Moses told to go?**  
**Answer:** Unto Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:1

**Q3. What message was Moses to give unto Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD, Let my people go, that they may serve me.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:1

---

### Exodus 8:2

**Q1. What would happen if Pharaoh refused to let the people go?**  
**Answer:** The LORD will smite all thy borders with frogs.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:2

---

### Exodus 8:3

**Q1. Where shall the river bring forth abundantly?**  
**Answer:** With frogs.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:3

**Q2. Where shall the frogs go up and come into?**  
**Answer:** Into thine house, and into thy bedchamber, and upon thy bed.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:3

**Q3. Into whose houses, servants, people, ovens, and kneadingtroughs shall the frogs come?**  
**Answer:** Into the house of thy servants, and upon thy people, and into thine ovens, and into thy kneadingtroughs.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:3

---

### Exodus 8:4

**Q1. Upon whom shall the frogs come up?**  
**Answer:** Upon thee, and upon thy people, and upon all thy servants.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:4

---

### Exodus 8:5

**Q1. Who spoke unto Moses regarding the rod?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:5

**Q2. What did the LORD tell Moses to say to Aaron?**  
**Answer:** Stretch forth thine hand with thy rod over the streams, over the rivers, and over the ponds, and cause frogs to come up upon the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:5

---

### Exodus 8:6

**Q1. Whose hand did Aaron stretch out?**  
**Answer:** His hand.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:6

**Q2. Over what did Aaron stretch out his hand?**  
**Answer:** Over the waters of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:6

**Q3. What came up and covered the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** The frogs.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:6

---

### Exodus 8:7

**Q1. Who did likewise with their enchantments?**  
**Answer:** The magicians.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:7

**Q2. What did the magicians bring up?**  
**Answer:** Frogs up upon the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:7

---

### Exodus 8:8

**Q1. Whom did Pharaoh call for?**  
**Answer:** Moses and Aaron.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:8

**Q2. What did Pharaoh ask Moses and Aaron to entreat?**  
**Answer:** The LORD, that he may take away the frogs from me, and from my people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:8

**Q3. What did Pharaoh say he will do if the frogs are taken away?**  
**Answer:** I will let the people go, that they may do sacrifice unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:8

---

### Exodus 8:9

**Q1. What did Moses say unto Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** Glory over me: when shall I intreat for thee, and for thy servants, and for thy people, to destroy the frogs from thee and thy houses, that they may remain in the river only?  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:9

---

### Exodus 8:10

**Q1. When did Pharaoh say the frogs should be destroyed?**  
**Answer:** Tomorrow.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:10

**Q2. What did Moses say in response to Pharaoh's answer?**  
**Answer:** Be it according to thy word.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:10

**Q3. Why did Moses want Pharaoh to know there is none like unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** That thou mayest know that there is none like unto the LORD our God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:10

---

### Exodus 8:11

**Q1. Where shall the frogs depart from?**  
**Answer:** From thee, and from thy houses, and from thy servants, and from thy people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:11

**Q2. Where shall the frogs remain only?**  
**Answer:** In the river.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:11

---

### Exodus 8:12

**Q1. Who went out from Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** Moses and Aaron.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:12

**Q2. Unto whom did Moses cry concerning the frogs?**  
**Answer:** Unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:12

---

### Exodus 8:13

**Q1. What did the LORD do according to the word of Moses?**  
**Answer:** He did according to the word of Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:13

**Q2. What happened to the frogs out of the houses, out of the villages, and out of the fields?**  
**Answer:** They died.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:13

---

### Exodus 8:14

**Q1. How were the dead frogs gathered together?**  
**Answer:** In heaps.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:14

**Q2. What did the land stink of because of the heaps?**  
**Answer:** The land stank.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:14

---

### Exodus 8:15

**Q1. What did Pharaoh see when he saw that there was respite?**  
**Answer:** That there was respite.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:15

**Q2. What did Pharaoh do to his heart?**  
**Answer:** He hardened his heart.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:15

**Q3. To whom did Pharaoh not hearken?**  
**Answer:** Unto Moses and Aaron.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:15

---

### Exodus 8:16

**Q1. What did the LORD say unto Moses regarding Aaron?**  
**Answer:** Say unto Aaron, Stretch out thy rod, and smite the dust of the land.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:16

**Q2. What will the dust become throughout all the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Lice.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:16

---

### Exodus 8:17

**Q1. What did Aaron stretch out with his rod?**  
**Answer:** His hand with his rod.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:17

**Q2. What did Aaron smite?**  
**Answer:** The dust of the earth.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:17

**Q3. Upon what did the lice become?**  
**Answer:** Upon man, and upon beast.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:17

---

### Exodus 8:18

**Q1. What did the magicians do with their enchantments to bring forth lice?**  
**Answer:** They did so with their enchantments to bring forth lice.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:18

**Q2. Were the magicians able to bring forth lice?**  
**Answer:** But they could not.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:18

**Q3. Upon whom and what were the lice?**  
**Answer:** Upon man, and upon beast.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:18

---

### Exodus 8:19

**Q1. What did the magicians say unto Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** This is the finger of God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:19

**Q2. What was the state of Pharaoh's heart?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh's heart was hardened.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:19

**Q3. Did Pharaoh hearken unto Moses and Aaron?**  
**Answer:** He hearkened not unto them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:19

---

### Exodus 8:20

**Q1. To whom did the LORD tell Moses to rise up early?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:20

**Q2. Where did the LORD say Pharaoh would come forth?**  
**Answer:** To the water.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:20

**Q3. What message was Moses to give Pharaoh by the water?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the LORD, Let my people go, that they may serve me.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:20

---

### Exodus 8:21

**Q1. What will happen if Pharaoh will not let the people go?**  
**Answer:** I will send swarms of flies upon thee, and upon thy servants, and upon thy people, and into thy houses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:21

**Q2. Whose houses shall be full of swarms of flies?**  
**Answer:** The houses of the Egyptians.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:21

**Q3. What else shall be full of swarms of flies?**  
**Answer:** The ground whereon they are.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:21

---

### Exodus 8:22

**Q1. In what land will the LORD sever in that day?**  
**Answer:** The land of Goshen.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:22

**Q2. Where did the LORD's people dwell?**  
**Answer:** In the land of Goshen.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:22

**Q3. Why did the LORD sever the land of Goshen where His people dwelt?**  
**Answer:** That no swarms of flies shall be there.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:22

---

### Exodus 8:23

**Q1. What will the LORD put between His people and Pharaoh's people?**  
**Answer:** A division.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:23

**Q2. When shall this sign happen?**  
**Answer:** Tomorrow.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:23

---

### Exodus 8:24

**Q1. Who did the LORD do so according to?**  
**Answer:** The LORD did so.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:24

**Q2. What came into the house of Pharaoh, and into his servants' houses, and into all the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** There came a grievous swarm of flies.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:24

**Q3. What happened to the land because of the swarm of flies?**  
**Answer:** The land was corrupted by reason of the swarm of flies.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:24

---

### Exodus 8:25

**Q1. Whom did Pharaoh call for?**  
**Answer:** Moses and for Aaron.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:25

**Q2. What did Pharaoh tell them to do?**  
**Answer:** Go ye, sacrifice to your God in the land.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:25

---

### Exodus 8:26

**Q1. Why did Moses say it is not meet to do so?**  
**Answer:** For we shall sacrifice the abomination of the Egyptians to the LORD our God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:26

**Q2. What would the Egyptians do if they saw the Israelites sacrifice the abomination of the Egyptians before their eyes?**  
**Answer:** Will they not stone us?  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:26

---

### Exodus 8:27

**Q1. How far did Moses say they must go into the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** Three days' journey.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:27

**Q2. To whom shall they sacrifice in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** To the LORD our God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:27

**Q3. Whose command were they to follow regarding the sacrifice?**  
**Answer:** As he shall command us.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:27

---

### Exodus 8:28

**Q1. What did Pharaoh say he will let the people do?**  
**Answer:** Go, that ye may sacrifice to the LORD your God in the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:28

**Q2. What limitation did Pharaoh place on their going?**  
**Answer:** Only ye shall not go very far away.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:28

**Q3. What did Pharaoh ask Moses and Aaron to do for him?**  
**Answer:** Intreat for me.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:28

---

**Q1. Where did Moses say he would go out from Pharaoh?**

**Verse:** Exodus 8:29

**Q2. Unto whom would Moses intreat?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:29

**Q3. When did Moses say the swarms of flies would depart from Pharaoh, his servants, and his people?**

**Verse:** Exodus 8:29

**Q1. Who went out from Pharaoh?**

**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:30

---

**Q1. What did the LORD do according to the word of Moses?**  
**Answer:** He did according to the word of Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:31

**Answer:** From Pharaoh, from his servants, and from his people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 8:31

**Verse:** Exodus 8:31

**Verse:** Exodus 8:32
