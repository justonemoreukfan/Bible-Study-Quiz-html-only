# Exodus 30 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 30:1

**Q1. What is thou shalt make to burn incense upon?**  
**Answer:** An altar to burn incense upon.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:1

**Q2. Of what material shalt thou make the altar to burn incense upon?**  
**Answer:** Of shittim wood.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:1

---

### Exodus 30:2

**Q3. How long shall the altar be?**  
**Answer:** A cubit.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:2

**Q4. How broad shall the altar be?**  
**Answer:** A cubit.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:2

**Q5. How high shall the altar be, and what shall the horns thereof be of?**  
**Answer:** Two cubits high; the horns thereof shall be of the same.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:2

---

### Exodus 30:3

**Q6. What shall thou overlay the top, sides, and horns of with pure gold?**  
**Answer:** The top thereof, and the sides thereof round about, and the horns thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:3

**Q7. What shall thou make unto the altar round about?**  
**Answer:** A crown of gold round about.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:3

---

### Exodus 30:4

**Q8. What shall thou make for the altar under the crown of it?**  
**Answer:** Two golden rings.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:4

**Q9. Upon which sides of the altar shall thou make the two golden rings?**  
**Answer:** Upon the two ribs of it, upon the two sides of it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:4

**Q10. What purpose shall the two golden rings serve?**  
**Answer:** For places for the staves to bear it withal.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:4

---

### Exodus 30:5

**Q11. Of what material shall thou make the staves?**  
**Answer:** Of shittim wood.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:5

**Q12. What shall thou overlay the staves with?**  
**Answer:** With gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:5

---

### Exodus 30:6

**Q13. Where shall thou put the altar?**  
**Answer:** Before the vail that is by the ark of the testimony.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:6

**Q14. Where shall the altar be placed in relation to the mercy seat?**  
**Answer:** Before the mercy seat that is over the testimony.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:6

**Q15. What will the Lord do where He will meet with Aaron?**  
**Answer:** Where I will meet with thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:6

---

### Exodus 30:7

**Q16. What shall Aaron burn upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** Sweet incense.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:7

**Q17. When shall Aaron burn the sweet incense, specifically regarding the lamps?**  
**Answer:** Every morning, when he dresseth the lamps.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:7

---

### Exodus 30:8

**Q18. When shall Aaron light the lamps at even?**  
**Answer:** When Aaron lighteth the lamps at even.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:8

**Q19. What kind of incense shall burn throughout your generations before the Lord?**  
**Answer:** A perpetual incense.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:8

---

### Exodus 30:9

**Q20. What kind of incense shall ye not offer upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** Strange incense.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:9

**Q21. What else shall ye not offer upon the altar?**  
**Answer:** Burnt sacrifice, nor meat offering; neither shall ye pour drink offering thereon.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:9

---

### Exodus 30:10

**Q22. How often shall Aaron make an atonement upon the horns of the altar?**  
**Answer:** Once in a year.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:10

**Q23. With what shall Aaron make the atonement upon the horns of the altar?**  
**Answer:** With the blood of the sin offering of atonements.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:10

**Q24. How often shall Aaron make the atonement throughout your generations?**  
**Answer:** Once in the year shall he make atonement upon it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:10

---

### Exodus 30:11

**Q25. To whom did the Lord speak?**  
**Answer:** Unto Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:11

---

### Exodus 30:12

**Q26. What shall Moses take when he taketh the sum of the children of Israel after their number?**  
**Answer:** A ransom for his soul unto the Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:12

**Q27. Why must every man give a ransom when numbered, concerning plagues?**  
**Answer:** That there be no plague among them, when thou numberest them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:12

---

### Exodus 30:13

**Q28. Who shall pass among those that are numbered?**  
**Answer:** Every one that passeth among them that are numbered.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:13

**Q29. What shall half a shekel be called according to the shekel of the sanctuary?**  
**Answer:** Half a shekel after the shekel of the sanctuary.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:13

**Q30. How many gerahs make a shekel?**  
**Answer:** Twenty gerahs are a shekel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:13

**Q31. What is the half shekel offered to the Lord called?**  
**Answer:** An offering to the Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:13

---

### Exodus 30:14

**Q32. Who shall pass among those that are numbered from twenty years old and above?**  
**Answer:** Every one that passeth among them that are numbered.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:14

**Q33. What shall everyone give unto the Lord?**  
**Answer:** An offering unto the Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:14

---

### Exodus 30:15

**Q33. Who shall not give more than half a shekel?**  
**Answer:** The rich.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:15

**Q34. Who shall not give less than half a shekel?**  
**Answer:** The poor.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:15

**Q35. When they give an offering unto the Lord to make an atonement for your souls, what must they give?**  
**Answer:** When they give an offering unto the Lord, to make an atonement for your souls.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:15

---

### Exodus 30:16

**Q36. What shall Moses take of the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The atonement money.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:16

**Q37. For what service shall Moses appoint the atonement money?**  
**Answer:** For the service of the tabernacle of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:16

**Q38. For whom shall the atonement money be a memorial before the Lord?**  
**Answer:** For a memorial unto the children of Israel before the Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:16

---

### Exodus 30:17

**Q39. To whom did the Lord speak?**  
**Answer:** Unto Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:17

---

### Exodus 30:18

**Q40. What shall thou make of brass?**  
**Answer:** A laver of brass.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:18

**Q41. Of what material shall his foot also be?**  
**Answer:** Of brass.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:18

**Q42. Where shall thou put the laver?**  
**Answer:** Between the tabernacle of the congregation and the altar.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:18

---

### Exodus 30:19

**Q43. What shall Aaron and his sons wash there?**  
**Answer:** Their hands and their feet.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:19

---

### Exodus 30:20

**Q44. When they go into the tabernacle of the congregation, what shall they wash with?**  
**Answer:** With water.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:20

**Q45. When they come near to the altar to minister, what shall they do?**  
**Answer:** Burn offering made by fire unto the Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:20

---

### Exodus 30:21

**Q46. What shall they wash so that they die not?**  
**Answer:** Their hands and their feet.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:21

**Q47. For whom shall this be a statute for ever?**  
**Answer:** To him and to his seed throughout their generations.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:21

---

### Exodus 30:22

**Q48. Moreover unto whom did the Lord speak?**  
**Answer:** Unto Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:22

---

### Exodus 30:23

**Q49. What principal spices shall thou take unto thee?**  
**Answer:** Pure myrrh, sweet cinnamon, sweet calamus, and cassia.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:23

**Q50. How much pure myrrh shall thou take?**  
**Answer:** Five hundred shekels.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:23

**Q51. How much sweet cinnamon and sweet calamus shall thou take?**  
**Answer:** Half so much, even two hundred and fifty shekels.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:23

---

### Exodus 30:24

**Q52. How much cassia shall thou take after the shekel of the sanctuary?**  
**Answer:** Five hundred shekels.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:24

**Q53. Of what oil shall thou take an hin?**  
**Answer:** Of oil olive.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:24

---

### Exodus 30:25

**Q54. What shall thou make of the ingredients?**  
**Answer:** An oil of holy ointment.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:25

**Q55. According to what art shall the ointment be made?**  
**Answer:** After the art of the apothecary.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:25

**Q56. What shall the mixture be called?**  
**Answer:** An holy anointing oil.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:25

---

### Exodus 30:26

**Q57. What shall thou anoint with the holy anointing oil?**  
**Answer:** The tabernacle of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:26

**Q58. What else shall thou anoint besides the tabernacle?**  
**Answer:** The ark of the testimony.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:26

---

### Exodus 30:27

**Q59. What table and vessels shall thou anoint?**  
**Answer:** The table and all his vessels.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:27

**Q60. What candlestick and his vessels shall thou anoint?**  
**Answer:** The candlestick and his vessels.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:27

**Q61. What altar shall thou anoint?**  
**Answer:** The altar of incense.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:27

---

### Exodus 30:28

**Q62. What altar of burnt offering shall thou anoint?**  
**Answer:** The altar of burnt offering with all his vessels.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:28

**Q63. What else shall thou anoint besides the altar of burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** The laver and his foot.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:28

---

### Exodus 30:29

**Q64. What shall thou sanctify them that they may be?**  
**Answer:** Most holy.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:29

**Q65. What will happen to whatsoever toucheth them?**  
**Answer:** Whatsoever toucheth them shall be holy.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:29

---

### Exodus 30:30

**Q66. Who shall thou anoint?**  
**Answer:** Aaron and his sons.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:30

---

### Exodus 30:31

**Q67. Unto whom shalt thou speak concerning the anointing oil?**  
**Answer:** Unto the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:31

**Q68. What shall the holy anointing oil be throughout your generations?**  
**Answer:** An holy anointing oil unto me throughout your generations.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:31

---

### Exodus 30:32

**Q69. Upon whose flesh shall it not be poured?**  
**Answer:** Upon man's flesh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:32

**Q70. What else shall you not make like it?**  
**Answer:** Any other like it, after the composition of it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:32

**Q71. How is the oil described in relation to holiness?**  
**Answer:** It is holy, and it shall be holy unto you.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:32

---

### Exodus 30:33

**Q72. Who shall be cut off from his people if he compounds any like it?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever compoundeth any like it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:33

**Q73. Who else shall be cut off from his people if he puts any of it upon a stranger?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever putteth any of it upon a stranger.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:33

---

### Exodus 30:34

**Q75. What sweet spices shall the Lord say unto Moses to take?**  
**Answer:** Stacte, and onycha, and galbanum; these sweet spices with pure frankincense.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:34

**Q76. How much of each sweet spice shall there be?**  
**Answer:** Of each shall there be a like weight.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:34

---

### Exodus 30:35

**Q77. What shall thou make the spices into?**  
**Answer:** A perfume.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:35

**Q78. According to what art shall the perfume be made?**  
**Answer:** After the art of the apothecary.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:35

**Q79. How is the perfume described regarding its condition?**  
**Answer:** Tempered together, pure and holy.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:35

---

### Exodus 30:36

**Q80. What shall thou beat some of the perfume very small before?**  
**Answer:** Before the testimony in the tabernacle of the congregation.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:36

**Q81. Where will the Lord meet with Moses?**  
**Answer:** Where I will meet with thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:36

**Q82. How shall the perfume be regarded by you?**  
**Answer:** It shall be unto you most holy.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:36

---

### Exodus 30:37

**Q83. What shall ye not make for yourselves regarding the perfume's composition?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall not make to yourselves according to the composition thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:37

**Q84. How shall it be unto thee?**  
**Answer:** It shall be unto thee holy for the Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:37

---

### Exodus 30:38

**Q85. What will happen to whosoever shall make like unto that, to smell thereto?**  
**Answer:** Shall even be cut off from his people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 30:38
