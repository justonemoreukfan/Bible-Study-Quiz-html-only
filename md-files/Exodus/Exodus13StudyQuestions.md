# Exodus 13 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 13:1

**Q1. Who spoke unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:1

**Q2. Who was spoken to by the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:1

**Q3. What did the LORD do regarding Moses?**  
**Answer:** Spake unto him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:1

---

### Exodus 13:2

**Q1. What must be sanctified unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** All the firstborn.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:2

**Q2. Among whom does the firstborn open whatsoever opens the matrix?**  
**Answer:** Among the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:2

**Q3. What belongs to the LORD among the children of Israel besides man?**  
**Answer:** Both of man and of beast.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:2

---

### Exodus 13:3

**Q1. What did Moses say unto the people?**  
**Answer:** Remember this day, in which ye came out from Egypt, out of the house of bondage.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:3

**Q2. By what means did the LORD bring them out from Egypt?**  
**Answer:** By strength of hand.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:3

**Q3. What kind of bread shall not be eaten?**  
**Answer:** There shall no leavened bread be eaten.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:3

---

### Exodus 13:4

**Q1. In what month came they out?**  
**Answer:** The month Abib.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:4

---

### Exodus 13:5

**Q1. Into whose land shall the LORD bring thee?**  
**Answer:** The land of the Canaanites, and the Hittites, and the Amorites, and the Hivites, and the Jebusites.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:5

**Q2. What did the LORD swear unto thy fathers to give thee?**  
**Answer:** A land flowing with milk and honey.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:5

**Q3. In what month shall ye keep this service?**  
**Answer:** In this month.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:5

---

### Exodus 13:6

**Q1. How many days shall ye eat unleavened bread?**  
**Answer:** Seven days.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:6

**Q2. What shall be kept in the seventh day?**  
**Answer:** A feast to the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:6

---

### Exodus 13:7

**Q1. What shall be eaten during the seven days?**  
**Answer:** Unleavened bread.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:7

**Q2. What shall not be seen with thee?**  
**Answer:** Neither shall there be leavened bread seen with thee, neither shall there be leaven seen with thee in all thy quarters.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:7

---

### Exodus 13:8

**Q1. What shall thou show thy son in that day?**  
**Answer:** This is done because of that which the LORD did unto me when I came forth out of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:8

**Q2. Who shall thou show this unto?**  
**Answer:** Thy son.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:8

**Q3. When did the LORD do this unto me?**  
**Answer:** When I came forth out of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:8

---

### Exodus 13:9

**Q1. What shall it be for a sign unto thee?**  
**Answer:** It shall be for a sign unto thee upon thine hand, and for a memorial between thine eyes.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:9

**Q2. Whereupon shall the LORD's law be?**  
**Answer:** In thy mouth.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:9

**Q3. With what has the LORD brought thee out of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** With a strong hand.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:9

---

### Exodus 13:10

**Q1. When shall thou keep this ordinance?**  
**Answer:** In his season.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:10

**Q2. From what to what time must this ordinance be kept?**  
**Answer:** From year to year.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:10

---

### Exodus 13:11

**Q1. When the LORD shall bring thee into the land of the Canaanites, what shall He do?**  
**Answer:** Give it thee, as he sware to thee and to thy fathers.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:11

---

### Exodus 13:12

**Q1. What shall thou set apart unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** All that openeth the matrix.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:12

**Q2. What belongs to the firstling that comes of a beast which thou hast?**  
**Answer:** The males shall be the LORD's.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:12

---

### Exodus 13:13

**Q1. With what shall thou redeem every firstling of an ass?**  
**Answer:** With a lamb.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:13

**Q2. What must you do if you will not redeem the firstling of an ass?**  
**Answer:** Then thou shalt break his neck.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:13

**Q3. Who among the children of men shall thou redeem?**  
**Answer:** All the firstborn of man among thy children shalt thou redeem.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:13

---

### Exodus 13:14

**Q1. Who shall ask thee in time to come?**  
**Answer:** Thy son.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:14

**Q2. What question shall thy son ask?**  
**Answer:** What is this?  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:14

**Q3. By what did the LORD bring them out from Egypt?**  
**Answer:** By strength of hand the LORD brought us out from Egypt, from the house of bondage.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:14

---

### Exodus 13:15

**Q1. Who would hardly let Pharaoh go?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:15

**Q2. What did the LORD slay in the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** All the firstborn in the land of Egypt, from the firstborn of man, to the firstborn of beast.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:15

**Q3. What do the firstborn of children do in consequence?**  
**Answer:** All the firstborn of my children I redeem.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:15

---

### Exodus 13:16

**Q1. What shall it be for upon thine hand?**  
**Answer:** For a token.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:16

**Q2. What shall it be as between thine eyes?**  
**Answer:** As frontlets.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:16

**Q3. By what power did the LORD bring them forth out of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** By strength of hand the LORD brought us forth out of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:16

---

### Exodus 13:17

**Q1. When Pharaoh had let the people go, through what land did God not lead them?**  
**Answer:** Through the way of the land of the Philistines.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:17

**Q2. Why did God not lead them through the land of the Philistines?**  
**Answer:** For that was near.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:17

**Q3. What reason did God give regarding war?**  
**Answer:** Lest peradventure the people repent when they see war, and they return to Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:17

---

### Exodus 13:18

**Q1. About what way did God lead the people?**  
**Answer:** Through the way of the wilderness of the Red sea.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:18

**Q2. In what manner did the children of Israel go up out of the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Harnessed.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:18

---

### Exodus 13:19

**Q1. Whose bones did Moses take with him?**  
**Answer:** The bones of Joseph.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:19

**Q2. What had Joseph strictly sworn the children of Israel to do?**  
**Answer:** God will surely visit you; and ye shall carry up my bones away hence with you.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:19

---

### Exodus 13:20

**Q1. From where did they take their journey?**  
**Answer:** From Succoth.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:20

**Q2. Where did they encamp?**  
**Answer:** In Etham.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:20

**Q3. In what location was Etham?**  
**Answer:** In the edge of the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:20

---

### Exodus 13:21

**Q1. Who went before them by day?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:21

**Q2. In what form did the LORD go before them by day?**  
**Answer:** In a pillar of a cloud.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:21

**Q3. In what form did He go by night?**  
**Answer:** In a pillar of fire.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:21

---

### Exodus 13:22

**Q1. What departed not from before the people by day?**  
**Answer:** The pillar of cloud.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:22

**Q2. What departed not from before the people by night?**  
**Answer:** The pillar of fire.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:22

**Q3. Before whom did the pillars not depart?**  
**Answer:** Before the people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 13:22
