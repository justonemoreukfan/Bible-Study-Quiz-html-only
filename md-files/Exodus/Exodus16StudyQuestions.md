# Exodus 16 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 16:1

**Q1. From where did all the congregation of the children of Israel take their journey?**  
**Answer:** Elim.  
**Verse:** Exodus 16:1

**Q2. To where did the congregation of the children of Israel come?**  
**Answer:** The wilderness of Sin.  
**Verse:** Exodus 16:1

**Q3. On what day of the second month after their departing out of the land of Egypt did they come to the wilderness of Sin?**  
**Answer:** The fifteenth day.  
**Verse:** Exodus 16:1

---

### Exodus 16:2

**Q1. Who murmured against Moses and Aaron in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** The whole congregation of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 16:2

**Q2. Against whom did the whole congregation of the children of Israel murmur in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** Moses and Aaron.  
**Verse:** Exodus 16:2

**Q3. Where did the whole congregation murmur against Moses and Aaron?**  
**Answer:** In the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Exodus 16:2

---

### Exodus 16:3

**Q1. By whose hand would that the children of Israel had died in the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** The hand of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 16:3

**Q2. When the children of Israel sat by what did they eat bread to the full?**  
**Answer:** The fleshpots.  
**Verse:** Exodus 16:3

**Q3. To what place have Moses and Aaron brought the assembly forth to kill this whole assembly with hunger?**  
**Answer:** This wilderness.  
**Verse:** Exodus 16:3

---

### Exodus 16:4

**Q1. What did the LORD say unto Moses He would rain from heaven?**  
**Answer:** Bread.  
**Verse:** Exodus 16:4

**Q2. What should the people go out and gather every day?**  
**Answer:** A certain rate every day.  
**Verse:** Exodus 16:4

**Q3. Why did the LORD test the people regarding whether they would walk in His law or not?**  
**Answer:** That I may prove them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 16:4

---

### Exodus 16:5

**Q1. On what day shall the people prepare that which they bring in?**  
**Answer:** The sixth day.  
**Verse:** Exodus 16:5

**Q2. How much shall they prepare on the sixth day compared to what they gather daily?**  
**Answer:** Twice as much as they gather daily.  
**Verse:** Exodus 16:5

---

### Exodus 16:6

**Answer:** A small round thing.

**Q2. How big was the small round thing upon the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** Small.  
**Verse:** Exodus 16:14

**Verse:** Exodus 16:14
