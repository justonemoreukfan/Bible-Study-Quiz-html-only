# Exodus 24 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 24:1

**Q1. Who was told to come up unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Moses, Aaron, Nadab, and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:1

**Q2. Who else was mentioned alongside Moses in the instruction to come up?**  
**Answer:** Aaron, Nadab, and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:1

**Q3. How were they instructed to worship in relation to the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Worship ye afar off.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:1

---

### Exodus 24:2

**Q1. Who alone was permitted to come near unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:2

**Q2. Who was explicitly not permitted to come near?**  
**Answer:** The others (the elders and Aaron, Nadab, and Abihu).  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:2

**Q3. Who else was forbidden from going up with Moses?**  
**Answer:** The people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:2

---

### Exodus 24:3

**Q1. What did Moses come and tell the people?**  
**Answer:** All the words of the LORD, and all the judgments.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:3

**Q2. What did all the people answer with one voice?**  
**Answer:** All the words which the LORD hath said will we do.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:3

**Q3. What did Moses do with all the words of the LORD after telling the people?**  
**Answer:** He came and told the people, and answered.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:3

---

### Exodus 24:4

**Q1. What did Moses write down?**  
**Answer:** All the words of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:4

**Q2. What did Moses rise up early in the morning to build?**  
**Answer:** An altar under the hill.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:4

**Q3. What did Moses build according to the twelve tribes of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Twelve pillars.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:4

---

### Exodus 24:5

**Q1. Whom did Moses send to do the burnt offerings?**  
**Answer:** Young men of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:5

**Q2. What kind of offerings did the young men offer?**  
**Answer:** Burnt offerings and sacrificed peace offerings of oxen unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:5

**Q3. What specific animals were sacrificed as peace offerings?**  
**Answer:** Oxen.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:5

---

### Exodus 24:6

**Q1. What did Moses take half of?**  
**Answer:** The blood.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:6

**Q2. What did Moses put the half of the blood into?**  
**Answer:** Basons.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:6

**Q3. What did Moses do with the other half of the blood?**  
**Answer:** He sprinkled it on the altar.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:6

---

### Exodus 24:7

**Q1. What book did Moses take?**  
**Answer:** The book of the covenant.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:7

**Q2. To whom did Moses read the book of the covenant?**  
**Answer:** In the audience of the people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:7

**Q3. What did the people say after hearing the book?**  
**Answer:** All that the LORD hath said will we do, and be obedient.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:7

---

### Exodus 24:8

**Q1. What did Moses take to sprinkle on the people?**  
**Answer:** The blood.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:8

**Q2. What did Moses say when he sprinkled the blood on the people?**  
**Answer:** Behold the blood of the covenant, which the LORD hath made with you concerning all these words.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:8

**Q3. Who made the covenant concerning all these words?**  
**Answer:** The LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:8

---

### Exodus 24:9

**Q1. Who went up after the blood was sprinkled?**  
**Answer:** Moses, and Aaron, Nadab, and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:9

**Q2. How many elders of Israel went up with Moses and Aaron?**  
**Answer:** Seventy.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:9

**Q3. Who joined Nadab and Abihu in going up?**  
**Answer:** Moses, Aaron, and seventy of the elders of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:9

---

### Exodus 24:10

**Q1. Whose God did they see?**  
**Answer:** The God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:10

**Q2. What was under His feet as it were?**  
**Answer:** A paved work of a sapphire stone.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:10

**Q3. What was the sapphire stone as it were in its clearness?**  
**Answer:** As it were the body of heaven in his clearness.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:10

---

### Exodus 24:11

**Q1. Upon whom did God not lay His hand?**  
**Answer:** Upon the nobles of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:11

**Q2. What did they see after they beheld God?**  
**Answer:** They did see God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:11

**Q3. What physical action did they perform after seeing God?**  
**Answer:** Did eat and drink.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:11

---

### Exodus 24:12

**Q1. What did the LORD say unto Moses?**  
**Answer:** Come up to me into the mount, and be there.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:12

**Q2. What did the LORD promise to give Moses?**  
**Answer:** Tables of stone, and a law, and commandments.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:12

**Q3. For what purpose had the LORD written the tables?**  
**Answer:** That thou mayest teach them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:12

---

### Exodus 24:13

**Q1. Who rose up along with Moses?**  
**Answer:** His minister Joshua.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:13

**Q2. Where did Moses go up?**  
**Answer:** Into the mount of God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:13

**Q3. Who was Moses' minister who rose up with him?**  
**Answer:** Joshua.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:13

---

### Exodus 24:14

**Q1. What did Moses say to the elders?**  
**Answer:** Tarry ye here for us, until we come again unto you.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:14

**Q2. Who was left with the elders while Moses and Joshua went up?**  
**Answer:** Aaron and Hur.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:14

**Q3. What did Moses say the people should do if they had any matters?**  
**Answer:** Let him come unto them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:14

---

### Exodus 24:15

**Q1. Where did Moses go?**  
**Answer:** Into the mount.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:15

**Q2. What covered the mount?**  
**Answer:** A cloud.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:15

**Q3. What kind of cloud covered the mount?**  
**Answer:** A cloud covered the mount.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:15

---

### Exodus 24:16

**Q1. Whose glory abode upon mount Sinai?**  
**Answer:** The glory of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:16

**Q2. What covered the mount for six days?**  
**Answer:** The cloud.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:16

**Q3. When did the LORD call unto Moses out of the midst of the cloud?**  
**Answer:** The seventh day.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:16

---

### Exodus 24:17

**Q1. How did the sight of the glory of the LORD appear to the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Like devouring fire on the top of the mount.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:17

**Q2. Where was the sight of the glory of the LORD located?**  
**Answer:** On the top of the mount.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:17

**Q3. In the eyes of whom did the glory appear like devouring fire?**  
**Answer:** In the eyes of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:17

---

### Exodus 24:18

**Q1. Where did Moses go into the midst of the cloud?**  
**Answer:** Into the midst of the cloud.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:18

**Q2. Where did Moses go up after entering the cloud?**  
**Answer:** Into the mount.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:18

**Q3. How long was Moses in the mount?**  
**Answer:** Forty days and forty nights.  
**Verse:** Exodus 24:18
