# Exodus 37 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 37:1

**Q1. Who made the ark of shittim wood?**  
**Answer:** Bezaleel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:1

**Q2. How long was the ark of shittim wood?**  
**Answer:** Two cubits and a half.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:1

**Q3. What was the width and height of the ark?**  
**Answer:** A cubit and a half the breadth thereof, and a cubit and a half the height thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:1

---

### Exodus 37:2

**Q4. With what did Bezaleel overlay the ark?**  
**Answer:** Pure gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:2

**Q5. Where did he overlay the pure gold?**  
**Answer:** Within and without.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:2

**Q6. What did he make to the ark round about?**  
**Answer:** A crown of gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:2

---

### Exodus 37:3

**Q7. What did he cast for the ark?**  
**Answer:** Four rings of gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:3

**Q8. Where were the four rings of gold set?**  
**Answer:** By the four corners thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:3

**Q9. How many rings were set on the two sides of the ark?**  
**Answer:** Two rings upon the one side of it, and two rings upon the other side of it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:3

---

### Exodus 37:4

**Q10. What did he make of shittim wood?**  
**Answer:** Staves.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:4

**Q11. What was done to the staves?**  
**Answer:** Overlayed with gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:4

---

### Exodus 37:5

**Q12. Where were the staves put?**  
**Answer:** Into the rings by the sides of the ark.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:5

**Q13. What was the purpose of putting the staves into the rings?**  
**Answer:** To bear the ark.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:5

---

### Exodus 37:6

**Q14. What did he make the mercy seat of?**  
**Answer:** Pure gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:6

**Q15. What were the length and breadth of the mercy seat?**  
**Answer:** Two cubits and a half was the length thereof, and one cubit and a half the breadth thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:6

---

### Exodus 37:7

**Q15a. What did he make of gold?**  
**Answer:** Two cherubims.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:7

**Q15b. How were the cherubims made?**  
**Answer:** Beaten out of one piece.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:7

**Q15c. Where were the cherubims placed?**  
**Answer:** On the two ends of the mercy seat.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:7

---

### Exodus 37:8

**Q16. Where was one cherub placed?**  
**Answer:** On the one end on this side.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:8

**Q17. Where was the other cherub placed?**  
**Answer:** The other cherub on the other end on that side.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:8

**Q18. How were the cherubims made with the mercy seat?**  
**Answer:** Out of the mercy seat made he the cherubims on the two ends thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:8

---

### Exodus 37:9

**Q19. What did the cherubims spread out on high?**  
**Answer:** Their wings.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:9

**Q20. What did the wings of the cherubims cover?**  
**Answer:** The mercy seat.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:9

**Q21. How were the faces of the cherubims turned?**  
**Answer:** One to another; toward the mercy seat were the faces of the cherubims.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:9

---

### Exodus 37:10

**Q22. What did he make the table of?**  
**Answer:** Shittim wood.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:10

**Q23. What was the length of the table?**  
**Answer:** Two cubits.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:10

**Q24. What was the breadth and height of the table?**  
**Answer:** A cubit the breadth thereof, and a cubit and a half the height thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:10

---

### Exodus 37:11

**Q25. With what was the table overlaid?**  
**Answer:** Pure gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:11

**Q26. What was made to the table round about?**  
**Answer:** A crown of gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:11

---

### Exodus 37:12

**Q27. What was made thereunto round about?**  
**Answer:** A border of an handbreadth.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:12

**Q28. What was made for the border thereof?**  
**Answer:** A crown of gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:12

---

### Exodus 37:13

**Q29. What did he cast for the table?**  
**Answer:** Four rings of gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:13

**Q30. Where were the rings put?**  
**Answer:** In the four corners that were in the four feet thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:13

---

### Exodus 37:14

**Q31. Where were the rings over against?**  
**Answer:** Over against the border.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:14

**Q32. What was the purpose of the rings?**  
**Answer:** For the places of the staves to bear the table.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:14

---

### Exodus 37:15

**Q33. What did he make the staves of?**  
**Answer:** Shittim wood.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:15

**Q34. What was done to the staves for the table?**  
**Answer:** Overlayed them with gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:15

**Q35. What was the purpose of the staves?**  
**Answer:** To bear the table.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:15

---

### Exodus 37:16

**Q36. What did he make of pure gold?**  
**Answer:** The vessels which were upon the table.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:16

**Q37. Name the types of vessels mentioned for the table.**  
**Answer:** His dishes, and his spoons, and his bowls, and his covers.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:16

**Q38. What were these vessels used to do?**  
**Answer:** To cover withal (or pour out withal).  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:16

---

### Exodus 37:17

**Q39. What did he make the candlestick of?**  
**Answer:** Pure gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:17

**Q40. How was the candlestick made?**  
**Answer:** Of beaten work.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:17

**Q41. What parts of the candlestick were made of beaten work?**  
**Answer:** His shaft, and his branch, his bowls, his knops, and his flowers.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:17

---

### Exodus 37:18

**Q42. How many branches went out of the sides of the candlestick?**  
**Answer:** Six branches.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:18

**Q43. How were the branches divided on the sides?**  
**Answer:** Three branches of the candlestick out of the one side thereof, and three branches of the candlestick out of the other side thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:18

---

### Exodus 37:19

**Q44. What was made after the manner of almonds in one branch?**  
**Answer:** Three bowls.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:19

**Q45. What accompanied the bowls on a branch?**  
**Answer:** A knop and a flower.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:19

**Q46. How many bowls, knops, and flowers were in the next branch?**  
**Answer:** Three bowls made like almonds in another branch, a knop and a flower.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:19

---

### Exodus 37:20

**Q47. In the candlestick itself, how many bowls were made like almonds?**  
**Answer:** Four bowls.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:20

**Q48. What else was on the candlestick itself?**  
**Answer:** Their knops and their flowers.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:20

---

### Exodus 37:21

**Q49. Under what was a knop of the same?**  
**Answer:** Under two branches of the same.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:21

**Q50. Under what was the second knop?**  
**Answer:** Under two branches of the same.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:21

**Q51. Under what was the third knop?**  
**Answer:** Under two branches of the same, according to the six branches going out of it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:21

---

### Exodus 37:22

**Q52. What were the knops and the branches of?**  
**Answer:** Of the same.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:22

**Q53. How was the whole work constructed?**  
**Answer:** All of it was one beaten work of pure gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:22

---

### Exodus 37:23

**Q54. How many lamps did he make for the candlestick?**  
**Answer:** Seven his lamps.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:23

**Q55. What else did he make of pure gold for the candlestick?**  
**Answer:** His snuffers, and his snuffdishes.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:23

---

### Exodus 37:24

**Q56. Of what talent of pure gold was the candlestick made?**  
**Answer:** Of a talent of pure gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:24

**Q57. What else was made of that same talent of pure gold?**  
**Answer:** All the vessels thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:24

---

### Exodus 37:25

**Q58. What did he make the incense altar of?**  
**Answer:** Shittim wood.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:25

**Q59. What was the length and breadth of the incense altar?**  
**Answer:** The length of it was a cubit, and the breadth of it a cubit.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:25

**Q60. What was the height of the incense altar?**  
**Answer:** Two cubits.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:25

---

### Exodus 37:26

**Q61. With what was the incense altar overlaid?**  
**Answer:** Pure gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:26

**Q62. What parts of the altar were overlaid with pure gold?**  
**Answer:** Both the top of it, and the sides thereof round about, and the horns of it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:26

**Q63. What was made to it round about?**  
**Answer:** A crown of gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:26

---

### Exodus 37:27

**Q64. What did he make for the incense altar under the crown?**  
**Answer:** Two rings of gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:27

**Q65. Where were the two rings placed?**  
**Answer:** By the two corners thereof, upon the two sides of it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:27

**Q66. What was the purpose of these rings?**  
**Answer:** To be places for the staves to bear it withal.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:27

---

### Exodus 37:28

**Q67. What did he make the staves of for the incense altar?**  
**Answer:** Shittim wood.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:28

**Q68. What was done to the staves?**  
**Answer:** Overlayed them with gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:28

---

### Exodus 37:29

**Q69. What holy oil did he make?**  
**Answer:** The holy anointing oil.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:29

**Q70. What pure incense did he make?**  
**Answer:** The pure incense of sweet spices.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:29

**Q71. Who made the holy oil and incense?**  
**Answer:** The apothecary.  
**Verse:** Exodus 37:29
