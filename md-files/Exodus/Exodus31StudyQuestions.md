# Exodus 31 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 31:1

**Q1. Who did the Lord speak unto in Exodus 31:1?**  
**Answer:** Moses  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:1

**Q2. What did the Lord do unto Moses in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Spake  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:1

**Q3. Whose voice spake unto Moses according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** The Lord  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:1

---

### Exodus 31:2

**Q1. Who has the Lord called by name?**  
**Answer:** Bezaleel  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:2

**Q2. Whose son is Bezaleel?**  
**Answer:** Uri  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:2

**Q3. Whose son was Uri, the father of Bezaleel?**  
**Answer:** Hur  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:2

---

### Exodus 31:3

**Q1. With what has the Lord filled Bezaleel?**  
**Answer:** The spirit of God  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:3

**Q2. In what two things was Bezaleel filled with wisdom?**  
**Answer:** In wisdom, and in understanding  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:3

**Q3. In what besides wisdom and understanding was Bezaleel filled?**  
**Answer:** In knowledge, and in all manner of workmanship  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:3

---

### Exodus 31:4

**Q1. To devise cunning works, what kind of materials must Bezaleel work with?**  
**Answer:** Gold, and in silver, and in brass  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:4

**Q2. What is the purpose of devising these cunning works in gold, silver, and brass?**  
**Answer:** To devise cunning works  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:4

**Q3. Who is the person mentioned in this verse who devises cunning works?**  
**Answer:** Bezaleel  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:4

---

### Exodus 31:5

**Q1. In the cutting of stones, what is Bezaleel to do?**  
**Answer:** To set them  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:5

**Q2. In what materials, besides stones, is Bezaleel to work?**  
**Answer:** In timber  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:5

**Q3. What general type of work is Bezaleel to perform with timber?**  
**Answer:** To work in all manner of workmanship  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:5

---

### Exodus 31:6

**Q1. Who has the Lord given with Bezaleel?**  
**Answer:** Aholiab  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:6

**Q2. Whose son is Aholiab?**  
**Answer:** Ahisamach  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:6

**Q3. Of what tribe is Aholiab?**  
**Answer:** The tribe of Dan  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:6

---

### Exodus 31:7

**Q1. What is the first item listed that the Lord's tent is to be made of in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The tabernacle of the congregation  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:7

**Q2. What is to be placed inside the tabernacle of the congregation according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** The ark of the testimony  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:7

**Q3. What else is mentioned to be made alongside the ark of the testimony?**  
**Answer:** The mercy seat that is thereupon, and all the furniture of the tabernacle  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:7

---

### Exodus 31:8

**Q1. What table and its furniture are mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The table and his furniture  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:8

**Q2. What pure item is mentioned in relation to the candlestick?**  
**Answer:** The pure candlestick  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:8

**Q3. What is associated with the pure candlestick in this verse?**  
**Answer:** All his furniture, and the incense altar  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:8

---

### Exodus 31:9

**Q1. What altar is listed first in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The altar of burnt offering  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:9

**Q2. What is mentioned along with the altar of burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** All his furniture  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:9

**Q3. What item is mentioned at the end of this verse?**  
**Answer:** The laver and his foot  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:9

---

### Exodus 31:10

**Q1. What cloths of service are mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The cloths of service  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:10

**Q2. Whose holy garments are mentioned?**  
**Answer:** Aaron the priest  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:10

**Q3. Whose garments, besides Aaron's, are mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The garments of his sons  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:10

---

### Exodus 31:11

**Q1. What oil is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The anointing oil  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:11

**Q2. What sweet incense is mentioned?**  
**Answer:** Sweet incense  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:11

**Q3. According to all that the Lord has commanded, how shall they make these things?**  
**Answer:** Even as I have commanded thee  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:11

### Exodus 31:12

**Q1. Who spake unto Moses in Exodus 31:12?**  
**Answer:** The Lord  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:12

**Q2. What did the Lord do unto Moses in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Spake  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:12

**Q3. Whose word is recorded as speaking unto Moses in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The Lord's  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:12

---

### Exodus 31:13

**Q1. To whom is Moses commanded to speak?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:13

**Q2. What must the children of Israel truly keep?**  
**Answer:** My sabbaths  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:13

**Q3. What is the sabbath said to be between the Lord and the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** A sign between me and you throughout your generations  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:13

---

### Exodus 31:14

**Q1. What shall the children of Israel do regarding the sabbath?**  
**Answer:** Keep the sabbath  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:14

**Q2. How is the sabbath described in relation to the people?**  
**Answer:** Holy unto you  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:14

**Q3. What shall happen to the person who defiles the sabbath?**  
**Answer:** He shall surely be put to death  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:14

---

### Exodus 31:15

**Q1. How many days may work be done?**  
**Answer:** Six days  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:15

**Q2. What is the seventh day called in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The sabbath of rest  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:15

**Q3. What must happen to anyone who does work on the sabbath day?**  
**Answer:** He shall surely be put to death  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:15

---

### Exodus 31:16

**Q1. Who shall keep the sabbath?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:16

**Q2. How is the keeping of the sabbath described in terms of time?**  
**Answer:** Throughout their generations  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:16

**Q3. What kind of covenant is the sabbath said to be?**  
**Answer:** A perpetual covenant  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:16

---

### Exodus 31:17

**Q1. What is the sabbath between the children of Israel and the Lord forever?**  
**Answer:** A sign between me and the children of Israel for ever  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:17

**Q2. In how many days did the Lord make heaven and earth?**  
**Answer:** In six days  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:17

**Q3. What did the Lord do on the seventh day?**  
**Answer:** He rested, and was refreshed  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:17

---

### Exodus 31:18

**Q1. When the Lord had made an end of speaking with Moses, where did He give him these items?**  
**Answer:** Upon mount Sinai  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:18

**Q2. What did the Lord give unto Moses upon mount Sinai?**  
**Answer:** Two tables of testimony  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:18

**Q3. What were the tables of testimony written with?**  
**Answer:** The finger of God  
**Verse:** Exodus 31:18
