# Exodus 21 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 21:1

**Q1. What is the subject of the judgments that the Lord commanded Moses to set before the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The judgments.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:1

**Q2. Who commanded the judgments that Moses was to set before the people?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:1

**Q3. Before whom was Moses to set the judgments?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:1

---

### Exodus 21:2

**Q1. If a Hebrew servant is bought, how many years shall he serve?**  
**Answer:** Six years.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:2

**Q2. In the seventh year, what shall the Hebrew servant go out for?**  
**Answer:** Free.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:2

**Q3. What payment or cost is required for the servant when he goes out free in the seventh year?**  
**Answer:** Nothing (he shall go out free for nothing).  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:2

---

### Exodus 21:3

**Q1. If a servant came in by himself, how shall he go out?**  
**Answer:** By himself.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:3

**Q2. If the servant was married, who shall go out with him?**  
**Answer:** His wife.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:3

---

### Exodus 21:4

**Q1. Who gives the servant a master's wife and children?**  
**Answer:** His master.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:4

**Q2. Whose shall the wife and her children be if the master gives them to him?**  
**Answer:** Her master's.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:4

**Q3. How shall the servant go out if his master gave him a wife and children?**  
**Answer:** By himself.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:4

---

### Exodus 21:5

**Q1. What might the servant plainly say regarding his master, wife, and children?**  
**Answer:** I love my master, my wife, and my children.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:5

**Q2. What does the servant declare he will not do because he loves his master, wife, and children?**  
**Answer:** I will not go out free.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:5

**Q3. Who are explicitly named by the servant as objects of his love alongside his master?**  
**Answer:** His wife and his children.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:5

---

### Exodus 21:6

**Q1. To whom shall his master bring the servant?**  
**Answer:** The judges.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:6

**Q2. Where shall the master also bring the servant to the door, or unto the door post?**  
**Answer:** To the door, or unto the door post.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:6

**Q3. How long shall the servant serve his master after his ear is bored through with an awl?**  
**Answer:** For ever.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:6

### Exodus 21:7

**Q1. What is the status of a man's daughter who is sold to be a maidservant?**  
**Answer:** She shall not go out as the menservants do.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:7

**Q2. How does a maidservant's departure differ from that of menservants?**  
**Answer:** She shall not go out as the menservants do.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:7

**Q3. What kind of servant is the daughter sold to be?**  
**Answer:** A maidservant.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:7

---

### Exodus 21:8

**Q1. What is the feeling of the master if the maidservant displeases him, who has betrothed her to himself?**  
**Answer:** If she please not her master, who hath betrothed her to himself.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:8

**Q2. What shall the master permit to be done if the maidservant displeases him?**  
**Answer:** Then shall he let her be redeemed.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:8

**Q3. To what kind of people does the master have no power to sell her, seeing he has dealt deceitfully with her?**  
**Answer:** Unto a strange nation.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:8

---

### Exodus 21:9

**Q1. Unto whom shall the master betroth the maidservant if he has betrothed her unto his son?**  
**Answer:** Unto his son.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:9

**Q2. According to what manner shall the master deal with her after betrothing her to his son?**  
**Answer:** After the manner of daughters.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:9

**Q3. Whose daughter is she treated as under this provision?**  
**Answer:** The manner of daughters.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:9

### Exodus 21:10

**Q1. If the master takes him another wife, what must he not diminish for her?**  
**Answer:** Her food, her raiment, and her duty of marriage.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:10

**Q2. What are the three specific things that shall not be diminished?**  
**Answer:** Her food, her raiment, and her duty of marriage.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:10

**Q3. Under what condition is the master forbidden to diminish her food, raiment, and duty of marriage?**  
**Answer:** If he take him another wife.  
**Verse:** Exodus 21:10

**Answer:** Without money.
