# Exodus 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 5:1

**Q1. Who went and told Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** Moses and Aaron.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:1

**Q2. Whose message were Moses and Aaron delivering to Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** The Lord God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:1

**Q3. What did the Lord God of Israel command Pharaoh to let His people do?**  
**Answer:** Go, that they may hold a feast unto me in the wilderness.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:1

---

### Exodus 5:2

**Q4. How did Pharaoh respond regarding the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Who is the Lord, that I should obey his voice to let Israel go?  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:2

**Q5. Did Pharaoh know the Lord?**  
**Answer:** I know not the Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:2

**Q6. What was Pharaoh's decision concerning letting Israel go?**  
**Answer:** Neither will I let Israel go.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:2

---

### Exodus 5:3

**Q7. What did they say the God of the Hebrews had met with?**  
**Answer:** Them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:3

**Q8. How far did they ask to go into the desert?**  
**Answer:** Three days' journey.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:3

**Q9. What might happen to them if they did not go to sacrifice?**  
**Answer:** Fall upon us with pestilence, or with the sword.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:3

---

### Exodus 5:4

**Q10. What did the king of Egypt call Moses and Aaron?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore do ye, Moses and Aaron, let the people from their works?  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:4

**Q11. What did Pharaoh tell Moses and Aaron to do regarding their works?**  
**Answer:** Get you unto your burdens.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:4

---

### Exodus 5:5

**Q12. What did Pharaoh observe about the people of the land?**  
**Answer:** The people of the land now are many.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:5

**Q13. What did Pharaoh say the people were making the people do?**  
**Answer:** Ye make them rest from their burdens.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:5

---

### Exodus 5:6

**Q14. Who did Pharaoh command the same day?**  
**Answer:** The taskmasters of the people, and their officers.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:6

---

### Exodus 5:7

**Q15. What were the taskmasters and officers no longer to give the people to make brick?**  
**Answer:** Straw.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:7

**Q16. What could the people now go and do for themselves?**  
**Answer:** Gather straw for themselves.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:7

---

### Exodus 5:8

**Q17. What number of bricks were the people required to make?**  
**Answer:** The tale of the bricks, which they did make heretofore.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:8

**Q18. Should the daily quota of bricks be diminished?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall not diminish ought thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:8

**Q19. Why were the people idle?**  
**Answer:** Therefore they cry, saying, Let us go and sacrifice to our God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:8

---

### Exodus 5:9

**Q20. What should be laid upon the men?**  
**Answer:** More work.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:9

---

### Exodus 5:10

**Q21. Who went out and spoke to the people?**  
**Answer:** The taskmasters of the people, and their officers.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:10

**Q22. What did the taskmasters and officers tell the people?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith Pharaoh, I will not give you straw.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:10

---

### Exodus 5:11

**Q22. Where were the people told to go?**  
**Answer:** Get you straw where ye can find it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:11

**Q23. Would their work be reduced?**  
**Answer:** Yet not ought of your work shall be diminished.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:11

---

### Exodus 5:12

**Q24. Where were the people scattered abroad throughout all the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Throughout all the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:12

**Q25. What did the people gather instead of straw?**  
**Answer:** Stubble.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:12

---

### Exodus 5:13

**Q26. What did the taskmasters demand?**  
**Answer:** Fulfil your works, your daily tasks.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:13

**Q27. When were the tasks to be fulfilled?**  
**Answer:** As when there was straw.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:13

---

### Exodus 5:14

**Q28. Who were beaten by Pharaoh's taskmasters?**  
**Answer:** The officers of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:14

**Q29. What had the officers been asked by the taskmasters?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore have ye not fulfilled your task in making brick both yesterday and today, as heretofore?  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:14

---

### Exodus 5:15

**Q30. Who came and cried unto Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** The officers of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:15

**Q31. What did the officers ask Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** Wherefore dealest thou thus with thy servants?  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:15

---

### Exodus 5:16

**Q32. What was no straw given to?**  
**Answer:** Thy servants.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:16

**Q33. What were they told to do with bricks?**  
**Answer:** Make brick.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:16

**Q34. Who did the officers say was at fault for the beating?**  
**Answer:** Thy servants are beaten; but the fault is in thine own people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:16

---

### Exodus 5:17

**Q35. What did Pharaoh call the people?**  
**Answer:** Ye are idle, ye are idle.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:17

**Q36. What reason did Pharaoh give for the people wanting to go sacrifice?**  
**Answer:** Therefore ye say, Let us go and do sacrifice to the Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:17

---

### Exodus 5:18

**Q37. What did Pharaoh tell them to do regarding work?**  
**Answer:** Go therefore now, and work.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:18

**Q38. Would straw be given to them?**  
**Answer:** There shall no straw be given you.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:18

**Q39. What must they deliver?**  
**Answer:** The tale of bricks.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:18

---

### Exodus 5:19

**Q40. What condition were the officers of the children of Israel in?**  
**Answer:** In evil case.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:19

**Q41. What phrase was said to them regarding the daily brick count?**  
**Answer:** Ye shall not minish ought from your bricks of your daily task.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:19

---

### Exodus 5:20

**Q42. Whom did the officers meet as they came forth from Pharaoh?**  
**Answer:** Moses and Aaron.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:20

**Q43. What position were Moses and Aaron standing in when they met them?**  
**Answer:** Who stood in the way.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:20

---

### Exodus 5:21

**Q44. What did the officers say the Lord should look upon?**  
**Answer:** The Lord look upon you, and judge.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:21

**Q45. Why did the officers say Moses and Aaron had made their savour to be abhorred?**  
**Answer:** Because ye have made our savour to be abhorred in the eyes of Pharaoh, and in the eyes of his servants.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:21

---

### Exodus 5:22

**Q46. To whom did Moses return?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:22

**Q47. What did Moses ask the Lord about?**  
**Answer:** Lord, wherefore hast thou so evil entreated this people?  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:22

**Q48. What final question did Moses ask the Lord regarding sending him?**  
**Answer:** Why is it that thou hast sent me?  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:22

---

### Exodus 5:23

**Q49. Since when had Moses come to Pharaoh to speak in the Lord's name?**  
**Answer:** For since I came to Pharaoh to speak in thy name.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:23

**Q50. How had Pharaoh treated the people after Moses spoke in the Lord's name?**  
**Answer:** He hath done evil to this people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:23

**Q51. What did Moses say the Lord had not done for His people?**  
**Answer:** Neither hast thou delivered thy people at all.  
**Verse:** Exodus 5:23
