# Exodus 15 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 15:1

**Q1. Who sang this song unto the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Moses and the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:1

**Q2. What did Moses and the children of Israel speak, saying?**  
**Answer:** I will sing unto the Lord, for he hath triumphed gloriously.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:1

**Q3. Whom has the Lord thrown into the sea?**  
**Answer:** The horse and his rider.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:1

---

### Exodus 15:2

**Q4. What is the Lord described as?**  
**Answer:** My strength and song.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:2

**Q5. What has the Lord become to the singer?**  
**Answer:** Salvation.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:2

**Q6. Whose God will the singer prepare an habitation for?**  
**Answer:** My father's God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:2

---

### Exodus 15:3

**Q7. What is the Lord?**  
**Answer:** A man of war.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:3

**Q8. What is the name of the Lord?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:3

---

### Exodus 15:4

**Q9. What hath the Lord cast into the sea?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh's chariots and his host.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:4

**Q10. Whose chosen captains are drowned in the Red sea?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh's chosen captains.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:4

---

### Exodus 15:5

**Q11. What covered the depths?**  
**Answer:** The dark waters.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:5

**Q12. Into what did the depths sink?**  
**Answer:** As a stone.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:5

---

### Exodus 15:6

**Q13. What is glorious in power?**  
**Answer:** Thy right hand, O Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:6

**Q14. What hath dashed in pieces the enemy?**  
**Answer:** Thy right hand, O Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:6

---

### Exodus 15:7

**Q15. What hast thou overthrown them in?**  
**Answer:** The greatness of thine excellency.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:7

**Q16. What went forth against them?**  
**Answer:** Thy wrath.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:7

---

### Exodus 15:8

**Q17. What gathered together with the blast of thy nostrils?**  
**Answer:** The waters.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:8

**Q18. What stood upright as an heap?**  
**Answer:** The floods.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:8

---

### Exodus 15:9

**Q19. Who said, I will pursue, I will overtake?**  
**Answer:** The enemy.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:9

**Q20. What would the enemy divide?**  
**Answer:** The spoil.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:9

---

### Exodus 15:10

**Q21. What didst thou blow with?**  
**Answer:** Thy wind.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:10

**Q22. What covered them?**  
**Answer:** The sea.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:10

---

### Exodus 15:11

**Q23. Who is like unto thee among the gods, O Lord?**  
**Answer:** Who is like thee, glorious in holiness.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:11

**Q24. What is fearful in?**  
**Answer:** Praises.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:11

**Q25. What does the Lord do?**  
**Answer:** Wonders.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:11

---

### Exodus 15:12

**Q26. What didst thou stretch out?**  
**Answer:** Thy right hand.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:12

**Q27. What swallowed them?**  
**Answer:** The earth.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:12

---

### Exodus 15:13

**Q28. Whom hast thou led forth in thy mercy?**  
**Answer:** The people which thou hast redeemed.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:13

**Q29. Whither hast thou guided them in thy strength?**  
**Answer:** Unto thy holy habitation.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:13

---

### Exodus 15:14

**Q30. Who shall hear and be afraid?**  
**Answer:** The people.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:14

**Q31. Who shall take hold of the inhabitants of Palestina?**  
**Answer:** Sorrow.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:14

---

### Exodus 15:15

**Q32. Who shall be amazed?**  
**Answer:** The dukes of Edom.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:15

**Q33. Who shall tremble?**  
**Answer:** The mighty men of Moab.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:15

---

### Exodus 15:16

**Q34. What shall fall upon them?**  
**Answer:** Fear and dread.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:16

**Q35. By the greatness of thine arm they shall be as still as what?**  
**Answer:** A stone.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:16

---

### Exodus 15:17

**Q36. Where shalt thou bring them in and plant them?**  
**Answer:** In the mountain of thine inheritance.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:17

**Q37. Who has made it for thee to dwell in?**  
**Answer:** O Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:17

---

### Exodus 15:18

**Q38. For how long shall the Lord reign?**  
**Answer:** For ever and ever.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:18

---

### Exodus 15:19

**Q39. Whose horses went in with his chariots and with his horsemen into the sea?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh's horses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:19

**Q40. Who brought again the waters of the sea upon them?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:19

---

### Exodus 15:20

**Q41. Who took a timbrel in her hand?**  
**Answer:** Miriam the prophetess, the sister of Aaron.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:20

**Q42. Who went out after her with timbrels and with dances?**  
**Answer:** All the women.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:20

---

### Exodus 15:21

**Q43. Who answered them?**  
**Answer:** Miriam.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:21

**Q44. What did Miriam sing to them?**  
**Answer:** Sing ye to the Lord, for he hath triumphed gloriously.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:21

---

### Exodus 15:22

**Q45. Who brought Israel from the Red sea?**  
**Answer:** Moses.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:22

**Q46. Into what wilderness did they go out?**  
**Answer:** The wilderness of Shur.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:22

**Q47. For how many days did they go in the wilderness and find no water?**  
**Answer:** Three days.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:22

---

### Exodus 15:23

**Q48. To what place did they come where they could not drink the waters?**  
**Answer:** Marah.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:23

**Q49. Why could they not drink of the waters of Marah?**  
**Answer:** For they were bitter.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:23

**Q50. After what was the name of the place called?**  
**Answer:** Marah.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:23

---

### Exodus 15:24

**Q51. What did the people do against Moses?**  
**Answer:** Murmured.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:24

**Q52. What question did the people ask Moses?**  
**Answer:** What shall we drink?  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:24

---

### Exodus 15:25

**Q53. To whom did Moses cry?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:25

**Q54. What did the Lord shew him?**  
**Answer:** A tree.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:25

**Q55. What became sweet when he cast it into the waters?**  
**Answer:** The waters.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:25

---

### Exodus 15:26

**Q56. If thou wilt diligently hearken to what, will the Lord put none of these diseases upon thee?**  
**Answer:** The voice of the Lord thy God.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:26

**Q57. What are the diseases the Lord said He would not put upon them?**  
**Answer:** The diseases which He brought upon the Egyptians.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:26

**Q58. What is the Lord proclaimed to be?**  
**Answer:** That healeth thee.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:26

---

### Exodus 15:27

**Q59. To what place did they come where there were twelve wells of water?**  
**Answer:** Elim.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:27

**Q60. How many palm trees were at Elim?**  
**Answer:** threescore and ten.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:27

**Q61. Where did they encamp by the waters?**  
**Answer:** There by the waters.  
**Verse:** Exodus 15:27
