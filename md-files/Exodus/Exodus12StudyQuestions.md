# Exodus 12 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 12:1

**Q1. In what land did the Lord speak unto Moses and Aaron?**  
**Answer:** In the land of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Exodus 12:1

**Q2. Who did the Lord speak unto in the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** Moses and Aaron.  
**Verse:** Exodus 12:1

**Q3. Who spoke unto Moses and Aaron in the land of Egypt?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 12:1

---

### Exodus 12:2

**Q1. What is this month to be unto them?**  
**Answer:** The beginning of months.  
**Verse:** Exodus 12:2

**Q2. What place shall this month be to them regarding the year?**  
**Answer:** The first month of the year to them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 12:2

**Q3. When is this month to be the first month of the year?**  
**Answer:** It is to be the first month of the year to them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 12:2

---

### Exodus 12:3

**Q1. To whom was Moses told to speak?**  
**Answer:** All the congregation of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 12:3

**Q2. On what day of the month shall every man take him a lamb?**  
**Answer:** In the tenth day of this month.  
**Verse:** Exodus 12:3

**Q3. For whose house should every man take a lamb?**  
**Answer:** For his house, a lamb for an house.  
**Verse:** Exodus 12:3
