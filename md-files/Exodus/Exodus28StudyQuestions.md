# Exodus 28 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Exodus 28:1

**Q1. Who is commanded to be brought unto Moses from among the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Aaron thy brother, and his sons with him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:1

**Q2. From whom are Aaron and his sons taken to minister unto God in the priest's office?**  
**Answer:** From among the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:1

**Q3. Name Aaron's sons who are to be brought with him to minister in the priest's office.**  
**Answer:** Nadab, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:1

---

### Exodus 28:2

**Q1. What is Moses commanded to make for Aaron his brother?**  
**Answer:** Holy garments.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:2

**Q2. For what purpose are the holy garments to be made for Aaron?**  
**Answer:** For glory and for beauty.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:2

---

### Exodus 28:3

**Q1. To whom shall Moses speak regarding the making of Aaron's garments?**  
**Answer:** All that are wise hearted.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:3

**Q2. With what have the wise hearted whom Moses speaks to been filled?**  
**Answer:** The spirit of wisdom.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:3

**Q3. What shall the wise hearted make in order to consecrate Aaron to minister in the priest's office?**  
**Answer:** Aaron's garments.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:3

---

### Exodus 28:4

**Q1. What are the names of the garments that the people shall make?**  
**Answer:** A breastplate, and an ephod, and a robe, and a broidered coat, a mitre, and a girdle.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:4

**Q2. For whom shall they make these holy garments?**  
**Answer:** Aaron thy brother, and his sons.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:4

**Q3. In what office shall Aaron and his sons minister when wearing these garments?**  
**Answer:** The priest's office.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:4

---

### Exodus 28:5

**Q1. What materials shall be used to make the garments?**  
**Answer:** Gold, and blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine linen.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:5

---

### Exodus 28:6

**Q1. What specific garment shall they make of gold, of blue, and of purple, of scarlet, and fine twined linen?**  
**Answer:** The ephod.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:6

**Q2. By what type of work shall the ephod be made?**  
**Answer:** Cunning work.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:6

---

### Exodus 28:7

**Q1. What shall the ephod have joined to the two edges thereof?**  
**Answer:** Two shoulder pieces.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:7

**Q2. Where shall the two shoulder pieces be joined?**  
**Answer:** At the two edges thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:7

---

### Exodus 28:8

**Q1. What is described as the curious girdle of the ephod?**  
**Answer:** The curious girdle of the ephod, which is upon it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:8

**Q2. Of what materials shall the curious girdle be made?**  
**Answer:** Of the same, according to the work thereof; even of gold, of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine twined linen.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:8

**Q3. How is the construction of the curious girdle related to the ephod?**  
**Answer:** It is of the same, according to the work thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:8

---

### Exodus 28:9

**Q1. What stones shall Moses take?**  
**Answer:** Two onyx stones.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:9

**Q2. What shall be engraved on the two onyx stones?**  
**Answer:** The names of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:9

---

### Exodus 28:10

**Q1. How many names shall be on one stone?**  
**Answer:** Six of their names.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:10

**Q2. How many names shall be on the other stone?**  
**Answer:** The other six names.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:10

**Q3. According to what order shall the names be engraved?**  
**Answer:** According to their birth.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:10

---

### Exodus 28:11

**Q1. With what kind of work shall the two stones be engraved with the names of the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** With the work of an engraver in stone, like the engravings of a signet.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:11

**Q2. In what must the engraved stones be set?**  
**Answer:** In ouches of gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:11

---

### Exodus 28:12

**Q1. Upon what part of the ephod shall the two stones be put?**  
**Answer:** Upon the shoulders of the ephod.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:12

**Q2. Whom do the two stones represent?**  
**Answer:** Stones for memorial of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:12

**Q3. Where shall Aaron bear the names of the children of Israel before the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Upon his two shoulders for a memorial.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:12

---

### Exodus 28:13

**Q1. What shall be made of gold?**  
**Answer:** Ouches of gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:13

---

### Exodus 28:14

**Q1. How many chains shall be made of pure gold?**  
**Answer:** Two chains.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:14

**Q2. What kind of work shall the two chains of pure gold be?**  
**Answer:** At the ends of wreathen work.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:14

**Q3. Where shall the wreathen chains of gold be fastened?**  
**Answer:** To the ouches.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:14

---

### Exodus 28:15

**Q1. What shall be made after the work of the ephod?**  
**Answer:** The breastplate of judgment.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:15

**Q2. By what kind of work shall the breastplate of judgment be made?**  
**Answer:** Cunning work.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:15

**Q3. What materials shall be used to make the breastplate of judgment, similar to the ephod?**  
**Answer:** Gold, of blue, and of purple, and of scarlet, and of fine twined linen.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:15

---

### Exodus 28:16

**Q1. What shape shall the breastplate be when it is folded?**  
**Answer:** Foursquare.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:16

**Q2. What shall be the dimensions of the folded breastplate?**  
**Answer:** A span shall be the length thereof, and a span shall be the breadth thereof.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:16

---

### Exodus 28:17

**Q1. What shall be set in the breastplate?**  
**Answer:** Settings of stones, even four rows of stones.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:17

**Q2. What is the first stone in the first row?**  
**Answer:** A sardius.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:17

**Q3. What stones follow the sardius in the first row?**  
**Answer:** A topaz, and a carbuncle.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:17

---

### Exodus 28:18

**Q1. What is the first stone in the second row?**  
**Answer:** An emerald.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:18

**Q2. What stones follow the emerald in the second row?**  
**Answer:** A sapphire, and a diamond.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:18

---

### Exodus 28:19

**Q1. What is the first stone in the third row?**  
**Answer:** A ligure.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:19

**Q2. What stones follow the ligure in the third row?**  
**Answer:** An agate, and an amethyst.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:19

---

### Exodus 28:20

**Q1. What is the first stone in the fourth row?**  
**Answer:** A beryl.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:20

**Q2. What stones follow the beryl in the fourth row?**  
**Answer:** An onyx, and a jasper.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:20

**Q3. How shall the stones be set in their places?**  
**Answer:** They shall be set in gold in their inclosings.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:20

---

### Exodus 28:21

**Q1. Whose names shall the stones be according to?**  
**Answer:** According to the names of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:21

**Q2. How many names shall be on the stones?**  
**Answer:** Twelve.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:21

**Q3. How shall each individual stone be engraved like a signet?**  
**Answer:** Every one with his name shall be according to the twelve tribes.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:21

---

### Exodus 28:22

**Q1. What shall be made upon the breastplate at the ends?**  
**Answer:** Chains at the ends of wreathen work.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:22

**Q2. What is the material of the chains made for the breastplate?**  
**Answer:** Pure gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:22

---

### Exodus 28:23

**Q1. What shall be made upon the breastplate to hold the chains?**  
**Answer:** Two rings of gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:23

**Q2. Where on the breastplate shall the two rings of gold be put?**  
**Answer:** On the two ends of the breastplate.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:23

---

### Exodus 28:24

**Q1. Where shall the two wreathen chains of gold be put?**  
**Answer:** In the two rings which are on the ends of the breastplate.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:24

---

### Exodus 28:25

**Q1. Where shall the other two ends of the two wreathen chains be fastened?**  
**Answer:** In the two ouches.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:25

**Q2. On what part of the ephod are the two ouches located?**  
**Answer:** On the shoulder pieces of the ephod before it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:25

---

### Exodus 28:26

**Q1. What else shall be made of gold for the breastplate?**  
**Answer:** Two rings of gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:26

**Q2. Where shall the two rings of gold be put on the breastplate?**  
**Answer:** Upon the two sides of it.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:26

**Q3. On which edge of the breastplate, in relation to the ephod inward, shall these two rings be placed?**  
**Answer:** In the border of the breastplate, which is in the side of the ephod inward.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:26

---

### Exodus 28:27

**Q1. How many other golden rings shall be made?**  
**Answer:** Two other rings of gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:27

**Q2. Where shall the two other golden rings be put?**  
**Answer:** On the two sides of the ephod underneath.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:27

**Q3. Toward what part of the shoulder pieces shall the rings be placed?**  
**Answer:** Toward the forepart thereof, over against the other coupling thereof, above the curious girdle of the ephod.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:27

---

### Exodus 28:28

**Q1. With what shall the breastplate be bound unto the rings of the ephod?**  
**Answer:** A lace of blue.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:28

**Q2. Above what girdle shall the breastplate be bound to the ephod?**  
**Answer:** Above the curious girdle of the ephod.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:28

**Q3. What must not be loosed from the ephod?**  
**Answer:** That the breastplate be not loosed from the ephod.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:28

---

### Exodus 28:29

**Q1. When shall Aaron bear the names of the children of Israel in the breastplate of judgment?**  
**Answer:** When he goeth in unto the holy place.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:29

**Q2. Upon what part of his body shall Aaron bear these names before the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Upon his heart.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:29

**Q3. For what purpose shall Aaron bear the names continually before the Lord?**  
**Answer:** For a memorial.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:29

---

### Exodus 28:30

**Q1. What shall be put in the breastplate of judgment?**  
**Answer:** The Urim and the Thummim.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:30

**Q2. Upon whose heart shall the Urim and the Thummim be when he goeth in before the Lord?**  
**Answer:** Upon Aaron's heart.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:30

**Q3. What shall Aaron bear continually before the Lord upon his heart?**  
**Answer:** The judgment of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:30

---

### Exodus 28:31

**Q1. What shall be made of all blue?**  
**Answer:** The robe of the ephod.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:31

---

### Exodus 28:32

**Q1. What shall be in the top of the robe, in the midst thereof?**  
**Answer:** An hole.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:32

**Q2. What shall be around the hole of the robe to prevent it from renting?**  
**Answer:** A binding of woven work round about the hole of it, as it were the hole of an habergeon.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:32

---

### Exodus 28:33

**Q1. What shall be made beneath upon the hem of the robe?**  
**Answer:** Pomegranates.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:33

**Q2. What materials and colors shall be used to make the pomegranates?**  
**Answer:** Of blue, and of purple, and of scarlet.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:33

**Q3. What shall be between the pomegranates round about the hem?**  
**Answer:** Bells of gold.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:33

---

### Exodus 28:34

**Q1. What alternating pattern shall be upon the hem of the robe round about?**  
**Answer:** A golden bell and a pomegranate, a golden bell and a pomegranate.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:34

---

### Exodus 28:35

**Q1. Upon whom shall the robe be when he ministereth?**  
**Answer:** Upon Aaron.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:35

**Q2. When shall his sound be heard?**  
**Answer:** When he goeth in unto the holy place before the Lord, and when he cometh out.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:35

**Q3. What will happen to Aaron if he wears the robe and has the bells?**  
**Answer:** That he die not.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:35

---

### Exodus 28:36

**Q1. What shall be made of pure gold?**  
**Answer:** A plate.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:36

**Q2. What shall be engraved upon the plate like the engravings of a signet?**  
**Answer:** HOLINESS TO THE LORD.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:36

---

### Exodus 28:37

**Q1. With what shall the golden plate be fastened?**  
**Answer:** A lace of blue.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:37

**Q2. Where upon the mitre shall the golden plate be placed?**  
**Answer:** Upon the mitre; upon the forefront of the mitre it shall be.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:37

---

### Exodus 28:38

**Q1. On whose forehead shall the golden plate be?**  
**Answer:** Upon Aaron's forehead.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:38

**Q2. What shall Aaron bear upon his forehead?**  
**Answer:** The iniquity of the holy things, which the children of Israel shall hallow.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:38

**Q3. For what purpose shall the plate be always upon his forehead?**  
**Answer:** That they may be accepted before the Lord.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:38

---

### Exodus 28:39

**Q1. What kind of coat shall be embroidered?**  
**Answer:** The coat of fine linen.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:39

**Q2. What shall be made of fine linen?**  
**Answer:** The mitre.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:39

**Q3. What kind of work shall the girdle be?**  
**Answer:** Needlework.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:39

---

### Exodus 28:40

**Q1. What shall Moses make for Aaron's sons?**  
**Answer:** Coats, and girdles, shall he make for them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:40

**Q2. What shall Moses make for Aaron's sons for glory and for beauty?**  
**Answer:** Bonnets.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:40

---

### Exodus 28:41

**Q1. Upon whom shall Moses put these garments?**  
**Answer:** Aaron thy brother, and his sons with him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:41

**Q2. What action shall be done to Aaron and his sons after putting on the garments?**  
**Answer:** Anoint them, and consecrate them, and sanctify them.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:41

**Q3. For what office are Aaron and his sons being anointed and consecrated?**  
**Answer:** That they may minister unto me in the priest's office.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:41

---

### Exodus 28:42

**Q1. What shall be made to cover their nakedness?**  
**Answer:** Linen breeches.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:42

**Q2. From where unto where shall the linen breeches reach?**  
**Answer:** From the loins even unto the thighs.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:42

---

### Exodus 28:43

**Q1. Who must wear the linen breeches when they go into the tabernacle of the congregation?**  
**Answer:** Aaron and his sons.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:43

**Q2. When must they wear the linen breeches when coming near to the altar?**  
**Answer:** When they come near unto the altar to minister in the holy place.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:43

**Q3. What consequence will befall them if they do not wear the linen breeches?**  
**Answer:** That they bear not iniquity, and die.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:43

---

### Exodus 28:44

**Q1. How long shall Aaron and his seed observe the rule of the linen garments?**  
**Answer:** A statute for ever unto him and his seed after him.  
**Verse:** Exodus 28:44
