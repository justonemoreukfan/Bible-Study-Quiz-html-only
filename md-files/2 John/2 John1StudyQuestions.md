# 2 John 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 John 1:1

**Q1. Who writes the epistle?**  
**Answer:** The elder.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:1

**Q2. To whom is the epistle addressed?**  
**Answer:** Unto the elect lady and her children.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:1

**Q3. In what manner does the elder love the elect lady and her children?**  
**Answer:** In the truth.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:1

---

### 2 John 1:2

**Q1. For whose sake do they love the truth?**  
**Answer:** For the truth's sake.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:2

**Q2. Where does the truth dwell?**  
**Answer:** In us.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:2

**Q3. How long shall the truth be with us?**  
**Answer:** For ever.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:2

---

### 2 John 1:3

**Q1. What shall be with us?**  
**Answer:** Grace, mercy, and peace.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:3

**Q2. From whom do grace, mercy, and peace come?**  
**Answer:** From God the Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:3

**Q3. In what two qualities shall grace, mercy, and peace be with us?**  
**Answer:** In truth and love.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:3

---

### 2 John 1:4

**Q1. What did the elder rejoice greatly to find?**  
**Answer:** That he found of his children walking in truth.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:4

**Q2. Whose commandment did they walk in?**  
**Answer:** As we have received a commandment from the Father.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:4

**Q3. How were the children walking?**  
**Answer:** In truth.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:4

---

### 2 John 1:5

**Q1. What does the elder beseech the lady to do?**  
**Answer:** That we love one another.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:5

**Q2. Is this a new commandment that the elder writes?**  
**Answer:** Not as though I wrote a new commandment unto thee.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:5

**Q3. How long has this commandment been had?**  
**Answer:** Which we had from the beginning.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:5

---

### 2 John 1:6

**Q1. What is love defined as in this verse?**  
**Answer:** That we walk after his commandments.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:6

**Q2. What is the commandment that ye have heard from the beginning?**  
**Answer:** That ye should walk in it.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:6

**Q3. Where did they hear this commandment?**  
**Answer:** From the beginning.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:6

---

### 2 John 1:7

**Q1. Who are entered into the world?**  
**Answer:** Many deceivers.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:7

**Q2. What do these deceivers not confess?**  
**Answer:** That Jesus Christ is come in the flesh.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:7

**Q3. What is a person called who does not confess that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh?**  
**Answer:** A deceiver and an antichrist.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:7

---

### 2 John 1:8

**Q1. What should they look to themselves for?**  
**Answer:** That we lose not those things which we have wrought.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:8

**Q2. What do they want to receive?**  
**Answer:** A full reward.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:8

**Q3. What should be looked to in order to receive a full reward?**  
**Answer:** That we lose not those things which we have wrought, but that we receive a full reward.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:8

---

### 2 John 1:9

**Q1. Who does not have God?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever transgresseth, and abideth not in the doctrine of Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:9

**Q2. Who has both the Father and the Son?**  
**Answer:** He that abideth in the doctrine of Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:9

**Q3. What must a person abide in to have both the Father and the Son?**  
**Answer:** The doctrine of Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:9

---

### 2 John 1:10

**Q1. What should be done if someone comes unto you and brings not this doctrine?**  
**Answer:** Receive him not into your house.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:10

**Q2. What should not be said to such a person?**  
**Answer:** Neither bid him God speed.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:10

**Q3. What is lacking in the doctrine of the person not to be received?**  
**Answer:** He brings not this doctrine.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:10

---

### 2 John 1:11

**Q1. What does he that biddeth such a man God speed become?**  
**Answer:** Partaker of his evil deeds.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:11

**Q2. Whose evil deeds does the person become a partaker of?**  
**Answer:** His evil deeds (the evil deeds of him who brings not the doctrine).  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:11

**Q3. What action causes someone to be a partaker of evil deeds?**  
**Answer:** He that biddeth him God speed.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:11

---

### 2 John 1:12

**Q1. What did the elder have to write unto them?**  
**Answer:** Many things.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:12

**Q2. With what instruments did the elder choose not to write?**  
**Answer:** Paper and ink.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:12

**Q3. Why did the elder trust to come unto them and speak face to face?**  
**Answer:** That our joy may be full.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:12

---

### 2 John 1:13

**Q1. Who greets the elect lady?**  
**Answer:** The children of thy elect sister.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:13

**Q2. Whose sister is the elect sister?**  
**Answer:** Thy elect sister.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:13

**Q3. How does the verse conclude?**  
**Answer:** Amen.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:13
