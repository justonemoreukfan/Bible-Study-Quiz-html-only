# 2 John 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 2 John 1:1

**Q1. Who is writing the letter?**  
**Answer:** The elder.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:1

**Q2. To whom is the letter written?**  
**Answer:** Unto the elect lady and her children.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:1

**Q3. In what does the writer love the elect lady and her children?**  
**Answer:** In the truth.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:1

---

### 2 John 1:2

**Q1. For whose sake is the truth mentioned?**  
**Answer:** For the truth's sake.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:2

**Q2. Where does the truth dwell?**  
**Answer:** In us.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:2

**Q3. How long shall the truth be with us?**  
**Answer:** For ever.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:2

---

### 2 John 1:3

**Q1. What three things are wished to be with the recipient?**  
**Answer:** Grace, mercy, and peace.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:3

**Q2. From whom do grace, mercy, and peace come?**  
**Answer:** From God the Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:3

**Q3. In what two things do these blessings come?**  
**Answer:** In truth and love.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:3

---

### 2 John 1:4

**Q1. Why did the writer rejoice greatly?**  
**Answer:** That he found of thy children walking in truth.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:4

**Q2. What did the writer find the children doing?**  
**Answer:** Walking in truth.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:4

**Q3. From whom was the commandment received?**  
**Answer:** From the Father.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:4

---

### 2 John 1:5

**Q1. What does the writer beseech the lady?**  
**Answer:** That we love one another.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:5

**Q2. Was the commandment to love one another a new commandment?**  
**Answer:** No, not a new commandment.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:5

**Q3. When did they have this commandment?**  
**Answer:** From the beginning.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:5

---

### 2 John 1:6

**Q1. What is defined as love in this verse?**  
**Answer:** That we walk after his commandments.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:6

**Q2. What is the commandment?**  
**Answer:** That ye should walk in it.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:6

**Q3. When did they hear the commandment?**  
**Answer:** From the beginning.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:6

---

### 2 John 1:7

**Q1. Who has entered into the world?**  
**Answer:** Many deceivers.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:7

**Q2. What do the deceivers not confess?**  
**Answer:** That Jesus Christ is come in the flesh.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:7

**Q3. What is a person who does not confess this called?**  
**Answer:** A deceiver and an antichrist.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:7

---

### 2 John 1:8

**Q1. What are the readers told to look to?**  
**Answer:** Look to yourselves.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:8

**Q2. What should the readers be careful not to lose?**  
**Answer:** Those things which we have wrought.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:8

**Q3. What should the readers receive?**  
**Answer:** A full reward.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:8

---

### 2 John 1:9

**Q1. Who hath not God?**  
**Answer:** Whosoever transgresseth, and abideth not in the doctrine of Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:9

**Q2. What happens to he that abideth in the doctrine of Christ?**  
**Answer:** He hath both the Father and the Son.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:9

**Q3. In what doctrine must one abide to have the Father and the Son?**  
**Answer:** The doctrine of Christ.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:9

---

### 2 John 1:10

**Q1. If someone comes and brings not this doctrine, what should you not do?**  
**Answer:** Receive him not into your house.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:10

**Q2. What other action is forbidden if someone brings not this doctrine?**  
**Answer:** Neither bid him God speed.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:10

**Q3. What is the condition for not receiving the person?**  
**Answer:** If they bring not this doctrine.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:10

---

### 2 John 1:11

**Q1. What happens to he that biddeth a deceiver God speed?**  
**Answer:** He is partaker of his evil deeds.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:11

**Q2. What is the person a partaker of?**  
**Answer:** His evil deeds.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:11

**Q3. What action leads to being a partaker of evil deeds?**  
**Answer:** Bidding him God speed.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:11

---

### 2 John 1:12

**Q1. Why did the writer decide not to write with paper and ink?**  
**Answer:** Because he trusts to come unto you and speak face to face.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:12

**Q2. How does the writer trust to communicate?**  
**Answer:** Speak face to face.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:12

**Q3. What would be the result of speaking face to face?**  
**Answer:** That our joy may be full.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:12

---

### 2 John 1:13

**Q1. Who greets the lady?**  
**Answer:** The children of thy elect sister.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:13

**Q2. To whom are the greetings sent?**  
**Answer:** To the elect lady.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:13

**Q3. Whose children are sending the greeting?**  
**Answer:** The children of thy elect sister.  
**Verse:** 2 John 1:13
