# Joel 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Joel 1:1
**Q1. What is declared in Joel 1:1?**  
**Answer:** The word of the LORD that came to Joel the son of Pethuel.  
**Verse:** Joel 1:1

**Q2. According to Joel 1:1, what truth is stated?**  
**Answer:** The word of the LORD that came to Joel the son of Pethuel.  
**Verse:** Joel 1:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Joel 1:1?**  
**Answer:** The word of the LORD that came to Joel the son of Pethuel.  
**Verse:** Joel 1:1

### Joel 1:2
**Q4. According to Joel 1:2, what is stated first?**
