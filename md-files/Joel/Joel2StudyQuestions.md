### Joel 2:1
**Q1. What is stated in the opening of Joel 2:1?**  
**Answer:** Blow ye the trumpet in Zion, and sound an alarm in my holy mountain.  
**Verse:** Joel 2:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Joel 2:1?**  
**Answer:** let all the inhabitants of the land tremble.  
**Verse:** Joel 2:1

**Q3. What further truth is recorded in Joel 2:1?**  
**Answer:** for the day of the LORD cometh, for it is nigh at hand.  
**Verse:** Joel 2:1
