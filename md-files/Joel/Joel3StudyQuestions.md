# Joel 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Joel 3:1
**Q1. According to Joel 3:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** in those days.  
**Verse:** Joel 3:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Joel 3:1?**  
**Answer:** and in that time.  
**Verse:** Joel 3:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Joel 3:1?**  
**Answer:** when I shall bring again the captivity of Judah and Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Joel 3:1
