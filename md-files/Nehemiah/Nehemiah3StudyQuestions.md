# Nehemiah 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Nehemiah 3:1

**Q1. Who rose up with his brethren the priests and builded the sheep gate?**  
**Answer:** Eliashib the high priest.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:1

**Q2. What did they sanctify and set up the doors of?**  
**Answer:** The sheep gate.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:1

**Q3. Unto which tower did they sanctify the wall?**  
**Answer:** Unto the tower of Meah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:1

---

### Nehemiah 3:2

**Q4. Who builded next unto Eliashib?**  
**Answer:** The men of Jericho.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:2

**Q5. Who builded next to them?**  
**Answer:** Zaccur the son of Imri.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:2

---

### Nehemiah 3:3

**Q6. Who did the sons of Hassenaah build?**  
**Answer:** The fish gate.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:3

**Q7. What did they lay the beams thereof of?**  
**Answer:** The fish gate.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:3

**Q8. What did they set up the doors thereof, and the locks thereof, and the bars thereof?**  
**Answer:** The fish gate.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:3

---

### Nehemiah 3:4

**Q9. Who repaired next unto them?**  
**Answer:** Meremoth the son of Urijah, the son of Koz.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:4

**Q10. Who repaired next unto Meremoth?**  
**Answer:** Meshullam the son of Berechiah, the son of Meshezabeel.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:4

**Q11. Who repaired next unto Meshullam?**  
**Answer:** Zadok the son of Baana.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:4

---

### Nehemiah 3:5

**Q12. Who repaired next unto them?**  
**Answer:** The Tekoites.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:5

**Q13. Who put not their necks to the work of their Lord?**  
**Answer:** Their nobles.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:5

---

### Nehemiah 3:6

**Q14. Who repaired the old gate?**  
**Answer:** Jehoiada the son of Paseah, and Meshullam the son of Besodeiah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:6

**Q15. What did they lay the beams thereof of?**  
**Answer:** The old gate.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:6

**Q16. What did they set up the doors thereof, and the locks thereof, and the bars thereof?**  
**Answer:** The old gate.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:6

---

### Nehemiah 3:7

**Q17. Who repaired next unto them?**  
**Answer:** Melatiah the Gibeonite, and Jadon the Meronothite.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:7

**Q18. Who were the men of Gibeon and of Mizpah unto?**  
**Answer:** The throne of the governor on this side the river.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:7

---

### Nehemiah 3:8

**Q19. Who repaired next unto them?**  
**Answer:** Uzziel the son of Harhaiah, of the goldsmiths.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:8

**Q20. Who repaired next unto Uzziel?**  
**Answer:** Hananiah the son of one of the apothecaries.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:8

**Q21. Unto what wall did they fortify Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Unto the broad wall.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:8

---

### Nehemiah 3:9

**Q22. Who repaired next unto them?**  
**Answer:** Rephaiah the son of Hur, the ruler of the half part of Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:9

---

### Nehemiah 3:10

**Q23. Who repaired next unto them?**  
**Answer:** Jedaiah the son of Harumaph.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:10

**Q24. Over against whose house did Jedaiah repair?**  
**Answer:** Even over against his house.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:10

**Q25. Who repaired next unto him?**  
**Answer:** Hattush the son of Hashabniah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:10

---

### Nehemiah 3:11

**Q26. Who repaired the other piece?**  
**Answer:** Malchijah the son of Harim, and Hashub the son of Pahathmoab.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:11

**Q27. What tower did they repair?**  
**Answer:** The tower of the furnaces.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:11

---

### Nehemiah 3:12

**Q28. Who repaired next unto him?**  
**Answer:** Shallum the son of Halohesh.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:12

**Q29. Who was Shallum the ruler of?**  
**Answer:** The half part of Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:12

**Q30. Who helped Shallum?**  
**Answer:** He and his daughters.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:12

---

### Nehemiah 3:13

**Q31. Who repaired the valley gate?**  
**Answer:** Hanun, and the inhabitants of Zanoah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:13

**Q32. What did they build and set up the doors thereof, the locks thereof, and the bars thereof?**  
**Answer:** The valley gate.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:13

**Q33. How much of the wall did they repair unto the dung gate?**  
**Answer:** A thousand cubits.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:13

---

**Answer:** The dung gate.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:14

---

### Nehemiah 3:15

**Q38. Who repaired the gate of the fountain?**  
**Answer:** Shallun the son of Colhozeh.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:15

**Q39. What was Shallun the ruler of?**  
**Answer:** Part of Mizpah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:15

**Q40. What did Shallun build, and cover it, and set up the doors thereof, the locks thereof, and the bars thereof?**  
**Answer:** The gate of the fountain.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:15

---

### Nehemiah 3:16

**Q41. Who repaired Nehemiah the son of Azbuk, the ruler of the half part of Bethzur, unto?**  
**Answer:** The place over against the sepulchres of David.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:16

**Q42. Unto what pool did Nehemiah repair?**  
**Answer:** Unto the pool that was made.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:16

**Q43. Unto the house of whom did Nehemiah repair?**  
**Answer:** Unto the house of the mighty.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:16

---

**Q44. After him, who repaired?**

**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:17

**Answer:** Rehum the son of Bani.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:17

**Answer:** Bavvai the son of Henadad.

**Answer:** The other half part of Keilah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:18

**Answer:** The ruler of Mizpah.

**Answer:** Between the going up of the corner unto the sheep gate.

**Answer:** The goldsmiths and the merchants.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 3:32

**Answer:** The sheep gate.
