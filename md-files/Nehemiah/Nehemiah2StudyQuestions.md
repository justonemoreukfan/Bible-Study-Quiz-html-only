# Nehemiah 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Nehemiah 2:1

**Q1. In what month and in what year of Artaxerxes the king was Nehemiah before him?**  
**Answer:** In the month Nisan, in the twentieth year of Artaxerxes the king.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:1

**Q2. What was set before the king when Nehemiah took up the wine?**  
**Answer:** Wine.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:1

**Q3. What had Nehemiah not been before time in the king's presence?**  
**Answer:** Sad.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:1

---

### Nehemiah 2:2

**Q1. What did the king say to Nehemiah when he saw his countenance was sad?**  
**Answer:** Why is thy countenance sad, seeing thou art not sick?  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:2

**Q2. Of what did the king say Nehemiah's sadness was a sorrow?**  
**Answer:** Sorrow of heart.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:2

**Q3. How did Nehemiah react after the king spoke to him?**  
**Answer:** Then I was sore afraid.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:2

---

### Nehemiah 2:3

**Q1. What did Nehemiah wish for the king?**  
**Answer:** Let the king live for ever.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:3

**Q2. Why should Nehemiah's countenance not be sad when the city of his fathers' sepulchres lieth waste?**  
**Answer:** Because the city, the place of my fathers' sepulchres, lieth waste.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:3

**Q3. What had been consumed with fire concerning the city?**  
**Answer:** The gates thereof.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:3

---

### Nehemiah 2:4

**Q1. What did the king say to Nehemiah?**  
**Answer:** For what dost thou make request?  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:4

**Q2. To whom did Nehemiah pray before answering the king?**  
**Answer:** The God of heaven.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:4

**Q3. Whose God was the God of heaven to whom Nehemiah prayed?**  
**Answer:** The God of heaven.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:4

---

### Nehemiah 2:5

**Q1. What did Nehemiah say to the king if it pleased the king?**  
**Answer:** If it please the king, and if thy servant have found favour in thy sight.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:5

**Q2. To what land did Nehemiah ask the king to send him?**  
**Answer:** Unto Judah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:5

**Q3. What specific city in Judah did Nehemiah want to build?**  
**Answer:** Unto the city of my fathers' sepulchres, that I may build it.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:5

---

### Nehemiah 2:6

**Q1. Who was sitting by the king when he asked Nehemiah about his journey?**  
**Answer:** The queen also sitting by him.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:6

**Q2. What did the king ask Nehemiah regarding his journey?**  
**Answer:** For how long shall thy journey be? and when wilt thou return?  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:6

**Q3. What did it please the king to do after Nehemiah set him a time?**  
**Answer:** So it pleased the king to send me; and I set him a time.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:6

---

### Nehemiah 2:7

**Q1. What did Nehemiah ask the king to give him for the governors beyond the river?**  
**Answer:** Letters to the governors beyond the river.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:7

**Q2. What purpose were the letters to the governors supposed to serve for Nehemiah?**  
**Answer:** That they may convey me over till I come into Judah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:7

**Q3. What kind of letters did Nehemiah specifically request besides the ones to the governors?**  
**Answer:** Letters.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:7

---

### Nehemiah 2:8

**Q1. To whom was the letter directed concerning the king's forest timber?**  
**Answer:** To Asaph the keeper of the king's forest.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:8

**Q2. What materials was Asaph to give timber for to make beams?**  
**Answer:** Timber to make beams for the gates of the palace which appertained to the house.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:8

**Q3. According to the hand of what entity upon Nehemiah did the king grant these requests?**  
**Answer:** According to the good hand of my God upon me.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:8

---

### Nehemiah 2:9

**Q1. To whom did Nehemiah come when he came to the governors beyond the river?**  
**Answer:** The governors beyond the river.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:9

**Q2. What did the king send with Nehemiah?**  
**Answer:** The king had sent captains of the army and horsemen with me.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:9

**Q3. What did Nehemiah give unto the governors?**  
**Answer:** The king's letters.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:9

---

### Nehemiah 2:10

**Q1. Who heard when Sanballat the Horonite and Tobiah the servant heard of Nehemiah's arrival?**  
**Answer:** Sanballat the Horonite, and Tobiah the servant, the Ammonite.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:10

**Q2. How did Sanballat and Tobiah feel when they heard that a man was come to seek the welfare of the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** It grieved them exceedingly.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:10

**Q3. What was the purpose of the man who had come to the children of Israel?**  
**Answer:** To seek the welfare of the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:10

---

### Nehemiah 2:11

**Q1. To which city did Nehemiah come?**  
**Answer:** To Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:11

**Q2. How long was Nehemiah there in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** And was there three days.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:11

**Q3. In what city did Nehemiah arrive and stay for three days?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:11

---

### Nehemiah 2:12

**Q1. In the night, who arose with Nehemiah?**  
**Answer:** Some few men with me.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:12

**Q2. What did Nehemiah not tell anyone?**  
**Answer:** Neither told I any man what my God had put in my heart to do at Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:12

**Q3. What was there besides Nehemiah when he arose?**  
**Answer:** Neither was there any beast with me, save the beast that I rode upon.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:12

---

### Nehemiah 2:13

**Q1. By what gate did Nehemiah go out by night?**  
**Answer:** By the gate of the valley.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:13

**Q2. Toward which dragon well did Nehemiah view the walls of Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Even before the dragon well.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:13

**Q3. What condition were the walls of Jerusalem in when Nehemiah viewed them?**  
**Answer:** And viewed the walls of Jerusalem, which were broken down, and the gates thereof were consumed with fire.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:13

---

### Nehemiah 2:14

**Q1. To which gate did Nehemiah go on after the valley gate?**  
**Answer:** To the gate of the fountain.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:14

**Q2. To which pool did Nehemiah go after the gate of the fountain?**  
**Answer:** And to the king's pool.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:14

**Q3. Why was there no place for the beast that Nehemiah was on to pass?**  
**Answer:** But there was no place for the beast that was under me to pass.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:14

---

### Nehemiah 2:15

**Q1. By what did Nehemiah go up in the night?**  
**Answer:** By the brook.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:15

**Q2. What did Nehemiah view while going up by the brook?**  
**Answer:** And viewed the wall.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:15

**Q3. Through what gate did Nehemiah turn back and enter?**  
**Answer:** And turned back, and entered by the gate of the valley, and so returned.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:15

---

### Nehemiah 2:16

**Q1. Who did not know where Nehemiah had gone or what he did?**  
**Answer:** And the rulers knew not whither I went, or what I did.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:16

**Q2. To whom had Nehemiah not yet told anything?**  
**Answer:** Neither had I as yet told it to the Jews, nor to the priests, nor to the nobles, nor to the rulers, nor to the rest that did the work.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:16

**Q3. Who were listed among those to whom Nehemiah had not yet told anything?**  
**Answer:** The Jews, nor to the priests, nor to the nobles, nor to the rulers, nor to the rest that did the work.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:16

---

### Nehemiah 2:17

**Q1. What did Nehemiah say unto them regarding the distress Jerusalem was in?**  
**Answer:** Ye see the distress that we are in, how Jerusalem lieth waste, and the gates thereof are burned with fire.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:17

**Q2. What action did Nehemiah invite the people to take with him?**  
**Answer:** Come, and let us build up the wall of Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:17

**Q3. What did Nehemiah say they should no longer be?**  
**Answer:** That we be no more a reproach.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:17

---

### Nehemiah 2:18

**Q1. Of what did Nehemiah tell them concerning the hand of his God?**  
**Answer:** Then I told them of the hand of my God which was good upon me.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:18

**Q2. What did Nehemiah tell them about the king's words?**  
**Answer:** As also the king's words that he had spoken unto me.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:18

**Q3. What did the people say after hearing Nehemiah's words?**  
**Answer:** Let us rise up and build.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:18

---

### Nehemiah 2:19

**Q1. Who heard when Sanballat, Tobiah, and Geshem heard it?**  
**Answer:** Sanballat the Horonite, and Tobiah the servant, the Ammonite, and Geshem the Arabian.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:19

**Q2. What did Sanballat, Tobiah, and Geshem do to Nehemiah and the others?**  
**Answer:** They laughed us to scorn, and despised us.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:19

**Q3. What question did Sanballat, Tobiah, and Geshem ask Nehemiah?**  
**Answer:** What is this thing that ye do? will ye rebel against the king?  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:19

---

### Nehemiah 2:20

**Q1. What did Nehemiah answer Sanballat, Tobiah, and Geshem?**  
**Answer:** The God of heaven, he will prosper us.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:20

**Q2. Who did Nehemiah say he and his servants would do?**  
**Answer:** Therefore we his servants will arise and build.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:20

**Q3. What did Nehemiah say the mockers had neither in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** But ye have no portion, nor right, nor memorial, in Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 2:20
