# Nehemiah 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Nehemiah 5:1

**Q1. What did there arise among the people and their wives?**  
**Answer:** A great cry.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:1

**Q2. Who did the great cry arise against?**  
**Answer:** Their brethren the Jews.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:1

**Q3. What was the relation of the people who cried out to those they cried against?**  
**Answer:** Their brethren.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:1

---

### Nehemiah 5:2

**Q1. Who said that they, their sons, and their daughters were many?**  
**Answer:** Some also.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:2

**Q2. What did they take up to buy, so that they might eat and live?**  
**Answer:** Corn.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:2

**Q3. What groups of people within their families were mentioned as being many?**  
**Answer:** Their sons and their daughters.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:2

---

### Nehemiah 5:3

**Q1. What did some also say they had mortgaged?**  
**Answer:** Their lands, vineyards, and houses.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:3

**Q2. Because of what did they mortgage their lands, vineyards, and houses?**  
**Answer:** Because of the dearth.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:3

**Q3. What were they trying to buy because of the dearth?**  
**Answer:** Corn.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:3

---

### Nehemiah 5:4

**Q1. Who said that they had borrowed money for the king's tribute?**  
**Answer:** There were also that said.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:4

**Q2. Upon what did they borrow money?**  
**Answer:** Their lands and vineyards.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:4

**Q3. What specific things were mortgaged for the king's tribute?**  
**Answer:** Their lands and vineyards.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:4

---

### Nehemiah 5:5

**Q1. Whose flesh was as the flesh of their brethren?**  
**Answer:** The flesh of their brethren.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:5

**Q2. Who were brought into bondage?**  
**Answer:** Their sons and their daughters.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:5

**Q3. What was the status of some of their daughters that were already brought unto bondage?**  
**Answer:** Some of their daughters are brought unto bondage already.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:5

---

### Nehemiah 5:6

**Q1. How did Nehemiah feel when he heard their cry and these words?**  
**Answer:** Very angry.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:6

**Q2. What did Nehemiah hear that caused him to become very angry?**  
**Answer:** Their cry and these words.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:6

**Q3. When did Nehemiah become very angry?**  
**Answer:** When he heard their cry and these words.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:6

---

### Nehemiah 5:7

**Q1. With whom did Nehemiah consult in his heart?**  
**Answer:** Himself (consulted with myself).  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:7

**Q2. Whom did Nehemiah rebuke?**  
**Answer:** The nobles, and the rulers.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:7

---

### Nehemiah 5:7 (continued)

**Q3. What did Nehemiah say the nobles and rulers were exacting?**  
**Answer:** Usury.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:7

---

### Nehemiah 5:8

**Q1. Who did Nehemiah say they had redeemed according to his ability?**  
**Answer:** Their brethren the Jews, which were sold unto the heathen.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:8

**Q2. Will the nobles and rulers even sell their brethren?**  
**Answer:** Yes (And will ye even sell your brethren?).  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:8

**Q3. How did the people responded to Nehemiah's question in this verse?**  
**Answer:** They held their peace, and found nothing to answer.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:8

---

### Nehemiah 5:9

**Q1. What did Nehemiah say was not good?**  
**Answer:** That which ye do.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:9

**Q2. In whose fear ought the nobles and rulers to walk?**  
**Answer:** In the fear of our God.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:9

**Q3. To avoid what reproach should they walk in the fear of our God?**  
**Answer:** Because of the reproach of the heathen our enemies.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:9

---

### Nehemiah 5:10

**Q1. Who else besides Nehemiah exacted of them money and corn?**  
**Answer:** I likewise, and my brethren, and my servants.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:10

**Q2. What did Nehemiah pray they would leave off?**  
**Answer:** This usury.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:10

**Q3. What things did Nehemiah and his brethren exact from the people?**  
**Answer:** Money and corn.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:10

---

### Nehemiah 5:11

**Q1. What did Nehemiah tell them to restore even that day?**  
**Answer:** Their lands, their vineyards, their oliveyards, and their houses.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:11

**Q2. What hundredth part of things were they told to leave off exacting?**  
**Answer:** The hundredth part of the money, and of the corn, the wine, and the oil.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:11

**Q3. What specific agricultural items were mentioned in relation to the hundredth part?**  
**Answer:** Corn, the wine, and the oil.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:11

---

### Nehemiah 5:12

**Q1. What did the people say they would do after Nehemiah spoke?**  
**Answer:** We will restore them, and will require nothing of them.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:12

---

### Nehemiah 5:12 (continued)

**Q2. Whose words did the people say they would do?**  
**Answer:** So will we do as thou sayest.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:12

**Q3. Whom did Nehemiah call, and what did he take of them?**  
**Answer:** The priests, and took an oath of them.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:12

---

### Nehemiah 5:13

**Q1. What did Nehemiah shake out when he spoke about shaking every man from his house?**  
**Answer:** His lap.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:13

**Q2. How did the congregation respond with their mouths?**  
**Answer:** Said, Amen, and praised the LORD.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:13

**Q3. What did the people do according to this promise?**  
**Answer:** Did according to this promise.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:13

---

### Nehemiah 5:14

**Q1. From what year to what year was Nehemiah appointed to be their governor?**  
**Answer:** From the twentieth year even to the two and thirtieth year of Artaxerxes the king.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:14

**Q2. How many years was Nehemiah appointed to be their governor in the land of Judah?**  
**Answer:** Twelve years.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:14

**Q3. What did Nehemiah and his brethren not eat?**  
**Answer:** The bread of the governor.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:14

---

### Nehemiah 5:15

**Q1. Who were before Nehemiah that were chargeable to the people?**  
**Answer:** The former governors that had been before him.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:15

**Q2. What did the former governors take of the people besides bread and wine?**  
**Answer:** Forty shekels of silver.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:15

**Q3. Even the servants of the former governors did what unto the people?**  
**Answer:** Bare rule over them.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:15

---

### Nehemiah 5:16

**Q1. In what work did Nehemiah continue?**  
**Answer:** In the work of this wall.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:16

**Q2. What did Nehemiah not buy?**  
**Answer:** Any land.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:16

**Q3. Where were all Nehemiah's servants gathered unto the work?**  
**Answer:** Thither.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:16

---

### Nehemiah 5:17

**Q1. How many of the Jews and rulers sat at Nehemiah's table?**  
**Answer:** An hundred and fifty men.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:17

**Q2. Who besides the Jews and rulers came unto Nehemiah from among the heathen?**  
**Answer:** Those that are about us.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:17

---

### Nehemiah 5:17 (continued)

**Q3. Whose table did the hundred and fifty men sit at?**  
**Answer:** Nehemiah's table.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:17

---

### Nehemiah 5:18

**Q1. What was prepared for Nehemiah daily?**  
**Answer:** One ox and six choice sheep.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:18

**Q2. What was prepared for Nehemiah once in ten days?**  
**Answer:** Store of all sorts of wine.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:18

**Q3. Why did Nehemiah not demand the bread of the governor?**  
**Answer:** Because the bondage was heavy upon this people.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:18

---

### Nehemiah 5:19

**Q1. What did Nehemiah ask God to think upon for him?**  
**Answer:** This people (Think upon me, my God, for good).  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:19

**Q2. According to what did Nehemiah ask God to think upon him?**  
**Answer:** According to all that I have done for this people.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:19

**Q3. To whom did Nehemiah address his prayer for remembrance?**  
**Answer:** My God.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 5:19
