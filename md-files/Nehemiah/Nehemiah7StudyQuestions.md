# Nehemiah 7 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Nehemiah 7:1

**Q1. What happened after the wall was built and the doors were set up?**  
**Answer:** The porters and the singers and the Levites were appointed.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 7:1

**Q2. Who was given charge over Jerusalem along with Hanani?**  
**Answer:** Hananiah the ruler of the palace.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 7:1

**Q3. What kind of man was Hananiah above many?**  
**Answer:** A faithful man, and feared God above many.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 7:1

---

### Nehemiah 7:2

**Q4. Whom did Nehemiah give charge over Jerusalem with his brother Hanani?**  
**Answer:** Hananiah the ruler of the palace.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 7:2

**Q5. How is Hananiah described in comparison to many others?**  
**Answer:** A faithful man, and feared God above many.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 7:2

---

### Nehemiah 7:3

**Q6. When did Nehemiah say the gates of Jerusalem should be opened?**  
**Answer:** Until the sun be hot.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 7:3

**Q7. What did the porters need to do while the doors were standing?**  
**Answer:** Shut them, and bar them.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 7:3

**Q8. Who was appointed to set watches of the inhabitants of Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The inhabitants of Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 7:3

---

### Nehemiah 7:4

**Q9. What was the city like regarding its size and people?**  
**Answer:** The city was large and great, but the people were few therein.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 7:4

**Q10. What condition were the houses in?**  
**Answer:** The houses were not builded.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 7:4

---

### Nehemiah 7:5

**Q11. What did God put into Nehemiah's heart?**  
**Answer:** To gather together the nobles, and the rulers, and the people.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 7:5

**Q12. What was the purpose of gathering the nobles, rulers, and people?**  
**Answer:** That they might be reckoned by genealogy.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 7:5

**Q13. What did Nehemiah find?**  
**Answer:** A register of the genealogy of them which came up at the first.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 7:5

---

### Nehemiah 7:6

**Q14. Who were the children of the province that went up out of the captivity?**  
**Answer:** Those that had been carried away, whom Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon had carried away.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 7:6

**Q15. Where did the children of the province return?**  
**Answer:** Unto Jerusalem and Judah, every one unto his city.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 7:6

---
