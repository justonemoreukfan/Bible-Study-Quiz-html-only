# Nehemiah 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Nehemiah 9:1

**Q1. In what month were the children of Israel assembled?**  
**Answer:** The twenty and fourth day of this month.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:1

**Q2. With what actions was the assembly held?**  
**Answer:** With fasting, and with sackclothes, and earth upon them.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:1

**Q3. Who assembled together at this time?**  
**Answer:** The children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:1

---

### Nehemiah 9:2

**Q1. From whom did the seed of Israel separate themselves?**  
**Answer:** All strangers.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:2

**Q2. What did they stand and confess?**  
**Answer:** Their sins, and the iniquities of their fathers.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:2

**Q3. Who stood and made this confession?**  
**Answer:** The seed of Israel.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:2

---

### Nehemiah 9:3

**Q1. For how much of the day did they stand in their place?**  
**Answer:** One fourth part of the day.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:3

**Q2. What book did they read in during another fourth part of the day?**  
**Answer:** The book of the law of the LORD their God.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:3

**Q3. What did they do besides reading the book of the law?**  
**Answer:** Confess, and worship the LORD their God.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:3

---

### Nehemiah 9:4

**Q1. Who stood up upon the stairs, of the Levites?**  
**Answer:** Jeshua, and Bani, Kadmiel, Shebaniah, Bunni, Sherebiah, Bani, and Chenani.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:4

**Q2. Unto whom did the Levites cry with a loud voice?**  
**Answer:** Unto the LORD their God.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:4

**Q3. What did the Levites do upon the stairs?**  
**Answer:** Cried with a loud voice unto the LORD their God.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:4

---

### Nehemiah 9:5

**Q1. Name the Levites who spoke to the people to stand up and bless the LORD.**  
**Answer:** Jeshua, and Kadmiel, Bani, Hashabniah, Sherebiah, Hodijah, Shebaniah, and Pethahiah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:5

**Q2. Whose name did they tell the people to bless for ever and ever?**  
**Answer:** The name of the LORD their God.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:5

**Q3. What is blessed above all blessing and praise?**  
**Answer:** Thy glorious name.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:5

---

### Nehemiah 9:6

**Q1. Who, besides the LORD, was made by Him?**  
**Answer:** The heaven of heavens, with all their host, the earth, and all things that are therein, the seas, and all that is therein.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:6

**Q2. What does the LORD preserve?**  
**Answer:** Them all.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:6

**Q3. Who worships the LORD?**  
**Answer:** The host of heaven.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:6

---

### Nehemiah 9:7

**Q1. Who is the God that did choose Abram?**  
**Answer:** The LORD God.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:7

**Q2. From what place did God bring Abram forth?**  
**Answer:** Ur of the Chaldees.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:7

**Q3. What new name did God give to Abram?**  
**Answer:** Abraham.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:7

---

### Nehemiah 9:8

**Q1. What did God find in Abraham?**  
**Answer:** His heart faithful before thee.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:8

**Q2. What covenant did God make with Abraham?**  
**Answer:** To give the land of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, and the Perizzites, and the Jebusites, and the Girgashites, to give it, I say, to his seed.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:8

**Q3. Why did God perform His words?**  
**Answer:** For thou art righteous.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:8

---

### Nehemiah 9:9

**Q1. What did God see in Egypt concerning their fathers?**  
**Answer:** The affliction of our fathers.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:9

**Q2. Where did God hear the cry of their fathers?**  
**Answer:** By the Red sea.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:9

**Q3. Who heard their cry?**  
**Answer:** Thou.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:9

---

### Nehemiah 9:10

**Q1. Against whom did God show signs and wonders?**  
**Answer:** Pharaoh, and on all his servants, and on all the people of his land.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:10

**Q2. What did God know about the Egyptians?**  
**Answer:** That they dealt proudly against them.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:10

**Q3. What did God get for Himself as it is this day?**  
**Answer:** A name.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:10

---

### Nehemiah 9:11

**Q1. What did God divide before them?**  
**Answer:** The sea.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:11

**Q2. How did they pass through the midst of the sea?**  
**Answer:** On the dry land.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:11

**Q3. Where did God cast those who persecuted them?**  
**Answer:** Into the deeps, as a stone into the mighty waters.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:11

---

### Nehemiah 9:12

**Q1. By what did God lead them in the day?**  
**Answer:** In the day by a cloudy pillar.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:12

**Q2. By what did God lead them in the night?**  
**Answer:** By a pillar of fire.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:12

**Q3. What did the pillar of fire give them in the night?**  
**Answer:** Light in the way wherein they should go.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:12

---

### Nehemiah 9:13

**Q1. Upon what mountain did God come down?**  
**Answer:** Mount Sinai.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:13

**Q2. From where did God speak with them?**  
**Answer:** From heaven.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:13

**Q3. What did God give them, besides right judgments and true laws?**  
**Answer:** Good statutes and commandments.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:13

---

### Nehemiah 9:14

**Q1. What holy institution did God make known unto them?**  
**Answer:** Thy holy sabbath.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:14

**Q2. What did God command them by the hand of Moses?**  
**Answer:** Precepts, statutes, and laws.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:14

**Q3. By whose hand did God command them precepts, statutes, and laws?**  
**Answer:** Moses thy servant.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:14

---

### Nehemiah 9:15

**Q1. What did God give them from heaven when they were hungry?**  
**Answer:** Bread.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:15

**Q2. What did God bring forth for them out of the rock when they were thirsty?**  
**Answer:** Water.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:15

**Q3. Whose hand was lifted up to swear that God would give them the land?**  
**Answer:** Thine hand.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:15

---

### Nehemiah 9:16

**Q1. How did our fathers act toward God's law?**  
**Answer:** Dealt proudly, and hardened their necks.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:16

**Q2. To what did our fathers not hearken?**  
**Answer:** Thy commandments.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:16

**Q3. What did our fathers do regarding God's commandments?**  
**Answer:** They refused to obey.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:16

---

### Nehemiah 9:17

**Q1. What kind of God is He, ready to pardon?**  
**Answer:** A God ready to pardon, gracious and merciful, slow to anger, and of great kindness.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:17

**Q2. When they made them a molten calf, what did they say?**  
**Answer:** This is thy God that brought thee up out of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:17

**Q3. What did the people commit against God?**  
**Answer:** Great provocations.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:17

---

### Nehemiah 9:18

**Q1. What did they make for themselves?**  
**Answer:** A molten calf.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:18

**Q2. What did they say the molten calf did?**  
**Answer:** Brought thee up out of Egypt.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:18

**Q3. What kind of provocations did they commit?**  
**Answer:** Great provocations.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:18

---

### Nehemiah 9:19

**Q1. Why did God not forsaking them in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** For thy manifold mercies' sake.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:19

**Q2. What departed not from them by day?**  
**Answer:** The pillar of the cloud.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:19

**Q3. What departed not from them by night?**  
**Answer:** The pillar of fire.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:19

---

### Nehemiah 9:20

**Q1. What did God give to instruct them?**  
**Answer:** Thy good spirit.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:20

**Q2. What did God withhold not from their mouth?**  
**Answer:** Thy manna.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:20

**Q3. What did God give them for their thirst?**  
**Answer:** Water.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:20

---

### Nehemiah 9:21

**Q1. How many years did God sustain them in the wilderness?**  
**Answer:** Forty years.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:21

**Q2. What lacked not during these forty years?**  
**Answer:** Their clothes.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:21

**Q3. What happened to their feet during this time?**  
**Answer:** Their feet swelled not.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:21

---

### Nehemiah 9:22

**Q1. What did God give them, besides kingdoms and nations?**  
**Answer:** Corners.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:22

**Q2. Whose land did they possess?**  
**Answer:** The land of Sihon, and the land of the king of Heshbon, and the land of Og king of Bashan.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:22

**Q3. What did God do with the kingdoms and nations?**  
**Answer:** Divided them into lots.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:22

---

### Nehemiah 9:23

**Q1. How did God multiply their children?**  
**Answer:** As the stars of heaven.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:23

**Q2. Into what place did God bring them?**  
**Answer:** Into the land, concerning which thou hadst promised to their fathers, that they should go in to possess it.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:23

**Q3. Whose fathers were promised this land?**  
**Answer:** Their fathers.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:23

---

### Nehemiah 9:24

**Q1. Whose land did the children go in and possess?**  
**Answer:** The land of the inhabitants of the land, the Canaanites.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:24

**Q2. Into whose hands did God subdue the inhabitants?**  
**Answer:** Into their hands.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:24

**Q3. What did they do with their kings, and the people of the land?**  
**Answer:** Did with them as they would.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:24

---

### Nehemiah 9:25

**Q1. What kind of cities did they take?**  
**Answer:** Strong cities.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:25

**Q2. What kind of land did they possess?**  
**Answer:** A fat land.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:25

**Q3. With what were the houses filled?**  
**Answer:** All goods, wells digged, vineyards, and oliveyards, and fruit trees in abundance.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:25

---

### Nehemiah 9:26

**Q1. How did the people behave after they were filled?**  
**Answer:** Were disobedient, and rebelled against thee.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:26

**Q2. What did they cast behind their backs?**  
**Answer:** Thy law.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:26

**Q3. Whom did they slay to turn them to God?**  
**Answer:** Thy prophets.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:26

---

### Nehemiah 9:27

**Q1. Into whose hand did God deliver them when they were vexed?**  
**Answer:** Into the hand of their enemies.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:27

**Q2. What did they do in the time of their trouble when they cried unto God?**  
**Answer:** Cried unto thee.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:27

**Q3. What did God send them from heaven according to His manifold mercies?**  
**Answer:** Saviours.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:27

---

### Nehemiah 9:28

**Q1. What did they do again after they had rest?**  
**Answer:** Did evil again before thee.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:28

**Q2. Into whose hand did God leave them to have dominion over them?**  
**Answer:** Into the hand of their enemies.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:28

**Q3. When they returned and cried unto God, what did God do from heaven?**  
**Answer:** Many times didst thou deliver them according to thy mercies.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:28

---

### Nehemiah 9:29

**Q1. Against what did God testify to bring them back to His law?**  
**Answer:** Against them.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:29

**Q2. How did they act toward God's commandments?**  
**Answer:** Dealt proudly, and hearkened not unto thy commandments, but sinned against thy judgments.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:29

**Q3. What happens to the man that does the judgments of God?**  
**Answer:** He shall live in them.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:29

---

### Nehemiah 9:30

**Q1. For how many years did God forbear them?**  
**Answer:** Many years.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:30

**Q2. By whose spirit did God testify against them?**  
**Answer:** By thy spirit in thy prophets.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:30

**Q3. Into whose hand did God give them because they would not give ear?**  
**Answer:** Into the hand of the people of the lands.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:30

---

### Nehemiah 9:31

**Q1. What kind of mercies did God have for them in His manifold mercies?**  
**Answer:** Didst not utterly consume them, nor forsake them.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:31

**Q2. What kind of God is God?**  
**Answer:** A gracious and merciful God.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:31

**Q3. Why did God not utterly consume them?**  
**Answer:** For thy great mercies' sake.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:31

---

### Nehemiah 9:32

**Q1. What kind of God is our God?**  
**Answer:** The great, the mighty, and the terrible God, who keepeth covenant and mercy.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:32

**Q2. Whose trouble is considered before God?**  
**Answer:** All the travail.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:32

**Q3. Since the time of what kings has this trouble come upon them?**  
**Answer:** The kings of Assyria unto this day.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:32

---

### Nehemiah 9:33

**Q1. In all that is brought upon us, how is God?**  
**Answer:** Just.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:33

**Q2. How has God done?**  
**Answer:** Right.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:33

**Q3. What has God done concerning what He has done?**  
**Answer:** Thou hast done right, but we have done wickedly.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:33

---

### Nehemiah 9:34

**Q1. Who have not kept God's law?**  
**Answer:** Our kings, our princes, our priests, and our fathers.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:34

**Q2. To what did they not hearken?**  
**Answer:** Thy commandments.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:34

**Q3. What else did they not hearken unto?**  
**Answer:** Thy testimonies.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:34

---

### Nehemiah 9:35

**Q1. When did they not serve God in His kingdom?**  
**Answer:** In their kingdom.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:35

**Q2. What did God give them that they served?**  
**Answer:** In thy great goodness that thou gavest them, and in the large and fat land which thou gavest before them.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:35

**Q3. From what did they not turn?**  
**Answer:** From their wicked works.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:35

---

### Nehemiah 9:36

**Q1. Who are servants this day?**  
**Answer:** We.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:36

**Q2. For what purpose is the land given to our fathers?**  
**Answer:** To eat the fruit thereof and the good thereof.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:36

**Q3. Where are they servants?**  
**Answer:** In the land that thou gavest unto our fathers.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:36

---

### Nehemiah 9:37

**Q1. To whom does the increase of the land yield because of our sins?**  
**Answer:** Unto the kings whom thou hast set over us.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:37

**Q2. Over what do the kings have dominion?**  
**Answer:** Over our bodies, and over our cattle, at their pleasure.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:37

**Q3. In what great distress are they?**  
**Answer:** In great distress.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:37

---

### Nehemiah 9:38

**Q1. Because of all this, what do they make?**  
**Answer:** A sure covenant.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:38

**Q2. Who writes the covenant?**  
**Answer:** Our princes, Levites, and priests.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:38

**Q3. What is upon the written covenant?**  
**Answer:** Sealed.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 9:38
