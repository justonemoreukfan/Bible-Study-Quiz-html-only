# Nehemiah 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Nehemiah 4:1

**Q1. Who heard that they built the wall?**  
**Answer:** Sanballat.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:1

**Q2. How did Sanballat feel when he heard that they built the wall?**  
**Answer:** Wroth, and took great indignation.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:1

**Q3. Whom did Sanballat mock?**  
**Answer:** The Jews.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:1

---

### Nehemiah 4:2

**Q1. Before whom did Sanballat speak?**  
**Answer:** Before his brethren and the army of Samaria.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:2

**Q2. What did Sanballat ask about the feeble Jews?**  
**Answer:** "Will they fortify themselves? will they sacrifice? will they make an end in a day? will they revive the stones out of the heaps of the rubbish which are burned?"  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:2

**Q3. What kind of stones did Sanballat mention reviving?**  
**Answer:** The stones out of the heaps of the rubbish which are burned.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:2

---

### Nehemiah 4:3

**Q1. Who was with Sanballat when he spoke?**  
**Answer:** Tobiah the Ammonite.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:3

**Q2. What did Tobiah say even if a fox went up on it?**  
**Answer:** "Even that which they build, if a fox go up, he shall even break down their stone wall."  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:3

**Q3. What animal did Tobiah use to illustrate breaking down the wall?**  
**Answer:** A fox.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:3

---

### Nehemiah 4:4

**Q1. Who spoke in prayer to God in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Nehemiah ("Hear, O our God").  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:4

**Q2. What did Nehemiah ask God to turn upon their own head?**  
**Answer:** Their reproach.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:4

**Q3. For what did Nehemiah pray regarding the enemies?**  
**Answer:** That they may be given for a prey in the land of captivity.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:4

---

### Nehemiah 4:5

**Q1. What did Nehemiah ask God not to cover regarding their iniquity?**  
**Answer:** Their iniquity.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:5

**Q2. What did Nehemiah ask God not to do to their sin from before Him?**  
**Answer:** Let not their sin be blotted out.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:5

**Q3. Why did Nehemiah ask God not to blot out their sin?**  
**Answer:** Because they have provoked thee to anger before the builders.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:5

---

### Nehemiah 4:6

**Q1. What did they build?**  
**Answer:** The wall.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:6

**Q2. To what height was all the wall joined together?**  
**Answer:** Unto the half thereof.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:6

**Q3. Why had the wall reached half its height?**  
**Answer:** For the people had a mind to work.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:6

---

### Nehemiah 4:7

**Q1. Who heard that the walls of Jerusalem were made up?**  
**Answer:** Sanballat, and Tobiah, and the Arabians, and the Ammonites, and the Ashdodites.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:7

**Q2. What had begun to be stopped?**  
**Answer:** The breaches.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:7

**Q3. How did these groups feel when they heard the news?**  
**Answer:** Very wroth.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:7

---

### Nehemiah 4:8

**Q1. What did all the enemies conspire together to do?**  
**Answer:** To come and to fight against Jerusalem, and to hinder it.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:8

**Q2. What else did they conspire to do besides fighting against Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** To hinder it.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:8

**Q3. What did they plan to do to Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** To do it hurt.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:8

---

### Nehemiah 4:9

**Q1. To whom did they make their prayer?**  
**Answer:** Unto our God.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:9

**Q2. What did they set against the enemies because of them?**  
**Answer:** A watch.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:9

**Q3. How often did they set a watch against them?**  
**Answer:** Day and night.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:9

---

### Nehemiah 4:10

**Q1. Who said that the strength of the bearers of burdens was decayed?**  
**Answer:** Judah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:10

**Q2. What was there much of among the builders?**  
**Answer:** Rubbish.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:10

**Q3. What did the people say they were not able to do?**  
**Answer:** Build the wall.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:10

---

### Nehemiah 4:11

**Q1. What did their adversaries say?**  
**Answer:** "They shall not know, neither see, till we come in the midst of them, and slay them, and cause the work to cease."  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:11

**Q2. What did the adversaries plan to do once they came in the midst of them?**  
**Answer:** Slay them.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:11

**Q3. What was the ultimate result the adversaries wanted to cause?**  
**Answer:** Cause the work to cease.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:11

---

### Nehemiah 4:12

**Q1. Who came when the Jews which dwelt by them came?**  
**Answer:** The Jews which dwelt by them (stated as: "And it came to pass, that when the Jews which dwelt by them came...").  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:12

**Q2. How many times did they say unto them from all places, "Ye must return unto us"?**  
**Answer:** Ten times.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:12

**Q3. From where did they say "Ye must return unto us"?**  
**Answer:** From all places.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:12

---

### Nehemiah 4:13

**Q1. Where did Nehemiah set the people?**  
**Answer:** In the lower places behind the wall, and on the higher places.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:13

**Q2. How did Nehemiah set the people?**  
**Answer:** After their families.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:13

**Q3. With what weapons did the people stand?**  
**Answer:** With their swords, their spears, and their bows.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:13

---

### Nehemiah 4:14

**Q1. Whom did Nehemiah look upon?**  
**Answer:** The nobles, and the rulers, and the rest of the people.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:14

**Q2. What did Nehemiah say to them after rising up?**  
**Answer:** "Be not ye afraid of them: remember the Lord, which is great and terrible, and fight for your brethren, your sons, and your daughters, your wives, and your houses."  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:14

**Q3. For whom did Nehemiah tell them to fight?**  
**Answer:** Their brethren, their sons, and their daughters, their wives, and their houses.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:14

---

### Nehemiah 4:15

**Q1. What did their enemies hear that God had done to their counsel?**  
**Answer:** That it was brought to nought.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:15

**Q2. Where did they return after their counsel was brought to nought?**  
**Answer:** Every one to his work.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:15

**Q3. Who brought the enemies' counsel to nought?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:15

---

### Nehemiah 4:16

**Q1. From that time forth, how many of Nehemiah's servants wrought in the work?**  
**Answer:** The half of them.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:16

**Q2. What did the other half of the servants hold?**  
**Answer:** Both the spears, the shields, and the bows, and the habergeons.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:16

**Q3. Who stood behind all the house of Judah?**  
**Answer:** The rulers.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:16

---

### Nehemiah 4:17

**Q1. Who built on the wall?**  
**Answer:** They that builded on the wall.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:17

**Q2. Who bare burdens?**  
**Answer:** They that bare burdens.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:17

**Q3. How did they handle their work and weapons?**  
**Answer:** With one of his hands wrought in the work, and with the other hand held a weapon.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:17

---

### Nehemiah 4:18

**Q1. How was every one of the builders girded while building?**  
**Answer:** With his sword by his side.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:18

**Q2. Who sounded the trumpet by Nehemiah?**  
**Answer:** He that sounded the trumpet.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:18

**Q3. What was by Nehemiah while he spoke?**  
**Answer:** He that sounded the trumpet.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:18

---

### Nehemiah 4:19

**Q1. To whom did Nehemiah speak?**  
**Answer:** To the nobles, and to the rulers, and to the rest of the people.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:19

**Q2. How was the work described by Nehemiah?**  
**Answer:** Great and large.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:19

**Q3. How were the workers separated upon the wall?**  
**Answer:** Far one from another.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:19

---

### Nehemiah 4:20

**Q1. What did Nehemiah say the people should resort to thither?**  
**Answer:** In what place soever ye hear the sound of the trumpet.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:20

**Q2. Who would fight for them?**  
**Answer:** Our God.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:20

**Q3. What instrument would sound to gather them?**  
**Answer:** The trumpet.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:20

---

### Nehemiah 4:21

**Q1. How did they labor in the work?**  
**Answer:** So we laboured in the work.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:21

**Q2. What did half of them hold while laboring?**  
**Answer:** The spears.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:21

**Q3. From when until when did they labor?**  
**Answer:** From the rising of the morning till the stars appeared.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:21

---

### Nehemiah 4:22

**Q1. What did Nehemiah say to the people at that time regarding lodging?**  
**Answer:** "Let every one with his servant lodge within Jerusalem."  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:22

**Q2. Why did Nehemiah want them to lodge within Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** That in the night they may be a guard to us, and labour on the day.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:22

**Q3. What would they do by night and by day according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** Be a guard by night and labor on the day.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:22

---

**Q1. Who did not put off their clothes?**  
**Answer:** Nehemiah, his brethren, his servants, and the guard of men that followed him.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:23

**Q2. What did every one of them have for defense?**  
**Answer:** Every one put them off for washing. (KJV: "every one put them off for washing", meaning they kept them on otherwise; or "saving that every one put them off for washing").  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 4:23
