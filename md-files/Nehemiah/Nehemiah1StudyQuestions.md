# Nehemiah 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Nehemiah 1:1

**Q1. Whose words are recorded in this book?**  
**Answer:** The words of Nehemiah the son of Hachaliah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:1

**Q2. In what month did these events take place?**  
**Answer:** In the month Chisleu.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:1

**Q3. In what year did these events take place?**  
**Answer:** In the twentieth year.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:1

---

### Nehemiah 1:2

**Q4. Who came, along with certain men of Judah?**  
**Answer:** Hanani, one of his brethren.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:2

**Q5. Concerning what two things did Nehemiah ask them?**  
**Answer:** Concerning the Jews that had escaped, which were left of the captivity, and concerning Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:2

**Q6. From where had the captivity escaped?**  
**Answer:** They were left of the captivity.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:2

---

### Nehemiah 1:3

**Q7. What condition were the remnant in the province in?**  
**Answer:** In great affliction and reproach.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:3

**Q8. What is the condition of the wall of Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Broken down.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:3

**Q9. What is the condition of the gates of Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Burned with fire.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:3

---

### Nehemiah 1:4

**Q10. How did Nehemiah react when he heard these words?**  
**Answer:** He sat down and wept, and mourned certain days.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:4

**Q11. For how long did Nehemiah mourn certain days?**  
**Answer:** He fasted and prayed before the God of heaven.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:4

**Q12. Before whom did Nehemiah fast and pray?**  
**Answer:** The God of heaven.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:4

---

### Nehemiah 1:5

**Q13. How did Nehemiah address God at the beginning of his prayer?**  
**Answer:** Lord God of heaven, the great and terrible God.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:5

**Q14. What does God keep with them that love Him and observe His commandments?**  
**Answer:** Covenant and mercy.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:5

**Q15. Who are the recipients of God's covenant and mercy?**  
**Answer:** Them that love him and observe his commandments.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:5

---

### Nehemiah 1:6

**Q17. What did Nehemiah ask God's ear to be?**  
**Answer:** Attentive, and his eyes open.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:6

**Q18. For what purpose did Nehemiah want God to hear the prayer?**  
**Answer:** That I may hear the prayer of thy servant.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:6

**Q19. Whose sins did Nehemiah confess before God?**  
**Answer:** The sins of the children of Israel, which we have sinned against thee: both I and my father's house have sinned.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:6

---

### Nehemiah 1:7

**Q20. How have the children of Israel dealt very corruptly against God?**  
**Answer:** They have dealt very corruptly against thee.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:7

**Q21. What did the people fail to keep?**  
**Answer:** The commandments, nor the statutes, nor the judgments.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:7

**Q22. Through whom were these commandments given?**  
**Answer:** Thy servant Moses.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:7

---

### Nehemiah 1:8

**Q23. What did Nehemiah ask God to remember?**  
**Answer:** The word that thou commandedst thy servant Moses.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:8

**Q24. What did God say would happen if the people transgressed?**  
**Answer:** I will scatter you abroad among the nations.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:8

**Q25. Who was instructed to give this command to the people?**  
**Answer:** Thy servant Moses.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:8

---

### Nehemiah 1:9

**Q26. What condition must the people turn unto God with?**  
**Answer:** If ye turn unto me, and keep my commandments, and do them.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:9

**Q27. Where did God say He would gather the outcasts from, even if they were there?**  
**Answer:** Unto the uttermost part of the heaven.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:9

**Q28. Where will God bring the gathered outcasts?**  
**Answer:** Unto the place that I have chosen to set my name there.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:9

---

### Nehemiah 1:10

**Q29. Whose servants and people are these?**  
**Answer:** Thy servants and thy people.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:10

**Q30. By what power did God redeem His people?**  
**Answer:** By thy great power, and by thy strong hand.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:10

**Q31. Who were redeemed by God's great power and strong hand?**  
**Answer:** Whom thou hast redeemed.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:10

---

### Nehemiah 1:11

**Q32. Whose ear did Nehemiah ask to be attentive to the prayer of His servant?**  
**Answer:** Lord, I beseech thee, let now thine ear be attentive to the prayer of thy servant.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:11

**Q33. Whose prayer did Nehemiah also mention alongside his own?**  
**Answer:** The prayer of thy servants, who desire to fear thy name.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:11

**Q34. What did Nehemiah ask God to grant him this day?**  
**Answer:** Prosperity, and grant him mercy in the sight of this man.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 1:11
