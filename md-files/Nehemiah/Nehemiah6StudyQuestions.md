# Nehemiah 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Nehemiah 6:1

**Q1. Who heard that Nehemiah had builded the wall?**  
**Answer:** Sanballat, and Tobiah, and Geshem the Arabian, and the rest of our enemies.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:1

**Q2. What had not been left at that time upon the wall, according to verse 1?**  
**Answer:** Any breach.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:1

**Q3. What had Nehemiah not done up to that point, even though the doors had not been set upon the gates?**  
**Answer:** He had not set up the doors upon the gates.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:1

---

### Nehemiah 6:2

**Q1. Who sent unto Nehemiah, saying, Come, let us meet together in some one of the villages in the plain of Ono?**  
**Answer:** Sanballat and Geshem.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:2

**Q2. What did Sanballat and Geshem think to do to Nehemiah?**  
**Answer:** They thought to do him mischief.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:2

**Q3. Where did they propose to meet together?**  
**Answer:** In some one of the villages in the plain of Ono.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:2

---

### Nehemiah 6:3

**Q1. Whom did Nehemiah send messengers unto?**  
**Answer:** Unto them.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:3

**Q2. What kind of work was Nehemiah doing?**  
**Answer:** A great work.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:3

**Q3. Why could the work not come down while Nehemiah came down to them?**  
**Answer:** Because the work would cease.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:3

---

### Nehemiah 6:4

**Q1. How many times did they send unto Nehemiah after this sort?**  
**Answer:** Four times.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:4

**Q2. What did Nehemiah answer them after this sort?**  
**Answer:** After the same manner.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:4

**Q3. How often did they send to Nehemiah with the same message?**  
**Answer:** Four times.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:4

---

### Nehemiah 6:5

**Q1. Who sent his servant unto Nehemiah the fifth time?**  
**Answer:** Sanballat.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:5

**Q2. In what manner did Sanballat send his servant the fifth time?**  
**Answer:** By his servant.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:5

**Q3. What was in the servant's hand when he was sent the fifth time?**  
**Answer:** An open letter.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:5

---

### Nehemiah 6:6

**Q1. What was reported among the heathen, and what did Gashmu say?**  
**Answer:** That thou and the Jews think to rebel.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:6

**Q2. For what reason did the letter say Nehemiah and the Jews built the wall?**  
**Answer:** That thou mayest be their king.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:6

**Q3. According to the letter, who said that Nehemiah and the Jews think to rebel?**  
**Answer:** Gashmu.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:6

---

### Nehemiah 6:7

**Q1. Whom had Nehemiah appointed to preach of him at Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Prophets.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:7

**Q2. What were the prophets to say concerning Nehemiah?**  
**Answer:** There is a king in Judah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:7

**Q3. What did the letter state these things should be reported to?**  
**Answer:** According to these words.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:7

---

### Nehemiah 6:8

**Q1. Who sent unto Sanballat?**  
**Answer:** Nehemiah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:8

**Q2. What did Nehemiah say was not done according to these words?**  
**Answer:** There are no such things done as thou sayest.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:8

**Q3. Where did Nehemiah say Sanballat feigned these things from?**  
**Answer:** Out of thine own heart.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:8

---

### Nehemiah 6:9

**Q1. What were all they trying to make Nehemiah and the others do?**  
**Answer:** Afraid.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:9

**Q2. What did they think the hands would be from the work?**  
**Answer:** Weakened.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:9

**Q3. What did Nehemiah pray after this?**  
**Answer:** Now therefore, O God, strengthen my hands.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:9

---

### Nehemiah 6:10

**Q1. Whose house did Nehemiah come unto?**  
**Answer:** The house of Shemaiah the son of Delaiah the son of Mehetabeel.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:10

**Q2. Why was Shemaiah shut up?**  
**Answer:** Who was shut up.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:10

**Q3. Where did Shemaiah suggest they meet together?**  
**Answer:** Into the house of God, within the temple, and let us shut the doors of the temple.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:10

---

### Nehemiah 6:11

**Q1. What did Nehemiah ask if such a man as he would do?**  
**Answer:** Should such a man as I flee?  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:11

**Q2. Who did Nehemiah say would go into the temple to save his life?**  
**Answer:** Who is there, that, being as I am, would go into the temple to save his life?  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:11

**Q3. What was Nehemiah's decision regarding entering the temple?**  
**Answer:** I will not go in.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:11

---

### Nehemiah 6:12

**Q1. What did Nehemiah perceive about God?**  
**Answer:** That God had not sent him.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:12

**Q2. Who pronounced this prophecy against Nehemiah?**  
**Answer:** Shemaiah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:12

**Q3. Why did Shemaiah pronounce this prophecy against Nehemiah?**  
**Answer:** Because Tobiah and Sanballat had hired him.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:12

---

### Nehemiah 6:13

**Q1. For what purpose was Shemaiah hired?**  
**Answer:** That I should be afraid, and do so, and sin.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:13

**Q2. What might they have matter for in order to reproach Nehemiah?**  
**Answer:** That they might have matter for an evil report.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:13

**Q3. What would they do with that evil report?**  
**Answer:** That they might reproach him.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:13

---

### Nehemiah 6:14

**Q1. Whom did Nehemiah pray to remember concerning their works?**  
**Answer:** My God.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:14

**Q2. Which prophetess did Nehemiah mention along with the rest of the prophets?**  
**Answer:** Noadiah the prophetess.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:14

**Q3. What did Nehemiah ask God to remember about Tobiah and Sanballat?**  
**Answer:** Remember thou Tobiah and Sanballat according to these their works.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:14

---

### Nehemiah 6:15

**Q1. On what day was the wall finished?**  
**Answer:** In the twenty and fifth day.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:15

**Q2. Of what month was the wall finished?**  
**Answer:** Elul.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:15

**Q3. How long did it take to finish the wall?**  
**Answer:** Fifty and two days.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:15

---

### Nehemiah 6:16

**Q1. Who heard about the wall being finished?**  
**Answer:** All our enemies.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:16

**Q2. Who saw about the wall being finished among the heathen about us?**  
**Answer:** All the heathen that were about us.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:16

**Q3. How did they feel when they saw the work was wrought of our God?**  
**Answer:** They were much cast down in their own eyes.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:16

---

### Nehemiah 6:17

**Q1. In those days, who sent many letters unto Tobiah?**  
**Answer:** The nobles of Judah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:17

**Q2. Whose letters came unto Tobiah?**  
**Answer:** The letters of the nobles of Judah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:17

**Q3. Whose letters went unto Tobiah?**  
**Answer:** The letters of Tobiah came unto them.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:17

---

### Nehemiah 6:18

**Q1. Who in Judah was the son-in-law of Shechaniah the son of Arah?**  
**Answer:** Johanan.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:18

**Q2. Whose son was Meshullam?**  
**Answer:** Berechiah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:18

**Q3. To whom was Tobiah sworn because he was the son-in-law of Shechaniah?**  
**Answer:** To Tobiah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:18

---

### Nehemiah 6:19

**Q1. What did they report before Nehemiah?**  
**Answer:** His good deeds.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:19

**Q2. Whose words did they utter to Tobiah?**  
**Answer:** Nehemiah's words.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:19

**Q3. What did Tobiah send to put Nehemiah in fear?**  
**Answer:** Letters.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 6:19
