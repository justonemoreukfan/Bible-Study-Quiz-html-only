# Nehemiah 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Nehemiah 8:1

**Q1. As one man, where did all the people gather themselves together?**  
**Answer:** Into the street that was before the water gate.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:1

**Q2. Whom did the people speak unto to bring the book of the law of Moses?**  
**Answer:** Ezra the scribe.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:1

**Q3. What did the people command Ezra the scribe to bring?**  
**Answer:** The book of the law of Moses, which the LORD had commanded to Israel.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:1

---

### Nehemiah 8:2

**Q1. Who brought the law before the congregation upon the first day of the seventh month?**  
**Answer:** Ezra the priest.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:2

**Q2. Who composed the congregation that Ezra brought the law before?**  
**Answer:** Both men and women, and all that could hear with understanding.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:2

**Q3. Where did Ezra bring the law before the congregation?**  
**Answer:** Before the street that was before the water gate.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:2

---

### Nehemiah 8:3

**Q1. From where until where did Ezra read therein before the street that was before the water gate?**  
**Answer:** From the morning until mid-day.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:3

**Q2. Before whom did Ezra read the book?**  
**Answer:** Before the men and the women, and those that could understand.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:3

**Q3. Whose ears were attentive unto the book of the law?**  
**Answer:** The ears of all the people.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:3

---

### Nehemiah 8:4

**Q1. Upon what did Ezra the scribe stand?**  
**Answer:** A pulpit of wood, which they had made for the purpose.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:4

**Q2. Who stood beside Ezra on his right hand?**  
**Answer:** Mattithiah, and Shema, and Anaiah, and Urijah, and Hilkiah, and Maaseiah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:4

**Q3. Who stood on Ezra's left hand?**  
**Answer:** Pedaiah, and Mishael, and Malchiah, and Hashum, and Hashbadana, Zechariah, and Meshullam.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:4

---

### Nehemiah 8:5

**Q1. What did Ezra do in the sight of all the people?**  
**Answer:** He opened the book.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:5

**Q2. What position was Ezra in above all the people when he opened the book?**  
**Answer:** He was above all the people.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:5

**Q3. What did all the people do when Ezra opened the book?**  
**Answer:** They stood up.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:5

---

### Nehemiah 8:6

**Q1. Whom did Ezra bless?**  
**Answer:** The great God.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:6

**Q2. How did all the people answer when Ezra blessed the great God?**  
**Answer:** Amen, Amen, with lifting up their hands.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:6

**Q3. What physical posture did the people take as they bowed their heads and worshipped the LORD?**  
**Answer:** With their faces to the ground.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:6

---

### Nehemiah 8:7

**Q1. Who caused the people to understand the law?**  
**Answer:** Jeshua, and Bani, and Sherebiah, Jamin, Akkub, Shabbethai, Hodijah, Maaseiah, Kelita, Azariah, Jozabad, Hanan, Pelaiah, and the Levites.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:7

**Q2. What were the people doing while standing in their place?**  
**Answer:** Understanding the law.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:7

**Q3. Where were the people standing while the Levites caused them to understand the law?**  
**Answer:** In their place.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:7

---

### Nehemiah 8:8

**Q1. In what did they read distinctly?**  
**Answer:** In the book in the law of God.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:8

**Q2. What did they give along with the reading of the book?**  
**Answer:** The sense.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:8

**Q3. What did the people understand as a result of this process?**  
**Answer:** The reading.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:8

---

### Nehemiah 8:9

**Q1. Who said unto all the people that Nehemiah is the Tirshatha?**  
**Answer:** Nehemiah, which is the Tirshatha, and Ezra the priest the scribe, and the Levites that taught the people.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:9

**Q2. Unto the LORD their God, what kind of day was it specified to be?**  
**Answer:** Holy.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:9

**Q3. How did the people react as they heard the words of the law?**  
**Answer:** They wept.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:9

---

### Nehemiah 8:10

**Q1. What did Nehemiah tell the people to go their way and eat?**  
**Answer:** The fat.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:10

**Q2. What did Nehemiah tell the people to drink?**  
**Answer:** The sweet.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:10

**Q3. What is the strength of the people according to Nehemiah 8:10?**  
**Answer:** The joy of the LORD.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:10

---

### Nehemiah 8:11

**Q1. Who stilled all the people?**  
**Answer:** The Levites.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:11

**Q2. What did the Levites tell the people to hold?**  
**Answer:** Their peace.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:11

**Q3. Why did the Levites tell the people not to be grieved?**  
**Answer:** Because the day was holy.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:11

---

### Nehemiah 8:12

**Q1. Where did all the people go to eat and to drink?**  
**Answer:** Their way.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:12

**Q2. What did they send to others?**  
**Answer:** Portions.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:12

**Q3. Why did the people make great mirth?**  
**Answer:** Because they had understood the words that were declared unto them.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:12

---

### Nehemiah 8:13

**Q1. On what day were gathered together the chief of the fathers of all the people, the priests, and the Levites?**  
**Answer:** The second day.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:13

**Q2. Unto whom were the chief of the fathers, the priests, and the Levites gathered?**  
**Answer:** Unto Ezra the scribe.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:13

**Q3. For what purpose were they gathered unto Ezra the scribe?**  
**Answer:** Even to understand the words of the law.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:13

---

### Nehemiah 8:14

**Q1. What did they find written in the law?**  
**Answer:** That the LORD had commanded by Moses, that the children of Israel should dwell in booths in the feast of the seventh month.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:14

**Q2. By whom had the LORD commanded the children of Israel to dwell in booths?**  
**Answer:** By Moses.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:14

**Q3. During which feast were the children of Israel commanded to dwell in booths?**  
**Answer:** In the feast of the seventh month.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:14

---

### Nehemiah 8:15

**Q1. What did they publish and proclaim in all their cities, and in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Go forth unto the mount, and fetch olive branches, and pine branches, and myrtle branches, and palm branches, and branches of thick trees, to make booths, as it is written.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:15

**Q2. What kind of branches were to be fetched along with olive, pine, and myrtle branches?**  
**Answer:** Palm branches, and branches of thick trees.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:15

**Q3. For what purpose were the branches to be fetched?**  
**Answer:** To make booths, as it is written.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:15

---

### Nehemiah 8:16

**Q1. Where did the people go forth to bring the branches?**  
**Answer:** Abroad.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:16

**Q2. Where did the people make themselves booths?**  
**Answer:** Every one upon the roof of his house, and in their courts, and in the courts of the house of God, and in the street of the water gate, and in the street of the gate of Ephraim.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:16

**Q3. Whose house courts did some of the people make booths in?**  
**Answer:** The house of God.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:16

---

### Nehemiah 8:17

**Q1. Who were all the congregation that were come again out of the captivity?**  
**Answer:** They made booths, and sat under the booths.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:17

**Q2. Since the days of whose son had not the children of Israel done so?**  
**Answer:** Joshua the son of Nun.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:17

**Q3. What was there among the congregation of those who came again out of the captivity?**  
**Answer:** Very great gladness.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:17

---

### Nehemiah 8:18

**Q1. From the first day unto the last day, what did Ezra read in?**  
**Answer:** The book of the law of God.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:18

**Q2. How many days did they keep the feast?**  
**Answer:** Seven days.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:18

**Q3. According unto what manner was the solemn assembly kept on the eighth day?**  
**Answer:** According unto the manner.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 8:18
