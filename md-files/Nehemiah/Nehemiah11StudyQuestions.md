# Nehemiah 11 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Nehemiah 11:1
**Q1. Where did the rulers of the people dwell?**  
**Answer:** At Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 11:1

**Q2. How did the rest of the people determine who would dwell in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** They cast lots, to bring one of ten to dwell in Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 11:1

**Q3. What title is given to Jerusalem in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The holy city.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 11:1

---

### Nehemiah 11:2
**Q4. Whom did the people bless?**  
**Answer:** All the men that willingly offered themselves to dwell at Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 11:2

**Q5. What did the blessed men willingly offer themselves to do?**  
**Answer:** To dwell at Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 11:2

**Q6. How did these men offer themselves to dwell at Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Willingly.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 11:2

---

### Nehemiah 11:3
**Q7. Who dwelt in Jerusalem according to the beginning of this verse?**  
**Answer:** The chief of the province.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 11:3

**Q8. Where did everyone dwell in his possession?**  
**Answer:** In the cities of Judah (in their cities).  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 11:3

**Q9. Which groups are listed as dwelling in the cities of Judah?**  
**Answer:** Israel, the priests, the Levites, the Nethinims, and the children of Solomon’s servants.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 11:3

---

### Nehemiah 11:4
**Q10. Children of which two tribes dwelt at Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** The children of Judah and the children of Benjamin.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 11:4

**Q11. Who of the children of Judah is identified by his ancestral line beginning with Uzziah?**  
**Answer:** Athaiah the son of Uzziah.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 11:4

**Q12. Of whose children was Athaiah descended?**  
**Answer:** The children of Perez.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 11:4

---

### Nehemiah 11:5

**Answer:** Col-hozeh.  
**Verse:** Nehemiah 11:5

**Answer:** The son of Shiloni.
