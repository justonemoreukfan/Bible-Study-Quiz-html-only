# 1 Chronicles 10 Study Questions (KJV)
---
### 1 Chronicles 10:1
**Q1. Who fought against Israel in this chapter?**
**Answer:** The Philistines.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:1

**Q2. What happened to the men of Israel when the Philistines fought against them?**
**Answer:** They fled from before the Philistines, and fell down slain.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:1

**Q3. Where did the men of Israel fall down slain?**
**Answer:** In mount Gilboa.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:1

### 1 Chronicles 10:2
**Q1. Who did the Philistines follow hard after?**
**Answer:** Saul, and after his sons.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:2

**Q2. Which sons of Saul were slain by the Philistines?**
**Answer:** Jonathan, and Abinadab, and Malchishua.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:2

**Q3. Who were the sons of Saul slain by the Philistines?**
**Answer:** Jonathan, and Abinadab, and Malchishua.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:2

### 1 Chronicles 10:3
**Q1. How did the battle go against Saul?**
**Answer:** The battle went sore against Saul.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:3

**Q2. Who hit Saul in the battle?**
**Answer:** The archers.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:3

**Q3. What happened to Saul after the archers hit him?**
**Answer:** He was wounded.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:3

### 1 Chronicles 10:4
**Q1. What did Saul tell his armourbearer to do?**
**Answer:** Draw thy sword, and thrust me through therewith.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:4

**Q2. Why did Saul want to be thrust through with the sword?**
**Answer:** Lest these uncircumcised come and abuse me.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:4

**Q3. How did Saul die after his armourbearer would not thrust him through?**
**Answer:** He took a sword, and fell upon it.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:4

### 1 Chronicles 10:5
**Q1. What did the armourbearer do when he saw that Saul was dead?**
**Answer:** He fell likewise on the sword, and died.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:5

**Q2. Who fell on the sword after Saul died?**
**Answer:** His armourbearer.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:5

**Q3. What was the outcome for the armourbearer?**
**Answer:** He died.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:5

### 1 Chronicles 10:6
**Q1. Who died together in this verse?**
**Answer:** Saul, and his three sons, and all his house.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:6

**Q2. How many sons of Saul died?**
**Answer:** Three sons.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:6

**Q3. Who else besides Saul and his sons died together?**
**Answer:** All his house.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:6

### 1 Chronicles 10:7
**Q1. What did the men of Israel in the valley do when they saw that they fled and that Saul and his sons were dead?**
**Answer:** They forsook their cities, and fled.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:7

**Q2. Who came and dwelt in the cities after the men of Israel fled?**
**Answer:** The Philistines.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:7

**Q3. Why did the men of Israel forsake their cities?**
**Answer:** Because they saw that they fled, and that Saul and his sons were dead.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:7

### 1 Chronicles 10:8
**Q1. When did the Philistines come to strip the slain?**
**Answer:** On the morrow.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:8

**Q2. Who did the Philistines find fallen in mount Gilboa?**
**Answer:** Saul and his sons.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:8

**Q3. Where were Saul and his sons found fallen?**
**Answer:** In mount Gilboa.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:8

### 1 Chronicles 10:9
**Q1. What did the Philistines take after they had stripped Saul?**
**Answer:** His head, and his armour.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:9

**Q2. Where did the Philistines send Saul's head and armour?**
**Answer:** Into the land of the Philistines round about.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:9

**Q3. Why did they send these items into the land of the Philistines?**
**Answer:** To carry tidings unto their idols, and to the people.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:9

### 1 Chronicles 10:10
**Q1. Where did the Philistines put Saul's armour?**
**Answer:** In the house of their gods.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:10

**Q2. Where did the Philistines fasten Saul's head?**
**Answer:** In the temple of Dagon.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:10

**Q3. In which temple was Saul's head fastened?**
**Answer:** The temple of Dagon.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:10

### 1 Chronicles 10:11
**Q1. Who heard all that the Philistines had done to Saul?**
**Answer:** All Jabeshgilead.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:11

**Q2. What did the people of Jabeshgilead hear?**
**Answer:** All that the Philistines had done to Saul.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:11

**Q3. Who heard about the actions of the Philistines?**
**Answer:** All Jabeshgilead.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:11

### 1 Chronicles 10:12
**Q1. Who arose and took away the body of Saul and the bodies of his sons?**
**Answer:** All the valiant men of Jabeshgilead.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:12

**Q2. Where did they bring the bodies of Saul and his sons?**
**Answer:** To Jabesh.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:12

**Q3. Where were the bones of Saul and his sons buried, and for how long did they fast?**
**Answer:** Under the oak in Jabesh, and they fasted seven days.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:12

### 1 Chronicles 10:13
**Q1. Why did Saul die according to this verse?**
**Answer:** For his transgression which he committed against the Lord, even against the word of the Lord, which he kept not.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:13

**Q2. What other action did Saul take that contributed to his death?**
**Answer:** Asking counsel of one that had a familiar spirit, to enquire of it.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:13

**Q3. To whom did Saul commit his transgression?**
**Answer:** Against the Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:13

### 1 Chronicles 10:14
**Q1. What was the result of Saul not enquiring of the Lord?**
**Answer:** Therefore he slew him.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:14

**Q2. To whom was the kingdom turned after Saul was slain?**
**Answer:** Unto David the son of Jesse.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:14

**Q3. Who was the recipient of the kingdom after Saul's death?**
**Answer:** David the son of Jesse.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 10:14
