# 1 Chronicles 26 Study Questions (KJV)
---
### 1 Chronicles 26:1
**Q1. Who was the son of Kore, of the sons of Asaph, among the divisions of the porters?**
**Answer:** Meshelemiah.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 26:1

**Q2. Meshelemiah was of which group?**
**Answer:** Of the Korhites.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 26:1

**Q3. Which son of Asaph was mentioned as a porter?**
**Answer:** Meshelemiah.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 26:1
