# 1 Chronicles 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Chronicles 5:1

**Q1. Who was the firstborn of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Reuben.  
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 5:1

**Q2. Why was Reuben's birthright given unto the sons of Joseph?**  
**Answer:** Forasmuch as he defiled his father's bed.  
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 5:1

**Q3. How is the genealogy reckoned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Not after the birthright.  
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 5:1

---

**Answer:** Eastward.

**Answer:** Unto the entering in of the wilderness from the river Euphrates.

**Q1. Who did the God of Israel stir up to carry them away?**
