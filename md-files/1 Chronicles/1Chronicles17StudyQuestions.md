# 1 Chronicles 17 Study Questions (KJV)
---
### 1 Chronicles 17:1
**Q1. Where was David when he spoke to Nathan the prophet?**
**Answer:** In his house.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:1

**Q2. What did David notice about his own dwelling?**
**Answer:** Lo, I dwell in an house of cedars.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:1

**Q3. Where did the ark of the covenant of the Lord remain?**
**Answer:** Under curtains.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:1

### 1 Chronicles 17:2
**Q1. Who did Nathan speak to?**
**Answer:** David.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:2

**Q2. What did Nathan tell David to do?**
**Answer:** Do all that is in thine heart.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:2

**Q3. Why did Nathan encourage David to do what was in his heart?**
**Answer:** For God is with thee.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:2

### 1 Chronicles 17:3
**Q1. When did the word of God come to Nathan?**
**Answer:** The same night.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:3

**Q2. To whom did the word of God come?**
**Answer:** To Nathan.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:3

**Q3. What happened that night?**
**Answer:** The word of God came to Nathan.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:3

### 1 Chronicles 17:4
**Q1. What was Nathan told to tell David?**
**Answer:** Thus saith the Lord, Thou shalt not build me an house to dwell in.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:4

**Q2. Who did the Lord call His servant in this verse?**
**Answer:** David.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:4

**Q3. What was the Lord's command regarding building a house?**
**Answer:** Thou shalt not build me an house to dwell in.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:4

### 1 Chronicles 17:5
**Q1. Since when had the Lord not dwelt in a house?**
**Answer:** Since the day that I brought up Israel unto this day.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:5

**Q2. Where had the Lord gone instead of dwelling in a house?**
**Answer:** From tent to tent, and from one tabernacle to another.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:5

**Q3. What had the Lord used instead of a house for dwelling?**
**Answer:** Tents and tabernacles.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:5

### 1 Chronicles 17:6
**Q1. To whom had the Lord spoken a word while walking with all Israel?**
**Answer:** Any of the judges of Israel.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:6

**Q2. What had the Lord commanded the judges of Israel to do?**
**Answer:** To feed my people.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:6

**Q3. What question had the Lord never asked the judges?**
**Answer:** Why have ye not built me an house of cedars?
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:6

### 1 Chronicles 17:7
**Q1. Who is the Lord of hosts speaking to in this verse?**
**Answer:** My servant David.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:7

**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:7

**Q3. Why did the Lord take David from the sheepcote?**
**Answer:** That thou shouldest be ruler over my people Israel.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 17:7

### 1 Chronicles 17:8

**Q3. What did the Lord make for David?**
