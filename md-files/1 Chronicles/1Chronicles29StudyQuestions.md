# 1 Chronicles 29 Study Questions (KJV)
---
### 1 Chronicles 29:1
**Q1. How did David describe Solomon in relation to the work of the palace?**
**Answer:** He said Solomon was yet young and tender, and the work is great.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 29:1

**Q2. For whom is the palace intended?**
**Answer:** For the Lord God.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 29:1

**Q3. Who did God alone choose?**
**Answer:** Solomon.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 29:1

### 1 Chronicles 29:2
**Q1. With what effort did David prepare for the house of his God?**
**Answer:** With all his might.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 29:2

**Q2. Which metals did David prepare for things to be made?**
**Answer:** Gold, silver, brass, and iron.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 29:2

**Q3. What other materials were prepared in abundance?**
**Answer:** Wood, onyx stones, stones to be set, glistering stones of divers colours, all manner of precious stones, and marble stones.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 29:2

### 1 Chronicles 29:3
**Q1. What did David give to the house of his God over and above what he had prepared?**
**Answer:** Of his own proper good, gold and silver.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 29:3

**Q2. Why did David give from his own proper good?**
**Answer:** Because he had set his affection to the house of his God.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 29:3

**Q3. What was given from David's proper good?**
**Answer:** Gold and silver.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 29:3

### 1 Chronicles 29:4
**Q1. How much gold of Ophir did David provide?**
**Answer:** Three thousand talents.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 29:4

**Q2. How much refined silver was provided?**
**Answer:** Seven thousand talents.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 29:4

**Q3. What was the purpose of the refined silver?**
**Answer:** To overlay the walls of the houses withal.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 29:4

### 1 Chronicles 29:5

**Q2. Who would make the work?**
**Answer:** Artificers.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 29:5

**Q3. What question did David ask at the end of this verse?**
**Answer:** "And who then is willing to consecrate his service this day unto the Lord?"
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 29:5
