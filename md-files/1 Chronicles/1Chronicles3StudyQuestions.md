# 1 Chronicles 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### 1 Chronicles 3:1

**Q1. Where were the sons of David born unto him?**  
**Answer:** In Hebron.  
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 3:1

**Q2. Who was the firstborn son of David and who was his mother?**  
**Answer:** Amnon, of Ahinoam the Jezreelitess.  
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 3:1

**Q3. Who was the second son of David and who was his mother?**  
**Answer:** Daniel, of Abigail the Carmelitess.  
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 3:1

---

### 1 Chronicles 3:2

**Q1. Who was the third son of David?**  
**Answer:** Absalom.  
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 3:2

**Q2. Who was Absalom's mother?**  
**Answer:** Maachah the daughter of Talmai king of Geshur.  
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 3:2

**Q3. Who was the fourth son of David and who was his mother?**  
**Answer:** Adonijah the son of Haggith.  
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 3:2

---

### 1 Chronicles 3:3

**Answer:** Abital.  
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 3:3

### 1 Chronicles 3:4

**Q1. How many sons were born unto David in Hebron?**  
**Answer:** Six.  
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 3:4

**Q2. How long did David reign in Hebron?**  
**Answer:** Seven years and six months.  
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 3:4

**Q3. How long did David reign in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Thirty and three years.  
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 3:4
