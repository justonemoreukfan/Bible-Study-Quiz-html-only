# 1 Chronicles 19 Study Questions (KJV)
---
### 1 Chronicles 19:1
**Q1. Who died and was succeeded by his son as king of the children of Ammon?**
**Answer:** Nahash.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:1

**Q2. Who reigned in the stead of Nahash?**
**Answer:** His son.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:1

**Q3. What was the position of the son of Nahash?**
**Answer:** King of the children of Ammon.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:1

### 1 Chronicles 19:2
**Q1. Why did David want to shew kindness unto Hanun the son of Nahash?**
**Answer:** Because his father shewed kindness to him.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:2

**Q2. What did David send to comfort Hanun?**
**Answer:** Messengers.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:2

**Q3. Where did David's servants go to comfort Hanun?**
**Answer:** Into the land of the children of Ammon.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:2

### 1 Chronicles 19:3
**Q1. What did the princes of the children of Ammon suggest to Hanun about David's messengers?**
**Answer:** That David did not honour his father by sending comforters.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:3

**Q2. For what purpose did the princes think David's servants had come?**
**Answer:** To search, and to overthrow, and to spy out the land.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:3

**Q3. Who advised Hanun against David's messengers?**
**Answer:** The princes of the children of Ammon.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:3

### 1 Chronicles 19:4
**Q1. What did Hanun do to David's servants?**
**Answer:** He shaved them and cut off their garments.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:4

**Q2. Where were the servants' garments cut off?**
**Answer:** In the midst hard by their buttocks.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:4

**Q3. What did Hanun do after shaving and cutting the garments of David's servants?**
**Answer:** He sent them away.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:4

### 1 Chronicles 19:5
**Q1. How did David react when he was told how his men were served?**
**Answer:** He sent to meet them.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:5

**Q2. Why did David send to meet his servants?**
**Answer:** For the men were greatly ashamed.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:5

**Q3. Where did the king tell the men to tarry until their beards were grown?**
**Answer:** At Jericho.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:5

### 1 Chronicles 19:6
**Q1. What did the children of Ammon do when they saw they had made themselves odious to David?**
**Answer:** Hanun and the children of Ammon sent a thousand talents of silver to hire chariots and horsemen.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:6

**Q2. From where did they hire chariots and horsemen?**
**Answer:** Out of Mesopotamia, and out of Syriamaachah, and out of Zobah.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:6

**Q3. How much silver did they send to hire the military support?**
**Answer:** A thousand talents of silver.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:6

### 1 Chronicles 19:7
**Q1. How many chariots were hired?**
**Answer:** Thirty and two thousand.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:7

**Q2. Who came and pitched before Medeba?**
**Answer:** The king of Maachah and his people.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:7

**Q3. What did the children of Ammon do after gathering from their cities?**
**Answer:** They came to battle.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:7

### 1 Chronicles 19:8
**Q1. Who did David send when he heard of the Ammonites' preparations?**
**Answer:** Joab, and all the host of the mighty men.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:8

**Q2. Who led the host of the mighty men?**
**Answer:** Joab.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:8

**Q3. When did David send Joab?**
**Answer:** When David heard that the children of Ammon had gathered to battle.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:8

### 1 Chronicles 19:9
**Q1. Where did the children of Ammon put the battle in array?**
**Answer:** Before the gate of the city.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:9

**Q2. Where were the kings that had come stationed?**
**Answer:** By themselves in the field.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:9

**Q3. Who put the battle in array before the gate of the city?**
**Answer:** The children of Ammon.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:9

### 1 Chronicles 19:10
**Q1. What did Joab observe about the battle array?**
**Answer:** That the battle was set against him before and behind.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:10

**Q2. Who did Joab choose to put in array against the Syrians?**
**Answer:** The choice of Israel.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:10

**Q3. Against whom did Joab put the choice of Israel in array?**
**Answer:** Against the Syrians.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:10

### 1 Chronicles 19:11
**Q1. To whom did Joab deliver the rest of the people?**
**Answer:** Unto the hand of Abishai his brother.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:11

**Q2. Against whom did Abishai and the rest of the people set themselves in array?**
**Answer:** Against the children of Ammon.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:11

**Q3. Who was Abishai in relation to Joab?**
**Answer:** His brother.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:11

### 1 Chronicles 19:12
**Q1. What did Joab say if the Syrians were too strong for him?**
**Answer:** Then thou shalt help me.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:12

**Q2. What did Joab say if the children of Ammon were too strong for Abishai?**
**Answer:** Then I will help thee.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:12

**Q3. Between whom was this agreement of mutual help made?**
**Answer:** Between Joab and Abishai.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:12

### 1 Chronicles 19:13
**Q1. What did Joab encourage himself and Abishai to be?**
**Answer:** Of good courage.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:13

**Q2. How did they intend to behave themselves?**
**Answer:** Valiantly.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:13

**Q3. For whom and what were they behaving valiantly?**
**Answer:** For our people, and for the cities of our God.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:13

### 1 Chronicles 19:14
**Q1. Who drew nigh before the Syrians unto the battle?**
**Answer:** Joab and the people that were with him.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:14

**Q2. What happened when Joab and his people fought the Syrians?**
**Answer:** They fled before him.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:14

**Q3. Against whom did Joab first draw nigh to battle?**
**Answer:** The Syrians.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:14

### 1 Chronicles 19:15
**Q1. What happened when the children of Ammon saw that the Syrians were fled?**
**Answer:** They likewise fled before Abishai his brother.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:15

**Q2. Where did the children of Ammon go after fleeing?**
**Answer:** They entered into the city.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:15

**Q3. Where did Joab go after the children of Ammon fled?**
**Answer:** To Jerusalem.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:15

### 1 Chronicles 19:16
**Q1. What did the Syrians do when they saw they were put to the worse before Israel?**
**Answer:** They sent messengers and drew forth the Syrians that were beyond the river.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:16

**Q2. Who went before the Syrians from beyond the river?**
**Answer:** Shophach the captain of the host of Hadarezer.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:16

**Q3. Who was Shophach?**
**Answer:** The captain of the host of Hadarezer.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:16

### 1 Chronicles 19:17
**Q1. What did David do after being told about the Syrians' reinforcements?**
**Answer:** He gathered all Israel and passed over Jordan.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:17

**Q2. Where did David set the battle in array?**
**Answer:** Against the Syrians.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:17

**Q3. Who fought with David after he put the battle in array?**
**Answer:** The Syrians.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:17

### 1 Chronicles 19:18
**Q1. What happened to the Syrians when they fought Israel?**
**Answer:** They fled before Israel.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:18

**Q2. How many Syrians in chariots did David slay?**
**Answer:** Seven thousand.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:18

**Q3. How many footmen did David slay, and who else was killed?**
**Answer:** Forty thousand footmen, and Shophach the captain of the host.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:18

### 1 Chronicles 19:19
**Q1. What did the servants of Hadarezer do when they saw they were put to the worse?**
**Answer:** They made peace with David and became his servants.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:19

**Q2. Who became David's servants after the battle?**
**Answer:** The servants of Hadarezer.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:19

**Q3. Did the Syrians help the children of Ammon any more?**
**Answer:** No.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 19:19
