# 1 Chronicles 28 Study Questions (KJV)
---
### 1 Chronicles 28:1
**Q1. Who did David assemble in Jerusalem?**
**Answer:** All the princes of Israel, the princes of the tribes, the captains of the companies that ministered to the king by course, the captains over the thousands, the captains over the hundreds, the stewards over all the substance and possession of the king and of his sons, the officers, the mighty men, and all the valiant men.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 28:1

**Q2. Where did David assemble these people?**
**Answer:** Unto Jerusalem.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 28:1

**Q3. Who were some of the groups David assembled?**
**Answer:** Princes of Israel, captains of the companies, stewards, officers, and mighty men.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 28:1

### 1 Chronicles 28:2
**Q1. What did David the king do when he stood up upon his feet?**
**Answer:** He said, "Hear me, my brethren, and my people."
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 28:2

**Q2. What had David in his heart to build for the ark of the covenant of the Lord?**
**Answer:** An house of rest.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 28:2

**Q3. What did David prepare for the building?**
**Answer:** He had made ready for the building.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 28:2

### 1 Chronicles 28:3
**Q1. Why did God tell David he should not build an house for His name?**
**Answer:** Because he had been a man of war and had shed blood.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 28:3

**Q2. What did God say to David regarding the house?**
**Answer:** "Thou shalt not build an house for my name."
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 28:3

**Q3. For what reason was David forbidden from building the house?**
**Answer:** Because he had been a man of war and had shed blood.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 28:3

### 1 Chronicles 28:7
**Q1. On what condition did God say He would establish Solomon's kingdom for ever?**
**Answer:** If he be constant to do His commandments and His judgments.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 28:7

**Q2. What did God promise to establish for ever?**
**Answer:** Solomon's kingdom.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 28:7

**Q3. What must Solomon do to have his kingdom established for ever?**

**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 28:7

### 1 Chronicles 28:8
**Q1. What did David tell the congregation of the Lord to keep and seek for?**
**Answer:** All the commandments of the Lord your God.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 28:8

**Q2. What was the reward for keeping the commandments of the Lord?**
**Answer:** That they may possess this good land and leave it for an inheritance for their children after them for ever.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 28:8

**Q3. Who was David speaking to in this verse?**
**Answer:** All Israel, the congregation of the Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 28:8

### 1 Chronicles 28:9
**Q1. What did David tell Solomon to do regarding the God of his father?**
**Answer:** To know the God of his father and serve him with a perfect heart and with a willing mind.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 28:9

**Q2. What does the Lord search and understand?**
**Answer:** All hearts and all the imaginations of the thoughts.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 28:9

**Q3. What happens if Solomon seeks the Lord, and what happens if he forsakes Him?**
**Answer:** If he seek him, he will be found of thee; but if thou forsake him, he will cast thee off for ever.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 28:9

### 1 Chronicles 28:12
**Q1. How did David obtain the pattern of the courts of the house of the Lord, the chambers, and the treasuries?**
**Answer:** By the spirit.

**Q3. What did David give to Solomon that was obtained by the spirit?**

**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 28:12
