# 1 Chronicles 21 Study Questions (KJV)
---
### 1 Chronicles 21:1
**Q1. Who stood up against Israel?**
**Answer:** Satan.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 21:1

**Q2. What did Satan provoke David to do?**
**Answer:** To number Israel.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 21:1

**Q3. Whom did Satan provoke?**
**Answer:** David.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 21:1

### 1 Chronicles 21:2
**Q1. Whom did David command to number Israel?**
**Answer:** Joab and to the rulers of the people.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 21:2

**Q2. What was the range for numbering Israel?**
**Answer:** From Beersheba even to Dan.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 21:2

**Q3. Why did David want the number of them brought to him?**
**Answer:** That he may know it.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 21:2

### 1 Chronicles 21:3
**Q1. What was Joab's response to the command to number Israel?**
**Answer:** The Lord make his people an hundred times so many more as they be.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 21:3

**Q2. Why did Joab question the requirement to number the people?**
**Answer:** Because they were all the king's servants.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 21:3

**Q3. What did Joab fear David's action would cause?**
**Answer:** A cause of trespass to Israel.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 21:3

### 1 Chronicles 21:4
**Q1. What prevailed against Joab?**
**Answer:** The king's word.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 21:4

**Q2. Where did Joab go after the king's word prevailed?**
**Answer:** Throughout all Israel.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 21:4

**Q3. Where did Joab return to after numbering Israel?**
**Answer:** To Jerusalem.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 21:4
