# 1 Chronicles 14 Study Questions (KJV)
---
### 1 Chronicles 14:1
**Q1. Who sent messengers to David?**
**Answer:** Hiram king of Tyre.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 14:1

**Q2. What did Hiram send besides messengers?**
**Answer:** Timber of cedars, with masons and carpenters.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 14:1

**Q3. Why did Hiram send masons and carpenters?**
**Answer:** To build him an house.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 14:1

### 1 Chronicles 14:2
**Q1. What did David perceive regarding his kingship?**
**Answer:** That the Lord had confirmed him king over Israel.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 14:2

**Q2. Why was David's kingdom lifted up on high?**

**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 14:2

**Q3. Who had confirmed David as king over Israel?**

**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 14:2
