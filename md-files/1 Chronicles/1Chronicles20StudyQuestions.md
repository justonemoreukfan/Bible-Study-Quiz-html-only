# 1 Chronicles 20 Study Questions (KJV)
---
### 1 Chronicles 20:1
**Q1. When did Joab lead forth the power of the army to waste the country of the children of Ammon?**
**Answer:** After the year was expired, at the time that kings go out to battle.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:1

**Q2. Which city did Joab besiege and destroy?**
**Answer:** Rabbah.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:1

**Q3. Where did David tarry while Joab smote Rabbah?**
**Answer:** At Jerusalem.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:1

### 1 Chronicles 20:2
**Q1. What did David take from off the head of the king of Rabbah?**
**Answer:** The crown.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:2

**Q2. How much did the crown weigh?**
**Answer:** A talent of gold.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:2

**Q3. What else was in the crown besides gold?**
**Answer:** Precious stones.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:2

### 1 Chronicles 20:3
**Q1. How did David treat the people that were in the city?**
**Answer:** He brought them out and cut them with saws, and with harrows of iron, and with axes.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:3

**Q2. With what did David cut the people of the city?**
**Answer:** With saws, and with harrows of iron, and with axes.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:3

**Q3. How did David deal with all the cities of the children of Ammon?**
**Answer:** Even so dealt David with all the cities of the children of Ammon.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:3

### 1 Chronicles 20:4
**Q1. Where did war arise with the Philistines after the Ammonites were dealt with?**
**Answer:** At Gezer.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:4

**Q2. Who slew Sippai, that was of the children of the giant?**
**Answer:** Sibbechai the Hushathite.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:4

**Q3. What happened to the Philistines at Gezer?**
**Answer:** They were subdued.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:4

### 1 Chronicles 20:5
**Q1. Who slew Lahmi the brother of Goliath the Gittite?**
**Answer:** Elhanan the son of Jair.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:5

**Q2. Who was Lahmi?**
**Answer:** The brother of Goliath the Gittite.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:5

**Q3. What was the spear staff of Lahmi like?**
**Answer:** Like a weaver's beam.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:5

### 1 Chronicles 20:6
**Q1. Where was there war again with the Philistines?**
**Answer:** At Gath.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:6

**Q2. What was notable about the man of great stature found at Gath?**
**Answer:** His fingers and toes were four and twenty, six on each hand, and six on each foot.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:6

**Q3. Who was the man of great stature the son of?**
**Answer:** The son of the giant.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:6

### 1 Chronicles 20:7
**Q1. Who slew the man of great stature when he defied Israel?**
**Answer:** Jonathan the son of Shimea David's brother.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:7

**Q2. Who defied Israel at Gath?**
**Answer:** A man of great stature who was the son of the giant.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:7

**Q3. Who was Jonathan in relation to David?**
**Answer:** David's brother.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:7

### 1 Chronicles 20:8
**Q1. Who were born unto the giant in Gath?**
**Answer:** Children of the giant.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:8

**Q2. By whose hand did the children of the giant fall?**
**Answer:** By the hand of David, and by the hand of his servants.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:8

**Q3. Where were these children of the giant born?**
**Answer:** In Gath.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 20:8
