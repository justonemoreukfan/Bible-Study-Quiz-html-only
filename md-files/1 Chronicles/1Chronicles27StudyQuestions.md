# 1 Chronicles 27 Study Questions (KJV)

### 1 Chronicles 27:1
**Q1. How many people were in every course of the chief fathers, captains, and officers that served the king?**
**Answer:** Twenty and four thousand.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 27:1

**Q2. How often did these courses come in and go out?**
**Answer:** Month by month throughout all the months of the year.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 27:1

**Q3. Who were the members of these courses?**
**Answer:** The chief fathers and captains of thousands and hundreds, and their officers.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 27:1
