# 1 Chronicles 22 Study Questions (KJV)
---
### 1 Chronicles 22:1
**Q1. What did David call the place he had chosen?**
**Answer:** The house of the Lord God.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:1

**Q2. What was the altar of the burnt offering for?**
**Answer:** For Israel.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:1

**Q3. What did David identify as the altar of the burnt offering?**
**Answer:** The altar of the burnt offering for Israel.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:1

### 1 Chronicles 22:2
**Q1. Who did David command to gather together?**
**Answer:** The strangers that were in the land of Israel.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:2

**Q2. What were the masons set to do?**
**Answer:** To hew wrought stones to build the house of God.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:2

**Q3. Why were strangers gathered?**
**Answer:** To set masons to hew wrought stones to build the house of God.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:2

### 1 Chronicles 22:3
**Q1. What did David prepare in abundance for the nails for the doors of the gates and for the joinings?**
**Answer:** Iron.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:3

**Q2. What else did David prepare in abundance without weight?**
**Answer:** Brass.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:3

**Q3. What was the iron used for?**
**Answer:** For the nails for the doors of the gates, and for the joinings.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:3

### 1 Chronicles 22:4
**Q1. What did David prepare in abundance?**
**Answer:** Cedar trees.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:4

**Q2. Who brought much cedar wood to David?**
**Answer:** The Zidonians and they of Tyre.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:4

**Q3. What wood was brought by the Zidonians and those of Tyre?**
**Answer:** Cedar wood.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:4

### 1 Chronicles 22:5
**Q1. Why did David feel he must make preparation for the house of the Lord?**
**Answer:** Because Solomon his son is young and tender, and the house that is to be builded for the Lord must be exceeding magnifical.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:5

**Q2. How did David describe the house to be built for the Lord?**
**Answer:** Exceeding magnifical, of fame and of glory throughout all countries.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:5

**Q3. When did David prepare abundantly?**
**Answer:** Before his death.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:5

### 1 Chronicles 22:6
**Q1. Whom did David call and charge to build an house for the Lord God of Israel?**
**Answer:** Solomon his son.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:6

**Q2. What was Solomon charged to build?**
**Answer:** An house for the Lord God of Israel.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:6

**Q3. Who was charged to build the house?**
**Answer:** Solomon.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:6

### 1 Chronicles 22:7
**Q1. What had been in David's mind to build for the name of the Lord his God?**
**Answer:** An house.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:7

**Q2. What did David want to build?**
**Answer:** An house unto the name of the Lord my God.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:7

**Q3. For whom did David want to build the house?**
**Answer:** For the name of the Lord his God.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:7

### 1 Chronicles 22:8
**Q1. Why did the Lord tell David he should not build an house unto His name?**
**Answer:** Because thou hast shed blood abundantly, and hast made great wars.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:8

**Q2. What did the Lord say David had done upon the earth in His sight?**
**Answer:** Shed much blood upon the earth.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:8

**Q3. What was the reason for the restriction on building the house?**
**Answer:** Because David had shed blood abundantly and made great wars.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:8

### 1 Chronicles 22:9
**Q1. Who was born to David as a man of rest?**
**Answer:** A son.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:9

**Q2. What did the Lord promise to give this son from all his enemies round about?**
**Answer:** Rest.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:9

**Q3. What was the name of this son, and what would the Lord give to Israel in his days?**
**Answer:** His name shall be Solomon, and I will give peace and quietness unto Israel in his days.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:9

### 1 Chronicles 22:10
**Q1. What would Solomon build for the Lord's name?**
**Answer:** An house.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:10

**Q2. What did the Lord say regarding the relationship between the Lord and Solomon?**
**Answer:** He shall be my son, and I will be his father.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:10

**Q3. What would be established for ever over Israel?**
**Answer:** The throne of his kingdom.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:10

### 1 Chronicles 22:11
**Q1. What did David say to Solomon about the Lord's presence?**
**Answer:** Now, my son, the Lord be with thee.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:11

**Q2. What did David encourage Solomon to do?**
**Answer:** Prosper thou, and build the house of the Lord thy God, as he hath said of thee.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:11

**Q3. For whom was the house to be built?**
**Answer:** For the Lord thy God.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:11

### 1 Chronicles 22:12
**Q1. What did David ask the Lord to give Solomon?**
**Answer:** Wisdom and understanding.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:12

**Q2. What did David ask the Lord to give Solomon regarding Israel?**
**Answer:** Give thee charge concerning Israel.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:12

**Q3. What was Solomon's responsibility regarding the law of the Lord?**
**Answer:** That thou mayest keep the law of the Lord thy God.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:12

### 1 Chronicles 22:13
**Q1. Under what condition would Solomon prosper?**
**Answer:** If thou takest heed to fulfil the statutes and judgments which the Lord charged Moses with concerning Israel.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:13

**Q2. What did the Lord charge Moses with concerning Israel?**
**Answer:** Statutes and judgments.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:13

**Q3. What did David tell Solomon to be?**
**Answer:** Be strong, and of good courage; dread not, nor be dismayed.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:13

### 1 Chronicles 22:14
**Q1. What did David prepare in his trouble for the house of the Lord?**
**Answer:** An hundred thousand talents of gold, and a thousand thousand talents of silver.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:14

**Q2. How much gold was prepared?**
**Answer:** An hundred thousand talents.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:14

**Q3. How much silver was prepared?**
**Answer:** A thousand thousand talents.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:14

### 1 Chronicles 22:15
**Q1. Who was available in abundance to help build the house?**
**Answer:** Workmen.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:15

**Q2. What kinds of workmen were prepared?**
**Answer:** Hewers and workers of stone and timber, and all manner of cunning men for every manner of work.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:15

**Q3. What was the role of the hewers and workers?**
**Answer:** To work stone and timber.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:15

### 1 Chronicles 22:16
**Q1. What did David say about the amount of gold, silver, brass, and iron?**

**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:16

**Q2. What did David urge Solomon to do?**
**Answer:** Arise therefore, and be doing, and the Lord be with thee.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:16

**Q3. Who was told to arise and be doing?**
**Answer:** Solomon.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:16

**Q2. Who was commanded to help Solomon?**
**Answer:** All the princes of Israel.

**Answer:** Rest.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:18

### 1 Chronicles 22:19
**Q1. What did David urge the princes to set their heart and soul to do?**
**Answer:** To seek the Lord your God.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:19

**Q2. What were the princes told to build?**
**Answer:** The sanctuary of the Lord God.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:19

**Q3. What should be brought into the house that is to be built?**
**Answer:** The ark of the covenant of the Lord, and the holy vessels of God.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 22:19
