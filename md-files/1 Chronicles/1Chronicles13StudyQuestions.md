# 1 Chronicles 13 Study Questions (KJV)
---
### 1 Chronicles 13:1
**Q1. Who did David consult with?**
**Answer:** The captains of thousands and hundreds, and with every leader.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:1

**Q2. With whom did David consult before speaking to the congregation?**
**Answer:** The captains of thousands and hundreds, and with every leader.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:1

**Q3. Which leaders did David consult with?**
**Answer:** Captains of thousands and hundreds, and every leader.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:1

### 1 Chronicles 13:2
**Q1. What did David say to the congregation of Israel?**
**Answer:** If it seem good unto you, and that it be of the Lord our God, let us send abroad unto our brethren every where, that are left in all the land of Israel, and with them also to the priests and Levites which are in their cities and suburbs, that they may gather themselves unto us.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:2

**Q2. To whom did David suggest sending abroad?**
**Answer:** Unto our brethren every where, that are left in all the land of Israel, and with them also to the priests and Levites which are in their cities and suburbs.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:2

**Q3. What was the purpose of sending abroad to the brethren, priests, and Levites?**
**Answer:** That they may gather themselves unto us.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:2

### 1 Chronicles 13:3
**Q1. What did David want to bring again to them?**
**Answer:** The ark of our God.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:3

**Q2. Why did David want to bring the ark back?**
**Answer:** For we enquired not at it in the days of Saul.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:3

**Q3. In whose days was it that they did not enquire at the ark?**
**Answer:** In the days of Saul.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:3

### 1 Chronicles 13:4
**Q1. What did the congregation say regarding David's proposal?**
**Answer:** That they would do so.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:4

**Q2. Why did the congregation agree to do so?**
**Answer:** For the thing was right in the eyes of all the people.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:4

**Q3. How was the proposal viewed by all the people?**
**Answer:** The thing was right in their eyes.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:4

### 1 Chronicles 13:5
**Q1. Whom did David gather together to bring the ark of God from Kirjathjearim?**
**Answer:** All Israel together.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:5

**Q2. From where to where did David gather Israel?**
**Answer:** From Shihor of Egypt even unto the entering of Hemath.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:5

**Q3. From which city were they bringing the ark of God?**
**Answer:** Kirjathjearim.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:5

### 1 Chronicles 13:6
**Q1. Where did David and all Israel go to bring up the ark of God?**
**Answer:** To Baalah, that is, to Kirjathjearim, which belonged to Judah.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:6

**Q2. To which tribe did Kirjathjearim belong?**
**Answer:** Judah.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:6

**Q3. How is the ark described in this verse?**
**Answer:** The ark of God the Lord, that dwelleth between the cherubims, whose name is called on it.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:6

### 1 Chronicles 13:7
**Q1. How was the ark of God carried out of the house of Abinadab?**
**Answer:** In a new cart.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:7

**Q2. From whose house was the ark carried?**
**Answer:** The house of Abinadab.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:7

**Q3. Who drave the cart?**
**Answer:** Uzza and Ahio.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:7

### 1 Chronicles 13:8
**Q1. How did David and all Israel play before God?**
**Answer:** With all their might, and with singing, and with harps, and with psalteries, and with timbrels, and with cymbals, and with trumpets.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:8

**Q2. What instruments were used to play before God?**
**Answer:** Harps, psalteries, timbrels, cymbals, and trumpets.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:8

**Q3. Besides music, how did they play before God?**
**Answer:** With all their might, and with singing.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:8

### 1 Chronicles 13:9
**Q1. Where did the oxen stumble?**
**Answer:** At the threshingfloor of Chidon.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:9

**Q2. What did Uzza do when the oxen stumbled?**
**Answer:** Uzza put forth his hand to hold the ark.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:9

**Q3. Why did Uzza put forth his hand to hold the ark?**
**Answer:** For the oxen stumbled.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:9

### 1 Chronicles 13:10
**Q1. How did the Lord react when Uzza put his hand to the ark?**
**Answer:** The anger of the Lord was kindled against Uzza.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:10

**Q2. What did the Lord do to Uzza?**
**Answer:** He smote him.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:10

**Q3. What happened to Uzza after the Lord smote him?**
**Answer:** There he died before God.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:10

### 1 Chronicles 13:11
**Q1. Why was David displeased?**
**Answer:** Because the Lord had made a breach upon Uzza.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:11

**Q2. What is that place called to this day?**
**Answer:** Perezuzza.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:11

**Q3. What happened to Uzza that caused the place to be named Perezuzza?**
**Answer:** The Lord made a breach upon him.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:11

### 1 Chronicles 13:12
**Q1. How did David feel that day?**
**Answer:** David was afraid of God.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:12

**Q2. What question did David ask himself?**
**Answer:** How shall I bring the ark of God home to me?
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:12

**Q3. Why was David afraid?**
**Answer:** Because of what happened to Uzza.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:12

### 1 Chronicles 13:13
**Q1. Did David bring the ark home to the city of David?**
**Answer:** No, he brought it not home to himself.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:13

**Q2. Where did David carry the ark instead?**
**Answer:** Into the house of Obededom the Gittite.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:13

**Q3. Who was the owner of the house where the ark was placed?**
**Answer:** Obededom the Gittite.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:13

### 1 Chronicles 13:14
**Q1. How long did the ark of God remain with the family of Obededom?**
**Answer:** Three months.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:14

**Q2. What did the Lord do for the house of Obededom?**
**Answer:** The Lord blessed the house of Obededom, and all that he had.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:14

**Q3. Where did the ark remain for three months?**
**Answer:** In the house of Obededom.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 13:14
