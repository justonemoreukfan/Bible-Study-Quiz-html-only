# 1 Chronicles 16 Study Questions (KJV)
---
### 1 Chronicles 16:1
**Q1. Where did they set the ark of God?**
**Answer:** In the midst of the tent that David had pitched for it.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 16:1

**Q2. What did they offer before God?**
**Answer:** Burnt sacrifices and peace offerings.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 16:1

**Q3. What was pitched for the ark of God?**
**Answer:** A tent.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 16:1

### 1 Chronicles 16:2
**Q1. What did David do after offering the burnt and peace offerings?**
**Answer:** He blessed the people in the name of the Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 16:2

**Q2. In whose name did David bless the people?**
**Answer:** In the name of the Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 16:2

**Q3. When did David bless the people?**
**Answer:** When he had made an end of offering the burnt offerings and the peace offerings.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 16:2

### 1 Chronicles 16:3
**Q1. What did David deal to every one of Israel, both man and woman?**
**Answer:** A loaf of bread, and a good piece of flesh, and a flagon of wine.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 16:3

**Q2. What were the three items David gave to every person?**
**Answer:** A loaf of bread, a good piece of flesh, and a flagon of wine.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 16:3

**Q3. Who received these gifts from David?**
**Answer:** Every one of Israel, both man and woman.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 16:3

### 1 Chronicles 16:4
**Q1. Whom did David appoint to minister before the ark of the Lord?**
**Answer:** Certain of the Levites.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 16:4

**Q2. Besides ministering, what were the appointed Levites to do?**
**Answer:** To record, and to thank and praise the Lord God of Israel.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 16:4

**Q3. Who were the Levites ministering before?**
**Answer:** The ark of the Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 16:4

### 1 Chronicles 16:5
**Q1. Who was the chief among the appointed Levites?**
**Answer:** Asaph.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 16:5

**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 16:5

**Q3. What instruments did Jeiel use?**
**Answer:** Psalteries and harps.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 16:5

### 1 Chronicles 16:6
