# 1 Chronicles 25 Study Questions (KJV)
---
### 1 Chronicles 25:1
**Q1. Who separated the sons of Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun for service?**
**Answer:** David and the captains of the host.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:1

**Q2. What were they to do with harps, psalteries, and cymbals?**
**Answer:** Prophesy.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:1

**Q3. What instruments did they use to prophesy?**
**Answer:** Harps, psalteries, and cymbals.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:1

### 1 Chronicles 25:2
**Q1. Who were the sons of Asaph under the hands of Asaph?**
**Answer:** Zaccur, and Joseph, and Nethaniah, and Asarelah.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:2

**Q2. What did the sons of Asaph do according to the order of the king?**
**Answer:** They prophesied.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:2

**Q3. Who prophesied according to the order of the king?**
**Answer:** The sons of Asaph.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:2

### 1 Chronicles 25:3
**Q1. Who were the sons of Jeduthun?**
**Answer:** Gedaliah, and Zeri, and Jeshaiah, Hashabiah, and Mattithiah.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:3

**Q2. How many sons of Jeduthun are listed?**
**Answer:** Six.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:3

**Q3. What did the sons of Jeduthun do with a harp?**
**Answer:** Prophesied, to give thanks and to praise the Lord.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:3

### 1 Chronicles 25:4
**Q1. Who were the sons of Heman?**
**Answer:** Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, and Jerimoth, Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, and Romamtiezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, and Mahazioth.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:4

**Q2. How many sons of Heman are listed?**
**Answer:** Fourteen.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:4

**Q3. Who are some of the sons of Heman?**
**Answer:** Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, and Jerimoth.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:4

### 1 Chronicles 25:5
**Q1. Who was the king's seer in the words of God?**
**Answer:** Heman.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:5

**Q2. What was Heman to do?**
**Answer:** To lift up the horn.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:5

**Q3. How many sons and daughters did God give to Heman?**
**Answer:** Fourteen sons and three daughters.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:5

### 1 Chronicles 25:6
**Q1. Under whose hands were the sons of Heman for song in the house of the Lord?**
**Answer:** Their father.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:6

**Q2. What instruments did they use for song in the house of the Lord?**
**Answer:** Cymbals, psalteries, and harps.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:6

**Q3. According to whose order were they serving?**
**Answer:** The king's order to Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:6

### 1 Chronicles 25:7
**Q1. How many people were instructed in the songs of the Lord and were cunning?**
**Answer:** Two hundred fourscore and eight.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:7

**Q2. Who were the people instructed in the songs of the Lord?**
**Answer:** The singers and their brethren.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:7

**Q3. What was the total number of cunning singers?**
**Answer:** Two hundred fourscore and eight.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:7

### 1 Chronicles 25:8
**Q1. How did the singers cast lots?**
**Answer:** Ward against ward, as well the small as the great, the teacher as the scholar.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:8

**Q2. Who cast lots?**
**Answer:** The singers.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:8

**Q3. Between whom were lots cast?**
**Answer:** The small, the great, the teacher, and the scholar.
**Verse:** 1 Chronicles 25:8
