# Zephaniah 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Zephaniah 2:1
**Q1. According to Zephaniah 2:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Gather yourselves together.  
**Verse:** Zephaniah 2:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Zephaniah 2:1?**  
**Answer:** gather together.  
**Verse:** Zephaniah 2:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Zephaniah 2:1?**  
**Answer:** O nation not desired.  
**Verse:** Zephaniah 2:1

### Zephaniah 2:2
**Q4. According to Zephaniah 2:2, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Before the decree bring forth.  
**Verse:** Zephaniah 2:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Zephaniah 2:2?**

**Verse:** Zephaniah 2:2

**Verse:** Zephaniah 2:4

**Q11. What additional detail is declared in Zephaniah 2:4?**

**Q25. What is stated in the first clause of Zephaniah 2:9?**

**Verse:** Zephaniah 2:9

**Q26. What additional detail is declared in Zephaniah 2:9?**  
**Answer:** the residue of my people shall spoil them, and the remnant of my people shall possess them.  
**Verse:** Zephaniah 2:9

**Q27. According to Zephaniah 2:9, what is the full truth recorded?**

**Q45. According to Zephaniah 2:15, what is the full truth recorded?**
