# Zephaniah 3 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Zephaniah 3:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Zephaniah 3:1 state?**  
**Answer:** Woe to her that is filthy and polluted.  
**Verse:** Zephaniah 3:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Zephaniah 3:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** to the oppressing city!  
**Verse:** Zephaniah 3:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Zephaniah 3:1?**  
**Answer:** Woe to her that is filthy and polluted, to the oppressing city!  
**Verse:** Zephaniah 3:1

---

### Zephaniah 3:2

**Verse:** Zephaniah 3:2
