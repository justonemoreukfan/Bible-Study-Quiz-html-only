# Zephaniah 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Zephaniah 1:1
**Q1. According to Zephaniah 1:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** The word of the LORD which came unto Zephaniah the son of Cushi.  
**Verse:** Zephaniah 1:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Zephaniah 1:1?**  
**Answer:** the son of Gedaliah.  
**Verse:** Zephaniah 1:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Zephaniah 1:1?**  
**Answer:** the son of Amariah, the son of Hizkiah, in the days of Josiah the son of Amon, king of Judah.  
**Verse:** Zephaniah 1:1
