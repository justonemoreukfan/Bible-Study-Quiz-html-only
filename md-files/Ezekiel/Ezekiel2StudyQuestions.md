# Ezekiel 2 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezekiel 2:1
**Q1. According to Ezekiel 2:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And he said unto me.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Ezekiel 2:1?**  
**Answer:** Son of man.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Ezekiel 2:1?**  
**Answer:** stand upon thy feet, and I will speak unto thee.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:1

---

### Ezekiel 2:2
**Q4. According to Ezekiel 2:2, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And the spirit entered into me when he spake unto me.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Ezekiel 2:2?**  
**Answer:** and set me upon my feet.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:2

**Q6. What further truth is given in Ezekiel 2:2?**  
**Answer:** that I heard him that spake unto me.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:2

---

### Ezekiel 2:3
**Q7. What is stated in the first clause of Ezekiel 2:3?**  
**Answer:** And he said unto me, Son of man, I send thee to the children of Israel, to a rebellious nation that hath rebelled against me.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:3

**Q8. What additional detail is declared in Ezekiel 2:3?**  
**Answer:** they and their fathers have transgressed against me, even unto this very day.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:3

**Q9. According to Ezekiel 2:3, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And he said unto me, Son of man, I send thee to the children of Israel, to a rebellious nation that hath rebelled against me: they and their fathers have transgressed against me, even unto this very day.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:3

---

### Ezekiel 2:4
**Q10. What is stated in the first clause of Ezekiel 2:4?**  
**Answer:** For they are impudent children and stiffhearted. I do send thee unto them.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:4

**Q11. What additional detail is declared in Ezekiel 2:4?**  
**Answer:** and thou shalt say unto them, Thus saith the Lord GOD.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:4

**Q12. According to Ezekiel 2:4, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** For they are impudent children and stiffhearted. I do send thee unto them; and thou shalt say unto them, Thus saith the Lord GOD.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:4

---

### Ezekiel 2:5
**Q13. According to Ezekiel 2:5, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And they.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:5

**Q14. What is declared next in Ezekiel 2:5?**  
**Answer:** whether they will hear.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:5

**Q15. What further truth is given in Ezekiel 2:5?**  
**Answer:** or whether they will forbear, (for they are a rebellious house), yet shall know that there hath been a prophet among them.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:5

---

### Ezekiel 2:6
**Q16. What is stated in the first clause of Ezekiel 2:6?**  
**Answer:** And thou, son of man, be not afraid of them, neither be afraid of their words, though briers and thorns be with thee, and thou dost dwell among scorpions.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:6

**Q17. What additional detail is declared in Ezekiel 2:6?**  
**Answer:** be not afraid of their words, nor be dismayed at their looks, though they be a rebellious house.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:6

**Q18. According to Ezekiel 2:6, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And thou, son of man, be not afraid of them, neither be afraid of their words, though briers and thorns be with thee, and thou dost dwell among scorpions: be not afraid of their words, nor be dismayed at their looks, though they be a rebellious house.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:6

### Ezekiel 2:7
**Q19. What is stated in the first clause of Ezekiel 2:7?**  
**Answer:** And thou shalt speak my words unto them, whether they will hear, or whether they will forbear.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:7

**Q20. What additional detail is declared in Ezekiel 2:7?**  
**Answer:** for they are most rebellious.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:7

**Q21. According to Ezekiel 2:7, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And thou shalt speak my words unto them, whether they will hear, or whether they will forbear: for they are most rebellious.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:7

### Ezekiel 2:8
**Q22. What is stated in the opening of Ezekiel 2:8?**  
**Answer:** But thou, son of man, hear what I say unto thee.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:8

**Q23. What is declared next in Ezekiel 2:8?**  
**Answer:** Be not thou rebellious like that rebellious house.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:8

**Q24. What further truth is recorded in Ezekiel 2:8?**  
**Answer:** open thy mouth, and eat that I give thee.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:8

### Ezekiel 2:10
**Q28. What is stated in the opening of Ezekiel 2:10?**  
**Answer:** And he spread it before me.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:10

**Q29. What is declared next in Ezekiel 2:10?**  
**Answer:** and it was written within and without.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:10

**Q30. What further truth is recorded in Ezekiel 2:10?**  
**Answer:** and there was written therein lamentations, and mourning, and woe.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 2:10
