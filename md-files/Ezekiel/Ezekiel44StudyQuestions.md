# Ezekiel 44 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezekiel 44:1

**Q1. To which gate of the outward sanctuary was the man brought back?**  
**Answer:** The gate which looketh toward the east.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:1

**Q2. What was the condition of the gate toward the east?**  
**Answer:** It was shut.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:1

**Q3. Who brought the man back to the gate?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:1

---

### Ezekiel 44:2

**Q1. What did the Lord say concerning this gate?**  
**Answer:** It shall be shut, it shall not be opened, and no man shall enter in by it.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:2

**Q2. Why shall the gate be shut?**  
**Answer:** Because the Lord, the God of Israel, hath entered in by it.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:2

**Q3. Therefore, what shall be done with the gate?**  
**Answer:** It shall be shut.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:2

---

### Ezekiel 44:3

**Q1. Who alone shall sit in the gate?**  
**Answer:** The prince.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:3

**Q2. For what purpose shall the prince sit in it?**  
**Answer:** To eat bread before the Lord.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:3

**Q3. By which way shall the prince enter and go out?**  
**Answer:** He shall enter by the way of the porch of that gate, and shall go out by the way of the same.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:3

---

### Ezekiel 44:4

**Q1. By way of which gate did the man bring the author?**  
**Answer:** The north gate before the house.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:4

**Q2. What did the author see when he looked?**  
**Answer:** Behold, the glory of the Lord filled the house of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:4

**Q3. What did the author do upon seeing this?**  
**Answer:** He fell upon his face.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:4

---

### Ezekiel 44:5

**Q1. What did the Lord say unto the author?**  
**Answer:** Son of man, mark well, and behold with thine eyes, and hear with thine ears all that I say unto thee concerning all the ordinances of the house of the Lord, and all the laws thereof.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:5

**Q2. What should the author mark well and behold?**  
**Answer:** All the ordinances of the house of the Lord, and all the laws thereof.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:5

**Q3. Concerning what house were these ordinances and laws?**  
**Answer:** The house of the Lord.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:5

---

### Ezekiel 44:6

**Q1. To whom shall the author say what the Lord commanded?**  
**Answer:** The rebellious house, even to the house of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:6

**Q2. What question does the Lord ask the house of Israel?**  
**Answer:** Let it suffice you of all your abominations?  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:6

**Q3. Whose house is addressed as rebellious?**  
**Answer:** The house of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:6

---

### Ezekiel 44:7

**Q1. In what manner have they brought strangers into the sanctuary?**  
**Answer:** Uncircumcised in heart, and uncircumcised in flesh.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:7

**Q2. Where were these strangers placed?**  
**Answer:** In my sanctuary, to pollute it, even my house.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:7

**Q3. When they offered my bread, what else did they offer?**  
**Answer:** The fat and the blood.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:7

---

### Ezekiel 44:8

**Q1. What did the people fail to keep regarding mine holy things?**  
**Answer:** Ye have not kept the charge of mine holy things.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:8

**Q2. What did they set in my sanctuary instead?**  
**Answer:** Set keepers of my charge in my sanctuary for yourselves.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:8

**Q3. For whom did they set keepers of the charge?**  
**Answer:** For yourselves.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:8

---

### Ezekiel 44:9

**Q1. Who shall not enter into my sanctuary?**  
**Answer:** No stranger, uncircumcised in heart, nor uncircumcised in flesh.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:9

**Q2. Who among the children of men is specified as being excluded?**  
**Answer:** Any stranger that is among the children of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:9

**Q3. What condition of the flesh and heart prevents entry?**  
**Answer:** Uncircumcised in heart, nor uncircumcised in flesh.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:9

---

### Ezekiel 44:10

**Q1. Who went far away from me?**  
**Answer:** The Levites that are gone away far from me.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:10

**Q2. When did the Levites go far away from God?**  
**Answer:** When Israel went astray.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:10

**Q3. Who went astray from God after their idols?**  
**Answer:** Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:10

---

### Ezekiel 44:11

**Q1. What shall the Levites be in my sanctuary?**  
**Answer:** Ministers.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:11

**Q2. Having charge at which places?**  
**Answer:** The gates of the house, and ministering to the house.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:11

**Q3. What shall they slay for the people?**  
**Answer:** The burnt offering and the sacrifice.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:11

---

### Ezekiel 44:12

**Q1. Why did the Levites minister before the people's idols?**  
**Answer:** Because they ministered unto them before their idols.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:12

**Q2. What was caused to the house of Israel by this?**  
**Answer:** A stumblingblock of iniquity unto the house of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:12

**Q3. Therefore, what did the Lord lift up against them?**  
**Answer:** Mine hand against them.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:12

---

### Ezekiel 44:13

**Q1. What shall the Levites not come near unto me to do?**  
**Answer:** Do the office of a priest unto me.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:13

**Q2. What else shall they not come near to?**  
**Answer:** Any of my holy things, in the most holy place.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:13

**Q3. What shall they bear because of this?**  
**Answer:** Their shame, and their abominations which they have committed.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:13

---

### Ezekiel 44:14

**Q1. What will the Lord make the Levites keepers of?**  
**Answer:** The charge of the house.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:14

**Q2. For what purpose shall they keep the charge of the house?**  
**Answer:** For all the service thereof, and for all that shall be done therein.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:14

**Q3. Who are the Levites mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** But I will make them keepers of the charge of the house.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:14

---

### Ezekiel 44:15

**Q1. Which priests are specified to keep the charge of my sanctuary?**  
**Answer:** The priests the Levites, the sons of Zadok.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:15

**Q2. When did the children of Israel go astray from me, what did these priests do?**  
**Answer:** They kept the charge of my sanctuary when the children of Israel went astray from me.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:15

**Q3. What shall they come near to me to do?**  
**Answer:** Minister unto me, and they shall stand before me to offer unto me the fat and the blood.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:15

---

**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:16

**Q2. Whose table shall they come near to?**

**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:16

**Answer:** My charge.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:16

### Ezekiel 44:17

**Q1. What gates shall they enter when they enter the gates of the inner court?**  
**Answer:** The gates of the inner court.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:17

**Q2. What kind of garments shall they be clothed with?**

**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:17

**Answer:** The utter court to the people.

**Q2. What must they take off before going forth?**  
**Answer:** Their garments wherein they ministered.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:19

**Answer:** In the holy chambers.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:19

**Answer:** Neither shall they shave their heads.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:20

**Q2. What shall they not suffer to grow long?**  
**Answer:** Nor suffer their locks to grow long.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:20

**Answer:** They shall only poll their heads.

**Answer:** When they enter into the inner court.

**Verse:** Ezekiel 44:22

**Answer:** Maidens of the seed of the house of Israel, or a widow that had a priest before.

**Answer:** A widow that had a priest before.
