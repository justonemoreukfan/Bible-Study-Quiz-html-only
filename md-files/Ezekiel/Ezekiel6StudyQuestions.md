**Verse:** Ezekiel 6:2

### Ezekiel 6:3
**Q7. What is stated in the opening of Ezekiel 6:3?**  
**Answer:** And say, Ye mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord GOD.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 6:3

**Q8. What is declared next in Ezekiel 6:3?**  
**Answer:** Thus saith the Lord GOD to the mountains, and to the hills, to the rivers, and to the valleys.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 6:3

**Q9. What further truth is recorded in Ezekiel 6:3?**  
**Answer:** Behold, I, even I, will bring a sword upon you, and I will destroy your high places.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 6:3

---

### Ezekiel 6:4
**Q10. What is stated in the first clause of Ezekiel 6:4?**  
**Answer:** And your altars shall be desolate, and your images shall be broken.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 6:4

**Q11. What additional detail is declared in Ezekiel 6:4?**  
**Answer:** and I will cast down your slain men before your idols.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 6:4

**Q12. According to Ezekiel 6:4, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And your altars shall be desolate, and your images shall be broken: and I will cast down your slain men before your idols.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 6:4

### Ezekiel 6:6
**Q16. What is stated in the first clause of Ezekiel 6:6?**  
**Answer:** In all your dwellingplaces the cities shall be laid waste, and the high places shall be desolate.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 6:6

**Q17. What additional detail is declared in Ezekiel 6:6?**  
**Answer:** that your altars may be laid waste and made desolate, and your idols may be broken and cease, and your images may be cut down, and your works may be abolished.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 6:6

**Q18. According to Ezekiel 6:6, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** In all your dwellingplaces the cities shall be laid waste, and the high places shall be desolate; that your altars may be laid waste and made desolate, and your idols may be broken and cease, and your images may be cut down, and your works may be abolished.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 6:6
