# Ezekiel 45 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezekiel 45:1

**Q1. When ye shall divide by lot the land for inheritance, what shall ye offer unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** An oblation unto the LORD, an holy portion of the land.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:1

**Q2. How long shall the length of the holy portion of the land be?**  
**Answer:** Five and twenty thousand reeds.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:1

**Q3. How wide shall the holy portion of the land be?**  
**Answer:** Ten thousand.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:1

---

### Ezekiel 45:2

**Q1. Of this holy portion, how much shall be for the sanctuary in length and in breadth?**  
**Answer:** Five hundred by five hundred reeds, square round about.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:2

**Q2. How much space shall there be round about the sanctuary for the suburbs thereof?**  
**Answer:** Fifty cubits round about.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:2

**Q3. What shape shall the measure of five hundred by five hundred reeds be?**  
**Answer:** Square round about.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:2

---

### Ezekiel 45:3

**Q1. What measure shalt thou measure of this great area?**  
**Answer:** The length of five and twenty thousand, and the breadth of ten thousand.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:3

**Q2. What shall be in the area measured in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The sanctuary and the most holy place.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:3

**Q3. What shall be contained within this specific portion of the land?**  
**Answer:** The sanctuary.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:3

---

### Ezekiel 45:4

**Q1. Whose holy portion of the land shall this be?**  
**Answer:** The priests the ministers of the sanctuary.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:4

**Q2. For what purpose shall the priests come near to minister unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** To minister unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:4

**Q3. What shall this holy portion be a place for unto the priests?**  
**Answer:** A place for their houses, and an holy place for the sanctuary.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:4

---

### Ezekiel 45:5

**Q1. What shall the five and twenty thousand of length and ten thousand of breadth be for?**  
**Answer:** The Levites, the ministers of the house.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:5

**Q2. What shall the Levites have for themselves as a possession?**  
**Answer:** Twenty chambers.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:5

**Q3. Who are the Levites described as in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The ministers of the house.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:5

---

### Ezekiel 45:6

**Q1. What shall ye appoint alongside the oblation of the holy portion?**  
**Answer:** The possession of the city.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:6

**Q2. What shall the dimensions of the possession of the city be?**  
**Answer:** Five and twenty thousand long, and five thousand broad.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:6

**Q3. For whom shall the possession of the city be appointed?**  
**Answer:** The whole house of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:6

---

### Ezekiel 45:7

**Q1. What shall be on the one side and on the other side of the oblation of the holy portion, and of the possession of the city?**  
**Answer:** The prince's portion.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:7

**Q2. Where shall the prince's portion be located relative to the holy oblation and the city possession?**  
**Answer:** Before the oblation of the holy portion, and before the possession of the city.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:7

**Q3. How far shall the prince's portion extend to the west and to the east?**  
**Answer:** Over against the west side westward, and over against the east side eastward.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:7

---

### Ezekiel 45:8

**Q1. In the land, what shall be the prince's possession in Israel?**  
**Answer:** Land shall be his possession in Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:8

**Q2. What shall my princes no more do to my people?**  
**Answer:** Oppress my people.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:8

**Q3. To whom shall the rest of the land be given according to their tribes?**  
**Answer:** The house of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:8

### Ezekiel 45:15

**Q1. From out of the fat pastures of Israel, how many lambs out of the flock shall be given for a meat offering?**  
**Answer:** One lamb out of the two hundred.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:15

**Q2. What are these offerings to be used for regarding the people?**  
**Answer:** For a meat offering, and for a burnt offering, and for peace offerings.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:15

**Q3. What purpose shall these offerings serve for the people?**  
**Answer:** To make reconciliation for them.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:15

---

### Ezekiel 45:16

**Q1. Who in Israel shall give this oblation?**  
**Answer:** All the people of the land.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:16

**Q2. With whom shall the people of the land give this oblation?**  
**Answer:** The prince in Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:16

---

### Ezekiel 45:17

**Q1. What shall be the prince's part to give?**  
**Answer:** Burnt offerings, and meat offerings, and drink offerings.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:17

**Q2. During what specific times is the prince required to give these offerings?**  
**Answer:** In the feasts, and in the new moons, and in the sabbaths, in all solemnities of the house of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:17

**Q3. What shall the prince prepare to make reconciliation for the house of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The sin offering, and the meat offering, and the burnt offering, and the peace offerings.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 45:17
