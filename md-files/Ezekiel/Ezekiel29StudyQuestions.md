# Ezekiel 29 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezekiel 29:1
**Q1. According to Ezekiel 29:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** In the tenth year.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 29:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Ezekiel 29:1?**  
**Answer:** in the tenth month.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 29:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Ezekiel 29:1?**  
**Answer:** in the twelfth day of the month, the word of the LORD came unto me.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 29:1
