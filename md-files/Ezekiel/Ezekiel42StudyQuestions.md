# Ezekiel 42 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezekiel 42:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Ezekiel 42:1?**  
**Answer:** Then he brought me forth into the utter court, the way toward the north.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 42:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Ezekiel 42:1?**  
**Answer:** and he brought me into the chamber that was over against the separate place, and which was before the building toward the north.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 42:1

**Q3. According to Ezekiel 42:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Then he brought me forth into the utter court, the way toward the north: and he brought me into the chamber that was over against the separate place, and which was before the building toward the north.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 42:1
