# Ezekiel 8 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezekiel 8:1
**Q1. According to Ezekiel 8:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And it came to pass in the sixth year.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 8:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Ezekiel 8:1?**  
**Answer:** in the sixth month.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 8:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Ezekiel 8:1?**  
**Answer:** in the fifth day of the month, as I sat in mine house, and the elders of Judah sat before me, that the hand of the Lord GOD fell there upon me.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 8:1
