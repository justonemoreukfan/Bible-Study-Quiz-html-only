# Ezekiel 4 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezekiel 4:1
**Q1. According to Ezekiel 4:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Thou also.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 4:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Ezekiel 4:1?**  
**Answer:** son of man.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 4:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Ezekiel 4:1?**  
**Answer:** take thee a tile, and lay it before thee, and pourtray upon it the city, even Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 4:1

---

### Ezekiel 4:2
**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Ezekiel 4:2?**  
**Answer:** And lay siege against it, and build a fort against it, and cast a mount against it.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 4:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Ezekiel 4:2?**
