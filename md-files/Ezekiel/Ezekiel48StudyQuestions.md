# Ezekiel 48 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezekiel 48:1

**Q1. What is the name of the first tribe listed by the north coast in this chapter?**  
**Answer:** Dan.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 48:1

**Q2. By whose way is the portion of Dan described as going?**  
**Answer:** The coast of the way of Hethlon, as one goeth to Hamath.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 48:1

**Q3. What is at the north coast regarding the direction of the coast of Dan?**  
**Answer:** Hazar-enan, the border of Damascus northward, to the coast of Hamath.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 48:1
