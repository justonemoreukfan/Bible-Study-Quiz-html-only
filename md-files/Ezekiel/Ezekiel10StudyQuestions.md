# Ezekiel 10 Study Questions (KJV)

### Ezekiel 10:1
**Q1. According to Ezekiel 10:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Then I looked.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 10:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Ezekiel 10:1?**  
**Answer:** in the firmament that was above the head of the cherubims there appeared over them as it were a sapphire stone.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 10:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Ezekiel 10:1?**  
**Answer:** as the appearance of the likeness of a throne.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 10:1
