# Ezekiel 9 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezekiel 9:1
**Q1. According to Ezekiel 9:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** He cried also in mine ears with a loud voice.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 9:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Ezekiel 9:1?**  
**Answer:** Cause them that have charge over the city to draw near.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 9:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Ezekiel 9:1?**  
**Answer:** even every man with his destroying weapon in his hand.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 9:1

---

### Ezekiel 9:2
**Q4. What is stated in the opening of Ezekiel 9:2?**  
**Answer:** And, behold, six men came from the way of the higher gate, which lieth toward the north, and every man a slaughter weapon in his hand.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 9:2

**Q5. What is declared next in Ezekiel 9:2?**  
**Answer:** and one man among them was clothed with linen, with a writer’s inkhorn by his side.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 9:2

**Q6. What further truth is recorded in Ezekiel 9:2?**  
**Answer:** and they went in, and stood beside the brasen altar.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 9:2

### Ezekiel 9:4
**Q10. Who spake unto him in Ezekiel 9:4?**  
**Answer:** LORD.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 9:4

**Q11. What did LORD declare or command in Ezekiel 9:4?**  
**Answer:** Go through the midst of the city, through the midst of Jerusalem, and set a mark upon the foreheads of the men that sigh and that cry for all the abominations that be done in the midst thereof.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 9:4

**Q12. What is recorded in Ezekiel 9:4?**  
**Answer:** And the LORD said unto him, Go through the midst of the city, through the midst of Jerusalem, and set a mark upon the foreheads of the men that sigh and that cry for all the abominations that be done in the midst thereof.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 9:4

### Ezekiel 9:5
**Q13. What is stated in the first clause of Ezekiel 9:5?**  
**Answer:** And to the others he said in mine hearing, Go ye after him through the city, and smite.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 9:5

**Q14. What additional detail is declared in Ezekiel 9:5?**  
**Answer:** let not your eye spare, neither have ye pity.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 9:5

**Q15. According to Ezekiel 9:5, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And to the others he said in mine hearing, Go ye after him through the city, and smite: let not your eye spare, neither have ye pity:.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 9:5
