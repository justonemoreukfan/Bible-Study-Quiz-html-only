# Ezekiel 40 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezekiel 40:1

**Q1. In the five and twentieth year of our captivity, in the beginning of the year, in what day of the month did the hand of the LORD come upon Ezekiel?**  
**Answer:** In the tenth day of the month.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 40:1

**Q2. In what year after that the city was smitten did the hand of the LORD come upon Ezekiel?**  
**Answer:** In the fourteenth year.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 40:1

**Q3. In the selfsame day, what did the hand of the LORD do upon Ezekiel?**  
**Answer:** The hand of the LORD was upon him, and brought him thither.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 40:1

---

### Ezekiel 40:2

**Q1. In the visions of God, whither brought God Ezekiel?**  
**Answer:** Into the land of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 40:2

**Q2. Upon what did God set Ezekiel?**  
**Answer:** Upon a very high mountain.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 40:2

**Q3. What was by the mountain where Ezekiel was set?**  
**Answer:** The frame of a city on the south.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 40:2
