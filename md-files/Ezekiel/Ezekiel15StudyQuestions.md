### Ezekiel 15:4
**Q10. What is stated in the first clause of Ezekiel 15:4?**  
**Answer:** Behold, it is cast into the fire for fuel.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 15:4

**Q11. What additional detail is declared in Ezekiel 15:4?**  
**Answer:** the fire devoureth both the ends of it, and the midst of it is burned. Is it meet for any work?  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 15:4

**Q12. According to Ezekiel 15:4, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Behold, it is cast into the fire for fuel; the fire devoureth both the ends of it, and the midst of it is burned. Is it meet for any work?  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 15:4

### Ezekiel 15:5
**Q13. What is stated in the first clause of Ezekiel 15:5?**  
**Answer:** Behold, when it was whole, it was meet for no work.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 15:5

**Q14. What additional detail is declared in Ezekiel 15:5?**  
**Answer:** how much less shall it be meet yet for any work, when the fire hath devoured it, and it is burned?  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 15:5

**Q15. According to Ezekiel 15:5, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Behold, when it was whole, it was meet for no work: how much less shall it be meet yet for any work, when the fire hath devoured it, and it is burned?  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 15:5

**Q21. What further truth is recorded in Ezekiel 15:7?**
