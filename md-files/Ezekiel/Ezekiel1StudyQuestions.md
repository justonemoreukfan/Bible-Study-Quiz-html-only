### Ezekiel 1:1
**Q1. According to Ezekiel 1:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** Now it came to pass in the thirtieth year.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 1:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Ezekiel 1:1?**  
**Answer:** in the fourth month.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 1:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Ezekiel 1:1?**  
**Answer:** in the fifth day of the month, as I was among the captives by the river of Chebar, that the heavens were opened, and I saw visions of God.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 1:1
