### Ezekiel 39:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Ezekiel 39:1?**  
**Answer:** Therefore, thou son of man, prophesy against Gog, and say, Thus saith the Lord GOD.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 39:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Ezekiel 39:1?**  
**Answer:** Behold, I am against thee, O Gog, the chief prince of Meshech and Tubal.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 39:1

**Q3. According to Ezekiel 39:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Therefore, thou son of man, prophesy against Gog, and say, Thus saith the Lord GOD; Behold, I am against thee, O Gog, the chief prince of Meshech and Tubal:.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 39:1
