# Ezekiel 46 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezekiel 46:1

**Q1. What is the gate of the inner court that looketh toward the east required to be during the six working days?**  
**Answer:** Shut.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:1

**Q2. On what day shall the gate of the inner court toward the east be opened?**  
**Answer:** The sabbath.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:1

**Q3. On what other occasion shall the gate of the inner court toward the east be opened besides the sabbath?**  
**Answer:** In the day of the new moon.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:1

---

### Ezekiel 46:2

**Q1. By what way shall the prince enter by the porch of the gate without?**  
**Answer:** By the way of the porch of that gate.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:2

**Q2. Where shall the prince stand while the priests prepare his burnt offering and his peace offerings?**  
**Answer:** By the post of the gate.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:2

**Q3. After worshipping at the threshold of the gate, when shall the prince go forth?**  
**Answer:** After he hath worshipped.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:2

---

### Ezekiel 46:3

**Q1. How shall the people of the land worship at the door of this gate?**  
**Answer:** In the spirit of worship before the LORD.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:3

**Q2. On what specific days shall the people of the land worship at the door of this gate?**  
**Answer:** In the sabbaths and in the new moons.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:3

**Q3. Where specifically shall the people of the land worship the LORD?**  
**Answer:** At the door of this gate.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:3

---

### Ezekiel 46:4

**Q1. What is the offering that the prince shall offer unto the LORD in the sabbath day?**  
**Answer:** Six lambs without blemish, and a ram without blemish.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:4

**Q2. Are the lambs offered by the prince on the sabbath required to be without blemish?**  
**Answer:** Yes.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:4

**Q3. How many rams without blemish shall be offered with the lambs on the sabbath?**  
**Answer:** One ram.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:4

---

### Ezekiel 46:5

**Q1. What is the meat offering for the ram?**  
**Answer:** An ephah for a ram.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:5

**Q2. What is the meat offering for the lambs?**  
**Answer:** As much as he is able to give.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:5

**Q3. How much oil shall be given with an ephah?**  
**Answer:** An hin of oil.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:5

---

### Ezekiel 46:6

**Q1. What animal is offered in the day of the new moon alongside the six lambs?**  
**Answer:** A young bullock without blemish.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:6

**Q2. How many lambs are offered without blemish in the day of the new moon?**  
**Answer:** Six lambs.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:6

**Q3. What else must the young bullock and the six lambs be besides young?**  
**Answer:** Without blemish.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:6

---

### Ezekiel 46:7

**Q1. What shall the prince prepare as a meat offering for a bullock?**  
**Answer:** An ephah.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:7

**Q2. What shall the prince prepare as a meat offering for a ram?**  
**Answer:** An ephah.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:7

**Q3. How much oil shall be given to the meat offering for the lambs?**  
**Answer:** According as his hand shall attain unto.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:7

**Q1. How shall the prince enter when he comes in?**  
**Answer:** By the way of the porch of that gate.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:8

**Q2. By what way shall the prince go out?**  
**Answer:** By the way thereof.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:8

**Q3. Where shall the prince go out by the way thereof?**  
**Answer:** By the way of the porch of that gate.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:8

### Ezekiel 46:9

**Q1. When the people of the land shall come before the LORD in the solemn feasts, how shall he that entereth by the way of the north gate to worship go out?**  
**Answer:** By the way of the south gate.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:9

**Q2. How shall he that entereth by the way of the south gate go out?**  
**Answer:** By the way of the north gate.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:9

**Q3. May anyone return by the way of the gate he came in by?**  
**Answer:** No, he shall not return by the way of the gate whereby he came in.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:9

---

### Ezekiel 46:10

**Q1. Who shall be in the midst of the people when they go in?**  
**Answer:** The prince.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:10

**Q2. What shall the prince do when the people go in?**  
**Answer:** Go in.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:10

**Q3. What shall the prince do when the people go forth?**  
**Answer:** Go forth together.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:10

---

### Ezekiel 46:11

**Q1. In the feasts and in the solemnities, what shall the meat offering be for a bullock?**  
**Answer:** An ephah to a bullock.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:11

**Q2. What shall the meat offering be for a ram in the feasts and solemnities?**  
**Answer:** An ephah to a ram.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:11

**Q3. How much oil shall be given to the meat offering for the lambs?**  
**Answer:** An hin of oil to an ephah.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:11

---

### Ezekiel 46:12

**Q1. When the prince shall prepare a voluntary burnt offering or peace offerings voluntarily unto the LORD, what gate shall be opened for him?**  
**Answer:** The gate that looketh toward the east.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:12

**Q2. After he prepares his voluntary offering, what shall the prince then do?**  
**Answer:** Then shall he open his gate as he did in the sabbath day.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:12

**Q3. After he goes forth, what shall be done to the gate?**  
**Answer:** Then he shall go forth; and after he is gone forth shall the gate be shut.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:12

---

### Ezekiel 46:13

**Q1. What kind of lamb of the first year shall be prepared daily unto the LORD for a burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** A lamb of the first year without blemish.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:13

**Q2. How often shall this lamb of the first year be prepared unto the LORD?**  
**Answer:** Daily.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:13

**Q3. By what time of the day shall this daily burnt offering be prepared?**  
**Answer:** Thou shalt prepare it every morning.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:13

---

### Ezekiel 46:14

**Q1. What meat offering shall be prepared with the daily lamb every morning?**  
**Answer:** A sixth part of an ephah.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:14

**Q2. How much oil shall be mixed with the fine flour for the daily meat offering?**  
**Answer:** The third part of an hin, to temper with the fine flour.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:14

**Q3. To whom is this meat offering a perpetual ordinance?**  
**Answer:** Continually, by a perpetual ordinance unto the LORD.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:14

---

### Ezekiel 46:15

**Q1. What shall be prepared every morning for a continual burnt offering?**  
**Answer:** The lamb, and the meat offering, and the oil.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:15

**Q2. How often shall the lamb, the meat offering, and the oil be prepared?**  
**Answer:** Every morning.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:15

**Q3. What kind of continual burnt offering is this?**  
**Answer:** A continual burnt offering.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:15

---

### Ezekiel 46:16

**Q1. If the prince give a gift unto any of his sons, whose inheritance shall it be?**  
**Answer:** It shall be his sons'.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:16

**Q2. By what right shall the sons possess the gift given by the prince?**  
**Answer:** It shall be their possession by inheritance.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:16

**Q3. Who is the one giving the gift to his sons?**  
**Answer:** The prince.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:16

---

### Ezekiel 46:17

**Q1. If the prince give a gift of his inheritance to one of his servants, until when shall it be his?**  
**Answer:** To the year of liberty.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:17

**Q2. After the year of liberty, to whom shall the gift return?**  
**Answer:** To the prince.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:17

**Q3. Whose inheritance shall the prince's sons' inheritance remain?**  
**Answer:** Theirs.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:17

---

### Ezekiel 46:18

**Q1. What part of the people's inheritance shall the prince not take?**  
**Answer:** Of their inheritance by oppression.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:18

**Q2. From whose possession shall the prince not thrust the people out of their possession?**  
**Answer:** His people's.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:18

**Q3. Out of what shall the prince provide his sons an inheritance?**  
**Answer:** Out of his own possession.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:18

**Q1. Where did the man say the priests shall boil the trespass offering and the sin offering?**  
**Answer:** Where the priests shall boil the trespass offering and the sin offering.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:20

**Answer:** Where they shall bake the meat offering.

**Answer:** That they bear not any ministering to the people.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:20

**Q1. Into which court did the man bring the prophet forth?**  
**Answer:** Into the utter court.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:21

**Q2. Where did the man cause the prophet to pass by?**  
**Answer:** By the four corners of the court.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:21

**Q3. What was in every corner of the court?**  
**Answer:** A court.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:21

### Ezekiel 46:22

**Q1. In the four corners of the court, what kind of courts were joined?**  
**Answer:** Courts joined.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:22

**Q2. What were the dimensions of the courts joined in the four corners?**  
**Answer:** Forty cubits long and thirty broad.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:22

**Q3. What feature was common to these four corners of courts joined?**  
**Answer:** These four corners were of one measure.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:22

---

**Q1. What was built round about inside them, even round about the four?**  
**Answer:** A row of building.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:23

**Q2. What was made under the rows round about?**  
**Answer:** Boiling places.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:23

**Q3. Where were the boiling places made under the rows?**  
**Answer:** Round about.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:23

### Ezekiel 46:24

**Q1. What did the man say unto the prophet regarding these buildings?**  
**Answer:** These are the places of them that boil.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:24

**Q2. Who shall boil the sacrifice of the people in these places?**  
**Answer:** The ministers of the house.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:24

**Q3. Whose sacrifice shall the ministers of the house boil?**  
**Answer:** The sacrifice of the people.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 46:24
