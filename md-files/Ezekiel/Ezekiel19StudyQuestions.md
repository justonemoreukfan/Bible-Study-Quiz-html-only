**Q4. What is stated in the first clause of Ezekiel 19:2?**  
**Answer:** And say, What is thy mother? A lioness.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 19:2

**Q5. What additional detail is declared in Ezekiel 19:2?**  
**Answer:** she lay down among lions, she nourished her whelps among young lions.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 19:2

**Q6. According to Ezekiel 19:2, what is the full truth recorded?**
