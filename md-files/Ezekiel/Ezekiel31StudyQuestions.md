# Ezekiel 31 Study Questions (KJV)

### Ezekiel 31:1
**Q1. According to Ezekiel 31:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And it came to pass in the eleventh year.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 31:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Ezekiel 31:1?**  
**Answer:** in the third month.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 31:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Ezekiel 31:1?**  
**Answer:** in the first day of the month, that the word of the LORD came unto me.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 31:1
