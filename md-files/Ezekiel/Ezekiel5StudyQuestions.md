# Ezekiel 5 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezekiel 5:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Ezekiel 5:1?**  
**Answer:** And thou, son of man, take thee a sharp knife, take thee a barber’s razor, and cause it to pass upon thine head and upon thy beard.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 5:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Ezekiel 5:1?**  
**Answer:** then take thee balances to weigh, and divide the hair.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 5:1

**Q3. According to Ezekiel 5:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And thou, son of man, take thee a sharp knife, take thee a barber’s razor, and cause it to pass upon thine head and upon thy beard: then take thee balances to weigh, and divide the hair.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 5:1
