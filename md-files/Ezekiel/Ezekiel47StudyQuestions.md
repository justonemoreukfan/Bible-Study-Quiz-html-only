# Ezekiel 47 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezekiel 47:1

**Q1. Where did the man bring Ezekiel again?**  
**Answer:** To the door of the house.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:1

**Q2. What did Ezekiel see issuing from under the threshold of the house eastward?**  
**Answer:** Waters.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:1

**Q3. In which direction did the forefront of the house stand?**  
**Answer:** Toward the east.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:1

---

### Ezekiel 47:2

**Q1. Out of what gate did the man bring Ezekiel?**  
**Answer:** The way of the gate northward.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:2

**Q2. Around which way did the man lead Ezekiel?**  
**Answer:** About the way without unto the utter gate by the way that looketh eastward.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:2

**Q3. What did the waters do on the right side?**  
**Answer:** Ran out.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:2

---

### Ezekiel 47:3

**Q1. In which direction went the man when the line was in his hand?**  
**Answer:** Forth eastward.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:3

**Q2. How many cubits did the man measure?**  
**Answer:** A thousand cubits.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:3

**Q3. How deep were the waters when Ezekiel was brought through them?**  
**Answer:** To the ankles.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:3

---

### Ezekiel 47:4

**Q1. How many cubits did the man measure again?**  
**Answer:** A thousand.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:4

**Q2. How deep were the waters when the man brought Ezekiel through them the second time?**  
**Answer:** To the knees.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:4

**Q3. How deep were the waters after measuring another thousand cubits?**  
**Answer:** To the loins.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:4

---

### Ezekiel 47:5

**Q1. How many cubits did he measure for the fourth time?**  
**Answer:** A thousand.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:5

**Q2. What had the waters become that could not be passed over?**  
**Answer:** A river.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:5

**Q3. What kind of waters were they that must be swum in?**  
**Answer:** Waters to swim in, a river that could not be passed over.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:5

---

### Ezekiel 47:6

**Q1. What did the man ask Ezekiel after causing him to return to the brink of the river?**  
**Answer:** Hast thou seen this, son of man?  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:6

---

### Ezekiel 47:7

**Q1. Where did Ezekiel go when he had returned?**  
**Answer:** To the brink of the river.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:7

**Q2. What did Ezekiel see upon the bank of the river?**  
**Answer:** Very many trees.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:7

**Q3. On which side of the river were the very many trees?**  
**Answer:** On the one side and on the other.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:7

---

### Ezekiel 47:8

**Q1. Where did the man say these waters issue out toward?**  
**Answer:** Toward the east country.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:8

**Q2. Into what place do the waters go down?**  
**Answer:** Into the desert.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:8

**Q3. Into what sea do the waters go, which being brought forth into the sea, the waters shall be healed?**  
**Answer:** Into the sea.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:8

---

### Ezekiel 47:9

**Q1. What shall live everywhere the rivers come?**  
**Answer:** Every thing that liveth, which moveth.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:9

**Q2. What shall be there in very great multitudes?**  
**Answer:** Fish.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:9

**Q3. What shall be healed when this water shall come thither?**  
**Answer:** The waters.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:9

---

### Ezekiel 47:10

**Q1. Who shall stand upon it from En-gedi even unto En-eglaim?**  
**Answer:** The fishers.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:10

**Q2. What shall be a place to spread forth nets?**  
**Answer:** A place for the spreading of nets.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:10

**Q3. Whose fish shall be according to their kinds, as the fish of the great sea?**  
**Answer:** Their fish.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:10

---

### Ezekiel 47:11

**Q1. What parts of the river shall not be healed?**  
**Answer:** The miry places thereof and the marshes thereof.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:11

**Q2. To what shall the miry places and marshes be given?**  
**Answer:** To salt.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:11

---

### Ezekiel 47:12

**Q1. What shall grow upon the bank of the river on this side and on that side?**  
**Answer:** All trees for meat.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:12

**Q2. What shall not fade on these trees?**  
**Answer:** Their leaf.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:12

**Q3. When shall the trees bring forth new fruit according to their months?**  
**Answer:** Because their waters issued out of the sanctuary.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:12

---

### Ezekiel 47:13

**Q1. According to what shall ye divide the land for inheritance?**  
**Answer:** According to the twelve tribes of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:13

**Q2. Who shall have two portions?**  
**Answer:** Joseph.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:13

---

### Ezekiel 47:14

**Q1. Concerning what did the Lord lift up His hand?**  
**Answer:** To give it unto your fathers.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:14

**Q2. What shall this land fall unto you for?**  
**Answer:** For inheritance.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:14

---

### Ezekiel 47:15

**Q1. What shall be the side of the land toward the north side?**  
**Answer:** From the great sea, the way of Hethlon, as men go to Zedad.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:15

---

### Ezekiel 47:16

**Q1. What place is mentioned alongside Hamath and Berothah?**  
**Answer:** Sibraim.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:16

**Q2. Between what borders is the house of Hhatticon located?**  
**Answer:** Between the border of Damascus and the border of Hamath.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:16

---

### Ezekiel 47:17

**Q1. From the sea, what shall the border be?**  
**Answer:** The court of Enan, the border of Damascus, and the north northward, and the border of Hamath.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:17

---

### Ezekiel 47:18

**Q1. Between what places shall the east side be measured?**  
**Answer:** From Hauran, and from Damascus, and from Gilead, and from the land of Israel by Jordan.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:18

**Q2. To what sea shall the east side measure?**  
**Answer:** Unto the east sea.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:18

---

### Ezekiel 47:19

**Q1. Toward what direction is the south side from Tamar?**  
**Answer:** To the waters of strife in Kadesh.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:19

**Q2. To what river does the south side extend?**  
**Answer:** The river to the great sea.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:19

---

### Ezekiel 47:20

**Q1. What shall the west side also be?**  
**Answer:** The great sea from the border, till a man come over against Hamath.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:20

---

### Ezekiel 47:21

**Q1. How shall ye divide this land unto you?**  
**Answer:** According to the tribes of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:21

---

### Ezekiel 47:22

**Q1. By what means shall ye divide the land for an inheritance?**  
**Answer:** By lot.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:22

**Q2. Unto whom shall the strangers have inheritance among the tribes of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The strangers that sojourn among you, which shall beget children among you.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:22

---

### Ezekiel 47:23

**Q1. In what tribe shall the stranger have his inheritance?**  
**Answer:** In what tribe the stranger sojourneth.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:23

**Q2. What did the Lord God say?**  
**Answer:** There shall ye give him his inheritance.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 47:23
