---

### Ezekiel 3:1
**Q1. What is stated in the first clause of Ezekiel 3:1?**  
**Answer:** Moreover he said unto me, Son of man, eat that thou findest.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 3:1

**Q2. What additional detail is declared in Ezekiel 3:1?**  
**Answer:** eat this roll, and go speak unto the house of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 3:1

**Q3. According to Ezekiel 3:1, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** Moreover he said unto me, Son of man, eat that thou findest; eat this roll, and go speak unto the house of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 3:1
