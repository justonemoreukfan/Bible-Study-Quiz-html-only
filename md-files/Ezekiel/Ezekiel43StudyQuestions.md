# Ezekiel 43 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Ezekiel 43:1

**Q1. Where did the man bring Ezekiel after he brought him to the gate?**  
**Answer:** To the gate that looketh toward the east.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:1

**Q2. What came from the way of the east according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** The glory of the God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:1

**Q3. Whose glory came from the way of the east?**  
**Answer:** The glory of the God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:1

---

### Ezekiel 43:2

**Q1. What came from the way of the east?**  
**Answer:** The voice of the God of Israel.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:2

**Q2. What was his voice compared to?**  
**Answer:** The noise of many waters.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:2

**Q3. What shone with the glory of the God of Israel?**  
**Answer:** The earth.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:2

---

### Ezekiel 43:3

**Q1. What was the vision according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** The vision which I saw, even according to the vision that I saw when I came to destroy the city.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:3

**Q2. What were the visions like that Ezekiel saw?**  
**Answer:** Like the vision that I saw by the river Chebar.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:3

**Q3. What did Ezekiel do when he saw these visions?**  
**Answer:** I fell upon my face.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:3

---

### Ezekiel 43:4

**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:4

**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:4

**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:7

### Ezekiel 43:13

**Q1. What are these the measures of the altar after?**  
**Answer:** The cubits.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:13

**Q2. What kind of cubit is mentioned in this verse?**  
**Answer:** A cubit is a cubit and an hand breadth.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:13

**Q3. What shall be the bottom and the breadth of the altar?**  
**Answer:** A cubit shall be the bottom, and a cubit the breadth.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:13

---

### Ezekiel 43:14

**Q1. From the bottom upon the ground up to the lower settle, how high shall it be?**  
**Answer:** Two cubits.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:14

**Q2. What shall be the breadth of the lower settle?**  
**Answer:** One cubit.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:14

**Q3. From the lesser settle up to the greater settle, how high shall it be?**  
**Answer:** Four cubits.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:14

---

### Ezekiel 43:15

**Q1. What is the altar called in this verse?**  
**Answer:** The altar shall be four cubits.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:15

**Q2. From the altar hearth and upward, what shall point upward?**  
**Answer:** Four horns.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:15

**Q3. How many horns shall there be from the altar and upward?**  
**Answer:** Four horns.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:15

**Answer:** Square in the four squares thereof.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:16

**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:16

### Ezekiel 43:26

**Q1. What shall they purge the altar of?**  
**Answer:** The altar.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:26

**Answer:** Purge the altar and purify it.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:26

**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:26

**Answer:** Your burnt offerings upon the altar, and your peace offerings.

**Q2. What offerings will the Lord accept?**  
**Answer:** Your peace offerings.  
**Verse:** Ezekiel 43:27
