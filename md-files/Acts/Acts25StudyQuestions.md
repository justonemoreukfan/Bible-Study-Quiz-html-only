# Acts 25 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Acts 25:1

**Q1. When Festus was come into the province, where did he go after three days?**  
**Answer:** From Caesarea to Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:1

**Q2. Whose province did Festus come into?**  
**Answer:** The province.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:1

**Q3. How many days after coming into the province did Festus ascend from Caesarea to Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** After three days.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:1

---

### Acts 25:2

**Q1. Who informed Festus against Paul?**  
**Answer:** The high priest and the chief of the Jews.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:2

**Q2. When the high priest and chief of the Jews informed Festus against Paul, what did they do?**  
**Answer:** They informed him.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:2

**Q3. Who was the person informed against?**  
**Answer:** Paul.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:2

---

### Acts 25:3

**Q1. What did the Jews besought Festus for?**  
**Answer:** Favour against him (Paul).  
**Verse:** Acts 25:3

**Q2. What would sending for Paul to Jerusalem allow the Jews to do?**  
**Answer:** Lay wait in the way to kill him.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:3

**Q3. Against whom did they desire favour?**  
**Answer:** Against him (Paul).  
**Verse:** Acts 25:3

---

### Acts 25:4

**Q1. Where did Festus answer that Paul should be kept?**  
**Answer:** At Caesarea.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:4

**Q2. How did Festus answer?**  
**Answer:** That Paul should be kept at Caesarea.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:4

**Q3. What did Festus say he himself would do shortly?**  
**Answer:** Depart thither.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:4

---

### Acts 25:5

**Q1. Who did Festus say should go down with him?**  
**Answer:** Them which among you are able.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:5

**Q2. What should those who are able do if there be any wickedness in the man?**  
**Answer:** Accuse him.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:5

**Q3. Where should they go down with Festus?**  
**Answer:** To Caesarea.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:5

---

### Acts 25:6

**Q1. How many days or more did Festus tarry among them?**  
**Answer:** More than ten days.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:6

**Q2. Where did Festus go down after tarrying among them?**  
**Answer:** Unto Caesarea.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:6

**Q3. What did Festus do the next day after arriving at the judgment seat?**  
**Answer:** Commanded Paul to be brought.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:6

---

### Acts 25:7

**Q1. When Paul was come, who stood round about him?**  
**Answer:** The Jews which came down from Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:7

**Q2. What did the Jews lay against Paul?**  
**Answer:** Many and grievous complaints.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:7

**Q3. Could the Jews prove the complaints they laid against Paul?**  
**Answer:** Which they could not prove.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:7

---

### Acts 25:8

**Q1. When Paul answered for himself, what did he say he had not offended against?**  
**Answer:** Neither against the law of the Jews, neither against the temple, nor yet against Caesar.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:8

**Q2. What law did Paul say he had not offended against?**  
**Answer:** The law of the Jews.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:8

**Q3. Against whom did Paul state he had not offended, besides the law of the Jews and the temple?**  
**Answer:** Caesar.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:8

---

### Acts 25:9

**Q1. Who was willing to do the Jews a pleasure?**  
**Answer:** Festus.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:9

**Q2. What did Festus ask Paul if he was willing to go up to?**  
**Answer:** Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:9

**Q3. What would happen to Paul at Jerusalem according to Festus's question?**  
**Answer:** Be judged of these things before me.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:9

---

### Acts 25:10

**Q1. Before whose judgment seat did Paul say he stood?**  
**Answer:** Caesar's judgment seat.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:10

**Q2. Where ought Paul to be judged?**  
**Answer:** Where I ought to be judged.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:10

**Q3. What did Paul say he had not done to the Jews?**  
**Answer:** To the Jews have I done no wrong, as thou very well knowest.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:10

---

### Acts 25:11

**Q1. If Paul had committed any thing worthy of death, what did he say he did not refuse?**  
**Answer:** I refuse not to die.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:11

**Q2. If none of these things whereof these accuse me be true, what did Paul say?**  
**Answer:** No man may deliver me unto them.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:11

**Q3. To whom did Paul appeal?**  
**Answer:** I appeal unto Caesar.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:11

---

### Acts 25:12

**Q1. When Festus had conferred with the council, what did he answer?**  
**Answer:** Hast thou appealed unto Caesar? unto Caesar shalt thou go.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:12

**Q2. With whom did Festus confer before answering?**  
**Answer:** With the council.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:12

**Q3. Where did Festus say Paul should go?**  
**Answer:** Unto Caesar shalt thou go.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:12

---

### Acts 25:13

**Q1. Who came unto Caesarea to salute Festus after certain days?**  
**Answer:** King Agrippa and Bernice.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:13

**Q2. What did King Agrippa and Bernice come unto Caesarea to do?**  
**Answer:** To salute Festus.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:13

**Q3. After how long did King Agrippa and Bernice come?**  
**Answer:** After certain days.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:13

---

### Acts 25:14

**Q1. When they had been there many days, what did Festus declare unto the king?**  
**Answer:** Paul's cause.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:14

**Q2. Who was left a prisoner by Felix?**  
**Answer:** A certain man.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:14

**Q3. To whom did Festus declare Paul's cause?**  
**Answer:** Unto the king (Agrippa).  
**Verse:** Acts 25:14

---

### Acts 25:15

**Q1. When Festus went to Jerusalem, who informed him about Paul?**  
**Answer:** The chief priests and the elders of the Jews.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:15

**Q2. What did the chief priests and elders desire concerning Paul?**  
**Answer:** Desiring to have judgment against him.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:15

**Q3. Where were the chief priests and elders when they informed Festus?**  
**Answer:** At Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:15

---

### Acts 25:16

**Q1. What did Festus answer the Jews was not the manner of the Romans?**  
**Answer:** To deliver any man to die.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:16

**Q2. Before delivering a man to die, what did Festus say the accused must have?**  
**Answer:** The accusers face to face.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:16

**Q3. What opportunity must the accused have license for concerning the crime laid against him?**  
**Answer:** License to answer for himself concerning the crime laid against him.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:16

---

### Acts 25:17

**Q1. When they were come hither, without any delay, what did Festus do the next day?**  
**Answer:** Sat on the judgment seat.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:17

**Q2. Who did Festus command to be brought forth?**  
**Answer:** The man.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:17

**Q3. When did Festus sit on the judgment seat?**  
**Answer:** On the morrow (the next day).  
**Verse:** Acts 25:17

---

### Acts 25:18

**Q1. When the accusers stood up, what accusations did they bring against Paul?**  
**Answer:** None of such things as I supposed.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:18

**Q2. Did the accusers bring accusations of evil things as Festus supposed?**  
**Answer:** No.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:18

**Q3. Who stood up against Paul?**  
**Answer:** The accusers.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:18

---

### Acts 25:19

**Q1. What questions did the accusers have against Paul?**  
**Answer:** Certain questions of their own superstition.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:19

**Q2. Of one Jesus, what did Paul affirm?**  
**Answer:** Which was dead, whom Paul affirmed to be alive.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:19

**Q3. Who did Paul affirm to be alive?**  
**Answer:** Jesus.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:19

---

### Acts 25:20

**Q1. Because Festus doubted of such manner of questions, what did he ask Paul?**  
**Answer:** Whether he would go to Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:20

**Q2. Where did Festus ask Paul if he would be judged of these matters?**  
**Answer:** There (Jerusalem).  
**Verse:** Acts 25:20

**Q3. Why did Festus doubt?**  
**Answer:** Because he doubted of such manner of questions.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:20

---

### Acts 25:21

**Q1. When Paul had appealed to be reserved unto the hearing of whom, did Festus command him to be kept?**  
**Answer:** Augustus.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:21

**Q2. Unto whom did Festus command Paul to be kept until he could send him to Caesar?**  
**Answer:** Till I might send him to Caesar.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:21

**Q3. Who was the emperor mentioned here alongside Caesar?**  
**Answer:** Augustus.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:21

---

### Acts 25:22

**Q1. What did Agrippa say unto Festus?**  
**Answer:** I would also hear the man myself.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:22

**Q2. When did Agrippa say he would hear the man?**  
**Answer:** Tomorrow.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:22

**Q3. Who answered Agrippa that he should hear him tomorrow?**  
**Answer:** Festus.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:22

---

### Acts 25:23

**Q1. With what pomp did Agrippa and Bernice come on the morrow?**  
**Answer:** With great pomp.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:23

**Q2. Where did Agrippa and Bernice enter into?**  
**Answer:** The place of hearing.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:23

**Q3. Who, besides the chief captains and principal men of the city, entered with them?**  
**Answer:** Festus's commandment. (Or: with the chief captains and principal men of the city).  
**Verse:** Acts 25:23

---

### Acts 25:24

**Q1. Who did Festus say all the multitude of the Jews had dealt with him about?**  
**Answer:** Paul.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:24

**Q2. Where did the multitude of the Jews cry out that Paul should not live any longer?**  
**Answer:** Both at Jerusalem, and also here.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:24

**Q3. What did Festus say about whether Paul ought to live?**  
**Answer:** That he ought not to live any longer.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:24

---

### Acts 25:25

**Q1. When Festus found that Paul had committed nothing worthy of death, what did he determine?**  
**Answer:** To send him.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:25

**Q2. To whom did Festus determine to send Paul?**  
**Answer:** To Augustus.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:25

**Q3. Why did Festus determine to send Paul to Augustus?**  
**Answer:** Because that he himself had appealed.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:25

---

### Acts 25:26

**Q1. Who did Festus say he had no certain thing to write unto?**  
**Answer:** Unto my lord (Caesar).  
**Verse:** Acts 25:26

**Q2. Before whom did Festus bring Paul forth especially?**  
**Answer:** Before thee, O king Agrippa.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:26

**Q3. What was the purpose of bringing Paul forth before Agrippa, after examination had?**  
**Answer:** That I might have somewhat to write.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:26

---

### Acts 25:27

**Q1. What did Festus think it unreasonable to send?**  
**Answer:** A prisoner, and not withal to signify the crimes laid against him.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:27

**Q2. Is it reasonable to send a prisoner without signifying the crimes laid against him?**  
**Answer:** Unreasonable.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:27

**Q3. What must be signified along with sending a prisoner?**  
**Answer:** The crimes laid against him.  
**Verse:** Acts 25:27
