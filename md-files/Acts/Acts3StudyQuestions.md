### Acts 3:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Acts 3:1 state?**  
**Answer:** Now Peter and John went up together into the temple at the hour of prayer.  
**Verse:** Acts 3:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Acts 3:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** being the ninth hour.  
**Verse:** Acts 3:1

**Verse:** Acts 3:1
