# Acts 18 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Acts 18:1
**Q1. What does the first part of Acts 18:1 state?**  
**Answer:** After these things Paul departed from Athens.  
**Verse:** Acts 18:1

**Q2. What does the second part of Acts 18:1 declare?**  
**Answer:** and came to Corinth.  
**Verse:** Acts 18:1

**Q3. What is recorded in Acts 18:1?**  
**Answer:** After these things Paul departed from Athens, and came to Corinth;.  
**Verse:** Acts 18:1
