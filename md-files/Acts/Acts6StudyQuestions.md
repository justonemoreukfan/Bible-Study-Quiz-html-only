# Acts 6 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Acts 6:1
**Q1. According to Acts 6:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** And in those days.  
**Verse:** Acts 6:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Acts 6:1?**  
**Answer:** when the number of the disciples was multiplied.  
**Verse:** Acts 6:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Acts 6:1?**  
**Answer:** there arose a murmuring of the Grecians against the Hebrews, because their widows were neglected in the daily ministration.  
**Verse:** Acts 6:1

### Acts 6:5
**Q13. What is stated in the first clause of Acts 6:5?**  
**Answer:** And the saying pleased the whole multitude.  
**Verse:** Acts 6:5

**Q14. What additional detail is declared in Acts 6:5?**  
**Answer:** and they chose Stephen, a man full of faith and of the Holy Ghost, and Philip, and Prochorus, and Nicanor, and Timon, and Parmenas, and Nicolas a proselyte of Antioch.  
**Verse:** Acts 6:5

**Q15. According to Acts 6:5, what is the full truth recorded?**  
**Answer:** And the saying pleased the whole multitude: and they chose Stephen, a man full of faith and of the Holy Ghost, and Philip, and Prochorus, and Nicanor, and Timon, and Parmenas, and Nicolas a proselyte of Antioch:.  
**Verse:** Acts 6:5
