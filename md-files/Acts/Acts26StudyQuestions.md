# Acts 26 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Acts 26:1

**Q1. Who said unto Paul, Thou art permitted to speak for thyself?**  
**Answer:** Agrippa.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:1

**Q2. What did Paul do before stretching forth the hand?**  
**Answer:** Answered for himself.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:1

**Q3. Whose hand did Paul stretch forth?**  
**Answer:** His own hand.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:1

---

### Acts 26:2

**Q1. Who did Paul think himself happy to answer for himself before?**  
**Answer:** King Agrippa.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:2

**Q2. Touching what things was Paul happy to answer before Agrippa?**  
**Answer:** All the things whereof he was accused of the Jews.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:2

**Q3. What day was Paul answering before Agrippa?**  
**Answer:** This day.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:2

---

### Acts 26:3

**Q1. Who was especially expert in all customs and questions which are among the Jews?**  
**Answer:** King Agrippa.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:3

**Q2. What did Paul beseech Agrippa to hear him of?**  
**Answer:** Patiently.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:3

**Q3. What was Agrippa expert in, according to Paul?**  
**Answer:** All customs and questions which are among the Jews.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:3

---

### Acts 26:4

**Q1. What did all the Jews know concerning Paul's manner of life from his youth?**  
**Answer:** His manner of life from his youth, which was at the first among his own nation at Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:4

**Q2. Where was Paul's manner of life at the first?**  
**Answer:** Among his own nation at Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:4

**Q3. From when did the Jews know Paul's manner of life?**  
**Answer:** From his youth.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:4

---

### Acts 26:5

**Q1. How long had the Jews known Paul, if they would testify?**  
**Answer:** From the beginning.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:5

**Q2. After what straitest sect of their religion did Paul live?**  
**Answer:** A Pharisee.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:5

**Q3. What did the Jews know Paul lived after?**  
**Answer:** The straitest sect of their religion a Pharisee.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:5

---

### Acts 26:6

**Q1. For what is Paul now stood and judged?**  
**Answer:** For the hope of the promise made of God unto our fathers.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:6

**Q2. Who was the promise made unto?**  
**Answer:** Our fathers.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:6

**Q3. Who made the promise?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:6

---

### Acts 26:7

**Q1. Unto which promise our twelve tribes, serving God continually day and night, hope to come?**  
**Answer:** The twelve tribes.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:7

**Q2. How did the twelve tribes serve God?**  
**Answer:** Continually day and night.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:7

**Q3. For whose hope's sake is Paul accused of the Jews?**  
**Answer:** King Agrippa's hope's sake (the promise).  
**Verse:** Acts 26:7

---

### Acts 26:8

**Q1. Why should it be thought a thing incredible with you?**  
**Answer:** That God should raise the dead.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:8

**Q2. Who should raise the dead?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:8

**Q3. What should be thought incredible?**  
**Answer:** That God should raise the dead.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:8

---

### Acts 26:9

**Q1. What did Paul verily think with himself that he ought to do?**  
**Answer:** Many things contrary to the name of Jesus of Nazareth.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:9

**Q2. Whose name did Paul think he ought to do things contrary to?**  
**Answer:** Jesus of Nazareth.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:9

**Q3. Where did Paul think he ought to do many things?**  
**Answer:** With himself.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:9

---

### Acts 26:10

**Q1. What did Paul do in Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** Shut up many of the saints in prison.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:10

**Q2. From whom did Paul receive authority to shut up the saints?**  
**Answer:** The chief priests.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:10

**Q3. What did Paul give when they were put to death?**  
**Answer:** His voice.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:10

---

### Acts 26:11

**Q1. How often did Paul punish the saints in every synagogue?**  
**Answer:** Oft.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:11

**Q2. What did Paul compel the saints to do?**  
**Answer:** Blaspheme.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:11

**Q3. Unto where did Paul persecute the saints, even unto strange cities?**  
**Answer:** Strange cities.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:11

---

### Acts 26:12

**Q1. Where was Paul going with authority and commission from the chief priests?**  
**Answer:** To Damascus.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:12

**Q2. From whom did Paul have authority and commission?**  
**Answer:** The chief priests.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:12

**Q3. What did Paul have from the chief priests?**  
**Answer:** Authority and commission.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:12

---

### Acts 26:13

**Q1. At what time of day did Paul see a light from heaven?**  
**Answer:** At midday.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:13

**Q2. Where was the light from that Paul saw in the way?**  
**Answer:** From heaven.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:13

**Q3. Above the brightness of what did the light shine?**  
**Answer:** The sun.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:13

---

### Acts 26:14

**Q1. When they were all fallen to the earth, what did Paul hear speaking unto him?**  
**Answer:** A voice.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:14

**Q2. In what tongue did the voice speak unto Paul?**  
**Answer:** The Hebrew tongue.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:14

**Q3. What did the voice say it was hard for Paul to do?**  
**Answer:** To kick against the pricks.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:14

---

### Acts 26:15

**Q1. When Paul asked, Who art thou, Lord, what did the Lord say?**  
**Answer:** I am Jesus whom thou persecutest.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:15

**Q2. Who did the Lord say He was?**  
**Answer:** Jesus whom thou persecutest.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:15

**Q3. Who asked, Who art thou, Lord?**  
**Answer:** Paul.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:15

---

### Acts 26:16

**Q1. What did the Lord tell Paul to do?**  
**Answer:** Rise, and stand upon thy feet.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:16

**Q2. For what purpose did the Lord appear unto Paul?**  
**Answer:** To make him a minister and a witness.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:16

**Q3. Of what things was Paul to be a witness?**  
**Answer:** Of those things which he had seen, and of those things in the which the Lord would appear unto him.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:16

---

### Acts 26:17

**Q1. From whom did the Lord say He would deliver Paul?**  
**Answer:** From the people, and from the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:17

**Q2. Unto whom was the Lord sending Paul?**  
**Answer:** Unto the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:17

**Q3. Who would deliver Paul from the people and from the Gentiles?**  
**Answer:** The Lord.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:17

---

### Acts 26:18

**Q1. To open what of the Gentiles was Paul sent?**  
**Answer:** Their eyes.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:18

**Q2. From what to what were the Gentiles to be turned?**  
**Answer:** From darkness to light, and from the power of Satan unto God.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:18

**Q3. What might the Gentiles receive through faith that is in Me?**  
**Answer:** Forgiveness of sins, and inheritance among them which are sanctified.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:18

---

### Acts 26:19

**Q1. Whereupon, O King Agrippa, what was Paul not?**  
**Answer:** Disobedient unto the heavenly vision.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:19

**Q2. Unto what was Paul not disobedient?**  
**Answer:** The heavenly vision.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:19

**Q3. Who was addressed as O king Agrippa in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Agrippa.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:19

---

### Acts 26:20

**Q1. To whom did Paul shew first?**  
**Answer:** Unto them of Damascus, and at Jerusalem, and throughout all the coasts of Judaea, and then to the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:20

**Q2. What did Paul show that they should do?**  
**Answer:** Repent and turn to God, and do works meet for repentance.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:20

**Q3. Where did Paul shew first?**  
**Answer:** At Damascus, and at Jerusalem, and throughout all the coasts of Judaea.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:20

---

### Acts 26:21

**Q1. For this cause, who caught Paul in the temple?**  
**Answer:** The Jews.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:21

**Q2. Where did the Jews catch Paul?**  
**Answer:** In the temple.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:21

**Q3. What did the Jews go about to do to Paul after catching him in the temple?**  
**Answer:** To kill him.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:21

---

### Acts 26:22

**Q1. Having obtained help of whom, Paul continued unto this day?**  
**Answer:** God.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:22

**Q2. To whom did Paul witness, both to small and great?**  
**Answer:** Both to small and great.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:22

**Q3. What did Paul say none other things than those which the prophets and Moses did say should come?**  
**Answer:** That Moses and the prophets should come.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:22

---

### Acts 26:23

**Q1. That Christ should suffer, and that He should be the first that should do what?**  
**Answer:** Rise from the dead.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:23

**Q2. Unto whom should Christ shew light?**  
**Answer:** Unto the people, and to the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:23

**Q3. Who should be the first that should rise from the dead?**  
**Answer:** Christ.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:23

---

### Acts 26:24

**Q1. As Paul thus spake for himself, who said with a loud voice, Paul, thou art beside thyself?**  
**Answer:** Festus.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:24

**Q2. What did Festus say much learning was doing to Paul?**  
**Answer:** Making him mad.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:24

**Q3. How did Festus speak when he said Paul was beside himself?**  
**Answer:** With a loud voice.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:24

---

### Acts 26:25

**Q1. Who did Paul say he was not mad before?**  
**Answer:** Most noble Festus.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:25

**Q2. What words did Paul speak before Festus?**  
**Answer:** The words of truth and soberness.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:25

**Q3. Whose words were the words of truth and soberness?**  
**Answer:** Paul's.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:25

---

### Acts 26:26

**Q1. Who knows of these things before whom Paul also speaks freely?**  
**Answer:** The king (Agrippa).  
**Verse:** Acts 26:26

**Q2. What was Paul persuaded that none of these things was hidden from Agrippa?**  
**Answer:** That none of these things was hidden from him.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:26

**Q3. Where was this thing done, according to Paul?**  
**Answer:** Not in a corner.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:26

---

### Acts 26:27

**Q1. Who did King Agrippa believe, according to Paul's question?**  
**Answer:** The prophets.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:27

**Q2. What did Paul know that Agrippa believed?**  
**Answer:** The prophets.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:27

**Q3. Who asked Agrippa if he believed the prophets?**  
**Answer:** Paul.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:27

---

### Acts 26:28

**Q1. What did Agrippa say unto Paul?**  
**Answer:** Almost thou persuadest me to be a Christian.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:28

**Q2. Who said, Almost thou persuadest me to be a Christian?**  
**Answer:** Agrippa.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:28

**Q3. To be what was Agrippa almost persuaded?**  
**Answer:** A Christian.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:28

---

### Acts 26:29

**Q1. What did Paul pray to God that not only Agrippa, but also all that heard him this day, were?**  
**Answer:** Both almost, and altogether such as I am.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:29

**Q2. Except what bonds did Paul wish others to be like him?**  
**Answer:** These bonds.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:29

**Q3. Who did Paul wish to be like him, both almost and altogether?**  
**Answer:** Agrippa and all that heard him this day.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:29

---

### Acts 26:30

**Q1. When Paul had thus spoken, who rose up?**  
**Answer:** The king, and the governor, and Bernice, and they that sat with them.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:30

**Q2. Who sat with the king and the governor?**  
**Answer:** Bernice and they that sat with them.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:30

**Q3. What did the king, the governor, Bernice, and they that sat with them do when Paul had spoken?**  
**Answer:** Rose up.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:30

---

### Acts 26:31

**Q1. When they were gone aside, what did they talk between themselves about?**  
**Answer:** This man (Paul).  
**Verse:** Acts 26:31

**Q2. What did they say Paul had done?**  
**Answer:** Nothing worthy of death or of bonds.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:31

**Q3. What was Paul worthy of, according to what they said?**  
**Answer:** Nothing worthy of death or of bonds.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:31

---

### Acts 26:32

**Q1. What did Agrippa say unto Festus concerning Paul?**  
**Answer:** This man might have been set at liberty, if he had not appealed unto Caesar.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:32

**Q2. Who might have been set at liberty?**  
**Answer:** This man (Paul).  
**Verse:** Acts 26:32

**Q3. Why was Paul not set at liberty?**  
**Answer:** Because he had appealed unto Caesar.  
**Verse:** Acts 26:32
