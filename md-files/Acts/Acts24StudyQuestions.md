# Acts 24 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Acts 24:1

**Q1. Who descended after five days?**  
**Answer:** Ananias the high priest.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:1

**Q2. Who came down with the high priest?**  
**Answer:** Certain elders, and a certain orator named Tertullus.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:1

**Q3. Whom did they inform against Paul to?**  
**Answer:** To the governor.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:1

---

### Acts 24:2

**Q1. Who was called forth?**  
**Answer:** Paul.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:2

**Q2. Who began to accuse Paul?**  
**Answer:** Tertullus.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:2

**Q3. What did Tertullus say they enjoyed by Felix?**  
**Answer:** Great quietness, and that very worthy deeds were done unto this nation by his providence.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:2

---

### Acts 24:3

**Q1. In what manner did they accept these things?**  
**Answer:** Always, and in all places, most noble Felix, with all thankfulness.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:3

**Q2. What did Tertullus say they accepted?**  
**Answer:** All things.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:3

**Q3. Whom did Tertullus address as "most noble"?**  
**Answer:** Felix.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:3

---

### Acts 24:4

**Q1. What did Tertullus pray Felix not to do?**  
**Answer:** That he would not be further tedious unto him.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:4

**Q2. What did Tertullus desire Felix to hear?**  
**Answer:** A few words of his clemency.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:4

**Q3. Whose clemency did Tertullus ask to be heard?**  
**Answer:** Felix's.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:4

---

### Acts 24:5

**Q1. What did they find this man to be?**  
**Answer:** A pestilent fellow, and a mover of sedition among all the Jews throughout the world.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:5

**Q2. Of what sect was this man a ringleader?**  
**Answer:** Of the sect of the Nazarenes.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:5

**Q3. Among whom was he a mover of sedition?**  
**Answer:** Among all the Jews throughout the world.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:5

---

### Acts 24:6

**Q1. What did this man also try to do?**  
**Answer:** Profane the temple.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:6

**Q2. Whom did they take?**  
**Answer:** Paul.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:6

**Q3. According to what did they intend to judge him?**  
**Answer:** According to their law.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:6

---

### Acts 24:7

**Q1. Who came upon them?**  
**Answer:** The chief captain Lysias.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:7

**Q2. With how much violence did Lysias take Paul away out of their hands?**  
**Answer:** With great violence.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:7

**Q3. Out of whose hands was Paul taken?**  
**Answer:** Out of their hands.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:7

---

### Acts 24:8

**Q1. Whom did Lysias command to come unto Felix?**  
**Answer:** Paul's accusers.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:8

**Q2. By examining whom could Felix himself take knowledge of all these things whereof they accused Paul?**  
**Answer:** By examining him.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:8

**Q3. Whose accusers were commanded to come unto Felix?**  
**Answer:** Paul's accusers.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:8

---

### Acts 24:9

**Q1. Who assented along with the accusers?**  
**Answer:** The Jews also.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:9

**Q2. What did the Jews say about these things?**  
**Answer:** That these things were so.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:9

**Q3. What did the Jews do concerning the matter?**  
**Answer:** They assented.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:9

---

### Acts 24:10

**Q1. Who beckoned unto Paul to speak?**  
**Answer:** The governor.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:10

**Q2. How long did Paul know the governor had been a judge unto this nation?**  
**Answer:** Of many years.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:10

**Q3. How did Paul answer the governor?**  
**Answer:** With good cheer.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:10

---

### Acts 24:11

**Q1. How many days are there since Paul went up to Jerusalem for to worship?**  
**Answer:** Yet but twelve days.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:11

**Q2. What can Felix understand?**  
**Answer:** That there are yet but twelve days since Paul went up to Jerusalem for to worship.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:11

**Q3. Why did Paul go up to Jerusalem?**  
**Answer:** For to worship.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:11

---

### Acts 24:12

**Q1. Where did they not find Paul disputing with any man?**  
**Answer:** In the temple.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:12

**Q2. Whose congregation did Paul not neither raise up?**  
**Answer:** The people's congregation.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:12

**Q3. Where else did Paul not find himself disputing or raising up the people?**  
**Answer:** Neither in the synagogues, nor in the city.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:12

---

### Acts 24:13

**Q1. What can they not prove?**  
**Answer:** The things whereof they now accuse Paul.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:13

**Q2. Whose things can they not prove?**  
**Answer:** The things whereof they now accuse Paul.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:13

**Q3. What did they do to the things?**  
**Answer:** Accused Paul of them.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:13

---

### Acts 24:14

**Q1. What did Paul confess unto Felix?**  
**Answer:** That after the way which they call heresy, so worship he the God of his fathers.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:14

**Q2. What did Paul believe?**  
**Answer:** All things which are written in the law and in the prophets.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:14

**Q3. What did he call the way they spoke of?**  
**Answer:** Heresy.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:14

---

### Acts 24:15

**Q1. What hope did Paul have toward God?**  
**Answer:** That there shall be a resurrection of the dead.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:15

**Q2. Who did Paul say would also allow this hope?**  
**Answer:** That they themselves also allow.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:15

**Q3. Whose resurrection did Paul say there would be?**  
**Answer:** Both of the just and unjust.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:15

---

### Acts 24:16

**Q1. In this did Paul exercise himself?**  
**Answer:** To have always a conscience void of offence toward God, and toward men.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:16

**Q2. Toward whom did Paul want a conscience void of offence?**  
**Answer:** Toward God, and toward men.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:16

**Q3. How often did Paul exercise himself to have this conscience?**  
**Answer:** Always.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:16

---

### Acts 24:17

**Q1. After how many years did Paul come?**  
**Answer:** Many years.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:17

**Q2. What did Paul bring to his nation?**  
**Answer:** Alms, and offerings.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:17

**Q3. To what nation did Paul bring alms and offerings?**  
**Answer:** To his nation.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:17

### Acts 24:19

**Q1. Who ought to have been here before Felix?**  
**Answer:** Certain Jews from Asia.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:19

**Verse:** Acts 24:19

**Q2. Before whom did Paul stand?**  
**Answer:** Before the council.  
**Verse:** Acts 24:20

**Verse:** Acts 24:20

**Q2. When did Paul cry out this voice?**  
**Answer:** Standing among them.

**Verse:** Acts 24:21
