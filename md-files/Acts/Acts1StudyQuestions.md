# Acts 1 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Acts 1:1
**Q1. According to Acts 1:1, what is stated first?**  
**Answer:** The former treatise have I made.  
**Verse:** Acts 1:1

**Q2. What is declared next in Acts 1:1?**  
**Answer:** O Theophilus.  
**Verse:** Acts 1:1

**Q3. What further truth is given in Acts 1:1?**  
**Answer:** of all that Jesus began both to do and teach.  
**Verse:** Acts 1:1
