# Acts 27 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Acts 27:1

**Q1. Who was determined that they should sail into Italy?**  
**Answer:** It was determined that we should sail into Italy.  
**Verse:** Acts 27:1

**Q2. Who was delivered unto Julius, a centurion of Augustus' band?**  
**Answer:** Paul and certain other prisoners.  
**Verse:** Acts 27:1

**Q3. To what band did the centurion Julius belong?**  
**Answer:** Augustus' band.  
**Verse:** Acts 27:1

---

### Acts 27:2

**Q1. What ship did they enter that was about to sail by the coasts of Asia?**  
**Answer:** A ship of Adramyttium.  
**Verse:** Acts 27:2

**Q2. Who accompanied Paul when they launched?**  
**Answer:** Aristarchus, a Macedonian of Thessalonica.  
**Verse:** Acts 27:2

**Q3. With them, meaning Paul and the others, who was present?**  
**Answer:** Aristarchus.  
**Verse:** Acts 27:2

---

### Acts 27:3

**Q1. Where did they touch the next day?**  
**Answer:** Sidon.  
**Verse:** Acts 27:3

**Q2. How did Julius courteously treat Paul?**  
**Answer:** By giving him liberty to go unto his friends to refresh himself.  
**Verse:** Acts 27:3

**Q3. What did Julius permit Paul to do with his friends?**  
**Answer:** To refresh himself.  
**Verse:** Acts 27:3

---

### Acts 27:4

**Q1. Why did they sail under Cyprus?**  
**Answer:** Because the winds were contrary.  
**Verse:** Acts 27:4
