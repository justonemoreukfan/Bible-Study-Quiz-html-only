# Acts 28 Study Questions (KJV)

---

### Acts 28:1

**Q1. When they were escaped, what did they know?**  
**Answer:** That the island was called Melita.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:1

**Q2. Who showed them no little kindness?**  
**Answer:** The barbarous people.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:1

**Q3. What did the barbarous people kindle?**  
**Answer:** A fire.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:1

---

### Acts 28:2

**Q1. Because of what condition did the barbarous people receive them?**  
**Answer:** Because of the present rain, and because of the cold.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:2

**Q2. What did the barbarous people do for every one of them?**  
**Answer:** Received them.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:2

**Q3. What was kindled because of the rain and cold?**  
**Answer:** A fire.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:2

---

### Acts 28:3

**Q1. What did Paul gather?**  
**Answer:** A bundle of sticks.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:3

**Q2. What came out of the heat when Paul laid the sticks on the fire?**  
**Answer:** A viper.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:3

**Q3. What did the viper do to Paul's hand?**  
**Answer:** Fastened on his hand.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:3

---

### Acts 28:4

**Q1. When the barbarians saw the venomous beast hang on his hand, what did they say among themselves?**  
**Answer:** No doubt this man is a murderer, whom, though he hath escaped the sea, yet vengeance suffereth not to live.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:4

**Q2. What did the barbarians see hang on Paul's hand?**  
**Answer:** The venomous beast.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:4

**Q3. Who did the barbarians say had escaped the sea?**  
**Answer:** This man (Paul).  
**Verse:** Acts 28:4

---

### Acts 28:5

**Q1. What did Paul do to the beast?**  
**Answer:** Shook off the beast into the fire.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:5

**Q2. What happened to Paul after shaking off the beast?**  
**Answer:** He felt no harm.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:5

**Q3. Into what did Paul shake the beast?**  
**Answer:** Into the fire.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:5

---

### Acts 28:6

**Q1. What did the people look when Paul should have swollen or fallen down dead suddenly?**  
**Answer:** When he should have swollen, or fallen down dead suddenly.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:6

**Q2. After they had looked a great while and saw no harm come to him, what did they change their minds about?**  
**Answer:** They changed their minds, and said that he was a god.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:6

**Q3. What did the people say Paul was after seeing no harm come to him?**  
**Answer:** A god.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:6

---

### Acts 28:7

**Q1. In the same quarters, whose possessions were there?**  
**Answer:** The chief man of the island, named Publius.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:7

**Q2. What was the name of the chief man of the island?**  
**Answer:** Publius.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:7

**Q3. How did Publius receive and lodge them?**  
**Answer:** Courteously three days.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:7

---

### Acts 28:8

**Q1. Who lay sick of a fever and of a bloody flux?**  
**Answer:** The father of Publius.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:8

**Q2. Who entered in, and prayed, and laid his hands on him, and healed him?**  
**Answer:** Paul.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:8

**Q3. What two conditions did the father of Publius have?**  
**Answer:** A fever and a bloody flux.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:8

---

### Acts 28:9

**Q1. When this was done, who came and were healed?**  
**Answer:** Others also on the island, which had diseases.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:9

**Q2. What happened to the others on the island who had diseases?**  
**Answer:** They came, and were healed.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:9

**Q3. Who healed the others on the island?**  
**Answer:** (Implied: Paul).  
**Verse:** Acts 28:9

---

### Acts 28:10

**Q1. With what did the people honor Paul and his companions?**  
**Answer:** Many honors.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:10

**Q2. When they departed, what did the people lade them with?**  
**Answer:** Such things as were necessary.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:10

**Q3. Who laded them with such things as were necessary?**  
**Answer:** The people (of the island).  
**Verse:** Acts 28:10

---

### Acts 28:11

**Q1. After how many months did they depart in a ship?**  
**Answer:** After three months.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:11

**Q2. What had wintered in the isle?**  
**Answer:** A ship of Alexandria.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:11

**Q3. What was the sign of the ship that wintered in the isle?**  
**Answer:** Castor and Pollux.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:11

---

### Acts 28:12

**Q1. When they landed at Syracuse, how long did they tarry there?**  
**Answer:** Three days.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:12

**Q2. Where did they land after leaving the isle?**  
**Answer:** Syracuse.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:12

**Q3. How many days did they remain in Syracuse?**  
**Answer:** Three days.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:12

---

### Acts 28:13

**Q1. From whence did they fetch a compass and come to Rhegium?**  
**Answer:** From thence.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:13

**Q2. After how many days did the south wind blow and they came the next day to Puteoli?**  
**Answer:** After one day.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:13

**Q3. Where did the south wind bring them the next day after Rhegium?**  
**Answer:** Puteoli.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:13

---

### Acts 28:14

**Q1. Where did they find brethren?**  
**Answer:** At Puteoli.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:14

**Q2. How long were they desired to tarry with the brethren?**  
**Answer:** Seven days.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:14

**Q3. After tarrying with the brethren, where did they go?**  
**Answer:** Toward Rome.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:14

---

### Acts 28:15

**Q1. From whence, when the brethren heard of them, did they come to meet them?**  
**Answer:** From Rome.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:15

**Q2. To what places did the brethren come to meet Paul?**  
**Answer:** Appii forum, and The three taverns.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:15

**Q3. When Paul saw the brethren, what did he do?**  
**Answer:** He thanked God, and took courage.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:15

---

### Acts 28:16

**Q1. When we came to Rome, to whom was the centurion delivered the prisoners?**  
**Answer:** The captain of the guard.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:16

**Q2. Where was Paul suffered to dwell?**  
**Answer:** By himself.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:16

**Q3. Who was permitted to dwell with Paul?**  
**Answer:** A soldier that kept him.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:16

---

### Acts 28:17

**Q1. After three days, whom did Paul call together?**  
**Answer:** The chief of the Jews.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:17

**Q2. What did Paul say he had committed against the people or customs of their fathers?**  
**Answer:** Nothing.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:17

**Q3. From whence was Paul delivered prisoner into the hands of the Romans?**  
**Answer:** From Jerusalem.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:17

---

### Acts 28:18

**Q1. When the Romans had examined Paul, what would they have done?**  
**Answer:** Let him go.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:18

**Q2. Why would the Romans have let Paul go?**  
**Answer:** Because there was no cause of death in him.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:18

**Q3. Who examined Paul according to this verse?**  
**Answer:** The Romans.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:18

---

### Acts 28:19

**Q1. When the Jews spake against it, what was Paul constrained to do?**  
**Answer:** To appeal unto Caesar.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:19

**Q2. Did Paul have aught to accuse his nation of after appealing?**  
**Answer:** Not that I had aught to accuse my nation of.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:19

**Q3. Who spake against Paul's release?**  
**Answer:** The Jews.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:19

---

### Acts 28:20

**Q1. For this cause, what did Paul call for the Jews to see and speak with them?**  
**Answer:** To see you, and to speak with you.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:20

**Q2. For the hope of whom was Paul bound with this chain?**  
**Answer:** The hope of Israel.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:20

**Q3. What did Paul have on him while speaking to them?**  
**Answer:** This chain.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:20

---

### Acts 28:21

**Q1. What did the Jews say they had received concerning Paul from Judaea?**  
**Answer:** Neither received letters out of Judaea concerning thee.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:21

**Q2. Did any of the brethren that came out of Judaea show or speak any harm of Paul?**  
**Answer:** Neither any of the brethren that came showed or spake any harm of thee.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:21

**Q3. From where did the Jews say they received no letters concerning Paul?**  
**Answer:** Out of Judaea.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:21

---

### Acts 28:22

**Q1. What did they desire to hear of Paul?**  
**Answer:** What thou thinkest.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:22

**Q2. Concerning what sect did they know that everywhere it was spoken against?**  
**Answer:** As concerning this sect.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:22

**Q3. What did they know about this sect everywhere?**  
**Answer:** That it is spoken against.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:22

---

### Acts 28:23

**Q1. When they had appointed him a day, how many came to him into his lodging?**  
**Answer:** Many.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:23

**Q2. To whom did Paul expound and testify the kingdom of God?**  
**Answer:** Them.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:23

**Q3. Out of what two things did Paul persuade them concerning Jesus, from morning till evening?**  
**Answer:** Out of the law of Moses, and out of the prophets.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:23

---

### Acts 28:24

**Q1. What did some believe of the things which were spoken?**  
**Answer:** Some believed the things which were spoken.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:24

**Q2. What did some do regarding the things spoken?**  
**Answer:** Believed not.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:24

**Q3. Did all of them believe the things spoken by Paul?**  
**Answer:** No (some believed not).  
**Verse:** Acts 28:24

---

### Acts 28:25

**Q1. When they agreed not among themselves, what did they do?**  
**Answer:** They departed.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:25

**Q2. What did Paul say after that one word?**  
**Answer:** Well spake the Holy Ghost by Esaias the prophet unto our fathers.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:25

**Q3. By whom did the Holy Ghost speak unto their fathers?**  
**Answer:** By Esaias the prophet.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:25

---

### Acts 28:26

**Q1. What did the prophet tell Paul to say to this people regarding hearing?**  
**Answer:** Hearing ye shall hear, and shall not understand.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:26

**Q2. What did the prophet say regarding seeing?**  
**Answer:** Seeing ye shall see, and not perceive.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:26

**Q3. Who spoke the words quoted in this verse?**  
**Answer:** Esaias the prophet.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:26

---

### Acts 28:27

**Q1. Whose heart of this people is waxed gross?**  
**Answer:** For the heart of this people is waxed gross.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:27

**Q2. How were their ears described in relation to hearing?**  
**Answer:** Their ears are dull of hearing.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:27

**Q3. What have they closed lest they should see and hear?**  
**Answer:** Their eyes.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:27

---

### Acts 28:28

**Q1. Unto whom is the salvation of God sent?**  
**Answer:** Unto the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:28

**Q2. Will the Gentiles hear the salvation of God?**  
**Answer:** And that they will hear it.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:28

**Q3. What did Paul want the people to know about this salvation?**  
**Answer:** Be it known therefore unto you, that the salvation of God is sent unto the Gentiles.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:28

---

### Acts 28:29

**Q1. When Paul had said these words, what did the Jews do?**  
**Answer:** The Jews departed.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:29

**Q2. What did the Jews have among themselves?**  
**Answer:** Great reasoning among themselves.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:29

**Q3. Who departed with great reasoning among themselves?**  
**Answer:** The Jews.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:29

---

### Acts 28:30

**Q1. How long did Paul dwell in his own hired house?**  
**Answer:** Two whole years.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:30

**Q2. Whom did Paul receive in his own hired house?**  
**Answer:** All that came in unto him.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:30

**Q3. Where did Paul dwell for two whole years?**  
**Answer:** In his own hired house.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:30

---

### Acts 28:31

**Q1. What did Paul preach?**  
**Answer:** The kingdom of God.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:31

**Q2. What did Paul teach concerning the Lord Jesus Christ?**  
**Answer:** Those things which concern the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:31

**Q3. With what manner of speech did Paul teach those things?**  
**Answer:** With all confidence, no man forbidding him.  
**Verse:** Acts 28:31
